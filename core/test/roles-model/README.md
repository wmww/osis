# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules (spec/permissions.md §5), S3's vouches and addenda gaps, account custody and epochs (§5.4-§5.5), and of the design for stated paths with signers as members: modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm and osis-engine dev-depend on it for their differentials). Module map and API: `src/lib.rs`; findings behind the role timeline: notes/role-timeline.md; disputes: notes/disputes.md.

## Searches
`cargo run --bin roles-model -- <samples> <mode>`; random searches split their samples over every core, exhaustive ones their families.

Tests: the hand worlds are default tests; every sampled search (random worlds, family samples, a control failing a search) is a property, `_plain` at its small size and `_large` at the gate's (`#[ignore = "property"]`). Every roles-model gate selects them, and nothing else can move them (this crate depends on no osis crate), so as default tests they only re-proved the same answer at every other branch's gate. A worker changing the model runs them: `cargo test -p roles-model -- --ignored _plain _large`.

## Vouches (`src/vouches.rs`)
S3 brief 2 (notes/redesign-plan.md; notes/account-membership-design.md §4, Role writes: vouched for like content) on today's walk: authority as `edges` gives it (one thing, every node a key actor, a frontier is what its demoter received), with trust sets (`trust::side`). Rule, properties and controls in the module doc. `EHist::extra` carries counting entries into the walk as frontier entries; empty is today's rule, so `edges::walk`, the digest and osis-perm's differentials are unchanged. S3's code brief adds vouches to perm and then runs a differential against `vouches::judge`. Of what no list keeps only today's first half is modelled (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 4, R never demotes its own author): the rest reads the listing write's own path, which today's walk does not define; it is modelled in `paths` (below) and built with stated paths (notes/redesign-plan.md A1).

**Readings where the notes are open:**
- A vouch is a role-class write that is not an edge write: its position is the start of its `ts`, so "after R" is a later `ts`. Its author's authority for an entry is `EHist::would_apply` of its own commit with the named commit's ops at that position in the first walk (a node may always vouch for a lowering of its own edge: a self-lowering applies).
- Survival: an entry falls when an applied explicit write by another node lowering the voucher's edge follows, its author had not received the vouch, and the voucher can no longer write the entry after it. Re-grant does not revive it; the voucher writes again.
- Honest writing: at receipt of R, and at every later time the node newly holds the authority for a listed commit. `FIXED` reads that authority off the canonical first walk (idealised: no node computes it); `Rules::held` off the node's own walk of what it holds (`EHist::held_by`). `Rules::state` is the state-based writing: every commit reaches every node by `SYNC`, and a node keeps each listed commit it could write, in its own current walk, named by a counting vouch of its own, writing one whenever one is not. `Rules::stands` adds the writers' leaving out (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, What a list leaves out): where the removal, trusted alone, does not apply with what the remover received of its victim (or with a vouch as written), the remover (voucher) names those commits one by one in the order it received them, keeping each only where the removal still applies; a commit the remover leaves out reads as not received by it.
- Kept as promised (`kept_end`, under `Rules::state` only): spec/permissions.md §5.1, What vouches keep, in its checked form: the reference lists, beside the counting entries, each commit of R's victim a witness holding R received by R's `ts` and could write at the end of the first walk (`kept_end_extra`; osis-perm's differential compares its timeline with that reference too).
- Set-asides: listing's `lowered` and `accomplice` are read off the generated world (`lowered` only where A received X's lowering before R or a witness would vouch for it); safety's bargain is the differential "the op applies with only the entries whose voucher is not its author and could write it at the vouch's position".

**Limits found** (hand worlds in `src/vouches/tests.rs`):
- Listing on today's walk: H's list naming X's removal of H (received, or vouched for by a witness) keeps it, and H's trusted removal of X is void; with an empty list it applies (`a_removal_listing_the_thiefs_removal_of_its_author_is_void`). The writers' leaving out answers it, the accomplice's world too (`the_writers_leave_out_what_their_removal_does_not_apply_with`): with it the set-aside is 0 in the family and 2 of 100,000 random worlds, both where the voucher's own vouch against another lowering (a demotion to write) keeps X's timed grant to the voucher, which lapses before the voucher's own later removal: `World::derive` chooses removers' lists before any vouch exists, and with them chosen again once the vouches are written (as the remover, writing later, would) the set-aside is 0 in both. The listing property holds only where nothing writes an edge H's removal may rest on below admin. Reading such an entry as absent needs H's stated path: read as "any grant to H", a removed node writes a junk grant to H and strikes an earlier removal of itself from H's list (`a_removed_node_writing_a_grant_to_the_remover_stays_removed`).
- An accomplice: X grants Y admin, Y removes H; H's removals of X and of Y stay void while listing what H received; a removal of Y omitting Y's removal of H applies both (`an_accomplices_removal_is_answered_by_a_removal_omitting_it`; notes/disputes.md, Hole: an accomplice).
- Writing from the held set misses where the witness never received a lowering of its own edge that the remover's list keeps: its vouch at receipt of R does not count, and its view never shows it newly holding the right, so it never writes again, even after a re-grant (`a_witness_unaware_of_its_own_demotion_never_vouches_again`). S3's code brief needs a writing trigger beyond "first holds the right in its own view": the state-based writing (`Rules::state`, eventual delivery) keeps that world and the whole family. In two of 1,000,000 random worlds it misses (`roles-model -- 1000000 vouches-state`: worlds 612558, kept and stays made, and 74440, one stays-made op), both for one cause: the witness held the authority as it applied R only through a commit it had not yet received (in 74440 the thief's own hidden backdated demotion of the node that had demoted the witness, which drops that demotion), then gave up its own edge, by its own later write, before receiving it. Kept's "holds the authority as it applies R" reads the canonical walk, which no node writing from its held set can know then: the oracle asks too much, not a hole of the rule. Under stated paths the state-based writing holds (Paths, below). The search's shrinking rewrites the honest vouches under the state rule at every step and runs for tens of minutes on one core: scan by world index instead.
- A node X lowered but keeping an invite vouches for its own backdated invite grant, which nobody else received, and it applies at its own `ts` (`a_demoted_node_vouches_for_its_own_backdated_invite`). "Nobody vouches for its own signer's commit" closes it; S3 leaves it to A1.
- A demotion X made that only its victim received stays made: the victim vouches for it (`a_demotion_only_its_victim_received_stays_made`).

## Addenda gaps (`src/addenda.rs`, `Gaps`)
S3 brief 3 on today's rules (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, Addenda reach admin-only content, Writing): `Kind::AdminOnly` is content on an admin-only aspect that is not role-class, judged with admin in place of write against demotions below admin (`joint::Steps::demotions_admin`, spec/permissions.md §5.2); `Gaps::admin_only` lets an addendum by a witness holding admin at its key list it; `Gaps::rewrite` makes a witness lacking the role as it applies the removal write its addendum when it first holds it after (else, today, never). `Gaps::default()` is today's rule, so `random_world`, `addenda-exh` and osis-perm's `addenda_model_agrees` are unchanged; S3's code brief flips perm to `GAPS` and the differential with it.

Properties (`check_gaps`): **reached** (a non-forged plain or admin-only commit of X, ordered no later than a demotion of X below the role it needs, that a node other than X and the demoter received before the demotion's `ts`, which received the demotion and holds that role at the end, is valid), one result for every input order, monotone, forged addenda gaining nothing beyond their author's power (admin for admin-only content). The gaps family is `exhaustive_worlds` with Bob an admin whose first write is admin-only, "grant Carol admin" in place of "remove Carol" and "demote Bob to write" in place of the re-grant.

## Epochs (`src/epochs.rs`)
Layer 2 of the account (spec/permissions.md §5.5) over `custody`'s chain: a world is a settled custody world (its parties, fight and settle) plus P's commits on one to three things in two sync IDs, the rotators' thing lists, listings, R1's and R2's addenda as writers of a thing, and in half the worlds a bound (2 or 3 IDs per thing list), written by the cutoff after the chain world is made. A lister or witness answers as an epoch comes to count at it (a rotation held with its chain, or its side moving), never again as what it holds changes; a commit of P counts as received there once its epoch is held with its chain (its signature verifies then, spec/permissions.md §5.5 Cutoff: `World::dt`). Each node's chain is custody's `View`; the module judges only what counts of P's commits (rule, policy and properties in the module doc). osis-perm `epochs_model_agrees` feeds every observer and receipt prefix of every world to `osis_perm::Epochs`, the chain (held, counting, heads, preferred head, warning, revocations taken) and the epoch rule (pending, counting, saved by an addendum) alike.

In half the worlds nodes snapshot and forget (spec/commits.md §5): outside writers and X snapshot a thing (P's honest nodes too in half of those), a cover carrying every write showing at its writer; a node forgets another's write a standing cover it holds carries (only its writer forgets under an own cover) and receives it anew once none does; an outside writer's covers fall at a random tick or never (its demotion by an admin whose frontier names none, the role layer's abstracted). Before its plain rotation an honest rotator writes its own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.5) of each thing where a write stamped since the epoch it rotates opened shows only through a cover, named by its list. An honest node writes a snapshot only where every write showing on the thing lasts there (Carrying: as once every head closes, an open head counting only for what a word or its cutoff names); else it waits. Re-adds: where custody's A re-adds T (removed by C), T writes at the first epoch before the settle (offline, its writes leave only then, or online from tick 2), takes its removal at the settle and comes back onto its roster at the re-add; a node coming back onto its roster answers then (off it, it answered nothing), naming its own writes from before the re-add (spec/permissions.md §5.5 Listings: no other node names them for it). A node offline in custody sends nothing before its floor.

Properties at every final reading: safety (an X write after its removal shows on the honest side only through an honest word written off the honest side or about what its writer received before it held the removal, at an open head (an unaware sibling's) while it is open, or through a cover carrying it by the first two: `allowed`), liveness (every write of a node on the honest side shows there, and every other write an honest-side lister's cutoff names), monotone (dropping an honest list, listing or addendum never makes more count), own cover (where a rotation counts and its rotator ends on the honest side, every write but X's after its removal that showed at the rotator only through a cover as it rotated shows). "Shows": counts, saved by an addendum, or carried by a standing cover. Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib epochs`) are the minimal world of each, each asserting its control fails: `a_write_received_before_the_removal_counts_a_later_one_does_not` (`NoCutoff`), `a_write_only_a_witness_received_in_time_counts_by_its_addendum` (`OwnOnly`), `a_siblings_write_at_its_epoch_counts_once_listed_after_the_merge`, `a_write_the_remover_forgot_shows_by_its_own_cover` (`NoOwnCover`: X's write before its removal reaches A and R1 only, both forget it under R1's snapshot, R1 is demoted), `an_open_heads_write_lasts_in_no_snapshot` (`OpenCarried`: X writes at unaware B's head after its removal; C, holding both heads, would snapshot it at an epoch no removal revokes, carrying it past the merge), `a_re_added_nodes_write_from_before_its_re_add_counts_by_its_own_listing` (`NoReanswer`: T, off its roster, answered nothing; coming back without answering, its write before the re-add, received by every other node after its removal, is lost). Properties: 20 random worlds (`random_worlds_hold_plain`) and each control but `NoOwnCover` and `OpenCarried` failing within 20; `random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000) the twin. Answering again whenever what a node holds changes (the spec's target once) names nothing more: every word it would add came from dating a receipt before its epoch's chain was held.

Choices the spec left open: a receipt in a tick comes before the receiver's own acts that tick and after nothing else (`World::dt`), and receipts of one tick are in exact order (the spec's (`ts`, author, commit ID), P the author of both: the tick each was made, a rotation first, `World::before`); a listing names per thing whatever it owes, not keyed by rotation (judgment never reads the key); only thing lists are bounded; a rotation's plain content past the bound is named by its rotator's later listings by the cutoff like anything else (named whatever the cutoff, a write X made after its removal, left out on X's side, was named by a listing after its rotator's switch: 1 world in 8,000). Not modelled: pairings of new devices, a refused rotation (the chain world cannot undo it: such worlds, about 1 in 9, skip liveness).

## Custody (`src/custody.rs`)
notes/engine-validity-epochs.md, greenfield, the chain under `epochs`: seed custody follows holding (a node opens every box to its key and derives every merge seed whose tips' seeds it holds, counting or not), the chain is D's roster (an epoch is its line of plain rotations; nodes on a line are the creation devices plus additions minus removals carried there; a removal hits its node's rotations off its line), a merge is only a join (its epoch the union of the joined lines, its seed a function of its tips'). Rule, parties, honest policy and properties in the module doc. Parties: A (removes X; in the settle, in some worlds where C removed T, re-adds T once its line carries that removal: `readd`, boxing T the seeds it holds of the line), B (offline, unaware: rotates boxing X), C (apart: removes T and rotates thing t at its own epoch, the listing-cycle shape), X (every header and old key: forks, revokes A, B or C, signs as X or as Z, adds Z or itself, takes A's removal or a revocation it withholds, answers honest rotations, certifies merges of epochs whose seeds it holds, re-seals its seeds to honest nodes, writes and lists), T (reads only), R1 (outside reader holding t), R2 (outside reader whose user may switch). Schedules random and lockstep; the removal reaching B and C only in the settle in half the worlds (they take X's revocation first); P's users on X's side switch to A's removal in the settle (B in round 0, 1 or 3, or never). Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib custody`) are the minimal world of each property, each also asserting its negative control fails.

The gate runs `custody::random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000 random worlds, about 125 CPU-s; every control below fails at 800) and `small_family_sample_holds_plain` (every 40th small-family world); `custody-exh` is a search, run by hand when `custody.rs` changes (about 2,600-2,900 CPU-s, over the gate's whole properties share).

Searches: `cargo run --bin roles-model -- N custody` (random), `-- 0 custody-exh` (the small family: 32 setups (schedule, B switching in round 1 or never, B offline, C apart removing T, X's tick-0 rotation) × the removal late to B and C or not × one move of X at tick 2 from a menu of nine (revoking A, revoking B boxed to B, revoking A claiming A's removal, revoking A taking one withheld, signing as Z adding Z, answering an honest rotation, a write, a merge certificate, a re-seal; at each epoch whose seed it holds; delivered to everyone, to B, C and R1 only, or no one before the settle) × the honest move at tick 3); negative controls `custody-judged`, `custody-roster-bare`, `custody-merge-random`, `custody-stale-shut`, `custody-merge-carries`, `custody-readd-beside` (random, N worlds). Properties: 40 random worlds and every 40th world of two small-family setups (`_plain`); `random_worlds_hold_large` the twin of the first, `custody-exh` of the second.

Choices the model fixed in the design (notes/engine-validity-epochs.md): a certificate's inner signature opens with any joined epoch's seed (else a node holding only some joined seeds reads certificates of one epoch otherwise); a plain rotation for a taken revocation is owed only while the node cannot merge (a taken revocation counts, so some head carries it); an answer is owed only to a rotation that does not stand or stands with a revocation not taken (not to one waiting on its parent epoch); D's roster removes only by taken revocations (a revocation held with its chain but not taken, its line merging a fork signed as a made-up key actor, removes no one: `a_held_revocation_not_taken_removes_no_one`). A certificate by a node a taken removal hits stands with nothing, so at a node on X's side an honest certificate may not stand while another of the same epoch does: certificates are compared among those standing at the node. Fixed by osis-perm's `epochs_model_agrees` (where the model contradicted the spec): rotations held in one tick are placed by tie (the differential's IDs; merge epochs after plain rotations, `epochs::MERGE_TIE`) before depth, as receipts of one millisecond by ID; a certificate is signed at its tips (`joined`, each one's merge signature), not at the heads it joins, so a rotation signed at a merge epoch a larger merge took in is not contested from below by it. A rotation stands only where every merge it is signed at has a certificate by a node of its line none hits, as the merge itself (a revocation at a merge X certified as a made-up key actor is taken nowhere, X's own view included: `a_revocation_through_a_merge_no_node_certified_is_taken_nowhere`). **Readings where the notes are open** (each one field of `Rules`, an argument, or one function):
- Vouching (`Vouch::BaseWalk`): a vouch names a removal and role writes on one thing; it counts when its path gives admin on that thing at its key in the walk with no vouch counting, and that walk applies its removal (finding 22); a counting vouch keeps what it names out of that removal's audit and seeds. `SameWalk` (judged in the walk it changes) has no fixpoint.
- Entries (`Entry::OnThing`): a counting entry keeps its commit's parts on the vouch's thing alone (finding 40); control `-vouch-whole` (the commit whole: an admin of T1 alone keeps a part on T2).
- A frontier entry names every part of its commit, in whichever leaf the node sees it (`World::in_frontier`).
- Re-grant (`Regrant::FirstChained`): from each edge's first chained grant.
- A removal is judged in the world its list makes: nothing is exempt from a lowering's drops and no entry is read as absent. Each lowering is judged once (finding 16; notes/write-path-design.md §1): the first time a pass reaches it holding its right and taking its edge from admin, it is void (`self_void`) where its own drops would take its authority, read back along the setters of its path (`keeps`), else applied, its drops added, the pass ended there and the next resumed from the earliest; the verdict stands for the walk (an applied one applies unaudited in every later pass unless dropped, and its drops stay; where its value is above its edge as restored in that pass it applies only by admin along its path, finding 27). A lowering without its right, or taking nothing, gets no verdict and is judged by a later pass. Control `-no-void`: a lowering whose drops take its authority applies. A trusted write the settled walk does not apply loses its seeds, all at once. Control `exempt_chain`: a removal never puts in question the grants its own path rests on, read from the held writes.
- The audit puts in question the held writes before it resting on its edge, not dropped and not void, as the seeds do (`audit_held`, finding 18); control `-audit-applied`, the applied ones.
- Last admin (`LastAdmin::Named`): an unmarked write taking a standing admin edge (admin, no `until`) to anything else, `until` included, applies only where the remaining admin is a standing admin edge other than the lowered one, as the pass stands at the write (its commit's earlier parts in effect): the path's last edge (`last_hop`: its final hop where that joins its last member to the thing in one edge; a descent of two steps or more and a self-removal have none), else the holder it names. A judged lowering is also void where the remaining admin it relies on stands no more past its drops and those of its commit's earlier lowerings (`remains_kept`). Controls: `AnyEdge` (every admin edge of the thing, timed or not), `NamedTimed`, `-remains-unkept`, `-last-any` (the edge (Ak, T) wherever it is).
- A commit of several edge writes on one target (`World::commit`; spec/commits.md §2): judged part by part like any, at its key: parts in holder order but the part setting an edge of the commit's stated path (a self-removal of the path's last member, as in a single-use link redemption, or the record edge a descent ends with; with both, in holder order) last, each judged with the earlier parts in effect (its path, invite and last-admin rule); a lowering's `keeps` passes over the drops of the commit's earlier lowerings (`commit_drops`; control `-own-drops`); no part's drops or seeds reach a part of its own commit (no list can name it); the pass ends once the commit took effect. Two parts on one holder's edge are bad input (the `roles.0` delta's keys would repeat). - `no_own_addendum`, `no_own_vouch`: no addendum or vouch counts for a commit of its own signer.
- `evidence_leaves`: a node sees a frontier's leaf for the things it syncs and every thing an edge write it holds as evidence is on.
- `leave_in_question`: a lowering of edge h puts a self-removal of h in question like a write through h (finding 14), a timed one and a holder thing's included.

- `stands` (writing policy): a remover whose walk does not apply its removal (trusted: with its seeds) with everything named, and a voucher likewise (judging the removal trusted beside its own trust set, whichever side it is on), names the role writes in the order received, each kept only where the removal still applies; `key_order` (control) in key order. A self-removal only lowers (it removes the grant, or raises none of role, invite and `until`; else it applies nowhere) and audits nothing; an `until` passing audits nothing.
- Descent: consecutive path members p, q are joined by the record edges from p down to q where p is a mint ancestor of q, else by their direct edge (so [A] reaches D1 by descent, [A2, T1] by A2's grant on T1 and T1's record edge).

- Keys (`Withdraw`): `AsWritten` reads spec/crypto.md §4; `SideOnly` is the fix.
- Invites (`invite_check`): an edge write carries `invite` (at most its role; an admin edge gives every invite). - What falls (`any_fall`): a lowering is judged, and audits, when it takes its edge as restored down in role or in invite (finding 23); its drops are the writes resting on the edge whose need there the restored value met and the new one does not: admin for a write applied by admin, a lowering, a vouch, a self-removal of the edge; for an invite grant (applied as one, or held and not applied) its role on the edges before the last and its role as an invite on the last; write for a finalize. - Purge authors (`purge_chain`, `purge_exact`): a commit states an index into [signer, path..]; a purge request by author P (`Kind::Purge`) is content-class, judged as a content write along its path (listed, re-granted; no addendum vouches for it, spec/permissions.md §6; vouches for purge requests, which membership §4 names, are not modelled); it holds the `author` right (the only class modelled) over the earlier deltas on its thing whose purge author is exactly P iff it is valid and P is on its chain. - One stated path (`World::stated`, notes/path-form.md): a commit states one path for every thing it writes; a thing that is the path's last is written along the path less itself, any other one hop past the end. The split form (`World::split`): one commit, each thing its own path, one purge author a member of every thing's path. Parts keep their members, so no rule reads either.

So a cover by a writer not yet holding a removal carries the removed party's late write for good: the accepted window (finding 26), counted in the story (`window`). The variant `-facts` (rejected: every snapshot would state each carried commit's signer, `ts` and path): a carried write is judged by its own facts, a valid cover standing in only for a commit not held. Bodies, keep bounds and refetching are not modelled.
- An honest explicit write carries its author's frontier, a re-grant included (finding 25).
- End dates (`Rules::until`, `Until::Plain`): a write giving admin at its `ts` with an `until` earlier than its edge's own end (a standing admin given an end date, or an end shortened) is a grant: it applies where its path gives admin in the pass, carries no frontier, is no candidate; the last-admin rule alone reads it. Variants (finding 38), each failing a required gate: `-until-at-key` (a lowering judged at its key: its drops, the writes resting on its edge whose need its value does not meet at their own `ts`, are none, so its one effect is the verdict) and `-until-past-end` (its drops and seeds also the later writes on its edge stamped at or past the end).

**Not modelled.** Bodies (which node holds a body, keep bounds, refetching after a cover falls); purge classes other than `author`, and a purge by author reaching copies of a commit not held; an own cover standing whatever demotes its author later; vouches for invite grants and purge requests; accomplices granted inside the theft window; sessions and overlapping dots; hidden mode; the thief's own rotations of A and secrecy; a user keeping what nobody received; re-keys of T, re-seals; bodies dropped as invalid on the wrong side; a node choosing its side by first received (sides are given); promotion (nothing reads whether a thing is independent); the differential against osis-perm.
