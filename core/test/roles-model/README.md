# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules (spec/permissions.md §5) and of account signing epochs (issues/account-signing.md): modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm dev-depends on it for its differentials). Module map and API: `src/lib.rs`; findings behind the role timeline: notes/permissions.md; disputes: notes/disputes.md. Choices the spec or plan left open: AMBIGUITIES.md.

## Epochs (`src/epochs.rs`)
One account P (node 0 the remover, X = 1, Y = 2, maybe a sibling), witness writers of other accounts, an optional admin demoting P on thing 0, one outsider. A removal is two commits: the removal commit on the devices actor at the old epoch, received by P's nodes alone on its own schedule, and the rotation, whose devices-actor list names it; a sibling boxes X unless it holds the removal commit, and rotates from the new epoch only once it holds it (the seed sits under the devices actor's read epoch the removal mints). A rotation whose chain is not held is pending: it rejects nothing and is no side. Things sit in one or two labels (a thing may sit in both); every node opens some labels. Generated: content and snapshots by any node, X's removal and in some worlds Y's after it, X re-added, X's own rotation shortly before its removal (made offline: a sibling may write or rotate at its epoch first), a sibling's concurrent rotation (made before the removal reaches it, boxing X), removed nodes' forks, counter-merges, X forking plainly from its old epoch again after every rotation of P's (the world ends with one unrejected), node 0 gone for good after its removals (it acts and receives no more, still on the roster), backdated writes and own listings, witness snapshots that fall, part bounds of 1 or 2 IDs, covered commits omitted or listed; honest listings and addenda after every change. The rules under test: a counting rotation closes every epoch that does not descend from it (siblings' concurrent epochs included); forks stand: every head is open, and any member of P rotates (superseding every head) when one is due from it (`Builder::due`: a removed node's rotation no authorized one supersedes, or a head whose line lacks a removal it holds with its rotation), never for a fork alone, none designated, rotations made at once standing; a rotation is signed at each fork it names, and a rotation signed at e rejects nothing on a line through e; one cutoff for every lister (others' commits received before the earliest rotation ending their epoch, `World::ends`: one descending from it, or beside it naming a removal its line lacks; a witness counts every closer); a rotation names only the forks D's roster authorizes, judged from what the merger holds (no rotation on their line beside a removal of its minter, `World::stands`), and a commit at an epoch left void counts once named, as at a closed epoch (honest nodes list their own there). Checked at every observer and every prefix of its receipts:
- Safety: no write by a node while removed counts at an observer siding with an honest line no removal leaves beside it and holding that removal with its chain; counted apart: a removed node's fork counting and making it valid, or a head beside the removal (a sibling's concurrent rotation, or the removed node's own from before it) still open (the window until a rotation of P's arrives; the write reads invalid without those rotations), an unaware sibling's rotation or listing naming it (stated limit), a re-added node's later word reviving it, a witness's valid snapshot carrying it (stated limit). A write valid because of something signed at a sibling's concurrent epoch once that epoch is closed is a hard failure of its own.
- Re-forks hide nothing: at an owner-sided observer holding the forker's removal, once the honest answers are in (its last reading), dropping a removed node's plain forks (rejecting nothing, nothing built on them) and their lists shows no more honest content. Counted: observer-prefixes with a removed node's rotation on a head's line (and of those in a dispute, told), and those reading an honest fork.
- Removal commit: wherever the rotation following it counts, a held removal commit counts (it is the rotator's own commit, named by its devices-actor list).
- Removal stands: at an observer holding a removal with its chain where every removed node's rotation it holds is signed at an epoch node 0's line is signed at (the removal's own, or a sibling's fork node 0's merge named), the removal counts on the observer's default side. This is the only check of the merge's fork signatures and of "rejects nothing on a line through e": without them the contest ends in a dispute, which the safety check (owner-sided observers only) does not see.
- Liveness, at observers holding everything an honest party holds under their labels: every legitimate content commit (at any epoch, void ones included) some party whose word stands received in time (oracle from the history alone: the receipt or authorship of a final member not gone, before the earliest honest rotation ending the commit's epoch, a witness's before the earliest rotation the epoch does not descend from, a rotator's list, a chain of honest listings; an epoch no honest rotation ends stays a head) is visible; nothing is visible beyond the upper bound (receipts before any closer's `ts`).
- Monotone: dropping an honest list, listing or addendum never makes more valid.
- Omit-covered loses nothing against listing every commit.

Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib epochs`) state the properties of the build plan's steps 4 and 5 on small worlds; `removed_node_holding_a_sibling_epoch` is the minimal sibling-epoch world; `any_node_supersedes_a_sibling_epoch_with_the_remover_gone` and `a_standing_fork_loses_no_write_when_its_writer_goes` the minimal worlds for no designated rotator and for `World::ends`.

### Results
| Run | Worlds | Hard failures |
|---|---|---|
| `-- 200000 epochs` | 200,000 (104,314 with a sibling's or X's own rotation, 50,910 re-forking after every rotation, 44,300 with the remover gone for good; honest content visible at a void epoch 1,510) | 0 |
| `-- 0 epochs-exh` A (one label) | 1,127,364 | 0 |
| `-- 0 epochs-exh` B (labels, successive removals, re-add, bound) | 1,283,040 | 0 |
| `-- 0 epochs-exh-c` C (a sibling rotating after a removal it has not received; X rotating before it; the remover gone) | 1,443,420 | 0 |

Forks, random run: observer-prefixes reading an honest fork (several heads, none a removed node's) 1,058,263; with a removed node's rotation on a head's line 1,576,638 (in a dispute, told: 76,890); a removed node's write valid through a head beside its removal still open 3,348 (until a node of P supersedes it). With node 0 the only member that rotates, or with every closer ending an epoch for the cutoff (no `World::ends`), 3000 random worlds fail liveness (18 and 8 worlds): a designated rotator gone for good, or a standing fork, leaves honest writes unnamed (hand tests `any_node_supersedes_a_sibling_epoch_with_the_remover_gone`, `a_standing_fork_loses_no_write_when_its_writer_goes`). Settle re-forks once, after the last rotation, and leaves that fork unrejected: re-forks hide nothing at the last reading.

With the old epoch rule (a void epoch's commits never count) the random run fails liveness, and `honest_write_at_a_removed_nodes_rotation_counts` fails; with rotations naming every head minted by a current node (a sibling's line on X's rotation included), rotations void themselves (removal voided, liveness).

With the sibling free to rotate from the new epoch before holding the removal commit (`wait_for_removal` off), 300,000 random worlds give 609 safety and 199 liveness failures: the sibling boxes X a seed descending from the removal (hand test `sibling_waits_for_the_removal_commit`).

Random run, dimension counts: several labels 66,458 worlds; a thing in two labels 47,830; the rotator missing a label holding P's content 16,321; successive removals 65,779; re-adds 66,346; a part past the bound 9,119; a rotation refused by the bound 4,919 (3,965 left a fork standing: final readings not checked); a sibling waiting for the removal commit 10,411. Final readings: a listing at a closed epoch counting 153,414 times; content saved under a label the rotator did not open 6,278; a re-added node's writes from while removed revived 15,069; plain content past the bound saved 7,506. Counted: an observer holding a removal's rotation without its removal commit and a write by the removed node 1,320,688 observer-prefixes (22,744 at P's nodes); fork window 240,683; unaware sibling 1,416; listed write pending its part 221,309; disputes in 22,026 worlds (74,771 observer-prefixes sided with a thief by default, not checked by the safety check).

Family A: 2 P nodes, 1 witness, 1 outsider, one label and thing, X removed at 3; one or two content commits (author, time 1..4, backdated, receipts immediate, late or never), X's rotation before or after its removal or none, X's counter-merge, a snapshot none / by node 0 / by the witness (falling or not), covered commits omitted or listed. Family B: 3 P nodes, labels L0 and L1, things in L0, in both, in L1; node 0 opens L0 or both, Y one or both, X both; a witness on the shared thing or the L1 thing; the outsider one label or both; X removed then Y, X removed and re-added, or both; bound none or 1; one content commit (author, thing, time 2/4/7, plain or special, receipts of node 0, the other P node and the witness immediate, late or never) alone or with one of four companions. Family C: 3 P nodes (S = 2), a witness and an outsider, one label and thing; X removed at 3, its rotation and removal commit reaching S at 7 and 7, 4 and 7, or 7 and 4; S tries to rotate from 3, 4 or 5 while it lacks either (boxing X unless it holds the removal commit, waiting while it holds the rotation alone), reaching node 0 after 1 tick, or after 3 with node 0 gone for good at 6 (S and node 0 rotating whenever one is due); X does nothing, forks at 6 from the newest epoch it holds, or also counter-merges at 9, and lists its own writes or not; one content commit (node 0, S, or X at its newest epoch or epoch 0, time 2/5/7, each other node's receipt immediate, late or never) alone or with one of four companions; no observer sides with X in it.
