# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules (spec/permissions.md §5), S3's vouches, what no list keeps and addenda gaps, account custody and epochs (§5.4-§5.5), and of the design for stated paths with signers as members: modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm and osis-engine dev-depend on it for their differentials). Module map and API: `src/lib.rs`; findings behind the role timeline: notes/role-timeline.md; disputes: notes/disputes.md.

## Vouches (`src/vouches.rs`)
S3's removal-gap rules (notes/redesign-plan.md S3 briefs 2-3; notes/account-membership-design.md §4, Role writes and The list) on today's walk: authority as `edges` gives it (one thing, every node a key actor, a frontier is what its demoter received), with trust sets (`trust::side`). Rule, properties and controls in the module doc. `edges::Lists` carries the two rules into the walk (counting entries added to frontiers; what no list keeps); its default is today's rule, so `edges::walk`, the digest and osis-perm's differentials are unchanged, and osis-perm (which has neither rule) is compared against the default only. S3's code brief adds the rules to perm and then runs a differential against `vouches::judge`.

**Readings where the notes are open:**
- A vouch is a role-class write that is not an edge write: its position is the start of its `ts`, so "after R" is a later `ts`. Its author's authority for an entry is `EHist::would_apply` of its own commit with the named commit's ops at that position in the first walk (a node may always vouch for a lowering of its own edge: a self-lowering applies).
- Survival: an entry falls when an applied explicit write by another node lowering the voucher's edge follows, its author had not received the vouch, and the voucher can no longer write the entry after it. Re-grant does not revive it; the voucher writes again.
- Honest writing: at receipt of R, and at every later time the node newly holds the authority for a listed commit, read off the canonical first walk (a node's own view of its authority is not modelled).
- What no list keeps: an entry naming an op of X that sets an edge R's authority rests on (R's author's own, and its grantors' closed, statically: `EHist::path`), other than X's own edge, to a value leaving it without admin at R's `ts`, is read as absent in R's audit and in R's seeds when trusted; vouches' entries are struck against R's author, the list they join. Lowerings within admin (an invite, a later `until`) are listed as any write. X's own leaving is exempt: a self-lowering audits nothing, so it takes nothing from a grant X made (striking it gave X its role back when its grantee then lowered it). A static "lowers its prior value" test was wrong both ways (a prior set by a write the walk skips).

**Results** (properties in the module doc; the family: H, X, W admins and K without an edge, up to three writes of X (grant K admin or write, demote W, remove H or W) received by H and W or not, H removing X, W receiving it or not, H re-granting or removing W, K granting or removing W, vouches reaching H or not):

**Limits found** (hand worlds in `src/vouches/tests.rs`):
- An accomplice: X grants Y admin, Y removes H, H received both and removes X. Trusted alone, H's removal is void as written and applies with an empty list: what no list keeps strikes X's writes, and Y's removal is Y's. H removing Y as well (both trusted) applies both, the rule striking Y's removal of H from H's removal of Y (`an_accomplices_removal_is_answered_by_removing_the_accomplice`). So an honest remover facing an accomplice must remove it too; nothing in S3 does that for it.
- A node X lowered but keeping an invite vouches for its own backdated invite grant, which nobody else received, and it applies at its own `ts` (`a_demoted_node_vouches_for_its_own_backdated_invite`; 3 random worlds in 1M). "Nobody vouches for its own signer's commit" closes it; S3 leaves it to A1.
- A demotion X made that only its victim received stays made: the victim vouches for it (`a_demotion_only_its_victim_received_stays_made`). Read literally, "an entry lowering an edge of the listing write's own path" would strike it from the victim's own vouch; the model strikes against R's author only.

## Addenda gaps (`src/addenda.rs`, `Gaps`)
S3 brief 4 on today's rules (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, Addenda reach admin-only content, Writing): `Kind::AdminOnly` is content on an admin-only aspect that is not role-class, judged with admin in place of write against demotions below admin (`joint::Steps::demotions_admin`, spec/permissions.md §5.2); `Gaps::admin_only` lets an addendum by a witness holding admin at its key list it; `Gaps::rewrite` makes a witness lacking the role as it applies the removal write its addendum when it first holds it after (else, today, never). `Gaps::default()` is today's rule, so `random_world`, `addenda-exh` and osis-perm's `addenda_model_agrees` are unchanged; S3's code brief flips perm to `GAPS` and the differential with it.

Properties (`check_gaps`): **reached** (a non-forged plain or admin-only commit of X, ordered no later than a demotion of X below the role it needs, that a node other than X and the demoter received before the demotion's `ts`, which received the demotion and holds that role at the end, is valid), one result for every input order, monotone, forged addenda gaining nothing beyond their author's power (admin for admin-only content). The gaps family is `exhaustive_worlds` with Bob an admin whose first write is admin-only, "grant Carol admin" in place of "remove Carol" and "demote Bob to write" in place of the re-grant.

It is notes/addenda.md's body loss, which only keeping invalid bodies closes (notes/priorities.md, Whole commits tail: invalid bodies kept at stores).

## Epochs (`src/epochs.rs`)
Layer 2 of the account (spec/permissions.md §5.5) over `custody`'s chain: a world is a settled custody world (its parties, fight and settle) plus P's commits on one to three things in two sync IDs, the rotators' thing lists, listings, R1's and R2's addenda as writers of a thing, and in half the worlds a bound (2 or 3 IDs per thing list), written by the cutoff after the chain world is made. A lister or witness answers as an epoch comes to count at it (a rotation held with its chain, or its side moving), never again as what it holds changes; a commit of P counts as received there once its epoch is held with its chain (its signature verifies then, spec/permissions.md §5.5 Cutoff: `World::dt`). Each node's chain is custody's `View`; the module judges only what counts of P's commits (rule, policy and properties in the module doc). osis-perm `epochs_model_agrees` feeds every observer and receipt prefix of every world to `osis_perm::Epochs`, the chain (held, counting, heads, preferred head, warning, revocations taken) and the epoch rule (pending, counting, saved by an addendum) alike.

In half the worlds nodes snapshot and forget (spec/commits.md §5): outside writers and X snapshot a thing (P's honest nodes too in half of those), a cover carrying every write showing at its writer; a node forgets another's write a standing cover it holds carries (only its writer forgets under an own cover) and receives it anew once none does; an outside writer's covers fall at a random tick or never (its demotion by an admin whose frontier names none, the role layer's abstracted). Before its plain rotation an honest rotator writes its own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.5) of each thing where a write stamped since the epoch it rotates opened shows only through a cover, named by its list. An honest node writes a snapshot only where every write showing on the thing lasts there (Carrying: as once every head closes, an open head counting only for what a word or its cutoff names); else it waits. Re-adds: where custody's A re-adds T (removed by C), T writes at the first epoch before the settle (offline, its writes leave only then, or online from tick 2), takes its removal at the settle and comes back onto its roster at the re-add; a node coming back onto its roster answers then (off it, it answered nothing), naming its own writes from before the re-add (spec/permissions.md §5.5 Listings: no other node names them for it). A node offline in custody sends nothing before its floor.

Properties at every final reading: safety (an X write after its removal shows on the honest side only through an honest word written off the honest side or about what its writer received before it held the removal, at an open head (an unaware sibling's) while it is open, or through a cover carrying it by the first two: `allowed`), liveness (every write of a node on the honest side shows there, and every other write an honest-side lister's cutoff names), monotone (dropping an honest list, listing or addendum never makes more count), own cover (where a rotation counts and its rotator ends on the honest side, every write but X's after its removal that showed at the rotator only through a cover as it rotated shows). "Shows": counts, saved by an addendum, or carried by a standing cover. Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib epochs`) are the minimal world of each, each asserting its control fails: `a_write_received_before_the_removal_counts_a_later_one_does_not` (`NoCutoff`), `a_write_only_a_witness_received_in_time_counts_by_its_addendum` (`OwnOnly`), `a_siblings_write_at_its_epoch_counts_once_listed_after_the_merge`, `a_write_the_remover_forgot_shows_by_its_own_cover` (`NoOwnCover`: X's write before its removal reaches A and R1 only, both forget it under R1's snapshot, R1 is demoted), `an_open_heads_write_lasts_in_no_snapshot` (`OpenCarried`: X writes at unaware B's head after its removal; C, holding both heads, would snapshot it at an epoch no removal revokes, carrying it past the merge), `a_re_added_nodes_write_from_before_its_re_add_counts_by_its_own_listing` (`NoReanswer`: T, off its roster, answered nothing; coming back without answering, its write before the re-add, received by every other node after its removal, is lost). Default tests: 20 random worlds and each control but `NoOwnCover` and `OpenCarried` failing within 20; `random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000) the twin. Answering again whenever what a node holds changes (the spec's target once) names nothing more: every word it would add came from dating a receipt before its epoch's chain was held.

Choices the spec left open: a receipt in a tick comes before the receiver's own acts that tick and after nothing else (`World::dt`), and receipts of one tick are in exact order (the spec's (`ts`, author, commit ID), P the author of both: the tick each was made, a rotation first, `World::before`); a listing names per thing whatever it owes, not keyed by rotation (judgment never reads the key); only thing lists are bounded; a rotation's plain content past the bound is named by its rotator's later listings by the cutoff like anything else (named whatever the cutoff, a write X made after its removal, left out on X's side, was named by a listing after its rotator's switch: 1 world in 8,000). Not modelled: pairings of new devices, a refused rotation (the chain world cannot undo it: such worlds, about 1 in 9, skip liveness).

## Custody (`src/custody.rs`)
notes/engine-validity-epochs.md, greenfield, the chain under `epochs`: seed custody follows holding (a node opens every box to its key and derives every merge seed whose tips' seeds it holds, counting or not), the chain is D's roster (an epoch is its line of plain rotations; nodes on a line are the creation devices plus additions minus removals carried there; a removal hits its node's rotations off its line), a merge is only a join (its epoch the union of the joined lines, its seed a function of its tips'). Rule, parties, honest policy and properties in the module doc. Parties: A (removes X; in the settle, in some worlds where C removed T, re-adds T once its line carries that removal: `readd`, boxing T the seeds it holds of the line), B (offline, unaware: rotates boxing X), C (apart: removes T and rotates thing t at its own epoch, the listing-cycle shape), X (every header and old key: forks, revokes A, B or C, signs as X or as Z, adds Z or itself, takes A's removal or a revocation it withholds, answers honest rotations, certifies merges of epochs whose seeds it holds, re-seals its seeds to honest nodes, writes and lists), T (reads only), R1 (outside reader holding t), R2 (outside reader whose user may switch). Schedules random and lockstep; the removal reaching B and C only in the settle in half the worlds (they take X's revocation first); P's users on X's side switch to A's removal in the settle (B in round 0, 1 or 3, or never). Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib custody`) are the minimal world of each property, each also asserting its negative control fails.

The gate runs `custody::random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000 random worlds, about 125 CPU-s; every control below fails at 800) and the default `small_family_sample_holds` (every 40th small-family world); `custody-exh` is a search, run by hand when `custody.rs` changes (about 2,600-2,900 CPU-s, over the gate's whole properties share).

Searches: `cargo run --bin roles-model -- N custody` (random), `-- 0 custody-exh` (the small family: 32 setups (schedule, B switching in round 1 or never, B offline, C apart removing T, X's tick-0 rotation) × the removal late to B and C or not × one move of X at tick 2 from a menu of nine (revoking A, revoking B boxed to B, revoking A claiming A's removal, revoking A taking one withheld, signing as Z adding Z, answering an honest rotation, a write, a merge certificate, a re-seal; at each epoch whose seed it holds; delivered to everyone, to B, C and R1 only, or no one before the settle) × the honest move at tick 3); negative controls `custody-judged`, `custody-roster-bare`, `custody-merge-random`, `custody-stale-shut`, `custody-merge-carries`, `custody-readd-beside` (random, N worlds). Default tests: 40 random worlds and every 40th world of two small-family setups; `random_worlds_hold_large` and `small_family_holds_large` the twins.

Choices the model fixed in the design (notes/engine-validity-epochs.md): a certificate's inner signature opens with any joined epoch's seed (else a node holding only some joined seeds reads certificates of one epoch otherwise); a plain rotation for a taken revocation is owed only while the node cannot merge (a taken revocation counts, so some head carries it); an answer is owed only to a rotation that does not stand or stands with a revocation not taken (not to one waiting on its parent epoch); D's roster removes only by taken revocations (a revocation held with its chain but not taken, its line merging a fork signed as a made-up key actor, removes no one: `a_held_revocation_not_taken_removes_no_one`). A certificate by a node a taken removal hits stands with nothing, so at a node on X's side an honest certificate may not stand while another of the same epoch does: certificates are compared among those standing at the node. A rotation stands only where every merge it is signed at has a certificate by a node of its line none hits, as the merge itself (a revocation at a merge X certified as a made-up key actor is taken nowhere, X's own view included: `a_revocation_through_a_merge_no_node_certified_is_taken_nowhere`). Fixed by the engine differential (osis-engine `rotation::tests_custody`, where the model contradicted the spec): the epoch rule's "held and not revoked" clause (a write at a closed epoch no counting removal closes counts unnamed); the rejection rule exempts every rotation signed at the rejector's epoch on a line with the rejected one, a certificate signed at each epoch it joins; merges are answered (named by a certificate) where they join a line not followed; a removal is a rotation of its own, then the node acts as at a pass end; a certificate boxes its joined line's roster, and a device it boxes gets the maker's seeds of that line besides; a device is seen holding a seed only by a box in an item counting at the node, tagged for that seed (one riding along in a box tagged for another is seen by no one); a plain rotation's membership changes open only with the seed of the epoch it is signed at (one whose changes do not open is minted by none); a re-seal is owed per head (the head's seed, its line's riding along), not per tip; a signing node's own commits count at it once a rotation closes their epoch (its rotation's list and listings name them as each rotation applies, so its re-key of t supersedes its own t epochs at revoked epochs: osis-engine `a_t_epoch_its_makers_rotation_closes_stays_current_spec_permissions_5_5`); a node not holding its preferred head's seed re-seals nothing (osis-engine `no_reseal_without_the_preferred_heads_seed_spec_crypto_9`, the engine's fix); placement depth counts a merge as one step, and rotations held at once tie by `World::tie` (the differential's commit IDs). Thing t follows the engine's encrypting rule: a node re-keys t while a current t epoch has a counting box to P at an epoch it names that does not count there or lacks a taken revocation its head carries, the history carrying its established t epochs, its box to P sealed jointly at every head its maker follows, so it opens only where each head's seed is held (spec/crypto.md §4 Account keysets), or none where a head is not named there (an epoch it supersedes, transitively, not held: its height unknown) or does not cover a removal from D it knows: P is left out and re-sealed (`TReseal`) at each current t epoch it lacks a box at once it no longer is (`p_seal`, `a_t_epoch_left_out_at_an_unnamed_head_reads_once_re_sealed`); a t rotation removes P's boxes at the epochs its history carries, so such a box opens only where it and its seeds were held before that rotation (`a_box_a_later_t_rotation_removed_opens_only_if_held_before`); a t epoch is current where its rotation counts, its secret held or not, and a node encrypts under, carries and re-seals only secrets it holds established (from a counting box or a history opened under one), a secret from an item not counting being read only; lacking every current epoch's secret (left out) it does not rotate for that, and its writes on t wait under the newest current epoch until it is re-sealed (re-keying only after its own removal, a node switching back from X's side leaves its writes there under a t epoch the honest side never opens: `writes`, about 1 in 400 random worlds; a history of every held t epoch hands X the honest side's: `secrecy`).

## Own cover (`src/forget.rs`, `Policy::HeldList`)
The built rule (spec/permissions.md §5.1, §5.5, Own cover): a removal lists by ID every commit of the leaver the remover holds; for the leaver's visible writes whose commits it forgot, the remover writes its own fresh snapshot (`Kind::RemSnap`), named by the removal, standing while the removal applies or as its author's snapshot; nodes forget under any valid cover (`Policy::Any`, the comparator). `-- 0 forget` (1,147,776 worlds): fails on a write lost that `Any` shows, a kept body dropped, nodes disagreeing, input order, a forgotten commit reading otherwise than never received, or an attack gaining or costing an honest write; 0 of each. Counted, against listing every commit (`Policy::ListAll`): `Any` loses in 2,482 worlds, `HeldList` in 4,954 (a cover that later counts for nothing: a removal voided with its remover, a witness's cover received late), 410 where the removal stands and a write the remover had received is lost (`held_list_late_cover_limit`: forgotten under the leaver's snapshot that arrived after the removal's `ts`, `Any`'s limit too). Hand tests: `own_snapshot_at_removal_outlives_another_writers_cover`, `own_snapshot_stands_with_its_removal`, `held_list_voided_removal_keeps_what_holders_held`, `kept_history_is_never_covered`. `addenda::random_world` gives half the removals the demoter's own cover, so osis-perm's `addenda_model_agrees` checks `Timeline::own_cover_stands`.

## Paths (`src/paths.rs`)
An oracle of a design that is not built and not in spec: a write states its path and is judged along it alone (notes/write-path-design.md §1-§2), an account is an ordinary independent thing whose members are signers, a key per node (notes/account-membership-design.md §2, §4). Self-contained: `joint` and `trust` judge authority as a maximum over every path, which is what this design removes. Findings: notes/path-model-findings.md. Rule, in the module doc: `judge(world, rules, view)`, a function of the held commits, the frontier leaves seen and the trust set.

**Modelled.** Signers and independent things with edges; commits with a stated path (content, edge writes with a frontier cut per thing, addenda, vouches for role writes); the walk along paths with audits by hop, restarts and trust-set seeds; content validity (held at its position, listed, vouched by an addendum as a least fixpoint, re-granted); the last-admin rule; disputes; a unit's evidence closure and which leaves a node sees; a keys layer for A's epochs under a dispute (`rotations`: knowing re-key, withdraw). The story (`Sim::build`): account A minted by H, nodes B, X (stolen at tick 5, removed by H at 6), N5 (paired by X); things T1 (minted with A) and T2 (minted with ORG, minted with A: a path of two hops above the signer's); account A2 (node K) with write on T1; readers of T1's unit and of T2's unit alone. H syncs T1 and T2 or not, B is offline or not, X's role write before the theft (a demotion of B, a removal of A2, a grant on T2) reached H or not, X holds write on A2 too or not (K removing it or not), B sides with X's removal of H until the settle or not, honest re-grants after the settle or not. The thief (`MOVES`): content stamped at any `ts` on a cut path, on one it still holds, on one it never had; grants to a made-up key Z on A, T1, T2; Z writing, removing H, re-granting X, vouching; X removing H, B, N5; X's own addendum and vouch; each delivered to each node at once or only at the settle. Honest policy: a frontier lists every commit through the removed edge its writer received; each node, as it receives a removal, writes per thing an addendum (and a vouch, where the rules have them) for what it had received by the removal's `ts` that the removal does not list; B answers at its switch.

**Properties** (`check`), at every honest node's final reading, against the wanted behaviour computed from the story: safety (no commit the thief made after the removal shows, but content on a path it still holds; Z and X end with no role; a removal X made before the theft that an admin received stays); liveness (every honest node's content and role writes show; X's writes from before the removal that the remover or a writer holding write at its addendum received show; a grant of X's an admin received applies, and the writes under it; a re-granted holder's writes show); alike (two nodes read alike every commit of a unit both sync: members against the readers holding the unit's evidence); the walk settles, nothing is dropped by a write that does not apply, no trusted removal is void.

**Assumptions where the notes are open** (each one field of `Rules`, an argument, or one function):
- Vouching for role writes (`Vouch`): `Off` is the notes' state. `BaseWalk` (in `Rules::fixed`): a vouch names a removal and role writes on one thing; it counts when its path gives admin on that thing at its key in the walk with no vouch counting; a counting vouch keeps what it names out of that removal's audit and seeds. `SameWalk` is the simplest reading (judged in the walk it changes) and has no fixpoint.
- Re-grant (`Regrant`): from the first applied point (spec), or from each edge's first chained grant (the membership note's fix).
- `own_chain_exempt`: a removal never puts in question the grants its own path rests on (the write-path note removes that rule; with it removed a removal can drop writes and then not apply).
- `no_own_addendum`: an addendum vouches for no commit of its own signer.
- Leaves (`Sim::view`): a node sees a frontier's leaf for the things it syncs; in the fixed reading also for every thing an edge write it holds as evidence is on. An entry in a leaf not seen is not listed.
- Evidence (`evidence`): edge writes on the edges of held commits' paths, closed; plus vouches against held removals. Every removal of X is logged under every unit X could read; whatever exists reaches every node syncing its unit by the settle.
- Last admin: counts admin edges on the thing as the walk stands; only a lowering of the last admin edge by a path through that edge can meet it.
- An audit puts in question edge-class commits when an edge falls from admin to below; a lowering from write audits no role write. The edge as restored is the edge's value just before.
- The oracle (`check`) takes findings 3 and 9's limits as accepted: X's writes that only a holder standing by a vouch or by a re-grant received are not wanted to show.
- Keys (`Withdraw`): `AsWritten` reads spec/crypto.md §4 (a valid rotation is current whatever it covers); `SideOnly` is the fix (an epoch covering a departure the node's walk does not apply is the other side's: read, never current, superseding nothing, not answered).

**Not modelled.** Dependent things and descent; `until`, invites, finalizes, self-removal by path end; snapshots, forgetting and own covers (so "a writer that compacts before holding the removal carries the thief's write" is not reached); demotion to write (a kiosk); accomplices granted inside the theft window; sessions and overlapping dots; hidden mode; purge authors; the thief's own rotations of A and secrecy (`rotations` is only the loop under a standing dispute); a user keeping what nobody received; secrets beyond A's epochs (re-keys of T, re-seals); bodies dropped as invalid on the wrong side and fetched after a switch; a node choosing its side by first received (sides are given).
