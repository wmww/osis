# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules (spec/permissions.md §5), S3's vouches and addenda gaps, account custody and epochs (§5.4-§5.5), and of the design for stated paths with signers as members: modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm and osis-engine dev-depend on it for their differentials). Module map and API: `src/lib.rs`; findings behind the role timeline: notes/role-timeline.md; disputes: notes/disputes.md.

## Vouches (`src/vouches.rs`)
S3 brief 2 (notes/redesign-plan.md; notes/account-membership-design.md §4, Role writes: vouched for like content) on today's walk: authority as `edges` gives it (one thing, every node a key actor, a frontier is what its demoter received), with trust sets (`trust::side`). Rule, properties and controls in the module doc. `EHist::extra` carries counting entries into the walk as frontier entries; empty is today's rule, so `edges::walk`, the digest and osis-perm's differentials are unchanged. S3's code brief adds vouches to perm and then runs a differential against `vouches::judge`. Of what no list keeps only today's first half is modelled (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 4, R never demotes its own author): the rest reads the listing write's own path, which today's walk does not define; it is modelled in `paths` (below) and built with stated paths (notes/redesign-plan.md A1).

**Readings where the notes are open:**
- A vouch is a role-class write that is not an edge write: its position is the start of its `ts`, so "after R" is a later `ts`. Its author's authority for an entry is `EHist::would_apply` of its own commit with the named commit's ops at that position in the first walk (a node may always vouch for a lowering of its own edge: a self-lowering applies).
- Survival: an entry falls when an applied explicit write by another node lowering the voucher's edge follows, its author had not received the vouch, and the voucher can no longer write the entry after it. Re-grant does not revive it; the voucher writes again.
- Honest writing: at receipt of R, and at every later time the node newly holds the authority for a listed commit. `FIXED` reads that authority off the canonical first walk (idealised: no node computes it); `Rules::held` off the node's own walk of what it holds (`EHist::held_by`). `Rules::state` is the state-based writing: every commit reaches every node by `SYNC`, and a node keeps each listed commit it could write, in its own current walk, named by a counting vouch of its own, writing one whenever one is not. `Rules::stands` adds the writers' leaving out (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, What a list leaves out): where the removal, trusted alone, does not apply with what the remover received of its victim (or with a vouch as written), the remover (voucher) names those commits one by one in the order it received them, keeping each only where the removal still applies; a commit the remover leaves out reads as not received by it.
- Kept as promised (`kept_end`, under `Rules::state` only): spec/permissions.md §5.1, What vouches keep, in its checked form: the reference lists, beside the counting entries, each commit of R's victim a witness holding R received by R's `ts` and could write at the end of the first walk (`kept_end_extra`; osis-perm's differential compares its timeline with that reference too).
- Set-asides: listing's `lowered` and `accomplice` are read off the generated world (`lowered` only where A received X's lowering before R or a witness would vouch for it); safety's bargain is the differential "the op applies with only the entries whose voucher is not its author and could write it at the vouch's position".

**Limits found** (hand worlds in `src/vouches/tests.rs`):
- Listing on today's walk: H's list naming X's removal of H (received, or vouched for by a witness) keeps it, and H's trusted removal of X is void; with an empty list it applies (`a_removal_listing_the_thiefs_removal_of_its_author_is_void`). The writers' leaving out answers it, the accomplice's world too (`the_writers_leave_out_what_their_removal_does_not_apply_with`): with it the set-aside is 0 in the family and 2 of 100,000 random worlds, both where the voucher's own vouch against another lowering (a demotion to write) keeps X's timed grant to the voucher, which lapses before the voucher's own later removal: `World::derive` chooses removers' lists before any vouch exists, and with them chosen again once the vouches are written (as the remover, writing later, would) the set-aside is 0 in both. The listing property holds only where nothing writes an edge H's removal may rest on below admin. Reading such an entry as absent needs H's stated path: read as "any grant to H", a removed node writes a junk grant to H and strikes an earlier removal of itself from H's list (`a_removed_node_writing_a_grant_to_the_remover_stays_removed`).
- An accomplice: X grants Y admin, Y removes H; H's removals of X and of Y stay void while listing what H received; a removal of Y omitting Y's removal of H applies both (`an_accomplices_removal_is_answered_by_a_removal_omitting_it`; notes/disputes.md, Hole: an accomplice).
- Writing from the held set misses where the witness never received a lowering of its own edge that the remover's list keeps: its vouch at receipt of R does not count, and its view never shows it newly holding the right, so it never writes again, even after a re-grant (`a_witness_unaware_of_its_own_demotion_never_vouches_again`). S3's code brief needs a writing trigger beyond "first holds the right in its own view": the state-based writing (`Rules::state`, eventual delivery) keeps that world and the whole family. In two of 1,000,000 random worlds it misses (`roles-model -- 1000000 vouches-state`: worlds 612558, kept and stays made, and 74440, one stays-made op), both for one cause: the witness held the authority as it applied R only through a commit it had not yet received (in 74440 the thief's own hidden backdated demotion of the node that had demoted the witness, which drops that demotion), then gave up its own edge, by its own later write, before receiving it. Kept's "holds the authority as it applies R" reads the canonical walk, which no node writing from its held set can know then: the oracle asks too much, not a hole of the rule. Under stated paths the state-based writing holds (Paths, below). The search's shrinking rewrites the honest vouches under the state rule at every step and runs for tens of minutes on one core: scan by world index instead.
- A node X lowered but keeping an invite vouches for its own backdated invite grant, which nobody else received, and it applies at its own `ts` (`a_demoted_node_vouches_for_its_own_backdated_invite`). "Nobody vouches for its own signer's commit" closes it; S3 leaves it to A1.
- A demotion X made that only its victim received stays made: the victim vouches for it (`a_demotion_only_its_victim_received_stays_made`).

## Addenda gaps (`src/addenda.rs`, `Gaps`)
S3 brief 3 on today's rules (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, Addenda reach admin-only content, Writing): `Kind::AdminOnly` is content on an admin-only aspect that is not role-class, judged with admin in place of write against demotions below admin (`joint::Steps::demotions_admin`, spec/permissions.md §5.2); `Gaps::admin_only` lets an addendum by a witness holding admin at its key list it; `Gaps::rewrite` makes a witness lacking the role as it applies the removal write its addendum when it first holds it after (else, today, never). `Gaps::default()` is today's rule, so `random_world`, `addenda-exh` and osis-perm's `addenda_model_agrees` are unchanged; S3's code brief flips perm to `GAPS` and the differential with it.

Properties (`check_gaps`): **reached** (a non-forged plain or admin-only commit of X, ordered no later than a demotion of X below the role it needs, that a node other than X and the demoter received before the demotion's `ts`, which received the demotion and holds that role at the end, is valid), one result for every input order, monotone, forged addenda gaining nothing beyond their author's power (admin for admin-only content). The gaps family is `exhaustive_worlds` with Bob an admin whose first write is admin-only, "grant Carol admin" in place of "remove Carol" and "demote Bob to write" in place of the re-grant.

## Epochs (`src/epochs.rs`)
Layer 2 of the account (spec/permissions.md §5.5) over `custody`'s chain: a world is a settled custody world (its parties, fight and settle) plus P's commits on one to three things in two sync IDs, the rotators' thing lists, listings, R1's and R2's addenda as writers of a thing, and in half the worlds a bound (2 or 3 IDs per thing list), written by the cutoff after the chain world is made. A lister or witness answers as an epoch comes to count at it (a rotation held with its chain, or its side moving), never again as what it holds changes; a commit of P counts as received there once its epoch is held with its chain (its signature verifies then, spec/permissions.md §5.5 Cutoff: `World::dt`). Each node's chain is custody's `View`; the module judges only what counts of P's commits (rule, policy and properties in the module doc). osis-perm `epochs_model_agrees` feeds every observer and receipt prefix of every world to `osis_perm::Epochs`, the chain (held, counting, heads, preferred head, warning, revocations taken) and the epoch rule (pending, counting, saved by an addendum) alike.

In half the worlds nodes snapshot and forget (spec/commits.md §5): outside writers and X snapshot a thing (P's honest nodes too in half of those), a cover carrying every write showing at its writer; a node forgets another's write a standing cover it holds carries (only its writer forgets under an own cover) and receives it anew once none does; an outside writer's covers fall at a random tick or never (its demotion by an admin whose frontier names none, the role layer's abstracted). Before its plain rotation an honest rotator writes its own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.5) of each thing where a write stamped since the epoch it rotates opened shows only through a cover, named by its list. An honest node writes a snapshot only where every write showing on the thing lasts there (Carrying: as once every head closes, an open head counting only for what a word or its cutoff names); else it waits. Re-adds: where custody's A re-adds T (removed by C), T writes at the first epoch before the settle (offline, its writes leave only then, or online from tick 2), takes its removal at the settle and comes back onto its roster at the re-add; a node coming back onto its roster answers then (off it, it answered nothing), naming its own writes from before the re-add (spec/permissions.md §5.5 Listings: no other node names them for it). A node offline in custody sends nothing before its floor.

Properties at every final reading: safety (an X write after its removal shows on the honest side only through an honest word written off the honest side or about what its writer received before it held the removal, at an open head (an unaware sibling's) while it is open, or through a cover carrying it by the first two: `allowed`), liveness (every write of a node on the honest side shows there, and every other write an honest-side lister's cutoff names), monotone (dropping an honest list, listing or addendum never makes more count), own cover (where a rotation counts and its rotator ends on the honest side, every write but X's after its removal that showed at the rotator only through a cover as it rotated shows). "Shows": counts, saved by an addendum, or carried by a standing cover. Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib epochs`) are the minimal world of each, each asserting its control fails: `a_write_received_before_the_removal_counts_a_later_one_does_not` (`NoCutoff`), `a_write_only_a_witness_received_in_time_counts_by_its_addendum` (`OwnOnly`), `a_siblings_write_at_its_epoch_counts_once_listed_after_the_merge`, `a_write_the_remover_forgot_shows_by_its_own_cover` (`NoOwnCover`: X's write before its removal reaches A and R1 only, both forget it under R1's snapshot, R1 is demoted), `an_open_heads_write_lasts_in_no_snapshot` (`OpenCarried`: X writes at unaware B's head after its removal; C, holding both heads, would snapshot it at an epoch no removal revokes, carrying it past the merge), `a_re_added_nodes_write_from_before_its_re_add_counts_by_its_own_listing` (`NoReanswer`: T, off its roster, answered nothing; coming back without answering, its write before the re-add, received by every other node after its removal, is lost). Default tests: 20 random worlds and each control but `NoOwnCover` and `OpenCarried` failing within 20; `random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000) the twin. Answering again whenever what a node holds changes (the spec's target once) names nothing more: every word it would add came from dating a receipt before its epoch's chain was held.

Choices the spec left open: a receipt in a tick comes before the receiver's own acts that tick and after nothing else (`World::dt`), and receipts of one tick are in exact order (the spec's (`ts`, author, commit ID), P the author of both: the tick each was made, a rotation first, `World::before`); a listing names per thing whatever it owes, not keyed by rotation (judgment never reads the key); only thing lists are bounded; a rotation's plain content past the bound is named by its rotator's later listings by the cutoff like anything else (named whatever the cutoff, a write X made after its removal, left out on X's side, was named by a listing after its rotator's switch: 1 world in 8,000). Not modelled: pairings of new devices, a refused rotation (the chain world cannot undo it: such worlds, about 1 in 9, skip liveness).

## Custody (`src/custody.rs`)
notes/engine-validity-epochs.md, greenfield, the chain under `epochs`: seed custody follows holding (a node opens every box to its key and derives every merge seed whose tips' seeds it holds, counting or not), the chain is D's roster (an epoch is its line of plain rotations; nodes on a line are the creation devices plus additions minus removals carried there; a removal hits its node's rotations off its line), a merge is only a join (its epoch the union of the joined lines, its seed a function of its tips'). Rule, parties, honest policy and properties in the module doc. Parties: A (removes X; in the settle, in some worlds where C removed T, re-adds T once its line carries that removal: `readd`, boxing T the seeds it holds of the line), B (offline, unaware: rotates boxing X), C (apart: removes T and rotates thing t at its own epoch, the listing-cycle shape), X (every header and old key: forks, revokes A, B or C, signs as X or as Z, adds Z or itself, takes A's removal or a revocation it withholds, answers honest rotations, certifies merges of epochs whose seeds it holds, re-seals its seeds to honest nodes, writes and lists), T (reads only), R1 (outside reader holding t), R2 (outside reader whose user may switch). Schedules random and lockstep; the removal reaching B and C only in the settle in half the worlds (they take X's revocation first); P's users on X's side switch to A's removal in the settle (B in round 0, 1 or 3, or never). Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib custody`) are the minimal world of each property, each also asserting its negative control fails.

The gate runs `custody::random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000 random worlds, about 125 CPU-s; every control below fails at 800) and the default `small_family_sample_holds` (every 40th small-family world); `custody-exh` is a search, run by hand when `custody.rs` changes (about 2,600-2,900 CPU-s, over the gate's whole properties share).

Searches: `cargo run --bin roles-model -- N custody` (random), `-- 0 custody-exh` (the small family: 32 setups (schedule, B switching in round 1 or never, B offline, C apart removing T, X's tick-0 rotation) × the removal late to B and C or not × one move of X at tick 2 from a menu of nine (revoking A, revoking B boxed to B, revoking A claiming A's removal, revoking A taking one withheld, signing as Z adding Z, answering an honest rotation, a write, a merge certificate, a re-seal; at each epoch whose seed it holds; delivered to everyone, to B, C and R1 only, or no one before the settle) × the honest move at tick 3); negative controls `custody-judged`, `custody-roster-bare`, `custody-merge-random`, `custody-stale-shut`, `custody-merge-carries`, `custody-readd-beside` (random, N worlds). Default tests: 40 random worlds and every 40th world of two small-family setups; `random_worlds_hold_large` and `small_family_holds_large` the twins.

Choices the model fixed in the design (notes/engine-validity-epochs.md): a certificate's inner signature opens with any joined epoch's seed (else a node holding only some joined seeds reads certificates of one epoch otherwise); a plain rotation for a taken revocation is owed only while the node cannot merge (a taken revocation counts, so some head carries it); an answer is owed only to a rotation that does not stand or stands with a revocation not taken (not to one waiting on its parent epoch); D's roster removes only by taken revocations (a revocation held with its chain but not taken, its line merging a fork signed as a made-up key actor, removes no one: `a_held_revocation_not_taken_removes_no_one`). A certificate by a node a taken removal hits stands with nothing, so at a node on X's side an honest certificate may not stand while another of the same epoch does: certificates are compared among those standing at the node. Fixed by osis-perm's `epochs_model_agrees` (where the model contradicted the spec): rotations held in one tick are placed by tie (the differential's IDs; merge epochs after plain rotations, `epochs::MERGE_TIE`) before depth, as receipts of one millisecond by ID; a certificate is signed at its tips (`joined`, each one's merge signature), not at the heads it joins, so a rotation signed at a merge epoch a larger merge took in is not contested from below by it. A rotation stands only where every merge it is signed at has a certificate by a node of its line none hits, as the merge itself (a revocation at a merge X certified as a made-up key actor is taken nowhere, X's own view included: `a_revocation_through_a_merge_no_node_certified_is_taken_nowhere`). Fixed by the engine differential (osis-engine `rotation::tests_custody`, where the model contradicted the spec): the epoch rule's "held and not revoked" clause (a write at a closed epoch no counting removal closes counts unnamed); the rejection rule exempts every rotation signed at the rejector's epoch on a line with the rejected one, a certificate signed at each epoch it joins; merges are answered (named by a certificate) where they join a line not followed; a removal is a rotation of its own, then the node acts as at a pass end; a certificate boxes its joined line's roster, and a device it boxes gets the maker's seeds of that line besides; a device is seen holding a seed only by a box in an item counting at the node, tagged for that seed (one riding along in a box tagged for another is seen by no one); a plain rotation's membership changes open only with the seed of the epoch it is signed at (one whose changes do not open is minted by none); a re-seal is owed per head (the head's seed, its line's riding along), not per tip; a signing node's own commits count at it once a rotation closes their epoch (its rotation's list and listings name them as each rotation applies, so its re-key of t supersedes its own t epochs at revoked epochs: osis-engine `a_t_epoch_its_makers_rotation_closes_stays_current_spec_permissions_5_5`); a node not holding its preferred head's seed re-seals nothing (osis-engine `no_reseal_without_the_preferred_heads_seed_spec_crypto_9`, the engine's fix); placement depth counts a merge as one step, and rotations held at once tie by `World::tie` (the differential's commit IDs). Thing t follows the engine's encrypting rule: a node re-keys t while a current t epoch has a counting box to P at an epoch it names that does not count there or lacks a taken revocation its head carries, the history carrying its established t epochs, its box to P sealed jointly at every head its maker follows, so it opens only where each head's seed is held (spec/crypto.md §4 Account keysets), or none where a head is not named there (an epoch it supersedes, transitively, not held: its height unknown) or does not cover a removal from D it knows: P is left out and re-sealed (`TReseal`) at each current t epoch it lacks a box at once it no longer is (`p_seal`, `a_t_epoch_left_out_at_an_unnamed_head_reads_once_re_sealed`); a t rotation removes P's boxes at the epochs its history carries, so such a box opens only where it and its seeds were held before that rotation (`a_box_a_later_t_rotation_removed_opens_only_if_held_before`); a t epoch is current where its rotation counts, its secret held or not, and a node encrypts under, carries and re-seals only secrets it holds established (from a counting box or a history opened under one), a secret from an item not counting being read only; lacking every current epoch's secret (left out) it does not rotate for that, and its writes on t wait under the newest current epoch until it is re-sealed (re-keying only after its own removal, a node switching back from X's side leaves its writes there under a t epoch the honest side never opens: `writes`, about 1 in 400 random worlds; a history of every held t epoch hands X the honest side's: `secrecy`).

## Own cover (`src/forget.rs`, `Policy::HeldList`)
The built rule (spec/permissions.md §5.1, §5.5, Own cover): a removal lists by ID every commit of the leaver the remover holds; for the leaver's visible writes whose commits it forgot, the remover writes its own fresh snapshot (`Kind::RemSnap`), named by the removal, standing while the removal applies or as its author's snapshot; nodes forget under any valid cover (`Policy::Any`, the comparator). `-- 0 forget` (1,147,776 worlds): fails on a write lost that `Any` shows, a kept body dropped, nodes disagreeing, input order, a forgotten commit reading otherwise than never received, or an attack gaining or costing an honest write; 0 of each. Counted, against listing every commit (`Policy::ListAll`): `Any` loses in 2,482 worlds, `HeldList` in 4,954 (a cover that later counts for nothing: a removal voided with its remover, a witness's cover received late), 410 where the removal stands and a write the remover had received is lost (`held_list_late_cover_limit`: forgotten under the leaver's snapshot that arrived after the removal's `ts`, `Any`'s limit too). Hand tests: `own_snapshot_at_removal_outlives_another_writers_cover`, `own_snapshot_stands_with_its_removal`, `held_list_voided_removal_keeps_what_holders_held`, `kept_history_is_never_covered`. `addenda::random_world` gives half the removals the demoter's own cover, so osis-perm's `addenda_model_agrees` checks `Timeline::own_cover_stands`.

## Paths (`src/paths.rs`, `src/paths/story.rs`)
An oracle of a design that is not built and not in spec: a write states its path and is judged along it alone (notes/write-path-design.md §1-§2, §5), an account is an ordinary independent thing whose members are signers, a key per node (notes/account-membership-design.md §2, §4). Self-contained: `joint` and `trust` judge authority as a maximum over every path, which is what this design removes. Findings: notes/path-model-findings.md. Rule, in the module doc: `judge(world, rules, view)`, a function of the held commits, the frontier leaves seen and the trust set. One rule set, `Rules::fixed`; `Rules::design` is the first round's reading the `repro_*` tests fail under; each control is `Rules::fixed` with one field changed. osis-perm's A1 walk is to be differentially tested against it.

**Modelled.** Signers and things with edges (role and `until`), record edges down mint chains (T2 minted with ORG minted with A; D1 a dependent of T1); commits with a stated path, hops by descent where a path member is a mint ancestor of the next, a self-removal by a path ending at the holder; edge writes with a frontier cut per thing, the remaining admin they name and the adminless mark; addenda and vouches; the walk along paths with audits by hop against the edge as restored, restarts, trust-set seeds, each lowering judged once (void where its own drops take its authority, else applied for good); content validity (held at its position, listed, vouched by an addendum as a least fixpoint, re-granted); the last-admin rule; disputes; invite grants and finalizes; purge authors and purge requests by author; covers (compaction snapshots) whose copies show what they carry; the evidence closure of what a node holds and which leaves it sees; a keys layer for A's epochs under a dispute (`rotations`). The story (`Sim::build`): account A minted by H (or by M, who pairs H and leaves naming B as the remaining admin, B's grant standing or timed), nodes B, X (stolen at tick 5, removed by H at 6), N5 (paired by X, its grant received by H or not; or paired by H and removed by X before the theft, its key the thief's); things T1 (minted with A), D1 (minted with T1), T2 (minted with ORG, minted with A); account A2 (node K) with write (or admin) on T1, its share inviting read, and B removing A2 from T1 by [A] at 6 (a removal of two hops); where X writes A2, X's invite of a guest G to read T1 through [A2]; content authored as the path's first holder at odd ticks, else as the signer; purges by author at 9 (B of A's edits on T2 along [A, ORG], K of A2's on T1 along [A2]); K's cover of T1 at 10; readers holding only T1 and D1, and only T2, with the evidence of what they hold; two nodes trusting nothing (N6 syncing what B does, RD3 only T1 and D1). H syncs T1 and T2 or not, B is offline or not, X's write before the theft (a demotion of B, a removal of A2, a grant on T2, a purge by author A on T1) reached H or not, X holds write or admin on A2 or not (K removing it or not), X removes H at the theft, honestly stamped, and H lists it or not, B sides with X's removal of H until the settle or not, honest re-grants after the settle or not. The thief (`MOVES`, 48): content stamped at any `ts` on a cut path (by descent too), on one it still holds, on one it never had; grants to a made-up key Z on A, T1, T2; Z writing, removing H, re-granting X, vouching; X removing H, B, N5; X's own addendum and vouch (along A2 for a backdated grant through A); X leaving A by itself, or setting its own edge timed; X making A leave T1, marked adminless; moves aimed at the remover (X or Z granting H admin, standing or until just after the removal; X removing M listing nothing or M's grant of H; X demoting H listing its own grant); with N5's key, stamped before X's removal of N5, a grant of admin to H (standing, timed), a removal or demotion of H, a vouch; X's twin of its removal of N5 at its `ts`, ordered first, listing less; N5 kept by H's list vouching for X's role writes (the accomplice); X removing A from T1 through A2 where B removes A2; invites of Z through A2 (at write, beyond A2's invite; at read; at read until the next tick, finalized and invited again); purges by author A along [A], by A2 along [A2] (its right where X keeps A2), by A along [A2] (no right: A is not on its chain); withholding the removal from K and writing T1 after it, K compacting T1 before it holds the removal; X's backdated cover of its own writes on T1. Each delivered to each node at once or only at the settle; some made at tick 5 and heard by H and B before the removal (`early`), so they meet H's list and the witnesses' vouches; in half the worlds where N5 was removed before the theft, the pair that sets receipt order against key order. Honest writing, the state-based rule (`Sim::answers`): from the removal to tick 15, a node whose own walk applies a removal it holds writes, per thing, an addendum (vouch) listing the commits through the removed edge it had received by the removal's `ts` that the removal does not list, whenever its path gives it write (admin, in the walk with no vouch counting) and some of them is named by no addendum (counting vouch) of its own; a remover and a voucher name the role writes by the writers' rule (`stands`).

**Properties** (`check`), at every honest node's final reading, against the wanted behaviour computed from the story: safety (no commit the thief made after the removal shows, held or carried by a held cover, but content on a path it still holds and its own leave, and the accepted cover window, counted: `window`; Z, X and N5 removed before the theft end with no role); liveness (every honest node's content and role writes show; X's writes from before the removal that the remover or a writer holding write (once it does) received show; a grant or lowering of X's an admin received applies, and the writes under it, but those aimed at the remover; a re-granted holder's writes show; M's leave applies iff the admin it names is standing; set aside: what a kept lowering of H by X heard before the removal dropped, finding 19); own path (H ends a standing admin of A wherever X lowered it); removal kept; admin (every thing keeps a standing admin edge: no story write is marked); alike (two nodes read alike every commit on a thing both sync: members against the readers holding part of the evidence, and the two nodes trusting nothing); the walk settles; no trusted removal is void (but by the accomplice: counted, its vouch the cause); one reading (`one reading`: a relabelling of commit IDs keeping the walk's order reads the same); at the nodes trusting nothing, where every honest removal they hold applies, safety (`safety N`: nothing the thief stamped after the removal or signed with N5's key shows, but by right; Z, X and N5 end with no role) and own path (`own path N`); every honest removal applies or is shown through the disputes (trusting the honest ones shown, and those then shown, applies it); a reading unlike the trusted side's, or unlike the reading of its own held set trusting every honest removal held, comes with a dispute shown, and trusting the honest ones shown reads as the latter; an honest history (no move of the thief, X removing H neither at the theft nor after) shows no dispute (`quiet`); a void lowering has no effect (`void`: the reading without it held is the same); purge (`purge`: the deltas the honest valid purge requests name, exactly by purge author and with that author on the request's chain, are purged, and nothing beyond them and what the thief's requests along [A2] name where X keeps A2); purged deltas are compared by `alike`, `one reading` and `void` too. Counted, not failures, at trusting nodes (`T`) and nodes trusting nothing (`N`): a stray drop (`stray`, from the reading alone: a dropped commit whose dropper is not applied, or not trusted and applied, an explicit lowering ordered after it whose edge it rests on and whose list does not name it), a thing with no standing admin where every honest removal applies (`admin N`), a reading unlike the trusted side's that spec/permissions.md §5.1's dispute leaves silent (every write shown applying), the audit over the applied writes reading otherwise (finding 18), the accomplice worlds; the largest number of restarts in one reading.

**Tiny worlds** (`tiny`, `-- N paths-tiny[-variant]`; `tiny_invites`, `-- N paths-tiny-invites[-variant]`): world i has 3 to 12 edge writes on A among H, B, N5, Z, N6 (each a grant of admin or a removal at a random `ts`, listing each earlier write with probability one half; of invites: also write inviting write, standing or until two ticks on, read, and finalizes, so a writer holding an invite grants by it), every commit held, trusting nothing (`N`) or (every third world) one lowering, which the settled walk applies (`T`) or not (`V`, its seeds lost and the walk redone). Failing: `one reading`, `void` (a void lowering has no effect), `unapplied N`/`T` (a lowering left with no verdict has no effect), `admin T` (A keeps a standing admin). Counted: `stray`, `unapplied V`, `admin N`/`V`. A failing or counted world prints with its index, shrunk by dropping commits.

**Readings where the notes are open** (each one field of `Rules`, an argument, or one function):
- Vouching (`Vouch::BaseWalk`): a vouch names a removal and role writes on one thing; it counts when its path gives admin on that thing at its key in the walk with no vouch counting, and that walk applies its removal (finding 22); a counting vouch keeps what it names out of that removal's audit and seeds. `SameWalk` (judged in the walk it changes) has no fixpoint.
- Re-grant (`Regrant::FirstChained`): from each edge's first chained grant.
- A removal is judged in the world its list makes: nothing is exempt from a lowering's drops and no entry is read as absent. Each lowering is judged once (finding 16; notes/write-path-design.md §1): the first time a pass reaches it holding its right and taking its edge from admin, it is void (`self_void`) where its own drops would take its authority, read back along the setters of its path (`keeps`), else applied, its drops added, the pass ended there and the next resumed from the earliest; the verdict stands for the walk (an applied one applies unaudited in every later pass unless dropped, and its drops stay; where its value is above its edge as restored in that pass it applies only by admin along its path, finding 27). A lowering without its right, or taking nothing, gets no verdict and is judged by a later pass. Control `-no-void`: a lowering whose drops take its authority applies. A trusted write the settled walk does not apply loses its seeds, all at once. Control `exempt_chain`: a removal never puts in question the grants its own path rests on, read from the held writes.
- The audit puts in question the held writes before it resting on its edge, not dropped and not void, as the seeds do (`audit_held`, finding 18); control `-audit-applied`, the applied ones.
- Last admin (`LastAdmin::Named`): an unmarked write taking a standing admin edge (admin, no `until`) to anything else, `until` included, applies only where the remaining admin is a standing admin edge other than the lowered one, as the walk stands: the path's last edge (for a self-removal that is never the thing's), else the holder it names. Controls: `AnyEdge` (every admin edge of the thing, timed or not), `NamedTimed`.
- `no_own_addendum`, `no_own_vouch`: no addendum or vouch counts for a commit of its own signer.
- `evidence_leaves`: a node sees a frontier's leaf for the things it syncs and every thing an edge write it holds as evidence is on.
- `leave_in_question`: a lowering of edge h puts a self-removal of h in question like a write through h (finding 14), a timed one and a holder thing's included.
- `rewrite` (writing policy, not a rule): the state-based writing; false, a node answers only the first time its walk applies a removal.
- `stands` (writing policy): a remover whose walk does not apply its removal (trusted: with its seeds) with everything named, and a voucher likewise (judging the removal trusted beside its own trust set, whichever side it is on), names the role writes in the order received, each kept only where the removal still applies; `key_order` (control) in key order. An honest frontier names what rests on the lowered edge: the commits through it and the self-removals of it.
- Disputes (`disputes`): a held edge write whose holder is not its signer, giving that edge below admin at its `ts` (a finalize aside: lowerings, grants below admin and invite grants alike), not trusted and not dropped by a trusted write's seeds, whose trust changes the reading (spec/permissions.md §5.1 asks that the walk not apply it and that it lower its edge once trusted: findings 16, 21, 25). An honest re-grant lists what rests on its edge, or `quiet` fails.
- Evidence (`evidence`): edge writes on the edges of held commits' paths and on the remaining admin edge a held lowering names, closed; plus vouches against held removals. Every removal of X reaches every node syncing a thing X could write; whatever exists reaches every node syncing its thing by the settle.
- An explicit write audits when the edge as restored (the highest role and invite since the last explicit write on it, values an `until` or self-removal took included) falls in role or invite (What falls, below). A self-removal only lowers (a higher value applies nowhere) and audits nothing; an `until` passing audits nothing.
- Descent: consecutive path members p, q are joined by the record edges from p down to q where p is a mint ancestor of q, else by their direct edge (so [A] reaches D1 by descent, [A2, T1] by A2's grant on T1 and T1's record edge).
- The oracle (`check`) takes finding 3's limit as accepted, finding 13's behaviour (a re-granted grantee vouching for its own grant) as wanted, the thief's writes heard before the removal as kept where the removal applies with them, and the accomplice as counted.
- Keys (`Withdraw`): `AsWritten` reads spec/crypto.md §4; `SideOnly` is the fix.
- Invites (`invite_check`): an edge write carries `invite` (at most its role; an admin edge gives every invite). An edge write whose path does not give admin applies as an invite grant where it adds a grant below admin to a holder with no current grant (an ended one counts as none) and its role is within the path's invite (the roles of the edges before the last, the last edge's invite); it is not explicit: it audits nothing, is judged by no verdict, raises the edge as restored. Control `-invite-role`: a role on the last edge suffices. An invite holder lowers nothing. A vouch for an invite grant needs admin (membership §4 allows the invite it needed: not modelled).
- Finalize (`Kind::finalize`): a removal with no frontier; it applies iff the grant is past its `until` at its `ts` and its path gives write and at least the grant's role; it audits nothing, cuts no content, leaves the edge as restored, is no lowering (no dispute candidate) and makes nothing standing.
- What falls (`any_fall`): a lowering is judged, and audits, when it takes its edge as restored down in role or in invite (finding 23); its drops are the writes resting on the edge whose need there the restored value met and the new one does not: admin for a write applied by admin, a lowering, a vouch, a self-removal of the edge; for an invite grant (applied as one, or held and not applied) its role on the edges before the last and its role as an invite on the last; write for a finalize. Control `-admin-falls`: only a fall from admin.
- Purge authors (`purge_chain`, `purge_exact`): a commit states an index into [signer, path..]; a purge request by author P (`Kind::Purge`) is content-class, judged as a content write along its path (listed, re-granted; no addendum vouches for it, spec/permissions.md §6; vouches for purge requests, which membership §4 names, are not modelled); it holds the `author` right (the only class modelled) over the earlier deltas on its thing whose purge author is exactly P iff it is valid and P is on its chain. Controls `-purge-any-path` (the chain not read), `-purge-contains` (deltas whose chain holds P).
- Covers (`cover_facts` false): a cover is content-class, judged along its writer's path, and carries content commits on its thing; a valid cover's copy shows a write whatever its own validity (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Copies, §5.3); a node judges only the commits it holds; a list entry or addendum naming a cover names what it carries (the own cover). So a cover by a writer not yet holding a removal carries the removed party's late write for good: the accepted window (finding 26), counted in the story (`window`). The variant `-facts` (rejected: every snapshot would state each carried commit's signer, `ts` and path): a carried write is judged by its own facts, a valid cover standing in only for a commit not held. Bodies, keep bounds and refetching are not modelled.
- An honest explicit write carries its author's frontier, a re-grant included (finding 25).

**Not modelled.** Bodies (which node holds a body, keep bounds, refetching after a cover falls); purge classes other than `author`, and a purge by author reaching copies of a commit not held; an own cover standing whatever demotes its author later; vouches for invite grants and purge requests; demotion to write (a kiosk), with a trusted lowering's seeds read by need (finding 23); accomplices granted inside the theft window; sessions and overlapping dots; hidden mode; the thief's own rotations of A and secrecy; a user keeping what nobody received; re-keys of T, re-seals; bodies dropped as invalid on the wrong side; a node choosing its side by first received (sides are given); promotion (nothing reads whether a thing is independent); the differential against osis-perm.
