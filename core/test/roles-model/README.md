# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules (spec/permissions.md §5), account custody and epochs (§5.4-§5.5): modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm and osis-engine dev-depend on it for their differentials). Module map and API: `src/lib.rs`; findings behind the role timeline: notes/role-timeline.md; disputes: notes/disputes.md.

## Epochs (`src/epochs.rs`)
Layer 2 of the account (spec/permissions.md §5.5) over `custody`'s chain: a world is a settled custody world (its parties, fight and settle) plus P's commits on one to three things in two sync IDs, the rotators' thing lists, listings, R1's and R2's addenda as writers of a thing, and in half the worlds a bound (2 or 3 IDs per thing list), written by the cutoff after the chain world is made. Each node's chain is custody's `View`; the module judges only what counts of P's commits (rule, policy and properties in the module doc). osis-perm `epochs_model_agrees` feeds every observer and receipt prefix of every world to `osis_perm::Epochs`, the chain (held, counting, heads, preferred head, warning, revocations taken) and the epoch rule (pending, counting, saved by an addendum) alike.

In half the worlds nodes snapshot and forget (spec/commits.md §5): outside writers and X snapshot a thing (P's honest nodes too in half of those), a cover carrying every write showing at its writer; a node forgets another's write a standing cover it holds carries (only its writer forgets under an own cover) and receives it anew once none does; an outside writer's covers fall at a random tick or never (its demotion by an admin whose frontier names none, the role layer's abstracted). Before its plain rotation an honest rotator writes its own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.5) of each thing where a write stamped since the epoch it rotates opened shows only through a cover, named by its list.

Properties at every final reading: safety (an X write after its removal shows on the honest side only through an honest word written off the honest side or about what its writer received before it held the removal, at an open head (an unaware sibling's), or through a cover carrying it so: `allowed`), liveness (every write of a node on the honest side shows there, and every other write an honest-side lister's cutoff names), monotone (dropping an honest list, listing or addendum never makes more count), own cover (where a rotation counts and its rotator ends on the honest side, every write but X's after its removal that showed at the rotator only through a cover as it rotated shows). "Shows": counts, saved by an addendum, or carried by a standing cover. Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib epochs`) are the minimal world of each, each asserting its control fails: `a_write_received_before_the_removal_counts_a_later_one_does_not` (`NoCutoff`), `a_write_only_a_witness_received_in_time_counts_by_its_addendum` (`OwnOnly`), `a_siblings_write_at_its_epoch_counts_once_listed_after_the_merge`, `a_write_the_remover_forgot_shows_by_its_own_cover` (`NoOwnCover`: X's write before its removal reaches A and R1 only, both forget it under R1's snapshot, R1 is demoted). Default tests: 20 random worlds and each control but `NoOwnCover` failing within 20; `random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000) the twin.

Choices the spec left open: a receipt in a tick comes before the receiver's own acts that tick and after nothing else (`World::dt`), so a write and a removal received at once leave the write unnamed; a listing names per thing whatever it owes, not keyed by rotation (judgment never reads the key); only thing lists are bounded; a rotation's plain content past the bound is named by its rotator's later listings by the cutoff like anything else (named whatever the cutoff, a write X made after its removal, left out on X's side, was named by a listing after its rotator's switch: 1 world in 8,000). Not modelled: honest re-adds and pairings (custody's), a refused rotation (the chain world cannot undo it: such worlds, about 1 in 9, skip liveness).

## Custody (`src/custody.rs`)
notes/engine-validity-epochs.md, greenfield, the chain under `epochs`: seed custody follows holding (a node opens every box to its key and derives every merge seed whose tips' seeds it holds, counting or not), the chain is D's roster (an epoch is its line of plain rotations; nodes on a line are the creation devices plus additions minus removals carried there; a removal hits its node's rotations off its line), a merge is only a join (its epoch the union of the joined lines, its seed a function of its tips'). Rule, parties, honest policy and properties in the module doc. Parties: A (removes X), B (offline, unaware: rotates boxing X), C (apart: removes T and rotates thing t at its own epoch, the listing-cycle shape), X (every header and old key: forks, revokes A, B or C, signs as X or as Z, adds Z or itself, takes A's removal or a revocation it withholds, answers honest rotations, certifies merges of epochs whose seeds it holds, re-seals its seeds to honest nodes, writes and lists), T (reads only), R1 (outside reader holding t), R2 (outside reader whose user may switch). Schedules random and lockstep; the removal reaching B and C only in the settle in half the worlds (they take X's revocation first); P's users on X's side switch to A's removal in the settle (B in round 0, 1 or 3, or never). Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib custody`) are the minimal world of each property, each also asserting its negative control fails.

The gate runs `custody::random_worlds_hold_large` (2,000 random worlds, about 125 CPU-s; every control below fails at 800) and the default `small_family_sample_holds` (every 40th small-family world); `custody-exh` is a search, run by hand when `custody.rs` changes (about 2,600-2,900 CPU-s, over the gate's whole properties share).

Searches: `cargo run --bin roles-model -- N custody` (random), `-- 0 custody-exh` (the small family: 32 setups (schedule, B switching in round 1 or never, B offline, C apart removing T, X's tick-0 rotation) × the removal late to B and C or not × one move of X at tick 2 from a menu of nine (revoking A, revoking B boxed to B, revoking A claiming A's removal, revoking A taking one withheld, signing as Z adding Z, answering an honest rotation, a write, a merge certificate, a re-seal; at each epoch whose seed it holds; delivered to everyone, to B, C and R1 only, or no one before the settle) × the honest move at tick 3); negative controls `custody-judged`, `custody-roster-bare`, `custody-merge-random`, `custody-stale-shut`, `custody-merge-carries` (random, N worlds). Default tests: 40 random worlds and every 40th world of two small-family setups; `random_worlds_hold_large` and `small_family_holds_large` the twins.

Counted, not failures (8,000 worlds): at P's nodes, X's write at an open epoch an unaware sibling boxed to X counting 2,109 node-prefixes (until the merge closes it); X holding seeds an unaware node minted (the unaware-sibling limit) 20,189 rotations. Choices the model fixed in the design (notes/engine-validity-epochs.md): a certificate's inner signature opens with any joined epoch's seed (else a node holding only some joined seeds reads certificates of one epoch otherwise); a plain rotation for a taken revocation is owed only while the node cannot merge (a taken revocation counts, so some head carries it); an answer is owed only to a rotation that does not stand or stands with a revocation not taken (not to one waiting on its parent epoch); D's roster removes only by taken revocations (a revocation held with its chain but not taken, its line merging a fork signed as a made-up key actor, removes no one: `a_held_revocation_not_taken_removes_no_one`). A certificate by a node a taken removal hits stands with nothing, so at a node on X's side an honest certificate may not stand while another of the same epoch does: certificates are compared among those standing at the node. Fixed by osis-perm's `epochs_model_agrees` (where the model contradicted the spec): rotations held in one tick are placed by tie (the differential's IDs; merge epochs after plain rotations, `epochs::MERGE_TIE`) before depth, as receipts of one millisecond by ID; a certificate is signed at its tips (`joined`, each one's merge signature), not at the heads it joins, so a rotation signed at a merge epoch a larger merge took in is not contested from below by it. Fixed by the engine differential (osis-engine `rotation::tests_custody`, where the model contradicted the spec): the epoch rule's "held and not revoked" clause (a write at a closed epoch no counting removal closes counts unnamed); the rejection rule exempts every rotation signed at the rejector's epoch on a line with the rejected one, a certificate signed at each epoch it joins; merges are answered (named by a certificate) where they join a line not followed; a removal is a rotation of its own, then the node acts as at a pass end; a certificate boxes its joined line's roster, and a device it boxes gets the maker's seeds of that line besides; a device is seen holding a seed only by a box in an item counting at the node, tagged for that seed (one riding along in a box tagged for another is seen by no one); a plain rotation's membership changes open only with the seed of the epoch it is signed at (one whose changes do not open is minted by none); a re-seal is owed per head (the head's seed, its line's riding along), not per tip; a signing node's own commits count at it once a rotation closes their epoch (its rotation's list and listings name them as each rotation applies, so its re-key of t supersedes its own t epochs at revoked epochs: osis-engine `a_t_epoch_its_makers_rotation_closes_stays_current_spec_permissions_5_5`); a node not holding its preferred head's seed re-seals nothing (osis-engine `no_reseal_without_the_preferred_heads_seed_spec_crypto_9`, the engine's fix); placement depth counts a merge as one step, and rotations held at once tie by `World::tie` (the differential's commit IDs). Thing t follows the engine's encrypting rule: a node re-keys t while a current t epoch has a counting box to P at an epoch it names that does not count there or lacks a taken revocation its head carries, the history carrying its established t epochs, its box to P sealed jointly at every head its maker follows, so it opens only where each head's seed is held (spec/crypto.md §4 Account keysets), or none where a head is not named there (an epoch it supersedes, transitively, not held: its height unknown) or does not cover a removal from D it knows: P is left out and re-sealed (`TReseal`) at each current t epoch it lacks a box at once it no longer is (`p_seal`, `a_t_epoch_left_out_at_an_unnamed_head_reads_once_re_sealed`); a t rotation removes P's boxes at the epochs its history carries, so such a box opens only where it and its seeds were held before that rotation (`a_box_a_later_t_rotation_removed_opens_only_if_held_before`); a t epoch is current where its rotation counts, its secret held or not, and a node encrypts under, carries and re-seals only secrets it holds established (from a counting box or a history opened under one), a secret from an item not counting being read only; lacking every current epoch's secret (left out) it does not rotate for that, and its writes on t wait under the newest current epoch until it is re-sealed (re-keying only after its own removal, a node switching back from X's side leaves its writes there under a t epoch the honest side never opens: `writes`, about 1 in 400 random worlds; a history of every held t epoch hands X the honest side's: `secrecy`).

## Own cover (`src/forget.rs`, `Policy::HeldList`)
The built rule (spec/permissions.md §5.1, §5.5, Own cover): a removal lists by ID every commit of the leaver the remover holds; for the leaver's visible writes whose commits it forgot, the remover writes its own fresh snapshot (`Kind::RemSnap`), named by the removal, standing while the removal applies or as its author's snapshot; nodes forget under any valid cover (`Policy::Any`, the comparator). `-- 0 forget` (1,147,776 worlds): fails on a write lost that `Any` shows, a kept body dropped, nodes disagreeing, input order, a forgotten commit reading otherwise than never received, or an attack gaining or costing an honest write; 0 of each. Counted, against listing every commit (`Policy::ListAll`): `Any` loses in 2,482 worlds, `HeldList` in 4,954 (a cover that later counts for nothing: a removal voided with its remover, a witness's cover received late), 410 where the removal stands and a write the remover had received is lost (`held_list_late_cover_limit`: forgotten under the leaver's snapshot that arrived after the removal's `ts`, `Any`'s limit too). Hand tests: `own_snapshot_at_removal_outlives_another_writers_cover`, `own_snapshot_stands_with_its_removal`, `held_list_voided_removal_keeps_what_holders_held`, `kept_history_is_never_covered`. `addenda::random_world` gives half the removals the demoter's own cover, so osis-perm's `addenda_model_agrees` checks `Timeline::own_cover_stands`.
