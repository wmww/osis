# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules (spec/permissions.md §5) and of account signing epochs (issues/account-signing.md): modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm dev-depends on it for its differentials). Module map and API: `src/lib.rs`; findings behind the role timeline: notes/permissions.md; disputes: notes/disputes.md. Choices the spec or plan left open: AMBIGUITIES.md.

## Epochs (`src/epochs.rs`)
One account P (node 0 the remover, X = 1, Y = 2, maybe a sibling), witness writers of other accounts, an optional admin demoting P on thing 0, one outsider. A removal is two commits: the removal commit on the devices actor at the old epoch, received by P's nodes alone on its own schedule, and the rotation, whose devices-actor list names it; a sibling boxes X unless it holds the removal commit, and rotates from the new epoch only once it holds it (the seed sits under the devices actor's read epoch the removal mints). A rotation whose chain is not held is pending: it rejects nothing and is no side. Things sit in one or two labels (a thing may sit in both); every node opens some labels. Generated: content and snapshots by any node, X's removal and in some worlds Y's after it, X re-added, a sibling's concurrent rotation (made before the removal reaches it, boxing X), removed nodes' forks, counter-merges, X forking plainly from its old epoch again after every merge of node 0's (the world ends with one unmerged), backdated writes and own listings, witness snapshots that fall, part bounds of 1 or 2 IDs, covered commits omitted or listed; honest listings and addenda after every change. The rules under test: a counting rotation closes every epoch that does not descend from it (siblings' concurrent epochs included); every node follows one head (of its side's line, else the line it received first) and that epoch alone is open, a merge ending a fork (`World::follow` off: the old rule, no epoch open in a fork); a merge is signed at each fork it names, and a rotation signed at e rejects nothing on a line through e; one cutoff for every lister (others' commits received before the earliest rotation closing their epoch). Checked at every observer and every prefix of its receipts:
- Safety: no write by a node while removed counts at an observer siding with node 0's line, holding that removal with its chain and following a line that holds it; counted apart: such a write at an observer following another line (a sibling's concurrent one, or the removed node's fork, received first: until the merge), a removed node's fork counting and making it valid (the window until node 0's merge arrives; the write reads invalid without the removed nodes' rotations), an unaware sibling's rotation or listing naming it (stated limit), a re-added node's later word reviving it, a witness's valid snapshot carrying it (stated limit). A write valid because of something signed at a sibling's concurrent epoch is a hard failure of its own.
- Re-forks hide nothing: at an observer following node 0's line, dropping a removed node's plain forks (rejecting nothing, nothing built on them) and their lists shows no more honest content. Counted: observers following a removed node's line, and how many of them are told (in doubt), and observers following node 0's line that are told.
- Removal commit: wherever the rotation following it counts, a held removal commit counts (it is the rotator's own commit, named by its devices-actor list).
- Removal stands: at an observer holding a removal with its chain where every removed node's rotation it holds is signed at an epoch node 0's line is signed at (the removal's own, or a sibling's fork node 0's merge named), the removal counts on the observer's default side. This is the only check of the merge's fork signatures and of "rejects nothing on a line through e": without them the contest ends in a dispute, which the safety check (owner-sided observers only) does not see.
- Liveness, at observers holding everything an honest party holds under their labels: every legitimate content commit some party whose word stands received in time (oracle from the history alone: a final member's receipt or its own authorship, a witness's before the earliest rotation the commit's epoch does not descend from, a rotator's list, a chain of honest listings) is visible; nothing is visible beyond the upper bound (receipts before any closer's `ts`).
- Monotone: dropping an honest list, listing or addendum never makes more valid.
- Omit-covered loses nothing against listing every commit.

Hand tests (`cargo test -p roles-model --lib epochs`) state the properties of the build plan's steps 4 and 5 on small worlds; `removed_node_holding_a_sibling_epoch` is the minimal sibling-epoch world.

### Results
| Run | Worlds | Hard failures |
|---|---|---|
| `-- 1000000 epochs` | 1,000,000 (103,615 with a sibling's rotation, 253,591 re-forking after every merge) | 0 |
| `-- 0 epochs-exh` A (one label) | 1,127,364 (187,894 re-forking after every merge) | 0 |
| `-- 0 epochs-exh` B (labels, successive removals, re-add, bound) | 1,283,040 | 0 |
| `-- 0 epochs-exh` C (a sibling rotating after a removal it has not received) | 481,140 | 0 |

Forks, random run: observer-prefixes following a removed node's line (it reached them first) 909,401, of them told (in doubt) 185,547; following node 0's line and told 878,228 (a removed node's counter-merge or superseding fork is a merge off the line), of them with no removed node's rotation held 3,311 (a reader following a sibling's line while node 0's merge, not yet naming it, sits on the other); a removed node's write valid at a reader holding the removal but following another line 36,660 (until the merge). Settle re-forks once, after the last merge: the merge-and-re-fork cycles are the timed phase's (re-forking after every settle merge never converged, and six rounds of merges and listings made the run five times as slow). With `follow` off (the old rule: a fork leaves no epoch open), the hand world `plain_reforks_after_every_merge_hide_nothing` hides the account's later content and fails the re-fork check.

With the sibling free to rotate from the new epoch before holding the removal commit (`wait_for_removal` off), 300,000 random worlds give 609 safety and 199 liveness failures: the sibling boxes X a seed descending from the removal (hand test `sibling_waits_for_the_removal_commit`).

Random run, dimension counts: several labels 333,553 worlds; a thing in two labels 242,040; the rotator missing a label holding P's content 62,830; successive removals 327,720; re-adds 332,753; a part past the bound 44,636; a rotation refused by the bound 18,384 (12,812 left a fork unmerged: final readings not checked); a sibling waiting for the removal commit 14,834. Final readings: a listing at a closed epoch counting 825,366 times; content saved under a label the rotator did not open 27,645; a re-added node's writes from while removed revived 71,576; plain content past the bound saved 43,260. Counted: an observer holding a removal's rotation without its removal commit and a write by the removed node 6,645,104 observer-prefixes (112,530 at P's nodes); fork window 1,070,243; unaware sibling 9,145; listed write pending its part 1,040,434; disputes in 96,375 worlds (164,422 observer-prefixes sided with a thief by default, not checked by the safety check).

Family A: 2 P nodes, 1 witness, 1 outsider, one label and thing, X removed at 3; one or two content commits (author, time 1..4, backdated, receipts immediate, late or never), X's rotation before or after its removal or none, X's counter-merge, a snapshot none / by node 0 / by the witness (falling or not), covered commits omitted or listed. Family B: 3 P nodes, labels L0 and L1, things in L0, in both, in L1; node 0 opens L0 or both, Y one or both, X both; a witness on the shared thing or the L1 thing; the outsider one label or both; X removed then Y, X removed and re-added, or both; bound none or 1; one content commit (author, thing, time 2/4/7, plain or special, receipts of node 0, the other P node and the witness immediate, late or never) alone or with one of four companions. Family C: 3 P nodes (S = 2), a witness and an outsider, one label and thing; X removed at 3, its rotation and removal commit reaching S at 7 and 7, 4 and 7, or 7 and 4; S tries to rotate from 3, 4 or 5 while it lacks either (boxing X unless it holds the removal commit, waiting while it holds the rotation alone), reaching node 0 after 1 or 3 ticks; X does nothing, forks at 6 from the newest epoch it holds, or also counter-merges at 9, and lists its own writes or not; one content commit (node 0, S, or X at its newest epoch or epoch 0, time 2/5/7, each other node's receipt immediate, late or never) alone or with one of four companions; no observer sides with X in it.
