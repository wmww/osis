# roles-model
> Summary: Rust reference models of the role rules along stated paths, accounts as ordinary things (spec/permissions.md §1, §2, §5.1, §5.2, §6): modules, searches with their sizes, and the latest results.

Independent of any osis crate: an oracle, never patched to match the implementation (osis-perm and osis-engine dev-depend on it for their differentials). Module map: `src/lib.rs`; findings: notes/path-model-findings.md.

## Searches
`cargo run --bin roles-model -- <samples> <mode>`; random searches split their samples over every core, exhaustive ones their families.

Tests: the hand worlds are default tests; every sampled search (random worlds, family samples, a control failing a search) is a property, `_plain` at its small size and `_large` at the gate's (`#[ignore = "property"]`). Every roles-model gate selects them, and nothing else can move them (this crate depends on no osis crate), so as default tests they only re-proved the same answer at every other branch's gate. A worker changing the model runs them: `cargo test -p roles-model -- --ignored _plain _large`.

## Paths (`src/paths.rs`, `src/paths/story.rs`)
An oracle of a design that is not built (its walk is spec/permissions.md §5; its evidence rule is in no spec): a write states its path and is judged along it alone (notes/write-path-design.md §1-§2, §5), an account is an ordinary independent thing whose members are node things, each minted with its own key (spec/permissions.md §8). Self-contained: `joint` and `trust` judge authority as a maximum over every path, which is what this design removes. Findings: notes/path-model-findings.md. Rule, in the module doc: `judge(world, rules, view)`, a function of the held commits, the frontier leaves seen and the trust set. One rule set, `Rules::fixed`; `Rules::design` is the first round's reading the `repro_*` tests fail under; each control is `Rules::fixed` with one field changed. osis-perm's A1 walk is to be differentially tested against it.

**Modelled.** Keys (notes/write-path-design.md, Keys K1 to K5): a commit is signed by a key as a thing (`Commit::key`, `World::push_signed`) and rests on its key hop (key, thing) first, then its path's edges (`World::rests`), in resting, the audit, `keeps`, seeds, content and evidence alike; a thing's record holds its own key (`own_key`), at admin, where it names no mint admin (`World::is_record`); the key hop is an edge like any: its value a role, `until` and the flag, written by the grant writes every edge is (by admin along the writer's path, or by may-grant), a path's role the minimum over it and the path's edges; a key lowering its own grant is a self-removal resting on that hop alone, applying whatever the grant gives; the keys' final roles are `Reading::keys`, apart from `roles`. **Readings where the notes are open** (each one field of `Rules`, an argument, or one function):
- A frontier entry in thing t's leaf names its commit's parts on t alone, read where the node sees that leaf (`World::in_leaves`; notes/evidence-model-findings.md 24): a reader holding one thing's leaf reads it as a node holding every leaf. The honest remover (`World::through`, `World::cut`) lists each part resting on the lowered edge in its own thing's leaf (`a_list_entry_names_its_part_on_its_leafs_thing_alone`).
- Re-grant (`Regrant::FirstByRight`, `first_by_right`; notes/write-path-design.md §1 C1, C2, finding 56): from each edge's first held grant whose grantor held the right at its key in the reading (the walk's history, `until` read at its `ts`), applied or dropped.
- A removal is judged in the world its list makes: nothing is exempt from a lowering's drops and no entry is read as absent. Each lowering is judged once (finding 16; notes/write-path-design.md §1): the first time a pass reaches it holding its right and taking its edge from admin, it is void (`self_void`) where its own drops would take its authority, read back along the setters of its path (`keeps`), else applied, its drops added, the pass ended there and the next resumed from the earliest; the verdict stands for the walk (an applied one applies unaudited in every later pass unless dropped, and its drops stay; where its value is above its grant as restored in that pass it applies only by admin along its path, finding 27). A lowering without its right, or taking nothing, gets no verdict and is judged by a later pass. Control `-no-void`: a lowering whose drops take its authority applies. A trusted write the settled walk does not apply loses its seeds, all at once. Control `exempt_chain`: a removal never puts in question the grants its own path rests on, read from the held writes.
- The audit puts in question the held writes before it resting on its edge, not dropped and not void, as the seeds do (`audit_held`, finding 18); control `-audit-applied`, the applied ones.
- What a drop takes (`Drops::Commit`): a part dropped (by an audit or a trusted write's seeds) takes the other grant-class parts on its thing of its commit, not dropped or void, as a list keeps a commit whole: a single-use redemption whose grant falls loses its leave too (notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 23; `a_single_use_link_redeemed_twice_stays_with_the_redemption_the_sharer_listed`). Control `-part-drops` (each part by its own need: a forged earlier redemption burns the link at nodes not trusting the sharer's removal; its hand world; by hand `part_drops_change_no_story_reading`: the story reads alike under both).
- What a cut's list answers (`Answers::PerCut`): a cut is answered by its own list or a counting addendum against it, and answers for itself alone: a delta (and a purge request's right) stands where, per edge of its path, every cut after it answers it and the edge gives the role just before it or its last write before it is an answering cut the edge gave the role before, read at the delta's `ts` (notes/path-model-findings.md 44; `stands`; `a_cuts_list_keeps_a_write_from_that_cut_alone`, `a_write_after_two_cuts_stands_where_each_lists_it`, `a_write_stands_only_where_every_later_cut_of_its_edge_answers_it`, `a_list_gives_no_role_the_path_never_gave`, `an_answered_cut_gives_back_only_what_the_edge_gave_at_the_write`). Control `-list-any-cut` (one answering cut of the path makes it valid: fails `purge safety` in the purge worlds' `o_cut`; the story and `tiny` worlds read alike under both).
- A commit of several grant writes on one target (`World::commit`; spec/commits.md §2): judged part by part like any, at its key: parts in holder order but every part on an edge the commit's stated path rests on (its final hop: a self-removal of the path's last member, as in a single-use link redemption; the record grant a descent ends with; the key hop: a key removing itself acting as its thing; among them, in holder order) after the others, whatever the holders' or keys' order (W7, finding 57: `a_key_replaces_itself_in_one_commit_in_either_key_order`), each judged with the earlier parts in effect (its path and flag); a lowering's `keeps` passes over the drops of the commit's earlier lowerings (`commit_drops`; control `-own-drops`); no part's drops or seeds reach a part of its own commit (no list can name it); the pass ends once the commit took effect. Two parts on one holder's edge are bad input (the `roles.0` delta's keys would repeat). Control `-refuse-shapes`: a commit carrying a self-removal or a part on its own path applies nowhere, which voids the redemption (hand worlds: redeemed once, twice concurrently, a thief with the link key after it).
- `no_own_addendum`: no addendum counts for a commit of its own signer.
- `evidence_leaves`: a node sees a frontier's leaf for the things it syncs and every thing a grant write it holds as evidence is on.
- `leave_in_question`: a lowering of edge h puts a self-removal of h in question like a write through h (finding 14), a timed one and a holder thing's included.
- `rewrite` (writing policy, not a rule): the state-based writing; false, a node answers only the first time its walk applies a removal.
- `stands` (writing policy, L3: `writers_keep`, `end_of`): a remover names what it received resting on the lowered edge; where its walk (the removal trusted) applies the removal with everything named and no hop of the removal's own path ends (`End::Never`), all of it; else the content, then the role writes one at a time in the order received, each kept only where the removal still applies with it and its own path's end (the earliest `ts` at or after the removal's at which a hop stops giving admin) is no earlier than with the list kept so far. `key_order` (control) in key order; `own_end` off (control `-no-end`) keeps wherever the removal applies.
- Sides (`Sides::Received`, `Sim::sides`; notes/write-path-design.md §1 F1 to F5): a story node records every explicit lowering it made, as a choice (`choose`: nothing that cannot stand beside it, `against`, stays recorded), and a held explicit lowering its reading applied just before an update brought a seed of it made by another party, where it has no side in that fight yet; nothing where the seed came first or with it. Its own writes are those it made (`Sim::wrote`), not those its key signed. Shown beside the open disputes: the counter-removals of its recorded sides (`countered`: an explicit lowering a recorded lowering's seeds take, where choosing it changes the reading). The two nodes trusting nothing are nodes with no order by fiat; `Sim::joins` makes any node one. Controls: `-no-own`, `-no-receipt`, `-unapplied` (recorded whether or not the walk applied it), `-tie` (both in one update counted as held first), `-both` (a second side recorded in one fight), `-choice-adds`, `-choice-seeds` (a choice dropping only seeds either way), `-key-own` (own writes by key), `-untold`, `-told-all`; variant `-lowered` (recorded only where it took its hop down). An honest frontier names what rests on the lowered edge: the commits through it and the self-removals of it.
- Disputes (`disputes`, `Candidates::Explicit`): a held explicit lowering whose holder is not its signer (no self-removal, none the walk trusting it applies as a may-grant grant), not trusted and not dropped by a trusted write's seeds, whose trust changes the reading (spec/permissions.md §5.1 asks that the walk not apply it and that it lower its edge once trusted: findings 16, 21, 25, 31). An honest re-grant lists what rests on its edge, or `quiet` fails. Control `-all-candidates` (every grant write of another holder's edge below admin but finalizes): an honest holder leave is shown.
- Seeds (`seeds_by_need`): a trusted write's seeds are the unlisted held writes before it resting on its edge whose least need there its value does not meet (finding 32); control `-seeds-all`.
- Evidence (`evidence`, spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit; the proofs searches use it too): a held commit's other parts on the same thing and grant writes on the edges held parts rest on or their grant writes set, closed. A part on another thing of a held commit is that thing's unit's, with its own path (notes/path-form.md). Every removal of X reaches every node syncing a thing X could write; whatever exists reaches every node syncing its thing by the settle.
- An explicit write audits when the grant as restored (the highest role and flag since the last explicit write on it, values an `until` or self-removal took included) falls in role or flag (What falls, below). A self-removal only lowers (it removes the grant, or raises none of role, flag and `until`; else it applies nowhere) and audits nothing; an `until` passing audits nothing.
- Descent: consecutive path members p, q are joined by the record grants from p down to q where p is a mint ancestor of q and the path does not mark the pair direct (`World::push_marked`; notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 21: no generator marks one), else by their direct grant (so [A] reaches D1 by descent, [A2, T1] by A2's grant on T1 and T1's record grant).
- The oracle (`check`) takes the thief's writes heard before the removal as kept where the removal applies with them.
- Keys (`Withdraw`): `AsWritten` reads spec/crypto.md §4; `SideOnly` is the fix.
- May-grant (`flag_check`, `min_check`; notes/write-path-design.md §1 G1 to G5, finding 54): a grant write carries a flag (`Kind::Edge::may_grant`). A path's last hop lets its holder grant its role where it carries the flag, every role at admin (`grantable`). A grant write whose path does not give admin applies as a may-grant grant where it adds a grant below admin to a holder with no current grant (an ended one counts as none), every hop before the last gives its role and the last gives it with the flag; the new grant may carry the flag; it is not explicit: it audits nothing, is judged by no verdict, raises the grant as restored. A lower level is an arrangement (G3): a thing holding the lower role with the flag, written by the person (the story's GU). Controls `-no-flag` (the last hop's role suffices), `-last-hop` (the hops before the last unread), `-role-falls` (clearing the flag puts nothing in question). A holder by the flag lowers nothing. A vouch for a may-grant grant needs admin (membership §4 allows the flag it needed: not modelled).
- Finalize (`Kind::finalize`): a removal with no frontier of another holder's grant that is no self-removal (a shape: `World::push` clears the flag on any other write); it applies iff the grant is past its `until` at its `ts` and its path gives write and at least the grant's role; it audits nothing, cuts no content, leaves the grant as restored, is no lowering (no dispute candidate) and makes nothing standing.
- What falls (`any_fall`): a lowering is judged, and audits, when it takes its grant as restored down in role or in the flag (`flag_falls`; findings 23, 54); its drops are the writes resting on the edge whose need there the restored value met and the new one does not: admin for a write applied by admin, a lowering, a self-removal of the edge; for a may-grant grant (applied as one, or held and not applied) its role on the hops before the last and its role with the flag on the last; write for a finalize. A trusted write's seeds read the same needs (held, not applied) against its value. Control `-admin-falls`: only a fall from admin.
- Purge authors (`purge_chain`, `purge_exact`): a commit states an index into [signer, path..]; a purge request (`Kind::Purge`, naming by author P, by whole aspect, by commit or the thing's creation: `Names`) is content-class, judged as a content write along its path (listed, re-granted; no addendum vouches for it, spec/permissions.md §6). Under `fixed` a request by author holds the `author` right alone over the earlier deltas on its thing whose purge author is exactly P iff it is valid and P is on its chain, purge-by unread, held deltas alone. Controls `-purge-any-path` (the chain not read), `-purge-contains` (deltas whose chain holds P). An index one past the path names the thing written, which no request's chain holds (notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 16).
- Purges (`Rules::purging`: the draft's readings; notes/purge-model.md): each content delta carries a purge-by (`PurgeBy`); a request holds, over each delta it names, each member of the delta's set it holds (`purge_classes`, `holds`: role r held where a delta of it needing r would be valid, cuts below r, lists, re-grant for r); a destruction holds `admin` alone over every delta of its thing, judged as any request; a request reaches the writes a valid cover shows of a commit not held (`purge_copies`); an own cover (`World::own_cover`) whose path gave write just before it stands while an explicit lowering by its writer naming it applies, whatever that lowering takes from its edge at the reading node (`own_cover`; `an_own_cover_stands_while_its_removal_applies_whatever_it_takes_here`, `an_own_cover_whose_path_gave_no_role_stands_by_nothing`). Proposed: `purge_addenda` (an addendum by a holder of the class's role vouches for a request; finding 41). Variant `purge_at_key` (the right read at the key alone).
- One stated path (`World::stated`, notes/path-form.md): a commit states one path for every thing it writes; a thing that is the path's last is written along the path less itself, any other one hop past the end. The split form (`World::split`): one commit, each thing its own path, one purge author a member of every thing's path. Parts keep their members, so no rule reads either.
- Covers (`cover_facts` false): a cover is content-class, judged along its writer's path, and carries content commits on its thing; a valid cover's copy shows a write whatever its own validity (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Copies, §5.3); a node judges only the commits it holds; a list entry or addendum naming a cover names what it carries (the own cover). So a cover by a writer not yet holding a removal carries the removed party's late write for good: the accepted window (finding 26), counted in the story (`window`). The variant `-facts` (rejected: every snapshot would state each carried commit's signer, `ts` and path): a carried write is judged by its own facts, a valid cover standing in only for a commit not held. Bodies, keep bounds and refetching are not modelled.
- An honest explicit write carries its author's frontier, a re-grant included (finding 25).
- Keys (`Rules::key_hop`; notes/path-model-findings.md 55): control `-no-key-hop` (nothing reads the key hop: a removed key keeps signing).
- End dates (`Rules::until`, `Until::Plain`): a write giving admin at its `ts` with an `until` earlier than its edge's own end (a standing admin given an end date, or an end shortened) is a grant: it applies where its path gives admin in the pass, carries no frontier, is no candidate. Variant (finding 38), failing a required gate: `-until-at-key` (a lowering judged at its key: its drops, the writes resting on its edge whose need its value does not meet at their own `ts`, are none, so its one effect is the verdict; dropping forward past the end instead fails liveness, as drops only grow).
