# Rotation model: ambiguities and findings
> Summary: Places where spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync.md §2/§5 and spec/permissions.md §3–§4 left a choice or failed a checked property. R1–R9 and residue 1 are resolved by spec edits and the model follows the new text (residue 1's chain rule since replaced by names, R16); residue 2 of issues/reseal-triggers.md (refused stranded heartbeat) still reproduces as the one expected failure (`MC_drop_refresh.cfg`). R10–R15 are settled: R10, R13 by spec edits, R11 by an earlier one, R12 a modeling limit, R14, R15 decided by the text. R17 (forged holder keysets) is closed by a spec edit; R18 is open (an expected failure).

## Resolved (model updated, rerun)
- **R1. Re-seal raced a removal.** permissions §3 and commits §2: the removal and the rotation it requires are one role commit; boxing a secret is encrypting under it (crypto §4), so a re-sealer that knows a departure re-seals only at covering epochs. Model: `Remove` is a rotation carrying the removal (`rem`), `Recv` learns it, the remover uploads it before anything else.
- **R2. A holder past a dropped claim set was never reached.** permissions §4: the stranded heartbeat is also placed when a claim on the way is refused; crypto §3: refreshing the deposit seed is a MAY. `MC_drop.cfg` passes `CurrentOpens`; with the seed refreshed by every rotation, residue 2 below.
- **R3. A re-sealed holder or grantee could not read older epochs.** crypto §4: a grant or re-seal boxing e re-writes e's history box. `MC_grant.cfg` passes.
- **R4. "Newer than the head" across a fork.** sync §5: compare by (n, nonce).
- **R5. Encrypting under the newest epoch when not held.** crypto §5: the newest current epoch whose secret it holds; holding none, only the stranded heartbeat, under the newest established secret held. Model: `EncEp`; `WriteContent` requires a held current epoch.
- **R6. Is re-sealing encrypting under?** crypto §4: yes. `Reseal` and `Grant` only at covering epochs; `RotateForced` also fires for a re-seal blocked by a non-covering epoch.
- **R7. Claim set after a re-seal.** sync §2: a holder re-sealed later opens by sync key.
- **Residue 1** (issues/reseal-triggers.md; a rotation read out of order, with a fork `MC_fork_reorder.cfg` or without `MC_reorder.cfg`: a node that holds a later rotation but not the one carrying the removal re-sealed the removed holder). crypto §4 **established epochs**: an epoch counts (current, newest, encrypted under, history boxes) only once the node applied its rotation commit and (then) every epoch it supersedes is established, so holding an epoch meant holding every departure its chain carried. Model: `Est(x)`, `Cur`, `HeldCur`, `EncEp`, `NeedsReg`, `ResealE` over it. Both instances passed `NoLeak` and `DepartedLocked`; they pass again under names (R16).
- **R9. A secret got by re-seal before its rotation commit leaked through a history box** (was `MC_hist.cfg`). crypto §4: a history box carries secrets of established epochs only (`Hist(a) = sec[a] ∩ Est(a)`, the `HistApplied` switch is gone); sync §5 and permissions §3: the rotation commits an admin holds go to the host before it registers the step (`Register` requires `kn[a] \ {0} ⊆ hostRot`; `UploadRot` no longer waits for registration), so the window the trace used is closed twice. `MC_hist.cfg` became identical to `MC_safety.cfg` and was deleted.
- **R8. "A sync pass that fetched everything" relative to what.** permissions §3: "at the end of a complete sync pass of the actor's labels (one that read the log of every host and peer it syncs them with to its end)". Model: `FullPass(a)` = drained at the host and at every co-reader peer `a` has received a rotation commit from (`via`) that is online. With established epochs the pass rule is no longer what keeps the reorder instances safe.

- **R16. Names replace the chain rule** (issues/epoch-establishment-redesign.md step 4). crypto §4: an epoch whose secret the node holds is established only once its history box's names are met: the removals its rotator held (and itself when it removes someone) applied here, and the holder actors' keyset epochs its boxes named held here; no chain through other rotators. Model: `Est(x)` over `nm` and `nk`. `MC_reorder` and `MC_fork_reorder` (residue 1) pass; `MC_withhold` (the host's log skips the removal for a2 forever, nor does it come by inbox) passes. Negative controls fail as expected: `MC_names_own` (a rotation names only itself, the departs mark: a2 establishes a1's merge without the removal it carried by its other parent and re-seals the removed holder) and `MC_keyset_nonames` (no keyset names: a2, not holding g's new keyset, establishes a1's epoch boxing g at it, then rotates boxing g at the old keyset with that epoch in the history box, so the departed device opens it; `MC_keyset` passes with them). No epoch starts are modeled, so the roster-snapshot hole of keyset names named by commit (fixed in spec/crypto.md §4 by naming the keyset epoch) is out of the model's reach.
- **R17. A removed admin of the holder actor forges its keyset.** Keyset names met by any held valid keyset (R16) are met by d's forgery where d's removal is withheld: `MC_keyset_forge_nogroup` fails `DevLocked` (a1 boxes g at keyset 1 naming it; d writes 3 listing 1; a2, lacking R_P, holds 3 valid, establishes a1's epoch and rotates boxing g at 3 with it in the history box). Naming the commit that wrote the keyset instead fails alike once g rotated again (`MC_keyset_forge_commit`: a1 names keyset 2, which a2 holds; 3 lists it). crypto §4 now names the departures from every actor below the rotated one: `MC_keyset_forge` and `MC_keyset_forge_rot` pass, and with a roster snapshot of g after R_P standing in for R_P (`GSnap`) and no keyset names, `MC_keyset_nonames` fails as before (the roster-snapshot escape, now in the model).
- **R18. An epoch current again after its successor lost establishment** (issues/unestablished-successor-history.md). a1, before R_P, rotates to e1 boxing g at 2, then to e2 boxing g at d's 3 (e1 in e2's history box); R_P arrives, 3 is invalid, e2's keyset name unmet, e1 is current again and covers d by its own box, and a1 writes under it. Fails `DevNoLeak` under the box rule; under frozen coverage (R19) e1 names no departure from g, so a1 rotates first: `MC_keyset_forge_write` passes.
- **R19. Frozen coverage replaces the box-inspection covers rule** (notes/epoch-establishment-study.md). Under the box rule (`Frozen = FALSE`) TLC finds the study's counterexamples: C2 (`MC_c2_box`, `NoLeak`, with a grant where the study has a re-seal: a2 rotates to e1, then grants m2 at e1; a1, not holding that grant's box, removes m2 as e2, a fork of e1, takes e1 (lower id), which as a1 knows holds no box for m2, and writes under it; m2 opens e1 from the grant box), C1 (`MC_c1_box`, `DevNoLeak`), C1' (`MC_c1f_box`, `DevNoLeak`, by the R18 shape first) and C3 (`MC_c3_box`, `DevGranted`). Frozen coverage: an epoch covers exactly the departures its names name (`CoversFrozen`); boxes decide nothing; a node boxes g only at a keyset it judges covering d's departure when it knows it (`GOK`) and only holding the names of the boxes to g it holds (`GNamesOK`). `MC_keyset_forge_write` passes; `MC_c2`, `MC_c1`, `MC_c1f` (MaxE 2) found no violation in 0.85-1.0 million distinct states each (stopped unfinished). Negative control `MC_c2_boxcovers` (no box counts as covering again) fails `NoLeak`. `DepartedLocked` is no property of frozen coverage (a fork sibling may be re-sealed to a departed holder that no writer relies on it for) and is checked only where it still holds.
- **R20. Whose names a boxer of a holder actor must hold** (study §3 "Box a holder actor"). The study's rule, the names of every held grant or re-seal box to g at a current epoch the boxer holds (`GNamesBy = "current"`), fails `DevGranted` two ways: `MC_c3_current` (a1 knows R_P but holds no covering keyset of g, so its grant leaves g out and no box carries its names; a2 reads the grant and rotates boxing g at keyset 0) and `MC_c3_boxed` (grants that leave g out refused: a2 rotates e1 before reading a1's grant at epoch 0; once read, that box is at a superseded epoch, so a2 rotates boxing g at keyset 0 with 0 and e1 in the history box). Tied to the grant instead (`GNamesBy = "grant"`: a boxer of g holds the names of every grant of g it holds, at any epoch, and a grant that would leave g out is refused), `MC_c3` and `MC_c3_lean` found no violation in 5.5 and 0.9 million distinct states (stopped unfinished). Negative control `MC_c3_nonames` (grant and re-seal boxes carry no names) fails `DevGranted`.

## Residue 2: refused stranded heartbeat (TLC: `MC_drop_refresh.cfg`, CurrentOpens fails, expected)
- issues/reseal-triggers.md. With the deposit seed refreshed at each rotation, a holder past a dropped claim set that never read the newest `keys.0` cannot deposit its stranded heartbeat, holds a box at every epoch (no missing-box trigger) and is never re-sealed. Trace (12 states): a1 and a2 rotate concurrently (e1, e2, one n) and upload; e2 is registered, then, once both are held, e1 (same n, lower nonce); the host drops the claim sets of the steps for 0 and e2; m1, still at epoch 0, is refused at e2's step and its heartbeat deposit too (it never read e1's `keys.0`). Owner call (accept for the MVP is recommended).

## R10. Re-registration: "holds the epochs a host's head registers" (resolved)
- sync §5: an admin that "holds the epochs a host's head registers and ... an established epoch newer than the head's" re-registers. Holds = holds the secret, or has the head's epoch established? Model: holds the secret (`Top.ep ∈ sec[a]`); the newer epoch must be established and held. Either reading passes every instance.
- Resolved: sync §5 now says "holds the epochs a host's head registers (the write and admin ones established)", as osis-node does (`step_due`, `head_keys`: stamps and steps sign under them, which is encrypting under; the read one only opens). The model is looser (one epoch for all roles, held from a valid box, not necessarily established); both pass.

## R11. Which peers a complete pass covers (resolved)
- permissions §3 "every host and peer it syncs them with": the model counts peers it received rotation commits from and that are online now. A peer that went offline mid-pass is not waited for; the instances that use peers have no offline steps, so this is not exercised.
- Resolved: permissions §3 already says "every peer it received the label from, can reach and that still reads it" (a peer it cannot reach is not waited for), as osis-node's `update_pass_peers` does. The model counts peers it received a rotation commit from, a subset.

## R12. Snapshot-established epochs (not modeled; modeling limit)
- crypto §4 lets a `secrets.0` compaction snapshot establish an epoch; permissions §3 re-seals only at chain-established epochs and lets such a node rotate instead. The model has no `secrets.0` snapshot, so every established epoch is chain-established and the accepted post-compaction residue (notes/sync.md Limits) is out of reach. An instance with a snapshot step is optional future work. Not an ambiguity: the text decides it.

## R13. Rotation by inbox: "holds no box at a current read epoch before it" (resolved)
- permissions §3: a rotation is also delivered by inbox to a holder it boxes that "holds no box at a current read epoch before it". Current as whom, and boxes as known to whom? Model: the epochs the rotation supersedes (the rotator's current ones) and the boxes the rotator knows (applied rotations, read grant and re-seal commits). A box the rotator has not seen yet only adds an inbox copy.
- Resolved: permissions §3 now adds "as the rotator's applied state stands", as osis-node's `rotation::unreached` does (its `secrets.0` boxes, epochs current at it). Model unchanged.

## R14. Wrap of a re-seal placed under the label (not an ambiguity)
- permissions §3 places a re-seal "under the actor's labels like any role commit", so it is wrapped under the re-sealer's newest current epoch (crypto §5), which is encrypting under that epoch too: the re-sealer must cover every known departure there as well, not only at the epochs it re-seals. Model: `Reseal` and `RotateForced` include `EncEp(a)`. The re-sealed holder usually cannot open the label copy (the trigger is that it lacks an epoch); its path stays the inbox.
- Not an ambiguity: crypto §4 counts a label wrap as encrypting under and permissions §3 places the re-seal under the labels, so the text decides it. osis-node holds back every placement while read is uncovered (sync pass `read_ok`).

## R15. Heartbeat of a node holding only reading-only secrets (not an ambiguity)
- crypto §4 (secrets from a pending or invalid commit are never encrypted under) against crypto §5 / permissions §4 (the stranded heartbeat "is wrapped under the newest established read secret the node holds"). A grantee whose grant box has not arrived but that holds a planted secret of an established epoch: "holds" must exclude the planted one, so it places no heartbeat until a valid box arrives. Model: `Heartbeat` needs an established secret from a valid box. Negative control (README): with `Enc(x) == sec[x] \cup fsec[x]` TLC finds this 4-state trace (grant, plant, heartbeat under the planted secret), so the crypto §4 sentence is what closes it. Not an ambiguity: that crypto §4 sentence ("never encrypted under until an applied valid box carries it") decides it. osis-keys `RoleEpochs::held` yields checked secrets only, so `stranded_secret` never picks a candidate.
