# Rotation model: assumptions
> Summary: What Rotation.tla abstracts away from spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync.md §2/§5 and spec/permissions.md §3–§4, and why.

- **One actor, one role rotated: read.** Its label sits at one host. Write and admin secrets are never rotated, so step signatures (parent's delete key) and stamps always verify. Rotating read is what decides who opens the label (sync key, claim keys, wraps).
- **Crypto is access.** `sec[x]` = epochs whose read secret x holds from an applied valid box. A rotation's boxes are `ep[e].ob`; grant and re-seal commits are records in `bx` (holder, epochs boxed, wrap epoch); history box = `ep[e].hist`, the established secrets the rotator held. Sealed vs symmetric box forms are not modeled; every holder but the group (below) is a single node with one static key. So the departed-rotator rule (an epoch written by a departed party does not cover its departure) is implied (a rotator boxes itself).
- **One holder actor** (`Groups`, at most one reader `g`): a node standing for its staying devices, plus a passive device d, an admin of g, that opens every box to g at keyset epoch 0 or 3 and the history boxes they carry (`DevSec`). g's keyset bodies are 0 (initial), 1 (R_P: d's removal, by g's admin outside the model; it carries the departure), 2 (`GRot`: g's admin rotates it again, listing 0 and 1, carrying nothing of d) and 3 (`Forge`: d, removed, writes a keyset with its own keys listing every keyset made; epoch IDs are public). `GLeave`, `GRot`, `Forge` are unfair and happen once. Admins learn bodies (`ks`, `LearnK`, fair); holding 1 is holding R_P (`gd`). `GSnap` gives a withheld admin a roster snapshot of g made after R_P (`gs`): it meets the name of the departure and makes 3 invalid (the ledger ends d's admin tenure) but, conservatively, teaches nothing to the covers rule (as for a reader's departure, which has no ledger entry). Body 3 is valid where neither R_P nor that snapshot is held (`VK`). A rotation, grant or re-seal boxes g at the newest valid keyset of g its writer holds (`GK`; ids order as n); the covers rule counts d's departure covered at an epoch, at a node holding R_P, only where no box to g it knows of names 0 or 3. With `Split`, R_P reaches A's admins as two parts learned separately and in any order: its `keys.0` part (body 1, `LearnK`) and its `roles.0` part (the departure, `LearnRP`, never to `Withhold`); `gd` is the roles part, and body 3 is invalid where it is held. `GFork` (once, unfair) adds keyset 4: an honest admin of g that has not seen R_P rotates g from keyset 0 (a fork of 1, boxing d, LWW-newer in `keys.0`). `GK` takes the greatest valid held body id, so an instance enables at most one of "rot", "forge" and "fork" when their order matters. Left out (permissions §3): a node knowing R_P boxes g only at a keyset other than 0, 3 or 4 (`GOK`). One departing device, one level of holders: no nested holder actors.
- **Box knowledge is per node** (`Holders(x, e)`): the boxes of the rotation commits x applied and of the grant and re-seal commits x read. The re-seal trigger, claim sets, the rotation-by-inbox test and the box-inspection covers rule (`Frozen = FALSE`) use it.
- **Frozen coverage** (`Frozen = TRUE`, the default; AMBIGUITIES.md R19, R20): e covers a removal iff a rotation commit its rotator held carries it or e's own does (`NamedRem`), and d's departure from g iff its rotator held R_P (or a snapshot after it) while g was a holder there (`ng`). A grant or re-seal record to g carries its writer's names for g as `kd` (it held R_P; `GrantNames = FALSE`: never) and the truth as `kn`. A node boxes g (rotation, grant, re-seal) only where `GNamesOK`: it holds R_P (or the snapshot) if a grant or re-seal of g it has read names it, among those boxing an epoch it holds current (`GNamesBy = "current"`, the study's rule), those too when grants that would leave g out are refused (`"boxed"`), or every grant of g it has read (`"grant"`). Knowing g is a holder comes only from reading its grant, so grant edge and grant box are one record (no projection of one without the other). `DevGranted` (g granted once, by a granter that held R_P: d opens nothing of A) is the second half of the study's NoLeak.
- **Planted boxes** (crypto §4): an unfair, bounded adversary step `Plant` gives a holder a secret of an existing epoch from a box a non-holder sealed to it in a pending or invalid commit (`fsec`). Forged secrets open nothing (the real secret differs); the node may read with them but `Enc(x)`, the secrets it encrypts under, excludes them. `leakP` records any wrap, box, history box or step signed under a non-valid secret.
- **Established epochs by names** (crypto §4): `Est(x)` = epoch 0 plus every rotation x holds (`kn`) whose names are met where x holds its secret: every removal its rotator held (`nm`, the rotation commits carrying one that it had applied; the commit itself carries its own, the departs mark) is in `kn[x]`, the departure from g its rotator held (`ng`: R_P or the snapshot) is held, and the group keyset epoch its box to g named (`nk`) is held: some valid body writes or lists it (`KeysetBy = "commit"`: the body of that epoch itself, a negative control). An own rotation is in `kn` at once (its commit applies when made). No epoch starts (roster snapshots) of the rotated actor, so no removal name is met by one; rotation commits are never invalid (every rotator stays an admin); one history entry per epoch (no forged second entry). Current, newest, encrypting under, history boxes, re-registration and re-seal all range over it; opening and reading use any held secret. No `secrets.0` compaction snapshots: every established epoch is established by its rotation commit, and the post-compaction residue (notes/sync.md Limits) is out of reach (AMBIGUITIES.md R12). Negative controls: `OwnNames` (a rotation names only itself), `KeysetNames = FALSE` (no keyset names), `GroupNames = FALSE` (no names of departures from g) and `KeysetBy = "commit"`.
- **Withholding** (`Withhold`, a set of admins): the host and every peer withhold every removal (a rotation commit carrying one) from them forever, by download and by inbox (in log order the host's log skips it for them), and they never learn g's keyset 1 (R_P). Instances using it check invariants only (the withheld node never converges).
- **Epoch ids are nonces**: epoch `k` is the k-th rotation; ties in `n` break by lowest id. Forks arise from concurrent rotations.
- **Holders** = `Admins ∪ Readers`; those not in `Late` are boxed at epoch 0, `Late` ones join by an unfair `Grant` (once; a Late holder is removed only after its grant) (boxes at the granter's held current epochs, delivered by inbox; the grant commit goes under the label wrapped under the granter's newest epoch, and other nodes learn the holder and its boxes from it). Only `Remover` removes, only holders of `Departing`. Demotion and `until` are not modeled (`until` is out of re-seal scope by permissions §3).
- **Removal is a rotation** (commits §2): one commit, carrying the removal (`rem`), boxing the remover's known holders minus the departed one, readable under `WrapOf(k)` = newest superseded epoch (crypto §5). Receiving it teaches the removal. The remover uploads it before any other rotation it holds.
- **Registration** (sync §5): an admin that holds the head's epoch and a newer established one first uploads every rotation commit it holds that the host lacks, then registers its newest epoch; its claim set is the epoch's box holders as the global `ep` shows. Steps never fork (one host). Steps without a rotation (the MAY in sync §2) are not modeled.
- **Open** is one atomic check (`CanOpen`): a sync key held for some step on the path, then for every later step either its sync key or a listed, not-dropped claim key. The n round trips and step verification are folded into that check.
- **Rotation by inbox** (permissions §3): a rotation is also sent by inbox to each holder it boxes that, as the rotator knows, holds no box at any epoch the rotation supersedes.
- **Delivery**: `InOrder = TRUE` is one host read in log order (cursor sync). `InOrder = FALSE` allows any order and lets an online co-reader peer serve the rotation commits it holds (range reconciliation, a second host, a peer).
- **Re-seal** (a SHOULD, treated as done: fair) only by an admin that has completed a full pass (R8): nothing readable left at the host (rotations and grants) and at every online co-reader peer it has received from. A re-seal goes by inbox and under the label (wrapped under the re-sealer's newest epoch, so every reader learns its boxes; the re-sealed holder usually cannot open it there). Re-seal and grant boxes, and their wraps, only at epochs covering every departure the node knows (boxing and wrapping are encrypting under); a needed re-seal at a non-covering epoch makes rotation forced. The manual re-share (an app action) is not modeled.
- **Inbox delivery** is reliable and needs no label access; it gives the boxed epochs' secrets and their history boxes.
- **Stranded heartbeat** is placed (by deposit, admins see it at once; wrapped under the newest established secret held from a valid box, none: no heartbeat) by a holder that does not know its own removal when it reached the head but cannot open its epoch and can download nothing more, or when a claim on the way is refused (`~CanOpen`). With `Refresh = TRUE` every rotation refreshes the deposit seed and the deposit is refused unless the node holds the rotation commit the head registers (it has read that `keys.0`).
- **Content** is one record per write: the epoch written under and the departures the writer knew. Writes happen only while the writer's newest epoch covers every departure it knows (otherwise `RotateForced` is enabled).
- **Claim-set drop** after `ROLE_KEEP` is an unfair host step on any superseded step (time abstracted).
- **Liveness under weak fairness** of forced rotation, registration, upload, download, grant receipt, re-seal, heartbeat and inbox receipt. Eager rotation, removal, grants, writes, claim-set drops and going offline are unfair. `MaxE` bounds rotations, so a forced rotation past the bound cannot happen; no liveness instance needs one (their only departure is a removal, which is itself the rotation).
