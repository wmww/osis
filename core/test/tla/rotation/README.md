# Rotation, claim steps, re-seal (TLA+)
> Summary: TLC model of read-role rotation for one actor and its label at one host (spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync.md §2/§5, spec/permissions.md §3–§4, spec/commits.md §2): epochs established by names (the removals a rotation's rotator held), frozen coverage (an epoch covers exactly the departures it names), a removal withheld forever from one admin, forced and eager rotation, forks, removal carried in its rotation, grants, re-registration after uploading the rotation chain, opens by sync key and claims, claim-set drops, stranded heartbeats (also on a refused claim), re-seal after a complete pass (by inbox and under the label), rotation by inbox to holders without a box at the epoch before, per-node box knowledge, co-reader peers, planted boxes (reading-only secrets). Holder actors are Depth0.tla's (depth-0 sealing, rule 4 for cycles); timed edges and finalizes Finalize.tla's. Written from spec/ alone.

Files: `Rotation.tla` (the model; constants come straight from each `.cfg`), `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `AMBIGUITIES.md` (the findings). `Depth0.tla`, `Finalize.tla`, `Cover.tla`, `Lockout.tla` and `Authority.tla` are separate models (sections below).

## Run
`../run.sh rotation` (and `depth0`, `lockout`, `finalize`, `cover`, `authority`) runs a model's listed instances, each model about a minute or less at one worker on a quiet box; `../run.sh <module> <cfg>...` runs others by name. It fetches TLC into `~/.cache/tla/` if missing. By hand, from this directory (Java 11+):
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir /tmp/tlc-states -config MC_grant.cfg Rotation
```

Times in the tables are wall clock at one worker on a quiet 16-core box for the run.sh instances; by-hand rows give their workers and load. By-hand rows record their last run: they were not rerun with the models as they stand.

## Properties
Invariants: `NoLeak` (crypto §4 covers rule: content written while the writer knew of M's departure is never readable by M), `DepartedLocked` (permissions §3: a departed holder never holds an epoch whose rotation did not box it), `NoPlantedKey` (crypto §4: a secret learned only from a pending or invalid commit is never encrypted under: no wrap, box, history box, step or heartbeat under it). `NoPlantedKey` holds because `Enc(x)` (what x encrypts under) is `sec[x]`; negative control: with `Enc(x) == sec[x] \cup fsec[x]` (the text before the edit) `MC_plant` fails in 4 states (a grantee heartbeats under a planted secret before its grant box arrives; AMBIGUITIES.md R15). Temporal, under weak fairness of honest steps: `CurrentOpens` (every current holder eventually holds every current epoch), `ReadsAll` (... and every epoch content was written under, older ones by history boxes). "A writer never encrypts under a superseded epoch after it holds the rotation" holds by construction (`EncEp` takes the newest held current epoch) and is not a separate check.

## Not covered
Write and admin rotation, holder actors (Depth0.tla), forged history entries, invalid rotation commits, demotion and `until` (`until` and finalizes: Finalize.tla), several hosts or branches of the step tree, steps without rotation, entry boxes and key-actor readers, `label.0` records, `ROLE_KEEP` compaction of steps in `label.0` and of rotation commits (`secrets.0` snapshots, so snapshot-established epochs and the post-compaction residue of notes/label-access.md Limits).

## Depth0.tla: depth-0 sealing (notes/keys-requirements-study.md, notes/crypto.md (Holder actors at depth 0))
Several actors and holder actors, read role only, an actor's keyset = its read epoch; crypto as access (a node's secrets: the closure over boxes and history boxes of the commits it holds; a removed device's: over every box ever written); any delivery order; ignorant forks, unprompted rotations and one forged keyset as one-shot steps; A's own departures by frozen coverage (no establishment, hosts or claims: Rotation.tla's). The rules: anchored sealing, listed staleness, merge (by a fork's merger, `MaxRace` bounding merges made while lacking an epoch of the actor; 99: unbounded), Seal waiting while the anchors fork (a tainted anchor still leaves the actor out), any removal going without its rotation (a superset of the built cases: a self-removal, Seal waiting, Authority failing; uncovered until a rotation covers it), `Blocked` (an epoch whose secret a node holds is blocked there when it misses a departure the node knows other than its own (`Judge`), or, for an admin, is stale, keyless or boxes an actor at a blocked epoch; a rotation leaves out an actor whose Seal keyset is blocked; nobody writes under a blocked epoch; `Walk` picks how far the box clause descends, issues/sealer-cannot-judge-holder.md), `CarryFree` (a re-seal boxes a holder at a blocked epoch too: a box carrying a secret is encrypted under its recipient's key), and for cycles rule 4 (no key cycles). Actor grants come in an agreed order (`Grants`); each node gives key access to the grants it holds greedily in that order (`KH`), seals no keyless grant, and rotates its actor while a current epoch boxes an actor without key access (`Keyless`). `Hidden` grants are missing at a node at first (learned fairly; made later, so initial epochs do not box them); `MaxBlind` bounds the boxes sealed to a holder with no true key access (the ignorance window as one-shot adversary steps). Taint: a keyset boxing an actor without key access is stale and never sealed to. Removed-member writer (issues/keys-depth0-removed-member-rotates.md): `Via` writers act on an actor through holder actors while they do not know their removal from one; their rotation is invalid where that removal is known (an epoch's own departures only travel with it; a removal also travels as a placement under a label). `NoSync` limits what a node learns to its labels and its targets' holders' `keys.0`; `Split` lets such a `keys.0` arrive without the keysets it names; Possession: such a writer rotates only sealing a holder actor at a keyset whose secret it holds; each rotation proves the holder actors it boxes at keysets whose secret it holds (`pf`), and a proof naming a stale keyset is a stale box of its epoch (`ProofOnly`: an unprompted rotation boxing no holder actor, proving each). The model invalidates such a writer's rotation wherever its removal is known; the build judges its role just before the commit's key, so one ordered before the removal stays valid and only staleness retires it (as in `D0_rm_proof`, where a1 never learns the removal); a rotation removing the rotator's own route and proving it (engine `route_removal_proved_leaks_nothing_spec_crypto_4`) is valid in the build and invalid here. First admin (spec/crypto.md §4 Key access; issues/derived-write-merge.md): `FirstAdm` record edges come first in `Grants`; a `Deps` entry may remove an actor edge (`<<holder actor, target, remover>>`), and a node judges key access over the grants it holds less the actor edges it knows removed (a keyless grant turns keyed and is re-sealed); a removed party's reach is its devices' (an actor's: its direct devices) that no longer read the content's actor. `Follow`: a node syncs an actor only while it holds a role on it as it sees it, and learns a holder actor's epoch under a target's label only once a node reading both and holding the target's newest epoch placed it, or (`Notice`) a node stranded on the target reading it through that holder actor reported the holder's current keysets to the target's writers. `Order`: an epoch whose secret the node lacks is blocked when made before a departure from its actor the node knows (the model's order is the order of making; `NoLeakOrder`: a device removed from a holder actor of a reads no content of a written by a writer knowing the removal, unless a keyset of the holder made after the removal does not know it). `Plain`: a thing that derived from its first admin, its initial epoch its own-secrets rotation (history carrying the derived secret); a node lacking it at first (`Unaware`) writes under the derived secret of the first admin's newest epoch, and the rotations of the thing hand on the derived secrets they hold (`dhist`). A writer that wrote under a derived secret no current epoch's history carries merges the thing (issues/derived-write-merge.md: modelled ahead of the build; its rotations only add behaviours, so every invariant but `NoLeakSettled` (whose premise asks that no rotation be due) holds for the built engine too, and `D0_fa_dmerge`'s `ReadsAll` is the built engine's stated limit until it lands). Instances are defined in `Depth0MC.tla` (a `.cfg` cannot write tuples), so run with module `Depth0MC`:
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir /tmp/tlc-states -config D0_nc.cfg Depth0MC
```
Properties: `NoLeakDirect`, `NoLeakMember`, `NoLeakMemberAll` (invariants), `NoLeakKnown`, `NoLeakKnownAll` (a device removed from the actor, or from an actor with no key path to it, reads no content written knowing the removal once every node holds every grant; `All`: and the writer holds every keyset and re-seal), `Excludes`, `CurrentOpens`, `ReadsAll`, `Settles` (fairness of learning, forced rotation, re-seal); `NoLeakLimit` (a device removed from an actor below A reads no content of A written by a writer holding, for each holder actor of A, a keyset it cannot open that lists every one it can: the stated limit at any depth), `NoLeakSettled` (... written once every node holds what it syncs and needs no rotation). Liveness checking dominates (fairness re-evaluates the recursive operators on every transition): safety-only runs go 10-1000 times faster, so only `D0_fa_dmerge` and `D0_gone_live` check liveness in run.sh.

## Timed grants and finalizes (`Finalize.tla`)
notes/permissions.md (Keys). One group, read role, writers A, B, X (admins), readers D1, D2. Edge writes are entries (spec/crdt.md §5.6): A's `until` writes on D1, D2 and its renewal of D2 (before any finalize) each reset the entry their author held live; a finalize resets the live entry of each ended grant (erase, or keep: an `until` 0 entry beside) and carries a rotation naming the departures its author applied, its own by the departs mark. Any delivery order, commits whole; boxes are global and secrets are the closure over boxes and history boxes. `Rule`: `named` (the rule) or `old` (a name met by its commit alone; the negative control). Invariants: `NoLeak` (content written knowing a departure that stands with every commit held is not readable by the departed reader; every honest writer writes whenever it may), `Settles` (once all is delivered and nobody has a key action left, the honest writers agree on the current epochs and may encrypt, and every holder, a renewed one included, holds each of them).

## Covering a departure (`Cover.tla`)
notes/crypto.md (Frozen coverage). One actor, read role; first admin O and admins P, Q honest; admin L leaves by self-removal (S) and forges: an entry at an epoch it holds listing S, a backdated rotation listing S, a rotation in S itself (`Forge`). With `Audit`, the audit-drop shape: P demotes L to read (K), L self-removes (S), O removes P (D), D's audit drops K; Q may hold K and D without S and rotate for P, boxing L. Only the entry bound to the rotation commit by the keyset's names digest counts, and only while the commit's author holds the role; the commit's own departure alike. `Attr`: `any` (a departure is attributed to the newest held lowering write or audit drop on the holder's edge) or `lowering` (the newest commit at whose point the holder's grant for keys got lower; the model has no dropped raise, which the engine also counts). Commits delivered whole in any order; boxes global (crypto is access). Rotating needs only the right to rotate: `Rotate(n)` needs no current secret (its history box carries the established secrets n holds), an epoch n cannot open covers nothing at n, and `HistAdd(n)` adds the earlier established secrets a current epoch's entries lack (the new entry copies a bound met entry's names, so it is bound alike). Invariant: `NoLeak` (content written knowing a departure is not readable by the departed holder, unless hostile L holds the epoch: what L holds it can hand any party out of band, and its forgeries only automate that; L's forged rotations box the holders its own view shows). Liveness is not modelled: a node holding everything covers all it knows by one rotation, so the honest rotations are bounded by the departures and forgeries each node learns of after the others' rotations (`MaxE` bounds them for the search; a `Settles` invariant at the bound only found the bound).

## Lockouts (`Lockout.tla`)
notes/crypto.md (Rotating needs only the right to rotate; Lockouts). One actor, one key role; admin a, honest writer h, rogue writers `Rogues` that rotate boxing themselves and `WBox` only, j granted by a's first removal. a removes each rogue once, in any order, with a full frontier (every rotation of it received) and the rotation that removal requires, whatever a holds (n above every current epoch, the keyset listing every current epoch, the history box carrying the established secrets a holds); with `Leave` a rogue may instead remove itself (no frontier, no rotation). Commits arrive in any order; a rogue's rotations count at a node unless it holds that rogue's removal by a and its frontier misses them. An honest rotation names the removals its maker held, and is established where its secret is held only with them held. Each honest node rotates up to `MaxH` times plus `MaxDue` rotations a known removal that a current epoch cannot be seen to cover (one it lacks covers nothing) makes due; honest nodes re-seal current holders lacking a current epoch they hold. `HistAdd`: any holder (j included) holding an earlier established secret no entry of a current epoch it holds lists adds one, unless that epoch misses a removal it knows. `HMissA`: h's rotations leave a out. Invariants: `NoLeak` (no rogue holds a secret honest content went under once its writer knew of that rogue's removal), `Unlocked` (settled: every rogue removed, every commit held, no add due and, with `MaxDue`, no rotation owed; a state where one is owed but `MaxDue` is spent is cut short, not checked: a, h and j hold every current epoch), `Alike`, `ReadsAll` (settled: a, h and j read every honest write), `RemoveOK` (each rogue a has yet to remove can be removed). Probes (expected to fail): `ProbeRecovered` (a removal superseding a rogue epoch a never held is reached and settles), `ProbeAdded` (a history add is reached). Mode `none` (a locked a refuses) is the negative control. With `MaxDue = 0` nothing counts as owed, so an instance with honest rotations (`MaxH > 0`) and no due rotation fails `Unlocked` at the bound (an ignorant honest fork nobody may merge): give it `MaxDue > 0`.

## Validity layering (`Authority.tla`)
spec/crypto.md §4 (Possession, Authority): a rotation failing Authority is invalid as a whole, keyset included, so keyset validity feeds Authority and Authority keyset validity, around holder-actor cycles. Actors in a cycle (`Hold`), `NR` rotations each by an author reaching its actor only through one holder actor; the adversary picks each rotation's actor, the keyset of that holder it names (or none), whether its possession signature verifies, and the key order; `Base` actors have a keyset valid by role alone. A node receives the rotations in any order and re-judges one at a time until nothing changes, as the engine does. Authority: the holder counts while one of its keysets is held valid; the proof shows it when signed by the named keyset's possession key, or when that keyset has no valid body (not judged). `Rule = "held"`: keysets as held now; `"order"`: as of just before the rotation's key (a body ordered at or after it counts as not held), as the author's roles are read. Properties: `OneResult` (a settled node holding everything holds the one fixpoint), `Ends` (re-judging ends). Module `AuthorityMC` (tuples).
