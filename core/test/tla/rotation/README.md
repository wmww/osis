# Rotation, claim steps, re-seal (TLA+)
> Summary: TLC model of read-role rotation for one actor and its label at one host (spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync.md §2/§5, spec/permissions.md §3–§4, spec/commits.md §2): epochs established by names (the removals a rotation's rotator held), frozen coverage (an epoch covers exactly the departures it names), a removal withheld forever from one admin, forced and eager rotation, forks, removal carried in its rotation, grants, re-registration after uploading the rotation chain, opens by sync key and claims, claim-set drops, stranded heartbeats (also on a refused claim), re-seal after a complete pass (by inbox and under the label), rotation by inbox to holders without a box at the epoch before, per-node box knowledge, co-reader peers, planted boxes (reading-only secrets). Holder actors are Depth0.tla's (depth-0 sealing, rule 4 for cycles); timed edges and finalizes Finalize.tla's. Written from spec/ alone.

Files: `Rotation.tla` (the model; constants come straight from each `.cfg`), `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `AMBIGUITIES.md` (the findings). `Depth0.tla`, `Finalize.tla`, `Cover.tla`, `Lockout.tla`, `Cut.tla`, `Authority.tla` and `Account.tla` are separate models (sections below).

## Run
`../run.sh rotation` (and `depth0`, `lockout`, `cut`, `finalize`, `cover`, `authority`, `account`) runs a model's listed instances, each model about a minute or less at one worker on a quiet box; `../run.sh <module> <cfg>...` runs others by name. It fetches TLC into `~/.cache/tla/` if missing. By hand, from this directory (Java 11+):
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir ../../../../target/tlc-states -config MC_grant.cfg Rotation
```

Times in the tables are wall clock at one worker on a quiet 16-core box for the run.sh instances; by-hand rows give their workers and load. By-hand rows record their last run: they were not rerun with the models as they stand.

## Properties
Invariants: `NoLeak` (crypto §4 covers rule: content written while the writer knew of M's departure is never readable by M), `DepartedLocked` (permissions §3: a departed holder never holds an epoch whose rotation did not box it), `NoPlantedKey` (crypto §4: a secret learned only from a pending or invalid commit is never encrypted under: no wrap, box, history box, step or heartbeat under it). `NoPlantedKey` holds because `Enc(x)` (what x encrypts under) is `sec[x]`; negative control: with `Enc(x) == sec[x] \cup fsec[x]` (the text before the edit) `MC_plant` fails in 4 states (a grantee heartbeats under a planted secret before its grant box arrives; AMBIGUITIES.md R15). Temporal, under weak fairness of honest steps: `CurrentOpens` (every current holder eventually holds every current epoch), `ReadsAll` (... and every epoch content was written under, older ones by history boxes). "A writer never encrypts under a superseded epoch after it holds the rotation" holds by construction (`EncEp` takes the newest held current epoch) and is not a separate check.

Re-seals fire in `MC_drop`, `MC_grant` and `MC_grant_remove` (checked with an action property) but never in `MC_safety`, `MC_reorder` or `MC_fork_reorder`: with established epochs nothing triggers one there, so those instances check that no re-seal misfires rather than that a firing one is safe. The any-order `MC_withhold` grew past 5 million distinct states (withholding lets a2 finish its passes early, so its re-seals fire in many more interleavings); an any-order `MC_grant_remove` passed 7 million states unfinished.

## Not covered
Write and admin rotation, holder actors (Depth0.tla), forged history entries, invalid rotation commits, demotion and `until` (`until` and finalizes: Finalize.tla), several hosts or branches of the step tree, steps without rotation, entry boxes and key-actor readers, `label.0` records, `ROLE_KEEP` compaction of steps in `label.0` and of rotation commits (`secrets.0` snapshots, so snapshot-established epochs and the post-compaction residue of notes/label-access.md Limits).

## Depth0.tla: depth-0 sealing (notes/keys-requirements-study.md, notes/crypto.md (Holder actors at depth 0))
Several actors and holder actors, read role only, an actor's keyset = its read epoch; crypto as access (a node's secrets: the closure over boxes and history boxes of the commits it holds; a removed device's: over every box ever written); any delivery order; ignorant forks, unprompted rotations and one forged keyset as one-shot steps; A's own departures by frozen coverage (no establishment, hosts or claims: Rotation.tla's). The rules: anchored sealing, listed staleness, a rotation due while a current epoch misses a known departure (a fork alone due nothing; `MaxRace` bounding derived-write merges made while lacking an epoch of the actor; 99: unbounded), no rotation boxing an actor whose anchors fork (the build seals it jointly: Joint.tla; a tainted anchor still leaves the actor out), any removal going without its rotation (a superset of the built cases: a self-removal, Authority failing; uncovered until a rotation covers it), `Blocked` (an epoch whose secret a node holds is blocked there when it misses a departure the node knows other than its own (`Judge`), or, for an admin, is stale, keyless or boxes an actor at a blocked epoch; a rotation leaves out an actor whose Seal keyset is blocked; nobody writes under a blocked epoch; `Walk` picks how far the box clause descends and whether a rotation records what it left out), `CarryFree` (a re-seal boxes a holder at a blocked epoch too: a box carrying a secret is encrypted under its recipient's key), and for cycles rule 4 (no key cycles). Actor grants come in an agreed order (`Grants`); each node gives key access to the grants it holds greedily in that order (`KH`), seals no keyless grant, and rotates its actor while a current epoch boxes an actor without key access (`Keyless`). `Hidden` grants are missing at a node at first (learned fairly; made later, so initial epochs do not box them); `MaxBlind` bounds the boxes sealed to a holder with no true key access (the ignorance window as one-shot adversary steps). Taint: a keyset boxing an actor without key access is stale and never sealed to. Removed-member writer (issues/keys-depth0-removed-member-rotates.md): `Via` writers act on an actor through holder actors while they do not know their removal from one; their rotation is invalid where that removal is known (an epoch's own departures only travel with it; a removal also travels as a placement under a label). `NoSync` limits what a node learns to its labels and its targets' holders' `keys.0`; `Split` lets such a `keys.0` arrive without the keysets it names; Possession: such a writer rotates only sealing a holder actor at a keyset whose secret it holds; each rotation proves the holder actors it boxes at keysets whose secret it holds (`pf`), and a proof naming a stale keyset is a stale box of its epoch (`ProofOnly`: an unprompted rotation boxing no holder actor, proving each). One role, so a proof is always at the role a rotation needs: the build's rule that it must be (spec/crypto.md §4, Authority) is outside the model. The model invalidates such a writer's rotation wherever its removal is known; the build judges its role just before the commit's key, so one ordered before the removal stays valid and only staleness retires it (as in `D0_rm_proof`, where a1 never learns the removal); a rotation removing the rotator's own route and proving it (engine `route_removal_proved_leaks_nothing_spec_crypto_4`) is valid in the build and invalid here. First admin (spec/crypto.md §4 Key access, Current): `FirstAdm` record edges come first in `Grants`; a `Deps` entry may remove an actor edge (`<<holder actor, target, remover>>`), and a node judges key access over the grants it holds less the actor edges it knows removed (a keyless grant turns keyed and is re-sealed); a removed party's reach is its devices' (an actor's: its direct devices) that no longer read the content's actor. `Follow`: a node syncs an actor only while it holds a role on it as it sees it, and learns a holder actor's epoch under a target's label only once a node reading both and holding the target's newest epoch placed it, or (`Notice`) a node stranded on the target reading it through that holder actor reported the holder's current keysets to the target's writers. `Order`: an epoch whose secret the node lacks is blocked when made before a departure from its actor the node knows (the model's order is the order of making; `NoLeakOrder`: a device removed from a holder actor of a reads no content of a written by a writer knowing the removal, unless a keyset of the holder made after the removal does not know it). `Plain`: a thing that derived from its first admin, its initial epoch its own-secrets rotation (history carrying the derived secret); a node lacking it at first (`Unaware`) writes under the derived secret of the first admin's newest epoch, and the rotations of the thing hand on the derived secrets they hold (`dhist`). A writer that wrote under a derived secret no current epoch's history carries merges the thing (spec/crypto.md §4 Current; engine `rotation::merge`). `Ends` (spec/crypto.md §4 Holder actors, Destroyed): an actor may end once; nobody writes it after (no rotation, box, removal, add or content), nobody new learns its epochs or re-seals (its bodies are dropped), and its removals travel as placements (frozen history); with `Expand` a destroyed actor's frozen holders (as the node sees its roster) stand in its place among the holders a node boxes, recursively, through grants with key access only (`KeyedPath`; the build caps a holder at the greatest role over such paths: per role r that is this rule over the grants of r or above, as its taint per role is `Keyless` there, so the one modelled role stands for each), and a box at a current epoch to a holder neither current nor direct (`GoneBox`: the destroyed actor, a frozen holder since known removed) blocks the epoch and makes its rotation due; `NoLeakDestroyed`: a device removed from a destroyed actor standing in a's holders (through ended ones) reads no content of a written knowing the removal; `NoLeakOrder` counts frozen holder actors as a's holder actors. Instances are defined in `Depth0MC.tla` (a `.cfg` cannot write tuples), so run with module `Depth0MC`:
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir ../../../../target/tlc-states -config D0_nc.cfg Depth0MC
```
Properties: `NoLeakDirect`, `NoLeakMember`, `NoLeakMemberAll` (invariants), `NoLeakKnown`, `NoLeakKnownAll` (a device removed from the actor, or from an actor with no key path to it, reads no content written knowing the removal once every node holds every grant; `All`: and the writer holds every keyset and re-seal), `Excludes`, `CurrentOpens`, `ReadsAll`, `Settles` (fairness of learning, forced rotation, re-seal); `NoLeakLimit` (a device removed from an actor below A reads no content of A written by a writer holding, for each holder actor of A, a keyset it cannot open that lists every one it can: the stated limit at any depth), `NoLeakSettled` (... written once every node holds what it syncs and needs no rotation). Liveness checking dominates (fairness re-evaluates the recursive operators on every transition): safety-only runs go 10-1000 times faster, so only `D0_fa_dmerge` and `D0_gone_live` check liveness in run.sh.

## Joint seal (`Joint.tla`)
A holder actor M of A whose anchors fork (spec/crypto.md §4, Seal): M's admins a1, a2 fork M at once, each removing a device the other keeps (x1, x2); A's writers w1, w2 (not in M: they judge M by its keysets alone) forked A, each sealing M at one fork, so A's anchors for M fork. A rotation of A is due. Any admin of M may rotate M (listing every current keyset it holds), grant a device, re-seal a member it knows at a current keyset lacking it, or leave for good; nothing makes an admin rotate M (no merge is owed). Crypto as access: a device opens a keyset boxed to it or listed by one it opens; it reads an epoch of A when it opens every keyset its box to M names, or a later epoch of A listing it. An epoch of A arrives with the keysets of M its box names (Carried). `Rule`: `"joint"` (the built rule: with no keyset of M past every anchor, M sealed jointly at the newest keyset past each), `"wait"` (A's rotation waits for a keyset past every anchor: M's merge), `"newest"` (M's newest keyset, anchors ignored). Properties: `NoLeak` (a device a keyset of M removes reads no epoch of A sealed past that keyset, or made after one), `Rotates` (A's due rotation completes; fairness for A's writers and deliveries only), `ReadsAll` (every member no fork removes, a device granted on one fork included, reads every epoch of A; fairness of re-seals by admins that stay). Outside the model: the build's `Blocked` (Depth0.tla), judged for a joint box as a whole (`JointRule.tla`, `every`): A's writers lack M's secrets, so judge M's keysets by order, and only the earlier-ordered of the two forks misses the other's removal; a joint box names a keyset past the later one too, so is never blocked, and the build seals as `"joint"` does. A single box at the earlier fork alone (a writer anchored there) the build leaves out where the model seals it: the model's behaviours are a superset. Module `JointMC`.

## Covering a departure (`Cover.tla`)
notes/crypto.md (Frozen coverage, The pin). One actor, read role; first admin O and admins P, Q honest; admin L leaves by self-removal (S) and forges: an entry at an epoch it holds listing S, a backdated rotation listing S, a rotation in S itself (`Forge`). With `Audit`, the audit-drop shape: P demotes L to read (K), L self-removes (S), O removes P (D), D's audit drops K; Q may hold K and D without S and rotate for P, boxing L. Only the entry bound to the rotation commit by the keyset's names digest counts, and only while the commit's author holds the role. Covers is the pin (spec/crypto.md §4): over the tail of the holder's edge (its writes after the newest applied one giving read), a write taking it to none that takes there (applies, or would over the edge before it), named applied or made by the rotation commit itself, and every write giving read that counts (its author held write at its point) and applies nothing there, named void. Names: applied lowerings, and void writes that count and give a role. `Dup`: Q removes L concurrently with S, in a commit carrying its rotation (S2 applies nothing where S is held, but would over the edge before it). With `Regrant`, the re-grant shape: O removes M (Rm) and demotes P to `DemoteTo` (Dp); P, not holding Dp, may remove M itself (W) and re-grant it read (Gr), boxing M at its current epochs, both ordered after Dp (invalid where Dp is held, not audited); P acts from what it holds (`PKnows` at the start) whether or not it is a writer. `Else`: Dp takes P's right through a holder actor P writes the actor through, so no rotation of the actor names it, and honest writers re-seal holders they see unboxed at a current epoch they hold; `MetVoid`: a void name is met only where its write applies nothing. `NoLeak` there excuses M reading what a writer not holding Gr wrote (a grant the writer has not received hands on what its author held). Commits delivered whole in any order; boxes global (crypto is access). Rotating needs only the right to rotate: `Rotate(n)` needs no current secret (its history box carries the established secrets n holds; its keyset lists, and its n passes, every current and unsuperseded epoch: `Unsup`, current were the names condition dropped; `Withdraw`: n rotates while an unsuperseded epoch is not current at it and n made it or its maker may not rotate), an epoch n cannot open covers nothing at n, and `HistAdd(n)` adds the earlier established secrets a current epoch's entries lack (the new entry copies a bound met entry's names, so it is bound alike). Invariant: `NoLeak` (content written knowing a departure is not readable by the departed holder, unless hostile L holds the epoch: what L holds it can hand any party out of band, and its forgeries only automate that; L's forged rotations box the holders its own view shows). Liveness is not modelled: a node holding everything covers all it knows by one rotation, so the honest rotations are bounded by the departures and forgeries each node learns of after the others' rotations (`MaxE` bounds them for the search). One write per holder at a time: the pin over many writes of one edge is `Tail.tla`'s. Its `Met` takes an applied name as met by holding its commit, weaker than the rule (met only where the write stands, `Tail.tla`): a node establishing less establishes nothing this model does not, so its `NoLeak` results stand for the rule.

One actor, one key role; admin a, honest writer h, rogue writers `Rogues` that rotate boxing themselves and `WBox` only, j granted by a's first removal. a removes each rogue once, in any order, with a full frontier (every rotation of it received) and the rotation that removal requires, whatever a holds (n above every current epoch, the keyset listing every current epoch, the history box carrying the established secrets a holds); with `Leave` a rogue may instead remove itself (no frontier, no rotation). ## History cuts (`Cut.tla`)
issues/history-less-grant.md (Design). One actor, one key role. Admins a and b rotate, grant, remove m and re-seal, a also rotating while it holds a fork; readers add history; CutTo are granted without history (a cut: a rotation whose history entry carries nothing; m's re-add after its removal), HistTo with it (one also in CutTo: showing history later). Each keyset carries a floor (a cut's own n, else the greatest floor it lists), at most its n, read as at least the floor of each epoch it lists that the node holds. A party's floor is its newest grant's: a cut's n; a grant with history, its author's. The rule (`Rule = "cut"`): an entry at e carries f only when f's floor is at least e's and at least e's maker's floor (rotations, grants' and re-seals' superset entries, History adds); a holder is boxed only at epochs of floor at least its own, and counts no epoch below it as current; a grant with history also boxes the grantee at each dropped epoch it holds (listed by a held rotation, not carried), and any holder of a dropped epoch boxes it to a holder allowed it that cannot open it from the live boxes it holds (tip add); a rotation removes the boxes it holds at the epochs it carries, a node counting a removal once the rotation is established; m's re-add also boxes m at each epoch its removal's rotation listed that the re-adder opens; a rotation places again, under its own epoch and projected to `roles.0`, the role commits it holds wrapped only under epochs below its floor (`Replace`). A commit counts at a node once it holds its author's grant. The attacker: `Hostile` parties rotate listing any epochs they know of with any floor up to n (`HiF` above it under `"nocap"`), at most `MaxHost` times, the rest of the rotation following the rules from that keyset; a hostile party grants `Accomp` with history. Commits arrive in any order a node can read them; box removal is observed-remove. Know(x): every secret x ever opens from any box or entry ever written; Fresh(x): what a new node of x opens from the live boxes. Invariants: `NoPast` (a holder granted without history obtains no epoch in its cut's causal past: an honest one none but what a hostile party obtains, a hostile one and its accomplices none), `NoAbsence` (m obtains no epoch made knowing its removal, the removal's own included, before any re-add: in the re-add's causal past; a new node of m opens a subset), and, settled (every readable commit held by every honest party, nothing due at one, one current epoch at a): `PriorKeep` (every honest holder with history opens every epoch from the live state), `NewReads` (an honest holder without history opens its cut and all that descends from it), `Unlocked`, `Alike`, `ReaddFresh` (a re-added m's new node opens every epoch its removal's rotator held). Probes: `StrictNoPast` (nothing but descendants of the cut; fails, accepted: content beside the grant under a racing cut), `NoFullRun` (no settled state after every step the bounds allow: vacuity). Sizes: every leak path the controls find lies two to five steps past the cut (the cut, a hostile or honest rotation, a re-seal, an add or a tip add, a merge), well inside the bounds; `MaxC` 5-6 leaves room for the cut, a concurrent rotation, a merge or due rotation and one or two adds, re-seals or tip adds after it. A hostile rotation ranges over every subset of the epochs its maker knows of times every floor, so instances with one beside an honest rotation and a reader exceed the heavy hold (`X_host_beside` with p at `MaxC = 5`: over 460,000 states unfinished in 226 s): each hostile instance drops one of them. `X_cut`, `X_two` and `X_later` at `MaxC` 7, 6, 6 ran 12-27 min (1.7-3.2 M states), past the heavy hold. A settled state with every shape's full set of steps is reachable in each instance, so the settled properties are not vacuous (probe `NoFullRun` fails on each hostile instance; `X_readd` under `"noreadd"` fails `ReaddFresh`, so its settled re-add states are reached). Without projection, `X_names` failed `Unlocked` (n held the removal commit whole, its rotation's keyset showing a pre-cut epoch current); with re-placing only at the cut, `X_rmrace` failed `Alike` (a due rotation named a removal made beside the cut, wrapped only under an epoch n may not open); without the own-floor clause of current, `X_readd` failed `Unlocked` (m held its removal commit, readable under an epoch it kept, and lacked the chain superseding it); counting a box removal once its rotation is established is the conservative reading (more tip adds, never fewer): `X_rmrace`, `X_gone` and `X_names` also pass counting every held removal. Not modelled: holder actors (Seal, left out, stale), withdraw, void names, compaction snapshots, a hostile n, labels and steps (issues/history-less-grant.md, Gaps).

## Validity layering (`Authority.tla`)
spec/crypto.md §4 (Possession, Authority): a rotation failing Authority is invalid as a whole, keyset included, so keyset validity feeds Authority and Authority keyset validity, around holder-actor cycles. Actors in a cycle (`Hold`), `NR` rotations each by an author reaching its actor only through one holder actor; the adversary picks each rotation's actor, the keyset of that holder it names (or none), whether its possession signature verifies, and the key order; `Base` actors have a keyset valid by role alone. A node receives the rotations in any order and re-judges one at a time until nothing changes, as the engine does. Authority: the holder counts while one of its keysets is held valid; the proof shows it when signed by the named keyset's possession key, or when that keyset has no valid body (not judged). `Rule = "held"`: keysets as held now; `"order"`: as of just before the rotation's key (a body ordered at or after it counts as not held), as the author's roles are read. Properties: `OneResult` (a settled node holding everything holds the one fixpoint), `Ends` (re-judging ends). Module `AuthorityMC` (tuples).
