# Rotation, claim steps, re-seal (TLA+)
> Summary: TLC model of read-role rotation for one actor and its sync ID at one store (spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync-ids.md §2/§5, spec/permissions.md §3–§4, spec/commits.md §2): epochs established by names (the removals a rotation's rotator held), frozen coverage (an epoch covers exactly the departures it names), a removal withheld forever from one admin, forced and eager rotation, forks, removal carried in its rotation, grants, re-registration after logging the rotation chain, opens by sync key and claims, claim-set drops, stranded stranded notices (also on a refused claim), re-seal after a complete pass (the commit's blob key sealed to the holder, Entry.tla, under the sync ID), rotation sealed so to holders without a box at the epoch before, per-node box knowledge, co-reader peers, planted boxes (reading-only secrets). Holder actors are Depth0.tla's (depth-0 sealing, rule 4 for cycles); timed edges and finalizes Finalize.tla's. Written from spec/ alone.

Files: `Rotation.tla` (the model; constants come straight from each `.cfg`), `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `AMBIGUITIES.md` (the findings). `Depth0.tla`, `Finalize.tla`, `Cover.tla`, `Lockout.tla`, `Cut.tla`, `Withdrawn.tla`, `LeftOut.tla`, `ForkRot.tla`, `ForkDepth.tla`, `Planted.tla`, `FireKey.tla`, `KemKeys.tla` and `Entry.tla` are separate models (sections below).

## Run
`../run.sh rotation` (and `depth0`, `lockout`, `cut`, `finalize`, `cover`, `withdrawn`, `leftout`, `forkrot`, `forkdepth`, `planted`, `firekey`, `kemkeys`, `entry`) runs a model's listed instances, each model about a minute or less at one worker on a quiet box; `../run.sh <module> <cfg>...` runs others by name. It fetches TLC into `~/.cache/tla/` if missing. By hand, from this directory (Java 11+):
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir ../../../../target/tlc-states -config MC_grant.cfg Rotation
```

Times in the tables are wall clock at one worker on a quiet 16-core box for the run.sh instances; by-hand rows give their workers and load. By-hand rows record their last run: they were not rerun with the models as they stand.

## Properties
Invariants: `NoLeak` (crypto §4 covers rule: content written while the writer knew of M's departure is never readable by M), `DepartedLocked` (permissions §3: a departed holder never holds an epoch whose rotation did not box it), `NoPlantedKey` (crypto §4: a secret learned only from a pending or invalid commit is never encrypted under: no wrap, box, history box, step or notice under it). `NoPlantedKey` holds because `Enc(x)` (what x encrypts under) is `sec[x]`; negative control: with `Enc(x) == sec[x] \cup fsec[x]` (the text before the edit) `MC_plant` fails in 4 states (a grantee stranded notices under a planted secret before its grant box arrives; AMBIGUITIES.md R15). Temporal, under weak fairness of honest steps: `CurrentOpens` (every current holder eventually holds every current epoch), `ReadsAll` (... and every epoch content was written under, older ones by history boxes). "A writer never encrypts under a superseded epoch after it holds the rotation" holds by construction (`EncEp` takes the newest held current epoch) and is not a separate check.

The any-order `MC_withhold` grew past 5 million distinct states (withholding lets a2 finish its passes early, so its re-seals fire in many more interleavings); an any-order `MC_grant_remove` passed 7 million states unfinished.

## Not covered
Write and admin rotation, holder actors (Depth0.tla), forged history entries, invalid rotation commits, demotion and `until` (`until` and finalizes: Finalize.tla), several stores or branches of the step tree, steps without rotation, entry boxes and link-key readers, `sync.0` records, `ROLE_KEEP` compaction of steps in `sync.0` and of rotation commits (`secrets.0` snapshots, so snapshot-established epochs and the post-compaction residue of notes/sync-access.md, What removal, compaction and re-sealing leave).

## Depth0.tla: depth-0 sealing (notes/holder-actors.md)
Several actors and holder actors, read role only, an actor's keyset = its read epoch; crypto as access (a node's secrets: the closure over boxes and history boxes of the commits it holds; a removed node's: over every box ever written); any delivery order; ignorant forks, unprompted rotations and one forged keyset as one-shot steps; A's own departures by frozen coverage (no establishment, stores or claims: Rotation.tla's). The rules: anchored sealing, listed staleness, a rotation due while a current epoch misses a known departure (a fork alone due nothing; `MaxRace` bounding derived-write merges made while lacking an epoch of the actor; 99: unbounded), no rotation boxing an actor whose anchors fork (the build seals it jointly: Joint.tla; a tainted anchor still leaves the actor out), any removal going without its rotation (a superset of the built cases: a self-removal, a remover that may not rotate; uncovered until a rotation covers it), `Blocked` (an epoch whose secret a node holds is blocked there when it misses a departure the node knows other than its own (`Judge`), or, for an admin, is stale, keyless or boxes an actor at a blocked epoch; a rotation leaves out an actor whose Seal keyset is blocked; nobody writes under a blocked epoch; `Walk` picks how far the box clause descends and whether a rotation records what it left out), `CarryFree` (a re-seal boxes a holder at a blocked epoch too: a box carrying a secret is encrypted under its recipient's key), and for cycles rule 4 (no key cycles). Actor grants come in an agreed order (`Grants`); each node gives key access to the grants it holds greedily in that order (`KH`), seals no keyless grant, and rotates its actor while a current epoch boxes an actor without key access (`Keyless`). `Hidden` grants are missing at a node at first (learned fairly; made later, so initial epochs do not box them); `MaxBlind` bounds the boxes sealed to a holder with no true key access (the ignorance window as one-shot adversary steps). Taint: a keyset boxing an actor without key access is stale and never sealed to. Removed-member writer (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions): `Via` writers act on an actor through holder actors while they do not know their removal from one; their rotation is invalid where that removal is known (an epoch's own departures only travel with it; a removal from a holder actor also travels in that actor's stub under the sync ID of each thing it holds a role on, logged there by any node syncing it that holds the removal: notes/stub-rewrite.md §2 rule 2's chain stubs). `NoSync` limits what a node learns to its sync IDs and its targets' holders' `keys.0`; `Split` lets such a `keys.0` arrive without the keysets it names. `Travel` (built): a keyset carries the removals of writers through holder actors and of admins, from its actor or below, that its rotator held, and a node holds it only with them. `Rogue`: the removed writer rotating the holder actor itself past its knowing keyset, then the actor; `Bogus`: a rotation sealing a holder actor at a made-up keyset or backdated. Stale reads boxes alone (spec/crypto.md §4 Stale; grants and re-seals record nothing, a rotation's records serve Foreign, which this model leaves out): `BoxBlind` nodes hold an actor's keysets but none of its boxes and judge its epochs by order; at an epoch whose secret it holds the model judges staleness for an admin only, the build for every node. Negative controls: `Travel = FALSE`; `Possession` (the rejected rule: such a writer rotates only sealing a holder actor at a keyset whose secret it holds); `AsOf` (the rejected candidate: a rotation's proof (`pf`, the keysets it seals holder actors at) naming a keyset not held before the rotation is a stale box). `NoLeakKnowing` is the requirement as written: a writer holding, for each holder actor of a above a removal, a keyset whose rotator knew it, writes nothing the removed node opens (`NoLeakLimit` counts a keyset descending from the knowing one, so it misses `Rogue`). The model invalidates such a writer's rotation wherever its removal is known; the build judges its role just before the commit's key, which with the removal held is the same for every commit the removal's frontier does not list. A rotation removing the rotator's own route is valid in the build (a write never demotes its own author; engine `rotation_removing_its_own_route_leaks_nothing_spec_crypto_4`) and invalid here. Mint admin (spec/crypto.md §4 Key access, Current): `MintAdm` record edges come first in `Grants`; a `Deps` entry may remove an actor edge (`<<holder actor, target, remover>>`), and a node judges key access over the grants it holds less the actor edges it knows removed (a keyless grant turns keyed and is re-sealed); a removed party's reach is its nodes' (an actor's: its direct nodes) that no longer read the content's actor. `Follow`: a node syncs an actor only while it holds a role on it as it sees it, and learns a holder actor's epoch under a target's sync ID only once a node reading both and holding the target's newest epoch logged it, or (`Notice`) a node stranded on the target reading it through that holder actor reported the holder's current keysets to the target's writers. `Order`: an epoch whose secret the node lacks is blocked when made before a departure from its actor the node knows (the model's order is the order of making; `NoLeakOrder`: a node removed from a holder actor of a reads no content of a written by a writer knowing the removal, unless a keyset of the holder made after the removal does not know it). `Dependent`: a thing that derived from its mint admin, its initial epoch its own-secrets rotation (history carrying the derived secret); a node lacking it at first (`Unaware`) writes under the derived secret of the mint admin's newest epoch, and the rotations of the thing hand on the derived secrets they hold (`dhist`). A writer that wrote under a derived secret no current epoch's history carries merges the thing (spec/crypto.md §4 Current; engine `rotation::merge`). `Ends` (spec/crypto.md §4 Holder actors, Destroyed): an actor may end once; nobody writes it after (no rotation, box, removal, add or content), nobody new learns its epochs or re-seals (its bodies are dropped), and its removals travel as logged copies (frozen history); with `NoKeys` a destroyed actor holds no keys and nobody stands in its place: no node boxes it, and a box to it at a current epoch (`GoneBox`) blocks the epoch and makes its rotation due, as a removal does; `NoLeakDestroyed`: a node below a destroyed holder actor of a (any depth), reading a no other way, reads no content of a written after the destruction. Instances are defined in `Depth0MC.tla` (a `.cfg` cannot write tuples), so run with module `Depth0MC`:
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir ../../../../target/tlc-states -config D0_nc.cfg Depth0MC
```
Properties: `NoLeakDirect`, `NoLeakMember`, `NoLeakMemberAll` (invariants), `NoLeakKnown`, `NoLeakKnownAll` (a node removed from the actor, or from an actor with no key path to it, reads no content written knowing the removal once every node holds every grant; `All`: and the writer holds every keyset and re-seal), `Excludes`, `CurrentOpens`, `ReadsAll`, `Settles` (fairness of learning, forced rotation, re-seal); `NoLeakLimit` (a node removed from an actor below A reads no content of A written by a writer holding, for each holder actor of A, a keyset it cannot open that lists every one it can: the stated limit at any depth), `NoLeakSettled` (... written once every node holds what it syncs and needs no rotation). Liveness checking dominates (fairness re-evaluates the recursive operators on every transition): safety-only runs go 10-1000 times faster, so only `D0_fa_dmerge` and `D0_gone_live` check liveness in run.sh.

## Joint seal (`Joint.tla`)

With `Regrant`, the re-grant shape: O removes M (Rm) and demotes P to `DemoteTo` (Dp); P, not holding Dp, may remove M itself (W) and re-grant it read (Gr), boxing M at its current epochs, both ordered after Dp (invalid where Dp is held, not audited); P acts from what it holds (`PKnows` at the start) whether or not it is a writer. `Else`: Dp takes P's right through a holder actor P writes the actor through, so no rotation of the actor names it, and honest writers re-seal holders they see unboxed at a current epoch they hold; `MetVoid`: a void name is met only where its write applies nothing. `NoLeak` there excuses M reading what a writer not holding Gr wrote (a grant the writer has not received hands on what its author held). Commits delivered whole in any order; boxes global (crypto is access). Rotating needs only the right to rotate: `Rotate(n)` needs no current secret (its history box carries the established secrets n holds; its keyset lists, and its n passes, every current and unsuperseded epoch: `Unsup`, current were the names condition dropped; `Withdraw`: n rotates while an unsuperseded epoch is not current at it and n made it or its maker may not rotate), an epoch n cannot open covers nothing at n, and `HistAdd(n)` adds the earlier established secrets a current epoch's entries lack (the new entry copies a bound met entry's names, so it is bound alike). Invariant: `NoLeak` (content written knowing a departure is not readable by the departed holder, unless hostile L holds the epoch: what L holds it can hand any party out of band, and its forgeries only automate that; L's forged rotations box the holders its own view shows). Liveness is not modelled: a node holding everything covers all it knows by one rotation, so the honest rotations are bounded by the departures and forgeries each node learns of after the others' rotations (`MaxE` bounds them for the search). One write per holder at a time: the pin over many writes of one edge is `Tail.tla`'s. Its `Met` takes an applied name as met by holding its commit, weaker than the rule (met only where the write stands, `Tail.tla`): a node establishing less establishes nothing this model does not, so its `NoLeak` results stand for the rule.

## Lockouts (`Lockout.tla`)
notes/keys.md (Rotating needs only the right to rotate; Lockouts). One actor, one key role; admin a, honest writer h, rogue writers `Rogues` that rotate boxing themselves and `WBox` only, j granted by a's first removal. a removes each rogue once, in any order, with a full frontier (every rotation of it received) and the rotation that removal requires, whatever a holds (n above every current epoch, the keyset listing every current epoch, the history box carrying the established secrets a holds); with `Leave` a rogue may instead remove itself (no frontier, no rotation). Commits arrive in any order; a rogue's rotations count at a node unless it holds that rogue's removal by a and its frontier misses them. An honest rotation names the removals its maker held, and is established where its secret is held only with them held. Each honest node rotates up to `MaxH` times plus `MaxDue` rotations a known removal that a current epoch cannot be seen to cover (one it lacks judged by order: made without the removal) makes due; honest nodes re-seal current holders lacking a current epoch they hold. `HistAdd`: any holder (j included) holding an earlier established secret no entry of a current epoch it holds lists adds one, unless that epoch misses a removal it knows. `HMissA`: h's rotations leave a out. Invariants: `NoLeak` (no rogue holds a secret honest content went under once its writer knew of that rogue's removal), `Unlocked` (settled: every rogue removed, every commit held, no add due and, with `MaxDue`, no rotation owed; a state where one is owed but `MaxDue` is spent is cut short, not checked: a, h and j hold every current epoch), `Alike`, `ReadsAll` (settled: a, h and j read every honest write), `RemoveOK` (each rogue a has yet to remove can be removed). Probes (expected to fail): `ProbeRecovered` (a removal superseding a rogue epoch a never held is reached and settles), `ProbeAdded` (a history add is reached). Mode `none` (a locked a refuses) is the negative control. With `MaxDue = 0` nothing counts as owed, so an instance with honest rotations (`MaxH > 0`) and no due rotation fails `Unlocked` at the bound (an ignorant honest fork nobody may merge): give it `MaxDue > 0`.

## History cuts (`Cut.tla`)
notes/history-less-grant.md (Principle and why each rule). One actor, one key role. Admins a and b rotate, grant, remove m and re-seal; readers add history; CutTo are granted without history (a cut: a rotation whose history entry carries nothing; m's re-add after its removal), HistTo with it (one also in CutTo: showing history later). Each history entry writes a floor; a node reads an epoch's floor as the greatest its opened entries at the epoch write, at most n (none opened: unknown, 0 for boxing and Current, no carry into it). A cut's floor is its n; any other honest rotation's the greatest floor among its maker and its holders. A party's floor is its newest grant's: a cut's n; a grant with history, its author's. The rule (`Rule = "cut"`): an entry at e carries f only when f's floor is at least e's and at least e's maker's floor (unknown without e's rotation commit: the greatest of the holders'; rotations, grants' and re-seals' superset entries, History adds); a holder is boxed only at epochs of floor at least its own, and counts no epoch below it as current; a grant with history also boxes the grantee at each dropped epoch it holds (listed by a held rotation, not carried), and any holder of a dropped epoch boxes it to a holder allowed it that cannot open it from the live boxes it holds (tip add); a rotation removes the boxes it holds at the epochs it carries, a node counting a removal once the rotation is established; m's re-add also boxes m at each epoch its removal's rotation listed that the re-adder opens; every entry carries the roles secret of each earlier epoch its writer knows one of, the carry rule aside (`Roles`), and a holder of an epoch's roles secret holds the role commits wrapped there (every commit here is one); an admin holding a current epoch whose floor is below a holder's rotates (the low floor, `Merge`), once it establishes every epoch it holds unsuperseded. A commit counts at a node once it holds its author's grant. The attacker: `Hostile` parties rotate listing any epochs they know of with any floor up to n (`HiF` above it under `"nocap"`), at most `MaxHostile` times, the rest of the rotation following the rules from that keyset, and put entries with any floor at epochs whose secret they hold (`MaxHostileF`); a hostile party grants `Accomp` with history. Commits arrive in any order a node can read them; box removal is observed-remove. Know(x): every secret x ever opens from any box or entry ever written; Fresh(x): what a new node of x opens from the live boxes. Invariants: `NoPast` (a holder granted without history obtains no epoch in its cut's causal past: an honest one none but what a hostile party obtains, a hostile one and its accomplices none), `HonestPast` (no honest box or entry hands it one, whatever floors hostile parties write), `NoAbsence` (m obtains no epoch made knowing its removal, the removal's own included, before any re-add: in the re-add's causal past; a new node of m opens a subset), and, settled (every readable commit held by every honest party, nothing due at one, no low floor at an admin): `PriorKeep` (every honest holder with history opens every epoch from the live state), `NewReads` (an honest holder without history opens its cut and all that descends from it, but an epoch whose own entry writes a floor below its cut's: a hostile writer's, or one made by a node not holding the grant that lists what descends from the cut; content written there never reaches it), `Unlocked`, `Alike` (but epochs hostile parties made: one whose commit a holder cannot open stays unestablished there), `Roster` (every honest holder holds every role commit by an honest author valid at it), `ReaddFresh` (a re-added m's new node opens every epoch its removal's rotator held). Probes: `StrictNoPast` (nothing but descendants of the cut; fails, accepted: content beside the grant under a racing cut), `NewReadsAll` (`NewReads` without its exception; fails, accepted), `NoFullRun` (no settled state after every step the bounds allow: vacuity). Controls: `Rule` `"old"` (no floor), `"oldadd"` (the History add as before), `"nocarry"`, `"nofloor"`, `"nomaker"` (the carry rule on e's floor alone), `"nogfloor"` (a grant with history gives floor 0), `"anyfloor"` (a floor put by a party lacking the epoch's secret counts: a keyset floor), `"nocap"` (floors above n count), `"noreadd"` (m's re-add boxes it only at the cut); `Roles = FALSE`. `MaxC` bounds commits (a state at it with something due is cut short, not settled). One thing, read role only. Admin A (honest, the only stepper), honest writer W1, malicious writer W2, reader R (removed at most once, by A or W1, with the rotation the removal carries), outsider O holding only `keys.0`. Crypto as access: an epoch (named by the id of the rotation that made it) is held by those its rotation boxes; a keyset's KEM key is genuine or junk. `keys.0` at a node is the LWW merge of the commits it holds: the `read` field (every rotation writes it; W2 may also write a copy of an epoch already made, with a junk KEM key) and the step keys (A's steps, carrying the read key). Stamps `(ts, actor)` order the LWW fields; an honest commit stamps above every stamp it holds, W2 picks any ts in `1..MaxTs`. Commits reach A and O in any order; with `Parts`, A may first hold a rotation's `keys.0` delta alone (its boxes and the removal it carries come with the whole commit). W2 holds everything; W1 rotates holding any set of commits including its own. With `ReadOnly`, honest rotations rotate read alone, so only the read key makes a step due; otherwise A also steps when it holds a read epoch newer than its head registers (a read rotation rotating sync). A steps when due, never while read is blocked at it (it knows R's removal and a current epoch does not cover it: it rotates first), and up to `OptSteps` times unprompted. W2 boxes any of {nobody, itself (the lockout), A and itself, every writer} or writes junk, never R (handing R a secret is a leak by hand). The sender reseals whenever the key in force changes and a peer may take a fire in any state, so fires are not state: the invariants judge the key O would seal to in every state.

`Rule`: `vouch` (the spec: A's step carries a read key for the epoch of the `read` field A holds, when A holds that epoch current, else none; in force while the `read` field is of its epoch; a step is due when none is in force and A vouches for the field's epoch); `seen` (the step names the field's epoch A held and carries A's newest held epoch's key, in force while the field is of the named epoch); `stamps` (A's newest held epoch's key, in force while the `read` field's stamp is no later than the step keys'); `newest` (seal to the `read` field's keyset); `nopend` (seal to the step's key whatever the `read` field holds). Under `vouch`, a field A holds whole and cannot vouch for (junk, a lockout, a copy) waits for an admin's read rotation; `ARotUnv` stands for it (an admin removing the writer, notes/keys.md Lockouts) and `FK_live` assumes it fair: no rule makes it due (issues/unvouched-read-keyset-stalls-fires.md).

Properties: `NoSwallow` (crypto §3 "admin-written and derived by a holder of its secret, so a writer's keyset never chooses it": a fire taken is sealed to a genuine key of an epoch A, W1 or a remaining R holds); `Masked` (O's `read` field, or the one A held when it made O's step keys, is a W2 write stamped after a knowing rotation O holds: W2 holds the new epoch and could hand R the fires anyway, not defended); `NoLeak` (requirements, Permissions, "stops reading new content once a party with the right to change the keys has done so knowing of the removal": unmasked, a fire taken while O holds a knowing honest rotation K is sealed to no epoch R holds, unless the key's commit and K are concurrent, as crypto §4 Encrypting under accepts for a keyset made without knowing a departure); `NoLeakStrict` (no concurrency exception); `NoLeakAll` (masking included); `Delivered` (temporal, weak fairness of A's due steps and rotations and of receipt: O comes to hold for good a key in force an honest node opens, so every pending fire is taken); `Healed` (temporal: O comes to seal for good to no epoch R holds once it holds a knowing rotation, masking aside: the concurrency exception is a window, not a state).

Findings:
- **F1 (closed: no stamps).** Under `stamps` (`FK_stamps`): W1 removes R with rotation e3 (ts 1), holding only the minting commit; W2 writes a self-boxed keyset (ts 1, later by actor); A holds only W2's commit, sees no read key in force and steps (ts 2) registering e1, which R holds; O holds e3 and A's step but not W2's keyset: the `read` field (ts 1) is no later than the step keys (ts 2), so e1's key is in force although O holds the knowing rotation.
- **F2 (an honest fork masks the knowing keyset; `FK_fork`; accepted by Tess as content's property, notes/sync-inbox.md).** W1 removes R with e3 (ts 1). A, not holding it, rotates (e5, ts 2, not knowing the removal, so boxing R) and steps vouching for e5. O holds e3, e5 and that step: the `read` field holds e5 (stamped after e3), so e5's key is in force and R opens what O seals, although O holds the knowing rotation. It lasts until A holds e3 (e5 then does not cover a departure A knows: A rotates and steps; `Healed` passes). The same window content has (crypto §4 Encrypting under), but here the sealer already holds the knowing keyset and the one-keyset `read` field hides it.
- **F3 (closed by `vouch`; `FK_seen`, `FK_partial`).** Under `seen` the step names the field's epoch whatever A holds of it and carries A's newest held epoch: A holding W1's knowing e3 as its `keys.0` delta alone (honest: its boxes still to come), or as W2's copy, steps naming e3 with e1's key; O holding e3 seals to e1, which R holds, and once A holds e3 whole nothing is due (the step names the field's epoch), so it lasts for good with read-only rotations. Under `vouch` A writes a key only for the field's epoch held current, so O waits until A holds e3.
- **Stated limit (W2 masking; `FK_mask`).** W2 writes a keyset stamped after e3: a self-boxed or junk one (O pends; A cannot vouch), or a copy of e1, which puts the step naming e1 back in force at O until A's next step reaches it. W2 holds e3 and could hand R every fire anyway; a writer holding no secret (a keyless grant) can copy too (issues/unvouched-read-keyset-stalls-fires.md).
- A2 (spec/sync-inbox.md §2): "If T's read key in force changes before a peer takes it, the sender seals it again": whether a change to none takes back a fire already sealed and offered (so it waits pending) is not said. The model takes it back (seal and take are one step); a fire sealed before O holds a knowing rotation and taken after would otherwise open to R.

## KemKeys.tla: KEM keys only where sealed to (spec/crypto.md §3 Published KEM keys, KEM key due; §4 Seal; spec/permissions.md §2 Granting to a thing)
Holder actor M of A, read role, M's keysets a line. m (an admin of M) rotates M (`MaxM` unprompted, a removal of x, a due re-key), learning M's grant on A only from a signal or a stub of A it holds (`Told`: fairly, else perhaps never: a grant need not reach its holder); `other` (a card, a role elsewhere, a signal) gives a KEM key until it ends. g (a writer of A that may not rotate M) grants M and seals it at each rotation of A by the newest keyset of M it holds (a card, M's readers' reports): there when it carries a KEM key, else M is left out (an older one is stale). x is a party removed from M, or the public granted read on M and removed (its open boxes make x every party). `Rule`: `written` (KEM key decided when written only), `due` (also KEM key due), `sticky` (`due`, and a keyset carries a KEM key wherever the one it supersedes does), `fallback` (control: Seal below a KEM-less newest keyset). `Check`: the granter's check. `StaleCard`: g starts one keyset behind, x removed. Invariants `NoLeak` (content g seals holding a keyset made knowing x's removal is not sealed to one x holds), `NoLeakAll` (none x opens at all); property `Live` (once granted, M boxed at its newest keyset for good).

The KEM rule judged by what the rotator holds needs the rotator to know of M's grants, which the stub design no longer guarantees (a grant need not reach its holder). The simplest rule that passes is `sticky`: a keyset carries a KEM key where, when written, the thing is minted with a key, a card stands, a signal is enabled, it holds a role as the writer knows it, or the keyset it supersedes carries one. It costs a KEM key per keyset of a thing once sealed to, for good.

Not modelled: forks of M's keysets (a foreign keyset makes nothing due; each side re-keys its own), several sealers (a no-candidate leave-out is no block: a sealer holding the KEM-less keyset's secret may box M there, which no record need stop).

## Entry.tla: how a holder gets secrets it cannot open by a wrap (notes/stub-rewrite.md Q1, Q2; spec/sync-ids.md §3, §6; spec/sync-opens.md §4)
A holder that cannot open X's newest rotation by its wrap (a signalled grantee, a reader left out of a rotation or past a dropped claim set, a sync-only holder at each new sync secret) gets X's current secrets, nothing kept with the holder. One thing X, one admin (rotates, removes, issues and registers steps), a granter that is not the admin, holders: readers and sync-only holders. Read and sync rotate together. A step carries its claim set: readers listed by claim key, and (entry boxes) claim-keyed boxes (found only by a holder holding that step with its claim set) and static entries (a credential held as a value, found at the head knowing no step: Behind naming none). Readers walk as spec/sync-ids.md §6 says (open by a credential of the head, Behind one sync key past the newest step they hold a credential of); a superseded step's claim set may be dropped. Sync-only holders keep S unread: a reader registers the steps past the store's head (to one it judges departed, only the first past its keys); the store stops at a head whose sync key it lacks. `Hidden` holders are granted beside the admin's knowledge (left out of rotations until it learns them); `Late` ones are granted later, `Sig` ones signalled (`Expire` unfair).

`Deliver`:
- `notice` (the design that passes): steps carry no per-holder box. A role commit boxing a holder that cannot open its wrap (a grant; a re-seal; a rotation, to each sync-only holder it boxes and each holder holding no box at the epoch it is wrapped under) also seals its blob key to that holder, as a notice does; it is an entry under X's sync ID like any other. A reader opening S toward the holder by its role (ByRole, J judged on X's roster) serves it; the holder opens those commits by its KEM key and the rotations after them by their wraps. A signal carries no credential.
- `entry` (Q1(a)/Q2(a)): boxes in the steps. `Reseal`: `request` (a stranded reader makes a static entry, sends its public half in its Stranded message, and the admin lists it with a box in every step until its lifetime ends, `Lapse`; the reader asks again while stranded) or `next` (Q2(a) as written: a claim-keyed box in the next step). `SyncBox`: `every` (every step boxes every current sync-only holder) or `next` (only a step bearing a new sync key). `SigEntry`: `stand` (a signal's static entry in every step while it stands) or `once`.
- `copies`: the deleted design (boxes kept under the holder): a control.

Properties: `Live` (a current holder that entered holds the current epoch for good, a sync-only one's store at the head), `SigLive` (a signal standing for good brings its holder in), `NoGain` (a removed holder holds no epoch made at or after its removal), `NothingKept` (nothing kept under the holder), `StepsBare` (a step carries no per-holder box, so a store keeping X unread sees only padded claim sets: spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read).
