# Rotation, claim steps, re-seal (TLA+)
> Summary: TLC model of read-role rotation for one actor and its label at one host (spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync.md §2/§5, spec/permissions.md §3–§4, spec/commits.md §2): epochs established by names (the removals a rotation's rotator held), frozen coverage (an epoch covers exactly the departures it names) against the box-inspection covers rule, a removal withheld forever from one admin, forced and eager rotation, forks, removal carried in its rotation, grants, re-registration after uploading the rotation chain, opens by sync key and claims, claim-set drops, stranded heartbeats (also on a refused claim), deposit-seed refresh, re-seal after a complete pass (by inbox and under the label), rotation by inbox to holders without a box at the epoch before, per-node box knowledge, co-reader peers, planted boxes (reading-only secrets). Holder actors are Depth0.tla's (depth-0 sealing, rule 4 for cycles); timed edges and finalizes Finalize.tla's. Written from spec/ alone.

Files: `Rotation.tla` (the model; constants come straight from each `.cfg`), `ASSUMPTIONS.md`, `AMBIGUITIES.md` (the findings). `Depth0.tla` is a separate model of depth-0 sealing (section at the end).

## Run
`../run.sh small` (CI-sized instances of both models) or `../run.sh all`; it fetches TLC into `~/.cache/tla/` if missing. By hand, from this directory (Java 11+):
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 1 \
  -metadir /tmp/tlc-states -config MC_live.cfg Rotation
```

## Properties
Invariants: `NoLeak` (crypto §4 covers rule: content written while the writer knew of M's departure is never readable by M), `DepartedLocked` (permissions §3: a departed holder never holds an epoch whose rotation did not box it; a property of the box rule only, AMBIGUITIES.md R19), `NoPlantedKey` (crypto §4: a secret learned only from a pending or invalid commit is never encrypted under: no wrap, box, history box, step or heartbeat under it). `NoPlantedKey` holds because `Enc(x)` (what x encrypts under) is `sec[x]`; negative control: with `Enc(x) == sec[x] \cup fsec[x]` (the text before the edit) `MC_plant` fails in 4 states (a grantee heartbeats under a planted secret before its grant box arrives; AMBIGUITIES.md R15). Temporal, under weak fairness of honest steps: `CurrentOpens` (every current holder eventually holds every current epoch), `ReadsAll` (... and every epoch content was written under, older ones by history boxes). "A writer never encrypts under a superseded epoch after it holds the rotation" holds by construction (`EncEp` takes the newest held current epoch) and is not a separate check.

The any-order version of `MC_withhold` grew past 5 million distinct states with a million queued and was stopped: withholding lets a2 finish its passes early, so its re-seals fire in many more interleavings. Negative-control traces: `MC_names_own` (a2 withheld e2, a1's removal of m1; a1 merges e1 and e2 into e3, wrapped under a2's e1; e3 names nothing, so a2 establishes it, sees m1 a holder missing a box there and re-seals e3 to it).

`run.sh small` runs `MC_live`, `MC_grant`, `MC_plant`, `MC_safety`, `MC_fork`, `MC_names_own`, `MC_c2_box` and `MC_c2_boxcovers`. `MC_grant_remove` is over the ~20 min budget on a loaded machine (the label-placed re-seals add their box records to the state; it was 1.76 million states before); it is kept because it is the only instance where a re-seal sits next to a removal.

Holder actors (a group g whose device d leaves it: keyset names, names of departures below A, keyset descent, forged and forked holder keysets, bound seals; the `MC_keyset*`, `MC_c1*`, `MC_c3*`, `MC_desc_*` instances, AMBIGUITIES.md R17, R18, R20) were modelled here until depth-0 sealing replaced them (issues/keys-depth0.md); see git history.

Hardening rerun: re-seals are placed under the label as well as sent by inbox, each node judges boxes by what it has applied and read (`Holders`), a rotation goes by inbox to a boxed holder without a box at the epoch before, and a planted box gives a reading-only secret. Every result is unchanged; probes (throwaway invariants) confirm that re-seals fire and are read by the other admin in `MC_grant`, and that rotation by inbox fires with a grant and two rotations.

Frozen coverage rerun (AMBIGUITIES.md R19; TLC 2.19, 2 workers, load 40-75, wall clock): every cfg now sets `Frozen = TRUE` but the `_box` ones. Rerun under it: `MC_live`, `MC_grant`, `MC_plant`, `MC_safety`, `MC_fork` pass with the state counts above; `MC_names_own` (`DepartedLocked`) fails as before. Not rerun under it: `MC_drop`, `MC_drop_refresh`, `MC_reorder`, `MC_grant_remove`, `MC_fork_reorder`, `MC_withhold`. `MC_c2` has MaxE = 2 (no rotation left after the trap: it checks that nobody writes under the epoch); with MaxE = 3 it passed millions of distinct states, unfinished.

Since registration waits for the rotation chain to be uploaded, the interleavings in which a step outran its rotation commit (and the stranded heartbeats and re-seals they caused) are gone, which is why most instances shrank by two orders of magnitude. Re-seals fire in `MC_drop`, `MC_grant` and `MC_grant_remove` (checked with an action property) but never in `MC_safety`, `MC_reorder` or `MC_fork_reorder`: with established epochs nothing triggers one there, so those instances check that no re-seal misfires rather than that a firing one is safe; `MC_grant_remove` covers a re-seal next to a removal (in log order; an any-order version passed 7 million states in 25 minutes and was stopped).

## Not covered
Write and admin rotation, holder actors (Depth0.tla), forged history entries, invalid rotation commits, demotion and `until` (`until` and finalizes: Finalize.tla), several hosts or branches of the step tree, steps without rotation, entry boxes and key-actor readers, `label.0` records, `ROLE_KEEP` compaction of steps in `label.0` and of rotation commits (`secrets.0` snapshots, so snapshot-established epochs and the post-compaction residue of notes/sync.md Limits).

## Depth0.tla: depth-0 sealing (notes/keys-requirements-study.md §4, issues/keys-depth0.md)
Several actors and holder actors, read role only, an actor's keyset = its read epoch; crypto as access (a node's secrets: the closure over boxes and history boxes of the commits it holds; a removed device's: over every box ever written); any delivery order; ignorant forks, unprompted rotations and one forged keyset as one-shot steps; A's own departures by frozen coverage (no establishment, hosts or claims: Rotation.tla's). The rules: anchored sealing, listed staleness, merge, and for cycles rule 4 (no key cycles). Actor grants come in an agreed order (`Grants`); each node gives key access to the grants it holds greedily in that order (`KH`), seals no keyless grant, and rotates its actor while a current epoch boxes an actor without key access (`Keyless`). `Hidden` grants are missing at a node at first (learned fairly; made later, so initial epochs do not box them); `MaxBlind` bounds the boxes sealed to a holder with no true key access (the ignorance window as one-shot adversary steps). `Taint`: a keyset boxing an actor without key access is stale and never sealed to. `Fresh = TRUE` swaps rule 4 for the failed Fresh candidate (cycle members left out and re-sealed by a check on their keyset), kept for its counterexample. Removed-member writer (issues/keys-depth0-removed-member-rotates.md): `Via` writers act on an actor through holder actors while they do not know their removal from one; their rotation is invalid where that removal is known (an epoch's own departures only travel with it; a removal also travels as a placement under a label). `NoSync` limits what a node learns to its labels and its targets' holders' `keys.0`; `Split` lets such a `keys.0` arrive without the keysets it names; `Rule` picks the candidate (`spec`, `ahead`, `remover`, `possess`). Instances are defined in `Depth0MC.tla` (a `.cfg` cannot write tuples), so run with module `Depth0MC`:
```
java -XX:+UseParallelGC -cp ~/.cache/tla/tla2tools.jar tlc2.TLC -workers 4 \
  -metadir /tmp/tlc-states -config D0_nc.cfg Depth0MC
```
Properties: `NoLeakDirect`, `NoLeakMember`, `NoLeakMemberAll` (invariants), `NoLeakKnown`, `NoLeakKnownAll` (a device removed from the actor, or from an actor with no key path to it, reads no content written knowing the removal once every node holds every grant; `All`: and the writer holds every keyset and re-seal), `Excludes`, `CurrentOpens`, `ReadsAll`, `Settles` (fairness of learning, forced rotation, re-seal); `NoLeakLimit` (a device removed from an actor below A reads no content of A written by a writer holding, for each holder actor of A, a keyset it cannot open that lists every one it can: the stated limit at any depth), `NoLeakSettled` (... written once every node holds what it syncs and needs no rotation). Liveness checking dominates: safety-only runs go 100-1000 times faster. The `D0_nc*` instances that finish are in run.sh. TLC 2.19, wall clock on a shared box.

The other failed candidates (the study's rules: unanchored sealing without merge, leaving cycle members out on direct departures with one box check; reachability; Fresh on the other shapes; a commit rule with rejoin rotations) and their traces are in git history and study §4.


## Timed grants and finalizes (`Finalize.tla`)
issues/withheld-departures-seeds.md. One group, read role, writers A, B, X (admins), readers D1, D2. Edge writes are entries (spec/crdt.md §5.6): A's `until` writes on D1, D2 and its renewal of D2 (before any finalize) each reset the entry their author held live; a finalize resets the live entry of each ended grant (erase, or keep: an `until` 0 entry beside) and carries a rotation naming the departures its author applied, its own by the departs mark. Any delivery order, commits whole; boxes are global and secrets are the closure over boxes and history boxes. `Rule` picks the Met rule (module header). Invariants: `NoLeak` (content written knowing a departure that stands with every commit held is not readable by the departed reader; every honest writer writes whenever it may), `Settles` (once all is delivered and nobody has a key action left, the honest writers agree on the current epochs and may encrypt, and every holder, a renewed one included, holds each of them). TLC 2.19, 4-8 workers, at load.

## Lockout recovery (`Lockout.tla`)
notes/lockout-recovery.md. One actor, one key role; admin a, honest writer h, writer w that rotates boxing `WBox` only, j granted by a's removal of w. a removes w once with a full frontier (every rotation of w it received) and the rotation that removal requires; commits arrive in any order; w's rotations count at a node unless it holds the removal and its frontier misses them. Invariants: `NoLeak` (w never holds a secret honest content went under once its writer knew of the removal), `Unlocked` (settled: a and h hold every current secret; one j lacks is held by an honest node, so re-sealable), `Alike`, `Repairable` (content j cannot read is held by an honest node that may rotate), `RemoveOK` (the removal can always be made). `ProbeRecovered` (expected to fail) confirms a recovery superseding an epoch a never held is reached. TLC 2.19, 4 workers.
