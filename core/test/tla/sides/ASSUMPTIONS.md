# Sides model: assumptions
> Summary: What Sides.tla abstracts from spec/sync.md §2, §5-§7 and notes/disputes-plan.md, and why.

- **Design exploration, not an independent check.** Written with notes/ and the sync model at hand, to test step 6 candidates before design. Rules not yet in spec/ (sides, Dd, the gates) are the plan's.
- **The dispute is over before the run.** X and Y removed each other; every commit (content, killers, snapshots, purges, placements) is written already and held by its author or under L. Writing order is not what step 6 is about.
- **Every node knows the dispute when it judges a disputed write.** A party's write is pending until its author's chain arrives, and that chain carries the other party's removal (chain certificates are built on every edge write, applied or not, closed under authors: spec/permissions.md §4). So a node never judges a disputed write without both removals. A judgment resting on a write *not held* (a placement, `pol = "invalid"`) can still miss it: modeled.
- **Validity is a constant per commit and side** (`Valid`). Dd, the set of writes valid on one side only, is what the walk would export as the closure of both parties' unlisted writes (plan step 3); the model takes it as given. The direct-seed gate uses `Seed` (written by X or Y themselves) instead.
- **Must-not-hold and covered are reasons on writes.** Each body lists reasons `[w, pol, kind]`: `pol = "valid"` holds while w is held and valid on the side (a superseder under purge on replace, a purge, a covering snapshot, an edge end that makes a card stale); `pol = "invalid"` holds while w is invalid or not held (an invalid write's fall under purge on replace, with w the body itself; a placement keeping a thing in the required set). Must-hold is: valid, no reason holds, not covered (no keep-at-least, no `nobody`). An invalid body with no reason is may-hold: nobody uploads or deletes it.
- **Bodies are whole commits; no encryption index, tombstones or carriers.** A re-upload after a delete is a fresh encryption in the real protocol, so tombstones never stop the loop; the model drops them. No live write survives a kill here, so carriers and snapshot-first never apply.
- **Deletes**: every node holds the delete keys of the label it acts at (all retention `writer`). A must-not-hold body is deleted every time it appears; compaction deletes a covered body once per body per node (sync §7), only with the covering snapshot under the label, and is optional (unfair).
- **Fork (c)**: one host. The parties race to register their rotation as L's next step; the first wins L, the loser makes R (sync §2 replacement, keys from its side's secrets) and deletes nothing at L (it does not control L's head). A node acts at L while its head is the pre-dispute step or its own side's, and at R if its side made R; it never acts with keys from a commit invalid on its side. Reading: a member boxed by both sides' rotations reads both labels; a party, and a fresh node granted after the dispute, read only its side's (a superseded step's sync key opens nothing but the walk to its child, and the winner's claim set does not list them: sync §5).
- **Host branching**: the loser's step is a child of the pre-dispute step, kept by the host as a second branch (R) instead of refused. The branch starts with the entries at L stamped while the pre-dispute step was the head (`pre`): what a holder of that step's keys could already read, never later uploads; entries deleted at L after the first one-sided step are not in it. Each side acts at its own branch for good. Gate lift (`Lift`): off at R, and at L once branched (one side acts at each); on at L before that, when both sides act under the shared step.
- **Concession**: a body valid on both sides, held at first by the party of the side giving up, role-class. A node holding it judges every write as side `CWin` does (Dd empty for it), and a member holding it acts as side `CWin`; the conceding party keeps its own side (it is removed everywhere and would erase) and is left out of Alike. No retraction.
- **Need rule** (`need`): judges b on both sides, which one fork makes two. With several forks, the sides multiply; not modelled.
- **Bridge**: a member (holding both sides' boxes) also uploads and deletes clean bodies (no disputed write on them or on a held reason) at a label it reads but does not act at. Using the other side's keys for that is itself a new dispute-aware site.
- **Role commits first** (`RoleC`): a node uploads its must-hold role-class commits (placements) before content and fetches those present before content (sync §6 first sync, order 0 first; notes/node-sync.md, The pass). Scenario Q0 drops this order.
- **Switching**: only the member switches, at most twice in all (unfair, bounded). Parties keep their own side. A switch changes the view at once; dropped bodies wanted again are fetched (cursor refetch, sync §6 Dropped bodies).
- **Liveness under weak fairness** of every honest step per node and body, and of registration and replacement. Switches and compaction are unfair.
- **Not covered**: several hosts, honest concurrent rotations under branching, a removed admin opening a branch, several disputes, keep-at-least and `nobody`, live writes surviving a kill (carriers, snapshot-first), covers resting on deaths that rest on a disputed write, node-card details, inbox and deposits, the party erasing itself on a switch (plan step 5), stale views and clock skew.
