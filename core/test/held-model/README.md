# held-model
> Summary: Brute-force executable spec of what a node holding a set of commits reads and keeps (apply and waits, validity with actors and invites, undo, purge, expiry, history by the thing's `retention^.0` settings, signals); greenfield models of the reference graph plan (closure), of evidence per sync ID along stated paths (evidence) and of connection-lived things (connlived); input format, input changes and how to call it.

Written from `spec/` alone, depending on no osis crate, only on the other models: crdt-model (the other independent reading, whose fragments are its values: join, prune, read, un-merge, strip, death, covering) and roles-model (`paths`, the judge of `evidence` alone), so it is an independent reading to test the engine against. Every query recomputes from scratch (`evaluate` is a pure function of `Held`). AMBIGUITIES.md lists the choices the spec left; ASSUMPTIONS.md what is out of scope.

## Input (`src/types.rs`)
`Held { now, me, records, commits, dots, external_fires }`:
- `now`: the node's wall clock (ms). `me`: the node (needed for "may I drop this").
- `external_fires`: when a fire of an external signal by a party holding no write is valid. `Any` (default: whatever the setting, for adapters of an engine without the rule; osis-engine `tests/it/held.rs` passes it), `Enabled` (the rule, spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires: the current setting has an entry of its own naming the signal), and two negative controls: `AtKey` (the setting at the fire's key) and `Arrival` (the last setting in input order).
- Ids: things, actors and nodes share one `u32` space.
- `dots`: which commit each write belongs to (spec/crdt.md §2.4): per session-form commit and aspect its source and counter range (`DotRange`); a commit no range names is commit form under `commit_source(id)` (`stamp(ts, id, ctr)` builds its stamps).
- `records`: the creation records the node holds apart from creations (key actors', a dependent's chain proofs), `Record { id, link }` with `RecordLink::Key` (a key actor: node, link key; admin of itself) or `RecordLink::Admin(a)` (its mint admin). A held `created.0` body (`Delta::Created(link)` on `Aspect::CREATED`) gives its record too, once its author's and mint admin's are known.
- `fetch.rs` models what is fetched and kept (notes/expiry-creation-design.md §3): sync IDs, boxes that may be forged, reading-only keys, the fetch loop to quiescence.
- A thing **exists** while a creation of it joins, or, no held commit listing a creation of it, while its record alone is known (a record is identity). A part on a thing that does not exist, or whose author or chain record is unknown, is not held (`DropWhy::Unknown`); a commit holding nothing else is thrown out whole (`Outcome::revisit`, removed by `act`, for the caller to fetch again). A thing every joined creation of which is expired or purged is **destroyed** (`Outcome::destroyed`): the destruction is judged with its data held, then every part on it counts as never received in the whole judgment (`DropWhy::Destroyed`). Records outlive their things: things relying on a destroyed thing keep their chains. Waiting (`Keep(Pending)`) is left for a missing lead and parked parts.
- `commits`: `Commit { id, signer, acting, ts, expires, kind, parts, purge, frontier, undoes, own_cover, boxes }`. The header is held; each `Part { thing, aspect, purge_by, body, stripped }` has `Body::Held(Delta)`, `Body::Missing`, `Body::Dropped` or `Body::Parked` (bytes it cannot interpret).
  - `signer`: the signing node; `acting`: the thing its path reads as acting on (notes/redesign-plan.md, Interim in code; the signer for the empty path). `undoes`: the undone commit. `own_cover`: a snapshot marked as its signer's own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.1): valid also by §5.2 Own cover, and a held body drops under it only at its signer (`me`).
  - `kind`: `Content`; `Role` (each part a `Delta::Edge(EdgeWrite { holder, role, invite, until })` on `Aspect::ROLES`, `role` None removes, `until` wall ms, `until` 0 a removal saying retain); `Snapshot` (writes are copies under their original stamps).
  - `stripped`: the commits whose values `act` stripped from the body (it holds its leftover).
  - Content and snapshot parts carry `Delta::Writes(Frag)`: a crdt-model fragment of any encoding (a write of another encoding than the aspect's others joins as spec/crdt.md §7 says), value-less writes as crdt-model holds them. A `retention^.0` part carries `Delta::Retention(Setting { rules, default })` on `Aspect::RETENTION` with purge-by `admin`: per aspect name a `SettingEntry { history, expires }` (`history` an `Opt`: `None`, `Keep { after, until }` (ms past replaced and/or wall ms, the later; `until` `NEVER` for good), `Purge`; `expires` the writer's expiry default, read by writers only). An `addenda.0` part carries `Delta::Addenda(Vec<(removal, listed commits)>)` on `Aspect::ADDENDA` (entries of a held body; a stripped one lists nothing).
  - `Aspect { name, admin_only, signal, external }`: `admin_only` is the `^` marker; `signal` the `~` marker; `external` the `@` before it (`Aspect::external`: no path needed, governed by its own entry only, never the default) (parts carry one root register write, never in a snapshot). `fixed()`: `roles.0`, `retention^.0`, `purge.0`, `addenda.0`, `created.0`: governed by no setting, never out of history, never covered.
  - `PurgeBy`: who may purge the write: `admin`, `author_or_admin` or `writer` (`PurgeBy::ALL`). What replaced content is kept is the thing's setting, never the write's. `purge: Vec<PurgeItem>` (a commit's delta on an aspect, a whole aspect, or an author on an aspect); `frontier` on demotions.
- `Body::Missing`: never received or counting so; `Body::Dropped(by)`: dropped here as covered by snapshot commit `by`'s part on the aspect: it contributes nothing while the cover stands (valid, held or itself dropped covered) and is never received once it does not (`Outcome::uncovered`).

## Output (`src/model.rs`, `evaluate(&Held) -> Outcome`)
- `parked`, `applied`, `valid` (per part of an applied commit; role parts: whether the edge write applied), `purged` (part to request).
- `reads` per `(thing, aspect)`: the crdt-model read, None absent; `states`: the canonical fragment.
- Applied commits, reads and validity read creations as above; a creation is always valid (a record needs no authority).
- `settings`: per thing its held valid `retention^.0` bodies with their values, by the write each carries (an admin-only delta at its key); the last is the current setting. `Outcome::out(thing, aspect, r, w)`: the moment a body replaced at wall ms `r`, its oldest dead write at `w` (`oldest_dead`), leaves history by the current setting (`thing_history::out`); `in_history`, `purges`. No validity depends on a setting.
- `fates` per held or parked body: `Keep(why)` (`Pending`: it or its commit waits; `Role`: role-class; `Until`: in history, or before a purge request's floor; `Live`: live before its aspect's live bound, `Outcome::live_until`; `Plain`: uncovered, fixed retention), `Leftover(why)` (strip values), `MayDrop(why)` (`Covered`: out of history or live past its live bound, any valid snapshot covers it, the right or not; `PastBound`: a signal body, whole or leftover, out of history), `MustDrop(why)` (`Covered`: under purge on replace, a valid snapshot whose commit holds the purge right covers it, at every node; `Gone`: a waiting part past its expiry or an unjoinable body expired or purged, which counts as never received; `Invalid`: judged invalid, not `me`'s own), `NeverReceived`. A waiting part of an applied commit that a request names is `Leftover(PendingPurged)`, not in `purged`.
- Signals: nothing of a signal aspect joins (read `Absent`, empty state); a valid content body on one is replaced at its own `ts`, so `history()` shows its write while in history under the entry governing its AK (`signal_kept`; no entry, never) unless purged.
- `replaced`: held valid bodies holding a write the canonical state does not, with `replaced_at`: the wall ms of the earliest valid replacing write held (by its signed `ts`; a snapshot at the latest death among the writes no later held valid snapshot carries; None: nothing held dates it, not history); `history`: per aspect the replaced writes of bodies in history (`history()`, a function of the held set); `under_purge`: replaced value bodies the current setting puts under purge on replace (its entry, or the floor joined over the held settings); `owed`: those no valid right-holding snapshot covers while `me` holds the right (the cover `me` owes, `cover`); `uncovered`: dropped bodies whose cover fell, to be fetched again.
- `deadlines`: every clock value after `now` at which the output may change with the held set unchanged (unparking, expiry, a body leaving history, a purge floor).
- `valueless`: deltas held value-less (expired, purged); `forbidden`: every value of theirs still held, with the body. An engine holding the same set must hold none of them once it has acted.

`Dots::new(&Held)` gives each write's commit (`commit`, `valueless`, `strip`, `values_of`). `act(&Held, &Outcome, may)` carries out the forced fates (strips, drops, makes the uncovered and never received missing, and one optional drop) and returns the next held set; `cover(&Held, &Outcome, thing, aspect, id, ts)` the covering snapshot `me` writes from its whole state (purge-by `writer`); `timeline(&Held)` the settled role timeline; `undo::undo(&Held, target, id, ts, author)` the undo commit node `me` writes (spec/crdt.md §4.6: each aspect written to read as without the undone commit, by crdt-model's diff, on the generator's aspects `gen::ty`); `gen::history(seed, n)` builds a random history (content through crdt-model's ops on a register, a map, a text, a set, a counter, a list and a struct holding a map; settings: things made with one, late ones, invalid ones; half the writers follow the setting they hold: its expiry default, no fire it would not keep) (writers cover what they owe most of the time), `gen::shuffle` reorders one.

## External fires (`src/external.rs`, `tests/it/props_external.rs`, `spec_signals::external`)
Worlds of a thing with two admins, a writer and a stranger: settings enabling the signal or not, fires stamped before and after them, the writer's removal, the admins removing each other; and `dispute`, the admins removing each other while toggling the setting. Every subset of a world's events is a held set. Properties under `Enabled`: every order judges every fire alike; a fire neither its author's authority nor the current setting makes valid is listed nowhere and not promoted (`promotes`), an enabled one is valid, a writer's ignores the setting (with a tally of the shapes reached); an older setting dropping changes no fire. Each fails on a control (`Arrival`; `Any`; `AtKey` twice).

## Overlapping sessions (`src/overlap.rs`, `tests/it/props_overlap.rs`)
`evaluate_with(&Held, &Overlap)` judges spec/commits.md §4's overlap (two session-form commits of one source whose ranges on an aspect intersect are void, whole but for `created.0`) by `Overlap::Held` (every applied commit counts, a void one counts for no other rule: `evaluate`'s default, as the engine), `Valid` (only deltas valid by every other rule, `Outcome::otherwise`, as a last layer no rule reads) or `Given(set)` (a probe); `Outcome::void`. `overlap::consistent` lists the void sets a rule reading verdicts could settle on; `rule_inputs` the session-form commits carrying addenda, settings or purges. Worlds: an admin X later removed, a writer W, a stranger; hand worlds (`removed_junk`, `stranger_junk`, `vouched_later`, `liar_setting`, `liar_addenda`) and random ones, every subset a held set. Under `Valid`: a function of the held set, junk moves no verdict, honest sessions never overlap, other sessions' plain writes never move X's (each with a control); it fails as a rule twice: held sets with no consistent verdict where rule inputs are session form, and a dot two commits claim has no owner for un-merge (issues/overlap-among-valid-commits.md).

## Signal edits (`src/edits.rs`, `tests/it/spec_edits.rs`)
`Part::replaces` is a signal edit's claim; `edits::listing(&Held)` is the signal's listing under the rule of notes/signal-edits-design.md (`Entry { place, version, shows }`), `edits::listed` the commits with a listed part. Each property runs on every subset of small worlds, smallest first.

## Thing-level history (`src/thing_history.rs`, `tests/it/spec_thing_history.rs`)
The judgment of spec/commits.md §5 The setting by the current setting: `out` (the moment a body replaced at `r` leaves history), `under_purge` (the entry purges on replace, or the body was written before the floor), `live_until`, `joined_floor` (the floor joined over every held setting), `written_after` (a writer writes the join), the oracle `purged` (replaced while the setting in force by stamp, over every setting written, purged: the past no node keeps), and, for the greenfield worlds, `history` and `Eager` (a node that drops at the first allowed moment and takes nothing back). `Rule` names the design and its negative controls (the current setting's floor alone; no floor; the floor against the dated replacement). `evaluate` judges every replaced body the same way (`Outcome::out`). Properties run over every small world (settings chained or by concurrent admins) and random larger ones on the functions, and again (`ev::`) through `evaluate`: purged content never shows at a node that learnt of the setting it was replaced under (holds it, or one whose writer knew it), whatever settings and bodies it holds and in any order (and does under every negative control), a carried join leaves the older settings adding nothing, bodies leave in replaced order but for those under the floor, kept while live, the current setting alone decides (a newer one re-shows a held body, a late older one changes nothing), a keep after purge on replace keeps what is written after it, who may purge changes no history, only valid settings are current and none judges validity, dropping shows a subset in any order.

## Deleting by link (`src/freed.rs`, `tests/it/spec_freed.rs`)
The freed judgment of notes/chunk-links.md: links carry Del (the writer's claim); a drop puts the body's links in the node's freed set; each link is judged when its delete is sent, against the sources of its thing the node holds then (reach needs every node blob on the way, held or fetched, else it waits): reached, it leaves the set; else an interior node's links join the set and the sync unit is deleted under each sync ID the node holds, signed by a key its Del names that the node holds; the peer accepts only a key the blob bears under the thing signed for. Properties over random worlds (reuse across authors and epochs, two sync IDs, forged cross-thing links, false Del, missing node blobs, drops and bodies held again interleaved with deletes): no delete of a sync unit a held source of its thing reaches (P1), every accepted delete borne by the blob under that thing (P3), and once settled a sync unit no node reaches is gone under each sync ID wherever a node that reached it, with nothing waiting, holds the role key and a key the blob bears that its honest links name (P2). Each property fails with its rule removed (no descent into freed nodes, no reach check, no thing binding at the peer).

## The reference graph (`src/closure.rs`, `tests/it/spec_closure.rs`)
A greenfield model of the reference graph (spec/data-model.md §8, spec/sync-set.md), apart from `evaluate`: keys by role grants followed transitively (`World::keys_of`), sync units with no leaves (`sync unit`, `piece_seen`), carrying per sync ID with grants as role commits, per-sync-ID stores and delete keys (`Sync`, `Carry`; `certify_revokes` is the fix for the revocation hole), the node closure walked sync unit by sync unit and the making rule (`closure`, `Walk`, `may_make`), release at a pass's end (`Net`, with ephemeral strongs and restarts) and the spanning-tree structure for removing a strong (`Tree`, counting work). Properties P1-P6, checks (a)-(d), counts and the hole found: notes/closure-model.md.

## Properties (`tests/it/props.rs`)
`settle` carries out the forced fates, writes the covers `me` owes, and (given the full history) fetches again what `uncovered` or `NeverReceived` names.
- The output is a function of the held set (any order of commits). The histories include every purge option, settings (late and invalid ones), undos (written by `undo::undo`), invites, `until`, self-removals, finalizes, link redemptions, acting for an account, parked parts and unknown records.
- Forced fates change no read and leave no forbidden value; each optional drop changes no read.
- Once a right holder has settled, it holds no body under purge on replace (but as an expired or purged leftover, or before a purge request's floor), no body is dropped before a purge request's floor but by purge, and `history()` shows no body the node may drop.
- A commit arriving after the node acted on the rest, and the same commits arriving one at a time in any order with the node acting as they come, read as one held from the start, once bodies whose cover or purge fell are fetched again; aspects where a part went at its expiry while waiting excepted (spec/requirements.md, Deterministic, its Limit). 2400 seeds pass.
- A signal's `history()` reads alike in both convergence properties above.
- The output is constant between `now` and its first deadline.
- Creations last read like creations first, once what was thrown out is fetched again. Destructions are repaired like any purge that falls: a destruction by purge keeps the thing's `roles.0` parts, and `settle` fetches the rest again.

## Adapter use
osis-node `tests/it/held.rs` maps what a node holds into `Held` and compares applied commits, validity, reads, the promise of purge on replace, bodies dropped as covered while in history or live before their live bound and `forbidden` on every content aspect (scope and normalizations in its module doc; bodies decoded from the engine's wire bytes); `order::held_set_decides` runs it on every node it judges (`Cfg::model`; also `held_model_agrees_*`), as the reference independent of the engine. The properties (tests/it/props.rs) run 30 seeds plus the rarer shapes' seeds in `_plain` twins and in `_large` twins from `OSIS_HELD_START` (default 0), `OSIS_HELD_SEEDS` many (300, 3000 under `OSIS_FULL=1`), a thread per core: `cargo test -p held-model --test it -- --ignored _plain _large`. Both are properties, not default tests: every held-model, crdt-model or roles-model gate selects them and nothing else can move them (this crate depends on no osis crate).

## Evidence per sync ID (`src/evidence.rs`, `tests/it/spec_evidence.rs`, `spec_open10.rs`, `spec_story.rs`)
A greenfield model of notes/write-path-design.md §2 (notes/redesign-plan.md M2, D2): commits judged by roles-model `paths` (`Rules::fixed`, a pending layer on top: `World::pend`), sync IDs with units given in time (`Ev::Join`, `Ev::Leave`), nodes syncing sync IDs (closed under the standing duty) and logging what their view requires (`World::req`: the evidence closure of a unit's bodies and of the grants naming its thing; a removal only as evidence of a body or by Role changes travel: lazy delivery, no errand, no reach), a granter delivering its grant under the holder's sync ID, the thief logging its own (never a lowering it withholds, nor what only that lowering brings), and readers holding one sync ID. A lowering's list is in the lowering: every copy carries it whole (`Lists::Whole`; the control `Lists::Unit`). A rotation's keyset (`World::rotate`) is a body carrying the demotions its writer holds, each whole: with `Rule::keysets` they are in the required set of every sync ID holding it, and a keyset pends without one (`p11_keysets` checks both, and that a rotator holds what it carries). A node logs only under sync IDs it can stamp (`Node::stamps`, `World::stamps_only`: the family's nodes stamp their own things and what they write); with `Rule::deposit` (D4, off by default) a node that cannot stamp deposits the removal copies its view requires (`d4_explore` by hand: the gap with and without, deposits never promoted). `Rule` names the design's reading and its variants (`Placement`, `Lists`, `keys_open`, `records`, `author_reach`, `Closure`); `Rule::fixed` is the drafted closure, `Stage::A2` puts OPEN 10's (iv) and keys-open travel on it (the design and the engine differential's oracle). **The reference** (`World::reference`): each reader is judged against a node holding everything written but the lowerings that take something away (`takes_away`) its sync ID lacks and no honest node syncing it holds (`undelivered`); where that node's verdict differs from the node holding everything is **the gap** (`World::gap`; issues/removed-key-writes-show-far.md), counted by the properties, never excluded silently. Checks per step: `p1` (P2 follows from it), `p4`, `p5_order`, `p8_promotions` (promotions on verdicts, `PromoteWhen`; a drop is at once), each against the reference; worlds: `family` (30,720 shapes of the stolen-node story over units, record edges on the route among them: `Shape::mint`), `random`, hand worlds (a split commit by `write_parts`); `cost` counts copies (P6). Default tests: a hand world per finding and named risk, the gap's hand worlds, a slice of the family. Properties: `one_sync_id_reader_judges_as_everything_plain` and `_large` (random seeds from `OSIS_HELD_START`; each prints its worlds with the gap): `cargo test -p held-model --test it -- --ignored one_sync_id_reader`. By hand: `explore` and `explore_random` print every variant's failures and gap; `show_random` one world (`OSIS_EV_RULE` names the variant).
Which writes the closure takes (notes/a2-spec/README.md OPEN 10): `Closure` (`Takes`: all, chained (`World::chained`), applied; joined paths; `CommitEdges`: what a commit's other edge writes on a thing bring), the pending rule `World::pend` (`Pend`), what joins a hop under `Pend::Granted` for joined paths and pending alike (`World::join`, `Join`: J1 any chained grant, J2 `Needed` one giving what the part needs there (`World::need`), the default, J3 `Refused` none judged invalid, J4 `PendNeeded` joined as J1 and pending as J2; by hand `OSIS_EV_JOIN` sets it for every world, OPEN 16, finding 23), entry creations (`World::creations`: only a part of a commit with a removal, `World::ends_grant`, waits for its reset's creation), the six `Open10` variants (`Open10::set` puts one on a rule and a world). Checks `p0_applied_chained`, `p9_leak` (no copy of a roster that gives no role on the unit), `p10_junk` (no copy of a write no walk applies). `story_world` turns a roles-model `paths::Sim` into an evidence world under one of `STORY_UNITS` unit crossings (checked at each tick's end, `spec_story::story_steps`). `cost_with` counts copies with churn and sync IDs carrying a thing. Default tests: a hand world per finding (`spec_open10`), one story world. Property `evidence_along_joined_chained_paths_plain` (about 50 CPU-s) and `_large`: (iv) over the family, random and story worlds. By hand: `open10_explore` (every variant over family and random worlds: `OSIS_OPEN10_STEP`, `OSIS_OPEN10_RANDOM`), `story_explore` (`OSIS_STORY_N`, `OSIS_EV_RULE` one variant), `open10_show_random`. Findings: notes/evidence-model-findings.md.
How removals reach a sync ID (notes/write-path-design.md §2 D1 to D3; notes/a2-spec/README.md OPEN 14; `spec_reach.rs`, `spec_gaps.rs`): `Rule::records` (record edges in keys-open travel: on; off the control, OPEN 17), `Rule::keys_open` (Role changes travel's keys-open half), `Rule::author_reach` (today's any-path chains kept for removals), `Closure::any_path` (today's chain certificates); `Stage` names the rules compared, each over (iv) (`Stage::set`): `A2` (the design: keys-open travel on), `A2Actor`, `Today`, `A2AuthorReach`; each judged against its reference with its gap counted (`spec_reach::stage_gaps`). The reference follows the rule's own delivery, so a delivery rule that brings less (keys-open travel off) shows as more gap, not as a failure: compare delivery rules by their gap. Generator `wide` (`Wide`: split commits, late path entries via `World::opens`, minted things; a read-hop move: A granted read on a thing no honest node syncs with the one the thief then writes through it), list entries naming their part on their own thing alone (finding 24: the judge, roles-model `paths::World::in_leaves`, reads them so), check `p1_trusting` (P1 under another trust set). Default tests: hand worlds per finding (`open14_*`: findings 17 and 18 as the gap, 19 keys-open travel; `open17_*`, `gaps_*`: finding 23 under each `Join`, 24, 20 and 28 as the gap), and `gaps_wide_worlds_reach_the_read_hop`. Property `evidence_reach_and_generator_gaps_plain` and `_large`: the design over `wide` worlds with per-reader trust, the gap counted. By hand: `reach_explore` (every stage over family, random and story worlds, with the worlds where a stage has the gap and today has none: `OSIS_REACH_STEP`, `OSIS_REACH_RANDOM`, `OSIS_STORY_N`; `OSIS_REACH_CONTROL=grants` reads every stage but today over grants only), `gaps_explore` (every stage over `wide` worlds per option set with trust: `OSIS_GAPS_N`, `OSIS_EV_RULE` one stage), `gaps_show` (one `wide` world and every stage's failures: `OSIS_GAPS_SEED`, `OSIS_GAPS_OPT`).

## Connection-lived things (`src/connlived.rs`, `tests/it/spec_connlived.rs`)
A greenfield model of issues/connection-lived-data.md: an oracle settling the rule on the connection graph after each event against a path-vector protocol with copy sync, messages in random order, compared at quiescence; variants for nodes keeping a thing unread (`Blind`), one table or the best route avoiding each neighbour (`alternates`), and drops held until routes settle (`settled_drops`). `explore` (by hand) prints every variant's failure count. Findings: the issue's Model section.
