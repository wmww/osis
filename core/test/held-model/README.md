# held-model
> Summary: Brute-force executable spec of what a node holding a set of commits reads and keeps (apply and waits, validity along stated paths, undo, purge, expiry, history by the thing's `retention^.0` settings, signals); greenfield models of the reference graph plan (closure), of certificate copies cut per sync ID (copy_cut), of evidence per sync ID along stated paths (evidence) and of connection-lived things (connlived); input format, input changes and how to call it.

Written from `spec/` alone, depending on no osis crate, only on the other models: crdt-model (the other independent reading, whose fragments are its values: join, prune, read, un-merge, strip, death, covering) and roles-model (`paths`: the judge of the role timeline, content validity and purge rights, `src/roles.rs`, and of `evidence`), so it is an independent reading to test the engine against. `roles.rs` holds no rule of the walk: it builds a `paths::World` from the joined commits (each part along the path its commit states for its thing; things and times renumbered in order; a commit's edge writes in the walk's order; an `until` in `ts` units; an addendum as one world part per cut its entries are against; a snapshot carrying the commits its held body has writes of; a purge request per thing, a destruction where its item names `created.0`) and reads `paths::judge_rights` under `Rules::purging`: which edge writes apply, which deltas are valid (admin-only ones needing admin), each request's and snapshot's right over a delta, the final roster. What the node itself could do now (`Timeline::my_right`, `Outcome::owed`, the path a cover states) is read off a request of its own, stamped after everything, along each path of standing edges giving it write: which paths it states is a writer's choice, each judged as stated. Every query recomputes from scratch (`evaluate` is a pure function of `Held`). AMBIGUITIES.md lists the choices the spec left; ASSUMPTIONS.md what is out of scope.

## Input (`src/types.rs`)
`Held { now, me, records, commits, dots, external_fires }`:
- `now`: the node's wall clock (ms). `me`: the node (needed for "may I drop this").
- `external_fires`: when a fire of an external signal by a party holding no write is valid. `Any` (default: whatever the setting, for adapters of an engine without the rule; osis-engine `tests/it/held.rs` passes it), `Enabled` (the rule, spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires: the current setting has an entry of its own naming the signal), and two negative controls: `AtKey` (the setting at the fire's key) and `Arrival` (the last setting in input order).
- Ids: things, actors and nodes share one `u32` space.
- `dots`: which commit each write belongs to (spec/crdt.md §2.4): per session-form commit and aspect its source and counter range (`DotRange`); a commit no range names is commit form under `commit_source(id)` (`stamp(ts, id, ctr)` builds its stamps).
- `records`: the creation records the node holds apart from creations (things minted with a key, a dependent's chain proofs), `Record { id, link }` with `RecordLink::Key` (minted with a key: a node thing, a link key; the key holds admin on it) or `RecordLink::Admin(a)` (its mint admin). A held `created.0` body (`Delta::Created(link)` on `Aspect::CREATED`) gives its record too, once its author's and mint admin's are known.
- `fetch.rs` models what is fetched and kept (notes/expiry-creation-design.md §3): sync IDs, boxes that may be forged, reading-only keys, the fetch loop to quiescence.
- A thing **exists** while a creation of it joins, or, no held commit listing a creation of it, while its record alone is known (a record is identity). A part on a thing that does not exist, whose signer's, thing's or listed things' records (or one up their chains) are unknown, or with an edge of its stated path that is neither a record edge nor set by a held grant (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable), is not held (`DropWhy::Unknown`); a commit holding nothing else is thrown out whole (`Outcome::revisit`, removed by `act`, for the caller to fetch again). A thing every joined creation of which is expired or purged is **destroyed** (`Outcome::destroyed`): the destruction is judged with its data held, then every part on it but its creations (gone: headers) and `roles.0` counts as never received in the whole judgment (`DropWhy::Destroyed`), however it was destroyed, so authority through it stands as its edge writes say. Records outlive their things: things relying on a destroyed thing keep their chains. Waiting (`Keep(Pending)`) is left for a missing lead, parked parts and parts whose commit lists a key no held step places (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys: `Part::keys` lists level-1 and level-3 keys and, on a `sync.0` part, the steps its body records; a key is right where a step bearing it is on a path from a genesis step every step of which a valid, joined `sync.0` part on a thing up the part's record chain records before the commit; the minting of a thing minted with a key, no step recorded before it, is not checked (First steps); a creation is never held back so). Such a part is not valid, held or not, until a step places its keys; `judge` joins again without a recording part judged invalid until every one that joins is valid.
- `commits`: `Commit { id, signer, author, paths, ts, expires, kind, parts, purge, frontier, undoes, own_cover, boxes }`. The header is held; each `Part { thing, aspect, purge_by, body, stripped }` has `Body::Held(Delta)`, `Body::Missing`, `Body::Dropped` or `Body::Parked` (bytes it cannot interpret).
  - `signer`: the thing signed as (minted with a key: no key grant is modelled, so its key hop is its record's key); `paths`: per thing written the path stated for it (`Path { things, direct }`: the listed things and the hops marked direct; no entry lists nothing; `Commit::along` states one path for every thing); `author`: its purge author, the signer or a listed thing (a part whose path's members leave it out is invalid). `undoes`: the undone commit. `own_cover`: a snapshot marked as its signer's own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.1): valid also by §5.2 Own cover, and a held body drops under it only at its signer (`me`).
  - `kind`: `Content`; `Role` (each part a `Delta::Edge(EdgeWrite { holder, role, may_grant, until })` on `Aspect::ROLES`, `role` None removes, `until` wall ms, `until` 0 a removal saying retain); `Snapshot` (writes are copies under their original stamps).
  - `stripped`: the commits whose gone writes `act` removed from the body (it holds its leftover).
  - Content and snapshot parts carry `Delta::Writes(Frag)`: a crdt-model fragment of any encoding (a write of another encoding than the aspect's others joins as spec/crdt.md §7 says) A `retention^.0` part carries `Delta::Retention(Setting { rules, default })` on `Aspect::RETENTION` with purge-by `admin`: per aspect name a `SettingEntry { history, expires }` (`history` an `Opt`: `None`, `Keep { after, until }` (ms past replaced and/or wall ms, the later; `until` `NEVER` for good), `Purge`; `expires` the writer's expiry default, read by writers only). An `addenda.0` part carries `Delta::Addenda(Vec<(removal, listed commits)>)` on `Aspect::ADDENDA` (entries of a held body; a stripped one lists nothing).
  - `Aspect { name, admin_only, signal, external }`: `admin_only` is the `^` marker; `signal` the `~` marker; `external` the `@` before it (`Aspect::external`: no path needed, governed by its own entry only, never the default) (parts carry one root register write, never in a snapshot). `fixed()`: `roles.0`, `retention^.0`, `purge.0`, `addenda.0`, `created.0`: governed by no setting, never out of history, never covered.
  - `PurgeBy`: who may purge the write: `admin`, `author_or_admin` or `writer` (`PurgeBy::ALL`). What replaced content is kept is the thing's setting, never the write's. `purge: Vec<PurgeItem>` (a commit's delta on an aspect, a whole aspect, or an author on an aspect); `frontier` on demotions.
- `Body::Missing`: never received or counting so; `Body::Dropped(by)`: dropped here as covered by snapshot commit `by`'s part on the aspect: it contributes nothing while the cover stands (valid, held or itself dropped covered) and is never received once it does not (`Outcome::uncovered`).

## Output (`src/model.rs`, `evaluate(&Held) -> Outcome`)
- `parked`, `applied`, `valid` (per part of an applied commit; role parts: whether the edge write applied), `purged` (part to request).
- `reads` per `(thing, aspect)`: the crdt-model read, None absent; `states`: the canonical fragment.
- Applied commits, reads and validity read creations as above; a creation is always valid (a record needs no authority).
- `settings`: per thing its held valid `retention^.0` bodies with their values, by the write each carries (an admin-only delta at its key); the last is the current setting. `Outcome::out(thing, aspect, r, w)`: the moment a body replaced at wall ms `r`, its oldest dead write at `w` (`oldest_dead`), leaves history by the current setting (`thing_history::out`); `in_history`, `purges`. No validity depends on a setting.
- `fates` per held or parked body: `Keep(why)` (`Pending`: it or its commit waits; `Role`: role-class; `Until`: in history, or before a purge request's floor; `Live`: live before its aspect's live bound, `Outcome::live_until`; `Plain`: uncovered, fixed retention), `Leftover(why)` (remove gone writes), `MayDrop(why)` (`Covered`: out of history or live past its live bound, any valid snapshot covers it, the right or not; `PastBound`: a signal body, whole or leftover, out of history), `MustDrop(why)` (`Covered`: under purge on replace, a valid snapshot whose commit holds the purge right covers it, at every node; `Gone`: a waiting part past its expiry or an unjoinable body expired or purged, which counts as never received; `Invalid`: judged invalid, not `me`'s own), `NeverReceived`. A waiting part of an applied commit that a request names is `Leftover(PendingPurged)`, not in `purged`.
- Signals: nothing of a signal aspect joins (read `Absent`, empty state); a valid content body on one is replaced at its own `ts`, so `history()` shows its write while in history under the entry governing its AK (`signal_kept`; no entry, never) unless purged.
- `replaced`: held valid bodies holding a write the canonical state does not, with `replaced_at`: the wall ms of the earliest valid replacing write held (by its signed `ts`; a snapshot at the latest death among the writes no later held valid snapshot carries; None: nothing held dates it, not history); `history`: per aspect the replaced writes of bodies in history (`history()`, a function of the held set); `under_purge`: replaced value bodies the current setting puts under purge on replace (its entry, or the floor joined over the held settings); `owed`: those no valid right-holding snapshot covers while `me` holds the right (the cover `me` owes, `cover`); `uncovered`: dropped bodies whose cover fell, to be fetched again.
- `deadlines`: every clock value after `now` at which the output may change with the held set unchanged (unparking, expiry, a body leaving history, a purge floor). Each clock read in `judge` and `fate` has its moment listed from the same function it compares with, for every part that can reach that read: so a signal's keep bound is listed whatever its validity (`me`'s own invalid fire is `Keep(Until)` to it), and a replaced body's `out` covers `kept`'s `now < r` (`out` is never before `r`).
- `gone`: deltas whose writes are gone (expired, purged; spec/crdt.md §4.7); `forbidden`: every write of theirs still held, with the body. An engine holding the same set must hold none of them once it has acted.

`Dots::new(&Held)` gives each write's commit (`commit`, `strip`, `writes_of`). `act(&Held, &Outcome, may)` carries out the forced fates (strips, drops, makes the uncovered and never received missing, and one optional drop) and returns the next held set; `cover(&Held, &Outcome, thing, aspect, id, ts)` the covering snapshot `me` writes from its whole state (purge-by `writer`); `timeline(&Held)` the settled role timeline; `undo::undo(&Held, target, id, ts, author)` the undo commit node `me` writes (spec/crdt.md §4.6: each aspect written to read as without the undone commit, by crdt-model's diff, on the generator's aspects `gen::ty`); `gen::history(seed, n)` builds a random history (content through crdt-model's ops on a register, a map, a text, a set, a counter, a list and a struct holding a map; settings: things made with one, late ones, invalid ones; half the writers follow the setting they hold: its expiry default, no fire it would not keep) (writers cover what they owe most of the time), `gen::shuffle` reorders one.

## External fires (`src/external.rs`, `tests/it/props_external.rs`, `spec_signals::external`)
Worlds of a thing with two admins, a writer and a stranger: settings enabling the signal or not, fires stamped before and after them, the writer's removal, the admins removing each other; and `dispute`, the admins removing each other while toggling the setting. Every subset of a world's events is a held set. Properties under `Enabled`: every order judges every fire alike; a fire neither its author's authority nor the current setting makes valid is listed nowhere and not promoted (`promotes`), an enabled one is valid, a writer's ignores the setting (with a tally of the shapes reached); an older setting dropping changes no fire. Each fails on a control (`Arrival`; `Any`; `AtKey` twice).

## Overlapping sessions (`src/overlap.rs`, `tests/it/props_overlap.rs`)
`evaluate_with(&Held, &Overlap)` judges spec/commits.md §4's overlap (two session-form commits of one source whose ranges on an aspect intersect are void, whole but for `created.0`) by `Overlap::Held` (every applied commit counts, a void one counts for no other rule: `evaluate`'s default, as the engine), `Valid` (only deltas valid by every other rule, `Outcome::otherwise`, as a last layer no rule reads) or `Given(set)` (a probe); `Outcome::void`. `overlap::consistent` lists the void sets a rule reading verdicts could settle on; `rule_inputs` the session-form commits carrying addenda, settings or purges. Worlds: an admin X later removed, a writer W, a stranger; hand worlds (`removed_junk`, `stranger_junk`, `vouched_later`, `liar_setting`, `liar_addenda`) and random ones, every subset a held set. Under `Valid`: a function of the held set, junk moves no verdict, honest sessions never overlap, other sessions' plain writes never move X's (each with a control); it fails as a rule twice: held sets with no consistent verdict where rule inputs are session form, and a dot two commits claim has no owner for un-merge (issues/overlap-among-valid-commits.md).

## Signal edits (`src/edits.rs`, `tests/it/spec_edits.rs`)
`Part::replaces` is a signal edit's claim; `edits::listing(&Held)` is the signal's listing under the rule of notes/signal-edits-design.md (`Entry { place, version, shows }`), `edits::listed` the commits with a listed part. Each property runs on every subset of small worlds, smallest first.

## Thing-level history (`src/thing_history.rs`, `tests/it/spec_thing_history.rs`)
The judgment of spec/commits.md §5 The setting by the current setting: `out` (the moment a body replaced at `r` leaves history), `under_purge` (the entry purges on replace, or the body was written before the floor), `live_until`, `joined_floor` (the floor joined over every held setting), `written_after` (a writer writes the join), the oracle `purged` (replaced while the setting in force by stamp, over every setting written, purged: the past no node keeps), and, for the greenfield worlds, `history` and `Eager` (a node that drops at the first allowed moment and takes nothing back). `Rule` names the design and its negative controls (the current setting's floor alone; no floor; the floor against the dated replacement). `evaluate` judges every replaced body the same way (`Outcome::out`). On the functions, over every small world (settings chained or by concurrent admins) and random larger ones: purged content never shows at a node that learnt of the setting it was replaced under (holds it, or one whose writer knew it), whatever settings and bodies it holds and in any order (and does under every negative control), a carried join leaves the older settings adding nothing, kept while live, concurrent admins lose no floor. Through `evaluate` (`ev::`): bodies leave in replaced order, the current setting alone decides (a newer one re-shows a held body, a late older one changes nothing), purged content is never history however dated, a keep after purge on replace keeps what is written after it, who may purge changes no history, only valid settings are current and none judges validity, dropping shows a subset in any order.

## Deleting by link (`src/freed.rs`, `tests/it/spec_freed.rs`)
The freed judgment of notes/chunk-links.md: links carry Del (the writer's claim); a drop puts the body's links in the node's freed set; each link is judged when its delete is sent, against the sources of its thing the node holds then (reach needs every node blob on the way, held or fetched, else it waits): reached, it leaves the set; else an interior node's links join the set and the sync unit is deleted under each sync ID the node holds, signed by a key its Del names that the node holds; the peer accepts only a key the blob bears under the thing signed for. Properties over random worlds (reuse across authors and epochs, two sync IDs, forged cross-thing links, false Del, missing node blobs, drops and bodies held again interleaved with deletes): no delete of a sync unit a held source of its thing reaches (P1), every accepted delete borne by the blob under that thing (P3), and once settled a sync unit no node reaches is gone under each sync ID wherever a node that reached it, with nothing waiting, holds the role key and a key the blob bears that its honest links name (P2). Each property fails with its rule removed (no descent into freed nodes, no reach check, no thing binding at the peer).

## Copies cut per sync ID (`src/copy_cut.rs`, `tests/it/spec_copy_cut.rs`)
A greenfield model of spec/permissions.md §4 Copies: a reader of S holding S's cut of each certificate copy (the parts its readers read, closed over evidence) judges as with whole copies. Worlds: an account A of 2-4 nodes (grants by admins and may-grant readers, junk boxes, rotations, removals, the nodes' own keyset rotations, some held by the reader by another route), T minted with admin A as S's whole unit, X granting T read. The walk is cut down to what tells parts apart (an edge write applies by admin or as a may-grant grant; no lowering judged; one key role). `World::cut(Rule)`, `World::whole`, `judge` (`Seen`: bodies, T's blocked epochs, A's keysets, X's secrets taken, X's steps). Control: `NoPartEvidence`, failing a hand world. The reader holds none of A's boxes and judges A's epochs by order (grants and re-seals record nothing; a rotation's box records are for Foreign, not modelled). Findings: notes/certificates.md (The cut), issues/account-evidence-per-unit.md.

## The reference graph (`src/closure.rs`, `tests/it/spec_closure.rs`)
A greenfield model of the reference graph (spec/data-model.md §8, spec/sync-set.md), apart from `evaluate`: keys by role grants followed transitively (`World::keys_of`), sync units with no leaves (`sync unit`, `piece_seen`), carrying per sync ID with grants as role commits, per-sync-ID stores and delete keys (`Sync`, `Carry`; no stubs: `certify_revokes` stands in for them, carrying a removal to the holder's sync ID), the node closure walked sync unit by sync unit and the making rule (`closure`, `Walk`, `may_make`), release at a pass's end (`Net`, with ephemeral strongs and restarts) and the spanning-tree structure for removing a strong (`Tree`, counting work). Properties P1-P6, checks (a)-(d), counts and the hole found: notes/closure-model.md.

## First entry under stubs (`src/closure/entry.rs`, `tests/it/spec_entry.rs`)
A greenfield model of notes/stubs.md (rules: spec/sync-set.md §2): a grant is its target's data, a node finds things by references, and nothing from outside reaches a node but a held signal (a value). A unit is a thing's unit or a node's local part (its root's references); what it carries (`Net::carry`, rule 2) is judged from its own data alone: a strong to a thing its keys open brings it whole, a weak its stub, a reference at sync its stub, each holder on a chain by which its keys open a carried thing a stub; and only what some node reading it logged there, holding the thing's role entries (`Net::logged`, kept while the rule carries it: `settle`). A node reads its node thing, its local part and what its units carry whole, holds their stubs, and holds a signal's stub while the signal is held where it reaches it and opens its holder. `Rule::SPEC` is the design; each control breaks one property `check` states at every settled state (`readers agree`: `reader_view`; `no grant-driven entry`: `by_grants`; `siblings`: `PRIVATE`; `chains`, against the path oracle `on_paths`: `Chains::Up`; `signal lifetime`: `keep_fetched`) or `founded` (no signal and no history leave nothing held: `Fix::Greatest`). Default tests: hand worlds (pairing, an unsignalled grant, signal then reference then expiry, a signal alone, a weak, a two-hop chain and its removal, a public thing, a reference at sync) and 120 random histories (`history`) with each control failing. `Rule::SPEC` reads a strong a unit carries as a stub by the node's own role: whole at read, kept unread at sync (`Held::unread`), a stub below; osis-sync's differential takes its oracle from it (notes/test-properties.md, Reference-graph differential).

## Properties (`tests/it/props.rs`)
`settle` carries out the forced fates, writes the covers `me` owes, and (given the full history) fetches again what `uncovered` or `NeverReceived` names.
- The output is a function of the held set (any order of commits). The histories include every purge option, settings (late and invalid ones), undos (written by `undo::undo`), may-grant grants, `until`, self-removals, finalizes, link redemptions, paths through a listed thing with either purge author (node 2 through its account among them), parked parts and unknown records.
- Forced fates change no read and leave no forbidden value; each optional drop changes no read.
- Once a right holder has settled, it holds no body under purge on replace (but as an expired or purged leftover, or before a purge request's floor), no body is dropped before a purge request's floor but by purge, and `history()` shows no body the node may drop.
- A commit arriving after the node acted on the rest, and the same commits arriving one at a time in any order with the node acting as they come, read as one held from the start, once bodies whose cover or purge fell are fetched again; aspects where a part went at its expiry while waiting excepted (spec/requirements.md, Deterministic, its Limit). 2400 seeds pass.
- A signal's `history()` reads alike in both convergence properties above.
- The output is constant between `now` and its first deadline.
- Creations last read like creations first, once what was thrown out is fetched again. Destructions are repaired like any purge that falls: a destruction by purge keeps the thing's `roles.0` parts, and `settle` fetches the rest again.

## Adapter use
osis-node `tests/it/held.rs` maps what a node holds into `Held` and compares applied commits, validity, reads, the promise of purge on replace, bodies dropped as covered while in history or live before their live bound and `forbidden` on every content aspect (scope and normalizations in its module doc; bodies decoded from the engine's wire bytes); `order::held_set_decides` runs it on every node it judges (`Cfg::model`; also `held_model_agrees_*`), as the reference independent of the engine. The properties (tests/it/props.rs) run 30 seeds plus the rarer shapes' seeds in `_plain` twins and in `_large` twins from `OSIS_HELD_START` (default 0), `OSIS_HELD_SEEDS` many (300, 3000 under `OSIS_FULL=1`), a thread per core: `cargo test -p held-model --test it -- --ignored _plain _large`. Both are properties, not default tests: every held-model, crdt-model or roles-model gate selects them and nothing else can move them (this crate depends on no osis crate).

## Evidence per sync ID (`src/evidence.rs`, `tests/it/spec_evidence.rs`, `spec_edges.rs`, `spec_story.rs`)
A greenfield model of notes/write-path-design.md §2 (notes/evidence-model-findings.md): commits judged by roles-model `paths` (`Rules::fixed`, a pending layer on top: `World::pend`, spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable, a pending edge write applying nothing; a removal waiting for the creation its reset names, `World::creation`: the entry in force in its writer's walk), sync IDs with units given in time (`Ev::Join`, `Ev::Leave`), nodes syncing sync IDs (closed as a node closure is) and logging what their view requires (`World::req`: the evidence closure of a unit's bodies and of its chain stubs' role entries, the edge writes on a chain of read edges and records from the unit's thing to a thing of the unit; a removal only as evidence of a body or a chain stub's entry: lazy delivery), the thief logging its own (never an edge write giving below write or taking something away, nor what only such a withheld write brings), and readers holding one sync ID. A lowering's list is in the lowering: every copy carries it whole (`Lists::Whole`; the control `Lists::Unit`). A rotation's keyset (`World::rotate`) is a body carrying the demotions its writer holds, each whole: with `Rule::keysets` they are in the required set of every sync ID holding it, and a keyset pends without one (`p11_keysets` checks both, and that a rotator holds what it carries). A node logs only under sync IDs it can stamp (`Node::stamps`, `World::stamps_only`: the family's nodes stamp their own things and what they write); with `Rule::deposit` (D4, off by default) a node that cannot stamp deposits the removal copies its view requires (`d4_explore` by hand: the gap with and without, deposits never promoted). `Rule` names the spec's reading and its controls (`Placement`, `Lists`, `chain_stubs`, `commit_edges`, `records`, `keysets`, `deposit`); `Rule::fixed` is the spec's (E1: every held edge write on a stated path's edges and on the edges its commit sets, chain stubs on); `Rule::bodies_only` its control without chain stubs. **The reference** (`World::reference`): each reader is judged against a node holding everything written but the edge writes its sync ID lacks and no honest node syncing it holds (`undelivered`); where that node's verdict differs from the node holding everything is **the gap** (`World::gap`; issues/removed-key-writes-show-far.md), counted by the properties, never excluded silently. Checks per step: `p1` (P2 follows from it), `p4`, `p5_order`, `p8_promotions` (promotions on verdicts, `PromoteWhen`; a drop is at once), each against the reference; worlds: `family` (30,720 shapes of the stolen-node story over units, record edges on the route among them: `Shape::mint`), `random`, hand worlds (a split commit by `write_parts`); `cost` counts copies (P6). Default tests: a hand world per finding and named risk, the gap's hand worlds, a slice of the family. Properties: `one_sync_id_reader_judges_as_everything_plain` and `_large` (random seeds from `OSIS_HELD_START`; each prints its worlds with the gap): `cargo test -p held-model --test it -- --ignored one_sync_id_reader`. By hand: `explore` and `explore_random` print each control's failures and gap; `show_random` one world (`OSIS_EV_RULE` names the control).
Which edge writes are evidence (`spec_edges.rs`): every one on a used edge is a copy whoever wrote it (finding 15), the entry a reset names, finding 9 against the control `commit_edges` off, the key hop (findings 42, 43). `cost_with` counts copies with churn and sync IDs carrying a thing. `story_world` turns a roles-model `paths::Sim` into an evidence world under one of `STORY_UNITS` unit crossings (checked at each tick's end, `spec_story::story_steps`). Default test: one story world. Property `evidence_over_story_worlds_plain` and `_large`. By hand: `story_explore` (`OSIS_STORY_N`). Findings: notes/evidence-model-findings.md.
The joint property (`World::joint`, `Joint`; `spec_joint.rs`): a node acting as a signer, holding one sync ID plus the stubs of the holders on its paths (`stub`: the holder's role entries on the edges into it from the path, with their evidence; `joint_held`), judges S's bodies as S's reference or everything (`J-judge`), judges a content write it would make along any path valid iff the node holding everything does (`J-act`), and one it logs with its view's required set reads alike at S's reader (`J-logged`); controls `Joint::Unit`, `UnitLog`, `Grants`, `WorldPaths`. Default tests: a slice and hand worlds (`joint_a_removed_hop_reaches_the_actor_through_the_stub`, `joint_a_stub_carries_a_lowering_with_the_hop_it_undoes`). Property `joint_one_sync_id_and_chain_stubs_act_as_everything_plain` (`OSIS_JOINT_STEP`, `OSIS_JOINT_RANDOM`, `OSIS_HELD_START`).
How removals reach a sync ID (notes/write-path-design.md §2 D1 to D3: lazy delivery; `spec_reach.rs`, `spec_gaps.rs`): `Rule::records` (record edges in chains: on; off the control), `Rule::chain_stubs`; `spec_reach::deliveries` names the rules compared, `stubs` (`Rule::fixed`) and `bodies` (`Rule::bodies_only`), each judged against its reference with its gap counted (`spec_reach::gaps_under`). The reference follows the rule's own delivery, so a delivery rule that brings less (chain stubs off) shows as more gap, not as a failure: compare delivery rules by their gap.
