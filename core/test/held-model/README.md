# held-model
> Summary: Brute-force executable spec of what a node holding a set of commits reads and keeps (apply and waits, validity with actors and invites, undo, purge, expiry, history by the thing's `retention^.0` settings, signals); input format, input changes and how to call it.

Written from `spec/` alone, depending on no osis crate but crdt-model (the other independent reading, whose fragments are its values: join, prune, read, un-merge, strip, death, covering), so it is an independent reading to test the engine against. Every query recomputes from scratch (`evaluate` is a pure function of `Held`). AMBIGUITIES.md lists the choices the spec left; ASSUMPTIONS.md what is out of scope.

## Input (`src/types.rs`)
`Held { now, me, records, commits, dots }`:
- `now`: the node's wall clock (ms). `me`: the node (needed for "may I drop this").
- Ids: things, actors and nodes share one `u32` space.
- `dots`: which commit each write belongs to (spec/crdt.md §2.4): per session-form commit and aspect its source and counter range (`DotRange`); a commit no range names is commit form under `commit_source(id)` (`stamp(ts, id, ctr)` builds its stamps).
- `records`: the creation records the node holds apart from creations (key actors', a dependent's chain proofs), `Record { id, link }` with `RecordLink::Key` (a key actor: node, link key; admin of itself) or `RecordLink::Admin(a)` (its first admin). A held `created.0` body (`Delta::Created(link)` on `Aspect::CREATED`) gives its record too, once its author's and first admin's are known.
- `fetch.rs` models what is fetched and kept (notes/expiry-creation-design.md §3): labels, boxes that may be forged, reading-only keys, the fetch loop to quiescence.
- A thing **exists** while a creation of it joins, or, no held commit listing a creation of it, while its record alone is known (a record is identity). A part on a thing that does not exist, or whose author or chain record is unknown, is not held (`DropWhy::Unknown`); a commit holding nothing else is thrown out whole (`Outcome::revisit`, removed by `act`, for the caller to fetch again). A thing every joined creation of which is expired or purged **ends** (`Outcome::ended`): the ending is judged with its data held, then every part on it counts as never received in the whole judgment (`DropWhy::Ended`). Records outlive their things: dependents keep their chains. Waiting (`Keep(Pending)`) is left for a missing lead and parked parts.
- `commits`: `Commit { id, author, actor, ts, expires, kind, parts, purge, frontier, undoes }`. The header is held; each `Part { thing, aspect, retention, body, stripped }` has `Body::Held(Delta)`, `Body::Missing`, `Body::Dropped` or `Body::Parked` (bytes it cannot interpret).
  - `actor`: the header actor (the author when acting for itself). `undoes`: the undone commit.
  - `kind`: `Content`; `Role` (each part a `Delta::Edge(EdgeWrite { holder, role, invite, until })` on `Aspect::ROLES`, `role` None removes, `until` wall ms, `until` 0 a removal saying retain); `Snapshot` (writes are copies under their original stamps).
  - `stripped`: the commits whose values `act` stripped from the body (it holds its leftover).
  - Content and snapshot parts carry `Delta::Writes(Frag)`: a crdt-model fragment of any encoding (a write of another encoding than the aspect's others joins as spec/crdt.md §7 says), value-less writes as crdt-model holds them. A `retention^.0` part carries `Delta::Retention(Setting { rules, default })` on `Aspect::RETENTION` with `Retention::FIXED`: per aspect name a `SettingEntry { history, expires }` (`history` an `Opt`: `None`, `Keep { after, until }` (ms past replaced and/or wall ms, the later; `until` `NEVER` for good), `Purge`; `expires` the writer's expiry default, read by writers only). An `addenda.0` part carries `Delta::Addenda(Vec<(removal, listed commits)>)` on `Aspect::ADDENDA` (entries of a held body; a stripped one lists nothing).
  - `Aspect { name, admin_only, signal }`: `admin_only` is the `^` marker; `signal` the `~` marker (parts carry one root register write, never in a snapshot). `fixed()`: `roles.0`, `retention^.0`, `purge.0`, `addenda.0`, `created.0`: governed by no setting, never out of history, never covered.
  - `Retention { by }`: who may purge the write: `admin`, `author_or_admin`, `writer` for content; `nobody` is fixed retention's only. What replaced content is kept is the thing's setting, never the write's. `purge: Vec<PurgeItem>` (a commit's delta on an aspect, a whole aspect, or an author on an aspect); `frontier` on demotions.
- `Body::Missing`: never received or counting so; `Body::Dropped(by)`: dropped here as covered by snapshot commit `by`'s part on the aspect: it contributes nothing while the cover stands (valid, held or itself dropped covered) and is never received once it does not (`Outcome::uncovered`); its commit applied when it was held, so it counts as a held part that does not wait (AMBIGUITIES.md 40).

## Output (`src/model.rs`, `evaluate(&Held) -> Outcome`)
- `parked`, `applied`, `valid` (per part of an applied commit; role parts: whether the edge write applied), `purged` (part to request).
- `reads` per `(thing, aspect)`: the crdt-model read, None absent; `states`: the canonical fragment.
- Applied commits, reads and validity read creations as above; a creation is always valid (a record needs no authority).
- `settings`: per thing its held valid `retention^.0` settings by key (an admin-only delta at its key; one in an expiring commit is none): its timeline. `Outcome::out(thing, aspect, r)`: the moment a body replaced at wall ms `r` leaves history (`thing_history::out`, one way); `in_history`, `in_force`. No validity depends on a setting.
- `fates` per held or parked body: `Keep(why)` (`Until`: in history, or before a purge request's floor; `Plain`: uncovered, fixed retention), `Leftover(why)` (strip values), `MayDrop(why)` (`Covered`: out of history, any valid snapshot covers it, the right or not; `PastBound`: a signal body, whole or leftover, out of history), `MustDrop(why)` (`Covered`: under purge on replace, a valid snapshot whose commit holds the purge right covers it, at every node; `Gone`: a waiting part past its expiry or an unjoinable body expired or purged, which counts as never received), `NeverReceived`.
- Signals: nothing of a signal aspect joins (read `Absent`, empty state); a valid content body on one is replaced at its own `ts`, so `history()` shows its write while in history under the entry governing its AK (`signal_kept`; no entry, never) unless purged.
- `replaced`: held valid bodies holding a write the canonical state does not, with `replaced_at`: the wall ms of the earliest valid replacing write held (by its signed `ts`; a snapshot at the latest death among the writes no later held valid snapshot carries; None: nothing held dates it, not history); `history`: per aspect the replaced writes of bodies in history (`history()`, a function of the held set); `under_purge`: replaced value bodies the setting in force at their replaced-at puts under purge on replace; `owed`: those no valid right-holding snapshot covers while `me` holds the right (the cover `me` owes, `cover`); `uncovered`: dropped bodies whose cover (or purge) fell, to be fetched again.
- `deadlines`: every clock value after `now` at which the output may change with the held set unchanged (unparking, expiry, a body leaving history, a purge floor).
- `valueless`: deltas held value-less (expired, purged); `forbidden`: every value of theirs still held, with the body. An engine holding the same set must hold none of them once it has acted.

`Dots::new(&Held)` gives each write's commit (`commit`, `valueless`, `strip`, `values_of`). `act(&Held, &Outcome, may)` carries out the forced fates (strips, drops, makes the uncovered and never received missing, and one optional drop) and returns the next held set; `cover(&Held, &Outcome, thing, aspect, id, ts)` the covering snapshot `me` writes from its whole state (retention `writer`); `timeline(&Held)` the settled role timeline; `undo::undo(&Held, target, id, ts, author)` the undo commit node `me` writes (spec/crdt.md §4.6: each aspect written to read as without the undone commit, by crdt-model's diff, on the generator's aspects `gen::ty`); `gen::history(seed, n)` builds a random history (content through crdt-model's ops on a register, a map, a text, a set, a counter, a list and a struct holding a map; settings: things made with one, late ones, invalid ones; half the writers follow the setting they hold: its expiry default, no fire it would not keep) (writers cover what they owe most of the time), `gen::shuffle` reorders one.

## Input changes
For adapters written against the previous input (one change per line):
- Values are crdt-model fragments: `Delta::Writes(Frag)`, reads `Option<Value>`, states `Frag`, `Held::dots` for session-form commits; `Aspect` has no kind; `forbidden` names writes by `death::key`.
- Retention is `{ by }`: history is the thing's `retention^.0` (`Delta::Retention`), never per write. Every drop is as covered (`Body::Dropped(by)`), purge or expiry, and the state is the join of the held bodies.
- Apply follows spec/commits.md §4 as of whole-commits step 3: no atomic apply, no "counts as held" clauses; feed a part the node does not hold as `Body::Missing`, and `Body::Dropped(by)` only for a body the node itself dropped as covered (its commit applied).
- A waiting part of an applied commit that a request names is `Leftover(PendingPurged)`, not in `purged`.

## Signal edits (`src/edits.rs`, `tests/it/spec_edits.rs`)
`Part::replaces` is a signal edit's claim; `edits::listing(&Held)` is the signal's listing under the rule of notes/signal-edits-design.md (`Entry { place, version, shows }`), `edits::listed` the commits with a listed part. Each property runs on every subset of small worlds, smallest first.

## Thing-level history (`src/thing_history.rs`, `tests/it/spec_thing_history.rs`)
The judgment of spec/commits.md §5 The setting: `out` (the moment a body replaced at `r` leaves history under a timeline of settings), `in_force`, `purge_owed`, and, for the greenfield small worlds, `history` and `Eager` (a node that drops at the first allowed moment and takes nothing back). `evaluate` judges every replaced body by `out` (`Outcome::out`). Properties run over every timeline of up to three settings on the functions, and again (`ev::`) through `evaluate` on held sets of settings and register writes or fires: bodies leave in replaced order, a later setting never brings one back and acts from its stamp, a setting is in force from its own commit, a late setting can re-show a held body, purge-on-replace content is never history however late it is dated (and is, without the one-way rule: the counterexample is a test), who may purge changes no history, only valid settings are in force and none judges validity, dropping loses nothing when nothing is late and shows a subset in any order.

## Properties (`tests/it/props.rs`)
`settle` carries out the forced fates, writes the covers `me` owes, and (given the full history) fetches again what `uncovered` or `NeverReceived` names.
- The output is a function of the held set (any order of commits). The histories include every purge option, settings (late and invalid ones), undos (written by `undo::undo`), invites, `until`, self-removals, finalizes, link redemptions, acting for an account, parked parts and unknown records.
- Forced fates change no read (but a waiting part gone at its expiry or purge, which can make its commit apply) and leave no forbidden value; each optional drop changes no read.
- Once a right holder has settled, it holds no body under purge on replace (but as an expired or purged leftover, or before a purge request's floor), no body is dropped before a purge request's floor but by purge, and `history()` shows no body the node may drop.
- A commit arriving after the node acted on the rest, and the same commits arriving one at a time in any order with the node acting as they come, read as one held from the start, once bodies whose cover or purge fell are fetched again; aspects where a part went at its expiry while waiting excepted (spec/requirements.md, Deterministic limits). 2400 seeds pass.
- A signal's `history()` reads alike in both convergence properties above.
- The output is constant between `now` and its first deadline.
- Creations last read like creations first, once what was thrown out is fetched again. Endings are repaired like any purge that falls: an ending by purge keeps the thing's `roles.0` parts, and `settle` fetches the rest again.

## Adapter use
osis-node `tests/it/held.rs` maps what a node holds into `Held` and compares applied commits, validity, reads, the promise of purge on replace, bodies dropped before an absolute keep-at-least and `forbidden` on every content aspect (scope and normalizations in its module doc; bodies decoded from the engine's wire bytes); `order::held_set_decides` runs it on every node it judges (`Cfg::model`; also `held_model_agrees_*`), as the reference independent of the engine. Until thing-history step 3 the engine has per-write history and `required^.0` and writes no setting: the adapter feeds purgeable-by only, leaves out aspects whose validity a `required^.0` setting decides or whose `author` part a purge names, and judges the engine's per-write promises from each part's wire mode against the model's covers and forced fates (its module doc). The properties run 30 seeds in the default tests and in `_large` twins from `OSIS_HELD_START` (default 0), `OSIS_HELD_SEEDS` many (300, 3000 under `OSIS_FULL=1`), a thread per core: `cargo test -p held-model --test it -- --ignored _large`.
