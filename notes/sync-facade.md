# Sync: the engine's facade
> Summary: `osis_engine::sync` (core/engine/src/sync/), the one surface sync reads the engine through: its calls grouped by purpose, the memo and `Effects` mechanism that makes sync's work follow change, the engine's parking place for what arrives early (pending commits, loose records), the engine tables it keeps for sync (sync records, deliveries, passes, falls), what each derived value covers, and what is open.

Spec: spec/sync.md and its area files. Overview: notes/sync.md. The memo layer: notes/listener-design.md and issues/derived-state-recomputed.md.

**Principle:** the engine owns plaintext and every judgement of what a thing carries; sync owns ciphertext, connections and what peers showed. Sync reads the engine through this facade of memo-backed queries and a few commands; the engine calls nothing of sync's (no hook trait, no callback, no queue sync must drain). Sync never reads `Core`, `ix`, `Tx` or commit `Meta`. The engine does not depend on osis-proto: proof signing and checking are osis-proto's `Prover` and `verified`.

## Calls
Every value call is a memo (or reads stamped rows), so a module reading it inside its own `Effects` settle depends on exactly what the engine read. Calls that read the store return `Result`.
- **Ingest**: `open_entry(s, blob, wrap, bytes) -> Opened` (`Commit(id, Ingested)`, `Linked`, `Inbox`, `NotOpen`, `Bad`: opens the wrap with a held read secret, `open_commit` (each section's opening), a copy with content S's group actor may not read now refused whole (`copy_stale`, spec/permissions.md §3: `NotOpen`), the wrap's epoch as an opening of the header (`note_unwrapped`), then ingests; keeps `blob_parts`), `ingest_relay`, `wants` (pages and header lists before apply), `commit_feed`, `holds_commit`.
- **Bodies and facts**: `sync_bodies(c)` (per sync ID its thing's `thing_sync` reads, whatever lists the thing, whose required set holds a body of `c`: parts held or counting missing, expiry, order, copy or shared, header-only), `body_facts(s, c)`, `read_facts`, `strict_subset`, `neither`, `blob_parts`, `blob_commit`, `commit_blobs`, `blob_dropped`, `blob_copy`, `freed`, `fall_mark`/`body_fall_mark` (falls of held parts per aspect, durable; the stand compares marks), `own_commits(s)`, `own_certificates`, `promotable(s, c)`, `destructions`/`destruction(t)`/`forget_destroyed(t)` (secrets dropped last).
- **Sealing** (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, built in `labeling::opened`; this is its one sync caller): `seal_body(s, c, held, rng)` is the only fresh encryption of a body: a certificate or per-sync-ID copy by `seal_to`, else `Engine::seal` with the sections of every held genuine blob of `c` (`held`: blobs the store holds with an entry's wrap; only shared blobs opened or sealed here count, never a copy's), and the wrap always `wrap_secret(S's thing, Some((c, parts carried)))`; `None` makes nothing now. `rewrap` (a held blob logged verbatim under another sync ID) wraps by the same `wrap_secret`. `wrap_secret` gives nothing under a read epoch blocked here (spec/crypto.md §4 Encrypting under; `wrap_blocked`: `Walk::Place` for a role commit, which goes as a certificate or the node's own, else `Walk::Encrypt`; a rotation's own wrap excepted): sync logs it once a rotation covers the departure. Cost: one blocked-epoch walk per wrap (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md, clock-relative asks). Rejected: sealing each section under the epoch it opened under (a per-commit epoch table): safe too (Reseal.tla), but a second rule beside `passes_on`.
- **The sync set**: `sync_things()` (the synced group's closure plus destroyed things being emptied) and `thing_sync(t) -> ThingSync { Read, Unread, Unsyncable, Plain }` per thing, for `Effects` (`osis_sync::set::SetFollow`, which `leave` alone follows); `sync_set()` their whole fold, for listing; `sync_holder(s)` (S's thing and how the node's keys hold S, whatever lists the thing: what a leaving S is held as while it waits), `sync_ids(t)` (`OwnedSyncId { id, path, hint }`), `sync_owner(s)`. Read exactly while a read secret of the thing is held.
- **Keys**: `rotations_superseding(s, e)` (the rotation commits superseding read epoch e of S's thing: an ended member's `until`), `read_credentials(s)` (recorded path's head, role keys per recorded step, read secrets, statics, `roster` digest, `proofs` deepest first, `acting`), `claim_key`, `signers(s, actor)`, `open_grant`/`learn_grant`, `step_needs`, `due_step(s)` (the step due here, with the rotations it registers) and the command `issue_due_step(s)`. `read_credentials` also carries `sync_keys`, every held sync secret's key newest first, which the walk tests after the recorded steps.
- **Peers**: `judge_peer(s, proven) -> J` (`Reader`, `SyncHolder`, `Departed`, `Unknown`), `covers_departures`, `provers` (proof order: node key, account at its open epoch, key actors by tid), `own_node`, `own_node_key`, `key_open`, `prove_rotations`/`take_rotations` (not built).
- **Records and inbox**: `sync_records`/`sync_record(t)`/`sync_record_feed`, `take_inbox(s, blob, accepted)`, `take_inbox_blob`, `invitations`, `inbox_of`, `inbox_blob`, `deliveries`/`delivery_feed`/`delivery_taken`/`delivery_names`/`delivered`, `logged_with(c)`.
- **Status**: `carrying(t)` (the sync IDs held carrying t: its own and its enclosing groups'), `waiting_held_elsewhere`/`held_elsewhere(c)` (a self-removal's secrets drop once held elsewhere), `passes_wanted`, `pass_clock`, `note_complete_pass`, `forget_read`, `history_complete`.
- **Dials**: `dial_peers(s)`, `profile_of(a)`, `publish_reach`, `publishes_reach`.
- **Time and config**: `next_timer`, `tick`, `set_now`, `clock_next`, `caps()` (`caps^.0` of this node).

## Effects: work in proportion to change
`osis_base::memo::Effects<K, V>` turns a memo into work in proportion to change: `want(k)` follows a key, `due()` lists followed keys whose last value read a stamp written since, `settle(k, f)` re-runs `f`, records what it read, and returns `Some((was, now))` when the value differs. Each `Stamps` keeps a log of keys written since its oldest live mark, so `due` costs the writes since the last call times their readers, never the keys followed. A `Feed` is one reader's cursor over an index's own log (`Stamps::feed`): nobody drains it and nothing writes it by hand, which is what separates it from a take-queue. Time is the memo clock: a value valid until `t` reads `until(Some(t))`; `Clock::next` gives the driver its wake.

A write on one aspect moves only that aspect's commits' stamps, so only their verdicts are judged again; a `group.0` change moves `sync_bodies` of the commits on the things that joined or left (adding a subtree costs its rows, a removal derives the set again, O(closure)). Effects' acted values are not durable, so a start settles every key once.

Some facade values are derived from walks whose reads move far more often than the value: each read thing's certificates, the things each actor publishes, a commit's bodies. (What the node syncs per sync ID is the store's `synced`, written by leave and followed per key, notes/sync-leave.md.) Each is kept per key, followed through `Effects`, so a change does work in proportion to what it moved: `sync::certified` keeps each read thing's certificates as pieces (`Piece`: the thing's own, an author's chain to a closure thing, an inbox actor's grant copy per thing it is named on), each derived alone and placed under every read thing it goes to, with rows per (commit, read thing) and records per read thing; which pieces go where is followed per key too (the read things, each one's inbox actors per sync ID, each thing with authors), new keys from feeds (`Indexes::authors_on`, `Held::pairs_feed`), never a walk of everything. `labeling::published` follows each labeled thing's publisher, read per (actor, thing) by a body judgement (`PublishedView`), whole by the label walk; a commit's candidate things (`body_things`) ask the same `Engine::publisher` (an account counts as its D, spec/sync-ids.md §2). A judgement asks its own pair: `sync_body(s, c)` (per sync ID: the closure body of S's thing, else its certificate row), and read keeps each (S, commit)'s body as its `bodies` effect last settled it (`Mine::meta`), so a body coming at one sync ID (a new read thing naming the account's card) re-judges nothing at another. Each refreshes when asked, and `Engine::follow` refreshes all of them: the driver calls it before each module's settle and settles again while it moved anything, since a module's `due` sees an entry move only once it is refreshed. Counted at n and 10n: osis-sync read.rs `an_event_on_one_sync_id_judges_what_it_moved` (commits under other sync IDs), `a_thing_published_under_an_actor_judges_what_it_moved_spec_sync_2`; engine facade `a_new_grant_copy_lists_in_proportion_to_change_spec_sync_inbox_5`, `an_account_named_on_many_things_derives_in_proportion_to_change_spec_sync_inbox_5`; kept equal to the whole derivation (`Engine::sync_certificates`): `sync::certified::tests`.

## Pending: the engine's parking place
The rule is notes/sync.md Arriving early. In the engine the parking place is `Indexes::pend_on` (`apply::waits::Wake`), written only by `set_pending` beside `pending` (journaled, rebuilt at load as `Wake::Open`): `Record(t)`, `Moved` (reach, actor secrets, epoch), `Commit(rotation)`, `Blobs`, `At(t)` (drift, expiry; `next_timer` names the first), `Thing(t)` (a purge or expiry on a thing it writes), `Open`. `reexamine` examines `wake`'s set only (`examined()` counts it); `blob_wants` reads `Blobs` alone. spec/commits.md §4 (Examined again) lists the wakes; its Limits name the coarse one (`Moved`: every role change). A pending commit awaiting several records parks under each unknown one and, if any is held (a reach), under `Moved`. An expiry already passed parks nothing (else `next_timer` stays due). The `order` harness checks after every step that a full scan applies nothing the wakes left (`Engine::lost_wakes`).

Records are the same rule one level down: a record from a record blob nothing here awaits is loose (`waits::Loose`, in memory), carried by its blob's store entry under the peer budget, learned when a `Record(t)` park or slot names it (`promote_loose`, with the loose records up its chain), and forgotten with its last carrier (`forget_carrier`). `carries_loose(b)` keeps the entry; `loose_feed` tells the store a carrier now gives something held (uncharge). A start opens the carriers again (the store parks them as entries that did not open), so nothing durable is kept for a record nobody named. An inbox payload's records are learned only where its chain or commit awaits them (`take_inbox` ingests them, learns, ingests again).

## Certificate bodies
`sync_bodies` adds, per read S carrying no closure body of the commit, the held parts its certificates name (`Engine::sync_certificates`, kept per piece by `sync::certified`: this node's own `certificates`; every node's chain for S's thing, which each syncing node logs (`Timelines::certificates_into` the thing: what ever reaches it); each author's chain to the readable closure things it wrote on (`Indexes::authors_on`; node authors left out); under D's home every card commit of D's other nodes; and under an actor's inbox its grant copies, `grant_copy` per (actor, thing named on): the newest applied edge write naming the actor there, projected as its inbox blob, spec/sync-inbox.md §5. Chains are read by what they are made of (`Timelines::certificates`: the edge writes and records of each thing that ever reaches the target, `Read::Ever`), never the whole component). Not every chain into every closure item: that logged a private group's roster holding a role on an item under S, which no S reader needs (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates, last case) less what the closure's bodies carry, as a copy (`copy` and `certificate` true), or a named header as a header-only copy. Only a commit with an edge, keys, card or `created.0` part or a request asks, so content never reads the certificates; read through `sync::certified`'s rows for the commit, a role change moves the bodies of the commits whose naming it changed. One body per (S, commit): where S carries a closure body, a certificate part on another thing of the same commit is not added (simplest reading of spec/sync-judgement.md §1, which names one blob per commit). `seal_body` seals a certificate as `seal_to` (one section under the blob key) wrapped by `wrap_secret` for its parts; `rewrap` shares a certificate copy of the same parts across sync IDs, only from a sync ID holding it as a certificate of the same parts too (a per-sync-ID copy is never shared).

Record blobs (`sync::recblobs`): `certificate_records(s)` names the records S's certificates need that no closure body carries; read's `records` effect per S logs one fresh record blob (`seal_records`, wrapped by `wrap_secret`) of those no record blob in the own store carries; `record_blobs(s)` (rows `rb/`, durable, one per record blob this node sealed while its own entry stays: read reports a gone one, `forget_record_blob`; all go with S at `forget_read`) is what each carries; read logs only while a reader can pull S (`pullable` is read inside the effect, so S is due again once one can); `open_entry` takes a record blob's records (`Opened::Records`; those nothing awaits stay loose, Pending above), and streams keeps a pulled one only as the carrier of a loose record. Read never judges a record blob (it names no commit), so it is never dropped or deleted but with S.

## Engine tables kept for sync
- **Synced group**: `synced^.0` on D maps each node to its synced group; the account's creation writes the first node's (one commit after the role commit); before a node reads its entry its root is D. `Engine::pair` writes the new node's (a copy of the pairing node's synced group's items and subgroups, with D) unless `synced^.0` already lists one for it still under home (D's closure holds it, its closure holds D: a re-sealed stranded node keeps its own; one cut from D gets a new one, the spec's "until it is paired again"), and sends it last in the reply, after the grant that lets the new node read it; `synced_group_in` makes both. Two nodes pairing one node at once each write a group: `synced^.0`'s entry converges on one, the other stays an unused item of D.
- **Sync records** (`sync/records.rs`, spec/sync.md §3): the account's record is `kept^.0` on D (`Indexes::sync_kept`, derived from D's state by `records::observe`: `Tx::derive`, and `observe_own` at a load, a reload and a pairing, once D is known). The offers held (`io/`, `Indexes::offers`/`offered`) give held secrets and dial hints. `sync_records` is the view sync reads: kept sync IDs, recorded and offered secrets (`SyncRecord::recorded`), the dial hint of an offer whose sync IDs are kept; `refresh` rebuilds a thing's view when either source moves (a `kept^.0` change decodes the whole map: O(things kept), rare writes). Written (`keep_in`, a commit on D) by an accept (sync IDs), a lowering from read (its own `sync.0` reading and sync secrets), `record_borne` (sync's unread judgement: a held secret a step of its path bears) and `learn_secret` (an entry box's sync secret, beside a kept sync ID). A kept sync ID is still the sender's word for which thing owns it: `thing_sync` leaves out one a held `sync.0` gives another thing.
- **Deliveries** (rows `ow/`): a flow owes `(commit, holder)` in the commit's transaction (which holders a rotation reaches depends on boxes before it, which the commit does not show); `inbox_blob` derives the rest when sealing (parts, things with sync IDs and delete keys, the author's chain plus the boxed things' `keys.0` for a reader, this node's `reach.0` as dial hint). A node holder is sent the commit itself. A row lives until sync calls `delivered` or its commit is forgotten. Untaken inbox blobs carrying a commit: rows `iw/`, listed by `invitations()`; one the user accepted here keeps its row (`accepted`) until it expires, not listed. Offers (`io/`) list a thing whose offered sync ID is not kept, wherever listed. `Engine::write` marks what an app write acts on as an invitation (`acted^.0` on D, `sync::inbox::mark_acted`): only from the group index's journal of that transaction, so a write changing no listing pays nothing; a mark already there adds no commit (an unchanged set writes none). Bound: one tid per thing ever acted on, never removed (a destroyed thing's stays); it grows only with writes that file or unfile things, never per session.
- **Passes** (rows `cp/`): the last complete pass per sync ID read here; `forget_read` when S stops being read (with S's record blob rows), and an open drops those of every S not read, so an S read again wants a pass of its own. `passes_wanted()`: per read sync ID a want at 0 (history complete), at the newest role commit on S's thing's reader actor from elsewhere (spec/permissions.md §3), and at the newest role commit of G or T for every sync ID of a dead tie's demotion scope this node would remove explicitly (spec/permissions.md §2); met by a noted pass with `began >= since`.
- **Others**: falls (`fm/`), held-elsewhere waits (`hw/`), blob parts (`bp/`) by commit (they go with their commit, memory rows for a memory commit), inbox hints (`ih/`: the sync ID a taken inbox blob named for a thing no applied `sync.0` gives one, followed through `sync_ids` with `hint` while the blob is unexpired, spec/sync-inbox.md §2). `InboxOpened::hints` is always empty: the hint goes through these rows.
- **Dial peers**: per S, this node's other nodes whose synced group has S's thing in its closure, at their `reach.0`; of S read, every account holding a role on S's thing's reader actor (other than its own), at its profile's `dial^.0` entries; of S kept unread, the dial hint of an offer held whose sync IDs are kept (`SyncRecord::dial`). `profile_of(a)` is the thing created in `a`'s creating commit whose first admin is `a`, other than its devices actor, so a co-writer of another of `a`'s things cannot plant a dial list. `publish_reach` writes `reach.0` and the profile's `dial^.0` entry, each only where changed; osis calls it after each listen that succeeded and at `rotate_peer_address`. Every node lists itself on the profile (`dial::lists_on_profile`): issues/sync-tess-calls.md.
