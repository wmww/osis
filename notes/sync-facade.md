# Sync: the engine's facade
> Summary: `osis_engine::sync` (core/engine/src/sync/), the one surface sync reads the engine through: its calls grouped by purpose, how sync's work follows change through it, the engine's parking place for what arrives early (pending commits, blob and path waits, loose records), certificate bodies and blobs, the engine tables kept for sync (passes, falls, dial peers), and the sync set rows.

Spec: spec/sync.md and its area files. Overview: notes/sync.md. The memo layer: notes/engine.md, Derived values.

**Principle** (notes/sync.md): the engine owns plaintext and judgement, sync ciphertext and peers; sync reads the engine only here, and the engine calls nothing of sync's (no hook trait, no callback, no queue sync must drain). Sync never reads `Core`, `ix`, `Tx` or commit `Meta`. The engine does not depend on osis-proto: proof signing and checking are osis-proto's `Prover` and `verified`.

## Calls
Every value call is a memo or reads stamped rows (`sync/mod.rs`), so a module reading it inside its own `Effects` settle depends on exactly what the engine read. Rustdoc has each call's contract; the map by purpose:
- **Ingest**: `open_entry` opens the wrap with a held read secret, then `open_commit`; a copy with content S's actor may not read now is refused whole (`copy_stale`, notes/engine-lost-access.md Copies); the wrap's epoch counts as an opening of the header (`note_unwrapped`); an ephemeral commit ingests by `ingest_memory` within `room`. Also `memory_woken_feed`/`apply_waiting_memory`, `memory_charge`, `take_relays`/`open_relay` (notes/ephemeral.md In osis-sync), `wants_blob`, `commit_feed`, `holds_commit`.
- **Bodies and facts**: `sync_bodies(c)`, `body_facts`, `read_facts` and `held_fate` (`memo::Cutoff` rows, `unfollow_facts`/`unfollow_fate`), `neither`, `blob_parts`, `blob_commit`, `commit_blobs`, `blob_gone`/`strip_of`/`blob_loses` (notes/whole-commits.md Strip), `freed`, `fall_mark`/`body_fall_mark` (durable falls of held parts per aspect; the stand compares marks), `own_commits`, `covered_drops`/`drop_body`, `forgettable`/`forget_commits`, `promotable`.
- **Sealing** (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on; the rule and its invariant are `sync_ids/opened.rs`'s doc; this is its one sync caller):
  - `seal_body(s, c, held, rng)` is the only fresh encryption of a body: a certificate or per-sync-ID copy by `seal_to`, else `Engine::seal` with the sections of every held genuine blob of `c` (only shared blobs opened or sealed here count, never a copy's), wrapped by `wrap_secret(S's thing, Some((c, parts carried)))`. `rewrap` (a held blob logged verbatim under another sync ID) wraps the same way.
  - `may_log(s, c, held)` (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Loggable) is what read's must-hold reads, as `cert_wrap` is for certificate blobs: true for a held blob `rewrap` would pass on, else a fresh encryption `seal_body` would make (each body part sealed by `section_secret`, the per-section rule `seal` uses). A section carries no header, so the header's gate `header_passes` is the wrap's alone, and a commit on n things is judged in O(n) (osis `commits::commit_work_linear_in_things_spec_requirements_decentralization`).
  - `held` is the blobs whose bytes the sync store holds under an entry of a sync ID carrying a body of `c` (`Live::held_blobs`): an engine row of a blob whose bytes left the store counts for nothing; a held blob tombstoned under S counts. Approximation: parts of a held section this node did not open are left out of the parts `wrap_secret` is asked for (they pass `passes_on` trivially; only a rotation's unopened `secrets.0` would change the wrap epoch).
  - `wrap_secret` gives nothing under a read epoch blocked here (spec/crypto.md §4 Encrypting under; `wrap_blocked`; a rotation's own wrap excepted): sync logs it once a rotation covers the departure. It reads the clock up to the next `until` or role commit in the components of S's actor and the node's account (`Core::bound_to_horizon`), so its readers wake there. Cost: one blocked-epoch walk and one horizon scan per wrap. Rejected: sealing each section under the epoch it opened under (a per-commit epoch table): safe too (Reseal.tla), but a second rule beside `passes_on`.
- **The sync set**: `sync_things`, `thing_sync(t)`, `in_sync_things(t)`, `things_entered` (Sync set rows below; the walk: notes/node-closure.md).
- **Keys**: `rotations_superseding`, `read_credentials(s)` (a memo per sync ID compared by public halves, as each key is a curve multiplication), `claim_key`, `signers`, `open_grant`/`learn_grant`, `step_needs`, `due_step(s)` and the command `issue_due_step(s)`.
- **Peers**: `judge_peer(s, proven) -> J`, `covers_departures`, `provers` (proof order: node key, account at its open epoch, key actors by tid), `own_node`, `own_node_key`, `key_open`, `prove_rotations`/`take_rotations` (notes/sync-session.md Proving again).
- **Stranded** (`sync/stranded.rs`, whose doc holds the rules; notes/sync-inbox.md `stranded`).
- **Suspect flags** (`retention/suspect.rs`, notes/engine-suspects.md): `suspect`, `suspect_feed`, `state_digest`, `suspect_keys`, `record_known`; repair: `reader_account`, `suspect_under`, `may_repair`, `repair_suspect`.
- **Tickets and the inbox**: `kept_sync_secrets(s)`, `ticket_for(t)`, `inbox_thing(a)`, `logged_with(c)`.
- **Status**: `carrying(t)`, `waiting_removals`/`removal_done(c)` (notes/engine-lost-access.md Self-removal drops), `passes_wanted`, `begin_pass`/`pass_place`, `note_complete_pass`, `forget_read`, `history_complete`.
- **Duties** (`osis_sync::life`): `role_things`, `named_a_holder(t)`, `joined_link_ended(k)`/`leave_link(k)`.
- **Dials**: `dial_peers(s)`, `profile_of(a)`, `publish_reach`, `publishes_reach` (Dial peers below).
- **Time and config**: `next_timer`, `tick`, `set_now`, `clock_next`, `caps`.

## Effects: work in proportion to change
Sync's modules follow facade values with `Effects` (notes/engine.md, `Effects<K, V>`); time is the memo clock, `Clock::next` the driver's wake. Acted values are not durable, so a start settles every key once. A write on one aspect moves only that aspect's commits' stamps, so only their verdicts are judged again; a change of a sync unit moves `sync_bodies` of the commits on the things that joined or left it.

Values derived from walks whose reads move far more often than the value are kept per key (notes/engine.md, Per-key rows):
- `sync::certified`: each read thing's certificates as pieces (Certificate bodies below), its module doc says how; kept equal to the whole derivation (`Engine::sync_certificates`): `sync::certified::tests`.
- `sync_body_now(s, c)`: per sync ID, the closure body of S's thing joined with its certificate row; read keeps each (S, commit)'s body as its `bodies` effect last settled it (`Mine::meta`), so a body coming at one sync ID re-judges nothing at another.
- `wrap_blocked` per (actor, walk) (`Core::wrap_blocks`), so a role change re-judges only the bodies whose wrap it blocks or unblocks.
- What the node syncs per sync ID is the store's `synced`, written by leave (notes/sync-leave.md).

`Engine::follow` refreshes them all once per settle round (notes/sync-driver.md). Following needs the whole engine, so it never runs inside a transaction (a `Tx` holds the `Core` alone). Counted at n and 10n: osis-sync `read::an_event_on_one_sync_id_judges_what_it_moved`.

## Pending: the engine's parking place
One parking place keyed by the missing thing. The rule is notes/sync.md Arriving early; the wakes are spec/commits.md §4 Examined again. In the engine it is `Indexes::pend_on` (`apply::waits::Wake`, its variants documented there), written only by `set_pending` beside `pending` (journaled, rebuilt at load as `Wake::Open`). `reexamine` examines `wake`'s set only (`examined` counts it), and the commits whose blob waits ended. A commit awaiting several records parks under each unknown one and, if any is held, under `Moved` (`Waits::records_of`). An applied commit's waiting parts park the same way in its pending copy, so `pends(c)` keeps their carrier. An expiry already passed parks nothing (else `next_timer` stays due). The `order` harness checks after every step that a full scan applies nothing the wakes left (`Engine::lost_wakes`; it sees only what its histories make).
- **Failures reload.** `wake` takes what woke outside the round's transaction, as other in-memory due lists are taken. So after any failed store write, and after a transaction acting on what was taken fails for any reason (`Engine::run_taken`: a `reexamine` round, `promote_loose`), the next transaction reads the store again as an open does (`Engine::reload_owed`; `next_timer` is now while owed; node-sync reloads at once, `engine_reloaded`): every pending commit examined, every due list an open derives rebuilt. A reload carries over what no open derives: `waits`, the compaction trigger, `fallen`. Tests: lib `apply::tests_failed_writes`, waits `a_failed_write_in_a_waking_arrival_leaves_the_wait_due_spec_commits_4`, compact `a_failed_write_in_a_step_leaves_its_aspects_due_spec_sync_7`.

**Blob waits** (`apply/reach.rs`, whose doc states the principle and the O(P) invariant). `WaitBlobs` parks under no `Wake`: each waiting slot (`reach::Slot`) keeps its walk's frontier in memory (what it lacks and the trees that lies in, nothing of what it reached and holds: W commits over one held tree cost W frontiers, `commits_over_one_held_tree_keep_only_what_they_lack_spec_commits_4`), indexed blob → (commit, slot) (`Walks::wanted`); a slot is kept only while it lacks a blob. A pass reaches each blob once but knows nothing of earlier passes: only a blob linked twice in one delta is reached again (a page so linked is refused by the decoders; a value's node is a held check).
- An arrival: every blob newly held is noted (`Walks::held`: by `Tx::store_blobs`, and for every hold of any path, own writes included, from the `Cipher`'s holds as the transaction commits); a commit about to be judged takes its noted walks on first (`reach::take_on`, so a copy arriving with its pages sees them), and each `reexamine` round takes the rest on (`reach::round`, `advance`: O(the blob's links)). A commit is examined only when a slot of it came to lack nothing.
- `examine` gates per slot: header lists whose walk lacks a blob are not resolved (the signature is checked first); a part whose walk lacks one is set aside without a decode, so a commit woken by a record or epoch decodes only the parts that can apply. A part judged waiting for pages gets its walk whole there (`judged_waiting`); a slot judged otherwise loses its walk.
- A walk's tree refused (`LinkTree`) ends it and every position nested in its leaves (`refuse`), so what a walk lacks is one set whatever order its pages came in; the judge then refuses the part, but only once its other positions lack nothing: the decoders stop at the first position missing a page, so refusing earlier would need them to go on past a missing position. A durable part waits for pages only (a large value's root standing alone is fetched lazily, spec/sync-streams.md §5); a memory commit's roots are waited for (its gate needs them). A root in a list of links reads as a page until held.
- Cost: P pages cost O(P) walk steps, one checked decode and one signature check per commit, whatever the server's batching or order (`tests_paged::a_trickled_fill_reads_each_page_a_bounded_number_of_times_spec_commits_4`: 442 pages one per delivery, 884 pages read, 442 walk steps, one examination; `a_blob_costs_only_the_waits_that_want_it_spec_commits_4`, `commits_sharing_pages_are_each_woken_once_per_page_spec_commits_4`, `a_refusing_page_refuses_as_it_is_held_spec_wire_6`).
- Re-derived, never journaled (an undo copy would cost O(walk) per arrival): an open or reload starts with none (`Wake::Open` derives the slots); a failed step or transaction drops the walks of the commits it touched (`reach::rolled_back`) and the next round examines those still here again (`Walks::lost`); one the rollback took away is due nowhere, else a transaction refused each time (a sealed fire) keeps `next_timer` at now (`a_failed_transaction_leaves_no_wait_due_for_what_it_brought`); a commit that stops pending loses its slots at its transaction's end (`reach::settle`), a forgotten one all (`unindex`). `next_timer` is now while a round has work (`Walks::due`). Test: `a_fill_goes_on_past_failed_writes_rounds_and_reloads_spec_commits_4`.
- Debug checks: `Engine::check_walks` after every durable transaction (no unnoted arrival, the index exactly what kept walks lack, a kept slot's commit pends); at every engine drop each walk against one derived afresh.
- Sync asks `wants_blob(b)` per entry it opens (O(log)), never the whole set (`blob_wants`, for tests).

Each wait's inputs and who moves them (a wake missing here is data that appears only at a restart):
- `MissingChain`/`UnknownCreated`, and the records a part awaits: every record and edge write goes through `Held` (`create`, `record_back`, the role commit writers, `set_counted`), which notes `records` (`Record(t)`) or `moved` (`Moved`); `forget_record` only makes things wait.
- `Outside(t)`: the node closure reading `t` (`wake_entered` at every transaction's end, from its member feed) or a kept copy, which is kept only at a loss of a thing the closure read, so it never turns `whole` on alone.
- `Drift`, an expiry: the clock (`At`).
- `WaitBlobs`: Blob waits above.
- `Epoch(e)`: the account chain holding `e` (`account::refresh_epochs`, `epochs_held`), and `Moved`.
- `Parked`, `Unexamined`: an open (`open_due`).
- Every stored copy: what is value-less on a thing it writes (`persist_valueless`, `purge::apply_request`: `Thing(t)`).
- A path waiting (an applied split commit with a thing whose path entry opens nowhere here; its parts invalid until it opens): `apply::paths::Wait`, whose doc says what moves it; `paths::refresh` at each `rejudge_all` pass, before the transaction end's last re-judge (so every write of an opener wakes its waiters by its transaction's end, whichever step made it) and at load settles only the commits whose openers moved. Tests: engine `validity::a_path_waiting_opens_when_its_roles_secret_comes_spec_commits_4`, `a_path_waiting_opens_when_a_pending_commit_gives_its_roles_secret_spec_commits_4`, `split_commits_waiting_on_a_path_cost_an_unrelated_write_nothing_spec_commits_4` (k = 1, 10: none settled), `split_paths_judge_alike_however_parts_come_spec_wire_4`.
- A box the reading-only budget refused: room in the budget, read at every `wake` (room freed with no arrival or tick waits for the next).

Named tests per wait: waits `a_record_examines_only_what_awaits_it_*`, `a_reach_wakes_a_commit_awaiting_a_record_too_*`, `drift_work`, `each_waiting_part_joins_at_its_own_record_*`, `pages_away_hold_back_only_their_part_*`; outside.rs; account_commits `account_commit_signed_at_its_epoch_*`. Owed (issues/sync-tests-owed.md): one small world per wait and waking event asserting `lost_wakes` empty, and a world for a refused box freed by room.

Records one level down: a record from a record blob nothing here awaits is loose (`waits::Loose`, in memory), carried by its blob's store entry under the peer budget, learned when a `Record(t)` park or slot names it (`promote_loose`, with the loose records up its chain), forgotten with its last carrier (`forget_carrier`). `carries_loose(b)` keeps the entry; `loose_feed` tells the store a carrier now gives something held (uncharge). A list blob whose rotation is neither applied nor pending is kept loose the same way (`Loose::keep_lists`, unverified); every durable path taking that rotation verifies and holds them in its own transaction (`apply::hold_rotation_lists`), so what they name never reads unlisted. A list blob of a held or pending rotation is verified and held at once (`hold_lists`). A start opens the carriers again, so nothing durable is kept for a record nobody named.

## Certificate bodies
`sync_bodies` adds, per read S, the held parts its certificates name (`Engine::sync_certificates`) less what the closure's bodies carry, as a copy, or a named header as a header-only copy. Only a commit with an edge, keys, card or `created.0` part or a request asks, so content never reads the certificates.

**Principle: every piece is the same at every node judging S** (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): a node-specific required set lets two honest nodes delete each other's copies, each logging again at every change of ground. The pieces:
- `Piece::Own` (`own_piece`; never this node's own `certificates`, which pairing alone uses): S's thing's record chain, destroyed things, key actors' cards (accounts with their chain); every holder's chain for S's thing; under D every card commit of D's other nodes.
- `Piece::Chain` per (author, target): the author's chain of each held commit on a readable closure thing (`Indexes::chains_on`: to the thing and, acting for another thing, to that thing: everything the commit's validity reads; node authors left out), however many things place it under S (`Certified::chain_by`). Chains are read by what they are made of, never the whole component. Rejected: every chain into every closure item (it logs a private thing's roster holding a role on a thing under S, which no S reader needs: spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates).
- `Piece::Account(p)` (`account_piece`): an account on a chain brings its record, card, current keys and every held line of its signing chain, so each writer's rotations are named at every node. Own and Chain pieces defer it (`Certs::deferring`: `cert_parts` names the account in `Certs::accounts` instead of reading its chain), and `Certified` places the account piece under each read thing while a piece there names it (`account_naming`). A rotation re-derives that one piece, not every piece naming the account (no count test checks it: issues/sync-tests-owed.md).
- `Piece::Holder(t, x)`: role changes travel as holder t's piece for each x an edge write names t holder on, under t's own sync ID (`certified::holder_own_on`): t's chain to x (which carries x's keys), x's `secrets.0` parts boxing to t and x's `sync.0` parts (`holder_piece`), so a reader of t holds the grant and x's sync ID with no ticket and no write to t. For this node's account the piece goes under its root D for each x a vertex of D's unit enters by a strong (`Closure::reaches_from`), without the chain. Why under D: otherwise a node paired later holds only D's ticket for x (a read secret of x's current epoch), while the commits giving x's epochs are wrapped under epochs the account took by its inbox's ticket fires (spec/crypto.md §5), so it opens neither x's sync secret nor its history (sync `flows::a_node_paired_after_the_fires_expire_holds_its_siblings_secrets_spec_sync_judgement_2`; `holding::a_reader_by_a_chain_through_a_holder_thing_reads_spec_sync_judgement_2`).
  - A granter that does not sync t fires its ticket on t sealed, the grant carrying the same piece, offered where an account holding a role on t is proven (`inbox::fire_to`); t's readers take it as they open the fire (`Engine::holder_grant`) and log it under t (`holding::a_grant_to_a_holder_its_granter_does_not_sync_reaches_its_readers_spec_sync_inbox_5`). Rejected: adding x's current `keys.0` (`current_keys_into`) to the piece: the chain already carries them, and the duplicates storm.
  - Followed per (t, x) pair from `Held::pairs_feed`, so a grant naming t places one chain (engine `a_grant_to_a_read_actor_costs_one_chain_spec_permissions_4`: 1 / 1 at 4 and 40 grants). Under each other read thing whose keys open t (`openers`, an account by its root), t's `Piece::Chain(t, x)` goes, an account root's only for an x it reaches by strongs, so a removal leaves the target's bodies there once that thing reads x no more; the grant chain between falls out. Openers are followed per holder (`Certified::opened_by`; `By` tells pairs placed by holder from those by authored thing), so a grant naming an opened holder places one chain (engine `a_grant_to_an_actor_a_read_thing_opens_places_its_chain_there_spec_permissions_4`: 3 / 3 at n and 10n).
- **Demotions** have no piece of their own: a chain's edge writes are every chained write on its paths, applied or not (spec/permissions.md §4), so a demotion on a demoted writer's path goes with each chain over it, and the demoted writer's new keysets carry it too (`carried_demotions`). A reader of a thing also syncs the sync ID of the thing's independent, whose `Piece::Own` holds every edge write into it; a node reaching a thing only through another sync ID S gets the demotion in the demoted writer's `Piece::Chain` under S.

One body per (S, commit) (spec/sync-judgement.md §1): where S carries a closure body and its certificates name parts beyond it, the two join as one per-sync-ID copy (`with_certificates`; notes/whole-commits.md Per-sync-ID copies). `rewrap` shares a certificate copy across sync IDs only from a sync ID holding it as a certificate of the same parts.

**Whether S's actor reads a thing**, for placing chain certificates (`certified::authored`) and thing lists (`listed::carriers_of`), is the same at every node (`cert_reads_in`): an account root reads as its account (`account_of_root`: a thing holding an `account.0` delta signed by its mint admin; any other signer's marks nothing, else a co-admin marking a shared thing would carry the account's chains and lists to its readers). Every access, sealing, boxing and opening decision (`Core::may_read`) reads only this node's own account root as its account. The two differ on purpose: read as "this node's account root only", another account's reader of D would place no author chain of D's writers under D, so it would judge their certificate copies outside D and drop them (`destruction.rs` remint tests).

## Certificate blobs (`sync::certblobs`, `sync::listed`)
Record blobs and list blobs (spec/sync-judgement.md §1); what each carries and how rows follow is in the two module docs.
- `certificate_records(s)` names the records S's certificates need that no closure body carries (a minting below the seal floor carries none: notes/history-less-grant.md, Records under a cut). `certificate_lists(s, r)`: a reader learns nothing of a thing it may not read, not even which commits P made on it (spec/requirements.md Confidentiality between readers), so an outsider gets the list of the thing it reads and of the account's own chain things, never D's. Counted at n and 10n rotations, rows equal to the whole derivation: `sync::listed::tests`, and after each step of `sync::certified`'s random histories.
- Read's `cert_blobs` effect per (S, kind) logs one fresh blob of all S carries of that kind when no own blob carries exactly those (`seal_records`, `seal_lists`), at order 0, so the own store settles on one blob per kind (osis-sync `read::list_blobs_follow_each_rotation_alone_spec_sync_judgement_1`). A record blob is wrapped by `wrap_secret`, a list blob by `list_wrap_secret`: as its rotation, under that epoch's roles secret, so a from-now holder judges the lists of rotations before its grant as an old reader does.
- `cert_blobs(s, kind)` (rows `cb/`) are kept while the own store holds the blob or some source's stream shows it, for `cert_delete_basis`; all go with S at `forget_read`. Streams keeps a pulled one charged to no one (read keeps one per set of certificates S needs, so a peer's extras leave). Read logs only while a reader can pull S (`pullable`). A certificate blob names no commit, so read never judges it by body. It commits to S's thing's writer delete key (spec/crypto.md §7 Certificate blobs; another prefix is `Opened::Bad`), so once S is heard out the same effect deletes at each source every one shown there that loses to another shown there (`CertBlobsMissing::deletes`; spec/sync-ids.md §6 Certificate blobs).
  - Nothing relogs one a store shows deleted: a store also drops entries for room or past a peer's budget, which the stream does not tell from a Delete, and relogging on every deletion storms (a store on an older head drops each fresh blob); issues/cert-blob-hostile-delete.md. Views of S's certificates are taken to agree once heard out (judged the same at every node): two writers with lasting different views would delete and relog each other's blob each exchange. Tests: growth `record_blobs_at_a_store_stay_flat_spec_sync_ids_6`, engine facade `a_record_blob_of_another_prefix_is_bad_spec_crypto_7`.

## Engine tables kept for sync
- **Passes** (rows `cp/`): the last complete pass per sync ID read here; `forget_read` when S stops being read, and an open drops those of every S not read, so an S read again wants a pass of its own. `pass_wanted(s)` (a `Cutoff` row per sync ID; `passes_wanted` folds them): per read sync ID a want at 0 (history complete), after the newest role commit from elsewhere on S's thing's independent (the re-seal trigger's), and after the place the loss of that thing was first judged (`Cause::Lost`, spec/permissions.md §3). Causes are rows `jc/`, kept while the cause holds (`judge_cause`, `forget_actor`, `forget_gone_causes`), so a role writer faster than a pass never holds a wait off. Met by a noted pass with `began > since` (`Core::place`: a role commit applied from elsewhere takes the next place, durably as `ra/` per thing; so does each pass begun; an open goes on from the highest stored).
- **Others**: falls (`fm/`), self-removal waits (`hw/`), blob parts (`bp/`) by commit.
- **Dial peers**: per S, this node's other nodes at their `reach.0` (no node sees what a sibling holds); of S read, every other account holding a role on S's thing's independent, at its profile's `dial^.0` entries; of S kept unread, its ticket's issuer by its profile. `profile_of(a)` is the thing created in `a`'s creating commit whose mint admin is `a`, other than its account root, so a co-writer of another of `a`'s things cannot plant a dial list. `publish_reach` writes `reach.0` and the profile's `dial^.0` entry, each only where changed, after each listen that succeeded and at `rotate_peer_address`. Every node lists itself on the profile (`dial::lists_on_profile`; issues/sync-tess-calls.md).
  - A removed node's entry goes (`dial::drop_departed`, at each transaction's end through an `Effects` on D's roster, the counting removals, the removed nodes' `reach.0` and the list): every node of P writing the profile drops it, whoever wrote it; a key some current node's `reach.0` names stays (a node about to be removed could name another's key). Only its own node writes a dropped entry back (per-key removes, a structural diff on `set`); an admin rewriting a removed key costs one drop commit per online node of P per rewrite. Readers cannot filter instead: contacts hold neither D's roster nor `reach.0`.

## Sync set rows (`sync/rows.rs`, `Core::sync_rows`)
The module doc holds the design; rows refresh at each ask until their fixed point (`memo::Quiet`). Every per-change reader goes by them (`reads_now`, `pass_wanted`, `caught_up`, a demotion's scope, `Certified`'s read things); `sync_set` folds the whole for listing (`osis` status, splits).
