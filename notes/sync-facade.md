# Sync: the engine's facade
> Summary: `osis_engine::sync` (core/engine/src/sync/), the one surface sync reads the engine through: its calls grouped by purpose, the memo and `Effects` mechanism that makes sync's work follow change, the engine's parking place for what arrives early (pending commits, loose records), certificate bodies and blobs, the engine tables kept for sync (passes, falls, dial peers), and the sync set rows.

Spec: spec/sync.md and its area files. Overview: notes/sync.md. The memo layer: notes/listener-design.md and issues/derived-state-recomputed.md.

**Principle** (notes/sync.md): the engine owns plaintext and judgement, sync ciphertext and peers; sync reads the engine only here, and the engine calls nothing of sync's (no hook trait, no callback, no queue sync must drain). Sync never reads `Core`, `ix`, `Tx` or commit `Meta`. The engine does not depend on osis-proto: proof signing and checking are osis-proto's `Prover` and `verified`.

## Calls
Every value call is a memo (or reads stamped rows), so a module reading it inside its own `Effects` settle depends on exactly what the engine read. Calls that read the store return `Result`.
- **Ingest**: `open_entry(s, blob, wrap, bytes, room) -> Opened` (`Commit(id, Ingested)`, `Linked`, `Records`, `Lists`, `NotOpen`, `Bad`): opens the wrap with a held read secret, then `open_commit` (each section's opening); a copy with content S's actor may not read now is refused whole (`copy_stale`, notes/engine-lost-access.md Copies: `NotOpen`); the wrap's epoch counts as an opening of the header (`note_unwrapped`); then ingests, an ephemeral commit by `ingest_memory` within `room`; keeps `blob_parts`. Also `memory_woken_feed`/`apply_waiting_memory`, `memory_charge`, `take_relays`/`open_relay` (relay-only commits, notes/ephemeral.md In osis-sync), `wants_blob(b)` (whether a waiting commit lacks blob b: a page or header list node, `Linked` when it does), `commit_feed`, `holds_commit`.
- **Bodies and facts**: `sync_bodies(c)` (per sync ID its thing's `thing_sync` reads whose required set holds a body of `c`: parts held, counting missing or dropped here, expiry, order, copy or shared, header-only), `body_facts(s, c)`, `read_facts` (kept per (S, commit) as a `memo::Cutoff` row, `unfollow_facts`), `neither`, `blob_parts`, `blob_commit`, `commit_blobs`, `blob_gone`/`strip_of`/`blob_loses` (per blob, notes/whole-commits.md Strip), `freed`, `fall_mark`/`body_fall_mark` (falls of held parts per aspect, durable; the stand compares marks), `own_commits(s)`, `covered_drops(c)`/`drop_body`, `forgettable`/`forget_commits`, `held_fate(c)` (a `memo::Cutoff` row per commit, `unfollow_fate`: read's per-commit `local`), `promotable(s, c)`.
- **Sealing** (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, built in `sync_ids::opened`; this is its one sync caller):
  - `seal_body(s, c, held, rng)` is the only fresh encryption of a body: a certificate or per-sync-ID copy by `seal_to`, else `Engine::seal` with the sections of every held genuine blob of `c` (only shared blobs opened or sealed here count, never a copy's), the wrap always `wrap_secret(S's thing, Some((c, parts carried)))`; `None` makes nothing now. `rewrap` (a held blob logged verbatim under another sync ID) wraps by the same `wrap_secret`.
  - `may_log(s, c, held)` (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Loggable) is what read's must-hold reads, as `cert_wrap` is for certificate blobs: a held blob `rewrap` would pass on verbatim, else a fresh encryption `seal_body` would make (a copy or certificate of parts all held, or each body part sealed here by `section_secret`, the per-section rule `seal` uses: epoch by `epoch_under`, parts by `passes_on`; a section carries no header, so the header's gate `header_passes` is the wrap's alone and a commit on n things is judged in O(n), osis `commits::commit_work_linear_in_things_spec_requirements_decentralization`).
  - `held` is the blobs whose bytes the sync store holds under an entry of a sync ID carrying a body of `c` (`Live::held_blobs`, the list `Live::fresh` seals from): an engine row of a blob whose bytes left the store counts for nothing; a held blob tombstoned under S counts (the store logs a fresh encryption carrying its sections instead). Approximation: parts of a held section this node did not open are left out of the parts `wrap_secret` is asked for (they pass `passes_on` trivially; only a rotation's unopened `secrets.0` would change the wrap epoch).
  - `wrap_secret` gives nothing under a read epoch blocked here (spec/crypto.md §4 Encrypting under; `wrap_blocked`: `Walk::Place` for a role commit, which goes as a certificate or the node's own, else `Walk::Encrypt`; a rotation's own wrap excepted): sync logs it once a rotation covers the departure. It reads the clock up to the next `until` or role commit in the components of S's actor and the node's account (`Core::bound_to_horizon`), so `may_log`/`cert_wrap` readers wake there. Cost: one blocked-epoch walk and one horizon scan per wrap (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md). Rejected: sealing each section under the epoch it opened under (a per-commit epoch table): safe too (Reseal.tla), but a second rule beside `passes_on`.
- **The sync set**: `sync_things` (the node closure, sync unit by sync unit, and links being redeemed) and `thing_sync(t) -> ThingSync { Read, Unread, Unsyncable, Plain }`; `in_sync_things(t)` and `things_entered` (a cursor over the candidates that may have entered since its last read), settled per thing through one `Effects` key by the sync set rows (below). The walk: notes/node-closure.md.
- **Keys**: `rotations_superseding(s, e)` (the rotation commits superseding read epoch e of S's thing), `read_credentials(s)` (recorded path's head, role keys per recorded step, read secrets, statics, `roster` digest, `proofs` deepest first, `acting`, and `sync_keys`: every held sync secret's key newest first, which the walk tests after the recorded steps; a memo per sync ID compared by public halves, as each key is a curve multiplication), `claim_key`, `signers(s, actor)`, `open_grant`/`learn_grant`, `step_needs`, `due_step(s)` (the step due here, with the rotations it registers) and the command `issue_due_step(s)`.

- **Stranded** (`sync/stranded.rs`, spec/permissions.md §4): `stranded(s)` (`Stranding`: actor, holder read through, the head's sync key, the holder's current keys, the actor's admins), `holder_keys`, `writer_of(s, proven)`, `fire_stranded`; the receiver's `take_stranded` (keys via `take_holder_keys`, then an ask, rows `rs/`, taken at the next pass place as a role arrival is). - **Suspect flags** (`retention/suspect.rs`): `suspect(t, ak)` (`(at, why)`, why 0 while dormant, `None` once gone), `suspect_feed`, `state_digest(t, ak)`, `suspect_keys` (dormant ones too, at a start), `record_known(t)`; repair: `reader_account(s, proven)`, `suspect_under(s, t)`, `may_repair(s, t, ak)`, `repair_suspect` (notes/engine-suspects.md).
- **Tickets and the inbox**: `kept_sync_secrets(s)` (the sync secrets held tickets and its own earlier reading give a kept-unread S), `ticket_for(t)`, `inbox_thing(a)`, `logged_with(c)`.

## Effects: work in proportion to change
`osis_base::memo::Effects<K, V>`: `want(k)` follows a key, `due` lists followed keys whose last value read a stamp written since, `settle(k, f)` re-runs `f`, records what it read, and returns `Some((was, now))` when the value differs. Each `Stamps` keeps a log of keys written since its oldest live mark, so `due` costs the writes since the last call times their readers, never the keys followed. A `Feed` is one reader's cursor over an index's own log (`Stamps::feed`): nobody drains it and nothing writes it by hand, which separates it from a take-queue. Time is the memo clock: a value valid until `t` reads `until(Some(t))`; `Clock::next` gives the driver its wake. Effects' acted values are not durable, so a start settles every key once.

A write on one aspect moves only that aspect's commits' stamps, so only their verdicts are judged again; a change of a sync unit moves `sync_bodies` of the commits on the things that joined or left it.

Values derived from walks whose reads move far more often than the value are kept per key and followed through `Effects`, so a change does work in proportion to what it moved (also `read_facts`, `held_fate`: notes/engine.md `Cutoff`):
- `sync::certified`: each read thing's certificates as pieces (Certificate bodies below), each derived alone and placed under every read thing it goes to, with rows per (commit, read thing) (what the pieces there name of it, counted per part, header and minting) and records per read thing (edited in place, per record its namers); which pieces go where is followed per key too (the read things, each thing with authors), new keys from a feed (`Indexes::chains_on`), never a walk of everything. Kept equal to the whole derivation (`Engine::sync_certificates`): `sync::certified::tests`.
- `sync_ids::published`: the publisher of each thing with a sync ID, per (actor, thing) by a body judgement (`PublishedView`), whole by the sync ID walk; a commit's candidate things (`body_things`) ask the same `Engine::publisher` (an account counts as its D, spec/sync-ids.md §2).
- `sync_body_now(s, c)`: per sync ID, the closure body of S's thing joined with its certificate row; read keeps each (S, commit)'s body as its `bodies` effect last settled it (`Mine::meta`), so a body coming at one sync ID re-judges nothing at another.
- `wrap_blocked` per (actor, walk), a `memo::Cutoff` row (`Core::wrap_blocks`), so a role change re-judges only the bodies whose wrap it blocks or unblocks.
- What the node syncs per sync ID is the store's `synced`, written by leave (notes/sync-leave.md).

Each refreshes when asked, and `Engine::follow` refreshes all of them: the driver calls it before each module's settle and settles again while it moved anything, since a module's `due` sees an entry move only once it is refreshed. Following needs the whole engine, so it never runs inside a transaction (a `Tx` holds the `Core` alone). Counted at n and 10n: osis-sync `read::an_event_on_one_sync_id_judges_what_it_moved`, `a_thing_published_under_an_actor_judges_what_it_moved_spec_sync_2`.

## Pending: the engine's parking place
The rule is notes/sync.md Arriving early; the wakes are spec/commits.md §4 Examined again (the coarse one is `Moved`). In the engine the parking place is `Indexes::pend_on` (`apply::waits::Wake`), written only by `set_pending` beside `pending` (journaled, rebuilt at load as `Wake::Open`): `Record(t)`, `Moved` (reach, an independent thing's secrets, epoch), `Epoch(e)` (the chain came to hold epoch e: holding its rotation alone opens nothing), `At(t)` (drift, expiry; `next_timer` names the first), `Thing(t)` (a purge or expiry on a thing it writes), `Open`. `reexamine` examines `wake`'s set only (`examined` counts it), and the commits whose blob waits ended (Blob waits below). A commit awaiting several records parks under each unknown one and, if any is held (a reach), under `Moved` (`Waits::records_of`). The waiting parts of an applied commit (pages, parked, unjudgeable) park the same way in its pending copy, so `pends(c)` keeps their carrier. An expiry already passed parks nothing (else `next_timer` stays due). The `order` harness checks after every step that a full scan applies nothing the wakes left (`Engine::lost_wakes`). `wake` takes what woke (held records and moves, the due flags) outside the round's transaction, as other in-memory due lists are taken outside the transaction acting on them; so after any failed store write (the device staying up), and after a transaction acting on what was taken failing for any reason (`Engine::run_taken`: a `reexamine` round, `promote_loose`; an engine fault erring a round rolls back its examinations), the engine reads its store again as an open does at its next transaction (`Engine::reload_owed`; `next_timer` is now while owed, and node-sync's step reloads at once, `engine_reloaded`): every pending commit is examined (`Wake::Open`) and every due list an open derives is rebuilt. A reload carries over what no open derives: `waits`, the compaction trigger, `fallen`. Tests: lib `apply::tests_failed_writes` (each write of an arrival waking a wait, a removal owing a vouch, a compaction step; a waking round erring, `an_arrival_waking_a_wait_whose_round_fails`), waits `a_failed_write_in_a_waking_arrival_leaves_the_wait_due_spec_commits_4`, compact `a_failed_write_in_a_step_leaves_its_aspects_due_spec_sync_7`.

**Blob waits** (`apply/reach.rs`). Principle: a blob arriving costs only the waits that want it, in proportion to what it brings; a part waiting for pages is decoded once, when none is missing. `WaitBlobs` parks under no `Wake`: each waiting slot (`reach::Slot`: a commit's header lists while they do not resolve, each part judged waiting for its pages, each applied memory part's value trees) keeps its walk (`reach::Walk`: the missing frontier, a `LinkTree` per position tree, what it reached), in memory, indexed blob → (commit, slot) (`Walks::wanted`); a slot is kept only while it lacks a blob.
- An arrival: every blob newly held is noted against the index (`Walks::held`: by `Tx::store_blobs` as it holds, and for every hold of any path, own writes included, from the `Cipher`'s holds as the transaction commits); a commit about to be judged takes its noted walks on first (`reach::take_on`, so a copy arriving with its pages sees them), and each `reexamine` round takes the rest on (`reach::round`, `advance`: from the blob only, O(its links); a blob held before the page linking it is walked through then). A commit is examined only when a slot of it came to lack nothing.
- `examine` gates per slot: header lists whose walk lacks a blob are not resolved (the signature checked first, as before), a part whose walk lacks one is set aside waiting without a decode, so a commit woken by a record or an epoch decodes only the parts that can apply (each part judged alone, spec/commits.md §4); a part judged waiting for pages gets its walk whole there (`judged_waiting`: one pass over its held pages; its walk must lack a blob, as the walk and the decoders apply one tree rule), and a slot judged otherwise loses its walk. A walk's tree refused (`LinkTree`) ends it and every position nested in its leaves, the roots there included (`refuse`), so what a walk lacks is one set whatever order its pages came in, and the judge then refuses the part whatever is missing in or below that tree (`walk_tree` checking goes on past a missing page of a nested position too); bytes it cannot follow end that branch. Limit: a refused tree refuses the part only once the part's other positions lack nothing (they may be fetched first, all within the writer's delivered pages for the part; withheld, the part waits): the decoders stop at the first position missing a page, so refusing earlier would need them to go on past a missing position. A durable part waits for pages only: a large value's root standing alone is not lacked (fetched lazily, spec/sync-streams.md §5); a memory commit's are (its gate needs them). A root in a list of links reads as a page until held, so it is waited for.
- So P pages cost O(P) walk steps, one checked decode and one signature check per commit, whatever batching or order the server picks (`tests_paged::a_trickled_fill_reads_each_page_a_bounded_number_of_times_spec_commits_4`: 442 pages one per delivery, 884 pages read, 442 walk steps, one examination; `a_blob_costs_only_the_waits_that_want_it_spec_commits_4`, `commits_sharing_pages_are_each_woken_once_per_page_spec_commits_4`, `a_refusing_page_refuses_as_it_is_held_spec_wire_6`).
- Re-derived, never journaled (an undo copy costs O(walk) per arrival): an open or reload starts with none (every pending commit is examined at `Wake::Open`, which derives its slots: one pass per waiting part); a failed step or transaction drops the walks of the commits it touched (`Walks::forget_since`, as `reach_log`) and the next round examines those again (`Walks::lost`, or walks an applied memory commit's trees again); a commit that stops pending loses its pending slots at its transaction's end unless it pends again (`reach::settle`); a forgotten commit loses all (`unindex`). `next_timer` is now while a round has work here (`Walks::due`). Test: `a_fill_goes_on_past_failed_writes_rounds_and_reloads_spec_commits_4`.
- Checks (debug): after every durable transaction no kept walk lacks a blob held here whose arrival is not noted, the index is exactly what the kept walks lack, and a kept slot's commit pends (`Engine::check_walks`); at every engine drop also each slot's walk against one derived afresh from its stored copy.
- Sync asks `wants_blob(b)` per entry it opens (O(log)), never the whole set (`blob_wants`, for tests).

Each wait's inputs and who moves them (read from `examine`, `wakes_of` and the writers; a wake missing here is data that appears only at a restart):
- `MissingChain`/`UnknownCreated`, and the records a part awaits: a thing's record (and its destruction) and the author's reach over edges. Every record and edge write goes through `Held` (`create`, `record_back` when a destruction falls, the role commit and vouch writers, `set_counted`), which notes `records` (`Record(t)`) or `moved` (`Moved`); `forget_record` only makes things wait.
- `Outside(t)`: `needed::whole(t)`, the node closure reading `t` (its member feed, `wake_entered` at every transaction's end) or a kept copy; a copy is kept only at a loss of a thing the closure read, so it never turns `whole` on alone.
- `Drift`, an expiry: the clock (`At`, `next_timer` names the first; every `wake` takes those due).
- `WaitBlobs`: a blob its walk lacks held here (Blob waits above): mutated by every blob hold (`Cipher::hold` under `Tx::store_blobs`, own writes), walks moved by `examine`, `memory_gate`, `advance`, dropped by `settle`, `unindex`, a failed step or transaction; woken by `take_on` and each `reexamine` round.
- `Epoch(e)`: the account chain holding `e` (`account::refresh_epochs`, `epochs_held`), and `Moved`.
- `Parked`, `Unexamined`: an open (`open_due`).
- Every stored copy, whatever its reason: what is value-less on a thing it writes (`persist_valueless`, `purge::apply_request`: `Thing(t)`).
- A path waiting (not a pending commit: an applied one with a thing whose split-path entry opens nowhere here, no key held and no roles secret opening it; its parts judged invalid until it opens; `View::paths`, `Indexes::paths_unknown`, journaled). Inputs: what may open the entry (`apply::paths::openers`: per thing up the thing's record chain, the epochs whose roles secret `Indexes::secrets` holds and an account's held seeds, and the records making the chain), followed per commit by a `memo::Effects` (`apply::paths::Wait`, in memory), and a late encryption's key (`atomic::join_late`). Mutating sites: every write of `ix.secrets`, `accounts` or a record. Wake: `paths::refresh` at each `rejudge_all` pass, after `flows::retake` in `atomic::rejudged` and at load: it settles only the commits whose openers moved and tries their entries; a commit followed anew is tried once. An open, a reload and a rollback build the wait again from `paths_unknown`, taking each commit's view as tried (it was decided against what was held when last judged). Tests: engine `validity::a_path_waiting_opens_when_its_roles_secret_comes_spec_commits_4` (the grant alone delivered, no restart), `split_commits_waiting_on_a_path_cost_an_unrelated_write_nothing_spec_commits_4` (k = 1, 10: none settled), `split_paths_judge_alike_however_parts_come_spec_wire_4`.
- A box the reading-only budget refused: room in the budget, read at every `wake` (room freed with no arrival or tick waits for the next).
The order property's `lost_wakes` sees only what its histories make; it reads a wake left untaken at its check as lost too. Named tests per wait: waits `a_record_examines_only_what_awaits_it_*`, `a_reach_wakes_a_commit_awaiting_a_record_too_*`, `drift_work`, `each_waiting_part_joins_at_its_own_record_*`, `pages_away_hold_back_only_their_part_*`; outside.rs; account_commits `account_commit_signed_at_its_epoch_*`; receive and parts (parked). Owed: one small world per wait and waking event asserting `lost_wakes` empty, and a world for a refused box freed by room.

Records one level down: a record from a record blob nothing here awaits is loose (`waits::Loose`, in memory), carried by its blob's store entry under the peer budget, learned when a `Record(t)` park or slot names it (`promote_loose`, with the loose records up its chain), and forgotten with its last carrier (`forget_carrier`). `carries_loose(b)` keeps the entry; `loose_feed` tells the store a carrier now gives something held (uncharge). A list blob whose rotation is neither applied nor pending is the same: its lists are kept loose by carrier (`Loose::keep_lists`, unverified), and every durable path taking that rotation (`ingest`, `ingest_valid`: `apply::hold_rotation_lists`) verifies and holds them in its own transaction (`lists_for`), so what they name never reads unlisted, then stamps their carriers. A list blob of a held or pending rotation is verified against its list root and held at once (`hold_lists`). A start opens the carriers again (the store parks them as entries that did not open), so nothing durable is kept for a record nobody named.

## Certificate bodies
`sync_bodies` adds, per read S, the held parts its certificates name (`Engine::sync_certificates`) less what the closure's bodies carry, as a copy (`copy` and `certificate` true), or a named header as a header-only copy. Only a commit with an edge, keys, card or `created.0` part or a request asks, so content never reads the certificates.

**Principle: every piece is the same at every node judging S** (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): a node-specific required set lets two honest nodes delete each other's copies, each logging again at every change of ground. The pieces:
- `Piece::Own` (`own_piece`, never this node's own `certificates`, which pairing alone uses): S's thing's record chain, destroyed things, key actors' cards (`shared_certificates`; key actors that are accounts get theirs with their chain); every holder's chain for S's thing (`Timelines::certificates_into`: what reaches it over chained writes); under D every card commit of D's other nodes.
- `Piece::Chain` per (author, target): the author's chain of each held commit on a readable closure thing (`Indexes::chains_on`: to the thing and, acting for another thing (its `actor`), to that thing: everything the commit's validity reads; node authors left out), however many things place it under S (`Certified::chain_by`). Chains are read by what they are made of (`Timelines::certificates`: the edge writes and records of each thing that ever reaches the target, `Read::Ever`), never the whole component. Not every chain into every closure item: that logged a private thing's roster holding a role on a thing under S, which no S reader needs (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates).
- `Piece::Account(p)` (`account_piece`): an account on a chain brings its record, card, current keys and every held line of its signing chain, so each writer's rotations are named at every node. Own and Chain pieces start `Certs::deferring`, so `cert_parts` names the account in `Certs::accounts` instead of reading its chain, and `Certified` places the account piece under each read thing while a piece there names it (`account_naming`). A rotation re-derives that one piece, not every piece naming the account (no count test yet). Every other `Certs` value holds the chains inline (`accounts` is `None`).
- `Piece::Holder(t, x)`: role changes travel as holder t's piece for each x an edge write names t holder on, under t's own sync ID (`certified::holder_own_on`): t's chain to x (which carries x's keys), x's `secrets.0` parts boxing to t and x's `sync.0` parts (`holder_piece`), so a reader of t holds the grant and x's sync ID with no ticket and no write to t. the thing whose sync ID stands for t's own is t when read; for this node's account, its root D for each x a vertex of D's unit enters by a strong (`Closure::reaches_from`), the piece then x's boxes and `sync.0` without the chain (x's own sync ID carries that). Why under D: a node paired later otherwise held only D's ticket for x, a read secret of x's current epoch, while the commits giving x's epochs are wrapped under epochs the account took by its inbox's ticket fires (spec/crypto.md §5), so it opened neither x's sync secret nor its history (sync `flows::a_node_paired_after_the_fires_expire_holds_its_siblings_secrets_spec_sync_judgement_2`; holding x from elsewhere, it enters x by the box: `holding::a_reader_by_a_chain_through_a_holder_thing_reads_spec_sync_judgement_2`). A granter that does not sync t fires its ticket on t sealed, the grant carrying the same piece among its chain, offered where an account holding a role on t is proven (`inbox::fire_to`); t's readers take it as they open the fire (`Engine::holder_grant`), whatever keeps the fire, and log it under t (`holding::a_grant_to_a_holder_its_granter_does_not_sync_reaches_its_readers_spec_sync_inbox_5`). Rejected: adding x's current `keys.0` (`current_keys_into`) to the piece: the chain already carries them, and the duplicate stormed. Followed per (t, x) pair: a read thing's pairs as it comes to be read, then each pair named from `Held::pairs_feed`, so a grant naming t places one chain (engine `a_grant_to_a_read_actor_costs_one_chain_spec_permissions_4`: the last of 4 / 40 grants, 1 / 1). Under each other read thing whose keys open t (`openers`: of the things on the record chain of t's independent and those holding a role on it, `Core::roles_now`, each read thing whose keys open t, an account by its root), t's `Piece::Chain(t, x)` goes, an account root's only for an x it reaches by strongs: the removal leaves the target's bodies there once that thing reads x no more. The grant chain between (the role records of the things between) falls out: each thing on it is a holder the same thing opens. Openers are followed per holder (`Certified::opened_by`, a row each pair's places read; pairs placed by holder or by authored thing are told apart, `By`), so a grant naming an opened holder places one chain (engine `a_grant_to_an_actor_a_read_thing_opens_places_its_chain_there_spec_permissions_4`: 3 / 3 at n and 10n); a change of t's roles re-examines its roster and re-places its pairs.
- **Demotions** (removals and lowerings) have no piece of their own: a chain's edge writes are every chained write on its paths, applied or not (spec/permissions.md §4), so a demotion on a demoted writer's path goes with each chain over it, and the demoted writer's new keysets carry it too (`carried_demotions`). A reader of a thing also syncs the sync ID of the thing's independent, whose `Piece::Own` holds every edge write into it; a node reaching a thing only through another sync ID S (a store keeping S unread, a reader cut off from the rest) gets the demotion as part of the demoted writer's `Piece::Chain` under S (core/sync/tests/it/demotions.rs).

Read through `sync::certified`'s rows for the commit, a role change moves the bodies of the commits whose naming it changed. One body per (S, commit) (spec/sync-judgement.md §1): where S carries a closure body and its certificates name parts beyond it (an account's `keys.0` in the commit making its profile), the two join as one per-sync-ID copy of both (`with_certificates`; notes/whole-commits.md Per-sync-ID copies). `seal_body` seals a certificate as `seal_to` (one section under the blob key) wrapped by `wrap_secret` for its parts; `rewrap` shares a certificate copy across sync IDs only from a sync ID holding it as a certificate of the same parts (a per-sync-ID copy is never shared).

**Whether S's actor reads a thing**, for placing chain certificates (`certified::authored`) and thing lists (`listed::carriers_of`), is the same at every node (`cert_reads_in`): an account root reads as its account (`account_of_root`: a thing holding an `account.0` delta signed by its mint admin; any other signer's marks nothing, else a co-admin marking a shared thing would carry the account's chains and lists to its readers). Every access, sealing, boxing and opening decision (`Core::may_read`, which `reads_in`, `reads`, `copy_stale` and client reads go through) reads only this node's own account root as its account. The two differ on purpose: read as "this node's account root only", another account's reader of D placed no author chain of D's writers under D, so it judged their certificate copies outside D and dropped them (`destruction.rs` remint tests).

## Certificate blobs (`sync::certblobs`, `sync::listed`)
Record blobs and list blobs (spec/sync-judgement.md §1).
- `certificate_records(s)` names the records S's certificates need that no closure body carries (a minting below the seal floor of S's thing carries none: notes/history-less-grant.md, Records under a cut). `certificate_lists(s, r)` names the things whose held list of rotation `r` S carries: the closure things S's actor may read as chain certificates place (`cert_reads_in`, a role at now on the memo clock; a reader learns nothing of a thing it may not read, not even which commits P made on it: spec/requirements.md Confidentiality between readers), and the things whose records its certificates name (`certified_records`). So an outsider gets the list of the thing it reads and of the account's own chain things, never D's.
- `sync::listed` keeps them as rows per (read thing, rotation): the read things are `Certified::read`'s (by its feed; following lists follows `certified` first); which read things carry each thing's lists is followed per thing (`carriers_of`: the read things whose unit holds it, read per member, and those naming its record, `Certified::namers`), so a thing entering a sync unit judges itself alone; lists held come from `Indexes::lists_on`'s feed, each placed under the read things carrying its thing, so a plain commit touches nothing and a rotation touches one row per carried thing it lists. `list_rotations(s)` (whole, as read first follows S) and `lists_feed` give read its keys. Counted at n and 10n rotations, rows equal to the whole derivation: `sync::listed::tests`, and after each step of `sync::certified`'s random histories.
- Read's `cert_blobs` effect per (S, kind: `None` records, `Some(r)` rotation r's lists) logs one fresh record blob of all the records S carries when no own record blob carries exactly those (`seal_records`), and likewise one list blob of r's lists (`seal_lists`), at order 0, so the own store settles on one blob per kind; each key reading its own row and blobs alone (osis-sync `read::list_blobs_follow_each_rotation_alone_spec_sync_judgement_1`). A record blob is wrapped by `wrap_secret`, a list blob by `list_wrap_secret`: as its rotation, under that epoch's roles secret, so a from-now holder judges the lists of rotations before its grant as an old reader does (the newest held read epoch of S's thing minted by no rotation, or one ordered before r; under a sync ID deriving from the rotated account, whose epochs have no `epoch_intro`, its newest held epoch, r's own included: only that account's nodes read there).
- `cert_blobs(s, kind)` (rows `cb/`, durable, one per certificate blob sealed here or opened from a peer under S, kept while the own store holds it or some source's stream shows it, with its prefix and blind (`CertBlob`) for `cert_delete_basis`: `open_entry` notes a record blob's records (`Opened::Records`; those nothing awaits stay loose) and a list blob's held lists (`Opened::Lists`); read reports a gone one, `forget_cert_blob`; all go with S at `forget_read`). Streams keeps a pulled one as it came (`Opening::Held`, charged to no one: read keeps one per set of certificates S needs, so a peer's extras leave). Read logs only while a reader can pull S (`pullable`, read inside the effect). A certificate blob names no commit, so read never judges it by body. It commits to S's thing's writer delete key (`writer_delete_key_in`, spec/crypto.md §7 Certificate blobs; a prefix of another shape is `Opened::Bad`), so the same effect deletes, at each store source, every one shown there that loses to another shown there, once S is heard out (`CertBlobsMissing::deletes`, by `delete_item` over `cert_delete_basis`) (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Certificate blobs). Nothing relogs one a store shows deleted: a store also drops entries for room or past a peer's budget, which the stream does not tell from a Delete, and relogging on every deletion stormed (a store on an older head dropping each fresh blob); issues/cert-blob-hostile-delete.md. Tests: growth `record_blobs_at_a_store_stay_flat_spec_sync_ids_6`, engine facade `a_record_blob_of_another_prefix_is_bad_spec_crypto_7`. Views of S's certificates are taken to agree once heard out (they are judged the same at every node): two writers with lasting different views would delete and relog each other's blob each exchange.

## Engine tables kept for sync
- **Passes** (rows `cp/`): the last complete pass per sync ID read here; `forget_read` when S stops being read, and an open drops those of every S not read, so an S read again wants a pass of its own. `pass_wanted(s)` (a `Cutoff` row per sync ID asked, refreshed by `follow`, let go at `forget_read`; `passes_wanted` folds them): per read sync ID a want at 0 (history complete), after the newest role commit from elsewhere on S's thing's independent (the re-seal trigger's), and after the place the loss of that thing was first judged (`Cause::Lost`, spec/permissions.md §3). Causes are rows `jc/`, kept while the cause holds (`judge_cause`; a loss's row and the thing's `ra/` go with its secrets, `forget_actor`; an open forgets a loss of a thing not held, `forget_gone_causes`), so a role writer faster than a pass never holds a wait off. Met by a noted pass with `began > since` (`Core::place`: a role commit applied from elsewhere takes the next place, durably as `ra/` per thing; so does each pass begun; an open goes on from the highest stored).
- **Others**: falls (`fm/`), self-removal waits (`hw/`), blob parts (`bp/`) by commit (they go with their commit; memory rows for a memory commit).
- **Dial peers**: per S, this node's other nodes at their `reach.0` (no node sees what a sibling holds); of S read, every other account holding a role on S's thing's independent, at its profile's `dial^.0` entries; of S kept unread, its ticket's issuer by its profile. `profile_of(a)` is the thing created in `a`'s creating commit whose mint admin is `a`, other than its account root, so a co-writer of another of `a`'s things cannot plant a dial list. `publish_reach` writes `reach.0` and the profile's `dial^.0` entry, each only where changed; osis calls it after each listen that succeeded and at `rotate_peer_address`. Every node lists itself on the profile (`dial::lists_on_profile`; issues/sync-tess-calls.md). A removed node's entry goes (`dial::drop_departed`, at each transaction's end through an `Effects` on D's roster, the counting removals, the removed nodes' `reach.0` and the list): every node of P writing the profile drops it, whoever wrote it, so an entry written back beside the removal goes too; a key some current node's `reach.0` names stays (a node about to be removed could name another's key). Removes are per-key ops and `publish_reach`'s `set` is a structural diff, so only its own node writes a dropped entry back; an admin rewriting a removed key costs one drop commit per online node of P per rewrite. Readers cannot filter instead: contacts hold neither D's roster nor `reach.0`.

## Sync set rows (`sync/rows.rs`, `Core::sync_rows`)
The sync set followed per thing (`things_entered`, `in_sync_things`, `thing_sync`) and folded per sync ID into stamped rows, refreshed at each ask until their fixed point (`memo::Quiet`, notes/engine.md): `sync_held(s)` and the read things (`SyncRows::read`, a feed for `Certified`). Every per-change reader goes by them (`reads_now`, `pass_wanted`, `caught_up`, a demotion's scope, `Certified`'s read things); `sync_set` folds the whole for listing (`osis` status, splits).
