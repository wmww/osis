# Sync: the engine's facade
> Summary: `osis_engine::sync` (core/engine/src/sync/), the one surface sync reads the engine through: its calls grouped by purpose, the memo and `Effects` mechanism that makes sync's work follow change, the engine's parking place for what arrives early (pending commits, loose records), the engine tables it keeps for sync (sync records, deliveries, passes, falls), what each derived value covers, and what is open.

Spec: spec/sync.md and its area files. Overview: notes/sync.md. The memo layer: notes/listener-design.md and issues/derived-state-recomputed.md.

**Principle:** the engine owns plaintext and every judgement of what a thing carries; sync owns ciphertext, connections and what peers showed. Sync reads the engine through this facade of memo-backed queries and a few commands; the engine calls nothing of sync's (no hook trait, no callback, no queue sync must drain). Sync never reads `Core`, `ix`, `Tx` or commit `Meta`. The engine does not depend on osis-proto: proof signing and checking are osis-proto's `Prover` and `verified`.

## Calls
Every value call is a memo (or reads stamped rows), so a module reading it inside its own `Effects` settle depends on exactly what the engine read. Calls that read the store return `Result`.
- **Ingest**: `open_entry(s, blob, wrap, bytes, room) -> Opened` (`Commit(id, Ingested)`, `Linked`, `Inbox`, `NotOpen`, `Bad`: opens the wrap with a held read secret, `open_commit` (each section's opening), a copy with content S's group actor may not read now refused whole (`copy_stale`, spec/permissions.md §3: `NotOpen`), the wrap's epoch as an opening of the header (`note_unwrapped`), then ingests, an ephemeral commit by `ingest_memory` within `room`; keeps `blob_parts`), `memory_woken_feed`/`apply_waiting_memory`, `memory_charge`, `take_relays`/`open_relay` (relay-only commits, notes/ephemeral.md In osis-sync), `wants` (pages and header lists before apply), `commit_feed`, `holds_commit`.
- **Bodies and facts**: `sync_bodies(c)` (per sync ID its thing's `thing_sync` reads, whatever lists the thing, whose required set holds a body of `c`: parts held, counting missing or dropped here, expiry, order, copy or shared, header-only), `body_facts(s, c)`, `read_facts`, `strict_subset`, `neither`, `blob_parts`, `blob_commit`, `commit_blobs`, `blob_dropped`, `freed`, `fall_mark`/`body_fall_mark` (falls of held parts per aspect, durable; the stand compares marks), `own_commits(s)`, `covered_drops(c)`/`drop_body`, `forgettable`/`forget_commits` (read's per-commit `local`), `promotable(s, c)`, `destructions`/`destruction(t)` (its requests: every held purge or release with an item on its `created.0`)/`forget_destroyed(t)` (secrets dropped last).
- **Sealing** (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, built in `labeling::opened`; this is its one sync caller): `seal_body(s, c, held, rng)` is the only fresh encryption of a body: a certificate or per-sync-ID copy by `seal_to`, else `Engine::seal` with the sections of every held genuine blob of `c` (`held`: blobs the store holds with an entry's wrap; only shared blobs opened or sealed here count, never a copy's), and the wrap always `wrap_secret(S's thing, Some((c, parts carried)))`; `None` makes nothing now; `may_log(s, c, held)` (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Loggable: a held blob `rewrap` would pass on verbatim, else a fresh encryption `seal_body` would make: a copy or certificate of parts all held, or each body part sealed here by `section_secret`, the one per-section rule `seal` uses, or carried by a held genuine blob; wrapped by `wrap_secret` for the parts carried) is what read's must-hold reads, as `cert_wrap` is for certificate blobs. `held` is the blobs whose bytes the sync store holds under an entry of a sync ID carrying a body of `c` (`Live::held_blobs`, the same list `Live::fresh` seals from), so what counts as passable is what logging can use: an engine row of a blob whose bytes left the store counts for nothing. A held blob tombstoned under S counts (the store logs a fresh encryption carrying its sections instead). Approximation: parts of a held section this node did not open are left out of the parts `wrap_secret` is asked for (they pass `passes_on` trivially; only a rotation's unopened `secrets.0` would change the wrap epoch). `rewrap` (a held blob logged verbatim under another sync ID) wraps by the same `wrap_secret`. `wrap_secret` gives nothing under a read epoch blocked here (spec/crypto.md §4 Encrypting under; `wrap_blocked`: `Walk::Place` for a role commit, which goes as a certificate or the node's own, else `Walk::Encrypt`; a rotation's own wrap excepted): sync logs it once a rotation covers the departure. It reads the clock up to the next `until` or role commit in the components of S's actor and the node's account (`Core::horizon`: who may rotate and what it may finalize change then), so `may_log`/`cert_wrap` readers wake there. Cost: one blocked-epoch walk and one horizon scan per wrap (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md, clock-relative asks). Rejected: sealing each section under the epoch it opened under (a per-commit epoch table): safe too (Reseal.tla), but a second rule beside `passes_on`.
- **The sync set**: `sync_things()` (the synced group's closure, key-home dependants included (notes/engine-roles.md Key homes), walked through no group sourced at another node (`Groups::sync_closure`, notes/client-port.md Hand-off), plus destroyed things whose own sync IDs are kept, `Indexes::destroyed_sync_ids`) and `thing_sync(t) -> ThingSync { Read, Unread, Unsyncable, Plain }` per thing, for `Effects` (`osis_sync::set::SetFollow`, which `leave` alone follows); `sync_set()` their whole fold, for listing; `sync_holder(s)` (S's thing and how the node's keys hold S, whatever lists the thing: what a leaving S is held as while it waits), `sync_ids(t)` (`OwnedSyncId { id, path, hint }`), `sync_owner(s)`. Read exactly while a read secret of the thing is held. A destroyed thing with own secrets held reads every own sync ID its destruction kept (`owned_sync_ids` and `sync_owner` return those, its record held or not) from the destruction transaction on, keeping or not, so the destroying request goes under S (spec/sync-judgement.md §5 Telling), until `forget_destroyed`; one rule, `thing_sync`, for every caller. While its secrets are held for deletes alone (keeping nothing), `read_secrets` gives none (nothing new opens) and no epoch of it is current (nothing is wrapped), but the head's delete key counts without establishment (`head_keys`: the rotations establishing it went with the thing; the step bearing the key vouches), else emptying could delete nothing and would forget at once.
- **Keys**: `rotations_superseding(s, e)` (the rotation commits superseding read epoch e of S's thing: an ended member's `until`), `read_credentials(s)` (recorded path's head, role keys per recorded step, read secrets, statics, `roster` digest, `proofs` deepest first, `acting`), `claim_key`, `signers(s, actor)`, `open_grant`/`learn_grant`, `step_needs`, `due_step(s)` (the step due here, with the rotations it registers) and the command `issue_due_step(s)`. `read_credentials` also carries `sync_keys`, every held sync secret's key newest first, which the walk tests after the recorded steps; a memo per sync ID (read asks it at each entry it judges; each held sync secret's key is a curve multiplication), compared by public halves.
- **Peers**: `judge_peer(s, proven) -> J` (`Reader`, `SyncHolder`, `Departed`, `Unknown`), `covers_departures`, `provers` (proof order: node key, account at its open epoch, key actors by tid), `own_node`, `own_node_key`, `key_open`, `prove_rotations`/`take_rotations` (not built).
- **Stranded** (`sync/stranded.rs`, spec/permissions.md §4): `stranded(s)` (`Stranding`: actor, the holder read through, the head's sync key, the holder's current keys, the actor's admins), `holder_keys`, `stranded_notice`, `writer_of(s, proven)`; the receiver's `take_stranded` (keys via `take_holder_keys`, then an ask, rows `rs/`, taken at the next pass place as a role arrival is, which `passes_wanted` and `pass_end` read).
- **Suspect flags** (`retention/suspect.rs`, spec/permissions.md §4): `suspect(t, ak)` per flagged aspect (`(at, why)`, why 0 while dormant, `None` once gone), new keys from `suspect_feed`, `state_digest(t, ak)`, `suspect_keys` (dormant ones too, at a start), `record_known(t)`; repair: `reader_account(s, proven)`, `suspect_under(s, t)` (t in S's closure and S's actor reads it: what S's list may name), `may_repair(s, t, ak)`, `repair_suspect` (notes/engine-suspects.md).
- **Records and inbox**: `sync_records`/`sync_record(t)`/`sync_record_feed`, `take_inbox(s, blob, accepted)`, `take_inbox_blob`, `invitations`, `inbox_of`, `inbox_blob`, `deliveries`/`delivery_feed`/`delivery_taken`/`delivery_names`/`delivered`, `logged_with(c)`.
- **Status**: `carrying(t)` (the sync IDs held carrying t: its own and its enclosing groups'), `waiting_removals`/`removal_done(c)` (a self-removal's secrets drop once sync is done with it: held elsewhere, or no peer of another account shares a sync ID it goes under), `passes_wanted`, `begin_pass`/`pass_place`, `note_complete_pass`, `forget_read`, `history_complete`.
- **Duties** (`osis_sync::life`): `lifetimes_due`/`judge_lifetimes`; `role_things` (a feed of the (holder, thing) pairs named, and the things named now), `named_a_holder(t)` with `joined_link_ended(k)`/`leave_link(k)` and `untie_due(t)`/`untie(t, g)`.
- **Dials**: `dial_peers(s)`, `profile_of(a)`, `publish_reach`, `publishes_reach`.
- **Time and config**: `next_timer`, `tick`, `set_now`, `clock_next`, `caps()` (`caps^.0` of this node).

## Effects: work in proportion to change
`osis_base::memo::Effects<K, V>` turns a memo into work in proportion to change: `want(k)` follows a key, `due()` lists followed keys whose last value read a stamp written since, `settle(k, f)` re-runs `f`, records what it read, and returns `Some((was, now))` when the value differs. Each `Stamps` keeps a log of keys written since its oldest live mark, so `due` costs the writes since the last call times their readers, never the keys followed. A `Feed` is one reader's cursor over an index's own log (`Stamps::feed`): nobody drains it and nothing writes it by hand, which is what separates it from a take-queue. Time is the memo clock: a value valid until `t` reads `until(Some(t))`; `Clock::next` gives the driver its wake.

A write on one aspect moves only that aspect's commits' stamps, so only their verdicts are judged again; a `group.0` change moves `sync_bodies` of the commits on the things that joined or left (adding a subtree costs its rows, a removal derives the set again, O(closure)). Effects' acted values are not durable, so a start settles every key once.

Some facade values are derived from walks whose reads move far more often than the value: each read thing's certificates, the things each actor publishes, a commit's bodies. (What the node syncs per sync ID is the store's `synced`, written by leave and followed per key, notes/sync-leave.md.) Each is kept per key, followed through `Effects`, so a change does work in proportion to what it moved: `sync::certified` keeps each read thing's certificates as pieces (`Piece`: the thing's own, an author's chain to a closure thing, an inbox actor's grant copy per thing it is named on), each derived alone and placed under every read thing it goes to, with rows per (commit, read thing) and records per read thing; which pieces go where is followed per key too (the read things, each one's inbox actors per sync ID, each thing with authors), new keys from feeds (`Indexes::authors_on`, `Held::pairs_feed`), never a walk of everything. `labeling::published` follows each labeled thing's publisher, read per (actor, thing) by a body judgement (`PublishedView`), whole by the label walk; a commit's candidate things (`body_things`) ask the same `Engine::publisher` (an account counts as its D, spec/sync-ids.md §2). A judgement asks its own pair: `sync_body_now(s, c)` (per sync ID: the closure body of S's thing, else its certificate row), and read keeps each (S, commit)'s body as its `bodies` effect last settled it (`Mine::meta`), so a body coming at one sync ID (a new read thing naming the account's card) re-judges nothing at another. `wrap_blocked` per (actor, walk), which every judgement's `may_log` asks, is kept as a `memo::Cutoff` row (`Core::wrap_blocks`, notes/engine.md), so a role change re-judges only the bodies whose wrap it blocks or unblocks. Each refreshes when asked, and `Engine::follow` refreshes all of them: the driver calls it before each module's settle and settles again while it moved anything, since a module's `due` sees an entry move only once it is refreshed. Counted at n and 10n: osis-sync read.rs `an_event_on_one_sync_id_judges_what_it_moved` (commits under other sync IDs), `a_thing_published_under_an_actor_judges_what_it_moved_spec_sync_2`; engine facade `a_new_grant_copy_lists_in_proportion_to_change_spec_sync_inbox_5`, `an_account_named_on_many_things_derives_in_proportion_to_change_spec_sync_inbox_5`; kept equal to the whole derivation (`Engine::sync_certificates`): `sync::certified::tests`.

## Pending: the engine's parking place
The rule is notes/sync.md Arriving early. In the engine the parking place is `Indexes::pend_on` (`apply::waits::Wake`), written only by `set_pending` beside `pending` (journaled, rebuilt at load as `Wake::Open`): `Record(t)`, `Moved` (reach, actor secrets, epoch), `Commit(rotation)`, `Blobs`, `At(t)` (drift, expiry; `next_timer` names the first), `Thing(t)` (a purge or expiry on a thing it writes), `Open`. `reexamine` examines `wake`'s set only (`examined()` counts it); `blob_wants` reads `Blobs` alone. spec/commits.md §4 (Examined again) lists the wakes; its Limits name the coarse one (`Moved`: every role change). A pending commit awaiting several records parks under each unknown one and, if any is held (a reach), under `Moved`, whatever its first reason (`Waits::records_of`). The waiting parts of an applied commit (pages, parked, unjudgeable) park the same way in its pending copy, so `pends(c)` keeps their carrier. An expiry already passed parks nothing (else `next_timer` stays due). The `order` harness checks after every step that a full scan applies nothing the wakes left (`Engine::lost_wakes`).

Records are the same rule one level down: a record from a record blob nothing here awaits is loose (`waits::Loose`, in memory), carried by its blob's store entry under the peer budget, learned when a `Record(t)` park or slot names it (`promote_loose`, with the loose records up its chain), and forgotten with its last carrier (`forget_carrier`). `carries_loose(b)` keeps the entry; `loose_feed` tells the store a carrier now gives something held (uncharge). A list blob whose rotation is not held (neither applied nor pending) is the same: its thing lists are kept loose by carrier (`Loose::keep_lists`, unverified), and every durable path taking that rotation (`ingest`, `ingest_valid`: `apply::hold_rotation_lists`) verifies and holds them in its own transaction (`lists_for`), so what they name never reads unlisted, then stamps their carriers. A list blob of a held or pending rotation is verified against its list root and held at once (`hold_lists`). A start opens the carriers again (the store parks them as entries that did not open), so nothing durable is kept for a record nobody named. An inbox payload's records are learned only where its chain or commit awaits them (`take_inbox` ingests them, learns, ingests again).

## Certificate bodies
`sync_bodies` adds, per read S carrying no closure body of the commit, the held parts its certificates name (`Engine::sync_certificates`, kept per piece by `sync::certified`). Principle: every piece is the same at every node judging S (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): a node-specific required set lets two honest nodes delete each other's copies, each logging again at every change of ground. So `Piece::Own` is `own_piece`, never this node's own `certificates` (that is what it would log of its own: its card, its chains to actors it acted for anywhere; used by pairing alone): S's thing's record chain, destroyed things, key actors' cards (`shared_certificates`); every actor's chain for S's thing (`Timelines::certificates_into` the thing: what ever reaches it); each author's chain to the readable closure things it wrote on (`Indexes::authors_on`; node authors left out); under D's home every card commit of D's other nodes; and under an actor's inbox its grant copies, `grant_copy` per (actor, thing named on): the newest applied edge write naming the actor there, projected as its inbox blob, spec/sync-inbox.md §5. An account on a chain brings its record, card, current keys and every held line of its signing chain, so each writer's rotations are named at every node, not only its own (`sync::certified::tests::two_nodes_name_the_same_certificates_whoever_logs_them_spec_sync_judgement_2`). In a piece these are one piece of their own, `Piece::Account(p)` (`account_piece`): Own and Chain pieces start `Certs::deferring()`, so `cert_parts` names the account in `Certs::accounts` rather than reading its chain, keys or mintings, and `Certified` places the account piece under each read thing while a piece there names it (`account_counts`). A rotation re-derives that one piece, not every piece naming the account (counted at n and 10n lines and at m and 10m things written on: `another_accounts_rotation_derives_pieces_in_proportion_to_change_spec_sync_judgement_2`). Every other `Certs` value holds the chains inline (`accounts` is `None`). Key actors holding S's thing that are accounts get no card from `shared_certificates`: theirs comes with their chain. Chains are read by what they are made of (`Timelines::certificates`: the edge writes and records of each thing that ever reaches the target, `Read::Ever`), never the whole component). Not every chain into every closure item: that logged a private group's roster holding a role on an item under S, which no S reader needs (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates, last case) less what the closure's bodies carry, as a copy (`copy` and `certificate` true), or a named header as a header-only copy. Only a commit with an edge, keys, card or `created.0` part or a request asks, so content never reads the certificates; read through `sync::certified`'s rows for the commit, a role change moves the bodies of the commits whose naming it changed. One body per (S, commit): where S carries a closure body, a certificate part on another thing of the same commit is not added (simplest reading of spec/sync-judgement.md §1, which names one blob per commit). `seal_body` seals a certificate as `seal_to` (one section under the blob key) wrapped by `wrap_secret` for its parts; `rewrap` shares a certificate copy of the same parts across sync IDs, only from a sync ID holding it as a certificate of the same parts too (a per-sync-ID copy is never shared).

Placing chain certificates (`certified::authored`) and thing lists (`listed::carried_lists`: a thing list is a chain certificate), whether S's actor reads a thing is the same at every node (`cert_reads_in`): a devices actor reads as its account (`devices_of`: a thing holding an `account.0` delta signed by its first admin, an account; any other signer's marks nothing, else a co-admin marking a shared group would carry the account's chains and lists to the group's readers: `another_admin_makes_no_devices_actor_spec_permissions_1`), not only this node's own. Every access, sealing, boxing and opening decision (`Core::may_read`, which `reads_in`, `reads`, `copy_stale`, client reads and the `NotSynced` diagnostic go through) reads only this node's own account's devices actor as its account: another account's devices never read as ours (`rotation::tests_reseal::another_accounts_devices_reads_as_itself_but_places_chains_as_its_account_spec_sync_judgement_1`). Read as "this node's account's devices actor only", another account's reader of D placed no author chain of D's writers under D (`Piece::Chain` needs it), so it judged their certificate copies outside D and dropped them (the remint tests in `destruction.rs`).

Certificate blobs (`sync::certblobs`): record blobs and list blobs. `certificate_records(s)` names the records S's certificates need that no closure body carries; `certificate_lists(s, r)` names the things whose held list of rotation `r` S carries: the closure things S's actor may read as chain certificates place (`cert_reads_in`, a role at now on the memo clock; a reader of S learns nothing of a thing it may not read, not even which commits P made on it: spec/requirements.md Confidentiality between readers; a devices actor reading as its account is that account's own nodes reading) and the things whose records its certificates name (`certified_records`: reading of spec/sync-judgement.md §1's "touched by a commit logged there as a certificate"; an outsider so gets the list of the thing it reads and of the account's own chain things, never D's, whose record no outsider's certificates name). `sync::listed` keeps them as rows per (read thing, rotation): the read things are `sync::certified`'s (`Certified::read`, by its feed: the sync set is followed once, a thing read no more settles to nothing; following the lists follows `certified` first, so `Engine::follow` follows each once); each read thing's carried things followed by `Effects` (a closure, role or certificate change re-derives that thing's set, O(closure)), the lists held from `Indexes::lists_on`'s feed (a `JSet` of (thing, rotation)), each placed under the read things carrying its thing (`carriers`), so a plain commit touches nothing and a rotation touches one row per carried thing it lists. `list_rotations(s)` (read whole, as read first follows S) and `lists_feed()` (rows as they come) give read its keys. Counted at n and 10n rotations, rows equal to the whole derivation: `sync::listed::tests`, and after each step of `sync::certified`'s random histories (account rotations among the steps). Following needs the whole engine, so it never runs inside a transaction (a `Tx` holds the `Core` alone). Read's `cert_blobs` effect per (S, kind: `None` records, `Some(r)` rotation r's lists) logs one fresh record blob of the records no own record blob carries (`seal_records`) and one list blob of r's lists no own list blob of r carries (`seal_lists`) at order 0, each key reading its own row and its own blobs alone (osis-sync `read::list_blobs_follow_each_rotation_alone_spec_sync_judgement_1`), a record blob wrapped by `wrap_secret`, a list blob by `list_wrap_secret` (as its rotation, under that epoch's roles secret, so a from-now holder judges the lists of rotations before its grant as an old reader does: the newest held read epoch of S's thing minted by no rotation, or one ordered before r; an account's epochs have no `epoch_intro`, so under a sync ID deriving from the rotated account a list wraps under its newest held epoch, r's own included: only that account's nodes read there, and they hold r's epoch with r); `cert_blobs(s, kind)` (rows `cb/`, durable, one per blob this node sealed while its own entry stays, indexed per (S, kind): read reports a gone one, `forget_cert_blob`; all go with S at `forget_read`) is what each carries; read logs only while a reader can pull S (`pullable` is read inside the effect, so S is due again once one can); `open_entry` takes a record blob's records (`Opened::Records`; those nothing awaits stay loose, Pending above) and a list blob's thing lists (`Opened::Lists`), and streams keeps a pulled one only as the carrier of something loose. Read never judges a certificate blob (it names no commit), so it is never dropped or deleted but with S.

## Engine tables kept for sync
- **Synced group**: `synced^.0` on D maps each node to its synced group; the account's creation writes the first node's (one commit after the role commit); before a node reads its entry its root is D. `Engine::pair` writes the new node's (a copy of the pairing node's synced group's items and subgroups, with D) unless `synced^.0` already lists one for it still under home (D's closure holds it, its closure holds D: a re-sealed stranded node keeps its own; one cut from D gets a new one, the spec's "until it is paired again"), and sends it last in the reply, after the grant that lets the new node read it; `synced_group_in` makes both. Two nodes pairing one node at once each write a group: `synced^.0`'s entry converges on one, the other stays an unused item of D.
- **Sync records** (`sync/records.rs`, spec/sync.md §3): the account's record is `kept^.0` on D (`Indexes::sync_kept`, derived from D's state by `records::observe`: `Tx::derive`, and `observe_own` at a load, a reload and a pairing, once D is known). The offers held (`io/`, `Indexes::offers`/`offered`) give held secrets and dial hints. `sync_records` is the view sync reads: kept sync IDs, recorded and offered secrets (`SyncRecord::recorded`), the dial hint of an offer whose sync IDs are kept; `refresh` rebuilds a thing's view when either source moves (a `kept^.0` change decodes the whole map: O(things kept), rare writes). Written (`keep_in`, a commit on D) by an accept (sync IDs), a lowering from read (its own `sync.0` reading, tied, and sync secrets), `record_borne` (sync's unread judgement: S tied to a thing, and its held secrets a step of S's path bears), `close_kept` (a closed sync ID leaves the record with its tie, the entry's recorded secrets too once it keeps none: `records::unkeep_in`, a write of the removed values, never a removal of the entry, which would hide a concurrent `keep_in` into it; sync closes it for every thing keeping it) and `learn_secret` (an entry box's sync secret, beside a kept sync ID). A kept sync ID is the sender's word for which thing owns it until a step ties it: `thing_sync` leaves out one a held `sync.0` gives another thing, and one tied to other things but not this one (`sync::kept_things`: the things a sync ID is synced for are those tied to it when any is, else every thing keeping it, so no accept order decides; `kept_records(s)` by `Indexes::record_ids`). Ties are account state like recorded secrets: a step this account verified, never a peer's word; a thing's tid cannot be checked unread, so a holder of the genuine sync secret boxing it as its own thing's ties both (stated limit).
- **Deliveries** (rows `ow/`): a flow owes `(commit, holder)` in the commit's transaction (which holders a rotation reaches depends on boxes before it, which the commit does not show); `inbox_blob` derives the rest when sealing (parts, things with sync IDs and delete keys, the author's chain plus the boxed things' `keys.0` for a reader, this node's `reach.0` as dial hint). A node holder is sent the commit itself. A row lives until sync calls `delivered` or its commit is forgotten. Untaken inbox blobs carrying a commit: rows `iw/`, listed by `invitations()`; one the user accepted here keeps its row (`accepted`) until it expires, not listed. Offers (`io/`) list a thing whose offered sync ID is not kept, wherever listed. `Engine::write` marks what an app write acts on as an invitation (`acted^.0` on D, `sync::inbox::mark_acted`): only from the group index's journal of that transaction, so a write changing no listing pays nothing; a mark already there adds no commit (an unchanged set writes none). Bound: one tid per thing ever acted on, never removed (a destroyed thing's stays); it grows only with writes that file or unfile things, never per session.
- **Passes** (rows `cp/`): the last complete pass per sync ID read here; `forget_read` when S stops being read (with S's certificate blob rows), and an open drops those of every S not read, so an S read again wants a pass of its own. `passes_wanted()`: per read sync ID a want at 0 (history complete), after the newest role commit from elsewhere on S's thing's reader actor (the re-seal trigger's), after the place the loss of that actor was first judged (`Cause::Lost`, the lost-access drop's, spec/permissions.md §3), and at the place a dead tie this node would remove explicitly was first judged for every sync ID of its demotion scope (`Cause::Tie`, spec/permissions.md §2); causes are rows `jc/`, kept while the cause holds (`judge_cause`; a loss's row, and the actor's `ra/`, go with its secrets, `forget_actor`; `Life` judges a thing named a holder no more once more, and an open forgets a loss of an actor not held or a tie whose group holds no edge on its target: `forget_gone_causes`), so a role writer faster than a pass never holds a wait off; met by a noted pass with `began > since`, both pass places (`Core::place`: a role commit applied from elsewhere takes the next, durably as `ra/` per actor; so does each pass begun; an open goes on from the highest stored).
- **Others**: falls (`fm/`), self-removal waits (`hw/`), blob parts (`bp/`) by commit (they go with their commit, memory rows for a memory commit), inbox hints (`ih/`: the sync ID a taken inbox blob named for a thing no applied `sync.0` gives one, followed through `sync_ids` with `hint` while the blob is unexpired, spec/sync-inbox.md §2). `InboxOpened::hints` is always empty: the hint goes through these rows.
- **Dial peers**: per S, this node's other nodes whose synced group has S's thing in its closure, at their `reach.0`; of S read, every account holding a role on S's thing's reader actor (other than its own), at its profile's `dial^.0` entries; of S kept unread, the dial hint of an offer held whose sync IDs are kept (`SyncRecord::dial`). `profile_of(a)` is the thing created in `a`'s creating commit whose first admin is `a`, other than its devices actor, so a co-writer of another of `a`'s things cannot plant a dial list. `publish_reach` writes `reach.0` and the profile's `dial^.0` entry, each only where changed; osis calls it after each listen that succeeded and at `rotate_peer_address`. Every node lists itself on the profile (`dial::lists_on_profile`): issues/sync-tess-calls.md. A removed node's entry goes (`dial::drop_departed`, at each transaction's end through an `Effects` on what it reads: D's roster, the counting removals, the removed nodes' `reach.0`, the list): every node of P writing the profile drops it, whoever wrote it, so an entry written back beside the removal goes too; a key some current node's `reach.0` names stays (a node about to be removed could name another's key in its own `reach.0`). Removes are per-key ops and `publish_reach`'s `set` is a structural diff, so no node writes a dropped entry back but its own node; an admin of the profile rewriting a removed key costs one drop commit per online node of P per rewrite. Readers cannot filter instead: contacts hold neither D's roster nor `reach.0` (both stay under home), so the writer's side is the one that knows which key was a node's.
