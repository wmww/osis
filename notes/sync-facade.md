# Sync: the engine's facade
> Summary: `osis_engine::sync` (core/engine/src/sync/), the one surface sync reads the engine through: its calls grouped by purpose, the memo and `Effects` mechanism that makes sync's work follow change, the engine tables it keeps for sync (sync records, deliveries, removal notices, passes, section epochs, falls), what each derived value covers, and what is open.

Spec: spec/sync.md and its area files. Overview: notes/sync.md. The memo layer: notes/listener-design.md and issues/derived-state-recomputed.md.

**Principle:** the engine owns plaintext and every judgement of what a thing carries; sync owns ciphertext, connections and what peers showed. Sync reads the engine through this facade of memo-backed queries and a few commands; the engine calls nothing of sync's (no hook trait, no callback, no queue sync must drain). Sync never reads `Core`, `ix`, `Tx` or commit `Meta`. The engine does not depend on osis-proto: proof signing and checking are osis-proto's `Prover` and `verified`.

## Calls
Every value call is a memo (or reads stamped rows), so a module reading it inside its own `Effects` settle depends on exactly what the engine read. Calls that read the store return `Result`.
- **Ingest**: `open_entry(s, blob, wrap, bytes) -> Opened` (`Commit(id, Ingested)`, `Linked`, `Inbox`, `NotOpen`, `Bad`: opens the wrap with a held read secret and takes what it holds; keeps the epochs and `blob_parts`), `ingest_relay`, `wants` (pages and header lists before apply), `commit_feed`, `holds_commit`.
- **Bodies and facts**: `sync_bodies(c)` (per sync ID of the sync set whose required set holds a body of `c`: parts, expiry, order, copy or shared, header-only), `body_facts(s, c)`, `read_facts`, `strict_subset`, `neither`, `blob_parts`, `blob_commit`, `commit_blobs`, `blob_dropped`, `blob_copy`, `freed`, `fall_mark`/`body_fall_mark` (falls of held parts per aspect, durable; the stand compares marks), `own_commits(s)`, `own_certificates`, `promotable(s, c)`, `destructions`/`destruction(t)`/`forget_destroyed(t)` (secrets dropped last).
- **Sealing**: `seal_body(s, c, rng)` (a copy's section under its blob key; else each section under the epoch it was written under and the wrap under the commit's epoch at S's thing, by `section_epoch`, rows `se/`; refused unless that epoch is established here, spec/crypto.md §5; a commit held but never opened from a blob is refused), `rewrap` (a held blob logged verbatim under another sync ID).
- **The sync set**: `sync_things()` (the synced group's closure plus destroyed things being emptied) and `thing_sync(t) -> ThingSync { Read, Unread, Unsyncable, Plain }` per thing, for `Effects` (`osis_sync::set::SetFollow`); `sync_set()` their whole fold, for listing; `sync_holding(s)`, `sync_ids(t)` (`OwnedSyncId { id, path, hint }`), `sync_owner(s)`. Read exactly while a read secret of the thing is held.
- **Keys**: `rotations_superseding(s, e)` (the rotation commits superseding read epoch e of S's thing: an ended member's `until`), `read_credentials(s)` (recorded path's head, role keys per recorded step, read secrets, statics, `roster` digest, `proofs` deepest first, `acting`), `claim_key`, `signers(s, actor)`, `open_grant`/`learn_grant`, `step_needs`.
- **Peers**: `judge_peer(s, proven) -> J` (`Reader`, `SyncHolder`, `Departed`, `Unknown`), `covers_departures`, `known_actor`/`known_actors`, `provers` (proof order: node key, account at its open epoch, key actors by tid), `own_node`, `own_node_key`, `key_open`, `prove_rotations`/`take_rotations` (not built).
- **Records and inbox**: `sync_records`/`sync_record(t)`/`sync_record_feed`, `stop_sync_record`, `removal_notices(s)`, `take_inbox(s, blob, accepted)`, `take_inbox_blob`, `invitations`, `inbox_of`, `inbox_blob`, `deliveries`/`delivery_feed`/`delivery_taken`/`delivery_names`/`delivered`, `logged_with(c)`.
- **Status**: `carrying(t)` (the sync IDs held carrying t: its own and its enclosing groups'), `waiting_held_elsewhere`/`held_elsewhere(c)` (a self-removal's secrets drop once held elsewhere), `passes_wanted`, `pass_clock`, `note_complete_pass`, `forget_pass`, `history_complete`.
- **Dials**: `dial_peers(s)`, `profile_of(a)`, `publish_reach`, `publishes_reach`.
- **Time and config**: `next_timer`, `tick`, `set_now`, `clock_next`, `caps()` (`caps^.0` of this node).

## Effects: work in proportion to change
`osis_base::memo::Effects<K, V>` turns a memo into work in proportion to change: `want(k)` follows a key, `due()` lists followed keys whose last value read a stamp written since, `settle(k, f)` re-runs `f`, records what it read, and returns `Some((was, now))` when the value differs. Each `Stamps` keeps a log of keys written since its oldest live mark, so `due` costs the writes since the last call times their readers, never the keys followed. A `Feed` is one reader's cursor over an index's own log (`Stamps::feed`): nobody drains it and nothing writes it by hand, which is what separates it from a take-queue. Time is the memo clock: a value valid until `t` reads `until(Some(t))`; `Clock::next` gives the driver its wake.

A write on one aspect moves only that aspect's commits' stamps, so only their verdicts are judged again; a `group.0` change moves `sync_bodies` of the commits on the things that joined or left (adding a subtree costs its rows, a removal derives the set again, O(closure)). Effects' acted values are not durable, so a start settles every key once.

## Engine tables kept for sync
- **Synced group**: `synced^.0` on D maps each node to its synced group; the account's creation writes the first node's (one commit after the role commit); before a node reads its entry its root is D. Pairing writes no synced group for the new node (it syncs home's closure).
- **Sync records** (`sync/records.rs`, rows `sy/`): written by `take_inbox` for a grant that boxes the recipient sync alone (the commit is not applied) and by `learn_secret` for a sync secret of a thing this node does not read; a lowering from read to sync writes the record from the sync secrets held as the read secrets drop. A record's sync IDs are its payloads' word: `thing_sync` leaves out one a held `sync.0` gives another thing. Removal notices (rows `rn/`, per sync ID) carry the sync epoch; `recipient` is the actor the blob opened under (its AD).
- **Deliveries** (rows `ow/`): a flow owes `(commit, holder)` in the commit's transaction (which holders a rotation reaches depends on boxes before it, which the commit does not show); `inbox_blob` derives the rest when sealing (parts, things with sync IDs and delete keys, the author's chain plus the boxed things' `keys.0` for a reader, removal signatures at the holder's newest boxed sync epoch, this node's `reach.0` as dial hint). A node holder is sent the commit itself. A row lives until sync calls `delivered` or its commit is forgotten. Untaken inbox blobs: rows `iw/`, listed by `invitations()`.
- **Passes** (rows `cp/`): the last complete pass per sync ID read here; `forget_pass` when S stops being read, and an open drops those of every S not read, so an S read again wants a pass of its own. `passes_wanted()`: per read sync ID a want at 0 (history complete), at the newest role commit on S's thing's reader actor from elsewhere (spec/permissions.md §3), and at the newest role commit of G or T for every sync ID of a dead tie's demotion scope this node would remove explicitly (spec/permissions.md §2); met by a noted pass with `began >= since`.
- **Others**: falls (`fm/`), held-elsewhere waits (`hw/`), section epochs (`se/`) and blob parts (`bp/`) by commit (both go with their commit, memory rows for a memory commit), inbox hints (`ih/`: the sync ID a taken inbox blob named for a thing no applied `sync.0` gives one, followed through `sync_ids` with `hint` while the blob is unexpired, spec/sync-inbox.md §2). `InboxOpened::hints` is always empty: the hint goes through these rows.
- **Dial peers**: per S, this node's other nodes whose synced group has S's thing in its closure, at their `reach.0`; of S read, every account holding a role on S's thing's reader actor (other than its own), at its profile's `dial^.0` entries; of S kept unread, the dial hint of the newest grant taken (`SyncRecord::dial`). `profile_of(a)` is the thing created in `a`'s creating commit whose first admin is `a`, other than its devices actor, so a co-writer of another of `a`'s things cannot plant a dial list. `publish_reach` writes `reach.0` and the profile's `dial^.0` entry, each only where changed; osis calls it after each listen that succeeded and at `rotate_peer_address`. Every node lists itself on the profile (`dial::lists_on_profile`): issues/sync-tess-calls.md.
