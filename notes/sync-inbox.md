# Sync: inbox, deposits and fires
> Summary: Deposits and sealed fires, engine and osis-sync `inbox` module (core/sync/src/inbox/): why the deposit budget, readers' deposits, clearing and promotion are as they are, what a stranger with `keys.0` can still do, no placement order, sync ID forks reaching readers by ticket; sealed fires in the engine (one fire path, opening, external fires, removal notices); what the module's parts add to their docs, its owed state and bounds, what is durable, seams, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-inbox.md, spec/sync-judgement.md §1, §3, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps and Register, spec/wire.md §10. Parts: the module doc of core/sync/src/inbox/mod.rs and each part's (the rules, work and bounds). Tickets in the walk and the account's tickets: notes/node-closure.md Tickets. Neighbours: notes/sync.md (index), notes/sync-access.md (re-seal, claims), notes/certificates.md.

**Principle:** a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry: unstamped, within one budget per sync ID, readable at once by co-readers, stamped once by a writer judging its author's rights, and cleared past a bound by any delete-key holder. Everything owed a peer is its record's and goes on each of its connections once the digests agree there.

## Deposits
- **A reader's deposits** (its account's chain certificates, its may-grant grant, self-removal, card) sit deposited in its own store until a writer promotes them or logs them as its own certificate copies; co-readers pull and read them meanwhile. A reader opens every deposit it takes, a Deposit item included (a peer judging it a source keeping S unread offers one): its bytes are then held, so the writer's stamp on the same blob, arriving later, fetches nothing and would open nothing.
- **A reader's copy under a superseded stamp** (any author's, ephemeral or durable) goes as a fresh encryption deposited where a source keeping S unread lacks it: after a write-key rotation, with that source's copy gone (an ephemeral entry at each of its restarts; a durable one on a new or wiped node) and no writer holding it, it is the only way back. A reader deposits a body only while it judges it valid: a demoted writer's writes refused under the rotated key are invalid there too, and deposited they would sit in the budget until cleared. It costs no new room: any deposit-key holder could fill the one budget already. Rejected: taking stamps under a superseded write key on relay (refused, code 9): a removed writer keeps the old write key and could fill S's room. Not built after a head move: issues/sync-unbuilt.md.
- **The budget is local policy**, not a format constant: senders never compute it, they see refusals (code 5). The recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2) is sized per read *holder*, while deposits come per *node*: a dependent thing whose mint admin is a shared space has a claim set of one, and thirty reader nodes' cards can approach it, so a node keeping S unread for a large read-only audience raises it (`caps^.0` policy `deposit_entries`, `deposit_bytes`). Deposits are unlinkable by design, so per-reader limits are impossible; the peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5) charges only entries that give nothing held.
- A node keeps offering its own deposit after losing read access: other nodes may hold parts of that commit from elsewhere and wait for the rest.
- Relay-only blobs are refused as deposits (code 14): held by nobody, a reader's would be a write of its own, and readers write no ephemeral data.

## Clearing (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Clearing deposits)
- **One rule, every sync ID**, for all a stranger (an inbox) or a reader (any sync ID) can put in the budget: junk, fires, sealed commits that open but are invalid, pending or parked commits, record blobs, far or no expiry. No "this is junk" verdict: a blob sealed to a key this node lacks yet looks like junk and stays while under the bound, and what stays blocks nothing, half the recommended budget being free.
- Cost: under a validity fork (permanent) a clearing node may clear what a writer on the other side would promote, only past the bound; so too an honest deposit pending at the clearing node. A reader's own must-hold body is not lost: it deposits afresh at each judgement finding the source lacks it, so past the bound it cycles until judged.
- Rejected: **age-out** of unstamped entries: at 0 nothing waits for a writer, at infinity the hole stays.

## Promotion (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Promotion)
- **Once per delta**: any reader can seal any commit it holds again, and role commits and cards outside the closure are may-hold (a writer cannot tell which of them another node's chain needs), so every duplicate stamped would be kept for good. Chain certificates are logged as the promoter's own copies, so a reader's deposited ones are duplicates left to clearing. Rejected: promoting certificate-class deltas on anything upstream of the closure: the reader's own account is upstream, so every edge it writes there and every thing it mints and grants there would be stamped.
- The writer checks the author's write right (`Engine::promotable`), not the depositor's: honest readers deposit others' content. So a reader's minted key actors' cards are never promoted.
- Never an entry whose bytes the writer dropped: shredding cleared its stamp, and promoting it would bring back a destroyed thing's record for a newcomer to read.
- **Bytes-only promotion** is one per commit while it stands: promoting every byte variant would let a depositor move unbounded variants off the deposit budget onto S's caps. A forger holding the slot starves the genuine deposit only until a writer that opens the forged section promotes it by its delta; a junk section opens nowhere (not Bad, just unopened), so no reader deletes a forgery and nothing else frees the slot.

## What a stranger with `keys.0` can still do (accepted; spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read)
- Fill the budget while no delete-key holder of the inbox is online: fires wait at their senders (offered again after back-off) until one comes back. A thing's readers can fill any of its sync IDs the same way.
- Flood without pause: no deposit rate limit is built (spec/sync-ids.md §6 allows one, code 4), so slots go as they free, an honest deposit races him, and each junk blob costs the node its bytes until he stops. Storage bound of promoted fires: issues/stranger-fire-bounds.md.
- Past the bound, flood or not, what goes may be real: a reader's deposited linked blob or record blob (a promoter lacking it logs its own), a commit still pending here, or a blob sealed to a key the node holds only later (a shared space's, whose secrets come from another sync ID). A node holding the head's read epoch holds its history, so readers' valid commits open and never count.
- A new deposit seed needs a new `keys.0` and step.

## Delivery
- **No placement order** (Tess): projections of a node's own commit go at once. A receiver holding any part of a role commit applies it and joins the rest on arrival (spec/commits.md §4), so nobody waits for a body that never reaches K's sync ID; copies waiting for the full commit would need a timeout, and receivers would still wait forever past it. Accepted cost: a K-syncer may see a grant before its boxes (an admin may re-seal needlessly).
- **A copy is never judged by its author's chain**: the chain moves after a copy (the granter's grant lowered, his node's key), so copies of a settled grant would be deleted for good, and a judge cannot tell a chain that will not come from one on its way. Promotion completes chains; duplicates logged concurrently by two writers are may-hold.
- **A sync ID fork reaches readers by ticket** (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Key changes): once both sides' sync IDs are seen every writer syncs only the greater, and a reader granted on the lesser side learns T's sync ID from a ticket alone; so while T owns several, a rotation tickets every holder it boxes (`rotation::unreached`). Key events are the only path needed: each side's independence made its own epoch, so a lesser-side holder is boxed by a re-seal or the superseding rotation before it could read anything new. A sync ID is never retired, so a forked thing's rotations ticket every boxed holder for life: at most one ticket per box written. Rejected: logging under every sync ID T owns (doubles every commit of a forked thing, forever); ticketing only holders lacking a box at a superseded current epoch (rests on that indirect invariant rather than the cause).

## Sealed fires (engine)
- **One fire path** (`flows::inbox::fire`): a node holding and reading the thing at a current epoch (a stranded node fires sealed) writes the fire as any commit where the setting keeps it (`keeps_fire`), else returns the setting's refusal (`NotEnabled`, `Misuse(FIRE_IN_NO_HISTORY)`), never a silent skip; `fire_ticket` drops a refused ticket on purpose, since the holder's readers hold the grant from the holder's own sync ID (spec/sync-judgement.md §2). Any other node owes a sealed fire: a durable row (`node/fires/`, `Indexes::fires`) keyed by BLAKE3 of `[thing, ak, value]` so one fire is owed once, its commit built once unapplied (`engine::write::sealed_commit`) and kept, so resealing on a key change carries the same commit. Kinds: `flows::inbox::Fire`.
- An owed sealed fire (`OwedFire`, `Indexes::fires_on` by thing) goes when its thing is forgotten whole (`retention::needed`: nothing held needs it): with no keyset left it could never be sealed again. Not at the thing's release: its certificates may stay, and the fire is still deliverable then.
- **A ticket fire carries its role commit**: the commit giving the ticket's epoch is wrapped under an epoch it supersedes, which a new holder lacks (taken as a carried commit, notes/node-closure.md Tickets). A ticket written into an inbox the node reads carries none.
- **Opening** (`Engine::open_sealed_fire`, `Engine::ingest_sealed_fire`): everything a fire brings is taken in one transaction kept only when the fire applies valid and no chain commit is parked or invalid, so a stranger's records are never learned for a fire that does not check out. Promotion waives the role check for external fires, validity deciding. A stranded notice opened by a writer is taken as a Stranded message (`Engine::take_stranded`).
- **External fires** (spec/permissions.md §5.2; design: notes/write-path-design.md §4) need no path to the thing (`apply::thing_wait`). Judged in `apply::validity` (`Judge::part`); `validity::setting_moved` re-judges through the one re-judging path (`Core::stale_commits`): on a move of held settings only the fires of signals whose enabling changed, on a setting's validity flipping all of them. What was taken while a fire was valid stands. A reader's write side refuses an unenabled fire (`check_role`); a sealed fire's sender reads no setting (`commit_or_build`). An account's inbox enables its three signals at creation; every other thing is default-off.
- **External fires keep only by name** (`RetentionSetting::entry`: the default entry never governs one), so a writer's fire on a signal no entry names is out of history at arrival and anyone else's invalid. A fire out of history or expired counts as gone for forgetting (`Engine::forgettable`): its commit, blob row and the signal's cursor (`leaving::unplaced`) go once node-sync knows no entry of it; no forgotten-`ts` row is kept for a signal.
- **A stranger's chain goes at the pass end that brought its fire**, the fire staying (notes/certificates.md Kept while needed; engine `strangers::*`).
- **Who owes a removal notice** (`flows::inbox::owe_removal_notices`, from `Core::notices_due`): every node that applies the removal and holds what it removes, not the writer alone: a removal written blind (its writer cut off from a sibling's grant) stands, and with no notice the removed reader never learns, as its fellow holders close S toward it at once. Re-judged only when a write on that edge arrives, so a duplicate costs one fire. The same arrivals on the account's own edge on T are the roster-coming trigger of carried commits (notes/node-closure.md Tickets).
- **An independent thing's sync ID is in its `keys.0`** (field 9): a sender knows where a fire goes from the public key record alone. An account's public record carries its inbox's (`account_chain`).
- **A removed node holds no deposit seed**: D has its own, fresh in every account rotation. The inbox is held by P's record edge, so a node removal makes its boxes to P stale and the inbox rotates all four roles, its sync role writing a fresh seed (`rotation::refreshes_deposit`, no exception for D).
- **An independent thing as its own inbox takes only what its own setting enables** (spec/sync-inbox.md §1): with `removal@~0` off it learns of its removal only from its writers' role commits; with `stranded@~0` off a stranded reader stays stranded until a writer connects; with `ticket@~0` off a grant reaches its readers only under its own sync ID. A grant fire to it carries what its own sync ID carries of T because that is in its required set: a granter not syncing it delivers the grant by the fire, and its readers log it there.

## The module's parts: beyond their docs
- `offers`: an offer counts the recipient proven now (`opens::counted` through `offers::Judge`); `Moved::Until` readies S's offers again after a member's end.
- `held`: a refused peer deposit (a sealed fire applying invalid or with a part waiting) is opened again only when what its rolled-back ingest read moves (`memo::Undoable`, notes/engine.md Stamps). Promotion is read's to act on (it stamps in place, notes/sync-read.md Own store); the author's chain counts under S only from stamped or own entries, never a deposit (`chain_under`); record blobs are read's certificate blobs, not waited for. A forged section a writer cannot open never covers the genuine deposit (`adds_delta`).
- `register`: plans and progress are per (record, S) (`Net::member_at`). A Register goes on the record's connection without waiting for digests to agree: it needs no secret and no open, and a departed store is registered on purpose. A reader refuses every Register (spec/sync-ids.md §6): its path comes from `sync.0` alone. A Register out is lost with its connection (`closed`). The branch rule: notes/sync-access.md, Why the gate and the claim.
- `serve`: a store failure answers refused, for Stranded as for Register.
- `suspect` (engine side notes/engine-suspects.md): the reporter's row `I/s/` is dropped only once an entry shows it stale. Rejected: dropping it on any matching entry (a reporter alternating the true digest with a false one gets a snapshot per pair). Rows stay bounded by S's readers times the aspects this node writes, since a node keeping S unread or a stranger gets no repair.
- `stranded`, receiver side (engine): taken only for a sync ID it reads, a current holder of S's actor and a role it may rotate at or below the holder's; the holder keys go in along the holder's authority chain only, each commit kept only if valid (`ingest_valid`); then one ask per (S's actor, holder) keyed by the holder's current keys, met at the end of S's actor's next complete pass (`Reseal::Asked`: written only where a box differs from what Seal gives). A refused re-seal leaves the ask open; an ask goes once its holder is not current, or with S's actor. Sender bound: one `I/x/` row per read S ever reported; a node re-sealed and later stranded at the same key does not report again, which the spec's once-per-key allows.
- `links`: following needs K unknown here or no other thing owning its sync ID (`sync_ids::actors::followable`). Redeem's osis future fails `Network` ("no holder answered") when given up, `Invalid` when ended (`share.rs` `redeem_over`, read off `Node::redeeming`). `dial_hints` are the driver's dial candidates; `may_deposit(conn)` keeps a dialed connection open while a deposit to it may land.

## State
What is owed a peer is its record's, named by no connection, and goes on each of the record's connections once the digests agree there (`Net::agreed_on`): an offer until moot, a suspect list once per connection and again when S's list moves. Neither is done for the record by an answer: a store may lose a deposit it took, a receiver a list it answered Ok. What is the connection's (one item out, back-offs, a refusal parked until the next filter, the list sent there) stays the connection's. Keys: P a peer, E the engine or opens, N this node.

| State | Keys | Per | Bound (flood test) | Goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `offers.items`, `loose`, `to` | N's own deposits and E's fires owed | node | own deposited entries and fires owed | stamped, dropped, sealed again, taken (a fire) |
| `offers.conns`, `who`, `agreed`, `active`, `wakes`, `parked` | a proven connection (E) | connection | connections × offers (`inbox::a_peer_never_answering_holds_nothing_across_reconnects_spec_sync_inbox_3`) | the connection's end |
| `register` plans, progress | (record, S) of a store member (E) | record | store members | S leaving the record, `record_gone` |
| `register.out`, `wakes` | N's Register requests | connection | one out per (record, S) | the answer, the connection's end |
| `suspects.members` | member-level `Moved` (E) | record | reader members | the member closing, `record_gone` |
| `suspects.owed`, `on` | S with a list × its reader members | record | reader members of S with a list (`suspect::tests::a_reconnect_sends_one_list_per_member_with_a_list_spec_permissions_4`) | the list going empty, the member closing, `record_gone` |
| `suspects.waiting` | (S, connection) of a list P sent mid first sync here | connection | one message per member | judged as the first sync ends, the member closing, the connection's end |
| `suspects.lists`, `flags`, `synced`, `first` | E's flags | node | flagged aspects | with the flag |
| `fires`, `tickets`, `held`, `links`, `reports` | E, N's store | node | the parts' docs | the parts' docs |

Messages: what a peer controls reaches the owed tables only by answering N's offers (0 marks done only what it was asked, on the connection it was asked on; a refusal costs one item per connection, as one never answering); a removed peer's members close or end in the step of the change, so nothing more goes there (`inbox::a_deposit_after_a_removal_while_away_goes_nowhere_spec_sync_inbox_3`); an unproven peer has no record and nothing goes before agreement. Clocks: monotonic for back-offs (`next_wake`), wall for a link's follow time. A restart changes nothing a user sees: the owed tables are in memory, so a start owes every offer and list again at each record's next agreed connection, as any reconnect does (a duplicate deposit is taken, a repeated list repairs nothing twice).

## Durable
Store rows `I/x/` (whom each stranded report went to), `I/s/` (the state last snapshotted per reporter and aspect), `I/r/` (links being redeemed, shredded: they hold the seed) and `I/p/` (joined links' dial hints); the engine's `node/fires/`. Everything else is followed again from the engine and the store at a start.

## Seams
mod.rs's module doc: the facade, the store and `inbox::Net`; each rule's decision a plain-value function the tests drive.

## Not built
issues/sync-unbuilt.md (inbox): a grant's `profile` ticket to a stranger; clearing at sources keeping S unread (needs streams' `known` to carry each entry's deposited flag and blob size). issues/sync-link-first-contact.md: K's commits do not apply at a redeemer.
