# Sync: inbox, deposits and fires
> Summary: Deposits and sealed fires, engine and osis-sync `inbox` module (core/sync/src/inbox/): the deposit budget, readers' deposits and copies under a superseded stamp, one clearing rule, promotion once, what a stranger with `keys.0` can still do, no placement order; sealed fires (one fire path, opening, external fires, removal notices); the module's parts (offers, fires, account's tickets, held, register, serve, suspect, stranded, links), what is durable, seams, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-inbox.md, spec/sync-judgement.md §1, §3, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps and Register, spec/wire.md §10. Parts: the module doc of core/sync/src/inbox/mod.rs (and each part's). Tickets in the walk and the account's tickets: notes/node-closure.md Tickets. Neighbours: notes/sync.md (index), notes/sync-access.md (re-seal, claims), notes/certificates.md.

**Principle:** a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry: unstamped, within one budget per sync ID, readable at once by co-readers, stamped once by a writer judging its author's rights, and cleared past a bound by any delete-key holder. Everything owed a peer is its record's and goes on each of its connections once the digests agree there.

## Deposits
- **A reader's deposits** (its account's chain certificates, its invite grant, self-removal, card) sit deposited in its own store until a writer promotes them (a body the closure carries) or logs them as its own certificate copies (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 promotes no chain certificate); co-readers pull and read them meanwhile.
- **A reader's copy under a superseded stamp** (any author's, ephemeral or durable) goes as a fresh encryption deposited where a source keeping S unread lacks it (relayed under its stamp only while the source's head write key verifies it): after a write-key rotation, with that source's copy gone (an ephemeral entry at each of its restarts; a durable one on a new or wiped node) and no writer holding it, it is the only way back. The promoting writer judges it by its author's rights at its `ts`. A reader deposits a body only while it judges it valid: a demoted writer's writes refused under the rotated key are invalid there too (kept for its user, spec/permissions.md §6), and deposited they would sit in the budget until cleared. It costs no new room: one budget per sync ID holds durable and ephemeral deposits alike (an ephemeral one also within the ephemeral cap), and any deposit-key holder could fill it already. Rejected: taking stamps under a superseded write key on relay (refused, code 9): a removed writer keeps the old write key and could fill S's room. Not built after a head move: issues/sync-unbuilt.md.
- **The budget is local policy**, not a format constant: senders never compute it, they see refusals (code 5). The recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2) is sized per read *holder*, while deposits come per *node*: a dependent thing whose mint admin is a shared space has a claim set of one, and thirty reader nodes' cards can approach it, so a node keeping S unread for a large read-only audience raises it (`caps^.0` policy `deposit_entries`, `deposit_bytes`). Deposits are unlinkable by design, so per-reader limits are impossible; the peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5) charges only entries that give nothing held.
- A node keeps offering its own deposit after losing read access (spec/sync-inbox.md §3 Sending): other nodes may hold parts of that commit from elsewhere and wait for the rest.
- Relay-only blobs are refused as deposits (§3, code 14): held by nobody, a reader's would be a write of its own, and readers write no ephemeral data.

## Clearing
- **One clearing rule, every sync ID**, for all a stranger (an inbox) or a reader (any sync ID: it holds the deposit key) can put in the budget (junk that opens to nothing, fires, sealed signed commits that open but are invalid, pending or parked commits, record blobs, far or no expiry): a delete-key holder leaves at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` (32) and `DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES` (1 MiB) of them per store and deletes the rest, in spec/sync-inbox.md §4's order. Exempt only while it would act on it: a deposit it would promote; a duplicate or a commit S does not need counts. No "this is junk" verdict: a blob sealed to a key this node lacks yet looks like junk and stays while under the bound, and what stays blocks nothing, half the recommended budget being free.
- Counted at blob size once its bytes arrived, whether or not the node still holds them (it drops the bytes of what it judged invalid).
- Cost: under a validity fork (permanent) a clearing node may clear what a writer on the other side would promote, only past the bound; an honest deposit pending at the clearing node past the bound goes the same way. A reader's own must-hold body is not lost: it deposits a fresh encryption at each judgement finding the source lacks it, so past the bound it cycles until judged.
- Rejected: **age-out** of unstamped entries: at 0 nothing waits for a writer, at infinity the hole stays.

## Promotion
- **Promotion once** (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): one stamped copy per delta, however many encryptions are deposited: any reader can seal any commit it holds again, and role commits and cards outside the closure are may-hold (a writer cannot tell which of them another node's chain needs), so every duplicate stamped would be kept for good. Chain certificates are not promoted: the promoter logs the author's chain of a promoted commit as its own certificate copies, so a reader's deposited chain certificates are duplicates left to clearing. Rejected: promoting certificate-class deltas on anything upstream of the closure: the reader's own account is upstream, so every edge it writes there and every thing it mints and grants there would be stamped.
- The writer checks the author's write right (`Engine::promotable`: applied, a body S must hold, its author's write on S's actor at its `ts`): honest readers deposit others' content, so the depositor's right says nothing. So a reader's minted key actors' cards are never promoted.
- Never an entry whose bytes the writer dropped: a purged or superseded part's (shredding cleared its stamp, and promoting it would bring back a destroyed thing's record for a newcomer to read).
- **Bytes-only promotion** is one per commit while it stands: promoting every byte variant would let a depositor move unbounded variants off the deposit budget onto S's caps. A forger holding the slot starves the genuine deposit only until a writer that opens the forged section promotes it by its delta; a junk section opens nowhere (not Bad, just unopened), so no reader deletes a forgery and nothing else frees the slot.

## What a stranger with `keys.0` can still do (accepted; spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read)
- Fill the budget while no delete-key holder of the inbox is online: fires wait at their senders (offered again after back-off) until one comes back. A thing's readers can fill any of its sync IDs the same way.
- Flood without pause: no deposit rate limit is built (spec/sync-ids.md §6 allows one, code 4), so slots go as they free, an honest deposit races him, and each junk blob costs the node its bytes until he stops. Storage bound of promoted fires: issues/stranger-fire-bounds.md.
- Past the bound, flood or not, what goes may be real: a reader's deposited linked blob (filler wrap, no expiry) or record blob (a promoter lacking it logs its own), a commit still pending here, or a blob sealed to a key the node holds only later (a shared space's, whose secrets come from another sync ID). A node holding the head's read epoch holds its history, so readers' valid commits (cards, certificate copies) open and never count.
- A new deposit seed needs a new `keys.0` and step.

## Delivery
- **No placement order** (Tess's decision): projections of a node's own commit (certificate copies) go at once. A receiver holding any part of a role commit applies it and joins the rest on arrival (spec/commits.md §4), so nobody waits for a body that never reaches K's sync ID; copies waiting for the full commit would need a timeout, and receivers would still wait forever past it. Accepted cost: a K-syncer may see a grant before its boxes (an admin may re-seal needlessly).
- **A copy is never judged by its author's chain** (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): the chain moves after a copy (the granter's grant lowered, his node's key), so copies of a settled grant would be deleted for good, and a judge cannot tell a chain that will not come from one on its way. Promotion completes chains. Two writers (or a writer and the author) may log the same chain parts concurrently: the duplicates are may-hold.
- An offer goes only to a peer that may hold the thing or S: a deposit tells its taker that this connection's node sends there.
- **A sync ID fork reaches readers by ticket** (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Key changes): two admins making T independent apart each make a sync ID; once both are seen every writer syncs only the greater, so nothing more is logged under the other, and a reader granted on that side (its ticket and `sync.0` name only the lesser) learns T's sync ID from a ticket alone. So while T owns several sync IDs, a rotation's tickets go to every holder it boxes (`rotation::unreached`), as a re-seal's always do (spec: every re-seal tickets; its commit is wrapped under an epoch the holder may lack). Key events are the only path needed: each side's independence made its own epoch, so a lesser-side holder lacks a box at a current epoch of the greater side and is boxed by a re-seal or by the rotation superseding it before it could read anything new there. A sync ID is never retired, so a forked thing's rotations ticket every boxed holder for life: at most one ticket per box the rotation writes. Rejected: logging under every sync ID T owns (doubles every commit of a forked thing, forever); ticketing only holders that lacked a box at a superseded current epoch (ends with the epoch fork, but rests on that indirect invariant rather than the cause).

## Sealed fires (engine)
- **One fire path** (`flows::inbox::fire`): a node holding and reading the thing (a read secret held, of a current epoch where one is established: a stranded node fires sealed) writes the fire as any commit (deposited by a non-writer as any write) where the setting keeps it (`keeps_fire`), else `fire` returns the setting's refusal (`NotEnabled`, or `Misuse(FIRE_IN_NO_HISTORY)`), never a silent skip: `fire_ticket` drops a refused ticket on purpose, since the holder's readers hold the grant from the holder's own sync ID (spec/sync-judgement.md §2); any other owes a sealed fire (one already owed stays sealed when the node comes to read the thing and the setting does not keep it), a durable row (`node/fires/`, `Indexes::fires`) keyed by BLAKE3 of `[thing, ak, value]` so one fire is owed once. Its commit is built once by the write path unapplied (`engine::write::sealed_commit`: no setting read, no chunks) and kept in the row, so resealing on a key change carries the same commit. A removal notice's value is made when first sealed (`Fire::Removal`), a stranded notice's commit expires `INBOX_LIFETIME` after it is made (`Fire::Expiring`).
- **A ticket fire carries its role commit** (`Fire::Ticket`, built when first sealed): the commit giving the ticket's epoch is wrapped under an epoch it supersedes, which a new holder lacks, so the fire carries its role parts on the thing with their chain, taken as a carried commit (notes/node-closure.md Tickets). A ticket written into an inbox the node reads (its own account's) carries none.
- **Opening**: an entry whose wrap no held secret opens is tried as a sealed fire with each held read KEM key of the thing (`Engine::open_sealed_fire`); one with any delta but one external fire on that thing, or a prefix disagreeing with its header, is bad. Its records, chain commits and commit are taken in one transaction kept only when the fire applies valid and no chain commit is parked or invalid (`Engine::ingest_sealed_fire`): a stranger's records are never learned for a fire that does not check out. Promotion is the ordinary one with the role check waived for external fires, validity deciding. A stranded notice opened by a writer is taken as a Stranded message is (`Engine::take_stranded`).
- **An external fire by a party holding no write is valid only while the thing's current setting enables its signal** (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires; the design: notes/write-path-design.md §4) and needs no path to the thing (`apply::thing_wait`). Judged in `apply::validity` (`Judge::part`); `validity::setting_moved` re-judges a thing's external fires through the one re-judging path (`stale_commits`): on a move of its held settings (held, dropped, purged) the fires of the signals whose enabling changed, so a setting dropped or covered at a transaction's end, after the last re-judge, moves none it leaves enabled; on a setting's validity flipping (mid-pass) all of them. Promotion, taking a ticket, removal notice or stranded notice and `take_fire` follow the fire's validity; what was taken while it was valid stands. A reader's write side refuses an unenabled fire (`Error::NotEnabled`, `check_role`); a sealed fire's sender reads no setting and is bound by none (`commit_or_build`). An account's inbox enables its three signals at creation; every other thing is default-off.
- **External fires keep only by name**: the entry of its own that enables an external signal also says how long its fires stay; the default entry of a history setting never governs one (`RetentionSetting::entry`), so a writer's fire on an external signal no entry names is out of history at arrival (a writer writes none: `flows::inbox::fire`, the write path's keep check), and anyone else's is invalid. A fire out of history (or expired) counts as gone for forgetting (`Engine::forgettable`), so its commit, blob row and the signal's cursor (`leaving::unplaced`) go once node-sync knows no entry of it; no forgotten-`ts` row is kept for a signal.
- **A stranger's chain goes at the pass end that brought its fire**, the fire staying (notes/certificates.md Kept while needed; engine `strangers::*`).
- **Who owes a removal notice** (`flows::inbox::owe_removal_notices`, spec/sync-inbox.md §5): every node that applies the removal and holds what it removes, its writer included. Each role commit applied or joined (or turning resolved) notes the edges it writes (`Core::notices_due`); at the transaction's end each applied write on them taking its holder from read to below read (not a self-removal or finalize, within `ROLE_KEEP` of its `ts`) owes its fire, unless already owed. Not the writer alone: a removal written blind (its writer cut off from a sibling's grant) stands, and with no notice the removed reader never learns, as its fellow holders close S toward it at once. Re-judged only when a write on that edge arrives, so a duplicate costs one fire. The same arrivals on the node's own account's edge on T are the roster-coming trigger of carried commits (notes/node-closure.md Tickets).
- **An independent thing's sync ID is in its `keys.0`** (field 9, written with its genesis step): a sender knows where a fire goes from the public key record alone. An account's public record carries its inbox's record and current `keys.0` (`account_chain`).
- **A removed node holds no deposit seed**: D has its own, fresh in every account rotation (each rotates D's sync role); the account holds none. The inbox is P's thing held by its record edge, so a node removal (P's rotation revoking the epoch the inbox's boxes to P are sealed at) makes those boxes stale and the inbox rotates all four roles, its sync role writing a fresh seed (`rotation::refreshes_deposit`, no exception for D).
- **An independent thing as its own inbox takes what its own setting enables**: core writes none on such a thing, so it takes tickets, removal and stranded notices only once its admins enable them; a fire a reader would write that the setting keeps no history of is not written. So with `removal@~0` off such a thing learns of its removal only from its writers' role commits; with `stranded@~0` off a stranded reader of it stays stranded until a writer connects; with `ticket@~0` off a grant to it reaches its readers only under its own sync ID, or by a ticket fire still held once the thing enables `ticket@~0`. A grant fire to such a thing carries what its own sync ID carries of T because that is in its required set: a granter not syncing it delivers the grant by the fire, and its readers log it there.

## The module's parts
- `offers`: Deposit items: one goes on a connection once its record's digests agreed there, and on each such connection until moot (stamped, dropped, sealed again): taken (answered 0) is done for that connection alone, since the store that took it may lose it while away (a restart, S shredded and synced again) and nothing it shows says so; never at a member being ended (its `until`: nothing past it, and an offer goes now; `Moved::Until` readies S's offers again); one unanswered per connection; the recipient proven on a connection is who its proofs count as now (`opens::counted`, one `Effects` key per connection through `offers::Judge`): a removed node or a closed account key stops its offers at once, one counted again readies its offers there; refused for now (4, 5, 12) backed off from `BACKOFF_MIN_MS` (2 s) doubling to `BACKOFF_MAX_MS` (10 min); one taken ends the others' back-off at that peer and S; 14 parked until the deposit key moves or the receiver's next filter. Who may take an offer: offers.rs module doc.
- `fires`: the engine's owed sealed fires (`Engine::fires_owed`, then `fires_feed`), one `Effects` key each over the read epoch it seals to (`Engine::fire_epoch`): sealed (`Engine::sealed_fire`) and offered from outside the store (`Offers::add_fire`, `Target::Fire`: where S is open, where the account holding a role is proven, or where the filter shows the deposit key), sealed again when that epoch moves, ended on the first answer 0 (`Offers::taken`, `Engine::fire_taken`). A start seals each again: blobs are kept in memory only, the engine's row is the durable part.
- `tickets`: the account's tickets: each thing entering the node closure (`Engine::closure_feed`) is followed by one `Effects` key over `Engine::account_ticket_due`; due, `write_account_ticket`. The account's creation writes the profile's and the inbox's in its own transaction by the same function.
- `held`: every entry through the store's entry feed, each judged by one `Effects` key: peers' deposits read promotes kept from clearing, own deposits offered to store sources until stamped, clearing past `DEPOSITS_WAITING`/`DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES`. Promotion is one judgement, read's to act on (it stamps in place, notes/sync-read.md Own store): `Engine::promotable`, one stamped copy per delta or one bytes-only promotion per commit while it stands (`adds_delta` over `commit_blobs`/`blob_of`: a forged section a writer cannot open never covers the genuine deposit), and the author's chain under S (`chain_under`: of each chain commit S must hold a body of, the parts the chain names held by entries of S stamped or own, never a deposit; read logs those as any certificate, so the wait ends; record blobs are read's certificate blobs, not waited for).
- `register`: the recorded steps (read issues a step once the rotations it registers are logged, notes/sync-read.md) after a store source's step (`Net::head`, the head its stream last named; a Hit names none), each once its stream showed the step's role commits (`Engine::step_needs`); at a source on a recorded step off the recorded path (`OwnedSyncId::tree`), the branch past it in one Register (notes/sync-access.md, Why the gate and the claim). With no head named and nothing registered there, the genesis step goes at once: a source holding no step takes it before the PullReply that follows, so it holds a head before any entry arrives; one holding a step refuses it (9) and its head event moves the plan on. A reader refuses every Register (spec/sync-ids.md §6): its path comes from `sync.0` alone. Plans and progress are per (record, S) (`Net::member_at`, `head` and `present` by the record), followed across the record's connections until S leaves the record or the record goes (`Inbox::record_gone`); a Register goes on the record's connection without waiting for the digests to agree (it needs no secret and no open, spec/sync-ids.md §6, and a departed store is registered on purpose); a plan reads whether a connection holds the record (`Net::conn_of`) only where a step is to go, so a reconnect judges none again and a step due while away goes once the record is connected. A Register out is lost with its connection (its progress cleared, `closed`).
- `serve`: Register and Delete (taken by the store's rules) and Stranded (`Engine::take_stranded`, answered Ok whatever it judges; a store failure answers refused, as Register's does).
- `suspect` (suspect.rs module doc; spec/permissions.md §4 Suspect list, spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Repair; engine side notes/engine-suspects.md):
  - Out: one `Effects` key per flagged aspect (`Engine::suspect`, `record_known`, grouped by `carrying(t)` filtered by `suspect_under`: S's actor may read t, so a reader of S never sees the tid of a closure thing it may not read; under no S whose sources' streams show a body the flag names (`Net::shown`), since that body is being fetched and a digest of the state before it would ask a repair for nothing; dormant flags followed too). Lists per S in memory, owed per (record, S) to reader members only (a store source keeps S unread and learns nothing of S's closure) at a member's open, when S's list moves, and as S's first sync ends (`Store::first_synced`, row `F/`): digests mid first sync come from a partial state and every writer would repair them for nothing. One owed goes on each connection of the record once its digests agreed there, and again there when S's list moves: per connection, not until answered Ok, since an Ok says only that it arrived (a receiver mid its own first sync keeps it in memory, lost with a restart or the connection). A reconnect costs one list per reader member of S with a list. Digests are taken as each list goes, so a write to a flagged aspect sends nothing.
  - In, answered Ok always: judged for the reporter account (`Engine::reader_account`: read or above on S's actor) by `Engine::repair_suspect`, keyed by row `I/s/ reporter thing ak` = the digest of the state last snapshotted for that reporter, so a flood or restart repairs once per state per reporter. The row is dropped only once an entry shows it stale (the reporter's digest is this node's state and the row names another): dropping it on any matching entry let a reporter alternate the true digest with a false one for a snapshot per pair. A row of a thing since forgotten stays until an entry shows it stale (issues/storage-owners.md); an aspect named twice in a message is judged once. Before S's first sync here ended, the peer's latest list per S waits and is judged as it ends.
  - The reporter is an account, not a node: an account adds nodes at will, so per node it could ask one repair per state per node; a snapshot written for one node reaches the account's others under S anyway. A node keeping S unread or a stranger gets no repair, so the rows stay bounded by S's readers times the aspects this node writes.
- `stranded`: this node's own reports (spec/permissions.md §4). Per read S in status's `strandings`: a Stranded message naming the holder with its holder keys (`Engine::holder_keys`) to each connected peer whose proved account writes S's actor (`Engine::writer_of`); with none connected, a stranded notice fired on S's thing (`Engine::fire_stranded`, once per report key, recorded as `To::Fire`). Once per account per report key (the sync key of the step reached, and the holder's current `keys.0` commits); a peer counts as told only on its Ok answer. Durable rows `I/x/` per S reported (its latest key), gone only when S is no longer read here, not when S is merely judged not stranded (also the state before status judged it: a restart, its only source reconnecting), so neither sends a report again. Bound: one row per read S ever reported; a node re-sealed and later stranded at the same key does not report again, which the spec's once-per-key allows. Runs only when the strandings or the proven connections move. A node keeping S unread sends none (it judges no roster).
  - The receiver alone judges a report: the engine takes it only for a sync ID it reads, a current holder of S's actor and a role it may rotate at or below the holder's; the holder keys go in along the holder's authority chain only, each commit kept only if valid (`ingest_valid`); then one ask per (S's actor, holder) keyed by the holder's current keys, met at the end of S's actor's next complete pass (`pass_end`, `Reseal::Asked`: written only where a box differs from what Seal gives). A repeated message asks nothing more; a refused re-seal leaves the ask open, tried at each later pass end; an ask goes once its holder is not current at a pass end, or with S's actor.
- `links`: first contact by link, redeemer's side (links.rs module doc; spec/permissions.md §7). Kept durably (`I/r/`) until the join is held at a source; meanwhile followed in the engine (`Engine::follow_link`: L held and K's sync ID in the sync set from the link text, only while K is unknown here or no other thing owns that sync ID, `sync_ids::actors::followable`). The join is written once `Engine::redeemable` (K listed alive on the target's roster, L on K's, and K's keyset held: K's sync ID brings L's grant before the keyset, which is wrapped under the epoch it supersedes and fetched last), tickets for K and the target in the account root by the account's-tickets rule once the join's strongs are there (`Engine::redeem`). Forgotten:
  - once the join is held at a source of K's sync ID (`Status::held_under`; the join counts as own while L is followed, `Engine::own_commits`), its peers then kept as dial hints (`I/p/` rows, no seed) while `Engine::joined_link` holds (a joiner keeps the link's peers while it holds the target);
  - once the account holds K by a grant (`Engine::joined_link`: a sibling's join; `redeem` then answers `Done` with no row, so redeeming again writes no second join);
  - once the link ended (`Engine::link_ended`);
  - or once the join is not written within the node's follow time (`Policy::follow_link_ms`, `FOLLOW_LINK_MS` 1 h by default: node policy, not the link's; kept from the redeem's start in its `I/r/` row, so a restart keeps the deadline; judged on the engine's memo clock, `Engine::reached`). A joined link has no follow time: the seed is its join's only way to a source and redeeming again joins nothing.
  A given-up redeem's osis future fails `Network` ("no holder answered"), an ended one's `Invalid` (`share.rs` `redeem_over`, read off `Node::redeeming`).

`dial_hints` (the peers of links being redeemed) are the driver's dial candidates; `may_deposit(conn)` keeps a dialed connection open while a deposit to it may land.

## State
What is owed a peer is its record's, named by no connection, and goes on each of the record's connections once the digests agree there (`Net::agreed_on`): an offer until moot, a suspect list once per connection and again when S's list moves. Neither is done for the record by an answer: a store may lose a deposit it took, a receiver a list it answered Ok. What is the connection's (one item out, back-offs, a refusal parked until the next filter, the list sent there) stays the connection's. Keys: P a peer, E the engine or opens, N this node.

| State | Keys | Per | Bound (flood test) | Goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `offers.items`, `loose`, `to` | N's own deposits (the store's entry feed) and E's fires owed | node | own deposited entries and fires owed | stamped, dropped, sealed again, taken (a fire) |
| `offers.conns` (ready, out, back-off, parked, filter seen, record once agreed), `who`, `agreed`, `active`, `wakes`, `parked` | a proven connection (E) | connection | connections × offers (`inbox::a_peer_never_answering_holds_nothing_across_reconnects_spec_sync_inbox_3`) | the connection's end |
| `register` plans, progress | (record, S) of a store member (E) | record | store members | S leaving the record, `record_gone` |
| `register.out`, `wakes` | N's Register requests | connection | one out per (record, S) | the answer, the connection's end |
| `suspects.members` (S → reader records) | member-level `Moved` (E), a record read from its rows at its take | record | reader members | the member closing, `record_gone` |
| `suspects.owed` (record → S → the connection it last went on), `on` | S with a list × its reader members | record | reader members of S with a list (`suspect::tests::a_reconnect_sends_one_list_per_member_with_a_list_spec_permissions_4`) | the list going empty, the member closing, `record_gone` |
| `suspects.waiting` | (S, connection) of a list P sent mid first sync here | connection | one message per member | judged as the first sync ends, the member closing, the connection's end |
| `suspects.lists`, `flags`, `synced`, `first` | E's flags | node | flagged aspects | with the flag |
| `fires`, `tickets`, `held`, `links`, `reports` | E, N's store | node | see the parts above | see the parts above |

Messages: what a peer controls reaches the owed tables only by answering N's offers (0 marks done only what it was asked, on the connection it was asked on; a refusal costs one item per connection, as one never answering); a removed peer's members close or end in the step of the change (opens), so nothing more goes there (`inbox::a_deposit_after_a_removal_while_away_goes_nowhere_spec_sync_inbox_3`); an unproven peer has no record and nothing goes before agreement. A received Suspect is judged for the account the peer proves; a Deposit by the store's rules. Clocks: the monotonic clock for offer and Register back-offs (`next_wake`), the wall clock for a link's follow time. A restart changes nothing a user sees: the owed tables are in memory, so a start owes every offer and list again at each record's next agreed connection, as any reconnect does (a peer that took one before answers 0 again: a duplicate deposit is taken, a repeated list repairs nothing twice, row `I/s/`).

## Durable
Store rows `I/x/` (whom each stranded report went to), `I/s/` (the state last snapshotted per reporter and aspect), `I/r/` (links being redeemed, shredded: they hold the seed) and `I/p/` (joined links' dial hints). Everything else is followed again from the engine and the store at a start.

## Seams
It reads the facade and the store directly (`Here`: engine, status at a settle, store, time, rng) and the network through `inbox::Net` (sessions, opens, streams; `Links` the product's).
