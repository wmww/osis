# Sync: inbox
> Summary: osis-sync's `inbox` module (core/sync/src/inbox/): Deposit offers, sealed fires out, the account's tickets, held entries (deposits promoted, clearing), Register at sources keeping S unread, serving Register/Delete/Stranded, this node's stranded reports, suspect lists out and their repair in, and first contact by link; how each follows change, what is durable, the reasons behind its rules, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-inbox.md, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps and Register, spec/wire.md §10. Principle and parts: the module doc of core/sync/src/inbox/mod.rs (and each part's). Design reasons for deposits and the inbox: notes/inbox.md. Overview: notes/sync.md.

## Parts
- `offers`: Deposit items, one unanswered per connection; the recipient proven on a connection is who its proofs count as now (`opens::counted`, one `Effects` key per connection through `offers::Judge`): a removed node or a closed account key stops its offers at once, an actor counted again readies its offers there; refused for now (4, 5, 12) backed off from `BACKOFF_MIN_MS` (2 s) doubling to `BACKOFF_MAX_MS` (10 min); one taken ends the others' back-off at that peer and S; 14 parked until the deposit key moves or the receiver's next filter. Who may take an offer: offers.rs module doc.
- `fires`: the engine's owed sealed fires (`Engine::fires_owed`, then `fires_feed`), one `Effects` key each over the read epoch it seals to (`Engine::fire_epoch`): sealed (`Engine::sealed_fire`) and offered from outside the store (`Offers::add_fire`, `Target::Fire`: where S is open, where the account holding a role is proven, or where the filter shows the deposit key), sealed again when that epoch moves, ended on the first answer 0 (`Offers::taken`, `Engine::fire_taken`). A start seals each again: blobs are kept in memory only, the engine's row is the durable part.
- `tickets`: the account's tickets (spec/sync-set.md §1): each thing entering the node closure (`Engine::closure_feed`) is followed by one `Effects` key over `Engine::account_ticket_due`; due, `write_account_ticket`. The account's creation writes the profile's and the inbox's in its own transaction by the same function.
- `held`: every entry through the store's entry feed, each judged by one `Effects` key: peers' deposits read promotes kept from clearing, own deposits offered to store sources until stamped, clearing past `DEPOSITS_WAITING`/`DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES`. Promotion (`promotion`, spec/sync-inbox.md §4) is one judgement, read's to act on (it stamps in place, notes/sync-read.md Own store): `Engine::promotable` (applied, a body S must hold, its author's write at its `ts`), one stamped copy per delta or one bytes-only promotion per commit while it stands (`adds_delta` over `commit_blobs`/`blob_of`: a forged section a writer cannot open never covers the genuine deposit), and the author's chain under S (`chain_under`: of each chain commit S must hold a body of, the parts the chain names held by entries of S stamped or own, never a deposit; read logs those as any certificate, so the wait ends; record blobs are read's certificate blobs, not waited for).
- `register`: the recorded steps (read issues a step once the rotations it registers are logged, notes/sync-read.md) after a store source's step (`Net::head`, the head its stream last named; a Hit names none), each once its stream showed the step's role commits (`Engine::step_needs`, a blob of each present at the source). With no head named and nothing registered there, the genesis step goes at once: a source holding no step takes it before the PullReply that follows, so it holds a head before any entry arrives; one holding a step refuses it (9) and its head event moves the plan on.
- `serve`: Register and Delete (taken by the store's rules) and Stranded (`Engine::take_stranded`, answered Ok whatever it judges; a store failure answers refused, as Register's does).
- `suspect` (suspect.rs module doc; spec/permissions.md §4 Suspect list, spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Repair): out, one `Effects` key per flagged aspect (`Engine::suspect`, `record_known`, grouped by `carrying(t)` filtered by `suspect_under`: S's actor may read t, so a reader of S never sees the tid of a closure thing it may not read; under no S whose sources' streams show a body the flag names (`Net::shown`, streams' known presence, stamped, so the key is due again when that changes): it is fetched, and a digest of the state before it would ask a repair for nothing; dormant flags followed too, they show again when their snapshot turns invalid), lists per S in memory, sent to reader sources only (a store source keeps S unread and learns nothing of S's closure) at a member's join, when S's list moves, and as S's first sync ends (`Store::first_synced`, row `F/`, set by streams when a listing from the start was read to its end); digests taken as each list goes, so a write to a flagged aspect sends nothing. In, answered Ok always: judged for the reporter account (`Engine::reader_account`: read or above on S's actor) by `Engine::repair_suspect` (closure, `may_snapshot`, state held, digest), keyed by row `I/s/ reporter thing ak` = the digest of the state last snapshotted for that reporter, so a flood or restart repairs once per state per reporter; the row is dropped only once an entry shows it stale (the reporter's digest is this node's state and the row names another): dropping it on any matching or unjudged entry let a reporter alternate the true digest with a false one for a snapshot per pair. `Engine::state_digest` is a memo per aspect, so an entry judged again costs a lookup; a row of a thing since forgotten stays until an entry shows it stale (issues/storage-follows-groups.md); an aspect named twice in a message is judged once. Before S's first sync here ended, the peer's latest list per S waits (entries it may snapshot) and is judged as it ends.
- `stranded`: this node's own reports (spec/permissions.md §4). Per read S in status's `strandings`: a Stranded message, naming the holder with its holder keys (`Engine::holder_keys`), to each connected peer whose proved account writes S's actor (`Engine::writer_of`); with none connected, a stranded notice fired on S's thing (`Engine::fire_stranded`, once per report key, recorded as `To::Fire`). Once per account per report key (the sync key of the step reached, the head or where the walk stopped, and the holder's current `keys.0` commits); a peer counts as told only on its Ok answer (closed first or answering otherwise: told again at the next proof of a connection); durable rows `I/x/` per S reported (its latest key), gone only when S is no longer read here (at a start and as strandings move): not when S is merely judged not stranded, which is also status not having judged it yet (a restart, its only source reconnecting), so neither sends a report again. Bound: one row per read S ever reported; a node re-sealed and later stranded at the same key (same step, same holder keys) does not report again, which the spec's once-per-key allows. Runs only when the strandings or the proven connections move. A node keeping S unread sends none (it judges no roster).
- `links`: first contact by link, redeemer's side (links.rs module doc): kept durably until the join is held at a source; meanwhile followed in the engine (`Engine::follow_link`: L held and K's sync ID in the sync set from the link text, only while K is unknown here or no other thing owns that sync ID, `sync_ids::actors::followable`); the join is written once `Engine::redeemable` (K listed alive on the target's roster, L on K's, and K's keyset held: K's sync ID brings L's grant before the keyset, which is wrapped under the epoch it supersedes and fetched last), tickets for K and the target in the account root by the account's-tickets rule once the join's strongs are there (`Engine::redeem`); forgotten once the join is held at a source of K's sync ID, its peers then kept as dial hints (`I/p/` rows, no seed) while `Engine::joined_link` holds (spec/permissions.md §7: a joiner keeps the link's peers while it holds the target) (`Status::held_under`; the join, L's, counts as own while L is followed, `Engine::own_commits`), once the account holds K by a grant (`Engine::joined_link`: a sibling's join; `redeem` then answers `Done` with no row, so redeeming again writes no second join), the link ended (`Engine::link_ended`) or the join is not written within the node's follow time (`Policy::follow_link_ms`, `FOLLOW_LINK_MS` 1 h by default: node policy, not the link's; from the redeem's start kept in its `I/r/` row, so a restart keeps the deadline; judged on the engine's memo clock, `Engine::reached`, whose wake is `clock_next`). A joined link has no follow time: the seed is its join's only way to a source (spec/permissions.md §7) and redeeming again joins nothing, so it is followed until the join is held. A given-up redeem's osis future fails `Network` ("no holder answered"), an ended one's `Invalid` (`share.rs` `redeem_over`, read off `Node::redeeming`).

`dial_hints` (the peers of links being redeemed) are the driver's dial candidates; `may_deposit(conn)` keeps a dialed connection open while a deposit to it may land.

## Durable
Store rows `I/x/` (whom each stranded report went to), `I/s/` (the state last snapshotted per reporter and aspect) and `I/r/` (links being redeemed, shredded: they hold the seed). Everything else is followed again from the engine and the store at a start.

## Seams
It reads the facade and the store directly (`Here`: engine, status at a settle, store, time, rng) and the network through `inbox::Net` (sessions, opens, streams; `Links` the product's). Each rule's decision is a plain-value function the tests drive (`held::act_of`, `adds_delta`, `to_clear`, `register::plan`); `Offers` reads the engine through `offers::Judge` (deposit keys, who a peer counts as) and runs over a test `Net`, a test `Judge` and a real store.

## Reasons
- Promotion checks the author's write right at the writer (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): honest readers deposit others' content, so the depositor's right says nothing.
- Bytes-only promotion is one per commit while it stands: promoting every byte variant would let a depositor move unbounded variants off the deposit budget onto S's caps; a forger holding the slot starves the genuine deposit only until a writer that opens the forged section promotes it by its delta. A junk section opens nowhere (not Bad, just unopened), so no reader deletes a forgery; nothing else frees the slot.
- A reader refuses every Register (spec/sync-ids.md §6): its path comes from `sync.0` alone.
- An offer goes only to a peer that may hold the thing or S: a deposit tells its taker that this connection's node sends there.

- A suspect list's reporter is an account, not a node: an account adds nodes at will, so per node one account could ask one repair per state per node it has; a snapshot written for one node reaches the account's others under S anyway. Only an account reading S's actor counts: a node keeping S unread (no flags) or a stranger gets no repair, and the rows stay bounded by S's readers times the aspects this node writes.
- A sender waits for its first sync of S: its digests mid first sync come from a partial state and every writer would repair them for nothing (written into spec/permissions.md §4).
- A stranded report is judged by the receiver alone (spec/permissions.md §4): the engine takes it only for a sync ID it reads, a current holder of the actor and a role it may rotate at or below the holder's; the holder keys go in along the holder's authority chain only, each commit kept only if valid (`ingest_valid`); then one ask per (actor, holder) keyed by the holder's current keys, met at the end of the actor's next complete pass (`pass_end`, `Reseal::Asked`: written only where a box differs from what Seal gives). A peer repeating a message asks nothing more; a refused re-seal leaves the ask open, tried at each later pass end; an ask goes, met or not, once its holder is not current at a pass end, or with the actor (lost or destroyed).
