# Sync: inbox, deposits and fires
> Summary: Deposits and sealed fires, engine and osis-sync `inbox` module (core/sync/src/inbox/): why the deposit budget, readers' deposits, clearing and promotion are as they are, what a stranger with `keys.0` can still do, no placement order; sealed fires in the engine (one fire path, what a fire is sealed to, opening, external fires); what the module's parts add to their docs, its owed state and bounds, what is durable, seams, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-inbox.md, spec/sync-judgement.md §1, §3, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps and Register, spec/wire.md §10. Parts: the module doc of core/sync/src/inbox/mod.rs and each part's (the rules, work and bounds). How a node learns a grant: notes/stubs.md. Neighbours: notes/sync.md (index), notes/sync-access.md (re-seal, claims), notes/certificates.md.

**Principle:** a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry: unstamped, within one budget per sync ID, readable at once by co-readers, stamped once by a writer judging its author's rights, and cleared past a bound by any delete-key holder. Everything owed a peer is its record's and goes on each of its connections once the digests agree there.

## Deposits
- **A reader's deposits** (certificate copies it logs, its may-grant grant, self-removal, card) sit deposited in its own store until a writer promotes them or logs them as its own certificate copies; co-readers pull and read them meanwhile. A reader opens every deposit it takes, a Deposit item included (a peer judging it a source keeping S unread offers one): its bytes are then held, so the writer's stamp on the same blob, arriving later, fetches nothing and would open nothing.
- **An entry under a stamp the source's head does not verify** goes there as a Deposit item of itself (same blob and wrap, signed for that head: `offers::Target::On`), a reader's or a writer's: a reader's copy under a superseded stamp (any author's, ephemeral or durable) after a write-key rotation, with that source's copy gone (an ephemeral entry at each of its restarts; a durable one on a new or wiped node) and no writer holding it, is the only way back; a writer's role commit pinned under a step's parent reaches a store moved past that parent only so (notes/sync-read.md Deposits). A reader deposits a body only while it judges it valid: a demoted writer's writes refused under the rotated key are invalid there too, and deposited they would sit in the budget until cleared. It costs no new room: any deposit-key holder could fill the one budget already. Rejected: taking stamps under a superseded write key on relay (refused, code 9): a removed writer keeps the old write key and could fill S's room. Not built after a head move: issues/sync-unbuilt.md.
- **The budget is local policy**, not a format constant: senders never compute it, they see refusals (code 5). The recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2) is sized per read *holder*, while deposits come per *node*: a dependent thing whose mint admin is a shared space has one read holder, and thirty reader nodes' cards can approach it, so a node keeping S unread for a large read-only audience raises it (`caps^.0` policy `deposit_entries`, `deposit_bytes`). Deposits are unlinkable by design, so per-reader limits are impossible; the peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5) charges only entries that give nothing held.
- A node keeps offering its own deposit after losing read access: other nodes may hold parts of that commit from elsewhere and wait for the rest.
- Relay-only blobs are refused as deposits (code 14): held by nobody, a reader's would be a write of its own, and readers write no ephemeral data.

## Clearing (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Clearing deposits)
- **One rule, every sync ID**, for all a stranger (an inbox) or a reader (any sync ID) can put in the budget: junk, fires, sealed commits that open but are invalid, pending or parked commits, record blobs, far or no expiry. No "this is junk" verdict: a blob sealed to a key this node lacks yet looks like junk and stays while under the bound, and what stays blocks nothing, half the recommended budget being free.
- Cost: under a validity fork (permanent) a clearing node may clear what a writer on the other side would promote, only past the bound; so too an honest deposit pending at the clearing node. A reader's own must-hold body is not lost: it deposits afresh at each judgement finding the source lacks it, so past the bound it cycles until judged.
- Rejected: **age-out** of unstamped entries: at 0 nothing waits for a writer, at infinity the hole stays.

## Promotion (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Promotion)
- **Once per delta**: any reader can seal any commit it holds again, and role commits and cards outside the closure are may-hold (a writer cannot tell which of them another node's chain needs), so every duplicate stamped would be kept for good. Certificate copies are logged as the promoter's own, so a reader's deposited ones are duplicates left to clearing. Rejected: promoting certificate-class deltas on anything upstream of the closure: the reader's own account is upstream, so every edge it writes there and every thing it mints and grants there would be stamped.
- The writer checks the author's write right (`Engine::promotable`), not the depositor's: honest readers deposit others' content. So the cards of things a reader minted with its keys are never promoted.
- Never an entry whose bytes the writer dropped: shredding cleared its stamp, and promoting it would bring back a destroyed thing's record for a newcomer to read.
- **Bytes-only promotion** is one per commit while it stands: promoting every byte variant would let a depositor move unbounded variants off the deposit budget onto S's caps. A forger holding the slot starves the genuine deposit only until a writer that opens the forged section promotes it by its delta; a junk section opens nowhere (not Bad, just unopened), so no reader deletes a forgery and nothing else frees the slot.

## What a stranger with `keys.0` can still do (accepted; spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read)
- Fill the budget while no delete-key holder of the inbox is online: fires wait at their senders (offered again after back-off) until one comes back. A thing's readers can fill any of its sync IDs the same way.
- Flood without pause: no deposit rate limit is built (spec/sync-ids.md §6 allows one, code 4), so slots go as they free, an honest deposit races him, and each junk blob costs the node its bytes until he stops. Storage bound of promoted fires: issues/stranger-fire-bounds.md.
- Past the bound, flood or not, what goes may be real: a reader's deposited linked blob or record blob (a promoter lacking it logs its own), a commit still pending here, or a blob sealed to a key the node holds only later (a shared space's, whose secrets come from another sync ID). A node holding the head's read epoch holds its history, so readers' valid commits open and never count.
- A new deposit seed needs a new `keys.0` and step.

## Delivery
- **No placement order** (Tess): projections of a node's own commit go at once. A receiver holding any part of a role commit applies it and joins the rest on arrival (spec/commits.md §4), so nobody waits for a body that never reaches K's sync ID; copies waiting for the full commit would need a timeout, and receivers would still wait forever past it. Accepted cost: a K-syncer may see a grant before its boxes (an admin may re-seal needlessly).
- **A copy is never judged by its author's standing**: that moves after a copy (the granter's grant lowered, his node's key changed), so copies of a settled grant would be deleted for good, and a judge cannot tell evidence that will not come from evidence on its way. Promotion completes the evidence; duplicates logged concurrently by two writers are may-hold.

## Sealed fires (engine)
- **One fire path** (`flows::inbox::fire`): a node holding and reading the thing at a current epoch (a stranded node fires sealed) writes the fire as any commit where the setting keeps it (`keeps_fire`), else returns the setting's refusal (`NotEnabled`, `Misuse(FIRE_IN_NO_HISTORY)`), never a silent skip. Any other node owes a sealed fire: a durable row (`node/fires/`, `Indexes::fires`) keyed by BLAKE3 of `[thing, ak, value]` so one fire is owed once, its commit built once unapplied (`engine::write::sealed_commit`) and kept, so resealing on a key change carries the same commit. Kinds: `flows::inbox::Fire`.
- An owed sealed fire (`OwedFire`, `Indexes::fires_on` by thing) goes when its thing is forgotten whole (`retention::needed`: nothing held needs it): with no keyset left it could never be sealed again. Not at the thing's release: its certificates may stay, and the fire is still deliverable then.
- **What a fire is sealed to** (spec/crypto.md §3 Read key in force; `sync_ids::read_key_in_force`, `vouched`, `Engine::fire_keys`): the read key an admin writes with each step, its vouch for the epoch of `keys.0`'s read keyset (held current with its secret; the KEM key derived from the secret by the issuer, `sync_ids::issue`), in force only while the read keyset is of that epoch; meanwhile the fire waits, and the next step is due at an admin that vouches for the keyset's epoch. Why: a writer may write the read keyset and only a holder of the epoch's secret can tell a junk KEM key, so sealing to the keyset let any writer make every outside fire open nowhere while its sender saw it delivered; and a sender holding `keys.0` alone can tell a writer's genuine rotation from junk only by an admin's word. Matching by epoch, not KEM key: an epoch names its secret (spec/crypto.md §4 nonce rule), so a writer rewriting the keyset at the read key's epoch changes nothing outsiders seal to. Vouching only for the keyset's own epoch, held current: a step naming the keyset its issuer held while registering its own newest epoch let an older epoch into force over a rotation its issuer held only the `keys.0` delta of (honest: the boxes arrive later; for good once they do, `FK_partial`), or a copy of it (`FK_seen`); and a step's key from before a writer's rotation is never in force over it (stamps instead: FireKey.tla F1). Cost: after a writer's read rotation, outside fires wait for an admin's step (a step per read rotation); a keyset no admin vouches for (a writer's junk, a lockout, a copy of a superseded epoch) holds them pending, visibly, until an admin rotates read or removes the writer, as from a lockout (notes/keys.md Lockouts; issues/unvouched-read-keyset-stalls-fires.md). Not defended: a writer writing a keyset stamped after an honest rotation (the field keeps one keyset), or a copy of an earlier epoch putting the step naming it back in force until the admin steps again: such a writer holds the new epoch (one holding none also can, an open part of the same issue) and may hand fires on anyway. Accepted (Tess): an admin's own fork made without knowing a removal hides the knowing keyset the same way until that admin hears of it; the same property content has (spec/requirements.md Permissions: a removed holder stops reading once a party with the right to change the keys has done so knowing of the removal), so an outside sender holding the knowing rotation still seals to the unknowing fork. Closing it would need every current read keyset in `keys.0` and fires pending through every fork (FireKey.tla `FK_fork`). Rejected: a keyset whose KEM key its secret does not derive counting for nothing (a verdict hanging on who holds the secret); sealing to the step's key whatever was written since (the party a writer's rotation left out reads the fires until an admin steps, against spec/requirements.md Permissions); fires sealed to the admin keyset and re-encrypted at promotion (every fire waits for an admin, and promotion stops being in place). Tests: `rotation::tests_stranded::a_writers_junk_read_keyset_swallows_no_outside_fire_*`, `a_junk_kem_key_at_the_steps_epoch_holds_no_fire_back_*`, `an_outside_fire_waits_for_the_step_after_a_writers_rotation_*`, `a_step_naming_a_keyset_its_issuer_lacks_puts_no_old_key_in_force_*`, `a_removed_writers_keyset_holds_no_fire_back_*`; model core/test/tla/rotation/FireKey.tla.
- **Opening** (`Engine::open_sealed_fire`, `Engine::ingest_sealed_fire`): everything a fire brings is taken in one transaction kept only when the fire applies valid and no chain commit is parked or invalid, so a stranger's records are never learned for a fire that does not check out. Promotion waives the role check for external fires, validity deciding. A stranded notice opened by a writer is taken as a Stranded message (`Engine::take_stranded`).
- **A fire's pages travel with it** (spec/wire.md §9, `osis_blob::fire::Sealed::pages`): the sealed payload carries every blob its commit's deltas link; the sender moves them from its blob store into the owed fire's row (`flows::inbox::built_pages`, `OwedFire::pages`), the receiver stores them in the transaction taking the fire and refuses a payload holding a blob the deltas do not link (`ingest_sealed_fire`). A held fire's value is read whole, its pages joined (`flows::inbox::fire_value`).
- **A sealed fire ahead of the clock waits** (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): `ingest_sealed_fire` keeps it pending `Reason::Drift` as any commit, its entry carrying it with the chain and records it brought; what it carries is taken once it applies valid (`Indexes::fires_due`, `take_carried`, `inbox::carries`). A role commit held before its fire gives its secrets when the fire comes (`flows::retake`). A stranded notice that applies at its opening is acted on there whatever the thing's history keeps; one that waited, only where its fire is held.
- **What a sealed fire brings is judged at once** (`Core::fire_brings`, `note_pass_due`, `Engine::settle_lost`): a deposit is taken outside any pass and wants none (a pass is wanted by role arrivals on a sync ID's own things), so what it brought (its signer's chain, the things its commit names) is judged as a lost need is, with no pass in flight. Any other arrival waits for the pass that brought it.
- **External fires** (spec/permissions.md §5.2; design: notes/write-path-design.md §4) need no path to the thing (`apply::thing_wait`). Judged in `apply::validity` (`Judge::part`); `validity::setting_moved` re-judges through the one re-judging path (`Core::stale_commits`): on a move of held settings only the fires of signals whose enabling changed, on a setting's validity flipping all of them. What was taken while a fire was valid stands. A reader's write side refuses an unenabled fire (`check_role`); a sealed fire's sender reads no setting (`commit_or_build`). An account's inbox enables its two signals at creation, each kept `INBOX_LIFETIME`; every other thing is default-off.
- **External fires keep only by name** (`RetentionSetting::entry`: the default entry never governs one), so a writer's fire on a signal no entry names is out of history at arrival and anyone else's invalid. A fire out of history or expired counts as gone for forgetting (`Engine::forgettable`): its commit, blob row and the signal's cursor (`leaving::unplaced`) go once node-sync knows no entry of it; no forgotten-`ts` row is kept for a signal.
- **A stranger's chain goes at the pass end that brought its fire**, the fire staying (notes/certificates.md Kept while needed; engine `strangers::*`).
- **An independent thing's sync ID is in its `keys.0`** (field 9): a sender knows where a fire goes from the public key record alone.
- **A removed node holds no deposit seed**: D has its own, fresh in every account rotation. The inbox is held by P's record edge, so a node removal makes its boxes to P stale and the inbox rotates all four roles, its sync role writing a fresh seed (`rotation::refreshes_deposit`, no exception for D).

## The module's parts: beyond their docs
- `offers`: an offer counts the recipient proven now (`opens::counted` through `offers::Judge`); `Moved::Until` readies S's offers again after a member's end.
- `held`: a refused peer deposit (a sealed fire applying invalid or with a part waiting) is opened again only when what its rolled-back ingest read moves (`memo::Undoable`, notes/engine.md Stamps). One taken (opened to a commit) is never opened there again: its commit forgotten since, it waits for read secrets as any entry of a read S (`store::Follow`); opened again there, retention and the judgement would take and forget it every settle round. Promotion is read's to act on (it stamps in place, notes/sync-read.md Own store); the commit's evidence counts under S only from stamped or own entries, never a deposit (`evidence_under`); record blobs are read's certificate blobs, not waited for. A forged section a writer cannot open never covers the genuine deposit (`adds_delta`).
- `register`: plans and progress are per (record, S) (`Net::member_at`). A Register goes on the record's connection without waiting for digests to agree: it needs no secret and no open, and a removed holder's store is registered on purpose (it moves past its keys). A reader refuses every Register (spec/sync-ids.md §6): its path comes from `sync.0` alone. A Register out is lost with its connection (`closed`). The branch rule: notes/sync-access.md, Why the gate and the claim.
- `serve`: a store failure answers refused, for Stranded as for Register.
- `stranded`, receiver side (engine): taken only for a sync ID it reads, a current holder of S's actor and a role it may rotate at or below the holder's; the holder keys go in along the holder's authority chain only, each commit kept only if valid (`ingest_valid`); then one ask per (S's actor, holder) keyed by the holder's current keys, met at the end of S's actor's next complete pass (`Reseal::Asked`: written only where a box differs from what Seal gives). A refused re-seal leaves the ask open; an ask goes once its holder is not current, or with S's actor. Sender bound: one `I/x/` row per read S ever reported; a node re-sealed and later stranded at the same key does not report again, which the spec's once-per-key allows.
- `links`: following needs K unknown here or no other thing owning its sync ID (`sync_ids::actors::followable`). Redeem's osis future fails `Network` ("no holder answered") when given up, `Invalid` when ended (`share.rs` `redeem_over`, read off `Node::redeeming`). `dial_hints` (links being redeemed, joined links' and taken offers' peers, all durable rows) are the driver's dial candidates; `may_deposit(conn)` keeps a dialed connection open while a deposit to it may land.

## State
What is owed a peer is its record's, named by no connection, and goes on each of the record's connections once the digests agree there (`Net::agreed_on`): an offer until moot. It is not done for the record by an answer: a store may lose a deposit it took. What is the connection's (one item out, back-offs, a refusal parked until the next filter) stays the connection's. Keys: P a peer, E the engine or opens, N this node.

| State | Keys | Per | Bound (flood test) | Goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `offers.items`, `loose`, `to` | N's own deposits and E's fires owed; entries read gives on a connection (`Target::On`) | node | own deposited entries and fires owed; read's `Act::Deposit`s carried on connections up | stamped, dropped, sealed again, taken (a fire); an `On` offer taken on, or the end of, each of its connections (read owns it across connections) |
| `offers.conns`, `who`, `agreed`, `active`, `wakes`, `parked` | a proven connection (E) | connection | connections × offers (`inbox::a_peer_never_answering_holds_nothing_across_reconnects_spec_sync_inbox_3`) | the connection's end |
| `register` plans, progress | (record, S) of a store member (E) | record | store members | S leaving the record, `record_gone` |
| `register.out`, `wakes` | N's Register requests | connection | one out per (record, S) | the answer, the connection's end |
| `fires`, `held`, `links`, `reports` | E, N's store | node | the parts' docs | the parts' docs |

Messages: what a peer controls reaches the owed tables only by answering N's offers (0 marks done only what it was asked, on the connection it was asked on; a refusal costs one item per connection, as one never answering); a removed peer's members close or end in the step of the change, so nothing more goes there (`inbox::a_deposit_after_a_removal_while_away_goes_nowhere_spec_sync_inbox_3`); an unproven peer has no record and nothing goes before agreement. Clocks: monotonic for back-offs (`next_wake`), wall for a link's follow time. A restart changes nothing a user sees: the owed tables are in memory, so a start owes every offer again at each record's next agreed connection, as any reconnect does (a duplicate deposit is taken).

## Durable
Store rows `I/x/` (whom each stranded report went to), `I/r/` (links being redeemed, shredded: they hold the seed) and `I/p/` (joined links' dial hints); the engine's `node/fires/`. Everything else is followed again from the engine and the store at a start.

## Seams
mod.rs's module doc: the facade, the store and `inbox::Net`; each rule's decision a plain-value function the tests drive.

## Not built
issues/sync-unbuilt.md (inbox): clearing at sources keeping S unread (needs streams' `known` to carry each entry's deposited flag and blob size). issues/sync-link-first-contact.md: K's commits do not apply at a redeemer.
