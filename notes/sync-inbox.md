# Sync: inbox
> Summary: osis-sync's `inbox` module (core/sync/src/inbox/): deliveries owed, Deposit offers, held entries (inbox blobs taken, deposits promoted, clearing), Register at sources keeping S unread, serving Register/Delete/Stranded, this node's stranded reports, suspect lists out and their repair in, and first contact by link; how each follows change, what is durable, the reasons behind its rules, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-inbox.md, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps and Register, spec/wire.md §9-§10. Principle and parts: the module doc of core/sync/src/inbox/mod.rs (and each part's). Design reasons for deposits and the inbox: notes/inbox.md. Overview: notes/sync.md.

## Parts
- `deliveries`: inbox blobs owed. The engine's `deliveries` at a start, then `delivery_feed`; each judged by `delivery_taken` (the engine's commit sign: a commit by the recipient on a named thing, stamped after the delivery's and opened under a current read epoch, `labeling::opened::opened_epochs`) or an open of a sync ID the blob names (`delivery_names`) on a connection where the recipient is proven, or, once logged stamped, by this node reading the inbox it logged it under (`took_own`; a deposited one would leave its Deposit item moot: it took it itself; a redeemer's join to its own account, a link's grant to K); seen taken it ends (`Engine::delivered`). Logged stamped by a writer of the inbox, else deposited and offered; sealed again at the blob's expiry (a timer per delivery) or once the inbox moved, never by walking what was sent. Signing keys from `Engine::signers(s, actor)`.
- `offers`: Deposit items, one unanswered per connection; refused for now (4, 5, 12) backed off from `BACKOFF_MIN_MS` (2 s) doubling to `BACKOFF_MAX_MS` (10 min); one taken ends the others' back-off at that peer and S; 14 parked until the deposit key moves or the receiver's next filter. Who may take an offer: offers.rs module doc.
- `held`: every entry through the store's entry feed, each judged by one `Effects` key: inbox blobs taken (and stamped in place by a writer of the inbox), deposits promoted (`Engine::promotable`), own deposits offered to store sources, clearing past `DEPOSITS_WAITING`/`DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES`. A deposit's commit from `blob_commit`, else opened once (`open_entry`); one stamped copy per delta, or one bytes-only promotion per commit while it stands (`adds_delta` over `commit_blobs`/`blob_of`: a forged section a writer cannot open never covers the genuine deposit). Decision logic only while `log_chain` is unbuilt: nothing is promoted yet.
- `register`: the recorded steps (read issues a step once the rotations it registers are logged, notes/sync-read.md) after a store source's step (`Net::head`, the head its stream last named; a Hit names none), each once its stream showed the step's role commits (`Engine::step_needs`, a blob of each present at the source). With no head named and nothing registered there, the genesis step goes at once: a source holding no step takes it before the PullReply that follows, so it holds a head before any entry arrives; one holding a step refuses it (9) and its head event moves the plan on.
- `serve`: Register and Delete (taken by the store's rules) and Stranded (`Engine::take_stranded`, answered Ok whatever it judges; a store failure answers refused, as Register's does).
- `suspect` (suspect.rs module doc; spec/permissions.md §4 Suspect list, spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Repair): out, one `Effects` key per flagged aspect (`Engine::suspect`, `record_known`, grouped by `carrying(t)` filtered by `suspect_under`: S's actor may read t, so a reader of S never sees the tid of a closure thing it may not read; dormant flags followed too, they show again when their snapshot turns invalid), lists per S in memory, sent to reader sources only (a store source keeps S unread and learns nothing of S's closure) at a member's join, when S's list moves, and as S's first sync ends (`Store::first_synced`, row `F/`, set by streams when a listing from the start was read to its end); digests taken as each list goes, so a write to a flagged aspect sends nothing. In, answered Ok always: judged for the reporter account (`Engine::reader_account`: read or above on S's actor) by `Engine::repair_suspect` (closure, `may_snapshot`, state held, digest), keyed by row `I/s/ reporter thing ak` = the digest of the state last snapshotted for that reporter, so a flood or restart repairs once per state per reporter; the row is dropped only once an entry shows it stale (the reporter's digest is this node's state and the row names another): dropping it on any matching or unjudged entry let a reporter alternate the true digest with a false one for a snapshot per pair. `Engine::state_digest` is a memo per aspect, so an entry judged again costs a lookup; a row of a thing since forgotten stays until an entry shows it stale (issues/suspect-rows-of-gone-things.md); an aspect named twice in a message is judged once. Before S's first sync here ended, the peer's latest list per S waits (entries it may snapshot) and is judged as it ends.
- `stranded`: this node's own reports (spec/permissions.md §4). Per read S in status's `strandings`: a Stranded message, naming the holder with its holder keys (`Engine::holder_keys`), to each connected peer whose proved account writes S's actor (`Engine::writer_of`); with none connected, a stranded notice (`Engine::stranded_notice`) logged under each admin's inbox like a delivery (`log_inbox_blob`: stamped where this node writes that inbox, else deposited and offered). Once per account and per admin per report key (the sync key of the step reached, the head or where the walk stopped, and the holder's current `keys.0` commits); a peer counts as told only on its Ok answer (closed first or answering otherwise: told again at the next proof of a connection); durable rows `I/x/` per S reported (its latest key), gone only when S is no longer read here (at a start and as strandings move): not when S is merely judged not stranded, which is also status not having judged it yet (a restart, its only source reconnecting), so neither sends a report again. Bound: one row per read S ever reported; a node re-sealed and later stranded at the same key (same step, same holder keys) does not report again, which the spec's once-per-key allows. Runs only when the strandings or the proven connections move. A node keeping S unread sends none (it judges no roster).
- `links`: first contact by link, redeemer's side (links.rs module doc): kept durably until the join is held at a source; meanwhile followed in the engine (`Engine::follow_link`: L held and K's sync ID in the sync set from the link text, only while K is unknown here or no other thing owns that sync ID, `labeling::actors::followable`); the join is written once `Engine::redeemable` (K listed alive on the target's roster, L on K's, and K's keyset held: K's sync ID brings L's grant before the keyset, which is wrapped under the epoch it supersedes and fetched last), filing the target under D in its transaction (`Engine::redeem`, spec/sync-set.md §4 A redeem is an accept); forgotten once the join is held, the link ended (`Engine::link_ended`) or the join is not written within the node's follow time (`Policy::follow_link_ms`, `FOLLOW_LINK_MS` 1 h by default: node policy, not the link's; from the redeem's start kept in its `I/r/` row, so a restart keeps the deadline; judged on the engine's memo clock, `Engine::reached`, whose wake is `clock_next`). A given-up redeem's osis future fails `Network` ("no holder answered"), an ended one's `Invalid` (`share.rs` `redeem_over`, read off `Node::redeeming`).

Grant copies (spec/sync-inbox.md §5) are no part of this module: the engine lists them among the inbox's certificates (notes/sync-facade.md, Certificate bodies) and read logs them as any certificate.

`dial_hints` (granting nodes of inbox blobs held, sharer nodes of links being redeemed) are the driver's dial candidates; `may_deposit(conn)` keeps a dialed connection open while a deposit to it may land.

## Taking inbox blobs
- `held` calls `take_inbox` inside an `Effects` settle. A take that writes what it read would be due again at once, every round of every step; `take_inbox` writes nothing when taking again changes nothing (tests/it/keep.rs `taking_an_inbox_blob_again_moves_nothing_spec_sync_inbox_2`).
- A take records every read of its ingest, so a blob whose commit applied is taken once: `held` keeps it in `taken` and, while the commit is held (`holds_commit`), judges it from that alone.
- A recipient below read on every thing named gets a boxes payload (`deliveries::boxes_blob`, spec/wire.md §9): its own boxes and the things' sync IDs, signed by the author only for a grant (`Owed::grant`, set by `flows::edges::deliver_grant`; `author_signer`). Nothing proves the author's key with nothing held: the signature names the granting account only where the recipient holds the author's key (`apply::open_key`).
- A boxes payload, or a commit payload whose boxes give the recipient sync alone, is never taken: the engine holds it as an offer (`records::Offer`, rows `io/`, `Taken::Offered`), its secrets held while it is, its sync IDs kept only by an accept (`mark_acted` on a listing, `Engine::accept_invite` where the thing is listed already: `keep_offered`, every offered one, whatever other thing the record keeps it for; a forger naming a kept thing's sync ID under a thing of its own takes nothing from it, since a sync ID is synced for the things a step ties it to, `sync::kept_things`), its dial hint given while every sync ID it names is kept. No stamp vouches for it, so it is never stamped in place; clearing counts it with the invite requests (last).
- A stamp vouches for an inbox blob (a commit payload) only under the inbox head's write key (`Store::stamp_verifies`): a node removed from the account keeps earlier steps' keys. A grant's signature names its granter only at an epoch open here (`apply::open_key`): a closed epoch's key is a removed node's too.
- An accept keeps the sync IDs of every offer held for the thing, a forger's included (spec/sync-hostile.md §2: storage under that sync ID within the caps): keeping only one would let a forger's offer arriving first displace the genuine grant until a second accept.
- Why no granter check: a node keeping a thing unread knows no role holders (spec/requirements.md), so a carried authority chain would leak the roster and still miss a removal, and no tid commits to a sync ID (a plain thing gains secrets later). So the account's own accept is the only anchor, and every later secret is checked against the node's own path (spec/sync.md §3; core/test/tla/net/NetTrust.tla).
- An entry whose prefix no inbox blob has is told from its bytes before any key is read (`Prefix::inbox_shaped`): an inbox is its actor's home sync ID, so it holds the actor's commits too, which no inbox key opens.

## Durable
Store rows `I/d/` (per delivery the blob sent and the open seen, so a restart does not seal again), `I/x/` (whom each stranded report went to), `I/s/` (the state last snapshotted per reporter and aspect) and `I/r/` (links being redeemed, shredded: they hold the seed). Everything else is followed again from the engine and the store at a start.

## Seams
It reads the facade and the store directly (`Here`: engine, status at a settle, store, time, rng) and the network through `inbox::Net` (sessions, opens, streams; `Links` the product's). Each rule's decision is a plain-value function the tests drive (`held::act_of`, `adds_delta`, `to_clear`, `register::plan`); `Offers` takes its deposit keys as a function and runs over a test `Net` and a real store.

## Reasons
- Promotion checks the author's write right at the writer (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): honest readers deposit others' content, so the depositor's right says nothing.
- Bytes-only promotion is one per commit while it stands: promoting every byte variant would let a depositor move unbounded variants off the deposit budget onto S's caps; a forger holding the slot starves the genuine deposit only until a writer that opens the forged section promotes it by its delta. A junk section opens nowhere (not Bad, just unopened), so no reader deletes a forgery; nothing else frees the slot.
- A reader refuses every Register (spec/sync-ids.md §6): its path comes from `sync.0` alone.
- An accept is written under home alone (spec/sync-set.md §4) so no other holder learns which accounts sync the thing; it is a subgroup, never an item, since an item's subgroups are outside the closure.
- An offer goes only to a peer that may hold the inbox or S: a deposit tells its taker that this connection's node sends to that inbox.

- A sign of taking counts only where the recipient could not show it without the delivery's grant: an open or a commit under a read epoch current here. A later `ts` proves nothing (the HLC follows wall time), and a member removed and granted again still holds its old epoch, so a write it made before taking the grant would end the delivery with the recipient unable to reach the grant any other way (it cannot open the thing's sync ID).

- A suspect list's reporter is an account, not a node: an account adds nodes at will, so per node one account could ask one repair per state per node it has; a snapshot written for one node reaches the account's others under S anyway. Only an account reading S's actor counts: a store host (no flags, it cannot read) or a stranger gets no repair, and the rows stay bounded by S's readers times the aspects this node writes.
- A sender waits for its first sync of S: its digests mid first sync come from a partial state and every writer would repair them for nothing (written into spec/permissions.md §4).
- A stranded report is judged by the receiver alone (spec/permissions.md §4): the engine takes it only for a sync ID it reads, a current holder of the actor and a role it may rotate at or below the holder's; the holder keys go in along the holder's authority chain only, each commit kept only if valid (`ingest_valid`); then one ask per (actor, holder) keyed by the holder's current keys, met at the end of the actor's next complete pass (`pass_end`, `Reseal::Asked`: written only where a box differs from what Seal gives). A notice taken again as its reads move, or a peer repeating a message, asks nothing more; a refused re-seal leaves the ask open, tried at each later pass end; an ask goes, met or not, once its holder is not current at a pass end, or with the actor (lost or destroyed).

## Not built
