# Engine: the epoch rule
> Summary: How the engine keeps spec/permissions.md §5.4-§5.5 (`flows/account.rs`, `lists.rs`, `signing.rs`): the filter, name rows, sides (first revocation seen), `JudgedEpochs`, what a node writes.

## Revoked (principle)
- A revocation ends the standing of what was signed at the epochs it closes; a rotation carrying no removal only re-keys (as a thing's key rotation ends no earlier write). An epoch is revoked once a counting closer carries a removal (`AccountView::carrying`: the header's `revokes`, read alike at every node; at a node of P a rotation whose sealed `devices` field belies it is left out, `Core::devices_read`). A removal exists only in the plain rotation carrying it (`Engine::remove_device` → `signing::rotate_with`), so nothing acts on D apart from its rotation. Commits at a closed unrevoked epoch count unnamed (`Epochs::unnamed` is `Counts`); names matter only at revoked epochs. One notion with sync-set §3: `AccountView::revoked` (`Engine::account_key_revoked`) is the signing keys of revoked epochs.
- Why: an outsider cannot tell a thing list not yet arrived from one never written; un-counting a granter's commits on a merge or plain rotation dropped the outsider's role, keys and sync ID before the list could come (`an_outsider_keeps_the_accounts_word_across_a_rotation_revoking_no_one_*`). With a removal it still does; sync's leave keeps the sync ID through a pass with a source meanwhile (`Engine::uncounted_role_on`, notes/sync-leave.md).
- Writers still name everything (lists, listings, addenda) whatever the revocation state, since a later removal revokes earlier epochs: `nameable` skips only commits a held counting word names (`signing::named_by_a_word`), never ones counting unrevoked.
- A sibling holding a rotation header-only (a Prove) holds the account's `keys.0` in part: `deposit_seed_of` is None while a valid part of the owner's `keys.0` is missing, so no step is issued from the older seed (two siblings stepped past each other's deposit keys for good). Taking a seed up redelivers the account's own commits with a missing part on it (`custody::keep`).

## The filter
- Runs first in each re-judge pass (`refresh_epochs`). A role commit that does not count stays held but no timeline reads it (`Held::set_counted`, left out of `HeldSet::edge_commits`). Every other part of a commit that does not count is invalid in `Judge::part` (`created.0` aside), but a plain part a counting addendum saves (`Vouching::saves`).
- Commits held nowhere (a relay-only signal, an inbox request) are judged whole by `Core::counts_at_epoch`.
- Addenda: one least fixpoint per thing over every author (perm `Timeline::vouching`, fed each addendum's standing). Monotone: addenda save plain content only, which neither a timeline nor an epoch reads. Tests: perm `content::addenda_fixpoint_runs_through_closed_epochs_*`, `rotation::tests_account::closed_epoch_write_falls_and_a_witness_saves_it_*`.

## Names
- Thing lists (`Engine::hold_lists`; `Engine::ingest_with_lists` holds a batch's list blobs before its rotation applies, or as they come for one held already, e.g. header-only from a Prove) and `listing.0` deltas (`lists::note_listing`) become name rows (`tn/` commit‖thing‖source, `tb/` source‖commit‖thing). Certs stay for good (`tl/`), as role commits do.
- A name is judged once its source is held: a rotation's lists are kept before it applies (`ingest_with_lists`, own writes) and it may wait for a head not here, so `put_names` marks a named commit due only when its source is held (a listing is indexed before its names are noted); a rotation's arrival judges what it names (`refresh_epochs`, a rotation whose count moved), whatever arrived while it waited.
- D has no role timeline of its own for membership: its roster is the chain's (`Core::devices_roster`), grants and removals on D are refused, and D re-keys only inside P's rotations (`signing::commit_rotation`). So no commit on D waits on a list: a removed node's later word as P is a rotation its removal hits.
- A list for a rotation not held is not kept: sync keeps the entry as `Kind::List { held: false }` until the rotation arrives.
- Inner and merge signatures are kept per role commit at an epoch, as facts of the commit whatever is known when it applies (`ix.role_sigs`, `note_role_sigs`); merge signatures are verified at chain build (`osis_commit::verify_merge`). Not persisted: an open reads them from the role commits' records, one record read per account role commit (issues/role-sigs-open-reads.md). Rejected: a `node/role sigs` table (one store page per role commit applied: `scale::role_history_join_is_linear` went past linear); noting only commits `devices_of` names as D's at apply (order-dependent: a rebuild from the records differed, `order::held_set_decides`).

## Sides (`osis_perm::chain`, `AccountView`)
- Principle (Tess): the first revocation seen holds, and nothing switches back by itself. A node places the held rotations in the order it came to hold each with what it needs (its line and what it takes: receipt ms of the latest, one ms's by ID; the user's chosen line first), ties ancestors first (by depth, never by ID alone: one ms's ID order would place a node's second removal before its first, and with what a revocation stands with read in order the node would disregard its own removal: `a_rotation_is_placed_after_its_line_whatever_its_id`), and decides each from what was placed before (spec/permissions.md §5.4). At a node of the account what the chosen line stands with (what a rotation on it takes, with its line) is placed with it, so a choice of a line standing with another revocation holds (`a_chosen_line_is_placed_with_what_it_takes`); outside, the line alone (a rotation it takes may be beside it, and outside `takes` decides only holding). Model: roles-model `custody` (README there); the engine's nodes, readers and honest policy are checked against it by `rotation::tests_custody` (module doc there: what is compared, what the mirror leaves to the engine).
- Outside the account every rotation beside a followed one ends it: a removed device's fork looks like any honest rotation, so a reader that held the removal first never counts the device's later forks. A node of the account reads `osis_perm::Devices` from the held rotations alone (`Core::devices_read`): each plain rotation's minter (its inner signature), the adds and removes its sealed `devices` field carries (opened with the seed of the epoch it is signed at), each merge's certifiers, and the creation device (the creation nonce, spec/crypto.md §3). It takes revocations first-seen as a consistent set, following what they leave standing: its devices' rotations made at once all count, a removed device's plain fork never outweighs its removal, of two revocations hitting each other the one held first holds. No input of the side is D's roster, so a fight is not circular.
- Who is a node on a line, what a removal hits and when a certificate stands: notes/account-forks-design.md (Roster and D, Merges); `osis_perm::Chain::roster`, `nodes_of`, `standing_certifiers`. An addition by a removed node is in a rotation its removal hits, so forged or backdated additions need no rule of their own.
- A device stands with every revocation it took (Tess's ruling applied: a device that took the other side's revocation is the other side, and the honest side treats its line exactly as it treats the thief's). Each rotation's header `takes` the revocations its maker took that are not on its line (`rotate_in`, from `AccountView::taken`); a node of the account reads a rotation as standing with the revocations on its line and those any rotation on its line takes, takes a revocation only if every other one it stands with is taken, and follows a rotation only if every one it stands with is taken. A rotation is held only once what it takes is held (placed after it), at every node: outside the account too, or a removed device's rotation taking a rotation no one holds would stay pending at the account's nodes, never answered, while readers outside it follow it unwarned (`a_rotation_taking_what_no_one_holds_is_held_nowhere_spec_permissions_5_4`). So a device that took the thief's revocation first, whose seed the honest side may not be able to tell apart (it signs at its own line, boxing the thief), is that side, whoever signs, for as long as its user stays, and once its user switches its old-side rotations stay disregarded at every node, in any order (`a_rotation_taking_a_revocation_not_taken_here_is_the_other_side`, `a_rotation_taking_the_thiefs_revocation_is_that_side_spec_permissions_5_4`). Outside the account `takes` decides nothing but when a rotation is held. Tradeoff: the header shows outsiders which rotations a device took as revocations (IDs only, not whom they remove); hiding it in the inner signature would let anyone holding the epoch's old inner secret rewrite it.
- Rejected: reading a device's side from what its rotations supersede (a line at odds with a taken revocation's: one disowns one on the other). A device that took the thief's revocation before it held the honest removal disowns nothing of the honest line, so its rotations (boxing the thief) were followed and merged by the honest side, round after round while its user stayed; at odds with any line followed before (not only a taken revocation's) splits honest nodes that placed a switching device's old-side rotation and the merge it disowned in different orders. Rejected too: reading which nodes a rotation's seed reaches (D's boxes at its `under` epoch): a node of P cannot open D's rotation commits of a device that dropped it.
- Custody (`flows::custody`, spec/crypto.md §9): a rotation's header boxes its seed to each device on its maker's roster (`seed_boxes`; a pairing's also hands the new device every seed held). `take_boxes` opens a box to this device as the rotation applies, counting or not, header-only included, and `keep` records the seed (`records::SEEDS`, the custody truth: a rolled-back transaction, a Prove's trial among them, keeps no record) and takes it up. So inner signatures open with every held seed and the chain is a function of held commits alone: no seed fixpoint. A node signs as P only on D's roster and holding its open epoch's seed (`Core::writer`, `signing::signs_for`); a rotation boxes the roster of the line it makes (`Chain::roster_joining`), so it never boxes a node that line removed. At the end of a complete pass of D's sync IDs it re-seals each held head's seed to the roster devices no counting box is tagged for (`custody::reseal_seeds`, header field 16). Every held seed opens boxes to P, stale ones included (`Core::account_keys_by_name`).
- A merge is pending until every fork it names is held: a line is whole when placed, so a fork arriving later never reopens a decision.
- Honest forks are owed a merge (`signing::merge_heads`, `rotations_due`): readers outside follow one head only. A merge carries only the join: its seed is `kdf("merge seed", tips' seeds)` (`custody::derive_merges` derives it wherever every tip's seed is held) and its epoch ID a function of its tips (`osis_commit::merge_epoch`), so nodes merging the same heads at once make certificates of one epoch, not another fork (`merges_made_at_once_settle_in_lockstep_*`). A node lacking a tip's seed leaves the merge to one holding it.
- Every rotation a node of the account disregards with no rotation on its line a counting rotation rejects, and that no rotation of its own supersedes, is superseded unnamed by its next rotation (`unanswered`), so every reader that followed it is told. Another node's superseding rotation answers nothing by itself: signed at the epoch the disregarded one is signed at, it rejects nothing of it (contested from below), and readers following it would never be told (`a_rotation_superseded_without_a_rejection_is_answered_spec_permissions_5_5`); once per node, as a node's own answer may be so contested too. One answer per line: a reader following the line holds that rejection already; answering every rotation has two sides answer each other's answers for as long as both stay online (roles-model `forks-every-answer`). The line test reads rejections, not supersedes: a removed node holding the answer's epoch key (an unaware sibling's head boxed to it) signs there accepting its answered fork, which puts that fork on a line through the answer's epoch and ends the rejection; its new rotation is then answered (`a_rotation_undoing_an_answer_is_answered_spec_permissions_5_5`). The answer cannot be undone again: its epoch is not boxed to the removed node.
- Warning: `Chain::contested` (disregarded tips in a rejection with a counting rotation) → `AccountView::doubt` → `Event::AccountDisputed` once on its rise, `Engine::account_disputes`, osis `Dispute::Account` (versions: the preferred head and those tips). Told at the account's own nodes too.
- The user's choice is kept in `sides::Sides::accounts` (`records::SIDES`), per node; the default is recomputed from the receipt order, so nothing is recorded for it. Not shared across the reader's devices: issues/account-side-shared.md.
- Rejected for the account's nodes (each failed in `forks`): judging as outsiders (two devices' rotations made at once split them); placing with a conflict relation (a plain fork held first outweighed its maker's removal and split the honest devices); revocations that drop earlier ones and revive (oscillates when a removed node's revocation hits its own line).
- Rejected: one followed head with a designated merger (the node with the lowest authorized head): listings, the warning and naming waited on one device that may be gone for good.
- A closer ends an epoch for the cutoff only if it descends from it or carries (a counting rotation on its line) the removal of a node the epoch's line names no removal of (`signing::ends`, `Departures`; at a witness every closer ends it). Without that, every write at a head made after its sibling arrived would be nameable only by its own writer, so lost with a gone writer (`the_lowest_heads_node_gone_for_good_leaves_nothing_waiting_spec_permissions_5_4`).
- The chain is rebuilt when `account_view`'s key moves (held rotations, choice, seeds held).
- A rotation finds heads by `at` and its line, never by a supersedes list, which a removed node's fork may fill.
- `AccountView::authorized_by`: the minter of a counting rotation (a merge: its lowest certifier). It decides what this node answers as its account's word, never what counts.
- Nothing honest is lost by an unnamed head: the epoch rule names commits at void epochs too, so each node's listing answering the rotation that closes them saves its own writes there (`an_honest_write_at_a_removed_nodes_rotation_counts_spec_permissions_5_5`). Only nodes signing at a head write a counting listing, so a removed node gains nothing.

## JudgedEpochs
- What the rule says per account is kept in `ix.epochs` and persisted (`node/epochs`): a standing is a lookup, and an open builds no chain and reads no list. `named` holds only held commits (`unindex_commit`), so it is bounded by the store.
- The epoch rule reads the chain as D's roster only at a node holding the account, so taking it up (`Engine::take_up`, pairing) or leaving it (`signing::leave`) judges its epochs again in that transaction (`signing::judge_account_again`); `Core::judged_from` covers only what moves while it is held. The take-up at open skips it: the persisted judgment was made with it held (`a_node_leaving_its_account_judges_its_chain_as_an_outsider_*`, `a_reader_paired_into_the_account_judges_its_chain_as_a_node_*`).
- `refresh_epochs` takes the accounts due (`ix.epochs_due`, from `note_rotation`; `ix.names_due`: a commit at a closed epoch, or a word naming a held commit, arrived) and judges by `osis_perm::Epochs` only the commits whose standing may have moved (at a moved epoch, named by a rotation whose count moved via `tb/`, due, and what listings among them name). So a rotation costs the commits at the epochs it moved, a list the commits it names, a write no chain build (`rotation_judges_its_epochs_once_and_writes_stay_flat`, `a_rotation_lists_its_epoch_only`, `a_listing_named_late_names_again_spec_permissions_5_5`).

## Writing (`flows/signing.rs`)
- Removing or pairing a node is one plain rotation of P carrying the change (`Engine::remove_device`, `Engine::pair`), D re-keyed in it to the roster of the line it makes; thing lists inline only.
- Every rotation applied is due an answer, a listing or addenda (`ix.rotations_due`, `rd/`), and again when it comes to count (`refresh_epochs`): a merge arriving before the seed its inner signature opens with is read as void (minter unknown) and answered by nothing; without the second answer the non-merger's writes at the heads it closes are never listed.
- An account's rotation wraps at D's sync IDs by the rule for a commit giving read epochs (spec/crypto.md §5; notes/crypto.md), no exception of its own.
- What the chain owes (`rotations_due`, any node of the account, none designated) is decided at the end of a complete pass of D's sync IDs (`Engine::signing_due`): a plain rotation where a disregarded rotation stands unanswered (`unanswered`) or a taken revocation is off the preferred line while no merge can be made; then the merge of every head; then seed re-seals (`custody::reseal_seeds`). A removed node's every re-fork costs one answering rotation per node of the account online, never a listing.
- Pairing carries the rotations with their lists (`Package::lists`), so D's earlier commits count at the new node.
- D's holders are its roster (`Core::devices_holders`): a rotation re-keys D to the roster of the line it makes. Leaving (`signing::leave_if_removed`, `kept`): of the held rotations counting here or whose seed is held and whose line stands under its own removals (`AccountView::stands_alone`), no tip's line keeps this node a node (`AccountView::keeps`); D's roster decides nothing. So a node removed on the line it follows stays while another held line keeps it (its user may switch there), and a rotation minted by no node of its line removes no one. A node off D's roster, or that took a revocation of itself, rotates nothing (`signs_for`). A pairing is a rotation (`Engine::pair`); it keeps D's deposit seed.
