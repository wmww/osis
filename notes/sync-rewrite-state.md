# Sync rewrite: state of the branch
> Summary: Where the one-branch sync rewrite stands: chain certificates, tickets, accepting, removal notices, taken grants, kept fires and holders' paths are deleted; a grant belongs to the thing granted (a node's role on a thing is by its grants where the node syncs it or holds an offer of it, none elsewhere; a grant from outside reaches its holder as an offer kept the inbox lifetime, holders are held for keys from the synced thing up); the whole workspace builds and its default tests pass but the listed ignores (15: engine 3, osis-sync 11, public `osis` 1, by cause); a sync ID carries of a commit its sync unit body and one certificate copy per thing outside the unit, each judged alone (`BodyId`); certificates are read off the one evidence derivation the engine keeps, fed per commit, and followed per read thing; what each next session builds, one piece per session, and the questions met. Deleted with the branch's landing.

Brief: delete first, rebuild from spec (spec/permissions.md §4, spec/sync-set.md §2, spec/sync-inbox.md §2 and §5, spec/sync-judgement.md, spec/crypto.md §7). One piece per session, in the order of Next.

## What the tree is now
- **No tickets anywhere**: the reference kind (osis-base, osis-schema, osis-crdt, crdt-model, public `osis`, cli, kit), `tickets.0`, the account's tickets (osis-sync `inbox`), ticket sync IDs and hints (`sync_ids::actors::held_sync_ids` is the `sync.0` record or a followed link's), the ticket context of the closure, accepting, removal notices (`removal@~0`).
- **No chain certificates**: `sync/certified.rs` keeps the same rows (`of`, `naming`, `records`, `namers`, `read`) and a follow reads them off the engine's kept evidence derivation (`evidence::kept`, notes/evidence-derivation.md The kept derivation: every held copy, each read thing a sync ID with unit `sync_unit(g)`, fed per commit, record, read thing and unit member; a write reads its own commit's copies whatever the held history): `Engine::sync_certificates(ev, g)` per read thing, followed by what deriving it read (`Effects<Tid, Certs>`: the derivation's rows are stamped, `evidence::Row`), so a follow derives the certificates of the read things whose inputs moved and writes the differences of their rows (`Certified::put`); `certified_differs` compares with every read thing's derived anew (`check_evidence`; debug builds at the 1st, 2nd, 4th, ... follow that derives). A write on one of 3 or of 30 read things derives the same things' certificates (engine `evidence::engine::a_write_in_one_of_n_read_things_costs_the_certificate_follow_the_same_at_n_and_10n`). It runs whenever `Core::generation` or the read things (`SyncRows::read`, which move with no transaction) moved since the last (`certificates_quiet`); every change of the held set bumps the generation (`apply::unindex` touches: a forgotten commit left the rows naming what it had needed). Its work runs uncounted; a follow is counted only where this thread's writes owed it.
- **Copies per (commit, thing)** (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies, spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Per-sync-ID copies):
  - `sync::BodyId { commit, on }` names one body under a sync ID: the sync unit body (`on` none: shared blob, per-sync-ID copy, or a header alone) or the certificate copy on one thing outside the unit. `Engine::bodies_at(s, c)` derives them from the things reading `s`: the closure body, per thing `x` no reader carries (`outside`) the copy of the named parts on `x`, and a named header alone where neither goes. `sync_bodies(c)` is keyed (sync ID, thing); `sync_body`, `read_facts`, `carriers`, `neither`, `may_log`, `seal_body`, `rewrap` take a `BodyId`. Nothing joins certificate parts into a closure body: a commit with parts inside and outside the unit has two entries under S.
  - `Engine::blob_body(s, b)`: a copy holding parts on one thing alone, that thing outside `s`'s unit, is that thing's copy; any other blob is the unit body's. A function of the blob and the unit, so a copy S names no more is a body with no meta: outside the required set, unlogged after a complete pass (`judge::outside`). `blob_loses` compares blobs of one body only.
  - osis-sync's read is keyed by (S, `BodyId`) throughout (`Ground`, `judge`, `Read`'s effects, `owed`, `pieces`, a stand's row key `stand_key`); `Live::body_blobs` filters a commit's blobs by `blob_body`. `Read::verdict(s, commit)` is the unit body's (status, inbox); promotion is the unit body's alone (a deposited copy is never stamped in place).
  - A copy's `bp/` row goes once no entry and no tombstone names its blob (`Engine::unnamed_blob`), whether or not its commit is held: a shared blob's row stays the commit's.
  - Records: a record S's certificates name goes in S's record blob unless a body delivers it: a thing of the unit, minted with no epochs, whose minting section S's readers open from a member of its record chain they hold epochs of (`certblobs::minting_opens`: the unit's actor or an independent thing of the unit). A thing minted under another tree (an account's profile reached through a contact thing) is minted where they open nothing.
  - An account's public record (`current_keys`) holds its creation's `keys.0` part beside its newest keysets' commits: only the creation writes the inbox (`flows::account::create_account_in`).
  - A holder of sync alone on a target (`roster_of(x)` gives it `Role::Sync`) gets in its key part that target's `secrets.0` and `sync.0` parts alone, and no evidence of them (`sync_certificates`); no test covers it.
  - Not built: sealed copies; the copy's parts as exactly what S names (a copy on one thing holding more parts than S names now, keys alone after the roles were named, stays S's log of it).
- **`sync_ids/certs.rs`** is the evidence form of what the spec keeps: `sync_certificates` (required copies as role-class parts per (commit, thing), records of both ends of each edge, `shared_certificates`, holders' current keys, under an account root its nodes' cards (`node_cards`), the key part `key_part(g)`: every held edge write naming g, boxing g, and `sync.0` on each thing named, with `closure_of` them), `add_copy`, `add_closure`, `close_records` (a fixed point: record chains, account chains, mintings), `evidence_of(c, parts)` (what promotion and tests hand with a commit), `certificates(g)` (pairing, `LocalPeer::send_chain`). An ask outside a follow reads the kept derivation (`evidence_now`).
- **Grants belong to the thing granted** (Tess; spec/sync-set.md §2 Own actors, spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Grant from outside; notes/holder-access-study.md The direction). Nothing is taken, accepted or proven by a fire:
  - **The role** (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own actors): `rotation::role_here(a)` (and `Engine::role_on`) is the best of the own actors' `Core::keyed_role_now` where `a`'s grants count (`rotation::lost::grants_count`), none elsewhere; what the node does as an actor reads `Core::role_now`. A thing's grants count where the node syncs it or holds an offer of it (`syncs`: an own actor or the own root; the closure holds it, read or unread; a held strong reaches it, `Closure::targeted`, which lets a sibling take a box before the walk enters; held for keys; a followed link's target; target of a held valid `grant@~0` fire; a dependent thing with the actor its secrets derive from), or where it is a holder found from such a thing up (a walk down `Held::named_on` over edges giving a role now). So an edge write held as another thing's certificate decides nothing (engine `external::a_held_grant_counts_only_where_its_thing_is_synced_spec_sync_set_2`), and a sibling node counts a grant once a strong reaches its target.
    - The closure reads the role and the role reads the closure: `units::settle` loops with `flows::retake` (which answers whether any box was due) until none is, since a thing newly reached or held for keys opens boxes the role had kept out and the walk enters by them.
    - **Load order**: `apply::load` derives which things are independent (`keys::refresh_independent`) before any box opens (`account::refresh_epochs` reopens what the account's epochs open): the role reads the actor a thing's secrets derive from, and a rebuild that opened boxes first counted a not-yet-independent thing through its mint admin and held secrets the live index did not (`Engine::checked_index` fails on it).
    - **An ended edge's holder** (`lost::was_ours`, read by `loss_seen` and `may_learn`) is an own actor or one an own actor reads by the held grants, read without `grants_count`: once the edge naming a holder ended, nothing finds the holder from below, its own secrets go, and a rule asking whether it still counts would never see the loss it brings (osis-sync `holding::a_removal_reaches_a_reader_opening_the_holder_through_a_second_thing`).
    - A thing a node makes is held from its node root, so its maker syncs it; another node of the account counts its grants once a strong reaches it (kit, the app and chat pass on it).
  - **A grant from outside is an offer** (`flows::inbox`): `deliver_copy` fires `grant@~0` on the holder's inbox for every edge write (a grant, a change, a removal) and every rotation or re-seal boxing a holder whose writer's node may not write the holder (`role_now(me, holder) < Write`); a writer of the holder fires nothing, the piece being the holder's key part (`key_part`). The fire carries the role commit's copy on the target with its evidence and records (`grant_value`), taken as an arriving role commit while the fire is held valid (`take_fire`). An inbox keeps both its signals `INBOX_LIFETIME` (`RetentionSetting::take_inbox_signals`).
  - **What an offer carries is held while the offer is** (`retention::needed::Need::Offered`, rows `gf/`: `Indexes::grant_fires`, `fires_carrying`; `Engine::grant_fires_held` is what an app lists offers by), and goes with it unless the node syncs the target. A fire past its keep when it arrives is never taken, and a removal's fire is kept as long as a grant's, so a grant's fire replayed after its edge ended meets the removal wherever a node still holds the edge (engine `external::a_replayed_offer_of_an_ended_grant_gives_no_role`, `an_offer_past_its_keep_is_never_taken`).
  - No judgement of which grants a holder counts, no fire kept as proof, no path a role commit states for its holders (spec/wire.md §4 has 17 header fields and the flags), no setting or fire commit as a role entry: a fresh session should not rebuild any of them (notes/holder-access-study.md, Why not down from the account).
  - **The engine harness** stands in for sync's fire step: `LocalPeer::pass_fires` (in `deliver`, `deliver_sealed`, `sync_changed`; `pass_fires_to` for a peer outside the net), `Engine::fire_own`, `LocalPeer::takes_grants` (enables the inbox signals on a holder thing), `hold_fires`, `hold_fires_of`, `order::owed_fires` and `take_fires`; a fire refused though the setting enables it fails the test. `flows::spread` and `thing_of` hold what they hand; a peer handed a thing's role commits by `LocalPeer::deliver` alone, fires held (`hold_fires`), neither syncs the thing nor holds an offer, and counts nothing.
- **Models**: held-model `closure::closure` walks from `NodeView { roots, own }` by the own actors' roles over the world's grants; `closure::keys::for_keys` is the walk up from the synced things through the holders their grants name, with `on_paths` its oracle (tests/it/spec_holders.rs). The sync differential (`harness/history.rs`) notes each edge between things that no writer of the holder wrote since it began (`Made::unoffered`: no history thing enables `grant@~0`, so the holder's readers learn no sync ID or box by it); `Model::closure` is the least closure in which such an edge counts once the node reads its target another way.
- **Product default**: kit, the app and chat enable the inbox signals on every shared thing they make (`osis_kit::store::start_setting`, `osis_chat::create`), so a group or tag a user creates is offered grants from outside and its admins can turn that off; a thing made through the bare `osis` API hears of none until its admin writes the setting (to confirm with Tess through Mizzen).
- **Independent by a box**: `Core::counts_independent` counts a secret a held valid box gave, established or not (spec/sync-set.md §2), so a grantee enters the target before it syncs it.
- **Held for keys, engine side** (spec/sync-set.md §2; `sync/units.rs` `held_for_keys`, `opened_holder`, `named_up`, `settle_keys`, `Closure::keys`): a holder is held for keys while it is an independent thing, no key actor, that the node closure does not read, of which the node holds a read secret of its own (a box gave it; the lost-access rule keeps it only while the node's role reads the holder), and that the grants of a thing the node syncs name: asked of one holder as a walk down its held edge writes (`Held::named_on`) to a thing the closure holds, each hop with key access, read or above between holders. Followed per thing with held secrets by an `Effects` key, settled after the closure at each transaction's end; `units::check_whole` compares it with a judgement afresh.
  - Of such a holder the node holds its certificates (`retention::needed::Root::Keys`); every other part waits outside the node closure and goes at the pass end. One coming to be held for keys wakes what waited for it (`Core::keys_came`, `apply::wake_entered`); one held so no more is judged for what it still needs (`note_lost`), so a holder named by nothing synced goes whole.
  - Its sync ID is in the sync set (`sync_things`, `in_sync_things`, `things_entered`) as `Holding::Keys` (`Engine::thing_holdings`: `thing_sync` answers `ThingSync::Read` for every thing opened by a read secret, and the holding is for keys where the closure holds the thing so; `rows::fold`: read over keys over kept unread). Inside the engine a sync ID held for keys is opened, judged, sealed and passed as one read (`Holding::opens`); history completeness (`caught_up`) counts read sync IDs alone.
- **Held for keys in osis-sync** (spec/sync.md §3, spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source, spec/sync-streams.md §4 Toward a peer holding S's thing for keys):
  - **The class says it, no `Pull` selector.** A side declares how it holds S in each Hit, Static and ByRole item and each accepting answer (`class`, spec/wire.md §11.2: 0 reader, 1 store, 2 keys; `osis_proto::SourceClass`, `types::class_of`). A selector on `Pull` would tell the server what to send and nothing else; the class also tells every rule reading a source that this peer holds none of S's content (held elsewhere counts it by its stream alone, `status` `reported`; no Register, Delete, deposit or suspect list goes to it, each of which targets a store or a reader), on every connection and before any Pull. A class that moves closes the member and opens it again as any class move does.
  - **Served**: `streams::serve::for_keys` and `role_entry`: of S a keys source gets Added events, Fetch answers of role entries alone, no ephemeral listing and no relay-only blob (`relay::pass_on`); Deleted and head events go as to any member. A server keeping S unread tells a role entry by its row (`Entry::role`, the domain its stamp or deposit signature verified under); one opening S by its own judgement (`Ground::role_entry`, `Engine::role_entry`: a record or list blob, or a blob of a `role_commit`).
  - **Role entries are stamped as such** (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries; `read::live` `own_entry`, `restamp`, `deposit`): a role commit, a commit carrying a purge request, and record and list blobs (`Engine::role_commit`, `role_entry`). So a store takes them outside its caps, as the spec says.
  - **At the node holding for keys**: wherever osis-sync asks whether S is opened by a read secret it answers as for a read (`Holding::opens`: credentials, taking and opening what a source shows, parking, own entries, steps, record blobs, promotion, passes, stranding, status), since its sources show it role entries alone; it takes no relay-only blob. Raised from held for keys to read, or from kept unread to held for keys, S is pulled anew from every source (`store::raised`).
  - Not built: own boxes only (a node holding for keys fetches every `secrets.0` page a role entry links, notes/a2-spec/README.md Open 5).
- **Delivery**: a rotation or re-seal boxing a holder its writer may not write goes as a `grant@~0` fire (`rotation::deliver_rotation`, the re-seal), Interim until sealed copies.
- **A sealed fire ahead of the clock waits** (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): `ingest_sealed_fire` keeps a fire pending `Reason::Drift` as any commit, its entry carrying it, with the chain and records it brought; what it carries is taken once it applies valid (`Indexes::fires_due`, `take_carried`: a grant's commit, a stranded notice's report, `inbox::carries`). An opened grant fire is taken by `take_fire`, which opens the boxes the roles had kept out (`flows::retake`): a role commit held before its fire gives its secrets when the fire comes. A stranded notice that applies at its opening is acted on there whatever the thing's history keeps; one that waited is acted on only where its fire is held (the setting keeps it).
- **A blob a waiting commit comes to want, held in sync's store already, is handed over** (`Engine::take_wanted`, `store::Follow::settle`): a reader takes an announced blob it cannot open as an unopened entry, parked under S's read secrets alone; a header list's chunk fetched ahead of the node above it was such an entry, and the commit pended `WaitBlobs` for good once the node arrived and the walk came to want the chunk (the `three` fixture on worlds of seeds 1, 2, 5 and 6: node 0 never applied the writer's list-linked write, and deleted the writer's account record and minting evidence at the store as outside S's required set). The engine names the blobs newly wanted (`Walks::newly`, filled where the wake index grows), the store follow hands over those it holds bytes of, and what the walk wants next comes at the next settle. `delete_keys::nothing_deletes_a_header_lists_chunks_by_level_2` asserts node 0 reads the write and names the chunks by the header's own list (`header_list_blobs`); the fixture's world reaches the node first, so no default test fails with the handover undone (issues/wanted-blob-handover-untested.md); on a world where the chunk comes first the harness's quiet check fails it (below).
- **A fire's pages travel with it** (spec/wire.md §9, `osis_blob::SealedFire::pages`; a held fire's value is read whole, its pages joined, `flows::inbox::fire_value`): the sealed payload carries every blob its commit's deltas link; the sender takes them out of its blob store into the owed fire's row (`flows::inbox::built_pages`, `OwedFire::pages`), the receiver stores them in the transaction taking the fire and refuses a payload holding a blob the deltas do not link (`ingest_sealed_fire`). A grant fire's value (the copy, its evidence, their records) pages at the default target in ordinary worlds.
- **Certificate values** (`sync::stranded::certs_values`: a fire's chain, a grant fire's evidence, holder keys) carry an account's older rotations as header-only copies and go in the order written, so a receiver that takes each as it comes holds a rotation before the commits at the epoch it mints.
- **`LocalPeer::share`** hands each grantee the grant's commits directly, as the grant fire would.

- **Fires in the hand-carried harnesses**: every harness that carries commits by hand carries fires apart, as sync's fire step does: the engine's `LocalPeer::pass_fires`, public `osis` `internals::pass_fires` (in the `deliver` of the `osis`, app and chat tests), osis-sim `Sim::fires` (in `sync_all` and in each gossip, lost whole at the drop rate); each offers a sealed fire by `Engine::offer_fire`. A granted thing is still written only once held (a strong): a test holds what it writes.
- **osis-sync's quiet checks** (`harness/mod.rs` `World::run`, each run's end): sources are members, every must-hold body is logged and reaches each caught-up reader, and no commit waits for a blob in its reach (`wanted_blobs_in_reach`: `Engine::blob_wants` against the node's own sync store and the entries of each connected source that is neither full nor refusing, under the sync IDs the node holds there). The last fails the handover undone wherever the chunk-first order is reached; every default osis-sync test passes it.
- **The sync differential's oracle** (`harness/history.rs`): a thing's making is a value write (`Made::made`, an event), so one its maker released before another node surely received it is lost: the reference is handed the making whole (the thing's record is in it) and its value reads as none (`check`'s `unmade`, `gone` in the closure and body clauses). A run gives a node a value write only where its account's model closure holds or stores the write's thing at that run (`ran`, `quiet_model`: a run with every node on and connected; at any other run a value write spreads to no node, which can only count as lost a write no node holds at the check); `differential::a_making_released_before_a_grantee_held_it_is_lost` is the script that failed the old rule (probed: it fails with every connected node counted). The sync-set clause wants `Holding::Keys` where held-model's `closure::keys::for_keys` holds the thing (`Model::closure` fills `Closure::for_keys`). The plain property passes its default seeds and 80 others.
- **What a sealed fire brings is judged with no pass in flight** (`Core::fire_brings`, `note_pass_due`, `Engine::settle_lost`): a deposit is taken outside any pass and none is wanted for it (a pass is wanted by role arrivals on a sync ID's own actors, not by a fire in an inbox), so what a fire brought (its signer's chain, the things its carried commit names) no longer waits in `Core::pass_due` for a pass end that may never come; it is noted as a lost need is and judged at once with none in flight. Any other arrival waits for the pass that brought it.
- **A delivery to a holder the node writes fires nothing** (`flows::inbox::deliver_copy`): a rotation or re-seal boxing a holder this node may write (its own account root D as a made thing's mint admin) is in that holder's key part under its own sync ID; fired at D it was owed for good (D enables no signal, no peer takes it).
- **What a role read depends on**: `Timelines`' `Held(thing)` stamp moves with the edge writes on that thing (one coming, going or changing its standing: `osis_perm::Changed::held` and `ops`), never with its signer's other commits. So a role read through the account moves with the things on its way, not with every role commit the account signs or every thing made (public `osis` `making::making_an_independent_thing_costs_what_it_changed`, `tracking::tracked_role_reads_follow_their_thing_at_n_and_10n`).
