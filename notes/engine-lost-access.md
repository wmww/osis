# Engine: lost access and forgetting
> Summary: How a node drops what it lost read access to (spec/permissions.md §3, spec/requirements.md Deletion): when an actor counts as lost, nothing taken back (boxes, entry boxes and copies opened later), the `l/` work list and its trigger, losses by an `until` alone and hearing labels out, the pending-grant guard, what forgetting erases and keeps, sealed pending boxes, kept copies, bearers, self-removal drops (`drop_left`) and link races. Code: `rotation/lost.rs`, `retention/forget.rs`, node-sync `sync/deliver.rs`. Not here: why removal works this way (notes/permissions.md Revocation, Retained copy), the cover check's own self-removals (notes/engine-roles.md).

## When an actor is lost
An actor is lost when the node holds a secret of it (reading-only included; epochs observed without one are not access), neither the node, a held key actor nor a link key it keeps reading as has a role on it now (`lost::readable`: timeline, any path, `until` honoured), and no held unapplied role commit (pending, readable parked, late parts) boxes secrets to the node, a held key actor or an actor the node reads now, on an actor whose role reaches it (itself, its record chain, or one with a role on it). A grant not yet applied, whose secrets may already be read (spec/crypto.md §4), is never a loss. Each actor is judged on its own, so what the node reached only through a lost one is lost with it.
- **Reading-only guard**: an actor whose secrets the node holds only from a commit not yet judged (`Waits::reading`) is kept while that commit's entries are; a checked-secret holder of a lost actor drops and re-learns when the grant applies.
- **Link race** (`contested`): where a held single-use redemption granting one of ours stands void only because an applied redemption by the same signer removed the signer's edge first, with no explicit write on that edge since, the loser keeps the secrets (and follows the labels) until the sharer's finalization arrives: it may drop the competing redemption and make ours stand (spec/permissions.md §3, §7). A plain leave followed by a void grant (a leaver's forgery) defers nothing.

## Nothing taken back (`rotation::may_take`)
The principle: a node holds no secret of an actor it cannot read. The trigger drops what the node holds; the one place secrets are taken enforces it for what comes later, so no box's arrival needs judging again (re-judging after a join would loop: forget, refetch, rejoin).
- **Boxes**: `Core::ingest_view` (every applied `secrets.0` opening: received deltas, late or rejoined parts, `reopen_dependents` when a holder actor's secrets change, `reopen_all` at open) keeps a role's newly opened secrets only where `may_take` allows: `may_hold` (reading the actor: every role's; sync alone: sync's, as `over_held` judges) or a `contested` link race reaching it. What it keeps out is listed by role, only where a secret was kept out (`Waits::untaken`, in memory; `take_account`'s `reopen_all` at open lists it again, a rolled-back transaction restores what it took off), and `flows::retake` opens that actor's whole `secrets.0` again once `may_take` allows a listed role: run by `apply::rejudged` (every apply that changed held role commits) and `keep_bearer`; its work is one `may_take` per listed actor and role (`lost_secrets::retake_judges_only_listed_actors_spec_permissions_3`). Without it a box applied before the grant that makes it ours (another admin's rotation boxing the grantee; commits of different authors apply in any order) would stay unopened (`lost_secrets::a_grant_and_a_rotation_in_either_order_give_the_newest_epoch_spec_permissions_3`). Pending boxes (`SecretsRead::Pending`, `read_pending_boxes`) are a grant still to be judged and stay reading-only as below.
- **Entry boxes** (`Engine::learn_secret`, `may_learn`): the same rule where the held timeline shows an ended edge on the actor of the node, a held key actor or an actor it reads; otherwise the box is taken (a link's redeemer opens the label before it holds the grant to its key, and may hold other role commits of the actor already), and the trigger judges the actor once role commits on it apply. Rejected: "any role commit of it held" as the test (the redeemer often holds some; links stall).
- **Copies** (`Engine::copy_stale`, node-sync `open_one`): a per-label copy's section is under its blob key, so a label reader opens it without the thing's secret. A copy left under a label after its group lost the thing would give the content back; node-sync takes no content part (`core_types::is_content`) on a thing under an own-keys actor the label's group actor may not read now, deletes the bytes, shreds the wrap, and keeps the entry as a dropped body's. `Waits::awaits_reading` lists the commit by the thing's secret actor; `Core::read_again` (secrets of it taken) names it in `Changed::wakes.woken`, and `reopen_undropped` fetches the entry again (a commit not held at all included). In memory: a restart refetches every missing part anyway (`reopen_undropped(None)`) and judges it again. Role-class parts are taken: each is judged as it is used (`secrets.0` boxes by `may_take`, entry boxes by `may_learn`, `account.0` seeds only under a devices epoch held for use; the rest is certificate material), and leaving one out would strand a slot whose copy carries it.
- Rejected: stripping such parts at engine ingest by "no secret held and no path reads" (`LocalPeer` and the order property hand plaintext to nodes that cannot read, and arrival before the secrets made what a node holds depend on order); marking the forgotten part `Missing::Forgot` (a copy arriving joins such a part anyway).

## The trigger (`l/` work list, `Engine::drop_lost_due`)
- Applying a received role commit (or its late `roles.0` parts) adds its `roles.0` targets to the work list in the apply's transaction, as does dropping a pending role commit as bad input (its aspects' things: the reading-only secrets its boxes gave go unless something else reads them). Local commits never do.
- Every applied role commit, local too, lists each of its targets at the earliest `until` ahead on its roster (`arm_untils`; an `until` passing is a loss with no role commit behind it, spec/permissions.md §2), as does a trigger run for the entries it takes. `ix.lost_due_at` answers by time, the list holding one entry per thing with an `until` ahead.
- node-sync runs the trigger at the end of every `handle` step (after the step's applies and re-examination) until nothing is due, and wakes at `Engine::next_lost_due`; an app driving the engine alone calls it after ingesting. One transaction takes the due entries and drops the held actors a role on a listed thing reaches that are lost (`LostScope::Due`), except those `lost_leaving` leaves to node-sync: lost through its own self-removal, or by time alone.
- Every scope re-lists each judged actor a plausible pending grant keeps, due when the earliest such grant ages out (value: that time, u64 BE; empty: now; an earlier entry stays), so a grant that never applies cannot keep lost secrets past it. Idempotent; a restart finds the list with its times (`load_lost_due`). `drop_lost_secrets` (and node-sync's `SyncEngine::drop_lost`) evaluates every held actor and takes the whole list.

## Lost by time alone
`lost_by_time`: walked with every edge's time ahead and every finalize undone, a role is there (a removal saying retain is written with `until` 0, gone at every clock, so it never counts). Such an actor gets a `T/` record when first judged and goes to node-sync's `drop_left` as `Leaving::timed`, which drops it once its labels are heard out, or `LOST_HEARD_WAIT_MS` (`ROLE_KEEP_MS`) after the record's time. So a renewal on its way and unsent writes from before the `until` come first, and no host holds an erasure off for good. The record goes with the drop or when the actor reads again (`prune_timed`). A role commit received beside the expired grant changes nothing (`keep::a_commit_beside_an_expired_edge_waits`).
- Labels judged: those whose closure holds a thing the drop would forget (`Engine::would_forget`, once per actor per engine generation).
- A label with a host is heard out when open somewhere and idle everywhere open, or refused or stranded at some host (`SyncEngine::heard`).
- A label with none (`heard_by_co_readers`): the node's own writes are `placed` (the last own commit by where the node log first held its entry, `last_own`: a co-reader's report at or past it covers every earlier one) and a co-reader source's stream of it was read to its end since the record's time (`peer_passes`, so a renewal it held came); or a co-reader refuses it (`co_readers_refusing`, once per `drop_left` run: on a connection whose shared set settled without it, the peer proved an actor J judges a holder of it).
- A co-reader that may write the label's actor finalizes the ended grant on its first pass past the `until` (`ensure_rotated`), judges the node departed and shares nothing with it; one that applied that finalize does the same. That refusal is the peer-only analog of a host serving a later step: the node's unsent writes are lost there as at such a host. A reading co-reader that has not finalized keeps sharing the label by the node's current credential and takes them (node `leaving::an_until_under_no_host_*`).
- Limit: a co-reader that knows the node only by the departed grant proves nothing to it (a known actor is needed), so its silence is no refusal; with only such co-readers (typically the finalizing admin) the node waits out `LOST_HEARD_WAIT_MS`.
- Rejected: Synced (`LabelStatus::synced`) as the peer-only test (a refusing co-reader never lets it read Synced, leaving only the 90-day wait); a refusal from a peer the node dials for the label (the dial list comes from self-declared `reach.0`: any member of a shared group could claim a co-reader's account and make it drop, `an_until_under_no_host_a_forged_reach_refuses_nothing`).

## Forgetting (`retention/forget.rs`)
- The drop removes the lost actors' `k/` and, in the same transaction, forgets every thing whose secret actor is one of them and that neither the node nor a held key actor may read now (`Core::may_read`). Of such a thing only `created.0` and the role-class aspects other than `secrets.0` stay (certificate material perm judges others' writes by: grants, public keysets, `label.0`).
- Shredded: aspect units (`drop_aspect`: state, tables); its parts in `c/` records (a commit left with no part is deleted, so a re-grant fetches it again; others keep their readable parts, not marked dropped, so the forgotten ones join late if they return: their secrets and, from a copy, their content only once the node reads them again, Nothing taken back above); pending parts; `a/` `v/` `p/` `s/` `w/`; timers of forgotten parts; sync records (`SyncRecords::forget`: everything about the forgotten groups' labels, blob records holding forgotten parts (their entries under kept labels stay, wraps shredded), certificates and carriers of deleted commits, hints); the ciphertexts nothing kept names. The thing's freed set goes (a removed party cannot delete), and the pieces the forgotten parts link go by `retention::freed::lost`: walked through held node blobs, released where no remaining source of the thing reaches them (its role-class states stay and may link pages) and the held prefix bears the link (a link copied from another thing releases nothing). Tests: `freed::tests::a_link_copied_from_another_thing_frees_nothing_spec_crypto_7`, `deletion::losing_a_thing_keeps_chunks_another_thing_links_spec_sync_7`.
- Two checks, two questions, neither standing in for the other: the engine rule says whether a chunk is still part of held data, for every app (one driving the engine without node-sync too); node-sync's `forget_records` says whether a kept sync record (an entry under a kept label, a blob record) still names a ciphertext, for every blob kind. `y/f` lists the things whose opened inbox blobs node-sync then deletes (`forget_inbox`, also at open: a grant's inbox blob opens under the node's own keys).
- **Sealed pending boxes** (`rotation::boxes_kept`, `b/`): forgetting keeps the `secrets.0` parts of an unapplied commit signed by an author who may grant on the actor now, whatever its age, that box the node a read epoch newer than every checked one it held (a grant from after a rotation that left the node out), so a re-grant applied after the loss brings its boxes. Marked `b/`, they give `read_pending_boxes` no candidates until the commit applies (else they would keep the lost secrets for good). A box only at a held epoch is a grant from before the removal, which gives no role, so it goes (a re-grant after a self-removal with no rotation between boxes a held epoch too and loses its boxes: stranded until a re-seal). Costs: such a grant that never applies keeps its sealed box; a grant whose author's chain is not held yet cannot be checked and loses its boxes, recovered by a stranded report and a re-seal.
- The engine then reloads its indexes (`reload` keeps schemas); node-sync re-judges every label on that change (`touched.full`). Event `Changed { cause: LostAccess }`, a `Removed` diagnostic per actor.
- A re-granted holder regains content by sync only: its own writes not uploaded before it forgot are gone (sim `revocation_bob` expects only what reached another node).

## Kept copies (`K/`; spec/permissions.md §3 Retain)
- `keeps` is a least fixpoint over ended grants saying retain (`Timeline::ended`, `EdgeEnd`; an `until` ending by `retain_in_force`), live ways from a kept holder and the held marks: a copy hangs on holder H while the node still has H (also while H's loss waits, `not_dropped`: secrets above sync held and H not in this judging; every drop re-judges every mark, so the copy follows H's fate) and the way through H keeps; a holder already recorded goes only on the timeline's word. Holders cannot keep each other. Re-judged when a due target touches a mark.
- A kept copy goes through `forget` with `Fate::Kept`: only `secrets.0`, its labels' sync records and pending content are taken; `apply::ingest_class` strips later content parts on it (`without_frozen`, `forget::frozen`), a commit left with none being dropped unrecorded so a re-grant fetches it, and a commit's purge list still applies if another part holds the commit.
- No secret, no sync, no write; reads still read what it held. The user drops one with `LostScope::Kept` (`Engine::kept_copies`). A client actor reads a kept copy only as a holder the copy hangs on or an app of its account (`copy_readable_by`, notes/client-port.md §3).

## Bearers (`B/`, `Engine::keep_bearer`; spec/permissions.md §7 Bearer use)
`import_contact` forgets the card's seed and keeps L's tid, and L's role counts as the node's for `readable` and `read_path` only: never entitlement, `may_read` of the node, or what it may share. The record goes when L holds no role on what it is named on (`drop_ended_bearers`). Without it, the seed gone, the first role commit touching the account (a client or node added) would drop the profile and its secrets at every contact: name gone, updates stopped, a `Removed` diagnostic per actor at someone who removed nothing.

## Self-removal drops (`drop_left`, node-sync `sync/deliver.rs`)
- Flows return `Done::drop_lost` instead of dropping in the commit's transaction. The trigger skips a lost actor with an own self-removal behind it (`departures_placing`) and arms node-sync's `drop_left`; `drop_left` (armed also by `carry_out` and at start) drops each lost actor (`LostScope::Only`) once the own self-removals behind it (`Engine::lost_leaving`) are `placed`, per actor: one that cannot be uploaded holds back nothing else, and a loss with no own self-removal behind it is never its (a full drop at start would also drop secrets read through a box with no grant).
- It recomputes `lost_leaving` only when the engine generation changes, rechecks the upload only when the sync state version does (hearing out, for `Leaving::timed`, every step), and stays armed while a pending grant keeps such an actor or a timed loss waits (`Leaving::recheck_at`).
- **Placed** (`SyncEngine::placed`, spec/sync.md §5): a source counts when it will pass the removal on to everyone the leaver would have reached. An own commit's entry counts held when present at a host, or when a co-reader source (`pass_source`: a known account, never an own node) the label was served to reported on its stream (`note_applied`) a position at or past where this node's log first held it, an ended member capped at its `until`. Under a label with no host any such report counts; under one with hosts only an admin's of the label's keys actor, for a commit writing that actor's `roles.0`: a co-reader uploads nothing to hosts, but an admin's re-registration uploads the actor's role commits a host lacks (sim `under_a_hosted_label_only_an_admins_report_places_spec_sync_5`, which also checks the host ends up holding it). Nothing counts while nobody pulls (the leaver keeps waiting rather than forget a removal that never left).
  - Reports covering a commit `placed` waits on (`placed_waiting`, in memory, filled by a `false` answer, which also notes what live connections reported so far) are recorded in `State::applied` (`y/A`, by label, entry, source) in that step's flush, dropped with the entry or its label: a connection's positions die with it, so a crash between report and forget would otherwise lose the confirmation (node `leaving::`). A stranger's Pull joining the label is refused, so it reports nothing.
  - A co-reader that judges the leaver departed closes the connection in that step (`drop_unwanted`), after the step's re-judging and settling: leaving the members drops what they owed, the position settles and its Applied goes before the close.
  - Own nodes: every node of the account waits on a self-removal by the account, itself or a key actor it holds (`rotation::departures` `own_leaving`: by author, or made here for a single-use redemption's L the redeemer dropped), so a sibling that pulled the removal from its maker keeps the group until a source has it. An own node is never a source: it leaves by the same commit and passes nothing on, so every own node waits for an outside one (sim `own_nodes_never_both_forget_before_an_outside_source_spec_permissions_3`).
  - Rejected: a refusal (code 1, 9) or a rotation leaving the leaver out as the signal (any rotation the leaver is not in gives it, a hostile host for free); a rotation naming its departures (a wire change leaking other departures' names and the fresh deposit seed); a bounded leaving period (a picked number; giving up is the user's call, issues/api-leaving-give-up.md).
- A removal that never places (host refusing for good) keeps the group for good, shown as `LabelStatus::leaving`/`GroupSync::leaving`; no timeout (node-sync's `drop_lost` drops it; not in the `osis` API). All from stored state, so a crash anywhere in between drops after restart; a self-removal whose `Done` was lost is placed by the next pass (it stays under its labels while the secrets are held).
- Rejected: gating on "every label idle" (it drops at reconnect before any pass ran, so an offline self-removal never goes up) and an ungated trigger (it drops a leaver that applied any received role change on the group before its removal went up).
