# Engine: lost access and forgetting
> Summary: How a node drops what it lost read access to (spec/permissions.md §3, spec/requirements.md Deletion): when an actor counts as lost, the `l/` work list and its trigger, losses by an `until` alone and hearing labels out, the pending-grant guard, what forgetting erases and keeps, sealed pending boxes, kept copies, bearers, self-removal drops (`drop_left`) and link races. Code: `rotation/lost.rs`, `retention/forget.rs`, node-sync `sync/deliver.rs`. Not here: why removal works this way (notes/permissions.md Revocation, Retained copy), the cover check's own self-removals (notes/engine-roles.md).

## When an actor is lost
An actor is lost when the node holds a secret of it (reading-only included; epochs observed without one are not access), neither the node, a held key actor nor a link key it keeps reading as has a role on it now (`lost::readable`: timeline, any path, `until` honoured), and no held unapplied role commit (pending, readable parked, late parts) boxes secrets to the node, a held key actor or an actor the node reads now, on an actor whose role reaches it (itself, its record chain, or one with a role on it). A grant not yet applied, whose secrets may already be read (spec/crypto.md §4), is never a loss. Each actor is judged on its own, so what the node reached only through a lost one is lost with it.
- **Pending-grant guard**: an actor the node holds checked secrets of is kept only by a plausible grant (signed, `authentic`, by an author who may grant on it now), and only until that grant ages out of `PENDING_GRANT_WAIT_MS` (24 h): anyone can send a pending commit, which is kept until it applies, so any other would keep a node that lost access holding it for good. Reading-only candidates go with the commit that gave them.
- **Link race** (`contested`): where a held single-use redemption granting one of ours stands void only because an applied redemption by the same signer removed the signer's edge first, with no explicit write on that edge since, the loser keeps the secrets (and follows the labels) until the sharer's finalization arrives: it may drop the competing redemption and make ours stand (spec/permissions.md §3, §7). A plain leave followed by a void grant (a leaver's forgery) defers nothing.

## The trigger (`l/` work list, `Engine::drop_lost_due`)
- Applying a received role commit (or its late `roles.0` parts) adds its `roles.0` targets to the work list in the apply's transaction, and so does dropping a pending role commit as bad input (its aspects' things: the reading-only secrets its boxes gave go unless something else reads them). Local commits never do.
- Every applied role commit, local too, lists each of its targets at the earliest `until` ahead on its roster (`arm_untils`; an `until` passing is a loss with no role commit behind it, spec/permissions.md §2), and so does a trigger run for the entries it takes. `ix.lost_due_at` answers by time, since the list holds one entry per thing with an `until` ahead (scanning it cost 22 µs per step at 10^4 entries, 0.6 ms at 10^5).
- node-sync runs the trigger at the end of every `handle` step (after the step's applies and re-examination) until nothing is due, and wakes at `Engine::next_lost_due`; an app driving the engine alone calls it after ingesting. One transaction takes the due entries and drops the held actors a role on a listed thing reaches that are lost (`LostScope::Due`), except those `lost_leaving` leaves to node-sync: lost through its own self-removal, or by time alone.
- Every scope re-lists each judged actor a plausible pending grant keeps, due when the earliest such grant ages out (value: that time, u64 BE; empty: now; an earlier entry stays), so a grant that never applies cannot keep lost secrets past it with no other role commit arriving. Idempotent; a restart in between finds the list with its times (`load_lost_due`). `drop_lost_secrets` (and node-sync's `SyncEngine::drop_lost`) evaluates every held actor and takes the whole list.
- **Lost by time alone** (`lost_by_time`: walked with every edge's time ahead and every finalize undone, a role is there; a removal saying retain is written with `until` 0, gone at every clock, so it never counts): such an actor gets a `T/` record (when first judged) and goes to node-sync's `drop_left` as `Leaving::timed`, which drops it once its labels are heard out (`SyncEngine::heard`: of the labels whose closure holds a thing the drop would forget, `Engine::would_forget`, each with a host is open somewhere and idle everywhere open, or refused or stranded at some host; computed once per actor per engine generation) or `LOST_HEARD_WAIT_MS` (`ROLE_KEEP_MS`, 90 days) after the record's time. So a renewal on its way and unsent writes from before the `until` come first, and no host holds an erasure off for good. The record goes with the drop or when the actor reads again (`prune_timed`). A role commit received beside the expired grant changes nothing (`keep::a_commit_beside_an_expired_edge_waits`).

## Forgetting (`retention/forget.rs`)
- The drop removes the lost actors' `k/` and, in the same transaction, forgets every thing whose secret actor is one of them and that neither the node nor a held key actor may read now (`Core::may_read`). Of such a thing only `created.0` and the role-class aspects other than `secrets.0` stay (certificate material perm judges others' writes by: grants, public keysets, `label.0`).
- Shredded: aspect units (`drop_aspect`: state, undo, pre-reset snapshots, tables), its parts in `c/` records (a commit left with no part is deleted, so a re-grant fetches it again; others keep their readable parts, not marked dropped, so the forgotten ones join late if they return), pending parts, `a/` `r/` `v/` `p/` `s/` `w/`, timers of forgotten parts, sync records (`SyncRecords::forget`: everything about the forgotten groups' labels, blob records holding forgotten parts, certificates and carriers of deleted commits, hints) and the ciphertexts nothing kept names. `y/f` lists the things whose opened inbox blobs node-sync then deletes (`forget_inbox`, also at open: a grant's inbox blob opens under the node's own keys).
- **Sealed pending boxes** (`rotation::boxes_kept`, `b/`): forgetting keeps the `secrets.0` parts of a not applied commit signed by an author who may grant on the actor now, whatever its age, that box the node a read epoch newer than every checked one it held (a grant from after a rotation that left the node out), so a re-grant applied after the loss brings its boxes. They are marked `b/` so `read_pending_boxes` takes no candidates from them until the commit applies (else they would keep the lost secrets for good). A box only at a held epoch is a grant from before the removal, which gives no role; kept, it would hold the lost secrets for good, so it goes (a re-grant after a self-removal with no rotation between boxes a held epoch too and loses its boxes: stranded until a re-seal). Costs: such a grant that never applies keeps its sealed box; a grant whose author's chain is not held yet cannot be checked and loses its boxes, recovered by the stranded heartbeat and a re-seal.
- The engine then reloads its indexes (`reload` keeps schemas); node-sync reloads its state, drops the forgotten labels' sessions and unsubscribes them (`stop_pushes`), and ignores pushes into a session not yet `Open` (a push left from the old session would set the cursor past what the new session's replay has not listed, so a re-granted holder never fetched the forgotten content again). Event `Changed { cause: LostAccess }`, a `Removed` diagnostic per actor.
- A re-granted holder regains content by sync only: its own writes not uploaded before it forgot are gone (sim `revocation_bob` expects only what reached another node).

## Kept copies (`K/`; spec/permissions.md §3 Retain)
- `keeps` is a least fixpoint over ended grants saying retain (`Timeline::ended`, `EdgeEnd`; an `until` ending by `retain_in_force`), live ways from a kept holder and the held marks: a copy hangs on holder H while the node still has H and the way through H keeps; a holder already recorded goes only on the timeline's word. Holders cannot keep each other. Re-judged (`rejudge`) when a due target touches a mark.
- A kept copy goes through `forget` with `Fate::Kept`: only `secrets.0`, its labels' sync records and pending content are taken; `apply::ingest_class` strips later content parts on it (`without_frozen`, `forget::frozen`), a commit left with none being dropped unrecorded so a re-grant fetches it, and a commit's purge list still applies if another part holds the commit.
- No secret, no sync, no write; reads still read what it held. The user drops one with `LostScope::Kept` (`Engine::kept_copies`). A client actor reads a kept copy only as a holder the copy hangs on or an app of the person it belongs to (`copy_readable_by`, notes/client-port.md §3).

## Bearers (`B/`, `Engine::keep_bearer`; spec/permissions.md §7 Bearer use)
`import_contact` forgets the card's seed and keeps L's tid, and L's role counts as the node's for `readable` and `read_path`; only those: never entitlement, `may_read` of the node, or what it may share. The record goes when L holds no role on what it is named on (`drop_ended_bearers`, with the things). Without it, the seed gone, the first role commit touching the account (a client or node added) would drop the profile and its secrets at every contact: the name gone, updates stopped, a `Removed` diagnostic per actor (K, P) at someone who removed nothing.

## Self-removal drops (`drop_left`, node-sync `sync/deliver.rs`)
- Flows return `Done::drop_lost` instead of dropping in the commit's transaction. The trigger skips a lost actor with an own self-removal behind it (`departures_placing`) and arms node-sync's `drop_left` instead; `drop_left` (armed also by `carry_out` and at start) drops each lost actor (`LostScope::Only`) once the own self-removals behind it (`Engine::lost_leaving`) are `placed`, per actor: one that cannot be uploaded holds back nothing else, and a loss with no own self-removal behind it is never its (a full drop at start would also drop secrets read through a box with no grant, `hinted_walk_claims_for_boxed_actor_spec_sync_5`).
- It recomputes `lost_leaving` only when the engine generation changes, rechecks the upload only when the sync state version does (hearing out, for `Leaving::timed`, every step), and stays armed while a pending grant keeps such an actor or a timed loss waits (`Leaving::recheck_at`); disarmed once nothing waits or may.
- A removal that never places (host refusing for good, a replacement label it cannot open) keeps the group for good, shown as `LabelStatus::leaving`/`GroupSync::leaving`; no timeout (node-sync's `drop_lost` drops it; not in the `osis` API). All from stored state, so a crash anywhere in between drops after restart; a self-removal whose `Done` was lost is placed by the next pass (it stays under its labels while the secrets are held).
- Rejected: gating on "every label idle" (it drops at reconnect before any pass ran, so an offline self-removal never goes up) and an ungated trigger (it drops a leaver that applied any received role change on the group before its removal went up).
