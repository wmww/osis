# Engine: lost access and forgetting
> Summary: How a node drops what it lost read access to (spec/permissions.md §3, spec/requirements.md Deletion): when an actor counts as lost, nothing taken back (boxes, entry boxes and copies opened later), the `l/` work list and its trigger, losses by an `until` alone, the pending-grant guard, what forgetting erases and keeps, sealed pending boxes, kept copies, bearers, self-removal drops and link races. Code: `rotation/lost.rs`, `retention/forget.rs`, the facade's `sync/held.rs`. Not here: why removal works this way (notes/permissions.md Revocation, Retained copy), the cover check's own self-removals (notes/engine-roles.md).

## When an actor is lost
An actor is lost when the node holds a secret of it (reading-only included; epochs observed without one are not access), neither the node, a held key actor nor a link key it keeps reading as has a role on it now (`lost::readable`: timeline, any path, `until` honoured), and no held unapplied role commit (pending, readable parked, late parts) boxes secrets to the node, a held key actor or an actor the node reads now, on an actor whose role reaches it (itself, its record chain, or one with a role on it). A grant not yet applied, whose secrets may already be read (spec/crypto.md §4), is never a loss. Each actor is judged on its own, so what the node reached only through a lost one is lost with it.
- **Reading-only guard**: an actor whose secrets the node holds only from a commit not yet judged (`Waits::reading`) is kept while that commit's entries are; a checked-secret holder of a lost actor drops and re-learns when the grant applies.
- **Link race** (`contested`): where a held single-use redemption granting one of ours stands void only because an applied redemption by the same signer removed the signer's edge first, with no explicit write on that edge since, the loser keeps the secrets (and syncs the sync IDs) until the sharer's finalization arrives: it may drop the competing redemption and make ours stand (spec/permissions.md §3, §7). A plain leave followed by a void grant (a leaver's forgery) defers nothing.

## Nothing taken back (`rotation::may_take`)
The principle: a node holds no secret of an actor it cannot read. The trigger drops what the node holds; the one place secrets are taken enforces it for what comes later, so no box's arrival needs judging again (re-judging after a join would loop: forget, refetch, rejoin).
- **Boxes**: `Core::ingest_view` (every applied `secrets.0` opening: received deltas, late or rejoined parts, `reopen_dependents` when a holder actor's secrets change, `reopen_all` at open) keeps a role's newly opened secrets only where `may_take` allows: `may_hold` (reading the actor: every role's; sync alone: sync's, as `over_held` judges) or a `contested` link race reaching it. What it keeps out is listed by role, only where a secret not held for use was kept out (one a pending read gave for reading only counts as absent: it goes once that commit is judged, `rotation::tests::box_applied_before_its_grant_is_taken_after_a_pending_read_spec_permissions_3`) (`Waits::untaken`, in memory; `take_account`'s `reopen_all` at open lists it again, a rolled-back transaction restores what it took off), and `flows::retake` opens that actor's whole `secrets.0` again once `may_take` allows a listed role: run by `apply::rejudged` (every apply that changed held role commits) and `keep_bearer`; its work is one `may_take` per listed actor and role. Without it a box applied before the grant that makes it ours (another admin's rotation boxing the grantee; commits of different authors apply in any order) would stay unopened (over random arrival orders with the trigger run each step, a reader holding as in order and a removed node nothing: `rotation::tests::arrival_order_holds_alike_spec_permissions_3`). The list is only a hint: `retake` opens through `ingest_view`, which judges `may_take` again, so listing more costs a retry, never a secret. Pending boxes (`SecretsRead::Pending`, `read_pending_boxes`) are a grant still to be judged and stay reading-only as below.
- **Entry boxes** (`Engine::learn_secret`, `may_learn`): the same rule where the held timeline shows an ended edge on the actor of the node, a held key actor or an actor it reads; otherwise the box is taken (a link's redeemer opens the sync ID before it holds the grant to its key, and may hold other role commits of the actor already), and the trigger judges the actor once role commits on it apply. Rejected: "any role commit of it held" as the test (the redeemer often holds some; links stall).
- **Copies** (`Engine::copy_stale`, `await_reading`): a per-sync-ID copy's section is under its blob key, so a reader of the sync ID opens it without the thing's secret. A copy left under a sync ID after its group lost the thing would give the content back, so no content part (`core_types::is_content`) on a thing under an own-keys actor the group actor may not read now is taken, and the commit waits by the thing's secret actor (`Waits::awaits_reading`); `Core::read_again` (secrets of it taken) names it in `Wakes::woken` to be opened again. In memory: a restart opens every missing part again. Nothing in osis-sync calls `copy_stale` or reads `Wakes` (issues/sync-unbuilt.md). Role-class parts are taken: each is judged as it is used (`secrets.0` boxes by `may_take`, entry boxes by `may_learn`, `account.0` seeds only under a devices epoch held for use; the rest is certificate material), and leaving one out would strand a slot whose copy carries it.
- Rejected: stripping such parts at engine ingest by "no secret held and no path reads" (`LocalPeer` and the order property hand plaintext to nodes that cannot read, and arrival before the secrets made what a node holds depend on order); marking the forgotten part `Missing::Forgot` (a copy arriving joins such a part anyway).

## The trigger (`l/` work list, `Engine::role_duties`)
- Applying a received role commit (or its late `roles.0` parts) adds its `roles.0` targets to the work list in the apply's transaction, as does dropping a pending role commit as bad input (its aspects' things: the reading-only secrets its boxes gave go unless something else reads them). Local commits never do.
- Every applied role commit, local too, lists each of its targets at the earliest `until` ahead on its roster (`arm_untils`; an `until` passing is a loss with no role commit behind it, spec/permissions.md §2), as does a trigger run for the entries it takes. `ix.lost_due_at` answers by time, the list holding one entry per thing with an `until` ahead.
- The driver of the engine runs the trigger after ingesting until nothing is due, and wakes at `Engine::next_lost_due`; osis-sync does not run it yet (issues/sync-unbuilt.md). One transaction takes the due entries and drops the held actors a role on a listed thing reaches that are lost (`LostScope::Due`), except those lost through an own self-removal still waiting (`held_wait`, below) or by time alone.
- Every scope re-lists each judged actor a plausible pending grant keeps, due when the earliest such grant ages out (value: that time, u64 BE; empty: now; an earlier entry stays), so a grant that never applies cannot keep lost secrets past it. Idempotent; a restart finds the list with its times (`load_lost_due`). `drop_lost_secrets` evaluates every held actor and takes the whole list.

## Lost by time alone
`lost_by_time`: walked with every edge's time ahead and every finalize undone, a role is there (a removal saying retain is written with `until` 0, gone at every clock, so it never counts). Such an actor gets a `T/` record when first judged and is listed in `Leaving::timed`: it drops once the sync IDs whose closure holds a thing the drop would forget (`Engine::would_forget`) are heard out, or `LOST_HEARD_WAIT_MS` (`ROLE_KEEP_MS`) after the record's time. So a renewal on its way and unsent writes from before the `until` come first, and no node keeping S unread holds an erasure off for good. The record goes with the drop or when the actor reads again (`prune_timed`). A role commit received beside the expired grant changes nothing. Hearing out in osis-sync is not built (issues/sync-unbuilt.md); a self-removal's done drops these too (`drop_lost_secrets`).
- A reader that may write the sync ID's actor finalizes the ended grant once past the `until` (`ensure_rotated`), judges the node departed and shares nothing with it; one that applied that finalize does the same, and the node's unsent writes are lost there.
- Rejected as the test: a refusal from a peer the node dials for the sync ID (the dial list comes from self-declared `reach.0`: any member of a shared group could claim a reader's account and make it drop).

## Forgetting (`retention/forget.rs`)
- The drop removes the lost actors' `k/` and, in the same transaction, forgets every thing whose secret actor is one of them and that neither the node nor a held key actor may read now (`Core::may_read`). Of such a thing only `created.0`, `retention^.0` and the role-class aspects other than `secrets.0` stay (`forget::KEPT`: certificate material perm judges others' writes by: grants, public keysets, `sync.0`).
- Shredded: aspect units (`drop_aspect`: state, tables); its parts in `c/` records (a commit left with no part is deleted, so a re-grant fetches it again; others keep their readable parts, not marked dropped, so the forgotten ones join late if they return: their secrets and, from a copy, their content only once the node reads them again, Nothing taken back above); pending parts; `a/` `v/` `p/` `s/` `w/`; timers of forgotten parts; the sync records of the forgotten groups' sync IDs but those a kept group owns, inbox hints; the ciphertexts nothing kept names (the store per sync ID drops its entries as their bodies leave `sync_bodies`). The thing's freed set goes (a removed party cannot delete), and the pieces the forgotten parts link go by `retention::freed::lost`: walked through held node blobs, released where no remaining source of the thing reaches them (its role-class states stay and may link pages) and the held prefix bears the link (a link copied from another thing releases nothing). Test: `freed::tests::a_link_copied_from_another_thing_frees_nothing_spec_crypto_7`.
- **Sealed pending boxes** (`rotation::boxes_kept`, `b/`): forgetting keeps the `secrets.0` parts of an unapplied commit signed by an author who may grant on the actor now, whatever its age, that box the node a read epoch newer than every checked one it held (a grant from after a rotation that left the node out), so a re-grant applied after the loss brings its boxes. Marked `b/`, they give `read_pending_boxes` no candidates until the commit applies (else they would keep the lost secrets for good). A box only at a held epoch is a grant from before the removal, which gives no role, so it goes (a re-grant after a self-removal with no rotation between boxes a held epoch too and loses its boxes: stranded until a re-seal). Costs: such a grant that never applies keeps its sealed box; a grant whose author's chain is not held yet cannot be checked and loses its boxes, recovered by a stranded report and a re-seal.
- The engine then reloads its indexes (`reload` keeps schemas); sync follows the facade values that moved. Event `Changed { cause: LostAccess }`, a `Removed` diagnostic per actor.
- A re-granted holder regains content by sync only: its own writes that reached no other node before it forgot are gone.

## Kept copies (`K/`; spec/permissions.md §3 Retain)
- `keeps` is a least fixpoint over ended grants saying retain (`Timeline::ended`, `EdgeEnd`; an `until` ending by `retain_in_force`), live ways from a kept holder and the held marks: a copy hangs on holder H while the node still has H (also while H's loss waits, `not_dropped`: secrets above sync held and H not in this judging; every drop re-judges every mark, so the copy follows H's fate) and the way through H keeps; a holder already recorded goes only on the timeline's word. Holders cannot keep each other. Re-judged when a due target touches a mark.
- A kept copy goes through `forget` with `Fate::Kept`: only `secrets.0`, its sync IDs' sync records and pending content are taken; `apply::ingest_class` strips later content parts on it (`without_frozen`, `forget::frozen`), a commit left with none being dropped unrecorded so a re-grant fetches it, and a commit's purge list still applies if another part holds the commit.
- No secret, no sync, no write; reads still read what it held. The user drops one with `LostScope::Kept` (`Engine::kept_copies`). A client actor reads a kept copy only as a holder the copy hangs on or an app of its account (`copy_readable_by`, notes/client-port.md §3).

## Bearers (`B/`, `Engine::keep_bearer`; spec/permissions.md §7 Bearer use)
`import_contact` forgets the card's seed and keeps L's tid, and L's role counts as the node's for `readable` and `read_path` only: never entitlement, `may_read` of the node, or what it may share. The record goes when L holds no role on what it is named on (`drop_ended_bearers`). Without it, the seed gone, the first role commit touching the account (a client or node added) would drop the profile and its secrets at every contact: name gone, updates stopped, a `Removed` diagnostic per actor at someone who removed nothing.

## Self-removal drops (the facade's `sync/held.rs`)
- One rule at every node of the account: applying a role commit (local or received, late `roles.0` parts too) that is an own self-removal (`rotation::own_self_removal`: the newest applied write that changed the holder's edge for keys, lowering it, a self-removal by its author, made here or authored by this node, its account or a key actor it holds; a sibling's concurrent removal of the same edge changes nothing and leaves the first one behind the departure) puts it in `held_wait` (`hw/`, durable; `Engine::waiting_removals`) in the apply's transaction. So the node that wrote it and every sibling that applies it wait alike (spec/permissions.md §3 Lost access: whichever node of the account made it).
- `own_commits` adds each waiting removal with a body under S, so status judges a sibling's copy held elsewhere as the writer's. Status calls `Engine::removal_done` once, under each read sync ID it has a body under, it is held elsewhere or no peer but the account's own nodes shares that sync ID (notes/sync-status.md Self-removals). The trigger skips a lost actor while an own self-removal behind it (`own_leaving`) waits.
- Only lost actors drop (`removal_done` judges again): a wait set by a key actor shared with another party, or any wrong `removal_done`, delays or ends a wait but never drops what the node still reads.
- `removal_done(c)` drops, per actor (`lost_leaving`, `LostScope::Only`), each lost actor with no other own self-removal behind it still waiting: one that cannot be placed holds back nothing else. The drop goes before `c` leaves the set, so a crash between finds it waiting and status tells it done again. No timeout: a peer that never takes it keeps it waiting while connected or recorded (the record budget bounds an away one).
- An own node is never the peer it waits for: it leaves by the same commit and passes nothing on, so with no outside peer they all drop at once.
- Rejected: a refusal or a rotation leaving the leaver out as the signal (any rotation the leaver is not in gives it, a hostile peer for free); a rotation naming its departures (a wire change leaking other departures' names and the fresh deposit seed); a bounded leaving period or a give-up call (a picked number, a user decision on every leave: the wait already ends once no outside peer shares where the removal goes); dropping in the removal's own transaction (it must still seal the removal under the sync IDs it leaves); setting the wait in the writing flow alone (a sibling that pulled the removal would drop before a source has it); an ungated trigger (it drops a leaver that applied any received role change on the group before its removal went out).
