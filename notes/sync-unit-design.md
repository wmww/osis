# Sync unit design: one independent thing and its dependents
> Summary: Design study for the decided sync unit (notes/sync-unit-scope.md): what the built model's multi-unit carriage consists of and what becomes of each piece, the member blobs by which a unit lists its direct independent members, how a holder thing's secrets reach a node from the member's own steps, the walk for readers and for nodes keeping a closure unread, promotion, attach and detach, removal, status over a closure, counted costs of many sync IDs, the wins with their numbers, and the cases that cross the account design. The staged build: notes/redesign-plan.md. Nothing here is built or modelled.

**Principle: a sync ID carries its independent thing and that thing's dependents, nothing else; every other fact about a closure is derived from data inside the units it is made of.** A reference between units is followed by entering the target's unit with a secret held for it, by readers and by nodes keeping the referrer unread alike. Nothing is written into a unit because of what refers to it, and nothing is written into a referrer because its target's keys changed.

Marks: **[checked: source]** read in the spec or code named; **[measured]** counted in a debug test (Costs, below); **[computed]** from byte layouts; **[argued]** reasoned, to be modelled before it is built. The design introduces no picked number. Open questions for Tess: notes/redesign-plan.md (Questions).

## 2. A unit's list of direct independent members
**Member blobs** [argued]. The list is not an aspect and not a field of a commit: it is derived data in the unit's required set, as certificates are.
- **What is in the set**: S(G)'s required set holds one member blob for each independent thing H such that G's unit holds a live strong to H or to a dependent of H (§6 on the second), and G holds sync or above on H with key access (spec/crypto.md §4 Key access), or H is public (Assumed of the account design). A function of what G's readers hold, so every reader computes the same set, and no member blob exists for a target G cannot enter (spec/requirements.md Data model: a reference from something that cannot read its target does not keep it).
- **Who logs it**: any writer of S(G) holding the data that puts it in the set, stamped under S(G)'s write key; the node writing the strong logs it in that transaction when G's role on H is already held. So a grant made after the strong, by someone who cannot write G, still gets its member blob from the next writer of G that holds both. Removing the last such strong, or G losing its role on H, puts the blob outside the required set: it leaves own stores with no tombstone and writers delete it at stores keeping S(G) unread, as record and list blobs go (checked: spec/sync-ids.md §6 Delete, Certificate blobs). Two writers logging one each: the lower blob ID wins, the other is deleted the same way.
- **What it holds**: H's sync IDs (one; each owned one after a forked promotion, spec/sync-ids.md §2) and, when its logger holds one, H's newest sync secret with its epoch, as a first credential. Nothing of what H is, who holds it, or where in G the reference sits. Sealed under `kdf("members", sync secret of G)` at G's sync epoch current at its logger.
- **Who opens it**: holders of a sync secret of G, so every holder of sync or above (checked: spec/permissions.md §1 Sync), and nobody else. Its prefix carries a blob class of its own, so a node keeping S(G) unread knows which entries to open; the class shows the number of members only to parties that hold S(G)'s ciphertext, which are those same holders.
- **Across G's sync rotations**: a step registering a new sync epoch carries the superseded sync secret sealed under the new one (48 bytes [computed]), so a holder of the head's sync secret opens member blobs logged under earlier epochs, and a removed holder opens none logged after its removal. Rejected: logging the list again at each rotation (work in the number of members at every removal from G; spec/requirements.md Scale has millions of things referenced from one thing).
- **Bytes** [computed from notes/thing-cost.md, Per entry, per sync ID a blob is under; spec/wire.md §5]: a blob of about 130 B (prefix about 62, sync ID 17, tag 16, optional secret about 41, padding) and its entry (blob ID 32, filler wrap 57, stamp 64); about 300 B on the wire and 400-450 B stored per member per node.
- **It is a role entry** (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries): taken outside caps' refusal, and applied at a source before the deletes it causes (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Role entries first).

**How the member's keys reach a holder of G: from the member's own steps** [argued].
- Today [checked: spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes, Holder actors; spec/sync-ids.md §2 What a sync holder learns; spec/sync-judgement.md §2]: a grant to G on H writes one box per granted role, each sealed to G's KEM key of that role. So H's sync secret is boxed to G's sync keyset and any holder of G's sync secret can open that box. It never gets the bytes: the box sits in H's `secrets.0` (a role part under H's roles secret), and its copy under S(G) is wrapped under G's read secret. A node keeping G unread therefore keeps H only by a ticket of its own, and gets each new sync secret of H by a fresh ticket (issues/one-open-proof.md).
- **Design**: H's step lists every holder thing with key access at sync or above, not only readers: a claim by the holder's sync-role X25519 key (today the read-role key, spec/sync-ids.md §3), and an **entry box** per such holder giving the sync secret the step registers (today entry boxes exist for link keys only, spec/wire.md §7). A holder of G's sync secret opens S(H) at any store by G's claim (spec/sync-opens.md §4, unchanged), receives the head step, opens G's entry box, and holds H's sync secret: it keeps S(H), opens H's member blobs, and goes on.
- **Consequences**: when H rotates, its next step carries the new secret to G's holders, so nothing is written in G and a node keeping the closure unread does not stop at H (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops) while G still holds its role. When G rotates, H's required rotation (spec/permissions.md §3) seals to G's new keyset. A grant or re-seal boxing a holder thing at sync is due a step listing it, as a rotation is (an admin may already issue a step for that: spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps); until an admin issues it, the member blob's first credential carries a node keeping G unread.
- **A writer that may write the reference but lacks H's sync secret**: it writes the strong. If it holds H's sync ID and G's role on H, it logs the member blob without the first credential; if not, no member blob is in the set as it sees it, and the next writer of G that does hold them logs it. No write is refused and nothing waits on H.
- Rejected: the entry carrying the member's current sync secret as its only source (a ticket). Every key change of H would owe a write in every unit that lists H, by a writer of that unit holding H's new secret, and each node keeping a referrer unread would drop H at the Register and fetch it again when the write arrives; a removal at a root rotates every unit below it, so every member blob in the closure would be owed again.
- **Bootstrap for readers** stays as built: H's boxes to G and H's `sync.0` are in S(G)'s required set (checked: spec/sync-judgement.md §2 A grant naming a holder travels under the holder's sync ID; spec/sync-ids.md §2 for a mint admin's record edge), the one place a unit carries role parts of another thing beside evidence. Moving the read secret into entry boxes too would end it (notes/redesign-plan.md, Q6).

## 3. A node keeping a closure unread
- **Walk** [argued]: the engine's closure tree (units.rs) gains out-edges for a unit kept unread: the sync IDs its held member blobs name. The node keeps S(H) while a unit it keeps lists H and it holds a credential of S(H)'s head (the claim through the referrer, or a sync secret); its roots are what its own strongs and tickets enter, as now (spec/sync-set.md §2, §4).
- **Dropping**: a unit no kept unit lists, and no own strong or ticket enters, leaves by spec/sync-set.md §3 (the pass in flight ended; no grace period). Reachability from the roots decides, not a count of referrers, so a cycle nothing outside references is released whole (spec/requirements.md Data model), by the removal search the tree already runs (checked: units.rs module comment, notes/closure-model.md P5).
- **Deletes that depend on the walk**: a member blob is deleted at a store only by a writer's Delete (above). A store that lost its only member blob for H to a hostile writer drops H's unit and fetches it again when an honest writer logs the blob again (spec/sync-judgement.md §4 Logged again once): a bounded writer, bounded churn; issues/cert-blob-hostile-delete.md is the same exposure for record blobs.
- **Caps** (`caps^.0`, spec/sync-ids.md §5): member blobs are entries and count; a per-thing cap on a root the node entered counts the entries of every unit that root reaches (a unit two roots reach counts under each), so a hostile writer listing members spends the root's room and nothing else. Past a cap the unit is full (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full), a defined outcome.
- **What it learns** that it does not today: at rest, for each unit it keeps, the opaque sync IDs of its direct members, hence the number of independent things in the closure and which refers to which; that the referrer holds sync or above on each member; overlap between closures (a unit two roots reach has one sync ID). Over time: which unit each entry is in, so activity per independent thing; when a member is listed or unlisted; at a promotion, which earlier entries of the former unit were the promoted thing's (§5). It still learns no tid, record, roster, role of a reference, content, author, or whether a strong targets the member or one of its dependents; nothing of strongs to things the referrer holds no role on; nothing of the dependents inside a unit.
- spec/requirements.md (Data a node cannot read) already says "the opaque sync units it keeps, which of them refer to which and which each item is in".

## 4. Readers
- **The walk** [argued]: inside a unit follow strongs between its things. At a strong to a thing whose independent H is another: enter H's unit **read** by a read secret held for H (held already, from H's boxes to a thing the node reads, or from a ticket); else **kept unread** by a sync secret or a claim through a holder (then its member blobs are followed, as §3: a reader keeping a unit unread and a node that reads nothing run one rule); else not entered. The node holds, of H's unit, what its strongs reach there.
- "The sync unit's keys open X" is replaced by "the node holds a secret for X's independent". Which secret a node holds for H is decided by H's roles and boxes alone; no rule asks whether a referrer could read.
- Tickets, entering on a ticket's word, and `NotCarried` (a strong whose target the node cannot enter) are as built.

## 5. Promotion
A dependent thing X of H becomes independent by one role commit P (`keys.0`, `secrets.0`, `sync.0` with S(X)'s genesis step; checked: spec/permissions.md §3, spec/sync-ids.md §2). X and the dependents whose independent it becomes move from H's unit to X's.
- **P is carried by both units for as long as H's unit references X**: S(H)'s required set keeps X's `created.0`, `sync.0` and P's boxes to the mint admin while the record edges stand (checked: spec/sync-ids.md §2, the nearest thing with a sync ID up the record chain carries them), and the member blob for X. So a reader of H that gets everything late still finds, in S(H) alone, that X moved and where. This is the rule that must not be lost: if P left S(H) with X's bodies, a late reader would see X frozen.
- **X's live bodies move by entries, not by bytes**: each is logged under S(X) as the held blob under a new wrap (checked: spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store, a held blob verbatim under a wrap for S), by the promoter in P's transaction and by any writer of S(X) holding a body S(X) lacks. P's history boxes carry the derived secrets (checked: spec/crypto.md §3 Making a thing independent), so a reader of X alone opens the sections. One entry per live blob of X's subtree: the one cost in the size of the thing, paid once per thing, as the built model pays it when S(X) is created.
- **They leave S(H) last**: X's bodies are outside S(H)'s required set once P is held, judged at the end of a complete pass begun after P arrived (checked: spec/sync-judgement.md §2 must-not-hold). New rule [argued]: a writer deletes such a body at a source keeping S(H) unread only once that source's stream of S(X) shows a carrier of each of its live deltas, a source full for S(X) counting as holding it (the Carriers rule of spec/sync-compaction.md §1, across the two sync IDs). So a node keeping the closure unread always holds X's content under one of the two.
- **A writer that has not seen P** writes X under S(H), sealed under a derived secret. Its stamp is good there, so stores take it; every reader of H holds X's unit too (H holds admin on X by its record edge), judges the body must-hold under S(X), and logs it there (verbatim where X's readers can open it; a fresh encryption under X's own epoch where it was sealed under a derived epoch newer than P's, spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on), then deletes it from S(H) by the rule above. The author does the same once it holds P. A reader of X alone gets it when the first node holding both units has synced, which the convergence requirement allows ("at the latest once a writer of that data has synced").
- **A node that gets P late**: until then it counts X dependent, logs X's bodies again under S(H) where others deleted them (bounded: once per change of ground, spec/sync-judgement.md §4), and misses writes made under S(X) only. P and the member blob are role entries in S(H), and the member blob's wrap has order 0 (fetched first, spec/sync-streams.md §5), so the first pass of S(H) ends the state. A node keeping H unread that gets the member blob late holds X's older content under S(H) until then and everything after.
- **What a node keeping both unread sees**: the same blob IDs appear under S(X) and then leave S(H), so it learns which earlier entries were X's. Logging fresh encryptions instead hides that at the cost of X's bytes sent again (notes/redesign-plan.md, Q5).
- **Open, to model before building**: a promotion judged invalid (the thing counts dependent again, spec/crypto.md §3) moves the bodies back, and under a dispute each side logs under the unit its view gives; forked promotions (two sync IDs, both listed in the member blob); a mixed commit on X and a thing staying in H's unit (one blob under each, the other's section sealed: spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Sealed sections).

## 6. Attaching, detaching, release
- **Attach** (a strong to an independent H added in G's unit): the commit, and one member blob where G holds a role on H. Nothing of H is touched or sent to anyone who holds it. **Detach**: the commit; the member blob leaves; H's unit is released at each node that nothing else reaches it from (spec/sync-set.md §3, with "for each sync ID that carried R" now one sync ID).
- **A thing in several closures**: one unit, one copy per node, listed by each referrer; kept while any kept unit lists it.
- **A referrer that cannot enter its target**: no member blob; a node enters H only by a secret of its own, and a node keeping G unread does not keep H for G's sake. A later grant to G puts the member blob in the set.
- **Goes**: spec/sync-set.md §3 A thing leaving a sync unit; the pull anew of an enclosing sync ID at a new strong (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies: the minting commit travels in the target's own unit); spec/sync-set.md §5's "a sync unit holding it syncs here" reduces to the thing's unit.
- **A strong from outside to a dependent thing brings its independent's unit, always** (decided, Tess). X is a dependent of H; G, another unit, references X strongly. The strong is a reference into H's unit: the member blob for H is in S(G)'s required set where G holds sync or above on H (§2), G's holders sync S(H), and a node keeping G unread keeps H's unit and what it lists. Nothing is promoted on reference. Whoever reads X reads H's unit already (X's secrets derive from H's), so nothing new is shown; whether X is independent changes cost, never behaviour, and the cheap route is making X independent, which sharing it does. A later promotion of X by someone who cannot write G keeps the path: P and X's member blob stay in S(H) (§5).

## 7. Removing a holder from a root
- **Rotations**: unchanged. A departure from A requires rotating, on every independent thing A's roles open, the roles the leaver could open through A (checked: spec/permissions.md §3 Required rotation): one rotation commit and one step per unit below, by whoever may rotate it, with one Register per store. The built model pays the same, since every independent thing already has its own sync ID with its own steps; what it adds is nothing here, and what it loses is nothing: the removed party could already fetch each inner thing under that thing's sync ID.
- **When the removed party stops fetching a unit from a node keeping it unread**: when that unit's next step is registered there (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops; spec/sync-opens.md §6), which needs an admin of that unit to issue it (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps). Per unit, not per root: a unit the remover does not administer keeps serving the removed party its ciphertext until one of its admins acts. Reading new content ends by the key rule, unchanged (spec/requirements.md Permissions).
- **What a node keeping the closure unread does**: follows each unit's Register; each new step's entry box for the referrer gives it the new sync secret (§2), in whatever order the units' steps arrive, since a unit kept at a head whose box it cannot open yet shows its older credentials and is moved on (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk, Behind).
- **Member blobs**: none is rewritten. New ones are sealed under the new sync epoch.
- **With the account redesign**: a removal of a signer is one entry under each unit where the signer could write or invite, in that unit's required set for the unit's life whether or not the unit holds a commit of the signer. The copy comes with the edge writes joining the holder the signer was removed from to the unit's thing, so the unit's readers judge it required from what the unit holds. Who logs it and how the units are found: §12, cases 2 and 6.

## 9. First sync and "caught up" over a closure
- Status stays per sync ID (spec/sync-status.md). **A closure's status** is derived: the units the walk reaches from a thing, each with its status, and a count of members listed but not yet open anywhere.
- **Caught up over a closure**: every unit the walk reaches is heard out (spec/sync-status.md §1) and no unit's member blobs name one not yet entered that the node could enter. It is a fixpoint: a pass of one unit can add members.
- **First sync of a closure**: member blobs have order 0 (fetched first, spec/sync-streams.md §5), so the walk outruns the content. Units found in one round open in one Offer and join in one Pull (checked: spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining), so round trips grow with the closure's depth, not its number of units [argued]; the measured 4 deliveries per unit says today's opens do not batch that well.
- **History complete for a thing**: a complete pass of its one unit. `wait_synced` on a thing waits on its unit; a wait over a closure waits on the fixpoint above and, like any wait, runs to its timeout while a unit some other node may hold is unconnected.

## 12. Cases crossing the account design
Each [argued] from this note, notes/write-path-design.md and notes/account-membership-design.md; S1 is a node's signer, A its account, both ordinary (A an independent thing).

1. **A commit with a stated path arrives in a unit.** S1 writes T, in unit U, with path [A, Org]. U's required set holds the commit's blob and, as evidence copies, what checks the path: the records of S1, A, Org and T's record chain; the edge writes on (S1, A) and (A, Org) with every held later change to them; (Org, T) is U's own role data; the vouches naming a held lowering; the same for those writes' own paths. The writer's node logs with the commit whatever of that U lacks (it holds all of it: it is a member of A and writes through Org); any node holding U and a later change to one of those edges logs that. A node keeping U unread stores the blob and the copies as entries it cannot tell apart, and learns nothing of the path. A commit that also writes a thing of another unit is one blob with a wrap under each, each unit holding the evidence of its own thing's path.
2. **A removal logged where the removed signer could write.** The remover reads A's unit: each grant A took at write or with an invite names its target's sync ID (Bootstrap, §2), so each is a unit to log under; a target that is a holder is followed through its own unit, and a promoted dependent through its record edge, which the former unit keeps (§5). Under each unit: one entry, the copy of R with that unit's leaves and the edge writes joining A to the unit's thing, stamped by the remover where its path gives write, deposited otherwise. A unit the remover does not hold it holds for the errand (case 3). What it cannot reach it keeps as a debt (R, sync ID); and any node holding R, a unit carrying it and a unit that one reaches logs it on, so a holder's unit the remover never held is walked by that holder's own members. Cost: an entry and a stamp per unit, a pass of each holder's unit; nothing per dependent thing (one unit), nothing in units the signer could only read.
3. **Hearing a unit out before removing, and what a unit lets a node fetch.** Nothing is opened at a peer for a sync ID the node does not sync (checked: spec/sync-opens.md §4, the shared set), and a commit's header is not fetched apart from its parts (checked: spec/sync-ids.md §1, one blob). So no header-only pull of a unit outside the closure exists, and none is added: the node **holds the unit for the errand**. One of its own things (its node's local part) gets a strong to the unit's thing; the walk enters the unit read by the secret held through A (§4); a complete pass brings its commit blobs; the strong goes and the unit is released (spec/sync-set.md §3). The same hold serves logging R under a unit outside the closure. A unit the account holds only sync on cannot be heard out, and need not be: the removed signer could not write it.
4. **A public profile.** The profile P is an independent thing public reads: one unit. Its steps list public by static entry, the same bytes on every public unit, with an entry box in symmetric form carrying the read and sync secrets (notes/write-path-design.md §3, special case 4), so anyone holding S(P) opens and reads it, a node keeping a referrer unread included; keeping P unread is then a choice, not an inability. Each unit G whose things reference P holds a member blob for P, whether or not G holds a role on it (§2), logged by a writer of G that holds P's sync ID (its owner, writing the reference). P is stored once per node and kept while any kept unit lists it. Against the five special cases: public signs nothing, so no member blob, step or deposit of P is public's own; public holds at most read, and a sync-only grant to it is allowed and pointless; public has no unit, so nothing is logged "under the holder" for it and Bootstrap has no public case; its keys are constants, so P's steps never go stale for it; a departure at or below read is due neither a rotation nor a step while public reads. What it shows: P's evidence names, to everyone, the signer that wrote each change of P and that signer's grant on A; and a node keeping G1 and G2 unread, opening their member blobs, sees both list S(P) (issues/sync-units-linked-at-rest.md). A hostile owner filling P spends the cap of each root that reaches it (§3, Caps), as a bounded writer of those units.
5. **An externally grantable thing.** A's admins enable `ticket@~0` on A's inbox I and place A's card on a thing outsiders read: A's tid and KEM keys, I's tid and record, its read KEM key, its sync ID and deposit seed (notes/write-path-design.md §3-§4). I is A itself, or a thing A administers with a unit of its own, listed by A's unit like any member. An outsider seals its fire (a commit by its own signer with its path, its evidence inside the sealed blob) to I's read KEM key and hands it, signed with the deposit key, to any peer that may hold S(I) (checked: spec/sync-inbox.md §2-§3). A node keeping S(I) unread checks the signature and the budget and stores it unstamped; it learns nothing of the sender. A writer of I promotes it once the fire is judged valid (the setting names the signal) and its evidence is under S(I). The outsider needs a peer holding S(I): one it meets, or one it dials from a dial entry it reads, which a public profile alone does not carry.
6. **Promotion while a removal of one of its writers is on its way.** X is a dependent of H; S1's account writes H, so X by descent; R removes S1. R's list has a leaf per thing, so X's leaf is the same before and after X is promoted. S(X) is a unit the removed signer could write, reached from H by X's record edge: the promoter logs under S(X), with X's bodies, each removal S(H) carries; a remover that walked H's unit before the promotion arrived missed S(X), and the standing duty covers it (any node holding R, S(H) and S(X) logs it). Until then a reader of X alone counts a backdated write of S1 that reaches it: availability, as for any unit owed. A write of S1 stamped before R and logged under S(H) by the late-writer rule (§5) is invalid at every reader of H, who hold R, so none logs it on under S(X). A promotion made by S1 itself and unlisted is invalid, which is the promotion judged invalid of §5 (to model there).
7. **The account as a unit.** A's unit is A and its dependents: home and every private thing minted with A, in any number, one sync ID. Its member list is A's roster; S(A)'s steps list each signer by a claim under its own key, with an entry box carrying the read and sync secrets, since a signer has no unit in which its grant could reach it. **Pairing**: the member grants the new signer a role on A and, as an admin, issues a step listing it; the new node gets A's tid and sync ID over the pairing connection, opens S(A) by its claim, reads the entry box and then A's role data. **Removing a node** is R, A's rotation and a step of S(A) in the remover's hands, so the removed node stops fetching A's unit at each store's next Register; units below wait for their own admins (§7). **What A's nodes hold**: A's unit, and what its strongs reach; a thing only some nodes should hold is referenced weakly in A's unit and strongly from that node's own things. **A node keeping A's closure unread** holds S(A)'s entries (commits of A and its dependents, role commits, evidence copies, deposits while A is its own inbox) and follows its member blobs to each independent thing A's unit references and may enter: accepted things, promoted home things, the inbox, the profile. It learns the number of units, which refers to which, their activity, and that S(A)'s claim set changed; no signer, tid or roster.

## Assumed of the account design
- An account is an independent thing with a sync ID; its signers are key things, each listed in its steps by a claim under its own key with an entry box (§12, case 7), so the chain from a node's signer to any unit's sync secret is the one mechanism of §2. Static entries stay for link keys and the public actor.
- Evidence is derived per unit from the commits it holds (notes/write-path-design.md §2), so a body moved at a promotion brings its evidence under S(X) by the same rule, logged by whoever logs the body.
- A removal of a signer is logged under each unit where the signer could write, invite or make an enabled reader-level act, by a writer of the unit or as a deposit, found and carried on as §12 cases 2 and 6 say (notes/account-membership-design.md §4).
- A public thing is one the public key actor reads; a member blob is in the set for a public H whether or not G holds a role on it, and anyone opens S(H) by the public actor's static entry. Which acts the public actor is barred from is that design's.
- A grant a holder takes is in the holder's unit with the target's boxes and `sync.0`; its members need no ticket. Tickets stay for what a node enters by a secret handed to it.

## Rejected
- **The list as an aspect, or a part of the commit writing the reference**: a node keeping the unit unread would merge a CRDT and judge covers and validity to know the current members; a derived blob it either holds or does not is all it can check.
- **The list in the clear, or as store operations**: fixed otherwise by Tess (notes/sync-unit-scope.md).
- **A count of referrers at the node**: a cycle never reaches zero (notes/sync-unit-scope.md).
