# Sync unit design: one independent thing and its dependents
> Summary: Design study for the decided sync unit (notes/sync-unit-scope.md): what the built model's multi-unit carriage consists of and what becomes of each piece, the member blobs by which a unit lists every thing its strongs target outside it, how a listed unit is entered by a secret of a thing its own steps list, the one walk for readers and for nodes keeping a closure unread, key paths through holders outside the closure, promotion, attach and detach, removal, status over a closure, counted costs of many sync IDs, the wins with their numbers, and the cases that cross the account design. The staged build: notes/redesign-plan.md. Nothing here is built; §2-§4 and §6 are modelled in part (notes/closure-model.md, The unit model).

**Principle: a sync ID carries its independent thing and the dependents that thing reaches, nothing else; every other fact about a closure is a function of data inside the units it is made of.** What a unit refers to is a function of its own strongs; who may enter a unit is a function of its own steps; a node enters a unit with a secret of a thing that unit's step lists, and holds a thing's secrets through units it has entered. So no rule reads a role across a unit boundary and none asks whether a referrer could enter its target; nothing is written into a unit because of what refers to it, and nothing is written into a referrer because its target's keys or roles changed. Readers and nodes keeping a closure unread run one walk.

Marks: **[checked: source]** read in the spec or code named; **[measured]** counted in a debug test (Costs, below); **[computed]** from byte layouts; **[argued]** reasoned, to be modelled before it is built. The design introduces no picked number. Open questions for Tess: issues/unit-dependent-release.md (§6), issues/unit-key-paths.md (§4).

## 2. What a unit lists
**Member blobs** [argued; the set modelled]. The list is not an aspect and not a field of a commit: it is derived data in the unit's required set, as certificates are.
- **What is in the set**: S(G)'s required set holds one member blob for each thing X outside G's unit that a live strong in G's unit targets. Nothing else is asked: not whether G holds a role on X's unit, not whether anyone can enter it. Every reader of G reads every strong of G's unit and holds the records and promotions that say which things are G's own (§5, §6), so every reader computes the same set whatever else it holds (notes/closure-model.md, P1 with `Member::Any`). A list that asked for G's role on the target would read a role record S(G) does not carry: readers of G would compute different lists and delete each other's blobs (there, Finding 1).
- **What it holds**. For holders of a sync secret of G: the sync IDs of X's unit (its independent's; each owned one after a forked promotion, spec/sync-ids.md §2), the key path where one is needed (§4), and, when its logger holds the grant giving G sync or above on X's independent and that thing's newest sync secret, that secret with its epoch as a first credential. For readers of G: a tag only they compute from X's tid (keyed as content hashes are, spec/data-model.md §7; one every reader computes at any epoch, a holder granted without history included), so each reader attributes a blob to its strong and a sync holder sees an opaque value. Nothing of what X is, who holds it, or where in G the reference sits. Sealed under `kdf("members", sync secret of G)` at G's sync epoch current at its logger.
- **Who logs it**: any writer of S(G) that holds the name of X's unit (it reads that unit, or reads a unit carrying its independent's `sync.0` by Bootstrap, below, and holds X's record chain), stamped under S(G)'s write key; the node writing the strong logs it in that transaction when it holds the name then. A writer that does not hold the name logs nothing; no write is refused and nothing waits on X. A strong nobody among G's writers can name lists nothing until one can: the reference is carried, its target is not (`NotCarried`, as built).
- **Who deletes it**, at stores keeping S(G) unread, as record and list blobs go (checked: spec/sync-ids.md §6 Delete, Certificate blobs); it leaves own stores with no tombstone:
  1. Its tag matches no live strong target of G's unit: any writer, judged alike by every reader of G from G's unit alone.
  2. Another blob of the same tag wins over it: one naming every sync ID and key path it names and more, or the same under a lower blob ID (the rule of record blobs, spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates). So two honest loggers that know different amounts end at the one that knows most.
  3. It names a sync ID that is not X's unit's, or a key path that does not stand, by data the writer holds (X's unit; the grants along the path): the writer logs the blob it counts right, then deletes this one. A writer not holding that data judges nothing of a blob's content.
  Rules 1 and 2 read G's unit and the two blobs only. Honest writers differ under rule 3 only while they hold different data about X's unit: until the next sync of that unit, or for good under a dispute over a promotion on X's record chain (§5, Open), where each logs again once per change of ground (spec/sync-judgement.md §4).
- **Who opens it**: the first part, holders of a sync secret of G, so every holder of sync or above (checked: spec/permissions.md §1 Sync), and nobody else. Its prefix carries a blob class of its own, so a node keeping S(G) unread knows which entries to open.
- **Across G's sync rotations**: a step registering a new sync epoch carries the superseded sync secret sealed under the new one (48 bytes [computed]), so a holder of the head's sync secret opens member blobs logged under earlier epochs, and a removed holder opens none logged after its removal. Rejected: logging the list again at each rotation (work in the number of members at every removal from G; spec/requirements.md Scale has millions of things referenced from one thing).
- **Bytes** [computed from notes/thing-cost.md, Per entry, per sync ID a blob is under; spec/wire.md §5]: a blob of about 160 B (prefix about 62, tag 32, sync ID 17, AEAD tag 16, optional secret about 41, 17 per key-path unit, padding) and its entry (blob ID 32, filler wrap 57, stamp 64); about 330 B on the wire and 450 B stored per node, per thing referenced across a unit boundary.
- **It is a role entry** (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries): taken outside caps' refusal, and applied at a source before the deletes it causes (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Role entries first).
- **What listing every target costs**: a blob per thing referenced, so many strongs into the dependents of one other unit cost a blob each (the cheap route is a strong to an independent thing, or one strong to the unit's thing); and the sync holders of G see the opaque sync ID of a unit G cannot enter (§3, What it learns). A blob per target unit would need every reader to know which unit each target is in to judge it, which is data outside G's unit.

**How a listed unit is entered: by a secret of a thing its own step lists** [argued].
- Today [checked: spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes, Holder actors; spec/sync-ids.md §2 What a sync holder learns, §3 Claim set; spec/sync-judgement.md §2]: a grant to G on H writes one box per granted role, each sealed to G's KEM key of that role. So H's sync secret is boxed to G's sync keyset and any holder of G's sync secret can open that box. It never gets the bytes: the box sits in H's `secrets.0` (a role part under H's roles secret), and its copy under S(G) is wrapped under G's read secret. A step's claim set lists the readers of its read epoch only. A node keeping G unread therefore keeps H only by a ticket of its own, and gets each new sync secret of H by a fresh ticket (issues/one-open-proof.md).
- **Design**: H's step lists every holder thing with a direct grant on H at sync or above with key access (spec/crypto.md §4 Key access: H's own roster, no chain), not only readers: a claim by the holder's sync-role X25519 key (today the read-role key, spec/sync-ids.md §3), and an **entry box** per such holder giving the sync secret the step registers (today entry boxes exist for link keys only, spec/wire.md §7). A node holding the sync secret of any listed thing opens S(H) at any store by that thing's claim (spec/sync-opens.md §4, unchanged), receives the head step, opens the entry box, and holds H's sync secret: it keeps S(H), opens H's member blobs, and goes on. Which listed thing, and how the node came by its secret, is not asked: the referrer, a unit two references up, a root of another closure the node keeps.
- **Consequences**: when H rotates, its next step carries the new secret to its holders' holders, so nothing is written in any referrer and a node keeping the closure unread does not stop at H (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops) while a thing whose secret it holds is listed. When a listed holder rotates, H's required rotation (spec/permissions.md §3) seals to its new keyset. A grant or re-seal boxing a holder thing at sync is due a step listing it, as a rotation is (an admin may already issue a step for that: spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps); until an admin issues it, the member blob's first credential carries a node keeping the referrer unread. A grant, a lowering or a removal on H changes no member blob anywhere.
- Rejected: the member blob carrying the member's current sync secret as its only source (a ticket). Every key change of H would owe a write in every unit that lists H, by a writer of that unit holding H's new secret, and each node keeping a referrer unread would drop H at the Register and fetch it again when the write arrives; a removal at a root rotates every unit below it, so every member blob in the closure would be owed again.
- **Bootstrap for readers** stays as built: H's boxes to G and H's `sync.0` are in S(G)'s required set (checked: spec/sync-judgement.md §2 A grant naming a holder travels under the holder's sync ID; spec/sync-ids.md §2 for a mint admin's record edge), the one place a unit carries role parts of another thing beside evidence. It names a direct grant only: the chain form of spec/permissions.md §4 Role changes travel (the role records of a chain's middle things under every unit above) goes with the readable-closure unit (§1), and a chain is used link by link (§3, §4). The read secret is in no step (Tess: not in this change; the step's X25519 exchange is not quantum-resistant, which a read secret must be, spec/requirements.md Confidentiality between readers; the form sealed to a KEM key is about 1.2 KB per holder per step).

## 3. The walk
One rule for a node that reads a closure, keeps it unread, or any mix [argued; modelled for direct grants].
- **State**: the units the node has entered and the secrets it holds. Its roots are what its own strongs and tickets enter, as now (spec/sync-set.md §2, §4).
- **Rule**: a unit named by a member blob of an entered unit is entered while the node holds a secret of a thing that unit's head step lists (or a ticket's secret for it): **read** when it holds a read secret of the unit's independent (from that thing's boxes to a holder whose unit the node reads, by Bootstrap, or from a ticket), else **kept unread** by the sync secret the step gives. Entering a unit gives its secrets, which may enter further units: the walk is the least fixpoint, and the order in which units and steps arrive does not matter. A unit the node cannot enter is not in its closure; a read of it fails and nothing else is affected.
- A node that reads a unit also resolves that unit's strongs by what it holds of their targets' units (it may hold a name no member blob carries yet; a writer then logs the blob, §2). A node keeping a unit unread follows its member blobs, which is all it can open.
- **Why a referrer's own role is not asked**: G → H → K with H holding no role on K. S(H) lists K. A node syncing H alone holds no secret K's step lists and does not enter K (spec/requirements.md Data model: a reference from something that holds no role on its target does not keep the target). A node syncing G, which holds a role on K, holds G's secret, which K's step lists, and enters K, read or unread (same item: a thing takes with it the part of its closure it can itself sync). A list holding only what H could enter would cut K off from every node keeping G unread (notes/closure-model.md, Findings, the one numbered 2).
- **A chain of grants through units of the closure** needs nothing more: G holds a role on W, W on H, and the closure reaches W. The walk enters W by G's secret and H by W's; a reader gets H's boxes to W from S(W) (Bootstrap), which it reads. A chain through a holder the closure does not reach: §4.
- **Dropping**: a unit no entered unit lists, and no own strong or ticket enters, leaves by spec/sync-set.md §3 (the pass in flight ended; no grace period); so does one whose step no longer lists a thing the node holds a secret of (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops). Reachability from the roots decides, not a count of referrers, so a cycle nothing outside references is released whole (spec/requirements.md Data model), by the removal search the tree already runs (checked: units.rs module comment, notes/closure-model.md P3, P5 of the readable-closure model).
- **Deletes that depend on the walk**: a member blob is deleted at a store only by a writer's Delete (§2). A store that lost its only member blob for X to a hostile writer drops X's unit, where nothing else lists it, and fetches it again when an honest writer holding the name logs the blob again (spec/sync-judgement.md §4 Logged again once): a bounded writer, bounded churn; issues/cert-blob-hostile-delete.md is the same exposure for record blobs. A forged blob makes a node enter only a unit whose step lists a thing it holds a secret of, so nothing it was not already given; the next honest writer deletes it by rule 1 or 3 of §2.
- **Caps** (`caps^.0`, spec/sync-ids.md §5): member blobs are entries and count; a per-thing cap on a root the node entered counts the entries of every unit that root reaches (a unit two roots reach counts under each), so a hostile writer listing members spends the root's room and nothing else. Past a cap the unit is full (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full), a defined outcome.
- **What a node keeping units unread learns** that it does not today: at rest, for each unit it keeps, the opaque sync IDs its strongs lead to and how many distinct things outside it they target, whether or not the node keeps or can enter those units; hence, among the units it keeps, how many there are in a closure and which refers to which; that a thing it holds a secret of holds sync or above on each unit it enters; the opaque units on a key path (§4); overlap between closures (a unit two roots reach has one sync ID). Over time: which unit each entry is in, so activity per independent thing; when a target is listed or unlisted; at a promotion, which earlier entries of the former unit were the promoted thing's (§5). It still learns no tid, record, roster, role of a reference, content or author; nothing of the dependents inside a unit beyond how many of them are referenced from a unit it keeps.
- spec/requirements.md (Data a node cannot read) says "the opaque sync units it keeps, which of them refer to which and which each item is in". The sync ID of a unit the node does not keep is more than those words: an opaque value that links nothing unless the node, or a party it shares with, meets that unit elsewhere. The item's wording follows at B1's spec brief (issues/sync-unit-dependent-tree.md).

## 4. Key paths: a role through a holder outside the closure
[argued, not modelled; built only if Tess keeps it: issues/unit-key-paths.md]
- **The case**: G → X, X in H's unit; G holds a role on H only through W (G on W, W on H), and no strong of the closure reaches W. The node holds G's secret; H's step lists W; W's secret comes from W's steps, and nothing the node holds names S(W) to a node that cannot read G (a reader of G holds W's `sync.0` and boxes by Bootstrap, one link deep).
- **The rule that follows from the principle**: a role through a holder is used with the holder's secrets, and a node holds them through the holder's unit. So the reference names the holders on its way: the member blob for X carries a **key path**, the sync IDs of the holder units from G's side to H's, logged by a writer that holds the grants along it (G's on W from S(G); W's on H from H's roster or S(W)), judged by such writers only (§2, rules 2 and 3), absent where G holds a role on H directly or none that the logger sees.
- **A unit on a key path is entered for keys**: the node opens it by a secret it holds, follows its steps, and, where it holds a read secret of it, pulls its role entries (order 0, where Bootstrap sits); it stores nothing else of it and does not follow its member blobs. It is dropped with the last member blob naming it.
- **Cost**: per reference through such a holder, 17 B per unit on the path, one open and one head step per path unit per store, and the role entries of that unit for a reader. Nothing at a rotation or a removal: the path names units, not secrets. A node keeping G unread learns the opaque units between G and H.
- **Without it** (what the plan builds first): a role through a holder counts for a closure while the closure holds the holder. The reference above is carried and its target is not (`NotCarried`), for readers and nodes keeping G unread alike, until something in the closure references W or G is granted a role on H itself. Sharing through a thing the grantees also reference (a realm and its documents, a group its members follow) is unaffected.
- Rejected: carrying a chain's role records and boxes under every unit above it (the built Role changes travel: a grant below writes into every unit above, and gives a node keeping those units unread nothing); a list in each unit of everything it holds a role on, searched when a listed unit cannot be entered (every reference to a thing the referrer cannot sync starts a search over the key graph); a step listing holders of holders (a unit would depend on who holds roles on its holders, against spec/requirements.md Permissions).

## 5. Promotion
A dependent thing X of H becomes independent by one role commit P (`keys.0`, `secrets.0`, `sync.0` with S(X)'s genesis step; checked: spec/permissions.md §3, spec/sync-ids.md §2). X and the dependents whose independent it becomes move from H's unit to X's.
- **P is carried by both units for as long as H's unit references X**: S(H)'s required set keeps X's `created.0`, `sync.0` and P's boxes to the mint admin while the record edges stand (checked: spec/sync-ids.md §2, the nearest thing with a sync ID up the record chain carries them), and the member blob for X. So a reader of H that gets everything late still finds, in S(H) alone, that X moved and where. This is the rule that must not be lost: if P left S(H) with X's bodies, a late reader would see X frozen.
- **X's live bodies move by entries, not by bytes**: each is logged under S(X) as the held blob under a new wrap (checked: spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store, a held blob verbatim under a wrap for S), by the promoter in P's transaction and by any writer of S(X) holding a body S(X) lacks. P's history boxes carry the derived secrets (checked: spec/crypto.md §3 Making a thing independent), so a reader of X alone opens the sections. One entry per live blob of X's subtree: the one cost in the size of the thing, paid once per thing, as the built model pays it when S(X) is created.
- **They leave S(H) last**: X's bodies are outside S(H)'s required set once P is held, judged at the end of a complete pass begun after P arrived (checked: spec/sync-judgement.md §2 must-not-hold). New rule [argued]: a writer deletes such a body at a source keeping S(H) unread only once that source's stream of S(X) shows a carrier of each of its live deltas, a source full for S(X) counting as holding it (the Carriers rule of spec/sync-compaction.md §1, across the two sync IDs). So a node keeping the closure unread always holds X's content under one of the two.
- **A writer that has not seen P** writes X under S(H), sealed under a derived secret. Its stamp is good there, so stores take it; every reader of H holds X's unit too (H holds admin on X by its record edge), judges the body must-hold under S(X), and logs it there (verbatim where X's readers can open it; a fresh encryption under X's own epoch where it was sealed under a derived epoch newer than P's, spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on), then deletes it from S(H) by the rule above. The author does the same once it holds P. A reader of X alone gets it when the first node holding both units has synced, which the convergence requirement allows ("at the latest once a writer of that data has synced").
- **A node that gets P late**: until then it counts X dependent, logs X's bodies again under S(H) where others deleted them (bounded: once per change of ground, spec/sync-judgement.md §4), and misses writes made under S(X) only. P and the member blob are role entries in S(H), and the member blob's wrap has order 0 (fetched first, spec/sync-streams.md §5), so the first pass of S(H) ends the state. A node keeping H unread that gets the member blob late holds X's older content under S(H) until then and everything after.
- **What a node keeping both unread sees**: the same blob IDs appear under S(X) and then leave S(H), so it learns which earlier entries were X's. Logging fresh encryptions instead hides that at the cost of X's bytes sent again (notes/redesign-plan.md, Q5).
- **Open, to model before building**: a promotion judged invalid (the thing counts dependent again, spec/crypto.md §3) moves the bodies back, and under a dispute each side logs under the unit its view gives; forked promotions (two sync IDs, both listed in the member blob); a mixed commit on X and a thing staying in H's unit (one blob under each, the other's section sealed: spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Sealed sections).

## 6. Inside a unit, attaching, detaching, release
- **A dependent thing is in its unit while its unit reaches it** [argued; waits on issues/unit-dependent-release.md]: S(H) carries H and each dependent of H reached from H by strongs through things of H's unit, and nothing for having been minted there (spec/requirements.md Offline and network; spec/sync-set.md §2 as it stands). Every reader of H reads those strongs, so every reader computes the same unit, and a node entering a unit holds it whole. A dependent the unit stops reaching leaves S(H) as built: holders drop it, delete-key holders delete its entries at stores keeping S(H) unread, a holder whose view still reaches it logs it again (spec/sync-set.md §3 A thing leaving a sync unit, on one sync ID); its own strongs stop listing anything (§2, rule 1). Work in proportion to what is released. The other rule, a unit carrying every dependent ever minted in it, makes the unit a function of records alone and never releases a thing (notes/closure-model.md, Findings, the one numbered 4).
- **What that costs**: a strong from another unit does not keep a dependent thing its own unit has dropped. G → X with X a dependent of H that H's unit no longer reaches is carried and holds nothing (`NotCarried`), though G may hold read on H; were X independent, the same strong would keep it. A node cannot leave a thing it makes in that state: the making rule (spec/sync-set.md §2 Making a thing) asks that a new dependent thing be reached inside its own unit or the local part.
- **Attach** (a strong to a thing outside the unit added in G's unit): the commit, and one member blob where its writer holds the name. Nothing of the target's unit is touched or sent to anyone who holds it. **Detach**: the commit; the member blob leaves; the target's unit is released at each node that nothing else reaches it from (spec/sync-set.md §3, with "for each sync ID that carried R" now one sync ID).
- **A thing in several closures**: one unit, one copy per node, listed by each referrer; kept while any entered unit lists it and a held secret enters it.
- **A referrer that cannot enter its target**: the member blob is there all the same, and gives a node nothing but a name: it enters the target only by a secret it holds, whatever reference led it there (§3). A later grant to the referrer needs no write in its unit.
- **Goes**: the pull anew of an enclosing sync ID at a new strong (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies: the minting commit travels in the target's own unit); spec/sync-set.md §5's "a sync unit holding it syncs here" reduces to the thing's unit; spec/sync-set.md §3 A thing leaving a sync unit shrinks to the first item above.
- **A strong from outside to a dependent thing brings its independent's unit, always** (decided, Tess). X is a dependent of H; G, another unit, references X strongly. The strong is a reference into H's unit: S(G) lists it (§2), and a node holding a secret H's step lists enters H's unit and holds it whole, X where that unit carries it. Nothing is promoted on reference. Whoever reads X reads H's unit already (X's secrets derive from H's), so nothing new is shown; the cheap route is making X independent, which sharing it does. A later promotion of X by someone who cannot write G keeps the path: P and X's member blob stay in S(H) (§5), and a writer of G holding P names X's own unit in S(G) (§2, rule 3).

## 7. Removing a holder from a root
- **Rotations**: unchanged. A departure from A requires rotating, on every independent thing A's roles open, the roles the leaver could open through A (checked: spec/permissions.md §3 Required rotation): one rotation commit and one step per unit below, by whoever may rotate it, with one Register per store. The built model pays the same, since every independent thing already has its own sync ID with its own steps; what it adds is nothing here, and what it loses is nothing: the removed party could already fetch each inner thing under that thing's sync ID.
- **When the removed party stops fetching a unit from a node keeping it unread**: when that unit's next step is registered there (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops; spec/sync-opens.md §6), which needs an admin of that unit to issue it (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps). Per unit, not per root: a unit the remover does not administer keeps serving the removed party its ciphertext until one of its admins acts. Reading new content ends by the key rule, unchanged (spec/requirements.md Permissions).
- **What a node keeping the closure unread does**: follows each unit's Register; each new step's entry box for a listed thing whose secret it holds gives it the new sync secret (§2), in whatever order the units' steps arrive, since a unit kept at a head whose box it cannot open yet shows its older credentials and is moved on (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk, Behind).
- **Member blobs**: none is rewritten, in the root's unit or below, whatever the number of members. New ones are sealed under the new sync epoch.
- **With the account redesign**: a removal of a signer is one entry under each unit where the signer could write or invite, in that unit's required set for the unit's life whether or not the unit holds a commit of the signer. The copy comes with the edge writes joining the holder the signer was removed from to the unit's thing, so the unit's readers judge it required from what the unit holds. Who logs it and how the units are found: §12, cases 2 and 6.

## 9. First sync and "caught up" over a closure
- Status stays per sync ID (spec/sync-status.md). **A closure's status** is derived: the units the walk reaches from a thing, each with its status, and a count of members listed but not yet open anywhere.
- **Caught up over a closure**: every unit the walk reaches is heard out (spec/sync-status.md §1) and no unit's member blobs name one not yet entered that the node could enter. It is a fixpoint: a pass of one unit can add members.
- **First sync of a closure**: member blobs have order 0 (fetched first, spec/sync-streams.md §5), so the walk outruns the content. Units found in one round open in one Offer and join in one Pull (checked: spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining), so round trips grow with the closure's depth, not its number of units [argued]; the measured 4 deliveries per unit says today's opens do not batch that well.
- **History complete for a thing**: a complete pass of its one unit. `wait_synced` on a thing waits on its unit; a wait over a closure waits on the fixpoint above and, like any wait, runs to its timeout while a unit some other node may hold is unconnected.

## 12. Cases crossing the account design
Each [argued] from this note, notes/write-path-design.md and notes/account-membership-design.md; S1 is a node's signer, A its account, both ordinary (A an independent thing).

1. **A commit with a stated path arrives in a unit.** S1 writes T, in unit U, with path [A, Org]. U's required set holds the commit's blob and, as evidence copies, what checks the path: the records of S1, A, Org and T's record chain; the edge writes on (S1, A) and (A, Org) with every held later change to them; (Org, T) is U's own role data; the vouches naming a held lowering; the same for those writes' own paths. The writer's node logs with the commit whatever of that U lacks (it holds all of it: it is a member of A and writes through Org); any node holding U and a later change to one of those edges logs that. A node keeping U unread stores the blob and the copies as entries it cannot tell apart, and learns nothing of the path. A commit that also writes a thing of another unit is one blob with a wrap under each, each unit holding the evidence of its own thing's path.
2. **A removal logged where the removed signer could write.** The remover reads A's unit: each grant A took at write or with an invite names its target's sync ID (Bootstrap, §2), so each is a unit to log under; a target that is a holder is followed through its own unit, and a promoted dependent through its record edge, which the former unit keeps (§5). Under each unit: one entry, the copy of R with that unit's leaves and the edge writes joining A to the unit's thing, stamped by the remover where its path gives write, deposited otherwise. A unit the remover does not hold it holds for the errand (case 3). What it cannot reach it keeps as a debt (R, sync ID); and any node holding R, a unit carrying it and a unit that one reaches logs it on, so a holder's unit the remover never held is walked by that holder's own members. Cost: an entry and a stamp per unit, a pass of each holder's unit; nothing per dependent thing (one unit), nothing in units the signer could only read.
3. **Hearing a unit out before removing, and what a unit lets a node fetch.** Nothing is opened at a peer for a sync ID the node does not sync (checked: spec/sync-opens.md §4, the shared set), and a commit's header is not fetched apart from its parts (checked: spec/sync-ids.md §1, one blob). So no header-only pull of a unit outside the closure exists, and none is added: the node **holds the unit for the errand**. One of its own things (its node's local part) gets a strong to the unit's thing; the walk enters the unit read by the secret held through A (§3); a complete pass brings its commit blobs; the strong goes and the unit is released (spec/sync-set.md §3). The same hold serves logging R under a unit outside the closure. A unit the account holds only sync on cannot be heard out, and need not be: the removed signer could not write it.
4. **A public profile.** The profile P is an independent thing public reads: one unit. Its steps list public by static entry, the same bytes on every public unit, with an entry box in symmetric form carrying the read and sync secrets (notes/write-path-design.md §3, special case 4), so anyone holding S(P) opens and reads it, a node keeping a referrer unread included; keeping P unread is then a choice, not an inability. Each unit G whose things reference P holds a member blob for P, as for any target (§2), logged by a writer of G that holds P's sync ID (its owner, writing the reference). P is stored once per node and kept while any kept unit lists it. Against the five special cases: public signs nothing, so no member blob, step or deposit of P is public's own; public holds at most read, and a sync-only grant to it is allowed and pointless; public has no unit, so nothing is logged "under the holder" for it and Bootstrap has no public case; its keys are constants, so P's steps never go stale for it; a departure at or below read is due neither a rotation nor a step while public reads. What it shows: P's evidence names, to everyone, the signer that wrote each change of P and that signer's grant on A; and a node keeping G1 and G2 unread, opening their member blobs, sees both list S(P) (issues/sync-units-linked-at-rest.md). A hostile owner filling P spends the cap of each root that reaches it (§3, Caps), as a bounded writer of those units.
5. **An externally grantable thing.** A's admins enable `ticket@~0` on A's inbox I and place A's card on a thing outsiders read: A's tid and KEM keys, I's tid and record, its read KEM key, its sync ID and deposit seed (notes/write-path-design.md §3-§4). I is A itself, or a thing A administers with a unit of its own, listed by A's unit like any member. An outsider seals its fire (a commit by its own signer with its path, its evidence inside the sealed blob) to I's read KEM key and hands it, signed with the deposit key, to any peer that may hold S(I) (checked: spec/sync-inbox.md §2-§3). A node keeping S(I) unread checks the signature and the budget and stores it unstamped; it learns nothing of the sender. A writer of I promotes it once the fire is judged valid (the setting names the signal) and its evidence is under S(I). The outsider needs a peer holding S(I): one it meets, or one it dials from a dial entry it reads, which a public profile alone does not carry.
6. **Promotion while a removal of one of its writers is on its way.** X is a dependent of H; S1's account writes H, so X by descent; R removes S1. R's list has a leaf per thing, so X's leaf is the same before and after X is promoted. S(X) is a unit the removed signer could write, reached from H by X's record edge: the promoter logs under S(X), with X's bodies, each removal S(H) carries; a remover that walked H's unit before the promotion arrived missed S(X), and the standing duty covers it (any node holding R, S(H) and S(X) logs it). Until then a reader of X alone counts a backdated write of S1 that reaches it: availability, as for any unit owed. A write of S1 stamped before R and logged under S(H) by the late-writer rule (§5) is invalid at every reader of H, who hold R, so none logs it on under S(X). A promotion made by S1 itself and unlisted is invalid, which is the promotion judged invalid of §5 (to model there).
7. **The account as a unit.** A's unit is A and its dependents: home and every private thing minted with A, in any number, one sync ID. Its member list is A's roster; S(A)'s steps list each signer by a claim under its own key, with an entry box carrying the read and sync secrets, since a signer has no unit in which its grant could reach it. **Pairing**: the member grants the new signer a role on A and, as an admin, issues a step listing it; the new node gets A's tid and sync ID over the pairing connection, opens S(A) by its claim, reads the entry box and then A's role data. **Removing a node** is R, A's rotation and a step of S(A) in the remover's hands, so the removed node stops fetching A's unit at each store's next Register; units below wait for their own admins (§7). **What A's nodes hold**: A's unit, and what its strongs reach; a thing only some nodes should hold is referenced weakly in A's unit and strongly from that node's own things. **A node keeping A's closure unread** holds S(A)'s entries (commits of A and its dependents, role commits, evidence copies, deposits while A is its own inbox) and follows its member blobs to each unit A's unit references that a secret it holds enters: accepted things, promoted home things, the inbox, the profile. It learns the number of units, which refers to which, their activity, and that S(A)'s claim set changed; no signer, tid or roster.

## Assumed of the account design
- An account is an independent thing with a sync ID; its signers are key things, each listed in its steps by a claim under its own key with an entry box (§12, case 7), so the chain from a node's signer to any unit's sync secret is the one mechanism of §2. Static entries stay for link keys and the public actor.
- Evidence is derived per unit from the commits it holds (notes/write-path-design.md §2), so a body moved at a promotion brings its evidence under S(X) by the same rule, logged by whoever logs the body.
- A removal of a signer is logged under each unit where the signer could write, invite or make an enabled reader-level act, by a writer of the unit or as a deposit, found and carried on as §12 cases 2 and 6 say (notes/account-membership-design.md §4).
- A public thing is one the public key actor reads; it is listed like any target (§2), and anyone opens S(H) by the public actor's static entry. Which acts the public actor is barred from is that design's.
- A grant a holder takes is in the holder's unit with the target's boxes and `sync.0`; its members need no ticket. Tickets stay for what a node enters by a secret handed to it.

## Rejected
- **The list as an aspect, or a part of the commit writing the reference**: a node keeping the unit unread would merge a CRDT and judge covers and validity to know the current members; a derived blob it either holds or does not is all it can check.
- **The list in the clear, or as store operations**: fixed otherwise by Tess (notes/sync-unit-scope.md).
- **A count of referrers at the node**: a cycle never reaches zero (notes/sync-unit-scope.md).
- **A member blob only where the referrer holds a role on the target**: by a chain of roles it reads role records the unit does not carry, so readers split (notes/closure-model.md, Findings, the one numbered 1); by a direct grant every reader agrees, and a node keeping a root unread still misses whatever the root can sync through a unit that cannot (the one numbered 2).
- **The root listing every unit of its closure**: only readers of the root can keep it right, so a reference written by someone who reads an inner unit alone waits for one (notes/sync-unit-scope.md).
- **A reader holding of a unit only what its strongs reach there**: a unit is then a different set at each node, and what a store keeping it unread must hold is no function of the unit.
