# A2: readings the spec does not hold
> Summary: Stage A2 (the sync rewrite, built: evidence per sync unit, grants with the thing granted; spec/permissions.md §4; spec/sync-set.md §2; spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§2; spec/sync-inbox.md §2, §4, §5; spec/crypto.md §7 step-checked delete keys; spec/wire.md §4, §9; spec/commits.md §4): the design readings behind the spec, and its questions still open with their options, by number.

The rules are the spec's (from notes/write-path-design.md §2, §4, F6 and notes/delete-keys.md; oracle held-model `evidence`). What the tree is and what it still owes: notes/sync-rewrite-state.md. This note holds only what neither says.

## Readings
- **No grant is taken**: a grant belongs to the thing granted; a node's role is by the grants of what it syncs (notes/stubs.md). A link redemption is a grant from outside the redeemer's account.
- **Evidence takes every grant write on a stated path's grants** (write-path-design §2 E1); re-grant reads "made by right".

## Open
<!-- registry -->
*taken* marks what the spec writes meanwhile, **settles** what closes it. Numbers kept: code and notes cite them.
2. **Which grant writes the closure takes**: settled, E1 (the coordinator): every held grant write on a stated path's grants, whatever its validity, and a part pends on any. Taking only grants that could take effect along joined paths would leave writes through unjoined paths waiting for good, an unbounded stall. E1's two costs, the roster leak (a stated path no grant joins copies a roster giving no role on the unit) and junk copies (a non-admin's grant writes on a used grant), are stated limits (spec/sync-hostile.md §2).
3. **A wrong delete key: invalid or pending**: settled, pending (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable): a part listing a key no held step places is unjudgeable, so a reader holding the write before the `sync.0` write recording its step keeps it until the step arrives; a writer of S listing made-up keys leaves parts unapplied for good, bounded as any content it may write is (notes/delete-keys.md, What a verdict does). Not modelled with steps (held-model `evidence`; issues/engine-evidence-trails-model.md).
4. **Steps under role compaction**: settled: a `sync.0` step entry carried by a role compaction snapshot keeps its recording write's stamp for "ordered before the commit", and the snapshot is a copy recording the step (notes/evidence-derivation.md, Engine hand tests).
7. **Pull of role entries alone**: settled: no selector on `Pull`. A node holding S's thing as a stub declares it as its class opening S (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source, `class` 2 in spec/wire.md §11.2 Offer), and a server shows such a peer S's role entries alone (spec/sync-streams.md §4). The class also says what a selector could not: that the peer is no source of S's content (spec/sync-status.md §2 counts it by its stream alone), on every connection and before any Pull.
10. **Deletes at peers resting on a verdict wait for a complete pass**: *taken* (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Deletes); not built (acting only after a complete pass). **Settles**: covers in `evidence`, property "no body is lost that the node holding everything keeps".
11. **Compaction on a provisional verdict**: *taken* at once (spec/commits.md §4 Provisional). **Settles**: Tess, only if the window widens.
12. **Hostile deletes of copies and record blobs at stores**: issues/cert-blob-hostile-delete.md.
13. **The derived-era check**: settled in spec (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys): a key is right when any thing up the delta's thing's record chain places it, so derived-era content and a writer that has not received the thing's own secrets list the former base's step (notes/delete-keys.md). The steps ride as the write's evidence. Not modelled.
14. **The grants a member writes**: settled: spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit takes the writes on each grant the commit's own grant writes set (finding 9: a removal waits for the creations its resets name) and on a descent's record grants, as held-model `Rule::commit_grants` and the derivation do.
15. **A wrong key is judged per thing**: settled in spec (spec/crypto.md §7, spec/commits.md §4): a commit listing a wrong key for a thing has its deltas on that thing unjudgeable, not the commit whole; a node holding one thing's evidence cannot judge the keys listed for another.
17. **A writer recording no step**: a link's redeemer holds K's boxes before it joins and writes its join there as the link key; with `sync.0` a role part it holds K's steps with K's role parts (notes/delete-keys.md, What a verdict does). What the key check leaves: issues/demoted-writer-deletes-at-stores.md.
