## Decided while writing the spec
- **Tickets go whole**, not cut to an offer: a grant by a holder's writers is the holder's own data, so its nodes learn it by syncing the holder; a grant from outside is itself the `grant@~0` fire on the holder, which tells the holder the thing exists and carries the boxes and `sync.0` (the offer is that message, nothing more). The ticket reference kind, its CBOR tag, `tickets.0`, the account's tickets, `ticket@~0`, key-change tickets, fetching on a ticket's word, and removal notices (`removal@~0`: a removal reaches the holder's nodes through the target they sync or hold for keys) are gone. For Tess: spec/requirements.md Concepts ("a **ticket** also carries what is needed to sync its target") and Offline ("shared only by granting a role and handing over a ticket") name the removed concept; no property breaks (sharing is a grant plus the grantee's own strong). Her wording change.
- **Box delivery stays where it must**: a rotation or re-seal boxing a holder that cannot open it from the target's own sync ID (sync alone, re-sealed) goes to the holder's sync ID as a *sealed copy* (a certificate copy sealed like a fire, promoted iff in the holder's key part, bound by no setting). This is the edge piece placed where it belongs, not a notification, but it is the one remaining push into a sync ID the sender does not sync.
- **No grant is taken**: a grant belongs to the thing granted; a `grant@~0` fire is an offer and a node's role is by the grants of what it syncs (notes/holder-access-study.md, The direction). A link redemption is a grant from outside the redeemer's account.
- **Interim placement until A4**: an account's key part is under its account root D's sync ID, and what reaches it from outside under its inbox's. The account's creation keeps `grant@~0` the inbox lifetime.
- **Chained writes go**: evidence takes every edge write on a stated path's edges (write-path-design §2 E1); re-grant already reads "made by right".
- **Role changes travel** keeps keys-open travel (a role commit naming H in the required set of each sync ID whose thing's keys open H), as finding 19 requires; the holder-side half is the key part.

Its two costs, the roster leak (P9) and junk copies (P10), are stated limits (spec/sync-hostile.md §2).
3. **A wrong delete key: invalid or pending.** *taken*: invalid on its thing (decided; Open 15), judged again as the base's `sync.0` moves. A writer lists its newest step, so a reader holding the write before the `sync.0` write recording that step judges it invalid and drops it, fetching it again once the step arrives (spec/commits.md §5 Invalid); with every holder so and the writer gone it is lost. Option: pend while no held step bears the key (a wrong key then never valid anywhere, kept within Pending storage bounds). **Settles**: held-model `evidence` with steps (not modelled), property P1 with steps arriving after bodies.
4. **Steps under role compaction**: settled: a `sync.0` step entry carried by a role compaction snapshot keeps its recording write's stamp for "ordered before the commit", and the snapshot is a copy recording the step (notes/evidence-derivation.md, Engine hand tests).
5. **Own boxes against "a commit applies with all its pages"** (holder-access-study weak spot 1): a node held for keys fetches only the `secrets.0` pages it opens; a re-sealer and the stale-box rules may read other boxes. *taken*: a node that may rotate fetches them all (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries). **Settles**: brief 4, issues/holder-key-churn.md.
6. **Role entries are wider than keys** (weak spot 5): commits carrying purge requests and blobs they link are role entries too, so a node held for keys pulls them. **Settles**: brief 4: filter them, or narrow the class.
7. **Pull of role entries alone**: settled: no selector on `Pull`. A node holding S's thing for keys declares it as its class opening S (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source, `class` 2 in spec/wire.md §11.2 Offer), and a server shows such a peer S's role entries alone (spec/sync-streams.md §4). The class also says what a selector could not: that the peer is no source of S's content (spec/sync-status.md §2 counts it by its stream alone), on every connection and before any Pull.
8. **A capped `grant@~0`**: a thing whose admins keep `grant@~0` for a time holds each offer that long; a grant stands on its target whatever becomes of the offer (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Grant from outside).
9. **Copies show more than the roster fact**: settled in spec: a copy in the edge piece of a holder of sync alone holds only the target's boxes to it and its `sync.0` part (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part; spec/wire.md §4).
