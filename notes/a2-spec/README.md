# A2: readings the spec does not hold
> Summary: Stage A2 (the sync rewrite, built: evidence per sync unit, key parts, grants with the thing granted; spec/permissions.md §4; spec/sync-set.md §2; spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§2; spec/sync-inbox.md §2, §4, §5; spec/crypto.md §7 step-checked delete keys; spec/wire.md §4, §9; spec/commits.md §4): the design readings behind the spec, and its questions still open with their options, by number.

The rules are the spec's (from notes/write-path-design.md §2, §4, F6, notes/holder-access-study.md and issues/delete-key-levels-unchecked.md Direction; oracles held-model `evidence`, `closure::keys` and `World::joint`). What the tree is and what it still owes: notes/sync-rewrite-state.md. This note holds only what neither says.

## Readings
- **How a holder hears of a grant or removal**: a grant by a holder's writers is the holder's own data, so its nodes learn it by syncing the holder; a grant from outside is the `grant@~0` fire on the holder, which tells the holder the thing exists and carries its boxes and `sync.0` (the offer is that message, nothing more). A removal reaches the holder's nodes through the target they sync or hold for keys; a removal from outside also goes as a `grant@~0` fire, since a removed holder is opened the target's sync ID no more.
- **Box delivery stays where it must**: a rotation or re-seal boxing a holder that cannot open it from the target's own sync ID (sync alone, re-sealed) goes to the holder's sync ID as a *sealed copy* (a certificate copy sealed like a fire, promoted iff in the holder's key part, bound by no setting): the edge piece logged where it belongs, the one push into a sync ID the sender does not sync. Not built: such a delivery goes as a `grant@~0` fire meanwhile (notes/sync-rewrite-state.md, Delivery).
- **No grant is taken**: a grant belongs to the thing granted; a `grant@~0` fire is an offer and a node's role is by the grants of what it syncs (notes/holder-access-study.md, The direction). A link redemption is a grant from outside the redeemer's account.
- **Interim placement until A4**: an account's key part is under its account root D's sync ID, and what reaches it from outside under its inbox's. The account's creation keeps `grant@~0` the inbox lifetime.
- **Evidence takes every edge write on a stated path's edges** (write-path-design §2 E1); re-grant reads "made by right". Nothing in the design reads which writes are chained; the models still do (issues/models-read-chained-sets.md).
- **Role changes travel** keeps keys-open travel (a role commit naming H in the required set of each sync ID whose thing's keys open H), as finding 19 of notes/evidence-model-findings.md requires; the holder-side half is the key part.

## Open

*taken* marks what the spec writes meanwhile, **settles** what closes it. Numbers kept: code and notes cite them.
2. **Which edge writes the closure takes**: settled, E1 (the coordinator): every held edge write on a stated path's edges, whatever its validity, and a part pends on any. The model's (iv), only grants that could take effect along joined paths, would leave writes through unjoined paths waiting for good, an unbounded stall. Its two costs, the roster leak (P9) and junk copies (P10), are stated limits (spec/sync-hostile.md §2).
3. **A wrong delete key: invalid or pending**: settled, pending (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable): a part listing a key no held step places is unjudgeable, so a reader holding the write before the `sync.0` write recording its step keeps it until the step arrives; a writer of S listing made-up keys leaves parts unapplied for good, bounded as any content it may write is (notes/delete-keys.md, What a verdict does). Not modelled with steps (held-model `evidence`; issues/engine-evidence-trails-model.md).
4. **Steps under role compaction**: settled: a `sync.0` step entry carried by a role compaction snapshot keeps its recording write's stamp for "ordered before the commit", and the snapshot is a copy recording the step (notes/evidence-derivation.md, Engine hand tests).
5. **Own boxes against "a commit applies with all its pages"** (notes/holder-access-study.md, Not modelled): a node held for keys fetches only the `secrets.0` pages it opens; a re-sealer and the stale-box rules may read other boxes. *taken*: a node that may rotate fetches them all (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries); as built every node held for keys fetches every page a role entry links. **Settles**: own boxes, with issues/holder-key-churn.md.
6. **Role entries are wider than keys** (weak spot 5): commits carrying purge requests and blobs they link are role entries too, so a node held for keys pulls them. **Settles**: with Open 5: filter them, or narrow the class.
7. **Pull of role entries alone**: settled: no selector on `Pull`. A node holding S's thing for keys declares it as its class opening S (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source, `class` 2 in spec/wire.md §11.2 Offer), and a server shows such a peer S's role entries alone (spec/sync-streams.md §4). The class also says what a selector could not: that the peer is no source of S's content (spec/sync-status.md §2 counts it by its stream alone), on every connection and before any Pull.
8. **A capped `grant@~0`**: a thing whose admins keep `grant@~0` for a time holds each offer that long; a grant stands on its target whatever becomes of the offer (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Grant from outside).
9. **Copies show more than the roster fact**: settled: a copy in the edge piece of a holder of sync alone holds only the target's boxes to it and its `sync.0` part (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part; spec/wire.md §4). Built for the key part; no test covers it (notes/sync-facade.md, Certificate bodies).
10. **Deletes at peers resting on a verdict wait for a complete pass**: *taken* (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Deletes); not built (acting only after a complete pass). **Settles**: covers in `evidence`, property "no body is lost that the node holding everything keeps".
11. **Compaction on a provisional verdict**: *taken* at once (spec/commits.md §4 Provisional). **Settles**: Tess, only if the window widens.
12. **Hostile deletes of copies and record blobs at stores**: issues/cert-blob-hostile-delete.md.
13. **The derived-era check**: settled in spec (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys): a key is right when any thing up the delta's thing's record chain places it, so derived-era content and a writer that has not received the thing's own secrets list the former base's step (notes/delete-keys.md). The steps ride as the write's evidence. Not modelled.
14. **The edges a member writes**: settled: spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit takes the writes on each edge the commit's own edge writes set (finding 9: a removal waits for the creations its resets name) and on a descent's record edges, as held-model `CommitEdges::AllWrites` and the derivation do.
15. **A wrong key is judged per thing**: settled in spec (spec/crypto.md §7, spec/commits.md §4): a commit listing a wrong key for a thing has its deltas on that thing unjudgeable, not the commit whole; a node holding one thing's evidence cannot judge the keys listed for another.
17. **A writer recording no step**: a link's redeemer holds K's boxes before it joins and writes its join there as the link key; with `sync.0` a role part it holds K's steps with K's role parts (notes/delete-keys.md, What a verdict does). What the key check still leaves: issues/delete-key-levels-unchecked.md, Left.
