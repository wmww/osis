## 4. Verification and application
- **Unjudgeable**: a commit is unjudgeable while its signer's `created.0` record is unknown, or while every held part of it is. A held part is unjudgeable while:
  - its thing's record, a record on its thing's chain (spec/permissions.md §1) or the record of a thing its stated path lists (or one up that thing's record chain) is unknown;
  - its commit's `path` is a `Split` and neither a read roles secret the node holds (of the thing or up its record chain) nor a path key a held encryption of the thing's parts carries opens the thing's entry (spec/wire.md §4; DRAFT, notes/path-form.md);
  - an edge of its stated path, or for an edge write naming a remaining admin (spec/wire.md §4 `remains`) that holder's edge, is no record edge and no held edge write on it is ordered before the part (spec/permissions.md §5.1 key; a part without edge-class writes at its commit's position), or the latest such write is itself an unjudgeable part: its evidence has not arrived (spec/permissions.md §4) (OPEN 10's proposal: a held chained grant giving what the part needs there (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 3, its least need; OPEN 16, finding 23: a grant giving too little joins nothing, so the part waits as it does where nothing was granted), and the latest held chained edge write). A fire of an external signal reads no edge (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires);
  - **(A3)** it has no edge-class part, a held lowering's list root shows a leaf for its thing, the part rests on that lowering's edge, and the node does not hold that leaf (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Frontier).
  - The sub-bullets stand.
- **Outside the node closure**, its first sentence: nothing but evidence and keys (§5 What a held thing needs) applies on a thing the node does not hold whole. *The rest stands.*
- **Examined again**, its role-inputs clause: the held role inputs moving (an edge write, a record, a rotation; **(A3)** a leaf), for one awaiting its evidence or an epoch. *The rest stands.*
- **Provisional**: a verdict is a function of what the node holds (Order independence), so a node holding part of a write's evidence (a lowering ordered before the write not yet arrived, whose edge has other held writes) judges it as a node without that part, and may judge it otherwise once the part arrives; no rule over the held set tells partial evidence from complete. Acts on a verdict therefore come in two kinds:
  - taken at once: those undone by the rules when a verdict moves (applying and reading, logging (the required set reads no verdict), relaying, notices and listeners), dropping a body judged invalid (spec/commits.md §5 Invalid, as it stands: valid again after the drop, it is fetched only where some node still holds it, so where every holder dropped it and its writer is gone it is lost though the node holding everything shows it: OPEN 11 (b)), and dropping its own body as covered and compacting, which nothing undoes: a snapshot made before its writer holds a removal carries what it showed for good, and a cover's writer removed later loses what only it carried, as when the removal is written after the drop (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Copies); the window lasts until the removal reaches the writer, and waiting for a complete pass would narrow it only to until the removal reaches S's sources, which a lagging delivery leaves open (OPEN 8);
  - not undone by any rule, taken only at the end of a complete pass of each sync ID the verdict's inputs are under (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun after those inputs last moved: a delete at a peer resting on a verdict (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Deletes), promoting a deposit (spec/sync-inbox.md §4). A complete pass gives agreement with what S's sources held, not with every input written: a later write (a backdated removal, a re-grant), or one written earlier that had not reached S's sources (a removal a bridge or a key rotation delivers), still moves the verdict. A promotion is undone by the rules (a promoted body judged invalid leaves the store); a delete at a peer is not: the wait narrows its loss to an earlier-ordered removal (voiding the cut, or removing a cover's writer) not yet at S's sources (notes/evidence-model-findings.md 33).

## 5. Retention and purge
- **What a held thing needs**, its first sentence: of a thing outside the node closure a node holds only **evidence** (its record, the edge writes on it some synced sync ID's evidence holds, spec/permissions.md §4) and **keys** (its `keys.0` commits and the secrets the node holds of it; of a key actor, which no node closure enters, its card: every part on it, spec/sync-set.md §1), and only while a held thing needs it; it goes whole, as never received, once nothing does. *The rest stands.*
