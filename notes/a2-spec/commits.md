## 4. Verification and application
- **Unjudgeable**: a commit is unjudgeable while its signer's `created.0` record is unknown, or while every held part of it is. A held part is unjudgeable while:
  - its thing's record, a record on its thing's chain (spec/permissions.md §1) or the record of a thing its stated path lists (or one up that thing's record chain) is unknown;
  - its commit's `path` is a `Split` and neither a read roles secret the node holds (of the thing or up its record chain) nor a path key a held encryption of the thing's parts carries opens the thing's entry (spec/wire.md §4; DRAFT, notes/path-form.md);
  - an edge of its stated path, or for an edge write naming a remaining admin (spec/wire.md §4 `remains`) that holder's edge, is no record edge and no held edge write on it is ordered before the part (spec/permissions.md §5.1 key; a part without edge-class writes at its commit's position), or the latest such write is itself an unjudgeable part: its evidence has not arrived (spec/permissions.md §4) (OPEN 10's proposal: a held chained grant giving what the part needs there (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 3, its least need; OPEN 16, finding 23: a grant giving too little joins nothing, so the part waits as it does where nothing was granted), and the latest held chained edge write). A fire of an external signal reads no edge (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires);
  - **(A3)** it has no edge-class part, a held lowering's list root shows a leaf for its thing, the part rests on that lowering's edge, and the node does not hold that leaf (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Frontier).
  - The sub-bullets stand.
- **Outside the node closure**, its first sentence: nothing but evidence and keys (§5 What a held thing needs) applies on a thing the node does not hold whole. *The rest stands.*
- **Examined again**, its role-inputs clause: the held role inputs moving (an edge write, a vouch, a record, a rotation; **(A3)** a leaf), for one awaiting its evidence or an epoch. *The rest stands.*
- **Provisional**: a verdict is a function of what the node holds (Order independence), so a node holding part of a write's evidence (a lowering ordered before the write not yet arrived, whose edge has other held writes) judges it as a node without that part, and may judge it otherwise once the part arrives; no rule over the held set tells partial evidence from complete. Acts on a verdict therefore come in two kinds:
  - undone by the rules when a verdict moves, taken at once: applying and reading, logging (the required set reads no verdict), relaying, dropping its own body as covered and compacting (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Copies: a snapshot made before the writer holds a removal carries what it showed; a cover's writer removed later loses what only it carried, as when the removal is written after the drop: OPEN 8), notices and listeners;
  - not undone by any rule, taken only at the end of a complete pass of each sync ID the verdict's inputs are under (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun after those inputs last moved: dropping a body judged invalid (spec/commits.md §5 Invalid: held unapplied until then; valid again after a drop it is fetched only where some node still holds it, and every reader dropping on the same partial view loses it: OPEN 11), a delete at a peer resting on a verdict (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Deletes), promoting a deposit (spec/sync-inbox.md §4), writing a vouch against a lowering it judges applied (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches, Writing). A later write can still move the verdict (a backdated removal, a re-grant): that is a new input, and these acts' own rules answer it as they answer any (a promoted body judged invalid leaves the store, a vouch whose removal is void counts nothing).

## 5. Retention and purge
- **What a held thing needs**, its first sentence: of a thing outside the node closure a node holds only **evidence** (its record, the edge writes and vouches on it some synced sync ID's evidence holds, spec/permissions.md §4) and **keys** (its `keys.0` commits and the secrets the node holds of it; of a key actor, which no node closure enters, its card: every part on it, spec/sync-set.md §1), and only while a held thing needs it; it goes whole, as never received, once nothing does. *The rest stands.*
