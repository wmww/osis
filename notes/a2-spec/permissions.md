## 2. Role grant writes
- **Logging a lowered grant**: a lowering has no logging rule of its own. It is an edge write on every path with that edge, so it is in the evidence (§4) of every held commit stating such a path, under each sync ID whose bodies hold such a commit; and, as every edge write, where its holder's role reaches (§4 Role changes travel). **(A3)** A removal is also logged where its reach is held (§4 Evidence of a sync ID).

## 4. Verifying signers: evidence
- A write's authority is checked along its stated path (§1 Path): each edge a record edge (the tid commits to the mint admin) or set by edge writes, its signer a key actor whose tid commits to its public key; **(until A4)** an account's commit also through its signing chain to the epoch it names (§5.4, §5.5). No secrets are needed. *Stands (A1).*
- **Chained writes**: *stands (A1)*. Read by §5.2 Re-grant only.
- **Evidence of a commit** for a thing T it writes: the least set holding, of what the node holds,
  - every edge write on an edge of the path the commit states for T (§1 Path: its direct edges; a descent's record edges have none), and, for an edge write whose header names a remaining admin (spec/wire.md §4 `remains`), on that holder's edge on its target: grants, lowerings, removals, self-removals, invite grants and finalizes alike, whatever their validity or order;
  - every vouch keyed by a lowering in the set (§5.1 Vouches);
  - closed: the evidence of each such edge write for its target (a self-removal's for its holder) and of each such vouch for its thing;
  - the creation records of every member of each of those paths and of the commit's own, and those up their record chains (spec/wire.md §3: record blobs, never a copy of a minting commit: a record outlives its minting's retention);
  - **(until A4)** for each member that is an account, the rotations of its signing chain the node holds, every line, as header-only copies, and their thing lists for things of the sync unit (§5.5): a commit at an epoch verifies by the signing key its minting rotation's header carries.
  - An external fire's (§5.2 External fires): its signer's record alone.
  - Nothing else: no `keys.0` commit of a member (judging needs no KEM key; a signer's key is in its record), no write on an edge of a path the commit does not state for T, no write of another path to T.
- **Evidence of a sync ID S**, at a node, over what it holds now: the least set holding
  - the evidence of each body under S (spec/sync-judgement.md §1: a commit's parts on things of S's sync unit) for each thing of S's sync unit it has a part on, and of nothing else of it (a commit's part on a thing outside the unit brings no evidence, whatever it writes);
  - each edge write naming S's actor holder (not its own record edge) and its evidence (Role changes travel);
  - **(A3)** each held removal (an explicit edge write giving its edge (h, t) below write at its `ts`) whose **reach** under S is held: the held edge writes giving write or above, at any `ts` and whatever their validity, that join t to a thing of S's sync unit (t itself in the unit: none needed); with that reach. Until A3: a removal goes under S as Role changes travel says.
  - **(A3)** each list leaf (§5.1 Frontier) of a lowering in the set, for a thing of the unit, while a body under S on that thing with no edge-class part rests on the lowering's edge (named by the leaf or not, stamped before or after the lowering); each addendum on a thing of the unit while it lists a held commit with no edge-class part (an addendum is no such commit) resting on its removal's edge; else neither is in the set.
  - closed as Evidence of a commit is.

  It is a function of the commits the node holds and of S's sync unit as the node judges it, computed again at every change of either (a retag included: a thing leaving the unit turns what the remaining bodies still need of its edge writes into copies). Nothing is in it for having been in it, and nothing is placed once: an entry leaves with the last held commit that needed it. Verdicts never decide it: it reads stated paths, never what applies.
- **Copies**: a part in S's evidence on a thing of S's sync unit is a body (spec/sync-judgement.md §1); one on a thing outside it goes under S as a **certificate copy** (spec/wire.md §4). A certificate copy is identified by (commit ID, thing): its commit's header and its edge-class parts on that one thing (its `roles.0` delta, its `vouches.0` delta; spec/data-model.md §9), nothing else of the commit. One copy serves every body under S whose evidence holds it, however many; a node logging one copy under several sync IDs logs one blob, wrapped under each (spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Certificate blobs).
  - A lowering's copy carries its frontier (its header's; a linked frontier's chunk blobs go with it, spec/sync-judgement.md §1). **(A3)** In place of that: its list root and role entries; its leaves go as evidence of their things' bodies (above), never with the copy.
- **Logging evidence**: any node holding an entry of S's evidence as its own view computes it, and S, logs it there as it logs bodies (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store: stamped by a writer of S, else deposited); none waits for the commit's signer. What it no longer requires leaves its store as spec/sync-judgement.md §2 says (outside the required set, at the end of a complete pass). Record blobs and **(until A4)** list blobs: *the record and list blob sub-bullets of today's Logging certificates stand* (one blob of all S carries of its kind, the winner rule, a record or list blob logged by any node counting once opened while held at the own store or a source).
  - A certificate copy logged by any node counts once the node has opened it as the same commit and checked each of its deltas against the header's delta hash (spec/commits.md §4), while it is held in its own store and at every source of S. *Stands.*
  - A blob of a rotation commit of S's thing (wrapped under the epoch it supersedes, spec/crypto.md §5) counts only if it opens under the newest read epoch. *Stands.*
  - The cards (record, `keys.0`) of the key actors other than accounts holding S's thing (spec/sync-judgement.md §1), and **(until A4)** the account root D's node cards: *stand*. They are keys for sealing and listing, not evidence.
  - Destroyed things: for each destroyed thing (spec/commits.md §5) a live thing of S's sync unit needs (a destroyed thing on a path a body under S states, named mint admin or holder on a thing of the sync unit that is not destroyed or on such a destroyed thing), the records up its record chain and every held edge write on it with its evidence (copies where S's sync unit does not hold it; its own bodies go with its destruction), and, for every thing whose record any of these carries, its witness as a header-only copy and, destroyed, the requests destroying it (spec/commits.md §5, A thing's mintings travel with its record), except what an entry under S already holds. *Stands, "chain certificates" read as evidence.*
- Deltas S's bodies carry (on things of its sync unit) go as sync unit blobs, never as copies; every other delta of S's evidence goes as a certificate copy. *Stands, "chain" read as evidence.*
- **Role changes travel**: *stands, with "as certificate copies" for "as certificate copies where the bodies do not carry them"* (a role commit naming holder A goes under A's own sync ID and under the sync ID of each thing whose keys open A, with each role commit of a chain by which they do; under A's own the target's boxes to A and its `sync.0`). It carries keys, not evidence: a reader of A judges what such a commit writes only where A's bodies' evidence holds it. **(A3)** The removal's `ROLE_KEEP` clause goes: a removal stays where its reach is held (Evidence of a sync ID).
- A demotion or `keys.0` write by another node is its signer's to log (§3); a node MAY wait up to `SETTLE_MS` after receiving one before copying it. *Stands.*
- A promoter completes the evidence it holds: spec/sync-inbox.md §4.
- **Stranded**: *stands*, with "with their evidence" for "with their authors' chain certificates" in its holder keys sub-bullet.
- **Suspect**, **Suspect list**: *stand*.
- The grantee's current `keys.0` commits go under the target's sync ID as certificate copies, so every admin can seal and list the grantee and every holder reaches its inbox. *Stands (keys, not evidence).*
- A commit's evidence for each thing it has a delta on travels with each body of the commit (above). A commit whose evidence has not arrived is pending (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable); **(until A4)** an account signer's commit at an epoch whose minting rotation the node does not hold is pending (§5.5).
- A node that does not sync a thing applies the evidence it holds of it; its timeline for that thing runs over those edge writes. *Stands, "certificate deltas" read as evidence.*
- A node keeps another thing's records, edge writes and vouches only while S's evidence needs them for a sync ID it syncs, and its `keys.0` commits only as keys (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs).

## 5.1 Frontier (A3, the leaf form)
**(A3)** Drafted for the evidence rule; A3's spec brief settles the bytes (spec/wire.md §4 draft, List root). A lowering's frontier is a **list root**: its role entries (the role commits it names) and, per thing on which it names commits with no edge-class part, a **leaf**: that thing and those commit IDs, committed to by a key only the thing's readers can compute and the leaf's hash. A node reads a role entry from the root, and a content entry only from a leaf it holds whose hash the root holds. A node holding a body that a leaf of a held lowering would read (Evidence of a sync ID) and not that leaf does not judge the body (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable).
