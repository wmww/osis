# Node sync: work sets and when a label passes
> Summary: How node sync keeps pass cost proportional to what changed (spec/requirements.md Scale): per (connection, label) work sets fed by engine and sync triggers, what forces a full pass, role moves, which labels a change marks, idle labels with no clock passes, many labels per node, the caches behind them, the differential checks that keep them honest, and the measured costs. Neighbours: notes/node-sync.md (the pass itself and the module map), notes/engine.md (engine change marks).

## Work sets (`sync/work.rs`, `sync/marks.rs`)
- A pass at a host judges only what its (connection, label) work set names (`LConn::work`): the bodies (`Engine::commit_bodies_under`) and entries of its commits, its non-commit blobs (chunks, certificate-linked blobs), plus what is per pass anyway (certificates, inbox copies, heartbeat, promotion of deposits, parked relays, re-seal). A label's first pass on a connection judges everything (`Work::default`), so marks made before a `SyncEngine` wraps the engine (open, restart) are not needed.
- **Engine trigger**: one hook, `Engine::take_changed` (`Core::changed`, kept only while a `SyncEngine` wraps the engine): the commits whose record, parts, validity or timers changed since the last take. The engine marks them where it changes them (`engine::Changed`): an applied or joined (late parts) commit; a commit whose validity flipped, or a snapshot whose carried writes' judgments did (`rejudge_all`); a body with a relative bound a join may have replaced (`retention::mark_killed`: the dying writes' own commits and the aspect's snapshots, so a write re-judges O(bodies it killed + snapshots)), or every such body of an aspect rebuilt (`mark_bounded`: flip, un-merge, drop, expiry); every commit on a thing whose `created.0` record came; a dropped part (`drop_part`: compaction, expiry, purge); a fired timer (`Engine::tick`). Late deltas mark their commit (a part that changes no read still changes the bodies other labels must hold). A mark from an aborted transaction stays (one extra judgment).
- An aspect whose state changed other than by a valid write's join (a validity flip, an un-merge on apply or late, expiry, purge, a late delta) is marked as an aspect (`Changed::aspects`): it wakes what waits on the state and judges no commit by itself (judging every commit on it would cost O(aspect history) per expiry or flip).
- `engine_events` drains the marks before and after each step's event (everything committed since the last event is taken in before the event is answered: a purge's bytes are gone before a peer's Fetch reads them) and at pass start (a carrier written by one pass is judged by the next): the commits go to every open label's set (`Touched::commits`), their aspects and the marked aspects wake the verdicts waiting on aspect states (`Touched::states`, `Left::waits`), their chunks the bodies waiting on a linked blob (`Left::links`), and they are `forget_dropped`'s candidates. Touches are spread at the end of each `handle` too, and dropped with no label open (a label opened later judges everything). Engine events stay for roles only (`Roster`, `created.0` and role commits; `LostAccess` judges everything).
- What the passes mark is drained after the step. What a step's passes mark (a refetch applied commits, an admin recorded a step) passes in the same `handle`, up to `PASS_AGAIN` (8) rounds, not at whatever event comes next: full passes had judged it already, and `work_sets_match_full_passes` showed it as a late arrival.
- **Sync's own triggers**: the sync state's entry, presence, deposited, tombstone and certificate records (`State::touch`, by label), carriers (`touched_commits`), its own drops (`drop_covered`: judged in the same pass), blobs that arrived (`log_events`).
- **Derived per pass** (`prepare`): a snapshot or another node's card commit in the set expands to every commit on its aspects; things entering or leaving the closure; aspects moving in or out of the unsettled sets; due times (keep-at-least, expiry); aspect states a verdict waits on (`Left::waits`: a dropped body with live writes waiting for a carrier, woken by any write to the aspect); linked blobs not here yet (`Left::links`).
- **Closures**: a group's closure is an `Arc` from `Groups::closures`, the same one while it does not change (kept on relisting when equal), so `prepare` diffs it with the set's only when it differs (`same_closure`); the bodies' `BodyCtx` shares it (taken again after `refetch` if that applied commits). The engine keeps a synced group's closure syncing as it changes (`Engine::set_syncing`, once per group), so a pass walks no closure.
- **Caches**: the home label's published things (`published_under`) come from the `label.0` index (`Core::labeled`, kept by `index_commit`), taken for every actor at once and cached against the change counts of the held records, `own_keys` and `labeled` (`Core::published`), not a scan of every held aspect key per pass. A group's `label.0` is decoded once per write of it (`Core::labels`, dropped by `Core::wrote` and on rollback) and shared (`labels_of`: `Arc<GroupLabel>`s; `served_labels` borrows the steps). The synced labels (`labels()`) are cached within a step keyed on the engine generation (which a reload never takes back), the retired count and the time (an admin grant's `until` drops a replaced label), with the clock only while a replaced label asks for a role. Debug builds check every cache and memo against a recomputation.
- **Once per change, not per pass**: what a pass reads that is a function of a change is judged when that change comes, so a pass that judges one blob costs about that blob. `usable_keys` runs `Engine::ensure_rotated` for the label's secret actor once per `Core::role_generation` (roles, secrets, record chains) and clock up to the next `until` or role commit ts ahead of it in the actor's component (`SyncEngine::rotations`, `horizon`; `Rotated`); a run that rotated runs again at the next ask. `must_hold` and `delete_stands` read a commit's `Engine::body_keeps` once per change of it (`SyncEngine::keeps`): the entry goes with the commit's engine mark (`engine_events`), the same marks that put it in the work sets; a commit marked and not drained yet is judged anew, and a bound its replacement dates is never kept: an earlier superseder of a write already dead, arriving after a later one, moves the death earlier with no mark (the join kills nothing). The work sets meet that unmarked move too: such a body is judged again at its old bound, so it may go later than the new one allows (never earlier: keeping longer is allowed). Both clear on `forget_lost`. Debug builds compare `keeps` with a fresh read once per `Engine::point` (`recheck`) and `rotations` at a power of four of an entry's hits (a run is a whole rotation walk). Rejected: an engine counter of every `head_keys` invalidation as `rotations`' key: the extra aspects it covers (`label.0`, `group.0`, `groups.0`, `node.0`) are read by no rotation.
- **Pass cost** (`label_bodies_meta`): each commit's bodies are kept without plaintext in `Core::bodies` (dropped when the commit's record is written, when a thing it touches gets its creation record, all of them when an actor gets secrets of its own or a changing transaction rolls back; a commit whose record chains are not all known is not kept); the pass projects a body's plaintext again only to seal it or walk its links (`body_plaintext`). A failed read-only transaction keeps every cache (`Core::rollback_to` drops them only after a change). Test `label_bodies_cached_per_commit_spec_sync_6`.
- **Roles** (`note_role_moves`, `roles_moved`): a pass reads rosters only through `may_read`/roles (entitlement, a superset's foreign deltas, `stale_card`'s departure), held epochs (wraps, unopened entries, delete keys) and reader actors (`secret_actor`, `own_keys`). Perm reports the (holder, target) grants that differ now or at any point of the walk, however the walk got there (`osis_perm::Changed::edges`, `engine::Changed::edges`); sync reads no commit's ops. A role comes from a holder up, so a changed grant moves roles only for `Held::upstream` of its holder and only on `Held::reach` of its target (`work::RoleMoves`: subjects and reached); a new `created.0` the same, and `Held::below` of it has a new record chain (`Touched::moved`).
  - A label is hit when its actor, its group or this node is a subject: `reached` then goes to `moved`; a `moved` holding the label's actor judges everything; otherwise closure and published things in it expand to their commits, the label's own commits with a delta on them too, other nodes' cards, and the roster horizon is taken again. Raises and lowerings go by one rule. A plain add has as subjects the new holder and those above it, so no other label and no other node's labels judge anything for it.
  - A card's author is a subject the rule does not look at: a card goes stale only by a departure, a departure brings a rotation, and that changes `Inputs` (a full pass); deleting those branches changed no differential run. Stated limit: another node's card copy (its `node.0`, `reach.0`) goes at the next full pass after its departure, normally the rotation's, so a host keeps it until then. Nothing trusts the copy: dialing asks `may_read` at the dial, rotations seal by roster. `member_churn` (scenario) is the add-heavy differential.
- **Full pass** (`Touched::full`): a new connection's label; a role or record chain of the label's actor moving, or a change of its `own_keys`; lost access; a forget (state reloaded); `set_hosts`/`set_peers`; a changed pass input (`Inputs`: head and epochs, usable roles, replaced, group actor, published things, may rotate, the group's held epochs); the roster clock horizon (`roster_horizon`: a grant's `until` or an edge write ahead of the clock, in the components of the actor and the closure's readers); a failed pass; a set past `work_max`.
- Epochs: the group's `ActorEpochs` is a pass input; deletes lacking a key (`Note::keyless`) wait in `keyless` until any role-class write, flip or late part.
- **Unsettled**: commits judged while anything was unsettled (deletions) or an entry was being fetched (must-hold) are kept (`held_back`) and judged again once that ends; becoming unsettled only withholds actions, so it judges nothing.
- A commit leaves the set once every send of it is present or tombstoned at the host and no judgment of it waits (relay wait, delete in flight or waiting, `covered` not established); entries touched mid-pass are pulled in (`pull`) with their commits' bodies. Aggregates kept with the set (rebuilt by a full pass): snapshot entries per aspect, unopened entries, unjudged authors (`certificates`), others' heartbeats (`reseal`). `covered_judged` is trimmed when consulted (a drop is a trigger). Indexes in `State`: entries by (label, commit), by kind, certificate entries, labels per blob, presence and tombstones per (label, blob). `forget_dropped` and `dropped_chunks` take candidates (chunk listers kept in `SyncEngine::listers`). `verdicts` judges everything. A label's waiting work is bounded (`SyncConfig::work_max`).
- **Local passes** (labels served with no host, `local_pass`, notes/peers.md) have a work set per label (`SyncEngine::local_work`) fed by the same touches and prepared, scoped and left as a host's (`take_inputs` shared); a non-commit entry waits on its prefix's expiry (`Waits::due`). Differentials `local_serve::local_work_sets_match_full_passes_spec_sync_5` (served state at every step equal to full passes) and `local_inbox_blob_expires_spec_sync_5` (an inbox blob under a host-less home label: `deliver_inbox` to an account created with no host; the only local check that fails without `Waits::due`). The deletion step's other `Left` bookkeeping (waits, due, held back) is redundant with engine triggers in every local scenario tried: leaving nothing fails only the inbox test.
- **The differential**: `SyncConfig::full_passes` turns the sets off; osis-sim `with_full_passes` runs a scenario both ways and the runs must end alike and reach the hosts at the same moments (`core/test/sim/tests/it/work_sets.rs`, scenario `work_sets`). It found: a carrier snapshot written by one host's pass and the next pass's set, a dropped body's blob coming back unforgotten, entries touched mid-pass judged without their bodies.

## Which labels a change marks
- **Who passes** (`engine_events`): a content change (no role commit, no role-class, creation, group or membership aspect, not on a key actor, no late drop) marks dirty only the labels whose closure holds the thing (`mark_dirty_of`); every other change marks all (`mark_dirty`; `cfg.full_passes` always, so `work_sets_*` compare the two). A label left out takes the change into its set and passes when marked or at its `pass_at`. Without this, one pass per label per message: the admin of a chat has a label per contact. Heartbeats: a pass reads only those touched since it last looked (`Work::beats_repair`, `beats_reseal`; a full pass reads all).
- **Many labels** (any thing may sync by its own label; `label_costs_do_not_grow_with_labels`, `a_burst_of_shares_costs_each_about_once`): a role, creation or group change passes only the labels it concerns (`downstream`: `Held::reach` of the things, their record chains, their named holders and those holders' record chains); a key actor's change still passes every label. A commit's bodies reaching a host pass the labels with its entries, and a certificate copy reaching one passes nothing more (`reached`). An opened inbox blob passes the labels of the actor it is sealed to.
  - Per-label work kept flat: claim tries only when the sync keys failed (`Try::Rest`); `vet_inbox_hints` reruns on an engine, entry or expiry change; `Engine::inbox_actors` is kept; a blob costs one KEM per actor naming that inbox. `InboxCopied`: blobs whose copy is all there are not walked again until a thing that moves the answer changes, an entry or host's copy goes, or a pass judges all. A pending commit's boxes refused by the reading-only budget wake as room appears (`Waits::wake_refused`), not all pending boxes at each applied grant. `Timeline::role` at the current grants is memoized per node until a followed `until` (`Memo::roles_now`); `closure` keeps the asker's reach (`Memo::forward`).
  - A label misjudged out of a mark still passes at its `pass_at`, so a missed trigger costs at most one heartbeat interval of delay.
  - Checked: `work_sets::honest_flows_wait_for_no_resync` (pairing, grants, writes, removals of an account, node and holder group, an invite) sees nothing reach a host later than with every label marked. What still waits is chains for actors a node acted for under labels those actors do not reach, which no verifier there needs until a write there marks the label. Its teeth: it fails with `downstream` cut to the things, but not with the inbox mark back to the actor alone, nor with `reached`'s `mark_dirty_of` gone (no flow there needs those two). `link_race` alone parts about a third of its seeds this way (19 or 20 of 60), under the oracle's 1% of all runs; seed 3 parts this way (seed 7 starts alike, not traced further): y's later copy of a chain part x already placed under K's label waits for y's next pass there (its `pass_at` at the latest), which moves every later seal and dice roll.

## Idle labels (`Work::quiet`, `resync_due`, `schedule_pass`; `scale::idle_labels_pass_only_when_due`)
- A subscribed label passes when marked, not by the clock. A resync (`timers`) passes it only if a pass left something (`Left::commits`, `blobs`, `again`: a relay or commit deposit a writer has not promoted), its own records were touched (`State::touch`), a spread change woke a wait (`woken_by`: `waits`, `at_most`, `links`, `keyless`), something is unopened or unsettled (`unopened`, `held_back`, `unsettled`, `settled`), a set is `full`, or its `pass_at` came. A pass for a resync sent nothing to the host and caught no missed push (the subscription's order does; a new connection judges all).
- `pass_at` (`schedule_pass`, after each pass): the first of the set's due times, the roster's clock horizon (`until`), the next heartbeat or stranded heartbeat (not one past due: the pass that just ran could not send it, so a label stranded once keeps its time for good); with none of them one `HEARTBEAT_MIN_INTERVAL` (6 h) on. So there is no separate safety resync: a missed trigger waits at most as long as a heartbeat. A change from elsewhere waiting in a set (`spread_touches`) wants no pass; past `work_max` the set goes `full` and passes at its next resync, which also ends what it holds.
- Named so they need no resync: the wait for peers ending (`reseal` asks `settle_by`), a grant with an `until` or a role write ahead of the clock (`Touched::clock`: the horizon is taken again at the marked pass; a plain add reads no horizon). `settle_at` keeps labels: what comes due passes those labels alone, in full-pass runs too (else one deposit passes them all).
- The node wakes once per `resync_ms` with anything open (`last_tick`), not per label, unless a label wants its own resync; ticks stay for what relies on them (`relink_lost`, the memory lane's `retry_refused`, reconciling, step-level timers). Every Tick asks for a wake again (waits on replies and dead retries left out, the engine's expiry and keep-at-most timers included, `Engine::next_timer`), and no wake is earlier than 1 s after the last Tick.
- `label_status`'s `last_sync` of an idle subscribed label is the current time: a resync touches no wire, so a stalled connection shows only once a ping finds it (`PING_INTERVAL_MS` 5 min + `STALL_TIMEOUT_MS` 2 min).
- `pass_at` is in corrected time, as what it comes from: a host's clock sample moves it with them. A clock gone back: a `pass_at` further off than an interval passes at the next resync, and a label's `last_sync` is clamped to now, so the bound holds (`scale::a_clock_gone_back_keeps_a_quiet_labels_bound`); what else counts in raw time waits for the clock (issues/clock-back-raw-timers.md).
- Delays of an idle label, checked against a pass at every resync in a world probe and `work_sets_match_full_passes`: expiry on reads exact (engine timer, no pass); a grant's `until` on roles exact, its pass, keep-at-least and other due times within two resyncs (the tick is per node, so a label's own resync may slip one); re-sends, deposit retries and tombstones keep their own wakes and sweeps; refused uploads, a lost reply and unsettled work are `again`: every resync.
- `resync_ms` paces retries only, so it could grow without changing idle cost; it stays, since the memory lane's retries and the opens' back-off use it.

## Counted (osis-node `scale::`, n and 10n; work sets turn full passes into set passes)
- `a_change_costs_a_few_passes` (3-node group, one host, 4 / 40 things): one write, a grant of admin on a thing (secrets of its own given with it), a bare edge write of the same (left to one admin: `own_secrets_wait`), a raise on the group: at most 8 passes and 2 full per node, flat; rotation runs at most one per node and role commit (6-9 in all), keep bounds flat (3-9 in all). Full passes still judge every body of the label (linear).
- `role_change_judges_its_labels_only` (3 labels of 10 / 100 commits): an edge write on an actor no closure reaches passes nothing and judges no body; one on a label's group re-judges that label alone.
- `sync_pass_is_flat` (20 / 200 commits, at a host and served by the writer alone): one more write judges 1-2 bodies and 1-3 entries at the writer, 1 and 1 at the reader, no full pass, at both sizes.
- `aspect_pass_is_flat` (10 / 100 keys in live bodies): one more key, an older commit of an expiring map expiring alone, one write with a closure as big: flat. A key overwrite killing one body judges every entry of the label at the writer (issues/kill-judges-label-entries.md).
