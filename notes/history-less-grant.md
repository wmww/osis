# Grants without history (from now)
> Summary: A grant that hands no earlier history ( osis `GrantOpts::reads`, CLI `grant --reads`; rules spec/crypto.md §4 Floors and State at a cut, §5 Passing on): Tess's decisions, the principle and why each rule (cut, floors, carry and boxing rules, roles secrets, low floor, re-add) is needed, how the engine builds it (`rotation::floors`, `osis_keys::RoleEpochs`, the state snapshots and expiring snapshots), what a newcomer gets, the Cut.tla model with its negative controls, and the gaps the model leaves. What is left to build: issues/history-less-grant.md.

## Tess's decisions
- **From now means current state**: a newcomer granted without history reads the current state but never a commit from before (no earlier version, no past signal fire): a document shows its finished text, a chat no past message but its settings and name. A grant with history stays for whoever wants the past. Hiding existing content while showing new additions is not wanted.
- **Per grant, with a default**: a grant may say "without history"; a per-thing default is an aspect, set the way the history retention setting is (spec/commits.md §5 The setting).
- **Two choices only**: a newcomer sees history from when it was granted, or all of it. No admin-chosen start point.
- **Showing history later**: allowed if easy (it is a later grant with history to that holder).
- **Removed and re-added without history**: keeps what it could read before its removal, and must not get what was written between its removal and its re-add.
- **Metadata**: a newcomer may be able to tell that earlier entries exist (counts, sizes, times), but osis exposes none of it by default (no API surfaces entries the holder cannot open, nor counts of them).
- **Decided later** (numbered as the model's README cites them):
  1. **New nodes see what old nodes display**: a member's new node may sync anything its account's existing nodes display. So a re-add without history boxes the member at the epochs its removal's rotation listed, and rotations take a removed member's boxes only where they carry, as anyone's.
  2. **Two history-less adds at once**: each newcomer can read what was written around the other's add, never anything from before its own (accepted).
  3. **Sync IDs and steps** (build): spec/sync-ids.md §2 role keys in use assumes every holder holds older secrets by history; a history-less holder opens from its cut's step on, and a walk to an older step must not strand it. Not modelled.

## Principle and why each rule
Principle: a box or history entry hands a secret only to a party entitled to it; a holder granted without history is entitled to what follows its grant's cut, and to what its account's nodes could already read. Every rule is that principle at one writer of boxes or entries, or at one holder of an epoch.
- **The cut** removes no box: a rotation removes only the boxes at epochs its history carries, so prior holders keep their boxes at the epochs the cut lists, which open everything before.
- **Floors are written in history entries, never in keysets**: only a holder of the epoch's secret writes an entry, whose holders could hand all it opens anyway, while any writer may write a `keys.0` body of an epoch it does not hold (a fork beside the cut): raising a fork's floor by the body, or by a forged rotation commit's lists, had co-holders carry the fork, whose history opens the past, to the newcomer (model `X_floor_any`). Floors read as at most n: else a holder's high floor on its own epoch lets honest rotations carry it past a later cut (`X_cap_nocap`).
- **A rotation's floor** is the greatest of its rotator's and the holders it boxes, not of what it lists: a lister may hold no listed secret and so know no listed floor, and a floor read from lists carried pre-cut secrets into an epoch a later lift handed the newcomer.
- **A party's floor** is its newest grant's: a party hands no history it was not given (a history-less admin or may-grant holder grants history to nobody, itself included). By several paths, the least: a path with history entitles it to everything. Through a holder actor, that holder actor's own floor on the thing. Read by the newest grant, never at a rotation's point: a member removed and re-added without history may stamp a rotation before its removal (a re-grant makes such writes valid again), where its floor was 0. A self-rewrite lowering a floor is no lowering (`osis_perm::lowers`), else a history-less admin of a holder actor hands it history by self-removal.
- **The carry rule judges the rotator too**: every other holder of e holds it by a box, and honest boxes follow the boxing rule; the rotator holds e by writing its rotation, so no box rule screens it. A history-less writer that lists only pre-cut epochs or writes a low floor would otherwise hold an epoch into which co-holders add pre-cut secrets (`X_hostile_norotator`). A rotation's own entry needs no rotator check, its floor being at least its rotator's.
- **The boxing rule is needed beside the carry rule**: a node holding the grant may hold a fork made beside the cut by a rotator that did not know the grant, and re-sealing the newcomer there hands it everything that fork's history carries (`X_cut_nofloor`). Its own nodes count no epoch below their floor as current: a re-added member holds its removal's rotation commit, wrapped under an epoch it kept, and would show it current with no secret (`X_readd`).
- **Only read has floors**: content is read's; write, admin and sync history hands no content. A cut's floor alone carries nothing below it (every earlier floor is below its n), so no cut flag reaches the carry rule.
- **Roles secrets**: a history-less holder must hold every role commit on the thing to judge as holders do: the roster, the commits names name, and every grant write a later demotion's audit may revive (an audit drops the demoted party's writes its frontier leaves out, so the write each dropped one overrode stands again; no projection of the role history is enough). So role parts go under each epoch's roles secret and every history entry carries every earlier roles secret its writer holds, the carry rule aside: a cut costs one entry of roles secrets, not the role history. Without them n never meets the cut's names (`X_names_noroles`) and a grant beside the cut never reaches n (`X_race_noroles`).
- **Low floor**: a current epoch whose floor is below a holder's (a fork beside a cut; a hostile low-floor epoch) bars that holder's re-seal there, so the read rotation is due; without it a role commit on such a fork waits at the newcomer for good. Not while the node holds an unsuperseded epoch whose names it does not meet: it may lack a removal and box the removed member (`X_rmrace`). The rotation reaches by inbox a holder not boxed at the epoch it is wrapped under.
- **Removed members** keep their boxes below a cut (no rotation carries an epoch below its floor), so a new node of one opens only what its earlier nodes could, never an epoch made knowing its removal (`NoAbsence`). **A re-add without history** is a cut that also boxes the member at each epoch its removal's rotation listed, which open what it read before its removal (`ReaddFresh`); epochs made knowing the removal sit below the re-add's floor unless they follow it.
- **Compaction snapshots** carry the history entries, so an epoch held by one keeps its floor; a rotator unknown (no rotation commit held) reads as the greatest floor of A's holders, erring toward carrying less.
- **Cost**: one integer per history entry and a roles secret per earlier epoch in it. Per cut nothing is placed again. A grant with history writes one box per dropped epoch (grows with cuts and cut-adjacent forks, not writes); a re-add without history one box per epoch its removal listed.

## How it is built
- **Floors** live on the read role of the established view only (`RoleEpochs::set_floors`, filled by `rotation::floors::set` in `Core::own_epochs`): each epoch's is the greatest its opened live history entries write (`EpochInfo::floor`), with no lift. The carry rule is `RoleEpochs::carries`/`carries_into`; the boxing rule `RoleEpochs::allows`/`Recipient::floor`, binding rotations, grants, re-seals, the re-seal trigger and the tip add. A node not holding an epoch reads its floor as 0; a party's floor comes from its edge in force (`Grant::floor`, at least its author's: a cycle of authors reads as the greatest written) and through holder actors it reads, the least; an unknown rotator reads as the greatest party floor. `party_floor` is 0 at once while no edge on the thing carries a floor, so things never cut pay nothing.
- **Roles secrets** (`osis_crypto::roles_secret`, `RoleEpochs::roles_secret`/`held_roles`/`opening_newest_first`): role parts (`osis_keys::UNDER_ROLES`: `roles.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0`; not `account.0`, whose seeds a section alone guards) are sealed in a section of their own and their blobs wrapped (`Engine::wrap_secret`) under the epoch's roles secret; `osis_keys::HistoryBox::roles` carries the earlier ones (History adds for them too). Keysets and history boxes go there too, so a newcomer establishes a concurrent cut's epoch it was re-sealed at. A roles secret from an entry opened under a reading-only secret is reading-only alike (`RoleEpochs::set_reading_roles`): a newcomer granted by an admin it does not yet know holds its grant pending and needs those roles secrets to open the grant that makes the admin's valid. The nonce rule runs through the roles secret, so it names its epoch.
- **Low floor**: `floors::low_floor_due` at pass end; the inbox copy by `rotation::unreached`. **Re-add**: `floors::removal_listed` (found by `epoch_intro` of the removal commit), the tip add for a re-adder lacking it `floors::readd_epochs`. **The current setting** goes again as a content commit after the grant (`validity::setting_now`), its floors as they read: a role commit may not carry `retention^.0`.
- **State at a cut**: commits stay out by the floor clause of Passing on (notes/keys-establishment.md, Passing on content); state goes in compaction snapshots, which carry state and no commit. A writer owes a snapshot of each content aspect, on the thing or a thing whose independent it is, holding a body below its seal floor (`floors::owe_state`, once per floor rise: the cut's author in the grant, everyone at pass end; `floors::late_part` for a body of known lower floor applied later, owed then but due only at the next pass end, since a first sync fetches pre-cut bodies before the cut's snapshots and would otherwise snapshot every aspect per batch). It is the purge's re-snapshot duty (`purge::owe_resnapshot`, `Engine::purge_resnapshots`; durable, answered by a held valid snapshot of floor at least the seal floor covering those bodies): one mechanism saying "a node that never held these bodies reads as this one". A dependent thing's derived epochs read its mint admin's epochs' floors (`ActorEpochs::derived`). Rejected: forbidding snapshots of pre-cut state (an aspect written before a cut is never compacted again; Tess chose current state); one snapshot commit carrying many aspects (the cost is per-aspect header overhead only, and compaction indexes one aspect per snapshot). Cost: one snapshot per aspect holding state, about 0.5 KB each plus the state; flat in history (8 aspects: 4344 B after 3 rounds of edits, 4352 B after 30); linear in aspects (500 aspects: 268 KB); a large value's snapshot links its chunk tree as written (692 B for 1 MiB).
- **Records under a cut**: a dependent thing's record rides in its minting's blob, which a minting from before the cut keeps under an epoch the newcomer never opens. So a minting whose `created.0` floor is below the node's seal floor (or not known) counts as no carrier (`Engine::minted_above_floor` in `cert_carriers`): the record goes in the read thing's record blob, as any record S's certificates name. Mintings note their openings like content parts (`opened::floored`), else every record of the things whose independent is a cut thing would go as certificates, the record blob growing with each new thing. The newcomer keeps that record loose until something awaits it; a held strong to a thing whose record it lacks does (`Closure::awaits_record`, set in the closure's `judge`), as the strong needs it to judge whether it brings the thing in. Rejected: the cut's snapshot carrying the record (a commit carries a record only as a minting, and a second minting is a remint).
- **Expiring writes**: the whole-state snapshot carries the state as it reads once every write with an end has ended (`snapshot_state`): none of them, but every write only they superseded. Beside it the owing writer writes one expiring snapshot per expiry T ahead of an owed body (`snapshot::write_expiring`): `expires: T`, the state as it reads just before T restricted to the writes bodies ending at T give (`expiring_carriers`), tables empty. So at every time the holder reads what the history reads (a removal or overwrite expiring brings back what it replaced; Tess: an expiry has no effect beyond what it removes): property crdt-model `cut_reads_as_history_spec_8`, by hand osis-engine `rotation::tests_cut::cut_holder_reads_what_expiring_writes_superseded_once_they_end_spec_crdt_8`. Cutting each from the state as it reads now loses those writes for good at the holder. At T its blob goes at every peer (the prefix carries T, which a lasting snapshot carrying a value with its expiry in `Facts` would break: rejected for it), and holders drop its body whole (`retention::expire_snapshot`). A forged expiry ends only its own copies, and an expiring snapshot covers only bodies ending no later (`expiry_covers`), so a forged one never drops a lasting body early. A write held from expiring snapshots alone ends there in what the holder writes from it (its lasting snapshots strip it, its undo writes it back with its expiry, `undo::copied_expiry`). The expiry is any writer's claim, so it decides only there: a write whose delta's header is held ends as that delta does, one a lasting body holds lasts (every write of a dot, value-carrying or not: a gone write has no effect); a forged later expiry at a node holding the write from expiring snapshots alone keeps it longer (no new power). Written only for a cut: in every compaction it would add one commit per live expiry. Cost: at most one commit per expiring body the writer holds; a later cut's covers the last's of the same expiry. Rejected: bucketing expiries (a value then ends early or late at the newcomer).
- **Edits**: an edit carries its original's floor (`AspectEntry::original_floor`); a node whose floor is above it lists the edit nowhere (`listing::listed`) unless it holds the original opened at or above its floor, so an edit after the cut of a message from before shows nothing, while an ordinary holder lacking the original still lists the edit alone. Where the original is not held the writer's claim stands (hiding an edit from from-now holders, or showing one of a past fire: its text is the writer's own): no new power. Rejected: "an edit whose original is not held lists nowhere" (a chat's default edit purges the original, so ordinary nodes would lose edits).
- **Tests**: osis-engine `rotation::tests_cut`, one per model shape plus planted faults each failing a test (carry rule off, boxing rule off, rotator ignored, own-floor clause off, author floor ignored, no clamp, floor read from lists, no tip add, no re-add boxes, no setting, no roles secrets in entries, role parts under the read secret, content under the roles secret, no reading-only roles secrets, no low-floor rotation, low floor with names unmet, the inbox judged by any current box); what users see: osis `api::from_now_grant_is_kept_until_a_grant_shows_everything`; across sync: osis-sync `flows::a_from_now_grantee_reads_the_current_state_and_no_earlier_write_spec_crypto_4`, `flows::a_from_now_grantee_reads_a_dependent_thing_minted_with_it_spec_crypto_4`. Known masking: the cut removing prior holders' boxes is caught only with the tip add off.

## What a newcomer gets
- Never a secret of an epoch in its grant's causal past, except one a hostile member holds (which it could box to anyone anyway).
- Every epoch that follows its cut, from the live state alone (a new node of it included), but one whose own entry writes a floor below its own (a hostile writer's, or one made by a node not holding its grant); what is written there never reaches it, the low-floor rotation boxes it at the next epoch.
- Every role commit on the thing, wherever it was made.
- Content written beside the grant on a fork whose rotator did not know it never reaches it, merge or not; content beside it under another cut of at least the same n does once merged (Decided 2).
- A hostile member granted without history (writer, admin or may-grant holder) gets nothing from before its cut, by its own rotations with any floor or listing, or through anyone it grants with history.
- The CRDT metadata a snapshot carries, which no read shows (spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut; accepted, Tess): the author and stamp of every live write, each sequence element's creation anchor and earlier position write, every max/min contribution and counter contribution with its author, concurrent writes hidden under a removal, and where and when text was deleted. Rejected: a cut snapshot form folding max/min bags and dropping hidden writes (CRDT rule changes for metadata only; the position write and stamps cannot go anyway).

## Costs and waits
- **Two commits per grant**: the grant (a role commit, wrapped under the epoch the cut supersedes, so it carries no setting) and the setting written again under the cut, in one local transaction. A newcomer holding the grant but not yet the setting reads no setting until it arrives.
- **The setting's cap**: the setting written again carries the floors joined over the held settings (`carry_floors`); where that passes `MAX_RETENTION_ENTRIES` the writer refuses it, as any setting write, and the grant fails with it.
- **Low floor behind unmet names**: the low-floor rotation waits while the node holds an unsuperseded epoch whose names it does not meet, until the named commit arrives or the epoch is withdrawn or superseded: the same wait as the withdraw it leans on (notes/keys-establishment.md, Unsuperseded and withdraw).
- The tip add needs the right to rotate read, and a writer's low-floor epoch after a cut never reaches the holder it bars (spec/crypto.md §4 Floors).

## Model (Cut.tla)
One thing, one key role. Admins a and b rotate, grant, remove, re-seal and make the low-floor rotation; readers add history; commits arrive in any order a node can read them and count once their author's grant is held. Hostile parties (a history-less writer h, a writer m with history) rotate listing any epochs with any floor and put entries with any floor; a hostile history-less admin grants an accomplice q with history. Properties: `NoPast`, `HonestPast` (no honest box or entry hands a newcomer anything before its cut, whatever floors hostile parties write), `PriorKeep`, `NoAbsence`, `NewReads`, `Unlocked`, `Alike`, `Roster`, `ReaddFresh`. Sizes and the full table: core/test/tla/rotation/README.md (History cuts).
- Passing shapes: a cut with a rotation beside it; history and history-less grants racing; a cut racing a removal; re-add without history (with b rotating beside it, and a new node of m); two concurrent cuts; showing history later; a cut naming an earlier removal; hostile h rotating once, twice or beside b; two history-less grantees; hostile entries with any floor; h granting q with history; m's floor then removal.
- Negative controls, each failing: no floor at all (`X_cut_old`, `X_readd_old`); History add without the carry rule (`X_cut_oldadd`); carry rule off (`X_cut_nocarry`); boxing rule off (`X_cut_nofloor`); no roles secrets (`X_names_noroles`, `X_race_noroles`); carry rule on e's floor alone (`X_hostile_norotator`); a grant with history giving floor 0 (`X_acc_nogfloor`); floors above n counting (`X_cap_nocap`); a keyset floor (`X_floor_any`); the re-add boxing only at the cut (`X_readd_fresh_noreadd`).
- Probes failing on accepted cases: `X_hostile2_all` (h's floor-0 epoch after n's cut is never boxed to n: spec/crypto.md §4 Floors); `X_two_strict` (Decided 2).

## Gaps the model leaves (argued, not modelled)
- **Holder actors**: a party's floor is the least over its paths; the model has single-node parties.
- **Content**: the model has role commits only. The build rests on two model facts: every epoch a holder opens has a floor at least its own (`NewReads`), and a part written before a cut has a floor below the cut's n (`HonestPast`). A node passing a part on reads its chosen epoch's floor from that epoch's rotation commit's own entry, and floors read as the greatest an opened entry writes, so no node reads the chosen epoch's floor below its true one.
- **Hostile n**: the model keeps a hostile rotation's n honest. A larger n raises every later rotation's n, so a later cut still sits above its floor; beside a cut it acts as a concurrent cut. A smaller n supersedes nothing at or above it.
- **Grant changes**: the history choice rides on the grant value, and a change (role, `until`, renewal) keeps it unless its writer shows history; else promoting or renewing a history-less holder would hand it history by the author's floor.
- **Hostile boxes**: extra boxes hand only what their writer opens; missing or removed boxes lock holders out (notes/keys.md, Lockouts).
- **A history-less re-adder** opens none of the removal's listed epochs, so a holder that opens them boxes them (a tip add, m allowed them by its re-add).
- **A hostile member re-added without history**: an epoch made knowing the removal is carried only by a node holding the removal, where m's later writes count only with its re-add, which gives m its floor.
- Certificate copies, withdraw, void names, sync IDs and steps (Decided 3; issues/history-less-grant.md).
