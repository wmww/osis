# Public things, cards, externally grantable, and the account library: the builder's brief
> Summary: Stages A5 and A6 of notes/redesign-plan.md built as one stage: the public (a holder that is no thing, its grant making a thing readable by every party), public pieces carried by referring units, cards (a thing's keys and inbox as data on any thing), KEM keys only where sealed to, the granter's checks, public links; and the account calls leaving the `osis` crate for a library in the same pass. What the spec says (written), why, the delete list, the pieces in order, the model behind the new rules, what is owed, risks and costs.

## What the spec says
- **The public** (spec/permissions.md §1): a holder that is no thing, named by the zero tid (no record hashes to it). A thing is public by an ordinary grant to it, written in `roles.0` like any. Nothing signs as it, holds a role on it or lists it on a path; it has no record, aspects, key, sync ID, inbox or card; it is no holder actor and never held for keys. Its boxes are of the **open form** (the secret in clear, spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes; `box` case `open 3`, AD form `[3]`, spec/wire.md §7). Rotations, departures and edge writes on it are ordinary: a reader leaving a public thing rotates read as anywhere.
- **Reading as the public**: a claim set lists the public, where it reads, by its **public entry** `BLAKE3("osis v1 public claim" || sync_id)` with an open entry box (spec/sync-ids.md §3, spec/wire.md §8); a Hit on it carries no proof, and its answer carries the head's entry box as a static entry's does (spec/sync-opens.md §4, §5). A **public link** (`Link` format 2: target, its sync ID, peers; spec/wire.md §10) is redeemed by opening the sync ID by the public entry and writing a strong to the target (spec/sync-set.md §4, §5); a contact card is one, to a profile (spec/sync-inbox.md §5).
- **T's keys open** what the public reads by its grant on the thing's independent itself (spec/sync-set.md §2), so a strong brings a public thing, or a dependent of one, into the referrer's unit whole. **Public pieces**: T's sync ID carries, for each thing a strong of its unit reaches, the public's edge piece on that thing's independent, as certificate copies (spec/sync-set.md §2; in the required set, spec/sync-judgement.md §2). They are what lets a reader of T enter a public thing founded.
- **Cards** (spec/sync-inbox.md §1, spec/data-model.md §9): `card.0` on any thing, `map<weak, card>`, `card = { inbox, keys, records }`: A's inbox where it is another thing, and A's and the inbox's current `keys.0` commits in the holder keys form. An entry keyed by A is invalid unless A is a member of its write's path (spec/permissions.md §5.2). A reader takes a card's commits as holder keys (only what is on the authority chain, judged). A's inbox is the thing its card names, else A. A is externally grantable while a card stands and the inbox enables `grant@~0`. A node of A that may write the card's thing and holds newer `keys.0` commits rewrites the card.
- **External signals on any thing**: `grant@~0` and `stranded@~0` are taken by whatever thing enables them (spec/data-model.md §9); a stranded notice goes on the stranded sync ID's thing itself. `keys.0` has no `inbox` field (spec/crypto.md §3).
- **Published KEM keys** (spec/crypto.md §3): a keyset carries its KEM key only where, when written, the thing is minted with a key, holds that role or above for keys somewhere (or is granted it in the same transaction), or a card of it stands; a read keyset also where an external signal is enabled. Step keys carry no read key beside a KEM-less read keyset. Seal takes a KEM-less keyset only where the sealer holds its secret (symmetric); a claim set omits a reader whose read keyset has no KEM key.
- **Writer's checks** (spec/permissions.md §2): a grant or change naming a thing H is written only where the node may write H or holds a card of H, and H's current keyset of each role boxed carries a KEM key or the node rotates that role of H in the same transaction (where it may); else refused. Removals and lowerings are never refused for this. The public is granted at most read, never may-grant. Delivery of a grant from outside fires on the inbox a held card names; none without a card (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece).
- **Accounts** (spec/permissions.md §8): the profile is granted the public read and holds A's card naming the inbox; the inbox enables both signals.

## Decisions and why
- **The public is no thing** (notes/principles.md: a concept that does not fit a thing is a concept of its own, as a key is). The design notes' well-known *public thing* needed a record naming no admin, constant keysets, a symmetric box form, no sync ID, a cap on what may be granted it, an exemption from rotation, and "takes every grant": a thing restricted in what may be granted on it or written to it, which the principle forbids. As a holder that is no thing, most of that follows (nothing to sign, mint, deliver to or hold for keys). Left as rules of its own: the zero tid, the open box form, the public entry, the clause in T's keys open with its pieces. Requirements wording: issues/public-holder-requirements.md.
- **Dropped**: the cap "at most read, no may-grant" as a validity rule (a writer's check now: nothing acts through the public, and whoever could grant it more could hand out the secrets anyway, spec/requirements.md "a secret once shared"); the rotation exemption for departures at or below read (a rare rotation, one rule fewer); the symmetric box to a constant key (open boxes are smaller and need no constant keyset).
- **Rejected: a per-thing public link key** (a link key whose seed derives from the target's tid). Anyone would hold its signing key and could self-remove its grant (unpublishing the thing), write as it, or rotate it. The holder must be one nobody signs as.
- **Rejected: the public's read counting through holders** (public read on H, H read on X carries X): a reader of T cannot found it (H's grant on X sits in X's bodies or H's key part, neither under T's sync ID), so logger and readers disagree on the unit (model control `chains`). Access through H still works; carriage needs the public's grant on X's independent.
- **The inbox is named by the card, not by an admin-only aspect** (the redesign plan named one listing the inbox and the cards). Who reaches A's inbox from outside holds a card; A's own nodes need no pointer (an offer names its holder in its copy, a stranded notice goes on the thing itself); fellow holders of a target changing A's grant need none either (A's nodes syncing the target see the change, a removed node learns it as departed). Refreshing cards without a list: a node of A rewrites a stale card it may write, read from what it holds (notes/principles.md, Close leaks: no list kept to know).
- **A card is A's own `keys.0` commits, not bare KEM keys**: Seal needs a held valid keyset (with its carried demotions), and the read key outsiders seal to must stay admin-vouched (spec/crypto.md §3 Read key in force). The card replaces the profile's carriage rule (a profile's sync ID carrying its mint admin's public key record): data on a thing, not a carriage special case.
- **Card validity by path membership**, not admin: a valid write through A needs write on its target along hops including A, so A's writers publish. Offers still need the inbox's admins to enable `grant@~0` (spec/requirements.md Permissions: grants from anyone else only where its admins allow); a card without that gives a granter keys and no delivery.
- **KEM keys first needed**: the granter rotates the holder's KEM-less roles in the grant's transaction, or refuses. One mechanism (rotation), no "add a KEM key to an epoch" write.

## A6 folded in
A5 rewrites exactly the account calls A6 moves: making an account (now: grant the profile to the public, write the card, enable the inbox's signals), `profile`, `profile_of`, `contact_card`, `import_contact`. Building them in `osis` and moving them after is the same code written twice. So:
- **The library crate comes first** (A6 brief 2), before A4's pairing and node-removal pieces are built: `Osis::pair`, `Joining`, `pair_offer`, `pair_accept` are still stubs in core/osis/src/account.rs, so A4 pieces 3, 4 and 6 build them in the library directly, over core's grant, removal and connection calls.
- `Engine::make_account` leaves the engine for the library, written over core calls; core keeps minting, granting, enabling a signal, writing a card (an ordinary aspect write) and the public's grant.
- The schema step (realm, home, account, profile, contacts: A6 brief 3) is where the profile and card live; the library writes them.
- What stays A6-only: `me`, `account_root`, `nodes`, `remove_node` and `Osis::create`'s split (core makes a node; the library makes an account), session default paths, CLI `me`/`pair`/`contact`, kit and app.

## Order of work (one piece a session, on main, delete first)
1. **Spec**: this branch (landed with piece 2 if policy refuses earlier).
2. **Library crate and the account calls moved** (delete from `osis` first): `make_account` out of the engine; profile, contact, pairing stubs in the library; CLI calls it. Tests: the account tests ignored under `accounts:` follow their functions.
3. **The public** in perm, keys and boxes: zero-tid holder in `roles.0`, open boxes, no holder actor, the writer's check on its grant; tests: a public grant boxes open at each rotation, a departure rotates.
4. **Public in sync**: the public entry and open entry box in steps, Hits without proof, the clause in T's keys open, public pieces in the required set, the public link; tests: a profile fetched by a reader served only by a store keeping the referrer unread; a strong to a public thing's dependent; a stranger opening by a public link.
5. **KEM keys and the granter's checks**: optional `kem_key`, the rotation-on-first-hold, Seal and claim sets on KEM-less keysets; tests: an independent thing nobody seals to publishes no KEM key; a grant to it rotates its roles first; a granter without card or write refused.
6. **Cards and signals on any thing**: `card.0` and its content rule, fires to the card's inbox, stranded notices on the thing, stale-card rewrite, the `inbox` field and `inbox_thing` deleted; tests: a stranger's sealed fire reaching an inbox kept unread and promoted; a card written off-path invalid.
7. **Library on top**: account making with public profile and card; contact cards as public links; `osis` API for the public's grant, enabling signals, writing a card (notes/api-design.md).
8. **Models and differentials, counting**: sims with public profiles; bytes per independent thing against notes/thing-cost.md; a commit on a profile referenced from n and 10n units.

## Models
held-model `closure::public` (tests/it/spec_public.rs): one unit thing T, a logger holding everything, a reader served only T's sync ID knowing the public's grants only from what it reads there, its unit the least fixpoint. `Rule::SPEC` holds `agree` (reader and logger compute one unit) and `owed` (every thing the public reads that a strong of the unit reaches is carried) over 5,000 random worlds. Controls, each failing its property and a hand world:
- `clause` off: the profile is not carried (`owed`).
- `pieces` off: the reader never enters the profile (`agree`): its only knowledge that the public reads it is in its own bodies, carried only once it is in the unit.
- `exact` (pieces only on independent things a strong reaches): a strong to a public thing's dependent is carried by the logger and not founded at the reader (`agree`). Found by the model; the spec puts the piece on the reached thing's independent.
- `chains` (the public's read through holders): `agree` fails (Rejected, above).

Owed (not modelled): the public's grant through the walk and rotation (roles-model: departures of and beside the public, open boxes; the rules are the ordinary ones, so a check that a holder with no keysets breaks nothing in Seal and Left out); KEM-less keysets in Seal and Left out (a holder sealed symmetrically then re-keyed); the card's content rule and stale cards (a removed member's keysets in a stale card: covered by Holder actors' "keyset written without knowledge of a removal"); public pieces over time (a removal of the public carried before the bodies leave the unit).

## Costs (against notes/thing-cost.md: 14.9 KB stored per independent thing, its rotation commit 9376 B)
- **An independent thing nobody seals to** (a shared note, chat, document): no KEM keys (4 × 1216 B in its keysets, 1216 B in its step keys' read key): rotation commit about 3.3 KB, stored about 8.8 KB per node [E], no ML-KEM keygens. A holder (an account, a group, a node thing) is unchanged.
- **A public thing**: its boxes to the public open (about 70 B a box against about 1.2 KB sealed); per referring unit a public piece (the commits writing the public's grant, copies) and an entry and stamp per commit on it under each referrer's sync ID (risk 6 of notes/redesign-plan.md: entries per commit grow with referrers, counted at n and 10n in piece 8).
- **A card**: A's and its inbox's `keys.0` commits with evidence, about 13 KB [E], carried whole by every unit carrying the thing it is on. On the profile that is every unit its owner writes in; the library should put the card on a public thing only would-be granters hold (one the profile references weakly, a contact import strongs).

## Risks
- A strong to any public thing pulls it whole into the referrer's unit: a public thing referenced widely is copied under every referrer's sync ID (accepted cost of the built unit; counted).
- Stores learn a unit is public from its claim set (accepted, spec/sync-hostile.md).
- A stale card seals grants to A's older keysets, which a party removed from A since may open until the card is rewritten.
- The requirements' "a holder is a thing" (issues/public-holder-requirements.md).
