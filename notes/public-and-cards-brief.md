# Public things, cards, externally grantable, and the account library: the builder's brief
> Summary: Stages A5 and A6 of notes/redesign-plan.md built as one stage: the public (a holder that is no thing, its grant making a thing readable by every party), public pieces carried by referring units, cards (a thing's keys and inbox as data on any thing), KEM keys only where sealed to, the granter's checks, public links; and the account calls in a library (osis-account, built first). What the spec says (written), why, the delete list, the library as built, the pieces in order, the models behind the new rules and their findings, risks and costs.

## What the spec says
- **The public** (spec/permissions.md §1): a holder that is no thing, named by the zero tid (no record hashes to it). A thing is public by an ordinary grant to it, written in `roles.0` like any. Nothing signs as it, holds a role on it or lists it on a path; it has no record, aspects, key, sync ID, inbox or card; it is no holder actor and never held for keys. Its boxes are of the **open form** (the secret in clear, spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes; `box` case `open 3`, AD form `[3]`, spec/wire.md §7). Rotations, departures and edge writes on it are ordinary: a reader leaving a public thing rotates read as anywhere.
- **Reading as the public**: a claim set lists the public, where it reads, by its **public entry** `BLAKE3("osis v1 public claim" || sync_id)` with an open entry box (spec/sync-ids.md §3, spec/wire.md §8); a Hit on it carries no proof, and its answer carries the head's entry box as a static entry's does (spec/sync-opens.md §4, §5). A **public link** (`Link` format 2: target, its sync ID, peers; spec/wire.md §10) is redeemed by opening the sync ID by the public entry and writing a strong to the target (spec/sync-set.md §4, §5); a contact card is one, to a profile (spec/sync-inbox.md §5).
- **T's keys open** what the public reads by its grant on the thing's independent itself (spec/sync-set.md §2), so a strong brings a public thing, or a dependent of one, into the referrer's unit whole. **Public pieces**: T's sync ID carries, for each thing a strong of its unit reaches, the public's edge piece on that thing's independent, as certificate copies (spec/sync-set.md §2; in the required set, spec/sync-judgement.md §2). They are what lets a reader of T enter a public thing founded.
- **Cards** (spec/sync-inbox.md §1, spec/data-model.md §9): `card.0` on any thing, `map<weak, card>`, `card = { inbox, keys, records }`: A's inbox where it is another thing, and A's and the inbox's current `keys.0` commits in the holder keys form. An entry keyed by A is invalid unless A is a member of its write's path (spec/permissions.md §5.2). A reader takes a card's commits as holder keys (only what is on the authority chain, judged). A's inbox is the thing its card names, else A. A is externally grantable while a card stands and the inbox enables `grant@~0`. A card's `since` says when its inbox was chosen (a move writes one above every card of A its writer holds; a refresh keeps it); a node's A inbox is the one its card of A with the greatest `since` names. A node of A that may write the card's thing along a path through A rewrites a card carrying older `keys.0` commits or an older `since`.
- **External signals on any thing**: `grant@~0` and `stranded@~0` are taken by whatever thing enables them (spec/data-model.md §9); a stranded notice goes on the stranded sync ID's thing itself. `keys.0` has no `inbox` field (spec/crypto.md §3).
- **Published KEM keys** (spec/crypto.md §3): a keyset carries its KEM key only where, when written, the thing is minted with a key, holds that role or above for keys somewhere (or is granted it in the same transaction), or a card of it stands; a read keyset also where an external signal is enabled. Step keys carry no read key beside a KEM-less read keyset. Seal takes a KEM-less keyset only where the sealer holds its secret (symmetric); a claim set omits a reader whose read keyset has no KEM key. **KEM key due**: a node that may rotate a role rotates it, at a pass's end, while a current keyset of it lacks a KEM key the rule now gives (the thing came to hold a role, a card or a signal after the keyset was written).
- **Writer's checks** (spec/permissions.md §2): a grant or change naming a thing H is written only where the node may write H or holds H's current `keys.0` (from a card of H, or H's public key record as a fellow holder), and H's current keyset of each role boxed carries a KEM key or the node rotates that role of H in the same transaction (where it may); else refused. Removals and lowerings are never refused for this. The public is granted at most read, never may-grant. Delivery of a grant from outside fires on H's inbox at the node: the one a held card names, else H itself where independent (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece).
- **Accounts** (spec/permissions.md §8): the profile is granted the public read and holds A's card naming the inbox; the inbox enables both signals.

## Decisions and why
- **The public is no thing** (notes/principles.md: a concept that does not fit a thing is a concept of its own, as a key is). The design notes' well-known *public thing* needed a record naming no admin, constant keysets, a symmetric box form, no sync ID, a cap on what may be granted it, an exemption from rotation, and "takes every grant": a thing restricted in what may be granted on it or written to it, which the principle forbids. As a holder that is no thing, most of that follows (nothing to sign, mint, deliver to or hold for keys). Left as rules of its own: the zero tid, the open box form, the public entry, the clause in T's keys open with its pieces. Requirements wording: issues/public-holder-requirements.md.
- **Dropped**: the cap "at most read, no may-grant" as a validity rule (a writer's check now: nothing acts through the public, and whoever could grant it more could hand out the secrets anyway, spec/requirements.md "a secret once shared"); the rotation exemption for departures at or below read (a rare rotation, one rule fewer); the symmetric box to a constant key (open boxes are smaller and need no constant keyset).
- **Rejected: a per-thing public link key** (a link key whose seed derives from the target's tid). Anyone would hold its signing key and could self-remove its grant (unpublishing the thing), write as it, or rotate it. The holder must be one nobody signs as.
- **Rejected: the public's read counting through holders** (public read on H, H read on X carries X): a reader of T cannot found it (H's grant on X sits in X's bodies or H's key part, neither under T's sync ID), so logger and readers disagree on the unit (model control `chains`). Access through H still works; carriage needs the public's grant on X's independent.
- **The inbox is named by the card, not by an admin-only aspect** (the redesign plan named one listing the inbox and the cards). Who reaches A's inbox from outside holds a card; A's own nodes need no pointer (an offer names its holder in its copy, a stranded notice goes on the thing itself); fellow holders of a target changing A's grant need none either (A's nodes syncing the target see the change, a removed node learns it as departed). Refreshing cards without a list: a node of A rewrites a stale card it may write, read from what it holds (notes/principles.md, Close leaks: no list kept to know).
- **A card is A's own `keys.0` commits, not bare KEM keys**: Seal needs a held valid keyset (with its carried demotions), and the read key outsiders seal to must stay admin-vouched (spec/crypto.md §3 Read key in force). The card replaces the profile's carriage rule (a profile's sync ID carrying its mint admin's public key record): data on a thing, not a carriage special case.
- **Card validity by path membership**, not admin: a valid write through A needs write on its target along hops including A, so A's writers publish. Offers still need the inbox's admins to enable `grant@~0` (spec/requirements.md Permissions: grants from anyone else only where its admins allow); a card without that gives a granter keys and no delivery.
- **KEM keys first needed**: rotation, one mechanism (no "add a KEM key to an epoch" write). The granter rotates the holder's KEM-less roles in the grant's transaction or refuses; that only saves the holder a stranded wait: liveness comes from KEM key due (a keyset written before its rotator learned of a grant, or as a card or signal ended, is otherwise never replaced: `KK_written`).
- **A card's `since`**: an inbox move must spread to every card of A and a keys refresh must not undo it; ordering by the card write's own stamp lets a node refreshing keys before it sees a move re-assert the old inbox later (control `Restamp`).

## Delete list
- Spec (done here): `keys.0` `inbox`; the profile's carriage of its mint admin's public key record; the public key record carrying its inbox's; "an inbox" as a kind (signals on any thing); contact card as a read link with certificates.
- Engine and schema: done (pieces 3, 5, 6). `fire_to`'s walk over the holder's holders stays: it is whom a fire is offered to where proven (spec/sync-inbox.md §3), no inbox lookup.
- Tests of the deleted behaviour (sync `links::` contact-card tests, `grants::` reaching a holder by its public key record's inbox).

## A6 folded in
A5 rewrites exactly the account calls A6 moves, so the library came first (piece 2, built) and pieces 3-8 change it in place.
- **Built** (libs/account, osis-account; schema/account.osis): `Account` (`make`, `of`, `thing`, `profile`, `nodes`, `remove_node`, `contact_card`, `import_contact`, `profile_of`, `offer_pairing` with `Offering::accept`), `pair` with `Joining::finish`; notes/api.md Accounts. The account is the thing the node keeps its settings on (`Osis::settings_thing`); its profile is `account:profile.0` on A and names A by `account:of.0`; the card is kept in `account:card.0`, imported profiles in `account:contacts.0` on the importer's account. Tests: libs/account `it` (making, pairing and removing, importing a card).
- **Core keeps**: `Osis::create` makes a node only; `node()` the node thing; `author()` what a handle writes as (the node thing, or a client's own thing; there is no `me` in core); `settings_thing()`; `publish_dial_on(&[Tid])` (node root `dial_on^.0`, schema/core.osis), which the library names A and the profile in; `redeem(link, joiner)`; the port's `make_link` row. Engine: `Engine::mint_independent` (several things minted with keys of their own and commits for them, one transaction: the library's making, a client's thing); `local_peer::make_account` (harness: the same arrangement for engine and sync tests); pairing (`pair`, `take_offer`, `node_keys`) and `remove_node` stay engine calls the library makes.
- **"me"**: the node acts as its node thing; "me" for a person is the account (`Account::thing`). CLI `me` is the account (a client with its own thing: that thing; a node acting for none: its node thing), so `create --admin me`, `grant T me`, `leave`'s default holder, `chat create`, `link redeem`'s joiner are the account; `whoami` prints both. Kit's `you()` is the person's profile (`Reader::person`); the app's chat marks a message mine when its author is this node or a node of the account.
- **Undo is per node** (decided by the model): undo and redo act on the handle's author's commits, and a node writes as its node thing, so each node undoes its own writes; another node's write is `revert`ed. Per-account undo would need the person's writes to name the account as purge author (`Write::act_as`) and undo to take that author: not built, no test asks for it.
- **Interim** (piece 7 replaces): the library reaches the engine through `osis::internals` for making (`mint_independent`, A's card on the profile by `flows::cards::put`), pairing and node removal; the `osis` API needs minting with keys of its own in a transaction and a grant's offer handed over by hand first. CLI and the app read the account by `Account::of`.
- **Left for later pieces**: the schema step (realm, home, contacts: A6 brief 3); the profile granted to the public, A's card, the inbox's signals on the profile's card (piece 7); a pairing offer carrying the pairer's dial hints (notes/accounts-switch-brief.md Open); `leave` of a holder reaching a target through a link still leaves the link key (core's link rule; the CLI leaves as the account, which does not meet it).

## Models
- **held-model `closure::public`** (tests/it/spec_public.rs): one unit thing T, a logger holding everything, a reader served only T's sync ID knowing the public's grants only from what it reads there, its unit the least fixpoint. `Rule::SPEC` holds `agree` (reader and logger compute one unit) and `owed` (every thing the public reads that a strong of the unit reaches is carried) over 5,000 random worlds. Controls: `clause` off (the profile not carried: `owed`); `pieces` off (the reader never enters the profile: `agree`); `exact` (pieces only on independent things a strong reaches: a strong to a public thing's dependent is not founded at the reader, `agree`; the spec puts the piece on the reached thing's independent); `chains` (the public's read through holders: Rejected, above).
- **held-model `closure::public_epochs`** (spec_public.rs): public pieces over time. X's history of the public's grants, removals and X's rotations (every history up to 5 events, exhaustive), T strongs X or its dependent D; X's role commits under T's sync ID as bodies while X is in the unit (each opening with the epoch before), pieces as copies (open to every T reader). The edge piece breaks neither `agree` (the reader counts X public exactly when it is, at every step) nor `reads` (where public, the reader holds X's current epoch).
  - Finding, fixed in spec/sync-set.md §2: the piece written as "the role commits writing the public's grant" leaves out X's rotations boxing the public. With T strong to D alone, X's bodies are not under T's sync ID, so after X's first rotation the reader stays at the grant's epoch and reads none of D's new commits (`GrantOnly`, hand world `[Grant, Rot]`). The piece is the public's edge piece as Key part defines it (each role commit boxing it once set), as an ordinary holder's.
  - Control `WhileRead` (pieces only while the public reads X): a reader that entered X never learns the removal (`agree`). A removal's piece stays carried while a strong reaches X; it carries nothing (First entry).
  - Checked, no change: a reader entering X founded opens the grant commit's open box from the piece's copy (role-class parts under its blob key) and follows X's later rotations, each sectioned under the epoch it supersedes; role commits are never compacted away, so the chain is never cut.
- **TLA `KemKeys.tla`** (core/test/tla/rotation/README.md; `run.sh kemkeys`): KEM-less keysets in Seal, the granter's check, the public's or a party's removal from a holder actor, stale cards.
  - Finding, fixed in spec/crypto.md §3 (KEM key due): KEM keys decided only when written leave a holder out for good (`KK_written` fails `Live`): g grants M on A sealing at k1 (a card of M stood); the card goes; M's admin rotates (k2, no KEM key) before M's edge piece reaches it; g finds k1 stale and k2 no candidate, leaves M out, and nothing ever rotates M again. A no-candidate leave-out is no block, so no left-out record names it and Re-key never fires. With KEM key due M's rotator re-keys once it learns the grant (`KK_due`); that alone suffices without the granter's check (`KK_due_nocheck`).
  - Control `KK_fallback`: Seal falling back below a KEM-less newest keyset to an older one with a KEM key seals at a keyset the removed party (or the public) holds (`NoLeak`): the stale box rule must leave M out.
  - The public as a removed party: its open box at M's old keyset makes every party x; nothing else differs (the public is no holder actor, never left out or sealed to), so the ordinary departure, rotation and stale-box rules bound what it reads as for any party. Granting the public read on a holder actor gives every party what that holder reads, by keys (not carried: `chains`).
  - Stale card: the granter seals to the card's keyset until M's newer keyset reaches it (`KK_stale` passes `NoLeak`; `KK_stale_all` fails `NoLeakAll`: the stated limit, Risks).
- **held-model `closure::cards`** (tests/it/spec_cards.rs): A's cards on 2-4 hosts, 2-3 nodes of A writing some hosts along a path through A and others only along other paths, A rotating and moving its inbox, rounds of passes; 20,000 random worlds. `Newest` (the spec) holds `converge` (every host some node may write through A ends with A's newest keys and, where the move spreads, the last move's inbox), `valid` and `quiet`.
  - Finding, fixed in spec/sync-inbox.md §1 and spec/data-model.md §9 (`since`): with the inbox read off "its card", a move written on one host reached no other (`KeysOnly` fails `converge`); ordering cards by their own write's stamp lets a keys refresh written before the refresher saw a move undo it (`Restamp`).
  - Finding, fixed in spec/sync-inbox.md §1: the rewriter is a node that may write C along a path through A; one that may write C otherwise writes an invalid card (spec/permissions.md §5.2), and keeps doing so (`AnyWriter` fails `valid` and `quiet`).
  - Misdirection: only A's writers (a path through A needs write at every hop) can name A's inbox or carry old keys in A's card; they read A anyway. A writer of A can redirect offers to an inbox it holds, which denies delivery to A's other members until a later move (a writer's power, as removing data is). A card on a thing no node of A may write any longer stays as written.

Not modelled: may-grant grants to the public (spec/permissions.md §2 May-grant grant reads "a grant for a thing or key": a may-grant reader cannot make a thing public; issues/public-holder-requirements.md).

## Costs (against notes/thing-cost.md: 14.9 KB stored per independent thing, its rotation commit 9376 B)
- **An independent thing nobody seals to** (a shared note, chat, document): no KEM keys (4 × 1216 B in its keysets, 1216 B in its step keys' read key): measured (notes/thing-cost.md) 9.6 KB stored at the maker against 16.0 KB, its commits 4208 B against 10.3 KB, no ML-KEM keygens against 5. A holder (an account, a group, a node thing) is unchanged.
- **A public thing**: its boxes to the public open (about 70 B a box against about 1.2 KB sealed); per referring unit a public piece (the commits writing the public's grant, copies) and an entry and stamp per commit on it under each referrer's sync ID (risk 6 of notes/redesign-plan.md: entries per commit grow with referrers, counted at n and 10n in piece 8).
- **A card**: A's and its inbox's `keys.0` commits with evidence, about 13 KB [E], carried whole by every unit carrying the thing it is on. On the profile that is every unit its owner writes in; the library should put the card on a public thing only would-be granters hold (one the profile references weakly, a contact import strongs).

## Risks
- A strong to any public thing pulls it whole into the referrer's unit: a public thing referenced widely is copied under every referrer's sync ID (accepted cost of the built unit; counted).
- Stores learn a unit is public from its claim set (accepted, spec/sync-hostile.md).
- A stale card seals grants to A's older keysets, which a party removed from A since may open until A's newer keyset reaches the granter (A's members logging it under the target once the offer reaches them); a card on a thing A may no longer write is never rewritten.
- The requirements' "a holder is a thing" (issues/public-holder-requirements.md).
