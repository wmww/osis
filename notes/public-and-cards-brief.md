# Public things, cards and the account library
> Summary: The public (a holder that is no thing, its grant making a thing readable by every party), cards (a thing's keys, its inbox among them, as data on any thing), KEM keys only where sealed to, the granter's checks, locators, and the account calls in a library (osis-account): what the spec says, why, how it is built, the models behind the rules and their findings, who hears the public's grant changes, redeeming, costs and accepted risks.

## What the spec says
- **The public** (spec/permissions.md §1): a holder that is no thing, named by the zero tid (no record hashes to it). A thing is public by an ordinary grant to it, written in `roles.0` like any. Nothing signs as it, holds a role on it or lists it on a path; it has no record, aspects, key, sync ID, inbox or card; it is no holder actor. Its boxes are of the **open form** (the secret in clear, spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes; `box` case `open 3`, AD form `[3]`, spec/wire.md §7). Rotations, departures and edge writes on it are ordinary: a reader leaving a public thing rotates read as anywhere.
- **Reading as the public**: the public has a claim at each sync epoch (**public's claim**, spec/wire.md §8) and an entry in each boxing commit, its W the payload in clear (spec/sync-ids.md §3, spec/wire.md §5 Entry blocks); any party opens S by it (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Admissible). A **locator** (`Link` format 2: target, its sync ID, peers; spec/wire.md §10) is redeemed by opening the sync ID by the public's claim and writing a strong to the target (spec/sync-set.md §4, §5); a contact card is one, to a profile (spec/sync-inbox.md §5).
- **T's keys open** what the public reads by its grant on the thing's independent itself (spec/sync-set.md §2), so a strong brings a public thing, or a dependent of one, into the referrer's unit whole.
- **Cards** (spec/sync-inbox.md §1, spec/data-model.md §9): A's card aspect `:<A>:card.0` on any thing, `card = { keys, records }`: A's and A's inbox's current `keys.0` commits in the holder keys form. A delta writing a card of A is invalid unless A is a member of its write's path (spec/permissions.md §5.2), snapshots alike, so it retains, snapshots and covers as any content aspect. A reader takes a card's commits as holder keys (only what is on the authority chain, judged). **A's inbox** is the `inbox` field of A's `keys.0` (an admin's write, spec/permissions.md §3), else A: at a node, the field in A's `keys.0` state there. A is externally grantable while a card stands and the inbox enables `grant@~0`. A node of A that may write the card's thing along a path through A rewrites a card carrying older `keys.0` commits of A or its inbox; a move is a `keys.0` write of A, so it spreads as any keys refresh does.
- **External signals on any thing**: `grant@~0` and `stranded@~0` are taken by whatever thing enables them (spec/data-model.md §9); a stranded notice goes on the stranded sync ID's thing itself.
- **Published KEM keys** (spec/crypto.md §3): a keyset carries its KEM key only where, when written, the thing is minted with a key, holds that role or above for keys somewhere (or is granted it in the same transaction), or a card of it stands; a read keyset also where an external signal is enabled. Step keys carry no read key beside a KEM-less read keyset. Seal takes a KEM-less keyset only where the sealer holds its secret (symmetric). **KEM key due**: a node that may rotate a role rotates it, at a pass's end, while a current keyset of it lacks a KEM key the rule now gives (the thing came to hold a role, a card or a signal after the keyset was written).
- **Writer's checks** (spec/permissions.md §2): a grant or change naming a thing H is written only where the node may write H or holds H's current `keys.0` (from a card of H, or H's public key record as a fellow holder), and H's current keyset of each role boxed carries a KEM key or the node rotates that role of H in the same transaction (where it may); else refused. Removals and lowerings are never refused for this. The public is granted at most read, never may-grant.
- **Accounts** (spec/permissions.md §8): A's `keys.0` names the inbox; the profile is granted the public read and holds A's card; the inbox enables both signals.

## Decisions and why
- **The public is no thing** (notes/principles.md: a concept that does not fit a thing is a concept of its own, as a key is). The design notes' well-known *public thing* needed a record naming no admin, constant keysets, a symmetric box form, no sync ID, a cap on what may be granted it, an exemption from rotation, and "takes every grant": a thing restricted in what may be granted on it or written to it, which the principle forbids. As a holder that is no thing, most of that follows (nothing to sign, mint or deliver to). Left as rules of its own: the zero tid, the open box form, the public's claim, the clause in T's keys open. A may-grant holder grants the public as any holder, within the public's cap (engine `tests_public`).
- **Not rules**: the cap "at most read, no may-grant" as a validity rule (a writer's check: nothing acts through the public, and whoever could grant it more could hand out the secrets anyway, spec/requirements.md "a secret once shared"); the rotation exemption for departures at or below read (a rare rotation, one rule fewer); the symmetric box to a constant key (open boxes are smaller and need no constant keyset).
- **Rejected: a per-thing locator key** (a link key whose seed derives from the target's tid). Anyone would hold its signing key and could self-remove its grant (unpublishing the thing), write as it, or rotate it. The holder must be one nobody signs as.
- **Rejected: the public's read counting through holders** (public read on H, H read on X carries X): a reader of T cannot found it (H's grant on X sits in X's bodies, not under T's sync ID), so logger and readers disagree on the unit (model control `chains`). Access through H still works; carriage needs the public's grant on X's independent.
- **The inbox is A's own admin data, a field of A's `keys.0`**: where grants to A go is A's to choose, as A's setting (`retention^.0`) decides whether the inbox takes them at all, so it needs admin on A. A writer of A choosing it could point grant signals at a thing it controls: A's other members never see them, and after its removal it still learns who grants A what, and suppresses it. `keys.0` already rides in every card and public key record and is judged on A's authority chain by every taker, so the card carries the inbox with no field, ordering or validity rule of its own; writers still refresh cards (they copy admin-written commits). Refreshing cards without a list: a node of A rewrites a stale card it may write, read from what it holds (notes/principles.md, Close leaks: no list kept to know).
   - Rejected: the inbox in the card with a per-hop need (a card delta writing it needs admin up to A, write past it): a second shape of need for one aspect, through osis-perm's walk, cuts, re-grants, addenda and both models.
- **A card is A's own `keys.0` commits, not bare KEM keys**: Seal needs a held valid keyset (with its carried demotions), and the read key outsiders seal to must stay admin-vouched (spec/crypto.md §3 Read key in force). The card replaces the profile's carriage rule (a profile's sync ID carrying its mint admin's public key record): data on a thing, not a carriage special case.
- **Card validity by path membership**, not admin: a valid write through A needs write on its target along hops including A, so A's writers publish (they copy keys, choosing nothing). Grant signals still need the inbox's admins to enable `grant@~0` (spec/requirements.md Permissions: grants from anyone else only where its admins allow); a card without that gives a granter keys and no delivery.
- **KEM keys first needed**: rotation, one mechanism (no "add a KEM key to an epoch" write). The granter rotates the holder's KEM-less roles in the grant's transaction or refuses; that only saves the holder a stranded wait: liveness comes from KEM key due (a keyset written before its rotator learned of a grant, or as a card or signal ended, is otherwise never replaced: `KK_written`).

## The account library
- **The calls** (libs/account, osis-account; schema/realm.osis): `Account` (`make`, `of`, `thing`, `realm`, `nodes`, `remove_node`, `offer_pairing` with `Offering::accept`), `pair` with `Joining::finish`, `Realm` (`make`, `profile`, `contact_card`, `import_contact`, `profile_of`); notes/api.md Accounts and realms. The account is the thing the node keeps its settings on (`Osis::settings_thing`), a realm and the person's home (notes/realms.md); its profile is `realm:profile.0` on A, names A by `realm:of.0`, is public and carries A's card naming the inbox; a contact card is `Osis::locator` to the profile, made per call (nothing stored); imported profiles are in `realm:contacts.0` on the importer's account. the reply carries the account's locator (its peers: dial hints while redeemed); the pairing offer is good once while the `Offering` is kept (no expiry: the app's hold on it is the bound). Tests: libs/account `it` (making with a public profile; pairing by the offer's hints, failing without them, and removing; a stranger importing a contact card and granting).
- **The card on the profile** (not on a thing the profile references weakly): a card costs every unit carrying its host, and the only strongs to a profile are A's `holds.0` (A's unit, once) and each importer's (who needs the card to grant), so the profile is the right host; a separate card thing would add a thing and a second strong per import for no saving.
- **Core keeps**: `Osis::create` makes a node only; `node()` the node thing; `author()` what a handle writes as (the node thing, or a client's thing; there is no `me` in core); `node_root()` (a `Write` holds from it and names the settings thing there, `core::SETTINGS`); `Write::create_with_keys` (the account, inbox and profile, independent at their minting in the making write); `settings_thing()`; `publish_dial_on(&[Tid])` (node root `dial_on^.0`), which the library names A and the profile in; `redeem(link, joiner)`; `make_link(t, role, admin, ..)` (the library's callers pass the account); `put_card`/`remove_card` (each makes `of` and the inbox independent where dependent and this node an admin), `Write::set_inbox`; keys by hand (`keys`/`take_keys`). The library uses only these (no `osis::internals`). Engine: `Engine::mint_independent` (several things minted independent, one transaction: a client's thing); harness only (local_peer.rs): `make_account`, `Engine::pair`/`take_pairing`/`node_keys`/`remove_node`, the library's arrangement for engine and sync tests.
- **"me"**: the node acts as its node thing; "me" for a person is the account (`Account::thing`). CLI `me` is the account (a client with its own thing: that thing; a node acting for none: its node thing), so `create --admin me`, `grant T me`, `leave`'s default holder, `chat create`, `link redeem`'s joiner are the account; `whoami` prints both. Kit's `you()` is the account's profile (`Reader::account`); the app's chat marks a message mine when its author is this node or a node of the account.
- **Undo is per node** (decided by the model): undo and redo act on the handle's author's commits, and a node writes as its node thing, so each node undoes its own writes; another node's write is `revert`ed. Per-account undo would need the person's writes to name the account as purge author (`Write::act_as`) and undo to take that author: not built, no test asks for it.
- **The schema**: realms, the account as a realm and home, profile and contacts as a realm's: schema/realm.osis, notes/realms.md; kit and app keep the person's state on A (notes/app.md, State in osis).

## How it is built
- **The public** in perm, keys and boxes: `Tid::PUBLIC` (zero tid) is an ordinary `roles.0` holder to perm (no path can list it: no record, nothing holds a role on it). `box` case `open 3 (blob)` (osis-crypto `open_form_box`/`read_open_box`); osis-keys writes the open form exactly to the zero tid (`Recipient::public`), with no `sealed` record, and opens it only there (any other form/holder pairing ignored). Engine: `members::sealing_at` boxes the public whole (no keysets, blocks or left-out records), `ingest_view` opens its boxes for every node, `role_here` counts the public's role (secrets only: no path lists it), `grant_history` refuses above read or may-grant and skips the holder's own secrets and rotations. Tests: engine `rotation::tests_public` (open boxes at each rotation and opened with nothing held, removal rotates it out, the writer's check), osis-crypto `open_box_form`. Left: a box of the wrong form to the public still counts as present for the re-seal trigger (`reseal_missing` reads keys only).
- **Public in sync**: Engine: `Core::keys_open` counts `public_reads` (the public's own grant on the thing's independent, by the roster held; never through holders), and what a sync ID's bodies carry is what its keys open (`sync_ids::blobs` `actor_reads`). The public's role counts wherever its grant is held (`rotation::lost::role_here`, not gated by `grants_count`). Links: osis-blob `Link { Share, Public }`, `Locator` (format 2); `Engine::follow_public` (a `FollowedLinks` entry with no key), `Engine::redeem_public` (the strong once `public_reads` holds); osis-sync `Redemptions::redeem_public` (in memory: nothing to keep across a start), `Node::redeem(&Link, ..)`; `osis::ShareLink` parses either format. Tests (sync `public::`): a reader served only by a store keeping T unread reads P and a public thing's dependent and computes the logger's unit (fails without pieces: the dependent; without the clause: both); removing the public takes P out of the reader's unit and nothing written since is read (fails without the removal's piece); a stranger reads by a locator. Entry for a redeemed locator is by the joiner's unit (the strong), so First entry has no locator clause.
   - A public redeem is memory only (`Redemptions::public`): before its strong it wrote nothing, so a restart leaves nothing half done and the caller redeems again (notes/api.md).
- **KEM keys and the granter's checks**: `keyset.kem_key` and `read_key.kem_key` are `option` (a step's read key names its epoch beside a KEM-less read keyset, so a read rotation still makes a step due, spec/permissions.md §3; fires wait for a KEM key). Engine `rotation::kem`: `publishes` (minted with a key; holds the role or above for keys on some thing, record edges included, with key access; `Tx::publishing`, what the transaction gives: a grant's holder, the mint admin of a thing minted after it in `mint_independent`, a minted thing whose setting the transaction enables a signal in; the setting: read for any external signal), `bare` (what `cut_into` writes without), `due` (KEM key due in `rotation_due_now` at a pass's end), `grant_holder`/`grant_keys` (the granter's check in `grant_history`: write on H or a card, then H's roles up to the grant rotated in the grant's transaction where a current keyset lacks a KEM key and this node may, else `Misuse`). Seal's candidates (`members::sealing_at`) skip KEM-less keysets whose secret is not held. Tests: engine `rotation::tests_kem` (no KEM key on an independent thing nobody seals to; a grant rotates H's sync and read first; a writer of H refused an admin grant; a granter without write or card refused; `KK_written`'s lockout ended by KEM key due, failing without it). Harness: `LocalPeer::takes_signals` rotates what its setting makes due at once (`Engine::kem_key_due`); tests granting to another peer's plain thing card it (`takes_signals`) or give it a role first.
   - Left: `Seal` skipping KEM-less keysets is not separately tested (the box path's `keyset_kem` already leaves such a holder out; KemKeys `KK_fallback` is the model control); a removal's own rotation judges the holder's grants before its edge writes apply, so a removal that gives a grant key access (a record edge's) writes a KEM-less keyset and KEM key due follows at the next pass. A thing others grant to from outside (a client's actor, a group, an organization) needs a card or a role of its own first.
- **Cards and signals on any thing**: Schema: type `card` (`{ keys, records }`), the value of the card aspect `:<A>:card.0` (`core_types::card_ak`, `card_of`); `keys.0` field `inbox 11`. Validity: a card aspect part writing a card of a thing off its stated members is invalid (`validity::note_cards`, kept in `Indexes::overreach`, which also holds a may-grant grant's box overreach; card parts decode whole); the inbox field needs admin (`validity::rotation_required`: every field but the writer's keysets and box records); its snapshots act as A (`snapshot::snapshot_actor`); a card is replaced whole (`cards::put`). Engine `flows::cards`: the cards of A are found by its card aspect's holders (`aspect_things`), the things naming T their inbox by the reference index (`keys.0` rows naming T, `naming_inbox`); `inbox_in` (the thing the field in A's `keys.0` state names, never A, where A is independent), `stands`; `put`/`put_inbox`, `Engine::write_card`, `Engine::set_inbox` (refused without admin on A; rewrites at once the cards of A this node may write), `take_cards` (in `take_carried`: a card's commits taken as A's holder keys, then as its inbox's, the inbox read from what was just taken), `refresh_cards` (at `settle_needs`, a pass's end: rewrite where this node may write the host along a path through A and the card is stale, `stale`: a current `keys.0` commit here on A or the inbox the card lacks, newer than every signature-verified card commit on that thing this node does not hold valid; a verified one held valid is superseded field by field in the state already, an unverified one counts for nothing; the current commits are what `card_keys` writes, so a rewrite settles). `take_holder_keys` skips a carried commit that is bad input (a bad signature), taking the rest. Due sets `cards_due`/`cards_stale` in memory, every card at open. Retention: a card on a thing the node closure holds needs its thing and the inbox its thing's `keys.0` names (`Need::Card`; a `keys.0` reference row going notes the loss). KEM keys: a card standing publishes every role; a setting publishes read only. Stranded notices already go on the stranded sync ID's thing. Account making (library and `local_peer::make_account`) names the inbox in A's `keys.0`, writes A's card on the profile and fills it. Harness: `LocalPeer::takes_signals` enables stranded notices and writes the thing's card on itself (it names no inbox); `accounts`/`fresh_reader` give each peer the others' profiles (by contact cards); `rotation::tests::target_thing` is a target taking stranded notices and no grant from outside (a card's evidence would bring a node role commits a test withholds). Tests: engine `cards` (off-path card invalid and refused writer-side; an admin's move spreads to every card; a writer's move refused writer-side and, forged, invalid everywhere; a writer's refresh after a move keeps the admin's inbox; a writer's card omitting the move, or carrying an unverified far-ahead copy, is rewritten; a stranger holding only a card grants and fires at the admin-chosen inbox).
   - **Decided (agent's call)**: the granter's check reads "holds H's keys", not "holds a card of H": a fellow holder already holds H's public key record (carried so every admin can seal to and list H), and refusing it would stop an admin of T raising a member's role on T without that member's card (a capability removed). spec/permissions.md §2, §4 and spec/sync-inbox.md §5 say so.
   - Left: a stranger's sealed fire reaching an inbox kept unread and promoted is not tested on its own here; the model `closure::cards` is the oracle the engine tests stand for, no differential.
- **The `osis` API and CLI**: `osis` API (notes/api.md Sharing and roles): the public is the holder `Tid::PUBLIC` to `grant`/`set_role` (`HolderKind::Public`; CLI alias `public`); `Link` (either format; `ShareLink` gone), `locator(t)` (port row 23; CLI `link public`); `put_card(on, of)`, `remove_card(on, of)` (`Engine::write_card`, `remove_card`), `Write::set_inbox(of, inbox)` (CLI `card put|rm|inbox`); enabling external signals stays the setting's. `Engine::entitled` (`Core::may_read`) counts the public's grant on the thing's independent, so a client acting as an actor reads public things (spec/sync-judgement.md §1). Library: The account library, above; CLI `contact show|import|profile` (the word card is core's). Tests: osis `sync::the_publics_grant_round_trip_*` (a client's thing reads by the public's grant, failing without `entitled`'s clause; a stranger reads by a locator; read at most; removal), `sync::put_card_*`; libs/account `it` (above).
   - Decided by the model: link commands on a lone node run to their timeout (notes/cli.md Delivering commands): whoever holds a link may take its thing, and no other node holds it until one does.
   - Seen: a card's keys are mostly redundant with the fellow-holder rule (a thing writing the host through A holds a role on it, so A's public key record is under the host's sync ID already); the card's own work is reaching strangers (the inbox's keys with A's) and publishing KEM keys.
- **Models and differentials, counting**: Harness shape as the library's: `local_peer::make_account` grants the profile the public's read; a contact card is a locator of it, redeemed for the importer's account (`Peer::import_contact` in `LocalPeer::accounts`/`fresh_reader`; the sim's setup follows, syncs, redeems and unfollows; sync `introduce`). Engine: a node rewrites stale cards also with no pass in flight (`settle_lost` runs `refresh_cards`): its own rotation ends no pass, so a contact never got the account's new inbox keys (sync `keep::a_grant_from_outside_reaches_an_account_that_removed_a_node`). Differential: `Give` to `Holder::Public`, `Redeem`, `Card`, `Inbox` (moves to a thing of the account's, back), the model's public from the rosters, `card_clauses` (each node takes every inbox as its reference); hand tests `differential::a_public_thing_redeemed_is_read_*`. Counting: engine `thing_cost::a_commit_on_a_public_thing_costs_an_entry_per_referring_unit`, `an_independent_thing_nobody_seals_to_carries_no_kem_key` (notes/thing-cost.md).

## Models
- **held-model `closure::entry`**: public things carried by stubs (the public's open boxes in every cut), notes/stubs.md (Models).
- **TLA `KemKeys.tla`** (core/test/tla/rotation/README.md; `run.sh kemkeys`): KEM-less keysets in Seal, the granter's check, the public's or a party's removal from a holder actor, stale cards.
  - Finding, fixed in spec/crypto.md §3 (KEM key due): KEM keys decided only when written leave a holder out for good (`KK_written` fails `Live`): g grants M on A sealing at k1 (a card of M stood); the card goes; M's admin rotates (k2, no KEM key) before M's grant reaches it; g finds k1 stale and k2 no candidate, leaves M out, and nothing ever rotates M again. A no-candidate leave-out is no block, so no left-out record names it and Re-key never fires. With KEM key due M's rotator re-keys once it learns the grant (`KK_due`); that alone suffices without the granter's check (`KK_due_nocheck`).
  - Control `KK_fallback`: Seal falling back below a KEM-less newest keyset to an older one with a KEM key seals at a keyset the removed party (or the public) holds (`NoLeak`): the stale box rule must leave M out.
  - The public as a removed party: its open box at M's old keyset makes every party x; nothing else differs (the public is no holder actor, never left out or sealed to), so the ordinary departure, rotation and stale-box rules bound what it reads as for any party. Granting the public read on a holder actor gives every party what that holder reads, by keys (not carried: `chains`).
  - Stale card: the granter seals to the card's keyset until M's newer keyset reaches it (`KK_stale` passes `NoLeak`; `KK_stale_all` fails `NoLeakAll`: the stated limit, Risks).
- **held-model `closure::cards`** (tests/it/spec_cards.rs): A's cards on 2-4 hosts, 2-3 nodes of A writing some hosts along a path through A and others only along other paths, A's `keys.0` written (a rotation or an inbox move, one step: the inbox is a field of it), rounds of passes; 20,000 random worlds. `Newest` (the spec) holds `converge` (every host some node may write through A ends with A's newest keys), `valid` and `quiet`.
  - Finding, fixed in spec/sync-inbox.md §1: the rewriter is a node that may write C along a path through A; one that may write C otherwise writes an invalid card (spec/permissions.md §5.2), and keeps doing so (`AnyWriter` fails `valid` and `quiet`).
  - Only A's writers (a path through A needs write at every hop) write A's card, carrying A's admin-written `keys.0`; they read A anyway. A writer may carry older keys or remove a card (loud: A stops being externally grantable there), never choose the inbox. A card on a thing no node of A may write any longer stays as written.

## Who hears the public's grant changes
The public's entry is in every boxing commit, so a party reading by it learns the public's removal from the removal's own rotation, and is served up to the end of the epoch it held (spec/sync-hostile.md, spec/sync-opens.md §6).
- **Rejected: delivering the public's grant to each sync holder** (a fan-out to the holders' inboxes): a fan-out per holder at every public grant change, where the commits every store already holds carry the public's entry.
- Not covered: a node that released X on hearing the removal never hears a later re-grant (nothing of X is held: redeem again).

## Redeeming
- **The grant's commit under an older epoch**: a commit giving read epochs is wrapped under an epoch they supersede (spec/crypto.md §5), so once X rotated past the public's grant the redeemer, holding only the head's epoch and the box's `earlier`, opens that commit by roles secrets alone. It takes what the history entries of the epochs it holds list, earlier epochs and roles secrets, whatever its roles (`ingest_view`, one rule with the store's below: a node holding no secret of that role takes none, so a loss keeps none; opened from the secrets held before the ingest alone, so a box's secret the role does not keep, as one concurrent with the node's removal, gives nothing: engine `apply::keys::tests::a_removed_node_takes_no_roles_secret_from_a_concurrent_box_*`). Without it no redeemer of a thing rotated since its public grant ever held the grant (osis-sync `public::a_stranger_redeems_a_locator_after_a_rotation_*`).
- **A dependent target** (D of public X): the link names X (spec/wire.md §10 `independent`), the redeemer follows X's sync ID, writes the strong to D, and Dependent entry (spec/sync-set.md §2) then keeps X's unit read, so D's later writes and the public's removal reach it with no bridge. Built: `units::given` gives `(Read(X), X)` from the edge to D; `Closure::by` counts the edges giving one such entry (several dependents, or X's own strong beside them), so it goes with the last. Where X's unit does not hold D, nothing carries D (Carriers).

## Costs (measured in notes/thing-cost.md)
- **An independent thing nobody seals to** (a shared note, chat, document): no KEM keys (4 × 1216 B in its keysets, 1216 B in its step keys' read key): measured (notes/thing-cost.md) 9.6 KB stored at the maker against 16.0 KB, its commits 4208 B against 10.3 KB, no ML-KEM keygens against 5. A holder (an account, a group, a node thing) is unchanged.
- **A public thing**: its boxes to the public open (about 70 B a box against about 1.2 KB sealed); per referring unit one entry per commit on it under each referrer's sync ID: measured exactly one per referrer at n and 10n, the commit's bytes flat (notes/thing-cost.md; the built unit's cost, accepted).
- **A card**: A's and its inbox's `keys.0` commits with evidence: measured 18.0 KB of value, its rewrite commit 10.8 KB, carried whole by every unit carrying the thing it is on, one entry per referrer. On the profile that is every contact's account; the library could put the card on a public thing only would-be granters hold (one the profile references weakly, a contact import strongs).

## Risks
- A strong to any public thing pulls it whole into the referrer's unit: a public thing referenced widely is copied under every referrer's sync ID (accepted cost of the built unit; counted).
- Stores learn a unit is public from the public's claim at its head (accepted, spec/sync-hostile.md).
- A stale card seals grants to A's older keysets, which a party removed from A since may open until A's newer keyset reaches the granter (A's members logging it under the target once the signal reaches them); a card on a thing A may no longer write is never rewritten.
