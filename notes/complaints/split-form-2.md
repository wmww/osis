# split-form-2
- A python resolve script failed its assertion and the same shell call went on to `git add` and `rebase --continue`: conflict markers landed in a commit (fixed in the next one). Keep a conflict resolve and `rebase --continue` in separate calls.
- `osis_commit::rewrite` keeps every path key, so `LocalPeer`'s opened packages give every reader all keys of a split commit: an engine test through `sync_all` cannot tell a key carried by the thing's own encryption from another's. Hostile and copy cases need hand-built envelopes (`validity::opened_sections`).
- `spread_sealed` in the `net` world did not deliver a granted thing to its reader (works in `flows::people`); unexplained, worked around with `sync_all`.
