# sync-tests-cleanup-4
> Summary: holding's cost is many distinct worlds' own runs, not shared setups; a module count charges whole fixtures the gate shares; partitioned runs were not the raced-grant test's cost.

- holding:: (about 200 G) is 57 tests whose marginal cost is their own runs after distinct grants: the only exact shared prefixes were siblings plus one or two writes and a run (`bobs_thing`, the forked-sync-ID start), worth about 2 G together. Most of a siblings test is product settle after its own heal or pairing; there is no cache lever left there, only fewer worlds.
- A per-module `icount` charges each fixture the module builds first (world_of(0, 4, 4) is 27 G of flows' 76 G, world_of(0, 3, 3) 11 G of holding's), though the gate's one process builds each once for every module: a module total overstates what moving or sharing a fixture saves. Judge a cache by the marginal it removes, not by the module delta.
- Two partitioned `Run`s in flows' raced-grant test (an hour of redials each) cost 0.6 G of its 40 G: the redial horizon is cheap; don't hunt it.
- Merging two tests whose worlds differ (a read test and a purge test on one world) saved 0.5 G: a big write plus a run is cheap next to a fixture; merges pay mostly in lines.
