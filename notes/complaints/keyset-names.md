# keyset-names
- TLC's `MC_names_own` sized like `MC_fork_reorder` took 1M states (57 s on 4 workers) to fail; withholding the removal from a2 and no write cut it to 14 s on 1 worker. A negative control is cheapest built on the withholding instance, not the any-order one.
- `cargo build --workspace --tests` after an osis-keys change took over 120 s and was moved to the background mid-call; start such builds in the background from the outset.
- The any-order (`InOrder = FALSE`) withholding instance never finished (5M+ distinct states, growing); an hour went to waiting before switching it to log order with the host's log skipping the removal (2.9M states, 19 min at load 50). `MC_grant_remove` alone took 60 min at that load: `run.sh all rotation` is a periodic-sized job, not a per-branch one.
