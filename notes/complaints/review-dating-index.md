# review-dating-index
> Summary: Workflow notes from reviewing the held-writes amend and leaving-body dating.

- A plant run's heavy hold waited behind another gate for most of its ten minutes; two batches of three plants cost about 20 minutes of wall time for under 5 of tests.
- Plants on the leaving path pass every default test and every retention `_plain` property: a valid covered drop moves nothing by design, so only a drop before its bound (`Engine::drop_body`) can show a missed leave. Brief plants for "follows removal" against a test that drops early, not against the properties.
