# a1-header
> Summary: Workflow notes from A1 step 1 (the header's signer and path).

- A header field rename touches every crate: compile-error-driven rewriting (cargo `--message-format=json`, rename at the reported column only where the type is the header, view or role commit) did hundreds of sites safely; blind `sed` of `author`/`actor` would have hit the purge-author class, holder actors and the `osis` API.
- `scale::expiry_is_flat` compares redb store bytes written at n and 10n with a 2x bound; the bytes move by whole pages, so a change in record sizes elsewhere flips it (baseline 28 313 / 49 993, now 23 461 / 51 281).
- A baseline needs a detached checkout of the base in the worktree and an engine rebuild each way (about 4 minutes): there is no cheaper way to read a test's numbers before a change.
