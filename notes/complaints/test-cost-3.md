# test-cost-3
- Outside rotation and engine `it` the costliest default tests are mostly product cost, not test design: osis-sync's per-delivery settles (`Read::settle`, `judge::body`) inside the cached worlds (`world(0, 4)` is about 27 G and eight tests build it per process; `world(0, 3)` about 12 G), osis's kept-history writes (issues/kept-history-write-cost.md), redb serializing its allocator bitmaps at every open and close (43% of osis-cli's `cli::*`). A test-speed job outside those finds only oracle-side waste (two found: perm's per-node role walks, held-model's repeated `paths::chained`).
- `tools/cpuprof` with `CPUPROF_INSTR` on a copied binary is the fastest way to tell product from oracle cost: a filter on the test's oracle function (`check`, `seen`) splits it in one report.
