- roles-model `epochs`: an outsider's default side ignored whether the party's chain was held, so a pending fork (parent not yet arrived) could become the side and void the owner's merge; the changed generator reached it once in 1M. `osis_perm::chain::Chain::new` has the same shape (judges whatever it is given): its step-6 caller must filter.
