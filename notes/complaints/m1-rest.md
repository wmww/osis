# m1-rest
- The model's evidence closure is complete by construction (`held_at` closes over every commit made), so a thief withholding a removal from one writer cannot be set up by delivery alone: the cover move removes the removal from the writer's view by hand. A story knob for partial sources would make availability moves cheaper to write.
- A `-- N paths` search shrinks one world per failing or counted property on one core; with five or more noted counters that is most of a short run's time.
- The full-size searches waited on the heavy lock behind another all-core run; queue them as one `tools/heavy bash -c` so a single hold covers the random, family, tiny and control runs.
