# g-outside
- `wt claim g-outside origin/main` left the branch 2 commits behind origin/main (it branched from an older ref); a `git merge --ff-only origin/main` fixed it. Check `git status` right after a claim.
- The brief sized this as one session; the engine change is small, but about 100 engine tests deliver content to peers that do not hold the thing (strangers, observers given every pack) and relied on it applying because they never end a pass. Each needs its own look (hold it, or assert on the held thing only), which is most of the work.
- (g-outside-2) The engine harness hands commits whole (`LocalPeer::packages`, `Engine::package`): a minting commit's part on the maker's account root D, and D's own content, reach nodes of other accounts, where under the rule they wait forever (a LocalPeer carrier is never let go). About half the red tests are this, not the rule. A receive-side strip decided by the receiver's records is arrival-order dependent (24 order:: tests broke); the strip has to be decided at the sender (a receiver-aware `packages_for(from, to)`).
- (g-outside-2) A full `cargo test -p osis-engine` run takes 2-5 min wall on a shared box; each harness experiment costs one, so budget harness changes before per-test edits.
