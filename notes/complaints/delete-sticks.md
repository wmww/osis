- `wt claim` gave a cold worktree (wt8): about 6 minutes before the first crdt test ran. Expected per the brief, noting the cost.
- The "creation lives only with content" normalization was documented in three places (spec §5.6, notes, two prune functions) and silently defeated the first attempt at this rule; `copies_agree_and_rejoin_spec_perm_5_2` caught it only because its seed happened to raise a map reset while a creation-only record sat in the stripped state. Worth a targeted law test (creation survives a prune on its own) rather than relying on the random one.
- The box ran at load 70-100 for most of the task: a node test binary rebuild took 3-5 min each time, and a `--nocapture` run piped through `sort` sat 330 s for a 2 s test. Probe-print-rebuild loops are expensive here; a `tools/bin-at`-style cached binary per probe round would not help since the probes change the binary.
- A timer key collision (expiry and keep-at-most timers at one instant sharing `(t, commit, ai)`) hid behind issue expired-entry-key-kept (closed) as a CRDT question; the state probe found it in two rounds (notes/crdt.md).
