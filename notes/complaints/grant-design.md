# grant-design
> Summary: mishaps while redesigning grant routes (labels only by `sync`, no-route status, chains in inbox blobs).
- `tied::grant_and_removal_orders_large` seed 15 under the SETTLE hold was a test-clock artifact: the admin reconnects, receives the removal and schedules the copy 30 s out, and the test asserts without advancing again. Read the debug trace's `now` before hunting a liveness bug: a hold that never fires in a test is usually a clock that never moved.
- Many tests shared a thing by `grant` on a plain thing and relied on the label the grant used to make; with labels only by `sync` each needed `share`. A grep for `.grant(` on cards and plain things found them faster than the failures did.
- `w.unplace`/`w.place` and `Write::unplace` exist; `alice.status().groups` lists only labeled groups, so a no-route entry needed a key of its own (the thing's tid) rather than a flag on a group row.
- Vectors: a payload format change regenerates `inbox/inbox.vec` (nothing is frozen before a release).
- Carrying the granter's chain in every inbox blob broke four sims (`snapshot_rights`, `purge_tombstones`, `ephemeral`, `coverer_revoked` at seed 1): a grantee that could apply at once acted under a label before its admin re-registered it. Bisected in minutes with an env toggle around the one line (`OSIS_NO_CHAIN`) and two `cargo test -p osis-sim` runs; a trace of `ingest` results per node found the missing card. issues/holder-ahead-of-the-head.md.
- Fix-disabled probes: a sed whose pattern does not match silently leaves the rule on and the test green; diff the file against a copy before trusting a "still passes". Two rules (the label.0 blob's copy and the per-item copy skip) each cover the other in the ordinary test, so each needed a test the other cannot pass (a sync 90 days after the grant; a co-admin's sync).
- The SETTLE hold never applied to grant copies (it is for frontier and `keys.0`-only commits); the one it held was an outsider's removal. Read the `due` filter's second clause before reasoning about which copies wait.
