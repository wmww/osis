# evil-host
- A minimal node test took long to find: the node's immediate fresh re-upload on a host Deleted hides the path; the case needs a removed blob with a live part a snapshot covers (bytes kept, other part re-placed). Probing `would_promote` in the failing sim seed (print the entries of the commit) gave the shape at once.
- `cargo test -q` hides eprintln of passing tests; probes need `-- --nocapture`.
- Backtraces from release-ish inlined frames named only the leaf (`with_work<...>`); `eprintln` at each caller was faster.
