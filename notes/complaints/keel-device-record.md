# keel-device-record
- The custody harness's setup syncs every card to every peer, standing for what retention once rooted for good (every key actor); once only needs kept them, the harness's early delivery hid that no rule fetched a dropped card again. A harness's delivery should stand for what sync carries, or it tests the old rule.
- LocalPeer has no store, so an engine's pull anew (`Cipher::refetch`) was invisible below osis-sync; `Wakes::pulled_anew` now exposes it to test peers.
- The custody replay of one seed takes 17-30 s; a 200-seed small run 150 s on 16 threads.
