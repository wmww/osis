# tla-trim
- `pgrep -f`/`pkill -f` with a pattern that also appears in the agent's own command line kills the agent's shell (exit 144). Kill by PID from a script whose text the command line does not contain.
- The last periodic TLC log's per-instance times were wall clock at load 30-100, so they overstate cost several times; judge TLC cost on a quiet box or by CPU-s.
- Review: a negative control that differs from its pass instance in more than the one switch cannot be checked by flipping the switch (`L_none` flipped failed `Unlocked` at the bound; `C_drop_any` with a third writer ran past 10 min). Now each is its pass instance plus the switch.
