# Deposits, the inbox and deliveries
> Summary: Why what must reach an actor from outside goes as a boxed inbox blob, and how the deposit path works and is bounded: deposits and their budget, invite requests (Tess: leaking an IP takes a click), clearing deposits under any label, stamping handled blobs, the accepted fill and push-out limits; node sync's inbox life (grant copies, hints, a label's thing per connection, re-sends), the outbox, Deposit items between readers and crash-safe deliveries, deposit pacing, relay wait and promotion, placement order. Rules: spec/sync.md §3, §5 (Deposit, Clearing deposits). Neighbours: notes/sync.md (index), notes/label-access.md (re-seal, claims), notes/permissions.md (grants, certificates).

## Why an inbox
- **Inbox blobs**: a non-holder cannot wrap anything under a label it does not read (a granter is not a reader of the grantee's home label), so everything that must reach an actor from outside goes boxed to its read KEM key: grants, grant changes and removals (so it erases), re-seals, each with the label IDs and hosts it needs. Not the target's `label.0` commit: a non-holder cannot wrap it, and it joins many commits anyway. Each carries a delivery expiry so an unconsumed one frees the budget by itself, and the inbox is a label the recipient's admins administer so they can delete consumed ones. Direct grants to a node go by pairing only: a node is listed by DH key and cannot enter a label cold.
- Rejected: plain (unboxed) grants under a shared label: every reader of that label would read them, against entitlement.
- **A reader's deposits** (its invite grant, self-removal, node card) sit deposited until a writer promotes them; co-readers read them meanwhile.
- **A reader's copy under a superseded stamp** (any author's, ephemeral or durable) is deposited where a host lacks it (`pass::send`: relayed only when the head's write key verifies its stamp): after a write-key rotation, with the host's copy gone (an ephemeral entry at every host restart; a durable one on a new or wiped host) and every writer lacking it, it is the only way back. A promoting writer judges it by its author's rights. A reader deposits a body only while it judges it valid: a demoted writer's own writes refused under the rotated key are invalid at it too (kept for its user, spec/permissions.md §6); deposited, no writer would promote them and they would sit in the budget until an admin clears them (`sync_review::demoted_writers_refused_writes_take_no_deposit_budget_spec_sync_5`). It costs no new room: the one budget per label holds durable and ephemeral deposits alike, an ephemeral one also within the label's ephemeral cap, and any deposit-key holder could fill that budget already. Rejected: hosts taking old stamps on relays (a removed writer keeps the old write key and could fill the label's room).
- **The budget is host policy**, not a format constant: clients never compute it, they see refusals and the remaining budget in the reply. The recommended minimum (64 + 4 per padded claim slot, 32 KiB each) is sized per reader *actor*, while deposits come per *node*: a plain group under a team has a claim set of one, and thirty reader nodes' cards can approach it. Hence the client order (certificates, edge writes and revokes first); hosts with big read-only audiences raise the budget (`deposit_factor`). Deposits are unlinkable by design, so per-reader limits are impossible.
- Reasons behind spec rules: re-send ignores a recipient acting elsewhere because the blob may have been cleared (§5). A grant copy leaves the chain's `keys.0` out because a verifier judges by creation records and edge writes, and a fresh deposit seed is not for that actor's readers; the copy is what lets the actor's other and future nodes and its readers find the grant. Inbox blob order (§3): a label told unknown may be on its way. A client keeps sending its own deposit after losing read access because other nodes may hold parts of that commit from elsewhere and wait for the rest (§5). Relay-only deposits are refused: held by nobody, a reader's would be a write of its own, and readers write no ephemeral data.

## Invite requests (spec/sync.md §3; Tess: leaking an IP takes a user click)
- `open_one` admits an inbox blob (`admits`) when its commit is held or its author is connected; else it records `Kind::Request(c)` and ingests nothing (`note_delete_hints` waits too: a stranger could evict real candidates).
- Connected: walk up from the author over `Held::named_on` (edge writes held, past and invalid ones too: a cheap candidate filter bounded by what the author is named on) to the roots (synced groups' record chains and `contacts`); the author's current role (`Core::role_now`) on a root met decides (an ex-holder, a removed stolen node, is a stranger again: `removed_holder_invite_is_request_spec_sync_3`); then the commit's signature is verified under the author's held record (a stranger can name any author tid).
- `vet_inbox_hints` re-judges requests first (`release_requests`: the sender became connected, or a sibling node accepted and its grant copy under the home label made the commit held) and ingests the admitted. `accept_invite` releases by commit; `decline_invite` turns the entries into `Kind::Inbox(Some(c))` (never ingested) and records `c` in `declined` (`y/m`, 32 B per click) so re-sends of the commit are held back too.
- A decline binds inbox blobs only: the commit arriving under a synced label (a sibling's acceptance copied under home) applies, so the sibling's acceptance wins (`sibling_acceptance_wins_over_decline_*`); refusing it there would leave this node holding, under its home label, a valid grant it never applies while its siblings join. Judged per node: contacts are per node, and an accept on one node reaches the others only through the grant's copy (which waits for the joined label to apply).
- What an attacker can still do:
  - Fill: requests are inbox entries, bounded by `DEPOSITS_WAITING` per host (clearing, below) and gone with their blobs. A declined blob is stamped out of the budget; an unanswered one holds a slot until it expires or is cleared.
  - Spoof: a request never comes from a node known as connected (no forged signature passes); the API shows nothing about its sender or thing (the header and payload are the sender's word; a known name there would be a spoof or a node not seen yet: Attribution, notes/api.md). An app can show only "someone invites you" (left: a payload field pointing at the sender's card, verified once fetched).
  - Vouching: any holder of a role on a group this node syncs who may grant there can make any node connected by a valid grant. Accepted: they share a group already.
  - Hint hosts are unsigned: whoever holds a connected sender's genuine grant to the actor (a reader of the target's label) can re-deposit it naming its own hosts, and the node dials them. Accepted: that reader holds a role on the target.

## Clearing and stamping (`pass/promote.rs` `clear_deposits`, `pass/inbox.rs`)
- **One clearing rule, every label**, for all a stranger (inbox) or a reader (any label: it holds the deposit key) can put in the budget (junk that opens to nothing, requests, re-seals, commits it sealed and signed itself that open but are invalid, pending or parked commits, record blobs, far or no expiry): an admin node leaves at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` (32) and 1 MiB of them per host and deletes the rest at each pass. A deposited commit stays out of the count only while the admin judges it valid (`Engine::judged_valid`, the judgment a reader deposits by), so a writer promotes it. No "this is junk" verdict: a blob sealed to a key this node lacks yet looks like junk and stays while under the cap, and what stays blocks nothing, half the budget being free.
  - Counted by size (`Entry::size`, recorded with the salt when bytes arrive, `keep_salts`): the admin drops the bytes of what it judged invalid and, outside an inbox, of what opens under none of its secrets. A deposit whose bytes never arrived is not counted yet.
  - Cost of judging: under a validity fork (permanent), an admin may clear what a writer on the other side would promote, only past the cap; an honest deposit pending at the admin past the cap goes the same way. Not a loss for an own role commit: its author's next pass finds no entry of it there and deposits a fresh encryption (`entry_for` skips gone entries), so past the cap it cycles until judged. A reader's copy of another's body is not placed again by that reader (`may_deposit`: own role commits only).
  - Rejected: **host-side eviction** (a full budget takes a deposit by dropping the oldest unpromoted one): judgment-free, but a taken deposit is answered, and an inbox blob is not sent again for `INBOX_LIFETIME`, so any holder of the actor's `keys.0` (or honest traffic past the budget while the actor's admins are away) erases pending grants instead of delaying them; readers' re-deposits of evicted entries also evict each other at every pass. **Age-out** of unstamped entries: at 0 nothing waits for a writer, at infinity the hole stays.
  - Order: requests with an honest expiry last, earliest to expire first (junk that opens to nothing never displaces one, so requests are lost only past 32 of them); before them everything else, far or no expiry first, then earliest. Requests displaced only by junk would not do: anyone with `keys.0` can seal requests that open, and 72 of them would hold the budget for `INBOX_LIFETIME` again. Keeping the oldest instead lets one fill refuse every later invite. A host rule on deposit expiry was rejected: certificate copies and other non-inbox deposits carry no expiry, and hosts cannot tell them apart.
  - Inbox blobs whose commit is pending are exempt, so one who holds a commit pending at the actor, or a connected sender with commits that stay pending, can hold slots until they expire (at most `INBOX_LIFETIME` plus drift).
- **Stamping** (`stamp_inbox`, after the same relay wait as a promotion): a handled blob leaves the deposit budget by a stamp in place, from any writer that holds the commit applied and valid or whose user declined it (an account otherwise took 72 grants per 90 days). A stamp goes only on the first blob of a commit under the label, of a valid commit about the actor with parts a copy would take or one declined, expiring within `INBOX_LIFETIME + 2 × MAX_DRIFT`: a stranger can seal any commit he holds in any number of blobs, so without both rules his re-seals of a held or once-declined commit, or of the actor's own card, would be stamped past the budget (`reseals_stay_in_the_budget_spec_sync_3`). A hostless served label has no entry budget (`serve.rs`: bytes only), so `local_promote` does not stamp there.
- **Accepted limit (Tess):** consumed inbox blobs are not deleted early (a MAY in spec/sync.md §3): later holders (a link's later joiners, an account's later nodes) open the named thing's label by the blob's hints while the copied `label.0` still waits for the granter's chain (deleting them failed every `link_redeem` sweep seed: a joiner never found the target's label), and an inbox at another group's label gets no copy at all. They expire after `INBOX_LIFETIME`; the cost is sealed storage at the host for 90 days per grant.

## What a stranger with `keys.0` can still do (accepted; spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read)
- Fill the budget while no admin node of the actor is online: grants wait at their senders (retried at the capped back-off) until one comes back, one pass later. A group's inbox is its home label, and waits for its admins alike. A group's readers can fill any of its labels the same way: readers' deposits (cards, self-removals, readers' copies) wait until an admin node passes (`inbox_retry::a_readers_invalid_fill_ends_with_an_admin_pass_spec_sync_5`).
- Flood without pause: up to the host's deposit rate (default 600 a minute; deletes 20 000) slots go as they free, so an honest deposit races him, and each junk blob costs the node its bytes (up to about 320 KiB/s at the default rate with 32 KiB blobs) until he stops. Tombstones: at that rate about 26 million in `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, about 2.6 GB of the label's 16 GiB; the log trims (2^20 records, about 15 hours), so offline nodes reconcile.
- Push out unanswered requests with requests of his own (they open): more than 32 displace the earliest. No flood needed: 40 real invites from unconnected people while the user is away lose the first 8 at her next sync, before she sees them, and their senders are not told; each re-sends after `INBOX_LIFETIME`. Any constant has this shape (the budget itself holds 68-72); a larger one is Tess's call. A node that does not know a connected sender yet (fresh, or behind) sees its grant as a request too, so past 32 it can cost that blob at this host, though a sibling that opened it holds the commit.
- Past the cap, flood or not, what goes may be real: a reader's deposited linked blob (filler wrap, no expiry; only a very large chain reaches 32 entries or 1 MiB) or record blob (a promoter lacking it places its own), a commit still pending here, or a blob sealed to a key it holds only later (a team's, whose secrets come from another label). A node holding the head's read epoch holds its history, so readers' valid commits (cards, certificate copies) open and never count.
- A decline frees a slot; a new deposit seed needs a new `keys.0` and chain step.
- Any sender holding a role on a group the actor syncs is connected, and its fresh grants of its own things to the actor are valid, so they pass the budget (stamped), bounded only by the host's deposit rate, label caps (defaults 600 a minute, 2^20 entries, 16 GiB) and expiry: about 14 hours to fill the home label at a host, after which that label takes no uploads there (code 12) for up to 90 days, with no node path to delete, its blobs on each node too and each grant's copy under home for good (unstamped they would fill only the budget: 72 entries, about 2.3 MiB). Removing the sender stops new ones only.

## Inbox life in node sync
- An opened inbox entry records its commit (`Kind::Inbox(Some(c))`). Inbox blobs not yet answered are sync state (`y/o`); direct commits for the app (`take_direct`, `y/j`) too. A stranded notice (spec/wire.md §9) opens to no commit: it is recorded `Kind::Inbox(None)` and asks for a re-seal (notes/label-access.md).
- **Grant copies** (`pass/inbox.rs` `inbox_certificates`, part of `certificates`; an actor's inbox is its home label). Once a blob's commit applied and is valid, a node with admin on the actor (its readers would flood a group's own label) places a certificate copy with the author's chain, of:
  - the carried parts of a role commit on the things where it writes a grant of the actor or boxes it (`Engine::holders_named`; a rotation's `keys.0` there goes along; anything else a stranger with the deposit seed sends is applied but never stamped), plus the newest valid `created.0` and every valid `label.0` commit of those things that the payload names, no `label.0` of a thing the actor reads no more. Every `label.0` commit, not the newest alone: a later `label.0` is a delta on the record an earlier one set, so the newest alone left the group's readers no label (`tied::grant_to_group_of_a_thing_rotated_before`); the payload's list is the sender's word.
  - The chain (`chain_under`) holds no `keys.0` but the author's own card: a verifier needs creation records and edge writes only (`apply::examine`, `validity`); a keyset is often a removal's fresh deposit seed, an admin that had the commit from the inbox may not hold it, and it moves at every rotation on the way (`tied::group_admin_copies_removal_without_new_keys`, `tied::grant_lost_and_regained_with_the_admin_away`, `tied::a_copy_survives_its_granter_rotating`, oracle `tied::grant_and_removal_orders_large`).
  - Nothing from a blob expiring less than `INBOX_COPY_MIN_LEFT` (`INBOX_LIFETIME` / 2) from now: a short sender expiry would free the deposit budget for the next copy at once. Not when entries there already hold the commit's parts (counted as for chain certificates, `held_parts`); the commit then counts as done. Every node holding a role on a group actor reads its inbox, and each re-placed K's grant on C with C's `label.0` and `created.0` (2.8 KB per chat join), and later C's new `label.0` after each rotation.
- **The walk**: a home-label pass walks the unexpired blobs not known copied (`InboxCopied`), a full pass all of them. What counts as held moves with which hosts the label's entries must be present at (`counted_at`: the connections carrying it) and the newest read epoch, so the cache is kept per label under those (`InboxAt`) and dropped when they differ; things in the answer moving, an entry going or changing kind or wrap (judged bad, shredded), a host's copy going, and a full pass drop it too. A full pass's `certificates` releases 80/640 blobs in 0.6/8.7 ms (7-13 us a blob, flat in things held: `Memo::roles_now`), about 100 us a blob more the first time after a role write dropped `holders_named` (issues/inbox-full-walk-scale.md).
- **An inbox chain only for a thing without a label** (`local.rs` `send_inbox`, `inbox_chain` from `chain_under`; spec/wire.md §9): the payload carries the author's chain for the carried parts and, for a grant or re-seal, the boxed things' current `keys.0`; left out when the blob would not fit `MAX_BLOB`. Without the keyset `Core::secret_actor` falls back to the first admin and `may_read` denies the holder (a thin client saw nothing of its account's unrouted grant). A labeled thing's label carries the chain (every shared thing has one, spec/sync.md §2): sending it always left writers' content pending `MissingChain` in sims (`snapshot_rights`, `purge_tombstones`, `ephemeral`, `coverer_revoked`: a grantee acting under the label before its admin re-registered it, a writer's card never placed); the cause was never pinned, so re-sweep those if the chain ever goes always. The chain is for a sender who holds no `label.0` of the thing, as far as it knows. The receiver ingests the chain before the commit, only for an admitted blob (`chain_commits`).
- **Hints** (`inbound.rs` `vet_inbox_hints`, run from scratch after each open batch and each `run_dirty`, and again on an engine, entry or expiry change; payloads cached in memory per entry, `inbox_opened`): per unexpired opened blob whose commit is applied or pending, the first payload thing with no known label the commit gave this node a read secret of (`Engine::boxed_by`: valid `secrets.0`, or a reading-only candidate from the pending commit), one per blob, so a stranger's hints are bounded by the deposit budget and go with the blob; a `via` hint (key actor follow) is never touched. A box that opens proves only that the sender knew the actor's KEM key, hence invite requests.
- **An opened inbox blob starts the labels' passes** (`open_one`): the copy is placed at a pass of the actor's home label, and applying a commit starts one only if the commit is new here. A grant to a group whose first admin is its granter has the granter's own inbox as the group's, so the granter's deposit comes back naming a commit it already holds: with no pass started, the group's members waited for the next unrelated pass (26-28 s of sim clock in one probe, at most `resync_ms`).
- **Which thing a label is for on a connection**: `Conn.labels` is keyed by label, its `LConn.group` fixed at `start_label`, and `wanted_labels` puts `labels()` first, then hints, so a hint under another thing's name would take a label whenever `labels()` leaves it out (stopped, no secret held). Hence a payload names only a thing's own label (spec/wire.md §9), `vet_inbox_hints` skips a label any `label.0` here records, and `add_new_labels` restarts a Failed label wanted under another thing or another index of a hint's thing (no Open in flight then; a label still opening keeps its thing until it fails: comparing the thing alone let a sender naming one thing at two indexes alternate them forever, `hint_turns_reach_the_group`).
  - Blobs naming one label under different things take turns: a failed open records (label, thing, index) in `hint_failed` (memory only, cleared at a Failed label's `resync_ms` retry, not at a refusal's shorter one, which a host a sender names could use to cut every round; dropped when no blob names the label any more); the next vet picks the first thing not in it, or the first if all failed (each retry round at most one open per named thing; no spin), and the hint carries the hosts of all of them, since one naming a dead host alone would never fail.
  - One label can so be on different things on different connections (a host a sender names, holding the real path, can walk a hinted open to the head and take the claim: a claim cannot be checked without the step's claim set), so `group_of(l, conn)` judges per connection: what a connection delivers is fetched, kept (`drop_unvouched`), opened and wrap-judged under that connection's group, the same group `Work::unopened`/`refetch` asked under. A judge differing from the asker is a Fetch loop: bytes that unwrapped under one group are dropped as unopened under another and asked again every pass (`hint_at_a_silent_host_does_not_hold_off_the_group`, `hint_at_a_claiming_host_does_not_hold_off_the_group`, claiming hosts in `hint_turns`). Without a connection: the group whose held `label.0` records the label (a hint is the sender's word until then), else an Open connection's, else any connection's, else the hint's thing. A hint's sync key opens only a head of that key.
  - Left: a reader of the label can box its read secret as another thing's secret; that hint opens, never fails, and holds the label until the blob expires. Accepted: only a reader of the label can.
- **Re-sends** (`local.rs` `send_inbox`): senders keep every flows inbox delivery (`Delivery::Inbox { resend }`, `y/v`) and, `INBOX_LIFETIME` after sending, seal it again to the holder's newest keyset and re-send, at most `MAX_INBOX_RESENDS` (3) times, unless the holder acted on it or, for a grant or re-seal, is no longer a current holder of a thing the commit writes grants or secrets of. "Acted" (`acted`) is a held commit newer than the delivered one, by the holder or a node of it, writing a named thing or one whose record chain (as created, not standing: evidence of receipt, not standing) reaches it: counting any commit of the holder, a recipient that wrote anything the sender syncs lost every share its inbox had cleared, for good (`cleared_share_sent_again_after_the_holder_acted_elsewhere_spec_sync_3`). A read-only grantee writing nothing under the thing is so re-sent all 3 times. A re-sent removal notice names no label (the removed holder must not learn the target's current one). App-made `deliver_inbox` blobs are not re-sent.
  - A delivery whose recorded label (`SentInbox::label`) is no longer the holder's inbox is due at once under the same rules and count: a replaced inbox is read by nobody (spec/sync.md §2). Looked at only for holders whose `keys.0` changed (`inbox_moved`, filled by `engine_events`; every holder at a start), so churn costs one re-send per replacement the sender sees, within the same cap (`a_delivery_under_a_replaced_inbox_is_sent_again_spec_sync_3`).
- Outstanding fetches stay in memory: every pass re-derives them (`refetch` of unopened present entries, `fetch_waiting` of waiting commits' blobs, `tree_wants`); only an app's lazy `fetch` is lost with a restart, and its read asks again.

## The outbox and deliveries (`sync/deliver.rs`, `local.rs`)
- `carry_out(Done)` sends inbox blobs and a non-writer's certificate copies of its own writes (`place_certificate_in`: an invite grant's deposit, a self-removal, a chain) to the outbox: sealed, wrapped and signed when queued (`queue_deposit`), kept durably (`y/o`, one per (host, label, blob): one blob may be queued under two labels at one host; an undecodable record is skipped and deleted; only the back-off and the connection it went on are in memory) until each of its hosts takes it or refuses it for good, and not recalled by forgetting the label. A pass does not deposit an entry still queued. Direct commits go to `take_direct`; `First` placements go under every label holding the things before other uploads; then certificate copies.
- **Refused for now** (13, 4, 5, 12, or an Error reply: the host's store failed; left unanswered it would hold the label's others for the connection's life) backs off from `retry_ms` doubling to `DEPOSIT_RETRY_CAP_MS` (1 h); a deposit taken at that host and label restarts the back-off of the others there, since the host does not say when a budget frees. No count limit: a full budget is the normal state while the label's writers are away, and a count limit dropped grants to an offline recipient after about 21 minutes. A deposit under a live label whose writers never return is so resent hourly forever (one small request per hour per stuck deposit).
- **Deposit items** (spec/sync.md §10 Shared set; `shared.rs` `shared_deposits`, `shared_deposit_verdict`, `shared_deposit_answered`): an inbox blob for an inbox whose `keys.0` lists no host, not this node's own (that one is logged here, `local_inbox`), is queued once for the inbox's nodes (`Dest::Nodes(inbox actor)`, outbox key `INBOX_NODES`) and goes as an Offer's Deposit item on any shared-set connection where J of the inbox actor is holder: the peer proved the recipient, or an actor holding its home's read role. One mechanism with the host deposit: the same record, signature, back-off (`deposit_back_off`), moot and re-sign rules; whichever node takes it ends it, and its stream gives it to the inbox's other nodes. The receiver takes it only for an inbox it holds that an actor it proved on this connection holds (`SyncEngine::proved`: what its Prove or Proved carried, never what it could prove now; judged over its rosters), signed under its served head's deposit key, durable class; else 14, the same for an inbox not held, so a depositor learns of no inbox it was not proved an actor for. Taken, it goes through `log_events_from` as a served deposit (charged as one, `charge_deposit`; past `peer_bytes` 5, refused for now). A 14 at the sender: re-signed and offered again if the current deposit key differs, else not offered on that connection until what this node holds changes (`deposits_refused`, cleared by `shared_changed`). One Deposit item unanswered per connection (bodies are large; the rare path).
  - One-way knowledge (the recipient's nodes do not know the sender): they prove nothing to it, so no connection judges them holders and the seal stays queued, counted pending (`deliveries_pending`, `pending_about`), and is re-sealed every `INBOX_LIFETIME` with no resend limit while it waits (`waits_for_nodes`). Whether the sender may prove "I hold your `keys.0`" instead is Tess's (issues/net-connect.md). Peers that cannot read the inbox (step 9 of issues/net-shared-set.md) take the same destination by the deposit-key entry in their filter.
  - A granted group's sync ID opens between readers by the read secret its box gave: a hint's thing's read secrets are credentials (`credentials`) before its `label.0` is held, as an Open by that secret's sync key was at a host.
- Moot deposits go (`deposit_moot`): an inbox seal carries its delivery key (`OutRec::delivery`) and goes when the `sent_inbox` record is forgotten or sealed again, so its life is bounded by `MAX_INBOX_RESENDS` × `INBOX_LIFETIME`; any deposit under a label seen replaced goes.
- NOT_HEAD (9: signed by an earlier deposit key, a refreshed seed; the same bytes never verify again) is signed again with the current key (`resign_deposit`) and sent at once, or dropped when this node holds no other key (the pass deposits it again once the node reads the new `keys.0`).
- Deposits refused `UNKNOWN_LABEL` go again at once when this node finishes registering that label there (`open::registered`): else an inbox to a just-created link's K races K's registration and backs off past the sharer going offline.
- **Deposit pacing** (`send_outbox`, `deposit_room`): deposits to a (host, label) go only as far as the last reported room, with one unanswered at any time, and a `BUDGET_FULL` backs off all of them: else at each deposit taken every queued body goes up and most come back refused (89 MB sent for 1000 shares carrying 24 MB; with the rule 500 shares send 18.8 MB in 3543 deposits). A restart forgets the room: one such burst. Test: `scale::a_burst_past_the_budget_sends_each_body_about_once` (by hand, 15 s in release, minutes in debug).
- **An invite never outruns its label** (spec/sync.md §3): an invite sent with the label's Open lets a nearby invitee ask before the Register lands, get `UNKNOWN_LABEL`, have nothing to register and sit `Failed` until `resync_ms`. So `send_outbox` holds a delivery while it names a label this node is opening or registering at a connected host and has open at none (`names_label_opening`; no timer: the reply ends it, a lost connection or the session's stall timeout frees it, every Register path leaves those phases; bodies still do not wait). A label's only host answering other requests but never this Open is closed at the stall time (the oldest request must move, spec/sync.md §6), and the redial asks again. `register_path` with nothing to register backs off from `retry_ms` instead (`Phase::Refused`, status `Unreachable`), for the sender cut from that host (`import_contact` of a card not up yet too).
- **Crash-safe deliveries**: every delivery but an inbox blob is made through `flows::owe`, which records it (`y/y`, keyed by the hash of its encoding, `SyncRecords::owe`) in the transaction of the commit that owes it, as `note_unsent` does for inbox blobs. `take_done` drops the record in the sync-state flush holding what carrying it out wrote, or, while a copy waits in `deferred` (tagged with the record's key), once the pass draining it places the last one; `SyncEngine::new` carries out what is recorded (`take_owed`; placed copies are skipped by `State::certs`, `first` is filled again).
  - Invariant: after any crash the placements and direct sends equal those without it. A flow replacing an inner transaction's delivery drops its record (`pair`, `flows::unowe`; a crash between the grant and that transaction sends the grant alone, a subset: pairing is redone). A copy waiting to rotate a group this node forgets is dropped with its record (`forget_lost`).
  - Re-deriving at start was rejected: the pass re-derives only some copies (a departure's revokes and keysets, the rotation's named commits, chain and card certificates), not a lowering's grant under the labels above, a removal under the labels holding the removed actor, L's card under K, or direct sends; recomputing each flow's `under` set after the fact means replaying each flow.
  - Not crash-safe: `place_certificate` (the app's contact card: a fresh group, never deferred) and `accept_inbox`'s copy between its ingest and the record (node sync opens inbox blobs itself). `first` priority after the record went (a crash after the step) is lost as on any restart; it only orders a pass's sends (own role commits go at priority 1 anyway).
  - Tests `deposit_queue::{first_copy,removal_copy,link_card}_survives_a_crash_*`, `inbox_life::direct_packages_survive_a_crash_before_carry_out_spec_sync_3`. A failed sync-state flush keeps its writes for the next flush.
- **No placement order** (Tess's decision): projections of a node's own commit (certificate copies, inbox blobs sealed from the `SentInbox` record flows write unsent in the commit's own transaction) go at once. A receiver that holds any part of a role commit applies it and joins the rest on arrival (spec/commits.md §4; `apply::examine`), so nobody waits for a body that never reaches K's label (author's disk lost, K's host never taking it); copies waiting for the full commit would need a timeout, and receivers would still wait forever past it. Accepted cost: a K-syncer may see a grant before its boxes (an admin may re-seal needlessly). Left: a content commit's `created.0` chain copy, or a snapshot's projection, still waits for the rest at a syncer of its thing. `placed` (`deliver.rs`) stays for one use: a self-removal holds back no encrypting until placed (spec/crypto.md §4, `placing`); cached per `State::version`, cleared on engine changes. osis `redeem` returns only once nothing on K is pending (before and after granting) and its own grant on K stands (`used_link_with_sharer_offline_spec_permissions_7`). A non-writer's own role-commit body is deposited through the outbox.

## Relay wait and promotion (`pass/promote.rs`)
- **Relay wait** (`relay_at`, `promote`): a writer uploads or relays another node's commit at a host lacking it, and promotes a deposit, only after a wait of `RELAY_MIN_MS` (5 s) to `SETTLE_MS` (30 s) since the commit arrived (the deposit was first seen; in memory), the point fixed per (node, blob) by a hash; its own commits (origin local, a held key actor's included) and `First` placements go at once. Without it writers online together each upload a fresh encryption of the same new commits and re-send every reader deposit; with it the author or the first co-writer is done and the others find the entry. A refused promotion (a step registered meanwhile) waits again.
  - Cost: a commit whose author is offline, a relay to a second host and a promotion wait up to 30 s. A coarse clock (the sim's `quiet`, 61 s steps) fires every waiter at once, so the sim's `concurrent_writers` moves time a second at a time. Residue: the wait is a spread, not an election; a writer whose view of a promotion lags may send it too (the sim sees a second or third upload of about one blob in 30 in a few seeds).
- **Promotion is per host**: presence records carry "deposited there" (`State::deposited`, from the host's unstamped add), cleared on the host's OK, not when queued (a refused stamped re-upload would leave the deposit unpromoted for good).
- **Promotion completes the author's chain** (spec/sync.md §5; `due_deposits`, `complete_chains`, `promote`): a writer promotes a due deposit only for a commit it applied (`Engine::author_chain`: the author's chain certificates to each thing the deposited parts touch at the commit's point, as `Timeline::certificates` plus the record chains, minus the deposit's own parts) whose chain `not_held` finds under the label; before the sends it places the missing parts it holds as its own certificate copies (uploaded in the same batch), else leaves the deposit. A lone promoted copy left a winner pending `MissingChain`. A deposited record blob carrying a record of a promoted commit's chain goes up stamped with it (`promote`): a record blob is no commit, so nothing else promotes it, and it would wait in the budget until cleared.
  - A copy is never judged by its author's chain: the chain moves after a copy (the granter's grant lowered, his node's key), so copies of a settled grant would be deleted for good (`tied::a_copy_survives_its_granters_grant_changing`), and a judge cannot tell a chain that will not come from one still uploading. Nothing needs it since promotion completes chains: a stamped partial upload by a writer rotated out stays pending at its readers while its entry is held (one row each, under the host's cap). Two writers (or a writer and the author) may place the same chain parts concurrently: the duplicates are may-hold.
