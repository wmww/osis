# Deposits
> Summary: Why deposits work as they do: the deposit budget, readers' deposits and copies under a superseded stamp, one clearing rule under every sync ID, promotion once, accepted limits of what a stranger with `keys.0` can do; no placement order, why a copy is not judged by its author's chain. Sealed fires, tickets and the account's tickets (engine side). Rules: spec/sync-inbox.md, spec/sync-judgement.md §1, §3. The code: notes/sync-inbox.md. Neighbours: notes/sync.md (index), notes/sync-access.md (re-seal, claims), notes/certificates.md.

## Deposits
- **A reader's deposits** (its account's chain certificates, its invite grant, self-removal, card) sit deposited in its own store until a writer promotes them (a body the closure carries) or logs them as its own certificate copies (the rest: spec/sync-inbox.md §4 promotes no chain certificate); co-readers pull and read them meanwhile.
- **A reader's copy under a superseded stamp** (any author's, ephemeral or durable) goes as a fresh encryption deposited where a source keeping S unread lacks it (relayed under its stamp only while the source's head write key verifies it): after a write-key rotation, with that source's copy gone (an ephemeral entry at each of its restarts; a durable one on a new or wiped node) and no writer holding it, it is the only way back. The promoting writer judges it by its author's rights at its `ts`. A reader deposits a body only while it judges it valid: a demoted writer's own writes refused under the rotated key are invalid at it too (kept for its user, spec/permissions.md §6); deposited, no writer would promote them and they would sit in the budget until cleared. It costs no new room: one budget per sync ID holds durable and ephemeral deposits alike, an ephemeral one also within the ephemeral cap, and any deposit-key holder could fill that budget already. Rejected: taking stamps under a superseded write key on relay (refused, code 9): a removed writer keeps the old write key and could fill S's room.
- **The budget is local policy**, not a format constant: senders never compute it, they see refusals (code 5). The recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2: 64 + 4 per claim of the current step, 32 KiB each) is sized per reader *actor*, while deposits come per *node*: a plain thing under a team has a claim set of one, and thirty reader nodes' cards can approach it, so a node keeping S unread for a large read-only audience raises it (`caps^.0` policy `deposit_entries`, `deposit_bytes`). Deposits are unlinkable by design, so per-reader limits are impossible; the peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5) charges only entries that give nothing held.
- Reasons behind spec rules (spec/sync-inbox.md):
  - A node keeps offering its own deposit after losing read access (§3 Sending): other nodes may hold parts of that commit from elsewhere and wait for the rest.
  - Relay-only blobs are refused as deposits (§3, code 14): held by nobody, a reader's would be a write of its own, and readers write no ephemeral data.

## Clearing
- **One clearing rule, every sync ID**, for all a stranger (an inbox) or a reader (any sync ID: it holds the deposit key) can put in the budget (junk that opens to nothing, fires, commits it sealed and signed that open but are invalid, pending or parked commits, record blobs, far or no expiry): a delete-key holder leaves at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` (32) and 1 MiB of them per store and deletes the rest. Exempt only while it would act on it: a deposit it would promote; a duplicate or a commit S does not need counts. No "this is junk" verdict: a blob sealed to a key this node lacks yet looks like junk and stays while under the bound, and what stays blocks nothing, half the recommended budget being free.
  - Counted at blob size once its bytes arrived, whether or not the node still holds them (it drops the bytes of what it judged invalid).
  - Cost of judging: under a validity fork (permanent) a clearing node may clear what a writer on the other side would promote, only past the bound; an honest deposit pending at the clearing node past the bound goes the same way. A reader's own must-hold body is not lost: it deposits a fresh encryption at each judgement finding the source lacks it, so past the bound it cycles until judged.
  - Order (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): no expiry first, then earliest to expire. Rejected: **age-out** of unstamped entries: at 0 nothing waits for a writer, at infinity the hole stays.

## Promotion
- **Promotion once** (spec/sync-inbox.md §4): one stamped copy per delta, however many encryptions are deposited: any reader can seal any commit it holds again, and role commits and cards outside the closure are may-hold (a writer cannot tell which of them another node's chain needs), so every duplicate stamped would be kept for good. Chain certificates are not promoted: the promoter logs the author's chain of a promoted commit as its own certificate copies, so a reader's deposited chain certificates are duplicates left to clearing. Rejected: promoting certificate-class deltas on anything upstream of the closure: the reader's own account is upstream, so every edge it writes on its account and every thing it mints and grants there would be stamped.
  - Never an entry whose bytes the writer dropped: a purged or superseded part's (shredding cleared its stamp, and promoting it would bring back a destroyed thing's record for a newcomer to read).
  - Limits: promotion needs the author's write on S's actor and a body S's closure carries (`Engine::promotable`), so a reader's minted key actors' cards are never promoted.
## What a stranger with `keys.0` can still do (accepted; spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read)
- Fill the budget while no delete-key holder of the inbox is online: fires wait at their senders (offered again after back-off) until one comes back. A thing's readers can fill any of its sync IDs the same way: readers' deposits (cards, self-removals, readers' copies) wait until a delete-key holder clears.
- Flood without pause: no deposit rate limit is built (spec/sync-ids.md §6 allows one, code 4), so slots go as they free, an honest deposit races him, and each junk blob costs the node its bytes until he stops.
- Past the bound, flood or not, what goes may be real: a reader's deposited linked blob (filler wrap, no expiry) or record blob (a promoter lacking it logs its own), a commit still pending here, or a blob sealed to a key the node holds only later (a team's, whose secrets come from another sync ID). A node holding the head's read epoch holds its history, so readers' valid commits (cards, certificate copies) open and never count.
- A new deposit seed needs a new `keys.0` and step.

## Delivery
- **No placement order** (Tess's decision): projections of a node's own commit (certificate copies) go at once. A receiver holding any part of a role commit applies it and joins the rest on arrival (spec/commits.md §4), so nobody waits for a body that never reaches K's sync ID (author's disk lost, no source ever taking it); copies waiting for the full commit would need a timeout, and receivers would still wait forever past it. Accepted cost: a K-syncer may see a grant before its boxes (an admin may re-seal needlessly).
- **A copy is never judged by its author's chain** (spec/sync-inbox.md §4: never deleted for an incomplete chain): the chain moves after a copy (the granter's grant lowered, his node's key), so copies of a settled grant would be deleted for good, and a judge cannot tell a chain that will not come from one still on its way. Nothing needs it since promotion completes chains. Two writers (or a writer and the author) may log the same chain parts concurrently: the duplicates are may-hold.

## Sealed fires and tickets (engine)
- **One fire path** (`flows::inbox::fire`): a node reading the thing writes the fire as any commit (deposited by a non-writer as any write); any other owes a sealed fire, a durable row (`node/fires/`, `Indexes::fires`) keyed by BLAKE3 of `[thing, ak, value]` so one fire is owed once. Its commit is built once by the write path unapplied (`engine::write::sealed_commit`: commit form, no setting read, no chunks: receivers fetch no blob of it) and kept in the row, so resealing on a key change carries the same commit. A removal notice's value is made when first sealed (`Fire::Removal`: the role commit applied by then), a stranded notice's commit expires `INBOX_LIFETIME` after it is made (`Fire::Expiring`).
- **Opening**: an entry whose wrap no held secret opens is tried as a sealed fire with each held read KEM key of the thing (`Engine::open_sealed_fire`); one with any delta but one open fire on that thing, or a prefix disagreeing with its header, is bad. Its records, chain commits and commit are taken in one transaction kept only when the fire applies valid and no chain commit is parked or invalid (`Engine::ingest_sealed_fire`): a stranger's records are never learned for a fire that does not check out. Promotion is the ordinary promotion with the role check waived for open fires (`Engine::promotable`).
- **Open fires keep only by name**: anyone may fire any `@~` AK, so the default entry of a history setting never governs one (`RetentionSetting::entry`); a fire on an open signal no entry names is out of history at arrival. A fire out of history (or expired) counts as gone for forgetting (`Engine::forgettable`), so its commit, blob row and the signal's cursor (`leaving::unplaced`) go once node-sync knows no entry of it; no forgotten-`ts` row is kept for a signal (an undo writes nothing back there).
- **A stranger's chain goes with its last fire**: the account record(s) and `keys.0` commits a sealed fire brings are needed by nothing once its fires are forgotten, and go in that transaction (notes/certificates.md Kept while needed; engine `strangers::strangers_fires_leave_no_row_*`: rows return exactly to what they were).
- **An open fire is valid whatever its author's role** and needs no path to the thing (`apply::validity`, `apply::thing_wait`); the write side checks no role for an `@` key (`check_role_with`).
- **Tickets** are found by their target in the ticket rows of the reference index (`refs::tickets_to`: a state's per aspect, a signal's per fire, so a lookup reads the target's tickets alone; `sync::tickets::held_in`) and opened as a `secrets.0` box of the target to their holder (`Core::open_ticket`). The walk judges every held ticket at a strong to a thing its context cannot follow while the node holds no established read secret of its own of it (`sync::units::judge`, `Core::reads_established`; not one derived from its first admin's, as a paired node reads the profile before its keyset arrives): a ticket's epoch is its maker's to choose, so a forged ticket taken first must not keep the honest one out; a ticket row appearing or going, or a removal notice arriving, judges again each held strong to its target (`Closure::rejudge`).
- **What a held ticket does** (`sync::tickets::role_on`, the one place; spec/sync-set.md §2): a ticket says how to sync, never caps the node's role. The node's role on the thing is the best of what the edge writes held on it show (`rotation::role_here`) and, while a held strong reaches it (`Closure::accepted`), each opened ticket's word (`Closure::ticket_words`, noted by `take_in` before it takes any secret) whose holder no held edge write on the thing names: a node keeping T unread holds only the role commits another thing's certificates bring (a holder piece's grant and removal of that holder), which cannot show its own grant. A ticket supplies its sync ID and secret up to the lower of its role and that one (`take_in`, through `learn_secret` and `rotation::may_learn` by role); `given_by` reads `role_on` too, so a role change judges the edge again. The lost-access trigger judges by the same role (`rotation::lost` `holds_at`, `over_held`: a sync secret is over-held below sync, any other below read). Why one rule decides both: a ticket taken while the trigger drops its secret undoes itself every settle.
  - **What judges it stays** (`Closure::accepted`, a root of `retention::needed`): per target, the held vertices whose strong reaches it with a ticket opening here. While any does, the thing's record and role commits stay through lost access and release, so not entering changes no input of the rule; once none does (strong or ticket gone) it goes whole, and accepting again is a first accept. Without it a release forgot the removal and the stale ticket counted on its word again.
  - **Fires taken at the strong** (`take_in` notes them in `Closure::fires_due`, `Engine::take_fire` from `follow` takes each part only where valid): a read ticket's fire's `grant` and the removal notices on the account's inbox naming the thing (`flows::inbox::removals_untaken`: O(the notices the inbox keeps), a notice names its thing only inside its commit), each only while its commit is not held whole (`fire_untaken`), so judging again retakes nothing. A re-grant after a removal is under a secret the holder lacks, and a holder removed before accepting is refused by every peer: the fires are all it learns its role by. A sync ticket's fire is not taken: it enters at sync at most, and taking it at every start cost commits per thing kept unread.
- **Own keys** (`Core::keyed`): by role commits held or an established secret of its own. A ticket's secret establishes nothing (a forged one must make no plain thing keyed). A thing without own keys is entered on its tickets' word at their entry role (`units::given_by`): read, `Ctx::Ticket`: its tickets' sync IDs are synced (`sync_ids::actors::ticket_hints`, hints with no path, every one while no held `sync.0` owns one, never one another thing owns here), none of its data held for it (`Closure::holds` skips that context, so it releases), and once its role commits arrive it is keyed and entered read; sync, `Ctx::Unread`.
- **S's things** (`Engine::sync_things_of`, spec/sync-inbox.md §5): every thing owning S (by `sync.0`, destruction, link, or held tickets naming it); the sync set's entry is all of them (`rows::fold`: read while any reads it), never one by tid. Per-S rules run for each: `read_things_of`/`opening_things` (opening, credentials, opening secrets, bodies joined by `join_body`, own scope, lists, records), `body_thing` (the one whose body of a commit S carries: sealing, logging, promotion), `judge_peer` the best of each, caps the largest. Path rules (signers, steps, stranding, register) use `recorded_owner`/`read_sync_id`: hints have no path. Several things claim S only while S is a hint (`ticket_hints` drops one another thing's `sync.0` records), so the genuine `sync.0` arriving under S ends a forged claim.
- **A ticket fire carries its role commit** (`Fire::Ticket`, built when first sealed as a removal notice is): the commit giving the ticket's epoch is wrapped under an epoch it supersedes, which a new holder lacks, so the fire carries its role parts on the thing with their chain, taken at the strong (What a held ticket does). A ticket a node writes into an inbox it reads (its own account's) carries none.
- **The account's tickets** (`flows::inbox::account_ticket_due`): due while the walk enters the thing from a vertex outside the local part (`Closure::entered_outside`) and D holds no ticket to the account of that role, per sync ID, at an epoch it holds the thing by there (`held_by`): read, a current (established) one, so a ticket accepted by its read secret alone is written once the thing's role commits arrive; kept unread, the newest n among the held sync secrets a step of S's stored path bears the key of (`Cipher::bears`, the store's path read through its row stamps, so a step arriving moves the `Effects` key), so a forged ticket's made-up epoch never reaches D and a genuine rotation's replaces the old one once the next step registers. Boxing every held secret instead would grow D by every rotation and keep forged ones for as long as the strong. Writing one removes D's tickets of that role naming that sync ID at a lower epoch n only, so siblings seeing different epochs, or two sides of a fork, never undo each other. Release removes D's tickets for what it drops in the same transaction. On `LocalPeer` (a store of blobs only, no paths) a thing kept unread gets no D ticket: tests of it live in osis-sync (`tickets::ds_ticket_for_a_thing_kept_unread_*`).
- **An actor's sync ID is in its `keys.0`** (field 9, written with its genesis step): a sender knows where a fire goes from the public key record alone. An account's public record carries its inbox's record and current `keys.0` (`account_chain`).
- **Actor inboxes keep what their own setting keeps**: core writes none on a team; a fire a reader would write that the setting keeps no history of is not written (it would be out of history at once).
