> Summary: The one plan a coordinator builds from for accounts on the ordinary rules, on the built sync unit (a thing's readable closure, so a thing may be carried under several sync IDs): Tess's answers it rests on, the stages in order (what each changes, removes, needs modelled or measured, how it is checked, what it unblocks), why they are ordered as they are, what is deleted when, what every other priority does meanwhile, and the risks with their cheapest early checks.

Designs: notes/account-membership-design.md, notes/write-path-design.md. The sync unit they are stated for is the built one (spec/sync-set.md §2, spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§2); one independent thing per unit was rejected (notes/sync-unit-scope.md). Model: roles-model `paths`, notes/path-model-findings.md. Nothing below is built. Marks: **[checked: ref]** read there for this plan; **[assumed]** taken from a design note or not verified; everything unmarked about the future is the plan.

## The order of the account redesign
- **Nothing waits on the sync unit.** Every stage is stated for the built unit, where a thing may be carried under several sync IDs: evidence goes under each sync ID that carries a commit, a removal under each that carries a thing where the removed signer could act. Validity is judged from held commits whatever sync ID brought them.
- **S1 is independent**: it fixes work per sync ID held, which every closure pays.
- **Paths (A1) before members (A4).** A signer that is not the account needs a path of at least [A]; today's header has one `actor` [checked: spec/wire.md §4]. With paths first, today's account key is simply the signer of a path that starts above it, so A1 lands with accounts unchanged.
- **Evidence (A2) before the removal's logging (A3)**: a removal's copy under a unit is carried with the evidence that needs it.
- **The ordinary removal complete (A3) before accounts rest on it (A4).** A4 deletes the account's own lists and listings; the per-thing list, the logging under every sync ID owed and vouches must hold first.
- **Public things and cards (A5) before the library (A6)**, so the account functions leave the `osis` crate once, in their final shape.
- A1's one transitional piece: chain certificates certify the stated path instead of searching for one, a narrowing of engine `sync/certified.rs` that A2 deletes.
- **Earliest deletion**: the signing chain at A4. Nothing old is kept beside its replacement inside a stage: formats change in place.

### M1. Model: the write path's second round

## Risks
1. **The path-judged walk with two-walk vouches does not hold up outside the modelled worlds** (invites, finalizes, own covers and forgetting; a component holding a vouch walked twice). Descent, `until`, self-removal by path end and removals of more than one hop hold in the model. A void lowering costs its check along its author's grant chain, so k void writes at the end of a chain of k grants cost k² setters read (notes/path-model-findings.md 17). Cheapest check: M1 brief 3, before the A1 briefs that build them; then A1 brief 7's count at n and 10n, the void rounds included.
2. **Evidence over a thing under several sync IDs**: a commit's evidence is owed under each sync ID that carries it, and a reader of one of them must judge alike; copies per (commit, thing) wrapped under each, placed by what each sync ID's unit carries, may leave one of them short where a unit joins or leaves (retag) between the commit and its evidence. Cheapest check: M2 with a thing under a root's sync ID and its own, retag included, before A2's code.
3. **Removal logging over a thing under several units**: the remover finds a target's own sync ID from the account's taken grants, but not every enclosing unit that carries the target; the plan relies on the standing duty (readers of an enclosing unit sync the target's own sync ID too, spec/sync-set.md §2 Sync set, so hold the copy and log it on) [assumed: that every such reader holds the copy in time, and that a node keeping the enclosing unit unread is reached through a writer of it]. Cheapest check: M1 brief 4 and a hand world in M2 (a stolen node's first write on a thing reached only through an enclosing root); A3 brief 5's count.
4. **A removal's units at an organization's scale, and the raised holder** (a signer removed while its account only read a unit, the account raised to write later). Cheapest checks: M1 brief 4; reading spec/crypto.md §4 Floors for whether readers take content sealed under a superseded epoch; A3 brief 5's count. If the raised holder is a real window, the removal is also logged at the raise, by the duty already stated.
5. **The walk read without trust differs from the trusted one wherever a removal's reach-back drops a write that acted**: a lowering applied at its key keeps its drops and stays applied when a later removal drops it or its author's grant (notes/path-model-findings.md 16), so a node trusting nothing reads otherwise than one trusting the removal, and is shown the removal as a dispute by the widened rule (a lowering whose trust changes the reading; honest histories raise none in the model). Most nodes trust nothing until a dispute is detected, so detection must catch these, at a walk per candidate lowering (Open 0). Cheapest check: A1 brief 7, counting walks per detection and disputes raised in honest histories of removals.
6. **A public thing referenced from many units**: each commit on it is entered under every referring unit's sync ID (an entry and stamp each). Cheapest check: A5's count at n and 10n referrers; the cost is the built unit's, stated (issues/enclosing-unit-commit-cost.md).
