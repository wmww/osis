# Sync: read
> Summary: osis-sync's `read` module (core/sync/src/read/): judgement of every body under a sync ID this node reads, what it calls for in the own store and at each source keeping S unread, destruction; what its ground reads of the engine and of other modules, how work follows change, the engine facts it needs, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§5, spec/sync-compaction.md §1, spec/sync-set.md §3. Models: core/test/tla/net/NetHold.tla, NetDestroy.tla. Principle and per-judgement outline: the module doc of core/sync/src/read/mod.rs. Overview: notes/sync.md. Design reasons for the convergence rule: notes/sync-convergence.md.

## What it owns
`Effects` for bodies per commit; the own judgement per (S, commit): verdict and own-store acts; minting copies per (S, thing); what a body calls for per (S, commit, source keeping S unread); each piece per (S, piece blob) (`read/pieces.rs`); the destruction per S; a memo `Clock` (expiries move judgements; `next_wake`), three stamped maps (verdicts, own role commits S must hold, S told destroyed), which status and inbox read (`Read::verdict`), and the outside waits (below). Nothing durable of its own: stands live in the store's record rows (`s`, the head and the fall mark), so a start judges every held commit once. A judgement under an S not held read (the engine's sync set lists S before leave sets the store's holdings) unfollows its key; S coming to be read (`Ground::holdings_moved`, the store's syncs feed) follows its bodies, doom, certificate blobs and steps again. `on_message` takes the answers to read's own requests (Delete, deposits).

`judge.rs` holds the pure judgements (`body`, `toward`, `doom`) over `Ground` values; `Read::settle` runs them inside its effects to a fixpoint, then acts on what a new judgement adds. A restart or a reconnect (a new `ConnId` in each act) carries each act out once more, never in a loop; logging again is bounded by the durable stand alone. Tests drive `settle_on` with a fake ground (core/sync/tests/it/read.rs).

## Every held entry is judged
- An entry of S in the own store whose commit this node holds is judged under S whether or not `sync_bodies` lists S (`Ground::entries_added`: the store's entry feed, the engine's `blob_feed` of blobs opened or sealed, and the commit feed, each mapped to (S, commit); at a start every entry of every held commit). One of a commit not held (pending, parked) is not judged: the pending bounds keep it (spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Unjudged blobs).
- **Outside S** (`judge::outside`, spec/sync-judgement.md §2): `sync_bodies` is not settled when a commit is held (roles, group membership and certificates arriving later put it under S), so a body judged outside S is unjudged until a complete pass of S begun after both the entry came (`arrived`, the pass place when the feed gave it) and what its bodies read last moved (`moved`, the place when its `bodies` effect was due) has ended (`Status::passed_since`). `moved` goes with the commit (`Local::Gone`), `arrived` with its judgement going idle, a wait once its pass ends. Read wants that pass (`Read::passes_wanted`, per S the newest place; status merges it with leave's, `module_passes`); a wait is kept only while an entry of it is held here or shown at a source. Then the entry leaves with no tombstone (`Act::Unlog`, `Store::unlog_entry`): judged wrongly, it is logged or pulled again once required (`read::an_entry_judged_outside_then_listed_is_held_again_spec_sync_judgement_2`). A writer of S deletes it at store sources only past the same pass (`toward` acts on no unjudged body).
- Store deletes of an outside drop carry the protocol's tombstone at the store (`TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, spec/sync-ids.md §6): judged wrongly, the body comes back there by §4 Logged again once (a fresh encryption), not by its old blob ID.
- Cost: status tells the engine of a pass's end only while the engine wants one of S, so read's passes run no engine pass-end duties; but an engine want arriving while read's pass is under way waits for one more pass, up to `PASS_WAIT_MS` with a recorded peer away (`duties::a_rotation_owed_by_the_account_is_made_at_a_pass_end_spec_permissions_3`).
- Rejected: judging outside S as soon as `sync_bodies` lacks S (`convergence::histories_converge` seed 7 diverges: ten (S, commit) pairs judged outside, then listed), and tombstoning such a drop (the entry never comes back by ID).

## Acts
- **Own store**: logs must-hold bodies with no entry standing as its log while a reader can pull S (a must-hold body is one this node may log under S, `Ground::may_log`, `Engine::may_log`, spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Loggable: else may-hold, never logged, relayed, deposited or owed; read in the judgement, so it moves when keys arrive; the usual such bodies: another account's closure commit under a sync ID this node writes, held only as a per-sync-ID copy from another sync ID, so no genuine section to carry and no epoch of the part's thing to seal it under; this node's own certificates under a sync ID it reads through a card or link, with no wrap epoch there) (a held blob verbatim, else a fresh encryption; a body counting missing only once held again), drops entries must-not-hold or covered and dropped (no delete key; never a must-hold one, nor a non-sole certificate may-hold only as not loggable: `covered_dropped` reads the facts, so a must-hold body demoted while it may not log is kept, never dropped or deleted at a source), unlogs with no tombstone each entry of a body judged invalid (`BodyFacts::invalid`: no part valid, each dropped as invalid or covered or held as an own commit, not a role commit, no request in force (`purge::drops_nothing`: a re-grant may give one force with its parts still invalid, and its author must log it then); the author's own included, its engine keeping the commit, so no peer keeps the header for its stream), lifts own tombstones of a body missing again.
  - Standing (`Ground::stands`): every entry of the body under S held here, an entry streams kept as it came included (notes/sync-store.md, A reader's store of S), so a reader logs nothing of its own for a body it pulled. Where this node holds the write key of the head its store serves (`live::stamp_key`), an entry, own or a peer's, stands only stamped under that key; else `Act::Restamp` stamps it in place (same blob and wrap, `Ground::restamp`, own from then on): each own entry of the body, or with none of its own and none standing, the lowest peer's (a peer's duplicates are never stamped). Exception: an entry of a role commit a recorded step registers (`Engine::step_needs`, `Live::step_parents`) stands under that step's parent and is restamped under the earliest such parent whose write key is held, so a store behind takes it before the step (else registration there never passes; a writer never deposits). A head move judges each body under S twice (the move, then the restamp) and restamps each once (`read::a_head_move_restamps_each_own_entry_once_spec_sync_judgement_3`, n and 10n). Pieces (`Live::piece`: `Own` only so stamped; `log_piece` restamps) and certificate blobs (`CertBlobsMissing::stale`) follow the same rule.
  - Served head, not the recorded one: the node log names a head (its head event, written as `store::Follow` serves the path) before any stamp under it, and the step's commit is logged before the restamps, so a reader applying the stream in order holds the step before the stamps under it. Signing under `Engine::signers` (the recorded head) stamped entries ahead of the head event, and readers kept the old stamp.
- **Fetching again** (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies): a body counting missing (`facts.missing`) opens again each entry of it under S the store still holds (`Act::Take`: a body whose cover fell or whose purge fell before its entries left has its bytes here, and no fetch would bring them), and calls for `Act::Fetch` of the others at each source of S (readers included) whose stream shows one; read hands them out of `settle` and the driver gives each to `Streams::fetch_again`, which queues a Fetch outside any message (a stream past the entry never announces it again). The judgement reads `sources(s)` only while the body is missing, so a source joining asks it too. The facade's `sync_bodies` lists missing and dropped parts (`Engine::bodies_under` with `unheld`), else a body dropped whole (as invalid, then vouched for by an addendum) has no body under S and is never judged.
- **Toward each source keeping S unread**, keyed by what its stream wrote (`seen_added`) and, where this node is no writer at its head, every must-hold body: deletes (its head's delete key; carriers in its `known`, a full source counting one it lacks; a covered-and-dropped body not before `BodyFacts::hosts_from`, `MAX_DRIFT` past its keep bound, read through the memo clock so it wakes then; duplicates by delta set and copy; never a body dropped as invalid), deposits, logs again once per ground; deletes and loggings again wait on role entries first.
- **Destruction**: told (sticky); emptying deletes at a source holding every request or past their expiry; a destroying request under S told is may-hold whatever else holds of it (its part on the thing dropped here included: it leaves at its expiry, never, for a purge without `expires`); told, every own entry of S but the requests' drops (`DropBlob`, from the store's whole list: an entry of a commit forgotten with a released thing names no commit the engine holds, so no body judgement reaches it); at a source keeping S unread whose stream shows nothing (as `known` holds it: only entries held, tombstoned or taken here, so an entry this node never took, refused or ranked never, does not delay it; the close drops it there), the requests past their expiry, it registers the closing step of that source's head (`Act::Close`, `Step::closing`; the source leaves S); forgets once every source is heard out with nothing known and none keeps S unread at a head whose delete key it holds (`Status::heard_out_or_alone`: at once with no peer to wait for on S, as the lifecycle duties read it), at once with no delete key, or untold past the requests' expiry (deleting nothing). Open gap (Tess): a purge without `expires` keeps S forever (issues/destroyed-sync-id-never-left.md).
- **Steps**: per S read here, one `Effects` key (`steps`): `Ground::step_ready` (the engine's `due_step` and each rotation it registers logged stamped in the own store) then `issue_step`, tried at each re-judge while ready (a role blocked here issues nothing until another rotation). No connection is needed; Register carries the recorded step to stores.
- **Minting copies** (spec/commits.md §5 A thing's mintings travel with its record): the engine's certificates name every held minting of a thing whose record goes under S (`BodyMeta::mints` on its header-only copy, empty for a request or any other body). One `Effects` key per (S, thing) (`judge::mintings`) ranks them over `Engine::mintings_of` (standing, `expires`, purge set and actor; witness by `retention::highest`) and the own store: `Mint::Log` for the witness unless an entry of S holds another minting of its standing, `Gone` for one standing lower where an entry holds a minting of the witness's standing that may stand in for it (`Minting::stands_in_for`: expires no earlier, purge rights contained in the lower one's, so no request or expiry ends it first), else `Keep`. It writes a stamped row per (S, minting, thing), which the body's judgement reads (`Must`, `May`, `MustNot(Superseded)`; a commit minting several things takes the most any gives it; unjudged until its rows are settled), so one minting moving re-judges only the copies whose place moved. The key leaves once no copy of the thing is in S's required set.
  - Readings: "an entry there" is an entry in this node's store of S (pulled ones included: a reader holds what its sources hold); a lower copy not gone and a tie are may-hold (kept, never logged, not deposited). A destroyed thing's witness or request that went is logged again by the ordinary rules (must-hold with no standing entry: logged in the own store; past a delete at a source, logged again once per ground), while the certificates name it (while the sync ID needs the thing).
- **Certificate blobs**: per S, one fresh record blob of the records S's certificates need that no own record blob carries, and one list blob per rotation of the thing lists S carries that no own list blob of it carries (notes/sync-facade.md, Certificate bodies). A certificate blob names no commit, so it is never judged by body: it stays while S lives, and under S told destroyed (emptying, spec/sync-judgement.md §2) the own ones drop (`DropBlob`) and none is logged, else the own store never empties and S is never forgotten.
- **Local, per held commit** (one `Effects` key, `judge::local`; spec/commits.md §5 Covered, Own replaces, Forgetting; spec/sync-compaction.md §1): its parts that drop as covered now drop (`Engine::covered_drops`, then `drop_body` each: a held valid snapshot or two own replaces covering it, past its keep bound on the memo clock, own live bodies only under own covers, under own replaces only whole); once the engine would forget it (`Engine::forgettable`) and no entry of a blob of it is known under a sync ID carrying a body of it (own entry, own tombstone within `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, a source showing one), it is forgotten, every such commit of a settle in one `forget_commits` (own entries and tombstones are looked up under every sync ID, `Ground::known_under`; a source is asked only under the sync IDs carrying it and those: a forgotten commit another sync ID's source shows is fetched once, logged, and kept until its entry goes); a commit no longer held leaves read (its bodies key and metas). A dropped part stays the body's under S (`sync_bodies` lists dropped parts as missing ones), so a body none of whose parts is held is may-hold (`BodyFacts::dropped`; one with a part held is judged by what is held) and its entries leave by the carriers rule and `hosts_from`, not at once; `BodyFacts::covered` counts the author's own replaces as a snapshot (spec/commits.md §5 Own replaces: the same test for must-hold) (`retention::a_body_out_of_history_stays_at_a_node_keeping_it_unread_for_the_margin_spec_commits_5`). Tested by `growth::*_grow_nothing_spec_commits_5`.
- Between readers nothing but streams.

## Pieces and freed links
- **Pieces** (spec/sync-judgement.md §1: a body's chunk, tree-node, page and header-list blobs go under S as the same blobs, filler wrap): one `Effects` key per (S, piece blob), judged alone (`judge::piece`), in `read/pieces.rs`. A must-hold body's judgement gives its roots (`Engine::piece_roots(c, S's parts)`, the parts its meta gives, `Mine::meta`: never `sync_bodies` again, which reads every sync ID's body of the commit); each root, and each kid of an acting held node (`Engine::piece_below`, a memo per link: a node is immutable), is linked by what links it (`Linker`: a body, or the node above). A held one with no own entry (and no tombstone: never logged again by ID) is logged (`log_piece`: a peer's entry of it becomes own and is uncharged, else a new entry with a filler wrap, stamped where this node writes S's head, else deposited). A missing one held as a peer's store entry is taken by the engine (`Engine::take_piece`; the engine reads only what it holds, so a piece arriving before its commit parks as an ordinary entry that did not open until the commit's judgement takes it); else it is fetched where a source's stream shows it. A reader pulls every entry of S anyway, so this fetch matters only for a piece shown after the pull passed it.
  - Keyed by blob, not link: an edit's new node can link a reused chunk by another link (another delete-key claim).
  - Dormant: a held piece nothing links stays followed, acting on nothing, until its blob is released (then dropped); linked again, it wakes with its kids and takes its current acts (`done` per piece: the acts taken since it woke) with no judgement. Without it a reader holding an edit's body before its new root (the usual order) drops the old tree and judges it all again when the root arrives. Bound: one key per (S, held piece some must-hold body under S linked here), so replaced bodies' pieces leave as the engine releases them (`read::replaced_bodies_leave_no_pieces_followed_once_released_spec_sync_judgement_1`); a restart follows only what must-hold bodies link.
- **Repair** (spec/sync-ids.md §1): a held piece some source shows deleted (a Deleted event, or a refusal as tombstoned) and none shows present, once S's sources are heard out (`heard_out`: every entry it can open applied), is lost (`Act::Repair`, deletes and `heard_out` read only once no source shows it). Read repairs it in each own body over it (walking `Linker`s up; `facts.own`, a snapshot included; `Judged::repairs`), but not by way of a repair over a blob it encrypted afresh (`facts.repair_fresh`), each body's lost pieces in one `Engine::relink(pieces, commit's things)`; again when a linker joins a piece already lost (a body coming to link it). Relink is idempotent (the rewritten position's tree no longer holds the piece). Reading of the spec: "its own store no longer holds it" is dropped (spec edited): the own store keeps the piece, and its stream shows it present to readers, but a store source that deleted it refuses it by ID for good, so only a fresh encryption reaches readers that pull through stores.
  - Relink writes the aspect's live value where its tree reaches a lost blob: current state, so Passing on's floors clause binds it not (`carried_passes_on`, floor `None`; the departures clause does); a blob only replaced versions reach is never written (spec/sync-ids.md §1).
  - Bound: relink marks its commit a repair with the blobs it encrypted afresh (`Meta::repair`: those its things' live trees reach after it and not before; record head origin byte 2, then the blob IDs), and those are never repaired by way of it; the chunks it reused are, as any own commit's (else, once the original own commit drops, a later purge of a reused chunk is never repaired). What a repair encrypts afresh only that commit links, so a source deleting it is refusing, not a purger that missed a reuse; without the mark a store refusing each re-encryption forced one commit per refusal. Each round re-encrypts other chunks, so repairs are bounded by the pieces of the value's tree per purge that missed a reuse. The mark is written in a second transaction after the commit (a crash between allows one more repair). Not reported in status beyond the source's `refusing`.
  - A connected reader source showing the piece present suppresses repair while it stays connected, though readers that reach only the deleting store then lack it (the spec's "none shows it present").
- **Freed links** (spec/sync-compaction.md §1): one `Effects` key per judged freed row (`freed_feed`, all rows at the first settle). `judge::freed`: under each read S carrying the thing (`Engine::carrying`), the own entry (own or a peer's) leaves (`Act::DropBlob`, tombstoned) and each store source whose stream shows it gets a Delete (`delete_freed`: S's head delete key when the engine held its bytes and they bore the link, else by level). Reader sources do not count: a reader judges its own entries. Once no own or store-source entry is known, `Engine::freed_done` and the key is dropped.
- Work: a piece is judged when its own inputs move (its blob held or released, its own-store row, each source's `known` row of it, where S's sources are: `Ground::reached`, the views alone); a body's judgement reads its roots alone. A piece never reads a source's S-wide rows (head, full): read at their absent-bucket stamp, every removal hashing to that bucket would judge every piece of S again. A small edit to a large value judges its new nodes and chunks and decodes each new node once at each node, the same at n and 10n pieces (`pieces::a_small_edit_to_a_large_value_costs_its_new_pieces`, `read::an_edit_judges_its_new_pieces_alone`). An absent store row (a tombstone, a delete seen), or any row after an open, reads its hash bucket's stamp, so a removal re-judges about a 64th of the pieces reading such rows (issues/absent-stamp-fanout.md). A source of S joining or leaving judges every piece of S, dormant ones included; repair's reads per piece are point reads (deletes seen per source), never a scan.

Work per change: a source's event judges that body at that source; a reader source joining judges nothing; a writer's store source joining judges what its stream shows; a non-writer's, each must-hold body (counted at n and 10n sources in `read.rs` `work_follows_sources`).

## The ground in a node (`read/live.rs`)
- Engine facade: `commit_feed` (`commit_ids` at the first settle), `read_facts`, `body_fall_mark`, `commit_blobs` against the store for own entries and tombstones, `blob_dropped` (an entry whose blob holds a part dropped here for good, purged, past its bounds, expired or destroyed, is must-not-hold itself; its rest is logged again as a fresh encryption; a part dropped as covered or while invalid does not count), `publishes_reach`, `rewrap` (a held shared blob of the commit, not tombstoned under S and holding nothing dropped, logged verbatim), else a fresh encryption; stamped where this node writes S's head, else deposited; `sync_holder`, `closure_whole` (S's thing settled).
- Other modules: sources per (S, source) from opens' moves (`read/views.rs`, stamped and fed), head and full from streams' `known`, what each source showed per blob mapped to commits by `commit_blobs`/`blob_commit` (a blob never opened or sealed here names no commit until opened), status's `heard_out`, the store's deletes seen of S at any source (`deletes_known`, brute force on the rare path of forgetting; streams writes one at each Deleted event and each refusal as tombstoned).
- Deposits: a deposited own entry is offered by inbox; an entry stamped under the source's head write key (the writer's, kept as pulled, included) is pulled as it is; else a fresh encryption is logged deposited. Nothing is deposited at a source on an earlier step of the recorded path (`judge::at_source`, `behind`): this node registers the path there first, and what it holds under a later step's stamp is taken once the source stands there; without it every head move deposited each body at each store not yet moved. Role commits are deposited there all the same: registering waits on the source holding those the step needs, and this node's copy may be stamped under no step the source holds (a later head's stamp taken by `new_stamp`, a deposit), so the wait would never end without a writer. A Register refused there (`register::Progress::stuck`) leaves the rest waiting until the source's step moves. A reader takes a writer's restamp of an entry it holds when applying the event in stream order (`streams::pull` `new_stamp`, `Store::take_stamp`, judged by the recorded head, `streams::Ground::head_write`): the store's served path may still be the old one.
- **Fresh encryptions** (logging again, a deposit, a body with no held encryption): `Live::fresh` alone, which hands `Engine::seal_body` every blob of the commit the store holds bytes of, each with an entry's wrap; the facade decides (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, notes/sync-facade.md Sealing). `None` makes nothing now; a body is must-hold only while `may_log` holds, which mirrors `rewrap` and `seal_body`. The harness checks at every `World::run` quiet that each node holds an entry of every body it judges must-hold where a reader can pull S and the body is not missing (`World::unlogged_must_holds`), and that it reaches every reader of S connected to it, caught up there and able to open its entry (`World::unreached_must_holds`: held with no part of its body missing, or fetched and parked). A part sealed below the reader's floor counts no part missing (spec/sync-judgement.md §1, `rotation::floors::sealed_below_floor`, applied in `read_facts` alone): it stays in the body, so covered, carriers and purges judge the body as every reader does (not dropped from the body: a smaller body let a floor holder judge covered, and delete, a body other readers must hold). A wrong may-hold at every holder shows as a reader unlike the reference in the convergence differential (`Ground::may_log` forced false fails it).
- A facade call failing mid-settle fails the node's step (every connection closes). A call answering `NotBuilt` makes read do nothing for that part.

## Engine facts read needs (`read_facts`, `body_facts`)
- `must_not`: `Purged`, `CardSuperseded` (a later valid card commit of the node on the same aspect with a blob opened or sealed here holding that part) or `CardEnded` (`node.0`/`reach.0` of a node that held D and has no live read grant on it now, `until` read on the memo clock). A card is judged by its header, before any body.
- Strict subset and Neither need the blobs held at a place, so they are facade calls of their own (`strict_subset(s, b, held)`, `neither(s, c, there)`) over `blob_only` (the body's deltas kept or live and established uncovered; `None` while not established, which keeps). A blob counts only as opened or sealed here, of `c`, of the body's kind (copy or shared), holding no part dropped for good. Read drops an own entry that is a strict subset of another own entry, deletes one at a store source beside its superset there, and relays to a source unless `neither` over what the source shows present.
- **Unnamed copies** (spec/sync-judgement.md §2, judged by the deltas a blob holds): a certificate copy of a body S names as a certificate, holding deltas none of which S's certificates name (an account's rotation once no longer among its current `keys.0`), leaves the own store by Unlog once the outside wait is final, in the judgement that logs the body's own copy (a header-only one) fresh; at a store source it is deleted only beside another entry of the commit there, so a source never holds neither (`judge::unnamed_copies`, read from `Ground::blob` and the body's meta, no facade call). Readers reaching that pass together each log one copy: same bodies in different blobs (issues/racing-fresh-copies.md).
- The "being fetched or pending" half of established is sync's and not read: leaving it out widens `blob_only`, which keeps for Neither (relays more) and drops a strict subset sooner, harmless as its superset is held and opened here.
- **Blob rows** (`Engine::blob_of`, `BlobOf`, the engine's `bp/` rows): what this node checked of a blob: the parts it opened and checked against the header, and the digest of every other section (not opened, or holding a part whose thing's record is not held: `unchecked`). Duplicates group by (parts, sealed digests) and a strict subset needs its sealed digests included in the larger's, so a forged or omitted section a node cannot check never makes it delete the genuine blob (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Only what the node checked). An `unchecked` row's commit is named for redelivery when it stops pending (`apply::unpend`): its entries open again and the row is rewritten; a blob opening bad loses its row.
- Cost of that rule: two genuine encryptions of a commit whose unopened sections differ in bytes (a section encrypted again by a reader) are no duplicates to a node that cannot open them; they stay until a delete-key holder that opens them judges (none may: then until expiry, purge or a cover). Their number is that of fresh encryptions (only where every held blob is tombstoned under S, a non-writer's deposit, or none held), each charged to S's caps; a forger's variants stay deposits on its own budget, a writer stamping at most one per commit.
- Deposit promotion (`inbox::held::adds_delta`) is sealed-aware by the same rows: a deposit whose unopened sections no one stamped entry covers byte for byte is promoted, one such per commit while it stands (spec/sync-hostile.md §2 states the slot a forger can hold). Not sealed-aware: a shared blob beside a per-sync-ID copy goes only with the same unopened sections (rare: a role commit's shared blob holds its thing's section alone).
- A fresh encryption (`seal_body`) copies held sections this node opened before ones it did not (`held_sections`), and its row counts a section copied unopened by its bytes even where the node holds its parts from another blob: a forger's section never enters a fresh blob counted as checked.
- Strict subset compares blobs of the body's kind only: a certificate copy beside the shared blob holds parts S's readers may not open there, however much of it this node opens.
- `sole_certificate` is always false (the store's to judge); `body_facts.kept` and `covered` move with the memo clock when a keep bound passes.

## Not built
- issues/sync-unbuilt.md (read): freed-link waits on a node blob not held (`Engine::freed_wants`) are not fetched, and a start judges no waiting row (`judge_freed_page` has no caller); freed deletes do not wait on role entries first; a thing leaving a group leaves its pieces under the old sync ID; a non-writer's pieces reach a store source only as deposits.
- issues/sync-unbuilt.md (read): `SrcView::role_unheld` is always false (deletes toward a source do not wait for a role commit it lacks); level-key deletes (freed links, spec/sync-compaction.md §1); a peer's entry counting as lacking for its wrap (Duplicates, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Wraps); HN_fallany (a snapshot arriving invalid moves the fall mark as one held valid falling does) lives in the engine's `fall_mark`, untested end to end.
