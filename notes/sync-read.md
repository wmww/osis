# Sync: read
> Summary: osis-sync's `read` module (core/sync/src/read/): judgement of every body under a sync ID this node reads, what it calls for in the own store and at each source keeping S unread, destruction; what its ground reads of the engine and of other modules, how work follows change, the engine facts it needs, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§5, spec/sync-compaction.md §1, spec/sync-set.md §3. Models: core/test/tla/net/NetHold.tla, NetDestroy.tla. Principle and per-judgement outline: the module doc of core/sync/src/read/mod.rs. Overview: notes/sync.md. Design reasons for the convergence rule: notes/sync-convergence.md.

## What it owns
`Effects` for bodies per commit; the own judgement per (S, commit): verdict and own-store acts; what a body calls for per (S, commit, source keeping S unread); each piece per (S, piece blob) (`read/pieces.rs`); the destruction per S; a memo `Clock` (expiries move judgements; `next_wake`), and three stamped maps (verdicts, own role commits S must hold, S told destroyed), which status and inbox read (`Read::verdict`). Nothing durable of its own: stands live in the store's record rows (`s`, the head and the fall mark), so a start judges every held commit once. `on_message` takes the answers to read's own requests (Delete, deposits).

`judge.rs` holds the pure judgements (`body`, `toward`, `doom`) over `Ground` values; `Read::settle` runs them inside its effects to a fixpoint, then acts on what a new judgement adds. A restart or a reconnect (a new `ConnId` in each act) carries each act out once more, never in a loop; logging again is bounded by the durable stand alone. Tests drive `settle_on` with a fake ground (core/sync/tests/it/read.rs).

## Acts
- **Own store**: logs must-hold bodies with no entry standing as its log while a reader can pull S (a held blob verbatim, else a fresh encryption; a body counting missing only once held again), drops entries must-not-hold or covered and dropped (no delete key; never a must-hold one), lifts own tombstones of a body missing again.
  - Standing (`Ground::stands`): every entry of the body under S held here, an entry streams kept as it came included (notes/sync-store.md, A reader's store of S), so a reader logs nothing of its own for a body it pulled; but where this node holds S's head write key, a peer's entry stands only stamped under it, else it is logged again verbatim (stamped in place, `log_own`'s verbatim path). Own entries stand whatever the head (no restamping on a head move).
- **Fetching again** (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies): a body counting missing (`facts.missing`) opens again each entry of it under S the store still holds (`Act::Take`: a body dropped as invalid keeps its entries, verdict may-hold, so its bytes are here and no fetch would bring them), and calls for `Act::Fetch` of the others at each source of S (readers included) whose stream shows one; read hands them out of `settle` and the driver gives each to `Streams::fetch_again`, which queues a Fetch outside any message (a stream past the entry never announces it again). The judgement reads `sources(s)` only while the body is missing, so a source joining asks it too. The facade's `sync_bodies` lists missing parts (`Engine::bodies_under` with `missing`), else a body dropped whole (as invalid, then vouched for by an addendum) has no body under S and is never judged.
- **Toward each source keeping S unread**, keyed by what its stream wrote (`seen_added`) and, where this node is no writer at its head, every must-hold body: deletes (its head's delete key; carriers in its `known`, a full source counting one it lacks; a covered-and-dropped body not before `BodyFacts::hosts_from`, `MAX_DRIFT` past its keep bound, read through the memo clock so it wakes then; duplicates by delta set and copy; never a body dropped as invalid), deposits, logs again once per ground; deletes and loggings again wait on role entries first.
- **Destruction**: told (sticky); emptying deletes at a source holding every request or past their expiry; a destroying request under S told is may-hold whatever else holds of it (its part on the thing dropped here included: it leaves at its expiry, never, for a purge without `expires`); forgets once every source is heard out with nothing known (`Status::heard_out_or_alone`: at once with no peer sharing S, as the lifecycle duties read it), at once with no delete key, or untold past the requests' expiry (deleting nothing). Open gaps: issues/destroyed-sync-id-never-left.md.
- **Steps**: per S read here, one `Effects` key (`steps`): `Ground::step_ready` (the engine's `due_step` and each rotation it registers logged stamped in the own store) then `issue_step`, tried at each re-judge while ready (a role blocked here issues nothing until another rotation). No connection is needed; Register carries the recorded step to stores.
- **Record blobs**: per S, one fresh record blob of the records S's certificates need that no own record blob carries (notes/sync-facade.md, Certificate bodies). A record blob names no commit, so it is never judged by body: it stays while S lives, and under S told destroyed (emptying, spec/sync-judgement.md §2) the own ones drop (`DropBlob`) and none is logged, else the own store never empties and S is never forgotten.
- Between readers nothing but streams.

## Pieces and freed links
- **Pieces** (spec/sync-judgement.md §1: a body's chunk, tree-node, page and header-list blobs go under S as the same blobs, filler wrap): one `Effects` key per (S, piece blob), judged alone (`judge::piece`), in `read/pieces.rs`. A must-hold body's judgement gives its roots (`Engine::piece_roots(c, S's parts)`); each root, and each kid of an acting held node (`Engine::piece_below`, a memo per link: a node is immutable), is linked by what links it (`Linker`: a body, or the node above). A held one with no own entry (and no tombstone: never logged again by ID) is logged (`log_piece`: a peer's entry of it becomes own and is uncharged, else a new entry with a filler wrap, stamped where this node writes S's head, else deposited). A missing one held as a peer's store entry is taken by the engine (`Engine::take_piece`; the engine reads only what it holds, so a piece arriving before its commit parks as an ordinary entry that did not open until the commit's judgement takes it); else it is fetched where a source's stream shows it. A reader pulls every entry of S anyway, so this fetch matters only for a piece shown after the pull passed it.
  - Keyed by blob, not link: an edit's new node can link a reused chunk by another link (another delete-key claim).
  - Dormant: a held piece nothing links stays followed, acting on nothing, until its blob is released (then dropped); linked again, it wakes with its kids and takes its current acts (`done` per piece: the acts taken since it woke) with no judgement. Without it a reader holding an edit's body before its new root (the usual order) drops the old tree and judges it all again when the root arrives. Bound: one key per (S, held piece some must-hold body under S linked here), so replaced bodies' pieces leave as the engine releases them (`read::replaced_bodies_leave_no_pieces_followed_once_released_spec_sync_judgement_1`); a restart follows only what must-hold bodies link.
- **Repair** (spec/sync-ids.md §1): a held piece some source shows deleted (a Deleted event, or a refusal as tombstoned) and none shows present, once S's sources are heard out (`heard_out`: every entry it can open applied), is lost (`Act::Repair`, deletes and `heard_out` read only once no source shows it). Read repairs it in each own body over it (walking `Linker`s up; `facts.own`, a snapshot included; `Judged::repairs`), but not by way of a repair over a blob it encrypted afresh (`facts.repair_fresh`), each body's lost pieces in one `Engine::relink(pieces, commit's things)`; again when a linker joins a piece already lost (a body coming to link it). Relink is idempotent (the rewritten position's tree no longer holds the piece). Reading of the spec: "its own store no longer holds it" is dropped (spec edited): the own store keeps the piece, and its stream shows it present to readers, but a store source that deleted it refuses it by ID for good, so only a fresh encryption reaches readers that pull through stores.
  - Relink writes the aspect's live value where its tree reaches a lost blob: current state, so Passing on's floors clause binds it not (`carried_passes_on`, floor `None`; the departures clause does); a blob only replaced versions reach is never written (spec/sync-ids.md §1).
  - Bound: relink marks its commit a repair with the blobs it encrypted afresh (`Meta::repair`: those its things' live trees reach after it and not before; record head origin byte 2, then the blob IDs), and those are never repaired by way of it; the chunks it reused are, as any own commit's (else, once the original own commit drops, a later purge of a reused chunk is never repaired). What a repair encrypts afresh only that commit links, so a source deleting it is refusing, not a purger that missed a reuse; without the mark a store refusing each re-encryption forced one commit per refusal. Each round re-encrypts other chunks, so repairs are bounded by the pieces of the value's tree per purge that missed a reuse. The mark is written in a second transaction after the commit (a crash between allows one more repair). Not reported in status beyond the source's `refusing`.
  - A connected reader source showing the piece present suppresses repair while it stays connected, though readers that reach only the deleting store then lack it (the spec's "none shows it present").
- **Freed links** (spec/sync-compaction.md §1): one `Effects` key per judged freed row (`freed_feed`, all rows at the first settle). `judge::freed`: under each read S carrying the thing (`Engine::carrying`), the own entry (own or a peer's) leaves (`drop_piece`, tombstoned) and each store source whose stream shows it gets a Delete (`delete_freed`: S's head delete key when the engine held its bytes and they bore the link, else by level). Reader sources do not count: a reader judges its own entries. Once no own or store-source entry is known, `Engine::freed_done` and the key is dropped.
- Work: a piece is judged when its own inputs move (its blob held or released, its own-store row, each source's `known` row of it, where S's sources are: `Ground::reached`, the views alone); a body's judgement reads its roots alone. A piece never reads a source's S-wide rows (head, full): read at their absent-bucket stamp, every removal hashing to that bucket would judge every piece of S again. A small edit to a large value judges its new nodes and chunks and decodes each new node once at each node, the same at n and 10n pieces (`pieces::a_small_edit_to_a_large_value_costs_its_new_pieces`, `read::an_edit_judges_its_new_pieces_alone`). An absent store row (a tombstone, a delete seen), or any row after an open, reads its hash bucket's stamp, so a removal re-judges about a 64th of the pieces reading such rows (issues/absent-stamp-fanout.md). A source of S joining or leaving judges every piece of S, dormant ones included; repair's reads per piece are point reads (deletes seen per source), never a scan.

Work per change: a source's event judges that body at that source; a reader source joining judges nothing; a writer's store source joining judges what its stream shows; a non-writer's, each must-hold body (counted at n and 10n sources in `read.rs` `work_follows_sources`).

## The ground in a node (`read/live.rs`)
- Engine facade: `commit_feed` (`commit_ids` at the first settle), `read_facts`, `body_fall_mark`, `commit_blobs` against the store for own entries and tombstones, `blob_dropped` (an entry whose blob holds a part dropped here for good, purged, past its bounds, expired or destroyed, is must-not-hold itself; its rest is logged again as a fresh encryption; a part dropped as covered or while invalid does not count), `publishes_reach`, `rewrap` (a held shared blob of the commit, not tombstoned under S and holding nothing dropped, logged verbatim), else a fresh encryption; stamped where this node writes S's head, else deposited; `sync_holder`, `closure_whole` (S's thing settled).
- Other modules: sources per (S, source) from opens' moves (`read/views.rs`, stamped and fed), head and full from streams' `known`, what each source showed per blob mapped to commits by `commit_blobs`/`blob_commit` (a blob never opened or sealed here names no commit until opened), status's `heard_out`, the store's deletes seen of S at any source (`deletes_known`, brute force on the rare path of forgetting; streams writes one at each Deleted event and each refusal as tombstoned).
- Deposits: a deposited own entry is offered by inbox; an entry stamped under the source's head write key (the writer's, kept as pulled, included) is pulled as it is; else a fresh encryption is logged deposited.
- **Fresh encryptions** (logging again, a deposit, a body with no held encryption): `Live::fresh` alone, which hands `Engine::seal_body` every blob of the commit the store holds bytes of, each with an entry's wrap; the facade decides (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, notes/sync-facade.md Sealing). `None` makes nothing now; the body is judged again when keys move its facts.
- A facade call failing mid-settle fails the node's step (every connection closes). A call answering `NotBuilt` makes read do nothing for that part.

## Engine facts read needs (`read_facts`, `body_facts`)
- `must_not`: `Purged`, `CardSuperseded` (a later valid card commit of the node on the same aspect with a blob opened or sealed here holding that part) or `CardEnded` (`node.0`/`reach.0` of a node that held D and has no live read grant on it now, `until` read on the memo clock). A card is judged by its header, before any body.
- Strict subset and Neither need the blobs held at a place, so they are facade calls of their own (`strict_subset(s, b, held)`, `neither(s, c, there)`) over `blob_only` (the body's deltas kept or live and established uncovered; `None` while not established, which keeps). A blob counts only as opened or sealed here, of `c`, of the body's kind (copy or shared), holding no part dropped for good. Read drops an own entry that is a strict subset of another own entry, deletes one at a store source beside its superset there, and relays to a source unless `neither` over what the source shows present.
- The "being fetched or pending" half of established is sync's and not read: leaving it out widens `blob_only`, which keeps for Neither (relays more) and drops a strict subset sooner, harmless as its superset is held and opened here.
- Strict subset compares blobs of the body's kind only: a certificate copy beside the shared blob holds parts S's readers may not open there, however much of it this node opens.
- `sole_certificate` is always false (the store's to judge); `body_facts.kept` and `covered` move with the memo clock when a keep bound passes.

## Not built
- issues/sync-unbuilt.md (read): freed-link waits on a node blob not held (`Engine::freed_wants`) are not fetched, and a start judges no waiting row (`judge_freed_page` has no caller); freed deletes do not wait on role entries first; a thing leaving a group leaves its pieces under the old sync ID; a non-writer's pieces reach a store source only as deposits.
- issues/sync-unbuilt.md (read): `SrcView::role_unheld` is always false (deletes toward a source do not wait for a role commit it lacks); level-key deletes (freed links, spec/sync-compaction.md §1); a peer's entry counting as lacking for its wrap (Duplicates, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Wraps); HN_fallany (a snapshot arriving invalid moves the fall mark as one held valid falling does) lives in the engine's `fall_mark`, untested end to end.
