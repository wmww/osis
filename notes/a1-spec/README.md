## Where the model overrode the design notes
Each is written as the model has it.
1. **Dispute candidates** (`disputes`): every held edge write giving below admin at its `ts` on a grant whose holder is not its signer, not trusted and not dropped by a trusted write, by its seeds or by its drops. notes/write-path-design.md §1 says "a held lowering of another holder's edge ... not dropped by a trusted write's seeds". The model also takes self-removals through a holder thing; the draft does not (OPEN 12).
2. **The remaining admin's own edge** (`refused`): the edge (Ak, T) (the signer for an empty path), read whether or not it is a hop of the path: for a path reaching T by a descent of two steps or more, Ak's direct grant on T where Ak holds one. The design says "the last edge of the write's own path"; the draft reads only a hop of the path (OPEN 9).
3. **The last-admin rule is read only by a part without a verdict**, before its drops (`refused` is skipped for `Verdict::Applied`; it reads the pass's state at the write). The design leaves both unsaid (OPEN 5).
4. **A vouch is an edge-class part of the walk** (`edge_class`, `order`): it applies iff its path gives admin on its thing at its key, is ordered by key, and is put in question and dropped by lowerings of its path's edges like any part. Today's spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches orders a vouch by its position and asks "W could write c" at each later lowering of W; the walk gives both.
5. **Re-grant from each edge's first chained write** (`Regrant::FirstChained`, `first_chained` along stated paths, strictly after its key). notes/account-membership-design.md §4 says so; today's spec/permissions.md §5.2 reads the first point the walk gave the role.
6. **Edge as restored is a role** (`Pass::restored`): the highest role since the last explicit write, the record edge admin from the start. Today's spec joins role, invite and `until`; invites are PENDING MODEL.
7. **Seeds and drops reach a self-removal of the lowered edge** (`leave_in_question`): today's spec/permissions.md §5.1 Seeds exception ("except a write lowering its author's own edge") goes.
8. **Only a lowering taking its edge from admin drops edge-class parts**; a cut from write drops nothing in the walk and cuts content only (`walk`: `before == Role::Admin`; `content`: `cuts`).
9. **The writers' list** (`Sim::stand`): where R, trusted, applies with everything received it lists everything; else the content, then the role writes one at a time in receipt order (ties by commit ID), each kept iff R still applies. The design says "leaving out a role write with which R ... would be void".
10. **Self-removal by path end**: a signer leaving by itself states the empty path and no edge (`World::push`: `leave`).

## Open
Each is a question with its options; *taken* marks what the draft writes meanwhile, **settles** what closes it. The model runs named are roles-model `paths` changes (one rule or world each, `Rules::fixed` held at the gate's size, with a control failing without it).
1. **What a removal's result names** (notes/path-model-findings.md 19): the members a kept lowering of the remover by X dropped, beside those X lowered. (a) Only X's own lowerings (*taken*: no spec text; it is the `osis` result): a co-admin's loss is not shown. (b) Also every part a lowering the list keeps dropped: the remover's walk computes it, one diff of two readings. (c) The writers' rule leaves out a lowering of the remover even where R applies with it: reopens the key-order class (control `-key-order`). The spec is unaffected in (a) and (b). **Settles**: the coordinator, at brief 5 (the `osis` result); (a) is safe to build on, (b) adds to it later without a spec change.
2. **The void check's cost** (notes/path-model-findings.md 17): k void lowerings at the end of a chain of k grants cost k² setters read, all forced by one party's own 2k role writes. (a) Cap nothing and state the cost (*taken*: the spec caps nothing; brief 7 counts it). (b) Memoize chain reads across checks with the same drops on the chain: linear for this attack, not in general; an implementation choice, no spec text. (c) A cap on the depth `keeps` reads: the verdict would depend on the cap, which notes/arbitrary-values.md rejects. **Settled** as (a), (b) left to the implementation.
3. **Detecting disputes at a node that trusts nothing** (notes/path-model-findings.md Open 0): the rule is a walk with each candidate trusted, so k held candidates cost k walks of their component per change. (a) The spec defines the dispute and leaves when it is computed to the node (*taken*). (b) Narrow the candidates to lowerings the walk leaves without a verdict or applies with drops that dropped a lowering or a grant a lowering applied under (the classes of findings 16 and 21). (c) Compute only on demand, when a thing's roles are shown: a fight on a thing nobody views is never reported, against spec/requirements.md Permissions ("reports the dispute"). Never part of validity in any option. **Settles**: a model run for (b) (`disputes` narrowed, property `dispute` at full size: every reach-back still shown; control: the unnarrowed rule giving the same set); until then the engine computes (a) per change for candidates on things in its node closure, counted in brief 7.
4. **The adminless reading at untrusting nodes** (`a_lowerings_check_does_not_read_what_its_drops_free`, one `tiny` world in 1,000,000): a lowering's check does not see what its drops free, so A can be left with no admin where nothing is trusted, shown as a dispute whose trust restores an admin. (a) Accept, stated (*taken*: nothing in the draft; a fight of admins, which spec/requirements.md Permissions leaves to trust sets). (b) Replay from the earliest drop before giving the verdict: a walk per check. (c) Read the last-admin rule again after the drops: circular with the verdict. **Settled** as (a).
5. **A remaining admin taken by the lowering's own drops**: the rule reads the walk before the drops, so a lowering naming B, whose grant it drops, applies and may leave no standing admin; only the writer's MUST covers it. (a) As modelled (*taken*). (b) Add the remaining-admin edge to `keeps(L)`: L void where its drops take the admin it names: one more edge per check. **Settles**: model run, before brief 3 (Modeller's briefs).
6. **Path bytes**: *taken* and settled: the form follows the count of things written, so a decoder checks it and a writer has no choice: one thing, its `Path` in the clear; several, a salted hash each, the Path in its sections. One form per count is the fewest cases; the common commit writes one thing and pays no hash or salt. Several things in one `Path` in the clear would show each reader of one thing the path of the others (a role fact about a thing it may not read), so it is not offered. The hash is `kdf("path", salt, Path)` with a 16-byte random salt, so a reader of one thing cannot test guesses at another's path; a node keeping the unit unread opens no blob and learns nothing of either.
7. **Where the remaining admin goes**: *taken* and settled: (a) header field `remains` at 17, `flags` to 18: positional, one presence rule, one byte (`[]`) on every commit. (b) after `flags` costs the same byte (flags no longer optional) and one more case; (c) in the `roles.0` value: a removal has none; (d) in `Path`: it is per target, not per thing written.
8. **Header-list chunks' delete key**: *taken* and settled: (b) an empty group. Today's spec already has the case (a commit listing no level-2 key, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Delete: such a blob goes by the delete key or at its expiry); a header list lives and dies with its header, which no purge removes. (a) the signer's level-2 key is not in the header where the signer is no purge author, so a decoder could not check it. **Owed**: brief 4 checks that nothing deletes a forgotten commit's header-list chunks by level 2 (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting, spec/sync-ids.md §6).
9. **The remaining admin by descent** (override 2): *taken*: the path's last edge is its final hop where that is one edge from Ak to T (Ak's direct grant, or T's record edge where Ak is its mint admin); a descent of two or more steps has none, and the write names its remaining admin. The model reads Ak's direct grant on T even where the path descends past it: that edge is in no certificate (§4 Chain certificates) and no evidence (`evidence_edges`), so a reader without its writes would judge otherwise; the draft's reading avoids it. (b) read the last hop (T's record edge, from T's mint admin). **Settles**: model run, before brief 3 (Modeller's briefs).
10. **A commit with several lowerings** (spec/commits.md §2, a removal of several holders on one target, none on an edge of its own path): not modelled. *Taken*: each applies or not by the state before the commit; each lowering is judged with the commit's earlier writes in effect, the earlier ones' drops in D and passed over by its `keeps`; one verdict per lowering; the pass ends once the whole commit has taken effect; each reads its remaining admin before the commit, so two lowerings may take each other's (the writer's MUST covers it). **Settles**: model run, before brief 3 (Modeller's briefs).
11. **"Its actor's secrets"** in spec/commits.md §4 Examined again and Hostile input: settled. The engine awaits no such input (core/engine `waits::Wake`: `Moved` for a reach or an epoch, `Epoch` for an account's chain); the draft drops the words.
12. **Self-removals as dispute candidates** (override 1): the model takes a held self-removal through a holder (holder not its signer) as a candidate, and trusting it seeds D with every unlisted write through the edge, so an honest leave is shown as a dispute: H grants A2 admin on T1 along [A], K grants Z write along [A2], K removes A2 from T1 by self-removal naming A: `disputes` gives the leave (checked by a probe, not on main). A self-removal carries no frontier and is never trusted (§5.1 Trust set). *Taken*: candidates are explicit lowerings only. **Settles**: model run (`disputes` skipping `leave`; the probe world as a `quiet` hand test; property `dispute` at full size: nothing shown before is lost but leaves).

## Modeller's briefs (OPEN 5, 9, 10)
- **5, remaining admin in `keeps`**: rule: `keeps(L)` also requires the remaining-admin edge L relies on (its last edge, else the named holder's) to keep admin past L's drops, else L is void. Property: no unmarked applied lowering leaves its target with no standing admin at nodes whose trusted writes apply (a new hand world: H removes X naming B, listing nothing, where X granted B; and the property in the story). Negative control: the rule off (`as modelled`) leaves A adminless in that world; the story's other properties hold under both.
- **9, last edge by descent**: give the story a holder on a dependent's mint chain two steps up that also holds a direct grant on the dependent (A on D1 by descent, A's direct grant on D1). Rule: the last edge is a hop of the path only. Property: the alike property (a reader holding one unit's evidence reads as a member) with that grant's writes absent from the reader's evidence. Negative control: the model's current `refused`, reading (Ak, T) off the path, splits reader from member there.
- **10, several lowerings per commit**: let `Commit` carry several edge writes on one target (holders other than its path's edges), judged as the draft says (step 2, step 3, `keeps` passing over the earlier lowerings' drops). Property: no applied commit's path loses its authority to the commit's own drops (a hand world: two lowerings each dropping one of two grants the path's last edge rests on); the story's properties with the theft's removal of H and B made one commit. Negative control: `keeps` passing over each lowering's own drops alone applies that hand world with its path's authority gone.

## Why MAX_PATH_THINGS is 64
A resource bound against hostile input (class R, notes/arbitrary-values.md): past it a writer refuses the path and a decoder rejects the header, alike everywhere. At 0 no write could go through a holder, so the bound must be at least the deepest nesting of holders anyone builds: a few levels (account, organization, team). Unbounded, one commit's path costs every reader a point query per hop (each up to a descent of `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH` record edges) and, while unjudgeable, a wait on a record per listed thing: a junk path of n made-up things parks a commit on n records, n set by the writer alone. 64 keeps a path's hops, and so a part's waits and point queries, at most 64 (each hop's descent bounded on its own by `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH`) and a `Path` at most about 1 KiB; it hurts only a holder nested 65 deep, which no design here builds. Any value well above real nesting serves; 64 matches `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH`, the like bound on a record chain.

## Bytes per commit
A tid is 15 bytes, 16 encoded. Today's `author` and `actor` are 32 bytes. A1's `signer`, `path` and `remains` for a commit writing one thing: signer 16, `path` `[i, A1..Ak]` 2 + 16k, `remains` `[]` 1: 19 bytes with the empty path (every commit at A1, accounts unchanged: 13 fewer than today), 35 with one listed thing (an account, from A4: 3 more), 51 with two (19 more). A commit writing n ≥ 2 things pays 1 + 34n header bytes of hashes and, in each of its sections, `Salted` of 20 + 16k bytes inside the ciphertext.
