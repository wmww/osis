## 1. Concepts
- **Signer** (in place of Actor): every commit is signed by one key actor, its **signer** (spec/wire.md §4), and acts on each thing it writes along the path it states for it (Path, below): its right rests on that path's edges alone (§5). Any thing may hold roles.
  - *Stands*: Key actor.
  - An **account** P is a key actor with **epochs**, its creation record marked so (spec/wire.md §3): its keys change by rotations of its **signing chain** (spec/crypto.md §3, §9; §5.4, §5.5). A node signs as itself only the commits on what is its own: its node thing's aspects (`keys.0`, `node.0`, `reach.0`, §4), its node root and the things whose record chain reaches the node through it (local, under no sync ID: spec/sync-set.md §1, §2); every other commit a node makes is signed by its account.
  - *Stands*: Node, Account root, Profile, Inbox.
  - Organizations, bots and shared identities are ordinary things with rosters; an account holding a role on one acts through it by listing it on its path.
  - *Stands*: A link's actor.
- **Path**: for each thing T it writes, a commit states a **path**: a list of things [A1, …, Ak], 0 ≤ k ≤ MAX_PATH_THINGS (spec/wire.md §4: the commit's one `Path` for every thing, or T's entry of a `Split`), and the commit has one purge author (§6). Its **end** is Ak (S where k = 0). The **members** for T are [S, A1, …, Ak, T]; where T is the end, [S, A1, …, Ak] (so one `Path` writes its end, what the end holds a direct grant on and what descends from it by record edges); for a self-removal (§2) [S, A1, …, Ak], ending at the holder of the grant it lowers; for a delta of a key actor on itself along the empty path, [S] (no hop: a thing holds admin on itself; along any other path on itself S is named twice). A part whose members name a thing twice applies nowhere and is invalid. DRAFT (notes/path-form.md): the end reading and the split form.
  - **Hops**: hop j joins members j and j + 1. Consecutive members p, q are joined by **descent** where p is on q's record chain (§1 Record edge) and the path does not mark the hop **direct**: the record edges from p down to q, in order; else by their direct edge, p's grant on q. A descent of one step is the record edge (p, q), which is also p's direct edge on q. The path's **edges** are its hops in order. A record edge is an edge like any (*Stands*: §1 Record edge): a descent rests on each of its record edges as it stands at the part's key, so one through a removed record edge gives nothing.
  - **Direct mark** (spec/wire.md §4 `Path`, the trailing `marks`; a `Split` entry's alike): a mark says the hop uses p's direct edge on q where p is on q's record chain two or more record edges above it, so unmarked the hop would be a descent. Any hop may be marked (the final hop into T, or a far mint ancestor's direct grant on a listed holder). A mark on any other hop makes the part invalid, applying nowhere, as a member named twice does: p not on q's record chain, or q's mint admin (its record edge is its direct edge: the unmarked hop already means it), or a hop position the part's path does not have. So each list of edges has one statement: a marked and an unmarked path are both valid only where they state different edges, two claims resting on two routes, each judged along its own (p holding its direct edge and the descent standing is a party with two grants: a remover takes both). Which route a write rests on is the writer's statement, judged as stated; no reader checks the choice. A writer marks a hop exactly when the edge its write rests on there is the direct edge of a mint ancestor two or more steps up. A marked hop where p holds no direct edge on q gives nothing, so the part applies nowhere.
    - The path's last edge (below) of a final hop marked direct is that direct edge: the last-admin rule (§1 The last admin) and the path's invite (§2) read it. A self-removal's members end at the holder X: the grant it lowers is the write's own (X's direct grant on T, a far mint ancestor's included) and no hop, so no mark reaches it. In one `Path`, the mark at the hop past the end (position k) marks the final hop of every thing written one hop past Ak; the end Ak has no such hop, so its part is invalid where that mark is set (a writer then states the split form). The purge-author index and `MAX_PATH_THINGS` are unchanged: an index into the members, and listed things; marks add no member.
  - A path gives a role on T at a point when every edge of it gives at least that role there (its value in the walk, §5.1, an `until` judged at the part's `ts`). Its **last edge** is its final hop where that is one edge from Ak (S where k = 0) to T: Ak's direct edge on T (a final hop marked direct among them), or T's record edge where Ak is T's mint admin. A path reaching T by an unmarked descent of two steps or more, and a self-removal's, has none: a direct edge Ak also holds on T is no edge of the path, and no rule reads it for this write.
  - Nothing but the stated path judges a part: another path that would also give the role does not (§5).
- **Effective role** of a thing X on a thing at time t: the maximum over paths from X to the thing of the minimum role along the path, evaluated with the timeline (§5.1) at t. A path climbs role grants, record edges included while they stand; a thing holds admin on itself. It decides keys (§3), sync units and holders; validity reads stated paths only (§5).
- *Stands*: Standing admin.
- **The last admin**: an edge write that takes a standing admin edge of a thing T to anything else (removing it, giving it a role below admin, or an `until`), and whose commit does not carry the adminless mark (§2), applies only where it has a **remaining admin**: a standing admin edge of T other than the one it takes, as the pass stands at the write, its commit's earlier edge writes in effect (§5.1 step 2): its path's last edge (§1 Path) where that is one and standing, else the edge on T of the holder its commit's header names (spec/wire.md §4 `remains`). With neither it does not apply. A lowering judged in the walk (§5.1 step 3) is also void where its drops, or those of its commit's earlier lowerings, would leave its remaining admin edge standing no more (§5.1 step 4). A commit carrying the mark relies on no remaining admin.
  - A node MUST NOT sign an unmarked role commit that, in its own walk with the commit applied, leaves a thing that had a standing admin with none, nor one whose edge write the rule above stops. This covers every way an edge write can do so, on a dependent or an independent thing: removing or lowering the last standing admin's edge (below admin, or giving it an `until`), an admin's self-removal (§2), and a removal whose drops (§5.1) take the edge writes that made the last standing admins. A thing's destruction (spec/commits.md §5) and an `until` passing are no edge writes and are not covered.
  - A dependent thing left so stays dependent: its content stays readable through derivation by the mint admin's holders.
- *Stands*: With no admin left, and every other bullet of §1.

## 2. Role grant writes
- **Edge writes**: granting, changing and removing a grant are writes to the target's `roles.0`, each in its own role commit (spec/commits.md §2), along a path giving admin on the target (§1 Path; a finalize, below: write). An edge write that is not a self-removal, a finalize or an invite grant is **explicit**. *The sub-bullets stand.*
- **Finalize**, its second sentence: It applies (§5.1) iff the grant is past its `until` at the removal's `ts` (as the timeline orders it: a renewal ordered before it counts, and the finalize then ends a superseded write and applies nothing) and its path gives write on the target and a role there at least the grant's (a timed admin grant needs admin); else it applies nothing, whoever wrote it. It carries no frontier, audits nothing, leaves the edge as restored (§5.1) as it was, gets no verdict and is no dispute candidate; it rests on its path's edges, needing write there (§5.1 step 3). *The rest stands.*
- **Path's invite** (in place of effective invite): the invite a path gives on T is the minimum of the roles of its edges but the last and the last edge's invite. An edge's invite at a point is every invite where it gives admin there (a record edge, so a last edge by descent, among them), else its `invite` at most the role it gives.
- **Invite grant**, its lead: an edge write whose path does not give admin on T applies as an invite grant where it adds a grant for a thing with no current grant on T (an expired grant counts as none), its role below admin and its invite at most its role, and its path gives that role on each of its edges but the last and as an invite on the last; it carries the grantee's boxes for those roles. It carries no frontier, audits nothing, gets no verdict and raises the edge as restored (§5.1) to its role and invite. An edge write whose path gives admin on T is explicit, whatever it sets. *The rest stands.*
- *Settled* (Finalize, Path's invite, Invite grant): roles-model `paths` `walk` (its invite and finalize arms), `need`; `a_may_grant_grant_applies_by_its_paths_flag` (control `-invite-role`), `a_finalize_ends_an_ended_grant_and_a_may_grant_grant_follows_it`, `the_explicit_removal_after_a_finalize_audits_the_grants_made_by_flag`; `tiny_flags`.
- **Self-removal**: an edge write on grant (X, T) whose path ends at X (§1 Path): X is its last listed thing, or its signer for an empty path. It applies iff its path gives admin on X (a signer leaving by itself, by no edge) and it lowers: removes the grant, or sets no component of role, invite and `until` above the grant's value and no `floor` below it (an unchanged rewrite and a removal of an absent grant lower); one that does not lower applies nothing.
- A self-removal needs no authority on the target, carries no rotation (§3) and audits nothing (§5.1); it rests on the grant it lowers as on its path's edges (§5.1).
- **Adminless mark** (spec/wire.md §4 field 18, bit 1): a role commit's signer sets it on a commit it means to leave a thing with no standing admin (§1, The last admin): with it, the commit's edge writes rely on no remaining admin. It is part of the signed header, read by every node alike.
- **Logging a lowered grant**: a removal or lowering of a grant has no logging rule of its own. It is an edge write on every path with that edge, so it goes in the chain certificates of every commit stating such a path (§4), under each sync ID whose required set holds such a chain (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): wherever a write it judges goes; and, as every edge write, where its holder's role reaches (§4 Role changes travel).

## 4. Verifying signers: certificates
- A write's authority is checked along its stated path (§1 Path): each edge an edge write or a record edge (the tid commits to the mint admin), its signer a key actor whose tid commits to its public key; an account's commit also through its signing chain to the epoch it names (§5.4, §5.5). No secrets are needed.
- **Chained writes**: the held edge writes, taken in key order (§5.1 step 1), each chained when its members name no thing twice and every edge of its stated path is a record edge or set at admin by a chained write of an earlier commit (applied or not, never removed, at the role it sets, `until` disregarded). A chained write that is not a self-removal, a finalize or an invite grant sets its edge at the role and invite it sets. Every write the walk applies (§5.1) is chained, in every walk and at every node; the set depends on the held writes and records alone and only grows with them, so a write leaves no chain once in it, and one whose stated path no record or earlier chained write gives (a stranger's, one ordered before its signer's authority) enters none. An invite grant (§2) is also chained where its members name no thing twice, every edge of its path but the last is a record edge or set by a chained write of an earlier commit at its role or above, and the last is a record edge or set by one at an invite of its role or above (a write at admin sets every invite); it sets its edge at its role and invite. A self-removal is chained where every edge of its path is so set at admin, a finalize where at write; a removal or lowering as any write, at admin; none of them sets an edge here. *Settled*: roles-model `paths` `first_chained`; per part held-model `evidence::World::chained` (osis-perm `Paths::is_chained`, `chained::chained_parts_agree`).
- **Chain certificates** of a commit for a thing T it writes are built on the members of the path it states for T. They are:
  - for each member, its creation record (and those up its record chain), raw in a record blob (spec/wire.md §3), never a copy of the commit that minted it: a record outlives that commit's retention;
  - for each member, its **current `keys.0` commits**: its newest `keys.0` commit writing more than box records, the rotation commits of its newest keyset of each role, forks included, and the commits holding box records at those keysets' epochs (spec/crypto.md §4, Stale), each projected to `keys.0` (the newest may write no keyset, and a rotation of what it holds names its keyset, spec/crypto.md §4), with the `roles.0` deltas of the commits those keysets carry and their path chains (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions);
  - for each member that is an account, the rotations of its signing chain the node holds, every line, as header-only copies (§5.5, Chains travel; one among its current `keys.0` commits goes as those do), and their thing lists for things under the sync ID (§5.5): a commit at an epoch verifies by the signing key its minting rotation's header carries;
  - every chained write on an edge of that path, and on the edge of the holder its header names as the remaining admin (§1, The last admin), removals and lowerings included;
  - closed under paths: for each such write, its own chain certificates for its target (a self-removal's: for its holder).
- A commit's **path chain** (in place of its author's chain) for a thing it has a delta on is its chain certificates for that thing (an external fire's: its signer's record alone, §5.2 External fires): every chain that thing's validity reads. A body of the commit under a sync ID carries the path chains of the things of its parts, and no other (DRAFT, notes/path-form.md: where the paths differ, a thing's chain goes only where its parts do, so no reader receives another thing's chain). A commit whose path chain to the things it writes has not arrived is pending (spec/commits.md §4). An account signer's commit at an epoch whose minting rotation the node does not hold is pending (§5.5).

## 5. Validity
Validity is a deterministic function of the commits a node holds and its trust set (§5.1, Trust set) and side (§5.4). An account signer's commit first passes the epoch rule (§5.5); one that does not count there is judged by nothing below.

### 5.1 Layer 1: role timeline
The timeline covers the **edge-class parts**: edge writes (writes to `roles.0`). Every other role-class part (`secrets.0`, `keys.0`, `sync.0`) is judged like content (§5.2) by the role its aspect requires (§1, §3).
- **Rests on**: an edge-class part rests on each edge of its stated path (§1 Path); a self-removal of grant h rests also on h.
- **Lowering**: an explicit edge write (§2) on edge h whose value gives below admin at its `ts` (no grant, a role below admin, or an `until` at or before its `ts`). An explicit edge write whose value gives admin at its `ts` with an `until` after it is no lowering, whatever h held before (a standing admin given an end date, or an end shortened): it applies as a grant (step 2), carries no frontier and is no dispute candidate; §1 The last admin reads it.
- **Edge as restored** of h at a point of a pass: a role and an invite. Its role is the highest role of the values h has taken in the pass since the last explicit write on it (that write's value and the invite grants' since included), each read with its `until` disregarded (so what an `until`, a self-removal or a finalize took away since counts), and at least the role h gives there. Its invite is the highest invite of the same values, each read at its write's `ts` (a value's invite: every invite where it gives admin, else its `invite` at most the role it gives), and at least the invite h gives there. For a record edge no explicit write in the pass has set, both are admin. A lowering **falls** where its value at its `ts` is below h as restored just before it, in role or in invite.
- A lowering's **list** is its frontier (below); it **names** a part whose commit's ID is in it. Nothing else speaks for a role part a list does not name.

1. **Order**: order every held edge-class part, over every thing, by (`ts`, signer, commit ID): a part's **key** is its position in this order; a commit's parts are ordered by the header's aspect order, then by holder tid, bytewise, but an edge write setting an edge of the path the commit states for its target (a self-removal of the path's last thing, as in a single-use link redemption, or the record edge a descent ends with; with both, in holder order) comes after the commit's other parts on that target.
2. **Passes**: the walk is a run of passes. Each starts before every part, with each thing's record edge in force (§1) and no other edge, and takes the parts in key order, skipping the drop set D. D and a **verdict** for each lowering judged (applied or void) carry from pass to pass; nothing else does. In a pass, a part with no verdict **applies** iff its members name no thing twice and every edge of its path gives, at that point, what it needs, and an edge write passes the last-admin rule (§1):
   - an edge write: admin; an invite grant, its path's invite (§2); a self-removal, admin up to its holder, and it lowers (§2); a finalize, as §2 says.
   A lowering with verdict applied applies, unread by the last-admin rule: where its value is at or below its edge as restored in this pass, in role and in invite, whatever its path gives there; where it is above in either it would raise the edge, and applies only where every edge of its path gives admin there. One with verdict void is skipped. A commit's edge writes are judged one at a time in key order, each as a write of its own with the commit's earlier ones in effect: whether it applies (its path, its invite, the last-admin rule), and for a lowering what it takes, puts in question and keeps (step 3), read the pass after them, the drops of the commit's lowerings judged applied before it being in D. Since the part on an edge of the commit's path comes last, no part's path is changed by an earlier part of its commit.
3. **Judging a lowering**: a lowering L of edge h with no verdict that applies in a pass and falls is judged:
   - L **puts in question** every held edge-class part of another commit ordered before it (no list can name its own commit) that rests on h, is not in D, is not void, and whose **need** at h the edge as restored met and L's value does not. A part's need at h is what it applied by in this pass: admin; an invite grant its role as an invite where h is its path's last edge and as a role elsewhere; a finalize write. A part this pass has not applied needs the least it could apply by: an edge write that could apply as an invite grant (§2) an invite grant's need, a finalize write, any other admin. Its **drops** are those of them its list does not name, and every other edge-class part on the same thing of a commit one of them belongs to, not in D and not void: a commit's edge-class parts on a thing fall together, as a list keeps them together (a single-use redemption's leave falls with its grant).
   - L is **void** iff its drops would take its authority or its remaining admin: `keeps(L)` fails (step 4). Void, L does not apply and drops nothing.
   - Otherwise its verdict is **applied**: L applies; if it has drops they join D at once, and the pass ends once L's commit has taken effect (its later edge writes taken in turn); the next pass starts from the start.
   - A lowering that applies without falling, or does not apply, gets no verdict in that pass and audits nothing; a later pass may judge it. A lowering is judged at most once in a walk; each walk (and each redo for trust) starts with D at its seeds and no verdict.
4. **Keeps**: for the lowering L being judged, `keeps(W)` holds for L, or for a part W the pass applied before L, iff every edge e of W's path keeps admin for W: of the edge writes the pass applied on e ordered before W, latest first, pass over each of L's drops, each drop of the lowerings of L's commit judged applied before L in this pass, and each grant G (an edge write giving admin at its own `ts`, or a finalize; not a self-removal) for which `keeps(G)` fails; a self-removal giving admin at W's `ts` is read through (it lowers: e gives admin past it only where e gave admin before it), one giving less decides that e does not keep admin; the first other one left decides: e keeps admin iff it gives admin at W's `ts`. With none left, e keeps admin iff it is a record edge. Where L relies on a remaining admin edge r (§1 The last admin: its path's last edge where that stood at L, else the named holder's), `keeps(L)` also needs r to stay standing, read the same way along the edge writes the pass applied on r before L (its commit's earlier ones included; a self-removal read through where it gives admin with no `until`): the first one left gives admin with no `until`, or with none left r is a record edge.
5. **Settling**: a pass that adds nothing to D ends the walk; it is the **settled walk**. Nothing is ever undropped. D and the verdicts are rebuilt from scratch whenever the held set or the trust set changes. The timeline is the settled walk: every rule that reads whether an edge-class part applies, and the roles at a point, reads it.

- **Frontier**: every lowering R (an explicit write giving its edge h below admin at its `ts`, a grant at write or read included) embeds, as commit IDs, never counters, what its signer's node A had received (Received, spec/commits.md §4) as of R's `ts` T of the commits **resting on h**: those with a part stating a path with edge h, and the self-removals of h, but the role commits The list leaves out. A received commit with a `Split` entry A cannot open (spec/wire.md §4) is listed (DRAFT: a list naming a commit none of whose parts rests on h changes nothing). No other write carries a frontier.
  - **Own cover**: every received commit resting on h that A still holds is listed: a commit is listed whether A still holds its body, dropped it or compacted it. A commit A no longer holds (forgotten, spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) cannot be listed: before R, for each aspect of a thing such commits write where a live write of such a commit stands in A's state only through a cover (its commit forgotten at A, or never received there), A writes a compaction snapshot of the aspect from its whole state marked as its **own cover** (spec/wire.md §4 field 18, bit 0), stamped before R, and R's frontier names it. An aspect A may not snapshot (no role, delete keys not here) gets none. Only the node that wrote an own cover drops a held body as covered under it (spec/commits.md §5 Covered), whether or not R is held, so a removal that does not apply leaves every other holder as it was. *Settled*: a list naming a cover names every commit it carries (roles-model `paths` `World::names`, `a_removal_naming_its_own_cover_keeps_what_the_cover_carries`); when A writes its own cover, roles-model `forget` (`Policy::HeldList`). OPEN: bodies are modelled by neither; that only an own cover's writer drops bodies under it is argued (spec/commits.md §5 Covered).
  - Where a frontier would pass the list bound a node resolves (`MAX_LIST_BYTES`), A leaves out, as few as fit: first the plain-content commits (§5.2, Addenda) it received last, which its own addenda list instead; then the purge-request commits it received last, each losing its right. A frontier over the bound without them is refused, so a removal whose role commits alone pass the bound cannot be written.
  - *Stands*: ephemeral commits listed as durable; a frontier MUST NOT list a commit A had not received as of T; a frontier above `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` is chunked.
  - **Nothing is exempt**: besides R itself, nothing but R's list decides which parts resting on h before R survive, whatever R's own path rests on.
  - **By leaf**: a list is read per thing (its **leaves**): an entry in thing t's leaf names its commit's parts on t alone, so a reader holding t's leaf alone reads it as a node holding every leaf. A lists a commit in the leaf of each thing it has a part on resting on h. The single list of spec/wire.md field 12 is the leaf of every thing: its entry names its commit's parts on every thing (A3's leaf form splits it per thing).
- **The list**: a node writing R lists every commit resting on h it had received as of T, where R, added to its own trust set, applies and is not void in its own walk with that frontier. Otherwise it lists the commits without edge-class parts, then takes the role commits it had received one by one in the order it received them (one millisecond's by commit ID), keeping each where R, so trusted, still applies with what is kept, and leaving it out otherwise. No reader checks any of this: a list naming what takes R's authority leaves R void (step 3).
- A demotion by `until`, self-removal or finalize audits nothing. The next explicit write on that grant, by any node, audits with its frontier against the edge as restored. *(Stands.)*
- **Trust set**: a node's walk takes one more input, its trust set: held edge writes it sides with, each carrying a frontier and lowering or setting the edge of a holder other than its signer (and other than its target). Nodes holding the same commits under the same trust set read alike. Which writes a node trusts is its own, kept across restarts, carried by nothing on the wire; how it chooses (defaults, a user's choice) is local behaviour.
  - **Seeds** of a trusted write R on edge h: every held edge-class part of another commit ordered before R that rests on h, that R's list does not name, and whose least need at h (step 3, as for a part a pass has not applied) R's value does not meet, with the other edge-class parts of its commit on its thing (as for drops, step 3): a trusted demotion to write keeps the invite grants under it, as its audit does.
  - D starts, before the first pass, at the seeds of every trusted write. A trusted write the timeline does not apply is **void**: every void write loses its seeds at once, and the walk is redone from scratch, until none is void.
  - A **dispute** is a held lowering R (as defined above: never a write giving admin at its `ts`) of a grant whose holder is not R's signer, an explicit write (never a self-removal, and not one the walk with R trusted applies as an invite grant: neither carries a frontier), not in the trust set and not dropped by a trusted write (its seeds or its drops), whose trust would change the node's reading: what applies, which deltas are valid (§5.2) or the roles in the final state, read with R added to the trust set. Disputes are keyed by (R, holder) and reported; the walk acts on the trust set alone.
  - A part a list keeps applies like any other: one that takes R's authority leaves R void (step 3), so the writers leave it out (The list).

### 5.2 Layer 2: content
- *Stands*: Position.
- **Content**: a delta on thing N whose commit states path P for N, needing role r (write; admin for an admin-only aspect, below), is valid iff it **stands** against the cuts of P below r, or P is re-granted for r (below). It stands where, for each edge e of P, every cut of e below r ordered after the commit answers it, and e gives at least r just before the commit's key (§5.1; grants' `until` judged at its `ts`) or e's last write the timeline applies before that key is a cut answering it, e's value just before that cut giving at least r at the commit's `ts`. So a delta no cut answers is valid iff P gives r just before it and no cut of P below r follows it; one that cuts answer is valid as if those cuts had not cut it, every other cut of its path still refusing it.
  - A **cut** of P below r is an explicit edge write the timeline applies on an edge e of P whose value gives below r at its `ts` and that takes e, as restored (§5.1), from r or above.
  - A cut L **answers** a delta where L's frontier lists its commit, whatever their order, or a counting addendum vouches for it against L (Addenda, below). An answer speaks for its own cut alone, as a lowering's list keeps from its own drops alone (§5.1): L's signer holds authority over L's edge only, so what another cut of the path refused stays refused however any later remover lists it.
- **Addenda**: a write of `addenda.0` on N (spec/data-model.md §9): an entry its delta creates (spec/crdt.md §5.6 `put_new`; a write into an entry it did not create says nothing), keyed by the commit ID of a removal R, holding a `frontier` listing commit IDs. An addendum A by signer W **vouches for** a delta on N stated along path P **against** R when all of:
  - R is a cut of P below the role the delta needs (write; admin for an admin-only aspect), ordered after the delta's commit and before A, and A's entry lists the delta's commit;
  - the path A states for N gives write on N just before A's key, and admin where the delta needs admin;
  - the delta's commit is not signed by W;
  - the delta is content: its commit is a content commit (not a compaction snapshot) carrying no purge request, and its aspect is not role-class;
  - A is valid: it is judged as W's plain delta on N along its own path by the rules above and the epoch rule (§5.5), itself vouched for by other addenda or not, against a removal or a rotation. Validity with addenda is the least fixpoint over every signer at once: the addenda valid without any addendum count first, then those they vouch for, until nothing changes.
  - A's list counts while its body is held; without the body (dropped, purged) A is still W's write at its key, listing nothing, so a later addendum may vouch for it and its body is fetched again once it is valid. An addendum written by a role commit is no addendum. Addenda are kept as role commits are (fixed retention, spec/commits.md §5) and never compacted.
  - Every cut's frontier answers that cut for every commit it lists, and every addendum answers its removal for the commits ordered before it, however many removals and re-grants follow; a later cut of the path needs its own answer.
  - *Stands*: Nothing checks when W received what it lists.
  - **Writing**: a node writes addenda unasked. For each cut R of an edge h applied in its view taking h below write or below admin, and each thing N in its node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing): whenever its own current view shows a content commit (no compaction snapshot) with a delta on N stated along a path with edge h, ordered before R, not signed by itself, that it had received as of R's `ts` (Received, spec/commits.md §4) and R's frontier does not list, that an addendum it wrote now would vouch for, and that no counting addendum of its own vouches for, it writes one addendum on N keyed by R listing every such commit it received as of R's `ts` that R's frontier does not list, whether or not it would vouch for it now. So it writes on applying R, when it first holds the role after (granted again, its side switched), and whenever what it holds or trusts changes so that none of its addenda vouches for such a commit. One addendum per thing, so no body of one thing travels in another thing's commit; R's signer writes them too, for what its frontier left out (§5.1). A node that cannot write on N yet (its delete keys not here) writes it once it can.
- **External fires**: a delta on an external signal (`@`, spec/data-model.md §2) of thing N valid by the rules above is valid (a writer's fire, whatever the setting). Any other is valid iff N's current setting (spec/commits.md §5 The setting, In force) **enables** the signal: an entry of its own in `rules` names the AK. It is so judged whatever its stated path gives, once its commit's signature verifies under its signer's record; it reads no edge (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable) and no cut touches it.
  - *Stands*: judged again whenever N's current setting changes; an invalid one is listed nowhere but as its signer's own (spec/commits.md §7 Listed parts) and never promoted.
- A delta of an **admin-only** aspect (AK marker `^`, spec/data-model.md §2) is judged by the two rules above with admin in place of write: its path giving admin on N, cuts below admin, addenda whose path gives admin (Addenda). A node whose path to N does not give admin MUST NOT write one; every node judges it alike from the AK alone, also on an ephemeral commit's part and a compaction snapshot's delta.
- *Stands*: the `retention^.0` delta bullet.
- **Compaction snapshot**: a snapshot S's delta of an aspect is judged as its signer's delta of that aspect along S's stated path by the rules above, at S's key, needing the strictest role a write it could carry needs (admin for `sync.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0` and admin-only aspects, else write). `created.0` is always valid.
  - **Own cover**: S marked as an own cover (spec/wire.md §4 field 18, bit 0) is also valid when its path gave the role S needs on the thing just before S's key, and an explicit lowering R (§5.1 Lowering: whatever it takes from its edge at the reading node) the timeline applies, signed by S's signer, ordered at or after S, has a list naming S's commit. It then stands while R applies, whatever cuts its path later: a later lowering of S's signer that does not name S leaves S standing where it names R (R then applies), and drops both where it names neither (a node that had not received R drops R with it, §5.1). *Settled* (`Rules::own_cover`: `an_own_cover_stands_while_its_removal_applies`, `an_own_cover_stands_while_its_removal_applies_whatever_it_takes_here`, `an_own_cover_whose_path_gave_no_role_stands_by_nothing`; purge worlds' gate `own cover`: B removes H after H's removal of X, having received H's own cover or not; control `-no-own-cover`). Built in the unwired walk (osis-perm `paths`, `By::OwnCover`).
  - *Stands*: an invalid S gives nothing; a valid S vouches for every write it carries.
- *Stands*: A write's own commit.
- **Copies**: a valid snapshot speaks for current state alone. A list (a frontier or an addendum's) naming a snapshot names the commits it carries, for the deltas of the snapshot's thing and aspect it carries copies of; a role commit is never carried (role commits are never compacted), so no list names one through a snapshot. Its writes join like any write (spec/crdt.md §4.1), wherever held, also where the receiver holds the write's own delta: a copy differing from the delta's is never compared with it; the copies join by the copy order. A node attributes a write's signer and time only to a commit it holds and verified. So a valid snapshot by a writer that did not yet hold a removal carries the removed party's write stamped after the removal for good, wherever it is held, and every later snapshot of the aspect carries it as state: the window lasts until that writer holds the removal (accepted). *Settled*: roles-model `paths` (`Reading::copied`, the story's `window`; `a_cover_by_a_writer_not_yet_holding_the_removal_carries_the_late_write`, `a_fallen_cover_takes_nothing_honest`); judging a carried write by its own facts (variant `-facts`) is rejected, as it makes every snapshot state each carried commit's signer, `ts` and path.
- *Stands*: a delta's writes count while it is valid; a record carrying no `ts`.
- **A snapshot that turns invalid, or whose body drops**: *stands*, with its last sub-bullet reading: **A cover is its signer's edit**: a cut of its path whose frontier does not list it makes it invalid (addenda vouch for no snapshot, Addenda above), and the live writes only it carried are lost wherever no node still holds their bodies; a re-grant brings them back only from a node holding them (§6 Invalid deltas).
- **Re-grant**: a path P giving at least r on N in the timeline's final state, every grant with an `until` counted removed, is **re-granted** for r: each delta on N stated along P needing r and positioned after the latest, over P's edges, of each edge's first chained write giving it r or above (§4 Chained writes; for a record edge, the start) is valid again, including those in the gap and those a cut's frontier left out. Edge writes in the gap stay dropped.
- *Stands*: one dot names one write; a delta that becomes invalid is un-merged.
- **Ephemeral parts**: a part of an ephemeral commit (spec/commits.md §2) is judged as the durable part of its kind would be, at the commit's key, along its stated path, needing the aspect's role (§1): a session-form one as its signer's content delta; a snapshot one as its signer's compaction snapshot delta. *The rest stands.*

## 6. Purge rights
- **Purge author**: a commit has one purge author, for every delta of it (spec/wire.md §4): a `Path`'s index (0 the signer, j the listed thing Aj; the thing written where it is the path's end), a `Split`'s stated tid or the signer. It is a member of every thing's members (§1); a delta on a thing whose members do not hold it is invalid (DRAFT, notes/path-form.md; a1-spec Open 16). *Settled* for the index (roles-model `paths` `World::purge_author`).
- **A purge request** is an edit: its commit's `purge.0` part (spec/commits.md §5) on each thing it names, content-class. A request may share a commit with writes along differing paths (DRAFT, notes/path-form.md); a request on `created.0` (Destruction, judged also by nodes not holding the thing) may too: its header-only certificate copies carry the destroyed thing's path key (spec/wire.md §4).
- **The right**: a delta's purge-by names who may purge it (spec/commits.md §5). A request holds, over each delta it names, each member of that set it holds, judged at the request's key along the path its commit states for the delta's thing, by the nodes holding that thing's deltas:
  - **admin** (in every set): the request holds the role admin;
  - **writer** (in `writer`): it holds the role write;
  - **author** (in `author_or_admin` and `writer`): it holds the role write, and its path holds the delta's purge author, as its signer or a listed thing.
- **Holding a role**: a request holds role r exactly where a delta of it needing r, along its path, at its key would be valid (§5.2): it stands against the cuts of its path below r (§5.2 Content, each answered by its own list alone), or its path is re-granted for r (from each edge's first chained write giving r or above). A cut whose list does not name it takes the right however early its `ts`; one that names it leaves the purge standing against that cut; a re-grant gives the right back to a request made in the gap, as to its writes. No addendum vouches for a purge request (OPEN 20: finding 41 proposes that one does, by a writer holding r).
- *Settled* (roles-model `paths` `purges`, `holds`, `Rules::purging`; purge worlds `-- N paths-purge`, gates `purge kept`, `purge safety`, `alike`, `could hold`; notes/purge-model.md): `a_request_holds_each_class_of_its_named_deltas_alone`, `a_removed_signers_backdated_request_or_destruction_takes_nothing`, `a_purge_by_author_takes_exactly_that_authors_deltas`, `a_purge_holds_the_right_only_through_its_own_chain`; controls `-purge-any-path`, `-purge-contains`; variant `-purge-at-key` (the right read at the key alone) fails `purge safety`. Built in the unwired walk (osis-perm `paths`: a request's right judged as a delta's validity and stored, a delta's purge a point query over the requests naming it; notes/perm-paths.md, Purges).
- **A purge request** drops a named delta iff it holds a right over it. What it names (spec/wire.md §4 field 8): by author P, the deltas on the aspect stamped before it whose purge author is exactly P, never every delta whose path holds P; by whole aspect, every delta stamped before it; by commit, that one delta, stamped before it. Each named delta is judged alone, so the class of one never widens the others (a writer's request by author leaves that author's `admin` deltas). A request is never an edge-class part: no lowering drops it; a cut takes its right as it takes an edit's validity, and the reading drops the purge, wherever the request arrived first (Judgment).
- **Copies**: a request names, and holds its right over, a delta whose header the node does not hold as it would the delta stamped as its copies are, with the class and purge author the held valid snapshots of its thing and aspect carrying it state (spec/commits.md §5 Purge, Without the header): it is dropped only where the right stands by what every one of them states; one stating nothing of it states nothing, and with nothing stated only a destruction (naming whatever `ts`) names it. A snapshot the node does not hold shows no copy (README Open 19). *Settled* by `Rules::purge_copies` (a request reaches the writes a valid cover shows of a commit the node does not hold; the model reads the commit's own facts, which an honest cover states): `a_request_reaches_the_copy_of_a_write_the_node_does_not_hold`; purge worlds' LJ (holding only what was made from tick 8). Built in the unwired walk (`Paths::purged_copies`, `copy_purged`; snapshots differing in what they state: `hand_purge::a_copy_is_purged_only_by_what_every_snapshot_states`).
- *Stands*: a node does not sign a request that would drop none of the held deltas it names; Judgment, all its bullets.
- **Destruction** (spec/commits.md §5): a request on `created.0` holds the `admin` right alone (the only purge-by a minting has) and names every delta of the thing, of every aspect, whatever its `ts`; a key actor's, by the key actor itself (its empty path gives it admin on itself). Its right is judged without the cuts on edges into the destroyed thing ordered after its position (the start of its `ts`: a cut at its own `ts` included), so a cut of its path on that thing ordered after it does not undo it whatever its list; one on another edge of its path does, as above. *Settled* for the right (`a_destruction_stands_against_a_later_cut_on_its_own_thing_alone`, `a_removed_signers_backdated_request_or_destruction_takes_nothing`; purge worlds' thief destructions along [A] and [A2]); edge writes on a destroyed thing are not modelled (content on it reads purged).
- **Who may undo a purge so**: the signer of a cut of the request's path whose list omits the request. A list may name a message and omit its purge, and the message is back wherever a copy is left. *Settled* as above.
- *Stands*: every other bullet of §6 (deletes at peers keeping a sync ID unread, compaction drops, delete keys at a peer keeping it unread, Invalid deltas, what the right promises).
