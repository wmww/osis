## 1. Concepts
- **Signer** (in place of Actor): every commit is signed by one key actor, its **signer** (spec/wire.md §4), and acts on each thing it writes along the path it states for it (Path, below): its right rests on that path's edges alone (§5). Any thing may hold roles.
  - *Stands*: Key actor.
  - An **account** P is a key actor with **epochs**, its creation record marked so (spec/wire.md §3): its keys change by rotations of its **signing chain** (spec/crypto.md §3, §9; §5.4, §5.5). A node signs as itself only the commits on what is its own: its node thing's aspects (`keys.0`, `node.0`, `reach.0`, §4), its node root and the things whose record chain reaches the node through it (local, under no sync ID: spec/sync-set.md §1, §2); every other commit a node makes is signed by its account.
  - *Stands*: Node, Account root, Profile, Inbox.
  - Organizations, bots and shared identities are ordinary things with rosters; an account holding a role on one acts through it by listing it on its path.
  - *Stands*: A link's actor.
- **Path**: for each thing T it writes, a commit states a **path**: a list of things [A1, …, Ak], 0 ≤ k ≤ MAX_PATH_THINGS, and a purge author (§6) (spec/wire.md §4). Its **members** are [S, A1, …, Ak, T], S the signer; for a self-removal (§2) they are [S, A1, …, Ak], ending at the holder of the grant it lowers; for a commit by a key actor on itself, [S]. A part whose members name a thing twice applies nowhere and is invalid.
  - **Hops**: consecutive members p, q are joined by **descent** where p is on q's record chain (§1 Record edge): the record edges from p down to q, in order; else by their direct edge, p's grant on q. A descent of one step is the record edge (p, q). The path's **edges** are its hops in order.
  - A path gives a role on T at a point when every edge of it gives at least that role there (its value in the walk, §5.1, an `until` judged at the part's `ts`). Its **last edge** is its final hop where that is one edge from Ak (S where k = 0) to T: Ak's direct edge on T, or T's record edge where Ak is T's mint admin. A path reaching T by a descent of two steps or more, and a self-removal's, has none: a direct edge Ak also holds on T is no edge of the path, and no rule reads it for this write.
  - Nothing but the stated path judges a part: another path that would also give the role does not (§5).
- **Effective role** of a thing X on a thing at time t: the maximum over paths from X to the thing of the minimum role along the path, evaluated with the timeline (§5.1) at t. A path climbs role grants, record edges included while they stand; a thing holds admin on itself. It decides keys (§3), sync units and holders; validity reads stated paths only (§5).
- *Stands*: Standing admin.
- **The last admin**: an edge write that takes a standing admin edge of a thing T to anything else (removing it, giving it a role below admin, or an `until`), and whose commit does not carry the adminless mark (§2), applies only where it has a **remaining admin**: a standing admin edge of T other than the one it takes, as the pass stands at the write, its commit's earlier edge writes in effect (§5.1 step 2): its path's last edge (§1 Path) where that is one and standing, else the edge on T of the holder its commit's header names (spec/wire.md §4 `remains`). With neither it does not apply. A lowering judged in the walk (§5.1 step 3) is also void where its drops, or those of its commit's earlier lowerings, would leave its remaining admin edge standing no more (§5.1 step 4). A commit carrying the mark relies on no remaining admin.
  - A node MUST NOT sign an unmarked role commit that, in its own walk with the commit applied, leaves a thing that had a standing admin with none, nor one whose edge write the rule above stops. This covers every way an edge write can do so, on a dependent or an independent thing: removing or lowering the last standing admin's edge (below admin, or giving it an `until`), an admin's self-removal (§2), and a removal whose drops (§5.1) take the edge writes that made the last standing admins. A thing's destruction (spec/commits.md §5) and an `until` passing are no edge writes and are not covered.
  - A dependent thing left so stays dependent: its content stays readable through derivation by the mint admin's holders.
- *Stands*: With no admin left, and every other bullet of §1.

## 2. Role grant writes
- **Edge writes**: granting, changing and removing a grant are writes to the target's `roles.0`, each in its own role commit (spec/commits.md §2), along a path giving admin on the target (§1 Path; a finalize, below: write). An edge write that is not a self-removal, a finalize or an invite grant is **explicit**. *The sub-bullets stand.*
- **Finalize**, its second sentence: It applies (§5.1) iff the grant is past its `until` at the removal's `ts` (as the timeline orders it: a renewal ordered before it counts, and the finalize then ends a superseded write and applies nothing) and its path gives write on the target and a role there at least the grant's (a timed admin grant needs admin); else it applies nothing, whoever wrote it. It carries no frontier, audits nothing, leaves the edge as restored (§5.1) as it was, gets no verdict and is no dispute candidate; it rests on its path's edges, needing write there (§5.1 step 3). *The rest stands.*
- **Path's invite** (in place of effective invite): the invite a path gives on T is the minimum of the roles of its edges but the last and the last edge's invite. An edge's invite at a point is every invite where it gives admin there (a record edge, so a last edge by descent, among them), else its `invite` at most the role it gives.
- **Invite grant**, its lead: an edge write whose path does not give admin on T applies as an invite grant where it adds a grant for a thing with no current grant on T (an expired grant counts as none), its role below admin and its invite at most its role, and its path gives that role on each of its edges but the last and as an invite on the last; it carries the grantee's boxes for those roles. It carries no frontier, audits nothing, gets no verdict and raises the edge as restored (§5.1) to its role and invite. An edge write whose path gives admin on T is explicit, whatever it sets. *The rest stands.*
- *Settled* (Finalize, Path's invite, Invite grant): roles-model `paths` `walk` (its invite and finalize arms), `need`; `an_invite_grant_applies_by_its_paths_invite` (control `-invite-role`), `a_finalize_ends_an_ended_grant_and_an_invite_follows_it`, `the_explicit_removal_after_a_finalize_audits_the_invites`; `tiny_invites`.
- **Self-removal**: an edge write on grant (X, T) whose path ends at X (§1 Path): X is its last listed thing, or its signer for an empty path. It applies iff its path gives admin on X (a signer leaving by itself, by no edge) and it lowers: removes the grant, or sets no component of role, invite and `until` above the grant's value and no `floor` below it (an unchanged rewrite and a removal of an absent grant lower); one that does not lower applies nothing.
- A self-removal needs no authority on the target, carries no rotation (§3) and audits nothing (§5.1); it rests on the grant it lowers as on its path's edges (§5.1).
- **Adminless mark** (spec/wire.md §4 field 18, bit 1): a role commit's signer sets it on a commit it means to leave a thing with no standing admin (§1, The last admin): with it, the commit's edge writes rely on no remaining admin. It is part of the signed header, read by every node alike.
- **Logging a lowered grant**: a removal or lowering of a grant has no logging rule of its own. It is an edge write on every path with that edge, so it goes in the chain certificates of every commit stating such a path (§4), under each sync ID whose required set holds such a chain (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): wherever a write it judges goes; and, as every edge write, where its holder's role reaches (§4 Role changes travel).

## 4. Verifying signers: certificates
- A write's authority is checked along its stated path (§1 Path): each edge an edge write or a record edge (the tid commits to the mint admin), its signer a key actor whose tid commits to its public key; an account's commit also through its signing chain to the epoch it names (§5.4, §5.5). No secrets are needed.
- **Chained writes**: the held edge writes, taken in key order (§5.1 step 1), each chained when its members name no thing twice and every edge of its stated path is a record edge or set at admin by a chained write of an earlier commit (applied or not, never removed, at the role it sets, `until` disregarded). A chained write that is not a self-removal, a finalize or an invite grant sets its edge at the role and invite it sets. Every write the walk applies (§5.1) is chained, in every walk and at every node; the set depends on the held writes and records alone and only grows with them, so a write leaves no chain once in it, and one whose stated path no record or earlier chained write gives (a stranger's, one ordered before its signer's authority) enters none. An invite grant (§2) is also chained where its members name no thing twice, every edge of its path but the last is a record edge or set by a chained write of an earlier commit at its role or above, and the last is a record edge or set by one at an invite of its role or above (a write at admin sets every invite); it sets its edge at its role and invite. A self-removal or a finalize sets no edge here. *Settled*: roles-model `paths` `first_chained`.
- **Chain certificates** of a commit for a thing T it writes are built on the members of the path it states for T. They are:
  - for each member, its creation record (and those up its record chain), raw in a record blob (spec/wire.md §3), never a copy of the commit that minted it: a record outlives that commit's retention;
  - for each member, its **current `keys.0` commits**: its newest `keys.0` commit writing more than box records, the rotation commits of its newest keyset of each role, forks included, and the commits holding box records at those keysets' epochs (spec/crypto.md §4, Stale), each projected to `keys.0` (the newest may write no keyset, and a rotation of what it holds names its keyset, spec/crypto.md §4), with the `roles.0` deltas of the commits those keysets carry and their path chains (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions);
  - for each member that is an account, the rotations of its signing chain the node holds, every line, as header-only copies (§5.5, Chains travel; one among its current `keys.0` commits goes as those do), and their thing lists for things under the sync ID (§5.5): a commit at an epoch verifies by the signing key its minting rotation's header carries;
  - every chained write on an edge of that path, and on the edge of the holder its header names as the remaining admin (§1, The last admin), removals and lowerings included;
  - closed under paths: for each such write, its own chain certificates for its target (a self-removal's: for its holder).
- A commit's **path chain** (in place of its author's chain) is its chain certificates for each thing it has a delta on (an external fire's: its signer's record alone, §5.2 External fires): every chain its validity reads, so it travels with each body of the commit. A commit whose path chain to the things it writes has not arrived is pending (spec/commits.md §4). An account signer's commit at an epoch whose minting rotation the node does not hold is pending (§5.5).

## 5. Validity
Validity is a deterministic function of the commits a node holds and its trust set (§5.1, Trust set) and side (§5.4). An account signer's commit first passes the epoch rule (§5.5); one that does not count there is judged by nothing below.

### 5.1 Layer 1: role timeline
The timeline covers the **edge-class parts**: edge writes (writes to `roles.0`) and vouches (`vouches.0` entries, Vouches below). Every other role-class part (`secrets.0`, `keys.0`, `sync.0`) is judged like content (§5.2) by the role its aspect requires (§1, §3).
- **Rests on**: an edge-class part rests on each edge of its stated path (§1 Path); a self-removal of grant h rests also on h.
- **Lowering**: an explicit edge write (§2) on edge h whose value gives below admin at its `ts` (no grant, a role below admin, or an `until` at or before its `ts`). An explicit edge write whose value gives admin at its `ts` with an `until` after it is no lowering, whatever h held before (a standing admin given an end date, or an end shortened): it applies as a grant (step 2), carries no frontier and is no dispute candidate; §1 The last admin reads it.
- **Edge as restored** of h at a point of a pass: a role and an invite. Its role is the highest role of the values h has taken in the pass since the last explicit write on it (that write's value and the invite grants' since included), each read with its `until` disregarded (so what an `until`, a self-removal or a finalize took away since counts), and at least the role h gives there. Its invite is the highest invite of the same values, each read at its write's `ts` (a value's invite: every invite where it gives admin, else its `invite` at most the role it gives), and at least the invite h gives there. For a record edge no explicit write in the pass has set, both are admin. A lowering **falls** where its value at its `ts` is below h as restored just before it, in role or in invite.
- A lowering's **list** is its frontier (below) and the entries of the counting vouches keyed by it (Vouches); it **names** a part whose commit's ID is in it.

1. **Order**: order every held edge-class part, over every thing, by (`ts`, signer, commit ID): a part's **key** is its position in this order; a commit's parts are ordered by the header's aspect order, then by holder tid, bytewise, but an edge write setting an edge of the path the commit states for its target (a self-removal of the path's last thing, as in a single-use link redemption, or the record edge a descent ends with; with both, in holder order) comes after the commit's other parts on that target.
2. **Passes**: the walk is a run of passes. Each starts before every part, with each thing's record edge in force (§1) and no other edge, and takes the parts in key order, skipping the drop set D. D and a **verdict** for each lowering judged (applied or void) carry from pass to pass; nothing else does. In a pass, a part with no verdict **applies** iff its members name no thing twice and every edge of its path gives, at that point, what it needs, and an edge write passes the last-admin rule (§1):
   - an edge write: admin; an invite grant, its path's invite (§2); a self-removal, admin up to its holder, and it lowers (§2); a finalize, as §2 says;
   - a vouch: admin on its thing.
   A lowering with verdict applied applies, unread by the last-admin rule: where its value is at or below its edge as restored in this pass, in role and in invite, whatever its path gives there; where it is above in either it would raise the edge, and applies only where every edge of its path gives admin there. One with verdict void is skipped. A commit's edge writes are judged one at a time in key order, each as a write of its own with the commit's earlier ones in effect: whether it applies (its path, its invite, the last-admin rule), and for a lowering what it takes, puts in question and keeps (step 3), read the pass after them, the drops of the commit's lowerings judged applied before it being in D. Since the part on an edge of the commit's path comes last, no part's path is changed by an earlier part of its commit.
3. **Judging a lowering**: a lowering L of edge h with no verdict that applies in a pass and falls is judged:
   - L **puts in question** every held edge-class part of another commit ordered before it (no list can name its own commit) that rests on h, is not in D, is not void, and whose **need** at h the edge as restored met and L's value does not. A part's need at h is what it applied by in this pass: admin; an invite grant its role as an invite where h is its path's last edge and as a role elsewhere; a finalize write. A part this pass has not applied needs the least it could apply by: an edge write that could apply as an invite grant (§2) an invite grant's need, a finalize write, any other admin. Its **drops** are those of them its list does not name.
   - L is **void** iff its drops would take its authority or its remaining admin: `keeps(L)` fails (step 4). Void, L does not apply and drops nothing.
   - Otherwise its verdict is **applied**: L applies; if it has drops they join D at once, and the pass ends once L's commit has taken effect (its later edge writes taken in turn); the next pass starts from the start.
   - A lowering that applies without falling, or does not apply, gets no verdict in that pass and audits nothing; a later pass may judge it. A lowering is judged at most once in a walk; each walk (the first and the second, Vouches, and each redo for trust) starts with D at its seeds and no verdict.
4. **Keeps**: for the lowering L being judged, `keeps(W)` holds for L, or for a part W the pass applied before L, iff every edge e of W's path keeps admin for W: of the edge writes the pass applied on e ordered before W, latest first, pass over each of L's drops, each drop of the lowerings of L's commit judged applied before L in this pass, and each grant G (an edge write giving admin at its own `ts`, not a self-removal) for which `keeps(G)` fails; the first one left decides: e keeps admin iff it gives admin at W's `ts`. With none left, e keeps admin iff it is a record edge. Where L relies on a remaining admin edge r (§1 The last admin: its path's last edge where that stood at L, else the named holder's), `keeps(L)` also needs r to stay standing, read the same way along the edge writes the pass applied on r before L (its commit's earlier ones included): the first one left gives admin with no `until`, or with none left r is a record edge.
5. **Settling**: a pass that adds nothing to D ends the walk; it is the **settled walk**. Nothing is ever undropped. D and the verdicts are rebuilt from scratch whenever the held set or the trust set changes. The timeline is the settled second walk (Vouches): every rule that reads whether an edge-class part applies, and the roles at a point, reads it.

- **Frontier**: every lowering R (an explicit write giving its edge h below admin at its `ts`, a grant at write or read included) embeds, as commit IDs, never counters, what its signer's node A had received (Received, spec/commits.md §4) as of R's `ts` T of the commits **resting on h**: those with a part stating a path with edge h, and the self-removals of h, but the role commits The list leaves out. No other write carries a frontier.
  - **Own cover**: every received commit resting on h that A still holds is listed: a commit is listed whether A still holds its body, dropped it or compacted it. A commit A no longer holds (forgotten, spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) cannot be listed: before R, for each aspect of a thing such commits write where a live write of such a commit stands in A's state only through a cover (its commit forgotten at A, or never received there), A writes a compaction snapshot of the aspect from its whole state marked as its **own cover** (spec/wire.md §4 field 18, bit 0), stamped before R, and R's frontier names it. An aspect A may not snapshot (no role, delete keys not here) gets none. Only the node that wrote an own cover drops a held body as covered under it (spec/commits.md §5 Covered), whether or not R is held, so a removal that does not apply leaves every other holder as it was. *Settled*: a list naming a cover names every commit it carries (roles-model `paths` `World::names`, `a_removal_naming_its_own_cover_keeps_what_the_cover_carries`); when A writes its own cover, roles-model `forget` (`Policy::HeldList`). OPEN: bodies are modelled by neither; that only an own cover's writer drops bodies under it is argued (spec/commits.md §5 Covered).
  - Where a frontier would pass the list bound a node resolves (`MAX_LIST_BYTES`), A leaves out, as few as fit: first the plain-content commits (§5.2, Addenda) it received last, which its own addenda list instead; then the purge-request commits it received last, each losing its right. A frontier over the bound without them is refused, so a removal whose role commits alone pass the bound cannot be written.
  - *Stands*: ephemeral commits listed as durable; a frontier MUST NOT list a commit A had not received as of T; a frontier above `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` is chunked.
  - **Nothing is exempt**: besides R itself, nothing but R's list decides which parts resting on h before R survive, whatever R's own path rests on.
- **The list**: a node writing R lists every commit resting on h it had received as of T, where R, added to its own trust set, applies and is not void in its own walk with that frontier. Otherwise it lists the commits without edge-class parts, then takes the role commits it had received one by one in the order it received them (one millisecond's by commit ID), keeping each where R, so trusted, still applies with what is kept, and leaving it out otherwise. A voucher builds its vouch's list the same way over R's list as written (Vouches, Writing). No reader checks any of this: a list naming what takes R's authority leaves R void (step 3).
- A demotion by `until`, self-removal or finalize audits nothing. The next explicit write on that grant, by any node, audits with its frontier against the edge as restored. *(Stands.)*
- **Trust set**: a node's walk takes one more input, its trust set: held edge writes it sides with, each carrying a frontier and lowering or setting the edge of a holder other than its signer (and other than its target). Nodes holding the same commits under the same trust set read alike. Which writes a node trusts is its own, kept across restarts, carried by nothing on the wire; how it chooses (defaults, a user's choice) is local behaviour.
  - **Seeds** of a trusted write R on edge h: every held edge-class part of another commit ordered before R that rests on h, that R's list does not name, and whose least need at h (step 3, as for a part a pass has not applied) R's value does not meet: a trusted demotion to write keeps the invite grants under it, as its audit does.
  - D starts, before the first pass, at the seeds of every trusted write. A trusted write the timeline does not apply is **void**: every void write loses its seeds at once, and both walks (Vouches) are redone from scratch, until none is void.
  - A **dispute** is a held lowering R (as defined above: never a write giving admin at its `ts`) of a grant whose holder is not R's signer, an explicit write (never a self-removal, and not one the walk with R trusted applies as an invite grant: neither carries a frontier), not in the trust set and not dropped by a trusted write (its seeds or its drops), whose trust would change the node's reading: what applies, which deltas are valid (§5.2) or the roles in the final state, read with R added to the trust set. Disputes are keyed by (R, holder) and reported; the walk acts on the trust set alone.
- **Vouches**: a `vouches.0` entry on thing N (spec/data-model.md §9) that its delta creates (spec/crdt.md §5.6 `put_new`; a write into an entry it did not create says nothing), keyed by the commit ID of a removal R, holding a `frontier` listing commit IDs. Its commit is a role commit, and its delta a role part (spec/crypto.md §4 Roles secrets) and an edge-class part: it applies in a pass iff its path gives admin on N (step 2), and it rests on its path's edges, put in question and dropped by lowerings of them like any part (step 3). A vouch's **voucher** is its commit's signer. OPEN: a vouch for an invite grant needing only the invite that grant needed (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, It counts) is not modelled; roles-model `paths` asks admin of every vouch.
  - **First walk**: the walk (steps 1 to 5) under the node's trust set, every vouch's entries unread.
  - An entry of vouch V keyed by R and listing commit c **counts** iff, in the first walk:
    - an edge write of R applies as an explicit write lowering a grant whose holder X is not R's signer: taking from the edge's value just before R in this walk (its last change to that edge, else the record value; the edge as restored not read) by removing it, or setting a role, invite or `until` below it or a `floor` above it;
    - V applies, ordered after R;
    - c has a role-class part on N, is ordered before R and rests on R's edge, and is not signed by V's signer.
  - **Second walk**: steps 1 to 5 under the same trust set, each counting entry's commit read as named by its removal's list, in drops, seeds and disputes alike. It is the timeline, and §5.2 reads each frontier so. Nothing judges a vouch in it. With no counting entry the two walks are one.
  - **Writing**: every node but R's signer and those signing as X writes vouches unasked, for each held removal R applying in its own first walk as an explicit write lowering X's grant on edge h, and each thing N in its node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing): whenever its own current first walk shows a commit c resting on h with a role-class part on N, ordered before R, not signed by itself, that it received as of R's `ts` and R's list does not name, such that a vouch of its own on N would apply at the present position (its path giving admin on N) and no vouch of its own counting in that walk names c for R, it writes one vouch on N keyed by R listing every such commit it received as of R's `ts`, as The list builds a voucher's list. So it writes on applying R, when it first holds the right after (granted again, its side switched), and whenever what it holds or trusts changes so that none of its vouches for such a c counts. A node that cannot yet write a role part on N writes once it can.
  - **What vouches keep**: once every node holds every commit and owes no vouch, the timeline gives every holder the roles it would give were R's frontier, beside its counting entries, also to list each commit c resting on h, ordered before R, not signed by its voucher, that a node other than R's signer and those signing as X received as of R's `ts`, that R's list does not name, with which R still applies (The list), and on whose thing this node's path gives admin at the end of the first walk.
  - Nothing checks when the voucher received what it lists: a voucher whose vouch applies makes c count, one X backdated included, as with addenda (§5.2). *(Stands.)*
  - A part a list keeps applies like any other: one that takes R's authority leaves R void (step 3), so the writers leave it out (The list).

### 5.2 Layer 2: content
- *Stands*: Position.
- **Content**: a delta on thing N whose commit states path P for N, needing role r (write; admin for an admin-only aspect, below), is valid iff P gives at least r on N just before the commit's key (§5.1; grants' `until` judged at its `ts`) and no cut of P below r is ordered after it.
  - A **cut** of P below r is an explicit edge write the timeline applies on an edge e of P whose value gives below r at its `ts` and that takes e, as restored (§5.1), from r or above.
- Otherwise it is valid iff the frontier of a cut of P below r lists its commit, whatever their order, a counting addendum vouches for it (Addenda, below), or P is re-granted for r (below).
- **Addenda**: a write of `addenda.0` on N (spec/data-model.md §9): an entry its delta creates (spec/crdt.md §5.6 `put_new`; a write into an entry it did not create says nothing), keyed by the commit ID of a removal R, holding a `frontier` listing commit IDs. An addendum A by signer W **vouches for** a delta on N stated along path P when all of:
  - R is a cut of P below the role the delta needs (write; admin for an admin-only aspect), ordered after the delta's commit and before A, and A's entry lists the delta's commit;
  - the path A states for N gives write on N just before A's key, and admin where the delta needs admin;
  - the delta's commit is not signed by W;
  - the delta is content: its commit is a content commit (not a compaction snapshot) carrying no purge request, and its aspect is not role-class;
  - A is valid: it is judged as W's plain delta on N along its own path by the rules above and the epoch rule (§5.5), itself vouched for by other addenda or not, against a removal or a rotation. Validity with addenda is the least fixpoint over every signer at once: the addenda valid without any addendum count first, then those they vouch for, until nothing changes.
  - A's list counts while its body is held; without the body (dropped, purged) A is still W's write at its key, listing nothing, so a later addendum may vouch for it and its body is fetched again once it is valid. An addendum written by a role commit is no addendum. Addenda are kept as role commits are (fixed retention, spec/commits.md §5) and never compacted.
  - Every cut's frontier counts for every commit it lists, and every addendum for the commits ordered before its removal, however many removals and re-grants follow.
  - *Stands*: Nothing checks when W received what it lists.
  - **Writing**: a node writes addenda unasked. For each cut R of an edge h applied in its view taking h below write or below admin, and each thing N in its node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing): whenever its own current view shows a content commit (no compaction snapshot) with a delta on N stated along a path with edge h, ordered before R, not signed by itself, that it had received as of R's `ts` (Received, spec/commits.md §4) and R's frontier does not list, that an addendum it wrote now would vouch for, and that no counting addendum of its own vouches for, it writes one addendum on N keyed by R listing every such commit it received as of R's `ts` that R's frontier does not list, whether or not it would vouch for it now. So it writes on applying R, when it first holds the role after (granted again, its side switched), and whenever what it holds or trusts changes so that none of its addenda vouches for such a commit. One addendum per thing, so no body of one thing travels in another thing's commit; R's signer writes them too, for what its frontier left out (§5.1). A node that cannot write on N yet (its delete keys not here) writes it once it can.
- **External fires**: a delta on an external signal (`@`, spec/data-model.md §2) of thing N valid by the rules above is valid (a writer's fire, whatever the setting). Any other is valid iff N's current setting (spec/commits.md §5 The setting, In force) **enables** the signal: an entry of its own in `rules` names the AK. It is so judged whatever its stated path gives, once its commit's signature verifies under its signer's record; it reads no edge (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable) and no cut touches it.
  - *Stands*: judged again whenever N's current setting changes; an invalid one is listed nowhere but as its signer's own (spec/commits.md §7 Listed parts) and never promoted.
- A delta of an **admin-only** aspect (AK marker `^`, spec/data-model.md §2) is judged by the two rules above with admin in place of write: its path giving admin on N, cuts below admin, addenda whose path gives admin (Addenda). A node whose path to N does not give admin MUST NOT write one; every node judges it alike from the AK alone, also on an ephemeral commit's part and a compaction snapshot's delta.
- *Stands*: the `retention^.0` delta bullet.
- **Compaction snapshot**: a snapshot S's delta of an aspect is judged as its signer's delta of that aspect along S's stated path by the rules above, at S's key, needing the strictest role a write it could carry needs (admin for `sync.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0` and admin-only aspects, else write). `created.0` is always valid.
  - **Own cover**: S marked as an own cover (spec/wire.md §4 field 18, bit 0) is also valid when its path gave the role S needs on the thing just before S's key, and an explicit removal or lowering R the timeline applies, signed by S's signer, on an edge of S's path, ordered at or after S, has a frontier naming S. It then stands while R applies, whatever cuts its path later (a node that had not received R drops R with it, §5.1). OPEN: whether an own cover outlives its author's later demotion; roles-model `paths` reads any cut listing a cover as keeping it (`content`), but no world demotes a cover's author after its removal.
  - *Stands*: an invalid S gives nothing; a valid S vouches for every write it carries.
- *Stands*: A write's own commit.
- **Copies**: a valid snapshot speaks for current state alone. Its writes join like any write (spec/crdt.md §4.1), wherever held, also where the receiver holds the write's own delta: a copy differing from the delta's is never compared with it; the copies join by the copy order. A node attributes a write's signer and time only to a commit it holds and verified. So a valid snapshot by a writer that did not yet hold a removal carries the removed party's write stamped after the removal for good, wherever it is held, and every later snapshot of the aspect carries it as state: the window lasts until that writer holds the removal (accepted). *Settled*: roles-model `paths` (`Reading::copied`, the story's `window`; `a_cover_by_a_writer_not_yet_holding_the_removal_carries_the_late_write`, `a_fallen_cover_takes_nothing_honest`); judging a carried write by its own facts (variant `-facts`) is rejected, as it makes every snapshot state each carried commit's signer, `ts` and path.
- *Stands*: a delta's writes count while it is valid; a record carrying no `ts`.
- **A snapshot that turns invalid, or whose body drops**: *stands*, with its last sub-bullet reading: **A cover is its signer's edit**: a cut of its path whose frontier does not list it makes it invalid (addenda vouch for no snapshot, Addenda above), and the live writes only it carried are lost wherever no node still holds their bodies; a re-grant brings them back only from a node holding them (§6 Invalid deltas).
- **Re-grant**: a path P giving at least r on N in the timeline's final state, every grant with an `until` counted removed, is **re-granted** for r: each delta on N stated along P needing r and positioned after the latest, over P's edges, of each edge's first chained write giving it r or above (§4 Chained writes; for a record edge, the start) is valid again, including those in the gap and those a cut's frontier left out. Edge writes in the gap stay dropped.
- *Stands*: one dot names one write; a delta that becomes invalid is un-merged.
- **Ephemeral parts**: a part of an ephemeral commit (spec/commits.md §2) is judged as the durable part of its kind would be, at the commit's key, along its stated path, needing the aspect's role (§1): a session-form one as its signer's content delta; a snapshot one as its signer's compaction snapshot delta. *The rest stands.*

## 6. Purge rights
- **Purge author**: a delta's purge author is the member its commit's path for its thing names by index (spec/wire.md §4): 0 the signer, j the listed thing Aj. *Settled* (roles-model `paths` `World::purge_author`).
- **The right**: a delta's retention names who may purge it (spec/commits.md §5), judged at the request's key along the path the request states for the delta's thing. Each member of the named set holds the right on its own:
  - **author** (in `author_or_admin` and `writer`): the request's path holds the delta's purge author, as its signer or a listed thing, and gives write on the thing. *Settled* (`purges`; controls `-purge-any-path`, `-purge-contains`; `a_purge_holds_the_right_only_through_its_own_chain`).
  - **admin** (in every set): the request's path gives admin on the thing. **writer**: the request's path gives write on the thing. OPEN: neither class is modelled; they are read as the `author` class is, by the request's validity as a delta of that role along its own path.
- A role counts toward the right exactly when a delta of the request needing that role, along its path, at the request's key would be valid (§5.2): a cut of that path whose frontier does not list the request takes the right however early its `ts`; one that lists it leaves the purge standing; a re-grant gives the right back to a request made in the gap, as to its writes. No addendum vouches for a purge request. *Settled* for the `author` class (`a_removed_signers_backdated_purge_takes_nothing`; story moves 43 to 45). OPEN: whether a vouch keeps a purge request (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, It counts: "the purge right for a purge request"); none does in the model.
- **A purge request** drops a named delta iff it held that right. A request by author names a thing and matches, on the aspect, the deltas stamped before it whose purge author is exactly that thing, never every delta whose path holds it. Each delta of a request by whole aspect or by author is judged alone, so the class of one never widens the others (a writer's request by author leaves that author's `admin` deltas). *Settled* for held deltas (`a_purge_by_author_takes_exactly_that_authors_deltas`, control `-purge-contains`). OPEN: a delta whose header the node does not hold, known only by the held snapshots carrying it: their stated class and purge author decide (spec/commits.md §5 Purge, Without the header), not modelled (the model purges held deltas only).
- *Stands*: a node does not sign a request that would drop none of the held deltas it names; Judgment, all its bullets.
- **Destruction** (spec/commits.md §5): a request holds the right over a `created.0` delta as over any delta of retention `admin`, the only retention a minting has; a key actor's, by the key actor itself (its empty path gives it admin on itself). Its right is judged without the edge writes on the destroyed thing stamped after the request, so a cut of its path on that thing stamped after it does not undo it whatever its frontier; one on another thing does, as above. OPEN: not modelled (the `admin` class).
- **Who may undo a purge so**: the signer of a cut of the request's path, whose frontier omits the request. A frontier may list a message and omit its purge, and the message is back wherever a copy is left. *Settled* for the `author` class, as above.
- *Stands*: every other bullet of §6 (deletes at peers keeping a sync ID unread, compaction drops, delete keys at a peer keeping it unread, Invalid deltas, what the right promises).
