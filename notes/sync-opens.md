# Sync: opens
> Summary: osis-sync's `opens` module (core/sync/src/opens/): per-peer records, agreement, filters and Hits, the shared set per connection, members' judging and ending, one connection per pair, durability and eviction, peers to wait for, and the policy seam.

Spec: spec/sync-opens.md §2-§6. Model: core/test/tla/net/NetOpen.tla (the one open rule, ending at a head move, claims, side bytes, answers and Lost tags; core/sync/tests/it/opens.rs holds its hostile cases). Overview: notes/sync.md.

**Principle:** each side opens S toward a peer only on its own proof, judged on what it holds now (spec/sync-opens.md §4 One open rule); every judgement follows by change, and what a source showed of S lives exactly as long as S is a member of that source's record or a stop of it is kept on the connection up.

## What it owns
- **Records** (`opens/records.rs`, rows in the peer's osis-store record): `R` own state (`acked_pos`, this side's filter generations and the positions they reach, the peer filter's shape); `F` the peer's filter per generation, Golomb-Rice coded, a whole one replacing those before it; `M` a member (the peer's class and this side's as declared, credential, `until`, beacon).
- **Per connection**: binding, phase, agreement, filter generations in flight, the Hits its filter makes possible, the Offer in flight, reported positions (`Positions`), and `lost_to` (the pair rule).
- **`changed_at` per S**: the store's `changed_note` row, keeping the digest of the credentials it was advanced for, so a restart advances it only for a value that moved.

## What it tells other modules
Each member opening or closing on a connection is a `Moved` (`Joined` with a `Rejoin`, `Left`, `Until`, `Forgot`); a kept record taken or let go by a connection is one fact (`Taken`, saying whether the record was just read from its rows, when the modules learn its members; `Released`: `conn_of`; no member moves); a record kept nowhere letting go its connection is each member `Left`; a member of a record no connection holds closing in the record is `Gone` (streams forgets what that source showed of S, status the member and its lacking count, inbox its register keys and lists owed). The driver hands each to streams, read, status and inbox at once; they key what they keep by record and read the connection only where they send or ask. Opens never calls streams; streams never diffs the shared set. Streams' reported positions come back as `Reported` and fold into `Positions` (O(1) per report; an Applied checks only ended members): a member opened on the connection has none (`Positions::opened`) until the peer's join names one (a merge before that moves nothing: a Pull crossing the open, the first included, cannot speak for it), and the requester joins every member opened on the connection, one at the stream's cursor from that cursor; a record's members a connection takes follow the stream (`streams::a_first_pull_crossing_an_open_names_no_position_for_it_*`).

## Work follows change
`NodeHolds::due` follows the sync IDs this node syncs (`set::SyncsFollow`) and, per S, the digest of its credentials (`node_creds`: `Engine::read_credentials(s)` for a read S, `Unread::credentials(s)` for one kept unread) as an `Effects` key. A moved value advances `changed_at(S)`, judges S's member in every record in memory holding it (`held`, by record; `conn_of` says whether a connection holds it), and makes a filter addition and a test due on each connection.
- Nothing walks every sync ID per connection but a whole filter (first contact) and the Hits index, built at a connection's first Hit and then kept per changed S, so a reconnect the peer Hits nothing on costs nothing per sync ID held (issues/opens-hit-index-cost.md).
- Rejected: S's roster in the credentials digest (every grant or removal would re-judge S on every connection).

## Judging
spec/sync-opens.md §6. A member is judged (`Opens::judge`) on S's credentials here (`admissible`): one whose credential is admissible and of the head stays; one admissible but not of the head (or opened by an older step's sync proof) is ended, its `until` judged then; any other closes at once.
- **In the step of the change**, whether or not a connection holds the record, so an ended member's `until` is the position of the change and a take judges nothing that did not move (`idle::an_idle_reconnect_sends_reads_and_judges_the_same_at_10n_spec_sync_streams_4` counts `opens: members judged`; `opens::an_until_is_the_position_of_the_change_while_away_spec_sync_opens_6`). With no connection, an end is set in the record (seen by the next take: `ending` restored there) and a close removes the member from the record keeping nothing of where it stopped (`Moved::Gone`; `acked_pos` re-marked below S), which the peer finds by the next take's digest (spec/sync-opens.md §3 Agreement).
- **A start follows every kept member** (`Opens::follow_kept`, at the first settle once S's credentials are known): each kept record with a member is read from its rows (its peer filter left for a take, `Record::told` false so the first take still tells the modules its members) and its members judged, so a change while its peer is away across this node's restart ends it in its step (`opens::a_removal_after_a_restart_serves_nothing_after_it_spec_sync_opens_6`). O(kept records and members) once per start, within `RECORD_BUDGET`. Rejected: judging at the next take only: a peer that never returns kept its member, and what its source showed (read by `present_anywhere` from the rows), until eviction.
- **Served past** (`Opens::served_past`): once an ended member closed at its `until` on a connection (`Conn::served`, the highest), a Hit there on an older step's sync proof whose `until` is no later opens nothing: else a peer that keeps S after learning re-tests and reopens without end.
- **Holding S no more** is one rule (`Opens::left`, spec/sync-set.md §3 Stopping): run when S's credentials go to none or the store's shred feed names S. A shred counts even when S is synced again before the settle sees it go (its credentials' digest unmoved): the store's rows of S went, so every record drops S, S's members close with a Lost naming none, and each kept stop of S is told; then S's credentials are taken as new (`changed_at` advanced afresh).

## Class
A member's class (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source: reader, store or stub, notes/sync.md The stub class) is what the peer declared (`class` in each Hit and each answer accepting one); the member also keeps the class this side declared (`Member::own`).
- A tester opens on an answer only while its class is the one its item declared (its holding may move with the Offer out). `rejudge` closes a member that stays, not ended, once this side's class moved (`Close::Class`, kept as at `Until`), so S opens again with both declared anew; a take re-judges alike. Cost: the members of the S whose credentials moved (a holding change moves them).
- A false declaration steers only duties and counting: deposits, Register and Deletes go to a declared store (nothing a reader cannot read), status counts a declared reader's reports as held (a peer could falsify its reports anyway). Anything disclosed is gated on proof, never on the class.
- Rejected: the class from the credential that opened S (a sync proof is shown by both kinds, so which side's Hit landed first decided).

## Ending
- **`until`** (`holds::member_until`): at a node keeping S unread the position of S's head event (`Store::head_at`); at a reader opened by an older step's sync proof the highest first-logged position in S of an entry of a rotation superseding that step's sync epoch (`Engine::rotations_superseding`, entries by `commit_blobs`), else the log's end; any other credential at a reader ends at the log's end. A store failure answers 0 (served to nothing, closed at once). A Lost's cursor below a reported position is `Store::cursor_at(until)`.
- **A stub's stop says nothing of content**: toward a member declared a stub a server sends role entries alone, so once this side holds S otherwise a close at its `until` or by the class move keeps nothing (`close_member`, as `AtOnce`) and the member rejoins from the start (`opens::a_stub_coming_to_read_rejoins_from_the_start_*`). A stub staying one keeps its stop: it re-pulls nothing.
- **An ended member stays ended until it closes**, also once it counts again: its peer's stream may already be served past `until` with S's entries there skipped, so only a close naming `until` and a join from there delivers them.
- **Where it stopped** (spec/sync-opens.md §6). Closes keep: at `until` (`Close::Until`) where this side's stream holds it (`Rejoin::Held`) until the peer's Lost, which every receiver of a Lost for a member open there sends; a received Lost's cursor; nothing for a credential no longer admissible, S left the sync set (its Lost names none) or a digest difference. `Record::stopped` is memory only; `release` reports each as `Moved::Forgot` and clears it. `Moved::Left` carries `Leaving` (`Connection`: the record keeps the member; `Kept`; `Forgot`), so streams drops presence, head and refusals with the stop (notes/sync-streams.md). A Lost re-marks S only when it closes a member open here (an answering Lost re-marking the side that just closed loops with a tester whose Hit failed).
- Rejected: keeping stores' members open across a head move (re-key in place). The store cannot tell remaining readers from the removed holder without fresh proof, a round trip during which the stream must stay capped at the head event while the reader's stream passes `until`; an in-place re-proof needs a report hold and a server-told rewind to `until`, two mechanisms rebuilding what Lost's cursor already says. A close keeping its stop costs a Lost each way, a Hit and a join from the cursor: O(1) per head move per reader (`growth::a_head_move_relists_nothing_between_a_reader_and_a_store_at_n_and_10n`).
- Rejected: keeping stops across connections. A peer that drops S while away never tells, and its presence would count for good (leave and `later_held` read it); a digest over stops would be a wire change for a rare path.

## Credentials as built
- **Read S** (`Creds::reader` over `Engine::read_credentials`): shows the sync proof of each distinct sync key on its path whose sync secret it holds and its head's deposit key; tests the sync proofs of the sync secrets it holds, newest first. A stub holder judges as a reader.
- **Kept unread**: `Creds::kept_unread` over `Unread::credentials` (notes/sync-unread.md, The rule). Showing nothing, `Creds` is empty; S stays synced, so its `changed_at` moves and the peer gets a generation. The scripted tests build their fake from the same function, so a test cannot pass on credentials the product lacks.
- A claim's Hit is answered signed by the head's sync key (`Creds::step`); `Adm::of_head` says whether a credential is of the head.
- **Claims**: both kinds also show their head claim keys (`Store::head_claim_keys`, from `store/claims.rs`: each entry's blocks indexed by the sync key they are bound to when its bytes arrive, each block verified once, rows dropped with the entry) and test the claim keys of the holders they reach S's thing through (engine `sync_ids/claims.rs`).
- **Head**: a node holding no path for S, a claim key's Hit refused at a peer that is no store, or a test finding none of its claim keys shown asks the peer Head (`Opens::ask_head`, once per change of S there); any node holding S answers (`head_answer`). The path an answer gives is held in memory, per connection; a node keeping S unread uses its store's path.
- **A filter arrival tests anew** the sync IDs whose tested beacons its new entries may show (a per-connection index of tested beacons), so a claim written at the peer after this side tested is found.
- **Stores owed a Register**: an ended member at a store source closes at its `until` only once the registrar (`inbox`, `Opens::on_owed`) owes it no Register; a class moved since an `until` was reached opens anew (spec/sync-opens.md §6).
- A Deposit item goes into the store charged to the peer's record, else the connection.

## One connection per pair
spec/sync-opens.md §1, judged before a record is taken (`one_per_pair`: the smaller `pair_order` loses, `lost_to` names the winner); two live connections never share one record's members.
- **One record per pair** (spec/sync-opens.md §3; every kept record names the node thing its peer proved first, so the node is the pair): a take drops every other record naming that node (`drop_others`, none a connection holds), and a take that loses the pair rule drops its record unless the winner holds it (`take`'s refused path); each goes at `flush` with its charges, as an eviction (`Opens::dropped`), its members re-marked on the connection that stays (`drop_record`; `load`s a record a start did not follow, to know its members). Each end drops by itself at its own take, so no exchange can be cut half-done: a crash before a drop is durable, or a closed connection, leaves the extra record until the pair's next take there (`a_crash_settling_crossed_dials_leaves_one_record_*`). Crossed first dials (each side's Resume out before the other's record exists) make a record per connection; the second take at each end finds the first connection by node and settles the pair.
- The re-mark matters: the survivor's `acked_pos` may already be past `changed_at(S)` for an S open only on the dropped record, which nothing would test there again.
- A dropped record's charges pass to the survivor's record for what the survivor's stream showed (the store's `gone` rule, notes/sync-store.md), so an entry both brought stays though the survivor's stream passed it.
- Rejected: a message exchange naming the other record by its pair key (a Twin: both ends drop on one answer). One side drops before the other learns, so a crash between leaves the record at one end with nothing left to name it, its members holding `wait_for` until eviction; and with the node proven first it found nothing the pair rule does not. One Resume nonce per set of proven things (so crossed dials derive the same `r`) lets any node proving that set learn the nonce half of another node's record key.

## Durability and eviction
- A kept record is created in the store at once, before the answer naming it goes and before any module writes rows in it; Resume lists and the listener's lookup read the store (`records_of_things`: by the things a record's claims were signed as).
- **Memory**: a connection taking a record loads it (O(members, filter entries)) unless memory holds it. A kept record with a member stays in memory from its first take until it goes (evicted, dropped for its pair); one with no member leaves memory at the `flush` that finds no connection holding it, so a dialer proving fresh things costs memory only while connected. Its peer filter's entries drop at that `flush` and are read again at the next take (`records::filter_values`, O(filter rows)), so what is judged per record (`member_at`, stamped per (source, S) and moved by no take or release; `conn_of`, the one fact "record R is connected on C", stamped per source) outlives its connections. Bound: the kept records with a member (their members and own state, all rows counted in `RECORD_BUDGET`; a member needs a proof that its peer may hold S), plus the records connections hold. In memory a peer filter is a sorted `Vec<u64>` behind an `Arc`, at most 2^22 entries (the format's bound).
- **Writes**: every other change marks the record dirty, and `Opens::flush` writes it at the end of the step, after streams wrote the positions it moved. A crash between leaves opens' older word, which streams' rows cover (a member that left is stopped there; a join's cursor is streams' `joining` row).
- **Eviction**: `flush` then evicts past `RECORD_BUDGET` (records in use, held by a connection or listed in a Resume out, stay), each evicted record with its charges in one transaction (`Store::write_records`), so no peer's entries outlive its record, and from memory with it. Every row counts toward the budget, the peer filter's included. `flush` returns the records that went (evicted, dropped for its pair) and the driver tells inbox and status (`record_gone`), whose per-record keys go with them.
- A store failure inside a step is noted (`fault`) and fails the step at `settle` or `flush`.
- A peer with no record has its unstamped charges per connection (spec/sync-ids.md §5 Peer budget): dropping and fetching again beats a row keyed by a node key.

## Peers to wait for
`Opens::wait_for` (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for) is the one rule for duties that act once no peer could still take or tell what S holds: leave, a self-removal's drop, life's heard out, and a complete pass with no source, all through `Opens::waited`, which judges each peer by `Engine::may_hold` of what its record's claims prove now (`Engine::proven`, no copies), a self-removal skipping peers proving this node's own things.
- **A peer counts** while it has S open, its kept record holds S, or who it may be on the rosters here says it may hold S (`Engine::may_hold`: a proven thing other than an own thing with a proven role on S's thing; S kept unread, anyone) and none of its records has acknowledged S's current credentials (`acked_pos` below `changed_at(S)`; S with no `changed_at` yet: every record). So a sync ID just joined (accept, redeem, a strong written while the thing's other holders are away) waits for them; a grantee told S that never syncs it blocks nothing (a grant moves the grantee's credentials, not the granter's `changed_at(S)`; a grantee whose records acknowledged S before is waited for once it opens S); a stranger's record never counts for a thing it holds no role on; a one-node account acts at once.
- **A peer is its proven things**: records naming the same things are one peer, told once one of them is.
- **Who a peer is**: what its record or connection names (`osis_store::Named`: its proofs' claims, each a thing, the key that signed as it and the path claimed; the node thing the first's) is judged now by `Engine::proven` into proven things, each at the role its path gives here; Stranded's writers, who it may be and the inbox's offers read those, a proven thing's proven role on S being the least of its path's and its own role on S. A connection judges with the copies its peer's proofs came with (a trial: taken in, judged, undone; the guard), a record with no connection on this node's reading alone (notes/accounts-switch-brief.md, Connection proofs).
- **Index** (`Acks`): kept records by proven things and the highest `acked_pos` per set of them, grouped by `Named`; a set of records none of which acknowledged S is judged record by record (`wait_for`), so a revoked record of it releases its wait. Loaded from the store at the first settle (`Transaction::records_all`, O(records)); before it loads, `wait_for` says wait. Kept by `set_acked`/`acks_moved` (an ack rising or re-marked, a record made, a record's proven things changed) and eviction. Bound: one entry per kept record, so `RECORD_BUDGET` bounds a peer that never returns. A restart reads what `flush` last wrote, members and `acked_pos` together.
- **Stamps** (`peers`, per S): members joining or leaving, S's `changed_at` moving, a record's `acked_pos` moving (each S whose `changed_at` lies between the old and new positions; every S past the lower one when its proven things changed or a record is made: what the record's next filter iterates anyway), whole on eviction and load.
- Rejected: a refusal ending the wait on its peer. A refusal answers an Offer tested after S's credentials last moved, so once answered that peer's record acknowledged S and holds no member of it: it is already no peer to wait for.

## Policy: whom to dial, prove to and sync with
spec/sync-opens.md §1 Policy (spec/requirements.md, Offline and network, leaves it to the account and node). One seam, `osis_sync::Policy` (types.rs, a `SyncConfig` field, reached as `Cx::policy`): `dial(&Address)` at `Dials::offer`, `identify(Option<&Proven>)` at the dialer's Prove and the listener's Proved (none: an empty proof list), `sync_with(&Proven)` once a side judged the peer's proofs (no: the session closes).
- Only the permissive policy exists (every answer yes), so no other code asks who the peer is before syncing: every proving pair keeps a record (`named` is every proven thing), every shown credential's beacon goes to every connection, a sync proof's Hit opens whoever proved. A peer proving nothing (a policy saying no at either side) still runs the set unkept (NoRecord, Resume Error 1).
- A future configuration (per account, overridable per node: anyone; identify to anyone but sync only with known accounts; only this account's nodes; one node at one address) adds fields to `Policy` and answers from them; answers needing the engine ("known account") need `Policy` to take the engine. Not built for it: re-judging connections or candidates when the policy changes (`Dials::set_node` keeps candidates offered before).
- Kept for other reasons: a node key proved only to this account's nodes (readers never learn which node wrote, spec/requirements.md Confidentiality); LAN's fresh transport key per network join (spec/sync-transport.md §4).
- **Records for strangers**: a flood of peers proving fresh things evicts records least recently used (spec/sync-hostile.md §2); each record holds the peer's filter (up to 2^22 entries plus as many additions, 8 bytes each), so a few connections with full filters (about 5 MB on the wire each) evict every other record. Accepted as a cost attack (the evicted peers are read from the start), simpler than a "known" judgement on records.

## Seams
The core (`Opens::message`, `tick`, `closed`) runs over `opens::Ctx { holds: &mut dyn Holds, wire: &mut dyn Wire, store, now }`: `Holds` is what the node holds per S (`NodeHolds` over the engine and unread; a test's table), `Wire` the session calls (`Sessions`; a test's pipe). So the rules run against scripted peers (core/sync/tests/it/harness/scripted.rs).

`RESYNC_MS` (one constant, types.rs, shared with streams): after a refused generation a whole filter follows.
