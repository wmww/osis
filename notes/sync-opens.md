# Sync: opens
> Summary: osis-sync's `opens` module (core/sync/src/opens/): per-peer records, agreement, filters and Hits, the shared set per connection and the walk; how it follows credentials by change, how records are kept durable and evicted, the seams its tests use, and what is stubbed.

Spec: spec/sync-opens.md §2-§6, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk. Model: core/test/tla/net/NetOpen.tla (each rule it flags is one function here, named in its comment). Overview: notes/sync.md.

## What it owns
- **Records** (`opens/records.rs`, rows in the peer's osis-store record under tags `R` own state: `acked_pos`, this side's filter generations and the positions they reach, the peer filter's shape; `F` the peer's filter per generation, Golomb-Rice coded, a whole one replacing those before it; `M` a member: the peer's class and this side's as declared, credential, `until`, beacon; `W` where this side's walk of S stopped at the peer). No other walk state is durable.
- Per connection: binding, phase, agreement, filter generations in flight, the Hits its filter makes possible, the Offer in flight, reported positions (`Positions`), walk state, and `lost_to` (the pair rule).
- `changed_at` per S: the store's `changed_note` row, keeping the digest of the credentials it was advanced for, so a restart advances it only for a value that moved.

Opens reports each member joining or leaving a connection's shared set as a `Moved` (`Joined` with a `Rejoin`, `Left`, `Until`); the driver hands each to streams, read, status and inbox at once. Opens never calls streams; streams never diffs the shared set. Streams' reported positions come back as `Reported` and fold into `Positions` (O(1) per report; an Applied checks only ended members).

## Work follows change
`NodeHolds::due` follows the sync IDs this node syncs (`set::SyncsFollow`: the sync set and each leaving one while it waits) and, per S, the digest of its credentials (`node_creds`: the engine's `read_credentials(s)` for a read S, `Unread::credentials(s)` for one kept unread) as an `Effects` key. A moved value advances `changed_at(S)`, re-judges S's members on the connections holding it, and makes a filter addition and a test due on each connection.
- **Standing** (spec/sync-opens.md §6): a member's standing is a function of its peer's proof as judged now, so each member of a connection up is one more `Effects` key, (connection, S) → J of what the connection names, counted now (`Holds::standing`: `counted` then `Engine::judge_peer`, read on the memo clock). Followed from its open or the take that re-judges it, unfollowed when it leaves (at the next tick). Whatever moves J (the peer's removal or demotion held, by sync or by a Prove's rotations; its account's key revoked; an `until` passing) makes exactly the members resting on it due, and `tick` re-judges those: O(members whose J read what was written: a role commit on S's actor makes each member of S due, re-judged without a filter or test), no timer. A record not held by a connection is judged at its next take (a restart's included). What changes: an open by J closes once J is neither reader nor sync holder; a credential open toward a peer J judges departed closes at once while S's epoch does not cover the departure (§4 Departed; a grant past its `until` departs for keys only at its finalize, so a credential open toward it stays until that rotation), except at a store source: there it is ended (`Member::departed`, persisted with the member), registering goes on without waiting for the steps' role commits, and it closes on the peer's Lost once the peer stops past its keys (a node keeping S unread learns its removal by a Register alone; closing at once left a removed store that took entries from a reader not yet knowing the removal keeping them for good). The same holds where S was not open at the removal (the peer a reader source then, moving to keep S unread just after: closed at once, it keeps S unread at a step the reader closed past): toward a departed peer the reader accepts a store's Hit on the sync proof of any recorded step (`Creds::walk`, `Adm::walk`, indexed beside the shows, never in a filter: the filter showed each while it was the newest), and the open is re-judged at once, so ended and registered past the store's keys. Why the store's Hit and not the reader's test: a reader tests only on its own changes and re-marks, and the store's new filter generation can arrive after the reader last tested S; the store tests its step's proof as it moves. A credential's epoch is judged in its own role (`Engine::covers_departures`: a sync key's or proof's sync epoch, a read secret's read epoch); a peer only no longer counted (a revoked account key, J unknown) keeps an open by a credential it still holds (it holds S's current secrets until S rotates). Rejected: S's roster in the credentials digest (every grant or removal re-judging S on every connection). Nothing walks every sync ID per connection but a whole filter (first contact) and the Hits index, built at a connection's first Hit and then kept per changed S, so a reconnect the peer Hits nothing on costs nothing per sync ID held (issues/opens-hit-index-cost.md).

## Policy: whom to dial, prove to and sync with
spec/requirements.md (Offline and network) leaves it to the account (and node): spec/sync-opens.md §1 Policy. One seam, `osis_sync::Policy` (types.rs, a `SyncConfig` field, reached as `Cx::policy`): `dial(&Address)` at `Dials::offer`, `identify(Option<&Proven>)` at the dialer's Prove and the listener's Proved (none: an empty proof list), `sync_with(&Proven)` once a side judged the peer's proofs (no: the session closes). Only the permissive policy exists (every answer yes), so no other code asks who the peer is before syncing: every proving pair keeps a record (`named` is every proven actor), every shown credential's beacon goes to every connection, a read secret's Hit opens whoever proved. A peer proving no actor (a policy saying no at either side) still runs the set unkept (NoRecord, Resume Error 1).
- A future configuration (per account, overridable per node; examples: anyone; identify to anyone but sync only with known accounts; only this account's nodes; one node at one address) adds fields to `Policy` and answers from them. Answers needing the engine ("known account": contacts, rosters) need `Policy` to take the engine. Not built for it: re-judging connections or candidates when the policy changes (`Dials::set_node` keeps candidates offered before).
- Kept, for other reasons: a node key proved only to this account's nodes (readers never learn which device wrote, spec/requirements.md Confidentiality); LAN's fresh transport key per network join (spec/sync-transport.md §4; already built, and a stricter policy would want it).
- Records for strangers: a flood of peers proving fresh actors evicts records least recently used (spec/sync-hostile.md §2); each record holds the peer's filter (up to 2^22 entries plus as many additions, 8 bytes each), so a few connections with full filters (about 5 MB on the wire each) evict every other record. Accepted as a cost attack (the evicted peers are read from the start), simpler than a "known" judgement on records.

## Peers to wait for
`Opens::wait_for` (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for) is the one rule the duties that act once no peer could still take or tell what S holds read: leave, a self-removal's drop, life's heard out, read's emptying, and a complete pass with no source (all through `Opens::waited`, which judges each peer by `Engine::roster_holders`; a self-removal skips this account and its nodes). A peer counts while it has S open, its kept record holds S, or the rosters say it may hold S (`RosterHolders::may_hold` on its proven actors) and none of its records has acknowledged S's current credentials (`acked_pos` below `changed_at(S)`; S with no `changed_at` yet: every record). So a sync ID just joined (accept, redeem, a strong written while the thing's other holders are away) waits for them, a grantee told S that never syncs it blocks nothing (a grant moves the grantee's credentials, not the granter's `changed_at(S)`: a grantee whose records acknowledged S before is waited for once it opens S), a stranger's record never counts for a thing it holds no role on, and a one-node account acts at once.
- Who a peer is, two rules over what its record or connection names (`osis_store::Named`: the account with the key it proved with, the node, the key actors), on what the node holds now. Trusted as (`opens::counted`: J, the inbox's judged reports): the account while that key's epoch is open, the node while ours, key actors. May be, for waits (`opens::awaited` over `Named`: waits, pass waits): the node while ours, else the account at the key proved with unless `Engine::account_key_revoked` (that key's epoch revoked: a counting rotation closing it carries a removal, spec/permissions.md §5.5 Revoked, spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for), key actors. A removed node of this account is never waited for. A node of another account away across a plain rotation is still that account; across a rotation carrying any removal it is not, since a removed or reset node is not told from an honest one at rest and would hold the wait forever (an honest one re-proves on its next connect and counts again; the cost is the stated limit). Outside the account the engine cannot read the inner signature or D's removals (logged under D only), so a counting rotation's removals list is trusted, a held one checked for being the account's commit at an epoch the rotation descends from. `Acks` groups by `Who` (`Named` without the key), so another node of that account acknowledging covers the old-key record; a set of records none of which acknowledged S is judged record by record (`wait_for`), so a revoked record of it releases its wait. A later step could let a removal name the node it removes to outsiders, so only that node's records stop counting.
- A peer is its proven actors: records naming the same actors are one peer, told once one of them is. Limit: two nodes of another account proving only the account are one peer.
- Rejected: a refusal (a ByRole refused, a walk stopped there) ending the wait on its peer (once a timed loss's own exit). A refusal answers an Offer tested after S's credentials last moved, so once it is answered that peer's record acknowledged S and holds no member of it: it is no peer to wait for already, and the rule had no case of its own.
- Index (`Acks`): kept records by actors and the highest `acked_pos` per actors, loaded from the store at the first settle (`Transaction::records_all`, O(records)), kept by `set_acked`/`acks_moved` (an ack rising or re-marked, a record made, a record's actors changed) and eviction. Before it loads, `wait_for` says wait. Bound: one entry per kept record, so `RECORD_BUDGET` bounds a peer that never returns. A restart reads what `flush` last wrote, members and `acked_pos` together.
- Stamps (`peers`, per S): members joining or leaving, S's `changed_at` moving, a record's `acked_pos` moving (each S whose `changed_at` lies between the old and new positions; every S past the lower one when its actors changed, or a record is made: what the record's next filter iterates anyway), whole on eviction and load.

## Class
A member's class (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source) is what the peer declared (`unread` in each Hit, Static and ByRole item and each answer accepting one); the member also keeps the class this side declared (`Member::own`). A tester opens on an answer only while its class is the one its item declared (its holding may move with the Offer out). `rejudge` closes a member that stays, not ended, once this side's class moved (`Close::Class`, kept as at `Until`), so S opens again with both declared anew; a take re-judges alike, so a restart or reconnect after a move does too. Cost: the members of the S whose credentials moved (a holding change moves them). Rejected: the class from the credential that opened S (a sync proof and a static entry are shown by both kinds, so which side's Hit landed first decided), and from J (rosters move without moving credentials, so nothing re-judges; a grantee given read keeps S unread until its secrets arrive). A false declaration steers only duties and counting: deposits, Register and Deletes go to a declared store (nothing a reader cannot read), status counts a declared reader's reports as held (a peer could falsify its reports anyway). Anything disclosed is gated on proof, never on the class: a suspect list goes only to a peer proving an account that may read S (`inbox/suspect.rs`, the receiver's own judgement of a list).

## Ending
- An ended member's `until` (`holds::member_until`): at a node keeping S unread the position of S's head event (`Store::head_at`); at a reader opened by a read secret of epoch e the highest first-logged position in S of an entry of a rotation superseding e (`Engine::rotations_superseding`, entries by `commit_blobs`), else the log's end; a credential with no epoch at a reader (sync key, claim, proof, static entry) ends at the log's end. A store failure answers 0 (served to nothing, closed at once). A Lost's cursor below a reported position is `Store::cursor_at(until)`.
- An ended member stays ended until it closes, also once it counts again: its peer's stream may already be served past `until` with S's entries there skipped, so only a close naming `until` and a join from there delivers them.
- **Where it stopped** (spec/sync-opens.md §6): one rule, what a source showed of S lives exactly as long as S is a member of that source's record or a stop of S is kept on the connection up. Closes keep: at `until` (`Close::Until`) where this side's stream holds it (`Rejoin::Held`) until the peer's Lost, which every receiver of a Lost for a member open there sends; a received Lost's cursor; nothing for J departed at a reader source, S left the sync set (its Lost names none) or a digest difference. `Record::stopped` is memory only; `release` reports each as `Moved::Forgot` and clears it. `Moved::Left` carries `Leaving` (`Connection`: the record keeps the member; `Kept`; `Forgot`), so streams drops presence, head and refusals with the stop (notes/sync-streams.md). A Lost re-marks S only when it closes a member open here (an answering Lost re-marking the side that just closed loops with a tester whose Hit failed).
- Why not keep stores' members open across a head move (re-key in place): the store cannot tell remaining readers from the removed holder without fresh proof, a round trip during which the stream must stay capped at the head event, and the reader's stream passes `until` meanwhile, so an in-place re-proof needs both a report hold (re-prove before reporting past the head event) and a server-told rewind to `until`: two mechanisms rebuilding what Lost's cursor already says. A close keeping its stop costs a Lost each way, a Hit and a join from the cursor: O(1) per head move per reader (`growth::a_head_move_relists_nothing_between_a_reader_and_a_store_at_n_and_10n`). Why not keep stops across connections: a peer that drops S while away never tells, and its presence would count for good (leave and `later_held` read it); a digest over stops was the alternative, a wire change for a rare path.

## Credentials as built
- Read S: shows each current read secret, every static entry of S's roster, and the sync proof of the newest recorded step; tests the read secrets, the static entries of the key actors it acts for (`ReadCredentials::acting`, a link it redeems), and the recorded steps deepest first by sync key, else claim key, then every held sync secret's sync key newest first, each distinct key once (`holds::walk_tests`).
- Kept unread: `Creds::kept_unread` over `Unread::credentials` (its steps' sync keys and proofs shown and tested: the head's while a held secret derives it, the head's deposit key and claim set shown too; else every sync secret's of the record that no step of its path bears, all of them with no head; a Hit on a sync key is answered by that key, anything else by `step`). Showing nothing, `Creds` is empty; S stays synced, so its `changed_at` moves and the peer gets a generation. The scripted tests build their fake from the same function, so a test cannot pass on credentials the product lacks.
- A Deposit item goes into the store charged to the peer's record, else the connection. A Behind is answered from the store's path, trying each key kind in turn (the item names none); a signature that does not verify answers 13 for a sync ID not kept unread, else 14. A Behind answered 13 does nothing more: once the peer keeps S, its own test opens it (it tests its step's sync proof, which a reader shows).
- A static Hit is answered from the path kept (a reader's recorded one, else the store's) with the head's entry box. `take_grant` opens the grant (`Engine::open_grant`: the path verifies from genesis through the newest step recorded here, the box opens for a held key actor, its sync secret derives the head's sync key) and learns it (`Engine::learn_grant`) only once the answer's signature verifies under that key.

## Durability and eviction
A kept record is created in the store at once, before the answer naming it goes and before any module writes rows in it; Resume lists and the listener's lookup read the store (`records_of_actors`). A connection taking a record loads it (O(members, filter entries)) unless memory holds it. Every other change marks the record dirty, and `Opens::flush` writes it at the end of the step, after streams wrote the positions it moved: a crash between leaves opens' older word, which streams' rows cover (a member that left is stopped there; a join's cursor is streams' `joining` row). `flush` then evicts past `RECORD_BUDGET` (records in use: held by a connection or listed in a Resume out), each evicted record with its charges in one transaction (`Store::write_records`), so no peer's entries outlive its record, and drops from memory records no connection holds. Every row counts toward the budget, the peer filter's included; in memory a peer filter is a sorted `Vec<u64>` behind an `Arc`, at most 2^22 entries (the format's bound). A store failure inside a step is noted (`fault`) and fails the step at `settle` or `flush`.

The walk: a reader tests a sync ID only on its own changes and re-marks, never at a peer's filter alone; a store that starts keeping S opens it by its own test, and one whose head moves ends the members open by the old key (a Lost, which re-marks). A walk Hit refused (a filter false positive) marks the walk `missed`: the next test skips its walk credentials and asks Behind. A Behind answer's steps are known once they verify (`holds::known_steps`: each a child of the step before, the first of a recorded or walk-known step, or a genesis), and a step learned or a refusal tests S again; an answer of steps already known changes nothing, so a walk asks each step once. A Behind answered 4 (refused for now) backs off by `inbox::backoff_ms` of the refusals in a row (`WalkState::backoffs`), the one refused-for-now back-off sync has: `Conn::retries` holds (when, S) and feeds `next_wake`; the tick takes a due one out (no wake in the past stays, whatever the test then finds: S opened meanwhile, a credential shown, an Offer still out) and marks its walk `retry`, which joins the next test as `missed` does; while backed off a test of S neither asks nor moves its stop. Bound: one per (connection, S), gone with the ask, S leaving the sync set or the connection.
- **Stops** (`walk_on`, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Stranded there): a test of S toward a sync holder that shows nothing stops the walk there when the newest step known there gives no credential (`step_creds`: its sync key, if a held sync secret derives it; a source's answer carries no claim set) or Behind refused it, or no credential is left to ask Behind by; it names the newest known step's sync key (none: the recorded head). Indexed per S in `stops` (stamped; status reads `walk_stop`) for the connections up, and kept in a kept record (`W` rows; spec/sync-opens.md §3 keeps the source's step there), so the record's next connection (a restart's) starts stopped there without testing S again. A stop goes when S opens there, a test there shows a credential, or S leaves the sync set (its rows go with S); a connection closing only unindexes it. Bound: one per (record, S), or per (connection, S) with no record. Rejected: keeping `acked_pos` below a stopped S instead, which re-tests every sync ID changed after it at each filter arrival.

One connection per pair (spec/sync-opens.md §1) is judged before a record is taken (`one_per_pair`: the smaller `pair_order` loses, `lost_to` names the winner); two live connections never share one record's members. Crossed first dials between accounts (each side's Resume out before the other's record exists: the differential's `introduce` does this for every pair) make a record per connection; the Twin exchange (`send_twins` after a dialer's new record, `twin`, `twinned`) finds them and `settle_pair` closes one, drops its record at both ends (`Opens::dropped`, removed at `flush` with its charges, as an eviction) and re-marks its members on the survivor. At least one dialer always sends a Twin the other side can match: the later of the two records' dialers finds the other connection's record made. Without it both connections stay for their whole life and every entry streams twice per pair; a later simultaneous redial resumes one record per connection (each side's most recent first), so it never heals by itself. The re-mark matters: a Hit refused on the survivor before the Twins landed is not tried there again until something re-marks S (test `a_twinned_records_members_are_remarked_on_the_survivor`). `dropped` lives in memory and `flush` runs at every step, so only a crash inside the step leaves the loser's record in the store: an orphan never resumed (the peer dropped its copy) whose members make `wait_for` wait on that peer until budget eviction; accepted as rare. Its charges pass to the survivor's record for what the survivor's stream showed (the store's `gone` rule, notes/sync-store.md), so an entry both brought during the round trip stays though the survivor's stream passed it.
- Rejected, no-message rules: "two records naming the same actors: the smaller order hash wins" closes a live connection to another node of that account (both prove only the account, §2 Limit); one Resume nonce per peer actor set, so crossed dials derive the same `r`, lets any node proving that actor set learn the nonce half of another node's record key, and needs nonce lifetime rules. Twin's tags are keyed by the pair key only the record's two ends hold.

## Seams
The core (`Opens::message`, `tick`, `closed`) runs over `opens::Ctx { holds: &mut dyn Holds, wire: &mut dyn Wire, store, now }`: `Holds` is what the node holds per S (`NodeHolds` over the engine and unread; a test's table), `Wire` the session calls (`Sessions`; a test's pipe). So the rules run against scripted peers (core/sync/tests/it/harness/scripted.rs).

## Reasons
- A peer with no record has its unstamped charges per connection (spec/sync-ids.md §5 Peer budget): dropping and fetching again beats a row keyed by a node key.
- `RESYNC_MS` (30 s): after a refused generation a whole filter follows; streams has its own `RESYNC_MS` for a refused join. They are separate constants of the same value.
