# Sync: opens
> Summary: osis-sync's `opens` module (core/sync/src/opens/): per-peer records, agreement, filters and Hits, the shared set per connection, members' standing and ending, the walk and its stops, one connection per pair, durability and eviction, peers to wait for, and the policy seam.

Spec: spec/sync-opens.md §2-§6, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk. Model: core/test/tla/net/NetOpen.tla (each rule it flags is one function here, named in its comment). Overview: notes/sync.md.

**Principle:** a member (S open toward a peer) stands exactly while the peer's proof, judged now, lets it hold S; every judgement follows by change (S's credentials, the peer's standing), and what a source showed of S lives exactly as long as S is a member of that source's record or a stop of it is kept on the connection up.

## What it owns
- **Records** (`opens/records.rs`, rows in the peer's osis-store record): `R` own state (`acked_pos`, this side's filter generations and the positions they reach, the peer filter's shape); `F` the peer's filter per generation, Golomb-Rice coded, a whole one replacing those before it; `M` a member (the peer's class and this side's as declared, credential, `until`, beacon); `W` where this side's walk of S stopped at the peer. No other walk state is durable.
- **Per connection**: binding, phase, agreement, filter generations in flight, the Hits its filter makes possible, the Offer in flight, reported positions (`Positions`), walk state, and `lost_to` (the pair rule).
- **`changed_at` per S**: the store's `changed_note` row, keeping the digest of the credentials it was advanced for, so a restart advances it only for a value that moved.

## What it tells other modules
Each member opening or closing on a connection is a `Moved` (`Joined` with a `Rejoin`, `Left`, `Until`, `Forgot`); a kept record taken or let go by a connection is one fact (`Taken`, saying whether the record was just read from its rows, when the modules learn its members; `Released`: `conn_of`; no member moves); a record kept nowhere letting go its connection is each member `Left`; a member of a record no connection holds closing in the record is `Gone` (streams forgets what that source showed of S, status the member and its lacking count, inbox its register keys and lists owed). The driver hands each to streams, read, status and inbox at once; they key what they keep by record and read the connection only where they send or ask. Opens never calls streams; streams never diffs the shared set. Streams' reported positions come back as `Reported` and fold into `Positions` (O(1) per report; an Applied checks only ended members).

## Work follows change
`NodeHolds::due` follows the sync IDs this node syncs (`set::SyncsFollow`) and, per S, the digest of its credentials (`node_creds`: `Engine::read_credentials(s)` for a read S, `Unread::credentials(s)` for one kept unread) as an `Effects` key. A moved value advances `changed_at(S)`, judges S's member in every record in memory holding it (`held`, by record; `conn_of` says whether a connection holds it), and makes a filter addition and a test due on each connection.
- Nothing walks every sync ID per connection but a whole filter (first contact) and the Hits index, built at a connection's first Hit and then kept per changed S, so a reconnect the peer Hits nothing on costs nothing per sync ID held (issues/opens-hit-index-cost.md).
- Rejected: S's roster in the credentials digest (every grant or removal would re-judge S on every connection).

## Standing
spec/sync-opens.md §6. Each member of a record is one `Effects` key, (record, S) → J of what the record names, counted now (`Holds::standing`: `counted` then `Engine::judge_peer`, read on the memo clock; followed and nothing it read moved, the kept value, `Effects::current`, so a take reads nothing again), or `None` where the peer is no one (`awaited` empty for a record naming someone: a revoked account key, a removed node of this account).
- **Followed** (`Opens::followed`) from its open or the take that judges it, across the record's connections, until S leaves the record or the sync set, the record goes (evicted, twin-dropped, a record kept nowhere let go) or the record's named actors move at a take. Bound: one per member of a kept record in memory, so `RECORD_BUDGET`.
- **Due on change**: whatever moves J or who the peer may be (the peer's removal or demotion held, by sync or by a Prove's rotations; its account's key revoked; an `until` passing) makes exactly the members resting on it due, and `tick` re-judges those, no timer: O(members whose J read what was written; a role commit on S's actor makes each member of S due, re-judged without a filter or test).
- **J moves alone** where nothing of S's credentials moves, so only this path judges the member: a self-removal (no rotation, spec/permissions.md §3) reaching a node that may not rotate, a peer's account rotating plainly (pairing: its old key no longer counts), a read grant's `until` passing. `opens::a_self_removal_alone_ends_what_is_served_spec_sync_opens_6` is the world case that serves differently.
- **In the step of the change**, whether or not a connection holds the record (`Opens::judge`, by record), so an ended member's `until` is the position of the change and a take judges nothing that did not move (`idle::an_idle_reconnect_sends_reads_and_judges_the_same_at_10n_spec_sync_streams_4` counts `opens: members judged`). With no connection, an end is set in the record (seen by the next take: `ending` restored there) and a close removes the member from the record keeping nothing of where it stopped (`Moved::Gone`; `acked_pos` re-marked below S), which the peer finds by the next take's digest (spec/sync-opens.md §3 Agreement).
- **A start follows every kept member** (`Opens::follow_kept`, at the first settle once S's credentials are known): each kept record with a member is read from its rows (its peer filter left for a take, `Record::told` false so the first take still tells the modules its members) and its members judged and followed, so a removal while its peer is away across this node's restart closes it in its step (`opens::a_peer_departed_after_a_restart_keeps_nothing_it_showed_spec_sync_judgement_3`). O(kept records and members) once per start, within `RECORD_BUDGET`. Rejected: judging at the next take only: a peer that never returns kept its member, and what its source showed (read by `present_anywhere` from the rows), until eviction. A record read at a take (refused for its pair, spec/sync-opens.md §1) leaves memory again, so no record is in memory with a member not followed (`opens::a_take_refused_for_its_pair_leaves_nothing_unjudged_spec_sync_opens_6`). A take whose connection names other actors than the record (every first reconnect after first contact, issues/many-sync-ids-cost.md) judges every member: world tests of a change while away reconnect once first, or that take hides a change not judged in its step.
- **What closes**: an open by J closes once J is neither reader nor sync holder. A peer only no longer counted (an account key at a closed epoch, J unknown) keeps an open by a credential it still holds (it holds S's current secrets until S rotates); away, it is an away member (it re-proves at the new key). A peer that is no one keeps it only while a connection holds the record (its session's proofs); once none does (judged in the step of the change, or at the settle after a release: `Opens::let_go`) every member closes, since it never resumes the record (`opens::a_peer_that_is_no_one_closes_once_no_connection_holds_its_record_spec_sync_opens_6`, world: `a_peer_that_is_no_one_keeps_nothing_it_showed_spec_sync_judgement_3`).

### Departed peers
A credential open toward a peer J judges departed closes at once while S's epoch does not cover the departure (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Departed; a grant past its `until` departs for keys only at its finalize, so a credential open toward it stays until that rotation). A credential's epoch is judged in its own role (`Engine::covers_departures`: a sync key's or proof's sync epoch, a read secret's read epoch).
- **At a store source** it is ended instead (`Member::departed`, persisted with the member): registering goes on without waiting for the steps' role commits, and it closes on the peer's Lost once the peer stops past its keys. A node keeping S unread learns its removal by a Register alone; closing at once left a removed store that took entries from a reader not yet knowing the removal keeping them for good.
- **Where S was not open at the removal** (the peer a reader source then, moving to keep S unread just after): toward a departed peer the reader accepts a store's Hit on the sync proof of any recorded step (`Creds::walk`, `Adm::walk`, indexed beside the shows and in its filter toward that peer), and the open is re-judged at once, so ended and registered past the store's keys. A filter that showed a step only while it was the newest left such a store keeping its copy for good. Cost: a departed peer's filter carries each recorded step of S.
- Why the store's Hit and not the reader's test: a reader tests only on its own changes and re-marks, and the store's new filter generation can arrive after the reader last tested S; the store tests its step's proof as it moves.

## Class
A member's class (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source) is what the peer declared (`unread` in each Hit, Static and ByRole item and each answer accepting one); the member also keeps the class this side declared (`Member::own`).
- A tester opens on an answer only while its class is the one its item declared (its holding may move with the Offer out). `rejudge` closes a member that stays, not ended, once this side's class moved (`Close::Class`, kept as at `Until`), so S opens again with both declared anew; a take re-judges alike. Cost: the members of the S whose credentials moved (a holding change moves them).
- A false declaration steers only duties and counting: deposits, Register and Deletes go to a declared store (nothing a reader cannot read), status counts a declared reader's reports as held (a peer could falsify its reports anyway). Anything disclosed is gated on proof, never on the class: a suspect list goes only to a peer proving an account that may read S (`inbox/suspect.rs`).
- Rejected: the class from the credential that opened S (a sync proof and a static entry are shown by both kinds, so which side's Hit landed first decided); the class from J (rosters move without moving credentials, so nothing re-judges; a grantee given read keeps S unread until its secrets arrive).

## Ending
- **`until`** (`holds::member_until`): at a node keeping S unread the position of S's head event (`Store::head_at`); at a reader opened by a read secret of epoch e the highest first-logged position in S of an entry of a rotation superseding e (`Engine::rotations_superseding`, entries by `commit_blobs`), else the log's end; a credential with no epoch at a reader (sync key, claim, proof, static entry) ends at the log's end. A store failure answers 0 (served to nothing, closed at once). A Lost's cursor below a reported position is `Store::cursor_at(until)`.
- **An ended member stays ended until it closes**, also once it counts again: its peer's stream may already be served past `until` with S's entries there skipped, so only a close naming `until` and a join from there delivers them.
- **Where it stopped** (spec/sync-opens.md §6). Closes keep: at `until` (`Close::Until`) where this side's stream holds it (`Rejoin::Held`) until the peer's Lost, which every receiver of a Lost for a member open there sends; a received Lost's cursor; nothing for J departed at a reader source, S left the sync set (its Lost names none) or a digest difference. `Record::stopped` is memory only; `release` reports each as `Moved::Forgot` and clears it. `Moved::Left` carries `Leaving` (`Connection`: the record keeps the member; `Kept`; `Forgot`), so streams drops presence, head and refusals with the stop (notes/sync-streams.md). A Lost re-marks S only when it closes a member open here (an answering Lost re-marking the side that just closed loops with a tester whose Hit failed).
- Rejected: keeping stores' members open across a head move (re-key in place). The store cannot tell remaining readers from the removed holder without fresh proof, a round trip during which the stream must stay capped at the head event while the reader's stream passes `until`; an in-place re-proof needs a report hold and a server-told rewind to `until`, two mechanisms rebuilding what Lost's cursor already says. A close keeping its stop costs a Lost each way, a Hit and a join from the cursor: O(1) per head move per reader (`growth::a_head_move_relists_nothing_between_a_reader_and_a_store_at_n_and_10n`).
- Rejected: keeping stops across connections. A peer that drops S while away never tells, and its presence would count for good (leave and `later_held` read it); a digest over stops would be a wire change for a rare path.

## Credentials as built
- **Read S** shows each current read secret, every static entry of S's roster, and the sync proof of the newest recorded step; it tests the read secrets, the static entries of the key actors it acts for (`ReadCredentials::acting`, a link it redeems), and the recorded steps deepest first by sync key, else claim key, then every held sync secret's sync key newest first, each distinct key once (`holds::walk_tests`).
- **Kept unread**: `Creds::kept_unread` over `Unread::credentials` (notes/sync-unread.md, The rule); a Hit on a sync key is answered by that key, anything else by `step`. Showing nothing, `Creds` is empty; S stays synced, so its `changed_at` moves and the peer gets a generation. The scripted tests build their fake from the same function, so a test cannot pass on credentials the product lacks.
- A Deposit item goes into the store charged to the peer's record, else the connection.
- A Behind is answered from the store's path, trying each key kind in turn (the item names none); a signature that does not verify answers `UNKNOWN_SYNC_ID` (13) for a sync ID not kept unread, else `DECLINED` (14). A Behind answered 13 does nothing more: once the peer keeps S, its own test opens it (it tests its step's sync proof, which a reader shows).
- A static Hit is answered from the path kept (a reader's recorded one, else the store's) with the head's entry box. `take_grant` opens the grant (`Engine::open_grant`: the path verifies from genesis through the newest step recorded here, the box opens for a held key actor, its sync secret derives the head's sync key) and learns it (`Engine::learn_grant`) only once the answer's signature verifies under that key.
- Opening by role (J) and the rotation's box to each holder both carry weight: a reader behind gets back in only by a peer opening on its role, a holder that only stores only by the rotation's box of the new sync secret, in its key part or fired to its inbox (spec/sync-opens.md §4; spec/permissions.md §4 Key part). Rejected (Tess): a sealed copy of the new secrets for each holder in the sync ID's steps, which would let an old secret catch up and drop both: a node keeping the data unread would learn how many holders a thing has (spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read).

## The walk
A reader tests a sync ID only on its own changes and re-marks, never at a peer's filter alone; a store that starts keeping S opens it by its own test, and one whose head moves ends the members open by the old key (a Lost, which re-marks).
- A walk Hit refused (a filter false positive) marks the walk `missed`: the next test skips its walk credentials and asks Behind. A Behind answer's steps are known once they verify (`holds::known_steps`: each a child of the step before, the first of a recorded or walk-known step, or a genesis), and a step learned or a refusal tests S again; an answer of steps already known changes nothing, so a walk asks each step once.
- A Behind answered `RATE_LIMITED` (4, refused for now) backs off by `inbox::backoff_ms` of the refusals in a row (`WalkState::backoffs`), the one refused-for-now back-off sync has: `Conn::retries` holds (when, S) and feeds `next_wake`; the tick takes a due one out (no wake in the past stays, whatever the test then finds) and marks its walk `retry`, which joins the next test as `missed` does; while backed off a test of S neither asks nor moves its stop. Bound: one per (connection, S), gone with the ask, S leaving the sync set or the connection.
- **Stops** (`walk_on`, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Stranded there): a test of S toward a sync holder that shows nothing stops the walk there when the newest step known there gives no credential (`step_creds`: its sync key, if a held sync secret derives it; a source's answer carries no claim set), or Behind refused it, or no credential is left to ask Behind by; it names the newest known step's sync key (none: the recorded head). Indexed per S in `stops` (stamped; status reads `walk_stop`) for the connections up, and kept in a kept record (`W` rows; spec/sync-opens.md §3 keeps the source's step there), so the record's next connection (a restart's) starts stopped there without testing S again. A stop goes when S opens there, a test there shows a credential, or S leaves the sync set (its rows go with S); a connection closing only unindexes it. Bound: one per (record, S), or per (connection, S) with no record. Rejected: keeping `acked_pos` below a stopped S instead, which re-tests every sync ID changed after it at each filter arrival.

## One connection per pair
spec/sync-opens.md §1, judged before a record is taken (`one_per_pair`: the smaller `pair_order` loses, `lost_to` names the winner); two live connections never share one record's members.
- **Twins**: crossed first dials between accounts (each side's Resume out before the other's record exists; the differential's `introduce` does this for every pair) make a record per connection. The Twin exchange (`send_twins` after a dialer's new record, `twin`, `twinned`) finds them and `settle_pair` closes one, drops its record at both ends (`Opens::dropped`, removed at `flush` with its charges, as an eviction) and re-marks its members on the survivor. At least one dialer always sends a Twin the other side can match: the later of the two records' dialers finds the other connection's record made. Without it both connections stay for their whole life, every entry streams twice per pair, and a later simultaneous redial resumes one record per connection, so it never heals by itself.
- The re-mark matters: a Hit refused on the survivor before the Twins landed is not tried there again until something re-marks S (`a_twinned_records_members_are_remarked_on_the_survivor_*`).
- `dropped` lives in memory and `flush` runs at every step, so only a crash inside the step leaves the loser's record in the store: an orphan never resumed whose members make `wait_for` wait on that peer until budget eviction (issues/twin-orphan-record.md). Its charges pass to the survivor's record for what the survivor's stream showed (the store's `gone` rule, notes/sync-store.md), so an entry both brought during the round trip stays though the survivor's stream passed it.
- Rejected, no-message rules: "two records naming the same key actors: the smaller order hash wins" closes a live connection to another node of that account (both prove only the account); one Resume nonce per set of key actors a peer proves (so crossed dials derive the same `r`) lets any node proving that set learn the nonce half of another node's record key, and needs nonce lifetime rules. Twin's tags are keyed by the pair key only the record's two ends hold.

## Durability and eviction
- A kept record is created in the store at once, before the answer naming it goes and before any module writes rows in it; Resume lists and the listener's lookup read the store (`records_of_actors`).
- **Memory**: a connection taking a record loads it (O(members, filter entries)) unless memory holds it. A kept record with a member stays in memory from its first take until it goes (evicted, twin-dropped); one with no member leaves memory at the `flush` that finds no connection holding it, so a dialer proving fresh key actors costs memory only while connected. Its peer filter's entries drop at that `flush` and are read again at the next take (`records::filter_values`, O(filter rows)), so what is judged per record (`member_at`, stamped per (source, S) and moved by no take or release; `conn_of`, the one fact "record R is connected on C", stamped per source) outlives its connections. Bound: the kept records with a member (their members, walk stops and own state, all rows counted in `RECORD_BUDGET`; a member needs a proof that its peer may hold S), plus the records connections hold. In memory a peer filter is a sorted `Vec<u64>` behind an `Arc`, at most 2^22 entries (the format's bound).
- **Writes**: every other change marks the record dirty, and `Opens::flush` writes it at the end of the step, after streams wrote the positions it moved. A crash between leaves opens' older word, which streams' rows cover (a member that left is stopped there; a join's cursor is streams' `joining` row).
- **Eviction**: `flush` then evicts past `RECORD_BUDGET` (records in use, held by a connection or listed in a Resume out, stay), each evicted record with its charges in one transaction (`Store::write_records`), so no peer's entries outlive its record, and from memory with it. Every row counts toward the budget, the peer filter's included. `flush` returns the records that went (evicted, twin-dropped) and the driver tells inbox and status (`record_gone`), whose per-record keys go with them.
- A store failure inside a step is noted (`fault`) and fails the step at `settle` or `flush`.
- A peer with no record has its unstamped charges per connection (spec/sync-ids.md §5 Peer budget): dropping and fetching again beats a row keyed by a node key.

## Peers to wait for
`Opens::wait_for` (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for) is the one rule for duties that act once no peer could still take or tell what S holds: leave, a self-removal's drop, life's heard out, and a complete pass with no source, all through `Opens::waited`, which judges each peer by `Engine::roster_holders` (a self-removal skips this account and its nodes).
- **A peer counts** while it has S open, its kept record holds S, or the rosters say it may hold S (`RosterHolders::may_hold` on its proven key actors) and none of its records has acknowledged S's current credentials (`acked_pos` below `changed_at(S)`; S with no `changed_at` yet: every record). So a sync ID just joined (accept, redeem, a strong written while the thing's other holders are away) waits for them; a grantee told S that never syncs it blocks nothing (a grant moves the grantee's credentials, not the granter's `changed_at(S)`; a grantee whose records acknowledged S before is waited for once it opens S); a stranger's record never counts for a thing it holds no role on; a one-node account acts at once.
- **A peer is its proven key actors**: records naming the same key actors are one peer, told once one of them is. So two nodes of another account proving only the account are one peer.
- **Who a peer is**, two rules over what its record or connection names (`osis_store::Named`: the account with the key it proved with, the node, the key actors), on what the node holds now:
  - Trusted as (`opens::counted`: J, the inbox's judged reports): the account while that key's epoch is open, the node while ours, key actors.
  - May be, for waits (`opens::awaited` over `Named`: waits, pass waits): the node while ours, else the account at the key proved with unless `Engine::account_key_revoked` (that key's epoch revoked: a counting rotation closing it carries a removal, spec/permissions.md §5.5 Revoked, spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for), key actors.
  - A removed node of this account is never waited for. A node of another account away across a plain rotation is still that account; across a rotation carrying any removal it is not, since a removed or reset node is not told from an honest one at rest and would hold the wait forever (an honest one re-proves on its next connect and counts again). Outside the account the engine cannot read the inner signature or D's removals (logged under D only), so a counting rotation's removals list is trusted, a held one checked for being the account's commit at an epoch the rotation descends from. A later step could let a removal name the node it removes to outsiders, so only that node's records stop counting.
- **Index** (`Acks`): kept records by proven key actors and the highest `acked_pos` per set of them, grouped by `Who` (`Named` without the key), so another node of that account acknowledging covers the old-key record; a set of records none of which acknowledged S is judged record by record (`wait_for`), so a revoked record of it releases its wait. Loaded from the store at the first settle (`Transaction::records_all`, O(records)); before it loads, `wait_for` says wait. Kept by `set_acked`/`acks_moved` (an ack rising or re-marked, a record made, a record's proven key actors changed) and eviction. Bound: one entry per kept record, so `RECORD_BUDGET` bounds a peer that never returns. A restart reads what `flush` last wrote, members and `acked_pos` together.
- **Stamps** (`peers`, per S): members joining or leaving, S's `changed_at` moving, a record's `acked_pos` moving (each S whose `changed_at` lies between the old and new positions; every S past the lower one when its proven key actors changed or a record is made: what the record's next filter iterates anyway), whole on eviction and load.
- Rejected: a refusal (a ByRole refused, a walk stopped there) ending the wait on its peer. A refusal answers an Offer tested after S's credentials last moved, so once answered that peer's record acknowledged S and holds no member of it: it is already no peer to wait for.

## Policy: whom to dial, prove to and sync with
spec/sync-opens.md §1 Policy (spec/requirements.md, Offline and network, leaves it to the account and node). One seam, `osis_sync::Policy` (types.rs, a `SyncConfig` field, reached as `Cx::policy`): `dial(&Address)` at `Dials::offer`, `identify(Option<&Proven>)` at the dialer's Prove and the listener's Proved (none: an empty proof list), `sync_with(&Proven)` once a side judged the peer's proofs (no: the session closes).
- Only the permissive policy exists (every answer yes), so no other code asks who the peer is before syncing: every proving pair keeps a record (`named` is every proven key actor), every shown credential's beacon goes to every connection, a read secret's Hit opens whoever proved. A peer proving no key actor (a policy saying no at either side) still runs the set unkept (NoRecord, Resume Error 1).
- A future configuration (per account, overridable per node: anyone; identify to anyone but sync only with known accounts; only this account's nodes; one node at one address) adds fields to `Policy` and answers from them; answers needing the engine ("known account") need `Policy` to take the engine. Not built for it: re-judging connections or candidates when the policy changes (`Dials::set_node` keeps candidates offered before).
- Kept for other reasons: a node key proved only to this account's nodes (readers never learn which node wrote, spec/requirements.md Confidentiality); LAN's fresh transport key per network join (spec/sync-transport.md §4).
- **Records for strangers**: a flood of peers proving fresh key actors evicts records least recently used (spec/sync-hostile.md §2); each record holds the peer's filter (up to 2^22 entries plus as many additions, 8 bytes each), so a few connections with full filters (about 5 MB on the wire each) evict every other record. Accepted as a cost attack (the evicted peers are read from the start), simpler than a "known" judgement on records.

## Seams
The core (`Opens::message`, `tick`, `closed`) runs over `opens::Ctx { holds: &mut dyn Holds, wire: &mut dyn Wire, store, now }`: `Holds` is what the node holds per S (`NodeHolds` over the engine and unread; a test's table), `Wire` the session calls (`Sessions`; a test's pipe). So the rules run against scripted peers (core/sync/tests/it/harness/scripted.rs).

`RESYNC_MS` (one constant, types.rs, shared with streams): after a refused generation a whole filter follows.
