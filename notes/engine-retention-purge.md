# Engine: leftovers and purge
> Summary: Gone writes (expired, purged) removed from held bodies and leftovers, purge requests (rights, re-snapshots, naming by stamp without the header, forgetting purged leftovers) and waiting parts at expiry or purge (`retention/leftover.rs`, `purge.rs`, `pending.rs`; spec/commits.md §5 Purge, §6, spec/permissions.md §6).

## Principle
A gone write (expired, purged) has no effect on the read (spec/crdt.md §4.7): it is removed from every body held and every copy arriving, the state is the join of what is left, so every node reads the same whatever it held or when, and a purge's force rests on its signers' roles as a write's validity does, judged again when they move.

## Leftovers (`retention/leftover.rs`, `w/`)
- A body a gone write reaches loses that write (`make_leftover`): one left with no write is dropped as gone; one still holding others' writes (a snapshot's, a waiting part a request names) is held as its leftover (`Hold::Leftover`): a held body for every reader (`each_held_piece`, `held_parts_at`, `stores_part`), no body for sync (`Meta::body_gone`).
- **Invariant** (`strip_leftover`, the one strip of purge, expiry and arrival): no held body holds a listed write, and none loses a write not listed, so a rebuild reads the same. A body carrying a copy of a listed write (a snapshot's; a key or undelete char is its own writer's value) is held as its leftover with only the copies stripped (`strip_carried`; `rebuild` when the list changed, `drop_gone` on arrival). A leftover whose own writes live (`Hold::Leftover::own_live`) is a body for keep bounds (`Meta::own_gone` is the other kind); sync deletes its blob as a dropped body with live writes.
- The gone list `v/` is the one join-time predicate (`apply::remove_gone_writes`): a snapshot's stale copy joins stripped, and `drop_gone` then only stores the snapshot as its leftover, rebuilding nothing (`review::late_snapshot_of_purged_value_reads_nothing_spec_crdt_4_7` fails, by the rebuild check, without the strip). Deriving it per join would walk the aspect. A late part of a known commit whose own writes are listed also joins without them and is rebuilt by `on_late` (`hold_gone`), so the strip is not what keeps those right.
- A part dropped as covered and then purged is gone from its valid coverers' copies. A part missing when purged has only the list (a copy joins stripped and becomes the leftover, `drop_gone`).
- **On its purge's fall** a part whose own writes were gone counts as never received: its writes leave the list, it is missing with its leftover row set aside (`unstrip`), the aspect is rebuilt and the node refetches (`refetch`). A copy joins whole (`forget_joined_leftovers`); its writes gone again first (a rise, its expiry), `make_leftover` adopts the row, so it holds what a node that never saw the fall does. An expired part's writes never leave the list. A body stripped only for carrying copies of the purged writes stays held through the fall, without them, until their own body joins (spec/crdt.md §4.7 Gone writes), and a whole copy replaces it (`takes_late`). Making it missing instead lost the other writes a carrying snapshot covered.

## Purge requests (`retention/purge.rs`)
- Every question comes down to two: does the request name a delta (`names`: an item on its aspect reaches it, stamped before the request), and has it the right? Two readings of the right: `holds_right`, a request's right as the walk judges it along the path it states (`Paths::judge_right`, notes/perm-paths.md Purges: every cut of that path, so a backdated request by a removed admin drops nothing; a snapshot standing for a body by its validity and its signer's role at its key), which what a request drops follows (`stands`, `rejudge_purges`); and `signers_could`, by roles of any validity at any time (`Held::ever_*`), for what must not turn on arrival (`could_stand`). A header never names role-class deltas.
- Requests are kept per aspect or per named stamp (`p/`, `pn/`), listed before the request's commit joins (`note_requests`), so later deltas are purged on arrival and a body killed by the request's own commit is purged, not dropped: its leftover is bare, so no cover drops it (`kill_and_purge_in_one_commit_drops_the_body_*`). A request by name costs other deltas nothing; an aspect-wide one costs one walk of the aspect per class and purge author.
- **A role change flipping a verdict** (`rejudge_purges`): a request's right rests on its signers' roles as a write's validity does, so a re-judge looks at the aspects named by the requests among the commits it re-judges (`purged_aspects`; a join of anyone else reads no request, `scale::purge_rejudge_is_linear_in_requests_spec_permissions_6`), and every request on a thing whose timeline was evaluated from scratch. a part a request comes to drop is purged as on arrival; one whose writes are gone for a request that no longer stands counts as never received; a body stripped for carrying copies of others' writes stays held. Each aspect is rebuilt once per re-judge, after its flips and every verdict (`validity::rejudge_once`, one `run_batched`): a rebuild per request cost O(requests x bodies) (`scale::purge_rejudge_is_linear_in_requests_spec_permissions_6`).
- **Re-snapshot** (spec/commits.md §5 Purge): a purged write has no effect on the read at any node (spec/crdt.md §4.7), so a node that never held it reads as a holder; a body arriving after its request is purged by the request's own path (`purge_arrived` → `purge_one`), a header arriving without its body is gone by the list alone. Whether the re-snapshot duty still serves anything is open (issues/gone-writes-followup.md): `purge::apply_request` (and `rejudge_carried` where it lists more) owes one on each named aspect where the request dropped something here, at a node with write there (`owe_resnapshot`: `Indexes::resnapshots_owed`, a local row `ro/` written in the purge's transaction, so a stop before the snapshot keeps the duty; in memory for an ephemeral aspect, as its data; one row per aspect, gone with the aspect's rows on forgetting; a local duty, not derivable from commits, so outside the index check; a refused or no-op request writes nothing). `Engine::purge_resnapshots` (run at the end of every complete pass, `Engine::pass_end`, after a grant without history, and by the order harness's writers) takes the due ones (`Core::resnapshots_due`: new, all at open, those `replace::wake_covers` wakes), each in its own transaction: may write only later (minting or delete keys missing) keeps it owed, waiting in `Indexes::cover_waits` with the purge-on-replace covers (a writer that loses write while waiting drops it at the next wake or open); otherwise the row goes and it writes a snapshot where the node may write, unless it holds a valid snapshot the purge left whole covering every named body (`bounds::valid_snapshot_held`); with no named body applied any whole valid snapshot will do, and with none it writes one (a vacuous skip let a fresh node read an older value, order seed 632724249436). Tests: engine it `resnapshot` (`owed_resnapshot_survives_a_stop_*`, `owed_resnapshot_waits_for_delete_keys_*`, `owed_resnapshot_whichever_comes_first_*`), order.rs `purge_of_a_part_dropped_while_invalid_reads_alike_spec_commits_5`. It is apart from `snapshot_first` (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 no carrier, for expiry as for purge), which then finds the re-snapshot as the carrier. Rejected: purging writes out of every state (trades this for the larger invalid-delta loss); fetching purged bodies to form leftovers, a part missing when purged included (races the purger's deletes at peers, so the re-snapshot is the repair anyway).
- **Named by stamp, judged without the header** (`purge::Target`, `rejudge_carried`; spec/commits.md §5 Purge, Without the header; spec/crdt.md §8 `Facts`): an item names a commit's `(ts, own source)`, never its ID, so a node that forgot the header or never got it still finds the copies. Purge-by and purge author come from each snapshot's facts (`snapshot::facts_of`). `rejudge_carried` lists the copies' dots as `Owned::Dots` where a request stands under every held snapshot's facts, on a request's apply, a snapshot's join, `rejudge_purges` and `forget_commits`, and asks for a re-snapshot when it lists more. Test: retention `tests_carried`.
  - Trust: facts are the snapshot writer's word, as its copies are. A cover counts only if it states what the body does for every commit both carry, a left-out fact as the narrowest (`covered_join`), and a request must stand under each held snapshot's facts. So a lie never widens a purge; it can block one at nodes without the header. Accepted: a writer can keep any value it reads alive by writing it again anyway (`tests_carried::a_wider_fact_never_covers_spec_commits_5`).
  - Rejected: the request carrying the named commit's facts (the purger's word would let any writer erase others' compacted writes wherever the header is gone); keeping headers of every live covered write (undoes compaction); the request carrying the named header itself (no help for by-author or aspect items).
- **A forgotten commit's name** (`retention::names`, `Indexes::forgotten_names`, rows `fn/`; spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting): forgetting a commit with own writes (`unindex_commit` → `names::note`) keeps, per aspect with a held snapshot, its stamp, class, purge author and `expires` from the header. `Core::purge_item` resolves an ID through it when the header is gone (an API `Purge::Commit` after automatic compaction forgot the write), `check_request` judges the right by it, and `rejudge_carried` judges carried copies of its stamp by it instead of the snapshots' facts. Only a value-carrying write keeps one (`each_valued`, spec/crdt.md §4.7), so a set removal's tombstone (a reset) keeps none, nor a commit past its `expires` (its writes are gone everywhere), and a churned set's names stay bounded by its live elements. It goes when no held snapshot of the aspect carries such a write stamped with it, the commit is held again, or its `expires` has passed (judged when the aspect is next marked): `atomic::forget_snapshot` (the one place a snapshot leaves the held set) and `index_commit` mark the aspect (`Core::forgotten_names_due`), `names::settle_due` reads the held snapshots' writes once per marked aspect at the transaction's end. Bounded by live writes carried; a dead write's name goes with the last snapshot carrying it. An expiring purge naming a name that expires later is refused as by the header (`check_request`). Rows `fn/` are in the index check (`testing::tables`). Tests: retention `tests_carried` (`purge_by_id_reaches_a_forgotten_write_*`, `a_dead_write_keeps_no_name_*`, `a_set_removal_keeps_no_name_*`; sync S1 `growth::releasing_own_things_returns_storage_to_a_fresh_nodes_*`, `an_expiring_purge_of_a_forgotten_write_is_refused_*`, the differential `purge_by_id_reads_alike_forgotten_or_not_*`).
  - A node that never held the header (a late joiner) has no name and refuses a purge by that ID (unknown commit); a request from a node with the name reaches its copies there, and by author or aspect it purges them itself.
  - Rejected: the snapshot's writer placing each forgotten commit's signed header beside the snapshot as a certificate while its writes live (a late joiner could check ID → stamp): one header per commit with a live carried write, kept for good on a long text or list aspect, undoes compaction (as keeping headers of every live covered write); a late joiner shows the snapshot as a value's commit (`osis::Stamp`), so it has no ID to purge by anyway.
  - Rejected: snapshots carrying ID → stamp for their covered commits (the snapshot writer's word, uncheckable once headers are gone: a writer could steer an admin's purge by ID onto another's write); requests carrying the commit's dots (the purger's word); never forgetting a nameable commit (a purge may come any time).
- A purge that dropped a permanent delta is noted (`z/`, `retention::lasting`): a new node needs the request to hold the delta's copy as purged. Noted, not walked: an aspect-wide request naming nothing would walk the aspect each pass.
- **Purged commits are forgotten** (`retention::kept_purged`, either class): a content commit holding no part but those gone for a standing request (no permanent or `created.0` delta) is `forgettable` once none of its purged parts is listed (`purged_unlisted`: spec/commits.md §7 Listed parts, a memo-clock wake at the bound) and no entry of it is known. `Engine::forget_commits` takes every due commit in one transaction, each aspect rebuilt once. Why: its gone writes read nothing, and each write-purge-delete round would otherwise leave a header for good. A fall then finds nothing, as for a write never received; a held re-snapshot keeps reads unchanged.
- Memory class alike (notes/ephemeral.md Principle): a purged memory part goes as a durable one does, a fall unstrips it (`Missing::ToFetch`) and the lane fetches it again.

## Waiting parts at expiry or purge (`retention/pending.rs`; spec/commits.md §5 Keep bounds)
- **Expiry**: a part pending at its expiry goes at T (`pending_drops`, `strip_pending`; parked copies included) and counts as never received; the commit is examined again without it. Ticks don't matter: before any ingest or `reexamine` reads a stored pending copy, its parts past T go as their timers would (`apply::expire_stored`, `retention::expire_waiting`). A part waiting aside (`pend_aside`) is judged alike; nothing is flagged (its writes have no effect anywhere, spec/crdt.md §4.7). Expiry does not strip: the whole part is past T.
- **Purge**: a pending part a held request names loses its writes (`strip_purged`, `purge_pending`). Once its thing's record is held it is stripped and kept as the part's leftover, its pending slot holding an empty-list delta (`retention::emptied`; `examine` trusts it only with the leftover row, `Parts::stripped`); once joined, `stripped_joined` holds it as its leftover, so every arrival order holds the same state (`shred::purge_of_a_waiting_part_reads_alike_spec_commits_5`, `outside::a_part_waiting_aside_is_purged_where_it_waits_spec_commits_5`). Applied after the commit, the request's `Wake::Thing` (`lists_moved`) re-examines the waiting copy (`pending_drops`); `purge_pending` does the same in the request's transaction, and no test tells them apart. With a page away or parked it cannot be stripped: it goes at once, flagging nothing (it reads as a part held and stripped), fetched again (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies) if the request falls before the commit applies.
- A waiting snapshot part carrying a listed write is stripped the same way (`strip_carried_waiting`), judged as a held snapshot is, by its `Carried` bound (`meets` the list), the bound decoded once while it waits (memo `Core::waiting_carried`, living while its pending entry does; the first look after storing decodes again, the entry being noted after the bound), so a purge or expiry it carries nothing of decodes nothing (`shred::waiting_snapshots_decoded_once_while_they_wait_spec_commits_5`). Its leftover keeps frontiers, `Absorbed` and facts and joins as a carrier (`apply::leftover_part`, `hold_carrier`); one not readable yet is stripped when it joins (`drop_gone`). Memory commits alike.
- sync forgets the blobs of an expired or stripped part at once (`Engine::spent_copy`). A blob also holding another part is kept only while it holds a true copy the engine lacks; junk holds no checkable slot, since `examine` checks hashes before storing a pending copy (spec/commits.md §4 Copies).
- **A purge waits for the record it needs**: a part on a thing whose record is away pends with its commit (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable), a purge request naming it pends alike, and both are judged when the record comes: purge is prompt only once the node holds what checks it (spec/requirements.md Deletion). The pending copy goes with its last carrier (Shred::Yes). A part of an applied commit awaiting its record waits aside in the commit's pending copy and is judged when the record comes (notes/engine-apply.md, A part waits alone). A destroyed thing's record that went is taken again as any record, from a record blob or certificate copy while something held awaits or needs it, its witness and requests travelling with it (notes/expiry-creation-design.md, Raw records travel, A thing's mintings travel with its record). Test `shred::pending_part_awaiting_its_record_is_judged_with_it_spec_commits_4`.
- A pending commit whose header lists are not resolved cannot be judged by a request; its parts keep their values until the lists come or its last carrier leaves (spec/commits.md §5).
