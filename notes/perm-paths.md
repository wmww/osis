# osis-perm: the walk along stated paths
> Summary: Design and build state of osis-perm's walk along stated paths (A1 steps 3 and 4a-b, `osis_perm::paths`; the switch under way): its one rule, the walk and content by point query, purges as a reading (rights by class and selector, stored per request; a delta's purge a point query; copies; own covers; could hold), chained-ness per part, input and output, the walk per component, replayed from the earliest key an update touches, that makes from-scratch evaluation, incremental update and forgetting one definition, components, trust per component, cost shapes and their count tests, what the differential against roles-model `paths` (`Rules::purging`) compares and at what size it passes, controls and mutations, the draft-spec corrections it forced, what the wiring owes, the query layer over it and what the switch has left.

**Principle: a part's right rests on the edges of the one path it states, read in key order, and a lowering is judged once, at the first pass that reaches it holding its right.** Every rule of spec/permissions.md §5.1 follows from it: what a part needs of each edge (admin, a may-grant grant's role and on its last edge the flag, a finalize's write), what a lowering puts in question (the parts resting on its edge whose need it no longer meets), when it is void (its drops would take what its own path rests on), seeds (a trusted write's drops read before the walk). Nothing searches for another path.

**Content (step 3b) follows from it: a delta's validity is a point query along its one path, derived at read from what the walk stores per edge; nothing is stored per delta but its facts, so a lowering costs nothing per delta it cuts.**

**Purges (step 4) follow from content: a purge request is an edit, so its right is its validity (judged per role its class needs), and a delta is purged iff a request holding a right over it names it. Purge is a reading, like validity: a function of the held set and trust set, by point query; a cut takes a request's right as it takes an edit's validity, and the reading restores what it no longer purges ("restore": notes/purge-model.md).**

## Step 3b design: content by point query
- **Stored per edge (timeline)**: its applied writes by key (value, value before, explicit, the edge as restored before in role and invite: `Paths::writes`; and the edge as restored after), and per level (write, admin) the set of its **cuts** (applied explicit writes whose value gives below r at their `ts` and that take the edge as restored from r or above), kept with the entries. Per edge, the **first chained** key giving write and giving admin (draft §4), kept by the timeline's chained pass (Reach, The chained pass).
- **Stored per delta**: its facts alone (`paths::Fact`: commit, position = the start of its `ts`, thing, aspect, hops as (holder, target) pairs, role needed, class, purge author, purge-by), in the store behind the seam (Scale, The delta-facts seam). No status; an addendum's validity is the store's too.
- **Indexes by what is named**: in memory, `named[(c, t)]`: the edge writes whose frontier's leaf of thing t holds c (role state; an entry names its commit's parts on its leaf's thing alone, `List`); in the store, the addenda whose list holds c, the snapshots carrying c (a list naming a snapshot names the commits it carries, for that snapshot's thing and aspect; read as empty for a held role commit, which no snapshot carries), requests holding a right by what they name (Purges) and by edge, own covers by signer, addenda by (edge, position). Kept by `put`, `remove` and `set_right`.
- **Judging one delta** (`Paths::content`): per hop a point query (value before its position, the last cut at or after it: O(log) each); then, only if it failed, the cuts its namers answer (`answered`: the edge writes whose frontier names its commit or a snapshot carrying it, and for `vouched` the removal parts a valid addendum naming it vouches against) and `stands`: per hop every cut at or after its position answered (`Walk::cuts_from`) and the hop giving the role before it, or its last write before it (`Walk::last_before`) an answered cut the hop gave the role before, read at its `ts` (draft §5.2 Content: a cut's answer speaks for that cut alone); then the re-grant (finals and first chained per hop). So a delta appended at the end costs O(hops · log), whatever the history, removals or addenda held; a cut delta costs that plus what names it plus one read per cut after it on its path's edges.
- **Addenda** are the one stored validity (draft §5.2: a least fixpoint over every signer), judged again only where an update reaches them (Scale, Addenda, by reach): never on a plain delta's arrival, never for an update elsewhere. Plain content reads the stored addenda set, so its query is not recursive.
- **`Changed`** names what the engine must re-judge, never per delta for a cut: `parts` (edge-class status changes, every new part, addenda whose validity changed), `edges` (per edge the earliest key its history changed: deltas positioned after it on a path through it), `cuts` (per edge the latest key of a cut added or removed: deltas positioned at or before it), `regrant` (edges whose final role or first chained point changed: every delta through them), `named` (commits whose namers changed: carried by a snapshot taken or forgotten, listed by an addendum or a frontier forgotten or by a commit updated in place, what a snapshot so listed carries). A delta's status can change only through one of these or through a delta naming it whose own verdict moved: the engine judges again what `Changed` reaches, then, for each judged delta whose verdict moved, the deltas it names (an addendum's list, a snapshot's carries), until none moves, and the deltas and copies each `purges` scope covers; a forgotten delta counts as moved, so what it named (an addendum's list) is judged again (`incremental::rejudged`, checked at every update of the incremental differential; over 200 content worlds' arrivals it judges 360 deltas again for 186 moved, of 4,500 held). A removal flipping 100,000 deltas reports one `cuts` entry. The engine keeps deltas indexed by (edge, position) to turn a range into the deltas it re-merges: that work is the re-merge's own, and a removal's frontier, which names what stays, is the same size.

## Purges (draft §6, §5.2 Own cover; spec/commits.md §4 Could hold, §5 Without the header)
- **The rule**: a request R on thing T (aspect a; a destruction on `created.0`) holds `admin` where a delta of R needing admin along R's path at R's key would be valid by (a) held, (b) listed or (d) re-granted (no addendum vouches for a request: Open 20, 4d), and `write` likewise for write; a destruction holds `admin` alone and reads no cut on an edge into T ordered after its position. R names: by author P the deltas of a stamped before it whose purge author is exactly P; by aspect every delta of a stamped before it; by commit that commit's delta of a stamped before it; a destruction every delta of T, every aspect, whatever `ts` (`Scope::covers`, the one naming rule the walk, the tests and the engine share). A held plain delta d is purged iff some request names it and holds: `admin`, or `write` where d's purge-by is `writer`, or `author_or_admin` with d's purge author on R's chain ([signer, A1..Ak]). Each named delta is judged alone (`Paths::is_purged`, `purged`).
- **Stored right**: each request's right (`Right { admin, write }`) is stored through the `Store` seam (`set_right`), judged again where an update reaches the request as it reaches any delta (an edge of its path in `Changed.edges` before its position, in `cuts` at or after it, in `regrant`; its commit in `named`) and on arrival (`Facts::rights_update`, by the index `requests_through` per edge). The store indexes a request by what it names, (T, a or none for a destruction, `Names`), and by what its right gives (`Holding`: admin; write; write for each member of its chain), with its `ts`, only while it holds a right: a delta's purge reads, per selector naming it (by its purge author, by its aspect, by its commit, a destruction of T) and per holding its purge-by asks, the latest stamp (`Store::latest`): at most eight point lookups, O(log), whatever the number of requests naming it (`shapes::thousands_of_requests_naming_one_delta_cost_what_none_do`: k = 0, 200, 2000 writers' requests over an `admin` delta, the same count). A request is 2 + chain-length index entries. Junk requests (no right) are no index entry.
- **`Changed.purges`**: the scope (T, a, `Names`, `ts`) of each request whose stored right changed, that arrived holding one, or that was forgotten holding one: a description, never a list of deltas (`counts::a_request_over_n_deltas_reports_one_scope`: one scope over n deltas at n = 100 and 1000). The engine finds the deltas and copies a scope covers in its own index (`Scope::covers`) and judges their purge again; a delta's or copy's purge moves only where it is new, a scope covers it, or (a copy) a snapshot carrying it changed its verdict. `naming` holds no requests.
- **Copies** (4b): a commit a valid held snapshot of (T, a) carries and no held delta of (T, a) is, is purged where a request holds the right over it by the facts every such snapshot states of it (`Stated`: `ts`, purge author, purge-by, per carried commit in `DeltaClass::Snapshot`); one stating nothing of it states nothing; with nothing stated only a destruction names it (`purged_copies`, `copy_purged`). A stated fact is its snapshot's signer's word: a writer's cover misstating a carried write's purge author or purge-by steers a purge where the original is not held (issues/forged-copy-undetected.md, by construction); a held original decides by its own facts (`hand_purge::a_misstated_fact_steers_a_purge_only_where_the_original_is_not_held`). A snapshot not held shows nothing (Open 19, `as_known`), so a request naming a commit not held reaches it only through a held valid snapshot.
- **Own cover** (4b): a snapshot marked own (`DeltaClass::Snapshot { own }`) is also valid (`By::OwnCover`) where its path gave the role it needs just before its position and an explicit lowering the timeline applies, signed by its signer, ordered at or after it, lists its commit (`named`). A lowering's status changing names in `Changed.named` the own covers of its signer it lists (`Facts::own_listed`, by the store's index of own covers per signer).
- **Could hold** (spec/commits.md §4): `Paths::could_give(request, r)`: every edge of its path a record edge or set at r or above by some held edge write, whatever its validity (`Facts::ever`, counts per edge and role of the held edge writes, in memory with role state); `could_hold(request, purge_by, author)` the class's role (a destruction: admin). What a store-side caller that cannot judge acts on.
- **4d seam** (Open 20, addenda for purge requests, the owner's call; not built): `Facts::holds_right` is where a request's role is judged; (c) "a valid addendum by a holder of r against a cut of its path ordered after it names it" joins its three clauses there, `vouchable` stops excluding requests, the addenda fixpoint (`addenda_update`) reaches requests, and `rights_update` then reads `Changed.parts`' addenda too.
- **Chained-ness per part** (draft §4, for A2's evidence derivation): the timeline's chained pass judges every edge-class part (an edge write as before; a removal or lowering by admin, a self-removal by admin, a finalize by write), only those setting an edge moving thresholds; `Paths::is_chained`, and `Changed.chained` names each part whose chained-ness flipped (arriving chained, falling, a chained part forgotten). Against held-model `evidence::World::chained` at every view of the tiny, content and purge worlds (`chained::chained_parts_agree`), hand worlds of each kind (an invite grant by its path's invite, a removal, a finalize, a self-removal; flips on arrival and forget). No verdict moved (the dump below).

## Input
Plain structs (`paths::Commit`, `Part`, `PartKind`, `EdgeWrite`, `Delta`, `DeltaClass`), independent of the header's types, so the header's renames do not reach them; step 2's adapter fills them from held commits.
- `Commit`: commit ID, `ts`, signer, its parts in header order (a part's index is its `WriteId` index). `Part`: the thing written (`on`), the stated path [A1..Ak], and an edge write (holder, value as `types::Edge` or a removal, frontier (`List`: per thing, the commits its leaf names; A1's single list is the leaf of every thing its commits write, which the adapter files so) or none) or a `Delta`.
- `Delta`: aspect (`Ak`), the role it needs (write; admin for an admin-only aspect, `sync.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0`, a snapshot carrying one), purge-author index into [signer, A1..Ak] (past the path: bad input, invalid), purge-by, class: `Plain` (a content commit's delta), `RoleClass` (judged like content), `Snapshot { carries, stated, own }` (what it states per carried commit; own cover mark), `Addendum { removal, list }`, `Purge { names }` (a request on this part's thing naming `Names::Author(tid)`, `Aspect`, `Commit(commit ID)` on its aspect, or `Destroy`; the header's field 8 items meet a path by the adapter, step 4c: a by-commit item's `[ts, source]` is resolved to the commit it names).
- Records: thing to mint admin, given at construction (descent reads them); a changed record set means a new `Paths`.
- Trust set: `WriteId`s of edge writes.
- A finalize is not flagged: it is a removal of another holder's grant with no frontier that is not a self-removal (spec/permissions.md §2 Finalize), read off the part.
- Order within a commit (§5.1 step 1): the parts on no hop of a path the commit states, then those on one (an edge of the path stated for any thing it writes, that thing as the path's last member: a self-removal of the path's last thing, the record edge a descent ends with); in each group by thing written, then holder tid.
- Bad input the walk sees: members naming a thing twice (content too), an edge-class part on its own signer (no edge write targets a key actor: a key actor's self-removal on itself would otherwise apply), an edge write whose holder is its target (spec/permissions.md §1; roles-model `paths` has the rule, its generators make none), two edge writes on one holder in a commit. A part on another key actor applies nowhere because no grant on a key actor exists (the engine writes none, today's walk applies none); the adapter, which knows which tids are key actors, may refuse it outright.
- A key actor's delta on itself along the empty path has the members [S] and no hop: valid (draft §1 Path). Along any other path it names S twice.

## Output
- Per edge-class part: `Status` (applied, applied as an invite grant, void, dropped, unapplied).
- Per delta: `Content` (valid by: held, listed, vouched, re-granted; copied; invalid), a point query (`content`, `content_counted`); `rests` (its hops and position, what the engine indexes) and `naming` (the addenda and snapshots whose verdict it follows).
- `copies`: commits a valid snapshot carries that no valid held delta is (held or not). `purged`/`is_purged`: held plain deltas a request holds a right over; `purged_copies`/`copy_purged`: copies of commits not held there likewise; `right`: a request's right; `could_give`/`could_hold`; `is_chained` per edge-class part.
- Per edge (the timeline): history (key, value), `writes` (with the value and edge as restored before each, explicit or not; `Applied::cuts(r)`), the value and role just before any key, finals and invites, `first_points` (per edge, the key of its first write giving write, admin, made by right: §5.2 Re-grant).
- The dispute set (`Candidates::Explicit`, `&mut self`): read over what applies, which deltas are valid and the final roles; per candidate the trust switched on and back in place, compared where the switch's `Changed` reaches (`disputes_counted` counts it).
- `Changed` from each update (design above; `purges` and `chained`: Purges).

## One definition: the walk, replayed from a key
The walk is a run of passes over the held parts in key order; D and the verdicts carry from pass to pass (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 2). Each component (Scale, below) keeps:
- the **pass**: per edge its applied writes by key, each with the edge's state just after it (value, edge as restored in what it gives and lets grant), so the state before any key is a point query and going back to a key is a truncation: every key is a checkpoint; per part, how it applied;
- the **events**, in the order given: each lowering judged (its verdict in its part; applied, its drops).

A pass that ends at a commit whose lowerings dropped does not start over: the pass is truncated to the earliest drop and goes on from there, since every part before it steps unchanged.

**An update replays from the earliest key it touches** (`Walk::run`). A component's input changing at key p (a part added or forgotten; a trust change is p = the start): the events before the first one at a key at or after p stand; that event and every later one are undone (verdict and drops cleared), the pass is truncated to the earliest of p and the undone events' and drops' keys, and the walk goes on from there. Why that equals the walk from scratch: a pass that gave an event at a key before p had read nothing at or after p, so the from-scratch walk over the new input gives the same events in the same order up to there; the first event at or after p may differ, and everything after it rests on its D and verdicts. Before the truncation point the kept pass differs from the from-scratch walk's in no drop and no verdict (every undone one is at or after it), so it is that walk's pass there. From scratch is the update from the start; a forget is the same update at the forgotten part's key.

**Cost** (spec/requirements.md, Cost; issues/perm-eval-cost.md): a part appended at the end costs its own step; a backdated or forgotten part, or a lowering applying with drops, the role history after the earliest key it touches in its component, once per restart. Memory: the checkpoints are the pass itself (an entry per applied write) and the events, so what a component keeps to restart from is bounded by its role history. A walk by reach (parts walked again only where what their step reads changed) is not built: simplest correct wins, and a very large number of role changes being fast is no priority (Tess).

**By right** (§5.2 Re-grant): each step also records whether its part is made by right in the pass as it stands before its key, dropped parts included (a part's last step is in the settled pass), so an edge's first point is its first setter made by right at the role, a scan of that edge's setters.

Trust (§5.1 Trust set) is the same machinery: seeds are a function of the held parts and the active trusted writes, set per part; an update of a component with void trusted writes activates them again and walks it from scratch, then the loop (walk, void what the pass does not apply, seed anew, walk from scratch).

**The key hop** keeps no edge until accounts are ordinary things (notes/redesign-plan.md A4): it gives admin where the signer's record holds its key (names no mint admin) and nothing elsewhere, since no key grant exists yet (a part signed as a thing with a mint admin is not `well`, `path::shape`), and the walk takes no write on one; both come with A4: an edge per (key, thing), first on every path, written and judged like any (notes/write-path-design.md, Keys), so the walk gains an edge whose holder is a key and no rule.

**The may-grant flag** (`Edge::may_grant`): `roles.0` carries a bool; `types::Edge` holds it as `invite`, the role the value lets grant (its own role where flagged), the form `timeline`'s walk reads, until that walk goes.
## Cost shapes
- A part appended at the end of key order: its own step: a point query per hop, plus, for a lowering that falls, the parts resting on its edge (the index `resting` per edge) and its void check. Under a trust set the same (the new part alone is checked against the active trusted writes).
- A void check (`keeps`) reads back along the setters of its path, each grant's own path in turn, each part's own check made once and each run of parts passed over for good (dropped, or a grant that does not keep) read once (`skip`): one check through an edge of m dropped grants reads about 2m setters. k void lowerings at the end of a chain of k grants cost k² (finding 17, accepted: notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 2).
- A backdated part, a forgotten edge-class part, or a lowering applying with drops: its component from the earliest key touched (One definition, Cost); other components do nothing.
- A merge: the smaller component's parts and events moved, then the walk from the joining part's key. Any held part joins the components of the edges it names, valid or not, and they stay joined (issues/perm-hostile-path-joins-components.md).
- From scratch: one step per part, plus per restart the parts from its earliest drop on. k lowerings at the end each dropping one early write restart k times through the n parts between: about k·n (`counts::k_lowerings_with_drops_restart_k_times`), which one admin forces with its own 3k writes.
- Trust: a void trusted write costs its component a walk from scratch per round on each update of it. A trust switch: its component from scratch.
- Memory: one history entry per applied part, one event per lowering judged, each part in at most one event's drops; per part an index entry per edge it rests on.
- Disputes (Open 3 (a)): no copy; per candidate the trust switched on and back in place, each an update of the candidate's component (the reading after equals the reading before, the store's addenda verdicts included); compared: the parts `Changed` reports (applied or not), the edges whose final role it reports, and the deltas it reaches through the store's (edge, position) index (`Store::through`) or names (an addendum it reports names its list's deltas: validity reads no other namer). A candidate on A beside n parts and deltas elsewhere: 7 steps and no verdict read at n = 100 and 1000 (`counts::a_dispute_probe_reads_its_component_alone`; mutation, every delta compared: 800 and 8,000).
- Content: see Step 3b design. A delta's query: per hop a binary search in the history and the last cut, then (failing) its namers, the addenda naming it, the re-grant. Addenda: judged again only in F (Scale), so k addenda arriving one at a time cost O(k) and an update elsewhere costs none.
- A delta under k cuts on its edge: one binary search and the edge's last cut per hop, the same at k = 10 and 100 where nothing names it (`counts::a_delta_under_k_cuts_is_a_point_query`); where cuts answer it, each cut after it is read once: 5 + 2k reads at k = 1, 2, 10 (`counts::a_delta_every_cut_answers_reads_each_cut_once`). Only the edge's admins make cuts, one per raise and lowering.
- Purges: a request by whole aspect over n deltas (n = 100, 1000): its arrival, its signer's removal cutting its right and the re-grant giving it back each report one scope, no walk step for the request, the same content work at both sizes; a delta's purge query the same (`counts::a_request_over_n_deltas_reports_one_scope`). k requests holding a right naming one delta (any holder of a class's role can write them): a delta's purge reads the latest stamp per selector and holding, the same at k = 0, 200 and 2000 (`shapes::thousands_of_requests_naming_one_delta_cost_what_none_do`); requests holding none are no index entry. A request's right is judged again where an update reaches it (its path's edges, cuts, re-grant, its commit named): like a delta's validity, one point query per reached request; a regrant of a hub edge reaches every request through it.
- Memory: the walk keeps edge-class state alone (parts, edges, logs, histories, role commits' facts, frontier names): the same at 1,000 and 10,000 content deltas (`counts::delta_facts_live_in_the_store`: 136 entries). Delta facts are the store's: `MemStore` keeps a `Fact` (about 0.5 KB with its index entries) per held delta, which the engine's store index replaces. Judging an appended delta: 2 store lookups (held? and its facts), the same at both sizes.
- **Attacker-drivable** (any signer whose commits a node holds, valid or not):
  - A cut delta's query reads every frontier and addendum naming its commit, junk included: k of them cost k per judgment (`shapes::thousands_of_addenda_naming_one_commit_cost_one_read_each`). The engine judges it again only when `Changed` reaches it; a bound needs the named lists indexed by validity.
  - An addendum listing a snapshot reads, in the fixpoint, every commit the snapshot carries: a writer's snapshot carrying m commits costs m per addendum naming it, each time it is in F.
- **Where components buy nothing**: an organization O whose admins act through O on k things O is admin of is one component by design (each such write rests on an edge on O): a backdated write on one of them replays the others' later parts.

## Built
- `core/perm/src/paths/` (`mod.rs` the input and output types and `Paths`: the walk, trust per component, forget and update, disputes; `walk.rs` one walk: components, the pass and its events replayed from a key, seeds, the chained pass; `path.rs` members and hops; `content.rs` deltas, namers, addenda by reach): every rule of draft §1 Path (hops, descent, the direct mark: `Part::direct`, a marked hop its pair's direct edge, a mark off a descent of two steps or more (on no descent, a one-step descent, past the hops) leaving the part not `well`; the last edge, [S]), §2, §5.1 steps 1 to 5, Trust set (void read in the timeline, the walk redone), disputes (content validity included), §5.2 Content, cut, the frontier listing, Addenda (least fixpoint, never for a role-class delta, a snapshot, a commit carrying a purge request, or a role commit's addendum), Re-grant from each edge's first write made by right, strictly after its key, Compaction snapshots judged along their path (copies of a valid one shown; a list naming a snapshot names what it carries for its thing and aspect), admin-only and role-class deltas by their role; §6 whole but Open 20 (Purges: classes, selectors, destruction, copies, could hold) and §5.2 Own cover; chained-ness per part. `add`, `forget`, `update` and from scratch by one path, per component. No unsafe, no new dependency, no indexing that can panic.
- A drop takes a commit's edge-class parts on a thing together (draft §5.1 step 3 and Seeds; README Open 23): `gone` (`Walk::keeps`) is a part's own drop or one of its `siblings`' (same commit, same thing, not dropped, seeded or void); the audit adds siblings not resting on the lowered edge, `reseed` a seed's siblings. Hand world `paths::hand::a_single_use_link_redeemed_twice_stays_with_the_redemption_the_sharer_listed`; no generator reaches a commit whose parts on a thing fall apart, so the differentials agree without it.

## Owed (not built)
- Step 4d (Open 20, the owner's call): addenda vouching for purge requests (Purges, 4d seam). From step 4c, with the wiring: field 8's items and their selectors to `Names` (a by-commit item's stamp resolved to its commit) judged by this walk, and Could hold read along stated paths (`could_hold`). Bodies (an addendum's list counting while its body is held; a dropped body still the signer's write) are the engine's.
- Step 5 (lists and certificates): writing addenda and own covers; the list a remover writes.
- Not in the walk, for the adapter or the engine: external fires (§5.2: valid by the setting where the path does not give the role), `created.0` always valid, addendum entries a delta creates (`put_new`; the adapter passes only those), ephemeral parts as their durable kind.
- The `Store` contract the engine's index keeps (its purge part: requests by their named key while their stored right is any, by edge, the stored rights, own covers by signer): lookups in any order (`Reversed` in `single.rs` reads alike); reads see the walk's own writes within an update (the addenda fixpoint writes verdicts with `set_valid_addendum` and reads them back in the same `add`, `forget`, `update`, `set_trust` or `disputes`); `put`, `remove` and the verdict writes belong to the transaction holding the commits they follow. `Paths` reads a commit as held when the store has its deltas (`add` skips it), so a `Paths` built at load over a store that kept its facts would skip those commits, a role commit's edge-class parts among them: the walk is rebuilt over an empty store (or one the rebuild fills), and the stored addenda verdicts, derived state, are written anew by that rebuild's `add` (every addendum judged), so a crash mid-update leaves nothing stale. `disputes` writes verdicts and switches them back.
- For the wiring: `Paths::single` is a test mode; `Counts::steps` counts parts taken by a pass, dropped ones included.
- Purges, for the wiring: erasing bodies (and copies' values) by `Changed.purges`' scopes from the engine's own index (`Scope::covers`), restoring by refetch where a reading stops purging, which brings a body back only from a node that never erased it: where every holder applied the request before its right fell the content is lost for good (no bin: spec/requirements.md Deletion; counted in the purge worlds' `bodies`, notes/purge-model.md, Today's rule); the store-side caller that cannot judge (`could_hold` over the request's path and the delta's purge-by and purge author); the adapter filling `Stated` from each snapshot's `Facts` (spec/crdt.md §8) and `own` from the header's mark.
- The wiring: the adapter from held commits (`Commit`, `Part`); calling `add`, `forget` (covered, expired, purged, released, access lost) and `update` (a frontier leaf arriving) as the engine holds and lets go; the trust set restricted to what the draft allows; parts on key actors other than the signer as bad input (the adapter knows key actors); key actors' own edges; the engine's `Store` over its store index (by `WriteId`, (commit, thing), what lists or carries a commit, requests by author, addenda by (edge, position), addenda verdicts) and its index of deltas by (edge, position) to turn `Changed` into deltas to judge again, with the same rule for a forgotten delta's namees; external fires, `created.0`, `put_new`.

## The switch (branch `mizzen-a1-switch`, red until done)
osis-perm holds one walk: `paths`. `timeline`, `incremental`, `cache`, the old `walk`, `content`, `trust`, `scope`, `cert`, `purge` and `held` are gone with their tests; `Edge` carries `may_grant: bool`. The workspace's default tests pass on it (`tools/heavy cargo test --workspace --no-fail-fast`; the one failure is tools/known_failures.txt's osis-app test), clippy is clean on core and GUI, `tools/policy.py` passes. At gate size the rotation properties, the perm `paths::` differentials and the sync `_plain` differentials pass; `order::held_set_decides_plain` does not (below).

**The query layer (`osis_perm::timeline::Timeline<S>`)**: one object over `Paths` for the whole held set (components are the walk's own). It keeps the final edge state (`graph::Edges`, set from `Changed.edges`), a log of applied writes by key (for `edges_at(point)`: the final state with an overlay of each edge written at or after the key, read by `value_before`), per target the holders an edge write names, and the held commits with edge writes (`RoleCommit`, by target). It answers, for edge writes by (commit, op): `status`, `is_applied`, `way` (`paths::Way`: explicit, leave, may-grant, finalize), `applied`, `is_chained`, `applies_over` (`Paths::would_apply`), `held_on`, `applied_on`, `role_commit`, `edge_op`, `in_force`; over the edge state: `lowered_unaudited` and `audits` (off `Paths::restored`), `edges_at`, `edges_unfinalized`, `roster`, `standing_since(_all)`, `lowered`, `ended`, `standing`/`class`/`may_grant`, `role`, `roles_on(_now)(_without)`, `role_unfinalized`, `path_now`, `roles_ever`, `roles_of`, `roles_without`. `graph.rs` computes over the edge state: `Class::MayGrant(r)` and `Need::MayGrant(r)` read §2 Path's may-grant (every edge at r, the last giving admin or carrying the flag). No memo inside: the engine's memos sit above.
- **Input**: `HeldSet { created(thing), commit(id) -> Option<timeline::Held> }`. `Held` is a commit's parts in the node's numbering (part `ai` for aspect `ai`, then one per purge item; `PartKind::None` where nothing is read) and its `RoleCommit` where its edge writes count; the layer numbers the ops after the parts in the walk and translates (commit, op) both ways. `Timeline::update(held, commits, things)` takes the IDs of every commit whose reading may have changed (held or gone) and the things whose record came or went, and makes one `Paths::sync(records, records gone, gone, commits)`. A commit with edge writes named with its reading unchanged is not taken again (its component would replay from its key).
- **Records**: `Walk::add_record` puts a record edge in force from the start (its component dirty from the start); `remove_record` takes it from the start. A held part with a path member whose record chain stops at a thing with no held record is filed under that thing (`Timeline::waiting`) and given again when the record comes; when a record goes, the commits with a part resting on, setting or stated along its record edge (`Paths::through`) are given again, so their hops read the chain without it: the one rule for a hop's edge going, as for a forgotten part.
- **Lists**: `List::whole(frontier)` names every part of each listed commit (A1's one header frontier), indexed under no thing (`Facts::named`); nothing is re-filed when a listed commit arrives.
- **A delta's purge author** is an index into its members [signer, A1..Ak, the thing] (spec/permissions.md §6: a member of the path), the thing itself included, as roles-model `paths` reads it (`World::purge_author`: one past the path names the thing written); a request's chain stays [signer, A1..Ak] (the model's `Rules::author_thing`, proposed, is off).
- **`timeline::Changed`**: the walk's `Changed`, the things whose record moved, `reached` (`Paths::reached`: the held deltas to judge again), `ops` (edge writes new or whose status moved), `was_applied` (of those, applied before) and `fresh` (commits not held before).

**The engine on it**:
- `apply::validity::reading(core, id)` is the walk's reading of a held commit, a function of the index (the header in `Meta`, the opened split paths in `View::paths`, `ix.held`, `ix.addenda`, `ix.rotation_role`, void, the epoch rule); `Core` implements `HeldSet`. `Held::touch_commit` journals that a commit's reading may have moved (its undo touches again, so a rollback gives the timeline the restored reading); `put_meta`, `take_meta`, `set_addendum`, `set_paths` and every commit named in `stale_commits` touch. An open touches every held commit: the timeline is derived state, built at each open over `MemStore`.
- `View::paths` keeps each thing's opened split path (`Decided`: `osis_commit::Path`, `None` where bad), persisted in the parts row.
- A commit is judged as it applies, before the walk takes it, and a writer checks its own purge request before it applies: `Paths::judge(commit, part)` and `judge_right` are the point queries of `content` and `right` over a commit's reading (`validity::reading_of`), held or not.
- The trust set is kept by (commit, op) (`Timeline::trusted`), so a trusted write held after the set was given (an open loads sides first) is trusted when it comes.
- An open decodes every header to give the walk its delta facts; the engine's check that an open from the persisted index decodes none is off (marked Interim) until the walk's store is the engine's index.
- `Judge::part` keeps the engine's own rules (`created.0`, the epoch rule, `path_known`, external fires, too deep, `listing.0`, the may-grant boxes exemption by `Way::MayGrant`) and asks `Paths::content` for the rest. The epoch rule's addenda against a rotation (§5.5) read the stored verdicts of the addenda on the thing; an addendum whose verdict moves has what it lists judged again.
- `timelines_changed` feeds `stale_commits` from `Changed.reached` and `ops`; role consequences are keyed by `Changed.edges()` and `created`.
- The frontier a lowering carries (`receipt::scope_of`): the signers and things of every held commit resting on the lowered edges (`Paths::through`); the frontier lists those signers' received commits on those things.
- Addenda owed (`flows::addenda::gather`): per cut R makes (`Applied::cuts(write | admin)`), the content commits with a delta stated along a path with that hop (`through`, `Paths::rests`).
- Purge rights (`retention::purge::right_holds`): a request by `Paths::right` of its item's part (admin; write where the delta's purge-by is `writer`, or `author_or_admin` with the delta's purge author on the request's chain); a snapshot standing for a body, and a commit not written yet, by its signer's effective role at its key (`role_right`).
- Sides (`sides.rs`): `Timeline::disputes` after each update that moved an edge write; the default side is held first (trusted iff the reading before the update applied it, by `was_applied` and `fresh`).
- Chain certificates (`sync_ids/chain.rs`): the any-path closure over chained writes (`Timeline::is_chained`) and records.

## Scale: components, forget, the seam
**Principle: a verdict reads only its component, so an update walks only the components it reaches, each from the earliest key it touches (One definition); forgetting a commit is adding in reverse; content is read by point query through a seam the engine backs with its store.** One code path: the global walk is the one-component case, from scratch is an update from the start.

**Components, by what a verdict reads.** A part **touches** its own edge and its path's edges. Its step reads those edges' pass state; its audit and seeds read the parts resting on its own edge, which touch it; `keeps` reads the setters of its hops (a setter touches the edge it sets), then each setter's hops; a drop takes its commit's other parts on its thing, joined when taken in. So one union-find over the edges, a held part joining the edges it touches, bounds every read; a component's parts, events and trusted writes live with its root.
- **The check**: `Paths::single` walks every part in one component, a test mode; `single::paths_components_agree_plain` and `paths_components_agree_large` compare every reading, void trusted writes and disputes, in shuffled arrival orders.
- A commit writing several components joins nothing: its parts in different components read no common edge.
- Every held part joins: one that can never apply (a path through an edge nobody set) joins the components of the edges it names all the same (issues/perm-hostile-path-joins-components.md).

**Merge** (a new part touching two components): the smaller component's parts and events move into the larger (its events after the larger's: the two walked nothing of each other, so any order keeping each one's is a walk of both); then the update from the joining part's key. Components never split.

**Trust per component**: One definition, Trust. A trusted write forgotten, arriving, switched or voided changes its component's active set: seeded anew, walked from scratch.

**Addenda, by reach.** An addendum's validity reads its own path's edges (held, listed, re-granted, its vouching path) and its namers' (a namer vouches by its own path and its removal's cut on the named delta's path, read on the named delta's path). An update re-judges the addenda whose hops `Changed` names (edges, cuts, re-grant), whose commit it names or that arrived, and every addendum their lists name, in turn (the forward closure F): F leaves the valid set, then F's members valid by themselves or by a valid namer outside F join it, and the worklist spreads inside F. Exact: outside F no read changed.

**Forget and update.** `forget(commits)`: each edge-class part leaves at its key, its component updated from there (as a backdated add); a delta leaves the indexes alone (no walk: its verdict was never stored), an addendum seeds F with what its list named, a snapshot names what it carried in `Changed.named`. A component that falls apart stays merged: components only bound work, splitting needs its connectivity again (its size); what it costs is later updates walking parts that are no longer joined. `update(commit)` (a chunked frontier whose leaf arrives late, a part's input changing in place): forget and add in one update, one `Changed`. Meanwhile the walk reads the frontier the node holds (the leaves it holds: the differential's views cut frontiers so), as the draft reads a list: it names what it names. Property: any order of adds, forgets and updates reads as from scratch over what is held, `Changed` exact.

**The void check**: Cost shapes.

**The delta-facts seam.** Content reads delta facts through a trait (`paths::Store`, `Paths<S: Store = MemStore>`, `Paths::with_store`): `put`/`remove` a `Fact`, `get` by `WriteId`, the deltas of a commit (on a thing), the addenda listing a commit, the snapshots carrying one, requests holding a right by (thing, aspect or none, `Names`) stamped after a `ts`, requests and addenda through an edge within a position range, the stored addenda verdicts and request rights, own covers by signer, and `all` (full readings: tests, disputes). A commit's purge flag is read off its deltas; whether it is a role commit is memory's (role commits' facts stay in memory, as do the frontiers naming commits). `MemStore` is the in-memory backing; the engine backs it with its store index, the same index by (edge, position) that turns `Changed` into deltas to judge again. In memory stays only the edge-class state (items, logs, histories: growing with role changes); the walk holds only commits with an edge-class part.

**Count tests (designed shapes).** An appended part in a world of c components: steps in its component alone. A backdated edge part: its component from its key. An update: addenda judged only in F. A trusted component: an appended part costs its own step. A merge: the smaller's entries moved plus the walk from the joining key. A forget: a delta, no walk step; an edge part, its component from its key. One void check: linear. From scratch: linear in parts. Memory: in-memory entries the same at n and 10n content deltas; store lookups per judged delta constant (its path read from the walk).

**Built**: `Paths::single` and the comparison against it (above); hand worlds (`single.rs`): all 720 arrival orders of a chain and a mutually resting pair, every subset of a world of every forget case forgotten and added again under three trust sets, a frontier leaf listing a drop arriving and going, a store returning every lookup reversed (`any_lookup_order_reads_alike`); components (one union-find over the edges, merges moving the smaller side), trust per component, the chained pass (a worklist: after a part whose chained-ness moved, the parts resting on the edge it sets), addenda by reach, `forget` and `update`; their count tests (Counts) and the `_plain` and `_large` properties `incremental::paths_forget_agrees_plain` / `paths_forget_agrees_large` (adds and forgets one to three at a time in random order, a forget of what is not held, forget then re-add, frontier leaves arriving by `update`, each `Changed` checked, the end against from scratch, disputes in one world in eight), and `shapes.rs`'s `robust` forgetting a shuffled half and re-adding it; the delta-facts seam (`counts::delta_facts_live_in_the_store`); disputes per component read off `Changed` (`counts::a_dispute_probe_reads_its_component_alone`). **Left**: a component that falls apart after a forget stays joined (Forget and update; by reach it costs only the event log's forgetting); keeping verdicts given after an update's key that nothing it changed reaches (Reach, What stays component-wide: the shape argued for verdicts with no drops).
