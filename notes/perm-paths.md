# osis-perm: the walk along stated paths
> Summary: Design and build state of osis-perm's walk along stated paths (A1 step 3, `osis_perm::paths`, unwired): its one rule, the two walks and content by point query, input and output, the event log that makes from-scratch evaluation and incremental update one definition, cost shapes and their count tests, what the differential against roles-model `paths` compares and at what size it passes, controls and mutations, the draft-spec corrections it forced, what is owed and by which step.

**Principle: a part's right rests on the edges of the one path it states, read in key order, and a lowering is judged once, at the first pass that reaches it holding its right.** Every rule of notes/a1-spec/permissions.md §5.1 follows from it: what a part needs of each edge (admin, an invite grant's invite, a finalize's write), what a lowering puts in question (the parts resting on its edge whose need it no longer meets), when it is void (its drops would take what its own path rests on, or its remaining admin), the last-admin rule (one named edge, read in the pass), seeds (a trusted write's drops read before the walk). Nothing searches for another path.

**Content (step 3b) follows from it: a delta's validity is a point query along its one path, derived at read from what the walk stores per edge; nothing is stored per delta but its facts, so a lowering costs nothing per delta it cuts.**

## Step 3b design: two walks, content by point query
- **Two walks** (draft §5.1 Vouches): `first` (vouch entries unread) and `timeline` (each counting entry read as naming its commit's parts on its vouch's thing in its removal's list), two `walk::Walk`s fed the same commits. Counting entries are derived from `first` after each update that changed it or added a role-class part: O(vouch entries held), a rare path. A changed entry changes only its removal R's list, which only R's own verdict and seeds read, so the timeline treats R as added again at its key (the event log cut there, as for a new part): one definition for incremental and from scratch. With a trust set, both walks are evaluated from scratch, and a trusted write the timeline does not apply is void (loop at `Paths`). Every reading (statuses, histories, finals, void trusted, disputes, content) is the timeline's. Cost: the role walk runs twice (a constant factor; sharing them while no entry counts is step 7's).
- **Stored per edge (timeline)**: its applied writes (key, value, value before, explicit, the edge as restored before in role and invite: `Paths::writes`), and per level (write, admin) the stack of its **cuts** (applied explicit writes whose value gives below r at their `ts` and that take the edge as restored from r or above), pushed by a step and popped by the rollback like the history. Per edge, the **first chained** key giving write and giving admin (draft §4), kept by a single pass over the held edge writes with its own undo stack, rolled back to the earliest added key (no restarts: chained-ness does not read D).
- **Stored per delta**: its facts alone (commit, position = the start of its `ts`, thing, aspect, hops as (holder, target) pairs, role needed, class, purge author, retention). No status.
- **Indexes by what is named**: `named[c]`: the lowerings and addenda whose list holds c; `carried[c]`: the held snapshots carrying c (a list naming a snapshot names the commits it carries, for that snapshot's thing and aspect; read as empty for a held role commit, which no snapshot carries); requests by author per (thing, aspect, author). Built at arrival, never rebuilt.
- **Counting entries** (draft §5.1 Vouches): an entry of applied vouch V on N keyed by R and listing c counts where an edge write of R on h applies as a lowering in the first walk (whatever it takes from the value: R falls against the edge as restored, so it may drop with nothing taken, README Open 18), V is ordered after it, and c, held, has a role-class part on N, rests on h, is not V's signer's and is ordered before R: by its edge-class parts' key, else at the start of its `ts` (§5.2 Position).
- **Judging one delta** (`Paths::content`): per hop a point query (value before its position, the last cut at or after it: O(log) each); then, only if it failed, its namers (`named` of its commit and of the snapshots carrying it), then the addenda naming it, then the re-grant (finals and first chained per hop). So a delta appended at the end costs O(hops · log), whatever the history, removals or addenda held; a cut delta costs that plus what names it.
- **Addenda** are the one stored validity (draft §5.2: a least fixpoint over every signer): recomputed from scratch by a worklist after each update that changed a walk or added an addendum or snapshot, O(addenda · (hops + namers) + entries); never on a plain delta's arrival. Plain content reads the stored addenda set, so its query is not recursive.
- **`Changed`** names what the engine must re-judge, never per delta for a cut: `parts` (edge-class status changes, every new part, addenda whose validity changed), `edges` (per edge the earliest key its history changed: deltas positioned after it on a path through it), `cuts` (per edge the latest key of a cut added or removed: deltas positioned at or before it), `regrant` (edges whose final role or first chained point changed: every delta through them), `named` (commits a new snapshot made named). A delta's status can change only through one of these or through a delta naming it whose own verdict moved: the engine judges again what `Changed` reaches, then, for each judged delta whose verdict moved, the deltas it names (an addendum's list, a snapshot's carries, a request's (thing, aspect, author)), until none moves (`incremental::rejudged`, checked at every update of the incremental differential; over 200 content worlds' arrivals it judges 360 deltas again for 186 moved, of 4,500 held). A removal flipping 100,000 deltas reports one `cuts` entry. The engine keeps deltas indexed by (edge, position) to turn a range into the deltas it re-merges: that work is the re-merge's own, and a removal's frontier, which names what stays, is the same size.

## Input
Plain structs (`paths::Commit`, `Part`, `PartKind`, `EdgeWrite`, `Delta`, `DeltaClass`), independent of the header's types, so the header's renames do not reach them; step 2's adapter fills them from held commits.
- `Commit`: commit ID, `ts`, signer, its parts in header order (a part's index is its `WriteId` index). `Part`: the thing written (`on`), the stated path [A1..Ak], and an edge write (holder, value as `types::Edge` or a removal, frontier or none, remaining-admin holder, adminless mark), a vouch (removal, list) or a `Delta`.
- `Delta`: aspect (`Ak`), the role it needs (write; admin for an admin-only aspect, `sync.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0`, a snapshot carrying one), purge-author index into [signer, A1..Ak] (past the path: bad input, invalid), retention, class: `Plain` (a content commit's delta), `RoleClass` (judged like content, counts for vouches), `Snapshot { carries }`, `Addendum { removal, list }`, `PurgeByAuthor { author }` (a request by author on this part's thing and aspect, judged as a delta needing write along its path: how the header's `[tid, ak, author]` item meets a path is step 4's adapter question, notes/a1-spec/README.md step 1 *For step 2* (c)).
- `remains` and the mark are per part, not per commit: the header carries one of each per commit (the adapter copies it to each part).
- Records: thing to mint admin, given at construction (descent reads them); a changed record set means a new `Paths`.
- Trust set: `WriteId`s of edge writes.
- A finalize is not flagged: it is a removal of another holder's grant with no frontier that is not a self-removal (spec/permissions.md §2 Finalize), read off the part.
- Order within a commit (§5.1 step 1): by thing written, then the part setting an edge of the commit's own path after every other part on that thing, vouches included (a self-removal of the path's last thing, or the final record edge of a descent), then `roles.0` before `vouches.0`, then holder tid. Assumed: the header orders its aspects by thing then AK; step 2 checks this against the header.
- Bad input the walk sees: members naming a thing twice (content too), an edge-class part on its own signer (no edge write targets a key actor: a key actor's self-removal on itself would otherwise apply), two edge writes on one holder in a commit. A part on another key actor needs the adapter, which knows which tids are key actors: the walk applies it nowhere only because no grant on a key actor exists.
- A key actor's delta on itself along the empty path has the members [S] and no hop: valid (draft §1 Path). Along any other path it names S twice.

## Output
- Per edge-class part: `Status` (applied, applied as an invite grant, void, dropped, unapplied).
- Per delta: `Content` (valid by: held, listed, vouched, re-granted; copied; invalid), a point query (`content`, `content_counted`); `rests` (its hops and position, what the engine indexes) and `naming` (the addenda, snapshots and requests whose verdict it follows).
- `copies`: commits a valid snapshot carries that no valid held delta is (held or not). `purged`: held plain deltas a valid request by author holds the right over.
- Per edge (the timeline): history (key, value), `writes` (with the value and edge as restored before each, explicit or not; `Applied::cuts(r)`), the value and role just before any key, finals and invites, `chained` (first chained key giving write, admin).
- `counting`: the counting entries, (removal, commit, the vouch's thing): an entry keeps the commit's parts on that thing alone (`Walk::listed`, `Facts::listed`). Trusted writes the timeline does not apply (void, seeds lost).
- The dispute set (`Candidates::Explicit`): read over what applies, which deltas are valid and the final roles; both walks per candidate.
- `Changed` from each update (design above).

## One definition: the walk as an event log
The walk is a run of passes over the held parts in key order; D and the verdicts carry from pass to pass. The implementation keeps:
- the pass state (per edge: value, edge as restored in role and invite, history; per part: applied, applied as invite) with an undo entry per applied part, so the state can be rolled back to any key;
- an **event log**: each verdict given (the lowering's key, its drops) in the order the walk gave it.
A pass that ends at a lowering whose drops join D does not restart from the start: it rolls the state back to the commit of the earliest drop and continues, since every part before that point replays unchanged (D and the verdicts changed only after it).

Adding commits whose earliest part is at key p (no trust set): the log is cut at the first event given at a key ≥ p; every event from there on is forgotten (its verdict and drops cleared), and the state is rolled back to the earliest of p, those events' keys and their drops' keys; the walk continues from there. Why that equals the from-scratch walk: every event before the cut was given by a pass that had walked only parts before p, so the from-scratch walk over the larger set gives the same events in the same order, and its pass state at the rollback point is the state the kept events give. From scratch is the same call with the log cut at the start. No cache, no fallback, one code path; debug builds may compare.

With a non-empty trust set both walks are evaluated from scratch on every change (seeds read every held part before each trusted write, and the redo for a void trusted write starts over). Components are not split yet: one walk holds every thing.

The chained pass (draft §4) is one pass over the held edge writes in key order, commit by commit (a commit's sets apply after the commit: "of an earlier commit"), with an undo stack: an update rolls it back to its earliest added key and replays from there. It reads no D, so it has no restarts.

## Cost shapes
- A part appended at the end of key order: its own steps only: a point query per hop (an edge value read), plus, for a lowering that falls, the parts resting on its edge (its candidates: the index `resting` per edge) and its void check.
- A void check (`keeps`) reads back along the setters of its path, each grant's own path in turn, memoized per check: k void lowerings at the end of a chain of k grants cost k² setters read (finding 17; accepted, Open 2). Iterative, never recursive (an adversary's chain is unbounded).
- A backdated part, or a lowering applying with drops: the walk from the earliest affected key: every part after it is walked again, over every thing (no components yet).
- From scratch: one step per part per pass, passes = 1 + restarts; each restart walks from its earliest drop. k lowerings at the end each dropping one early write restart k times, about k·n steps over n parts (`counts::k_lowerings_with_drops_restart_k_times`): a party holding admin forces it with its own 3k writes. R restarts walk at most R·n parts. One void check rescans an edge's history for each setter it chases through it: m grants chased through an edge of m dropped grants cost m² setters in one check (`counts::a_void_check_rescans_an_edge_per_setter_through_it`), so k such lowerings cost k·m² from one party's own 2m + k writes; at most n² per check (owed: step 7).
- Memory: one undo entry per applied part, one event per lowering judged, each part in at most one event's drops (D only grows within a walk), so a node holding n parts keeps logs of size O(n), rebuilt by each settle. An update's journal copies each touched edge's history from its earliest changed key.
- Disputes: both walks from scratch per candidate, and every delta's verdict (Open 3 (a)).
- Content: see Step 3b design. A delta's query: per hop a binary search in the history and the last cut, then (failing) its namers, the addenda naming it, the re-grant. Addenda: a fixpoint from scratch after an update that changed a walk or the counting entries; after addenda or snapshots alone it grows from the stored one (monotone), so k addenda arriving one at a time cost O(k) addenda work. Counting entries: re-derived after an update that changed the first walk or added a role-class part, O(vouch entries).
- Two walks: every role-walk cost above twice (`counts.rs` counts both).
- A delta under k cuts on its edge: one binary search and the edge's last cut per hop, the same at k = 10 and 100 (`counts::a_delta_under_k_cuts_is_a_point_query`).
- Memory: a delta's facts are 320 B inline (`D`) plus its hops, chain, aspect and index entries, about 0.5 KB per held delta, every thing's deltas in memory at once: the wiring keeps them where the engine keeps its index, not in a second copy.
- **Attacker-drivable** (any signer whose commits a node holds, valid or not; owed step 7 unless noted):
  - Any update that changes the walk (a new edge-class part, a stranger's that applies nowhere included) judges every held addendum again, over every thing: k addenda and m such writes cost k·m (`counts::any_walk_change_judges_every_addendum_again`: 100 and 1000 addenda, tenfold). Owed: components, and judging again only the addenda whose path, or whose named deltas' paths, an update reached.
  - A cut delta's query reads every frontier and addendum naming its commit, junk included: k of them cost k per judgment (`shapes::thousands_of_addenda_naming_one_commit_cost_one_read_each`). The engine judges it again only when `Changed` reaches it; a bound needs the named lists indexed by validity.
  - An addendum listing a snapshot reads, in the fixpoint, every commit the snapshot carries: a writer's snapshot carrying m commits costs m per addendum naming it, per fixpoint.

## Built
- `core/perm/src/paths/` (`mod.rs` the input and output types and `Paths`: the two walks, trust, counting, disputes; `walk.rs` one walk; `path.rs` members and hops; `content.rs` deltas, the chained pass, namers, addenda): every rule of draft §1 Path (hops, descent, the last edge, [S]), §2, §1 The last admin, §5.1 steps 1 to 5, Trust set (void read in the timeline, both walks redone), disputes (content validity included), Vouches (first walk, counting with the draft's four conditions, the second walk as the timeline), §5.2 Content, cut, the frontier listing (a counting entry too), Addenda (least fixpoint, never for a role-class delta, a snapshot, a commit carrying a purge request, or a role commit's addendum), Re-grant from each edge's first chained write strictly after its key, Compaction snapshots judged along their path (copies of a valid one shown; a list naming a snapshot names what it carries for its thing and aspect), admin-only and role-class deltas by their role, §6 the `author` class over held plain deltas. Incremental `add` and from scratch by one path. No unsafe, no new dependency, no indexing that can panic.

## Owed (not built)

- Step 5 (lists and certificates): own covers (the own-cover validity clause, and whether one outlives its author's demotion: OPEN); writing vouches and addenda; the list a remover writes.
- OPEN in the draft, not built: a vouch for an invite grant needing only the invite that grant needed (a vouch needs admin on its thing); a list naming a snapshot not held (README Open 19, the walk names through held snapshots).
- Not in the walk, for the adapter or the engine: external fires (§5.2: valid by the setting where the path does not give the role), `created.0` always valid, vouch and addendum entries a delta creates (`put_new`; the adapter passes only those), ephemeral parts as their durable kind.
- Wiring (step 3 with the engine): forgetting a commit and a chunked frontier's leaf arriving (`add` takes neither: from scratch until a `forget` exists, counted); the trust set restricted to what the draft allows; parts on key actors other than the signer as bad input; the engine's index of deltas by (edge, position) to turn `Changed` into deltas to re-merge.

## The differential
`core/perm/tests/it/paths/` against roles-model `paths` under `Rules::fixed` (vouches counted by the first walk, read by the second). Per node view (held set, the frontier leaves it sees, its trust set) it compares every edge-class part's status, every edge's history, the final roles and invites, the void trusted writes, every delta's validity, the copies (the model's `copied`), the purged deltas, and the dispute set (the model's `disputes`: what applies, what is valid, the roles). The model's kinds map to deltas: content and purge requests on `doc.0`, covers as its snapshots, addenda on `addenda.0`, retention author or admin.
- Generators: the six `tiny` kinds (edge writes only), the story (content, addenda, covers, purges, vouches at every node), and `content.rs`'s worlds (drawn here over the model's `World`: H pairs B, X, N5, shares T1 with A2, K gives Z A2; halfway H removes X; random edge writes on A and T1 (some commits writing both), content along [A] and [A2] with either purge author, addenda and vouches (on T1 along [A] or [A2]) mostly against that removal and stamped after it, covers, purges by author; one view in four drops a quarter of the commits; every third trusts a lowering). Over 20,000 worlds they give every verdict: held, listed (8k), vouched (195), re-granted (411), copied (1.2k), purged (1k), counting entries (822), void trusted (1k).
- Every world is compared. The model is read as the view knows the world (`as_known`): a cover the node does not hold carries nothing, since roles-model's `World::names` reads every cover's carries world-wide and the walk names through held snapshots only (README Open 19: 69 of 20,000 content worlds name a cover not held; 3 read otherwise without it).
- Hand worlds: `hand.rs` (3a: edge-class rules), `hand_content.rs` (a counterpart of each rule-naming model hand test on content, vouches, addenda, covers and purges, same names, at the model test's views, with the walk's reason where the rule is named; the walk's own: a key actor on itself, an edge write on its signer, a retain removal (Open 15, the draft's words), a vouch against a removal of a holder already gone (Open 18), a counting entry keeping its commit's edge writes and role-class deltas on its vouch's thing alone and counting only where c's part there rests on R's edge, admin-only content and addenda, an addendum only after its removal, a re-grant from the latest edge's first chained write, a stranger's grant moving no first point, a commit carrying a purge request, a vouch naming a commit after its removal; per rule no control names: an addendum for nothing stamped after its removal, only on its own thing, for no snapshot; a snapshot naming and copying only its own thing and aspect, carrying no role commit; a counting entry listing a role-class delta, never its removal's own commit, ordering a commit with no edge-class part at its `ts` start; validity along a chain of three addenda; addenda naming each other supporting nothing (support only runs back in position, so the least fixpoint has no self-support to exclude); a counting change named in every arrival order).
- Input no generator makes (`shapes.rs`): 3a's hostile shapes; vouches keyed by or listing themselves, their signer's commits or their own removal; addenda keyed by grants or themselves naming role commits; snapshots claiming copies of commits not made, unknown, role commits and themselves, and one by a removed party; deltas along 64 made-up things, a repeated thing, a purge author past the path, a request by an author off its chain; 2,000 addenda naming one commit (judging it reads each once: 200 and 2,000, tenfold); 400 random soups of parts and deltas. None panics; every arrival order reads as from scratch; each update's `Changed` checked (below), under each single trusted write too.
- `Changed` (`incremental::check`): exact for edge-class statuses, history, cuts and re-grant edges; every new part reported; any other delta reported changed its validity (an addendum's); every delta whose verdict or purge moved is in `rejudged` (above). Arrivals one at a time and in batches of one to four; trust switches in tiny and content worlds.
- Default: `tiny_worlds_agree`, `story_worlds_agree`, `content::content_worlds_agree` (660 worlds, disputes in 60), `incremental::incremental_equals_from_scratch` (200 worlds of each tiny kind and 200 content worlds, commits in a shuffled order, one at a time and batched), `incremental::changed_reaches_every_moved_delta_and_little_more`.
- Properties: `paths_tiny_agree_large` (160,000), `paths_story_agree_large` (8,000 at every node), `paths_content_agree_large` (200,000, disputes in a tenth), `paths_incremental_agrees_large` (60,000 over the seven kinds). 0 disagreements, every world compared, at full size (`OSIS_FULL=1`, about 520 CPU-s together, the content property about 100) and at the gate's (a tenth: 49 CPU-s together, 24 more than 3a's).

## Mutations and controls
Each control of the model that reaches edge-class parts fails the default tests by its named hand world (3a): `-no-void`, `-exempt`, `-audit-applied`, `-admin-falls`, `-own-drops`, `-remains-unkept`, `-leave`, `-invite-role`, `-any-edge`, `-timed`, `-last-any`, `-all-candidates`, `-seeds-all`, `-refuse-shapes`; and 3a's rules with no control (its commit messages). Step 3b, each taken by the walk as a source edit and failing a default test: `-own-vouch` (`a_removed_signer_vouches_for_nothing_of_its_own`), `-facts` (no copies: the cover worlds), `-purge-any-path` (`a_purge_holds_the_right_only_through_its_own_chain`), `-purge-contains` (`a_purge_by_author_takes_exactly_that_authors_deltas`), `-first-applied` (the re-grant worlds), the vouches module's `-off` (`a_dispute_whose_write_is_a_no_op_where_trusted_is_shown`, `a_counting_entry_keeps_its_commits_parts_on_its_vouchs_thing_alone`), `-vouch-whole` (`a_vouch_keeps_a_commits_parts_on_its_own_thing_alone` and the walk's own, the content differential), the addenda module's `-no-admin` (`an_addendum_vouches_for_admin_only_content_only_by_admin`). Rules no control names: a delta held with no cut check, a cut by any explicit write, a cut not taking the edge as restored from the role, an addendum vouching before its removal, without its path's role, for its own signer, for a commit carrying a purge request, the re-grant bound the earliest hop's, chained writes needing no authority, copies of an invalid snapshot, the counting conditions (V after R; c before R; c with a role-class part on N; c resting on R's edge by a part on N; R taking from the value), `Changed` without cuts, the addenda fixpoint not grown on arrival. Each also failing a default test (hand worlds above, else the differentials): the fixpoint stopping a round early; an addendum's path read at the final role; the re-grant from at or after the first chained `ts`; cuts at one level for every delta (either level); a purge reaching deltas stamped after it; `Changed.cuts` the earliest changed cut; `named` without counting changes; addenda grown, not recomputed, when the walk changed in the same update; a cut in the delta's millisecond read as before it; an addendum's removal before the delta; an addendum on another thing; a snapshot of another aspect or thing naming or copying; counting entries unread by content; the re-grant without the final role; the fixpoint seeded by held addenda alone; addenda vouching for snapshots; a counting entry naming its removal's own commit or a commit not resting on the lowered edge; counting by unapplied lowerings; the first chained point moving to later writes; an addendum for its own signer's delta; a role commit named through a snapshot; a commit with no edge-class part ordered at its commit key; counting only removals taking from the value. Not applicable: `-once`, `-listed`, `-key-order` and the modules' `-no-rewrite`, `-held`, `-state`, `-stands` (writing policy: step 5).

## Counts (`counts.rs`, at n = 100 and 1000; walk counts are both walks')
- A part appended at the end: 1 step per walk, 1 pass, nothing audited.
- A backdated part: n + 1 steps per walk, also on a thing the history never touches: no components yet.
- A lowering applying with drops: 1 restart per walk, from its earliest drop; audited = the parts resting on its edge.
- A void lowering at the end of a chain of k grants: about k setters; k of them about k² (finding 17, accepted). One check through an edge of m dropped grants: about m².
- k lowerings with drops: k restarts, about k·n steps. From scratch: one step per part.
- A delta appended at the end: no walk step, no addenda work; its query reads the same at n and 10n.
- A removal cutting n deltas: its walk steps, no content work; `Changed` holds one `cuts` entry and the removal; each cut delta's query reads the same at n and 10n.
- k addenda arriving one at a time: addenda work linear in k (100 and 1000: tenfold).
- One delta named by k addenda: its query reads each once (`shapes.rs`).

## Draft spec corrections
- notes/a1-spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 4: the writes `keeps` passes over when their own `keeps` fails are grants and finalizes.
- §1 Path: "[S]" is the members of a key actor's delta on itself along the empty path; any other path on itself names S twice.
- §5.1 Vouches, first and third conditions: R's edge is the edge of an edge write of R meeting the first condition (R may hold several).
- §5.2 Copies: a list naming a snapshot names the commits it carries for that snapshot's thing and aspect, in a frontier and an addendum alike; a carried role commit is named by nothing (role commits are never compacted).
- §5.1 Vouches, first condition: an edge write of R applies as a lowering in the first walk, whatever it takes from the value (README Open 18); an entry keeps c's parts on its vouch's thing alone, and c rests on R's edge by a part there.

## What step 7 owes
- Components: a backdated part or a restart walks only its component (things joined by a part's thing, holder, signer, path, named holder and record chains); without them any backdated part walks every later part over every thing (`counts::a_backdated_part_walks_again_what_follows_it` asserts n + 1 for a thing the history never touches; with components it is 1).
- The void check linear within one check: a skip pointer per edge per check past what it passed over (a check rescans an edge's history for each setter it chases through it: m² setters for m grants chased through an edge of m dropped grants, `counts::a_void_check_rescans_an_edge_per_setter_through_it`; notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 2).
- Trusted walks incremental (every change under a non-empty trust set is a walk from scratch, and each redo for a void trusted write another); disputes walked per component, not over every thing (Open 3).
- One walk while no vouch entry counts (the two walks are then equal; both run today: twice the role-walk work).
