# Engine: role commits, keys and rotation
> Summary: How the engine reads and writes role-class data: edge writes from `roles.0` deltas, `secrets.0` ingestion and established epochs, the roles `secrets.0`/`keys.0` deltas need, invite-grant boxes, key actors and other flows hooks, the flows' readings (departures, finalizes, the cascade), the rotation and secrets invariants, the cover check's own self-removals, head keys and `groups.0`. Rules: spec/permissions.md, spec/crypto.md §4; reasons: notes/permissions.md, notes/crypto.md (flows implementation: notes/permissions.md, Implementation (role flows)). Not here: dropping lost access (notes/engine-lost-access.md), writer-side role rules (notes/engine-apply.md, Writer side).

## Edge writes from `roles.0` deltas (spec/permissions.md §2: a grant change is remove + put)
- An entry the delta creates (it carries the entry's dot) sets the grant it reads; a reset of a known entry dot removes it (keys learned from the creating deltas, `ix.holder_keys`); a delta writing inside an entry it did not create is bad input (`check_holders_parts`, in `examine`, so the writer refuses it too).
- A reset whose key is unknown (its creation not held) names no holder: the commit's other writes go to perm and the commit stays in `unresolved`, derived again when keys arrive (`resolve_unresolved`) and once after an open has read every commit (it reads them in `ts` order).
- `flows::put_edge` always writes remove + put, so a grant over an entry the walk dropped (still live in the state) resets it (`regrant_over_dropped_entry_spec_perm_2`). Nested field writes over the entry's creation would compose two changes wrongly (a role raise losing an `until`) and write a grant over a dropped entry into it, leaving the grantee unresolved for good.
- Perm consumes these as abstract edge-write records (`validity::add_role_commit`, `EdgeParts`), persisted per commit in `node/edges` (notes/engine-store.md).

## `secrets.0`, `keys.0` and established epochs
- **Ingestion** (`atomic::observe_touched`, `Core::ingest_keyed`): a join reads only the box and history-box entries its delta touched (`secrets::touched_keys`; a reset of the value, a field or a map reads the whole state), then the history box of every epoch with a secret held, repeatedly (what `open_histories` reaches over the whole state). An opener's new secrets reopen only the boxes keyed to it (`ix.boxes_to`, `flows::reopen_dependents`).
- **Established epochs** (`apply/keys.rs`, spec/crypto.md §4): a held valid role commit's `secrets.0` write is recorded with its `keys.0` write (`SecretsWrite::rotated`, whole keysets: flows replace the field); an invalid commit's epochs count for nothing and are never recorded. A held `secrets.0` compaction snapshot keys its epochs (`snapshot_keyed`). A delta found invalid stops counting (`keys::invalidated`, which unsets only the rotations that commit made, never every epoch its history boxes name: else another commit's epoch goes unapplied and a later removal rotation never establishes); valid again it is recorded again (`revalidated`, the keysets before taken as the current `keys.0`). `Core::own_epochs` establishes from these and the held commits. A removal is a keyless reset, so box keys come from an index of held keyed entries, and a write whose removed keys are unknown waits until they arrive, so a rotation received before the one it supersedes is not established. Epochs persist (`k/`), never re-inferred. `secrets.0` compaction is gated on `may_compact` (`osis_keys::may_compact_secrets`). `Engine::epochs(actor)` exposes the view.
- Late parts (`join_late`, `with_keys`): late `secrets.0` parts establish epochs with the commit's held `keys.0` like a whole commit's; a late `keys.0` part applies the held `secrets.0` of its thing again. `note_required` judges a late `secrets.0` part with every held part of the commit and clears the mark when the whole commit fits; the late parts' validity is recomputed before they join (`judge_late`; other aspects' flips go through the re-judge). Judging a late part alone makes a redemption's `secrets.0` overreach (no grantee) when its `roles.0` part came first by a certificate copy.
- **Roles they need**: `secrets.0` and `keys.0` deltas need the highest role they rotate or box (at least write): admin for an admin box, history key or keyset, and for any delta that is not a plain struct (`Fragment::is_plain_struct`: a register write or root reset replaces every field); such a `secrets.0` delta gets no invite-grant exemption.
- **Invite grants** (spec/permissions.md §2) may reset nothing: `put_secrets(.., invite)` writes fresh puts (`Op::Put`, spec/crdt.md §5.6 `put_new`), and `validity::grantee_boxes_only` accepts its one `secrets.0` delta only when every box and history map is `Fragment::is_put_only_map` over the delta's own dots (`Owned::owns`: no record at another's dot) with keys fitting its grantees; the header must list one delta for the aspect, so a node missing a part judges alike. All from the commit alone: looking up reset dots in an index of held boxes would make validity depend on arrival order. Fresh puts stack live entries per key, so only invite grants use them; two puts of one key in one commit do not net out like two commits (same stamp), which no writer does.

## Flows hooks in the engine
- `Core::key_actors` (held link keys, in memory only: `hold_key_actor`): `Mutation::sign_as` signs as one in commit form, `Core::epochs` derives from it, `ingest_secrets` opens its boxes.
- `observe_state` calls `flows::reopen_dependents` when secrets of an actor are learned (boxes sealed to it in other actors' `secrets.0` open then; actors found through `boxes_to`).
- `DiagnosticKind::Removed` when the node drops an actor's secrets.
- Re-seal and registration are pull hooks for node-sync (`reseal_due`, `own_secrets_candidates` at the end of a complete pass; `issue_step` per host head); flows only return deliveries.
- `reseal_due` makes the rotations and finalizes due on the actor (`ensure_rotated`) before reading its candidates when any are due: a rotation or a finalize going alone can box or leave out a candidate, and a list read before it named a holder its re-seal could no longer seal, failing the whole pass (`reseal_candidates_follow_the_finalize_before_them_spec_permissions_3`).

## Flows readings
- Departures are recomputed from the timeline (applied lowering edge writes, a finalized timed edge counting as its role's departure) and from state (a box at a current epoch whose holder holds less than that role now), holders re-granted since dropping out, from the actor alone (a holder actor's departures reach it as stale boxes, notes/crypto.md). Keys read no clock: an edge past its `until` is no key departure until finalized.
- **Finalize** (spec/permissions.md §2): a node with write about to encrypt under an actor first writes the finalize of each edge there past its `until` of role at most its own (`rotation::finalize_due`, `flows::edges::finalizable`, `finalize_in`): a removal with no frontier carrying the rotation. While it cannot (it lacks a current secret the rotation needs) every encrypting under the actor waits for a re-seal. A renewal of an ended edge is not finalized first: the grant writes it anew.
- Rotation authority is the signer's (`who`: the node, or a held key actor). A self-removal carries no rotation and the leaver drops what it lost; an explicit demotion runs the cascade on the target, then carries the target's rotation.
- Every encrypting path runs the cascade first (`ensure_rotated`/`require_covered`: depth first, the holder actors with key access `who` may rotate, then the actor; each gets its finalizes first): grants, re-seals, own secrets (the first admin's epochs), `create_label`, `issue_step`. It returns the roles still blocked; nothing may encrypt under those (uncovered, stale, a rotation waiting while Seal waits or for want of possession, notes/crypto.md Holder actors).
- A rotation boxes the timeline's current holders whose keys are held; inbox blobs of removals carry the `roles.0` parts only; re-seal commits go by inbox and under the actor's labels (later removals reset their box entries).
- `put_secrets` writes boxes and history boxes as remove + put, so a grant or re-seal re-writing them unchanged (spec/crypto.md §4) still carries them and one live entry per key stays (a nested write into a rotation's entry the holder never held would be unreadable to it); an inbox grantee reads pre-grant content, from the pending grant already (`read_pending_boxes`).

## Rotation and secrets invariants
- A rotation also goes by inbox to every holder it boxes that held no box at a current read epoch (`rotation::unreached`): a holder with no box at the epoch the commit is wrapped under (a single-use link race's winner, boxed by the sharer's second rotation) could not reach its box, while the re-seal trigger sees it boxed.
- A rotation that boxes nobody writes no boxes field: `engine::secrets::secrets_value` (used by `flows::secrets_in`, apply's observe and the `apply/keys.rs` snapshot and compaction gates) reads a never-written map field as empty.
- Forgetting on lost access drops plaintext states, `c/` records, wraps and blob keys of unreadable things, not only `k/`; it runs from the work list with the pending-grant guard, since a naive "role none now" test counts secrets read from a not-yet-applied grant as lost (notes/engine-lost-access.md).

## Own self-removals in the cover check (`rotation::departures_placing`, spec/crypto.md §4)
- node-sync's cover check (`placing` under `uncovered`) ignores a departure whose newest edge write (on the innermost actor of its way) is a self-removal made here (a single-use redemption's removal of L from K, signed by L the redeemer drops at once) or by the node, its account or a held key actor, whichever node of the account made it, until that commit is `placed` (sync state: entries, presence, co-reader reports, outbox), after any rotation the node may make; derived from stored state, so a restart keeps it. Bounded by the upload, not by rotation: the departed party's other holders (L's link holders, other holders of an actor X the node removed from G while staying in G itself) would otherwise read all the node's later writes under the old epoch.
- The node's own departures (its account's, its node's) count nowhere, placed or not (`rotation::others`, inside `blocked_epoch`): the departed party is the node itself. This bullet's rule is for the other holders a self-removal departs.
- A self-removal on things with no label here counts as placed at once (its copies elsewhere are then held back: rare). Other departures (a removal the node did not know of) hold its uploads back.
- node-sync's `drop_left` drops each actor heard out or whose self-removal is placed (`LostScope::Only` takes exactly what it names): a thing read through the lost actor with secrets of its own is listed, heard out and dropped on its own, so its unsent writes wait for its labels, not the actor's (notes/engine-lost-access.md).

## Head keys and `groups.0`
- Head keys (`labeling/blobs.rs` `matching`): a reading-only secret finds the head's epoch (open, walk) but never yields the write or delete key (stamps and deletes are encrypting under, spec/crypto.md §4).
- `groups.0` (`engine/groups.rs`): a `group.0` change unlists and relists the changed group and the groups above it (O(their reach)); open sets every definition, then derives once. Every rebuild of `group.0` updates the index, so a member gone by expiry, purge, a keep bound or validity leaves it.
