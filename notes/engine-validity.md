# Engine: validity, un-merge and snapshot copies
> Summary: How the engine keeps every delta's validity a function of the held set: what is re-judged after a held-set change and how cheaply, the one rebuild path for un-merge and re-merge (and its cost), snapshot authority (an invalid snapshot gives nothing), carried writes and vouching (a valid snapshot's copies join like any write), uncovering bodies dropped as covered, and the possible-holders helper. Rules: spec/permissions.md §5 and §5.2, spec/crdt.md §4.6; reasons and Tess's decisions: notes/permissions.md (Snapshots and history, Revocation). Not here: perm itself (notes/permissions.md Implementation), deaths and keep bounds (notes/engine-retention.md), suspect flags (notes/engine-suspects.md).

## Re-judging (`apply/validity.rs` `rejudge_all`)
- After a held-set change only the deltas whose validity may have moved are judged again, so the cost follows the change, not the held set:
  - the deltas of authors whose role may have moved, on things it may have moved on (`Core::stale_authors`, `stale_under`: `Held::upstream` and `Held::reach` of what `osis_perm::Changed` reports; `by_thing` is keyed by (thing, author) for this), and the commits writing a reported grant whoever signed them (an invite grant overtaken on its grant stops applying and its boxes lose their exemption with no role of its author moving);
  - every delta on the things a component walked anew or a new record's record chain reaches (`Core::stale_things`);
  - commits whose void, required-role or overreach inputs changed (`stale_commits`), and those acting for a thing whose timeline changed (`acts_for`); deltas answer to their thing's `required.0^` (`in_force`), so where one of those is judged again they are too;
  - everything after a rebuild at open (`stale_all`).
- `rejudge_all` runs again while a rebuild left something stale (a part `retention::uncover` made missing loses what its body judged); it ends because `uncover` only turns parts dropped as covered missing, never back. A rollback restores validity flags with the journal. Debug builds assert nothing stale is left at the end of a transaction, that no flag changed outside the re-judged set, and the walks and order harnesses check after every reopen that each flag is what judging it gives (`Engine::misjudged`: every input of a judgment that comes from a body, rotation role, overreach, possession shown, is persisted in the commit's `node/metas` row); `incremental::role_moves_as_reported_*` checks the reported moves against every timeline answer.
- Rejected: re-judging every held delta on any role commit (no staleness tracking). Measured (`scale::role_change_and_open_cost`, release, load 20-40, 10^4 commits held): about 1.9 µs per held commit per role commit, a grant 2 ms → 22 ms, 1000 edge writes' re-judging 58 ms → 17.6 s; past about 2.5×10^4 held commits a received role commit misses the 50 ms budget (notes/testing.md Performance budgets).
- Perm's `Timelines::update_touched` re-partitions around the things whose held inputs changed (`Held::take_touched`; a commit naming only things of its component skips that and continues the walk, `Timelines::append`) and reports the components evaluated anew (all their things) and those whose walk only went on or only gained new leaves (`created.0` of things nothing names): for those, only the things authority flows to from the touched ones (`Held::reach`: things below them and targets of their grants, transitively). Writing a new thing re-judges that thing's deltas only; so does a grant to a holder new to every walk (perm `Timelines::append` joins it).
- The re-judge (`Judge`) takes each commit once, under the first re-judged thing among its aspects, looks record depth and the timeline up once per thing, and answers an author's deltas from where it holds the role (perm `Timeline::valid_from`, `delta_valid`'s re-grant case, asked once per (thing, author, role)) without a per-delta query. A voided commit is re-judged alone.
- Validity follows the held set, not arrival order: a new leaf takes its first admin's demotion records (notes/permissions.md Implementation), so a holder offline through a revoke whose frontier missed a write, applying the demotion before the item's `created.0`, still records the demotion on the item. Tests: `apply/tests_order.rs` every arrival order, perm `incremental` late leaves, timed_dead.rs `late_holder_judges_revoke_alike_*`.

## Un-merge and re-merge: one rebuild path
- Un-merge is by invalid delta, not by author: the writes carried by deltas invalid here (`Owned::owns`: session counter range or commit ID) go, with the records they created and others' writes nested in them (`atomic::Unmerge`), so another writer's nested write under an invalid creation goes on every node alike.
- **Every** validity flip, body drop, strip, re-arrival and invalid delta rebuilds the aspect from what is held (`apply::rebuild` → `rebuilt`; issues/drop-rejoin-robust.md item 1): every held body, leftovers included, joined without the invalid deltas' writes. An invalid delta joins nothing on apply; the aspect is rebuilt once the commit is stored (its sequence records come back as hidden anchors, spec/crdt.md §4.6). An invalid commit's aspect whose part is not held rebuilds too (`arrived_invalid`: others' writes under its creations go, also when they joined first). A valid snapshot joining with writes of invalid deltas rebuilds the aspect (they count again, others' writes nested under them with them). There is no in-place un-merge: re-merging needs the bodies (a register's earlier value, another's write nested under a revalidated creation come back only from them), and one path is what keeps every case alike.
- The rebuild writes the state shredded; members gone by expiry, purge, a keep bound or validity leave the group index with it (`group.0`).
- A debug build checks after every transaction that the state equals what a rebuild from the held bodies gives (`apply::check_rebuilt`: every aspect a change other than a valid join touched, and a sample of those joins), so for rebuilt aspects that a rebuild is a fixpoint. osis-crdt `unmerge_spec_4_6` checks un-merge per delta on random histories.
- **Cost**: O(aspect history) per flip. `scale::validity_flip_cost_spec_permissions_5_2` (release, load about 11, a set of 10^4 one-element commits): a revoke flipping one add invalid 278 ms, an invalid add arriving 256 ms, almost all of it the rebuild re-reading 10^4 bodies (`rebuild: body` 319 ms over both). Within the rare-event budget; issues/node-core-gaps.md.

## Snapshot authority (spec/permissions.md §5.2)
- A compaction snapshot's part is valid iff its author could write the aspect at it (`Judge::part`: `delta_valid` at its point, `required_role(ak)`, never the rotation role; `created.0` always).
- An invalid snapshot gives nothing: its copies never join (on apply, late, or in `rebuilt`), it is left out of deaths replays and death carriers (`copies::invalid_snapshot`), and it covers nothing (`covered` → `Some(false)`), so a demoter lists the commits it would have covered. A snapshot's frontiers and `Absorbed` are no receipts (`validity::inflated_snapshot_claims_count_nothing_spec_crdt_8`), so a demotion frontier does not depend on a snapshot's validity (`validity::flipped_snapshot_leaves_frontier_alike_spec_perm_5_2`).
- A commit whose part is away applies without it whatever snapshots are held (`validity::listed_part_alike_in_any_order_*`, `part_listed_by_invalid_snapshot_applies_*`; sim `coverer_revoked`).
- A snapshot dropped other than as covered (bound, purge, expiry) still settles suspect flags (`Dropped::stands_in`, indexed by `ix.dropped_snapshots`); one dropped as covered needs not (its coverer is valid and stamped no earlier, spec/commits.md §5). A coverer dropped other than as covered carries while valid: its writes stay in the state (`bound_dropped_coverer_still_carries_spec_commits_5`; `validity::dropped_snapshot_leaves_frontier_spec_commits_5`). Purge keeps them too.
- A valid snapshot vouches for every write it carries (Tess's decision "Snapshots vouch", notes/permissions.md): nothing is judged per claimed author. Durable snapshots name no `Authors`.

## Carried writes and vouching (spec/permissions.md §5.2)
A write's own delta is the one carrying its dot (`copies::any_delta`: the session range holding it; for `Commit(C)`, C if commit-form with the aspect). Three cases:
- **A valid snapshot's copies join like any write** (Tess: history is verified or absent; a snapshot speaks for current state alone, notes/permissions.md Snapshots and history): never compared with a held delta's, never stripped, nothing recorded or reported about them. Copies that differ (a forged snapshot, a stale copy) settle by the copy order (spec/crdt.md §4.1, total), so nodes holding the same bodies read alike, and a node holding only the snapshot reads its copy until the delta arrives (`validity::differing_copies_join_by_copy_order_alike_*`, `walks::open_equals_rebuild_random_histories_forged`, which drops covered bodies too). A valid snapshot joining costs one join, O(part). Rejected (built once, removed): comparing a valid snapshot's copies with the held delta's and stripping the delta's writes where they differ ("the snapshot's copy governs"): it stored and acted on a writer's claim about others' history, read the whole state per snapshot join, kept an index of what snapshots carry per aspect, and rebuilt the aspect per differing copy.
- **Delta invalid**: its writes go (un-merge) except under dots a held valid snapshot holds a write under (`copies::vouched`, from each valid snapshot's `copy_index`, as records: a map entry's creation copy names its key, so a copy under another key keeps others' nested writes only in its own record, `Unmerge::vouches`; read only when an invalid delta's dot is in play, `Unmerge::probe`). Dropping a valid snapshot's body rebuilds the aspect (it vouches no more). `covered` keeps a valid snapshot body's copies (the cover must carry them all), so a cover never drops a voucher's vouching.
- **Delta never received**: the write is in the state only because a valid snapshot gave it (nothing indexes which: tests read them from the held snapshot bodies, `Engine::carried_dots`; a record with no `ts` carries no write). When a snapshot turns invalid, `given_by` (read from its body at the flip: its writes under dots of deltas held with their bodies (which give them back, also where the snapshot placed its copy otherwise: a nested write in a key-erased record, order seed 27039), never received, or dropped as covered with no residue-only coverer, `copies::regiven`; a write under a cover dropped for its bound stays) is stripped from the state and the aspect rebuilt, so other held valid carriers give their own copies back (keeping the dots they share would leave a demoted writer's differing copy in the state where it came first: `invalidated_copy_of_shared_dot_goes_alike_*`): alike with a node that got the demotion first (`demoted_compactor_snapshot_by_frontier_*`). An invalid snapshot arriving removes nothing, so a reader's copy voids no write.
  - Residue: a write given only by a valid snapshot since dropped stays in the state; if a later snapshot carrying it turns invalid, its `given_by` removes it at a node that holds that later snapshot as valid first, not at one that got the demotion first. Where the first carrier dropped under the later one's cover it goes missing again when the later one falls (Uncovering) and comes back once fetched.
- Tess's scenario (the Limit in spec/requirements.md Permissions): a removed holder's unseen write compacted by an honest writer before it learned of the removal stays at every node holding that snapshot (also where the original is held: vouched), `removed_holder_write_compacted_earlier_stays_alike_*`; an admin's reset or revert removes it; one no valid snapshot carries goes (revocation exact).
- Limit (clock skew around expiry): a snapshot written after a map entry's expiry T carries its creation value-less, which vouches only the dot's key-erased record; a node before T holds the keyed record, which that copy does not vouch, so others' valid writes nested in it go there while they stay at nodes past T. The dot is hidden either way (a value-less creation hides it), so reads agree while states differ until T.

## Uncovering (`retention::uncover`)
- A body dropped as covered (`Dropped::as_cover`; `Engine::drop_body` is node-sync's compaction; bound, expiry and purge drops stay dropped), once none of its coverers carries it (each invalid, or itself dropped as covered under covers none of which does; one dropped for its bound, expiry or a purge carries while valid), goes missing again when the next snapshot on the aspect falls invalid (from the re-judge's rebuild): its `Meta::dropped` entry removed, `missing` set, its own writes stripped from the state, and the aspect flagged suspect (`Why::Uncovered`). A missing snapshot body is covered by nothing until it arrives (`covered` → `None`: else any older valid snapshot would "cover" it and the compactor drop it for good). It joins as a late part when it arrives again.
- node-sync (`Changed::undropped` → `reopen_undropped`) turns its shredded entries back to `Unknown` (also at start, `SyncEngine::new`: a crash between the engine's change and the sync state's flush), so a pass fetches them where present (a shredded entry counts as unopened, `work.rs`; the fetch reply brings the wrap); a holder finds the body uncovered (must-hold) and uploads it again where it was deleted (sim `coverer_revoked`; `coverer_demoted_after_cover_drop_alike_*`, `covered_drop_coverer_and_remover_revoked_alike_*`). Without a copy anywhere the write is gone at every node alike.
- A missing part dropped later (a keep-at-most timer set before) is dropped and no longer missing (`drop_part`), as an open reads it. Chains un-drop whole: a delta dropped under S1 and S1 under S2 both go missing when S2 falls, so the delta's writes come back from S1's copies once S1 is fetched again (counted as a never-received delta's then). The walks tests drop covered bodies after half their snapshots, so open-equals-rebuild covers un-drops.
- A part dropped while invalid that turns valid is missing again the same way (`retention::valid_again`; spec/commits.md §5 Never received).

## Possible holders (`Held::ever_admins`, `ever_holders`, `ever_writers`)
The one helper for any "could this signer ever have held the role" that must not depend on arrival (purge rights, `required.0^` settings; spec/commits.md §4 possible admins and writers): a least set grown from the thing through first admins and grants signed by members, of any validity. One set, not one per thing (per-thing sets cost 20 s per call on a chain of 1000 groups). Any timeline read for the signer is order dependent.
