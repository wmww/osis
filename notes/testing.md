# Testing
> Summary: How osis is tested and how to run each layer: principles, tiers and CI, conformance vectors and their freeze policy, fuzzing, size budgets and benches, coverage, policy checks, the spec-to-test map, and the conventions every crate follows when it adds tests, vectors, fuzz targets or benches.

Strategy items not built (a 10^4-seed sweep, membership-model refactors, a TLA+ walk model) are in issues/tests-unbuilt.md.

## Principles
- The spec is the oracle: every MUST has a test that fails without it, and every test cites the spec section it checks, so `grep` (or notes/testing-map.md) answers "what checks §X".
- Determinism: seeded randomness, injected clocks, in-process transport. A failure is a seed; a flake is a bug.
- Peer bytes never panic: every parser is fuzzed; decoders take limits.
- Models (models/) are independent oracles; the implementation never depends on them and they are never patched to match it.

## Tiers and CI
| tier | what | how | where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | unit, small proptest (`PROPTEST_CASES=32`), vectors | `cargo test -p <crate>`; `cargo test --workspace` at integration | `ci.yml`, every push |
| 2 | `#[ignore]` tests: large property runs, size budgets, differential at size | `OSIS_SLOW=1 PROPTEST_CASES=1024 cargo test --workspace --release -- --include-ignored` | `nightly.yml` `slow` |
| 3 | fuzz, sim seed sweeps, big model instances, TLC, benches, coverage | see below | `nightly.yml` |

`ci.yml` jobs: `test` (build, tier 1, vectors, clippy `-D warnings`), `policy` (tools/policy.py, `tools/spec_map.py --uncited` and `--gaps`), `deny`. ## Models (models/)
- `models/crdt` and `models/membership`: Rust reference models (notes/crdt.md, notes/permissions.md); the membership model's exhaustive tiers and random samples run in nightly `models`.
- Differentials against models: crdt join and wire (osis-crdt `tests_seq`, `tests_join`), the crdt decoder (osis-crdt `tests_decoder.rs`: mutated writer deltas and snapshots of random histories accepted or rejected alike, accepted ones decoding to equal fragments; notes/crdt.md, Reference model), perm (osis-perm `tests/differential.rs`).
- `models/tla/sync` and `models/tla/rotation`: TLA+ models written from spec/ alone, checked with TLC. Sync covers one label at one blind server: upload, download, deposit and promotion, token deletes (blob-ID carrier tiebreak) and timed deletes (snapshot first when writes stay live), expiry, compaction, purge and rewrite, due snapshots (also before a live drop), tombstones, offline nodes, a dropping/replaying/reordering server; properties: no live write leaves the label uncarried, must-hold bodies converge to the server, must-not-hold bodies and duplicates do not persist, entries settle, deposits get promoted, purges reach every node, members end up holding a carrier for their live state. Rotation covers read-role rotation of one actor: established epochs, forced and eager rotation, forks, removal carried in its rotation, grants, registration steps after the rotation chain is uploaded, claims and claim-set drops, stranded heartbeats (also on a refused claim), deposit-seed refresh, re-seal after a complete pass (by inbox and under the label, so other nodes learn its boxes), rotation by inbox to members without a box at the epoch before, per-node box knowledge, member peers, and an adversary planting boxes (secrets from pending or invalid commits are reading-only); properties: no departed member reads content written with its departure known, no departed member holds an epoch that did not box it, nothing is encrypted under a planted secret (a negative control with the rule removed fails in 4 states), every current member eventually opens every current epoch and reads everything. `models/tla/membership` (written from spec/, checked against osis-perm where the text was subtle) covers the membership walk and tenure ledger (permissions §1–§2, §5.1, §5.2, §6): owner chains, B as a member of A, `until`, self-removals, explicit demotions with frontiers, the demoter's kept standing, several-edge removals, layer-2 content, the depth cap, ledger production and replay; a state is a history (any ts, any frontier subset); properties: drop order does not matter, roster snapshots at every point are neutral, a removed member's later writes are invalid, demoters keep and are audited on their standing, owner-chain authority confers write within the depth cap. Instances: 12, every one as expected; the passing ones run 9 s to 12 min at 1 worker (snapshot neutrality is the costly check: `MC_snapA` 72k states 9 min, `MC_snapB` 73k 12 min, `MC_until` 108k 10 min). Each README gives the instances with results, state counts and times; each AMBIGUITIES.md the findings (all resolved but residue 2 of issues/reseal-triggers.md, `MC_drop_refresh`; membership M1 issues/chain-write-forgery.md `MC_forge`, M2 sticky drops `MC_sticky`, `MC_sticky_snap`, M3 issues/ledger-same-ts-commits.md `MC_samets`; and minor readings S17, R10–R15, M4–M10).
- **CI decision**: TLC runs nightly, not per push. `models/tla/run.sh small|all [filter]` downloads the jar to `~/.cache/tla` if missing and runs the listed instances with 1 worker (`TLC_WORKERS` overrides); `small` (about two minutes) is `sync/deposit`, `sync/mostabs`, `rotation/MC_live`, `MC_grant`, `MC_plant`, `MC_safety`, `MC_fork`, `membership/MC_core`, `MC_multi` and the membership expected failures; `all` adds instances of up to about 20 minutes each, run by hand. Instances that reproduce an open finding (`MC_drop_refresh`, membership `MC_forge`, `MC_sticky`, `MC_sticky_snap`, `MC_samets`) and the negative control `MC_keepoff` are listed in the script as expected failures: the script exits non-zero on any other violation, on an expected failure that passes (update the docs), or on a TLC error.
- **Not covered by the TLA+ models**: several labels or servers, projections and certificate copies, retagging, parked bodies and unfetched pages, clock skew, range reconciliation details, token levels, `nobody`/`admin`/`author` purge rights, nodes offline past `MEMBERSHIP_KEEP`, connections and timeouts, signals; write and admin rotation, keysets and departures inside a member, demotion and `until` in rotation, step-tree branches, steps without rotation, entry boxes and key actors, `/label` and `/secrets` compaction (so snapshot-established epochs), the stale-view carrier race; in the membership model invites and links, read-level tenures, a second snapshot, more than two things. The models are oracles for the spec, never patched to match code; a spec change that touches their sections updates and re-runs them.
- **Method for a new TLA+ target**: a fresh agent given spec/ and the target's sections only (never notes/ or code) writes the model under `models/tla/<target>/` with a README (instances, results, times), an assumptions list and an ambiguity list (the findings); crypto is access (secrets as set membership, boxes as edges); 2–3 nodes, 1–2 servers, tiny bounds, symmetry sets; safety as invariants, liveness under weak fairness of honest steps; each property cites its spec sentence. Findings go to spec first, then the model is updated to the fixed text and rerun. TLA+ when notation independence matters and the state is small; stateright when an actor-shaped model would also serve the simulator.

## Conformance vectors (vectors/, crate osis-vectors)
- Format, subjects and rule IDs: vectors/README.md (language-neutral, ships with the spec). `.vec` text files of `key: value` records; every case has `case` and `spec`; `pending: <reason>` marks a case not yet checked.
- `vectors/MANIFEST` lists every required file (subjects: vectors/README.md): `pending` (crate not landed), `provisional`, or `frozen <blake3> <FC-n>`. A frozen file changes only with a `## FC-n` entry in vectors/FORMAT_CHANGES.md naming it (toplevel review) and `gen-vectors freeze <path> FC-n`; the `manifest_policy` test enforces this.
- Generated files (all but `cbor/` and `padme/`) must equal a fresh `cargo run -p osis-vectors --bin gen-vectors`; hand-written files have a checker that re-derives each case. Fixed inputs: seeds are 32 repeated bytes, randomness injected (each generator in `vectors/src/subjects/` states its inputs).
- State (MANIFEST is the source): frozen FC-1 (`cbor/values.vec` hand-written, `tid/thing.vec`, `chunker/gear.vec`, boundaries cross-checked by an independent Python implementation), FC-2 (`padme/padme.vec` hand-written, `tid/key-actor.vec`, `kdf/purposes.vec`, `box/boxes.vec`, `wrap/wrap.vec`, `token/tokens.vec`, `sign/signatures.vec`, `schema/core.vec`, `chunker/pages.vec`, `blob/prefix.vec`, `step/steps.vec`, `signal/signal.vec`, `bundle/bundle.vec`, `proto/reconcile.vec`, `proto/messages.vec`), FC-3 (`link/link.vec`), FC-4 (`chunker/tree.vec`, `keys/seed.vec`: tree-node lengths covered by the node hash). Provisional, waiting on owner calls (their token tables): `commit/headers.vec` (issues/token-admin-secret.md, issues/header-list-commitments.md), `blob/blobs.vec` (the commit blob case's chunk tokens), `inbox/inbox.vec`.

## Fuzzing (fuzz/, outside the workspace)
fuzz/README.md: running, the target table (live and planned per crate), crash handling. Seeds come from the vectors via `gen-vectors corpus` (osis-vectors `corpus.rs`). Nightly fuzzes every target `cargo fuzz list` prints for `fuzz_seconds` (default 300). Never commit `fuzz/artifacts/` or run output; corpora stay small.
- Hardening panic audit: all 15 targets 120 s each (`-max_total_time=120`, two at a time on a shared 4-core box), no crash, leak or timeout; 0.2–27 M executions per target (slowest `box` 0.2 M, `schema_dsl` 0.34 M, `bundle` 0.46 M). `delta` and `box` were added then; still planned: `join` (needs `arbitrary`) and `page`. `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets -- -D warnings` is clean, so the workspace deny of `unwrap_used`, `expect_used`, `panic` and `indexing_slicing` holds in every library crate (allowed only in tests, the cli binary crate, and two commented one-line exceptions: the chunker's u8 index into its 256-entry table, a whole-slice zeroize). Casts on peer numbers checked by hand: every `as usize`/`as u32` in decoders widens (frame lengths, u32 record fields, u8 table indices) or narrows a bounded local length; fuzzing runs with overflow checks on.

## Simulator (sim/, crate osis-sim)
- Shape: `Sim::new(name, seed, Config)` boots `nodes` real engines, each over its own `MemoryBackend` + `MemoryRoot` sharing one `osis_store::Faults` plan, a `ManualClock` and a seeded rng; all randomness comes from one `Dice` seeded by the run seed. `Config`: per-node `ClockSpec { skew_ms, drift_ppm }` (wall = T0 + elapsed + skew + elapsed·drift/10^6), `NetFaults { drop_pct, dup_pct, max_delay_ms, reorder }`, scheduler `Weights { write, gossip, deliver, tick, crash, restart }`, `max_tick_ms`, `check_every_step`.
- `step()` picks one event: a random write (1–3 random ops of `workload`, a schema with one aspect per encoding, on things in `sim.things` the node may write), gossip (node → random peer: packages of every applied commit the receiver lacks — an oracle standing in for reconciliation until node-sync's `SyncEngine`; each through drop/dup/delay), delivery of a due message (earliest, or any under reorder; to a down node it is lost), a tick (time passes, clocks set, `Engine::tick`), a crash (immediate, or at one of the node's next durable writes via `Faults::at(n, Crash)`: mid-write), a restart (`Peer::restart` over the same backend). `run(n)`, `run_until_quiescent(max_rounds)` (no new writes or crashes, every node up, everything delivered, pairwise sync without faults, time advanced 10 min at a time while commits pend). Setup helpers: `create_thing`, `grant`, `revoke`, `commit`, `deliver`, `sync_all`, `advance`, `set_clock`, `read`, `engine`.
- Invariants (`check`): per step, every group of up nodes holding the same applied commits reads the same values (every stored aspect); per applied event, own `ts` strictly increasing across restarts and within `MAX_DRIFT` (+1 ms) of the wall clock, no remote commit applied more than `MAX_DRIFT` ahead, no node-form counter range of one author overlapping another commit's on one aspect; at quiescence, every node holds the same commits and nothing pends. Engine errors on honest input (BadInput on an honest package, any error on a node not crashed) and panics (caught per run) are failures.
- Failures carry scenario, seed, step and the last 40 trace lines, and print `osis_sim::replay("<scenario>", <seed>)`, which reruns it exactly (a test checks a run twice gives the same `Report`, digest included). A failing sweep seed becomes a tier-1 test with a one-line description.
- Scenarios (`SCENARIOS`): `two_devices` (person actor owned by device 0, device 1 admin, a data thing the person owns), `three_random` (two shared things, all faults, skew ±5 min, drift ±200 ppm), `crash_restart` (plus crashes), `drift_parks` (a node jumps 2 h ahead: its commit pends with `Drift`, unread, until the receiver's clock is within `MAX_DRIFT`), `revoke_while_writing` (a writer removed while writing). Tier 1 runs seed 1 of two_devices, three_random and drift_parks, the others are tier 2; `OSIS_SIM_SEEDS=N [OSIS_SIM_ONLY=a,b] cargo test --release -p osis-sim sweep -- --ignored` is a small sweep (every unblocked scenario, or the named ones); the nightly one is `sim-sweep` (Phase C).

### API world (sim/src/world.rs)
`World`: `osis::Osis` handles on `Runtime::Manual` and osis-server `Hub`s over in-memory queues under one `ManualClock` (the pattern of crates/osis/tests/common/world.rs, which the osis crate keeps its own copy of): `add_server`, `cfg`/`person`, `run`, `advance`, `until(future, step, n)`, `set_online` (offline databases still tick), `traffic` (bytes per database) and `start_tally`/`tally` (upstream frames by message kind), `max_pump` (storm bound). The example apps' e2e tests use it (notes/apps.md).

### Phase B: sync engines and blind servers (sim/src/net)
- `NetSim::new(name, seed, NetConfig)`; `add_server` (osis-server `Hub` over `BackendStore<MemoryBackend>` with its own `Faults`, true time), `replicate(a, b)` (servers dial each other: `Hub::peer_up` on the dialer, `accept` with the dialer's static key on the other, for echo suppression), `add_node(servers, ClockSpec)` (`SyncEngine` over an `Engine` on `MemoryBackend`+`MemoryRoot` sharing a `Faults`, its own skewed `ManualClock`). Actions are carried engine to engine: `Dial` makes a link (or a `Down` if the server is down or the pair blocked), `Send` queues bytes on the stream, `Close` drops it, `Wake` is fired when the node's clock reaches it; hub outputs the same way. Connections are ordered byte streams: `LinkFaults { cut_pct, dup_pct, max_delay_ms, reorder }`: a lost message cuts its connection (both ends told), delays keep per-stream order, reorder picks any due stream head, duplication repeats a message (a protocol violation the receiver must survive).
- Scheduler (`NetWeights`): write (random ops on `groups[..].items` the node may write), deliver, tick (time, wakes, hub ticks when `next_due`, server redials), node crash (now or at a durable write) and restart over its store, server crash (now or mid-write) and restart, partition / heal of a (node, server) or (server, server) pair. Also `sever` (a pair never connects), `wipe_server` (data loss), `lose_disk` (node gone for good), `reconnect_all`. Setup is calm (no faults): flows helpers `person`, `pair` (card direct, `Engine::pair`, `take_direct` delivered, `paired`), `introduce` (cards direct), `group` (+ `create_label`), `grant`, `remove` (demotion), `item`, `carry` (`carry_out` + direct packages), `quiet(max)` (settle until idle and unchanged); `run(n)` turns faults on, `run_until_quiescent(max)` heals, restarts everything, settles a resync interval (61 s) at a time until idle and unchanged for two rounds, then checks.
- Quiescent invariants (`net/check.rs`): convergence by held deltas (projections: two nodes holding the same deltas of an aspect read it the same); nothing pends; member liveness (every device of a person on an admin's roster, or of the owner, reads the group) and agreement (they read the same items); per server and label: no live entry tombstoned or back after a tombstone, every body a writer judges must-hold (`SyncEngine::verdicts`, the pass's own judgment) in a live entry (a known blob of that commit with a superset of the parts; `known` maps blob IDs to commits from nodes' `entries`), no entry a node may delete as must-not-hold still live, deposited entries promoted by writers that applied them; purged bodies at no server and no honest node (`holds_blob`); state size (spec/requirements.md Scale): a node's stored bytes (its `MemoryBackend` dump) ≤ 2 × must + 2 KiB × entries + 16 KiB × labels + 64 KiB, where must and entries are the bytes and count of the live entries of its labels at the fullest honest server (never its own holdings, so junk or pending commits raise no bound; `net/check.rs` `STATE_*`; release sweep of seeds 0..3 of every scenario: nodes stored at most 2.11x must and 0.47 of the bound; each node's numbers are trace lines); no ping-pong (three more resync rounds add or delete nothing). A run that never settles reports the churn (what the rounds added/deleted, with commits and aspects); a settle of more than 5000 deliveries is a message storm. Phase A's per-event checks (HLC, counters) run on every node. `OSIS_SIM_TRACE=1` prints every trace line with the proto messages.
- Waivers (`NetSim::waivers`, constants in `net/mod.rs`) switch one invariant off for a scenario while its issue is open (none now); `excused` nodes (removed, lost) are left out of liveness, agreement and purge checks.
- Scenarios (`sim/tests/net.rs`; tier 1 keeps the quickest, see Phase C): `two_devices_server` (pairing, crashes mid-write), `three_persons_two_servers` (replication, grants by inbox, server crashes, partitions), `removed_writer` (removal, then a writer without the delete key purges the removed writer's body: deleted by token), `reader_relays` (writer severed from s1, a reader carries its stamped entries there; the readers' opens race the grants' registrations), `stranded_reseal` (a device off past `MEMBERSHIP_KEEP` across two rotations is refused, then re-paired), `server_loses_data` (writers re-register and re-upload), `offline_rotation` (a reader off across two removals walks the steps; removed members hold no later epoch), replay exactness. `compaction` (two writers compact while writing; covered bodies deleted, then a purge), `disk_loss` (a device recovered from the card writes and converges)., `grant_lost_on_crash` (a grant's inbox deposit survives the granter's crash), `large_edits` (two devices edit an 8 MiB blob in turn through a server; bytes sent per edit within 1.2x of the changed chunks plus the root node and 32 KiB; `NetNode::sent_bytes`).

### Phase C: scenario library, adversaries, version skew (sim/src/net/{library, adversary, skew, rogue}.rs)
- Library (setup by flows, a faulty `run` around the story's moment, the story's outcome, then the quiescent invariants; tests in `sim/tests/library.rs`): `revocation_bob`, `revocation_carol` (X and Y invalid at Alice and Carol; a re-grant restores both), `stolen_device` (`set_skew` winds the thief's clock back; a revoke backdated to the theft drops the forged edit, keeps the earlier one), `demotion_synced` / `demotion_unsynced` (the pre-sync keeps the late edit; `Unsynced { proceed }` loses it), `link_redeem` (two concurrent redemptions of a multi-use read link; the revoked link refuses a third), `link_race` (single use; the loser, rotated out of K, is excused), `move_import` (copy with history and its own label, re-grant of a writer that does not read the team, `/moved`, second move refused), `chat_join_leave` (personas per notes/chat.md; a removal rotates C), `folders` (a folder actor owning a subfolder shared separately), `label_replacement`, `label_replacement_offline` (the old label keeps membership-class commits and certificates only), `bundles` (a label exported and imported by hand to a node cut off from servers), `disappearing` (expiring notes under crashes: gone from nodes and servers), `keep_at_most` (replaced bodies gone after the bound), `concurrent_writers` (an admin, three writers and two readers granted at once, all online, time a second at a time: each body the admin wrote goes up once, by the admin; rarely a second upload by a lagging writer; `NetSim::count_uploads`). Story marks go in `/s/story` (`library::tags`, `add_tag`), which random writes leave alone (they may remove whole aspects).
- Adversaries: hand-driven `ClientSession`s straight into a `Hub` (`NetSim::rogue(s)`, `rogue_do(r, Command) -> events`, `rogue_raw`, `rogue_close`; `label_keys(n, s, g)`: a node's head keys, newest read secret and deposit key at the server's head). `evil_server` (`NetServer.evil = Evil { drop_pct, hold_pct, replay_pct }` on a server's outgoing messages, held ones flushed by `quiet`; `NetSim::dishonest` servers are skipped by the label checks), `evil_server_drops`, `removed_replayer` (stale step refused, purged blob not back, post-removal write never read; colluding with the server, its secrets open no body written after its author held the removal: revocation reaches a writer when it next syncs, spec/requirements.md Out of scope), `malformed_blobs`, `deposit_flood` (bounded by the deposit budget), `equivocating_writer` (a writer's key signs a second commit reusing its counters on one aspect, built outside its engine and uploaded stamped: every node, its own included, holds both and reads neither; `NetSim::equivocation` exempts that commit from the honest-counter check), `tombstone_liar` (after a purge the server re-announces the purged entries as they were, `NetSim::server_says` injecting server-to-node frames on open connections: no node takes the body back or re-uploads it; nodes answer with a delete again). Expected outcomes are in each doc comment. A stolen pairing offer is out of scope (app texts authenticated by the offer secret; osis crate tests).
- Version skew: `future_format` (a commit rewritten to `FORMAT_VERSION + 1`, stamped by its writer: parked everywhere (`NetSim::parked_ok` exempts it from the pending check), relayed, kept through an old writer's compaction), `newer_schema` (revision 2 adds a struct field with a default and an aspect; the old node's revision-1 write and compaction keep both).
- Blocked: `osis_sim::BLOCKED` maps scenarios to the issue that breaks them (empty after sync round 2); their tests are ignored with the issue's name and the sweeps skip them. A failing sweep seed of an unblocked scenario becomes an ignored test `<scenario>_seed_<n>` naming its issue.
- `sim-sweep` (release): `--seeds N --from S --only a,b --blocked --budget-secs T --jobs J --out DIR --list`; runs seed-major across scenarios on J threads until done or out of budget, prints seeds run and failures per scenario and each failing seed's `osis_sim::replay` call, writes `DIR/failing-seeds.txt`, exits 1 on any failure. Nightly: `--seeds 10000 --budget-secs 6000`. About 2 s per scenario seed per thread in release on a loaded 4-core machine (2 to 25 s in a debug build).
- Phase A's stories on the real engine: `two_devices_server`, `three_persons_two_servers` (crashes, partitions), `revocation_bob` and `removed_writer` cover `two_devices`, `three_random`, `crash_restart` and `revoke_while_writing`; `drift_parks` stays phase A (it needs hand delivery).
- Tier 1 runs seed 1 of the quickest (phase A: two_devices, three_random, drift_parks; phase B: two_devices_server and its seed 23, server_loses_data, grant_lost_on_crash, replay exactness; library: demotion_synced, stolen_device, keep_at_most, disappearing seed 104): about 6 s for the crate. Everything else is `#[ignore = "tier 2"]` (`cargo test --release -p osis-sim -- --ignored`; 3-16 s each in debug) and swept nightly.

## Size budgets and benches (crates/osis-bench)
- Budgets: `crates/osis-bench/tests/budgets.rs`, each `#[test] #[ignore] fn budget_<name>()` calling `assert_budget(name, actual, budget)` with the source number plus a margin (about 10%), citing its source. They use public APIs only (osis-bench is layer 9 and may depend on any crate).
- Live: `budget_creation_record`, `budget_minimal_commit` (commit blob plaintext of a header plus one register delta: 288 B against about 300, notes/crdt.md Commit floor), `budget_text_snapshot_ratio` (notes/crdt.md, Text size).
- Pending budgets: roster snapshot of N members, membership boxes about 1.2 KB per member and role (notes/crypto.md); revoke frontier after a long tenure (osis-perm, osis-commit).

## Spec-to-test map
`python3 tools/spec_map.py` writes notes/testing-map.md: per numbered spec section, the tests and live vector cases citing it; the rule checklist; then gaps and uncited tests. Regenerate at integration (it is not checked for freshness in CI, since concurrent branches would conflict on it); `--check` tests freshness, `--uncited` (CI) fails on a test in crates/, vectors/ or sim/ that cites nothing, `--gaps` (CI) fails on a rule with neither a test nor an issue, or a MUST sentence no rule anchors.
- **Rules**: every MUST / MUST NOT sentence of spec/*.md plus the refusal rules of spec/permissions.md and spec/sync.md ("cannot", "refused"), each a `RULES` entry (file, §, anchor, a substring of the sentence) in the script. A test or an issue covers a rule by citing `spec/<file>.md §N (anchor)`; a new MUST sentence fails `--gaps` until it gets an entry. `VACUOUS` lists rules nothing can test yet (no batch verification; format version 1 only). State: 46 rules (32 MUST), 43 tested, 3 vacuous.
- **Not covered by the map**: SHOULDs (listed in the map, not gated; re-seal triggers, pre-sync before a revoke, seed discard, clock offset estimation, request timeouts; some have tests by section), policy (budgets, rate limits, caps: osis-server tests cite them as `spec: none` or by section), declarative normative text with no MUST (covered only at section level: a section can be cited without each of its statements tested), spec/requirements.md (no numbered sections or MUSTs; its items are covered by the plans' reviews, not the map), and the out-of-scope list of spec/requirements.md.
- **Metadata tests** (crates/osis-node/tests/metadata.rs, real engines over an osis-server hub): stored bytes hold no value, map key, tid, AK, schema ID, node tid or signing key, or role secret (a label-ID control proves the search works); every stored blob's padded length is a Padmé length and 48 value lengths fall in at most 5 size classes (a 1-byte and a 100-byte value differ in class: Padmé leaks O(log log n) bits by design); two labels share no blob ID, salt or wrap; claim sets of 3 and 4 readers are both 4, of 5 readers 8, shuffled; heartbeats write only `/seen` on their node. ## Conventions for every crate
- **Spec citations**: put `// spec/<file>.md §N[.M]: <what>` right above each `#[test]` (inside `proptest!` too); a bare `§N` refers to the last file named in that comment. A test without its own citation inherits the file's `//!` header citations. Infrastructure tests with no spec section say `// spec: none (<why>)`.
- **Network in tests**: tier 1 uses `osis_transport::{Net, Sched}` (in-process, virtual steps, seeded faults and scheduling; no tokio); real-socket tests (QUIC on localhost) are tier 2.
- **Tiers**: tier-2 tests are `#[ignore]` (optionally also gated on `OSIS_SLOW=1`); a crate's tier 1 stays under about 10 s.
- **Vectors**: in the plan step that lands an encoding: write a generator `fn() -> VecFile` in `vectors/src/subjects/<subject>.rs` from fixed inputs (32 repeated bytes per seed), register it in `subjects::get`, add the crate as an osis-vectors dependency, flip the MANIFEST line to `provisional`, run `gen-vectors`, and add the file to `vector_files!` in vectors/tests/vectors.rs. Hand-written vectors get a checker instead. Rejections use rule IDs documented in vectors/README.md. Toplevel review freezes them (FC entry).
- **Fuzz**: a target per parser of peer or disk bytes (fuzz/README.md, "Adding a target"), seeds in `corpus.rs`; a crash becomes a regression test in the owning crate citing the spec section and `fuzz: <target>`.
- **Budgets and benches**: in osis-bench as above; a new external dependency for them needs its line in notes/architecture.md §5.
