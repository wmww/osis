# Delete keys
> Summary: How the engine lists and checks delete keys (spec/crypto.md §7; `sync_ids::steps`): a level-1 or level-3 key is a step key of a base's sync ID, listed from the newest step the base's `sync.0` records and checked per (commit, thing) against the steps placed from held valid `sync.0` state; a part listing a key no held step places is unjudgeable, never invalid; `sync.0` is a role part, so whoever opens a thing's role parts opens its steps; what re-judges what, why the verdict is per thing and takes any base up the record chain; a writer recording no step (a fire from outside) lists the step keys `keys.0` publishes, written with the step, so no rotation's key is listed before its step.

**Principle: a key is right iff a step places it, from what is held.** A level-1 key is the delete key and a level-3 key the write key of a step on a verified path from the genesis step of a sync ID that a thing up the delta's thing's record chain records, each step recorded by a valid `sync.0` write stamped before the commit. No secret is read and nothing is remembered: every node holding the same writes places the same keys.

## Listing (`CommitSecrets::key`, `Core::listed_step`, `published_keys`)
The delete base is `Core::delete_base` (its independent: the nearest independent thing up the record chain, the thing minted with a key at its top at the latest). Its step is the deepest placed step (ties by lowest ID) of the greatest sync ID it owns here (a step of it bears a sync key of a held sync secret; every placed one while no sync secret of the base's own is held). A commit making a thing independent lists its former base's keys: its own step is recorded by that commit, not before it. A base the commit mints with a key lists its genesis step (First steps, spec/crypto.md §7).
- **Recording none**: the base's step keys in its `keys.0` (field `step`), which every commit recording a step writes (`flows::sync_ids::put_step`): the same stamp as the step, so a later commit listing them is right. What a fire from outside lists (its sender holds `keys.0`, no `sync.0`), and a link's redeemer writing its join.
- **No held secret is read** for levels 1 and 3: a rotation's keyset is written before the step registering it (the step waits for the rotation to be logged; a writer's rotation of write waits for an admin), so a key a held secret derives may be one no step bears yet, and a commit listing it would be unjudgeable for good. Rejected: "before" reading a keyset's stamp for a key a keyset publishes: a key then counts from a write whose step may be recorded after the commit, so a commit's verdict could hang on a `sync.0` write whose validity hangs on that commit (two fixpoints, live and loaded differing).
- **No wait**: a valid role on a thing was judged by keys a held step up its chain places, and the commit giving a thing secrets of its own records its first step, so no setup was found where a writer holds a role and no step to list; `usable_role` waits for the setting alone, and a write that finds none fails `Permission` (`framing_for`).

## Placing (`Indexes::steps`, `steps::Recorded`)
Derived from the `sync.0` state at every write of it (`Tx::derive`; again at open), so from the held valid writes alone, a role compaction's snapshot included (a carried step entry keeps its recording write's stamp). Per step: its keys, depth, and `since`, the latest stamp on its path. A step verified once is not verified again (its ID commits to its bytes and parent). Not persisted.

## Checking (`Core::wrong_keys`, `Indexes::miskeyed`)
- **Per (commit, thing)**, not per commit: a node holding one thing's evidence cannot judge the keys a commit lists for another thing, so "invalid whole" would differ between nodes. A thing whose record chain is not known up to the thing minted with a key at its top is not judged (nothing on it applies meanwhile).
- **Any base up the chain**: derived-era content lists the former base's step, and so does a writer that has not received the commit making the thing independent; the nearest base alone would invalidate every write such a writer makes offline. A thing's own readers could tell only from what the writer knew, which no header says.
- **Any sync ID a `sync.0` records**: ownership turns on held secrets, a verdict must not.
- "Before": `since` < the commit's `ts`.
- Persisted as bit 7 of each part's byte in the commit's parts row. Judged again (`Core::rekey`) when the base's placed steps move (`steps_recorded`: a step added re-judges only the commits held as wrong under it; a step gone, every commit on a thing under the base), a record up a chain arrives (`timelines_changed`), or an account's held epochs move (`rejudge_once`). A verdict that moves names the commit stale and has a role commit's edge writes read again (`Indexes::rekeyed`).
- Debug builds compare the stored verdict with `wrong_keys` at every re-judge and at the end of each engine's history (`validity::misjudged_one`).

## What a verdict does (`Core::miskeyed`)
- **Not right is unjudgeable, never invalid** (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable; `apply::unjudgeable`): a node cannot tell a wrong key from a step whose `sync.0` write it does not hold (a thing may have been made independent by a commit not here yet), so a miskeyed part applies nowhere and is neither dropped nor forgotten as invalid.
  - **Received**: `apply::examine` takes a miskeyed part as any unjudgeable one (`Reason::Miskeyed`): it waits alone under Pending storage, and a commit whose every held part is miskeyed pends whole, its entry charged to its source's bounds on unvouched input, so made-up keys grow no store past them. It parks under `Wake::Steps` of each base up the thing's record chain (`waits::keyed_of`), woken when that base's placed steps move (`steps_recorded`). A node's own commit is not checked there: one listing a wrong key applies the part invalid and is refused.
  - **Held**: `Indexes::miskeyed` is the verdict of applied commits (a header applied with miskeyed parts waiting; a verdict flipped by a `sync.0` write falling): such a part reads invalid (`Judge::part`, `add_role_commit`: its edge writes read by nothing) and is judged again when the steps, a record or an account epoch arrive (`rekey`).
  - Judged invalid, a content header arriving before its thing's `sync.0` would be dropped and forgotten for good, and a reader would lack a must-hold body. A rule keyed on "a base known independent here with no genesis step" has the same hole: independence and the genesis step come in one commit.
- **The steps ride with the role parts** (`osis_keys::UNDER_ROLES`, spec/crypto.md §4 Roles secrets): `sync.0` is sealed in the roles section, so a node opening a thing's role parts in a shared blob opens the steps that judge them, whether or not it holds the thing (spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Sealed sections); on a thing outside the unit they ride as certificate copies by the derivation (`evidence::Reason::Step`). A link's redeemer reads K's role parts before K is in its node closure: with `sync.0` in the content section it would hold K's `keys.0`, `roles.0` and `secrets.0` and no step, and every key of K would read as wrong.
- A fire from outside lists the step keys of the inbox's `keys.0` its sender holds (spec/sync-inbox.md §2), recorded before the fire wherever the sender took them (`rotation::tests_stranded::a_fire_sent_between_a_rotation_and_its_step_is_taken_*`). The `step` field needs admin, as `sync.0` does (spec/permissions.md §3: a writer's `keys.0` delta touches only the keysets it rotates and box records; `validity::rotation_required` names the writer's fields, every other needs admin): a writer's junk step keys are invalid and change no fire (`a_writers_junk_step_keys_change_no_outside_fire_*`). Junk the sender cannot check is an admin's alone; the read key it seals to is in the same admin-written field (notes/sync-inbox.md, What a fire is sealed to).

## Links (spec/crypto.md §7 Page links)
- **Pages**: a page or link page names exactly its delta's listed keys (`osis_commit::verify`'s `Keyed`, every `verify_part`/`stream_part`) and its blob's prefix is the delta's framing: that link's salt, one group of commitments to its keys under the link's thing blind, the commit's expiry and class (`BlobReader::judging`, at `judge_part` and `verify_waiting`; re-reads of a judged part skip it). Failing is `PageError::Framing`: bad by itself. One group because a Delete needs a signature per group: the delta's keys split into groups would need every holder at once. Pages are framed by their delta alone, so honest ones always pass; whoever writes a delta writes its pages and links, so the check is of the writer.
- `tree::bears` (reuse, freed blobs, page judging) takes exactly one group; `Framing::keeps` also needs the reusing delta's class (an ephemeral blob is held in memory at stores).
- **Chunks are not checked against the delta**, by design: a delta names links it did not frame (a removal's hash-only link, a list edit's lent items, a large map key on its path, a snapshot's carried values written under earlier steps and other purge-bys, chunks reused under `Framing::keeps`), and a reader cannot tell named from introduced. Rejected: "levels 1 and 3 equal the delta's" for chunk links (wrong for every snapshot after a rotation and every lent item).
  - A chunk's prefix (keys, expiry, class) is its writer's to set as its bytes are, and holds only bytes of that writer's write. Framing it otherwise lets the keys' holder make that value read absent where the blob goes, as a link to a never-stored blob does; a writer may also pick a purge-by that lets it delete its own write. A new write reusing the chunk re-encrypts it unless it bears the new delta's framing (`Framing::keeps`). Verbatim carriers (snapshots, lent items) do not re-encrypt: a deletion there is repaired by a node whose own commit or snapshot names the blob (spec/sync-ids.md §1 Repair).
  - Left: honest purgers cannot delete such a chunk at stores (only the keys it bears can); and a demoted writer keeps deleting it through any level-1 key it chose, past the fix planned in issues/demoted-writer-deletes-at-stores.md.

## Tests
- Hand-built commits take their secrets from `Engine::hand_secrets(records)` (`HandSecrets`): each thing's dedup secret and the keys the engine asked lists (ask the thing's maker), made up where it lists none, at level 2, and whole with `all_made_up()` (a forger's keys). A hand-built commit is stamped after the write recording the step it lists.

## Signing a Delete
A holder tries every held epoch up the thing's record chain (`Core::delete_chain`, `delete_secret`) and signs with the one whose step key the header lists (`DeleteSecret::at_level`).
