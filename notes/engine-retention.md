## Principle (spec/commits.md §5 Bodies and deaths)
A body drops in one of four ways: as covered once past its keep bound (live bodies included), by expiry, by purge, or as invalid. A node without write drops only expired and invalid content and what writers' covers cover. Outside the four: a thing's bodies go whole when the node loses read access to it (spec/permissions.md §3, `retention/forget.rs`), and memory-class bodies vanish when superseded (notes/ephemeral.md Superseded expiring writes) or at a delete here (issues/eph-parity.md).
- The thing's setting only limits when a covered drop may happen, at every node alike; it never decides who may drop a body (purgeable-by, per write) nor deletes anything promptly.
- Out of history any held valid writer's cover drops a body, the purge right or not (Tess's decision 4, notes/engine-retention-history.md): nothing promised to keep it. With no history promised the trigger compacts live and replaced bodies alike.
- Purge on replace is a duty to write the cover; covering is the join and nothing else (notes/engine-retention-covers.md).
- The fixed bounds are keep-at-least, so they too only delay a covered drop: `ROLE_KEEP` after replaced for role-class bodies, a purge request's floor on its commit's blobs, `created.0` and `retention^.0` never out of history.
- A purge never drops a snapshot: a snapshot has no values of its own, so no request names one (`purge::names`), and its copies of purged values go as any carrying body's (held as its leftover). So a cover falls only by turning invalid (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Limit).
- Expiry and purge hold a body as its value-less leftover, which leaves only as covered (past T) or with its thing. A waiting part keeps its body until it applies (no cover can judge it); only expiry and purge take it earlier.
- An invalid body goes at the end of the transaction that judged it: every read is a function of the valid writes held (a sequence's order included, spec/crdt.md §6.1), so the drop changes no read.

Timers: expiry; one per (thing, AK) at the next body leaving history; `T_KEPT` (a part kept to its commit's purge floor by a purge request's header, a purged fire's tombstone); `T_FLOOR` (a covered purge-on-replace body of a commit carrying a request waits for its floor, spec/commits.md §5 Purge). There is no keep-at-most bound: it needed death dating, a live-write judgment per body and a carve-out per kind of body (snapshots dropped live, bodies never received after a flip); the cover rule needs none of them.

## Bodies
- Each part of an applied commit is in one state (`Meta::hold`, `engine::Hold`): its body (`whole`: an own whole replace), its leftover (`whole`, `valued`), dropped (with what it keeps), or missing (plain, `Forgot`, `ToFetch`). A part held again as its leftover is a whole replace by its leftover, as a load judges it. Stored: dropped and leftover parts in the record head; missing, whole and forgot as meta-row bits; `ToFetch` is memory only.
- A body is the commit's one blob (notes/whole-commits.md), but listing and retention judge each part alone: its liveness (`delta_live`), its keep bound (`part_bounds`, `Engine::part_keeps`), dated by its own writes and kept by its own aspect's timeline. So a part drops on its own and the blob is rewritten without it. A setting governs its aspect's data and nothing else (Fathom). Rejected: a keep bound per retention class (the latest of its parts'): a kept part kept its commit's replaced purge-on-replace part past its cover (setting.rs `purge_on_replace_part_goes_whatever_its_commit_keeps_*`, `a_part_keeps_by_its_own_aspect_*`).
- Expiry is per commit; purge drops single deltas. node-sync deletes a blob at hosts only when no part it holds must be carried (`must_carry`, the latest `hosts_from`).
- "Held" is judged by the stored blob holding the part (`apply::stores_part`), not meta alone: during a join the commit is indexed before it is stored. A part still to arrive is not held (`retention::held_part`): not judged, not dropped.
- `drop_part` keeps a dropped part's rotation role, overreach, keysets and shown possession (`Dropped::role`, `overreach`, `keysets`, `shown`), so re-judging and rebuilds need no body; `reopen` forgets them. A bare leftover is never dropped (it stays for un-merge); one holding values (`Hold::Leftover::valued`) drops as a whole body.
- An index entry derived from a dropped body goes with it (`apply::forget_snapshot` for `Core::snapshots`), since a rebuild cannot read it (order seed 1291).

## Invalid bodies (`retention::drop_invalid`; spec/permissions.md §6)
- `Core::drop_due` collects every commit with a part held and invalid. At the end of `Engine::run`, after `answer_removals` (this node's addenda vouch first) and before `settle_none`, each such part goes (`DropFor::Invalid`) with no rebuild (it gave nothing; one whose drop uncovers parts rebuilds). Memory parts go alike; their commit stays known (notes/ephemeral.md What follows from memory).
- Kept (`goes_invalid`): role commits whole (the timeline and possible holders read every held edge write; keeping only the `roles.0` part split a commit a label holds whole, sim `link_race`), and this node's own commits (`OwnWriteInvalid`: its user may write it again, `osis chat resend`; it stays must-hold so a re-grant finds it).
- A `retention^.0` part's drop takes it out of `ix.retention` and judges its thing's history again. An invalid addendum's drop marks nothing stale. An invalid snapshot dropped stands in for nothing (`stands_in` only for `DropFor::Other`).
- While invalid a copy arriving again is refused (`takes_late`), so a relay costs nothing. Valid again, `valid_again` makes it missing and flags the aspect `Why::Uncovered`; node-sync fetches it where present (`reopen_undropped`, which also forgets bytes still held when the drop and the rise fell in one step).
- No right deletes an invalid body at hosts: node-sync leaves alone any blob holding a part it dropped as invalid (`Engine::left_at_hosts`), unless a right dropped another part of it (purge, expiry, cover), which wins (else a purged sibling stayed at the host, `purge_beside_invalid_delta_leaves_host_*`). Such a part frees no link (`retention::freed`). So hosts keep what was uploaded while valid. Never-valid junk at hosts goes only by expiry, purge or the thing leaving the label (issues/invalid-junk-at-hosts.md). A body no holder has stays gone at every node alike (`regrant_with_no_holder_reads_alike_*`).
- Cost (rare path, brute force): a part invalid on arrival is stored then dropped; one arriving before what makes it valid is fetched twice.
- Tests: retention.rs `invalid_write_drops_regrant_fetches_it_*`, `revoked_writers_late_edit_goes_*`; setting.rs `writers_setting_keeps_nothing_*`; held-model `Fate::MustDrop(DropWhy::Invalid)`.

## Bodies coming back
- **Never received** (spec/commits.md §5 Keep bounds): a body dropped other than as covered counts as never received. `retention::takes_late` admits its late copy (not a memory part, a value-less one only without a residue); `reopen` clears its `Dropped`, the copy joins and `on_late` judges it again. Nothing fetches it on its own; a holder that judges it must-hold re-uploads it.
- **Uncovered** (spec/permissions.md §5.2; notes/engine-validity.md Uncovering): a body dropped as covered whose covers no longer carry it is missing again and fetched where present. Compaction deleted it at hosts, so often only readers hold it, who upload nothing: its live writes are lost (the stated limit).
- **A snapshot falling** is reported once (`Engine::take_fallen`): node-sync's `relist_fallen` lifts its own tombstones of the entries of the commits dropped under it and relists memory entries (`stream_relist`), so a copy a host still has comes back (`compaction::fallen_own_snapshot_*`). A durable commit forgotten under it comes back only as a holder's must-hold upload.
- **A dropped snapshot's liveness**: a snapshot not held is live while the state holds a write stamped at or before its `ts`, or one without a `ts` (a sequence record whose creation is gone): an over-approximation, so its delete at hosts waits for a carrier (`snapshot_live`; `deletion::dropped_snapshot_waits_for_its_carrier_spec_sync_7`).
- Liveness for host deletes (`Engine::delta_live`; a body with a live write leaves a label only with a carrier, spec/sync.md §7 Carriers) is per delta on the current state, cached until its aspect's state changes (`Core::forget_live`; a tombstone holds no write of its creator, `Fragment::holds_owned_write`, order seed 64197). A deleted text element's creation stays as its anchor, so a fully erased text body is live too.
