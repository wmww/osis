## Principle (spec/commits.md §5 Bodies and deaths)
A body drops in one of four ways: as covered once past its keep bound (live bodies included), by expiry, by purge, or as invalid. A node without write drops only expired and invalid content and what writers' covers cover. Outside the four: a thing's bodies go whole when the node loses read access to it (spec/permissions.md §3, `retention/forget.rs`), and a thing outside the node closure nothing held needs goes whole with its record and secrets (`retention/needed.rs`, notes/certificates.md Kept while needed), and memory-class bodies vanish when superseded (notes/ephemeral.md Superseded expiring writes) or at a delete here (issues/eph-parity.md).
- The thing's setting only limits when a covered drop may happen, at every node alike; it never decides who may drop a body (purge-by, per write) nor deletes anything promptly.
- Out of history any held valid writer's cover drops a body, the purge right or not (Tess's decision 4, notes/engine-retention-history.md): nothing promised to keep it. With no history promised the trigger compacts live and replaced bodies alike.
- Purge on replace is a duty to write the cover; covering is the join and nothing else (notes/engine-retention-covers.md).
- The fixed bounds are keep-at-least, so they too only delay a covered drop: `ROLE_KEEP` after replaced for role-class bodies, a purge request's floor on its commit's blobs, `created.0` never out of history.
- A covered drop (`Engine::drop_body`) judges again what a keyset rebuild marked, as `drop_invalid` does: nothing flips, but the marks must not outlive the transaction.
- A purge never drops a snapshot: a snapshot has no values of its own, so no request names one (`purge::names`), and its copies of purged values go as any carrying body's (held as its leftover). So a cover falls only by turning invalid (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
- Expiry and purge hold a body as its value-less leftover, which leaves only as covered (past T) or with its thing. A waiting part keeps its body until it applies (no cover can judge it); only expiry and purge take it earlier.
- An invalid body goes at the end of the transaction that judged it: every read is a function of the valid writes held (a sequence's order included, spec/crdt.md §6.1), so the drop changes no read.

Timers: expiry; one per (thing, AK) at the next body leaving history; `T_KEPT` (a part kept to its commit's purge floor by a purge request's header, a purged fire's tombstone); `T_FLOOR` (a covered purge-on-replace body of a commit carrying a request waits for its floor, spec/commits.md §5 Purge). There is no keep-at-most bound: it needed death dating, a live-write judgment per body and a carve-out per kind of body (snapshots dropped live, bodies never received after a flip); the cover rule needs none of them.

## Decided (Tess) and definitions the code follows
- **Fallback for purge on replace**, if compaction on replace causes problems: replaced writes become `writer` purgeable and the writer attaches a purge request for them to each edit, generated automatically. Costs: a purge cannot express a partial replacement of a body, a request is kept to its floor (`ROLE_KEEP`), purged writes leave leftovers. Acceptable as a fallback.
- **Small values**: per-commit overhead matters less than extra mechanisms; no special commit forms for small durable writes (a whole replacement covering the old body directly only if cheap).
- **Replaced** is "the body holds a write the canonical state does not hold" (what a snapshot from the state leaves out), not "has a dead write" (spec/crdt.md §4.8): a root reset is dead once a later write implies one, yet canonical form keeps it, so by deaths every cover of such an aspect is replaced for good and covers chase each other.
- **Covered-equal** snapshots at one `ts`: only the lower commit ID covers (spec/sync-compaction.md §1), else two covers drop each other.
- A local drop as covered needs no right of the dropping node (the snapshot's commit holds it).

## Bodies
- Each part of an applied commit is in one state (`Meta::hold`, `engine::Hold`): its body (`whole`: an own whole replace), its leftover (`whole`, `valued`), dropped (with what it keeps), or missing (plain, `Forgot`, `ToFetch`). A part held again as its leftover is a whole replace by its leftover, as a load judges it. Stored: dropped and leftover parts in the record head; missing, whole and forgot as meta-row bits; `ToFetch` is memory only.
- A body is the commit's one blob (notes/whole-commits.md), but listing and retention judge each part alone: its liveness (`delta_live`), its keep bound (`part_bounds`, `Engine::part_keeps`), dated by its own writes and kept by its own aspect's entry. So a part drops on its own and the blob is rewritten without it. A setting governs its aspect's data and nothing else (Fathom). Rejected: a keep bound per purge-by class (the latest of its parts'): a kept part kept its commit's replaced purge-on-replace part past its cover (setting.rs `purge_on_replace_part_goes_whatever_its_commit_keeps_*`, `a_part_keeps_by_its_own_aspect_*`).
- Expiry is per commit; purge drops single deltas. A blob holding a part that strips (a purge's leftover, past its bounds, destroyed, or dropped as covered under purge on replace by a snapshot reaching it by its tables) is must-not-hold wherever it is logged and is rewritten without it (`Engine::blob_gone`, notes/whole-commits.md Strip).
- "Held" is judged by the stored blob holding the part (`apply::stores_part`), not meta alone: during a join the commit is indexed before it is stored. A part still to arrive is not held (`retention::held_part`): not judged, not dropped.
- `drop_part` keeps a dropped part's rotation role, overreach and keysets (`Dropped::role`, `overreach`, `keysets`), so re-judging and rebuilds need no body; `reopen` forgets them. A bare leftover is never dropped (it stays for un-merge); one holding values (`Hold::Leftover::valued`) drops as a whole body.
- An index entry derived from a dropped body goes with it (`apply::forget_snapshot` for `Core::snapshots`), since a rebuild cannot read it (order seed 1291).

## Invalid bodies (`retention::drop_invalid`; spec/permissions.md §6)
- `Core::drop_due` collects every commit with a part held or missing and invalid. At the end of `Engine::run`, after `addenda::write_owed` (this node's addenda vouch first) and before `settle_none`, each such part goes (`DropFor::Invalid`) with no rebuild (it gave nothing; one whose drop uncovers parts rebuilds). Memory parts go alike; their commit stays known (notes/ephemeral.md What follows from memory).
- Kept (`goes_invalid`): role commits whole (the timeline and possible holders read every held edge write; keeping only the `roles.0` part split a commit a sync ID holds whole), and this node's own commits (`OwnWriteInvalid`: its user may write it again, `osis chat resend`; sync logs it no more while it stays invalid and logs it again once valid, so a re-grant finds it at its author). Judging at open knows the account (`Engine::load` sets it before `reload`), else a restart dropped the own invalid parts.
- A `retention^.0` part's drop takes it out of `ix.retention` and judges its thing's history again. An invalid addendum's drop marks nothing stale. An invalid snapshot dropped stands in for nothing (`stands_in` only for `DropFor::Other`).
- While invalid and known a copy arriving again is refused (`takes_late`), so a relay costs nothing. Valid again, `valid_again` makes it missing and flags the aspect `Why::Uncovered`; sync fetches it where present (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies).
- Forgetting (`Engine::forgettable`): a commit holding no part, each dropped as invalid or covered, one at least invalid, is forgettable, either class; one carrying a purge request only where `purge::drops_nothing` (the widest class over every thing the author could write that could write the item's thing gives no right) and it is not lasting (a drop as invalid makes nothing lasting). Sync's `BodyFacts::invalid` takes the same test: a re-grant can give a request force with its parts still invalid, and its author then logs it. Sync forgets it once no entry is known: every node unlogs its own entries of a body judged invalid (notes/sync-read.md Acts), so the header lasts only while a source shows one. Valid again after that, it comes back as any commit never received: from its author, which keeps it and logs it once must-hold, or any node holding it valid (an addendum's writer).
- Last holder: honest nodes drop a body only where they judge it invalid; the author's account keeps it on every node of it; a node judging it valid (it lacks the removal, or holds an addendum vouching for it) keeps logging it. So a re-grant finds it wherever any of those still holds it, a store's copy included; with none it is gone at every node alike (spec/permissions.md §6).
- No right deletes an invalid body at other nodes: a part dropped as invalid makes no blob must-not-hold (`Engine::blob_gone`), unless a right dropped another part of it for good (purge, past bounds), which wins (else a purged sibling stayed). Such a part frees no link (`retention::freed`). So stores keep what was logged while valid. Never-valid junk goes only by expiry, purge or the thing leaving the sync ID (issues/invalid-junk-kept.md). A body no holder has stays gone at every node alike.
- Judged invalid, held or missing alike, a part is neither (`drops_invalid`, spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable): `drop_invalid` drops a held one with its body and a missing one (never received, or reopened by `valid_again`) as invalid with none, whatever its history; else `needed::needs` kept a missing part's commit's other things where a node given the same in another order released them (disputes.rs `a_part_missing_again_that_turns_invalid_drops_as_invalid`). A dropped part made to count as never received (`reopen_to_fetch`: its covers fell, its purge fell, its thing's record came back) while judged invalid is dropped as invalid in place and judged again (valid: `valid_again`). A missing part is put on `drop_due` wherever its flag reads invalid (`index_validity`, `put_meta`, and each re-judge leaving it invalid: a record come makes it judgeable with no flip).
- Judged invalid is `!Meta::valid` and judgeable (`apply::unjudgeable`: the author's and acting thing's records held, its split path opened, `thing_wait` satisfied, the rule `examine` reads of a held part). `Meta::valid` reads false for both, so `!valid` alone does not mean judged invalid: a part never received whose thing's record, chain or path key is not held stays missing and its copy is taken (order.rs `chainless_part_creation_missing_spec_commits_4`, validity.rs split-commit `_spec_wire_4` tests). Rejected: telling a reopened part from one never received (a history carve-out), and filtering missing parts in `needs` (one reader fixed, `Hold` still a function of arrival order for `forgettable`, a release's `keeps`, fetching).
- Cost (rare path, brute force): a part invalid on arrival is stored then dropped; one arriving before what makes it valid is fetched twice.
- Tests: setting.rs `writers_setting_keeps_nothing_*`; held-model `Fate::MustDrop(DropWhy::Invalid)`.

## Bodies coming back
- **Never received** (spec/commits.md §5 Keep bounds): a body dropped other than as covered counts as never received. `retention::takes_late` admits its late copy (not a memory part, a value-less one only without a residue); `reopen` clears its `Dropped`, the copy joins and `on_late` judges it again. Nothing fetches it on its own; a holder that judges it must-hold logs it again.
- **Uncovered** (spec/permissions.md §5.2; notes/engine-validity.md): a body dropped as covered whose covers no longer carry it is missing again and fetched where present. Compaction dropped it everywhere a cover carried it, so often nobody serves it: its live writes are lost.
- **A snapshot falling** moves the aspect's fall mark (the facade's `sync/falls.rs`), and the bodies dropped under it count missing again, fetched where a source still shows them (spec/sync-judgement.md §4 Logged again once). A durable commit forgotten under it comes back only as a holder's must-hold logging.
- **A dropped snapshot's liveness**: a snapshot not held is live while the state holds a write stamped at or before its `ts`, or one without a `ts` (an element identity whose record carries none): an over-approximation, so its delete waits for a carrier (`snapshot_live`).
- Liveness for deletes (`Engine::delta_live`; a body with a live write leaves a sync ID only with a carrier, spec/sync-compaction.md §1 carriers) is per delta on the current state, a memo (`Core::live_bodies`; a tombstone holds no write of its creator, `Fragment::holds_owned_write`, order seed 64197). A deleted text element's creation stays as its anchor, so a fully erased text body is live too.
