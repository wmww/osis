# Standard data: notes, contacts, events
> Summary: The first-party schemas over the core (`schema/*.osis`, scenario tests in the osis crate's test binary), decided with the owner: a note is `/name` plus `/body`; contacts and profiles share one aspect set with the contact overriding the linked profile; events use wall-clock times with a zone and civil days (`localtime`, `date`), start and end decoupled, a bounded recurrence subset; what was left out of vCard and iCalendar and why; the scenario tests. Tags: notes/tags.md.

## Direction (owner)
- No example apps. First-party data that is not core lives in `schema/`: one `.osis` per schema; apps depend on `osis` and take the schema files from there, generating typed keys with `osis_schema::generate_file` in a build script. The scenario tests (notes, tags, contact, event) are modules of core/osis's `it` binary over osis-sim's `World`, their typed keys generated by core/osis's build.rs; the gate checks every schema file (`osis schema check`).
- Notes are markdown and cover every simple text document: a note is `/name` plus `/body` (core), no notes namespace. `/body` is the one freeform markdown aspect, also the notes on an event or a contact; apps show its first line where a description would go.
- Chat is thrown out until a good schema is designed (post-prototype, pre-1.0). notes/chat.md keeps the old pattern and its measured costs.
- Be very careful with dates and times: milliseconds since the epoch are right for many things and wrong for the rest, and the rest gets complex fast.
- Events are shared with read or write like anything else; no attendee or RSVP machinery now (link people in `/body`, or an app-level aspect later).
- Avoid API convenience functions that only save calling existing ones; the one addition here is `Osis::profile()` and `profile_of(person)`, because the profile thing is the API's own creation.

## Time (spec/data-model.md §3)
Four kinds, three types:
- **Instants**, `timestamp`: when something happened. Milliseconds since the epoch, UTC.
- **Civil days**, `date`: a birthday, a holiday, an all-day event. Days since 1970-01-01, proleptic Gregorian. Never a timestamp: "March 3" starts at a different instant in every zone, and storing midnight UTC shifts all-day events when the viewer travels.
- **Wall-clock times**, `localtime` with an IANA zone name (or a fixed `+HH:MM` offset for imports): how people mean calendar events. Resolved to an instant at read time under the reader's current zone rules, so a rule change after the write moves the instant and keeps the wall time, and a weekly 9:00 stays 9:00 across daylight-saving changes. Gap: resolve forward by the gap. Overlap: the earlier instant. UTC is zone `UTC`; a missing zone is a floating time (7:00 wherever I am), allowed explicitly.
- **Durations**, `duration`: exact milliseconds. Calendar intervals ("every month") are recurrence, not durations.
- Dates and local times are **scalars**, not structs: a struct of year, month, day, hour merges field by field, so two people moving one meeting concurrently could get a day from one and an hour from the other. A scalar is a register and merges whole. Conversion to civil fields is a dozen lines (tests/it/event.rs `date`).
- Partial dates (a birthday without a year) are a contact detail (`annual`), not a core type.

## Contact (schemas/contact.osis)
- Many aspects, so a thing with only emails is a contact; display name `/name`, notes `/body`, groups are tags.
- A **profile** is the same aspects on the person's profile thing (`Osis::profile()`: the contact card's target, a group of itself under its own label), written by the person; everyone holding the card reads it and keeps receiving changes (the import's bearer follow stays). `Osis::profile_of(person)` gives the profile a card import brought.
- A contact of mine that is an osis user carries `/contact/profile`; my aspects override the profile's values field by field (tests/it/contact.rs `view`). A contact without it is just my data.
- Entries are keyed maps (`emails`, `phones`, `urls` by value; `addresses`, `dates` by label), not lists: concurrent additions merge and one entry's label is editable alone. `labeled { label, pref }` marks the preferred one of a kind.
- Left out of vCard on purpose: photo (a file type later), gender, language, timezone, geo, keys, sound, logo, categories (tags), and UID and revision bookkeeping, which belongs to the importing app.

## Event (schemas/event.osis)
- `/event/start`: `at(wall)` or `day(date)`; absent means not scheduled yet. `/event/end`: `at(wall)` with its own zone (a flight lands in another zone), `day(date)` inclusive (iCalendar's exclusive date end is the commonest import bug; the import converts), or `after(duration)` from the start, so moving a start moves the end. Absent: a moment, or one day. An end whose kind does not match the start, or precedes it, is an app-level validation error (start and end are separate aspects and merge independently).
- The enum splits by kind of time because the kind changes the payload and the reading; everything else is a field. UTC is a zone, floating is no zone, undated is no aspect. Coarser kinds ("sometime in June") would be new cases, additive, when an app needs them.
- `/event/repeat`: daily to yearly, interval, weekdays with ordinal, month days, until or count, skipped occurrences. Occurrences are identified by nominal start (`occurrence`), so a zone rule change never orphans an exception. A modified occurrence is its own event with `/event/overrides`. `/event/alerts`: durations before the start. A calendar is a tag (color on `/tag`).
- Left out of iCalendar: attendees and RSVP, sub-daily frequencies, BYSETPOS and the rarer BY rules, RDATE, attachments, priority, transparency, sequence numbers, todos and journals.

## Tests (core/osis's `it` binary in `cargo test --workspace`; tier 2 `--ignored`)
- core/osis/tests/it/notes.rs: two devices, offline edits merging, undo across devices; a notebook (a tag owning its notes) shared and unshared, a private tag on a shared note staying private; a large body streamed; local undo, redo and history.
- core/osis/tests/it/tags.rs: notes/tags.md end to end (per-item share through one roster, sub-tags chaining rosters, only what you may share, tag-owned cards with demotion and invites, ownership transfer as a copy).
- core/osis/tests/it/contact.rs: profile filled, read through the card and updated; a linked contact overriding it; a contact without a profile.
- core/osis/tests/it/event.rs: the civil-date conversion; a timed series with a skip and an override, an all-day span, a flight across zones, all in a read-only shared calendar.
- Tier-1 time (idle 4 cores, debug): about 10 s; persons and introductions cost about 1 s each, so the tags cases over 10 s are `#[ignore = "tier 2"]`.
