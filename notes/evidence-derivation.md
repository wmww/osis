# Evidence derivation
> Summary: Design and build state of A2 briefs 2 and 2b and A3 brief 3 (notes/a2-spec/README.md, notes/a3-spec/README.md, Order of the code briefs): `osis_engine::evidence`, the set of certificate copies a sync ID requires, derived from a node's held commits and the sync ID's unit as notes/a2-spec/permissions.md §4 says (OPEN 10's proposal (iv)), built unused beside `sync/certified.rs`; inputs, output, what re-derives what and its cost; keys-open travel as an indexed graph over chained grants and record edges; carried demotions as the far route of a removal (design, not built); a lowering's list leaves as evidence (which (lowering, thing) leaves a sync ID requires: read only where the lowering cuts, far leaves at home; addenda leaving), which leaves judge a held part (a reader lacking one waits), indexed with its costs counted; the differential against held-model `Stage::A2` with and without `Rule::listed` (0 disagreements at every size), mutations, counts, the draft sentences fixed, the walk-level check (a reader holding bodies plus the required copies judges as the writer under osis-perm `paths`), what is left (the engine-level check and finding 9's engine test: the engine does not run the walk yet; admin-only content's cut), what the switch (A3 brief 5b) must know.

**Principle: a sync ID's evidence is a function of the commits the node holds and the sync ID's unit: the least set the draft's rules close over them. Nothing is placed once and remembered; a change re-derives exactly what it can move, and the incremental answer is the from-scratch answer by construction (same closure step, same seeds).**

## Input
Plain facts per held **copy** (commit ID, thing), held whole (a commit's edge writes on a thing are one `roles.0` delta: spec/crdt.md §2, one delta per tid and aspect; a node not holding it holds no copy of it): the commit's parts on that thing (`evidence::Part`), as the derivation's own structs (independent of header types, as osis-perm's walk input is):
- `pos`: the part's place in key order as a verdict reads it (draft §5.1 key for an edge-class part, the start of its `ts` for content: spec/permissions.md §5.2 Position); "ordered before" is `key(j) < pos(m)`.
- `hops`: the edges of the path the commit states for that thing, each marked record or not and with what the part **needs** there (`Need`: its least need, spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 3: a role, write for content (admin for an admin-only aspect), admin for an edge write, write for a finalize, a may-grant grant's role; on a may-grant grant's last edge its role as may-grant: `Need::MayGrant`); `None` while the path is not known here (a split entry waiting to open: notes/path-form.md). A part with no known path brings nothing (finding 8); the entry opening is an update (`open_path`).
- the kind: content; an edge write (holder, whether it is a **grant** (sets a role above none: no self-removal, no finalize) and what it **gives** (`Gives`: its role and the role it lets grant, `until` disregarded), a **removal** (gives below write at its `ts`, no finalize: what may cut, Leaves), whether it **gives write** or above (what a removal takes away), the remaining admin it names, the **creation** of the entry it resets as the reset names it (spec/permissions.md §2 Edge writes)).
- `chained` per edge-class part: whether it is a chained write (draft §4 Chained writes). An input, never computed here: the walk owns the rule (osis-perm `paths`' chained pass; held-model `World::chained` in the differential). It only grows with arrivals and may fall on a forget; the caller reports each flip (`set_chained`).
- Per sync ID: its actor (the holder whose grants travel under it) and its unit's things (`join`, `leave`).
- **Record edges** (`add_record`): each (mint admin, thing it minted), from the creation records the node holds; standing for good (records are identity: a thing's record is never taken back while the thing is held). An edge of the read graph (admin, so read) and what a removal on it takes write away from.
- `Config::keys_open`: Role changes travel's keys-open half on or off (off: writes naming the actor alone). On at the switch (finding 19) and at A3 (notes/evidence-model-findings.md 31: read access through a holder thing reads it): `Stage::A2` has it on.
- (A3) `Config::leaves`: list leaves as evidence and addenda leaving. Per edge write `leaves`: the things its list root holds a leaf pair for (notes/a3-spec/wire.md: the root shows which things have a leaf, not what a leaf names). An addendum part (`Kind::Addendum`): its removal's edge (read from the removal, held or not: the model reads it from the world) and the copies it lists.
- Per edge write: `removal` (the shape: gives below write at its own `ts`, no finalize: a self-removal and a grant of read have it, a demotion from admin to write has not; whether it cuts the derivation decides, Leaves), `gives_write` (write or above, any `until`), `gives_read` (read or above, any `until`, no self-removal). From the header and the `roles.0` entry; no verdict.

## Output
(A3) `leaves(sid)`: the (lowering commit ID, thing) leaves S requires, each with why (`LeafWhy::Home`, `Reads`); `reading(c)`: the leaves judging held copy `c`, `waits(c, has)` the one it lacks (it waits); `judged_by(r, t)`: what lowering copy `r`'s leaf for `t` judges among the held copies (what a node receiving it can judge now).

`required(sid)`: every copy on a thing outside the unit in S's evidence, with one **reason** each (`Reason`: a taken write on an edge the set uses, the creation a member's reset names, a write naming a holder whose role changes travel under S). Bodies (held copies on unit things) are S's sync unit blobs, not output. `from_scratch(sid)` the same from nothing. A copy is a whole (commit, thing): its edge-class parts on that thing travel together (finding 9's "apply together"), never a part alone and never keyed by commit alone (a role commit on two things is two copies).

## The rule (draft §4 with OPEN 10 (iv))
S's evidence is the least set of copies holding:
- **seeds**: the bodies (held copies on unit things); each held copy with a chained edge write naming a holder in **open(S)** (Role changes travel: S's actor, and with `keys_open` what it opens, below).
- **closure**, for each member m (body or copy):
  - each part of m whose path is **joined** (each non-record edge holds a held chained grant giving what the part needs there, ordered before the part: a hop granted too little joins nothing, README OPEN 16, finding 23) brings its path's edges; an edge write brings its named remaining admin edge (joined or not);
  - an edge brings every held copy with a chained edge write on it;
  - an edge write brings the creation of the entry it resets, held;
A copy on a unit thing reached by the closure is a body already.

## Keys-open travel
Reachability over the **read graph**: an edge (holder → target) per held chained part giving read or above, no self-removal, and every record edge, multi-edges kept as their parts. "At any time": an edge stays while a part setting it is held and chained, whatever later writes on it say; a **record edge** (mint admin → the thing it minted) stands for good (spec/permissions.md §1 Record edge; spec/crypto.md §4 Key access: today's keys open through it, README OPEN 17). The graph only grows with arrivals; a forget or a chained flip to false shrinks it. **open(S)** is S's actor and every thing reachable from it (S's thing holds a role on it, so its keys open it; held-model `World::opened`, `Rule::keys_open`). S's travel seeds: each held copy with a chained edge write naming a holder in open(S). With `keys_open` off, open(S) is the actor alone.

**The index.** Parts per edge, the record edges, the adjacency holder → target; per sync ID its open set and travel seeds; reverse: thing → sync IDs whose open set holds it. No transitive relation is stored: each change walks from itself. `check` compares every sync ID's open set and travel seeds with from scratch, the graph with the held chained parts, and the reverse index.

| change | keys-open work |
|---|---|
| chained read grant (x, y), a new edge | per sync ID whose open set holds x and not y: walk what y opens beyond it, take the writes naming each newly opened holder (holder index): O(what it opens + its writes) |
| record edge added (`add_record`) | as a new read grant edge |
| chained write naming holder h | the sync IDs whose open set holds h take it: O(those) |
| a read edge gone (forget, chained flip to false) | sync IDs whose open set held both ends recompute open and travel seeds; a write gone: the sync IDs it travelled under drop it |
| sync ID registered | from scratch |

A forget is the shrinking direction: recompute per affected sync ID (not reference counts: paths share edges and the graph has cycles). Forgets of role commits are rare (a release or an unsync).

## Leaves (A3; notes/a3-spec/permissions.md §4 the leaf bullet, §5.1 Reading a list)
A lowering L in S's set (a member, or a body: a held copy on a unit thing) requires its leaf for thing t where
- **reads**: L **cuts** (removal-shaped and taking write away on its edge: a record edge, or a held chained write giving write or above ordered before it, `takes_write` over the write keys, the first write-giving part per edge by key; L itself chained or not) and some body of S on t has a content-class part (content, an addendum) whose known path uses L's edge, in any order (finding 27: a lowering cutting nothing is read by no verdict);
- **home**: t is not L's own thing and L is a body of S (findings 30, 34: a far leaf stays wherever its lowering is a body, so a node holding the removal holds the leaf a write it judges elsewhere needs; its own thing's leaf leaves with the last body it reads).
A held content-class copy on t **waits** for L's leaf for t where L is held (any chainedness), its root holds a pair for t, it cuts, and the copy rests on its edge, and the node lacks the leaf (P7). An **addendum** is a body only while it lists a held copy with a content part resting on its removal's edge; one listing nothing it can keep brings nothing (no atoms, no rest, no leaf).

**The index.** Global: per (edge, thing) the held write parts with a leaf for that thing (`leaf_on`); per edge and per thing the copies with such parts (`leafy_on`, `leafy`); per (edge, thing) the held content-class copies resting on the edge (`rests_on`); per listed copy the addenda listing it (`listers`); per held copy whether it is live. Per sync ID: `rest`, the bodies with a content-class part on each (edge, unit thing); `leaves`, per lowering copy in the set the things it requires. No scan of removals or bodies: a change sends exactly the (sync ID, lowering) pairs it can move to decide again (`settle_leaves`), and a sync ID whose closure was rebuilt decides its members' leaves again (O(members)): a rebuild moves which lowerings are members, never a body's leaves, which rest counts, cuts, liveness and the unit move, each sending its pair.

| change | leaf work |
|---|---|
| a body arriving on t in S | its rest counts; where one passes 0 → 1, the lowerings of that (edge, t) with a leaf decide again: O(r), r the leaf-bearing lowerings of its edges for t, nothing of other edges or things (count: 2r checks, a later body 0, flat at 10, 100, 1,000 others) |
| which leaves judge a held part (`reading`, `waits`) | O(r) lookups: the leaf-bearing lowerings of the edges it rests on for its thing (count: r, flat in others) |
| a leaf arriving (`judged_by`) | the held parts resting on its lowering's edge on its thing: O(those) (count: n judged, flat in m resting elsewhere) |
| a lowering with leaves entering S's set (a member by the closure, or a body) | its own leaves: O(its leaves) |
| what cuts on an edge moves (its first write-giving part, a record edge) | each leaf-bearing lowering of the edge decides again in each sync ID it is in: O(those × their sync IDs), rare |
| a forget, a chained flip to false, a retag | the closures rebuilt decide their members' leaves again: O(members), with the close itself; a body's far leaves cost nothing there (count: 0 leaf checks for r removals with k far leaves each at (r, k) = (10, 10), (10, 100), (100, 10)) |
| an addendum's listed copy arriving, leaving, or learning its path | the addenda listing it decide again whether they are bodies: O(those) |

**Memory per sync ID**: its rest counts (O(edges its bodies rest on × their things)), and its leaves: the far leaves of its own removals at home (k per removal listing k other things), plus one per (cutting lowering in the set, unit thing a body rests on) (counted: a document keeps its own leaf alone, the removal's home all k, at k = 10, 100). A required leaf is a (lowering, thing) pair; its bytes are brief 2's.

## Carried demotions (design for A3: not built)
A removal reaches a thing other than its own by two routes, neither of them the derivation's own seed: the evidence closure (the removal is a chained write on a body's path edge, so wherever a node holds both it and a write it judges), and **carried demotions** (spec/crypto.md §4): a keyset of M lists the explicit demotions below write or admin its rotator holds applied on M or below it; a keyset is held only with the edge writes of the commits it carries, so every reader of a thing whose keyset carries a removal holds that removal, from the rotation on. At A3 the demotions a required keyset carries become a seed of the required set (notes/a3-spec/crypto.md; OPEN 8): one copy identity per (removal commit, thing) with the closure's, one blob per sync ID, never a carried-demotion blob and an evidence blob. To check first in the model: held-model has no keysets; brief 7 adds them (a keyset per rotation naming the held demotions) and checks that every carried demotion a reader's keyset judging reads is in the required set. A carried demotion arrives without its leaves (finding 35, OPEN 7).

## Incremental
State per sync ID: `atoms` (per edge, the number of bodies bringing it: the bodies' own contribution), its travel (open set and seeds), and the closure over those (`members`, `edges`). Travel seeds are kept incrementally (a chained write naming an open holder added or taken out directly), so a write naming an actor reads none of its earlier ones. Global indexes over the held copies: chained writes per edge, chained grants per edge (ordered: the first one decides joined-ness), parts per resting edge ordered by `pos`, chained writes per holder, resetters per creation, held copies per thing; reverse: edge → sync IDs using it, copy → sync IDs holding it as a member, thing → sync IDs whose unit holds it; the keys-open index above. Joined-ness is per part, global (no sync ID in it).

Monotone changes extend the closure from what they add (one BFS step function, `close`); a change that can shrink it (a forget, a chained write falling, a part no longer joined, a retag) marks the sync IDs it touches dirty, and each dirty sync ID is closed again from its atoms and seeds (O(its closure), never O(its bodies)).

| change | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| body appended on a path of k edges | k atom increments in each sync ID whose unit holds its thing; an edge new to S closes from it | O(k log) per such sync ID, independent of history |
| copy (role commit) arrives with a chained write on edge e | e → sync IDs; added to each and closed from it; a chained grant moving e's first grant meeting a need earlier (grants are indexed per (edge, need): each grant under every need it meets, at most eight) rechecks joined-ness of the parts resting on e with that need ordered after it | O(sync IDs using e + what it brings + parts newly joined); no body scan |
| junk (an unchained edge write) arrives | indexed, its own joined-ness computed; nothing else | O(its parts' hops) |
| path entry opens (`open_path`) | the part's atoms added where it is a body | O(k) per sync ID |
| chained flips true | as the arrival of that part | as above |
| chained flips false, forget | sync IDs holding the copy as member, or whose atoms drop to zero, dirty; first grant moving later unjoins the resting parts in between | O(dirty closures + changed parts) |
| body forgotten (compaction, purge) | its path's atoms out; S closes again only where the body was the last on an edge (its writer's last body on S, or the last through that path) | O(k) per body; a close O(S's closure), one per `forget_all` batch |
| thing joins S's unit (b bodies) | b bodies' atoms; S dirty if it held copies on the thing | O(b·k + closure) |
| thing leaves | b bodies' atoms out; S dirty | O(b·k + closure) |
| keys-open travel | as Keys-open travel says | indexed: a change reaches only the sync IDs it can move |
| from scratch for S | every body on its unit's things, then seeds, then the closure | linear in bodies·k + closure |
Memory per sync ID: its used edges, its members and their reasons (the output), atom counts per used edge: O(edges its paths use + copies), not O(bodies). Global indexes: O(held parts · k), the size of holding the parts.

**The engine's memo machinery.** The module is pure: it takes changes by call (`add`, `forget`, `forget_all`, `set_chained`, `open_path`, `join`, `leave`) and does work in proportion to them. Brief 3 drives it from `memo::Feed`/`Effects` followers over the held set, the units and the walk's chained flips, as `sync/certified.rs` follows `Indexes::chains_on` and `apply/paths.rs` follows `paths_unknown`; the derivation itself keeps no stamps.

## What the switch (A3 brief 5b, A2 brief 3) must know
- The adapter gives each held copy every part of its commit on that thing; per edge-class part its chained-ness from the walk: osis-perm `Paths::is_chained` per part (may-grant grants, removals, self-removals and finalizes included), its flips in `Changed::chained` (each one a `set_chained`).
- `creation` per edge write is the entry its reset names (spec/permissions.md §2); OPEN 13 decides whether a grant's counts.
- A split body whose entry has not opened is added with `hops: None` and `open_path` when `apply::paths::refresh` opens it.
- Switch with `Config { keys_open, leaves }` on; its differential is the one built here against `Stage::A2` (copies: held-model carries a lowering's list whole, so the leaves have no oracle and their tests in engine `tests/it/evidence/a3.rs` are ignored until A3 deletes them), at 0 disagreements on main before the flag day. `Piece::Chain`'s placement under openers is then the derivation's travel seeds, not deleted without them (finding 19); keys-open travel stays at A3 (finding 31). Brief 5a (the leaf form's flag day) is not the derivation's: its differential is the osis-perm walk's against roles-model `paths` with the per-leaf reading.
- Leaves: the adapter gives each edge write the things its root holds a pair for (`Write::leaves`), each addendum its removal's edge and listed copies; a required leaf is logged as the leaf blob (lowering commit ID, thing), wrapped per sync ID as a copy is; a held part for which `waits` names a leaf pends (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable, the leaf clause).
- Per edge write the adapter reads `removal`, `gives_write`, `gives_read` from the `roles.0` entry and the header's `ts` (an `until` at or before `ts` makes a grant a removal too); the walk's chained-ness must cover may-grant grants (above), since a may-grant grant is a step of the read graph.
- Accounts: Role changes travel's "an account root counting for its account only on a thing it reaches by strongs" is not in the derivation or the model (no accounts before A4): the adapter registers each sync ID's actor; an account's root's sync ID needs that clause as an input (which things count) or waits for A4.
- Keys-open travel for good (README OPEN 15): today's words keep a removal there for `ROLE_KEEP`; the derivation has no clock.
- Carried demotions (spec/crypto.md §4) require a writer removal's edge writes under every sync ID whose thing's keyset carries it: the same (removal commit, thing) copy the closure requires where it does, logged once per sync ID (one copy identity, not two blobs): Carried demotions, above.
- Record edges: the adapter calls `add_record` for each creation record it holds (mint admin, thing), from the records the walk reads; held-model `World::opened` reads the same (README OPEN 17).
- `gives_write` is read from the role alone (`role >= Write`, as `World::gives_write`): a finalize at write takes write away from nothing.
- A forget is the rare path: the engine forgets a commit only where `retention::forgettable` says so (a content commit or snapshot whose every part is covered, dropped invalid, spent or on a thing no longer held; any commit held only as purged leftovers, once covered). `roles.0` is never compaction-snapshotted, so a role commit is never forgotten by a compaction, only once purged; `chained` falls only with a forget.
- Forget a compaction's bodies with one `forget_all` (one close per sync ID it shrinks). A close is closure-sized (`a_forgotten_body_closes_again_only_when_it_was_the_last_on_an_edge`: 1,000 copies visited under an organisation of 1,000 writers), paid when a writer's last body on a path leaves: a delete-and-rederive from the lost edge (or per-copy support counts, where the closure has no cycles) would make it O(what it loses); not needed while closes follow compactions, needed if per-body forgetting becomes routine.
- Records (members' creation records up their chains) are not output: taken from `required`'s copies' and the bodies' paths.
- The derivation keeps its own indexes (per edge, per holder, per thing): the engine's own (`ix.held`, the walk's resting index) can feed or replace them.

## The differential
Against held-model `evidence` (`Open10::Granted` with entry creations, J2 joins (`Join::Needed`: the adapter gives each hop its part's `World::need`, each grant what it gives), keys-open travel on: `Stage::A2`), at every held set: A2's derivation (leaves off) against `Stage::A2` over `Rule::fixed`. The model carries a lowering's list whole (notes/write-path-design.md §2, E4), so the leaves have no oracle: their hand worlds (`a3.rs`) are ignored until A3 deletes them. The adapter gives the world's record edges first (`add_record`) and turns each held model part into a `Part` (chained from `World::chained` over the held set, creation from `World::creation`, `removal` the shape `World::removes` gives, `pos` the model's key or `ts` start, no leaves, an addendum's edge from its removal). The model writes each part of a commit as an event of its own, so a held set can hold some of a commit's parts on a thing: no node holds such a set (Input), and the model's closure reads it otherwise in both directions. Such held sets are not compared; the derivation is still stepped through them and `check`ed. At every step of a world, for every node's held set and every sync ID's held entries (its one-sync-ID reader), for every sync ID:
- copies kept incrementally (fed the step's new copies, chained flips, forgotten copies and retags) and from scratch against the model's required parts on things outside the unit, by (commit, thing), under each rule;
- Default: every 291st family world (106 worlds over the three `Shape::mint` values), the story world of seed 0 under each of the six unit crossings.
- Property: `the_derivation_agrees_with_the_model_large_spec_permissions_4` (full: every 17th family world, 1,500 random, 60 story seeds; the gate's a tenth of the seeds and every 97th family world). No `_plain` twin: at the gate's one offset (`OSIS_HELD_START`) it would run a subset of the same worlds again.
- Keys-open hand worlds (`travel.rs`), written from the draft's words: finding 19 (`open14_keys_open_travel_carries_a_removal_to_the_things_reading_through_it`: required under A by keys-open travel alone), keys opening through a mint admin's record edge (OPEN 17, with the control: records not given).
- Hand worlds (`hand.rs`): each model hand test naming a rule or finding (f2, f9, f11, f13, f14, f15, f23 (a read hop joins no write: no copy of the path past it at a node holding everything) and OPEN 10's junk and admin counts, split, P4) with the copy its rule decides; the other model hand worlds (f1, f3 to f6, f12, risk 3, P2, the addendum) as differentials; the derivation's own: a body whose path entry waits brings nothing until it opens, a chained grant ordered after a write joins nothing, a removal is evidence only where a body rests on its edge.
