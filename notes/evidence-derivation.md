# Evidence derivation
> Summary: Design and build state of A2 briefs 2 and 2b (notes/a2-spec/README.md, Order of the code briefs): `osis_engine::evidence`, the set of certificate copies a sync ID requires, derived from a node's held commits and the sync ID's unit as notes/a2-spec/permissions.md §4 says (OPEN 10's proposal (iv)), built unused beside `sync/certified.rs`; inputs, output, what re-derives what and its cost; reach and keys-open travel as indexed graphs (what each is exactly, the index, each change's cost, counted), the storage every sync ID under a hub pays with reach on; the differential against held-model `evidence::World::req` under A3's rule and brief 3's (`Stage::ReachNoErrand`) (0 disagreements), mutations, counts, the draft sentences fixed, the walk-level check (a reader holding bodies plus the required copies judges as the writer under osis-perm `paths`), what is left (the engine-level check and finding 9's engine test: the engine does not run the walk yet), what brief 3 (the switch) must know.

**Principle: a sync ID's evidence is a function of the commits the node holds and the sync ID's unit: the least set the draft's rules close over them. Nothing is placed once and remembered; a change re-derives exactly what it can move, and the incremental answer is the from-scratch answer by construction (same closure step, same seeds).**

## Input
Plain facts per held **copy** (commit ID, thing), held whole (a commit's edge writes on a thing are one `roles.0` delta, its vouches one `vouches.0` delta: spec/crdt.md §2, one delta per tid and aspect; a node holding fewer of its edge-class deltas holds no copy of it): the commit's parts on that thing (`evidence::Part`), as the derivation's own structs (independent of header types, as osis-perm's walk input is):
- `pos`: the part's place in key order as a verdict reads it (draft §5.1 key for an edge-class part, the start of its `ts` for content: spec/permissions.md §5.2 Position); "ordered before" is `key(j) < pos(m)`.
- `hops`: the edges of the path the commit states for that thing, each marked record or not; `None` while the path is not known here (a split entry waiting to open: notes/path-form.md). A part with no known path brings nothing (finding 8); the entry opening is an update (`open_path`).
- the kind: content; an edge write (holder, whether it is a **grant** (sets a role above none: no self-removal, no finalize), a **removal** (gives below write at its `ts`, no finalize: A3's reach), whether it **gives write** or above (A3's reach), the remaining admin it names, the **creation** of the entry it resets as the reset names it (spec/permissions.md §2 Edge writes)); a vouch (the copy of the lowering it is keyed by).
- `chained` per edge-class part: whether it is a chained write (draft §4 Chained writes). An input, never computed here: the walk owns the rule (osis-perm `paths`' chained pass; held-model `World::chained` in the differential). It only grows with arrivals and may fall on a forget; the caller reports each flip (`set_chained`).
- Per sync ID: its actor (the holder whose grants travel under it) and its unit's things (`join`, `leave`).
- `Config::vouches`: vouch evidence on or off (the owner may drop role vouches: the derivation runs either way).
- `Config::reach`: the reach clause on or off; `Config::keys_open`: Role changes travel's keys-open half on or off (off: writes naming the actor alone). Brief 3 switches with both on (README OPEN 14, *proposed*: without reach main is less safe than today, finding 18; without keys-open travel too, finding 19); A3's errand replaces keys-open travel.
- Per edge write, for those: `removal` (gives below write at its own `ts`, no finalize: a self-removal and a grant of read are removals, a demotion from admin to write is not), `gives_write` (write or above, any `until`), `gives_read` (read or above, any `until`, no self-removal). From the header and the `roles.0` entry; no verdict.

## Output
`required(sid)`: every copy on a thing outside the unit in S's evidence, with one **reason** each (`Reason`: a taken write on an edge the set uses, the creation a member's reset names, a vouch keyed by a member lowering, a write naming a holder whose role changes travel under S, a removal whose reach is held, a grant of such a reach). Bodies (held copies on unit things) are S's sync unit blobs, not output. `from_scratch(sid)` the same from nothing. A copy is a whole (commit, thing): its edge-class parts on that thing travel together (finding 9's "apply together"), never a part alone and never keyed by commit alone (a role commit on two things is two copies).

## The rule (draft §4 with OPEN 10 (iv))
S's evidence is the least set of copies holding:
- **seeds**: the bodies (held copies on unit things); each held copy with a chained edge write naming a holder in **open(S)** (Role changes travel: S's actor, and with `keys_open` what it opens, below); with `reach`, each held chained removal of (h, t), t outside the unit, whose **reach** under S is held, with that reach (below).
- **closure**, for each member m (body or copy):
  - each part of m whose path is **joined** (each non-record edge holds a held chained grant ordered before the part) brings its path's edges; an edge write brings its named remaining admin edge (joined or not);
  - an edge brings every held copy with a chained edge write on it;
  - an edge write brings the creation of the entry it resets, held;
  - (`vouches`) a member lowering brings every held chained vouch keyed by it on a thing some member is on.
A copy on a unit thing reached by the closure is a body already.

## Reach and keys-open travel
Both are reachability over a graph of held chained edge writes, edges (holder → target), multi-edges kept as their parts. "At any time": an edge stays while a part setting it is held and chained, whatever later writes on it say (a grant since removed, an `until` passed, still joins). Both graphs only grow with arrivals (chained-ness only grows); a forget or a chained flip to false shrinks them.
- **Reach** (draft §4 Evidence of a sync ID (A3); held-model `World::reach`): the **write graph** has an edge per chained part giving write or above. A chained **removal** on t (its copy's thing) **reaches** S when t is outside S's unit and some path in the write graph leads from t into the unit (through any things, unit things included: to the end, finding 1). Its **ways** under S: every edge (x, y) with x reachable from t and y leading into the unit, i.e. every edge on some path from t into the unit. S's reach seeds: each reaching removal, and each chained write-giving part on a way whose copy is outside the unit. A removal on a unit thing has no reach (it is a body; its ways are not taken). A removal's own edge (h, t) is no step: reach runs from its target t, the thing the removed holder acted through.
- **Keys-open travel** (Role changes travel's keys-open half; held-model `World::opened`, `Rule::keys_open`): the **read graph** has an edge per chained part giving read or above, no self-removal. **open(S)** is S's actor and every thing reachable from it there (S's thing holds a role on it, so its keys open it). S's travel seeds: each held copy with a chained edge write naming a holder in open(S). With `keys_open` off, open(S) is the actor alone (a grant naming S's actor travels under it).

**The index.** Per graph: parts per edge and the adjacency both ways; chained removals per target thing; per sync ID its open set, travel seeds, reach seeds and reach ways; reverse: thing → sync IDs whose open set holds it, copy → sync IDs whose reach seeds hold it, edge → sync IDs whose ways hold it, thing → sync IDs whose unit holds it (the closure's). No transitive relation is stored (no thing → reachable sync IDs table): each change walks from itself. From scratch for S (`reach_of`): walk backward from S's unit (the things leading into it and their edges), take the removal targets among them, walk forward from those over the edges just walked (every edge so walked is a way, none wasted). `check` compares every sync ID's open set, travel seeds, reach seeds and ways with from scratch, the graphs with the held chained parts, and the reverse indexes.

| change | reach work | keys-open work |
|---|---|---|
| chained removal on t arrives | walk what t leads to once, then per sync ID whose unit it reaches (t outside it) the ways back into that unit: O(what t can write + the seeds given), never the sync IDs it does not reach (count: 7r + 3 for r reached of s = 10, 100, 1,000) | as any write naming its holder |
| chained write grant (a, b), a new edge | walk what leads to a; no removal among it: done (O(those)); else walk what b leads to, and per sync ID below b take the removals above (outside its unit) with the ways above, (a, b) and the ways below: O(above + below + removals above × sync IDs below), the product being what the property asks: every sync ID below must learn of every removal above (count: walks 5a + 5b + 4, seeds b (3a + 3)) | |
| write grant on an edge already in the graph | the sync IDs whose ways hold the edge take the copy: O(those) | |
| chained read grant (x, y), a new edge | | per sync ID whose open set holds x and not y: walk what y opens beyond it, take the writes naming each newly opened holder (holder index): O(what it opens + its writes) |
| chained write naming holder h | | the sync IDs whose open set holds h take it: O(those) |
| content, junk (unchained), finalize, vouch | none (counted 0) | none |
| forget or chained flip to false of a part reach used | each sync ID whose reach seeds hold the copy or whose ways hold the edge computes its reach from scratch: per such sync ID O(what leads into its unit) (count: 5a + 6 each, b of them, an unreached sync ID nothing), then closes again if a seed left | a read edge gone: sync IDs whose open set held both ends recompute open and travel seeds; a write gone: the sync IDs it travelled under drop it |
| thing joins or leaves S's unit | S's reach from scratch: O(what leads into the new unit) | none (open runs from the actor) |
| sync ID registered | from scratch | from scratch |

A forget is the shrinking direction: recompute per affected sync ID (not reference counts: paths share edges and the graph has cycles, so counting support is a closure of its own). Forgets of role commits are rare (role commits are kept; a forget is a release or an unsync), so it is the rare path.

**Memory**: the graphs, O(held chained edge writes); per sync ID its reach seeds (O(removals reaching it + the grants on their ways off its unit)), ways and open set, and the same again in the reverse indexes. Organisation of 1,000 documents (each its own sync ID, O writes each, a member writes each through [O]) and 100 members removed from O (`an_organisations_documents_each_carry_every_removal_above_them`, flat in documents): per document 100 reach seeds and one way, and 201 required copies (each removal and the member grant its reset names, plus the writer's grant) against 1 with reach off. In memory, engine IDs (commit ID 32 B, tid 15 B: a copy ID 47 B, a seed entry about 100 B with its reason, BTreeMap overhead included roughly 130 B, a reverse index entry about 20 B): reach seeds and their reverse entries about 15 MB for the 100,000 (removal, document) pairs, and the required set (output, two copies per removal) about 30 MB more.

**Storage every reader pays (reach on).** A removal on a thing whose write grants lead into a unit is required there for good while held (reach reads grants at any time), with the grant its reset names: every sync ID under a hub carries every removal above it. In the organisation above: 2r + 1 copies per document against 1 with reach off; a node holding all 1,000 documents' sync IDs logs 200,000 entries (each copy one blob held once, wrapped under each sync ID: spec/sync-judgement.md §1). A copy measures 782 B (header 331 B, `roles.0` delta 85 B, the rest blob framing and the section wrap; osis-sync `reach_cost::*`, by hand), so a reader of one document fetches 1.6 KB per removal more. Today that reader already holds every *writer* removal on the hub: the hub's and the document's later keysets carry it (spec/crypto.md §4 Carried demotions, which A2 keeps), and each removal also rotates the document: measured 21 KB per removal per first read of the document (80 KB at r = 1, 143 KB at r = 4), so reach adds under 8% there. What is new: reach's "removal" includes a fresh grant of read and a reader's removal (carried demotions leave readers out), so every reader added to or removed from a hub is required under every document below it for good (`evidence::reach::a_fresh_read_grant_on_a_hub_*`): notes/a2-spec/README.md OPEN 16. A3's leaves do not shrink it (a removal's root and role entries stay whole, OPEN 4).

## Incremental
State per sync ID: `atoms` (per edge, the number of bodies bringing it: the bodies' own contribution), its travel (open set and seeds) and reach (seeds and ways), and the closure over those (`members`, `edges`, vouches waiting for a member on their thing). Travel seeds are kept incrementally (a chained write naming an open holder added or taken out directly), so a write naming an actor reads none of its earlier ones. Global indexes over the held copies: chained writes per edge, chained grants per edge (ordered: the first one decides joined-ness), parts per resting edge ordered by `pos`, chained writes per holder, chained vouches per keyed copy, resetters per creation, held copies per thing; reverse: edge → sync IDs using it, copy → sync IDs holding it as a member, thing → sync IDs whose unit holds it; the reach and keys-open indexes above. Joined-ness is per part, global (no sync ID in it).

Monotone changes extend the closure from what they add (one BFS step function, `close`); a change that can shrink it (a forget, a chained write falling, a part no longer joined, a retag) marks the sync IDs it touches dirty, and each dirty sync ID is closed again from its atoms and seeds (O(its closure), never O(its bodies)).

| change | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| body appended on a path of k edges | k atom increments in each sync ID whose unit holds its thing; an edge new to S closes from it | O(k log) per such sync ID, independent of history |
| copy (role commit) arrives with a chained write on edge e | e → sync IDs; added to each and closed from it; a chained grant moving e's first grant earlier rechecks joined-ness of the parts resting on e ordered after it | O(sync IDs using e + what it brings + parts newly joined); no body scan |
| junk (an unchained edge write) arrives | indexed, its own joined-ness computed; nothing else | O(its parts' hops) |
| path entry opens (`open_path`) | the part's atoms added where it is a body | O(k) per sync ID |
| chained flips true | as the arrival of that part | as above |
| chained flips false, forget | sync IDs holding the copy as member, or whose atoms drop to zero, dirty; first grant moving later unjoins the resting parts in between | O(dirty closures + changed parts) |
| body forgotten (compaction, purge) | its path's atoms out; S closes again only where the body was the last on an edge (its writer's last body on S, or the last through that path) | O(k) per body; a close O(S's closure + held vouches), one per `forget_all` batch |
| thing joins S's unit (b bodies) | b bodies' atoms; S dirty if it held copies on the thing | O(b·k + closure) |
| thing leaves | b bodies' atoms out; S dirty | O(b·k + closure) |
| reach and keys-open travel | as Reach and keys-open travel says | indexed: a change reaches only the sync IDs it can move |
| from scratch for S | every body on its unit's things, then seeds, then the closure | linear in bodies·k + closure |
Memory per sync ID: its used edges, its members and their reasons (the output), atom counts per used edge: O(edges its paths use + copies), not O(bodies). Global indexes: O(held parts · k), the size of holding the parts.

**The engine's memo machinery.** The module is pure: it takes changes by call (`add`, `forget`, `forget_all`, `set_chained`, `open_path`, `join`, `leave`) and does work in proportion to them. Brief 3 drives it from `memo::Feed`/`Effects` followers over the held set, the units and the walk's chained flips, as `sync/certified.rs` follows `Indexes::chains_on` and `apply/paths.rs` follows `paths_unknown`; the derivation itself keeps no stamps.

- A forget is the rare path: the engine forgets a commit only where `retention::forgettable` says so (a content commit or snapshot whose every part is covered, dropped invalid, spent or on a thing no longer held; any commit held only as purged leftovers, once covered). `roles.0` is never compaction-snapshotted, so a role commit is never forgotten by a compaction, only once purged; `chained` falls only with a forget. - A close also scans every held vouch (`body_vouches`): index vouches by their keyed copy's thing if vouches stay on.
- Records (members' creation records up their chains) are not output: taken from `required`'s copies' and the bodies' paths.
- The derivation keeps its own indexes (per edge, per holder, per thing): the engine's own (`ix.held`, the walk's resting index) can feed or replace them.

## The differential
Against held-model `evidence::World::req` under `Open10::Granted` with entry creations (`World::creations`), reach on, vouches counting and not, at every held set twice: keys-open travel off (A3's rule, `Rule::fixed`) and on (brief 3's, `Stage::ReachNoErrand`), each against a derivation with `keys_open` alike. A world runs under A3's delivery (the errand) with vouches counting and brief 3's (no errand) without, so held sets of both deliveries are compared. The adapter turns each held model part into a `Part` (chained from `World::chained` over the held set, creation from `World::creation`, `pos` the model's key or `ts` start). The model writes each part of a commit as an event of its own, so a held set can hold some of a commit's parts on a thing: no node holds such a set (Input), and the model's closure reads it otherwise in both directions (it brings the creation an unheld sibling part names; it does not bring a creation that is itself an unheld part of a held copy). Such held sets are not compared (about 0.5% of them: 14,602 at full size); the derivation is still stepped through them and `check`ed. At every step of a world, for every node's held set and every sync ID's held entries (its one-sync-ID reader), for every sync ID: the derivation's copies kept incrementally (fed the step's new copies, chained flips, forgotten copies and retags) and from scratch against the model's required parts on things outside the unit, by (commit, thing).
- Default: every 97th family world (`the_derivation_agrees_with_the_model_on_a_family_slice_spec_permissions_4`, over 1,000 comparisons with copies), the story world of seed 0 under each of the six unit crossings.
- Properties: `the_derivation_agrees_with_the_model_plain_spec_permissions_4` (106 family, 20 random, 2 story seeds × 6 crossings × vouches: 150 worlds), `_large` (full: every 17th family world, 1,500 random, 60 story seeds; the gate's a tenth of the seeds and every 97th family world: 328 worlds); both at gate size 11 s wall under the lock. 0 disagreements at every size; full size: 2,823 worlds, 5,934,901 comparisons with copies (both rules), 125 s wall under the lock (16 cores). Each held set is compared twice (keys-open travel off and on), about twice the derivation work of one rule; the default family slice 5.5 CPU-s and story test 7.6 (4.0 and 4.8 with one rule).
- Reach hand worlds (`reach.rs`), written from the draft's words: findings 1 and 17 (`open14_with_no_honest_node_between_only_the_errand_reaches_d`: the node holding everything requires the removal under D, D's reader under brief 3 never holds it), finding 1's reach two grants deep with no body on the removed edge (`f1_*`), finding 3 (`f3_*`: a grant written after the removal brings it), reach at any time (a grant since removed still reaches), finding 18 (`open14_a_removed_writers_other_route_carried_the_removal_today`: required under D by reach alone, under R by the thief's path), finding 19 (`open14_keys_open_travel_carries_a_removal_to_the_things_reading_through_it`: required under A by keys-open travel alone), a removal commit on two things reaching by each copy; a grant removed before the removal still reaching (a backdated write through it is judged by the removal's frontier); a removal on a unit thing (no reach); a cycle and two paths (every way, the back edge too); an unchained removal (nothing); a fresh read grant on a hub required under its document (OPEN 16); a way's last edge held as a body, forgotten.
- Hand worlds (`hand.rs`): each model hand test naming a rule or finding (f2, f9, f10, f11, f13, f14, f15 and OPEN 10's junk and admin counts, split, P4, the vouch switch) with the copy its rule decides; the other model hand worlds (f1, f3 to f6, f12, risk 3, P2, the addendum) as differentials; the derivation's own: a body whose path entry waits brings nothing until it opens, and a chained grant ordered after a write joins nothing.
