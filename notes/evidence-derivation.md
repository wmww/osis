# Evidence derivation
> Summary: Design and build state of A2 briefs 2 and 2b (notes/a2-spec/README.md, Order of the code briefs): `osis_engine::evidence`, the set of certificate copies a sync ID requires, derived from a node's held commits and the sync ID's unit as notes/a2-spec/permissions.md §4 says (OPEN 10's proposal (iv)), built unused beside `sync/certified.rs`; inputs, output, what re-derives what and its cost; reach and keys-open travel as indexed graphs over chained grants and record edges (what each is exactly, which removals reach (those taking write away, with their witness), the index, each change's cost, counted), the storage every sync ID under a hub pays with reach on; the two mechanisms carrying writer removals (reach, keysets' carried demotions) and what one would look like at A3 (design, not built); A3 brief 3 (notes/a3-spec/README.md): a lowering's list leaves as evidence (which (lowering, thing) leaves a sync ID requires: read only where the lowering cuts, far leaves at home; addenda leaving), which leaves judge a held part (a reader lacking one waits), and the delivery duty's targets per held removal (to the end, standing, with the runner's mode by its role), each indexed with its costs counted; the differential against held-model `Stage::A3` with `Rule::listed` (copies, leaves, waits, the duty's targets against the model's errand) and `Stage::ReachNoErrand` (0 disagreements at every size), mutations, counts, the draft sentences fixed, the walk-level check (a reader holding bodies plus the required copies judges as the writer under osis-perm `paths`), what is left (the engine-level check and finding 9's engine test: the engine does not run the walk yet; admin-only content's cut), what the switch (A3 brief 5b) and the sync-side duty (brief 4) must know.

**Principle: a sync ID's evidence is a function of the commits the node holds and the sync ID's unit: the least set the draft's rules close over them. Nothing is placed once and remembered; a change re-derives exactly what it can move, and the incremental answer is the from-scratch answer by construction (same closure step, same seeds).**

## Input
Plain facts per held **copy** (commit ID, thing), held whole (a commit's edge writes on a thing are one `roles.0` delta, its vouches one `vouches.0` delta: spec/crdt.md §2, one delta per tid and aspect; a node holding fewer of its edge-class deltas holds no copy of it): the commit's parts on that thing (`evidence::Part`), as the derivation's own structs (independent of header types, as osis-perm's walk input is):
- `pos`: the part's place in key order as a verdict reads it (draft §5.1 key for an edge-class part, the start of its `ts` for content: spec/permissions.md §5.2 Position); "ordered before" is `key(j) < pos(m)`.
- `hops`: the edges of the path the commit states for that thing, each marked record or not and with what the part **needs** there (`Need`: its least need, spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 3: a role, write for content (admin for an admin-only aspect), admin for an edge write, write for a finalize, an invite grant's level; on an invite grant's last edge its level as an invite); `None` while the path is not known here (a split entry waiting to open: notes/path-form.md). A part with no known path brings nothing (finding 8); the entry opening is an update (`open_path`).
- the kind: content; an edge write (holder, whether it is a **grant** (sets a role above none: no self-removal, no finalize) and what it **gives** (`Gives`: its role and the invite it gives, `until` disregarded), a **removal** (gives below write at its `ts`, no finalize: A3's reach), whether it **gives write** or above (A3's reach), the remaining admin it names, the **creation** of the entry it resets as the reset names it (spec/permissions.md §2 Edge writes)); a vouch (the copy of the lowering it is keyed by).
- `chained` per edge-class part: whether it is a chained write (draft §4 Chained writes). An input, never computed here: the walk owns the rule (osis-perm `paths`' chained pass; held-model `World::chained` in the differential). It only grows with arrivals and may fall on a forget; the caller reports each flip (`set_chained`).
- Per sync ID: its actor (the holder whose grants travel under it) and its unit's things (`join`, `leave`).
- **Record edges** (`add_record`): each (mint admin, thing it minted), from the creation records the node holds; standing for good (records are identity: a thing's record is never taken back while the thing is held). An edge of the reach and read graphs (admin, so write and read).
- `Config::vouches`: vouch evidence on or off (the owner may drop role vouches: the derivation runs either way).
- `Config::reach`: the reach clause on or off; `Config::keys_open`: Role changes travel's keys-open half on or off (off: writes naming the actor alone). Both on at the switch (README OPEN 14, *proposed*: without reach main is less safe than today, finding 18; without keys-open travel too, finding 19); keys-open travel stays at A3 (notes/evidence-model-findings.md 31: read access through a holder thing reads it, and the errand cannot replace it where the remover is lost before its first pass): `Stage::A3` has it on, and the derivation's A3 configuration matches.
- (A3) `Config::leaves`: list leaves as evidence and addenda leaving; `Config::duty`: the duty's targets kept. Per edge write `leaves`: the things its list root holds a leaf pair for (notes/a3-spec/wire.md: the root shows which things have a leaf, not what a leaf names). An addendum part (`Kind::Addendum`): its removal's edge (read from the removal, held or not: the model reads it from the world) and the copies it lists.
- (duty) `duty_add`/`duty_forget`: copies read under a target's own sync ID while the node holds it for the duty (the grants naming the target as holder): edges of the duty's walk only, never evidence of anything (draft sync-set §2: nothing of it enters the node closure); `set_role(t, role)`: the node's own role on t by a path, as its walk reads it.
- Per edge write, for those: `removal` (the shape: gives below write at its own `ts`, no finalize: a self-removal and a grant of read have it, a demotion from admin to write has not; whether it is a removal for reach the derivation decides, below), `gives_write` (write or above, any `until`), `gives_read` (read or above, any `until`, no self-removal). From the header and the `roles.0` entry; no verdict.

## Output
(A3) `leaves(sid)`: the (lowering commit ID, thing) leaves S requires, each with why (`LeafWhy::Home`, `Reads`); `reading(c)`: the leaves judging held copy `c`, `waits(c, has)` the one it lacks (it waits); `judged_by(r, t)`: what lowering copy `r`'s leaf for `t` judges among the held copies (what a node receiving it can judge now); `duty()`: every (removal copy, target thing, `Mode`, the edge first reached by) this node runs (`Run`); `reached(r)`: what removal `r` reaches whatever the node's roles.

`required(sid)`: every copy on a thing outside the unit in S's evidence, with one **reason** each (`Reason`: a taken write on an edge the set uses, the creation a member's reset names, a vouch keyed by a member lowering, a write naming a holder whose role changes travel under S, a removal whose reach is held, a grant of such a reach). Bodies (held copies on unit things) are S's sync unit blobs, not output. `from_scratch(sid)` the same from nothing. A copy is a whole (commit, thing): its edge-class parts on that thing travel together (finding 9's "apply together"), never a part alone and never keyed by commit alone (a role commit on two things is two copies).

## The rule (draft §4 with OPEN 10 (iv))
S's evidence is the least set of copies holding:
- **seeds**: the bodies (held copies on unit things); each held copy with a chained edge write naming a holder in **open(S)** (Role changes travel: S's actor, and with `keys_open` what it opens, below); with `reach`, each held chained removal of (h, t), t outside the unit, whose **reach** under S is held, with that reach (below).
- **closure**, for each member m (body or copy):
  - each part of m whose path is **joined** (each non-record edge holds a held chained grant giving what the part needs there, ordered before the part: a hop granted too little joins nothing, README OPEN 16, finding 23) brings its path's edges; an edge write brings its named remaining admin edge (joined or not);
  - an edge brings every held copy with a chained edge write on it;
  - an edge write brings the creation of the entry it resets, held;
  - (`vouches`) a member lowering brings every held chained vouch keyed by it on a thing some member is on.
A copy on a unit thing reached by the closure is a body already.

## Reach and keys-open travel
Both are reachability over a graph of held chained edge writes and record edges, edges (holder → target), multi-edges kept as their parts. "At any time": an edge stays while a part setting it is held and chained, whatever later writes on it say (a grant since removed, an `until` passed, still joins); a **record edge** (mint admin → the thing it minted) is an admin edge standing since the thing's creation, ordered before every write (spec/permissions.md §1 Record edge; spec/crypto.md §4 Key access), in both graphs for good, a record edge lowered or removed later included (as a grant removed later is). Today's keys open through record edges (spec/sync-set.md §2: effective role climbs them; a dependent's secrets derive from its mint admin's) and today's chains climb them, so a graph over grants alone under-reads today (README OPEN 17: finding 19's world with O minted by A fails P1 at every stage but A3 without them). Both graphs only grow with arrivals (chained-ness only grows; records are never taken back); a forget or a chained flip to false shrinks them.
- **Reach** (draft §4 Evidence of a sync ID (A3); held-model `World::reach`): the **write graph** has an edge per chained part giving write or above, and every record edge. A chained part of a removal's shape on edge (h, t) **takes write away** (held-model `Removals::TakesWrite`, README OPEN 16) when (h, t) is a record edge or a held chained part on (h, t) giving write or above is ordered before it (by key); its **witness** is the first such part (none on a record edge: the record is its witness). Only such a **removal** reaches: a fresh grant of read or a reader's removal takes no write away (no write can rest on that edge before it), so it reaches nothing and costs no document anything. Monotone in held commits: a write grant arriving later with an earlier key makes a held removal one then (finding 3's shape: `open16_a_backdated_write_grant_*`). A removal on t (its copy's thing) **reaches** S when t is outside S's unit and some path in the write graph leads from t into the unit (through any things, unit things included: to the end, finding 1). Its **ways** under S: every edge (x, y) with x reachable from t and y leading into the unit, i.e. every edge on some path from t into the unit. S's reach seeds: each reaching removal with its witness, and each chained write-giving part on a way whose copy is outside the unit (a record edge on a way is no copy). A removal on a unit thing has no reach (it is a body; its ways are not taken). A removal's own edge (h, t) is no step: reach runs from its target t, the thing the removed holder acted through.
- **Keys-open travel** (Role changes travel's keys-open half; held-model `World::opened`, `Rule::keys_open`): the **read graph** has an edge per chained part giving read or above, no self-removal, and every record edge. **open(S)** is S's actor and every thing reachable from it there (S's thing holds a role on it, so its keys open it). S's travel seeds: each held copy with a chained edge write naming a holder in open(S). With `keys_open` off, open(S) is the actor alone (a grant naming S's actor travels under it).

**The index.** Per graph: parts per edge, the record edges, and the adjacency both ways; per edge the chained write-giving parts by key (the first decides which removals take write away and is their witness) and the chained parts of a removal's shape by key; chained removals taking write away per target thing; per sync ID its open set, travel seeds, reach seeds and reach ways; reverse: thing → sync IDs whose open set holds it, copy → sync IDs whose reach seeds hold it, edge → sync IDs whose ways hold it, thing → sync IDs whose unit holds it (the closure's). No transitive relation is stored (no thing → reachable sync IDs table): each change walks from itself. From scratch for S (`reach_of`): walk backward from S's unit (the things leading into it and their edges), take the removal targets among them, walk forward from those over the edges just walked (every edge so walked is a way, none wasted). `check` compares every sync ID's open set, travel seeds, reach seeds and ways with from scratch, the graphs with the held chained parts, and the reverse indexes.

| change | reach work | keys-open work |
|---|---|---|
| chained removal on t arrives | walk what t leads to once, then per sync ID whose unit it reaches (t outside it) the ways back into that unit: O(what t can write + the seeds given), never the sync IDs it does not reach (count: 7r + 3 for r reached of s = 10, 100, 1,000) | as any write naming its holder |
| chained write grant (a, b), a new edge | walk what leads to a; no removal among it: done (O(those)); else walk what b leads to, and per sync ID below b take the removals above (outside its unit) with the ways above, (a, b) and the ways below: O(above + below + removals above × sync IDs below), the product being what the property asks: every sync ID below must learn of every removal above (count: walks 5a + 5b + 4, seeds b (4a + 3)) | |
| write grant on an edge already in the graph | the sync IDs whose ways hold the edge take the copy: O(those) | |
| chained read grant (x, y), a new edge | | per sync ID whose open set holds x and not y: walk what y opens beyond it, take the writes naming each newly opened holder (holder index): O(what it opens + its writes) |
| first write-giving part on an edge moves (a write grant with an earlier key than every held one: the late backdated grant; or it is forgotten) | each removal-shaped part on the edge counted again: one now taking write away arrives as above; one no longer, or one whose witness moved, recomputes the sync IDs holding it: O(removals on the edge), rare | |
| record edge added (`add_record`) | as a new write grant edge, then every removal on it counted (no witness) | as a new read grant edge |
| chained write naming holder h | | the sync IDs whose open set holds h take it: O(those) |
| content, junk (unchained), finalize, vouch, a fresh read grant or a reader's removal (taking no write away) | none (counted 0) | a read grant as above |
| forget or chained flip to false of a part reach used | each sync ID whose reach seeds hold the copy or whose ways hold the edge computes its reach from scratch: per such sync ID O(what leads into its unit) (count: 5a + 6 each, b of them, an unreached sync ID nothing), then closes again if a seed left | a read edge gone: sync IDs whose open set held both ends recompute open and travel seeds; a write gone: the sync IDs it travelled under drop it |
| thing joins or leaves S's unit | S's reach from scratch: O(what leads into the new unit) | none (open runs from the actor) |
| sync ID registered | from scratch | from scratch |

A forget is the shrinking direction: recompute per affected sync ID (not reference counts: paths share edges and the graph has cycles, so counting support is a closure of its own). Forgets of role commits are rare (role commits are kept; a forget is a release or an unsync), so it is the rare path.

**Memory**: the graphs, O(held chained edge writes + records); per sync ID its reach seeds (O(removals reaching it, their witnesses, the grants on their ways off its unit)), ways and open set, and the same again in the reverse indexes. Organisation of 1,000 documents (each its own sync ID, O writes each, a member writes each through [O]) and 100 members removed from O (`an_organisations_documents_each_carry_every_removal_above_them`, flat in documents and in readers added and removed): per document 200 reach seeds (each removal and its witness, the member grant its reset names too) and one way, and 201 required copies (each removal and its grant, plus the writer's grant) against 1 with reach off. In memory, engine IDs (commit ID 32 B, tid 15 B: a copy ID 47 B, a seed entry about 100 B with its reason, BTreeMap overhead included roughly 130 B, a reverse index entry about 20 B): reach seeds and their reverse entries about 30 MB for the 100,000 (removal, document) pairs, and the required set (output, two copies per removal) about 30 MB more.

**Storage every reader pays (reach on).** A removal taking write away on a thing whose write grants or record edges lead into a unit is required there for good while held (reach reads grants at any time), with its witness (the grant its reset names, where the holder was granted write first): every sync ID under a hub carries every writer removal above it. In the organisation above, w writers removed and x readers granted and removed: 2w + 1 copies per document, independent of x, against 1 with reach off (held-model `open16_a_document_holds_two_copies_per_writer_removal_whatever_its_readers`, engine `an_organisations_documents_*` at x = 0, 100, 1,000); a node holding all 1,000 documents' sync IDs logs 200,000 entries at w = 100 (each copy one blob held once, wrapped under each sync ID: spec/sync-judgement.md §1). A copy measures 782 B (header 331 B, `roles.0` delta 85 B, the rest blob framing and the section wrap; osis-sync `reach_cost::*`, by hand), so a reader of one document fetches 1.6 KB per writer removal more. Today that reader already holds every writer removal on the hub: the hub's and the document's later keysets carry it (spec/crypto.md §4 Carried demotions, which A2 keeps), and each removal also rotates the document: measured 21 KB per removal per first read of the document (80 KB at r = 1, 143 KB at r = 4), so reach adds under 8% there, and nothing for readers (carried demotions leave a reader's removal out; so does reach). A3's leaves do not shrink it (a removal's root and role entries stay whole, OPEN 4).

## Leaves (A3; notes/a3-spec/permissions.md §4 the leaf bullet, §5.1 Reading a list)
A lowering L in S's set (a member, or a body: a held copy on a unit thing) requires its leaf for thing t where
- **reads**: L **cuts** (removal-shaped and taking write away on its edge: a record edge, or a held chained write giving write or above ordered before it: reach's witness test, `takes_write`; L itself chained or not, as the model's `cuts_write`) and some body of S on t has a content-class part (content, an addendum) whose known path uses L's edge, in any order (finding 27: a lowering cutting nothing is read by no verdict);
- **home**: t is not L's own thing and L is a body of S (findings 30, 34: a far leaf stays wherever its lowering is a body, so every holder of a removal holds what any runner carries; its own thing's leaf leaves with the last body it reads).
A held content-class copy on t **waits** for L's leaf for t where L is held (any chainedness), its root holds a pair for t, it cuts, and the copy rests on its edge, and the node lacks the leaf (P7). An **addendum** is a body only while it lists a held copy with a content part resting on its removal's edge; one listing nothing it can keep brings nothing (no atoms, no rest, no leaf).

**The index.** Global: per (edge, thing) the held write parts with a leaf for that thing (`leaf_on`); per edge and per thing the copies with such parts (`leafy_on`, `leafy`); per (edge, thing) the held content-class copies resting on the edge (`rests_on`); per listed copy the addenda listing it (`listers`); per held copy whether it is live. Per sync ID: `rest`, the bodies with a content-class part on each (edge, unit thing); `leaves`, per lowering copy in the set the things it requires. No scan of removals or bodies: a change sends exactly the (sync ID, lowering) pairs it can move to decide again (`settle_leaves`), and a sync ID whose closure was rebuilt decides its lowerings again (members plus the leafy bodies of its unit: O(closure), not O(bodies)).

| change | leaf work |
|---|---|
| a body arriving on t in S | its rest counts; where one passes 0 → 1, the lowerings of that (edge, t) with a leaf decide again: O(r), r the leaf-bearing lowerings of its edges for t, nothing of other edges or things (count: 2r checks, a later body 0, flat at 10, 100, 1,000 others) |
| which leaves judge a held part (`reading`, `waits`) | O(r) lookups: the leaf-bearing lowerings of the edges it rests on for its thing (count: r, flat in others) |
| a leaf arriving (`judged_by`) | the held parts resting on its lowering's edge on its thing: O(those) (count: n judged, flat in m resting elsewhere) |
| a lowering with leaves entering S's set (a member by the closure, or a body) | its own leaves: O(its leaves) |
| what cuts on an edge moves (its first write-giving part, a record edge) | each leaf-bearing lowering of the edge decides again in each sync ID it is in: O(those × their sync IDs), rare (as reach's recount) |
| a forget, a chained flip to false, a retag | the closures rebuilt decide again whole: O(closure), with the close itself |
| an addendum's listed copy arriving, leaving, or learning its path | the addenda listing it decide again whether they are bodies: O(those) |

**Memory per sync ID**: its rest counts (O(edges its bodies rest on × their things)), and its leaves: the far leaves of its own removals at home (k per removal listing k other things), plus one per (cutting lowering in the set, unit thing a body rests on) (counted: a document keeps its own leaf alone, the removal's home all k, at k = 10, 100). A required leaf is a (lowering, thing) pair; its bytes are brief 2's.

## The duty's targets (A3; notes/a3-spec/permissions.md §4 The delivery duty)
For each held removal R taking write away on (h, t) (reach's removals: chained, a witness held or a record edge), the things reached from t over the **duty's walk**: the held chained writes giving write or above, the record edges, and the chained write-giving writes the node reads for the duty (`duty_add`), at any time, followed to the end (finding 1), through things the node cannot read too (it may hold their grants otherwise). Each reached thing is a **target** where the node's own role there (`set_role`) gives read or above (finding 32: per target, not per removal); its **mode** is stamp where that role gives write or above, deposit where it gives read (`runs`: the one predicate a narrowing of who runs, OPEN 2, changes; it is given the removal too). A thing with no sync ID of its own (a dependent) is the caller's to skip: its enclosing units' readers require the removal by reach from its own sync ID's (draft: "each sync ID enclosing a reached thing gets R from that thing's own sync ID").

**Standing** (finding 3): kept as the graph, the removals and the roles move: per removal the things it reaches (each with the edge first reached by), and the reverse thing → removals. A removal arriving (or starting to take write away) walks from its target: O(what it reaches) (count: 2r + 1 for r reached, flat at 10, 100, 1,000 unreached things). A new edge (a, b) in the walk (a held write grant, a record edge, a duty read): each removal reaching a and not b walks on from b: O(what it newly reaches). An edge gone (forget, chained flip, duty read released): each removal reaching both ends walks again from nothing (rare). A removal leaving (forgotten, no longer taking write away): its targets go. Roles are read at query time (`duty()`), so a role moving costs nothing and shows at once. A hub of d documents: d + 1 targets per removal at this node (count 2d + 1 walked at d = 10, 100): N devices × D documents across an organisation, as For Tess 3 states; per node memory O(targets of its removals).

## Two mechanisms carry writer removals (design for A3: not built)
- **Carried demotions** (spec/crypto.md §4): a keyset of M lists the explicit demotions below write or admin its rotator holds applied on M or below it (the walk records a demotion on everything the lowered edge's target reaches); a keyset is held only with the edge writes of the commits it carries. So every reader of a thing whose keyset (or an opener's keyset sealed to it) carries a removal holds that removal: carried to whoever reads the keys, as keys, from the rotation on. It exists so a reader can judge a keyset (a removed party's later keysets and rotations invalid), and so a removal reaches readers by the rotation it forces. It carries no reach (no grants on the way) and nothing for a removal no rotation follows (a reader's).
- **Reach** (this note): a removal taking write away is required under every sync ID its target's write grants and record edges lead into, with its witness and the grants on its ways, for good, whether or not anything rotates: so a stolen key of the removed party cannot write any thing it could ever reach without that thing's readers holding the removal. It exists for evidence (finding 18: a removed writer's other route), not keys.
- Today and after brief 3 both deliver the same writer removal to the same sync IDs as the same (removal commit, thing) copy: the keyset's carried demotion is the removal's `roles.0` delta on t, which is reach's copy. Brief 3 must log it as **one copy identity**, whichever rule names it (one blob per sync ID, the required set's union), never a carried-demotion blob and an evidence blob; the 21 KB a first read costs is mostly the keysets' key material, not the removal.
- **Two mechanisms at A3** (notes/a3-spec/README.md, One mechanism or two; OPEN 8): not folded; the keyset's demotions become a seed of the required set (one copy identity with reach's). The fold considered: it is a function of held commits, needs no keys, covers removals no rotation follows and is what the errand delivers. Carried demotions then stop being a delivery path: a keyset still *names* the demotions its rotator held (the judging rule is about the keyset's validity), but a reader holds them because the sync ID's evidence requires them (reach, or the path clause), not because the keyset does; "a held keyset needs the edge writes it carries" becomes "a keyset is judged against the removals the sync ID's evidence holds", and a carried demotion reach would not require (a demotion admin → write, which takes no write away, on a thing whose readers read through it) is required by the keyset clause alone, as today. To check first in the model: held-model has no keysets; the A3 brief either adds them (a keyset per rotation naming the held demotions) and checks that every carried demotion a reader's keyset judging reads is in the required set under reach, or states the demotion-to-write residue as the keyset clause's only remaining copies.

## Incremental
State per sync ID: `atoms` (per edge, the number of bodies bringing it: the bodies' own contribution), its travel (open set and seeds) and reach (seeds and ways), and the closure over those (`members`, `edges`, vouches waiting for a member on their thing). Travel seeds are kept incrementally (a chained write naming an open holder added or taken out directly), so a write naming an actor reads none of its earlier ones. Global indexes over the held copies: chained writes per edge, chained grants per edge (ordered: the first one decides joined-ness), parts per resting edge ordered by `pos`, chained writes per holder, chained vouches per keyed copy, resetters per creation, held copies per thing; reverse: edge → sync IDs using it, copy → sync IDs holding it as a member, thing → sync IDs whose unit holds it; the reach and keys-open indexes above. Joined-ness is per part, global (no sync ID in it).

Monotone changes extend the closure from what they add (one BFS step function, `close`); a change that can shrink it (a forget, a chained write falling, a part no longer joined, a retag) marks the sync IDs it touches dirty, and each dirty sync ID is closed again from its atoms and seeds (O(its closure), never O(its bodies)).

| change | what it does | cost |
|---|---|---|
| body appended on a path of k edges | k atom increments in each sync ID whose unit holds its thing; an edge new to S closes from it | O(k log) per such sync ID, independent of history |
| copy (role commit) arrives with a chained write on edge e | e → sync IDs; added to each and closed from it; a chained grant moving e's first grant meeting a need earlier (grants are indexed per (edge, need): each grant under every need it meets, at most eight) rechecks joined-ness of the parts resting on e with that need ordered after it | O(sync IDs using e + what it brings + parts newly joined); no body scan |
| junk (an unchained edge write) arrives | indexed, its own joined-ness computed; nothing else | O(its parts' hops) |
| path entry opens (`open_path`) | the part's atoms added where it is a body | O(k) per sync ID |
| chained flips true | as the arrival of that part | as above |
| chained flips false, forget | sync IDs holding the copy as member, or whose atoms drop to zero, dirty; first grant moving later unjoins the resting parts in between | O(dirty closures + changed parts) |
| body forgotten (compaction, purge) | its path's atoms out; S closes again only where the body was the last on an edge (its writer's last body on S, or the last through that path) | O(k) per body; a close O(S's closure + held vouches), one per `forget_all` batch |
| thing joins S's unit (b bodies) | b bodies' atoms; S dirty if it held copies on the thing | O(b·k + closure) |
| thing leaves | b bodies' atoms out; S dirty | O(b·k + closure) |
| reach and keys-open travel | as Reach and keys-open travel says | indexed: a change reaches only the sync IDs it can move |
| from scratch for S | every body on its unit's things, then seeds, then the closure | linear in bodies·k + closure |
Memory per sync ID: its used edges, its members and their reasons (the output), atom counts per used edge: O(edges its paths use + copies), not O(bodies). Global indexes: O(held parts · k), the size of holding the parts.

**The engine's memo machinery.** The module is pure: it takes changes by call (`add`, `forget`, `forget_all`, `set_chained`, `open_path`, `join`, `leave`) and does work in proportion to them. Brief 3 drives it from `memo::Feed`/`Effects` followers over the held set, the units and the walk's chained flips, as `sync/certified.rs` follows `Indexes::chains_on` and `apply/paths.rs` follows `paths_unknown`; the derivation itself keeps no stamps.
