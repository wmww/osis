# Certificates
> Summary: Why signed role commits are the certificates (spec/permissions.md §4), what they leak, why a copy counts per sync ID, the cut (a copy holds the parts its sync ID's readers read, closed over evidence; boxes cut per holder, the sync-only cut without a header, its models and what is left), current `keys.0` and its measured cost, how long certificates a node took are kept (needs, dropped at a pass end), and the rejected alternatives. Which copies a sync ID requires: notes/evidence-derivation.md.

Principle: **signed role commits are the certificates** and travel under the sync IDs where they are needed: checking a write on N needs the role grants of N's record chain and of the things the author acts through, which only holders can read. No verify role, no certificate format, no extra key.

## What travels
- Leaks: a reader of L learns, for every author under L, every path it stated to the things it writes there (every edge write on those paths' edges, applied or not, each `created.0` record and hence that thing's dedup secret), never whole rosters. Author identity is inherent anyway.
- **Per sync ID, not per node**: a certificate delta already under the sync ID counts whoever logged it, so a joiner logs only the copies its own writes need and holders do not all re-copy a rotated thing's new `keys.0`. Sound because a copy counts only once this node checked every delta of it against the header's delta hash (opening checks the prefix only, so before the check anyone able to deposit under the sync ID could post the header with garbage deltas and stop the real copy; the same check guards `carried_by` and the duplicate and strict-subset judgements), only while held in the node's own store and at every source of the sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4), and, for a rotation commit of the sync ID's thing, only under the newest epoch. Older epochs otherwise count: a newcomer gets the history boxes with its grant and opens older copies like older content; counting only newest-epoch copies would make every holder re-copy every copy after each rotation (40 KB per holder per chat removal, measured). Residue: a holder of a link key alone (newest secret, no history box) reads none of the older entries. Deletion power is the same either way (a copy commits to its commit's delete keys).
- Deltas the sync ID's bodies carry (things of its unit) are never copied; a certificate's deltas on things outside the unit are, one copy per (commit, thing), cut (below). Every holder syncing S logs what S's required set lacks at once, another node's demotion or `keys.0` included (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates: none waits for the signer); copies are idempotent, so a holder learning of a rotation before the remover's copy arrived logs its own (3.3 KB per holder per removal).
- **Current `keys.0`** (`Engine::current_keys`, spec/permissions.md §4): every held commit whose `keys.0` body has a write live in the thing's `keys.0` state (a compaction snapshot among them), so a card, holder keys or public key record rebuilds the state whole: the newest keysets with their rotation's box records, and whichever commits last wrote the sync ID, deposit seed and step keys. One rule for every reader of "current keys"; a narrower one (newest rotations and the newest `keys.0` commit) left a stranger with a rotated inbox's keysets but no step, so no read key in force to seal a fire to (`cards::holder_keys_rebuild_the_whole_keys_state_after_a_rotation_*`). A keyset then often reaches a node before any secret of its thing: `Core::unboxed_epochs` gives its mint admin's nodes the derived read epochs, reading only, so they still open the thing's sync ID. Cost: one more commit per thing whose newest `keys.0` carries no keyset. Joiner's bytes down, measured on a chat: 100 members syncing with one peer, about 2.0 MB (the extra commits add 0.2%); in another measured run, 795 KB, of which the extra commits are 139 KB (21%).
- Accepted: revocation reach is eventual (L's readers accept a revoked node until some holder of the revoking thing syncs L); a record chain's depth equals nesting depth, at most `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH` (64) record links (deeper nesting would make every standing query cost more).
- Rejected: a transitive "verify" role (outside collaborators learn full rosters; every rotation re-wraps to everyone below); per-commit certificate chains (a few hundred bytes per commit against a 300-byte floor).

## The cut (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies)
Principle: **a copy carries, of its commit on its thing, what the sync ID's readers read there, and the evidence that judges it.** A box they do not open or a keyset nothing of theirs seals to is no part of it.
- **Per part, boxes per holder**: the signed header binds each delta by its hash, so a copy holding some parts is checked part by part (Logging certificates), and a role commit applies part by part (a missing part is a late part, notes/whole-commits.md): a cut copy is judged as the whole one. `secrets.0` is the one aspect whose readers differ within a delta (each box opens for one holder), so its write comes in **pieces**, one per holder and one of the rest (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces), each a part with its own header hash, judged alone: a stub or fire carries its holder's piece and, where that holder reads X, the rest (the history boxes, keyed with no holder), never another holder's; a holder of only sync gets its piece alone (`cut_parts`: the sync role's history box names X's departed holders). The rest rides along because the holder opens the history boxes by the epochs its own box gives, while the commit's body under X's sync ID is wrapped under an epoch they supersede (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what), one a new holder lacks: without it a grantee with history reads nothing before its grant (osis-sync's must-hold check caught every such reader). Cost: about 34 B of header and 32 B of delta framing per holder for every reader of the commit, against 1.2 KB a role a holder in each unit carrying the rotation (figures: issues/account-evidence-per-unit.md). Judged alone means a grantee's box stands beside a junk box of the same commit (whole, the junk voided both); judging the pieces as one would read a cut copy otherwise than the whole (held-model `box_cut` `Rule::AspectVerdict`). Rejected: one aspect per holder (its AK names the holder in every header, a roster to every copy's reader, or needs a blinded name stable across epochs that only readers compute); pages per holder (content-defined boundaries are the paging rule; a page link and blob cost more than a hash); a Merkle root in the header (O(log k) per carrier, but a second binding mechanism beside delta hashes, for a header already O(k) in a k-thing commit).
- **Sync-only cut: the sync ID alone** (spec/wire.md §4 Sync-only cut): a holder of only sync judges nothing of X (no evidence, X kept unread), and a header names the signer and its path, holders of X it may not learn (spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read: "knows none of its role holders"). So it carries no header and no box: a node reading the unit learns X's sync ID from it and takes its sync secrets from its entries there (spec/sync-ids.md §3), each proving itself by deriving the sync key its block is signed by.
- **Kept unread** (spec/sync.md §3): `Core::unread(X)` is the one derivation of what a node keeping X unread holds: the sync secrets of X its applied role commits gave (`own_record`'s held sync epochs), kept only where a verified step bears the sync key; a sync-only cut gives only X's sync ID (`Core::cut_sync_ids`). X's record alone is no such word: it says only how X was minted, which a rotation may since have changed.
- **What reads what**: an edge write's verdict reads its path's edge writes only (the timeline is over edge-class parts, spec/permissions.md §5.1); a `keys.0` part's reads its own delta and its path's edge writes; a `secrets.0` part's those and, for a may-grant grant, its commit's `roles.0` on the thing (§2: boxes to its grantees only, judged from the commit alone; a may-grant grant writes no `keys.0`). That one cross-part read is why the cut adds the commit's edge-class parts beside a box; beside a `keys.0` part they carry the rotation's own edge writes (a locator's redeemer learns the public's removal from its keys copy). Holder keys (a card, a stranded report) hold each commit's `sync.0` beside its keys cut: a commit recording a step writes its step keys beside it. Per part matches the engine (`validity::rotation_required`, `note_required`'s `overreach`); spec/permissions.md §3 says so.
- **Outsiders judge by order**: a reader outside A holds A's keysets (keys copies) and none of A's boxes, so it judges A's epochs by their keysets' lists and order alone; grants and re-seals record nothing in `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records: a rotation's records serve Foreign), so A's public key record does not grow with its grants. What that gave up: notes/holder-actors.md (Stale boxes, box records).
- **One copy per distinct cut**: S's cut of (commit, thing) is S's; two sync IDs with equal cuts share a blob, others encrypt one each. Rejected: one copy per part (shareable by every sync ID, but a header per part: a grant's copy pays two, about 290 B more); a sub-part cut (below).
- **Model**: held-model `copy_cut` (`spec_copy_cut`): an account A of 2-4 nodes (grants by admins and by may-grant readers, junk boxes, rotations, removals, the nodes' keyset rotations, some held by the reader by another route), T minted with admin A as S's unit (bodies along [node, A, T], T's rotations boxing A), X granting T read (by its admin or a may-grant reader). The reader judges bodies, which of T's epochs it may encrypt under (a box to A at a superseded epoch of A: by order, as it holds none of A's boxes), A's keysets it seals to, the X secrets it takes and X's steps: whole copies and the cut agree on 3000 worlds. Control, failing a hand world: no evidence for a `keys.0` part (an admin's rotation unjudgeable: its keyset not sealed to). `roles.0` beside a box is no control there: a holder's cut holds the `roles.0` part naming it anyway. held-model `box_cut` (`spec_box_cut`): X granting a holder T (sync or read) and others, by admins and may-grant readers, junk boxes beside grantees', rotations and grants with history boxes (each earlier secret of the role and the rotation's names), removals, forged boxes, junk steps; a reader of T's cut takes from T's pieces and the rest what it takes from whole copies, and a holder of only sync learns no holder of X (its removal by another admin included) and is judged holding sync exactly while X's newest step bears its boxes' secret, on 3000 worlds. Controls, each failing a hand world: the whole part (the roster shown), pieces judged as one (cut and whole differ), a header beside a sync-only cut (the rotator shown), no step check (a forged secret taken), no rest (a grantee lacks the epochs before its grant), the rest to a holder of only sync (the sync role's history box names departed holders), a removal with its header (the remover shown), holding by any box (a removed holder kept), read by any held write (a forged write naming T read: the granter shown). The sender judges what T holds by the valid writes it holds; forged edge writes naming T read (a holder neither admin nor may-grant: invalid; one holding no role: pending) change nothing R receives.
- **Pieces in the engine**: a part is a header entry (`osis_commit::AspectEntry`, one per piece; its index the part index the wire and every engine index use), so holding, validity, `ou/`, late parts and the meta row are per piece with no other key. The writer splits a role commit's `secrets.0` at build (`apply::keys::secrets_pieces`, `Fragment::split_by_key`) after shuffling its records' ops (`write::shuffle_records`); a receiver drops a piece holding two holders' records, or a holder's beside another, as not matching (`osis_commit` `PieceShape`); `holders_named` names each piece's holder (the cut's key). Per piece: validity (a junk box beside a grantee's), a rotation made by the rest piece alone (`keys::invalidated`) with its keyset valid (`keys::keyset_validity`: a rest piece is history boxes alone, a history add's shape needing only the role it names, so a reader's rotation would otherwise count). Per aspect: the state joins every valid held piece (`atomic::rebuilt`); a state stored as a body names the commit, its pieces joined (`Tx::stored_body`); retention dates, keeps and drops a commit's pieces on the aspect as one body (`retention::body_parts`), as they join as one delta (spec/crdt.md §2.4). The part standing for a commit on an aspect (`Meta::aspect`, every per-aspect reader: rows, listing, purge, covers) is a held piece where any is (valid first), never one the node lacks: a holder holds its piece without its siblings (another holder's, or the rest it never gets), and a body judged by its first piece went undated (`tests_validity` ). The rest goes in a cut only to a holder reading X (`cut_parts`: none to a holder of only sync or a removed one). A role compaction snapshot of `secrets.0` is pieced alike (`retention::snapshot::secrets_pieces`): a holder's piece states only its own fragment's tables, the rest piece (always written) the snapshot's whole, so a holder's piece names no other holder's commits; receivers hold it to the piece shape as a role commit's. Its index entry joins its held pieces (`atomic::note_snapshot` joins tables, `PartsCarried` per part), covering reads its held pieces joined (`snapshot::snapshot_at`, `retention::join_gives`), and only the part `View::aspect` names lists what it carries in the walk (`validity::read`: one `WriteId` a snapshot, as `carriers` names it). With no piece held, `Meta::aspect` takes a dropped piece before one never got. Tests: `tests_validity` `a_grantees_box_stands_beside_a_junk_box_*` (each piece a piece, any two joined none); engine `scale::removing_a_node_*`; `a_readers_rotation_rotates_nothing_*`.
- **Left** (issues/account-evidence-per-unit.md): `Contents` paging (spec, not built); a held copy holding more than S's cut is no log of it but is not removed (outside the required set only when disjoint from the cut, `unnamed_copy`).

## Kept while needed (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting, What a held thing needs; `retention/needed.rs`)
Certificates a node took (records, `keys.0` commits, rotations, an inbox's record) stay while a held thing needs it and goes whole, as never received. No count of its own.
- **Needs**: strongs in the current values or held history of a thing in the node closure (`refs::to` less fires, `refs::holds_target`, ephemeral aspects included; `refs::kept`, fires included; a weak only points and keeps nothing (Tess); a thing kept only as a certificate needs nothing by what its state names, so needs stay bounded by what the node holds); an account's inbox (its `keys.0`) and profile; `Held::named_on` (records naming it mint admin, edge writes naming it holder); a thing in the node closure an edge write on it names holder (the holder's sync ID carries the grant); the pending commits it signed or whose record or chain they await (`Indexes::pend_by`, `pend_on`), by the things they write on; a held commit acting as it (`Indexes::by_actor`; Interim, its one purge author), by the things it writes on: the record judges the commit (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable), so dropping it would leave an applied commit unjudgeable, and pending it again would make the record awaited, keeping it; and a held commit on it with a part missing, by the things those parts are on (`Meta::missing_parts`: what that part brings may reference it, so a commit received in part keeps the things it makes until it is whole; a part stopping missing notes the commit's things, `Core::note_on`).
- **Dropped when the pass that brought it ends**: what arrived (a record learned or awaited) or lost a need (a strong row or an account's `keys.0` row gone, a signed commit left pending, a holder unnamed) is noted in `Core::pass_due` and judged by `Engine::settle_needs` (sync: each complete pass's end, `status::live`; tests: `LocalPeer::sync`, `sync_all`, `spread`, the order harness's delivery end; the first call after an open judges every held and awaited record); a release judges what it released in its own transaction. So what a node holds outside its closure at quiet is a function of the commits it holds and its node closure, never of arrival order. A thing whose record something pending awaits is held for itself (`Root::Awaited`): nothing on it applies before its record, so what came for it pends, kept while carried (Pending storage, `apply::carriers`: gone with its last carrier) and applying when the record comes, so a commit reaching a node a pass before its thing's minting is not lost. Not per transaction: an ancestor's record (another account's root, a chain) arrives before what needs it, and judging each transaction dropped it at once and forced a refetch into every one-shot delivery.
- **Certificates alone outside the node closure** (`needed::Holding`, the one rule a pass end and a release judge by): a thing outside the node closure that something needs keeps its record, its role-class parts (`retention^.0` is content) and its secrets (`forget::certificate`, `Fate::Local`), never content, whether or not it was ever in the node closure. Rejected: a release keeping a still-needed thing whole (smaller, but a node then keeps content of a thing it lost access to while, say, an edge write on it names a held holder). Tests: `closure::a_thing_once_in_the_node_closure_holds_as_one_never_in_it_*`, `a_needed_account_keeps_its_role_class_parts_alone_*`; work per pass end, `needed: things judged`/`searched`, at n and 10n held.
- **Content outside waits, never applies** (spec/commits.md §4 Outside the node closure; `needed::applies_on`, `apply::Reason::Outside`, `Wake::Enters`): a received content part on a thing not held whole waits under Pending storage and applies in the transaction the thing enters (`apply::wake_entered`, run to a fixpoint after `units::settle`, and again after the end-of-transaction flows' own writes bring a thing in, so a commit minting a thing with its strong applies whole). Nothing of a thing outside is ever applied and stripped, so the pass-in-flight exception of the pull anew (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) only ever skips what the node never held or still holds. A waiting part is carried only while the node cannot judge its thing in no synced sync unit: once it holds the thing's minting commit (`needed::minted_here`, a held `created.0` part), the thing outside the closure is in no unit it syncs, and at the pass end the part counts as never received (`needed::uncarried`, `apply::unreceive_outside`, in `needed::settle`); a later strong then finds the record held, or brings it in a late part of that commit, and pulls anew (the exemption, `Core::brought`, names only things a minting of arrived while the node held none in the pass in flight, nothing of them forgotten since: notes/node-closure.md, Fetched again): forgetting keeps the minting known while a part of it may still bring the thing in (`forget::forget`'s `keeps`), so a thing with no minting held has no content dropped. Judged on the held mintings, never on the arriving commit being new: a minting needs no authority, so a writer minting a released thing again with a strong to it would otherwise keep its dropped content from being fetched (`closure::a_thing_minted_again_with_the_strong_back_is_pulled_anew_*`). Before that (record from a record blob or fire alone) a strong may still come with the record in one transaction, pulling nothing, so the part waits while carried and roots its thing (`Root::Awaited`). Rejected: keeping every waiting part while any entry holds it (the same commits end differently by arrival order: a thing held a while then released forgets its content, one never entered kept it rooted, `order::a_thing_held_for_a_while_reads_as_one_never_held_once_the_pass_ends_*`); a durable mark per forgotten thing (new state with its own flood bound); pulling anew on every strong newly reaching a thing outside (O(unit) per mint at every reader). Tests: `closure::a_part_outside_the_node_closure_waits_beside_one_in_it_*`, `a_record_arriving_with_the_strong_*` (D before W's record blob and C), `waiting_and_pulling_anew_cost_the_thing_not_the_node_closure_*` (n vs 10n); tests/it/outside.rs (a grant making a strong valid wakes, content dropped as not carried pulled anew, storage at n and 10n). `disputes::content_dropped_as_not_carried_comes_back_with_a_minting_valid_again_*` (the minting's strong invalid, the thing gone whole, then valid again).
- The judgement walks needs up to the first rooted thing, O(1) while needed, brute force over the unrooted component when not (cycles of holder actors go together). A pending commit wholly on things that go leaves with them (`forget`).
- **Keeping an account on purpose is holding something of it**: a contact is a strong to its profile (which needs its mint admin); the harness's `Peer::know` writes a strong to the account itself in its node root's `holds.0` (the profile tid is not known before the chain arrives; `LocalPeer::of`, `sync`, `sync_all` and `flows::spread` introduce their peers); keys held here keep nothing: a link being redeemed keeps its sync ID in the sync set, so what a pass end drops of K comes again, and the join is written once a pass brought what it needs.
- **Nothing is kept for whose it is**: a node's own released things (minted with admin its node thing or node root) go as another's. A stranger's chain goes at the pass end that brought its fire, the fire staying (the weak to its own account a fire carries keeps nothing; the sealed fire's blob carries its chain for any other reader); a co-admin's with the last held thing it holds a role on or a held thing holds a strong to. Rejected: a need from each held commit to its author and actor. Every author but a sealed fire's holds a role where it wrote (named holder, or through holder actors that are), and a fire's verdict and listing need no chain once judged, so it kept only strangers' chains, for nothing (`strangers::a_kept_fire_keeps_nothing_of_its_sender_*`).

## Validity does not decide evidence
Evidence takes every edge write on a stated path's edges whatever its validity (notes/a2-spec/README.md Open 2). The read graph is no evidence: whether a holder's pieces carry headers reads the writes valid here (Sync-only cut above).
