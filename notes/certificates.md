# Certificates
> Summary: Why signed role commits are the certificates (spec/permissions.md §4), what they leak, why a copy counts per sync ID, the cut (a copy holds the parts its sync ID's readers read, closed over evidence; boxes cut per holder, sync copies without a header, its models and what is left), current `keys.0` and its measured cost, how long certificates a node took are kept (needs, dropped at a pass end), and the rejected alternatives. Which copies a sync ID requires: notes/evidence-derivation.md.

Principle: **signed role commits are the certificates** and travel under the sync IDs where they are needed: checking a write on N needs the role grants of N's record chain and of the things the author acts through, which only holders can read. No verify role, no certificate format, no extra key.

## What travels
- Leaks: a reader of L learns, for every author under L, every path it stated to the things it writes there (every edge write on those paths' edges, applied or not, each `created.0` record and hence that thing's dedup secret), never whole rosters. Author identity is inherent anyway.
- **Per sync ID, not per node**: a certificate delta already under the sync ID counts whoever logged it, so a joiner logs only the copies its own writes need and holders do not all re-copy a rotated thing's new `keys.0`. Sound because a copy counts only once this node checked every delta of it against the header's delta hash (opening checks the prefix only, so before the check anyone able to deposit under the sync ID could post the header with garbage deltas and stop the real copy; the same check guards `carried_by` and the duplicate and strict-subset judgements), only while held in the node's own store and at every source of the sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4), and, for a rotation commit of the sync ID's thing, only under the newest epoch. Older epochs otherwise count: a newcomer gets the history boxes with its grant and opens older copies like older content; counting only newest-epoch copies would make every holder re-copy every copy after each rotation (40 KB per holder per chat removal, measured). Residue: a holder of a link key alone (newest secret, no history box) reads none of the older entries. Deletion power is the same either way (a copy commits to its commit's delete keys).
- Deltas the sync ID's bodies carry (things of its unit) are never copied; a certificate's deltas on things outside the unit are, one copy per (commit, thing), cut (below). Designed, not built (issues/sync-unbuilt.md, facade): another node's demotion or `keys.0` may be left to its author for `SETTLE_MS` (30 s) after this node received it (the remover must log them): otherwise every holder learning of a rotation before the remover's copy arrived copies it (3.3 KB per holder per removal); measured from receipt, not the commit's `ts`, which its author sets.
- A removal of G's grant goes in G's key part under G's sync ID as well as with the target: a node of G's that no longer syncs the target learns it there, and is opened the target's sync ID no more (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part).
- **Current `keys.0`** (`Engine::current_keys`): a thing's newest `keys.0` commit may be an inbox or deposit seed write, so certificates carry that and the rotation commits of its newest keyset per role. A keyset then often reaches a node before any secret of its thing: `Core::unboxed_epochs` gives its mint admin's nodes the derived read epochs, reading only, so they still open the thing's sync ID. Cost: one more commit per thing whose newest `keys.0` carries no keyset. Joiner's bytes down, measured on a chat: 100 members syncing with one peer, about 2.0 MB (the extra commits add 0.2%); in another measured run, 795 KB, of which the extra commits are 139 KB (21%).
- Accepted: revocation reach is eventual (L's readers accept a revoked node until some holder of the revoking thing syncs L); a record chain's depth equals nesting depth, at most `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH` (64) record links (deeper nesting would make every standing query cost more).
- Rejected: a transitive "verify" role (outside collaborators learn full rosters; every rotation re-wraps to everyone below); per-commit certificate chains (a few hundred bytes per commit against a 300-byte floor).

## The cut (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies)
Principle: **a copy carries, of its commit on its thing, what the sync ID's readers read there, and the evidence that judges it.** A box they do not open or a keyset nothing of theirs seals to is no part of it.
- **Per part, boxes per holder**: the signed header binds each delta by its hash, so a copy holding some parts is checked part by part (Logging certificates), and a role commit applies part by part (a missing part is a late part, notes/whole-commits.md): a cut copy is judged as the whole one. `secrets.0` is the one aspect whose readers differ within a delta (each box opens for one holder), so its write comes in **pieces**, one per holder and one of the rest (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces), each a part with its own header hash, judged alone: an edge piece, stub, fire, sealed copy or offer carries its holder's piece and, where that holder reads X, the rest (the history boxes, keyed with no holder), never another holder's; a holder of sync alone gets its piece alone (`cut_parts`: the sync role's history box names X's departed holders). The rest rides along because the holder opens the history boxes by the epochs its own box gives, while the commit's body under X's sync ID is wrapped under an epoch they supersede (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what), one a new holder lacks: without it a grantee with history reads nothing before its grant (osis-sync's must-hold check caught every such reader). Cost: about 34 B of header and 32 B of delta framing per holder for every reader of the commit, against 1.2 KB a role a holder in each unit carrying the rotation (figures: issues/account-evidence-per-unit.md). Judged alone means a grantee's box stands beside a junk box of the same commit (whole, the junk voided both); judging the pieces as one would read a cut copy otherwise than the whole (held-model `box_cut` `Rule::AspectVerdict`). Rejected: one aspect per holder (its AK names the holder in every header, a roster to every copy's reader, or needs a blinded name stable across epochs that only readers compute); pages per holder (content-defined boundaries are the paging rule; a page link and blob cost more than a hash); a Merkle root in the header (O(log k) per carrier, but a second binding mechanism beside delta hashes, for a header already O(k) in a k-thing commit).
- **Sync alone: no header** (spec/wire.md §4 Sync copy): a holder of sync alone judges nothing of X (no evidence, X kept unread), and a header names the signer and its path, holders of X it may not learn (spec/requirements.md, Data a node cannot read: "knows none of its role holders"). Its copies are its piece and X's `sync.0` delta, no header; a box proves itself by a step of X's sync ID bearing its secret's sync key (spec/crypto.md §4 Established), which is also what makes a copy count. Model: held-model `box_cut` (`Rule::SyncHeader`, `Rule::NoStepCheck` controls). **Sync alone** is one predicate, `Core::sync_alone_on(h, x)`: no held edge write on X valid here gives H read or above (self-writes aside) and H is not X's mint admin: no edge of the evidence derivation's read graph (`Core::edge_gives_read`, the one reading of a read edge, also the derivation's `gives_read`), so every path reads the same answer, a removal included (by the roster now, a removed holder's whole edge piece went with headers). Read is by valid writes so that no invalid or pending write changes what H's readers receive: a writer of H or of X, or an offer on H's inbox, gets any edge write held, and one giving H read counted whatever its validity turned H's pieces and the real grant's header into certificate copies (key part, Role changes travel, and the forged write's own evidence, every write on (H, X)). Evidence itself stays validity-free: only which holder's pieces carry headers reads a verdict, the sender's choice of what to send, which no reader relies on to judge. Models: held-model `box_cut` (`World::reads`; control `Rule::AnyValidity` shows the granter), `evidence` (`World::read_edges`, the engine differential's input). A holder no held write gives a role (a remover that never held the grant, `holding::a_removal_its_writer_made_blind_*`) is one of sync alone: the blind remover sends it nothing, and the removed reader is told by notices (notes/sync-opens.md Departed peers). A holder that read X keeps certificate copies for good (a lowering to sync, a later removal): its readers hold X's record and judge X's role commits; sync copies would freeze their roster. Engine (`sync_ids/unread.rs`): `osis_commit::SyncCopy`; every path to a sync-alone holder carries copies alone: the key part and a stub (`Certs::sync_copies` (commit, thing, holder), `edge_pieces_into`: a stub's pieces are all sync copies, T's keys not opening X), a grant, change or rotation while H holds a role (sealed form 2, `deliver_copy`/`deliver_sealed`, with copies of X's `sync.0` writes held), an offer (`offers::Pieces::SyncAlone`: taken only where one of its copies counts by a box, a refused offer's rows dropped). Role changes travel only on an edge giving read (`evidence`: a write travels where `reads` has its edge; held-model `evidence` seeds alike), so no write of a sync-alone holder travels.
- **Removal told by its step**: a sync-alone holder's removal goes nowhere (`deliver_copy`); the step past its keys is its notice, since a holder kept unread judges by steps (spec/sync.md §3 Holders by copies). `Unread::holders` names a holder only while a head (per sync ID the newest step held, recorded or a copy's; where X's admins' word is held (`Core::copies_speak`), a copy's steps only on a recorded sync ID: a child of a placed step is an admin's signature, a genesis anyone's) bears the sync key of a secret its box gives. The key part carries every `sync.0` write while H is named, the notice among them; outside H's writers, `issue_step` sends a holder of sync alone each step that bears, or whose parent bears, a sync key of a secret X's `secrets.0` boxes to it (`inbox::deliver_step`, keys by `apply::keys::sync_boxed`; the holder one an applied edge write gave a role, so a write of no authority (a removed admin's grant, deposited junk) makes no admin send to a holder of its choosing; the box of any validity, since a junk box names an epoch its writer knew, whose steps it could hand on itself: `tests_delivery::a_step_goes_to_no_holder_of_an_invalid_grant_*`) with X's other `sync.0` writes held that this node has not sent H (`Indexes::sync_sent`, rows `node/sync_sent/`: each write goes to H once, so k steps cost O(k) copies per holder, `tests_delivery::each_sync_write_reaches_a_holder_of_sync_alone_once_*`; another admin's step this node holds goes with its next one, `a_step_goes_with_another_admins_step_its_holder_lacks_*`): a box at an epoch whose step comes later counts by it, and the first step past its keys is its removal however many steps bearing them came between (a blocked rotation, another admin's step), and no later one. A lone step delta reads as nothing without the state it joins, so a copy's steps are read from its records (`steps::written_steps`, as a commit's evidence reads them), joined across every copy H's readers keep: a step verifies only with its whole path among them, so a copy lost at H's readers breaks every later step's path there. They keep each by their own log under H's sync ID (`sync_copies_due`), and the sealed deposit goes once logged (`SyncCopiesDue::logged_deposits`, spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Clearing deposits): left waiting, logged sealed copies filled the `DEPOSITS_WAITING` window, and clearing (no prefix expiry, so in blob order) dropped a new one before it was taken (osis-sync `growth::rotations_keep_keysets_flat_*` asserts each rotation's secret kept). Rejected: resending the whole path with each step to cover such losses (O(k²) copies per holder). The store side is unchanged: a Register past its keys stops it (spec/sync-judgement.md §6). Residuals: a step's copy arriving before the rotation copy boxing a kept holder reads as its removal until the rotation copy comes (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 accepts the same for a store: a late box keeps S again); a party able to bring a copy to H's readers (H's writers, a depositor) can keep H holding at nodes holding none of X's admins' word by a forged genesis bearing H's old sync key (it gains nothing of X: X's own sync ID stops), and, as a writer of H, can show H's readers X's writes on H's edge by a body stating a path through it (E1 evidence: spec/sync-hostile.md §2, issues/sync-alone-stated-path-evidence.md); a remover that never held H's grant takes H as one of sync alone and sends it nothing (a removed reader is told by notices, notes/sync-opens.md Departed peers). Model: held-model `box_cut` (`Seen::holds` against `World::t_holds`; controls `RemovalHeader`, `HolderKept`). Tests: `tests_delivery::a_sync_alone_holders_grant_lowered_travels_nowhere_*` (a lowering on a sync-only edge), `tests_delivery::a_forged_edge_write_giving_read_shows_a_sync_holder_nothing_*` (a writer of X's invalid write naming H read: no certificate copy, no header byte), `tests_delivery::a_read_grant_dropped_turns_a_holder_back_to_sync_alone_*` (a read grant applied, then dropped by its granter's concurrent removal: travels, then not; kept derivation the one derived anew), `tests_delivery::a_sync_alone_holders_removal_is_told_by_its_step_alone_*` (bytes: no fire, no certificate copy under T's or a reader's sync ID, sealed step copies without header bytes; T's reader names T holder no more), `tests_delivery::a_step_after_a_rotation_reaches_a_holder_of_sync_alone_*`, `tests_delivery::a_step_past_a_removed_holder_reaches_it_after_a_step_bearing_its_key_*`. A taken copy (sealed, opened under a sync ID in `open_entry`, framed as a record blob is, or offered) is a row of `su/` keyed by blob (`HeldSyncCopy`: the thing it came under, its holder (its piece's), sealed or not, content key), bad input where its piece holds two holders' records or a history box, or its holder is neither the thing it came under nor one that thing holds sync on with key access. A forger may name a sync ID of its own making (its own genesis bearing its own secret): at a node holding none of X's admins' word, kept beside the real one, never displacing it, since a node keeping X unread syncs every sync ID it keeps, and so does one reading X while the copies speak (`thing_sync_now`, spec/sync-ids.md §2; a reader syncing the greatest alone took a forged one above X's own in its place: `tests_delivery::a_record_alone_moves_no_copys_word_*`); at one holding that word, nothing (Kept unread).- **Kept unread** (spec/sync.md §3): `Core::unread(X)` is the one derivation of what a node keeping X unread holds: the sync secrets of X its sync copies' boxes give (opened by the holder's own held secrets, never a derivation that could ask X again) and its applied role commits gave (`Indexes::secrets`), kept (`secrets`: what the walk, ownership and independence read) only where a verified step bears the sync key: X's `sync.0` state as judged (an admin's word) and, only where the node holds none of X's admins' word on its keys (`Core::copies_speak`: no step X's `sync.0` places, no keyset part of X, no secret X's record chain derives), the copies' own steps (a genesis is anyone's making: beside that word a copy's step would make a dependent X count independent, or give a reader of X a forged sync ID in place of X's own; `tests_delivery::a_forged_*`). X's record alone is no such word: it says only how X was minted, which a rotation may since have changed, and a node reading X by its public entry takes it (a record blob under X's sync ID) before the role commits; judging by it alone made X dependent there with nothing left to fetch the role commits by (osis-sync `differential::a_public_reader_keeps_a_thing_on_taking_its_record_*`, engine `tests_delivery::a_record_alone_moves_no_copys_word_*`), every one held (`candidates`) for osis-sync's unread judgement, which takes a secret only by a step of its stored path (Registers: a rotation's copy arrives before any step bearing its secret reaches the node's `sync.0` view, and the unread store follows the registered head); the copies' steps joined (where they speak only); the copies that count, and the holders of those that count by a box (a copy boxing nothing, a `sync.0` write's, which any syncer of X holds and anyone may seal, log or offer, names no role: `tests_delivery::a_boxless_copy_names_no_holder_*`). Readers: `counts_independent`, `enter`, `holds_own_sync`, `held_sync_keys`, `owned_sync_ids` (X's `sync.0` state; where the copies speak, their steps), `kept_sync_secrets`, `head_keys`, `stubs_of` (a counted copy's holder holds sync on X).
- **Sync copies logged and bounded**: what S carries (`Engine::sync_copies_carried`, by content key): the copies its read things' certificates name of commits held here, and every counted row under one of them (a sealed copy's included: H's writers log what it carries). osis-sync's read logs one blob per content (`read::sync_copies`, `Live::sync_copies_due`: missing logged fresh by `seal_sync_copy`; of two blobs of one content in the own store, the lower blob ID is the log and the other is unlogged; an own blob of a copy carried no more unlogged; the log stamped in place under the head). Keyed by content, so a forged copy of the same commit (another box) is another copy and never displaces the real one. A row lives exactly as long as a store entry of its blob (`Engine::unnamed_copy`: the store's unnamed feed, a pulled copy not kept, a start's sweep), and that entry is charged to its bringer unless stamped (`Opened::SyncCopy` gives nothing held, spec/sync-ids.md §5): anyone may seal or deposit a copy, and a forged genesis counts where none of X's admins' word is held, so the bound is the bringer's budget. Readers with no record of X find its sync IDs and its holders from the rows (`ix.sync_copy_ids` for `sync_things_of`, `Core::syncs_by_copies` for a unit's carried edge and stubs, `Core::copies_naming` for held for keys and whose grants count (`rotation::lost::grants_count`), `pieces_on` for first entry). The copies live with entries under the holder's sync ID, so the holder is held for keys from the moment it leaves the node closure (`opened_holder` reads the closure, not things left and awaiting release): its sync ID stays synced and the copies stay. Tests: `tests_delivery` `a_sync_alone_holder_gets_no_header_on_any_path_*` (key part, grant, offer: bytes), `a_forged_duplicate_does_not_displace_the_real_copy_*`, `sync_copy_rows_go_with_their_entries_*`.
- **What reads what**: an edge write's verdict reads its path's edge writes only (the timeline is over edge-class parts, spec/permissions.md §5.1); a `secrets.0` or `keys.0` part's reads its own delta, its path's edge writes and, for a may-grant grant, its commit's `roles.0` on the thing (§2: boxes and records to its grantees only, judged from the commit alone). That one cross-part read is why the cut adds the commit's edge-class parts beside a box or record. Per part matches the engine (`validity::rotation_required`, `note_required`'s `overreach`); spec/permissions.md §3 says so.
- **Box records make boxes redundant to outsiders**: every box to a holder actor is recorded in `keys.0` in its commit (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), so a reader outside A judges A's stale boxes from the records its keys copies carry and never needs A's `secrets.0`. A record naming other than its box (a lying writer) is the stated case (D0_rs_lie); with whole copies an outsider saw a liar's box only where that grant happened to be evidence.
- **One copy per distinct cut**: S's cut of (commit, thing) is S's; two sync IDs with equal cuts share a blob, others encrypt one each. Rejected: one copy per part (shareable by every sync ID, but a header per part: an edge piece of a grant pays two, about 290 B more); a sub-part cut (below).
- **Model**: held-model `copy_cut` (`spec_copy_cut`): an account A of 2-4 nodes (grants by admins and by may-grant readers, junk boxes, rotations, removals, the nodes' keyset rotations, some held by the reader by another route), T minted with admin A as S's unit (bodies along [node, A, T], T's rotations boxing A), X granting T read (by its admin or a may-grant reader). The reader judges bodies, which of T's epochs it may encrypt under (a box to A at an epoch of A superseded or with a stale box or record), A's keysets it seals to, the X secrets it takes and X's steps: whole copies and the cut agree on 3000 worlds. Controls, each failing a hand world: no `roles.0` beside a box or record (a may-grant grant's record counts for nothing: a stale box unseen), no evidence for a `keys.0` part (an admin's rotation unjudgeable: its keyset not sealed to), no grant box records (finding 1's lever: below). held-model `box_cut` (`spec_box_cut`): X granting a holder T (sync alone or read) and others, by admins and may-grant readers, junk boxes beside grantees', rotations and grants with history boxes (each earlier secret of the role and the rotation's names), removals, forged boxes, junk steps; a reader of T's key part takes from T's pieces and the rest what it takes from whole copies, and a holder of sync alone learns no holder of X (its removal by another admin included) and is judged holding sync exactly while X's newest step bears its boxes' secret, on 3000 worlds. Controls, each failing a hand world: the whole part (the roster shown), pieces judged as one (cut and whole differ), a header beside a sync copy (the rotator shown), no step check (a forged secret taken), no rest (a grantee lacks the epochs before its grant), the rest to a holder of sync alone (the sync role's history box names departed holders), a removal with its header (the remover shown), holding by any box (a removed holder kept), read by any held write (a forged write naming T read: the granter shown). The sender judges what T holds by the valid writes it holds; forged edge writes naming T read (a holder neither admin nor may-grant: invalid; one holding no role: pending) change nothing R receives.
- **Engine** (`sync_ids/certs.rs`): `Cut` names what a copy is for (evidence, holder H's edge piece, keys) and `Engine::cut_parts` its parts on the thing; `sync_certificates` names cuts for required copies (`sync.0` where `Evidence::stepped`), edge pieces (`edge_pieces_into`: the key part, and a stub as the edge pieces of the holders its unit's thing holds its role through, `stub_holders`), public key records (the unit thing's mint admin's among them: its holder by the record edge), carried demotions and frozen history; `add_closure` cuts every closure copy to evidence. A fire's, sealed copy's or pairing offer's edge piece is cut alike, to its one holder (`Engine::edge_piece` takes the `Cut`; `Fire::Grant` names its holder: a commit re-boxing every holder, a cut grant or a removal, fires each its own piece, `a_fire_carries_its_holders_piece_alone_*`); an offer's trust records are cut to evidence (edge-class parts, no boxes). Logging: a copy blob is the body only holding exactly S's cut (`body_held_by`), and a held blob is relogged under S only so (osis-sync `log_own`).
- **Pieces in the engine**: a part is a header entry (`osis_commit::AspectEntry`, one per piece; its index the part index the wire and every engine index use), so holding, validity, `ou/`, late parts and the meta row are per piece with no other key. The writer splits a role commit's `secrets.0` at build (`apply::keys::secrets_pieces`, `Fragment::split_by_key`) after shuffling its records' ops (`write::shuffle_records`); a receiver drops a piece holding two holders' records, or a holder's beside another, as not matching (`osis_commit` `PieceShape`); `holders_named` names each piece's holder (the cut's key). Per piece: validity (a junk box beside a grantee's), a rotation made by the rest piece alone (`keys::invalidated`) with its keyset valid (`keys::keyset_validity`: a rest piece is history boxes alone, a history add's shape needing only the role it names, so a reader's rotation would otherwise count). Per aspect: the state joins every valid held piece (`atomic::rebuilt`); a state stored as a body names the commit, its pieces joined (`Tx::stored_body`); retention dates, keeps and drops a commit's pieces on the aspect as one body (`retention::body_parts`), as they join as one delta (spec/crdt.md §2.4). The part standing for a commit on an aspect (`Meta::aspect`, every per-aspect reader: rows, listing, purge, covers) is a held piece where any is (valid first), never one the node lacks: a holder holds its piece without its siblings (another holder's, or the rest it never gets), and a body judged by its first piece went undated (`tests_validity` `a_piece_held_without_its_siblings_is_dated_*`). The rest goes in an edge piece only to a holder reading X (`cut_parts`: none to sync alone or removed; `an_edge_piece_carries_the_rest_only_to_a_reader_*`, `a_grantee_reads_its_history_by_the_rest_*`, held-model `granted_after_a_removal`). A role compaction snapshot of `secrets.0` is pieced alike (`retention::snapshot::secrets_pieces`): a holder's piece states only its own fragment's tables, the rest piece (always written) the snapshot's whole, so a holder's piece names no other holder's commits; receivers hold it to the piece shape as a role commit's. Its index entry joins its held pieces (`atomic::note_snapshot` joins tables, `PartsCarried` per part), covering reads its held pieces joined (`snapshot::snapshot_at`, `retention::join_gives`), and only the part `View::aspect` names lists what it carries in the walk (`validity::read`: one `WriteId` a snapshot, as `carriers` names it). With no piece held, `Meta::aspect` takes a dropped piece before one never got. Tests: `tests_validity` `a_grantees_box_stands_beside_a_junk_box_*` (each piece a piece, any two joined none), `a_secrets_snapshot_travels_in_pieces_*`; engine `scale::removing_a_node_*` (one node's edge piece); `a_readers_rotation_rotates_nothing_*`.
- **Independent by a box**: an edge piece carries no `keys.0`, so a holder's readers hold X's secret before any rotation commit of X: X counts independent by that box where a step of X's `sync.0` (carried in the piece) bears a boxed sync secret's key (`Core::counts_independent`, `Core::own_record`: a box is any writer's, a step an admin's; held-model `independent`) for its secret actor (`secret_actor`, so `keys_open` reaches X's dependents through a bridge) and for reading X's sync ID (`thing_sync_now`); the epoch is established once X's rotation arrives under X's sync ID.

## Kept while needed (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting, What a held thing needs; `retention/needed.rs`)
Certificates a node took (records, `keys.0` commits, rotations, an inbox's record) stay while a held thing needs it and goes whole, as never received. No count of its own.
- **Needs**: strongs in the current values or held history of a thing in the node closure (`refs::to` less fires, `refs::holds_target`, ephemeral aspects included; `refs::kept`, fires included; a weak only points and keeps nothing (Tess); a thing kept only as a certificate needs nothing by what its state names, so needs stay bounded by what the node holds); an account's inbox (its `keys.0`) and profile; `Held::named_on` (records naming it mint admin, edge writes naming it holder); a thing in the node closure an edge write on it names holder (the holder's sync ID carries the grant); the pending commits it signed or whose record or chain they await (`Indexes::pend_by`, `pend_on`), by the things they write on; a held commit acting as it (`Indexes::by_actor`; Interim, its one purge author), by the things it writes on: the record judges the commit (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable), so dropping it would leave an applied commit unjudgeable, and pending it again would make the record awaited, keeping it; and a held commit on it with a part missing, by the things those parts are on (`Meta::missing_parts`: what that part brings may reference it, so a commit received in part keeps the things it makes until it is whole; a part stopping missing notes the commit's things, `Core::note_on`).
- **Dropped when the pass that brought it ends**: what arrived (a record learned or awaited) or lost a need (a strong row or an account's `keys.0` row gone, a signed commit left pending, a holder unnamed) is noted in `Core::pass_due` and judged by `Engine::settle_needs` (sync: each complete pass's end, `status::live`; tests: `LocalPeer::sync`, `sync_all`, `spread`, the order harness's delivery end; the first call after an open judges every held and awaited record); a release judges what it released in its own transaction. So what a node holds outside its closure at quiet is a function of the commits it holds and its node closure, never of arrival order. A thing whose record something pending awaits is held for itself (`Root::Awaited`): nothing on it applies before its record, so what came for it pends, kept while carried (Pending storage, `apply::carriers`: gone with its last carrier) and applying when the record comes, so a commit reaching a node a pass before its thing's minting is not lost. Not per transaction: an ancestor's record (another account's root, a chain) arrives before what needs it, and judging each transaction dropped it at once and forced a refetch into every one-shot delivery.
- **Certificates alone outside the node closure** (`needed::Holding`, the one rule a pass end and a release judge by): a thing outside the node closure that something needs keeps its record, its role-class parts (`retention^.0` is content) and its secrets (`forget::certificate`, `Fate::Local`), never content, whether or not it was ever in the node closure. Rejected: a release keeping a still-needed thing whole (smaller, but a node then keeps content of a thing it lost access to while, say, an edge write on it names a held holder). Tests: `closure::a_thing_once_in_the_node_closure_holds_as_one_never_in_it_*`, `a_needed_account_keeps_its_role_class_parts_alone_*`; work per pass end, `needed: things judged`/`searched`, at n and 10n held.
- **Content outside waits, never applies** (spec/commits.md §4 Outside the node closure; `needed::applies_on`, `apply::Reason::Outside`, `Wake::Enters`): a received content part on a thing not held whole waits under Pending storage and applies in the transaction the thing enters (`apply::wake_entered`, run to a fixpoint after `units::settle`, and again after the end-of-transaction flows' own writes bring a thing in, so a commit minting a thing with its strong applies whole). Nothing of a thing outside is ever applied and stripped, so the same-transaction exception of the pull anew (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) only ever skips what the node never held. A waiting part is carried only while the node cannot judge its thing in no synced sync unit: once it holds the thing's minting commit (`needed::minted_here`, a held `created.0` part), the thing outside the closure is in no unit it syncs, and at the pass end the part counts as never received (`needed::uncarried`, `apply::unreceive_outside`, in `needed::settle`); a later strong then finds the record held, or brings it in a late part of that commit (never learned, `hold_record`), and pulls anew: forgetting keeps the minting known while a part of it may still bring the thing in (`forget::forget`'s `keeps`), so a strong whose record comes in a commit held nothing of brings in no thing whose content was dropped. Before that (record from a record blob or fire alone) a strong may still come with the record in one transaction, pulling nothing, so the part waits while carried and roots its thing (`Root::Awaited`). Rejected: keeping every waiting part while any entry holds it (the same commits end differently by arrival order: a thing held a while then released forgets its content, one never entered kept it rooted, `order::a_thing_held_for_a_while_reads_as_one_never_held_once_the_pass_ends_*`); a durable mark per forgotten thing (new state with its own flood bound); pulling anew on every strong newly reaching a thing outside (O(unit) per mint at every reader). Tests: `closure::a_part_outside_the_node_closure_waits_beside_one_in_it_*`, `a_record_arriving_with_the_strong_*` (D before W's record blob and C), `waiting_and_pulling_anew_cost_the_thing_not_the_node_closure_*` (n vs 10n); tests/it/outside.rs (a grant making a strong valid wakes, content dropped as not carried pulled anew, storage at n and 10n). `disputes::content_dropped_as_not_carried_comes_back_with_a_minting_valid_again_*` (the minting's strong invalid, the thing gone whole, then valid again).
- The judgement walks needs up to the first rooted thing, O(1) while needed, brute force over the unrooted component when not (cycles of holder actors go together). A pending commit wholly on things that go leaves with them (`forget`).
- **Keeping an account on purpose is holding something of it**: a contact is a strong to its profile (which needs its mint admin); the harness's `Peer::know` writes a strong to the account itself in its node root's `holds.0` (the profile tid is not known before the chain arrives; `LocalPeer::of`, `sync`, `sync_all` and `flows::spread` introduce their peers); keys held here keep nothing: a link being redeemed keeps its sync ID in the sync set, so what a pass end drops of K comes again, and the join is written once a pass brought what it needs.
- **Nothing is kept for whose it is**: a node's own released things (minted with admin its node thing or node root) go as another's. A stranger's chain goes at the pass end that brought its fire, the fire staying (the weak to its own account a fire carries keeps nothing; the sealed fire's blob carries its chain for any other reader); a co-admin's with the last held thing it holds a role on or a held thing holds a strong to. Rejected: a need from each held commit to its author and actor. Every author but a sealed fire's holds a role where it wrote (named holder, or through holder actors that are), and a fire's verdict and listing need no chain once judged, so it kept only strangers' chains, for nothing (`strangers::a_kept_fire_keeps_nothing_of_its_sender_*`).

## Validity does not decide evidence
Evidence takes every edge write on a stated path's edges whatever its validity (notes/a2-spec/README.md Open 2). The read graph is no evidence: which holders' role changes travel, and whether a holder's pieces carry headers, reads the writes valid here (Sync alone above).
