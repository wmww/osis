# Keys: what an epoch covers
> Summary: Why an epoch covers exactly the departures its rotator named while holding the role (frozen coverage, spec/crypto.md §4 Covers), the pin over a holder's tail of grant writes and Met, departs marks, box-held departures, the accepted re-grant leak, and the attribution rules that failed. Models: core/test/tla/rotation/Tail.tla, Cover.tla.

Principle: **only the rotator's word counts, and only while it holds the role**; boxes decide nothing, because a missing box means either "excluded" or "unknown".

## Frozen coverage
- **Frozen coverage** (spec/crypto.md §4 Departures and covering): an epoch covers exactly the departures from its thing that its rotator named, or made in the rotation commit itself. A history entry covers nothing unless a held valid keyset body of the epoch (a rotation commit's `keys.0` delta or a compaction snapshot) publishes its names' digest, by an author holding the role on the thing now (`members::role_for_keys`: every `until` disregarded, through holder actors only by grants with key access; counting a keyless path let a writer holding read only through a keyless grant keep its made epoch current after its self-removal's rotation: `leaver_through_keyless_grant_spec_crypto_4`). The entry's departs mark covers the rotation commit's own departure while the node it names holds the role.
- Why only the rotator's word: the departed party can forge every other shape (an entry at an epoch it holds listing its self-removal in a backdated commit, a backdated rotation of its own naming it, a rotation in its self-removal, an entry naming a predicted departs mark) and holds the role in none of them. The honest cost is nil: a rotator lowered below r after rotating r has a departure of its own that its epochs of r do not name, so r is due anyway (a rotator of a current epoch holding less than its role now, by any path with key access, departed it).
- **Why not boxes**: any rule reading coverage from boxes ("holds no box for M") credits an epoch with a departure its rotator never knew. Shape (C2; TLA AMBIGUITIES R19): G grants D at e0; a2 rotates to e1 before reading the grant (e1 boxes no D); a1 holds the grant, not e1, and removes D as e2 (a fork of e1); W, holding e1, e2 and the removal, takes e1 (lower nonce), which holds no box for D, so "covers", and writes under it; a2, holding e1 and the grant but not the removal, re-seals D at e1; D reads W's content.
- Rejected: binding an entry to its writer by its CRDT dot (a compaction snapshot carries foreign dots, so a hostile compactor could forge the writer); names in cleartext in `keys.0` (O(history) bytes in every keyset, and other things' departure commits named); reading names only from the rotation commit's own delta (re-opening them when a secret arrives later needs the stored bodies, and a snapshot has none); requiring every node to rotate itself for each departure it learns (two writers each see the other's merge as foreign and rotate again: endless).

## The pin
- **The pin** (the one property the covers rule states; spec/crypto.md §4 Covers): an epoch e of r covers holder M's grant departure at node Z when e's rotator, holding r, pinned M below r over every write on M's grant that Z holds and that could hold M at r. With M's tail the writes on its grant after the newest one Z applies that gives M r or more, e names (or, by its departs mark, is) a tail write taking M below r that takes at Z (`GrantWrite::takes`), and names void every tail write giving M r or more that counts (its author held write at its point). Then every node V where e is established holds the write taking M below r and judges each named write as the rotator did (**Met**, `NamesHeld::met`: a void name only where its write applies nothing; an applied name, and the departs mark's commit, only where its write stands, `GrantWrite::stands`), so V boxes M into e only on a write Z has not received.
- Why Met asks that applied names stand: met by holding the commit alone, a V lacking what voids the demotion of a removal's author (a demotion of the demoter on a holder actor it wrote through, which no name of the thing carries) judged the removal void, met the names and re-sealed M (`applied_name_void_elsewhere_through_holder_actor_spec_crypto_4`; Tail.tla `T_chain_commit`). Why the pin asks a write that takes, not one that stands: met only where the write takes never establishes a finalize's rotation where the grant it ends was renewed since (`renewed_holder_resealed_spec_crypto_4`; `T_fin_takes` fails `Live`; `T_fin_pinstands`).
- Each coverage case is a case of the pin: a raise that applies nothing at Z is a tail write needing a void name (`raise_named_without_the_removal_before_it_spec_crypto_4`); an audit drop is a write applying nothing, named void by itself, not by the demotion that dropped it; a dropped write that lowered M is no tail write giving r; concurrent removals each pin (`concurrent_removals_fork_once_spec_crypto_4`); every void raise since the removal must be named, an older one included. A tail write applying nothing at Z pins only where it would apply over the grant before the tail took M down: a finalize is judged by the grant it ends, so one voided at Z by a renewal, or by its author's demotion Z holds, takes nothing (`T_fin_any`).
- Names (`GrantLog::names`, read by `departures::names` from the same `grant_logs` as `direct_departures`, so a rotator's own epoch covers what it knows; else a livelock): every applied write lowering a holder, and every write applying nothing that counts and gives a role (void), each by commit key and holder (a commit's writes are judged one by one).
- The tail starts after the newest applied write giving the role, not after the anchor, because a finalize anchor takes only over the grant it ends (`T_fin_fromanchor`). For an explicit removal anchor that asks more than safety needs: concurrent removals of a holder with an audit-dropped grant before them take one more rotation (Cover.tla `C_dup_audit` fails `DupCovers`; no leak). Liveness: two rotators alternate at most once per write one holds and the other does not.
- Rejected: attributing a departure to one event (its newest lowering) and covering it by a name of that event: each shape the event missed took a patch, and each patch left a hole the next shape found; the pin is what they approximated. Naming what took a raise's author's right instead of the raise (one rotation per demotion, not per write): sound only by recursing through the author's own raises and every path that gave it authority. An epoch boxing the holder covering nothing (box removals by a superseding rotation not yet established here hide it).
- Tests: osis-keys `covers::pins_hold_the_tail_spec_crypto_4` (write by write); the engine counterexamples `regrant_invalid_here_*`, `raise_*_spec_crypto_4`, `newest_event_names_departure_spec_crypto_4`, `audit_dropped_grant_named_spec_crypto_4`. Model: Tail.tla (the pin over every small grant history and view triple, against the rejected newest-event attribution), Cover.tla (dynamic).

## Box-held departures
A box holder or rotator no grant shows departed (`Departure::grant` none) is covered by nothing until a rotation supersedes the epoch: a box at a current epoch to a non-holder makes that epoch's rotation due. Such a departure concerns the box's or made epoch's role alone: spanning every role up to it, a link's sharer that left kept the link's admin epoch (which nobody may rotate) counting against the link's other roles, and nodes rotated for it for good.

## Departs marks
The one name its author can predict (`ts` and node). Establishment reads a mark as met by any held resolved role commit of that `ts` and node with a `roles.0` delta on the thing (`NamesHeld::departed`), and holds the epoch back until the removal's grant writes are held and stand (`Held::departed`), a snapshot keying the epoch included; coverage reads it only as the tail write of that commit, from an entry bound to a keyset by a holder, while that node holds the role, so a leaver naming or predicting its own mark covers nothing. Rejected: binding a mark to a rotation commit of that epoch (a node holding the epoch by a snapshot alone lacks that commit's body and would rotate needlessly: `compacted_rotation_covers_its_names_spec_crypto_4`). Tests `rotation::tests_departs` (one per forged shape) and the rotation property, whose leavers forge each shape at random.

## Accepted leak (Tess)
**A demoted admin's void re-grant**: a write that does not count is not pinned. Admin P, concurrently demoted to reader, re-grants removed reader X; wherever the demotion is held the grant is void, yet X keeps the key P gave it and reads new content until something else rotates read (`invalid_grant_box_judges_nothing_spec_crypto_4`; Cover.tla `C_regrant_reader` fails `NoLeak`, run as the accepted case). Demoted to writer instead, the void grant counts and forces a rotation. Tess accepted it (kept out of spec/requirements.md; fix only if a way without significant trade-offs appears). Rejected for their cost: pinning void re-grants by readers (any reader forces one rotation per junk commit); one rotation per demotion (a recursive rule over how P got its rights). P, still a reader, could hand X the key anyway.
