# The node closure (engine)
> Summary: How the engine holds spec/sync-set.md §1-§2: the node root, the closure as one spanning tree over (context, thing) vertices followed per edge, the node's role, how a grant is learned, the making rule, work counts, fetching again at a new strong, and what carries a pending commit, with the state table of a thing outside the closure.

Spec: spec/sync-set.md, spec/sync-inbox.md §5. Model: notes/closure-model.md (held-model `closure`). Code: core/engine/src/sync/units.rs (module doc: the structure and its costs), flows/roots.rs, sync/set.rs, retention/release.rs, apply/carriers.rs (module doc: the carrier invariant). Fires and deposits: notes/sync-inbox.md.

**Principle:** a node holds exactly what its node root reaches by strongs, sync unit by sync unit; the structure is kept by edits, each costing what it changed.

## Design rules (Tess)
- **A strong grants nothing.** It says the target belongs to this closure; anyone knowing a tid may write one. Permission is a separate act by someone who may grant, and removing a reference revokes nothing.
- **A thing goes when its last reference goes** (spec/sync-set.md §3). That is the intended result, never a risk to design around: nothing is kept because a release might be a mistake.
- **Core holds only what cannot be built correctly on top of it.** Home (the account, a schema-level realm: notes/realms.md), which offers to act on, contact lists and "what is on my other nodes" are built above it.
- **Lean on osis for storing data.** Node and account state are ordinary things and aspects. No aspect is exempt from sync; what is local is local by where it sits.
- **A weak keeps nothing.** What a node keeps outside its closure is only what held things need (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs). A contact is a strong to its profile.
- **Nothing grows only.** No set of everything ever seen or granted. Every stored row has an owner (Storage owners, below).

Why a sync unit stops where its root has no read access: a closure granted read on a large thing carries just what it references of it, so one row of a table syncs without the table and without keys per cell (spec/requirements.md, no unit synced whole). So Alice's A, admin of T, references T; Bob, a reader of A, adds a strong to T from his B; Alice removes hers: B never carried T (Bob reads T, B does not), so T is gone for both, unless Alice shared T with Bob or with B (sync `holding::a_strong_grants_nothing_and_its_removal_revokes_nothing_*`).

An edit of a thing carried under two sync IDs reaches a node syncing only one of them through a node that syncs both (notes/closure-model.md (d)); in an admin dispute the sides differ on what is valid, and so on what belongs.

Kept, each following from a rule above (don't simplify them away): release's one wait (an own self-removal); the retained copy (an option keeping its property); fetching again at a new strong (rare-path brute force); a stranded notice acted on whatever the history setting.

## Storage owners
Tess: every stored row is attributable to what the node holds, and releasing everything drops storage to the node's own things. A row lives exactly while its last owner does and goes in the transaction that owner goes; nothing waits for a release's writes to be held elsewhere (spec/sync-set.md §3). Owners:
- **Whole things**: the node closure. Such a thing keeps all it holds: content, role-class aspects, secrets, derived rows; a thing outside it is kept only while a held thing needs it (`retention/needed.rs`).
- **Store entries** of each sync ID in the sync set (or leaving it), limited by the required-set judgement (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): unit bodies, lasting headers, certificate copies, deposits. Engine data taken from an entry about no whole thing is owned by the entries carrying it.
- **Peer records**: what a connection proved, owned by the record, the record by its members (the sync IDs both share), bounded by `RECORD_BUDGET`.
- **The node's identity rows** (`seed`, `hlc`, `u/`, `d/`), held unconditionally.

Links between owners are counts over acyclic structure (entry → blob `X/`; blob → commit `bp/`, `cb/`; commit → thing; thing or commit → derived rows keyed owner-first), so no walk beyond the closure is needed. Releasing everything also leaves, each bounded: inbox fires for the inbox's history setting, a link's sync ID while redeemed, role changes under a former grantee's sync ID for `ROLE_KEEP`, tombstones for `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`. Tests: osis-sync `growth::` own things, expiry, held history, grant and decline, redeem (storage after releasing equals a fresh node's).

## Roots
- The node root: made at the first start with the node thing (`flows::roots::first_start`; local record `records::NODE_ROOT`), holding a strong to it. The node writes it as its node thing, in commits of its own (a commit writing it beside a shared thing travels with that thing: issues/node-root-independent.md). Making an account (osis-account) adds the root's strong to the account A.
- **First entry** (spec/sync-set.md §2): the walk enters an independent thing at a strong only where a unit it syncs carries it, whole or as a stub. Own things enter unconditionally.

## The structure (`Closure`: journaled, built again by an open)
The vertices, contexts (`Ctx`) and out-lists are units.rs's module doc and `given_by`; the rule is spec/sync-set.md §2. Beside it:
- Per-commit judgements (`BodyCtx::holds`) and own commits read `Closure::unit_holds` (stamped per (context, thing)) and `member_feed`, so they move with what entered or left, never with the unit's size; `sync_unit(T)` (whole) is for listings and whole derivations. `units_holding(t)` names every sync ID carrying a thing, which is why an independent thing inside a read unit is entered though it holds nothing more.
- Followed per edge by an `Effects` key; strong rows appearing or going come from the reference index (`refs::note` → `Closure::note_row`), since store table reads stamp a whole table. `units::settle` runs at each transaction's end and after an open. Only a rollback of a transaction whose settle ran refollows everything (`Follow::rolled_back`, O(closure)).
- Two trees over the same out-lists (`Tree`): the node closure's and the sync set's (`Closure::synced`), where an edge into a thing sourced elsewhere (`cut`, spec/sync-set.md §5, set by `Engine::set_sourced`, kept by a reload) leads nowhere; it follows `moved` edges at each settle's end. `in_sync_things`/`things_entered` read it per thing and by feed; `sync_things` is the whole, for listings.
- A thing out of the node closure at a settle's end is noted in `left` with every context it left that transaction, until `released` (notes/sync-leave.md).
- Work counts (`closure:` costs; engine `closure::removal_costs_what_it_held_spec_sync_set_2`, `closure::a_sourced_thing_costs_what_is_below_it_spec_sync_set_5`, both at n and 10n): removing a non-tree referrer, a tree edge with referrers outside, or one with an alternative path of any depth costs the same at both sizes; releasing a subtree of n costs 3n; with A sourced elsewhere, a thing entering or leaving is constant, moving A away constant in the closure's size and linear in what is below A.
- Checked: `units::check_whole` (`Engine::check_closure`, inside `check_index`; the closure tests run it after every edit). It shares `given_by`, so `given_by`'s own rules need behaviour tests (`units_holding`, `not_carried`).

## The node's role
spec/sync-set.md §2 Own things. Oracle: held-model `closure` (`hc::closure`: the walk by the own things' roles over the world's grants), which the sync differential's `Model::closure` runs over its history.
- `rotation::role_here` (and `Engine::role_on`): the best the node's own things (spec/permissions.md §1: the node thing, a link key it redeems by) hold by the thing's grants as the node holds them, with key access, where those grants count (`rotation::lost::grants_count`: the node syncs the thing or holds its stub). What the node does as one of its actors reads that actor's role (`Core::role_now`), as a judgement of any other party does.
- A role that grew opens the boxes kept out for want of it (`flows::retake`): after every re-judge, and as a notice is taken. The closure reads the role and the role reads the closure, so `units::settle` loops with `retake` until no box is due.
- Lost access keeps what that same role reads (`retention::forget::read_path`). An ended edge's holder (`lost::was_ours`, read by `loss_seen` and `may_learn`) is an own thing or one an own thing reads by the held grants, read without `grants_count`: once the edge naming a holder ended nothing finds it from below, its own secrets go, and a rule asking whether it still counts would never see the loss.
- Load order: `apply::load` derives which things are independent (`keys::refresh_independent`) before any box opens, since the role reads the thing a dependent's secrets derive from; opened first, a rebuild counted a not-yet-independent thing through its mint admin and held secrets the live index did not (`Engine::checked_index` fails on it).
- A role read moves with the edge writes on the things on its way (`Timelines`' `Held(thing)` stamp, `osis_perm::Changed::held`), never with its signer's other commits (osis `making::making_an_independent_thing_costs_what_it_changed`, `tracking::tracked_role_reads_follow_their_thing_at_n_and_10n`).
- A thing that is no account is offered a grant by a party that does not write it only while its `keys.0` names an inbox thing whose setting enables `grant@~0` (spec/sync-inbox.md §1; none unless its admins write both): a holder thing granted a role by another party's admin tells its readers nothing until then (the grant stands on its target either way). Kit, the app and chat enable no external signal on what they mint (Tess: an account's inbox enables both, nothing else by default), so a stranded notice from a non-writer of such a thing is invalid (issues/shared-things-refuse-readers-stranded-notices.md).

## Learning a grant
- **By syncing the target**: a grant is the target's own data.
- **Before the target syncs, by a held grant signal** (spec/sync-inbox.md §5): optional; a grant nobody is told of is a valid state.
- **Independent here**: `Core::counts_independent` (doc comment).
- **Openers**: a holder that can open more (`flows::take_up_opener`, `reopen_dependents`) opens the boxes keyed to it and, in turn, to what they give; boxes the node's roles kept out are taken where the roles now give them (`flows::retake`).
- **S's things** (`Engine::sync_things_of`): the sync set's entry is all of them (`rows::fold`), never one by tid. Per-S rules run for each: `read_things_of`/`opening_things` (opening, credentials, bodies joined by `join_body`, lists, records), `body_thing` (sealing, logging, promotion), `judge_peer` the best of each. Path rules use `recorded_owner`/`read_sync_id`.

## Writing and making a thing
- **A node writes only what it holds** (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing): `Core::wrote_on`, `units::check_written` after the settle, `Error::NotHeld` (osis `Invalid`). Checked as written, so holding and writing in one transaction, or a write that releases its thing, passes. Without it a node could write a thing it was granted but never held and read its own write there for good, out of every closure, never released.
- Owed writes follow it: an external fire goes as a write only on a thing held and read (`inbox::fire`, else sealed); `write::writable_split` holds back what goes to a thing not held. Without it an owed addendum on an unheld thing would fail every ingest transaction.
- Making the next thing costs what it changed (osis `making::`, every counted kind at n and 10n, the least of three makes so a compaction's spike is spread): no per-thing or per-sync-ID memo reads a whole unit, a whole permission component (`Timelines::on`/`role`, `Core::bound_to_horizon`), a whole store table, or every sync ID's body of a commit (`Engine::sync_body`; `Live::may_log` tries S's own entries first). Certificate records are followed per record (`CertRecords`, `Engine::cert_carriers`), and a new leaf's record judges no component's key access. What a thing costs: notes/thing-cost.md. Things an app keeps account-wide and dependent are minted with the account as admin.
- Harnesses that carry commits by hand carry fires apart, as sync's fire step does: the engine's `LocalPeer::pass_fires` (in `deliver`, `deliver_sealed`, `sync_changed`; `pass_fires_to`, `hold_fires`, `take_fires`), public `osis` `internals::pass_fires`, osis-sim `Sim::fires` (lost whole at the drop rate); each offers a sealed fire by `Engine::offer_fire` (the order harness's `take_fires` through `Peer::offer_fires`, which offers one not held again only once what its last offer read moved, as sync's inbox keys a deposit), and a fire refused though the setting enables it fails the test. A peer handed a thing's role commits by `LocalPeer::deliver` alone, fires held, neither syncs the thing nor holds an offer, and counts nothing.
- Test helpers hold what they make where it lives (`Engine::hold_where_it_lives`; osis `common::Held`; engine `common::new_thing` at every peer, `set_edge` by the flow, `edge_write` bare). `LocalPeer::hold`/`hold_at` write a strong from the receiving peer's node root, so a peer keeps another's thing only as a real node would: held, opened (a grant by the flow, not a raw edge write) and needed. Engine `net` peers know each other's accounts (`net_apart`: strangers); an observer is a reader (`common::reader`, `fresh_copy`) or a node of the maker's account (`net_one`, `pair_into`), given its access before what it reads, since content a node cannot yet open goes at the next need-judgement. A node of an account writes as the account, so a commit cannot mix the node root with account-written things.

## Not carried
The write path's warning reads the strongs the write's ops name (`osis_engine::strongs_in`, `Engine::not_carried_among`): O(the write). `Engine::not_carried` lists the whole node closure's, O(its strongs).

## Fetched again
- At a new read-unit edge to a thing the node did not hold when the transaction began (`units::refetch`, doc comment: the conditions; `Follow::before`/`known` decide "new"): the commits minting the thing lose their own tombstones and each sync ID the unit is held by (`sync_ids::actors::held_sync_ids`) is pulled anew from every source through the ciphertext door (`Cipher::unreceived`, `Cipher::refetch`: the sync store's `N/` mark); the sync store's follower reopens what was parked under it. So a thing released and reached again, record kept or not, or a creation opened before its strong, is fetched (sync `holding::a_thing_left_by_one_view_is_logged_again_by_one_still_holding_it_spec_sync_set_3`; engine `closure::a_thing_entering_again_with_its_record_held_is_pulled_anew_*`). A thing independent here rejoins the sync set at entry and is pulled from the start anyway. The exception for a thing a minting of arrived while the node held none in the pass in flight, nothing of it forgotten since (`Core::brought`, `Core::note_brought`: its `created.0` part applied, or its record taken alone from its sealed section with the commit, `Engine::ingest_carrying`; emptied at each pass end, `Engine::settle_needs`; a thing leaves it once anything of it is forgotten sooner: `forget::forget`, a release or lost access; `apply::unindex_unreceived`, a commit forgotten whole, its parts waiting on other things with it; `apply::unreceive_outside`; `carriers::forget`) ends alike either way (`closure::a_record_arriving_with_the_strong_*`): content outside the node closure never applies (notes/certificates.md, Content outside waits) and counts as never received only while a commit minting the thing is held and once the pass that brought it ends (`needed::uncarried`), the minting kept known by forgetting while a part of it may still bring the thing in (`forget::forget`'s `keeps`); so a thing with no minting held when the pass began has had nothing dropped, and a late part of a held minting, or a new minting of a thing with one held (`closure::a_thing_minted_again_with_the_strong_back_is_pulled_anew_*`), is no exemption, nor is a thing released in the pass (`closure::a_thing_released_in_the_pass_that_brought_it_is_pulled_anew_*`) or one a part waited on that went with its commit (`closure::a_part_waiting_on_a_thing_that_goes_with_its_commit_is_pulled_anew_*`). Lost access keeps the sync store's own entries, opened again when the read returns, so it drops nothing a pull anew must bring. In memory: an open starts it empty, so a strong then pulls anew. A thing another node makes, dependent (its minting carries the strong) or independent (its rotation mints it, its content commit carries the strong, later in the pass), therefore pulls nothing anew at a reader (sync `growth::a_thing_another_node_makes_pulls_nothing_anew_*`, `growth::an_independent_thing_another_node_makes_pulls_nothing_anew_*`). Rejected: a durable per-thing mark of something forgotten (new state, notes/certificates.md); the pass bound is the reason itself. Both halves: `disputes::content_dropped_as_not_carried_comes_back_with_a_minting_valid_again_*`.
- Forgetting as never received (`apply::unindex_unreceived`, `retention::forget::unreceive`) ends the node's own tombstones of the commit's blobs: a forgotten body leaves nothing keeping it out.
- Cost: a pull anew lists the unit's sync IDs whole at each source, once per such new edge, also where nothing comes of it (a strong to a gone thing). Rare-path brute force by intent; if it shows in counts, narrow the trigger.

## Pending carriers (spec/commits.md §4 Pending storage)
The invariant, who adds and who removes, and the bound: carriers.rs's module doc. One persisted set per pending commit (`Indexes::carriers`, rows `pc/`), settled at the end of every durable transaction after the closure and before the needs loop.
- Paths adding nothing: `ingest_valid(_, None)`, so what they leave pending goes in their transaction. A memory commit's copy is the memory lane's.
- A report taken under an earlier actor of its sync ID is replaced only within that actor (issues/stranded-report-actor-change.md).
- **Handed again** (`apply::rearrival`, arrival paths only): a copy that brings nothing (each part held, or byte for byte the stored pending copy under its signatures, not expired, no clock wake due) returns what an examination would at a lookup's cost; its carrier is still noted. Bytes decide, not the part's header hash: a pending commit may await the record its dedup secret comes from, and a differing part (forged, or true after junk) is judged (`with_pending`). Parked copies and memory commits take the full path. A re-arrival is never a retry: every ground change wakes what it frees (`waits::wake`), so a `Wake::At` already due takes the full path. Counted: engine `carriers::a_pending_commit_handed_again_costs_a_lookup_*`.
- **Restart**: rows reload (`carriers::load`); a fire or report carrier goes in the transaction its fire or holder goes; an entry gone while down is released by sync's start sweep.
- Tests: engine tests/it/carriers.rs; `rotation::tests_stranded::a_report_letting_go_forgets_what_only_it_carries_*`, `a_sealed_fire_carries_its_chain_until_it_goes_*`; `closure::a_commit_awaiting_its_record_is_kept_while_carried_and_no_longer_*`.

What a node holds of a part on thing T, by what it holds of T (each row the same in every arrival order, `closure::every_order_of_a_commit_its_record_and_its_minting_ends_alike_*`):

| T | certificate part (record, role-class, secrets) | content part | header of a commit missing a part on T |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| held whole (closure, leaving, retained copy) | applies | applies | kept, part fetched |
| outside, minting commit held | applies; kept while needed | waits; never received at the pass end (`needed::uncarried`) | kept while a part on a thing in the closure is missing, else goes with T |
| outside, minting commit not held (record from a record blob or fire, or none) | applies once the record is known | waits while carried, rooting T | kept while carried |

A commit whose every held part is unjudgeable pends whole (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable; `Exam::Awaits`): its header counts nowhere (removal lists, receipt) until its thing enters or its record comes. Applying the header alone would count a commit on aspects of things nothing here judges.
