# The node closure (engine)
> Summary: How the engine holds spec/sync-set.md §1-§2: the node root, the closure as one spanning tree over (context, thing) vertices followed per edge, roles taken up, tickets in the walk, the making rule, work counts, fetching again at a new strong, and what carries a pending commit, with the state table of a thing outside the closure.

Spec: spec/sync-set.md, spec/sync-inbox.md §5 (tickets). Model: notes/closure-model.md (held-model `closure`). Code: core/engine/src/sync/units.rs (module doc: the structure and its costs), sync/tickets.rs, flows/roots.rs, sync/set.rs, retention/release.rs, apply/carriers.rs (module doc: the carrier invariant). Fires and deposits: notes/sync-inbox.md.

**Principle:** a node holds exactly what its node root reaches by strongs, sync unit by sync unit; the structure is kept by edits, each costing what it changed.

## Design rules (Tess)
- **A strong grants nothing.** It says the target belongs to this closure; anyone knowing a tid may write one. Permission is a separate act by someone who may grant, and removing a reference revokes nothing.
- **A thing goes when its last reference goes** (spec/sync-set.md §3). That is the intended result, never a risk to design around: nothing is kept because a release might be a mistake.
- **Core holds only what cannot be built correctly on top of it.** Home, which invitations to accept, contact lists and "what is on my other nodes" are the app's.
- **Lean on osis for storing data.** Node and account state are ordinary things and aspects. No aspect is exempt from sync; what is local is local by where it sits.
- **A weak keeps nothing.** What a node keeps outside its closure is only what held things need (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs). A contact is a strong to its profile.
- **One mechanism.** A ticket is the one way to say "here is how to sync this", wherever it is stored; core never copies one.
- **Nothing grows only.** No set of everything ever seen, accepted or granted. Storage: issues/storage-owners.md.

Why a sync unit stops where its root has no read access: a closure granted read on a large thing carries just what it references of it, so one row of a table syncs without the table and without keys per cell (spec/requirements.md, no unit synced whole). So Alice's A, admin of T, references T; Bob, a reader of A, adds a strong to T from his B; Alice removes hers: B never carried T (Bob reads T, B does not), so T is gone for both, unless Alice shared T with Bob or with B (sync `holding::a_strong_grants_nothing_and_its_removal_revokes_nothing_*`).

An edit of a thing carried under two sync IDs reaches a node syncing only one of them through a node that syncs both (notes/closure-model.md (d)); in an admin dispute the sides differ on what is valid, and so on what belongs.

Kept, each following from a rule above (don't simplify them away): the refused removals of the node root's and D's strongs (Roots); D's tickets only for what is entered outside the local part; release's one wait (an own self-removal); the retained copy (an option keeping its property); an independent thing as its own inbox; nothing before the strong, so a grant counts only at a strong; a grant naming a holder in the holder's required set, needs and fire (one fact in three places); fetching again at a new strong (rare-path brute force); a stranded notice acted on whatever the history setting.

## Roots
- The node root: `Engine::make_node_root` (local record `records::NODE_ROOT`). The node writes it and its dependents as itself (`Core::nodes_own`, `made_nodes_own` for things a commit makes): no account holds a role there.
- The account's creation writes D's strongs and the node root's strong to D in its transaction; pairing writes the node root's (`Engine::paired`). No strong reaches a node thing: retention keeps every node the account's chain names, a removed one too, as the minter of rotations it follows (`retention::needed`, `AccountView::named_device`).
- The refused removals (spec/sync-set.md §1): `commit_in` compares the kept strongs before and after (`roots::kept`, `still_kept`), O(1), however the commit removed them.

## The structure (`Closure`: journaled, built again by an open)
The vertices, contexts (`Ctx`) and out-lists are units.rs's module doc and `given_by`; the rule is spec/sync-set.md §2. Beside it:
- Per-commit judgements (`BodyCtx::holds`) and own commits read `Closure::unit_holds` (stamped per (context, thing)) and `member_feed`, so they move with what entered or left, never with the unit's size; `sync_unit(T)` (whole) is for listings and whole derivations. `units_holding(t)` names every sync ID carrying a thing, which is why an independent thing inside a read unit is entered though it holds nothing more.
- Followed per edge by an `Effects` key; strong rows appearing or going come from the reference index (`refs::note` → `Closure::note_row`), since store table reads stamp a whole table. `units::settle` runs at each transaction's end and after an open. Only a rollback of a transaction whose settle ran refollows everything (`Follow::rolled_back`, O(closure)).
- Two trees over the same out-lists (`Tree`): the node closure's and the sync set's (`Closure::synced`), where an edge into a thing sourced elsewhere (`cut`, spec/sync-set.md §5, set by `Engine::set_sourced`, kept by a reload) leads nowhere; it follows `moved` edges at each settle's end. `in_sync_things`/`things_entered` read it per thing and by feed; `sync_things` is the whole, for listings.
- A thing out of the node closure at a settle's end is noted in `left` with every context it left that transaction, until `released` (notes/sync-leave.md).
- Work counts (`closure:` costs; engine `closure::removal_costs_what_it_held_spec_sync_set_2`, `closure::a_sourced_thing_costs_what_is_below_it_spec_sync_set_5`, both at n and 10n): removing a non-tree referrer, a tree edge with referrers outside, or one with an alternative path of any depth costs the same at both sizes; releasing a subtree of n costs 3n; with A sourced elsewhere, a thing entering or leaving is constant, moving A away constant in the closure's size and linear in what is below A.
- Checked: `units::check_whole` (`Engine::check_closure`, inside `check_index`; the closure tests run it after every edit). It shares `given_by`, so `given_by`'s own rules need behaviour tests (`units_holding`, `not_carried`).

## Roles taken up (spec/sync-set.md §2 Taken up, spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Nothing before the strong)
Goes at A2 (notes/holder-access-study.md, The direction, What goes at A2): accepting a grant, clauses 3 and 7, and every rule below reading "taken up"; clause 8 and the Tickets section's fetching on a ticket's word; the account's tickets (Tickets, below); the key-change ticket on a holder's inbox (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Key changes). Roles then never feed what syncs, and a ticket is only the offer that bootstraps a node. As built, a grant gives a role; only accepting takes it up. Oracles: held-model's `roles_from_reached`, the sync differential's `Model::closure`.
1. The node's **own actors**: its account, the node, each key actor it holds. Only the account takes a grant up by accepting; the node and a key actor (a link key, taken up by following its link) by being held.
2. **Reached**: kept as `Closure::targeted` beside `accepted` at every edge `judge` sees (strongs of contexts that read strongs only).
3. The account's own edge on X counts only while X is reached; every edge after it counts as held, so a chain through a reached T1 into an unreached T2 counts.
4. The node's role: `rotation::role_here`, over the edges 3 counts, with key access.
5. The closure is the least fixpoint of reached → role (`tickets::role_on`) → secrets kept and taken (lost access, `may_take`) → what the walk enters → reached. No separate pass: `targeted` is a journaled, stamped index, so every memo and `Effects` key that read a role moves with it.
6. Lost access keeps what that same role reads (`retention::forget::read_path`), so content read through a grant not taken up goes with its secrets.
7. An accepted-then-dropped grant is an unaccepted one: dropping the strong to T1 is a loss (`loss_seen` reads `role_shown`), never an `until` passing (`lost_by_time` skips untaken edges); it drops with T1's release, and the account's own untaken grant keeps no copy (`keeps`).
8. **D's tickets record words, never role commits' judgements.** A node keeping T unread writes D's sync ticket for T only while a counting ticket word gives the role (a granter's, or D's own). A role from role commits alone (a chain through a holder the node reads) gets none: every node of the account reading that holder has T's sync secret by the holder's box. Otherwise the ticket, whose sync word always counts, echoes a chain judgement back as a role nothing lowers: D's word still holds T after the chain's first grant is no longer taken up, and D's ticket goes only at T's release, which the word prevents.
- Rejected: clause 3 dropped alone (roles over every held role commit): the role then hangs on certificates that happened to arrive, and a role through holders the node does not sync still fails for want of their keys (issues/roles-apart-from-syncing.md). A filter on `role_here`'s result: it must apply to the first hop only. D's own sync word judged by `Held::upstream` like a read word: a direct sync grantee whose role commits show a path through an unreached thing would lose its direct grant.

## Tickets (spec/sync-set.md §2 What a held ticket does, spec/sync-inbox.md §5)
Cut to the offer at A2 (Roles taken up, above): fetching on a ticket's word and the account's tickets go.
- **Found** by target in the reference index's ticket rows (`refs::tickets_to`, `sync::tickets::held_in`: a lookup reads the target's tickets alone), opened by `Core::open_ticket`.
- **Judged** at a held strong while no established read secret of the node's own is held (`units::judge`, `Core::reads_established`: not one derived from the mint admin's, as a paired node reads the profile before its keyset arrives). A ticket's epoch is its maker's to choose, so a forged ticket taken first must not keep the honest one out: a ticket row appearing or going, or a removal notice arriving, re-judges every held strong to its target (`Closure::rejudge`).
- **The node's role**: `sync::tickets::role_on` (doc comment: the rule and both words). Words are noted (`Closure::ticket_words`) by `take_in` before any secret is taken; secrets go through `learn_secret`/`rotation::may_learn`; `given_by` reads `role_on`, so a role change re-judges the edge. The lost-access trigger judges by the same role (`rotation::lost` `holds_at`, `over_held`): a ticket taken while the trigger drops its secret would undo itself every settle. Tests: holding::`a_sync_ticket_beside_a_removed_holders_role_commits_keeps_the_thing_unread`, `a_removal_reaches_a_reader_opening_the_holder_through_a_second_thing`. Rejected: a read word dropped once any role commit of the thing is held (a reader holding other holders' commits but not yet its chain loses bodies); D's tickets never counting (past the inbox fire's lifetime they keep a thing kept unread; siblings read by them).
- **What judges it stays** (`retention::needed` `Need::Strong`): without it a release would forget the removal and the stale ticket count again. `Closure::accepted` roots nothing: it gates the word and notes a lost need as its last vertex goes.
- **Carried commits**: one taker, `Engine::take_fire`, from `follow` through `Closure::fires_due`; due at the strong (`tickets::take_in`: `grant`, `fire_untaken`, and `flows::inbox::removals_due`, O(the notices the inbox keeps), as a notice names its thing only inside its commit) and, for a removal notice, once the roster is held (`Engine::notice_arrives`, which also re-judges; `owe_removal_notices` as the roster comes). Each trigger is needed: a holder removed before accepting is refused by every peer, so the fires at the strong are all it learns its role by; a notice can come before the roster (sync `holding::a_removal_notice_taken_before_its_roster_acts_once_the_roster_comes_*`). Rejected: a grant taken by the roster too (a role by chain through a never-accepted thing then counts: `holding::a_role_by_chain_through_an_unaccepted_thing_counts_nothing_*`); every notice taken as it arrives (a declined grant's notice applies with its records, which stay unneeded: `growth::a_declined_grant_returns_storage_to_a_fresh_worlds_*`). A sync ticket's fire is not taken at the strong: it enters at sync at most, and taking it at every start would cost commits per thing kept unread.
- **A holder fire's `grant`** is taken by H's readers as the fire arrives, outside the gate: it is in H's own sync ID's required set (spec/sync-judgement.md §2), so a node reading H logs it whether or not it holds T.
- **Independent here**: `Core::counts_independent` (doc comment). Not independent: entered on its tickets' word (`given_by`), read as `Ctx::Ticket`, a root alone in both trees, its tickets' sync IDs synced (`sync_ids::actors::ticket_hints`); `Closure::holds` skips it, so none of its data is held for it and a thing held so alone releases; its sections open (`Closure::reaches`, notes/whole-commits.md Sealed sections). Its tickets' sync IDs (`Closure::ticket_ids`, by sync ID `naming_ticket_id`) are cleared at the settle's end once no vertex of the thing is held: they are what its `sync.0` still has to arrive by as it moves from `Ticket` to `Read`.
- **Openers**: per thing, the holders its held tickets are boxed to (`Closure::openers`, `by_opener`). A holder that can open more (`flows::take_up_opener`, `reopen_dependents`) re-judges the edges into those things alone (`Closure::opener_moved`; engine `closure::a_take_up_costs_what_the_opener_opens_spec_sync_set_2`: a key actor taken up costs nothing, `create_link` a constant, at n and 10n).
- **S's things** (`Engine::sync_things_of`, spec/sync-inbox.md §5 S's things): the sync set's entry is all of them (`rows::fold`), never one by tid. Per-S rules run for each: `read_things_of`/`opening_things` (opening, credentials, bodies joined by `join_body`, lists, records), `body_thing` (sealing, logging, promotion), `judge_peer` the best of each. Path rules use `recorded_owner`/`read_sync_id`: hints have no path.
- **The account's tickets** (spec/sync-set.md §1; `flows::inbox::account_ticket_due`): due while `Closure::entered_outside`, at an epoch from `held_by`; for a thing kept unread the stored path's steps decide (`Cipher::bears`, read through row stamps so a step arriving moves the `Effects` key). So a forged ticket's made-up epoch never reaches D and a genuine rotation's replaces the old one once its step registers; boxing every held secret instead would grow D by every rotation and keep forged ones as long as the strong. Writing removes only lower-epoch tickets naming that sync ID, so siblings seeing different epochs, or two sides of a fork, never undo each other. On `LocalPeer` (no paths) a thing kept unread gets no D ticket: its tests are osis-sync's (`tickets::ds_ticket_for_a_thing_kept_unread_*`).

## Writing and making a thing
- **A node writes only what it holds** (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing): `Core::wrote_on`, `units::check_written` after the settle, `Error::NotHeld` (osis `Invalid`). Checked as written, so accepting and writing in one transaction, or a write that releases its thing, passes. Without it a node could write a thing it was granted but never accepted and read its own write there for good, out of every closure, never released.
- Owed writes follow it: an external fire goes as a write only on a thing held and read (`inbox::fire`, else sealed); `write::writable_now` waits while a thing is not held. Without it an owed addendum on an unheld thing would fail every ingest transaction. `publish_reach` writes the profile's dial entry only once the profile is held.
- A thing a local commit mints with an account as mint admin is made independent before its first content write (`Core::own_secrets_due`, `write::independent_first`, `rotation::own_secrets_new`; notes/thing-cost.md The record in the rotation), else at its creating transaction's end (`rotation::own_secrets_due`). Role writes go first, so a thing its maker makes independent itself (a link's K, its listing L) is not given secrets twice. Otherwise content under derived keys would be re-encrypted, chunks and all, by the first write after the rotation.
- Making the next thing costs what it changed (osis `making::`, every counted kind at n and 10n, the least of three makes so a compaction's spike is spread): no per-thing or per-sync-ID memo reads a whole unit, the account root's `tickets.0`, a whole permission component (`Timelines::on`/`role`, `Core::bound_to_horizon`), a whole store table, or every sync ID's body of a commit (`Engine::sync_body`; `Live::may_log` tries S's own entries first). Certificate records are followed per record (`CertRecords`, `Engine::cert_carriers`), and a new leaf's record judges no component's key access (`Changed::leaves`). What a thing costs: notes/thing-cost.md. Things an app keeps account-wide and dependent are minted with the account root as admin.
- Test helpers hold what they make where it lives (`Engine::hold_where_it_lives`; osis `common::Held`; engine `common::new_thing` at every peer, `set_edge` by the flow, `edge_write` bare). `LocalPeer::hold`/`hold_at` write a strong from the receiving peer's node root, so a peer keeps another's thing only as a real node would: held, opened (a grant by the flow, not a raw edge write) and needed. Engine `net` peers know each other's accounts (`net_apart`: strangers); an observer is a reader (`common::reader`, `fresh_copy`) or a node of the maker's account (`net_one`, `pair_into`), given its access before what it reads, since content a node cannot yet open goes at the next need-judgement. A node of an account writes as the account, so a commit cannot mix the node root with account-written things.

## Not carried
The write path's warning reads the strongs the write's ops name (`osis_engine::strongs_in`, `Engine::not_carried_among`): O(the write). `Engine::not_carried` lists the whole node closure's, O(its strongs).

## Fetched again
- At a new read-unit edge to a thing the node did not hold when the transaction began (`units::refetch`, doc comment: the conditions; `Follow::before`/`known` decide "new"): the commits minting the thing lose their own tombstones and each sync ID the unit is held by (`sync_ids::actors::held_sync_ids`) is pulled anew from every source through the ciphertext door (`Cipher::unreceived`, `Cipher::refetch`: the sync store's `N/` mark); the sync store's follower reopens what was parked under it. So a thing released and reached again, record kept or not, or a creation opened before its strong, is fetched (sync `holding::a_thing_left_by_one_view_is_logged_again_by_one_still_holding_it_spec_sync_set_3`; engine `closure::a_thing_entering_again_with_its_record_held_is_pulled_anew_*`). A thing independent here rejoins the sync set at entry and is pulled from the start anyway. The exception for a record learned in the same transaction (`Core::learned`: brought by a commit the node held nothing of) ends alike either way (`closure::a_record_arriving_with_the_strong_*`): content outside the node closure never applies (notes/certificates.md, Content outside waits) and counts as never received only while a commit minting the thing is held (`needed::uncarried`), which forgetting keeps known while a part of it may still bring the thing in (`forget::forget`'s `keeps`); so a commit held nothing of brings in no thing whose content was dropped, and a late part's record (`hold_record`) is never learned. Both halves: `disputes::content_dropped_as_not_carried_comes_back_with_a_minting_valid_again_*`.
- A node the account's chain names whose record the node lacks (`flows::account::fetch_devices`): D's sync IDs, which carry every named node's card, are pulled anew (`flows::sync_ids::pull_anew`), so a card that went before the chain named its node comes again (`closure::a_device_named_after_its_card_went_is_fetched_again_spec_commits_5`). Test peers see each pull (`Wakes::pulled_anew`) and deliver what it brings themselves.
- Forgetting as never received (`apply::unindex_unreceived`, `retention::forget::unreceive`) ends the node's own tombstones of the commit's blobs: a forgotten body leaves nothing keeping it out.
- Cost: a pull anew lists the unit's sync IDs whole at each source, once per such new edge, also where nothing comes of it (a strong to a gone thing; an account root's strong whose ticket arrives later). Rare-path brute force by intent; if it shows in counts, narrow the trigger.

## Pending carriers (spec/commits.md §4 Pending storage)
The invariant, who adds and who removes, and the bound: carriers.rs's module doc. One persisted set per pending commit (`Indexes::carriers`, rows `pc/`), settled at the end of every durable transaction after the closure and before the needs loop.
- Paths adding nothing: `ingest_uncarried` (a Prove's rotation) and `ingest_valid(_, None)`, so what they leave pending goes in their transaction. A memory commit's copy is the memory lane's. A pairing payload's entry, naming no blob, goes at the next start.
- A report taken under an earlier actor of its sync ID is replaced only within that actor (issues/stranded-report-actor-change.md).
- **Handed again** (`apply::rearrival`, arrival paths only): a copy that brings nothing (each part held, or byte for byte the stored pending copy under its signatures, not expired, no clock wake due) returns what an examination would at a lookup's cost; its carrier is still noted. Bytes decide, not the part's header hash: a pending commit may await the record its dedup secret comes from, and a differing part (forged, or true after junk) is judged (`with_pending`). Parked copies and memory commits take the full path. A re-arrival is never a retry: every ground change wakes what it frees (`waits::wake`), so a `Wake::At` already due takes the full path. Counted: engine `carriers::a_pending_commit_handed_again_costs_a_lookup_*`.
- **Restart**: rows reload (`carriers::load`); a fire or report carrier goes in the transaction its fire or holder goes; an entry gone while down is released by sync's start sweep.
- Tests: engine tests/it/carriers.rs; `rotation::tests_stranded::a_report_letting_go_forgets_what_only_it_carries_*`, `a_removal_notice_carries_what_it_brings_until_it_goes_*`, `a_sealed_fire_carries_its_chain_until_it_goes_*`; `closure::a_commit_awaiting_its_record_is_kept_while_carried_and_no_longer_*`.

What a node holds of a part on thing T, by what it holds of T (each row the same in every arrival order, `closure::every_order_of_a_commit_its_record_and_its_minting_ends_alike_*`):

| T | certificate part (record, role-class, secrets; key actor's card) | content part | header of a commit missing a part on T |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| held whole (closure, leaving, retained copy) | applies | applies | kept, part fetched |
| outside, minting commit held | applies; kept while needed | waits; never received at the pass end (`needed::uncarried`) | kept while a part on a thing in the closure is missing, else goes with T |
| outside, minting commit not held (record from a record blob or fire, or none) | applies once the record is known | waits while carried, rooting T | kept while carried |

A commit whose every held part is unjudgeable pends whole (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable; `Exam::Awaits`): its header counts nowhere (removal lists, receipt) until its thing enters or its record comes. Applying the header alone would count a commit on aspects of things nothing here judges.
