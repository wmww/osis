# Accounts as ordinary things: the builder's brief
> Summary: Accounts as ordinary things (notes/redesign-plan.md A4): what the spec says and why (home is the account, first entry, node thing and node root, dial list, link keys, connection proofs, leaving), what runs on main after the old machinery's deletion and the tests ignored per piece, the code pieces still to build and which builds each model-found rule, the models behind those rules (held-model entry, roles-model proofs and leave), open questions and risks.

## What the spec says now
- **Signing**: a commit is signed as a thing by a key holding a grant on it (`signers.0`, the key hop: spec/permissions.md §1). A thing minted with a key is independent from its minting commit (a rotation of all four roles from none, with its sync ID); its secrets are its minter's, held by the minting key store, boxed to nobody (spec/permissions.md §3, spec/crypto.md §3). No epochs, signing chain, custody, inner or merge signatures, thing lists or listings anywhere; key actors are gone as a concept.
- **A node** has a node thing N (minted with its key, an ordinary independent thing with a sync ID, holding the node's roles) and a node root (minted with admin N, local, no sync ID, holding the node's strongs, a strong to N among them) (spec/sync-set.md §1). Its **own things** are what its keys hold grants on: N, and a link key it redeems by; its role on anything is their effective role (spec/sync-set.md §2).
- **An account** is an arrangement a library makes, read by no core rule (spec/permissions.md §8): an independent thing A minted with the first node thing as mint admin; nodes hold admin (pair, remove) or write on it and sign as their node thing stating paths through A; things made for the account are A's dependents; A's inbox and profile are independent things minted with admin A, held by strongs from A, the inbox named in A's `keys.0`. Pairing is the ordinary grant plus an offer of A and the pairer's trust records handed over the pairing channel; removing a node is the ordinary removal with A's rotation, saying erase.
- **First entry** (spec/sync-set.md §2): a node enters a thing only by a unit it syncs carrying it (whole; its stub, which a unit carries for an independent thing a strong of it reaches where the unit's thing holds sync or above and its keys do not open it; or its edge piece in the key part of an own thing or a thing the node reads) or an offer it holds. A key part has a piece for every edge its holder holds, the record edge included (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part), so the node minting A enters it by N's piece. Entry is founded: the least state the rules give, recomputed from data, no entry kept for supporting itself. A second node of an account needs nothing of its own: A's key part sits under A's own sync ID by the general rule.
- **Dialing**: `dial.0` on any thing, each node keeping its own entry; a node dials what the `dial.0` of things it reads lists (spec/sync-set.md §1, spec/sync-opens.md §1). Siblings dial each other by A's `dial.0`, contacts by the profile's. `reach.0`, `node.0` and node cards are gone; the `peer://` URL is computed from listeners and relays (spec/sync-transport.md §3).
- **Inbox** (spec/sync-inbox.md §1): any thing's inbox is the thing its `keys.0` names in `inbox`, else itself. An account's is a thing of its own. A thing's public key record carries its inbox's.
- **Connection proofs** (spec/sync-opens.md §2, spec/wire.md §11.2 Prove): a proof of a thing by a key holding a grant on it, plus the path it claims (N, [A]), with certificate copies of the claimed edges as evidence, judged with what the verifier holds and with the sides taking the copies in would record (Held first), for that judgement alone, and kept with the connection. A proof proves nothing where the reading it is judged on undoes a lowering the verifier's own reading applies. A proven thing's **proven role** on S is the least of its path's (key hop included) and the claimed thing's role on S: J, who it may be (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for) and Stranded (spec/permissions.md §4) read it.
- **Leaving**: the self-removal exception of Encrypting under (spec/crypto.md §4) covers a holder the node's own things administer, the account among them, and only a node that may not rotate r; one that may rotates first and logs its leave under the new epoch. Held elsewhere (spec/sync-status.md §2): a peer counts for a self-removal only where a thing it proved holds a role on the thing left with the leave applied.
- **Link keys** (spec/permissions.md §7): a link key L is a thing minted with the key the link's seed derives, its secrets derived from the seed, its creation record naming K in field 3 (spec/wire.md §3). The one special case left: a reader that is a link key of S's thing is listed in S's steps by its static entry with an entry box (spec/sync-ids.md §3).
- **Wire** (spec/wire.md): header fields 14-16, `inner`, merge signatures, `Rotation`, `Reseal`, the list tree and list blobs are gone, `flags` is field 14; the creation record lost `account` (field 4); Prove/Proved carry `[proof*], [copy*], [creation*]`, `proof := [creation, key, sig, [tid*]]`, `MAX_PROOF_COPIES` (the rotation cap is gone).

## Decisions and why
- **Home is A itself**, not a dependent of A. Home is by convention the mint admin of what the user makes; A already is one, with A's members as home's members, so home falls out with no extra thing, pointer or first-entry case. A dependent home would add a thing and a pointer and buy only sharing home without sharing the account, which a separate realm gives (notes/write-path-design.md §5: granting a role on home is granting the account). The spec names no home: it is the schema's. Tess: suspicious but fine; a home thing admined and referenced by A can be added later if a reason shows.
- **The node thing and the node root are two things.** A node thing holding the node's strongs would make its sync unit the node's whole readable closure (its keys open A and all A reaches), so every node would log every commit once more under its own sync ID. Split, the node thing's unit is itself alone and the node root stays local. The design notes call the signing thing "node root"; the spec says node thing.
- **A minted with N as mint admin**, made independent at once (a key of its own would add a key nobody holds or a shared key; the record edge is an ordinary removable admin edge).
- **Sibling dialing by `dial.0` on any thing** (A6's dial aspect, pulled forward), not an Interim dial list: the general rule costs no more than the special one.
- **Link keys keep seed-derived secrets and the static entry**, named as the one special case (a bearer secret must open K from a store with nothing else): the record's field 3 marks the link key and scopes the static entry to K's steps. Node things get no static entry (a stable entry would link a node across units at stores).
- **The inbox pointer stays `keys.0`'s `inbox`**, generalized to any thing, until A5's card replaces it.
- **Pairing hands the offer over the channel** in place of a `grant@~0` fire: the new node thing enables no external signal.
- **Stubs widened to sync** (notes/stubs.md rule 2): with "keys open" alone a thing held at sync through a holder (A read on B, B sync on C, A strongs C) is never entered, while the same role granted directly is.
- **First entry only by own and read things' key parts**: a piece from a holder held for keys for another thing makes entry hang on an unrelated reference.

## State on main
The spec is in and the old account machinery is gone from code, models and TLA (no signing chain, epochs, custody, thing lists, listings, account root, node cards, `reach.0`, `node.0`, header fields 14-16, inner or merge signatures; `dial.0` on any thing). What runs in its place, each the simplest thing true to the spec, Interim until the piece named builds the real one:
- **The node thing is minted with its key** (piece 1, minting with a key): `Identity` is a signing seed and the record of the thing minted with its key (the node thing, random nonce; a link key, nonce and K from the seed), both persisted in the seed record. A node's first start (`flows::roots::first_start`) mints N in one transaction with its node root (minted with admin N, holding a strong to N): N's minting, signed by its key as N, is a rotation of all four roles from none (n 1, `rotation::own_secrets_new`), with its keysets, sync ID and deposit seed, boxing nobody; its secrets live in the node's own secrets store (`Indexes::secrets`) and open their history there (`osis_keys::ingest_held`). Boxes to N are sealed at its keysets as to any holder (symmetric from its own node). `Core::me` is N's tid. Account disputes are gone from `osis`.
  - The key-actor concept is gone (`CreationRecord` is one struct: `minter` mint admin or key, `link`, `expires`; one `Opener` and one `HolderKeys` form, no fixed epoch, no `keys.0` `key` field). A thing minted with a key is an ordinary independent thing: holder actor, key access, Seal, delete base, closure, sections, all as any.
  - **Link keys**, the one kept special case: L's record names K in `link` (field 3, beside the key); its first secrets are `kdf("role <role>", seed)` (`ActorEpochs::link_key`, `Identity::link_epochs`), its static entry is listed in K's steps (`flows::sync_ids::reader_key` reads `link_key_of() == K`), its entry box sealed to its first read keyset. The sharer mints L (`flows::links`, signed by L's key, `rotation_into` with the seed) after K and before L's grant; a held link key opens by its seed (`Core::link_key_epochs`).
  - **First steps** (spec/crypto.md §7): a thing's minting by its record's key, with no step on its chain recorded before it, lists the keys of the genesis step it records itself, unchecked (`Core::first_steps`); a later commit carrying `created.0` again (a record key since lowered) is checked as any. Not the general "a commit's own `sync.0` write counts for it": that needs the walk's verdict on that write (a non-admin's invalid `sync.0` delta would place its own key), and the key check comes before the walk and gates it; the record alone makes the signer admin only before any step.
  - **Making a thing** (spec/sync-set.md §2): a thing made with role entries alone may be held as a holder of a thing in the node closure instead (`Written::Holder`); a link key is made so, held by no strong, its record and keys under K's sync ID.
  - Every independent holder's public key record (record and newest `keys.0`) goes under each thing it holds a role on (`shared_certificates`, spec/sync-inbox.md §1): a link's redeemer learns L's record from K's sync ID.
  - Interim: `Core::is_node` (minted with a key, no `link`) and `Core::provable` (a key holds a grant on it) stand where proofs read nodes, until piece 5.
  - A node thing enables no external signal: a grant fire to N is refused as invalid where it lands; offers reach a node through its account's inbox. The order harness takes such a refusal as expected (`take_fires`).
- Dialing: `publish_reach` writes the node's entry in the `dial.0` of its node thing and of the things it is given (`osis` and the sync harness: the account and its profile), replacing its entries under its earlier keys and keeping other nodes'; `dial_peers` reads the `dial.0` lists of S's things, so contacts dial each other by the profile's.
- Proofs: a node proves its node thing first, then its link keys (`Engine::provers`); `Proven` and `Named` (peer records) carry `node` and `link_keys`; Prove and Proved carry no rotations. `RosterHolders` names the provable things holding a role on S's thing (`Core::provable_holders_of`).
- Things minted with the node as admin are its dependents; a link's seed keeper (minted with K's mint admin S) and K are held from S's `holds.0`, a redeem's strongs from the joiner's (`osis_sync::Node::redeem` takes the joiner: `osis` and the harness pass the account; `Engine::joiner_of` finds it, `leave_link` leaves as it); certificate blobs are record blobs only.
- **Key grants** (piece 1, wire and key grants): `osis_base::Holder` (a thing or a `SignKey`) holds every grant; osis-perm's walk takes the key hop (key, S) as an edge first on every path, a record's key holding admin from the start as a mint admin's record edge does (spec/permissions.md §1); components join only through edges a held part sets. The timeline's thing queries (rosters, effective roles, standing, key access) read grants of things alone; `Timeline::key_grant(s)` reads keys'.
  - Wire: the header's `Signer` second form `[tid, signing_key]` (`Header::key`; `CommitBuilder::name_key` names the signing key itself, so a writer never names another). One source of a commit's key, `apply::commit_key`: the named key, else the record's; a second form naming the record's key or a first form on a keyless record is bad input. The signature is checked under it and the walk rests the commit on its hop (`validity::signing_key`, reading `View::key`): the key judged is the key verified.
  - `signers.0` (schema/core.osis, role-class, never compacted, admin to write) is read as perm's edge writes on key holders (`validity::Grants`: an edge aspect's entries keyed by a weak or a `signing_key`; `holder_keys` is keyed by aspect too, since two edge aspects of one commit share counters); a key grant with `retain`, `floor` or `until` 0 is bad input. Read back from the walk (`get` of `signers.0`). The path wait waits on a key hop's writes as on any edge; the last-admin check, content rules, removal scope (`receipt::scope_of`: a key's grant holds up only commits signed as its target), disputes (`Sides`, `DisputeInfo::holder` a `Holder`) take key grants.
  - `Engine::grant_key(target, key, edge)`: an admin's explicit write (a lowering with its frontier) or a may-grant grant; no boxes, rotation or delivery. `Mutation::sign_as(thing)` signs by a held key holding a grant on the thing (`Core::key_for`: the record's key first, else a granted one, named in the header). Tests: engine `it` `key_grants::`.
  - The evidence derivation takes key hops as edges (`evidence::Holds`, the engine's `Ev` over `Holder`): the `signers.0` writes on a key hop are evidence of what the key signs. Interim: the `osis` API lists no dispute over a key's grant (`Dispute::of`, piece 6).

## Models
Each rule above that a model found carries a control: a flag whose removal fails its property.

**First entry** (held-model `closure::entry`, tests/it/spec_entry.rs): a node is its node thing (its unit: itself) and a local root holding its strongs; it enters a thing only as spec/sync-set.md §2 First entry says; its role is by the grants that count at it. `Rule::FIXED` is the spec; `Rule::SPEC` the rule without the three entry fixes. Runs: 20,000 random worlds per rule (`entry_explore`), the exhaustive family of 118,098 worlds (`entry_family`: A and three things, every grant none/sync/read, one strong from A or the roots); under `Rule::FIXED` only "history" (a node keeping a self-supporting entry, which the founded rule forbids) fails.
- Controls: `record_pieces` (without the record edge's piece, the node minting A never enters it, nor any independent thing minted with admin a node thing, while a paired node enters A by its grant's piece); `stubs_at_sync` (209 of 20,000 random worlds: two paths giving one role differ); `founded_pieces` (A read on H, H sync on Y and X, A strongs Y, the root strongs X: X entered only while A references Y); `Fix::Greatest` (the greatest fixpoint enters what only the root references, by walking down every opened holder's key part: the per-holder list the design removed). "history" (1% of random worlds, 2.5% of the family): a node keeping a thing entered by an offer after the offer goes, while the account's other node never had it.
- What follows: the account's two nodes with the same strongs hold the same things in every world (property "symmetry"), and no per-holder record is needed: B's grants are found from C up. A thing reached only through holders and referenced only from a node's root is held by no node of the account once its offer goes, as notes/stubs.md says: a library keeping a granted thing files it under A (a redeem writing into the root's `holds.0` loses it at the offer's end unless an own thing holds the edge, as a link key does).
- The split: the node thing's unit is itself, a node's held set is its node thing and root plus the account (A, its dependents, inbox, profile), its sync set N, A, inbox and profile; pairing enters A by the offer, then by the node's own piece; removal leaves the node its node thing and root; neither node holds the other's node thing for keys. Control `merged` (the node's strongs on its node thing): its unit is the whole account.

**Connection proofs** (roles-model `paths::proofs`, searches `proofs`, `proofs-exh`): A minted with N1, N2 admin, N3 a role, then edge writes on A by any of them (removals, demotions, re-grants, self-removals, any `ts`, frontiers honest over part of what came before); a verifier V holding an evidence-closed part (as its sources logged it), its sides recorded as it took them in (Held first); a prover P ∈ {N1, N2, N3} claiming [P, A] with any set of copies V lacks; A holds write on S. `ProofRules::fixed` is the spec, `spec` the rules without the three fixes. Runs: 20,000 random worlds per rule, the whole family of 225,792 worlds (two moves of any node at `ts` 2 or 4, V holding each commit or not, in key order or its reverse); under `fixed` nothing fails. Evidence is `paths::evidence` (spec/permissions.md §4).
- The guard (security), control `-no-guard`, property `revive`: a removed node's copies can undo its removal at V: its own counter-removal of the remover stamped before (by sync V would side with the removal, Held first; as copies it records nothing), or a third party's genuine removal of the remover whose own undoing it withholds (N3 removed N2 at 3, N1 had removed N3 at 2), which nothing ever brings V, the copies being kept with the connection alone. Hand worlds `a_removed_node_carrying_its_counter_removal_proves_nothing`, `a_cherry_picked_removal_of_the_remover_proves_nothing`.
  - Cost: V denies a member whose genuine commits it lacks undo its lowering (the remover's own later leave, a real earlier removal of the remover; counted `denied`). Each such commit is an edge write on an edge of the lowering's path, so in its evidence: ordinary sync brings it, and with it the proof stands (property `waits`).
- Sides on receipt, control `-no-receipt`, property `fresh` (random worlds reach it rarely): without them an honest member's copy that seeds a lowering V applies voids that lowering and proves less than receipt would (N1's removal of N3 at 4, a seed of N2's demotion of N1 at 5: received, N3 keeps read), until sync brings it, which no unit V syncs may carry. Hand world `honest_copies_are_judged_with_the_sides_their_receipt_records`.
- The proven role (security), control `-no-j`, property `j-path`: read at the claimed thing's own role, a node holding sync on A is J's reader of every S A reads (opens by J and ByRole, its reports trusted as a reader's), and one holding read on A gets Stranded messages as a writer of S.
- What the model shows (no change asked):
  - A verifier that does not hold a node's removal counts it as the account (counted `stale`): it is judged on what V holds. It gains there what any proven reader gets: opens by J and ByRole (S's stream; once S re-keys for A's rotation, ciphertext under epochs it does not hold, and what writers not knowing the rotation sealed under old ones, which its old secrets open at any store anyway), Stranded messages, its reports trusted, and being waited for (spec/sync-set.md §3), no longer than an away honest node of A: until it acknowledges S's credentials or `PASS_WAIT_MS`. Deposits go by credentials (spec/sync-inbox.md §3), not proofs. Holding old keys gives it nothing a proof decides.
  - Once the removal arrives (as evidence of any unit V syncs where the node wrote through A, or in A's key part), the claim is re-judged, J turns departed, and its members end (spec/sync-opens.md §6). With the guard no copy holds the removal off.
  - A node demoted concurrently (admin to write or read) proves the account at its proven role, and is trusted as that.
  - Both sides of a permanent fork: each verifier proves by its own side; no copy switches it (the guard and receipt sides together).

**Leaving** (roles-model `leave`, searches `leave`, `leave-exh`): nodes N1, N2 (of A), B (of a group G) and O (T's admin, staying); every grant set of A and B on G, of A, G, N1 and B on T; X ∈ {A, G} leaving T by a self-removal made by a node administering X, judged at any node; the leave spreading by up to 3 syncs (6 in random worlds) among nodes holding T, a node losing T by it dropping T once held elsewhere. `LeaveRules::fixed` is the spec, `spec` the rules without both fixes; 20,000 random worlds and the whole family of 9,547,776 worlds (2,088,576 with a valid leave) hold under `fixed`; each control fails both.
- Rotating first (security), control `-rotating`, property `content` (`log` checks every leaver can still log its leave): with the exception for any node, N1 making A leave T while writing T directly goes on writing under T's epoch boxed to A, which N2 (holding T through A alone) opens; for a group G that A administers, G's other members open it (`a_group_leaving_leaks_to_its_other_members_as_written`). "Whichever node made it" (a sibling exempt for N1's leave of A) is harmless so: after A left, N2 may not rotate T unless it holds T otherwise, and then it rotates.
- Held elsewhere by staying peers (data loss), control `-any-source`, property `lost`: with any peer counting, A's two nodes each see the other hold A's leave and drop T (spec/permissions.md §3 Lost access), and no node keeping T ever gets it (`a_leave_held_only_by_leaving_nodes_is_not_held_elsewhere`); a group's leave held by its member B alike.

## Risks
- Flag day for every account test and sim world; split it.
- Things minted with admin A become dependents: app and chat things sync only inside A's unit until made independent for sharing.
- Node things show to every reader (their records and key hops are evidence wherever they write), and the path adds A's tid to every commit: accepted costs.
