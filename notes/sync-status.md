# Sync: status
> Summary: osis-sync's `status` module (core/sync/src/status/): sources and caught up, heard out, lacking counts, own commits held elsewhere, complete passes and `wait`; read when asked in O(sources of S); the readings it implements beyond the spec's words, its costs, and what is not built.

Spec: spec/sync-status.md. Model: core/test/tla/net/NetStream.tla (`Synced`, `CaughtUpHolds`). Principle: the module doc of core/sync/src/status/mod.rs. Overview: notes/sync.md.

## What it owns
What a source is caught up on, full or refusing is streams' (stamped per connection and per (source, S)), so a sync ID's status is read when asked (`Status::of`, `Node::status_of`), O(its sources): nothing is kept per S that a push would have to move. Status keeps only what needs acting on, each an `Effects` key: the members of S per source and the connection per source (State, below), the projection of the sync IDs this node syncs (a row per S held, kept unread and leaving ones included: `Store::syncs`), lacking counts per (S, record of a source keeping S unread: kept across its connections, gone with S leaving the record or the record, `record_gone`; by the record's rows, the connection holding it read only for an entry they do not show), own commits per read S and those not held elsewhere, self-removals, complete passes. Durable: held elsewhere, a row per entry beside it (`Store::set_elsewhere`), so it goes with the entry and recording it moves no reader of the entry (inbox's judgement reads the entry). A source showing or reporting an entry is recorded whatever read's verdict on the commit: status's own reading (`unheld`, `wait`) counts a may-hold or must-not-hold commit held at once, but leave and a timed loss read the rows alone (`leave::own_elsewhere`), since a node that lost access may log none of its own commits, so its verdict says nothing of them must be held (spec/sync-status.md §2 By a source's report).

An own commit's entries under S are the entries of S holding a blob the engine's `commit_blobs(c)` names (both stamped). A source that left since the last settle still counts by where it stood as it left (`Moved::Left`'s `LeftAt`: its reported position and `until`) and by what its record's stream showed: a peer that judges this node departed by the self-removal it just applied reports it and closes S in one step. `wait(Thing(t))` covers the engine's `carrying(t)`: t's own sync IDs and those of the sync units holding it, read or kept unread; `wait` skips each S held by no other node (the driver passes the test in).

## State
One rule, as read's (notes/sync-read.md, State): what status follows of a peer is the record's (its members of S and their class), kept across its connections; what is the connection's stays the connection's (caught up, full, reported positions, `until`, who it proved). S's sources are its members whose record a connection holds (`sources_of`), read where asked, so a take or release moves only what read that record's connection: a stranding judged past the engine's word or a walk stop, a commit not recorded held elsewhere, a pass under way. Keys: P a peer, E the engine or opens, N this node.

| State | Keys | Per | Bound (flood test) | Goes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `members` (S → source → class), `of_source` | opens' member-level `Moved` (E: a member opens only by proof that the peer may hold S): `Joined` of an open, `Taken` of a record read from its rows (every member, once per load) | record | members of the kept records in memory (`RECORD_BUDGET`) and of records kept nowhere (their connection) | the member closing (`Left`, `Gone`), `record_gone`; a record kept nowhere with its last member |
| `up` (source → connection, account, own node) | `Moved::Taken`/`Released` (E), a record kept nowhere at its first member; who the peer proved from sessions | connection | connections | the release, `record_gone` |
| `lacking_fx`, `lacking` | (S, source) of a store member | record | store members | with the member |
| `leaving` (S, connection → source, `LeftAt`) | a member closing on a connection (E), with P's reported position and its `until` | connection | members closing in one step | each settle; a release keeps none (the next connection's Pull reports again) |
| `holding`, `own_fx`, `held_fx`, `unheld`, `by_commit`, `held`, `removals`, `release_fx` | E (the sync set, own commits, self-removals) | node | sync IDs held × own commits | S leaving the set, the commit no longer own |
| `wanted_fx`, `passes`, `pass_fx`, `again`, `done`, `modules_fx` | read S (E, leave, read) | node | read S | the pass's end, S leaving |
| `stranding_fx`, `strandings` | read S | node | read S | S not read |
| elsewhere rows (durable) | an entry of N's held at a source by P's stream or report | node | own entries | with the entry |

Messages: status sends none of its own (a pass's fresh Pull goes through streams). What a peer controls reaches it only through streams (caught up, full, refused, what its stream showed, its reported positions) and opens (membership, which needs proof that it may hold S); an unproven peer is no member, a removed one's members close in the step of the change (notes/sync-opens.md), so it stops being a source wherever it is, and a hostile reader can only claim positions (counted for held elsewhere, spec/sync-status.md §2). Clock: the monotonic `clock` for `PASS_WAIT_MS`, woken by `next_wake`. A restart changes nothing a user sees: nothing but the elsewhere rows is durable, and no peer is a source before its connection takes its record, which is when status learns a record read from its rows.

Tests: status.rs `a_reconnect_moves_no_member_spec_sync_status_1` (n and 10n: no member moved, no lacking count taken again), `a_taken_record_is_caught_up_per_connection_spec_sync_status_1`, `a_release_keeps_no_report_spec_sync_status_2`, `lacking_is_kept_per_record_across_connections_spec_sync_status_1`; idle.rs counts `status: members moved` at an idle reconnect.

## Self-removals
Each self-removal the engine waits on (`Engine::waiting_removals`: an own self-removal applied here, made here or at a sibling, notes/engine-lost-access.md Self-removal drops; `own_commits` lists it under each S it has a body under) is one `Effects` key: done once, under each read S it has a body under (`by_commit`), it is held elsewhere or no peer but this account's own nodes is to be waited for on S (`Ground::waited`: `Opens::waited` skipping a peer whose proven key actors include this account or a node the engine calls `own_node`; a dialer lacking this node's `reach.0` proves only the account; notes/sync-opens.md Peers to wait for); with no such S, at once. Then `Engine::removal_done` (the engine drops each thing lost that no other waiting removal is behind). The same rule as leaving (notes/sync-leave.md): it waits only while a peer could take it; own nodes leave by the same commit, so counting them would leave two nodes of one account waiting on each other for good. A peer that applies the removal judges this node departed and closes S, dropping it from its record: told and not sharing S, it is no longer waited for, which also ends the wait where the same-step report was lost (a restart between). `by_commit` is a plain map: every change of it writes `unheld` of that S, which the judgement reads. Own-node-ness is read unstamped: it moves only with D's roster, and a node leaving D while skipped needs nothing delivered. Skipping own nodes is safe because they learn the removal from this account's own sync IDs and inbox (spec/permissions.md §2: a write removing a grant is delivered to its holder's inbox), never only from S. `held_at`'s own-node skip reads `Src::own_node` (node proven), so an own node proven only by its account still counts as holding it there: harmless, that node waits on the same removal itself (spec/sync-status.md §2). Tests: status.rs `self_removal_*`, flows.rs `a_self_removal_*`, siblings.rs `a_siblings_self_removal_*`.

Stranded per read S (`strandings`, one `Effects` key per read S, stamped for inbox's reports): the engine's `stranded` (a current read epoch of S's thing known, none held) once S is heard out, the reading of spec/permissions.md §4's "after a complete pass there"; or, with no source of S, opens' walk stopped at a source (`Opens::walk_stop`, notes/sync-opens.md Stops; the report from `Engine::stranding` naming the step it stopped at). The judgement reads the engine's word and the walk stop first and S's sources only where either could strand it, so a source joining or leaving (a reconnect) judges no other S. `SyncStatus::stranded` is that or unread's stranded.

`SyncStatus { holding, sources, stranded, pass_waits, unheld }` with `heard_out`, `readers` (by account), `synced` (heard out and `unheld == Some(0)`). `Node::idle` is `Sessions::idle` (no request, fill or Hello outstanding).

Seams: `status::Net` (read when asked: streams, unread) and `status::Ground: Net` (the settle's: engine, store, opens, read; `status/live.rs` the product's), so core/sync/tests/it/status.rs runs it on a fake.

## Caught up and passes
- Streams gives `caught_since(conn, s)`: caught up both ways, with the place (`Streams::pull_seq`, a count of Pulls sent) of the Pull the last reply answered, so a Pull answered moves only the passes. Two stamped parts: the connection's (`caught`: this side's last reply at the end with no Pull out, no gap and no reply in hand, `SN_rearm`; the peer's last Pull answered to the end and followed) and S's (`busy` per (connection, S): a push carrying S in hand, an event of S served and not covered by the peer's Applied or by the cursor its next Pull names (a reply sent while a listing from the start is under way gets no Applied), logged past the follow cursor and not pushed, listed in memory and not sent, a join of S left in pages). One sync ID's traffic moves only its own key, so a commit judges one S's stranding, not every S shared on the connections its stream crosses. `track` keeps both: O(members busy there and events logged since its last read of the log).
- A complete pass reads `caught_since` against its start and asks `Streams::pull_again` (a Pull sent once any under way ends). Opens lists `records_holding(store, s)` (the store's member index merged with records in memory; at a pass's start only).
- Wants come from the engine's `pass_wanted(s)`, followed per read S (notes/sync-facade.md); and from leave (`Leave::passes_wanted`, notes/sync-leave.md) and read (`Read::passes_wanted`, notes/sync-read.md Outside S), merged by `Ground::module_passes`, all by pass place. A pass keeps when it began on the node's clock (its `PASS_WAIT_MS` deadline), the Pulls sent before it (a source is fresh by a later Pull), and its pass place (`Engine::begin_pass`: one count with role arrivals from elsewhere, so a pass begun before an arrival never counts after it, whatever the clock reads), kept per S (`Status::passed_since`, for leave and read). Its end is told back by `note_complete_pass` only while the engine wants a pass of S, then `pass_end` on each of S's things (`sync_owners`): the engine re-seals and adds history once every sync ID of that thing had its pass (spec/permissions.md §3 Complete pass). Telling it of passes only leave or read wanted ran those duties on no cause of the engine's ( then never forgot S). A new want past a pass under way queues at most one more pass of S, each bounded by `PASS_WAIT_MS`.

## Readings
Written into spec/sync-status.md: caught up is per sync ID on its connection (what is the connection's, a Pull out, a gap, a reply in hand, is shared by every S there); heard out needs one source not full or refusing (only such sources is not heard out); a source keeping S unread counts for held elsewhere by its stream alone (its Applied passes what it refused for room, `Full` names no entry, and an unstamped entry it never takes and never refuses); a reader also by a reported position at or past the entry's first log position, capped at a member's `until`; a sync ID held by no other node holds no wait (a one-node account's home, carrying every thing it files, would hold every wait on a thing for good), by the engine's `roster_holders` (another account with a role on S's thing, another own node syncing it, S kept unread). Not `Opens::waited` (what leave and life read, notes/sync-opens.md Peers to wait for): a redeemer's first contact has no record before its dial completes, so the wait would end before the data came; the caller's timeout bounds a wait; a wait on a thing runs to its timeout while one sync ID carrying it some other node may hold is unconnected.

Kept in code only: lacking applies to sources keeping S unread (a reader re-encrypts and judges what it keeps), counted as the stamped entries here its stream did not show (deposits offered are not counted); the heard-out half of a pass is "every waited source caught up since it began" (with none: vacuous), so a pass with no source waits only on its records and, while any, the peers to be waited for on S (`Opens::waited`, own nodes counted: a sync ID just joined waits for its other holders); a full or refusing source is not waited on in a pass; stranded is per S (unread's for S kept unread, `strandings` for S read); a walk stop counts only while no source has S open (a source open: heard out decides), and of stops at several sources the report names one naming a step; `wait(All)` is every S held; a wait whose scope holds a stranded S answers `Waiting::Stranded`, which the osis API fails `Stranded` naming the S's owners (notes/api-design.md: a wait on `Stranded` fails).

Model check: core/sync/tests/it/streams.rs `caught_up_matches_what_each_side_holds_spec_sync_status_1` runs random histories (logs, inline and fetched bodies, frame orders, pushes refused for room) against both sides' holdings.

## Costs
A lacking count is taken again whole, O(S's stamped entries), at each change of what that source showed or of S's entries (issues/status-lacking-count.md).

## Not built
issues/sync-unbuilt.md (status): covered by a snapshot at a source (spec/sync-status.md §2: a body a source dropped under a snapshot before showing it never counts, an under-claim).
