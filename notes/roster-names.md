# Roster names: keeping holder actors' churn out of a rotation's names
> Summary: Design for issues/keyset-names-placement-rosters.md. Neither direction in the issue keeps frozen coverage (a departed admin forges the holder's keyset where its removal is unknown, and devices are their account's admins). The rule that does, "keyset descent": a keyset proves it was written knowing its predecessor (a signature under the superseded epoch's delete key), a rotation of A names only A's own departures and the keyset of each holder actor it boxes, and a boxer of a holder actor seals only to a keyset descending from the ones named. Names of departures below A survive only for a holder actor the rotation leaves out. Not built: it adds a signature to keysets and reworks the box judgement, so the choice goes to Tess.

Reads: spec/crypto.md §3, §4, §7; notes/crypto.md (Frozen coverage); notes/epoch-establishment-study.md; `rotation::departures` and `rotation::mod` (`Seal`, `left_out`, `keeps_out`); core/test/tla/rotation. Analysis and a TLA extension (`Descent`); no Rust changed.

## 1. What the names below A are for
A rotation of A boxes holder actor M at M's keyset f. Three parties can put a departed D (a device that left M) behind a keyset of M that a later boxer of M picks: D itself, forging M's `keys.0` where its removal is not held (valid there: D was an admin of M); an honest admin of M that has not seen the removal, forking M and boxing D (C1); and an honest boxer of M at A's level that holds such a keyset and not the removal. Today's answer is to name every departure inside M (and below) so that whoever establishes A's epoch holds the removal: D's forgery is then invalid there, the ignorant fork is judged as boxing D (`presumed_boxes`), and the boxer leaves M out or finds a keyset made knowing the departure. The cost is the issue's: every reader of A learns M's churn and its roster snapshots (placed whole by `placed_with`), and the names grow with it.

## 2. The two directions of the issue
- **Direction 2, admins only.** An account's nodes are its admins (spec/permissions.md §1), so every device removal is an admin departure: nothing is saved for the case the issue measures. It is also unsound for reader departures: an ignorant fork of M boxing a departed reader is an honest valid keyset that nothing at A's level distinguishes from a later legitimate one.
- **Direction 1, follow M's history box.** M's history box names M's departures, but an admin of A that is no holder of M cannot open it, and a holder of M needs nothing (it establishes M's epoch and knows them). What a non-holder can check about a keyset of M is only its validity and what it lists, and both are the forger's word where the removal is unknown. The chain is followable only if a keyset carries a proof that its writer held the predecessor's secret: that is the rule below.

## 3. The rule: keyset descent
- **Descent.** A keyset of role r of M at epoch f lists, as today, the epochs it supersedes; for each listed epoch g whose secret the rotator held (an established epoch), it also carries an Ed25519 signature under g's delete key (spec/crypto.md §7, seeded by g's secret; its public key is in g's keyset and, below, in the keyset name) over the keyset's own digest (epoch, KEM key, suite, delete key, the list). f **descends** from g when it carries that signature, or descends from a keyset that does, through bodies the node holds valid. Signing is encrypting under g: only an established g. A fork sibling the rotator holds no secret of is listed without a signature (superseded, not descended from). The first own keyset of a thing lists and signs for the derived epochs it replaces (today they are not listed).
- **Names of a rotation of A**: A's epoch start, the departures from A itself (lowering writes, expiries, audit drops on A since the start), the departs mark, and per boxed holder actor M and rotated role r a keyset name (M, r, f, f's delete key). Nothing from below A, except: for a holder actor M it leaves out of r (no held keyset of M passes its judgement for a departure inside M it knows), it names the keyset names it was itself bound to for M (carried forward) and every departure inside M it knows concerning r (today's `thing_names` of M and the things below it). Grant and re-seal boxes to M carry the same: the keyset name of the box, no departures (a grant that cannot box M is refused, as today).
- **Established**: as today without the names below A. A keyset name is met by a held valid `keys.0` body of M writing f, or one listing f with a valid signature under the named delete key (the compaction escape of today, now verifiable).
- **Boxing holder actor M** (rotation, grant, re-seal of A, role r): the **anchors** are the keyset names (M, r, ·) of every current epoch of r of A the node holds, of every names-carrying box to M it holds at those epochs, and of every held put of M's edge on A (`departures::sealers` today, with keyset names in place of departure names); every departure name among them must be met (held). The node seals M only to the newest held valid keyset of M for r that is, or descends from, every anchor and keeps out every departure inside M it knows (below); none: M is left out. Bound seals (no names-carrying box held) stay as today: the keyset of the `keys.0` commit the roster entry names, no names carried.
- **Keeps out** (the box judgement, replacing `keeps_out`, `presumed_boxes` and the "newest keyset" condition): keyset f of M keeps out departure d of holder D from M when f is, or descends from, the keyset of r that d's commit wrote (a removal or demotion carries its rotation, spec/permissions.md §3), or the node holds f's secret and f or an epoch f descends from has a live history entry, names met, naming d (or M's epoch start after it, or d by its departs mark). For a departure inside a holder M' of M: the node holds M's `secrets.0` and f's box to M' names a keyset of M' that keeps the rest out, or f's chain names it. A departure with no keyset (self-removal, expiry, audit drop) is judged only by a holder of M's secret, as today in practice (a non-holder presumed the box).
- **Covers** at a node: a departure from A by name (unchanged); a departure inside M concerning r by name (the left-out case), or when the box (M, r, e) in A's `secrets.0` names a keyset that keeps it out. No box to M at e and no name: not covered (a missing box is never "excluded").

## 4. Why it keeps frozen coverage's guarantee
Invariant to keep: every box carrying s_e (e's own, a later grant or re-seal at e, a history box of an epoch that holds s_e) is sealed to no key D can derive, for every departure the sealer, or a sealer it was bound to, knew.
- **An epoch covers only what its rotator named.** Directly for A's own departures. For a departure d inside M, e covers d only when e's box to M (the rotator's, or a sealer's bound to the rotator's anchors) names a keyset f that keeps d out: f was written by the removal itself, or by a chain of rotators of M each of which established its predecessor and so held that predecessor's names, which name d (M-level frozen coverage: the removal's keyset names d by its departs mark, every later rotator of M names what its epoch start and timeline show). The rotator relied on nothing a box did not prove: a signature under g's delete key is made only by a holder of g's secret, and every holder of g's secret established g and held its names (a secret learned otherwise is reading-only). The ignorant fork (C1) descends from the pre-departure keyset only; the forgery (C1') carries no valid signature under the post-departure keyset, since D never held its secret. Both fail the descent check at every boxer, wherever the removal is unknown, which is exactly where validity failed to help.
- **A departed holder never gets a later epoch.** Direct holders: unchanged (names, departs mark, validity). D inside M: s_e reaches M's holders only through boxes to M; every such box, by the anchors, is at a keyset descending from the one e's rotator judged, and by induction over descent that keyset's writer knew what e's rotator knew about M (or more). A later rotation e2 of A carries s_e in its history box; its rotator holds e's names (established e) and so e's anchors for M, and boxes M only at a descendant. A rotation that leaves M out names the anchors forward and the departures it could not judge away, so the next boxer of M under s_e knows them (that is the one place the old names survive, and only there). Grants of M after e (C3) anchor every later boxer to the granter's keyset: an ignorant re-sealer cannot move M to a fork or forgery.
- **Liveness.** A rotator's own epoch covers what it knows: it boxes M at a keyset it judged, or leaves M out and names the rest. Compared with today, C1 improves: the establisher that holds the removal's keyset and the ignorant fork boxes M at the removal's keyset instead of leaving M out (the fork wins `keys.0` LWW but does not descend). A fork of M's keysets that nobody has merged leaves M out of A's rotations until merged (today: boxed at the LWW winner when it passes the judgement). A node holding M's `keys.0` only from a compaction snapshot two rotations past the anchor cannot box M until A's next rotation re-anchors: as today for establishing.
- **Residuals** (unchanged): a sealer whose box never arrives while another's does; the compaction residue; the later admin path one level up (issues/keyset-name-later-admin-path.md); a departure with no keyset judged by nobody holding M's secret leaves M out until M's roster snapshot (today the same, through `presumed_boxes`).

## 5. What it stops placing under A, and the size
- `placed_with` places named commits whole under A's labels. Today: every lowering write, audit-drop demotion and roster snapshot of every thing below A since its epoch start. Under descent: nothing below A unless M is left out (then today's set for M), so no member's roster snapshot (its whole device roster) and no device removal reaches A's label through the names. Removals still reach it as certificates when the remover tracks A (spec/permissions.md §3), projected to the edge, as elsewhere.
- Names per history box and per grant copy: today about 60 B per boxed holder actor per role plus about 75 B per departure in its closure since its epoch start (the issue's 150 KB per rotation for 1000 accounts with two removals each). Under descent: about 92 B per boxed holder actor per role (the delete key adds 32 B), nothing per departure: 92 KB for 1000 accounts, bounded by the roster, not the churn. `keys.0` gains 64 B per superseded epoch with a held secret (one or two per rotation per role), carried by certificates and compaction snapshots.

## 6. Spec changes
- spec/crypto.md §3: a keyset's `supersedes` entries carry an optional signature; the signature's domain and digest; a thing's first own keysets list and sign for the derived epochs. §4 names: strike "and from each thing below A"; add the keyset name's delete key; the left-out clause; establishment's keyset-name rule with the signature; "keeps out" rewritten as descent or readable names (no box inspection, no "newest keyset", no presumed boxes); covers for a departure inside a holder by the box's keyset; boxing a holder actor by anchors and descent. §7: the delete key also signs descent (domain `"osis v1 descent"`), only under an established epoch.
- spec/wire.md §7: keyset names `[thing, role, n, nonce, delete key]`; the keyset's `supersedes` as `set<[epoch, Opt(signature)]>` (spec/data-model.md §9).
- spec/permissions.md §3 (Left out): the keyset a box names is a descendant of the anchors.

## 7. Verdict and the choice
The rule holds on paper and in the TLA instances (README: `Descent`). It is not a change to what is named alone: it adds a signature to every keyset, a 32-byte key to keyset names, and replaces the box judgement (`keeps_out`, `presumed_boxes`, `held_actors`' presumed view, `fresh_box_epoch`) with descent checks over held `keys.0` bodies, about a thousand lines across osis-crypto, osis-schema, osis-keys and osis-engine plus the spec, in the area with the most review rounds. Options for Tess:
1. Build descent (this note). Closes the issue's privacy and growth points; the only mechanism found that keeps frozen coverage without naming the closure.
2. Keep the names; stop placing roster snapshots whole: `placed_with` places a named snapshot projected to its reset-only delta (an epoch start on M without M's roster, as certificates already do; a name is met by the commit "applied with its `roles.0` delta on the name's thing", which the reset delta is). Closes the roster exposure; removals stay (they are certificates on A's label anyway when the remover tracks A); growth unchanged. Small.
3. Leave as is, with the exposure recorded in notes/sync.md Limits.
