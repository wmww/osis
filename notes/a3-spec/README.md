# A3: what a removal as evidence still owes
> Summary: Stage A3 (notes/redesign-plan.md, A3), after A4: a removal's list stays inside the removal, as built; far delivery as decided (lazy, the reader's deposit if built in its promoted form), the open points kept by number, the two parked leaks, what A3 changes in spec.

Design: notes/write-path-design.md §2 (E4: the list in the removal; How a removal reaches a far thing). Model: held-model `evidence` (lists whole in every copy), findings notes/evidence-model-findings.md. A list split into per-thing leaves is not the design: a removal carried down by key rotations two grants below arrives without the leaf for the thing it lands on (finding 35), and every rule tried for where leaves live either logged them once or kept every leaf with its removal for good (findings 30, 34). With the list in the removal whoever holds a removal holds what it keeps.

## Far delivery
A removal reaches a thing other than its own (decided, Tess) with that thing's next key rotation by a writer holding it (its keyset carries the demotion: spec/crypto.md §4 Carried demotions), and wherever a node holds both the removal and a write it judges (the removal is on the write's path, so in the evidence of the write's sync IDs). Nothing logs a removal anywhere for its own sake. Where neither happens (a thing no remaining member writes, its readers served only by stores), its readers show the removed key's later writes there: issues/removed-key-writes-show-far.md.
- **The reader's deposit on sight** (wanted if simple and good; not built): notes/write-path-design.md §2, D4; modelled (notes/evidence-model-findings.md 41): its promoted form needs no mechanism of its own; judging by unpromoted deposits is write-path Open 3.
- **The model**: held-model `evidence` models lazy delivery and lists whole in every copy; each reader is judged against a reference holding every removal some honest node syncing its sync ID holds, and the gap is counted (notes/evidence-model-findings.md 38).

## What users observe
Within spec/requirements.md as written (Tess): how long a far thing shows a removed key's writes (a node missing data reads as if it never had it); a write shown valid then invalid; hostile writers keeping a removal away (a removal binds the nodes that receive it); grants by a removed member showing until the removal arrives. spec/requirements.md Scale reads "on it, or on what its writers act through", which a removal carried as evidence falls under.

## The two parked leaks
Both wait for the account redesign (notes/priorities.md item 3); A3 closes neither.
- **A removed node reads later grants' role parts** (issues/removed-device-reads-later-grants.md): A3 wraps its copies as any certificate copy (under the sync ID's current wrap), so they show a removed party nothing it lacks. Smallest scenario: X removed from S at the rotation minting e; Y's grant of read to Z on S, wrapped under the epoch e supersedes; X, still opening S at a store that has not taken S's new step, opens the grant's role parts (Z's name, the box keys). Re-judged at A5, which gives every enabled thing an inbox as a card aspect and publishes KEM keys only for holders and enabled things.
- **A departed party reads the fresh deposit seed** (issues/removed-device-reads-later-grants.md): a thing's current `keys.0` travels as evidence no more; A3 closes nothing more (its keyset seed moves only demotions). Smallest scenario: X removed from M; M's rotation writes a fresh seed in M's `keys.0`; A's next rotation, sealing M at its new keyset, logs M's rotation commit (seed included) under A's sync IDs, which X still reads as a direct holder of A. Re-judged at A5, which replaces `keys.0`'s inbox field by the card aspect and the admin-only aspect naming a thing's inbox and cards, where the seed can live sealed under the new epoch.
