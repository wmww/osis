## 4. Verification and application
- **Unjudgeable**, the leaf sub-bullet: it has no edge-class part, rests on the edge of a held lowering that cuts it (notes/a3-spec/permissions.md §5.1 Reading a list: the lowering takes away what the part needs there, by the held chained writes on that edge), the lowering's list root holds a pair for the part's thing, and the node does not hold that leaf.
- **Provisional**, its last sentence: a complete pass gives agreement with what S's sources held, not with every input written: after it a verdict still moves when a later write arrives (a backdated removal, a re-grant) and when one written earlier reaches S (a removal arriving with a key rotation or with a write it judges: one that cuts the write, or one that voids the cut or removes a cover's writer). A promotion is undone by the rules (a promoted body judged invalid leaves the store). A delete at a store resting on a verdict is not: where every node holding the body so acted and its writer is gone, the write is lost though the node holding everything shows it (notes/a3-spec/README.md OPEN 12).
