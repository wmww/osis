## 2. Role grant writes
- **Logging a lowered grant**: a lowering has no logging rule of its own. It is an edge write on every path with that edge, so it is in the evidence (§4) of every held commit stating such a path; a removal taking write away is also in the evidence of every sync ID its reach joins (§4 Evidence of a sync ID), delivered there by the delivery duty (§4).

## 4. Verifying signers: evidence
- **Evidence of a sync ID S**, its removal and leaf bullets (the rest of A2's draft *stands*):
  - each held removal that **takes write away** on an edge (h, t) of a thing t outside S's sync unit whose **reach** under S is held, with its **witness** and that reach. A removal is an edge write giving its edge below write at its `ts` (a self-removal, a grant of read and a grant whose `until` is not after its `ts` included; a finalize, and a demotion from admin to write, not). It takes write away where (h, t) is a record edge, or a held chained write on (h, t) giving write or above (any `until`) is ordered before it by key; the first such is its witness (none on a record edge). Its reach: the held chained edge writes giving write or above (at any `ts`, any `until`, one since lowered included) on each edge of some way from t into the unit, a way being a chain of such writes and record edges (each from a holder to the thing it is granted on, or from a mint admin to the thing it minted, whatever writes later did to that edge), t first, ending on a thing of the unit, through any things, the unit's too. A removal taking no write away (a fresh grant of read, a reader's removal) reaches nothing; a removal on a thing of the unit is a body and brings no reach.
  - each **leaf** (§5.1 Frontier) of a lowering L in the set for a thing of the unit while a body under S on that thing with no edge-class part rests on L's edge and L **cuts** it: L takes write away (above) where content needs write, admin away where it needs admin (an admin-only aspect: L gives below admin, after a held chained write giving admin on its edge, or the edge is a record edge) (named by the leaf or not, stamped before or after L); and, for a lowering that is a body under S (on a thing of S's unit), each leaf of it for a thing other than its own, while it is held (its own thing's leaf is read only by bodies there, which are under S with it). Each addendum on a thing of the unit while it lists a held commit with no edge-class part (an addendum is no such commit) resting on its removal's edge.
- **Copies**, the lowering sentence: a lowering's copy carries its list root (§5.1: role entries and leaf pairs); its leaves go as evidence of their things' bodies (above), never with the copy.
- **The delivery duty**: every node holding a removal R that takes write away on (h, t) (the remover, any other holder: no node is designated) logs R, its witness, its reach and their evidence, and R's leaves it holds that are evidence there, under the own sync ID of each **target**: t and each thing reached from t by held chained edge writes giving write or above and record edges (any `ts`, any `until`), followed to the end, each reached thing's grants read under that thing's own sync ID; it runs for each target it holds read or above on by a path (a reader of a reached thing runs it there and below, whether or not it reads t). It holds each such sync ID for the duty (spec/sync-set.md §2) and logs there as it logs evidence (Logging evidence: stamped where its role through the way gives write, else deposited; a writer of the target holding R logs it as its own copy, spec/sync-inbox.md §4); the keys are those the way's grants box to their holders, which travel under each holder's own sync ID (Role changes travel). Each sync ID enclosing a reached thing gets R from that thing's own sync ID by its readers' own required set (spec/sync-set.md §2: a reader of an enclosing unit syncs the target's own sync ID too).
  - **Standing**: it is run whenever the node's view shows a target sync ID where it holds no record that a source showed R's copy: on holding R, on a new chained write giving write or above (a holder raised after R, a grant backdated before it, a new holder of a reached thing), on a retag. A node records per (R, sync ID) that a source showed the copy and lets the hold go; a copy is identified by (commit ID, thing), so several nodes running the duty log one entry. A copy deleted at a source after that is logged there again only by a node whose view requires it under that sync ID (Logging evidence), never by the duty.
  - A target it cannot reach (no source online) stays due at every pass until one shows the copy. A target whose way no longer gives the node read is not reachable by it: R reaches that target only through another node holding R and a way (README OPEN 6).
  - It reads no verdict: whether R applies is for the target's readers to judge from the evidence it brings.
- **Role changes travel**: *stands* whole (both halves: under A's own sync ID with the target's boxes to A and its `sync.0`, and under the sync ID of each thing whose keys open A with the chain's role commits), but "a removal goes there for `ROLE_KEEP` after its `ts`": a removal stays under each such sync ID while held, for good (notes/a2-spec/README.md OPEN 15). The keys-open half carries grants and readers' removals that read access through a holder thing reads (spec/sync-set.md §2 T's keys open) and a removal whose duty no live holder runs (finding 31); **(until A4)** the account-root clause.
- *Every other bullet of A2's draft §4 stands.*

## 5.1 Layer 1: role timeline
- **Frontier**: every lowering R (an explicit write giving its edge h below admin at its `ts`, a grant at write or read included) carries a **list**, as commit IDs, never counters, of what its signer's node had received (Received, spec/commits.md §4) as of R's `ts` T of the commits **resting on h** (those with a part stating a path with edge h, and the self-removals of h), but the role commits The list leaves out, in two forms:
  - **role entries**, in the list root itself: the role commits listed, each naming its commit's edge-class parts (a role commit writes one thing);
  - a **leaf** per thing t on which it lists a commit with no edge-class part (content, a purge request, an addendum, a compaction snapshot, an own cover): t and those commits, each listed in the leaf of each thing it has a part on resting on h, an entry of t's leaf naming its commit's parts on t alone. The list root holds, per leaf, a key only t's readers compute and the leaf's hash (notes/a3-spec/wire.md): a thing has a leaf iff the root holds its key.
  - A received commit with a `Split` entry the node cannot open (spec/wire.md §4) is listed in the leaf of the thing it cannot open, as A1's draft says (DRAFT).
  - *Stands (A1)*: Own cover (an own cover is listed in its thing's leaf); Nothing is exempt; ephemeral commits listed as durable; a list MUST NOT name a commit its node had not received as of T.
  - **Bound**: each list (the role entries, the leaf pairs, each leaf) is inline or linked, each linked one at most `MAX_LIST_BYTES` (spec/wire.md §4). Where a leaf would pass it the node leaves out, as few as fit, first the plain-content commits it received last on that thing, which its own addenda list instead, then the purge-request commits it received last there, each losing its right. A list root whose role entries alone pass it is refused (README OPEN 10).
- **Reading a list**: a node reads a role entry from the root of a held R, and a leaf's entries only from a leaf it holds whose (key, hash) the root holds. A part with no edge-class part on t, resting on h, is read against R's list only where R **cuts** it (§4 Evidence of a sync ID: R takes away what the part needs on h, by the held chained writes on h); there:
  - the root holds no key for t: R lists nothing on t, so it names no part on t;
  - the node holds t's leaf: it names the part iff the leaf lists its commit;
  - else the part is unjudgeable (spec/commits.md §4) until the leaf arrives.
  So "listed", "not listed" and "nothing listed" are each decided from what a reader of t holds, and a lowering that cuts nothing has its leaves read by no verdict and waited on by nobody.
- **The list** (who lists what): *A1's words stand*, per form: a node writing R lists every commit resting on h it had received as of T, the commits with no edge-class part in their things' leaves, where R, added to its own trust set, applies and is not void in its own walk with that list; otherwise it lists every commit with no edge-class part, then takes the role commits it had received one by one in the order it received them (one millisecond's by commit ID), keeping each where R, so trusted, still applies with what is kept, and leaving it out otherwise. Leaves never decide whether R applies (they name no edge-class part), so the leaving out reads the role entries alone and every leaf is written whole. A voucher builds its vouch's list the same way over R's role entries as written (Vouches, Writing). No reader checks any of this.
- **A remover that does not hold a thing**: it received nothing on t, so R's root holds no key for t: every part on t resting on h that R cuts is unnamed, and stands only by an addendum on t (a writer of t that received it, §5.2 Addenda) or, for a role write, by a vouch of a holder of the right that received it (Vouches; under `remake`, by that holder writing it again as its own). Nothing else keeps it; core waits for nothing before a removal (no hear-out).
- **Leaves travel as evidence**: a leaf is logged under a sync ID as evidence (§4: of the bodies there it is read for, and, a leaf for a thing other than the lowering's own, with its lowering wherever the lowering is a body), never inside the lowering's copy; a node keeps a leaf while a synced sync ID's evidence holds it (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs).
- *Every other bullet of A1's draft §5.1 stands* (Trust set, Vouches, steps 1-5).

## 5.2 Layer 2: content
