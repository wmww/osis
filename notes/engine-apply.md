# Engine: receiving and writing commits
> Summary: How the engine examines, pends, applies and re-examines commits (`apply/mod.rs`, `apply/waits.rs`, `apply/atomic.rs`) and what its writer refuses: sessions and counters, the HLC, how a part waits alone, the chain-certificate and chain-depth readings, junk and bad parts, late parts, the sync set, the re-examination cache, the writer-side rules, and apply and first-sync costs. The rules themselves are spec/commits.md §2-§4; this note says how the code keeps them. Not here: validity and re-judging (notes/engine-validity.md), retention on apply (notes/engine-retention.md), role commits (notes/engine-roles.md), streamed joins (notes/engine-paged.md).

## Sessions, counters and the HLC
- **Counters** (spec/crdt.md §2.1): every content commit writes under this open's session (`Core::session`, a random nonce per `Engine::create`/`open`); expiring memory commits under a second one (`Core::expiring`), so an expired commit forgotten with its counters leaves no gap in `session`'s ranges that a snapshot frontier could not pass. Counters per (aspect, session) are memory overlay rows (`Store::counter`/`reserve_counters`, never on disk; rolled back with an aborted transaction, so a session leaves no gap), reserved in the transaction that applies the signed commit.
- A restored or cloned store is a new session, so nothing it writes overlaps what the lost or sibling copy wrote: no restore gate, no counter adoption, no `OverlappingCounters` on honest nodes (osis `sync::restored_store_writes_at_once_spec_crdt_2_1`, node `crash::two_live_copies_of_one_store_converge_spec_crdt_2_1`, sim `restore_backup`). Two live copies still answer for one dialable key (`reach.0`) and LAN tag: a connectivity flap (LAN receivers keep the first source address they verified), not a correctness problem.
- When an own write of the current sessions turns invalid, later writes go under fresh sessions (`Core::fresh_session`: same random first 8 bytes, next count), past the hole, with an `OwnWriteInvalid` diagnostic.
- Rejected: counters per (aspect, node) under a per-node source with a restore gate. It costs one frontier entry per node that ever wrote an aspect in every later snapshot, two live copies void each other, and a lying frontier can push a restored copy to commit form for good.
- Role commits and snapshots are commit form (`write::commit_in`, `retention::snapshot_if`); `Mutation::commit_form` asks for commit form on a content commit.
- **HLC** (`engine/clock.rs`): a writer exhausting `logical` within one ms moves to the next ms instead of waiting.

## Examining a commit (`apply::examine`)
spec/commits.md §4 gives the rules; the code's shape:
- **Invariant**: what applies is a function of the parts held and everything else held, never of arrival order or which parts came together. A header or signature fault refuses the commit; each part is judged alone from the signed header, its bytes and held data; every wait ends only as the held set grows, so the applied parts are the least fixpoint of a monotone rule (osis-node `order::held_set_decides`).
- A commit pends whole only for drift, an unresolved header list (the held author's signature is checked first, so a false one costs no list), or when every held part waits (pages, parked). Its author's or actor's record unknown: thrown out (`Exam::Awaits`). A commit with no part held applies as its header alone.
- **A part waits alone** (`Aside`, `Waiting`) for its pages (`WaitBlobs`) or parked; a part `thing_wait` holds back (record, chain, author's reach) is thrown out (`Parts::unjudged`). The commit's other parts apply, so a commit can be applied and listed by `pending()` at once. Only parts the blob holds can wait: a thing whose part is not held (another's private thing in a mixed commit) makes nothing wait, and its delta joins late if it comes. Nothing in apply reads what the node syncs. A commit with unchecked boxes keeps them until its last part applies (`forget_unchecked`). Accepted cost (notes/whole-commits.md): a node syncing two labels fresh may show one thing's part before the other thing's record arrives.
- **Chain certificates missing** (`MissingChain`): the author's or an record chain's `created.0` is missing, or the author is not on the thing's record chain and no path of held edge writes and record links leads from it to a thing on that chain (`validity::reaches`: searched backward from the chain, from a thing to its first admin and its named holders (`Held::holders_of`), as perm's `roles_to_any`, so things below the author never enter it). Revoked authors keep their certificates, so their commits apply, judged invalid; a re-grant re-merges.
- **Chain depth** (spec/permissions.md §1): `validity::too_deep` makes every delta but `created.0` invalid on a thing whose chain is not known to reach its key actor within `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH` links (deeper, or a record on it not held: forgetting a record never makes a deep delta valid); per delta, from creation records alone, so nodes holding different parts agree. `thing_wait` stops waiting for the chain there (`ChainStep::TooDeep`). A deep thing's `roles.0` writes never reach perm (`add_role_commit_in`); the writer refuses such a commit.
- **Junk and bad parts**: a part not hashing to its header entry, or on an aspect of the wrong storage class, is dropped alone (diagnostic) and the commit applies without it; a blob holding only such parts is refused. A commit held pending for drift keeps the first copy of each slot; when one proves junk the commit is named (`Core::redeliver`) and node-sync ingests its other held copies again (`SyncEngine::redeliver`). A part that hashes but is bad by itself (`judge_part`: the author signed garbage) is dropped alone, unmarked: only those bytes can ever hash there. A node that could open only a bad section holds nothing of that commit, where a node opening none applies its header.
- **Prefix vs header** (spec/commits.md §4 last rule) is checked by node-sync where blobs are opened, for linked header lists once resolved (the pass); `ingest` takes plaintext.
- **`created.0` minting** (spec/commits.md §2): `carried_records` refuses a durable commit whose `created.0` part is not exactly one minting (`write::is_minting`: one source, one stamp, equal to `mint_created` under the `creation` view); several commits' mintings join. Header validation refuses `created.0` in a durable snapshot, and `Engine::snapshot` refuses it, so no junk can enter a state kept forever. A writer delta already cannot carry slots or foreign stamps (codec); the minting rule adds refusing resets and untyped structure.
- **Role-class content rules** are writer-side only: a received commit breaking them is applied, and perm judges each edge write at its point.

## Late parts (`apply::late_parts`, `atomic::join_late`)
A part of an applied commit arriving later: one that waits is kept pending under the commit's ID and joined once it can be; an invalid one joins and un-merges as on apply. Late `created.0` records and `roles.0` parts reach perm as on apply (the commit's edge writes re-added from every held part: a certificate copy may bring a commit's `roles.0` part after its `created.0` one), then held deltas are re-judged. Late `secrets.0` parts establish epochs with the commit's held `keys.0` like a whole commit's (`with_keys`; notes/engine-roles.md). A snapshot part that comes back judged invalid gives nothing: the parts dropped under its cover alone go missing again (`retention::uncover`), as when a held snapshot turns invalid.

## Sync set (`Core::syncs`)
`ix.syncing` holds only what the node was asked to sync (a group root per label, asked for at the first entry of its label the node opens, or its first pass); `syncs` adds what `groups.of` puts in those groups, so the set follows `group.0` in the transaction, validity flips included. `check_synced` (`Mutation::require_synced`) reads the labeled set for the thing and for the groups it is in, so a window in a group that is an item of a labeled group stays synced.

## Unjudgeable commits and parts (`apply/waits.rs`; notes/expiry-creation-design.md §3)
- A commit or part that cannot be judged (`Reason::MissingChain`, `UnknownCreated`) is thrown out after its boxes are read (`Ingested::Awaits`); nothing of it is stored. Node-sync keeps `Kind::Awaits` and fetches it again on `Changed::wakes`; one awaiting a record that a later batch of a fill brought is queued again at once (`open_entries`), so it is asked before the fill's next batch: what judging needs comes before the rest (spec/sync.md §6), also when an order-0 commit (a purge request by an admin) arrives ahead of its thing's creation. A part of an applied commit stays in `m.missing`, and its commit is named in `Wakes::woken`.
- Reading-only secrets a commit's boxes gave are sourced from it (`Waits::reading`, rebuilt at load) and go at its last part's apply (`forget_unchecked`), when it is judged bad, or when its last entry leaves (`SyncEngine::sweep_unchecked`). A refused box is read again when the budget frees (`Wakes::reread`).
- `reexamine` examines what is left pending (`Drift`, `WaitBlobs`, `Parked`) at every call.
- Tests with no sync layer fetch through `local_peer::Peer::receive`, driven by the same `Wakes`; `Peer::tick` and `Peer::reexamine` wake them too (a drift-parked creation that runs at a tick brings the record a thrown-out commit awaits). Every test peer runs what is due through those, never `<peer>.engine.tick()`/`reexamine()` (`tools/policy.py`); a test ingesting straight into `engine` (no fetch stand-in) may reexamine through an `&mut Engine` it holds.

## Writer side
- **Writer-side role rules**: at most one `roles.0` grant per role commit (excepted: a redemption's grant plus self-removal; an explicit removal of several others' grants on one target with a frontier, `revoke_joiner`); a frontier only on a commit that lowers a grant, a removal of an absent or expired grant included; a removal or demotion of another holder on a thing with a `keys.0` keyset carries its `secrets.0` and `keys.0` deltas (its rotation), a self-removal none (`validity::self_removal`: the author's own grant, or admin on the holder with no admin on the target without the holder's grant). The rules take a remove + put of one grant as its new grant, so a raise carries no rotation. A local commit breaking them, or with any delta invalid at this node, fails before it leaves.
- **Commit form encoding**: own writes of a commit-form delta are re-sourced to a commit placeholder before encoding (`build_and_apply`), so entries order as a reader decoding with the real commit source orders them (own commit first, spec/crdt.md §3.2); encoding them under the signer's own session would break canonical bytes when foreign dots share a map (a link redemption re-writing history boxes).
- `Mutation::dead_resets`: map tombstones for entry dots whatever their liveness (`osis_crdt::reset_entries`: a removal of an absent grant).

## Costs
Counted (osis-node `scale::`, notes/testing.md Scale checks): a grant with n and 10n content commits held does the same work (`grant_is_flat_in_content_commits`); an open from the persisted index rebuilds nothing (open costs: notes/engine-store.md); a first sync's commits ingested and bodies and entries judged grow linearly, and no pending commit is read again (`first_sync_is_linear`: without the wait cache every pending commit was examined again after each applied one, quadratic).
