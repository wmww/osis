# Chat
> Summary: The group-chat pattern (entitlement, per-thing roles, writer stamps, inbox blobs) as built in examples/chat: chat actor, personas, join by link, removal through the link actor with rotation, disappearing messages with expiring placements, hide set, reactions, read marker, typing signals; what each step costs (measured) and the limits the owner should know. Not reviewed by the owner.

## Structure (built: examples/chat, tests in examples/chat/tests/e2e.rs)
- **Chat actor C**: a group owned by the creator's person (better: a root actor, notes/permissions.md Patterns), created `Unsynced` and synced on the creator's servers under its own label (not placed in the home group); C's label is the chat's label. Moderators are C's admins. C holds the title and the hidden-message set.
- **Every participant is a writer of C** (spec/requirements.md, Permissions): a reader could neither stamp its own messages (they would sit as deposits) nor add itself to C's `/group`. Viewers that never post can be readers.
- **Persona P_i** per participant per chat: a group owned by the participant's person, placed in C's items (for its own writes: `/created`, `/group`, secrets; a subgroup alone is not a member, spec/data-model.md §8) and C's subgroups (for its messages). `grant(P_i, C, read)` (written by P_i's owner) lets C's actor read what P_i owns and gives P_i secrets of its own; P_i needs no label: its commits live under C's label. It owns the participant's messages (placed in P_i), reactions and read marker, and its nick.
- **Messages**: things owned by P_i, created in P_i (`create_expiring`) with their text; the timeline's time is the creating commit's `min(ts, received)` (`Osis::created`). One commit each.
- **Reactions**: `map<message tid, set<string>>` on P_j, a set add at the message's key (the first creates the entry; concurrent first reactions both count, spec/crdt.md §5.6). **Read marker**: a `tid` register on P_j. **Typing**: a `bool` signal on P_j, heard through one `on_signal_in(C)`. **Hide**: an admin-written `set<tid>` on C; the timeline skips it; nobody edits or purges another owner's message.
- **Disappearing messages**: `expires` on the message's text and its placement (`create_expiring`); gone on every node, offline ones too, at expiry.
- **Join by link**: K (a write link to C); the redeemer joins K by an invite grant, then makes its persona. A single-use link refuses a second redeemer.
- **Removal**: the moderator removes P_i from C's subgroups and items, then `demote(C, person)` (the person from `owner_of(P_i)`): on C for a direct grant; for a link joiner the API removes the joiner and L from K in one commit (`revoke_joiner`, also when the link was revoked or used up: the joiner may keep the seed, spec/permissions.md §9; one rotation; the other joiners stay members of K). K's rotation cuts the joiner off C's new content; what P_i posts afterwards reaches nobody (P_i is outside C's closure). The node does not delete P_i's old entries with C's delete key; other members just stop listing them.
- **Timeline**: no query API yet: C's `/group.subgroups`, each persona's `/group.items`, each message, sorted by its creation's display time, then `ts` and tid.
- **Not built**: time-bucket history groups (a label has no partial download, so one group per time bucket owned by C would let a new device fetch recent months first); deleting a removed persona's entries.

## Costs
- Measured (`costs_join_message_removal`, tier 2, asserts these with margin; `OSIS_CHAT_N` sets the size, `OSIS_TRACE_UPLOADS=1` prints one line per entry sent):
  - **Join 45 KB** (any size): the joiner's chain certificates under K's and C's labels (≈ 11.5 KB each, mostly hybrid KEM keys of 1216 B per role in its person's and recovery key's `/keys`; certificates other nodes already placed are not copied again), the grant (1.7 KB), the persona's own secrets (5.4 KB), its grant on C and content (2.9 KB), two inbox deposits (4.5 KB: to its own person, to C), the grant's copy under its home label with K's `/label` and `/created` (3.8 KB, spec/sync.md §3).
  - **Message 2.2 KB** on the wire: one commit, 3 projections (`/created`, text, the placement on the persona's `/group`), each repeating the 467 B header and the signature.
  - **Removal of a link joiner 26 KB at 4 members, 37 KB at 8, 61 KB at 16**: one commit removes the joiner and L from K with one rotation (2.7 KB of sealed boxes per remaining member, `/keys` 2.6 KB), its copy under C, one cascade rotation of C (≈ 4 KB, K is C's only boxed member), the `/label` steps; ≈ 3 KB per further member, ≈ 0.6 MB at 200. Other members send only heartbeats (0.4 KB each).
- The chat actor is created `Unsynced` and synced under its own label, not placed in the creator's home group: the creator's devices find it through the home label's `/label` cross-publish, and its rotations are not copied under the home label (notes/api.md, `create`).
- Rotation box cost measured at scale (osis-node `scale.rs` `roster_removal_cost`): a read+write rotation of 100 writers is 272 KB (2747 B per remaining member), of 1000 writers 2.70 MB (2707 B); committing it at 1000 took 29 s (a clone of the joined map per path op), 1.05 s with the map key index (0.10 s at 100: linear).
- **Owner decision: these costs are accepted; keep the simple format.** Cheaper only by wire-format changes, both declined: the header once per commit (message ≈ 1.4 KB at the price of a second fetch per message); one KEM key per actor and one box per member for all roles (halves the per-member removal cost, but done plainly gives an actor's read-only members its write and admin secrets; the safe variants either make role separation classical-only, handing delete power to a future quantum insider, or share keys only while the roles' holders coincide, which delays reads after a lower-role grant). Reasoning: removals are rare, groups of hundreds are rare, and 1 MB per removal is small; simplicity and the full post-quantum promise win. Downloads multiply the upload by the member count (≈ 120 MB of server egress per removal at 200), still only on removals. Removal breakdown at 4 members: K's rotation with both removals 9.8 KB (2.7 KB per remaining member: a read and a write sealed box; `/keys` 2.6 KB), its certificate copy under C 3.4 KB, C's rotation 4.2 KB, `/label` steps 3.6 KB, inbox blob to the removed joiner 1.9 KB, registrations 0.9 KB; each further member adds ≈ 3 KB; claim sets are padded to a power of two (32 B per slot, 8 KB per step per label at 200).
- Vandalism: any participant can edit C's `/group` and `writer`-purgeable bodies of C's own aspects; honest clients re-add their own persona deterministically and admins remove the vandal (authenticated malicious writers are bounded, not prevented).
