# Growth census
> Summary: Every known cause of permanent or unbounded growth of stored or synced data, in a node's own store and in its serving store (what it serves for others). Each row says what it grows with, an approximate size, whether it is inherent or closable, and the issue that tracks it. Rows are ordered by how badly a "plain data, no history, frequent overwrites" workload grows. Every change that adds a new kind of permanent data adds or updates a row here.

Tess's session rule: no per-session growth where there would otherwise be no growth. A write that adds permanent data anyway may carry more, whether the first of a session or every write. A leak is something that should hold steady growing with each session: a steady aspect overwritten and compacted, or a session that writes nothing.
Tests: `sessions::session_writing_nothing_leaves_nothing`, `sessions::steady_aspect_holds_steady_across_sessions` (24 overwrites and compactions of a register and a map written whole cost the same store bytes over 24 launches as in one), `retention::frontier_bytes_per_launch_probe` (a register written once per launch, compacted each, 1000 launches: 1 frontier entry, `Frontiers` 4 B + `Sources` 34 B in an 88 B delta).

Where: **Node** = a node's own store, **Serving** = its serving store (`host.redb`), **Both** = both. Marks: **M** = measured, **E** = estimated from encodings or spec, **C** = the code says so.

Measured (`growth::single_writer_replace_grows_nothing`): one writer replacing one register 50 and 500 times, with a month of tombstone ageing after each, then idle months: flat at the writer, a reader and the host, rows and bytes (heartbeats replaced by the node's next ones are forgotten once two later ones cover them). Before forgetting: about 830 B per write in the node store (394 B commit row + 239 B entry + 116 B blob record + 83 B present row) and about 390 B at the host.

The M probe for rows 1-2: 400 overwrites of one register by one node, compacted after each 200; store bytes are MemoryBackend `dump()` key+value totals, page overhead included, debug build, measured without row padding (notes/local-store.md Row padding adds about 4% on rows, small units at least 512 B). Node store 47 KB to 186 KB to 326 KB: about **690 B per overwrite, kept after compaction**, still there after 32 days once tombstones and entry rows have gone. Serving store 7.5 KB to 119 KB, then 66 KB after 32 days: about 146 B per overwrite of log records (row 2), which the log trim by age drops.

## Purge on replace (the option's own cost, spec/requirements.md Scale)
Every replacing edit of a purge-on-replace aspect makes the editing writer write a snapshot of the aspect from its whole state (notes/engine-retention.md Purge on replace); the replaced bodies and the edit's own body drop under it, so the aspect holds steady at one snapshot, but each edit writes and uploads O(aspect state). Counted (osis-node `scale::purge_on_replace_pays_its_state`, store bytes written per edit): a map of 200 / 2000 entries of 100 B, one entry overwritten: 70 / 713 KB per edit, linear in the map, against 43 / 32 KB with no history promised. Of the 713 KB, 246 KB is the snapshot's sealed body blob and 465 KB store rows (about twice the body: its opened commit record and the rows rewritten around the replaced snapshot); spec/requirements.md's "about 0.5 MB" counted the rows alone. Nothing permanent is added: no row outlives the bodies it judged.
