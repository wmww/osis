# Growth census
> Summary: Every known cause of permanent or unbounded growth of stored or synced data, in a node's own store and in what it keeps for others. Each row says what it grows with, an approximate size, whether it is inherent or closable, and the issue that tracks it. Rows are ordered by how badly a "plain data, no history, frequent overwrites" workload grows. Every change that adds a new kind of permanent data adds or updates a row here.

Tess's session rule: no per-session growth where there would otherwise be no growth. A write that adds permanent data anyway may carry more, whether the first of a session or every write. A leak is something that should hold steady growing with each session: a steady aspect overwritten and compacted, or a session that writes nothing.
Tests: `sessions::session_writing_nothing_leaves_nothing`, `sessions::steady_aspect_holds_steady_across_sessions` (24 overwrites and compactions of a register and a map written whole cost the same store bytes over 24 launches as in one).

Where: **Node** = a node's own store, **Serving** = what it keeps for sync IDs it keeps unread, **Both** = both. Marks: **M** = measured, **E** = estimated from encodings or spec, **C** = the code says so.

Multi-node growth: osis-sync `growth::single_writer_replace_grows_nothing_spec_commits_5` and `several_writers_grow_nothing_spec_commits_5` (one or two writers replacing a register, a reader and a node keeping it unread, past the tombstone lifetime: rows by kind and blobs at 3 and 30 more writes); osis-sync drops covered bodies and forgets covered commits (notes/sync-read.md, Local). A start after a session commits the same at n and 10n things and logs no body the session left unlogged, and a start with nothing new commits nothing (`read::a_start_after_a_session_finds_nothing_owed_at_n_and_10n_spec_sync_judgement_3`); a reconnect with nothing new stores a constant per connection: a cursor row per side in streams and the record in opens (notes/sync-streams.md).

## Purge on replace (the option's own cost, spec/requirements.md Scale)
Every replacing edit of a purge-on-replace aspect makes the editing writer write a snapshot of the aspect from its whole state (notes/engine-retention-covers.md Purge on replace); the replaced bodies and the edit's own body drop under it, so the aspect holds steady at one snapshot, but each edit writes and logs O(aspect state). Counted (store bytes written per edit): a map of 200 / 2000 entries of 100 B, one entry overwritten: 75 / 296 KB per edit, against 50 / 30 KB with no history promised; at 2000, 246 KB is the snapshot's sealed body blob, and the bodies dropped under it rewrite only the state pages holding their writes (`scale::covered_drop_is_flat`). Nothing permanent is added: no row outlives the bodies it judged.
