OPEN 10 modelled: six closures, of which chained writes along joined paths with pending on chained grants (iv) holds every property (no copy of junk, no roster leak), the residual a writer's finalizes and an inviter's grants, and every logging path taking the same closure; the story worlds of roles-model `paths` as a generator; OPEN 14 modelled: without the errand a removal reaches no further than today, brief 3 without reach is less safe than today where a removed writer has another route (reach restores it), Role changes travel's keys-open half must stay; the `wide` generator (paths of four holder things, churn and junk on every edge, second routes, split commits, late path entries, per-reader trust): no new failure; record edges as edges of reach, keys-open travel and the errand (the model under-read today without them, OPEN 17); a removal reaches only where it takes write away, with its witness (OPEN 16: a document's copies 2w + 1 whatever its readers); P3 owes a removal where it has reach (a path joined through a read hop carries no write); under J2 brief 3 loses a removal today delivers riding a write through a read hop (28); a removal's leaves for other things kept wherever it is a body (30, 34); Role changes travel's keys-open half still read by read access through a holder thing (31); a drop after a complete pass wrong while an earlier removal lags (33); a demotion carried by a keyset alone strands the content it lists (35); invites outside reach (37); what the generators still do not produce. The errand and reach are the drafted A3 delivery, not the decided one (no eager delivery: notes/handoff.md); the model keeps them until it is restated for lazy delivery (notes/a3-spec/README.md, Far delivery).

## The rule as modelled (`Rule::fixed`)
- **Required set of sync ID S over a held set H**: the evidence closure (`paths::evidence`) of these seeds: the bodies (H's commits on things of S's unit); the grants naming S's thing as holder; every removal in H (an edge write giving below write at its `ts`) of an edge (h, t) that **takes write away** (`Removals::TakesWrite`: (h, t) a record edge, or a held taken write on it giving write or above ordered before it, its **witness**, the first such, a seed too) whose **reach** is held (edge writes giving write or above, at any time, and record edges, joining t to a thing of the unit), with that reach. The closure adds every held edge write on an edge of a seed's path or on the remaining admin edge a lowering names, every held vouch against a held removal on a thing the set holds something on, and closes over what it adds. What lies on a thing outside the unit is a copy (per (commit, thing)), the rest bodies.
- **A function of current holdings**: every honest node logs under each sync ID it syncs what its own view requires there now; what no syncing node's view requires leaves (a delete-key holder deletes it, a holder still requiring it logs it again). Nothing is kept for having once been required: a removal's copy stays as long as some held reach shows its holder could write in the unit, which, reach counting grants at any time, is for good while the unit holds the thing.
- **Delivery**: a node syncs a sync ID and, by the standing duty, the sync ID of every thing in that unit; it holds what those carry and what it wrote; a writer syncs the thing it writes and its path's things. A granter logs a grant under its holder's sync ID. The thief logs its own bodies and the grants they rest on, never a removal.
- **The remover's errand, a standing duty** (with reach, the drafted A3 delivery: parked in `Rule::fixed` until stage D, notes/a3-spec/README.md Far delivery): the remover of (h, t) logs the removal (one taking write away), its witness, its reach and its own evidence under the own sync ID of t and of every thing reached from t by taken write grants and record edges (each read under the reached thing's own sync ID, held for the errand), followed to the end; and again whenever its view shows a new target.
- **Readers**: a reader holding one sync ID holds its entries, sees frontier leaves for its unit's things and for every thing a held edge write is on.
- **Pending**: a commit pends while an edge it reads (no record edge) has no held edge write ordered before it, or the latest such pends.

- **List leaves** (`Rule::listed`: `Lists::Travels(LeafKeep::Cuts)`, `leaves_home`, issues/zero-growth.md Proposals 1, A3): a lowering's content entries sit in a leaf per thing; the role entries stay in it. Leaf (R, t) is in S's required set while S requires R and a body on t with no edge-class part resting on R's edge (named or not, any order) whose need R takes away (R takes write away there: finding 27), and, for t not R's own thing, wherever R is a body (findings 30, 34); a node holds a leaf it was given while a synced sync ID requires it, the remover its own; an errand carries the leaves the remover holds that its target unit requires. A body a held R's root shows a leaf for, that the leaf would read, pends without the leaf.
- **Addenda**: an addendum is required while it lists a held commit (no edge-class part, no addendum) resting on its removal's edge. A reader sees every role entry.
- **Acting** (`Act`, `ActWhen`): a reader promotes (or a cover drops what it covers) on a valid body, drops an invalid one, vouches against an applied lowering; `AfterPass` acts on S's whole entries, the control `WhenNothingPends` on any arrival where the body does not pend.

**OPEN 14's delivery rules** (`Stage`, `Rule::reach`, `Rule::keys_open`, `Rule::author_reach`, `Closure::any_path`): reach on or off; Role changes travel's keys-open half (an edge write naming holder h a seed under each sync ID whose thing holds read or above on h through taken grants and record edges, any time: `ROLE_KEEP` read as for good) on or off; `Rule::records` (record edges in reach, keys-open travel and the errand: on; off is the control, finding 21); `Rule::removals` (`TakesWrite`, finding 22; `Any` the control); today's chain certificates (`any_path`: a member brings the taken writes on edges between things of its signer's chain to its thing over record edges and chained grants, and an edge write its signer's chain to its holder; a body's signer's chain to S's thing as the account's; no joined paths); today's chains kept for removals alone. Each over (iv)'s closure and judge, so the stages differ in delivery only.

**OPEN 10's closures** (`Closure`, `Open10`, `Pend`; notes/a2-spec/README.md OPEN 10): which edge-class commits the closure takes (`Takes`: all, chained, applied by the logging node's walk), whether a member brings its stated path's edges only where the path is **joined** (each non-record edge holds a chained grant ordered before it), and when a commit pends (`Pend::Any`: no held edge write ordered before on an edge; `Pend::Granted`: no held chained grant giving what the part needs on the hop (`Join::Needed`, finding 23), or the latest chained write pends). **Chained** (`World::chained`): spec/permissions.md §4 Chained writes, read with `first_chained`'s authority, self-removals, finalizes and vouches included (a superset of what any walk applies: P0). Every logging path takes the same closure: a node's view, the errand's items and its walk over taken grants, a granter's delivery under the holder, the thief's own logs. **Entry creations** (`World::creations`, `CommitEdges`): an edge-class part pends while a part of its commit on its thing leaving its holder no grant resets an entry whose creation (the first chained grant on that edge before it) is not held (finding 25); the closure brings every edge write's creation.

Choices where the design was open, each the smallest reading: "the closure over the paths" is `paths::evidence` (hop edges' writes of any validity, the named remaining admin edge, vouches against held removals on a thing the set holds something on, closed); records are static (record blobs not modelled; a record edge is changed by edge writes like any edge); a removal is a write taking an edge below write (a demotion from admin to write is not one), reaching only where it takes write away (finding 22); reach is by write grants and record edges only (invites and reader-level acts not modelled: an invite grant through a hop holding read and an invite is the one act a read hop carries, finding 20); taken-ness of a grant is not modelled (every grant counts); dependent things have no sync ID; unit membership is an input in time (the closure model checks the walk); role commits write one thing.

## Properties and results
Each a named check over every step of a world (`World::p1`..`p5_order`), the arrival-order check at the end:
- **P7** (`p7_leaf_waits`) a body held without a leaf that reads it pends, never valid or invalid. **P8** (`p8_acts`) every act a reader takes agrees with the node holding everything at that step.
- **P1** a one-sync-ID reader's verdict (pending, valid, invalid) on every body it should carry equals the node holding everything (a body owed by an honest writer and absent fails too). **P2** a body under several sync IDs is judged alike under each. **P3** every honest removal the full closure of S's bodies holds that can move a verdict there (it takes write away and its reach under S is held over everything written: finding 20) is in S's reader's own required set. **P4** every honest entry S holds is in its reader's required set; forgetting each content body moves no other verdict and leaves no copy only it needed. **P5** retags, a holder raised later and taken grants are moves of the worlds, checked by P1 at every step; arrival order: the reader missing any part of a body's evidence judges it as the node holding everything but that part (locality).
- **P9** (`p9_leak`) no roster leak: every copy an honest node logs under S is on a thing whose roster gives a role on a thing of S's unit (`roster_reach`: joined to it by record edges and chained grants, at any time), or in the closure of the grants naming S's thing (Role changes travel: S's own roles). **P10** (`p10_junk`) junk costs nothing: no honest node logs under S an edge-class commit on a thing outside S's unit that is unchained over everything written. **P0** (`p0_applied_chained`) the model's own: every write the node holding everything applies is chained.
- **Worlds**: the family (`family`, 30,720 worlds: route [A] or [A, O], five unit timelines with D or O joining or leaving R's unit, listed or not, a thief, a re-grant, an addendum, a raise after the removal, K's grant to Z vouched by Q, who syncs what, O apart from D's admin, record edges on the route: `Shape::mint`); random worlds of 14 moves (`random`); hand worlds per finding and risk.
- **OPEN 10** (`spec_open10`, `spec_story`; P0 to P10, vouches counting and not): over a family slice and 100 random worlds (moves added: a member's edge write on A or O to any holder, junk where it holds no admin; content along [A, R] with A writing R half the time, R holding nothing on D, or [O, A]; X removing or re-granting two members in one commit) and 40 story seeds under the six unit crossings: (iv) holds everything; the draft fails P9 and P10 only; (ii) and (iii) fail P1, P2, P5, P8 (finding 13), (v) P9, (vi) P1 (finding 14). Property `evidence_along_joined_chained_paths_plain` (about 50 CPU-s) and `_large`.
- **OPEN 17 and 16** (`spec_reach` `open17_*`, `open16_*`, `reach_explore` with `OSIS_REACH_CONTROL`): record edges and the narrowing change no stage's tally over the family (with minted shapes), random and story worlds, and no stage is worse than today; with record edges left out (`grants`), A3 fails P1, P3, P5 and P8 in 5 minted family worlds (the errand stops at a record edge), so the generators reach the class; the hand worlds stand for the keys-open and second-route shapes, which the sampled worlds reach for no stage.
- **OPEN 14** (`spec_reach`, `spec_gaps`): A3 holds everything (with J2, finding 23, and lists read per leaf, finding 24); every rule without the errand fails finding 17's worlds (P1, P3, P5, P8), today's included; brief 3 as drafted is less safe than today in finding 18's world, the derivation's actor seed alone also in 19's (property `evidence_reach_and_generator_gaps_plain` and `_large`: A3 over `wide` worlds with per-reader trust). Today fails P3, P4 and P9 (and P1 with a reader showing invalid what the node holding everything shows valid) in some `wide` worlds where every stated-path rule holds: its any-path closure takes chains a reader's walk does not read and misses entries along stated paths no chained grant reaches from the signer.
- **`Rule::fixed` holds everything**: the whole family and 300 random worlds (`explore`, `explore_random`), the default slice (every 291st family world), `_plain` (every 17th, 40 random) and `_large` (every 5th, 400 random). **`Rule::listed` holds everything** on the default slice, 300 random worlds (`explore_random`) and half of each property's worlds (with `Cuts` and `leaves_home`; `Judged` fails finding 27's world). Gate cost: `one_sync_id_reader_judges_as_everything_plain` about 40 CPU-s.

## Findings
<!-- registry -->
1. **The errand must follow taken grants to the end.** World: X removes K from A; K writes D through [A, O]; O's admin W syncs O alone, D's admin Y syncs D and R, nobody honest syncs both O and D. By A's taken grants the errand reaches O's sync ID only; D's reader holds K's write and the grants it rests on but not the removal, and judges K's write valid where it is invalid (P1, P3). Fix (`Errand::Transitive`): the remover reads each reached thing's taken grants under its own sync ID (held for the errand) and logs under every thing reached. The family: 98 worlds fail under `Direct`, none under `Transitive`. Risk 3's one-hop form (a dependent reached only through an enclosing root, `risk3_*`) holds with `Direct`; with no errand it fails.
2. **Copies are a function of current holdings, never placed once.** Placed when the commit needing them is logged and left alone (`Placement::AtLog`, the literal "logged with the commit that needs it"): (a) O on K's path is in R's unit when K's write is logged (A's grant on O a body, no copy) and leaves; the grant leaves R with O and R's reader pends on K's write for good (Risk 2); (b) a removal is no body of an enclosing unit, so placed with nothing it reaches R only by an errand aimed at D's own sync ID: R's reader judges K's write valid; (c) copies outlive what needed them (P4). 6,272 family worlds fail. Fix (`Placement::Function`, the design's own words "a function of the commits U holds"): every honest holder logs what its view requires now.
3. **The errand is a standing duty.** A reads D only when K is removed, so no unit lets K write; Y raises A to write later and the stolen K writes D. An errand run once at the removal never reaches D (Risk 4); run again whenever the remover's view shows a new target (the raise, a grant naming A, under A's sync ID) it does. Also covered when an honest node syncs A and D: its view requires the removal under D once the raise and K's write are held.
4. **A copy carries its leaf for the thing it is on.** K grants Z on A, Z writes D, X's removal of K lists the grant in A's leaf. A reader seeing only its unit's leaves reads the grant unlisted, drops it and judges Z's write invalid (roles-model finding 5, here through units). `Leaves::Evidence` (leaves for every thing a held edge write is on) holds; 300 random worlds give 10 P1 failures under `Leaves::Unit` (the family none: its removals list nothing on A).
5. **A verdict on partial evidence is provisional until a complete pass.** A reader holding K's write and K's grant but not a removal ordered before the write finds a setter on every edge, so nothing pends, and judges the write valid until the removal arrives. No rule over the held set alone tells this apart from complete evidence (1,334 such provisional verdicts over 300 random worlds); a missing edge write with no other setter pends. Judgement is local (the reader missing a part judges as the node holding everything but that part does: P5 holds), so the rule is: verdicts are final for a sync ID only after a complete pass of it (spec/sync-status.md §1), as must-not-hold already is; promotion of a deposit and anything shown as settled wait for it.

6. **A leaf is evidence of every commit its verdict reads, not only those it names.** World (`f6_a_leaf_kept_with_the_commits_it_names_strands_one_it_does_not_name`): K writes c0 and c1 on D along [A]; X, having received c0 alone, removes K listing c0. Kept only with the commits it names (`LeafKeep::Named`, the growth study's literal rule), the leaf leaves when c0 is covered and forgotten; c1, unnamed and invalid, then pends for good at D's reader (the root shows a leaf it does not hold), where the node holding everything judges it invalid (P4-forget; over 300 random worlds 54 P4-forget and 18 P1 failures). Fix (`LeafKeep::Judged`): the leaf is evidence of every commit on its thing with no edge-class part resting on the lowered edge, in any order (a list is read for a commit stamped after its lowering too, roles-model `paths` `content`), so it stays while one is held and leaves with the last. Still closes the growth leak: a leaf outlives no commit it judges.
7. **Acting before a complete pass is reversed by what was lacking; after one, by a later write or by an earlier one whose delivery lagged (findings 29, 33)** (`p8_*`). On finding 5's world a reader acting where nothing pends promotes the thief's write that the node holding everything judges invalid (`ActWhen::WhenNothingPends` fails); acting on S's whole entries never disagrees at any step of the family slice or the searches. An act after a complete pass is still reversed by a write made later (a re-grant makes a dropped invalid write valid again): every act is either undone by the rules or waits for a complete pass (spec draft: notes/a2-spec/commits.md Provisional).
8. **The split form adds no evidence requirement across things** (`split_a_part_on_another_thing_adds_no_evidence_requirement`): K writes T1 along [A] and T2 along [B] in one commit, Y removes K from B: T1's sync ID requires nothing of B's edges and judges T1's part valid, T2's reader the other invalid, each as the node holding everything. A sync ID's evidence is computed from its bodies' parts on its own unit's things, whose path entries exactly its readers open (notes/path-form.md).

9. **A commit's edge writes on one thing apply together, so their evidence goes together** (`f9_a_removal_of_two_holders_waits_for_each_creation`, `World::creations`). X removes h1 and h2 from A in one commit C (several holders, one frontier, spec/commits.md §2); h1 writes D through [A] after C. D's evidence held (h1, A)'s writes, so C, but not h2's grant G2 on A, whose entry C resets; spec/permissions.md §2 has C apply none of its edge writes until that creation is held ("one whose creation the reader never gets never applies there"; engine `add_role_commit`), so D's reader never applies C and shows h1's write, which the node holding everything judges invalid, for good. Fix: an edge write in the set brings the creation of each entry its commit's parts on that thing reset (`CommitEdges::Creations`), or, as the draft words it, every edge write on each other edge its commit writes there (`AllWrites`, more copies); both hold, `Off` fails P1 (reader pending, the node holding everything invalid).
10. **A vouch is read only on its own thing** (`f10_a_vouch_on_a_thing_the_evidence_holds_nothing_on_stays_out`). A vouch on N keyed by R counts parts on N alone (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches), so a vouch on a thing S's evidence holds nothing on is read by no verdict under S. "Every vouch keyed by a lowering in the set" copied it, with its writer's path, under every sync ID holding R: cost, and N's roster shown to readers of things N gives no role on. Fix: vouches on a thing an entry of the set is on (roles-model `paths::evidence`, the draft). It stays wide enough under counting by rounds (the voucher-standing study): an entry that moves a verdict on a set member lists a commit on that member's thing.
11. **The closure trusts stated paths and writes that never apply** (`f11_a_non_admin_of_a_path_thing_grows_the_evidence_with_writes_that_never_apply`: 3 and 30 junk grants, 27 more copies under D). Every edge write on a path edge is evidence whatever its validity, and its own stated path is closed over. (a) Cost: a writer of A without admin signs any number of edge writes on any edge (h, A) (none applies; role commits are kept whatever their validity, spec/permissions.md §6); every honest node holding them and the bodies of a thing written through (h, A) logs them there: the dependents' storage grows with what a non-admin of A writes, unbounded, and none of their writers or admins can stop it. Today's and A1's chain certificates take chained writes only (spec/permissions.md §4 Chained writes: each path edge a record edge or set at admin by an earlier chained write), which no non-admin can make. (b) Confidentiality: a writer of D stating a path [M1, M2, A] through things an honest node U holds, where M2 holds no role on A, makes U copy the writes on (M1, M2) under D: a roster that gives no role on D, to D's readers. The README's induction ("each edge's holder holds a role on T") holds only where the stated path is joined. Modelled (`spec_open10`): the draft fails P10 (`open10_junk_*`: 27 more copies for 27 more junk grants, none under any chained closure) and P9 (`f11_a_stated_path_no_grant_joins_leaks_a_roster_unless_paths_must_be_joined`, and the random worlds' [A, R] path), each over the random and story worlds too. Fixed by (iv) (OPEN 10), with findings 13 to 16.
12. **Dropping on a provisional invalid verdict can lose a valid write** (`f12_a_drop_on_partial_evidence_discards_a_valid_body`). X removes K (5) and grants it again (6); K writes c (7). A reader holding the removal but not the re-grant judges c invalid (a held edge write before c sets the edge, so nothing pends). Dropped at once, c leaves its own store; if every reader of D drops it on the same view and K's node is gone, c is gone where the node holding everything shows it, and "fetched where shown" has no source. The draft listed the drop among the acts taken at once; it waits for a complete pass like the other acts nothing undoes (OPEN 11). A cover's drop of what it covers at once stays: a cover whose writer is removed loses what only it carried even when the removal is written after the drop (spec/permissions.md §5.2), so waiting narrows nothing that the rule does not already accept (OPEN 8).
13. **A chained closure needs pending on chained grants** (`f13_a_path_set_only_by_junk_waits_at_the_reader_where_the_draft_refuses`). Q writes D through [A] where (Q, A) holds only K's junk grant. The node holding everything holds an edge write on (Q, A) before the write, so under the draft's pending rule (any write) it judges the write invalid; a reader whose closure takes chained writes holds none there and pends: P1, and with it P2, P5, P8 (ii, iii: 8 and 9 of 111 worlds). The pending rule read a write no walk applies. Fix: a part pends while an edge of its path holds no held chained grant ordered before it, or the latest held chained edge write so ordered pends (`Pend::Granted`; A1's own "a removal leads nowhere"). Then the node holding everything pends too: a write no chained grant reaches waits wherever it is, as a write through a path nobody ever wrote on already does under the draft. Refused or waiting, nobody shows it.
14. **The closure cannot take applied writes only** (`f14_a_dropped_grant_is_read_by_the_regrant_so_applied_only_strands_a_write`; (vi), 7 to 9 family worlds of 111). The re-grant rule reads each edge's first chained grant, dropped or not (spec/permissions.md §5.2), so a write the walk does not apply is read by a verdict. Q (admin by X) grants K (g1); K writes D; X removes Q, dropping g1; X grants K again: the node holding everything counts K's path from g1 and shows the write, a closure without g1 pends it. Applied-ness is also non-monotone (a write applying over a set may not over a larger one, and the reverse), so a fixpoint "take a write iff it applies over the closure so far" has no stable meaning; chained-ness only grows with arrivals and is exactly what the walk reads of writes it does not apply. So chained is the least closure the walk admits.
15. **What a non-admin can still force under (iv)** (`f15_a_writers_finalizes_and_an_inviters_grants_are_chained_and_copied`). Chained writes include a writer's finalizes (chained by write on the target, spec/permissions.md §4) and an inviter's invite grants (chained by its invite), and a holder thing's admins' self-removals: each is copied under every dependent whose bodies' paths use the edge, whether or not it ends or grants anything (27 more copies for 27 more). So the bound is: copies under S are the chained writes on edges S's bodies use; a party forces as many as it makes such writes: an admin any, an inviter invite grants, a writer finalizes, a holder's admin self-removals; a writer with neither admin nor an invite nothing else. Options (OPEN 10 in the README): accept (a writer of t already grows t's own storage without bound; this extends it to dependents' copies on used edges), or a finalize chained by admin only (who may finalize: a spec change), or a finalize or invite grant evidence only where it can take effect (needs a rule over the edge's walk: not monotone, not modelled).
16. **Every logging path takes the same closure.** Run with the filter in a node's view alone, the errand logged an honest node's own unchained "removal" (an edge write giving read: below write) under a target its walk over unchained grants reached, and a granter delivered its unchained grant under the holder's sync ID: entries no reader requires (P4-held, 12 of 71 worlds), so logged and deleted forever. The errand's items, its walk over taken grants, the granter's delivery and the reach all take the closure's writes (`Closure` read in `errands`, `errand_targets`, `seeds`). The built derivation must be one function used by each of them (the draft's Logging evidence and the (A3) reach bullet say "whatever their validity": OPEN 10).
17. **Without the errand a removal reaches no further than today** (`open14_with_no_honest_node_between_only_the_errand_reaches_d`, OPEN 14). Every rule with no errand (brief 3 as drafted, reach on without it, today's chain certificates) logs a removal under S only where an honest node holding it syncs S and its view requires it there. Finding 1's world (no honest node syncs A with O or D) fails P1, P3, P5 and P8 at D under each: D's reader shows the stolen K's write valid, the node holding everything invalid. Today fails the same worlds (family, random, story and `wide` alike): between A2 and A3 a user sees what they see today. Only the errand closes it (A3).
18. **Brief 3 without reach is less safe than today where the removed writer has another route** (`open14_a_removed_writers_other_route_carried_the_removal_today`). Y grants K write on D directly and A write on D and R; K writes D directly (stated path [], no hop through A); X removes K from A; the stolen K writes R through [A]. X syncs A and D, Y syncs D and R, nobody honest syncs A and R. Today K's write on D carries K's chain to D, which runs through A (chain certificates take any path), so X logs the removal under D and Y carries it to R as evidence of the thief's write. By stated paths D's evidence holds nothing of (K, A): under brief 3 as drafted R's reader shows the thief's write (P1, valid where invalid). Fixed by reach (A joins D, so the removal is required under D; no errand needed) or by today's any-path chains kept for removals alone (`Rule::author_reach`). The sampled worlds never reach it (a second route plus a bridge through the thing it reaches): the hand world is the evidence.
19. **Role changes travel's keys-open half carries removals reach does not** (`open14_keys_open_travel_carries_a_removal_to_the_things_reading_through_it`). K writes R through [A, O, D]; Y removes O from D; the stolen K writes R again. Y syncs D and A, N syncs A and R, R's admin V syncs R alone. A reads through O, so the removal goes under A (Role changes travel) and N carries it to R. Reach puts it under R alone (D joins R), where nobody holding it syncs. The derivation's actor seed alone (brief 3's deletion of the chain placed under a holder's openers) and reach in place of keys-open travel each fail P1 at R; today, brief 3 as drafted and reach with keys-open travel hold. So keys-open travel stays a seed of the required set until the errand (which reaches R from D's taken grants) lands.

20. **P3 owes a removal only where it has reach** (`gaps_a_removal_on_a_path_through_a_read_hop_is_owed_once_it_has_reach`; found by `evidence_reach_and_generator_gaps_large` at `wide` seed 14204407699, split and late). ## Cost (P6)
- Under (iv) (`cost_copies_follow_path_edges_and_churn_never_writes`, `cost_with`): copies under D are the chained writes on the outside edges its bodies' paths use, flat in writes (path of k = 1 to 4 holder things, m outside: m copies at 3 and 30 writes), one more per re-grant on a used outside edge (churn inside the unit is bodies), and repeated under each sync ID whose unit carries D (D in c units, a path of 3 with 1 outside D's own unit: 1 + 3(c - 1)). The worst a non-admin forces: nothing but its finalizes and invite grants on used edges (finding 15); an admin: its own writes on used edges (`open10_an_admins_writes_on_a_used_edge_are_copies_in_every_variant`); the draft: anything any writer of a path thing signs on a used edge (finding 11).
- Copies under a sync ID are the edge writes on outside things of the paths its writes state, shared by every write: n writes along a path of k holder things, m outside the unit, hold m copies at n = 3 and at n = 30 (`p6_*`, `cost`); bodies grow by one per write. Copies grow with role churn on the used outside edges (every held edge write on such an edge is in the closure), never with writes.
- Against issues/many-sync-ids-cost.md ("about half a sync ID's entries are certificate copies": a root's rotation, the grant, two account chains, logged per member's sync ID): along stated paths a unit written by one signer through [A] holds one copy (the signer's grant on A) plus the changes to that edge, and nothing for rotations or chains. Against issues/zero-growth.md: copies are a function of commits held (P4), so releasing the commits releases the copies; repeated role changes on a used edge grow the copies, the accepted "growth with role changes".
