# Evidence model findings
> Summary: What the held-model `evidence` model (M2) says about evidence per sync ID (notes/write-path-design.md §2): the rule as modelled and each choice made where the design was open, the properties and their sizes, the findings (the remover's errand must follow taken grants to the end; copies must be a function of current holdings, never placed once; the errand is a standing duty; each copy carries its leaf; a verdict on partial evidence is provisional until a complete pass), the cost shape, and what A2's briefs inherit.

Model: core/test/held-model/src/evidence.rs, tests tests/it/spec_evidence.rs, README (held-model, Evidence). Judging is roles-model `paths` with `Rules::fixed` (the rule A1 builds).

## The rule as modelled (`Rule::fixed`)
- **Required set of sync ID S over a held set H**: the evidence closure (`paths::evidence`) of these seeds: the bodies (H's commits on things of S's unit); the grants naming S's thing as holder; every removal in H (an edge write giving below write at its `ts`) of an edge (h, t) whose **reach** is held (edge writes giving write or above, at any time, joining t to a thing of the unit), with that reach. The closure adds every held edge write on an edge of a seed's path or on the remaining admin edge a lowering names, every held vouch against a held removal, and closes over what it adds. What lies on a thing outside the unit is a copy (per (commit, thing)), the rest bodies.
- **A function of current holdings**: every honest node logs under each sync ID it syncs what its own view requires there now; what no syncing node's view requires leaves (a delete-key holder deletes it, a holder still requiring it logs it again). Nothing is kept for having once been required: a removal's copy stays as long as some held reach shows its holder could write in the unit, which, reach counting grants at any time, is for good while the unit holds the thing.
- **Delivery**: a node syncs a sync ID and, by the standing duty, the sync ID of every thing in that unit; it holds what those carry and what it wrote; a writer syncs the thing it writes and its path's things. A granter logs a grant under its holder's sync ID. The thief logs its own bodies and the grants they rest on, never a removal.
- **The remover's errand, a standing duty**: the remover of (h, t) logs the removal, its reach and its own evidence under the own sync ID of t and of every thing reached from t by taken grants (each read under the reached thing's own sync ID, held for the errand), followed to the end; and again whenever its view shows a new target.
- **Readers**: a reader holding one sync ID holds its entries, sees frontier leaves for its unit's things and for every thing a held edge write is on.
- **Pending**: a commit pends while an edge it reads (no record edge) has no held edge write ordered before it, or the latest such pends.

Choices where the design was open, each the smallest reading: "the closure over the paths" is `paths::evidence` (hop edges' writes, the named remaining admin edge, vouches against held removals, closed); records are static (record blobs not modelled); a removal is any write taking an edge below write (a demotion from admin to write is not one); reach is by write grants only (invites and reader-level acts not modelled); taken-ness of a grant is not modelled (every grant counts); dependent things have no sync ID; unit membership is an input in time (the closure model checks the walk); role commits write one thing.

## Properties and results
Each a named check over every step of a world (`World::p1`..`p5_order`), the arrival-order check at the end:
- **P1** a one-sync-ID reader's verdict (pending, valid, invalid) on every body it should carry equals the node holding everything (a body owed by an honest writer and absent fails too). **P2** a body under several sync IDs is judged alike under each. **P3** every honest removal the full closure of S's bodies holds is in S's reader's own required set. **P4** every honest entry S holds is in its reader's required set; forgetting each content body moves no other verdict and leaves no copy only it needed. **P5** retags, a holder raised later and taken grants are moves of the worlds, checked by P1 at every step; arrival order: the reader missing any part of a body's evidence judges it as the node holding everything but that part (locality).
- **Worlds**: the family (`family`, 10,240 worlds: route [A] or [A, O], five unit timelines with D or O joining or leaving R's unit, listed or not, a thief, a re-grant, an addendum, a raise after the removal, K's grant to Z vouched by Q, who syncs what, O apart from D's admin); random worlds of 14 moves (`random`); hand worlds per finding and risk.
- **`Rule::fixed` holds everything**: the whole family and 300 random worlds (`explore`, `explore_random`), the default slice (every 97th family world), `_plain` (every 17th, 40 random) and `_large` (every 5th, 400 random). Gate cost of the two properties about 50 CPU-s.

## Findings
1. **The errand must follow taken grants to the end.** World: X removes K from A; K writes D through [A, O]; O's admin W syncs O alone, D's admin Y syncs D and R, nobody honest syncs both O and D. By A's taken grants the errand reaches O's sync ID only; D's reader holds K's write and the grants it rests on but not the removal, and judges K's write valid where it is invalid (P1, P3). Fix (`Errand::Transitive`): the remover reads each reached thing's taken grants under its own sync ID (held for the errand) and logs under every thing reached. The family: 98 worlds fail under `Direct`, none under `Transitive`. Risk 3's one-hop form (a dependent reached only through an enclosing root, `risk3_*`) holds with `Direct`; with no errand it fails.
2. **Copies are a function of current holdings, never placed once.** Placed when the commit needing them is logged and left alone (`Placement::AtLog`, the literal "logged with the commit that needs it"): (a) O on K's path is in R's unit when K's write is logged (A's grant on O a body, no copy) and leaves; the grant leaves R with O and R's reader pends on K's write for good (Risk 2); (b) a removal is no body of an enclosing unit, so placed with nothing it reaches R only by an errand aimed at D's own sync ID: R's reader judges K's write valid; (c) copies outlive what needed them (P4). 6,272 family worlds fail. Fix (`Placement::Function`, the design's own words "a function of the commits U holds"): every honest holder logs what its view requires now.
3. **The errand is a standing duty.** A reads D only when K is removed, so no unit lets K write; Y raises A to write later and the stolen K writes D. An errand run once at the removal never reaches D (Risk 4); run again whenever the remover's view shows a new target (the raise, a grant naming A, under A's sync ID) it does. Also covered when an honest node syncs A and D: its view requires the removal under D once the raise and K's write are held.
4. **A copy carries its leaf for the thing it is on.** K grants Z on A, Z writes D, X's removal of K lists the grant in A's leaf. A reader seeing only its unit's leaves reads the grant unlisted, drops it and judges Z's write invalid (roles-model finding 5, here through units). `Leaves::Evidence` (leaves for every thing a held edge write is on) holds; 300 random worlds give 10 P1 failures under `Leaves::Unit` (the family none: its removals list nothing on A).
5. **A verdict on partial evidence is provisional until a complete pass.** A reader holding K's write and K's grant but not a removal ordered before the write finds a setter on every edge, so nothing pends, and judges the write valid until the removal arrives. No rule over the held set alone tells this apart from complete evidence (1,334 such provisional verdicts over 300 random worlds); a missing edge write with no other setter pends. Judgement is local (the reader missing a part judges as the node holding everything but that part does: P5 holds), so the rule is: verdicts are final for a sync ID only after a complete pass of it (spec/sync-status.md §1), as must-not-hold already is; promotion of a deposit and anything shown as settled wait for it.

Not found: re-grants and vouches reach every reader without travelling as removals do (`Scope::Edge` adds nothing in the family or the random worlds): the party they restore logs them (a re-granted writer's node holds the re-grant as evidence of its own writes; a vouched holder's node the vouch), and the voucher syncs the thing. A world with the restored party gone and no honest node syncing both would need it; the design's "only removals travel at once" stands until such a world matters.

## Cost (P6)
- Copies under a sync ID are the edge writes on outside things of the paths its writes state, shared by every write: n writes along a path of k holder things, m outside the unit, hold m copies at n = 3 and at n = 30 (`p6_*`, `cost`); bodies grow by one per write. Copies grow with role churn on the used outside edges (every held edge write on such an edge is in the closure), never with writes.
- Against issues/many-sync-ids-cost.md ("about half a sync ID's entries are certificate copies": a root's rotation, the grant, two account chains, logged per member's sync ID): along stated paths a unit written by one signer through [A] holds one copy (the signer's grant on A) plus the changes to that edge, and nothing for rotations or chains. Against issues/zero-growth.md: copies are a function of commits held (P4), so releasing the commits releases the copies; repeated role changes on a used edge grow the copies, the accepted "growth with role changes".

## What A2's briefs inherit
- The required set as above, computed from the node's own holdings at every change (not at logging time), retag included: a thing leaving a unit turns the evidence still needed into copies, logged by any holder whose view requires them.
- The remover's errand: taken grants followed to the end, each thing's read under its own sync ID; a standing duty rerun on every new grant naming a reached thing (A3's briefs 3-4).
- A lowering's copy carries its frontier leaf for its own thing and for the unit's things.
- Verdicts final only after a complete pass of the sync ID; promotion waits for it.
- Delivery rests on: every sync ID has an honest syncing node, a writer syncs what it writes and its path's things, a granter delivers under the holder's sync ID. A unit whose only writers are the thief is out of scope.
