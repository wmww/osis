# Evidence model findings
> Summary: What the held-model `evidence` model (M2) says about evidence per sync ID (notes/write-path-design.md §2): the rule as modelled and each choice made where the design was open, the properties and their sizes, the findings (the remover's errand must follow taken grants to the end; copies must be a function of current holdings, never placed once; the errand is a standing duty; each copy carries its leaf; a verdict on partial evidence is provisional until a complete pass), the cost shape, and what A2's briefs inherit; list leaves as evidence (a leaf is evidence of every commit its verdict reads, not only those it names), acting on verdicts only after a complete pass, the split form; a commit's edge writes on one thing travel together, vouches only where read, the closure trusting unvalidated writes and stated paths (cost and a roster leak, open), drops of invalid bodies waiting for a pass; what the generators do not produce.

Model: core/test/held-model/src/evidence.rs, tests tests/it/spec_evidence.rs, README (held-model, Evidence). Judging is roles-model `paths` with `Rules::fixed` (the rule A1 builds).

## The rule as modelled (`Rule::fixed`)
- **Required set of sync ID S over a held set H**: the evidence closure (`paths::evidence`) of these seeds: the bodies (H's commits on things of S's unit); the grants naming S's thing as holder; every removal in H (an edge write giving below write at its `ts`) of an edge (h, t) whose **reach** is held (edge writes giving write or above, at any time, joining t to a thing of the unit), with that reach. The closure adds every held edge write on an edge of a seed's path or on the remaining admin edge a lowering names, every held vouch against a held removal on a thing the set holds something on, and closes over what it adds. What lies on a thing outside the unit is a copy (per (commit, thing)), the rest bodies.
- **A function of current holdings**: every honest node logs under each sync ID it syncs what its own view requires there now; what no syncing node's view requires leaves (a delete-key holder deletes it, a holder still requiring it logs it again). Nothing is kept for having once been required: a removal's copy stays as long as some held reach shows its holder could write in the unit, which, reach counting grants at any time, is for good while the unit holds the thing.
- **Delivery**: a node syncs a sync ID and, by the standing duty, the sync ID of every thing in that unit; it holds what those carry and what it wrote; a writer syncs the thing it writes and its path's things. A granter logs a grant under its holder's sync ID. The thief logs its own bodies and the grants they rest on, never a removal.
- **The remover's errand, a standing duty**: the remover of (h, t) logs the removal, its reach and its own evidence under the own sync ID of t and of every thing reached from t by taken grants (each read under the reached thing's own sync ID, held for the errand), followed to the end; and again whenever its view shows a new target.
- **Readers**: a reader holding one sync ID holds its entries, sees frontier leaves for its unit's things and for every thing a held edge write is on.
- **Pending**: a commit pends while an edge it reads (no record edge) has no held edge write ordered before it, or the latest such pends.

- **List leaves** (`Rule::listed`: `Lists::Travels(LeafKeep::Judged)`, issues/zero-growth.md Proposals 1, A3): a lowering's content entries sit in a leaf per thing; the role entries stay in it. Leaf (R, t) is in S's required set while S requires R and a body on t with no edge-class part resting on R's edge (named or not, any order); a node holds a leaf it was given while a synced sync ID requires it, the remover its own; an errand carries the leaves its target unit requires. A body a held R's root shows a leaf for, that the leaf would read, pends without the leaf. An addendum is required while it lists a held commit (no edge-class part, no addendum) resting on its removal's edge. A reader sees every role entry.
- **Acting** (`Act`, `ActWhen`): a reader promotes (or a cover drops what it covers) on a valid body, drops an invalid one, vouches against an applied lowering; `AfterPass` acts on S's whole entries, the control `WhenNothingPends` on any arrival where the body does not pend.

Choices where the design was open, each the smallest reading: "the closure over the paths" is `paths::evidence` (hop edges' writes of any validity, the named remaining admin edge, vouches against held removals on a thing the set holds something on, closed); records are static (record blobs not modelled); a removal is any write taking an edge below write (a demotion from admin to write is not one); reach is by write grants only (invites and reader-level acts not modelled); taken-ness of a grant is not modelled (every grant counts); dependent things have no sync ID; unit membership is an input in time (the closure model checks the walk); role commits write one thing.

## Properties and results
Each a named check over every step of a world (`World::p1`..`p5_order`), the arrival-order check at the end:
- **P7** (`p7_leaf_waits`) a body held without a leaf that reads it pends, never valid or invalid. **P8** (`p8_acts`) every act a reader takes agrees with the node holding everything at that step.
- **P1** a one-sync-ID reader's verdict (pending, valid, invalid) on every body it should carry equals the node holding everything (a body owed by an honest writer and absent fails too). **P2** a body under several sync IDs is judged alike under each. **P3** every honest removal the full closure of S's bodies holds is in S's reader's own required set. **P4** every honest entry S holds is in its reader's required set; forgetting each content body moves no other verdict and leaves no copy only it needed. **P5** retags, a holder raised later and taken grants are moves of the worlds, checked by P1 at every step; arrival order: the reader missing any part of a body's evidence judges it as the node holding everything but that part (locality).
- **Worlds**: the family (`family`, 10,240 worlds: route [A] or [A, O], five unit timelines with D or O joining or leaving R's unit, listed or not, a thief, a re-grant, an addendum, a raise after the removal, K's grant to Z vouched by Q, who syncs what, O apart from D's admin); random worlds of 14 moves (`random`); hand worlds per finding and risk.
- **`Rule::fixed` holds everything**: the whole family and 300 random worlds (`explore`, `explore_random`), the default slice (every 97th family world), `_plain` (every 17th, 40 random) and `_large` (every 5th, 400 random). **`Rule::listed` holds everything** on the default slice, 300 random worlds (`explore_random`) and half of each property's worlds. Gate cost of the two properties about 76 CPU-s (13 and 63).

## Findings
1. **The errand must follow taken grants to the end.** World: X removes K from A; K writes D through [A, O]; O's admin W syncs O alone, D's admin Y syncs D and R, nobody honest syncs both O and D. By A's taken grants the errand reaches O's sync ID only; D's reader holds K's write and the grants it rests on but not the removal, and judges K's write valid where it is invalid (P1, P3). Fix (`Errand::Transitive`): the remover reads each reached thing's taken grants under its own sync ID (held for the errand) and logs under every thing reached. The family: 98 worlds fail under `Direct`, none under `Transitive`. Risk 3's one-hop form (a dependent reached only through an enclosing root, `risk3_*`) holds with `Direct`; with no errand it fails.
2. **Copies are a function of current holdings, never placed once.** Placed when the commit needing them is logged and left alone (`Placement::AtLog`, the literal "logged with the commit that needs it"): (a) O on K's path is in R's unit when K's write is logged (A's grant on O a body, no copy) and leaves; the grant leaves R with O and R's reader pends on K's write for good (Risk 2); (b) a removal is no body of an enclosing unit, so placed with nothing it reaches R only by an errand aimed at D's own sync ID: R's reader judges K's write valid; (c) copies outlive what needed them (P4). 6,272 family worlds fail. Fix (`Placement::Function`, the design's own words "a function of the commits U holds"): every honest holder logs what its view requires now.
3. **The errand is a standing duty.** A reads D only when K is removed, so no unit lets K write; Y raises A to write later and the stolen K writes D. An errand run once at the removal never reaches D (Risk 4); run again whenever the remover's view shows a new target (the raise, a grant naming A, under A's sync ID) it does. Also covered when an honest node syncs A and D: its view requires the removal under D once the raise and K's write are held.
4. **A copy carries its leaf for the thing it is on.** K grants Z on A, Z writes D, X's removal of K lists the grant in A's leaf. A reader seeing only its unit's leaves reads the grant unlisted, drops it and judges Z's write invalid (roles-model finding 5, here through units). `Leaves::Evidence` (leaves for every thing a held edge write is on) holds; 300 random worlds give 10 P1 failures under `Leaves::Unit` (the family none: its removals list nothing on A).
5. **A verdict on partial evidence is provisional until a complete pass.** A reader holding K's write and K's grant but not a removal ordered before the write finds a setter on every edge, so nothing pends, and judges the write valid until the removal arrives. No rule over the held set alone tells this apart from complete evidence (1,334 such provisional verdicts over 300 random worlds); a missing edge write with no other setter pends. Judgement is local (the reader missing a part judges as the node holding everything but that part does: P5 holds), so the rule is: verdicts are final for a sync ID only after a complete pass of it (spec/sync-status.md §1), as must-not-hold already is; promotion of a deposit and anything shown as settled wait for it.

6. **A leaf is evidence of every commit its verdict reads, not only those it names.** World (`f6_a_leaf_kept_with_the_commits_it_names_strands_one_it_does_not_name`): K writes c0 and c1 on D along [A]; X, having received c0 alone, removes K listing c0. Kept only with the commits it names (`LeafKeep::Named`, the growth study's literal rule), the leaf leaves when c0 is covered and forgotten; c1, unnamed and invalid, then pends for good at D's reader (the root shows a leaf it does not hold), where the node holding everything judges it invalid (P4-forget; over 300 random worlds 54 P4-forget and 18 P1 failures). Fix (`LeafKeep::Judged`): the leaf is evidence of every commit on its thing with no edge-class part resting on the lowered edge, in any order (a list is read for a commit stamped after its lowering too, roles-model `paths` `content`), so it stays while one is held and leaves with the last. Still closes the growth leak: a leaf outlives no commit it judges.
7. **Acting before a complete pass is reversed by what was lacking; after one, only by a later write** (`p8_*`). On finding 5's world a reader acting where nothing pends promotes the thief's write that the node holding everything judges invalid (`ActWhen::WhenNothingPends` fails); acting on S's whole entries never disagrees at any step of the family slice or the searches. An act after a complete pass is still reversed by a write made later (a re-grant makes a dropped invalid write valid again): every act is either undone by the rules or waits for a complete pass (spec draft: notes/a2-spec/commits.md Provisional).
8. **The split form adds no evidence requirement across things** (`split_a_part_on_another_thing_adds_no_evidence_requirement`): K writes T1 along [A] and T2 along [B] in one commit, Y removes K from B: T1's sync ID requires nothing of B's edges and judges T1's part valid, T2's reader the other invalid, each as the node holding everything. A sync ID's evidence is computed from its bodies' parts on its own unit's things, whose path entries exactly its readers open (notes/path-form.md).

9. **A commit's edge writes on one thing apply together, so their evidence goes together** (in words: the model has one edge write per commit and no entry creations). X removes h1 and h2 from A in one commit C (several holders, one frontier, spec/commits.md §2); h1 writes D through [A] after C. D's evidence held (h1, A)'s writes, so C, but not h2's grant G2 on A, whose entry C resets; spec/permissions.md §2 has C apply none of its edge writes until that creation is held ("one whose creation the reader never gets never applies there"; engine `add_role_commit`), so D's reader never applies C and shows h1's write, which the node holding everything judges invalid, for good. Fix (draft permissions §4): an edge write in the set brings every edge write on each other edge its commit writes on that thing.
10. **A vouch is read only on its own thing** (`f10_a_vouch_on_a_thing_the_evidence_holds_nothing_on_stays_out`). A vouch on N keyed by R counts parts on N alone (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches), so a vouch on a thing S's evidence holds nothing on is read by no verdict under S. "Every vouch keyed by a lowering in the set" copied it, with its writer's path, under every sync ID holding R: cost, and N's roster shown to readers of things N gives no role on. Fix: vouches on a thing an entry of the set is on (roles-model `paths::evidence`, the draft). It stays wide enough under counting by rounds (the voucher-standing study): an entry that moves a verdict on a set member lists a commit on that member's thing.
11. **The closure trusts stated paths and writes that never apply** (`f11_a_non_admin_of_a_path_thing_grows_the_evidence_with_writes_that_never_apply`: 3 and 30 junk grants, 27 more copies under D). Every edge write on a path edge is evidence whatever its validity, and its own stated path is closed over. (a) Cost: a writer of A without admin signs any number of edge writes on any edge (h, A) (none applies; role commits are kept whatever their validity, spec/permissions.md §6); every honest node holding them and the bodies of a thing written through (h, A) logs them there: the dependents' storage grows with what a non-admin of A writes, unbounded, and none of their writers or admins can stop it. Today's and A1's chain certificates take chained writes only (spec/permissions.md §4 Chained writes: each path edge a record edge or set at admin by an earlier chained write), which no non-admin can make. (b) Confidentiality: a writer of D stating a path [M1, M2, A] through things an honest node U holds, where M2 holds no role on A, makes U copy the writes on (M1, M2) under D: a roster that gives no role on D, to D's readers. The README's induction ("each edge's holder holds a role on T") holds only where the stated path is joined. A filter keeps the walk (a write the walk applies is chained) but a part whose path edge holds no chained write then waits at a reader where the node holding everything judges it invalid: shown by neither. OPEN 10.
12. **Dropping on a provisional invalid verdict can lose a valid write** (`f12_a_drop_on_partial_evidence_discards_a_valid_body`). X removes K (5) and grants it again (6); K writes c (7). A reader holding the removal but not the re-grant judges c invalid (a held edge write before c sets the edge, so nothing pends). Dropped at once, c leaves its own store; if every reader of D drops it on the same view and K's node is gone, c is gone where the node holding everything shows it, and "fetched where shown" has no source. The draft listed the drop among the acts taken at once; it waits for a complete pass like the other acts nothing undoes (OPEN 11). A cover's drop of what it covers at once stays: a cover whose writer is removed loses what only it carried even when the removal is written after the drop (spec/permissions.md §5.2), so waiting narrows nothing that the rule does not already accept (OPEN 8).

Not found: re-grants and vouches reach every reader without travelling as removals do (`Scope::Edge` adds nothing in the family or the random worlds): the party they restore logs them (a re-granted writer's node holds the re-grant as evidence of its own writes; a vouched holder's node the vouch), and the voucher syncs the thing. A world with the restored party gone and no honest node syncing both would need it; the design's "only removals travel at once" stands until such a world matters.

## Cost (P6)
- Copies under a sync ID are the edge writes on outside things of the paths its writes state, shared by every write: n writes along a path of k holder things, m outside the unit, hold m copies at n = 3 and at n = 30 (`p6_*`, `cost`); bodies grow by one per write. Copies grow with role churn on the used outside edges (every held edge write on such an edge is in the closure), never with writes.
- Against issues/many-sync-ids-cost.md ("about half a sync ID's entries are certificate copies": a root's rotation, the grant, two account chains, logged per member's sync ID): along stated paths a unit written by one signer through [A] holds one copy (the signer's grant on A) plus the changes to that edge, and nothing for rotations or chains. Against issues/zero-growth.md: copies are a function of commits held (P4), so releasing the commits releases the copies; repeated role changes on a used edge grow the copies, the accepted "growth with role changes".

## What A2's briefs inherit
- The required set as above, computed from the node's own holdings at every change (not at logging time), retag included: a thing leaving a unit turns the evidence still needed into copies, logged by any holder whose view requires them.
- The remover's errand: taken grants followed to the end, each thing's read under its own sync ID; a standing duty rerun on every new grant naming a reached thing (A3's briefs 3-4).
- A lowering's copy carries its frontier leaf for its own thing and for the unit's things.
- Verdicts final only after a complete pass of the sync ID; promotion, deletes at peers resting on a verdict and vouch writing wait for it (7).
- A3: content entries in leaves kept with every commit they read (6); addenda kept while they list a held commit resting on their removal's edge.
- Evidence holds a commit's other edge writes on the same thing (9) and vouches only on things it holds something on (10); whether only chained writes go (11) and drops waiting for a pass (12) are the README's OPEN 10 and 11.

## What the generators do not produce
P1 is checked only over what the family, the random worlds and the hand worlds make, so the derivation's differential inherits these gaps: role commits signed along non-record paths (every grant and removal but K's grant to Z is a mint admin's on a record edge, so a lowering's own path is barely closed over); the remaining admin edge (`relies`) and the last-admin rule; invites, finalizes and `until`; covers, purge requests; trust sets (disputes); several edge writes per commit and entry creations (9); split commits outside one hand world; vouches on things other than A and addenda other than on D; paths longer than two; edge writes that never apply on a path edge outside the random K-grants-Z move (11); records (static). Each is judged by the same `paths` walk, which has its own worlds for them (roles-model), but whether the closure carries what they read under one sync ID is checked only by argument (notes/a2-spec/README.md (a)).
- Delivery rests on: every sync ID has an honest syncing node, a writer syncs what it writes and its path's things, a granter delivers under the holder's sync ID. A unit whose only writers are the thief is out of scope.
