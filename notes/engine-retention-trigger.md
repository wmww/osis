# Engine: the compaction trigger
> Summary: When a writer writes a compaction snapshot, durable and ephemeral alike (`retention/compact.rs`; spec/sync-compaction.md §1 trigger): what is counted and weighed, the estimate gating it, frontier gaps, and weighings owed until a node may write.

## Principle
A writer compacts when what a snapshot would free outweighs the snapshot, counted and dropped by one judgment, so nothing counted is left after it and no snapshot sets another off. Readers weigh nothing.

## Weighing
- A writer of a compactable aspect (content or the setting, no fixed retention, not a signal; or `keys.0`, `secrets.0`, `sync.0` by an admin: a replaced body counts once past `ROLE_KEEP`, a live one never, being kept for good) writes a snapshot once the bodies it holds that the snapshot would drop, two at least, cost more than `FACTOR` times the snapshot. A reader weighs nothing (`may_snapshot` first). No randomized delay: concurrent snapshots are covered-equal duplicates and the higher ID goes.
- **Counted and dropped by one judgment**: a body counts only where a cover drops it (`retention::drops_as_covered`, also asked by `drop_part_for` and `Engine::droppable_if_covered`, never an expiring body: no snapshot covers it) and where the snapshot claims its writes (`snapshot::claims`, the writer's own frontier walk). So nothing counted is left after the snapshot; counted apart, held purged bodies fired a wasted snapshot at every weighing. A body a held valid snapshot already covers and the node drops under it (another node's, or an own one replaced or under an own snapshot: covered drops, spec/sync-compaction.md §1) counts for nothing (`covered_already`, asking sync's own drop judgments, `retention::own_kept_under` and `snapshot::drops_under`; durable only; ephemeral covered bodies drop as they apply): it drops without a new snapshot, and counted it would fire one whenever a redundant body (a stale copy, a lagging holder's) is applied before its drop. Snapshots are not counted as bodies: each ephemeral author keeps its own until its next, and counting them would make two writers compact each other's for good.
- **A body weighs what dropping it frees**: its delta bytes, the pages they link (`body_bytes`, `apply::memory::each_page`) and the tree blobs its values link that no live state of the thing links (`freed_under`, stopping at blobs a live state links; nothing while a state's tree is not all held), plus `PART_OVERHEAD` and `ENTRY_CHARGE`. A held snapshot the new one would drop adds the tree blobs it alone links but counts as no body (`Candidate::body`): an edit's replaced chunk is mostly linked by the snapshot that last carried the value. A snapshot weighs its encoded state alone, root and pages as `body_bytes` weighs a held one (`snapshot_cost`: encoded a page at a time into a sink counting the pages, keeping none), a large value its root link (`compact::tests::a_snapshot_costs_its_values_roots_alone_spec_sync_7`). Blobs weigh by length (`Cipher::len`), none read. Overcount accepted: a blob a kept body still links counts as freed (compacts a little early).
- **The estimate** gates weighing (which reads bodies and encodes the state): weigh only once it passes `FACTOR` times the snapshot cost last found. It is that cost plus each body applied since that its setting does not keep live (`Trigger::applied`) or that left history (`leaving::left`), at its deltas' bytes (snapshots and parts no cover drops: nothing, `Trigger::counts`), plus per join the bytes of the trees it unlinked that the trees it linked do not hold (`Trigger::unlinked`, `unlinked_len`: both walked down where their node blobs differ, so an edit costs its changed path); 0 after a snapshot. A snapshot arriving adds nothing (two writers never compact each other's in turn). A validity flip, expiry, setting change or live bound passing (`Trigger::at`, run by `Engine::tick`) forces a weighing (`Trigger::weigh`).
- Under a setting keeping live bodies the weighing looks only at bodies out of history (`leaving` rows up to the cursor, `droppable`), walking each session's ranges up to its last candidate (`frontiers_upto`): flat in kept history.
- osis-sync's driver calls `Engine::compactions` after each step and drops what a durable snapshot covers at once; an ephemeral one's covered bodies go as it applies. A failed store fails the step (what was not weighed is due again): `Error::Store`, or any error once a store or blob write failed during it (`reload_owed`, `blobs_unsettled`), so a store fault wrapped in another error is never taken for the aspect's own. Any other error of one aspect's weighing or snapshot (one it cannot encode or fit in a blob) costs that aspect alone: reported (`OwnWriteFailed`, the thing and the aspect, deduped) and counted (`compact: snapshots failed`), weighed again once its estimate passes `FACTOR` times what it was at the failure (`Trigger::failed`) or when a weighing is forced, never per body (each try encodes the whole state: tries cost a geometric series of what the bodies brought), the step goes on to the others and does not fail, since a failed step closes every connection of the node (driver `failed`) and would at every step while the aspect stays so (`a_failing_snapshot_leaves_the_other_aspects_compacted_spec_sync_7`). Gap: an aspect whose estimate passed just before a crash weighs at its next write only.
- Test tools: `Engine::compact_memory_now`. A test that needs a large value's own replaces with no snapshot, or links another thing's chunks (they weigh as freed), keeps its writes by a history setting: a large value's snapshot costs about what a write of it does.

## Gaps (`Est::stops`)
- Per session source the weighing keeps where its frontier walk stopped short of held bodies (a missing, invalid or purged body, counters not held). A body behind a stop counts for nothing, so a lasting gap costs writes no weighing.
- A body filling the gap (its first counter, or a durable own whole replace past it) or a snapshot whose frontier passes it (`Trigger::part_held`, applied or late) continues the walk (`snapshot::walk_source`) and counts each body passed (under a keeping setting, out-of-history ones too, `leaving::out_of_history`): each body once however the gaps close; a gap held open at two writers sets neither off. Tests: `compact::tests` `*gap*`.
- Stops are memory only: after a restart arrivals count until a weighing finds the gap again.
- Rejected: an applied snapshot marking its aspect due or re-adding every blocked body (two writers' snapshots set each other off; a lasting gap had every write weigh again).

## Owed weighings (`Est::owed`, `owe_unwritable`)
- An aspect due at a node that may not write it keeps its estimate and is owed a weighing, woken by what lets it write: a role moving (`Core::timelines_changed` calls `Trigger::wake` for every thing under the moved roster) or its minting or delete keys arriving (`Later`: the thing waits in `Indexes::cover_waits`, woken by `replace::wake_covers`, as purge-on-replace covers do, notes/engine-retention-covers.md). A woken aspect is weighed whatever its estimate; one still unwritable is owed again.
- At open (`Engine::owe_unwritable_held`) every compactable aspect of more than one body the node may not write is owed: estimates and waits are memory only, and the trigger kept every writable aspect within `FACTOR` while running. No timer: a role commit is in force from arrival at the clock the trigger asks at, and an `until` passing only takes a role away.
- Cost: a wake reads a range of the thing's estimates; an open, a permission check per multi-body compactable aspect.
- Tests: `compact::tests::writer_compacts_once_it_may_write_again_spec_sync_7` (with and without a restart).
