# Time limits: hits and investigations
> Summary: What overruns the time limits and why (recurring patterns per stage and box), the investigations, and the raw hits not yet folded into the patterns; the limits and their tooling: notes/build.md, Time limits.

Each box's checks go to its own `~/.cache/osis/limits.log`. `tools/limits.py summary` lists that box's hits, flags the investigation triggers (notes/coordination.md, Time limits) and prints the hits after the `Digested through` time below that Hits lacks, in the entry format.

## Logging a hit
Whoever ran it (for a gate, its reviewer) appends one line per run at the end of Hits and commits it with the branch's review; two branches' appends merge keep-both. Format, starting as `summary` prints it:

`- <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM> <box> `<limit>` [<what>] CPU <a>/<b> s, wall <c>/<d> s at load <N>: <why>`

A gate is one line under `gate`: `- <date time> <box> `gate` <branch> review (<crates changed; N of 26 selected>), <lock wait>, <failures>: CPU a/b s (Nx), wall c/d s (Nx) at load N; `gate.hold` ...; over their shares: `<stage>` CPU a/b s, wall c/d s at load N (<cause>), ...: <why>. <No limit raised, or the raise>.` The why names the pattern below that fits and its issue; a new cause, or a stage far off its pattern's numbers, gets a sentence.

A watchdog or prose editor folds Hits into Patterns, deletes them and moves `Digested through` to the newest folded; `git log -p notes/time-limits.md` keeps every entry.

Both boxes have 16 threads (fastbox-0 u 1.69, ostrich u 2.3) and run other agents' work beside a gate, usually at load 15-40.
- **ostrich: every gate over `gate` and `gate.hold`, every hold stage over its share, CPU and wall.** `gate` 5200-11,700 CPU-s, 535-1600 s against 4094/299; the hold 4200-7500 CPU-s, 390-1150 s against 3795/241: default tests 2490-3200 CPU-s, 200-490 s (1610/115), properties 1400-2700 by selection, 93-460 s (1219/78), sim 1395-2200, 94-250 s (943/46). - **fastbox-0: CPU at its shares, wall over by load.** CPU within or up to 1.2x (default tests 1150-1350 CPU-s against 1183, drifting 1.01-1.10x over it on main, sim 600-780 against 693, properties 900-1150 against 896 with every large property selected); wall 1.1-3.9x, tracking load (default tests 110-300 s against 84, sim 38-105 s against 34, properties 60-110 s against 58): at load 30 a hold's tests get about 9 cores of 16. Most fastbox-0 gates at load 20-60 are over `gate` wall (232-850 s against 220), none over its CPU but by a rebuild. A warm gate at load under 15 fits (cleanup-cli 141 s, purge-tombstones-perm 177 s). Wall over with CPU within is load; CPU over 1.2x is worth a look.
- **Builds after a low-crate change.** Any change below osis-engine since the target's last build, the branch's or main's (osis-base, -crypto, -label, -crdt, -commit, -store, -schema, -keys, -perm), recompiles every dependent's test binaries. Floor, warm target (one comment line in osis-base, `cargo test --no-run`, 240 units): 330-390 CPU-s, 41-53 s at load 9-14 on fastbox-0, about 4x the `build` share's CPU and 3x its wall; its critical path is osis-engine compiled twice (lib 26 s, lib with its in-src tests 27 s), osis-crdt's lib tests (25 s) and osis-node `it` (23 s), with only 4-7 units running at once. Gates observed: `build` 290-1350 CPU-s, 37-370 s on fastbox-0 against 93/17; 440-2600 CPU-s, 76-500 s on ostrich against 126/23; clippy 2-6x with it (a full re-check). Above the floor is a target warm for an older commit (a reviewer gating in another worktree than its worker's: 3 of 4 code gates on 2026-11-14, up to 54 commits behind) and, for wall, load. Only a gate with nothing below its own top crates changed fits (8-108 CPU-s). A rebase over a main change reaching the GUI (osis, osis-store, osis-kit) also recompiles the GUI lint from cold: `gate.gui-lint` 210-480 CPU-s against 8-12. Investigations, 2026-11-14 fastbox-0 build; issues/gate-five-minutes.md item 7.
- **A manifest or lock change selects everything.** Any `Cargo.toml`, `Cargo.lock` or `.cargo/` change (a new dependency, a comment) selects all 26 crates and runs the unification check, which rebuilds the GUI: `gate.unify` 900-2200 CPU-s, 17-150 s on fastbox-0 against 34/5; 1700-4400 CPU-s, 60-180 s on ostrich against 46/7, its `gate.gui-lint` about 500 CPU-s against 12. By design (notes/gate.md, Paths outside a crate).
- **The reviewer's own builds in the gating worktree.** `tools/bin-at` or a by-hand variant build leaves the target at another revision; the gate after it rebuilds every dependent (`build` 7-10x). An edit after the reviewer's last build is compiled by `gate.clippy` (36-90 s wall against 7).
- **`gate.gui-tests` compiles inside its stage.** The build stage prebuilds core's test binaries only; the GUI tests compile kit, app and masonry test binaries (10-13 s of 10-21 s wall, up to 60 s at load 50 when a GUI test was edited) against a 3 s share: issues/gate-five-minutes.md.
- **Tiny wall shares trip on load.** `gate.deny`, `gate.gui-lint` and `gate.checks` (1-3 u wall: 2-5 s on fastbox-0) go over by wall in most gates under load (1.2-7x) with CPU within: issues/gate-five-minutes.md.
- **A storm seed or a slow tail in the sim.** `gate.sim` wall 3-5x with CPU 1.3-1.7x: a seed running to the 5000-delivery storm cutoff, 2-3 min single-threaded. Check the stage's slow and failing seeds before blaming load. Without a storm, the seed offset alone moves `gate.sim` CPU around its share (Investigations, cover-once-quiet).
- **By-hand holds over `heavy`.** 300-seed sweeps of `order::held_set_decides_plain` (12,000-14,500 CPU-s), several runs in one hold (A/B, plants: 5000-11,000 CPU-s), roles-model exhaustive families (`epochs-exh-c` 10,450 CPU-s grown by half, 4500-5550 trimmed, against 5060; `epochs-exh` with family C 8300-9000 before the split) and by-hand TLC configs (700-3400 s wall; liveness checking runs on one thread, so wall-bound) go 1.04-5x over `heavy`, which fits a gate-size hold. Size by design, not an investigation; log what ran.
- **Single tests over `test`.** `tools/limits.py tests` finds about 10 default tests over the limit alone: `order::held_set_decides` (166 CPU-s, 12-15 s), the `shred_sync` forensic-attack tests (17-42), `net_records::no_connection_resumes_*` (18), osis `sharing::holders_say_what_each_actor_is` (16, single-threaded), `shred::past_expiry_and_purge_alike` (13-14), held-model's repair properties (12-13), `rotation_holds_spec_crypto_4` (11-12): issues/tests-4x.md.
- **The disk fills.** A gate can abort at its build on a full disk while worktree targets are a normal 9-16 GB each: the rest is elsewhere (stray targets, scratch dirs: the watchdog's disk check, notes/coordination.md Standing agents). Not a limit: a gate that fails to build reruns.
- **A hung test.** A default test with no test of its target finishing for 10x the `test` wall limit is stopped and failed "hung" (notes/gate.md, Hung tests). A property seed can also run to its 600 CPU-s timeout (`gate.properties` wall about 2x).
