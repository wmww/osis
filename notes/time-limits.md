# Time limits: hits and investigations
> Summary: What overruns the time limits and why (recurring patterns per stage and box), the investigations, and the raw hits not yet folded into the patterns; the limits and their tooling: notes/build.md, Time limits.

Each box's checks go to its own `~/.cache/osis/limits.log`. `tools/limits.py summary` lists that box's hits, flags the investigation triggers (notes/coordination.md, Time limits) and prints the hits after the `Digested through` time below that Hits lacks, in the entry format.

## Logging a hit
Whoever ran it (for a gate, its reviewer) appends one line per run at the end of Hits and commits it with the branch's review; two branches' appends merge keep-both. Format, starting as `summary` prints it:

`- <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM> <box> `<limit>` [<what>] CPU <a>/<b> s, wall <c>/<d> s at load <N>: <why>`

A gate is one line under `gate`: `- <date time> <box> `gate` <branch> review (<crates changed; N of 26 selected>), <lock wait>, <failures>: CPU a/b s (Nx), wall c/d s (Nx) at load N; `gate.hold` ...; over their shares: `<stage>` CPU a/b s, wall c/d s at load N (<cause>), ...: <why>. <No limit raised, or the raise>.` The why names the pattern below that fits and its issue; a new cause, or a stage far off its pattern's numbers, gets a sentence.

A watchdog or prose editor folds Hits into Patterns, deletes them and moves `Digested through` to the newest folded; `git log -p notes/time-limits.md` keeps every entry.

- **The reviewer's own builds in the gating worktree.** `tools/bin-at` or a by-hand variant build leaves the target at another revision; the gate after it rebuilds every dependent (`build` 7-10x).
- **`gate.gui-tests` compiles inside its stage.** The build stage prebuilds core's test binaries only; the GUI tests compile kit, app and masonry test binaries (10-13 s of 10-21 s wall, up to 60 s at load 50 when a GUI test was edited) against a 3 s share: issues/gate-five-minutes.md.
