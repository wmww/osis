# Sync protocol: wire choices, osis-proto, osis-label
> Summary: Why the sync wire is laid out as it is (fixed prefix, Padmé marker, request/reply framing, what opens bind), and how osis-proto (frames, Hello, messages and their decode caps, relay-only gate) and osis-label (blobs, chunk trees, large values, steps, proofs, bundles) are built. osis-proto holds bytes only, no session: the session and the store are osis-sync's (notes/sync-boundary.md). Layouts: spec/wire.md.

## Wire choices
- The blob prefix is a fixed byte layout so a host parses it without a CBOR decoder; the AEAD binds it as AD so a reader cannot re-prefix a ciphertext (new expiry or commitments) under a fresh blob ID.
- Padmé length recovery: a `0x80` marker then zeros inside the ciphertext (ISO 7816-4 style). One byte, no length field, and canonical because the padded length must equal `padme(len + 1)`.
- Header lists, the delete-key list and purge go by link above `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` (128 KiB) up to `MAX_LIST_BYTES` (64 MiB); header-list chunks carry the level-2 commitments only (notes/crypto.md).
- Request/reply by id over length-prefixed frames; both Hellos go out at once, so negotiation costs no round trip; per-entry status, so a batch partly succeeds; a Status names a stale key (9) but never carries the head (notes/label-access.md).
- Opens bind the opened side's Hello challenge and static key (no replay elsewhere); stamps, deposits and deletes do not, so stamped entries and deletes stay relayable.
- Retired wire kinds (4-6, 8-10, 12, 13, 15-18, 21-23) and status codes (10, 15) decode as unknown; their numbers are never reused.

## osis-proto
- Codecs only, no session (the session is osis-sync's, notes/sync-boundary.md). Modules: `limits` (every protocol-version-1 limit and per-message cap of spec/wire.md §2, §11-§13 as a named constant; node-policy values the sync side applies: `PASS_WAIT_MS`, `deposit_budget`); `frame` (`FrameDecoder`, `encode_frame`; a peer's first frame at most `MAX_HELLO_BODY`); `hello` (Hello, `negotiate`, `estimate_offset`: median of per-sample offsets; `ClockSamples`: the offset once three Pongs came back; round trips over 10 s ignored); `msg` (`Msg`, one variant per kind; `Code`; `Cursor` (at most 256 bytes by construction); `DepositItem`, `BehindItem`, `OfferItem`, `SharedItem`, `Grant`, `Event`, `Fetched`, `Join`; `flow(kind)`: request, reply or push; `peek_head` for a frame still arriving; `stream_event_len`, an event's exact size in a reply, for paging); `dec` (typed reads of decoded arrays); `prove` (connection proofs bound to role, challenge and listener key; `verified` stops at the first bad one); `filter` (Golomb-Rice pages; `count_gcs` checks a page and counts it without holding values); `relay` (`RelayGate`); `api` (client-port outcome, payload, `ApiCode`); `bundle` (`BundleImport` over osis-label's importer); `examples` (a fixed sync ID, a message of every kind, a rejection of every kind: tests, vectors, fuzz seeds).
- `SyncId` is osis-base's `LabelId` re-exported under its spec name until the base type is renamed (issues/deprecated-terms.md).
- Decoding (`Msg::decode`): CBOR items counted before anything is built (`MAX_MESSAGE_ITEMS`), nesting at most `MAX_OBJECT_DEPTH`, embedded values (Call args, Sub filter, Reply and Notify values) at most `MAX_VALUE_DEPTH`; then the kind's exact layout. Rules beyond the layouts, each the simplest reading of spec/wire.md §11: ids by `flow` (an Error may carry 0); kinds 36-42 have the top bit set, NoRecord exactly 2^31; Full and Refused events only in a Stream with no cursor, which has `seq` 0 and no `report`; a Refused event's code is neither 0 nor 12 (room is told by Full); a Shared answer other than 0 carries nothing and a `sig` only beside a tag; Status at most 1024 codes (one per Delete item); a Beacons width in 8..=30, `lo < hi <= 2^f`, its page decoding inside the range with fewer than 8 zero padding bits, at most 2^22 values. Session-level rules (page order across Beacons, one Prove and one Resume per connection, a Sub's id reuse) are the session's.
- **Relay-only** (`RelayGate`, in memory, one per node; spec/sync-ids.md §6 Relay-only): admitted by prefix expiry and size, counted against a per-sync-ID rate (`RelayLimits`: 6000 relays and 32 MiB per minute), passed on once per blob within its expiry window; at most `MAX_RELAY_SEEN` (2^16) pairs remembered, past which the earliest-expiring is forgotten and a replay of it passes again.
- Code 14 is `DECLINED` (refused for no stated reason) beside 1 `REFUSED` (a signature, credential or session that does not stand): spec/wire.md names both "refused".

## osis-label
- Modules: `blob` (the prefix with its class, `Class`: durable, ephemeral, relay-only; frame/open; blob ID; `host_prefix`; `Prefix::for_keys`/`for_delete_keys`, the one place commitments are computed; prefix/header agreement `Prefix::agrees`; the blinded key at a level `Prefix::key_at`; `unwrap_any` newest first); `tree`; `chunks`; `step` (`Step`, `StepId`, `StepKeys`, `register` (genesis or child; claim set and entry boxes; a step without rotation passes the parent's keys), `path_to`, `ClaimSet`, `open_entry_box`, `match_secret`, `genesis_label`); `auth` (stamps, deposits, `DeleteItem`: `sign` by the label delete key, `sign_by` a level's delete key plus `key_for_levels`' label key, `verify`); `inbox`; `addr` (strict `address` values and their text form; base64url is osis-base's); `labels` (`label.0` records to verified steps); `link` (`ShareLink`); `bundle` (`BundleWriter`, `BundleReader`, `Importer` yielding `BundleEvent`s).
- `tree` re-exports osis-commit's chunk trees (streaming `TreeWriter`/`TreeReader` over `TreeSink`/`LinkSource`; errors are `osis_commit::Error`, converted by `From`). The reader is retry-safe: a `Missing` blob can be fetched and the call repeated; it checks every length it passes and `seek`s by node lengths, fetching only the path; `HashSink` gives shape and hashes without encryption. `BlobWriter`/`BlobReader` frame chunk, node, page and link-page blobs under a `Framing` (dedup secret, expiry, delete keys, each blob blinded by its own link blind; `keeps(prefix, link)` is the re-encryption rule) and implement osis-crdt's `PageSink`/`PageSource`; `Written { id, blind }` names each blob written; `Reuse::index` plus `BlobWriter::reusing` dedup by reuse; `BlobSink`/`BlobFetch`, `BlobStore`, `MemoryBlobs`.
- `chunks` (large atomic values): `LargeParams`; `chunk_value` (inline at or below the threshold, else the canonical bytes as a tree); `read_value` (whole, through `TreeReader`, rejects a chunked value at or below the threshold); `string_head` (a large blob or string's length from its first bytes). Streams of large values walk the tree with `TreeReader` (osis `ValueStream`).
- `label.0`, `address`, `step_record` and `box` codecs are osis-schema core types, re-exported. From osis-commit label uses only the chunk trees and their `Error`: the inbox payload carries the commit as its `CommitBlobPlaintext` value, and prefix agreement takes the header's `(level, delete key)` pairs, its blind and the expiry.
- Limits: a prefix has at most one commitment per level (3); expiry 0 means none (a `Some(0)` expiry is refused); blobs `MAX_BLOB` (8 MiB); tree nodes 1..=512 links, level 1..=48, the root always interior (an empty value is one empty chunk); claim sets a power of two up to `MAX_CLAIMS` (65 536), every entry box key among the claims, boxes strict `box` values; steps, claim sets, inbox payloads and bundle frames decode with depth 512 and `MAX_MESSAGE` bytes; links 8 addresses, 4 peers, 4 relays, 256-byte URLs; bundle frames 1..=`MAX_MESSAGE`.
- Importer rules beyond the spec: an entry must follow its blob in the bundle and a stamped entry its label's path (the writer emits them in that order); deletions pass through unverified, like a peer's log events.
- The address text form `<url>#<base64url key>` is provisional and client-side only.
