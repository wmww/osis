# Node sync as built (osis-engine `labeling/`, osis-node `sync/`)
> Summary: How a node syncs its labels: the module map of `labeling/` and `sync/`, the sync records under `y/`, the pass in order, certificates (revokes about authors, others' cards, linked blobs), inbound entries and reconciliation, pass records and complete passes, local requests, the driver, handed-off groups, junk copies, first sync newest first, the simulator hook and the invariants the simulator established. Topics with their own notes: opens and re-registration (notes/label-access.md), deletion judgments (notes/sync-convergence.md), inbox, outbox and promotion (notes/inbox.md), work sets and idle labels (notes/node-sync-work-sets.md), co-reader peers and local passes (notes/peers.md), the memory lane (notes/ephemeral.md), the engine (notes/engine.md).

## Module map
- `labeling/` (osis-engine, pure judgments over engine state):
  - `actors`: reader actor = the nearest thing up the record chain with secrets of its own; `secret_actor` up the record chain; `may_read(x, t)`: `t`'s secrets derive from `x`'s, or `x` reads that actor on the timeline; `closure(g)` from the `groups.0` index; `group_labels` from `label.0`.
  - `blobs`: `Body` = one held commit, `parts` those the label's actor may read (`readable_parts`), the blob carrying every held part. `label_bodies(g)`: commits touching the closure with a readable part, plus, on a home label, the `label.0` and `created.0` writes of labeled groups whose nearest labeled record-chain actor it is (through standing record edges) (`to_label`: sealed per label under the label's secret). `seal` (one section per thing, `extra` sealed sections copied verbatim), `seal_to` (one section under a given secret: copies, heartbeats), `open_commit` (sections under the thing's, the label group's derived and own secrets; prefix vs header, spec/commits.md §4; `OpenedBlob::unknown` for the sealed-section retry in `redeliver`). `wrap_secret` with the rotation exception (the commit's `keys.0` delta writes a read keyset of the label's secret actor: the newest held epoch it supersedes). `head_keys` (sync key from any held secret, write/delete only established, deposit key from the actor's seed). `delete_secrets_of` (per commitment group of the blob's listed aspects) and `chunk_delete_secrets`: the header's delete keys matched over every held epoch along the record chains (`Core::delete_chain`, `delete_secret`); an entry's `Kind::Commit` keeps its blob's prefix salt and the aspects its sections list for the signatures.
  - `certs`: `certificates(g)`: node card commits and perm's chain certificates as (commit, aspect indexes); `part_body`; `revoke_certificates`. Labels and steps are flows' `create_label`/`issue_step`.
  - `converge`: the pure rule, `classify(Facts) -> Hold`, `may_delete(why, live, carried)`.
- `sync/` (osis-node; `SyncEngine::handle(Event) -> Vec<Action>`, sans-I/O; `Event::{Tick, Local, Up, Bytes, Sent, Down, Reply, Notify, WantRoom, End}`, `Action::{Dial, Send, Close, Wake, Reach, Host, Request, Room, Ended, Authed}`; one write batch per `handle`):
  - `conn` (connections to hosts and co-readers: dialing, labels on each, requests in flight), `open` (opens and walks), `inbound` (entries arriving, reconciliation, invite requests, hints), `pass/` (the pass: `bodies` must-hold and placing, `certs`, `beats` heartbeats/repair/re-seal, `inbox` stamping/clearing/grant copies, `promote` relay wait and promotion, `register` steps, `delete`), `work` and `marks` (work sets; notes/node-sync-work-sets.md), `timers` (resyncs, wakes, starting Syncs, running marked passes), `deliver` (flows' results), `local` (app and flows requests, the outbox), `fill` (first sync), `handoff` (groups handed off to another node), `memory` (the memory lane: notes/ephemeral.md), `peers`, `serve` and `stream` (co-readers, `reach.0`, LAN, co-reader serving, the node log stream: notes/peers.md), `host` (hosting: notes/serving.md), `bundle` (file bundles, spec/sync.md §11), `status`, `io` (config, inputs, outputs), `state` (records), `driver` (tokio).
- One `ClientSession` per host connection, dialed with an ephemeral transport key (hosts are never dialed `dial_as`). The hosts are the union of the synced labels' host lists, `SyncConfig.hosts` and inbox hints; a label's `peer://` addresses are not hosts (`peers::host_keys`: never dialed from `label.0`), so a label listing only those is served here (notes/peers.md).

## Records (`sync/state.rs`, local records under `y/`)
Entries per (label, blob) with wrap, stamp, certificate flag and what the blob is (`y/e`; `Kind::{Unknown, Commit{commit, parts}, Inbox, Request, Parked, Bad}`); presence per (host, label, blob) with a deposited-there flag (`y/p`; no gained-ID lists: an upload goes to each host lacking the entry); cursors (`y/c`) and heads (`y/h`) per (host, label) (a co-reader's stream position is in its record, notes/peers.md The stream); blob keys of commit blobs (`y/b`; `by_commit`: an encryption is reused under other labels, a certificate's never); certificates placed (`y/k`); heartbeat times (`y/t`); host tombstones and useless-wrap marks (`y/g`, the wrap in `y/3`: a fresh encryption next time); label hints from inbox blobs (`y/i`); carriers (`y/s`); the outbox (`y/o`); sent inbox deliveries (`y/v`); owed deliveries (`y/y`); stranded heartbeat times (`y/u`); direct commits for the app (`y/j`); retired replaced labels (`y/q`); forgotten things' inbox blobs (`y/f`); filling labels (`y/4`); declined invites (`y/m`); handed-off groups (`y/w`); tombstones (`y/x`: time and what the entry was, certificate flag and kind, every kind alike: a host reporting it within the tombstone, by an announcement or an OK answer, gets the entry recorded again quietly as present there, `host_holds`, so the host pass judges it there as any present entry; the tombstone stays, so announcements of it are still not fetched, but bytes this node asked for of a recorded entry are taken: a writer's chunk delete fetches the chunk for its salt; `deleted` is rebuilt from them at load); peers' records (`y/r` dialable secret, `y/a` contacts, `y/l` link sharers); the host secret (`y/z`); the client listener secret (`y/2`). A failed sync-state flush keeps its writes for the next flush. Growth of these: notes/growth.md.

## The pass (`sync/pass/`)
Runs after the log was read to its end (`Synced`) and on every later change marked into its work set:
1. Head keys recomputed. No read epoch of the head held: open what it can and place a stranded heartbeat, nothing else (notes/label-access.md). A holder whose box at the head's epoch does not open (a holder actor's keyset withheld) cannot claim the head either (its claim key is at that keyset), so the open fails and `label_status` reports the label stranded (osis `SyncState::Stranded`), never synced (`sync_review::no_secret_of_the_heads_read_epoch_is_stranded_spec_sync_5`). An admin with a newer established epoch re-registers first (role commits, step, then the rest after `Registered`).
2. Must-hold bodies (before keep-at-least, or not covered by a snapshot entry of the label), role commits first: uploaded stamped, relayed with their stamp by readers, deposited when a non-writer's own edge write. Chunk, page and list blobs of uploaded commits and the held blobs their deltas link though not listed (reused chunks, a snapshot's values) go with filler wraps.
3. This node's certificates and node card per label (a fresh encryption), only the deltas no genuine entry of the same commit present at every host holds (any node's copy or closure blob), never closure deltas; another node's demotion or `keys.0` only `SETTLE_MS` after receipt; inbox grant copies; a heartbeat per `HEARTBEAT_MIN_INTERVAL`.
4. Writers promote deposited entries (notes/inbox.md).
5. Deletions of must-not-hold entries present there, carriers first (notes/sync-convergence.md). Expired entries are forgotten, never deleted.
- Entries (`entry_for`) take any entry of the body (a certificate copy too) and prefer one present at the host (spec/sync.md §6); certificate copies are wrapped like the body (rotation exception), so others open them as the body.
- An entry that unwraps but does not open as a commit is `Kind::Bad` or `Kind::Parked(commit)` (handed to `ingest`, parked, relayed when stamped), never retried and never unsettling the label: one junk deposit would stop every upload under it.
- Entries present at a host, unwrapping and never opened, are opened or fetched again at each pass (`refetch`); a fetch the host cannot serve is not repeated on that connection until it announces the blob again; every fetch goes through `fetch_on`.

## Certificates (`pass/certs.rs`, `pass/delete.rs`; spec/permissions.md §4)
- `certificates` places the aspects its copies lack outside the closure (else a copy duplicating closure parts is deleted as a strict subset and re-placed each pass) and counts the aspects of any present blob of the commit under the label as placed. Copies after a rotation (`place_chain`) carry only grant, `keys.0` and `created.0` deltas of things outside the label's closure. A copy whose deltas another entry of the commit holds is skipped only when the entry's deltas were checked against the header, it is present at every host, and a rotation commit's exception-wrapped blob counts only under the newest epoch (notes/permissions.md, Certificates). Placing a certificate never rotates (`uncovered(g, false)`): under an uncovered epoch the copy waits in `deferred` for the next pass, which rotates first.
- **Revokes about authors** (`Engine::revoke_certificates`): the authors seen under a label are the authors of its commit entries that this node judges invalid in a part the entry holds or, for a role commit, in any grant op (aspect validity holds while any op on the aspect is valid: a single-use race loser's void grant sits beside its valid removal of L), or cannot judge yet (pending) (`Engine::judged_valid`). A revoke changes no verdict on writes its frontier covers, and placing it for them cost every link joiner the remover's whole chain (about 12 KB) under its home label at each joiner removal; a later write the revoke voids brings it then (revocation reaches a label when a holder next syncs it, spec/requirements.md).
- Applied edge writes lowering a grant on a thing outside the closure go under the label with what judges them (`Timeline::write_certificates`: their authors' chains), placed like chain certificates. Another node's demotion or `keys.0` is left to its author for `SETTLE_MS` when the author reads the label's actor and so places it itself (`Delivery::Removal` goes under the labels holding the holder that the author syncs); one by an author who does not, an outsider's removal from the inbox among them, is copied at once: the hold only dedupes co-readers' copies, and a blanket hold delayed a group's readers of such a removal by 30 s for nothing (`tied::grant_and_removal_orders_large` seed 15 asserts within it). A grant is never held: the hold is for frontier and `keys.0`-only commits, and no author places a grant's copy but the holder's admins.
- **Uncovering departures**: under a read epoch that does not cover a known departure, a node that may not rotate places only the revokes of departures inside holders of the label's actor that leave its epoch uncovered now, each the whole write on that actor (with its rotation's `keys.0` and `secrets.0`: a reader that syncs the holder waits for every delta of it, so a grants-only copy left the label's admin pending in `link_race`), taking the newest write lowering the departed holder's grant on the actor it left, the last on its chain (not the second hop, which finds nothing two levels down); plus the newest `keys.0` commit of each actor on the chain, projected to that `keys.0` like a chain certificate (not whole: a covered removal that rotated X stays unplaced), with its author's chain. Without those an intermediate rotated apart from the departure write, or M rotated after a self-removal, is unknown to a rotator holding none of its secrets: it seals to the old keyset, which the departed holds, so `rotation_due` refuses and the label strands. They tell the rotators what keeps the epoch uncovered and what to seal to; nothing else goes under it (spec/permissions.md §4 exception). Tests in `core/node/tests/it/revokes.rs` (`nested_departure_*`).
- **Others' cards** (`stale_card`, judged once settled): another node's card entry (only its own `node.0`, `reach.0`, `keys.0`) is outside the required set when a newer valid write of that node on each of those aspects is present at the host in an entry checked genuine (`Engine::latest_by`: the newest applied commit whose delta there is valid, by the given author; `ts` is the author's word, so anyone's later commit on another's card stood for the current one before), or, for `node.0` and `reach.0`, when the node departed: it reads the label's actor no more and an applied write on its way there removed a grant or passed its `until` (its own grant, or one of an actor it reads through: its account's; a departed node is never dialed). "Reads no more" alone misjudged holders whose chain the view lacked, and their cards churned in the sweep (tests `account_removed_node_card_deleted_spec_permissions_4`, `until_expired_node_card_deleted_spec_permissions_4`, `unheld_chain_card_kept_spec_permissions_4`). Its `keys.0` stays: chains of its old writes may need it. Admins (the delete key) delete them.
  - `certificates` never re-places this node's own deleted copy, except its own card while it reads the label's actor (`own_card_read`): a re-granted node whose card an admin deleted places it again (`regranted_node_card_placed_again_spec_permissions_4`). Until both converge on the role commit (an admin that has not applied the re-grant still judges the node departed) the two ping-pong once per pass each.
- Every "newest commit on an aspect" choice (chain certificates, inbox copies) takes the newest valid applied write.
- **Linked blobs**: certificate copies carry the blobs their parts link (`place_linked`: header-list trees such as a demotion's linked frontier) as certificate entries, so a verifier syncing nothing else resolves them. Once this node knows no entry of the copied commit under the label (one placed and not confirmed yet counts) they go (at a host by delete key, by an admin; in this node's store, of copies no host holds, by `local_deletions`, the entry only, the bytes being the engine's), unless another commit entry there links them (`copy_links`, `linked_by`).

## Inbound (`sync/inbound.rs`)
- New entries from a host that no writer vouched for (no stamp under the head's write key; before a head is known, any step's this node knows: a superseded step's key may be a removed writer's) and that open under nothing held keep no bytes (fetched again once they unwrap, `refetch`). Past `SyncConfig::unvouched_cap` (2^16; inboxes a sixteenth, bytes kept, since an inbox blob opens by its bytes) such entries per (host, label) keep not even the presence there, nor the entry once no host has it: one host's junk does not crowd out another's deposits. Pages a waiting commit lists are kept and fetched whether or not an entry was seen.
- Commit blobs with linked header lists get their prefix checked against the resolved header in the pass (`check_linked_prefixes`, once per blob per run); a disagreeing blob is `Kind::Bad`. Bodies and certificate copies of such commits are classed by the resolved header.
- Blobs are stored by ID; unwrapped with the group's held read secrets newest first, else as an inbox blob for an actor the label is an inbox of (`Engine::inbox_actors`) (the read KEM of each held read epoch); commit plaintexts ingested role commits first; unopened entries retried as new secrets arrive. Commit blobs announced without bytes are fetched at once, the blobs a waiting commit's header lists before apply, the rest lazily (`fetch`, `fetch_missing`, or one value's tree: `fetch_tree(tid, link)` fetches what `Engine::tree_missing` lists, node blobs below held ones and the chunks held nodes list, called again as nodes arrive; osis-commit's `Missing` names no blob, the engine walks the tree instead).
- Memory-class and relay-only entries (by prefix class) go to the memory lane (`sync/memory.rs`; notes/ephemeral.md, Memory commits): judged by the pass like durable bodies, with every record about them in memory, never in the sync records or the store.
- **Presence after a cursor-less Sync** (a first sync, or a cursor lost in a crash) is a replay of live entries only, so presence for that (host, label) starts over from it: stale presence after a host wipe would keep a writer from re-uploading.
- **Reconciliation** (`reconciled`): presence follows it. After code 15 (`Requests::trimmed`, set by `NeedReconcile { trimmed }`, taken by the reply on that connection) an entry in `have` present at that host goes through `absent_at` (as a Deleted record: tombstone and `deleted` once present nowhere, a lost chunk relinked); otherwise only its presence goes: a cursor of another log (a wiped or replaced host) must not tombstone what the node knew (peers' copies would be refused for `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, and re-uploads judged as after a delete). The memory lane's reconciliation after a start (cursor kept) leaves durable presence to the Sync from that cursor: clearing it there would lose what a following code 15 needs (without code 15 a reader holding a body it has no delete key for would keep its entry, and its co-reader feed, for good after a trimmed log). Code 15 gives a host no power a Deleted record lacks (that one needs no presence there). Residual: a connection lost (or a crash) between the 15 and the reconciliation's reply loses `trimmed`, and the cursor is already cleared, so the next connection replays and those entries stay (one round trip wide). Tests `sync_more::trimmed_log_*`, `other_log_reconcile_keeps_entries_spec_sync_6`.

## Complete passes and pass records
- `SyncEngine` implements flows' `SyncState`. `complete_pass` (spec/permissions.md §3) is judged from the labels' actors and pass ends at their hosts and at the co-readers it received the label from while connected to them (a push run counts as one); peers are waited for at most `peer_pass_wait_ms` (5 min) after the actor's last remote role commit (`role_change`, memory only; from the engine's start, `started`, when none applied in this run: a restarted node hears its peers out first); a label with no host and no such peer, served here alone, passes once that wait is over, and its local pass decides re-seals. `passed_since` (untie) reads the same `label_passed`. Co-reader details: notes/peers.md.
- Pass records (`SyncEngine::passes`) outlive the connection: `complete_pass` and link finalization read them as "a pass ended after X" (X = the actor's last remote role commit, or the link's `until` by wall time), which a pass from before going offline still proves; anything newer under the actor arrives as a remote role commit and restarts them. The only loss is the race any pass has (a box written right after its end: a duplicate re-seal, one box). Clearing them on disconnect would stall re-seals and finalization while any of a label's hosts is down. An expired link whose K labels list only `peer://` addresses is finalized at once by its maker, as with none.
- Pending role commits' boxes are opened read-only (`apply/keys.rs::read_pending_boxes`).

## Requests and driver
- App and flows requests (`sync/local.rs`): `create_label` and `replace_label` (over flows' `Engine::create_label`), `compact` (a snapshot, then drop covered bodies this node may purge past keep-at-least; deletions follow once the snapshot is at the host), `place_certificate(g, commit, aspects)`, `deliver_inbox(recipient, commit, aspects)` (deposited at each inbox host on the next connection), `complete_pass(actor)`, `sync_now`, `export_bundle`/`import_bundle`.
- Driver (`sync/driver.rs`, feature `driver`, default): `run(sync, transport, clock, control, log)` = `run_serving` with no listeners; `run_shared` drives an engine a `Shared` owns (the osis handle). Per `Dial` a task `link::dial`s (the dialable key for a co-reader's node, `Action::Dial.dialable`, else ephemeral) and forwards bytes; each listener's sessions (`link::serve`) go to `accept` with that listener's key. `Control::{Local, Tick, Call, Apply, Reach, Listen, ClientListen, Lan, LanRejoin, Shutdown}` (`Listen` and `ClientListen`: notes/client-port.md §5; `Reach`, `Lan`: notes/peers.md); `Action::Host` runs the host task (notes/serving.md). Engine calls run under `block_in_place`.
- Tests: tier 1 on `InProcess` through one node's host (its own copy over the loopback, its self-dials cut), and peer to peer over `P2p<MemUdp>` on the NAT model; tier 2 (ignored) on localhost QUIC and P2P, the LAN loop (`lan`) and a relaying node.

## Handed-off groups (`sync/handoff.rs`)
- The mechanism of osis `group_source`: a group whose calls go to another node is handed off here (`SyncEngine::set_handed_off`, record `y/w`; set as the source lands, cleared before the move home catches up, so every handed-off group has a source node). It is left out of `labels()`, so nothing of it syncs, is served, or gets passes, certificates (`deliver::groups_holding` skips it) or step records; what was stored stays, stale (`Engine::readable_by` denies what is routed away to clients). `untie_unplaced` skips a pair whose target, group or removal scope meets a handed-off group (left to holders). osis status and `wait_synced` leave it out.
- Checks: `sync::removal_from_partial_node_keeps_unheld_edits_spec_permissions_5_2` (the phone places nothing under a handed-off group's label while it removes a writer), `tied::scope_handed_off_left_to_holders`, `sources::status_shows_a_group_as_its_source_does`.

## Junk copies (`SyncEngine::redeliver`; spec/commits.md §4 Copies)
- A pending commit's slot and signature keep their first copy; a junk one proved names the commit (memory only; `name_after_crash` looks at what a crash could have lost, once per start: what pends, commits neither applied nor pending, applied ones with an entry on an aspect they miss), and node sync ingests its entries that bring an empty slot, label ones and inbox ones (not a declined invite's), skipping those `genuine` judged false.
- A slot counting as never received (`Engine::settled_slots`: body dropped, part past its expiry) is not brought back: only a new copy does that, or a restart would change what a node reads. A copy with a junk part or signature is no source even for its true parts: a sender holding the true part could withhold it anyway.
- Limits: a true copy deleted from every host while junk holds the slot waits for a holder's new upload (issues/pending-unbounded.md); a linked header unreadable when the true record came (issues/linked-header-junk-record.md); The start's look costs a read of each such commit and a look at its entries, per start, not per event. A visit bound hit keeps the name for the next step (from reading: reaching it takes a chain of records inside one round; no test).

## First sync: newest first (spec/sync.md §6)
- Why the wrap carries the order: hosts must not tell blobs apart, and a blob's header sits in its ciphertext, so for a chat message the header is the whole body. A wrap is listed for free and opened by readers only, so 8 B of order there beat a host-visible class, a separate label for role commits, or reading the log from its end (role commits sit anywhere in it, so a newest-first log read cannot stop early). No host change. A writer's lie only moves a blob in the liar's readers' queues: a flood marked 0 delays the newest page by its size (a stated limit, not defended).
- Priority, not partiality: everything is fetched, so "not fetched" needs no meaning beyond "not received", and no aspect is special: a state-reading aspect reads partial during the fill, and a recent snapshot helps it. The price is listing every entry (about 160 B each; on the dev box 115 us each, most of it the stamp's signature).
- Order 0 covers a purge request too: a purge names only commits with a smaller `ts`, so newest first brings it before its targets anyway, except a target that is itself order 0 (a thing's first message). A thing per item (not a chat's one long aspect) makes most commits order 0: that workload fills in log-like order.
- As built (`sync/fill.rs`): `LConn.fill` holds the queue, the batch asked (in `Requests::fetching` for its truth) and the host's position; `y/4` marks (host, label) so a restart or lost connection rebuilds the queue from the entries; the cursor is saved at the listing's end. `ready()` lets passes run past order 0; `idle()` and `bump_pass` wait for the end. `mark_dirty` leaves a filling label alone (a pass per batch costs what its work set holds, and nothing waits on it): passes come at `resync_ms`, on an own write, and at the end. The end asks one more Sync with the configured `inline_limit` (pushes use the last Sync's), and that Sync's end is the pass end. Entries that do not open are fetched too (as a replay brought them inline; `drop_unvouched` judges them on arrival): those the host holds deposited (unstamped: inbox blobs, so a new device's waiting invite) right after order 0, the rest (chunks, epochs not held) last. The deposit budget bounds the first kind whatever the label holds. Rejected: what does not open first at an inbox label: a wrap cannot tell an inbox blob from a chunk, and every group's home label is its inbox (notes/permissions.md, One inbox rule), so a chat's files came before its newest messages and held its first pass; none first at all: a new device's invites waited for its whole home label, however big. Limit: a handled inbox blob is stamped, so it comes last; its grant comes as its order-0 copy, except one accepted with under `INBOX_COPY_MIN_LEFT` left (no copy).
- Limits: a Sync with a cursor brings bytes inline, oldest first, whatever the gap, and a restart's reconciliation fetches what is new in ID order: a node back after a long time gets no newest-first (only no cursor at all fills; issues/partial-history-fetch.md). Order 0 goes in batches of `fill_batch` in ID order, so a commit there can await a record a later order-0 batch brings: an entry woken while its label fills goes back into the fill's queue at its rank (`reopen_awaiting`), so it comes before the rest. An order 0 entry another host's fill had in flight, on a connection then lost, waits for this fill's end (`refetch`). A host that lists an entry and never answers its fetch keeps that fill open until the stall timeout redials: the same stuck pass as a host not answering a Sync. A compaction part way is safe: a snapshot's frontiers stop at the first gap, so it covers nothing it has not got.

## Simulator hook (core/test/sim/src/net; notes/testing.md)
- `SyncEngine::new(engine, raw clock, rng, cfg)`, `Event`s fed (`Up` on a `Dial`, `Bytes` from the host's `Hub`, `Tick` at `Wake`), `Send` bytes to `Hub::input`; `is_idle`, `describe` (head per label), `take_diagnostics`, `entries(label)` and `verdicts(host, label)` (the pass's must-hold bodies, deletable must-not-hold entries, unsettled aspects) for invariants. tests/it/net/mod.rs is a minimal version of that driver.
- A connection it closes gets nothing more, queued or held back (`Net::forget_conn`); messages to a node carry its `life` (restart count) and one from before a restart is dropped: a restarted node numbers connections from 1 again, and a stale host Hello on the new connection made every Open there refused (`random_window_inflight` seeds 3300065, 173899; the sim keys by link id and cannot have it). The node ignores a second Hello (a broken frame), so it stays stuck on such a connection; a real transport never delivers an old connection's bytes on a new one.

## Invariants the simulator established
Each is a rule the code keeps; breaking one brought back churn or a stuck node. Most sit with their subject: heads (notes/label-access.md, node sync), promotion per host and durability of the outbox (notes/inbox.md), compaction agreement and carriers (notes/sync-convergence.md), requests in flight and wakes (notes/flow-control.md, notes/node-sync-work-sets.md), entries, certificates and presence (above). Also:
- Own dots are written under a zero commit ID placeholder from the start (`own_source`), so the writer's own source sorts above foreign dots (spec/crdt.md §3.3 rule 2).
- Clock: a node's own commits are never refused for drift; `estimate_offset` ignores samples with a round trip over 10 s.
