# Path model findings
> Summary: What the roles-model `paths` module (stated paths, signers as members: notes/write-path-design.md, notes/account-membership-design.md) found in that design: each hole with its scenario, the rule allowing it and a fix or question; what held; what the model does not cover.

The model: core/test/roles-model/README.md (Paths). Each finding has a hand world in `src/paths/tests.rs`: a `repro_*` test, ignored, asserting the wanted behaviour under the reading it broke and failing, or a test asserting the hole and its fix side by side. Two rule sets stand in the model: `Rules::fixed`, the fixes found one by one, which findings 12 and 15 break; and `Rules::whole`, the rule that replaces the fixes of 4, 7, 12 and 15 with one (notes/write-path-design.md §1, A removal is judged in the world its list makes), which is the design. Making it the model's only rule is the first item of To model next. X is the stolen node, H its remover, R the removal, B another node of account A, K a node of the collaborator account A2, T1 a thing A administers, M a mint admin of A who paired H.

## Findings
Numbered as found. 4, 7, 12 and 15 are one class (how a removal's own authority meets what it audits and lists) and have one answer; 13 is no hole.

1. **Removing a node undoes its role writes where the remover did not hold them, removals included** (`repro_a_grant_the_remover_did_not_hold_stands`, `repro_a_removal_the_removed_node_made_stays`).
   - X shared T1 with A2; H does not sync T1; H removes X. R's audit drops the grant, and every write of K falls, with no list made for them. The other direction is a safety matter: X had removed A2 from T1; R drops that removal and A2 writes T1 again.
   - Allowed by: an audit drops every unlisted role write through the lowered edge (write-path §1, Attribution and demotion); addenda vouch for plain content only (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
   - Fix modelled: a vouch (one per thing, naming R and the role writes its writer had received by R's `ts`), counting when its path gives admin on that thing at its key in the walk with no vouch counting; a counting vouch keeps what it names out of R's audit and R's seeds. Grants and lowerings alike.
2. **A vouch judged in the walk it changes has no fixpoint** (`repro_a_vouch_judged_in_the_walk_it_changes_settles`).
   - Honest world: X had demoted B to write on A; H never received that; R drops it, so B is an admin again and vouches for its own demotion; the vouch standing, B is no admin at the vouch's key, so it does not count, so the demotion falls, so it counts.
   - Fix modelled: read the voucher's authority from the walk with no vouch counting (two walks, no iteration). It settles and gives no escalation (`a_vouch_gives_nothing_to_whoever_could_not_have_made_the_write`). Its cost is finding 3.
3. **A holder kept by a vouch vouches for nothing** (`repro_a_holder_kept_by_a_vouch_vouches_in_turn`).
   - X paired N5 and shared T1; B received the pairing, only N5 the share. B's vouch keeps N5; N5's vouch for the share does not count (N5 is no admin in the walk without vouches), so the share falls. Accepted (membership §4, No circle). Addenda are not limited so: they are judged in the settled walk, so a writer kept by a vouch vouches for content.
4. **A removal that does not list its own grant drops what it does not list and then does not apply** (`repro_nothing_falls_by_a_removal_that_does_not_apply`).
   - X (stolen, admin by H's grant) removes H with an empty frontier. The audit drops X's own grant and B's; on the restart X holds nothing, so its removal does not apply, yet both grants stay dropped and no dispute is raised.
   - Fix modelled (`Rules::whole`, `void_unapplied`): a lowering that made drops and that the settled walk does not apply is void: the walk is redone without it and its drops (`a_removal_that_does_not_apply_once_its_drops_are_made_is_void`; the same for a removal whose author's grant a later removal drops, `a_removal_whose_author_falls_later_drops_nothing`). Control `paths-whole-sticky`: stray drops, and a trusted removal void.
   - Exempting the removal's own grants from its audit instead (`own_chain_exempt`) is finding 15.
5. **A reader holding only its thing's leaf of a frontier misjudges role writes on A** (`repro_a_reader_with_its_things_leaf_reads_as_a_member`).
   - Fix modelled: a reader holding R sees the entry, present or absent, of each role commit in the evidence it holds (`Rules::evidence_leaves`).
6. **Both sides of a standing dispute rotate A at every pass, without end** (`repro_a_standing_dispute_stops_rotating`).
   - Fix modelled: an epoch covering a departure the node's walk does not apply is the other side's: read, never current, superseding nothing, neither answered nor withdrawn (`Withdraw::SideOnly`).
7. **A frontier listing everything received blesses the thief's removal of the remover** (`repro_a_removal_listing_all_it_received_applies`).
   - The thief removes H, honestly stamped, before H acts; H receives it and removes X, listing it. The listed removal stands, H holds nothing at R's key, and R is void under every trust set.
   - Fix modelled (`Rules::whole`, `stands`): the list is its writer's. A remover whose walk does not apply R with everything named names the role writes in the order received, leaving out each R would not apply with; a witness builds its vouch the same way (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`; story: `Setup::x_first`, properties `own path`, `removal`). Every other lowering X made stays made (`a_lowering_the_removed_node_made_before_stays_made`). Control `paths-whole-listed`: the removal void, X admin.
   - A reader's rule reading such an entry as absent (`ListOwn::Hops`, by the edges of R's stated path) is finding 12.
8. **The last-admin rule reading every admin edge of a thing splits readers from members** (`repro_a_reader_judges_the_last_admin_rule_as_a_member`).
   - H pairs B and leaves A. A reader of T1 where B never wrote holds H's leaving and not B's grant: it refuses the leave and shows H's later writes; A's members do not.
   - Fix modelled: an unmarked write taking a standing admin edge (admin, no `until`) to anything else applies only where the remaining admin it relies on is a standing admin edge other than the lowered one: its path's last edge, else the holder it names, whose edge writes are its evidence (`LastAdmin::Named`; `a_reader_judges_the_named_last_admin_rule_as_a_member`). A timed admin is no remaining admin (`a_timed_admin_is_no_remaining_admin`: counted as one, a thing goes adminless on a date with no marked write).
9. **The collateral grantee** (`repro_a_collateral_grantees_writes_count_after_its_re_grant`): fixed by counting a re-granted path from each edge's first chained grant (`Regrant::FirstChained`). A grant the thief backdates is chained, so a holder granted later may stamp content back to it (`a_backdated_grant_moves_a_current_holders_first_point_back`): no new right, an earlier `ts`. A re-granted writer's addendum written before its new grant vouches for nothing; the one it writes after does (`a_re_granted_writers_later_addendum_vouches`; the state-based writing writes it).
10. **A removed node vouches for its own backdated writes through the cut path** (`repro_a_write_made_after_the_removal_does_not_show_by_its_signers_addendum`).
    - X, removed from A, still writes T1 through A2: it stamps a write before R stating path [A] and names it in its own addendum by path [A2]; the same with a grant and its own vouch where X administers T1 through A2.
    - Fix modelled: no addendum or vouch counts for a commit of its own signer (`no_own_addendum`, `no_own_vouch`; `a_removed_signer_vouches_for_nothing_of_its_own`; story: thief move 19). A colluding writer still can (nothing checks receipt, as today).
11. **Pairing after a fight** (`repro_a_node_paired_after_a_fight_is_a_member_at_itself`): with its pairer's trust entries the new node reads as its pairer (`a_node_paired_with_its_pairers_trust_entries_is_a_member_at_itself`).
12. **No reader's rule for "an entry that unmakes its removal" is both checkable and safe** (`a_removal_listing_the_thiefs_removal_of_its_granter_is_void`).
    - M (mint) paired H and X. X, stolen, removes M listing nothing, which drops M's grant of H; H receives it and removes X, listing it. Read over the edges of R's stated path (`ListOwn::Hops`) the entry is kept, since it lowers (M, A), and R is void under every trust set: the thief removes the remover's pairer first.
    - Read over the held grants on those edges, closed (`ListOwn::Chain`), a removed node writes a junk grant to the remover and strikes the remover's listing of its own earlier removal (`a_removed_node_writing_a_grant_to_the_remover_stays_removed` fails under it). Read from the walk it is circular (what R rests on is decided by what R drops). A writer leaving out "a lowering of an edge a grant on the path rests on" undoes an honest removal: M paired H and X; X, before the theft, removes M listing M's grant of H; left out, it is dropped and M is admin again.
    - Answer modelled: finding 7's. The writer leaves out by effect (what R does not apply with), in the order received; no reader reads an entry as absent (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`, both the thief's removal of M and the honest one). In key order instead, the thief makes an honest earlier removal the entry to leave out (`a_remover_keeps_what_it_received_first`).
13. **A grantee granted again vouches for its own fallen grant** (`a_re_granted_grantee_vouches_for_its_own_fallen_grant`): no hole, the design's intent (notes/account-membership-design.md §4).
    - X paired N5 (H never received it); N5 wrote content and a grant; R drops N5's grant and all of it. H pairs N5 again. N5, admin again in the first walk and holding X's grant of it from before R, vouches for it (X's commit, not N5's): the grant applies at its own key and N5's gap writes stand, role writes included. The same through a holder: X grants A2 admin on T1, R drops it, B grants A2 again, K vouches along [A2].
    - It is the rule (a remaining party that could write it had received it), and the same terms as content. Barring a vouch for a write that sets the voucher's own edge would bar a node keeping X's demotion of it (`a_lowering_the_removed_node_made_before_stays_made`: only B received it), would miss the holder case, and stops no hostile admin, which vouches for a backdated grant to another key instead. With the state-based writing it happens wherever the grantee is granted again, so `Regrant::FirstChained` is reached only where the grantee is granted below admin.
14. **A removed node's backdated self-removal undoes its own earlier role writes** (`a_backdated_leave_undoes_nothing_the_removed_node_made`).
    - X, stolen, leaves A by a self-removal stamped before role writes it made honestly (a demotion of B, a pairing of N5), which H received and lists. A self-removal audits nothing and states no hop through (X, A), so no audit reaches it; X held no admin at its later writes, so they fall: X's demotion of B is undone, N5 is no member.
    - Allowed by spec/permissions.md §5.1 Seeds ("except a write lowering its author's own edge") and the self-removal's path ending at its holder (write-path §1, The end): no lowering puts it in question.
    - Fix modelled (`Rules::leave_in_question`): a lowering of edge h puts a self-removal of h in question like a write through h; unlisted, the backdated leave is dropped. Honest leaves before R are received and listed. Alternative: R's audit judges the victim's later writes against the edge as restored (which already counts what a self-removal took), never against the current value.

15. **A removal's own chain exempt from its audit, read from the held writes, spares the thief's writes** (`repro_a_made_up_key_regranting_the_remover_holds_nothing`, `repro_a_backdated_timed_grant_to_the_remover_changes_nothing`; both fail under `Rules::fixed`).
    - X backdates a grant of admin to a made-up key Z, and Z a grant of admin to H (admin already). Z's grant sets an edge of R's path, so X's grant of Z is exempt (`own_chain_exempt`) and Z stays admin wherever R is trusted. With `until` the first half alone suffices: X backdates a grant of admin until 7 to H; it stands; H's edge is timed, so R, naming no other admin, is refused and, trusted, void: X keeps A.
    - Fix modelled (`Rules::whole`): nothing is exempt. Both writes rest on the removed edge (or are made under one that does), are unlisted, and fall (`nothing_through_the_removed_edge_is_exempt`); what the exemption was for is finding 4's fix. The search reaches the area by the moves aimed at the remover (`AIMED`); control `paths-whole-exempt` (and `paths-fixed-aimed`, the earlier rule set under those moves): safety, the removal void.

## To model next
For one modeller, in this order; each with its property and negative control. The rule: notes/write-path-design.md §1 (A removal is judged in the world its list makes); notes/account-membership-design.md §4 (The list, Writing).
1. **One rule set.** `Rules::whole` becomes `Rules::fixed`; `own_chain_exempt`, `chain`, `ListOwn` and `aimed` go (the aimed moves join `MOVES`), with the hand tests that assert the earlier readings rewritten to the rule's (`a_removal_listing_the_thiefs_removal_of_its_author_applies`, `a_removal_never_puts_its_own_grant_in_question`, `a_removal_listing_the_thiefs_removal_of_its_granter_is_void`, `what_no_list_keeps_reads_every_edge_of_the_path`, the two finding 15 repros un-ignored). README tables rerun at full size (400,000 random, the whole family) for the rule and its three controls (`-sticky`, `-listed`, `-exempt`).
2. **No stray drop, as a check that can fail**: every dropped commit's dropper applies in the settled walk, at every reading (today `Reading::stray` is empty by construction under the rule). Count the redo rounds per reading and print the largest. Hand worlds: two lowerings each un-applying the other through its drops (the walk voids the earlier by key; show the reading is the same in any input order, and say which consistent world it picks where two exist and that it picks a fixed one where none does); voiding every unapplied dropper at once in place of the earliest, as a control, differs on one of them.
3. **Readings with an empty trust set.** Every final view in the story trusts the removal; add a node that trusts nothing (a node paired after the fact) and check there: the walk settles, no stray, alike with another such node, and each trusted-side verdict is either the same or a dispute this node is shown (`disputes`). A backdated write aimed at the remover makes the removal a dispute there, never a silent loss.
4. **One set of drops.** The audit drops the applied writes in question; a trusted removal's seeds drop the held ones. Run both readings of the audit (applied, held) over the search: if no reading differs, the spec states one set ("the earlier held writes resting on h that the list does not name") for both.
5. **The writers' leaving out, against a thief that stamps before the theft and is heard before the removal.** Thief moves made at tick 5 and received by H and B by then, so the remover's and witnesses' lists meet several candidates: X demotes H listing its own grant; X removes M listing M's grant of H, and not listing it; X grants H admin with an `until`; pairs of them. Setup: N5 removed by X before the theft (listed by R), its key held by the thief, writing as N5 before that removal's `ts`: a grant of admin to H (standing, timed), a demotion or removal of H, a vouch. Property: R applies wherever trusted; N5 stays removed; every lowering X made before the theft that H or a witness received stays made. Negative control: the remover keeping in key order (N5 is back, by `a_remover_keeps_what_it_received_first`'s world).
6. **Equivocation**: X signs a second removal of N5 at the first one's `ts` with another list (dropping H's or B's grant); received before R it is a candidate left out, received after it is dropped; the first stays made.
7. **Vouches that void.** Property: where a trusted removal by an honest remover is void, a counting vouch names a write the removal does not apply with, and its voucher holds admin on that thing in the first walk after the removal (the accomplice, no wider). Thief moves: N5 (paired by X, kept by H's list, hostile) vouching for X's removal of H; Z and X doing the same (count for nothing). Count, not a failure: the accomplice worlds.
8. **A removal stating more than one hop** (H removing A2 from T1 by [A]): the thief lowers an edge of that path above the signer's ((A, T1), by X through A), backdated; a member of A2 that is the thief's. Same properties.
9. **Self-removal and time**: X's backdated leave carrying an `until` in place of none; X setting its own edge timed, backdated; a leave of a holder thing (X makes A leave T1) stamped before role writes A's members made through (A, T1), with a removal of (A, T1) by a co-admin listing them: the leave is put in question like a write through the edge.
10. **Vouches on today's walk** (`vouches`, for S3): the witness leaving out what the removal does not apply with takes the Listing limit's set-aside ("X lowered A") to 0 for witnesses; the remover's own list by the same rule. If it holds, spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches, Writing, gains the condition (a writer's rule: it needs no stated path).

## What held
- Under `Rules::whole`: safety, liveness, alike (readers holding only the commits on the things they read and those commits' evidence, against members), the remover's path kept, the last admin standing, no stray drop or void trusted removal: 20,000 random worlds and every 49th world of the small family (28,087), the thief's moves aimed at the remover included (README.md, Paths, for counts); the full-size runs are owed (To model next, 1). Each of its three controls fails the search.
- Under `Rules::fixed` the same holds against a thief that never grants to the remover (400,000 random worlds and the whole family), and fails with those moves (`paths-fixed-aimed`). Each rule's negative control fails the search.
- Descent: a write to a dependent is judged along the record edges down its mint chain; lowering a descent edge audits every path through it (`a_write_by_descent_is_judged_along_the_record_edges`). Validity reads record edges only, so a thing on a path becoming independent changes no verdict (nothing in the model reads independence).
- Self-removal by path end audits nothing and raises nothing (`a_self_removal_by_path_end_audits_nothing`, `a_self_removal_raises_nothing`); a removal after it audits against the edge as restored (`a_backdated_leave_shields_nothing_from_the_removal`). An `until` passing audits nothing; the next explicit write on that edge does (`an_until_passing_audits_nothing_the_next_explicit_write_does`).
- The state-based writing (a node writes whenever its own walk shows an owed commit it could write that nothing of its own covers) holds liveness under stated paths; answering only the first time a node's walk applies R fails it (a witness standing by a vouch, a re-granted writer).
- A switch takes (`a_switch_takes`); two nodes removing each other never leave A without an admin; a removal logged only where the node wrote leaves the window Tess's answer closes (`a_removal_logged_only_where_the_node_wrote_leaves_a_window`).

## Not covered
Invites and finalizes; purge authors; own covers and forgetting (so "a writer that compacts before holding the removal carries the thief's write" is not reached); demotion to write (a kiosk); accomplices granted inside the theft window; sessions and overlapping dots; hidden mode; the thief's own rotations of A and secrecy (the keys layer is only the rotation loop under a standing dispute); re-keys of things above A and re-seals; bodies a node on the wrong side dropped as invalid; how a node picks its side (sides are given); finding 8's reader shape in the story (the reader of T1 always holds R and so the remover's chain, so the any-edge control fails only by hand; the named admin's grant reaching a reader only as the leave's evidence is in the story: M names B, who may write nothing on T1); writes of a party removed earlier whose key the thief holds; thief writes received by the remover before it acts, other than its removal of the remover; a node trusting nothing; removals stating a path of more than one hop (To model next); the differential against osis-perm (nothing is built).
