# Path model findings
> Summary: What the roles-model `paths` module (stated paths, accounts as ordinary things: notes/write-path-design.md, notes/account-membership-design.md) found in that design: each hole with its scenario, the rule allowing it and a fix or question; what is open for the design notes; what held; what the model does not cover.

The model: core/test/roles-model/README.md (Paths). Each finding has a hand world in `src/paths/tests.rs`: a `repro_*` test, ignored, asserting the wanted behaviour under the reading it broke and failing, or a test asserting the hole and its fix side by side, or, for an accepted one, a test asserting the outcome and why it is accepted. The model has one rule set, `Rules::fixed` (the design: each lowering judged once at its key, finding 16); `Rules::design` is `fixed` with each fix undone, the reading the `repro_*` tests fail under, and each control is `Rules::fixed` with one rule undone. X is the stolen node, H its remover, R the removal, B another node of account A, K a node of the collaborator account A2, T1 a thing A administers, M a mint admin of A who paired H, N5 a node of A (paired by X, or paired by H and removed by X before the theft, its key the thief's), N6 and RD3 nodes trusting nothing.

Numbered as found; gaps are findings that went with the rule they were about. 4, 7, 12 and 15 are one class (how a removal's own authority meets what it audits and lists) and have one answer; 13 and 24 are no hole.

1. **Removing a node undoes its role writes where the remover did not hold them, removals included** (`a_grant_the_removal_does_not_list_falls_and_a_listed_one_stands`, `a_removal_the_removed_party_made_stands_only_where_listed`).
   - X shared T1 with A2; H does not sync T1; H removes X. R's audit drops the grant, and every write of K falls, with no list made for them. The other direction is a safety matter: X had removed A2 from T1; R drops that removal and A2 writes T1 again.
   - Decided (Tess): the rule. Nothing speaks for a role write R's list does not name, whoever else received it (issues/role-vouch-contested.md: no rule over the held set tells the stolen key's world from the honest one); a user who learns of it redoes the write as their own.
4. **A removal that does not list its own grant drops what it does not list and then does not apply** (`repro_nothing_falls_by_a_removal_that_does_not_apply`).
   - X (stolen, admin by H's grant) removes H with an empty frontier. The audit drops X's own grant and B's; on the restart X holds nothing, so its removal does not apply, yet both grants stay dropped and no dispute is raised.
   - Fix modelled (`self_void`): a lowering whose own drops would take its authority is void and drops nothing (`a_removal_whose_drops_take_its_own_standing_is_void`; finding 16). Control `paths-no-void`: A left with no admin, safety, liveness, removal and own path fail.
   - Exempting the removal's own grants from its audit instead (control `exempt_chain`) is finding 15.
5. **A reader holding only its thing's leaf of a frontier misjudges role writes on A** (`repro_a_reader_with_its_things_leaf_reads_as_a_member`).
   - Fix modelled: a reader holding R sees the entry, present or absent, of each role commit in the evidence it holds (`Rules::evidence_leaves`).
6. **Both sides of a standing dispute rotate A at every pass, without end** (`repro_a_standing_dispute_stops_rotating`).
   - Fix modelled: an epoch covering a departure the node's walk does not apply is the other side's: read, never current, superseding nothing, neither answered nor withdrawn (`Withdraw::SideOnly`).
7. **A frontier listing everything received blesses the thief's removal of the remover** (`repro_a_removal_listing_all_it_received_applies`).
   - The thief removes H, honestly stamped, before H acts; H receives it and removes X, listing it. The listed removal stands, H holds nothing at R's key, and R is void under every trust set.
   - Fix modelled (`stands`): the list is its writer's. A remover whose walk does not apply R (trusted, with its seeds) with everything named names the role writes in the order received, leaving out each R would not apply with; judging R trusted beside its own trust set (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`; story: `Setup::x_first` and the thief's moves heard before the removal, properties `own path`, `removal`). A lowering X made stays made where the list names it (`a_lowering_the_removed_node_made_before_stays_made_only_where_listed`). Controls `paths-listed` (the removal void, X admin) and `paths-key-order` (the remover keeping in key order: N5 is back, `the_remover_keeps_what_it_received_first_in_the_story`).
   - A reader's rule reading such an entry as absent (by the edges of R's stated path) is finding 12.
9. **The collateral grantee** (`repro_a_collateral_grantees_writes_count_after_its_re_grant`): fixed by counting a re-granted path from each edge's first grant made by right, dropped or not (`Regrant::FirstByRight`, `first_by_right`; finding 56). A grant the thief backdates to before its removal is made by right, so a holder granted later may stamp content back to it (`a_backdated_grant_moves_a_current_holders_first_point_back`): no new right, an earlier `ts`. A re-granted writer's addendum written before its new grant vouches for nothing; the one it writes after does (`a_re_granted_writers_later_addendum_vouches`; the state-based writing writes it).
10. **A removed node vouches for its own backdated writes through the cut path** (`repro_a_write_made_after_the_removal_does_not_show_by_its_signers_addendum`).
    - X, removed from A, still writes T1 through A2: it stamps a write before R stating path [A] and names it in its own addendum by path [A2].
    - Fix modelled: no addendum counts for a commit of its own signer (`no_own_addendum`). A colluding writer still can (nothing checks receipt).
11. **Pairing after a fight** (`repro_a_node_paired_after_a_fight_is_a_member_at_itself`): with its pairer's trust entries the new node reads as its pairer (`a_node_paired_with_its_pairers_trust_entries_is_a_member_at_itself`).
12. **No reader's rule for "an entry that unmakes its removal" is both checkable and safe** (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`).
    - M (mint) paired H and X. X, stolen, removes M listing nothing, which drops M's grant of H; H receives it and removes X, listing it. Read over the edges of R's stated path the entry is kept, since it lowers (M, A), and R is void under every trust set: the thief removes the remover's pairer first.
    - Read over the held grants on those edges, closed, a removed node writes a junk grant to the remover and strikes the remover's listing of its own earlier removal (the world of `a_removed_node_writing_a_grant_to_the_remover_stays_removed`). Read from the walk it is circular (what R rests on is decided by what R drops). A writer leaving out "a lowering of an edge a grant on the path rests on" undoes an honest removal: M paired H and X; X, before the theft, removes M listing M's grant of H; left out, it is dropped and M is admin again.
    - Answer modelled: finding 7's. The writer leaves out by effect (what R does not apply with), in the order received; no reader reads an entry as absent (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`, both the thief's removal of M and the honest one). In key order instead, the thief makes an honest earlier removal the entry to leave out (`a_remover_keeps_what_it_received_first`; in the story with N5's key, control `paths-key-order`).
14. **A removed node's backdated self-removal undoes its own earlier role writes** (`a_backdated_leave_undoes_nothing_the_removed_node_made`).
    - X, stolen, leaves A by a self-removal stamped before role writes it made honestly (a demotion of B, a pairing of N5), which H received and lists. A self-removal audits nothing and states no hop through (X, A), so no audit reaches it; X held no admin at its later writes, so they fall: X's demotion of B is undone, N5 is no member.
    - Allowed under `Rules::design` by seeds excepting a write lowering its author's own edge and the self-removal's path ending at its holder (write-path §1, The end): no lowering puts it in question.
    - Fix modelled (`Rules::leave_in_question`): a lowering of edge h puts a self-removal of h in question like a write through h; unlisted, the backdated leave is dropped. Honest leaves before R are received and listed. Alternative: R's audit judges the victim's later writes against the edge as restored (which already counts what a self-removal took), never against the current value.

15. **A removal's own chain exempt from its audit, read from the held writes, spares the thief's writes** (`a_made_up_key_regranting_the_remover_holds_nothing`, `a_backdated_timed_grant_to_the_remover_changes_nothing`).
    - X backdates a grant of admin to a made-up key Z, and Z a grant of admin to H (admin already). Z's grant sets an edge of R's path, so X's grant of Z is exempt and Z stays admin wherever R is trusted. With `until` the first half alone suffices: X backdates a grant of admin until 7 to H; it stands; H's edge is timed, so R, naming no other admin, is refused and, trusted, void: X keeps A.
    - Fix modelled: nothing is exempt. Both writes rest on the removed edge (or are made under one that does), are unlisted, and fall (`nothing_through_the_removed_edge_is_exempt`); what the exemption was for is finding 4's fix. The thief's menu aims moves at the remover (23 to 29); control `paths-exempt` (`exempt_chain`): safety, the removal void.
16. **A void write's effect, chased by redoing the walk, is a fixpoint that is not monotone; each lowering is judged once instead** (`a_void_write_has_no_effect`, `a_lowering_dropped_later_keeps_its_drops`, `a_lowering_dropping_the_grant_it_rests_on_is_void`, `a_lowering_dropping_its_granters_grant_is_void`, `a_lowering_taking_nothing_is_judged_when_a_restart_gives_it_the_edge`, `a_stray_drop_in_a_fight_of_admins_is_shown`, `small_world_830267_judged_once` and its two siblings, `a_party_removed_before_the_theft_stays_removed`, `a_removal_whose_author_falls_later_keeps_its_verdict`).
    - Chased property: a void write has no effect, the reading being the reading with it not held. Un-making a void write's drops is what made it circular: voiding by rounds (earliest, latest, all at once), a consistent void set, and judging in key order with the overruled writes re-resolved each failed it, and the earliest first let the thief bring back a party removed earlier (`n5_back`'s world); each cost a walk of the component per self-voiding write (17).
    - Modelled (`Rules::fixed`): one walk whose drops only grow. A lowering reached holding its right (path giving admin) and taking its edge from admin is judged once: void where its own drops would take its authority (`keeps`: its path's edges read back along the applied writes that set them, its own drops and grants that do not keep in turn passed over), else applied for good, its drops added, the pass ended there and the next resumed from the earliest of them; neither verdict is revisited. One reached without its right, or taking nothing, gets none and is judged by a restart reaching it with the right and the edge: else X's honest earlier removal of N5 stays unapplied where X's backdated self-demotion is dropped (N5 back at nodes trusting nothing), or stays a no-op audit where X's backdated removal of N5 is dropped (a grant N5's key backdates, unlisted, survives it). The audit puts the held writes in question (18). Text: notes/write-path-design.md §1.
    - What it gives: an idle commit (no part applied, dropped or trusted) changes nothing users observe, or the reading shows a dispute, at every node trusting or not, but where it decides a fight (findings 34 to 36; gates `void`, `unapplied`, `idle`, `honest` in the story and `tiny`). The weaker truth: **a lowering applied at its key keeps its effect for good**, staying applied and its drops made where a later lowering drops it or its author's grant (a stray drop). Smallest: the thief backdates a removal of B before H's removal of X (one move); trusting nothing, it drops B's grant of A2 on T1 at its key, H's removal drops it, and B's grant stays dropped: K's writes on T1 fall. Trusting H's removal its seeds take it before the walk and nothing falls. Counted: none at trusting nodes in the story; a few per thousand readings at nodes trusting nothing (most are drops of writes that never applied). The smallest `T` one where the drop costs a role is a fight of four admins: N6 removes B; Z, paired by B, removes N6 not listing that removal, which drops it; H removes Z, which drops Z's removal. N6 stays an admin, and B stays one too: N6's removal stays dropped. The trusted write there (N5 demoting B before B is paired) is a no-op, so the node reads as one trusting nothing; trusting H's removal of Z reads B removed, and that removal is shown as a dispute (`a_stray_drop_in_a_fight_of_admins_is_shown`, without the no-op). N6, still an admin, can remove B again.
    - So the walk trusting nothing reads otherwise than the trusting one wherever a removal's reach-back drops a write that acted. A dispute (spec/permissions.md §5.1) is a lowering of another holder's edge whose trust changes the reading (`disputes`), which shows every one, trusting the honest ones shown reading as the trusting side (0 failing). Grants are no candidates: trusted, a grant's seeds drop its holder's earlier writes, so an honest re-grant would be shown (7% of readings of honest histories); with lowerings alone an honest history shows none (`quiet`, 95,542 readings). Most nodes trust nothing until a dispute is detected, so detection must find these (Open 0).
    - Every property holds at the trusting nodes, and at the nodes trusting nothing where every honest removal they hold applies (safety, the remover's standing). What the void check does not read is what a lowering's own drops free: a drop can make an earlier lowering take an edge again, and its drops can take the first lowering's authority, which keeps its verdict (`a_lowerings_check_does_not_read_what_its_drops_free`, the one `tiny` world of 1,000,000 leaving A with no admin: H pairs N6, N6 pairs N5; H removes N6 listing that pairing, then again listing its first removal; N5 removes H listing H's second removal. Its walk: N5's removal applies and drops H's first removal, the restart lets H's second removal take N6's edge and drop N6's pairing of N5, and N5's removal keeps its verdict. Two admins removing each other, N5 keeping in its list a removal that unmakes its own, which the writers' rule leaves out; at a node trusting nothing, or trusting either removal that ends void; shown as a dispute, H's second removal, whose trust keeps H). Reading it would need the walk from the lowering's earliest drop replayed before its verdict, a walk per such check.
17. **What a party with admin can make a reader walk** (`a_partys_self_voiding_writes_cost_linear_work`, `a_void_check_reads_its_own_grant_chain`).
    - X grants k keys of its own admin and each removes X listing nothing; H removes X. Trusting nothing, each removal of a key is void by its check (its path's setter is X's grant of it, its own drop): commits visited 34 and 304, setters read 10 and 100 at k 10 and 100, one restart, for H's removal. Trusting H's removal, X's grants are seeds and no key holds the right: 13 and 103, no restart.
    - Bound: a lowering that drops nothing costs its place; a void one its check, the setters along its own grant chain; an applied one that drops its drops and one restart from the earliest. A void check grows with its author's path: X grants Z1, each Zi grants Zi+1, and Zk removes X k times, each check reading the chain back to X: k² setters for 2k writes (200 at k 10, 20,000 at k 100, two walks), all the party's own role writes. Here every check has the same drops on the chain, so a memo across checks makes it linear; lowerings with different lists defeat that memo (accepted: notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 2). A restart is paid once per applying lowering, which drops something each time.
18. **The audit puts the held writes in question, as a trusted write's seeds do** (`the_audit_puts_the_held_writes_in_question`).
    - Read over the applied writes (control `paths-audit-applied`), a removal's audit misses the removed party's writes that were unapplied when it is reached and that its own drops free: X backdates its own leave to 1 and removes H at 5, honestly stamped; H removes X at 6. Its audit drops the leave alone; the restart applies X's removal of H, which takes H, and H's removal keeps its verdict: H and X both removed where nothing is trusted. Over the held writes it drops both. The control fails safety (a backdated grant through X's freed edge) and own path at nodes trusting nothing (67 and 292 of 8,000 worlds). One set for audit and seeds is also one mechanism.
19. **A thief heard before the removal drops a co-admin's grant by demoting the remover and granting it admin again.**
    - X, heard by H before R: X demotes H to write listing only X's own grant (its audit drops H's pairing of B), and grants H admin again. R applies with both named, so H keeps both, and B's grant is lost with what was written under it (M's leave naming B included). No worse than X removing B while heard, which the design keeps (membership §4: a removal X made stays made); but R's result names the members X lowered, not those a kept lowering of the remover dropped. The oracle sets these aside (`heard_lowering`).
21. **A dispute whose own write is a no-op where trusted is not raised as spec/permissions.md §5.1 words it** (`a_dispute_whose_write_is_a_no_op_where_trusted_is_shown`).
    - X, admin on A2 (A2 admin on T1), backdates through A2 a removal of A from T1 before its own removal of A2 from T1 (which H's removal of X lists). At a node trusting nothing the thief's write applies, so X's removal of A2 and B's removal of A2 from T1 by [A] finds no path. Trusted, B's removal drops the thief's write by its seeds and applies, but A2 is off T1 already, so it lowers nothing: the spec's dispute must lower its edge, so none is raised and the node reads A off T1 silently. The rule (16's, spec/permissions.md §5.1): a dispute is a lowering whose trust changes the reading (`disputes`): a lowering that applies once trusted changes what applies, whatever it lowers.
23. **A may-grant grant rests on a writer's edge, so a lowering is judged where it takes its edge down at all, not only from admin** (`a_may_grant_grant_by_a_removed_writer_falls_unless_listed`, `a_write_clearing_the_flag_audits_the_grants_made_by_it`).
    - X writes A2; GU, reached from A2 by descent, holds read with the flag on T1; X grants Z read through [A2, GU]; K removes X from A2 listing nothing. The removal takes (X, A2) from write to none: write-path §1 judges a lowering only where it takes its edge from admin, so it audits nothing and Z keeps read on T1 at a node trusting nothing (control `-admin-falls`: safety N). At a node trusting K's removal its seeds drop the grant, so the sides read otherwise.
    - Adopted (write-path §1, Each lowering is judged once, G5; modelled `Rules::any_fall`, `Rules::flag_falls`): a lowering is judged, and audits, where it takes its edge as restored down in role or in the flag; its drops are the writes resting on the edge whose need there (`need`: admin for a write applied by admin; for a may-grant grant its role on the hops before the last and its role with the flag on the last) the restored value met and the new one does not. A re-grant at the same role without the flag is such a lowering: it audits the grants made by the flag, and an honest one lists them (25). Also in `tiny` worlds of may-grant (`tiny_flags`).
    - A trusted lowering's seeds read the same needs against its value (32).
24. **No removal's authority rests on a may-grant grant** (no hole). A path gives may-grant at admin only where every hop gives admin: that grant applies by admin, an explicit grant (so G2's "below admin" adds nothing: 54). A may-grant grant gives below admin, so the void check (`keeps`) never meets one deciding an edge of a lowering's path, and a grant-then-finalize edge (the granter's grant ended and finalized, the granted one standing) is never a remover's standing. A finalize ends a grant already ended: it audits nothing, makes nothing standing and is no dispute candidate (`a_finalize_ends_an_ended_grant_and_a_may_grant_grant_follows_it`); the next explicit removal of the ended edge audits the grants its holder made by the flag against the edge as restored (`the_explicit_removal_after_a_finalize_audits_the_grants_made_by_flag`, spec/permissions.md §7's link).
25. **A grant below admin is a lowering, so a dispute candidate; an honest explicit write carries its frontier, a re-grant included, or an honest history raises a dispute** (story: B's re-grant of A2 on T1, `quiet`).
    - X removed A2 from T1 listing its grant of G (made through [A2] by the flag); B grants A2 write again. Listing nothing, a node trusting nothing applies the re-grant, and trusted, the re-grant's seeds drop X's grant (it rests on (A2, T1), unlisted), so trusting it changes the reading and it is shown as a dispute. By write-path §1's definition (a lowering is an explicit write giving its edge below admin at its `ts`) a grant at write or read is a lowering: a demotion and a grant of the same value cannot be told apart from the write alone.
    - Adopted (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 4: every explicit write carries its author's frontier, whichever of grant, change or removal it is): honest re-grants list what rests on their edge as received. Dispute candidates take grants below admin as lowerings (explicit only: 31). With honest re-grants listing nothing, `quiet` fails (honest histories showing a dispute). Alternative not modelled: candidates read off the walk (a write whose value is below the edge's value just before it), which costs a walk per candidate as Open 0 does.
26. **A cover made by a writer not yet holding a removal carries the removed party's late write for good: the accepted window** (`a_cover_by_a_writer_not_yet_holding_the_removal_carries_the_late_write`).
    - The thief X withholds H's removal of X from K (serving K alone: the model's evidence closure is complete, so the withholding is set by hand, story move 41), writes T1 after the removal, and K, an honest writer of T1 that does not hold the removal, compacts T1 carrying the write. The write is invalid everywhere; under spec/permissions.md §5.2 Copies and §5.3 ("writes of it that a valid snapshot by another writer carries stay wherever that snapshot is held") K's valid cover shows it at every node holding the cover, and every later snapshot carries it as state. Hostile: X alone. Bound: K's receipt of the removal; from then K's walk shows nothing new of X's. Membership §1 (Cutoff and Carrying) and §2 step 2 accept the window.
    - Modelled (the rule, `copies`): a cover is content-class, judged along its writer's path; a valid cover's copies show what it carries; a node judges only the commits it holds. A list or addendum naming a cover names what it carries (the own cover); under copies that reads as naming the cover, whose copies show the same. The story counts the window (`window`, `window N`: a thief's write shown only by a cover's copy first carried by a cover whose writer lacked a removal made by then), never as a safety failure; every other way a thief's write shows fails safety (X's own backdated cover, move 42, falls with X's writes).
    - Rejected (the variant `-facts`): a carried write judged by its own facts (signer, `ts`, stated path, ID), a valid cover only standing in for a commit not held. It closes the window but makes every snapshot state those facts per carried commit (spec/crdt.md §8 `Facts` holds class and actor), paid per snapshot for good (notes/thing-cost.md), and the design accepted the window knowingly.
27. **A lowering applied by its verdict raised its edge where its author's standing had fallen** (`a_lowering_applied_by_its_verdict_raises_nothing`; `tiny_flags` worlds, 1 in 1,000,000, smallest 5 writes).
    - N6, admin by H's grant, grants Z write and demotes it to read: a fall from write, judged and applied. B removes H not listing H's grant of N6; the restart leaves N6 no authority, N6's grant of Z does not apply, and the demotion, applying by its verdict "whatever earlier writes now give its author", set Z from none to read: a grant by a party with no right, at a node trusting nothing, and the grant (unapplied in the end) had an effect (`unapplied N`). A lowering to a value below admin, in role or flag, can raise its edge where its author's standing fell.
    - Modelled: a lowering applied by its verdict applies for good where it takes its edge as restored in that pass down or keeps it; where its value is above that edge in role or flag it would raise it, and applies there only as any write does, by its path giving admin (write-path §1, Each lowering is judged once). Skipping it whatever its author holds instead fails `unapplied N` the other way (an admin's later demotion is a grant where an earlier removal took the edge in the restarted pass), and comparing with the edge's current value rather than as restored skips a demotion over an expired grant whose drops stand. 0 failing in 1,000,000 `tiny_flags` worlds and at full size (What held).

28. **A commit of several lowerings on one target is judged part by part, its earlier drops in `keeps`** (`a_commit_whose_drops_together_take_its_path_applies_only_what_keeps_it`; property `self-taken`).
    - X's edge on A is granted by B and by N5; X removes B and N5 in one commit, listing nothing. In holder order the removal of B drops B's grant of X and keeps (N5's grant still gives X its edge): applied. The removal of N5, judged with that drop in D, would leave X's edge no grant: void. X stays an admin by N5's grant, trusted or not.
    - Modelled (`World::commit`, spec/commits.md §2): the commit's edge writes take effect one at a time in key order (holder order, the part on an edge of its own path last: 37), each judged with the earlier ones in effect, a lowering among them with the earlier ones' drops in D and passed over by its `keeps`; the pass ends once the whole commit took effect. Control `-own-drops` (`keeps` over each lowering's own drops alone): both removals apply and the commit's path ends with no authority (`self-taken`, checked where the commit took effect in the pass judging it).

31. **A holder thing's honest leave is no dispute** (`an_honest_holder_leave_is_no_dispute`; story: J's leave of D1 from T2, `quiet`).
    - H grants A2 admin on T1, K grants Z write through A2, K makes A2 leave T1 naming A. As a candidate the leave, trusted, seeds D with K's grant (a self-removal carries no frontier), so an honest history shows a dispute (control `-all-candidates`: `quiet`).
    - Modelled (`Candidates::Explicit`): candidates are explicit lowerings of another holder's grant: no self-removal, none the walk trusting it applies as a may-grant grant (neither carries a frontier, so neither is trusted). `dispute` holds at full size: no reading unlike the trusted side's is left without a dispute shown.
32. **A trusted demotion to write dropped the grants made by the flag that its audit keeps** (`a_trusted_demotion_to_write_keeps_the_grants_made_by_flag`; story: the kiosk, `Setup::x_a2` 5).
    - A2 holds admin on T1; X, a writer of A2, grants G read through [A2] at 4; B, offline, demotes A2 on T1 to write with the flag. Trusting nothing, the demotion's audit keeps the grant (its need, read with the flag, is still met); trusted, its seeds took every unlisted write resting on (A2, T1): G lost read at the trusting nodes and the honest demotion was shown as a dispute (control `-seeds-all`: liveness, `quiet`).
    - Modelled (`Rules::seeds_by_need`): a trusted write's seeds are the unlisted held writes before it resting on its edge whose least need there (as the audit reads a write the pass has not applied) its value does not meet. A trusted grant at admin seeds nothing.
33. **A grant that does not apply decides whether a later removal shows as applied; nothing users observe changes** (`an_unapplied_grant_changes_nothing_observed`).
    - H grants N5 admin at 3 and again at 11; N5 gives N6 write with the flag until 10 at 8, removes N6 at 9, and demotes H to write with the flag at 11 listing only H's second grant. N5's removal of N6 falls from write, is judged and applied; N5's demotion of H drops H's first grant of N5; the restart leaves N5 no right at 8 and 9, so the grant of N6 does not apply, while the removal of N6 still applies by its verdict. Without the grant held the removal falls from nothing, gets no verdict and does not apply.

58. **A lowered holder's write can stand where nothing is trusted, and the lowering is then shown** (`a_lowered_keys_grant_standing_where_nothing_is_trusted_is_shown`; `tiny_keys` `key unaudited, shown`).
