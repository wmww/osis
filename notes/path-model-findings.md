# Path model findings
> Summary: What the roles-model `paths` module (stated paths, signers as members: notes/write-path-design.md, notes/account-membership-design.md) found in that design: each hole with its scenario, the rule allowing it and a fix or question; what is open for the design notes; what held; what the model does not cover.

The model: core/test/roles-model/README.md (Paths). Each finding has a hand world in `src/paths/tests.rs`: a `repro_*` test, ignored, asserting the wanted behaviour under the reading it broke and failing, or a test asserting the hole and its fix side by side. The model has one rule set, `Rules::fixed` (the design, the lowerings judged in key order: finding 16); `Rules::design` is the first round's reading the `repro_*` tests fail under, and each control is `Rules::fixed` with one rule undone. X is the stolen node, H its remover, R the removal, B another node of account A, K a node of the collaborator account A2, T1 a thing A administers, M a mint admin of A who paired H, N5 a node of A (paired by X, or paired by H and removed by X before the theft, its key the thief's), N6 and RD3 nodes trusting nothing.

## Findings
Numbered as found. 4, 7, 12 and 15 are one class (how a removal's own authority meets what it audits and lists) and have one answer; 13 is no hole.

1. **Removing a node undoes its role writes where the remover did not hold them, removals included** (`repro_a_grant_the_remover_did_not_hold_stands`, `repro_a_removal_the_removed_node_made_stays`).
   - X shared T1 with A2; H does not sync T1; H removes X. R's audit drops the grant, and every write of K falls, with no list made for them. The other direction is a safety matter: X had removed A2 from T1; R drops that removal and A2 writes T1 again.
   - Allowed by: an audit drops every unlisted role write through the lowered edge (write-path §1, Attribution and demotion); addenda vouch for plain content only (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
   - Fix modelled: a vouch (one per thing, naming R and the role writes its writer had received by R's `ts`), counting when its path gives admin on that thing at its key in the walk with no vouch counting; a counting vouch keeps what it names out of R's audit and R's seeds. Grants and lowerings alike.
2. **A vouch judged in the walk it changes has no fixpoint** (`repro_a_vouch_judged_in_the_walk_it_changes_settles`).
   - Honest world: X had demoted B to write on A; H never received that; R drops it, so B is an admin again and vouches for its own demotion; the vouch standing, B is no admin at the vouch's key, so it does not count, so the demotion falls, so it counts.
   - Fix modelled: read the voucher's authority from the walk with no vouch counting (two walks, no iteration). It settles and gives no escalation (`a_vouch_gives_nothing_to_whoever_could_not_have_made_the_write`). Its cost is finding 3.
3. **A holder kept by a vouch vouches for nothing** (`repro_a_holder_kept_by_a_vouch_vouches_in_turn`).
   - X paired N5 and shared T1; B received the pairing, only N5 the share. B's vouch keeps N5; N5's vouch for the share does not count (N5 is no admin in the walk without vouches), so the share falls. Accepted (membership §4, No circle). Addenda are not limited so: they are judged in the settled walk, so a writer kept by a vouch vouches for content.
4. **A removal that does not list its own grant drops what it does not list and then does not apply** (`repro_nothing_falls_by_a_removal_that_does_not_apply`).
   - X (stolen, admin by H's grant) removes H with an empty frontier. The audit drops X's own grant and B's; on the restart X holds nothing, so its removal does not apply, yet both grants stay dropped and no dispute is raised.
   - Fix modelled (`void_unapplied`): a lowering that made drops and that the settled walk does not apply is void: the walk is redone without it and its drops (`a_removal_that_does_not_apply_once_its_drops_are_made_is_void`; the same for a removal whose author's grant a later removal drops, `a_removal_whose_author_falls_later_drops_nothing`). Which one a round voids: finding 16. Control `paths-sticky`: stray drops, and a trusted removal void.
   - Exempting the removal's own grants from its audit instead (control `exempt_chain`) is finding 15.
5. **A reader holding only its thing's leaf of a frontier misjudges role writes on A** (`repro_a_reader_with_its_things_leaf_reads_as_a_member`).
   - Fix modelled: a reader holding R sees the entry, present or absent, of each role commit in the evidence it holds (`Rules::evidence_leaves`).
6. **Both sides of a standing dispute rotate A at every pass, without end** (`repro_a_standing_dispute_stops_rotating`).
   - Fix modelled: an epoch covering a departure the node's walk does not apply is the other side's: read, never current, superseding nothing, neither answered nor withdrawn (`Withdraw::SideOnly`).
7. **A frontier listing everything received blesses the thief's removal of the remover** (`repro_a_removal_listing_all_it_received_applies`).
   - The thief removes H, honestly stamped, before H acts; H receives it and removes X, listing it. The listed removal stands, H holds nothing at R's key, and R is void under every trust set.
   - Fix modelled (`stands`): the list is its writer's. A remover whose walk does not apply R (trusted, with its seeds) with everything named names the role writes in the order received, leaving out each R would not apply with; a witness builds its vouch the same way (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`; story: `Setup::x_first` and the thief's moves heard before the removal, properties `own path`, `removal`). Every other lowering X made stays made (`a_lowering_the_removed_node_made_before_stays_made`). Controls `paths-listed` (the removal void, X admin) and `paths-key-order` (the remover keeping in key order: N5 is back, `the_remover_keeps_what_it_received_first_in_the_story`).
   - A reader's rule reading such an entry as absent (by the edges of R's stated path) is finding 12.
8. **The last-admin rule reading every admin edge of a thing splits readers from members** (`repro_a_reader_judges_the_last_admin_rule_as_a_member`).
   - H pairs B and leaves A. A reader of T1 where B never wrote holds H's leaving and not B's grant: it refuses the leave and shows H's later writes; A's members do not.
   - Fix modelled: an unmarked write taking a standing admin edge (admin, no `until`) to anything else applies only where the remaining admin it relies on is a standing admin edge other than the lowered one: its path's last edge, else the holder it names, whose edge writes are its evidence (`LastAdmin::Named`; `a_reader_judges_the_named_last_admin_rule_as_a_member`). A timed admin is no remaining admin (`a_timed_admin_is_no_remaining_admin`: counted as one, a thing goes adminless on a date with no marked write).
9. **The collateral grantee** (`repro_a_collateral_grantees_writes_count_after_its_re_grant`): fixed by counting a re-granted path from each edge's first chained grant (`Regrant::FirstChained`). A grant the thief backdates is chained, so a holder granted later may stamp content back to it (`a_backdated_grant_moves_a_current_holders_first_point_back`): no new right, an earlier `ts`. A re-granted writer's addendum written before its new grant vouches for nothing; the one it writes after does (`a_re_granted_writers_later_addendum_vouches`; the state-based writing writes it).
10. **A removed node vouches for its own backdated writes through the cut path** (`repro_a_write_made_after_the_removal_does_not_show_by_its_signers_addendum`).
    - X, removed from A, still writes T1 through A2: it stamps a write before R stating path [A] and names it in its own addendum by path [A2]; the same with a grant and its own vouch where X administers T1 through A2.
    - Fix modelled: no addendum or vouch counts for a commit of its own signer (`no_own_addendum`, `no_own_vouch`; `a_removed_signer_vouches_for_nothing_of_its_own`; story: thief move 19). A colluding writer still can (nothing checks receipt, as today).
11. **Pairing after a fight** (`repro_a_node_paired_after_a_fight_is_a_member_at_itself`): with its pairer's trust entries the new node reads as its pairer (`a_node_paired_with_its_pairers_trust_entries_is_a_member_at_itself`).
12. **No reader's rule for "an entry that unmakes its removal" is both checkable and safe** (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`).
    - M (mint) paired H and X. X, stolen, removes M listing nothing, which drops M's grant of H; H receives it and removes X, listing it. Read over the edges of R's stated path the entry is kept, since it lowers (M, A), and R is void under every trust set: the thief removes the remover's pairer first.
    - Read over the held grants on those edges, closed, a removed node writes a junk grant to the remover and strikes the remover's listing of its own earlier removal (the world of `a_removed_node_writing_a_grant_to_the_remover_stays_removed`). Read from the walk it is circular (what R rests on is decided by what R drops). A writer leaving out "a lowering of an edge a grant on the path rests on" undoes an honest removal: M paired H and X; X, before the theft, removes M listing M's grant of H; left out, it is dropped and M is admin again.
    - Answer modelled: finding 7's. The writer leaves out by effect (what R does not apply with), in the order received; no reader reads an entry as absent (`a_list_keeping_what_unmakes_its_removal_voids_it`, both the thief's removal of M and the honest one). In key order instead, the thief makes an honest earlier removal the entry to leave out (`a_remover_keeps_what_it_received_first`; in the story with N5's key, control `paths-key-order`).
13. **A grantee granted again vouches for its own fallen grant** (`a_re_granted_grantee_vouches_for_its_own_fallen_grant`): no hole, the design's intent (notes/account-membership-design.md §4).
    - X paired N5 (H never received it); N5 wrote content and a grant; R drops N5's grant and all of it. H pairs N5 again. N5, admin again in the first walk and holding X's grant of it from before R, vouches for it (X's commit, not N5's): the grant applies at its own key and N5's gap writes stand, role writes included. The same through a holder: X grants A2 admin on T1, R drops it, B grants A2 again, K vouches along [A2].
    - It is the rule (a remaining party that could write it had received it), and the same terms as content. Barring a vouch for a write that sets the voucher's own edge would bar a node keeping X's demotion of it (`a_lowering_the_removed_node_made_before_stays_made`: only B received it), would miss the holder case, and stops no hostile admin, which vouches for a backdated grant to another key instead. With the state-based writing it happens wherever the grantee is granted again, so `Regrant::FirstChained` is reached only where the grantee is granted below admin.
14. **A removed node's backdated self-removal undoes its own earlier role writes** (`a_backdated_leave_undoes_nothing_the_removed_node_made`).
    - X, stolen, leaves A by a self-removal stamped before role writes it made honestly (a demotion of B, a pairing of N5), which H received and lists. A self-removal audits nothing and states no hop through (X, A), so no audit reaches it; X held no admin at its later writes, so they fall: X's demotion of B is undone, N5 is no member.
    - Allowed by spec/permissions.md §5.1 Seeds ("except a write lowering its author's own edge") and the self-removal's path ending at its holder (write-path §1, The end): no lowering puts it in question.
    - Fix modelled (`Rules::leave_in_question`): a lowering of edge h puts a self-removal of h in question like a write through h; unlisted, the backdated leave is dropped. Honest leaves before R are received and listed. Alternative: R's audit judges the victim's later writes against the edge as restored (which already counts what a self-removal took), never against the current value.

15. **A removal's own chain exempt from its audit, read from the held writes, spares the thief's writes** (`a_made_up_key_regranting_the_remover_holds_nothing`, `a_backdated_timed_grant_to_the_remover_changes_nothing`).
    - X backdates a grant of admin to a made-up key Z, and Z a grant of admin to H (admin already). Z's grant sets an edge of R's path, so X's grant of Z is exempt and Z stays admin wherever R is trusted. With `until` the first half alone suffices: X backdates a grant of admin until 7 to H; it stands; H's edge is timed, so R, naming no other admin, is refused and, trusted, void: X keeps A.
    - Fix modelled: nothing is exempt. Both writes rest on the removed edge (or are made under one that does), are unlisted, and fall (`nothing_through_the_removed_edge_is_exempt`); what the exemption was for is finding 4's fix. The thief's menu aims moves at the remover (23 to 29); control `paths-exempt` (`exempt_chain`): safety, the removal void.
16. **Which lowerings are void decides the reading, and only judging them in key order gives every void write no effect** (`a_void_write_has_no_effect`, `an_earlier_void_write_has_no_effect`, `a_party_removed_before_the_theft_stays_removed`, `a_later_lowering_overrules_an_earlier_one_it_drops`, `a_lowering_dropping_what_its_author_rests_on_is_void`).
    - The property: a void write has no effect (the reading is the reading with it not held), and so every dropped commit's dropper applies. Checked failing (`void`, `stray`) in the search, the family and 100,000 small worlds of admins granting and removing each other (`tiny`, trusting nothing or one lowering), with one reading under any relabelling of commit IDs keeping the walk's order (`one reading`).
    - Voiding by rounds after a walk with restarts fails it either way: a drop stays made by a lowering the restart left unapplied. Earliest first: H pairs B and N5; B pairs Z; Z removes H listing B's grant (dropping H's pairing of N5), then removes B listing nothing, which drops B's pairing of Z; the earliest first voids the removal of H, then the one of B, which did it. Against safety: X removed N5 before the theft (`out`); the thief, heard before H acts, removes H listing nothing and backdates with N5's key a grant of admin to H before `out`; earliest first voids `out` and N5 is back wherever R is trusted. Latest first: H grants B admin at 2 and at 10; B removes H at 9 listing neither and at 10 listing the first; the removal at 9 dropped the first grant, so the one at 10 is unapplied in that walk and voided first, then the one at 9 (4 commits; 3 of 100,000 `tiny` worlds, 2 of 100,000 story worlds at nodes trusting nothing). Earliest first fails it in 18 `tiny` worlds (smallest 5 commits) and 136 of 8,000 story worlds, all at once in 29 and 394.
    - The first walk without drops (`Stratified`: a lowering not applied with no drops made is void, the rest drop in a second walk) misses every removal whose own drops take its author's standing (finding 4's world: N6, paired by H, removes H listing nothing): stray 3,927 of 100,000 `tiny`; in 8,000 story worlds stray, safety, liveness, removal, own path, dispute and admin all fail.
    - The consistent void set ((a): a set V with nothing left unapplied that made drops, each member void judged with only the others voided) is not unique and need not exist: two in 2 of 68,502 `tiny` worlds trusting nothing, one of them reached by no order of voiding one lowering at a time; none in 1 (H grants Z; Z grants N5, removes H at 9 listing that, which drops H's own grant of N5 at 3; N5 removes Z at 11 listing nothing, dropping Z's removal of H and its grant of N5: each removal, judged with the other held, applies by overruling it) and in `a_later_lowering_overrules_an_earlier_one_it_drops`. A tie rule is a priority, and the one that gives the property is key order. With the earlier lowering kept where two sets are consistent it agrees with key order in all but that one world; the search over sets is exponential.
    - Modelled (`VoidOrder::KeyOrder`, the model's rule): the lowerings are judged in key order, each in the walk of the held writes up to it with the void ones before it left out; the writes it overrules (other lowerings its drops leave unapplied with drops made) are voided first, the latest first; it is void if it has made drops and then does not apply, and stays void. So the earlier of two lowerings that unapply each other applies where the later's own drops take the later's standing (`two_lowerings_unapplying_each_other_settle_on_the_earlier`, `a_lowering_dropping_what_its_author_rests_on_is_void`), and the later applies where it drops the earlier and holds its standing without it (`a_later_lowering_overrules_an_earlier_one_it_drops`: B's removal of N6 drops N6's removal of H, so H's grant of B stands again). A void write is left out of every later judgement, so it has no effect by construction; a write a later lowering overrules is checked the same and holds. Every story property holds with it, at 400,000 random worlds and every 157th family world (README.md, Paths). Its tie rule is key order itself, a function of the held set.
    - Weaker, and all a rule can give: a write that does not apply for want of authority may still have an effect, since it may be what voids another (4 of 100,000 `tiny` worlds under every order, counted as `unapplied`: H pairs N5 and B; N5 removes H at 5; H removes B at 8, unapplied; B removes N5 at 9 listing nothing, which drops N5's removal, so H's removal of B applies and B's does not: no reading applies B's removal, and without H's it applies).
17. **A party with admin forces one walk per write that voids itself** (`void_rounds_grow_with_a_partys_writes`).
    - X grants k keys of its own admin and each removes X listing nothing; H removes X. At a node trusting nothing each removal of a key drops every grant of X and is void, one walk each over the component (each reading walks twice, the first with no vouch counting): rounds 20 and 200, commits visited 352 and 21,412 at k 10 and 100 in key order (latest first 462 and 31,512; first walk only 98 and 818, but wrong; the consistent-set search 84,686 at k 10, exponential). At a node trusting H's removal X's grants are seeds, no removal of a key audits, and nothing is walked twice: 26 and 206 commits, no round.
    - Bound: a lowering whose audit drops nothing costs its own place in the walk; one that drops costs a walk from its earliest drop (the restart), plus one per earlier lowering it overrules. In an honest history that is a removal's restart, as today. Under attack a party holding admin buys a walk of the component per write it makes, O(k²) for 2k writes, paid by every node not trusting a removal of it (issues/perm-eval-cost.md). Seeds end it at the nodes that trust the removal.
18. **The audit's writes in question and a trusted write's seeds are two sets** (`the_audit_over_the_held_writes_differs_only_where_nothing_is_trusted`).
    - The audit puts in question the applied writes before R, the seeds the held ones. Read over the held writes too (`audit_held`, mode `paths-audit-held`), readings differ only at nodes trusting nothing (never at a trusting node under the model's rule): X backdates its own leave to 1 and removes H at 5, honestly stamped; H removes X at 6. Over the applied writes H's removal drops the leave, X's removal of H then applies and H's removal is void (the leave stands: X left either way); over the held writes it drops both and applies. Where X had made an honest grant after 1 that a witness vouched for, the applied reading loses it there. Both readings hold every property. So the spec must say which; the held set is one mechanism with the seeds and keeps the removal where the thief's own backdated leave would void it.
19. **A thief heard before the removal drops a co-admin's grant by demoting the remover and granting it admin again.**
    - X, heard by H before R: X demotes H to write listing only X's own grant (its audit drops H's pairing of B), and grants H admin again. R applies with both named, so H keeps both, and B's grant is lost with what was written under it (M's leave naming B included). No worse than X removing B while heard, which the design keeps (membership §4: a removal X made stays made); but R's result names the members X lowered, not those a kept lowering of the remover dropped. The oracle sets these aside (`heard_lowering`).
20. **An accomplice's vouch voids a trusted removal and leaves no trace in the reading** (`a_current_admins_vouch_voids_the_removal_and_leaves_no_trace`).
    - N5, paired by X and listed by H, is the thief's: it vouches for X's removal of H. Trusted, R is void by that vouch and stands without it (the accomplice, no wider: Z's or X's vouch counts for nothing; story move 36, the worlds counted as `accomplice`). Once R is void the walk applies X's removal of H, so R does not apply in the first walk and the vouch counts no more: the final reading holds no counting vouch, and a node cannot show which party voided R. The accepted hole (notes/disputes.md) stands; showing its cause needs the trusted evaluation's first walk.
21. **A dispute whose own write is a no-op where trusted is not raised as spec/permissions.md §5.1 words it** (`a_dispute_whose_write_is_a_no_op_where_trusted_is_shown`).
    - X, admin on A2 (A2 admin on T1), removed A2 from T1 before the theft (a witness vouches); the thief backdates through A2 a removal of A from T1. At a node trusting nothing H's removal of X drops X's removal of A2, so the thief's write applies and B's removal of A2 from T1 by [A] finds no path. Trusted, B's removal drops the thief's write by its seeds and applies, but A2 is off T1 already, so it lowers nothing: the spec's dispute must lower its edge, so none is raised and the node reads A off T1 silently. Modelled: a dispute is a write that applies once trusted (`disputes`). The spec's clause "with a value below the edge's value just before it ... and not equal to it" is to drop when the dispute rule is next written (A1).
22. **A vouch counts only where its removal applies in the first walk; counted by its key alone it keeps the thief's writes at a node trusting nothing** (`a_vouch_counts_only_where_its_removal_applies_in_the_first_walk`).
    - X removed N5; the thief backdates X's own edge to write, removes H before R, and with N5's key vouches for all three. At a node trusting nothing R is void in the first walk (X's removal of H applies once R drops the self-demotion), and N5 is an admin there; counted by its key alone, N5's vouch would keep all three and R would apply in the second walk with N5 back, unlike the trusted side, with no dispute shown. The design's condition (membership §4, It counts: R applies in the first walk) is what closes it; the model holds it, and A1 must.

## Open
For the design notes before A1's spec brief, each with what the model gives it:
0. **Key order does not give every void write no effect** (16): `void` fails in 3 of 1,000,000 `tiny` worlds (234147 trusting one lowering; 360379, 830267 trusting nothing), 0 in the first 100,000. Each later lowering's judgement re-resolves the earlier overrules, the latest first, and can void a lowering judged applying (`repro_a_void_write_has_no_effect_in_key_order`: a removal of B dropping nothing voids N6's removal of H). In 360379 the overruled write is one its overruler drops, reported void rather than dropped. Carrying each judgement's overrules into the next (the walk before a lowering is the settled one after the previous) passes that world and every default test and 8,000 story worlds, but fails `void` in 2 of 1,000,000 `tiny` (234147, 664649). Also not pinned by any test: the nested order (earliest first changes 1 of 100,000 `tiny` readings, 91788, every check passing) and "never revisited" (1 of 1,000,000, 547289).
1. **The walks a party can force** (17): k self-voiding writes, k walks, paid where no removal of the party is trusted. A bound, or the cost stated, for issues/perm-eval-cost.md and A1 brief 7.
2. **Which writes the audit puts in question** (18): the applied ones or the held ones, one sentence in write-path §1 and spec/permissions.md §5.1.
3. **The dispute clause** (21): a dispute is a write that applies once trusted; the spec's "lowers its edge" clause goes when A1 rewrites §5.1.
4. **What a removal's result names** (19): the members a kept lowering of the remover by X dropped, beside those X lowered.

## What held
- Under `Rules::fixed`, at 400,000 random worlds and every 157th world of the small family (README.md, Paths, for counts): safety, liveness, alike (readers holding only the commits on the things they read and those commits' evidence, against members; two nodes trusting nothing), the remover's standing kept, removal kept, the last admin standing, no stray drop checked from the reading alone, no void trusted removal but by the accomplice (its vouch the cause), no void write with an effect, one reading under a relabelling keeping the order, and at nodes trusting nothing every honest removal applied or shown through its disputes and every reading unlike the trusted side's shown a dispute. The thief's moves include those aimed at the remover, those heard before the removal (several at once, with N5's key, a party removed before the theft), equivocation, the accomplice's vouch, a lowering above the signer of a removal of two hops, and timed self-removals. Each control fails the search: `-sticky`, `-listed`, `-exempt`, `-latest`, `-earliest`, `-at-once`, `-key-order`, `-leave`, `-once`, `-own-vouch`, `-any-edge`, `-timed`; `-first-applied` is moot (13).
- The void step terminates: each lowering is judged once, in key order, and within its judgement the writes it overrules only grow; a void decision is never revisited (at most 8 walks redone in a story reading at full size, 21 in a `tiny` one); its cost is 17.
- Receipt order against key order: the remover and witnesses keeping in key order fail safety in the search (`paths-key-order`, N5 back), not only by hand.
- A reading is a function of the held set, the leaves seen and the trust set: a relabelling of commit IDs keeping the walk's order changes nothing (`one reading`, checked at every node of the search and in every `tiny` world).
- On today's walk (`vouches`, `Rules::stands`): the writers' leaving out takes the listing set-aside "X lowered A" from 112,892 to 0 in the family and from 555 to 2 of 100,000 random worlds, with kept, stays made and safety holding (README.md, Vouches). Both remaining worlds are the model's writing order: a voucher's own vouch against another removal keeps X's timed grant to the voucher, which lapses before the voucher's own later removal, whose list the model chose before any vouch existed; chosen with the vouches present, as the remover would, the set-aside is 0 in both. With the state-based writing one random world (48946) breaks kept, kept as promised and stays made: the remover and the witness leave out the victim's earlier timed demotion of the remover (the removal does not apply with it), and the properties' reference still keeps every commit a witness received and could write. The reference, and spec/permissions.md §5.1 What vouches keep, need the writers' condition: only commits with which R still applies.
- Descent: a write to a dependent is judged along the record edges down its mint chain; lowering a descent edge audits every path through it (`a_write_by_descent_is_judged_along_the_record_edges`). Validity reads record edges only, so a thing on a path becoming independent changes no verdict (nothing in the model reads independence).
- Self-removal by path end audits nothing and raises nothing (`a_self_removal_by_path_end_audits_nothing`, `a_self_removal_raises_nothing`); a removal after it audits against the edge as restored (`a_backdated_leave_shields_nothing_from_the_removal`). An `until` passing audits nothing; the next explicit write on that edge does (`an_until_passing_audits_nothing_the_next_explicit_write_does`).
- The state-based writing (a node writes whenever its own walk shows an owed commit it could write that nothing of its own covers) holds liveness under stated paths; answering only the first time a node's walk applies R fails it (a witness standing by a vouch, a re-granted writer).
- A switch takes (`a_switch_takes`); two nodes removing each other never leave A without an admin; a removal logged only where the node wrote leaves the window Tess's answer closes (`a_removal_logged_only_where_the_node_wrote_leaves_a_window`).
