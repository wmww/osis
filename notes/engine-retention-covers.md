# Engine: covers
> Summary: How bodies drop as covered (`retention/snapshot.rs`, `replace.rs`, node-sync `drop_covered`; spec/commits.md §5, spec/sync.md §7): the cover rule per part, own live bodies, purge on replace as the replacing writer's cover, frontiers and covering by the join, own replaces, forgetting and its accepted losses.

## The cover rule
- A cover drops a body when it carries every live write of it and the body is past its keep bound, judged per part (`drop_part_for`). Judged per commit, a creation commit's live parts kept its replaced purge-on-replace part, and writers without the right re-covered it for good (sim `snapshot_rights`).
- One rule for durable and ephemeral, live or replaced (Tess: durable compaction on). A live delta's keep bound is its aspect's `history::Kept::live_until` (`bounds::live_keep`; spec/commits.md §5 Kept while live): a kept aspect's current parts are kept history (listings and undo read them) and pay their storage; an unkept aspect's live writes merge into snapshots (`compaction::map_written_apart_holds_bounded_bodies_*`).
- Cost accepted (spec/commits.md §5 Covered, Limit): a snapshot cannot give back what it dropped, so a purge that later falls, or a cover that later counts for nothing, restores no live write of an unkept aspect once every holder dropped it (the author keeps its own, below).
- Every covered-drop path asks the keep bound after the cover: node-sync's `drop_covered`, `own_covers_every_part`, `settle_none` (replaced bodies only, `replace::replaced`; a live part is the trigger's), memory's `drop_covered`.
- Out of history a drop needs only a held valid writer's snapshot covering the body (`Engine::drop_from`, no right asked). The purge right counts only where a promise is kept: purge on replace's duty (`replace::coverer`), deleting at hosts, purge requests (`purge::snapshot_right`).
- **Cover candidates**: node-sync judges an aspect's candidates when a snapshot arrives (`Core::cover_judged_on`). A durable delta is one from apply (`apply::join_part`) until found kept for good while live (`Engine::settle_kept`), and again when one of its writes dies (`mark_killed`), its aspect is rebuilt (`mark_bounded`), its thing's setting moves a live bound (`retention::settings_changed`) or the node opens. Limit: a write dying by a rebuild re-judges an unkept aspect's bodies only at the next snapshot or full pass (kept longer, never dropped early; tombstone.rs `a_body_replaced_before_a_restart_drops_under_a_later_cover_*`).
- Tests: compaction.rs; sync_more `flip_after_drop_a_writers_cover_keeps_the_killer_*`.

## Own live bodies (spec/sync.md §7 Own bodies)
A node drops a delta it wrote none of whose writes is dead only under its own covers. Why: another writer's snapshot can later count for nothing (a demotion whose frontier does not list it), and once every holder dropped the write and hosts deleted it nothing brings it back (retention.rs `none_writer_covers_once_the_cover_it_relied_on_falls_*`); the author still holding it re-places it as must-hold. Cost: an author holds its live writes until it writes a cover (the trigger counts them). Replaced bodies drop under any writer's cover: nothing visible rests on them.

## Purge on replace (`retention/replace.rs`)
- **The writer's cover** (Tess, issues/retention-modes.md): at the end of every transaction (`settle_none`), over the aspects whose purge-on-replace bodies or snapshots changed (`Core::none_due`: commits on such aspects, rebuilds, setting changes, and timeline changes, since a grant can make this node a writer owing a cover, retention.rs `none_granted_writer_covers_what_it_holds_replaced_*`), a node that may write a snapshot (`snapshot::may_snapshot`) writes one from its whole state (at most one per aspect per transaction) when a held body under purge on replace (the setting in force at its replaced-at, `under_purge`) is replaced and no held valid snapshot holding the right over it covers it (`coverer`). Every covered body out of history then drops, at every node. The cover is written in a `Tx::step` (`Step::Cover`): a failure leaves the body to other writers, is reported (`OwnWriteFailed`) and never fails a receive.
- **Replaced** (`replaced`): the body is valid, holds a live write by dot the state does not (or a winning write that no longer wins), and joins into the state's part with its canonical bytes unchanged: dead, not un-merged under an invalid record (a snapshot then cannot cover it, and one would be written every transaction). Not "has a dead write": a root reset dies once a later write implies one, yet canonical form keeps it (held-model-4). A delta with an expiry is judged with its values value-less, as the cover carries them.
- The edit's own body drops under the same snapshot (purge on replace keeps no live write), so the aspect holds one snapshot at the writer after each transaction (`timed_dead::replaced_register_goes_under_its_cover_*`). Readers keep replaced bodies until a writer's snapshot arrives; with no writer online they keep them.
- **No useless covers**: a writer whose own snapshot would hold no right over the body (`purge::would_hold_right`) owes none while another held valid snapshot covers it. Without this, under a narrow purge set every plain writer wrote a useless cover per replacement (sim `snapshot_rights`: 3 covers per edit, headers kept for good, a node over its state bound).
- **Waiting to write** (`MayWrite::Later`: role held, minting or delete keys not here yet): the thing waits in `Indexes::cover_waits` under every thing on its record chain (journaled, so a rollback gives back a wait it ended). `replace::wake_covers` reads the transaction's journals of key-bearing commits, secrets, own keys and delete hints, and re-judges only the waiting things under those; roles, record edges and epochs come through `Core::timelines_changed`. So whichever of body, replacement, role and keys arrives last triggers the cover (retention.rs `writer_covers_once_its_delete_keys_come_in_any_order_*`). O(what changed); memory only (the first transaction after an open settles every held purge-on-replace aspect). Rejected: marking every such aspect below a base whose keys changed (O(the base's aspects) per rotation).
- **Cost** (spec/requirements.md Scale, the cost the app chooses): a snapshot of the aspect per replacing edit, in practice by the editor alone: it uploads the snapshot with the edit, and node-sync ingests a batch's snapshots before its plain commits (`open_entries`), so other writers find the body covered. (notes/growth.md).
- Privacy: erased data persists in its body at a writer for no transaction, at a reader until a writer's snapshot arrives, at a host until a right holder next syncs. The read state loses it at the kill.
- Rejected: writer-side purge per edit (Tess's fallback if this proves costly): a purge cannot express a partial replacement, its commit keeps its blobs `ROLE_KEEP`, and every edit leaves a leftover.
- Tests: retention.rs `none_*`, setting.rs, held.rs, sim `snapshot_rights`.

## Frontiers and covering (`retention/snapshot.rs`)
- **Frontiers** list the sessions occurring in the state (`osis_crdt::sources_of`; a 0 entry for a session with no contiguous prefix applied, spec/crdt.md §8), each covering its counters applied and valid on the aspect (`snapshot::frontiers_now`): from the held valid snapshots' frontiers on, then held commits while they join without a hole, a held valid own whole replace passing a hole below it. Why both: a commit forgotten once covered takes its counter range with it, and a walk from counter 1 would stop at that hole for good (`compaction::writes_after_forgotten_ones_compact_*`). The own walk also stops below a purged part (notes/engine-retention-purge.md).
- **`covered`** judges a held body by the join, with the snapshot's copies under the body's dots stripped first so a differing copy never covers; the body's value-less writes are left out of that strip (`Fragment::valueless_dots`), since a snapshot whose writer held a purge this node does not holds a live write value-less and would otherwise never cover. Comparisons must stay on canonical bytes, not `Fragment ==`: a deleted text element's char survives a join in memory but not in canonical bytes (deletion::text_partly_erased).
- An expiring delta held with its values is covered once none of its values lives, before T as after (the cover carries them value-less; nothing visible can return). "Expiring is never covered" is not a rule. One not held and not value-less is not covered, though the frontier alone would say it is (retention.rs `purge_on_replace_covers_dead_expiring_write_*`, ephemeral.rs `dead_expiring_ephemeral_write_covered_before_expiry_*`).
- **A body not held** is covered by tables alone, except that a snapshot holding any of its writes value-less covers it only where those writes are value-less here for good (past expiry): a snapshot whose writer held a request this node does not, or one held through its purge's fall, gives no value, and counting the missing body covered would leave it never fetched (order seed 7310; retention.rs `snapshot_stripped_for_a_fallen_purge_covers_nothing_of_it_*`). Cost: the snapshot index keeps a value-less bound beside the valued one (`Carried`), so the body is read only where it meets the part's writes (`holds_valueless`). So a writer applying a purge that names a body it dropped as covered re-snapshots even where another's whole snapshot holds the body value-less (`purge_resnapshots`).

## Own replaces (`own_*` in `retention/snapshot.rs`; spec/commits.md §5 Own replaces)
- A plain whole replace (root reset or register write) is its own cover. `Indexes::own_held` and `own_replaces` key held plain parts by (thing, aspect, author), so finding covers never walks the aspect. It takes two valid held own replaces later than the body: one late replace that later counts for nothing loses nothing.
- A commit goes only whole (`own_covers_every_part`): a reader cannot rewrite a blob, so dropping one part would leave it unable to relay the rest. Such a commit stays while any part is uncovered (issues/zero-growth.md Left). Out of history the author needs no purge right, so a single writer of an `admin` aspect stays flat.

## Forgetting (node-sync `forget_forgettable`; spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting)
- A cover that turns invalid, drops other than as covered, or goes missing at its purge's fall gives back what it alone covered (`uncover`, notes/engine-validity-uncover.md), until the body is forgotten.
- A node forgets a content commit or snapshot, ID included, once every part dropped as covered, by any writer's cover, and no entry or delete within its tombstone names a blob of it (`Engine::forgettable`). Else each write another writer compacts leaves a header for good (`growth::several_writers_grow_nothing`, `several_writers_expiring_grow_nothing`). Event-driven (`State::forget_due`), one look at every commit after an open. A coverer forgotten before a body dropped under it hands its covers down (`apply::unindex::hand_down`), so the chain of covers stays whole.
- Forgetting reads as never having received: a removal frontier cannot list a forgotten commit, and the cover stands for it. A snapshot is forgotten under the next that covers it, whoever wrote it.
- **Accepted losses** (Tess: permission changes over inconsistent connections cannot all work out): a cover that reaches a node before a removal naming none of it and counts for nothing after loses the writes only it carried, its predecessors' content included (roles-model `forget.rs`, `previous_snapshot_bounds_the_loss`; others' live writes come back from their authors); a cover standing only on an addendum that later falls; a hostile device's snapshot claiming a sibling's edit without its value (`forget_account.rs`).
- Rejected, both against a loss that needs a late cover whose author is removed: keeping the previous snapshot (`OWN_PREV`, `ANY_PREV`: twice every compacted aspect's state at every node and host); a receipt per commit another writer covers (about 50 B per write; roles-model `forget-any`, `any_loses_with_a_cover_whose_author_falls`).
