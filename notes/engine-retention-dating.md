# Engine: when a body was replaced
> Summary: How the engine dates a body's replacement for keep-for-D, a function of the held set kept as the body's history row (`retention/bounds.rs`, `leaving.rs`; spec/commits.md §5 Bodies and deaths, spec/crdt.md §4.8): which writes died and when, the rows and why they stay exact, why a covered drop moves no date, dating only where history is kept, the unread delete margin, costs.

## Principle
- Tess: a body under keep-for-D is not dropped until D after the earliest valid replacing write the node holds. The date is a function of the held bodies (`bounds::index_date`), never of arrival order or of how a part is paged. On a dated aspect it is kept as the body's history row (Rows below), which every reader reads (sync's `part_keeps`, `drop_from` before a compaction drop, `Engine::replaced_at`, listing, undo).
- Rejected: dates recorded by arrival (death records, replays, crediting, recomputes after flips: the class of bugs a function of the held set removes); dating at the superseder the state holds now (a register changing within D keeps its old bodies for good); deaths reported by the join (a stamp names many writes, so per-stamp reports dated snapshots early); dating by the superseder's header (a header names the aspect, not the writes); walking the later bodies per judgment, joining each against the part (a pass quadratic in the aspect's bodies and their sizes); keeping a covered-dropped body while it dates a held write (purge on replace drops it at once).

## Which writes died
- The part's winning writes (`Fragment::winners`, spec/crdt.md §4.8: by position, kind and stamp, never stamp alone, since one op stamps many writes) that a held valid write supersedes (`ByWrite::first_superseding` over the aspect's held writes finds one). Winners, not absence: a position write beaten as `cur` stays as `prev`, a deleted text creation stays in its record, a write beaten in another record of its map dot stays in its own. `winners` is checked against a pairwise from-scratch definition (osis-crdt `tests_winners`). The part's own superseded writes are not its winners. None dead: past a relative bound. Debug builds check it against the state: the winners that do not win once the part joins a copy of the state at its positions (`bounds::deaths`, `whole_date`; a dead write with no held superseder is `Error::Corrupt`).

## When (`HeldWrites`; spec/crdt.md §4.8 When)
- A dead write dies at the earliest `ts` among held valid writes superseding it alone (osis-crdt `supersedes`, as held-model's `replaced_at`), read from any held valid body other than the part's own. Always the write's own stamp, never the holding body's: a valid snapshot's copy of a write whose delta is held invalid here (vouched) counts as the write itself (Fathom). Dating it at its snapshot instead let a later cover's drop redate it and bring a body out of history back (setting.rs `vouched_copy_dates_at_its_write_*`). A writer stamping early shortens what history keeps: storage reclaim only, never erasure (notes/deletion.md).
- A text element's creation dies only by a delete stamped after the latest held undelete of the element (`ByWrite::first_superseding`), so dropping the first delete of delete, undelete, delete moves nothing.
- Every body dates at the latest death of its writes, deltas too: a body drops only after every dead write it alone holds has left history. A snapshot first leaves out the dead writes a later held valid snapshot holds as winners (`HeldWrites::carried`: that one keeps their history; holding one as `prev` or deleted keeps none). Counting every carried write instead kept every snapshot of an aspect whose long-lived writes keep dying within D: held snapshots grew with the edit count, not the window (held-model `snapshot_replaced_at_last_death_no_later_snapshot_carries_*`). Holding more bodies dates no later; holding the same dates alike.
- The index: the aspect's held valid bodies are read once into `osis_crdt::ByWrite`, every write each holds, winning there or not (`Fragment::held_writes`: a deleted text creation still supersedes position writes below it), with the two earliest bodies holding each. `first_superseding` reads the positions on the dead write's path in stamp order, stopping past the best date found.
- Late arrival: a body arriving after its earliest superseder dropped is dated later, by what is held, and may show where a node holding both would not: an arrival (Tess's decision 1: what a node holds decides; held-model's any-order property allows exactly that).
- A snapshot dropped while a later one carried its dead writes is not brought back if that later one turns invalid before their D passes: those writes' history goes with it.

## Invariant: a valid covered drop moves nothing
A valid covered drop never changes whether any held body is in history, nor its listing, nor the keep bound of a held body in history, nor the replaced-at of a part in history (a part out of history may redate, staying out); only arrivals (bodies, settings, validity changes) and the clock do. A drop is valid only past the body's keep bound (`drop_from <= now`); `Engine::drop_body` skips the bound and is outside it. Why a drop of body B (out: `out(r_B) <= now`) moves no date (`out` keeps replaced order), per date read:
- a superseder's `ts`: a live write of B stays in the cover with its stamp; a write W only a dead write d of B dated redates to at most death(d) <= r_B (supersession is transitive), so W was out and stays out;
- a snapshot's dead writes a later snapshot S carries: S dropping adds back deaths <= r_S; a body in history has r > r_S;
- a text creation's latest undelete: an undelete's body drops only once the undelete is dead, by a delete no earlier than the creation's date, so the creation was out and only dates earlier;
- the state (`deaths`, `delta_live`): a cover carries every live write of B;
- headers, which outlive every drop (a fire's `ts`, the purge floor, a purged part's request `ts`, `kept_whenever_replaced`, undo's bound), the current setting (a cover of a setting body carries its newest write, so reads the same setting) and the clock (`at_clock`).

Each argument is per aspect. Forgetting a header is outside it: undo then counts the forgotten `ts` (notes/engine-retention-history.md Undo).

Tests: setting.rs `covered_drops_move_nothing_*` (random histories, covered drops in random order), `an_unkept_part_dates_no_other_part_*`, `vouched_copy_dates_at_its_write_*`, `a_covered_drop_redates_nothing_*`, `a_body_dies_at_its_latest_death_*`, `text_creation_dies_at_delete_after_undelete_*`.

## Dated only where history is kept (spec/commits.md §5 Keep bounds)
- A covered drop needs the body out of history on the node's clock, `out(r) <= now`, and `out(r) >= r`, so a replacement dated after the clock waits for the clock (`bounds::at_clock` adds `r` to the drop bound: `part_keep`, `part_keeps`, so `drop_from`, `settle_none`, memory drops).
- Per part: a part is dated only where the current setting keeps history for its own aspect (`bounded`, in `part_replaced_at`), by its own writes, kept by that aspect's entry; elsewhere it adds no bound, ahead of the clock or not. Rejected: dating per thing (an aspect waited on unrelated aspects) or a commit's parts at the latest death of all of them (a covered drop on an unkept aspect redated another part and brought a body out of history back: `an_unkept_part_dates_no_other_part_*`).
- Listing reads `body_history_keep` (the part's own history and the purge floor alone: purge on replace and no history list nothing, held-model AMBIGUITIES 68).
- A wall clock set back is not noticed, so sync's cached keeps judged before it stand.

## Unread delete margin (Fathom)
`MAX_DRIFT` applies to deletes at nodes keeping S unread only (spec/commits.md §5). A local drop changes only this node's reads, which follow its clock, so it happens at the keep bound; a right holder deletes at those nodes `MAX_DRIFT` later, once every clock agrees. `drop_part_for` records it as `Dropped::hosts_from` (the keep bound judged while the body is held, plus `MAX_DRIFT`; 0 when it had none). No timer or row of its own. The facade gives it as `BodyFacts::hosts_from` (a held part's keep bound plus `MAX_DRIFT`); osis-sync's read sends a covered-and-dropped body's Delete only once its clock reaches it (a memo clock read: a wake), the own log dropping it at once (`read::covered_delete_waits_max_drift_spec_commits_5`).

## Rows (`leaving::date_due`)
- Invariant: at every transaction's end each held valid part with values on a dated aspect has its row at `index_date` (none when nothing of it is dead), and no other part has one; inside a transaction a date on an aspect it changed is judged from the held writes (`leaving::dating_due`, a scan of the transaction's log per read: issues/dating-due-scans-the-log.md). Debug builds check every row of each aspect dated incrementally (`rows_exact`; one dated whole had each part checked against the state, `whole_date`).
- Safe direction, per reader: a date later than the true one keeps a body longer (covered drops, memory drops, sync's must-hold, listing show it longer: storage only), an earlier one, or none where a write of it is dead (read as live: droppable under a cover), drops or stops promising a body still in history (data loss). Exactness gives both: never early, and alike at every node holding the same bodies, in any arrival order (spec/commits.md §4).
- What moves a date: the aspect's held valid bodies (the writes `index_date` reads). A transaction's changed commits (`Commits::before`) say how: on an aspect that only gained bodies (applied, a late part come, turned valid) the parts dated again are the changed ones and those owning or carrying (snapshots, `HeldWrites::carried`) a write whose earliest superseder the new bodies' writes may move (`ByWrite::moved_by`: per kind at each position at or below the new write's, the greatest write stamped no later, since every lower one is superseded by it no later; an undelete's element creations; a value removal's set elements; the write itself, for its hiding copies). O(what the new writes supersede), whatever the history (`listing::a_kept_write_rederives_only_what_it_replaces_spec_sync_2`). Where a held body left or changed (dropped, stripped, turned invalid, so a superseder may be gone and dates move later), on a rebuild (`mark_bounded`), on rows forgotten (`forget_aspects`) and on an aspect turning dated, every part is dated again: O(the aspect's history), as the rebuild. `moved_by` is checked against noting bodies one at a time (osis-crdt `tests_winners::moved_by_check`).
- A transaction's end dates twice (before the settings' walks and once everything held has settled, after purge-on-replace drops and carriers): a later pass compares each part with what the earlier one dated (`Core::dated_as`), not with the meta before the transaction (`order::a_part_held_and_dropped_in_one_transaction_is_dated_out_*`). An aspect a setting kept dated with no cursor yet turned dated in this transaction before `settings_changed` gave it one: dated whole (`order::a_register_turning_dated_beside_its_replacing_write_dates_its_bodies_*`).
- A superseder judged invalid later: its part turns invalid, its writes leave the held writes, the aspect is dated whole and its victims' dates move later or go (`order::a_superseder_turning_invalid_*`).
- Rows are durable (`hr/`), so a restart dates nothing; a rollback restores them with the indexes.

## Costs
- The index per aspect (`Core::held_writes`, notes/engine.md Derived values), recomputed when the aspect's held bodies or their validity moved; a body joining only amends it (`amend_held_writes`: a write decodes its own body, not the aspect's) unless validity moved (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md). A part's keep bound reads its row (`replaced_bound_judged_once_per_change_of_its_aspect_spec_commits_5`). Dating a joined body reads it whole once more at the join (`tests_paged::a_trickled_fill_reads_each_page_a_bounded_number_of_times_*`). Counted: `a_join_decodes_its_own_body_not_the_aspects`.
- A judgment walks the part for its winners a fixed number of times (`retention::tests::reset_judged_walks_parts_once_*`, `Core::death_walks`); judging every body of an aspect reads about one body per judgment (`scale::judging_every_body_reads_each_once_spec_commits_5`). The part is decoded whole, O(part) memory (notes/engine-paged.md Streamed readers, Left whole).
- A kept, frequently edited aspect holds snapshots within its D window (3 at 8 and at 80 edits; `scale::kept_snapshots_stay_within_their_window_spec_commits_5`). A late delta older than n and 10n held commits costs flat (`scale::late_delta_deaths_are_flat_spec_crdt_4_8`).
- Tests: the independent truth is held-model's `replaced_at`: engine order.rs `kept_snapshot_replaced_at_latest_death_any_order_*`, `replaced_alike_in_any_order_*`, `text_redelete_*`, `snapshot_after_late_dated_death_not_credited_*`. The multi-node dating tests (deaths never early against a node keeping every body, skewed clocks) await sync tests (issues/sync-tests-owed.md).
