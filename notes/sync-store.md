# Sync: store
> Summary: osis-sync's `store` module (core/sync/src/store/): the one ciphertext store per node for every sync ID, read or kept unread; what its module docs leave out about its two parts, own entries, parked entries, served heads, carriers, caps and the policy, and the bounds on what peers make it keep.

Spec: spec/sync-ids.md §5-§6, spec/sync-streams.md §3, spec/sync-inbox.md §3 Deposits, spec/sync-judgement.md §4 (stand state). Code: the module docs of core/sync/src/store/mod.rs (what it owns, one transaction per operation, stamping), entries.rs (what differs by part), rows.rs (row layout), cipher.rs (the engine's door), follow.rs (what it follows, parking, carriers). Overview: notes/sync.md. The underlying store: notes/local-store.md. Why one shredding store: notes/sync-reasons.md, Nodes.

**Principle:** one store of ciphertext per node, for every sync ID read or kept unread: it decrypts nothing and judges no open; every operation is one transaction (a crash keeps all of its rows or none) and every row change stamps, so memos and `Effects` may read it. What a peer makes it keep is charged to that peer.

## What it owns, beyond mod.rs
- Tombstones are indexed by blob too (`tombstoned_under`): read's forgetting asks whether a delete of a commit's blob is recent under any sync ID.
- A repair's freshly encrypted blobs are linked only by the repair, so a source deleting one refuses it.
- **Rows other modules keep** on a peer's record (`record_put_row`, `record_put_wide_row`), one tag per owning module (`store::rows::tag`); they go with the record, a sync ID's also with the sync ID. `with_tx` gives other modules a transaction (`with_tx_in` either part).
- **A source's rows** (`store::source`): one mechanism for what a source keeps of S whatever the source. Durably, a record's rows (osis-store's index answers which records hold a row); in the memory part, any source's (`C/`, indexed `K/` by (S, tag, key)), gone with the source (`gone`; each owner may forget its own tags sooner, `source::forget_tags`: streams' `Known::forget_source`), S (`shred`) or a restart. A connection's live only in memory; a record's memory rows are those holding only while an ephemeral entry does. Users: streams' `Known` (what a stream showed), the store's stands and deletes seen (`Store::source_put`, in the source's own part), which age out after `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME` by the one `w/` sweep, and the store's room marks (`ROOM_AT`, notes/sync-streams.md Full and room marks).

## Two parts, one rule set (`Inner::ram`; entries.rs)
`Inner::find` says which part holds an entry, the blob's class which part takes a new one. Beyond entries.rs:
- An ephemeral entry's charge goes with its source, never to an `heir`; the caps differ (`make_room`: S's entries/bytes and the durable total, kept counted in `Env::total`; S's bytes and the memory total, summed); a durable entry may wait for its bytes, an ephemeral one is taken with them only.
- Path rows, the node log, records and every other row are the durable store's alone, so a read of the head or a peer's durable charge (`source_charge`) always opens the durable store.
- **Paths kept parsed** (`path::Paths`, in `Env`): each S's path parsed at its first read, then changed by the writes that change its rows (`append`, `truncate`, `drop_claims`, each before its rows move), staged in the transaction and taken at its commit; a read stamps `PathOf` in `Memory::stamps`, a committed change moves it. A path read is O(1) however many steps it has; a new path writer must go through `change`.
- Not the durable store's own memory overlay: the engine's suspended transaction (`CipherStore`) may hold no memory rows (`Transaction::suspend` refuses them), and in memory mode that store still writes its node log, records and `changed` rows durably.
- The memory part's writes (log events, feeds, charges in `Memory`) are not undone when its transaction fails: nothing assumes a module's memory survived a store failure, which closes every connection and restarts the modules (notes/sync-driver.md, Failures).

## Blobs the engine makes again (`M/`, notes/slim-design.md B)
- A commit blob the engine keeps a seal of (`BlobOf::seal`) has no bytes here: `CipherStore::made` (after the engine's store commits, noted with the releases) drops them and writes `M/ b` with the length, so its entries stay servable (`entries::stored_len` is a blob's length either way). Every reader of a blob's bytes goes through one door, `store::blob_bytes` (`Engine::regenerate` first, then the kept bytes); streams' `Ground::bytes` and inbox's `Judge::bytes` are that door behind their traits. Presence checks read `Store::blob_len`.
- `CipherStore::unmade` (in the engine's transaction, before its store commits, as holds are) gives the bytes back, made a last time, wherever an entry names the blob: the engine calls it before what made the blob changes (a part leaving the record, the record or pending copy going, the row going). A seal of a blob nothing names goes without bytes (`Engine::unnamed_blob`; `made` of an unnamed blob feeds `unnamed_feed`, so a fresh encryption never logged, a Want's answer, keeps no seal). `M/` is the engine's to remove: an entry arriving between a blob's last entry going and the engine hearing of it finds `M/` and keeps no bytes.
- A Delete by levels at a node keeping S unread reads a made blob's prefix through the door (`Store::take_delete_made`).

## Own entries
A commit the engine forgets other than by lost access (`sync::bodies::forget`) hands each of its blobs to the door (`CipherStore::forgot`) after the engine's store commits, noted with the releases so a crash finishes it at the next open; `forgot` drops the node's own entries of the blob under every sync ID, untombed. Lost access keeps the engine's blob rows, so its own entries stay, parked, and open again when access returns. Peer entries are left to read's judgement.

## Parked entries (`follow::Parked`, `Awaits`)
The store's form of the one parking rule (notes/sync.md, Arriving early); what an entry awaits and what opens it: follow.rs's `Awaits` and `Parked` docs. Beyond them:
- An entry awaiting S's read secrets opens again too as one of S's things comes to be held whole: a commit thrown out as outside the node closure before S's thing entered it is never pulled again, since its blob is held. An own certificate blob, naming no commit, waits for nothing.
- Awaiting a thing's secrets (`Engine::blob_awaits_secrets`, notes/whole-commits.md Sealed sections), for example: a sibling's account rotation, whose seed's section sits under the removal's D epoch, established only once the rotation's header and list count; an account rotation's part on the account, opening only from D's sync IDs; a commit held from a certificate copy under another sync ID, whose full blob under a link's K holds the target's `created.0` sealed under the target's secrets (the redeemer's join waits on that record).
- Found by the entry feed (which reports removals, so a gone entry unparks), by `follow::Unheld` (`Engine::unheld_feed`/`unpending_feed`/`unnoted_feed`: e.g. a released holder's keying commit its readers judge must-hold, whose entry names no commit and waits for S), and by one pass over S's entries when S becomes read (a raise from kept unread needs nothing more: spec/sync-judgement.md §6).
- Not parked: a held commit's part opened on a thing not held whole. The engine names such a commit for redelivery as the thing comes to be held whole (`apply::wake_entered`, `Engine::take_redeliver`); parking it here would make what an entry awaits move with the closure, which no feed of the store's follows.
- Only the entries parked under a value open again when it moves; a pull anew of S opens every parked entry of S. One that gives something held goes off its peer budget. A thing's key opens nothing at its first settle unless it names a secret, so a part on a thing the node reads nothing of costs a set entry, never a reopen. Pending commits are the engine's to re-examine (notes/sync-facade.md Pending).

## Served heads
`follow::charge_unvouched` (doc comment): a stamp vouches only under S's served head, so at a start and each head move each peer's stamped carrier not verifying under it is charged to its bringer (`EntryRow::brought`, kept for every uncharged peer entry, passed on by `gone`). An honest rotation drops nothing within budget. O(entries of S) per head move.

## Carriers (`follow::Carriers`, doc comment)
The engine keeps a loose record only while a store entry names a blob carrying it, and a pending copy only while carried (notes/node-closure.md, Pending carriers). A blob unnamed also drops the loose records and lists only it carried (`forget_carrier`). A body not kept (past its peer's budget) is released at once (`streams::Ground::unkept`).

## Time
`settle_at` expires entries (no tombstone, no event), ends tombstones and aged stand rows after `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, drops claim sets superseded for `ROLE_KEEP`, sweeps the log's deletion events, and lets the unread sync ID using most give way past the total cap.

## Caps and policy
- `Policy::from_caps` reads each value from `caps^.0`'s `policy` by field name; per-thing caps replace `entries` and `bytes`.
- Caps apply to sync IDs kept unread only, but `memory_bytes`, which also bounds what a reader holds of S's ephemeral commits (`memory_room`; a held one's blob counts at `Engine::memory_charge`, at least its bytes, under every sync ID an entry of it is under, keyed by blob: `set_memory_charge`). Role entries count toward none.
- A memory-part entry or tombstone counts what it holds there (`entries::overhead`, `tomb_overhead`: rows at their bytes and `osis_store::MEM_ROW_COST` each, a held-elsewhere row whether written or not, its blob's place, its log event, `memory::EVENT`/`REMOVAL`), so the memory caps bound what the memory part holds (`Store::memory_held`; at n and 10n by `store::memory_caps_count_what_memory_holds_spec_sync_ids_6`). Durable ones count `ENTRY_OVERHEAD`/`TOMB_OVERHEAD`, an approximation of disk. Uncounted: issues/sync-memory-uncounted.md.
- The deposit budget is the policy's raised to `osis_proto::limits::deposit_budget` for the head's claim-set size. A child step past the caps is taken at most once per `register_every_ms` per sync ID (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Storage caps; why: notes/sync-reasons.md, Nodes).

## What peers can make it keep
- **Tombstones**: a Delete of an entry not held writes nothing, so tombstones never outnumber what was held.
- **Stamp verdicts** (notes/engine.md, Signature verdicts): an entry's row keeps `vouch` (`rows::Vouch`, made only by `rows::check_stamp`/`verify_stamp`), checked once where the entry enters (`log`, `take_stamped`, `take_stamp`). Every later check goes through `Store::stamp_under`: the kept verdict answers when it is that key's, else the stamp is checked and nothing kept (a write would stamp the row and move its readers). So each stamp is checked once per node at its head; a head move to another write key checks each carrier once more.
- **Peer budget**: unstamped entries a peer brought count against its budget. A peer's stamp not verifying under S's head write key is charged as unstamped (and replaces no stamp held), so a forged stamp floods nothing. So what one peer makes pend in the engine is bounded by its own budget and never evicts another's.
- **Inheriting charges** (`gone`, `heir`): at a connection's close, a record's drop or eviction, or a start, each durable entry charged to the peer (and each it brought uncharged) goes to the first peer record, in record ID order, whose stream showed it present (osis-store `records_with_row`) and whose budget it fits; else the entry goes. A record inherits only what its stream lists, the charge fetching it from that peer would have made, so a hostile peer fills an honest one's budget no more than by giving it junk to relay. Why inherit: a duplicate keeps its first bringer's charge, so a stream that passed the entry (the survivor of crossed dials, spec/sync-opens.md §3 One record per pair, or any other peer) would never bring it again. Not inherited: by a recordless connection (`heir` reads records' presence alone; a connection's charges go when it does); ephemeral entries go with their source, relisted at the next join and living at most their drift window.
- **Deposits** per sync ID by the shared deposit budget (notes/sync-inbox.md).

## A reader's store of S
Its served path is what its `sync.0` records (`path::serve`), but a superseded step's claim set is the store's to drop (the `ROLE_KEEP` sweep): one the store lacks that `sync.0` still records is no difference, else every start serves it again for the sweep to drop again (every thing has a superseded step from birth, its genesis step).

Its own entries plus those it pulled:
- one that opened to a commit held here (`Opening::Held`) is kept as it came, so readers pass the writer's blob on and encrypt afresh only where spec/crypto.md §5 requires. Charged to no one when it brought something new; a duplicate of what is held is charged to its bringer unless stamped, so a reader's fresh encryptions of held commits fill only its own peer budget;
- one that did not open, or opened to a commit pending in the engine (`Engine::pends`): streams' `take_body` logs it as the commit's carrier, charged to the peer when unstamped;
- a pulled record or list blob is kept as it came (judged by read's `cert_blobs`); a pulled piece is taken and not kept (`Opening::Taken`).
