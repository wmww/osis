# Sync: store
> Summary: osis-sync's `store` module (core/sync/src/store/): the one ciphertext store per node for every sync ID, read or kept unread; its rows, the engine's ciphertext door, what it follows of the engine, caps and the policy, the bounds on what peers make it keep, and the carriers of pending commits.

Spec: spec/sync-ids.md §5-§6, spec/sync-streams.md §3, spec/sync-inbox.md §3 Deposits, spec/sync-judgement.md §4 (stand state). Overview: notes/sync.md. The underlying store: notes/local-store.md.

## What it owns
Entries (wrap or filler, stamp, deposited flag, own, role, class, expiry; held elsewhere in a row of its own, status's), tombstones with what the entry was (also by blob, `tombstoned_under`: read's forgetting asks whether a delete of a commit's blob is recent under any sync ID), blob bytes once per node, the path per sync ID (steps and claim sets), the node log with a per-sync-ID index (osis-store `nodelog`; a join costs that sync ID's events), usage per sync ID against the caps and the deposit budget, charges against each peer's budget, and the rows a source's record keeps for `read` (stands, deletes seen). Ephemeral entries, their tombstones and bytes and the log's memory part live in memory (`store/memory.rs`, lost at a restart). The row layout is in `store/rows.rs`' module doc. It decrypts nothing and judges no open.

Every operation is one transaction of the underlying store, so a crash keeps all of its rows or none (an entry and its log event, a removal and its tombstone). Every row change stamps, so memos and `Effects` may read it.

## The engine's ciphertext door
`Store` is a cheap `Clone` handle over one locked store and implements `osis_store::CipherHost` (`store/cipher.rs`): the engine holds and releases blob bytes through it, inside the engine's own transactions; sync locks it only between engine calls, so one step never holds both. A blob's bytes stay while the engine or any entry of any sync ID holds them.

## Rows other modules keep
A peer's record holds other modules' rows (osis-store `record_put_row(id, tag, sync_id, key, value)`, or `record_put_wide_row` for a row about the record as a whole), one tag per owning module (`store::rows::tag`): store `b` charges, `s` stands, `d` deletes seen; opens and streams theirs (notes/sync-opens.md, notes/sync-streams.md). They go with the record, a sync ID's also with the sync ID. `with_tx` gives other modules a transaction for their own rows.

## What it follows (`store/follow.rs`, settled first in every round)
- The sync IDs this node syncs (`set::SyncsFollow` over `Store::syncs_feed`; `leave` writes them, `Store::set_holdings`), the caps (`Engine::caps`, `caps^.0`), and for each S read here its served path (`Store::serve_path` from `sync.0`), each an `Effects` value.
- **Parked entries**: an entry whose commit is neither held nor pending here (a peer's that did not open, or opened to a commit thrown out; an own log of a commit forgotten with lost access, which opens again when access returns; an own certificate blob, naming no commit, waits for nothing; certificate blob carriers included: the engine's loose records and lists are in memory), or held with a part missing, on a thing whose read secrets the node holds some of, while a section of its blob did not open (`Engine::blob_awaits_secret`: a sibling's account rotation, whose seed's section sits under the removal's D epoch, established only once the rotation's header and list count; any such part, not only S's body's: an account rotation's part on the account opens only from D's sync IDs; a part on a thing the node reads nothing of would reopen at every start for nothing), is parked under S's read secrets (`Follow::parked`, in memory), found by the store's entry feed (which also reports removals, so a gone entry unparks) and by one pass over S's entries when S becomes read (a start, a grant, a raise from kept unread: S's first read-secret settle then opens them, which is all a raise needs, spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Read and unread move; `flows::a_writer_lowered_to_sync_and_raised_reads_its_own_write`). Each time S's read-secret epochs gain one, only S's parked entries are opened again (notes/sync.md Arriving early); one that gives something held goes off its peer budget. The engine re-examines its pending commits itself (`reexamine`: notes/sync-facade.md Pending), so sync opens nothing again for them.
- Per read S, its served head: a stamp vouches only under the head; otherwise the entry is charged. At a start and each move, `charge_unvouched` charges each peer's stamped carrier (its commit pending, or a loose record) whose stamp no longer verifies to the peer that brought it (`EntryRow::brought`, kept for every uncharged peer entry, passed on by `gone`); past that peer's budget, or with it gone, the entry is not kept (no tombstone; its pending copy goes as its blob goes unnamed). An honest rotation drops nothing within budget. O(entries of S) per head move.
- **Carriers** (pending commits and loose records, spec/commits.md §4 Pending storage): the engine keeps a pending copy, or a loose record, only while a store entry names a blob of it. `Carriers` follows the store's `unnamed_feed` (a blob no entry names any more: removed, expired, shredded, gone with its peer) and calls `Engine::forget_pending` once no blob of the commit is named (`store::release`; a commit that came by no entry, an inbox payload or pairing, names no blob and is left); a body not kept (past its peer's budget) is released at once (`streams::Ground::unkept`); a start sweeps every pending commit once. A blob unnamed also drops the loose records and lists only it carried (`forget_carrier`). A commit held here, or a certificate blob whose loose records were all learned or lists held (`loose_feed`), takes its carriers off their peers' budgets.

`settle_at` expires entries (no tombstone, no event), ends tombstones and aged stand rows after `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, drops claim sets superseded for `ROLE_KEEP`, sweeps the log's deletion events, and lets the unread sync ID using most give way past the total cap.

## Caps and policy
`Policy` (`Policy::from_caps`) reads each value from `caps^.0`'s `policy` by field name, defaults where it names nothing; per-thing caps replace `entries` and `bytes`. Caps apply to sync IDs kept unread only, but `memory_bytes`, which also bounds what a reader holds of S's ephemeral commits (`memory_room`; a held one's blob counts at `Engine::memory_charge`, at least its bytes, under every sync ID an entry of it is under: `set_memory_charge` keys the charge by blob, kept while an entry names it); role entries count toward none. The deposit budget is the policy's raised to `osis_proto::limits::deposit_budget` for the head's claim-set size. A child step past the caps is taken at most once per `register_every_ms` per sync ID (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Storage caps).

## What peers can make it keep
- A Delete of an entry not held writes nothing, so tombstones never outnumber what was held.
- **Stamp verdicts** (notes/engine.md, Signature verdicts): an entry's row keeps `vouch`, the hash of the key its stamp verified under with S, blob, wrap and stamp (`rows::Vouch`, made only by `rows::check_stamp`/`verify_stamp`), checked once where the entry enters: `log` (any stamped entry, against S's served head; a copy of a held one reuses the held verdict), `take_stamped`, `take_stamp` (a peer's new stamp for a held entry, against the head `sync.0` records: the rule of spec/sync-streams.md §4 Applying). Every later check of a held entry's stamp goes through `Store::stamp_under` (read's own-entry, step-parent and deposit checks, `stamp_verifies`, `charge_unvouched`): the kept verdict answers when it is that key's, else the stamp is checked and nothing kept, since a write would stamp the entry's row and move its readers. So each entry's stamp is checked once per node at its head (`store::a_stamp_is_checked_once_per_node_spec_sync_ids_5`); a head move to another write key checks each carrier once more (then it is charged, or restamped, and nothing asks again).
- Unstamped entries a peer brought count against its peer budget (`log` and deposits alike). When its connection closes, its record is dropped or evicted, or at a start (every connection that was charged or brought something: connection numbers start again), `gone` hands each durable entry charged to it (and each it brought uncharged) to the first peer record whose stream showed it present (`heir`: the record's PRESENT row, osis-store `records_with_row`) and whose budget it fits; else the entry goes. A record inherits only what its stream lists (what that peer holds), the charge fetching it from that peer would have made, so a hostile peer fills an honest one's budget no more than by giving it junk to relay; "first" is record ID order. A duplicate keeps its first bringer's charge, so without this a stream that passed the entry (Twin's survivor, any other peer) would never bring it again. Known limits: a recordless connection's presence is in memory (streams' `Known`), out of the store's transaction, so it inherits nothing; ephemeral entries (in memory, charged per source) go with their source, relisted at the next join and living at most their drift window. `log` checks a peer's stamp against S's head write key: one that does not verify is charged as unstamped (and replaces no stamp held), so a forged stamp floods nothing. So what one peer makes pend in the engine, commits and records alike, is bounded by its own peer budget and never evicts another's; what a writer stamped is charged to its bringer once its key is not the head's.
- A record-less connection's stand and delete-seen rows live in memory and age out after `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME` like a record's.
- Deposits per sync ID by the shared deposit budget, which any holder of the deposit key can fill until a delete-key holder clears (spec/sync-inbox.md §4).

## A reader's store of S
Its own entries plus those it pulled: each that opened to a commit held here (`Opening::Held`, kept as it came: read judges it as any entry of the body, so readers pass the writer's blob on and encrypt afresh only where spec/crypto.md §5 requires; charged to no one when it brought something new, else, a duplicate of what is held, charged to its bringer unless stamped, so a reader's fresh encryptions of held commits fill only its own peer budget), that did not open, or that opened to a commit pending in the engine (`Engine::pends`: unjudgeable, an epoch or pages away, drift; streams' `take_body` logs them as the commit's carrier, charged to the peer when unstamped). A pulled piece, inbox blob or record blob is taken and not kept (`Opening::Taken`).

## Reasons
- One store for every sync ID, read or kept unread, and a shredding one: a node keeping S unread may read other sync IDs on the same machine, so wraps and blobs surviving a delete in freed pages would break spec/requirements.md Deletion (notes/local-store.md).
- A store failure closes every connection and restarts the modules from the store (notes/sync-driver.md); nothing here assumes a module's memory survived.
