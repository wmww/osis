# Rotation model: ambiguities and findings
> Summary: Places where spec/crypto.md §3–§5, spec/sync.md §2/§5 and spec/permissions.md §3–§4 left a choice or failed a checked property. R1–R7 are resolved by spec edits and the model follows the new text; the two residues of issues/reseal-triggers.md reproduce as expected failures (`MC_fork_reorder.cfg` and, without a fork, `MC_reorder.cfg`; `MC_drop_refresh.cfg`); R8 and R9 are new (R9 is a TLC counterexample, `MC_hist.cfg`).

## Resolved (model updated, rerun)
- **R1. Re-seal raced a removal.** permissions §3 and commits §2: the removal and the rotation it requires are one membership commit, so no node holds that rotation without the departure; a re-seal is decided after a sync pass that fetched everything; boxing a secret is encrypting under it (crypto §4), so a re-sealer that knows a departure re-seals only at covering epochs. Model: `Remove` is a rotation carrying the removal (`rem`), `Recv` learns it, and the remover uploads it before anything else. The original trace is gone; `MC_safety.cfg` (one server, log order) passes `NoLeak` and `DepartedLocked`. Out of order, residue 1 remains, with or without a fork: below.
- **R2. A member past a dropped claim set was never reached.** permissions §4: the stranded heartbeat is also placed when a claim on the way is refused, by deposit, wrapped under the newest held secret; crypto §3: refreshing the deposit seed is a MAY. `MC_drop.cfg` passes `CurrentOpens`. With the seed refreshed by every rotation, `MC_drop_refresh.cfg` fails as expected (residue 2 below).
- **R3. A re-sealed member or grantee could not read older epochs.** crypto §4: a grant or re-seal boxing e re-writes e's history box. Model: `InboxRecv` gives `{e} ∪ ep[e].hist`. `MC_grant.cfg` (grant after epoch 0, raced by a rotation) passes `ReadsAll` and `CurrentOpens`.
- **R4. "Newer than the head" across a fork.** sync §5: re-registration by an admin that holds the head's epochs and a newer one; unchanged reading (compare by (n, nonce)).
- **R5. Encrypting under the newest epoch when not held.** crypto §5: "the newest current read epoch whose secret it holds"; a node holding no current secret places only its stranded heartbeat, under the newest secret it holds. Matches the model.
- **R6. Is re-sealing encrypting under?** crypto §4: "sealing or wrapping a box carrying its secret" is encrypting under. Model: `Reseal` and `Grant` only at covering epochs; `RotateForced` also fires for a re-seal blocked by a non-covering epoch.
- **R7. Claim set after a re-seal.** sync §2: "a member re-sealed later opens by sync key". Matches the model.

## Residue 1: re-seal of a removed member by a node that lacks the removal rotation (TLC: `MC_fork_reorder.cfg`, `MC_reorder.cfg`, DepartedLocked fails, expected)
- issues/reseal-triggers.md residue 1. Trace (8 states): a2 rotates e1 (boxing m1); a1 removes m1 in its rotation e2, concurrently; a1 gets e1 from peer a2 and rotates e3 superseding e1 and e2 (no box for m1). a2 gets e3 from peer a1 before e2 (e3 is wrapped under e1, the newest it supersedes; e2's secret comes in e3's history box, but not e2's commit and its removal). To a2, m1 is current and has no box at e3; a2 re-seals m1 at e3 and m1 holds e3.
- Needs a reorder (or a second source) and a partial state that still counts as "a sync pass that fetched everything" (R8).
- **It needs no fork** (TLC: `MC_reorder.cfg`, DepartedLocked fails, 10 states): a1 removes m1 in rotation e1, then rotates again to e2 (no box for m1, no departure carried: only e1 carries it) and registers e2 before uploading either. a2 heartbeats stranded and a1 re-seals it at e2 (with e2's history box, so a2 holds e1's secret but not e1's commit). a2 gets e2 from peer a1, then completes a pass at the server, which has nothing yet. To a2, m1 is current with no box at e2, so a2 re-seals m1 at e2. The spec's argument "a rotation omitting a current member means its author did not know the member" holds only for the rotation that carries the removal, not for later rotations by nodes that know it. With one server in log order (`MC_safety.cfg`) the remover uploads e1 first and the pass rule closes it.
- Option (beyond the issue's): every rotation carries (or names) the departures it covers that its author knows of since the last epoch it supersedes, or option (b)/(c) of the issue.

## Residue 2: refused stranded heartbeat (TLC: `MC_drop_refresh.cfg`, CurrentOpens fails, expected)
- issues/reseal-triggers.md residue 2. With the deposit seed refreshed at each rotation, a member that never read the newest `/keys` cannot deposit its stranded heartbeat, holds a box at every epoch (no missing-box trigger) and is never re-sealed.

## R9. A secret got by re-seal before its rotation commit leaks through a history box (new; TLC: `MC_hist.cfg`, DepartedLocked fails)
- Text: crypto §4 history box "of every distinct earlier `secret_r` the rotator holds, forks included"; "Encrypting under an epoch means ... sealing or wrapping a box carrying its secret"; "An epoch whose rotation commit is pending or invalid ... is never encrypted under"; sync §5 re-registration happens before any upload, so the head can name an epoch whose rotation commit is not at the server yet; permissions §4 a member that reached the head but cannot open its epoch places a stranded heartbeat; permissions §3 re-seal on a stranded heartbeat.
- Trace (10 states, one server, log order): a1 removes m1 in rotation e1 and registers e1 before uploading it. a2 reaches the head, cannot open e1, heartbeats stranded; a1 re-seals a2 at e1. a2 now holds e1's secret but not e1's commit, so it does not know m1 left. a2 rotates (e2, a fork with e1) boxing m1, and e2's history box carries e1's secret. m1 is re-sealed by a2 (stranded at the head) or simply downloads e2, and opens e1.
- The leak needs no reorder, only the window between registering a step and uploading its rotation.
- Model: `HistApplied = TRUE` (every other instance) puts in a history box only secrets whose rotation commit the rotator applied, reading "pending" as also covering a commit not yet received; `MC_hist.cfg` checks the literal text (`FALSE`).
- Options: (a) say so: a secret whose rotation commit the node has not applied is not boxed, in a history box or elsewhere, and its holder does not rotate over it (history boxes of compacted chains need care: a member holding only a `/secrets` snapshot has no rotation commits); (b) a re-seal carries the rotation commit of each epoch it boxes (or its departures), so the recipient learns them with the secret; (c) a remover uploads the rotation before registering the step (contradicts "re-register before any upload"). (b) is the smallest.

## R8. "A sync pass of the actor's labels that fetched everything" relative to what (new; assumed: one source)
- Text: permissions §3 "it decides after a sync pass of the actor's labels that fetched everything".
- Everything at one server, at every server in `/label`, or also from member peers (sync §5 lets a member peer serve a label)? A node can receive a rotation from one source (a peer, another server) and then complete a pass at a source that lacks the removal rotation. Model: a pass at the server, or (`InOrder = FALSE`) at any online member peer, counts; this is what lets residue 1 happen at one server. Reading it as "every server the label lists, and nothing applied from elsewhere before the pass" narrows residue 1 to servers the remover has not reached yet.
