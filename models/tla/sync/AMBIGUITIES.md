# Sync convergence model: ambiguities and findings
> Summary: Places where spec/sync.md §5–§7 and spec/commits.md §5 left a choice or failed a checked property. S1–S15 are resolved by spec edits or accepted limits (notes/sync.md) and the model follows the text; every instance passes. S16 is a minor reading of the second-round text.

## Resolved (model updated, rerun clean)
- **S1. A purge compacted away never reached a late node.** commits §5: a commit carrying a purge request MUST NOT carry `expires` and its blobs are kept at least until `wall_ms(ts) + MEMBERSHIP_KEEP`, whatever its deltas' bounds. `purge_reach.cfg` now passes `PurgeReachesAll` (within MEMBERSHIP_KEEP; see S15).
- **S2. Absolute keep-at-most stranded live writes.** commits §5: live writes stay in the node's state after the drop; a body with live writes is deleted at a label only while another entry carries them; snapshot deltas take the schema's default bounds, never a commit's. sync §7: a right holder that must delete such a body and finds no carrier uploads a covering snapshot first. `mostabs.cfg` passes `NoLiveLoss`, `LiveKept`, `Clean` (but see S11).
- **S3. Must-hold and must-not-hold overlap.** sync §6: "A body meeting a must-not-hold condition is must-not-hold whatever else holds of it."
- **S4. Strict subset against a superset that is itself must-not-hold.** Text unchanged (literal). The window in which a node that has not applied a purge re-uploads the purged body closes once it gets the purge commit, which commits §5 now keeps MEMBERSHIP_KEEP; `purge.cfg` passes `Clean` and `Quiesce`.
- **S5. Which deletions wait.** sync §7: carriers are a covering snapshot, a blob of the commit holding a superset of the deltas, or an identical body.
- **S6. What a reader relays.** sync §6: "the stamped must-hold entries it holds that the label lacks".
- **S7. "Dropping them once".** sync §6: once per body (commit and delta set).
- **S8. Header-only rewrite.** commits §5: a node dropping a body for a purge keeps the commit as a blob without the purged deltas, header-only when none remain. Model: any node does so (was: writers only).
- **S9. Coverage of a body with no delta.** commits §5: "A body holding no delta is covered."
- **S10. Who must snapshot.** sync §7 (snapshot before deleting a body with live writes that nothing carries) and commits §5 (re-snapshot after a purge). The model makes only these snapshot writes fair; `mostabs.cfg` and `purge.cfg` pass `Clean` with compaction unfair.
- **S11. A body past its absolute keep-at-most before it reached the label** (was `mostabs_serve.cfg`, `Serves` failed: the live write ended in one node's state with no body or snapshot anywhere). commits §5 keep at most: "A node that may write the aspect and holds no snapshot covering a body with live writes writes one before dropping the body". Model: `Drop` waits for a held covering snapshot (`NeedsSnapToDrop`) and `SnapDue` makes that snapshot write fair. `mostabs.cfg` now also checks `Serves` and passes; `mostabs_serve.cfg` is gone.
- **S12. Carrier checks race.** sync §7: an identical body carries only a duplicate with a higher blob ID ("the lower waits for another carrier"); covered-equal snapshots: the compactor drops the higher commit ID. Model: `Carrier` takes a strict superset or a lower-`v` identical body. Deletion still updates every view at once and every scenario has one snapshot commit, so the race itself and covered-equal snapshots are not exercised.
- **S13. Timed deletes of bodies with live writes.** commits §5 at most D after replaced: the delete timed at the bound goes out "after the snapshot of spec/sync.md §7 when the body still has live writes". Model: `SendTimed` (the replacer, once per entry, timed at first death plus D, after a covering snapshot is under the label when needed); `SnapDue` fires for it. New `timed_live.cfg` (two-delta body, one write stays live) passes `NoLiveLoss`, `Serves`, `Clean`, `Quiesce`.
- **S14. A header-only rewrite is never must-hold.** Accepted as written (notes/sync.md): headers persist at the nodes that applied the commit, not at the label. Model literal.
- **S15. A node offline longer than MEMBERSHIP_KEEP never applies a purge.** Accepted limit (notes/sync.md); not checked (time stays below `PurgeKeep`).

## S16. Which local drops need the snapshot first (reading)
- Text: commits §5, in the keep-at-most bullet: "A node that may write the aspect and holds no snapshot covering a body with live writes writes one before dropping the body". Read literally it covers every drop, including a strict subset (its writes are in the superset) and a purged body (its live writes are in the rewrite it keeps).
- Model: only drops past keep-at-most, the one drop reason that leaves the live writes in no held blob (expiry kills every write of the body). The literal reading would make a purge drop wait for a snapshot. Option: say "a body with live writes no other held blob carries".
