# Every reader logs its own encryption of every durable body
> Summary: a reader keeps no entry of a pulled durable blob whose commit it holds (`Opening::Taken`), so read's Own store finds no held blob and logs a fresh encryption of each must-hold body: every reader adds one more blob per commit, and a node keeping S unread stores one per reader (stamped or deposited).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, read, streams

spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store and spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on: a held blob of the commit goes on verbatim, a fresh encryption only where none is held. In osis-sync, `streams::pull::take_body` opens a pulled durable blob and keeps nothing when its commit is held (`Opening::Taken`); read then judges the body must-hold with no entry under S (`Act::Log`), and `log_own` finds no stored bytes to copy (`blob_len` none), so it calls `seal_body`. Measured in the ephemeral `three()` world (node 0 writes, node 1 keeps S unread, node 2 reads and writes): node 2 holds only its own encryption of node 0's commit, and node 1 holds both. Ephemeral commits do not double: their entry is kept (`Opening::Memory`).

Tried: keeping the pulled entry as it came when its commit is held (an `Opening::Held` in place of `Memory`, the stamp added in place where the node writes S). It removes the second blob, but breaks `flows::a_removed_writers_last_write_reads_alike_at_every_holder_spec_sync_judgement_3` (a body dropped as invalid, then valid again, is no longer fetched again: its kept entry's drop leaves a tombstone) and `pieces::a_small_edit_to_a_large_value_costs_its_new_pieces` (pieces judged grow with the tree: 46 at n, 132 at 10n). Keeping it only while `blob_taken` (held, not pending, nothing missing) or as `own` changed neither. So the fix touches how read treats an entry it did not log: which entries count as its own log (deposits, leave, restamping on a head move), what a must-not drop of a pulled entry leaves, and why a kept writer's blob makes the pieces judgement walk the tree.
