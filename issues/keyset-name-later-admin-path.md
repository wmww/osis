# A grant's names are unreadable one level up
> Summary: Under frozen coverage (notes/epoch-establishment-study.md) a grant of holder actor N on A carries the granter's names for N (departures inside N, N's keyset) under A's read secret, so an A-level boxer must hold them before boxing N. A boxer of A on U (one level up) is no reader of A and never sees them: it judges A's keyset knowing nothing about N. Question: is that a hole in NoLeak's "never held a role" half, or an accepted residual?
> Tags: security, keys, engine

Filed on the devbox `keyset-names` branch (c0b63e8e); this copy carries the answer. The branch text was not available here; assumed rule: names tied to the grant, readable by every reader of A's label (the boxer of N is an A-admin, not a holder of N), the named commits placed with the grant, a grant that leaves the actor out of every role refused, history-box names under the read secret.

## Answer (devbox fable)
Scenario assumed: D leaves N (C_N); N holds admin on A; A holds a role on U. Before C_N, D reached U through N → A. Y, an admin of U and no reader of A, seals U's epoch to A at A's newest valid keyset at Y. Y holds the N → A and D → N edges (D's chain certificates when D wrote on U's label as A) but not C_N.

**1. Which boxers the "never held a role" half binds.** Only boxers that hold, or had to hold before boxing, a rotation or grant made knowing D's exclusion. NoLeak is a statement about knowledge chains, not omniscience: the study's invariant is "no box carrying s_e is sealed to a key D can derive, for every departure named by a commit the sealer had to hold". A boxer required to hold nothing naming C_N is outside it; its box at a D-reachable keyset is the ignorant-boxer class of notes/sync.md Limits (an incomplete view boxes a departed holder; healed by the next knowing rotation). State the second half as: a D that never held a role on A never learns s_e through a box whose maker held, or had to hold, a grant or rotation made knowing D's exclusion. Against Y it holds once some U-level box or epoch names C_N.

What Y can do wrong is narrow. An honest A-admin never produces a D-reachable keyset of A: it boxes N only holding the grant's names (so C_N), and one lacking the grant does not box N at all. So A's keyset at Y is D-reachable only if D forged it (an A-rotation or a bare `keys.0` write, valid at Y because Y lacks C_N and D's path to A is in Y's certificates). Then D reads what Y boxes: U content under the epoch Y uses. Frozen coverage still guarantees no U-writer that knew C_N has content there: C_N is a departure inside A relevant to U, unnamed by U's epoch, so every knowing U-writer rotated first, and Y cannot establish that epoch without C_N. Exposed: content by U-writers ignorant of C_N, written after it; D had read U before. The lost-device window.

**2. Is there a simple sound rule?** No rule inside U's epochs can make Y know: nothing Y must hold references C_N until a U-level rotation or box is made by a node holding it; making the grant's names readable above A means plaintext names (A's whole closure churn visible to anyone holding A's certificates) or re-encryption under U's secret by a node holding both, which is the propagation below with more machinery. Candidates checked:
- Y seals A only at the keyset the current U box to A names, or one set by a departure commit of A: a forged "removal of Z + rotation" by D is a departure commit valid at Y. Unsound.
- Y holds every grant's names for every edge into A it holds: under A's secret; Y cannot read them. Dead.
- The grantor places the grant's named commits under every label A holds a role on: Y is not required to hold them (nothing it holds names them); a withheld copy leaves Y where it was. Liveness help only.

Recommendation: **accepted residual, stated as such**, with the existing propagation made to fit frozen coverage. permissions §4's exception already lets a node that may not rotate U place, under a U read epoch that does not cover a departure inside a holder, that departure's revoke with the chain's current `keys.0` commits, "so U's rotators learn of them". Under frozen coverage "does not cover" reads "does not name": an A-admin holding C_N that syncs U sees U's epoch unnamed for it and places C_N there; the next U-rotator names it; Y then cannot establish the new epoch without C_N, and with it D's forgery is invalid. Add to spec/crypto.md §4 one sentence: "An epoch's names bind only nodes that establish it or box a holder it names; a departure inside a holder actor that no current epoch or box of U names is covered by nothing of U, and a node boxing that holder before a rotation of U names it may seal to a keyset forged by the departed party; U-writers that know it rotate first, so only content by writers that did not know is exposed." Record the window in notes/sync.md Limits beside the compaction residue.

Difference from the study's compaction residual: the one-level-up window needs a forgery (honest A-admins cannot produce a D-reachable keyset of A under the grant-names rule); the compaction residual does not. Both heal on the next knowing rotation; both depend on §3's placement MUST reaching U's label when the remover tracks it.
