# Compaction by one writer starts an upload/delete storm
> Summary: After a writer compacts an aspect and drops the covered deltas, it projects a commit that also touched an uncompacted aspect into a smaller body (the live deltas only) and deletes the old blob as `Dropped`; a co-writer that has not dropped those deltas still projects the whole body as must-hold and re-uploads it (fresh encryption, the old ID is tombstoned), which the first deletes again, and the co-writer deletes the smaller blob as a strict subset. The two never settle (spec/sync.md §6: no ping-pong; §7: every right holder drops covered bodies as it sees the snapshot).
> Tags: sync, node, tests

- Seen in the simulator: `osis_sim::replay("compaction", 1)` (tier 1 test `compaction`, ignored): writers A and B, B compacts `/s/tags`, A `/s/reg` and `/s/map`; the label at the server alternates between `[(0,0),(1,0)]` (`/s/tags` + `/s/count`) and `[(1,0)]` (`/s/count`) blobs of the same commits, thousands of messages within one settle ("message storm").
- Likely parts: (1) `compact` drops covered bodies only on the compacting node; spec/sync.md §7 has every other right holder do the same once it sees the snapshot; (2) a node's must-hold body should not be re-uploaded while a blob of the commit holding a superset or subset of its live deltas is there and another node's drop explains it (the two views of the projection differ by the dropped deltas).
