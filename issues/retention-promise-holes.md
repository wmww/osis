# Where `author` and `nobody` bodies still leave without the right
> Summary: Owner call. Two routine paths remove bodies a purge set protects, both as the spec is written: another's snapshot makes the author's own node drop a superseded `author` body (no keep-at-least), and an admin taking a thing out of its group deletes its `nobody` and `author` bodies at that label's hosts. Neither is a purge, but both defeat "you know things aren't deleted".
> Tags: owner-call, spec, permissions, node-sync

1. **Supersession under `author`.** spec/commits.md §5 lets a right holder drop a covered body and spec/sync.md §7 makes every right holder do it on any snapshot it holds under the label. So any writer can overwrite an author's value and compact, and the author's own node drops the old body and deletes it at hosts (`deletion::author_only_compaction_spec_sync_7`; a writer's snapshot takes the same path as the admin's there). The author asked for nothing. `keep_at_least: forever` stops it; `nobody` is unaffected. Live writes stay in the snapshot; only superseded history goes.
   Options: (a) keep: `by` says who may delete, `keep_at_least` how long history stays, and only their pair promises keeping (notes/permissions.md says so of `author` with at least forever); document it in the setting's meaning. (b) Under `author`, a right holder drops as covered only by a snapshot of its own actor: one check in `drop_covered`; storage then waits on the author's own compaction, and a demoted or gone author's history stays for good, which is what the setting says. The `by` set of the other classes stays as it is: they hold an admin or writer who can act anyway. Lean: (b).
2. **Leaving a group.** spec/sync.md §3, §6: a body of a thing out of the closure is outside the required set, so a label delete key holder deletes it whatever its retention (seen with `nobody`: gone at the host, both nodes keep their copies). If the thing is under no other label a new device cannot get it. A body with `keep_at_least` goes too, though commits §5 words that bound as "the labels of its things' groups". Options: keep (a label holds what belongs to it), or hold bodies with no right holder until another label holds them. Moves re-import, so they are not affected.
