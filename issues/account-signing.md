# Account signing: what is left
> Summary: Outsiders verify a commit by its account's signing chain (epochs whose keys every node of the account holds); the node's own signature travels sealed to the account's nodes. Built and specified (spec/permissions.md §5.4-§5.5, spec/crypto.md §9; notes/engine-validity-epochs.md). Left: a re-added node's takes moving with seeds it gains, own cover's scan cost, commit growth.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: perm, crypto, sync, spec

Stage A4 (notes/redesign-plan.md) deletes the signing chain and epochs; this closes there. Take it up before only if the model sends the redesign back.

Model: roles-model `custody` and `epochs` (README there). Decided design and rejected alternatives: spec/permissions.md §5.4-§5.5, notes/engine-validity-epochs.md.

## Left
- **Takes move with seeds gained** (roles-model `custody::random_worlds_hold_large`, world 0..2000 of `OSIS_ROLES_START=0`: `flip`, 1 world): T, removed by C, holds X's revocation x of B (signed at C's removal's epoch, whose seed T lacks, so x is minted by no one there) before A's removal of X, and takes A's removal. A re-adds T; the newcomer box hands T C's epoch's seed, x's minter reads as X, and x, held before A's removal, is taken first in held order: T drops A's removal for x and ends on X's side. Revocations are taken "in the order it came to hold them" (spec/permissions.md §5.4), and a rotation whose minter becomes readable later is placed by when it arrived. Likely rule: a revocation is placed by when it came to stand there (held with its chain and its minter read), so new seeds never reorder what was taken. Needs a model change first (then engine and spec); the engine differential has not reached it.
- **Own cover's scan**: `signing::own_covers` reads every aspect with a held snapshot or a forgotten commit stamped since the oldest epoch the rotation closes opened (the first epoch: every such aspect), each one's state, at every plain rotation: O(those aspects of the node), not O(P's). Rare (removals, pairings, answers); index carried-only writes by author if it shows.
- **Commit growth**: signing as an account grows a one-field commit by 131 B over a key actor's (the inner signature 95 B, the epoch's ID 32 B, heads; `an_accounts_one_field_commit_grows_by_its_signing_fields_only_spec_wire_4`), past the 100 B once aimed at. Only shrinking the inner signature (its signing node's tid, its tag) would reach it.
- **Thing list cost**, measured (`a_removals_lists_cost_a_path_per_thing_and_an_id_per_edit_spec_permissions_5_5`): about 1.3 KiB per thing a removal lists (its 32-hash path 1,088 B) plus 34 B per commit, the same at 2 and 20 things. That is the shape spec/requirements.md Cost asks for (per thing edited, by its edits, nothing by history); sharing path prefixes within a list blob would save only about 34 B per level of the log₂(things) the paths share, so it is not built.
- Where node cards and `reach.0` are held is the reference graph's (spec/sync-set.md §1: a node thing's card under D's sync ID).
