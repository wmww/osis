# Account signing: what is left
> Summary: Outsiders verify a commit by its account's signing chain (epochs whose keys every node of the account holds); the node's own signature travels sealed to the account's nodes. Built and specified (spec/permissions.md §5.4-§5.5, spec/crypto.md §9; notes/engine-validity-epochs.md). Left: own cover at the account level, per-rotation re-judging cost and listing gaps, the API tests, and the thing-list cost check.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: perm, crypto, sync, spec

Model: roles-model `epochs` (README there). Decided design and rejected alternatives: spec/permissions.md §5.4-§5.5, notes/engine-validity-epochs.md.

## Left
- **Own cover at the account level** (spec/permissions.md §5.5 Own cover, marked target): before its rotation the rotator snapshots each aspect where a live write of P stamped since the oldest closed epoch opened stands only through a cover (`retention::snapshot::carried_only`), signed at the closed open epoch, and its lists name them; lands in `flows::signing::rotate_in`. Tests: the rotation names what it holds and its own cover for what it forgot; an outsider reads the forgotten writes from it; a later rotation writes none for epochs already closed.
- **Cost and gaps in judgment**: `refresh_epochs` re-judges every held commit of the account at every older epoch on each rotation (a new closer changes each epoch's `Closed` set): O(held commits of the account) per rotation at every reader. Not built: listings past the rotator's overflow cutoff and the listing bound; a witness answering again when what it holds changes; "every commit of a re-added node" in listings; merges authorized by inner signature beside the roster side. The cutoff compares receipt wall ms strictly: a commit received in the same ms as the earliest closer is named only by its author's listing.
- **One cutoff for witnesses** (coordinator's decision, not yet modelled): a witness cuts at the earliest counting closer it holds on the rotation's line, as lists and listings do. Model it in the next step that touches witnesses.
- **API tests** through `osis`: an outsider sees the account as author and cannot open the inner signature, a sibling can; a removed node's write after removal does not show where the removal is held; an offline sibling's earlier write shows after it syncs; a removed node opens no box sealed to the account after removal; two siblings' role commits at one `ts` have distinct departs marks; a one-field commit grows by at most 100 B. Most have an engine twin in `rotation::tests_signing` / `tests_account`.
- **Thing list cost**: each thing list carries a 1 KiB path, so a removal costs about 1 KiB per thing P touched since its last rotation plus 32 B per commit; measure, and if it misses Cost, share path prefixes within a list blob.
- Where node cards and `reach.0` are held is the reference graph's (issues/reference-graph.md: a node thing's card under D's sync ID).
