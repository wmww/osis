# Sign as the account; no per-node data in groups
> Summary: Build plan (design decided, modelled in roles-model `epochs`): outsiders verify a commit by its account's signing key, which every node of the account holds; the node's own signature travels sealed to the account's nodes. Groups carry no per-node data. The account is a key actor with rotating epochs; its node list is a private devices actor; removing a node rotates the epoch, and what survives a closed epoch follows the demoter-frontier rule with addenda. Eight steps, models first. Prerequisite of issues/net-rework.md.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: perm, crypto, sync, spec, tess-call

## Today
Every commit is signed by a node key, checked against the node's chain to its account. So each node places its chain certificates and node card (`keys.0`, `node.0`, `reach.0`) under every label it syncs, plus `seen.0` heartbeats: per (node, label), about 13 KB once and 2.4 MB a day under a 1000-member label (notes/chat-cases.md, footnote 44), redone under every label when a node is added or its card changes.

## Principle
To everyone outside account P, P is its **signing chain**: epochs, each a key every node of P holds, each rotation signed at the epoch it rotates (a merge also at each fork it joins). A commit of P counts while its epoch is open, and an epoch is open only where it descends from every counting rotation the reader holds: a rotation closes every epoch not descending from it, its own and every one made beside it, which may box the node it removes (the signing twin of the key level's covering, notes/crypto.md: an epoch covers the removals on its line and is trusted only where it covers every one known). Closing an epoch ends the standing of what was signed at it, exactly as a demotion ends a holder's standing on a thing. What survives an ended standing is one rule at both levels: (1) what the ender had received (a demotion's frontier; a rotation's list); (2) what other writers of the thing had received before the earliest ending (the demotion's `ts`; the earliest rotation closing the epoch) (addenda, spec/permissions.md §5.2); (3) the party's own later word where it stands again (a re-grant; a listing at an open epoch). Nothing admits a commit by its author's `ts`; nothing reads the inner (node) signature, which only guides what P's honest nodes write.

## Design (decided)
- **Signing**: a commit's `author` is the account plus its signing epoch, signed with that epoch's key. Beside `signature` it carries the inner signature (node tid, Ed25519 over the commit ID), sealed under a secret only P's nodes hold. Outside P, validation is the epoch rule then P's roles as any actor's (§5); acting through other actors goes through their rosters (readable by co-readers).
- **Account shape**: outside, P is a key actor with epochs (per epoch one seed, deriving signing, KEM and role secrets as a key actor's seed does; the tid commits to the first signing key); admin on itself; no roster. Inside, its epoch seeds, inner-signature secret and node grants live on its **devices actor** D, read only by P's nodes; D's roster is the node list, P is D's admin, D is never a judgment input. Its **profile** is an ordinary thing P is first admin of, read by contacts. Teams, bots and shared identities stay ordinary actors with readable rosters and act through a member account; only accounts sign. A node names its account, not itself, as first admin.
- **Removal is the rotation**: removing node X from D and rotating P's epoch are one role commit; X is not boxed the new seed.
- **Epoch rule**: a rotation's line is it and every rotation it descends from, through the epoch it is signed at and the forks it names. A counting rotation closes every epoch that does not descend from it: its own, those it supersedes, and every epoch minted beside it. Commit c of P at epoch e counts while e is open at the reader; once e is closed, c counts iff a counting list or listing names it, or an addendum on the thing vouches for it. It filters before §5; never by `ts` or counters.
- **One cutoff**: a list, listing or addendum names another party's commit at epoch e only if the lister received it before the earliest rotation closing e (in its view; a witness answering rotation r: a closer on r's line); its own commits always. Nobody acts on a rotation it cannot verify: it answers, merges out or sides with one only once it holds its chain, and lists a commit only once it holds its epoch's chain.
- **Rotation's list**: the rotator's receipts of P's commits at the closed epochs (any kind, no ephemeral, less covered commits), one part per label the rotator opens, each naming the commits with an entry under that label, by the cutoff. Within a part, per-thing lists are chain certificates and travel where their commits go. Nothing waits on a part: the rotator uploads a label's part with the rotation, and a commit whose part has not arrived reads as unlisted until it does (a reader cannot know which Merkle-hidden parts exist). A label the rotator did not open gets no part: P's commits there count only once listed or vouched.
- **Chains travel**: every node of P places under each label it opens the rotations and per-thing lists it holds for things there, as the certificates its own commits and listings need (they are signed at epochs descending from the rotation). A label no honest node of P opens any more never learns the rotation: the unaware-reader limit.
- **Listings**: after each rotation it holds, a node of P writes, per label it opens, a role commit at an open epoch naming what nothing it holds names: its own commits at closed epochs (judged by inner signature), others' by the cutoff (the rotator's own overflow among them), and every commit of a re-added node. With no open epoch (concurrent rotations close each other's) it waits for the merge: a listing at a closed epoch counts only once listed, and no merge's cutoff admits it. A listing is an ordinary commit; the next rotation lists it by ID. Listings compose by union.
- **Addenda against a rotation**: as built for demotions, with the rotation in the removal's place and the cutoff above; every writer of a thing answers every rotation of P whose chain it holds, again whenever what it holds changes; plain content only. Witnesses may be other accounts' writers.
- **Covered commits**: lists, listings and addenda leave out commits the lister's own compaction would drop (covered by a snapshot it holds valid), at both levels. Without this the account level is O(history) at every reader.
- **Bound**: per rotation and label, one ID per uncompacted commit of P there. Role-class and purge-request commits keep today's list bound; plain content past it goes into the rotator's later listings.
- **Demoting an account** on a thing: today's frontier, addenda, trust set and disputes, author an account; independent of the epoch rule.
- **Forks**: rotations form a DAG. Concurrent rotations fork; both count, each closing the other's epoch until a merge. A merge names the fork rotations it accepts and is signed at each one's epoch too (only a holder of a fork's epoch can accept it; one signature per named fork, merges only); one it supersedes unnamed is rejected with everything below it, if the merge counts. A line is contested only from below: a rotation signed at e rejects nothing on a line through e (a line holding a rotation signed at e). So a removed node cannot void its removal where the owner's merge is held, only fork; before it arrives, a fork from a sibling's epoch rejects the removal at that reader, as the owner's merge rejects a removed node's fork (the two DAGs are one with the roles swapped, so no rule outsiders can evaluate tells them apart): the fork window. Mutually rejecting lines are an account-level dispute, settled per node by its trust set; default the line holding the first-received contested rotation; P's nodes side with the line D's roster authorizes and merge out every fork whose chain they hold (so the merge is signed at the fork's root).
- **Other node names**: order key `(ts, account, commit ID)`; session dots `Session(account tid, random nonce)`; departs mark `(ts, author, blind)`; trust-set seeds by actors and signing-chain lines. Nodes stay key actors for D's boxes, the inner signature and connections between own nodes.
- **Leaves groups**: node chain certificates, node cards, their reader deposits, heartbeats, `reach.0`. `stranded` goes to a connected holder that may re-seal, else a message to the group's admins' inboxes; suspect lists go over connections; `last_sync` is the node's own record; `reach.0` lives under the account's own sync ID; serving nodes that cannot read are listed on the account's profile (issues/net-one-peer.md).
- **Stays**: per-sync-ID write stamps; link keys sign as themselves.

**Model**: `core/test/roles-model/src/epochs.rs` (`-- N epochs`, `-- 0 epochs-exh`; README there): one account, things in one or two labels, per-label parts as per-thing lists, listings, witnesses, successive removals, a re-add, the part bound, optional admin demotion, forks, thieves, siblings rotating before a removal reaches them (boxing the removed node), snapshots that may fall. 1M random worlds and 2.38M exhaustive (family C: a sibling rotating after a removal it has not received): no safety, liveness, monotonicity or removal-stands failure. Counted limits: a removed node's fork counts until the owner's merge arrives (from a sibling's epoch it rejects the removal meanwhile; its own epoch is closed wherever the removal is held); an unaware sibling's concurrent rotation names the removed node's later writes it received first; readers without the removal (or its chain) count the removed node's writes; a hostile write stays visible through a witness's valid snapshot (today's stated limit). Honest cost: concurrent rotations close each other's epochs at a reader holding both, so writes on either fork read invalid there until the merge and the authors' listings at the merged epoch; another party's commit at a sibling's epoch received only after the removal counts by its author's listing alone (lost if the author is removed first, as any unlisted write at a closed epoch).

**Rejected**: one account-wide list (shows every label the IDs in others); lists as separate commits after the rotation (P's content reads invalid until they arrive, on every removal); closing per label (a removed node writes on wherever P's nodes have not synced); listing every commit (O(history)); later word as addenda only (sibling role writes would need re-issuing); rotations rejecting siblings at their own epoch (a removed node could void its removal); closing only what a rotation supersedes (a sibling's concurrent epoch boxed to the removed node stays open); a rule that stops a fork rooted in a closed epoch from rejecting the removal (the same rule stops the owner's merge rejecting a removed node's fork: the DAGs are one); merges named without the fork's signature (a removed node names its removal and escapes its closure; the post-merge contest stays a dispute); revealing node marks or group signatures (every signature shows its node, or pairing crypto); per-node subkeys certified to readers (per-node data in every label).

## Settled design items
- **Role on D**: any grant on D makes a node a node of P; D's read role opens P's epoch seeds. Every write as P is P's and the inner signature is never judged, so a finer role on D could not be enforced. A node acting for several accounts holds a grant on each one's D and signs each commit as one of them; accounts rotate independently; no further rule.
- **Unlinkability scope**: outside the account, readers see which account wrote each change, never which device, nor the device list. They can still group changes made in one session (shared nonce), tell that two concurrent changes came from different sessions, and see when a device was removed (a rotation); peers see which connection sent what (network metadata, as stated today). Requirements wording below.
- **Inner-signature size**: 95 B (15 B tid, 64 B signature, 16 B tag; key `kdf("inner", secret, commit ID)`, zero nonce, one plaintext per key), on every commit so its presence tells nothing; plus the epoch number. Within Cost's few hundred bytes per small commit; no picked number.
- **List parts**: Merkle root in the header, each part at a random leaf of a tree of fixed depth `LIST_TREE_DEPTH` = 32, each empty subtree a fresh random value, so a part's path (1 KiB) shows nothing of how many labels the rotator opens. Flat pinning shows every reader of every label that count, and padding to the actual depth still shows its log: both leaks. The depth is a resource bound: at 0 a rotator could cover one label, at infinity paths are unbounded; 2^32 labels is past any store, and a rotator past it refuses the rotation. Goes in notes/arbitrary-values-table.md with step 5.
- **Profile**: created with the account (step 6); the serving-node listing on it is issues/net-one-peer.md's.

- Awaiting her requirements rewrite (drafts below; she approved the direction).
- To raise with it (no decision blocks the build): a removed device's changes can count at a reader until the account's merge reaches it (the fork window, whichever epoch the device forks from); and two devices rotating at once (say each removing a device) make each other's later changes wait for a merge at readers holding both rotations.

### Draft requirements wording
- Permissions: "Every write is attributable to an account and verifiable by anyone who may read it. Which of the account's devices made it is visible only to the account's own devices."
- Permissions: "Removing a device from an account works like removing a holder: its later changes stop counting, and which of its earlier changes still count depends on what the account's other devices and the other writers had received by then, never on its own timestamps."
- Confidentiality between readers: replace "each other's node identities" with: "Readers of shared data learn which accounts take part, never which devices an account has. They can generally not tell which device made a change; they may tell that changes were made in one session, or at the same time on different devices, and when a device was removed."
- Cost: "Removing a device costs about the account's recent changes not yet compacted, once per group they are in, plus its role and purge changes under the existing limit."

## Steps
Each: one worker plus a reviewer. Tier-1 tests name their property and stay small.
1. **Model the rest** (roles-model `epochs`): done, results in Model above; the hand tests there state steps 4 and 5.
2. **Covered commits at member level** (on hold, tess-call: model run shows omitting covered commits saves 0.78 to 0.75 listed IDs per removal, loses visible writes in 64 of 1M worlds, and needs a new re-answer mechanism the engine and model lack; a lister who lost write or whose device is gone can never re-answer, a weaker property than today's full listing. Coordinator recommends reversing the decision: list covered commits at both levels, drop this step). Property: a demotion's frontier and addenda leave out covered commits, and a write still counts when its covering snapshot later turns invalid (the lister answers again). Model: addenda.rs `PLAN` with `omit_covered`, rerun. Tests: a frontier after a covering snapshot omits the commit and the write reads valid; the snapshot falls and the write still reads valid. Deletes: listing compacted commits in frontiers (spec/permissions.md §5.1 "listed whether A still holds its body"). Done: spec, perm, model agree.
3. **Spec**. Every section in "Spec to change", as the target the later steps build. Done: no spec text names a node as author, card holder or group member outside D and connections; reviewer greps `node card`, `seen.0`, `heartbeat`, `signing node`.
4. **Signing chain** (keys/perm, pure): done, `osis_perm::chain` (spec/crypto.md §9, spec/permissions.md §5.4; tests `osis-perm` `chain::`); keyset anchoring in osis-keys uses its `joins`. Property: chain counting is a function of the held rotations and the side. Tests: fork rotations both count; a merge rejects an unnamed superseded line; a rotation from above rejects nothing; an ill-formed supersedes entry is ignored; mutual rejection reports a dispute and the default side is the first-received line; a removed node's fork is void once the owner's merge is held; a removal closes a concurrent sibling's epoch; a merge signed at a fork's epoch protects its line from a fork rooted there; a name without its signature accepts nothing. Done: the model's hand worlds pass as unit tests.
5. **Epoch rule in judgment** (perm). Property: a commit at a closed epoch counts iff listed, vouched by an addendum, or named by a counting listing. Tests: rotator-received write counts; write only a witness received counts; a later write by X does not, at its old epoch or a sibling's; a sibling's listing revives its offline write; a listing at a closed epoch counts once listed; covered commits omitted; a part's Merkle path checks and a wrong path is bad input; role part over the bound refused. Done: perm-level, synthetic commits.
6. **Accounts sign**. Account creation (tid, D, profile), header author and epoch, inner signature, session source, departs mark, order key, removal as D removal plus rotation, honest listings, merging forks, addenda against rotations, node names its account as first admin. Tests (API): an outsider sees the account as author and cannot open the inner signature; a sibling can; a write by a removed node after removal does not show where the removal is held; an offline sibling's earlier write shows after it syncs; a removed node opens no box sealed to the account after removal; two siblings' role commits at one `ts` have distinct departs marks; a one-field commit grows by at most 100 B (byte count). Deletes: node as commit author or first admin, the node-level removal frontier, node delete bases; retires issues/frontier-certificate-cost.md.
7. **Node certificates out of groups**. Tests: a reader of a group finds no node tid in anything it opens there; adding a node to an account places nothing under the account's groups (blob count); chains verify with account certificates alone. Deletes: node links in core/perm/src/cert.rs, the node card in core/engine/src/labeling/certs.rs, node placing in core/node/src/sync/pass/certs.rs, the `node.0` aspect, spec/permissions.md §4 node card and placing of it, spec/sync.md §3 node certificate blobs.
8. **Heartbeats and `reach.0` out of groups** (may run beside 4-6). Tests: a stranded node is re-sealed via a connected holder, and via the admins' inbox with none connected; a suspect aspect is repaired over a connection; own nodes find each other's addresses under the account's sync ID; no group sync ID holds `seen.0` or `reach.0`. Deletes: core/node/src/sync/pass/beats.rs, the `seen.0` aspect, `HEARTBEAT_MIN_LIFETIME_MS`, `HEARTBEAT_MAX_LIFETIME_MS`, `HEARTBEAT_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`, `MAX_HEARTBEAT_SUSPECTS` (and their arbitrary-values-table rows), heartbeat reads in core/osis/src/share.rs and the sim's net checks; spec/permissions.md §4 heartbeat, spec/data-model.md §9 placement.
