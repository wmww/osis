# Certificate copies carry whole commits, duplicate closure blobs, and churn
> Summary: After a rotation, `Delivery::First` → `place_all` places every aspect of the commit, `/secrets` included, as a certificate copy under every labeled group that holds the actor. The copy under the actor's own label duplicates its closure blob. Other members then place smaller cert copies of the same commit, which the admin deletes because a blob of a commit that is a strict subset of another is must-not-hold. Most of this goes against spec/permissions.md §4 and spec/sync.md §6.
> Tags: sync, node, flows

Seen in examples/chat `costs_join_message_removal` (chat of 4; K = the link actor, C = the chat):
- **Whole commit as a certificate**: K's rotation commit (`/keys` 2.6 KB, `/members`, `/secrets` 7.6 KB) goes to C's label as a 3/3-part cert copy. A chain certificate is the edge deltas plus the current `/keys` (spec/permissions.md §4), about 3.3 KB. The `/secrets` part is 70% of the copy, and it grows by 2.7 KB per member of K. At 200 members that is about 530 KB of boxes per rotation, in a label whose readers cannot open them. A removal sends 30 KB of these extra boxes at 4 members and 75 KB at 8.
- **Duplicate under the actor's own label**: `place_all` also places K's rotation under K's label, next to the closure blob of the same commit: 21 KB per removal at 4 members, 43 KB at 8.
- **Upload then delete**: the other members of K (and the removed one) place their chain projection of the same commit (`/keys` + `/members`, 3.3 KB) under C and K. By spec/sync.md §6 it is a strict subset of the admin's copy, so it is must-not-hold, and the admin deletes it. That is about 19 KB per member per removal, uploaded, stored and then deleted. Joiners do the same with chain commits the label already holds: 7.5 KB deleted plus 12 KB of copies of commits already there, per join.
- Fix: (1) `place_all` (deliver.rs) places the certificate projection (`certificates()`' aspect choice: edge deltas, `/keys`, `/created`), not every aspect, and skips a label whose closure already holds the commit. (2) Before placing a cert copy, a node checks the label's entries. If a blob of that commit with a superset of the copy's parts is there, it places nothing, since the copy would be must-not-hold anyway. Expected saving at 4 members: about 50 KB from the remover and 19 KB from each other member per removal, and about 20 KB per join. At 200 members: hundreds of KB per removal per label.
