# What a stranger's open fires leave behind
> Summary: Open fires let anyone holding a thing's public key record write: three costs do not follow what is held. Any `x@~0` on any thing is kept by the thing's default history entry; records and chains a sealed fire carries are learned for good; a ticket lookup reads every held fire of the signal.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, security, tess-call

- **Any open signal name, kept by the default.** Every `@~` AK is open (spec/data-model.md §2), so a stranger may fire `junk@~0` on any thing with own keys. `retention::history::of` reads the setting's default entry for it: a thing whose default keeps history keeps every such fire for that long, forever under `keep` with no `after`. Proposed rule (needs Tess: it changes what `@` means): an open signal is kept only by a setting entry naming its AK; the default never applies to `@`. The inbox names its three.
- **Records and chains outlive the fire.** `Engine::open_sealed_fire` learns every creation record the fire carries (`learn_records`, before the commit is ingested or its signature checked) and ingests its chain commits (`ingest_valid`). A learned record of a thing never held is forgotten only by destruction; a stranger minting a fresh account per fire grows the record table without bound, past `INBOX_LIFETIME`. Learn only what the author's chain to its actor needs, and drop records and chain certificates no held thing needs once the fire goes (issues/reference-graph.md Storage: every row hangs off something held).
- **Ticket lookup reads the whole signal.** The reference index keys rows by (target, source, AK): for a signal, `sync::tickets::held_in` and `engine::refs::fires` (a validity rebuild of the signal) read every held fire of it. With F fires in the inbox one lookup costs F, and each new ticket for a held strong's target triggers one (`Closure::note_ticket`), so F tickets cost F². Also `judge` runs `take_in` (a `refs::to` scan of the target's whole fan-in) at each judgement of a strong into a thing not read established. Index signal rows per fire (commit) so a lookup costs the fires naming the target.
- **Rate × `INBOX_LIFETIME`.** A promoted fire leaves the deposit budget, so fires stored on an inbox are bounded by the sender's rate times its history setting only (notes/inbox.md accepts no rate limit). State it as a storage bound (issues/reference-graph.md Storage) or bound it.
