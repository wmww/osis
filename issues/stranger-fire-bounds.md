# What a stranger's open fires leave behind
> Summary: Open fires let anyone holding a thing's public key record write: two costs do not follow what is held. The author's chain a sealed fire brings (records, `keys.0` commits) stays after the fire goes; a ticket lookup reads every held fire of the signal.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, security

- **The author's chain outlives the fire.** `Engine::ingest_sealed_fire` takes a sealed fire's records and chain commits only with a fire that applies valid, and the fire's own rows go once it is out of history and forgotten, but the author's account records and `keys.0` commits stay (`t/`, `c/`, `a/`, `g/`, `ra/`: about 10 rows, 10 KB per stranger, counted by osis-engine tests/it/strangers.rs). Forgotten only by destruction, and `retention::forget` keeps certificate material on purpose, so a stranger minting a fresh account per fire grows the store by one chain per fire, past `INBOX_LIFETIME`. Wanted: a record or chain commit is kept only while something held needs it (its thing held, or it certifies a held commit), dropped in the transaction that forgets its last dependant. Nothing indexes that dependency today: a held commit's author chain has no reverse index (`chains_on` is keyed by thing), and records name no dependants. This is issues/storage-owners.md item 2 (other accounts' chains) for a stranger; build it there for every path, then tighten the test to equal rows (and drop the `CHAIN` families).
- **Ticket lookup reads the whole signal.** The reference index keys rows by (target, source, AK): for a signal, `sync::tickets::held_in` and `engine::refs::fires` (a validity rebuild of the signal) read every held fire of it. With F fires in the inbox one lookup costs F, and each new ticket for a held strong's target triggers one (`Closure::rejudge`), so F tickets cost F². Also `judge` runs `take_in` (a `refs::to` scan of the target's whole fan-in) at each judgement of a strong into a thing not read established. Index signal rows per fire (commit) so a lookup costs the fires naming the target.
- **Rate × `INBOX_LIFETIME`.** A promoted fire leaves the deposit budget, so fires stored on an inbox are bounded by the sender's rate times its history setting only (notes/inbox.md accepts no rate limit). State it as a storage bound (issues/reference-graph.md Storage) or bound it.
