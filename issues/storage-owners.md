# Storage audit: every stored row has an owner
> Summary: The storage design (every row has an owner; what releasing everything leaves) and an audit, written without Tess's review, of the row families on main that outlive what the node syncs, and how each would hang off an owner (a thing the node closure holds, a store entry of a synced sync ID, a peer record, or the node root). The growth tests' own-things and grant arms pass against a fresh node. Check each row before relying on it.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, retention

Tess's principle: every stored row is attributable to what the node holds, and releasing everything drops storage to the node root and the account's own things. Nothing may wait for a release's writes to be held elsewhere (spec/sync-set.md §3): a statement here that implies it is wrong.

## Releasing everything
A row goes with its last owner (below). Releasing everything leaves the node root, the account root, the profile, the inbox and the node thing, plus only these, each bounded:
- fires on the inbox, for its history setting (`INBOX_LIFETIME` by default);
- siblings' node things under D, while they are on D's roster;
- a link's sync ID while it is redeemed, until the join is held or the redeemer stops (spec/sync-inbox.md §6);
- role changes carried under a former grantee's sync ID, for `ROLE_KEEP` after replaced (a removal: after its `ts`, spec/sync-judgement.md §2);
- tombstones for `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`, peer records within `RECORD_BUDGET`.

## Owners
Every durable row and blob has an owner; a row lives exactly while its owner does and goes in the transaction its last owner goes. Owners:
- **Whole things**: the node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2: what the node root reaches by strongs). Such a thing keeps all it holds: content, role-class aspects, secrets, derived rows.
- **Store entries**: the entries of each sync ID in the sync set (or leaving it), which the required-set judgement (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) already limits to what S justifies: sync unit bodies, lasting headers, certificates of the chains S's things need, deposits. Engine data taken from an entry that is about no whole thing (another account's chain, a destroyed dependency's record and requests) is owned by the entries carrying it.
- **Peer records**: what a connection proved (a Prove's rotations, presence, charges, filters), owned by the record, and the record by its members (the sync IDs both share) or by being a node of this account.
- **The node root**: identity rows (`seed`, `hlc`, `account`, `u/`, `d/`) and the account root, held unconditionally.

The links between owners are counts over acyclic structure, so no walk beyond the closure is needed:
- entry → blob: `X/ b s` (the store keeps a blob while an entry names it);
- blob → commit: `bp/ c b`, `cb/ s b` (a commit is held while a part of it is on a whole thing, or a held blob of it was taken from an entry, or it is an own commit not yet held elsewhere);
- commit → thing: a thing is **known** while a held commit has a part on it or a held record blob names it;
- thing or commit → derived rows: keyed owner-first, dropped with it.

Cycles exist only in the closure (strongs), which the node closure's walk handles. Authority needs (record chains, holders' and authors' chains) need no walk of their own: what a held thing needs to verify travels as certificates in the required set of the sync IDs carrying it (spec/permissions.md §4), so it is owned by those entries and goes with the last.

## Exceptions on main and how each would be covered
Checked on main (2b5ac0318) unless marked "to confirm".
1. **Role-class state of unsynced things.** `retention::forget` with `Scope::Local` keeps `created.0`, `retention^.0`, `roles.0`, `keys.0`, `sync.0` and `secrets.0` (`forget::KEPT`, `Fate::Local`), so their role commits, headers, `node/metas`, `node/edges`, `node/keysets`, `node/epochs`, `node/walks`, thing lists (`tl/`, `tn/`, `tb/`), `g/`, `dw/` and secrets (`k/`) stay. Role commits are forgotten only with their thing's release (`retention/needed.rs`) or as covered (`retention/mod.rs`). Covered: the thing is no longer whole; its role commits stay only where an entry still carries them (a certificate another synced S needs), else they go with the content. Built (`retention/needed.rs`): a thing released or never in the closure, whoever's, is kept only while a held thing needs it, record and secrets included (engine `closure::own_things_are_released_whole_spec_sync_set_3`).
2. **Blob rows after their entries went.** A held commit's `bp/` rows (keyed commit then blob) outlive every entry of a blob. Closed for per-sync-ID copies: a copy is sealed for its sync ID's thing alone, so before S's shred the engine drops its notes of S's copies no other sync ID's entry or tombstone names (`Store::alone_under`, `Engine::shredding`; notes/sync-leave.md). A shared blob's note stays its held commit's (bounded by held commits): a peer still listing it is how a commit dropped as invalid is found again (sync `growth::a_write_valid_again_*`), so it cannot go with the local entries alone.
4. **Plain things leaving every closure** (to confirm by the test below): a thing without secrets of its own that leaves the node closure while the sync ID that carried it stays is released (spec/sync-set.md §3).
5. **Dispute sides** (`sides`: one record, entries for good). Covered: one row per disputed actor, owned by it.
6. **Purge marks** on role-class aspects and `z/` rows of a fallen purge. Content-aspect marks (`p/`, `pn/`, `v/`, `w/`, `ro/`, `f/`) already go with the aspect (`destroy::forget_aspect_rows`). Covered by the thing's ownership; `z/` is keyed thing first.
7. **Peer records** (`core/store/src/record.rs`): bounded by `RECORD_BUDGET` (256 MiB) and least-recently-used, not by sync; presence rows outlive membership (issues/presence-outlives-membership.md); a joined link's kept peers (`I/p/`). Covered: a record goes with its last member sync ID unless the peer is a node of this account; presence follows that issue's P1 (a member leaving ends its rows); `I/p/` is owned by the link's target thing.
8. **Suspect repair rows** `I/s/ reporter thing ak` (osis-sync `inbox/suspect.rs`): each goes only when an entry shows it stale, so rows of a thing forgotten here, of a shredded S and of a reporter that never reports again stay for the node's life. Covered: keyed thing first, owned by the thing; a reporter's rows also go when its role on the thing ends.
9. **Tickets an account holds**: D's, one per thing its nodes enter outside the local part, removed at release (spec/sync-set.md §1 The account's tickets).
10. **Forgetting finds its rows by owner**: pending commits by the things their headers write on (`apply::Wake::Thing`), timers by part (`xc/`), `z/` keyed thing first, freed links by thing (`ft/`); engine `scale::losing_access_reads_no_rows_of_what_stays`.
12. **Set tombstones in the roots' `holds.0` and `tickets.0`**: accepted by spec/requirements.md Scale ("state grows with the number of edits and removals ever made, a few bytes each"). A removed set element stays as a tombstone until the set is reset (spec/crdt.md §5.5), so D's and the node root's snapshots carry one per strong or ticket ever removed: about 66 B per make-and-release round (three strongs and a ticket) in the snapshot records (`c/`) and about 50 B in their blobs. No cover can drop one (notes/crdt.md, No set/bag tombstone GC): it is what keeps a removal final against the add from a node that held it without the removal. A reset carries no value, so it keeps no forgotten name (`fn/`). S1 bounds them at 32 B per removal (`growth::TOMBSTONE_BYTES`).
14. **`cv/` after the trigger's snapshots**: a held part dropped as covered names a coverer later forgotten (the trigger's intermediate snapshot of D's `holds.0`), so `retention::handed` keeps its covers: one row, flat, where a fresh node's part is covered by the final snapshot directly. Bounded by held parts dropped as covered (notes on `handed`); S1 allows it.

Closed by the growth tests' grant arms (sync `growth::a_granted_and_revoked_thing_*`, `a_declined_grant_*`, default): a missing part needs only the things in the node closure it is on (a part fetched again); a holder grant's target is needed by its holder in the node closure (the holder's sync ID carries its chain: the granter keeps the chain of a thing it reads through a holder); a lost need is judged with no pass in flight (`Engine::settle_lost`, from status; a thing also arrived since waits for its pass); a blob note (`bp/`) of a commit neither held nor pending goes once no entry names it (`Engine::unnamed_blob`, from the store follower as the commit goes unheld or the blob unnamed: a commit forgotten with lost access left one per round); an own certificate blob carrying nothing S's certificates hold leaves the own store (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store: a record blob of a fire's chain stayed in the grantee's inbox); a thing's openings go with it (`sync_ids::opened::forget_thing`, `Indexes::opened_under`: header openings of the maker's chain under each released thing stayed). An account needs no account root (spec/commits.md §5): a contact keeps another's root D only as the first admin of a held thing under it (the grantee kept the maker's D record, `t/`, `a/`, `g/`, after the thing went); a thing going whole takes its keyset notes (`g/`) though a held commit keeps its other parts, and a role arrival (`ra/`) is noted only for an actor whose record is held.

Closed by the growth tests' own-things arm (sync `growth::releasing_own_things_returns_storage_to_a_fresh_nodes_*`, default): a content commit whose other parts are missing on things outside the node closure goes once its rest is covered (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting; it stayed for good with a released thing's creation part); no `f/` row or fall mark (`fm/`) for a thing whose record is not held, and a fall mark goes with its aspect (`destroy::forget_aspect_rows`); a shredded sync ID's blobs of commits not held (forgotten with lost access, entries parked) take their notes (`Engine::shredding`).

Already conforming: own entries of commits forgotten other than by lost access (`CipherHost::forgot`); the sync store's per-sync-ID rows (`Store::shred`: entries, tombstones, path, usage, node log, `changed_at`, `N/`, `F/`, `R/`), `cp/` passes, content aspects and their rows, `hr/`/`hf/`, `fn/`, `cv/`, `ow/`, `ih/`, `T/`, `B/` (with its link), inbox fires (the inbox's history setting), `K/` (a kept-by-choice root).

## Purge tombstones
A lasting header is in the required set of each sync ID whose sync unit holds the purged thing (spec/commits.md §5), so it is kept and synced onward while any synced sync unit carries the thing, and goes with the last. Local purge marks are owned by the purged thing (whole) or the purge commit. A copy of the purged content arriving later through a peer that ignored the purge is accepted, as at a newcomer.

## Test
One count test: a node holding several things (shared with a peer, a purge, an admin dispute, certificates from other accounts, a retained copy, an ended share, an imported contact) releases everything; its store rows and bytes by family must equal those of a fresh node of the same account that synced nothing, and the work counted must be flat between n and 10n of what stays. Re-syncing must read as a node that never left.
Built arms: osis-sync `growth::` own things, expiry, held history, grant and decline; the redeem arm fails (issues/redeem-leaves-a-blob.md, `repro_a_redeemed_and_ended_link_*`).
