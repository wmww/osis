# A grant held only as a projection carries no names to its holder
> Summary: Study C3 is closed only where the boxer holds the grant's `secrets.0` part. A node holding the grant's `roles.0` part alone (a certificate copy; the label copy withheld) sees the holder actor N current, holds none of the grant's names, and boxes N at a keyset a departed admin of N forged. The simple fix (a held grant whose `secrets.0` part is missing counts as unmet) strands single-use link redeemers for good. Answer: keep the fix, add the granter's keyset name to the edge write, and let a boxer lacking the names seal only to that keyset.
> Tags: security, keys, engine

Filed from the keyset-names branch review (text there; copied here with the answer).

Repro: `rotation::tests::repro_grant_projection_names_unheld_spec_crypto_4` (osis-engine, ignored): `grant_names_before_boxing_holder_spec_crypto_4` with X given the grant without its `secrets.0` part on A; X's rotation of A boxes N at D's forged keyset and D, never a holder of A, opens X's epoch.

Tried in review: `grant_names_held` returning false while such a commit's `secrets.0` part on the target is missing (`CommitMeta::missing`; dropped parts count as held). Closes the repro, but breaks `deposit_queue::projection_applies_when_k_never_gets_it_spec_commits_4`: a sharer applies a single-use link redemption from its chain copy (the `secrets.0` part only reaches K's label by the redeemer's deposit), then rotates K for the link's removal leaving the redeemer's person out; the deposit never reaches it after that, so the person is never re-sealed.

Directions offered: (i) carry the grant's names in the `roles.0` part (plaintext keys there expose the holder closure's churn to every holder of the certificate); (ii) make the sharer's rotation box the redeemer when the grant came from its own link (the link's grant names are the redeemer's own); (iii) wait for the part only where the label copy is expected (not for deposits).

## Answer 2 (devbox fable)
**Why the strand is permanent, not a delay.** spec/crypto.md §3: an admin MUST write a fresh deposit seed in each rotation of the read role it makes. The sharer's finalizing rotation of K leaves P out and refreshes the seed; P, without a box at the new epoch, cannot read the new `keys.0`, so neither the redeemer nor any other device of P can ever deposit the missing part (or anything else) under K again, and the sharer can never hold the names it needs to re-seal P. Deadlock, not latency. Any rule of the form "wait for the part" has this shape wherever the only channel from the grantee's side to A's rotators is deposit under A's label. So the review's fix is the right principle (a boxer must hold what every earlier sealer of N knew; a missing part is unheld knowledge) but needs a fallback that works with the `roles.0` part alone.

**The three directions.**
- (i) Sound only as "names must be met" (the named commits applied); then the link case still waits for C_P, which the redeemer places by the same dead deposit. Making the names alone sufficient needs the departed tid in each name plus an author-chain check on N's `keys.0` writes (reject a keyset whose writer's chain passes a named tid), and the keys in a certificate expose N's churn to every label A's chain reaches. More machinery than (v) below, and (v) covers everything it would.
- (ii) Exact (the sharer holds the seed thing, so it knows the grant's author is its own L) but unsound in the C1/C1' shape for the one holder that matters: S boxes P at P's newest keyset at S, which may be D's forgery (S lacks C_P) or Z's honest ignorant fork boxing D; no forgery needed for the second. A worse residual than the later-admin-path one.
- (iii) Can be made exact as a procedure: a label copy of the `secrets.0` part always exists (spec/sync.md §3 projects one blob per readable subset), so "expected" can only mean uploaded (stamped) versus deposited, and a node knows which from the author's effective role on A at the grant's `ts` (write or above uploads; a read invite or a link's L deposits), which it judges anyway for validity. But exactness buys nothing: the deposit class is exactly where the hole lives (every link redemption with r = read, every read-invite grant of a holder actor); the repro with G a read-invite holder reproduces under (iii). It is (ii) widened. Reject.

**Recommendation, (v): the granter's keyset choice travels in the edge write.** A grant (or role raise) of a holder actor N already names, in its `secrets.0` names, the applied `keys.0` commit of N whose keyset it sealed to. Copy that one name into the `roles.0` edge write (`keyset: commit key` on the edge record; nothing for key-actor grantees). Rule, replacing the vacuous "names of every held box": a boxer of N on A must hold the names of the standing edge write's commit (its `secrets.0` part on A held or dropped, not missing) and of every held valid box to N at a current epoch of A. **A boxer lacking the standing edge write's names seals N only to the keyset of that write's named `keys.0` commit, which it must hold applied and valid, and only if that keyset passes its own departure judgement (the conservative rule for departures inside N it knows); otherwise it leaves N out.** Once the part arrives, the normal rule.

Soundness: X seals A's new secret to exactly the key the granter G sealed A's secret to, so the set of parties that can open it is unchanged. For every departure G knew, G's keyset choice is D-safe by the frozen-coverage induction; for every departure X knows, X's own judgement applies; for departures neither knew, nobody relies on X's epoch (frozen coverage: a writer that knows one rotates first). D's forged `keys.0` of N and Z's ignorant fork are different commits from the named one and are never sealed to. The binding is on the standing edge write, which every boxer of N necessarily holds, so a later boxer Y that holds a bound boxer's rotation (whose names say nothing about N's earlier departures) and still lacks G's part is bound too; no flag has to travel. Over-restriction: Y may be bound although it holds a knowing rotator's box at a newer keyset of N with its names met; Y then leaves N out and the knowing nodes re-seal. Refinement if that matters: a "names held" bit in each holder box's names frees a later boxer that holds such a box met; not needed for soundness.

Checks: the repro (X holds the `roles.0` copy and f_new's commit through N's chain certificate or G's placement: boxes N at f_new; D's f_D ignored); C1 and C1' (f' and f_D are not the named commit); C2 (direct holders, untouched); C3 (closed: the knowledge that cannot reach X by name reaches it as the key G chose). Single-use link: at finalization S holds the redemption's `roles.0` part with the keyset name and P's current `keys.0` commit from P's chain certificates under the target's label (the test's chain copy), S knows no departure of P, so it boxes P at that keyset at once; P then reads the new epoch, the new deposit seed, and deposits the missing part (and anything later), after which the normal rule applies. Residual: the deposit never lands and P rotated (a device removal) between redemption and finalization so that S lacks, or its own knowledge rejects, the named keyset: P stays out and needs a new link. Two independent conditions, one a race; acceptable.

Costs: one commit key per edge write to a holder actor (about 50-70 bytes), repeated in every chain certificate copy of the edge; no privacy change (N's current `keys.0` commit already travels in N's chain certificates); liveness strictly better than the review's rule, which this keeps as its first branch; one extra rotation of A when a writer later learns a departure inside N that G named and the bound boxer's epoch did not (the general frozen-coverage cost). Engine: the edge field, the fallback branch in the boxer, and the retained judgement of a dropped `secrets.0` part must keep its names (spec/commits.md §4, "what the part was judged stays"), or a drop reopens the hole.

A fourth channel considered and rejected: the link carrying the sharer's inbox and deposit seed so the redeemer sends the whole redemption to the sharer's person. More wire, a new spam surface, and it fixes only the link case.
