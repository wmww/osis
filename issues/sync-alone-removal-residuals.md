# Sync-alone removal: a blind remover's header, a removed reader untold
> Summary: gaps in telling a removed holder its removal: a remover that never held H's grant fires the removal with its header; a departed reader cannot open the removal or its evidence by sync alone (wrapped under epochs past its departure, or logged past its `until`).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, engine, sync, permissions

- **Blind remover**: `sync_alone_on` is false where no held write gives H a role (only the removal held, e.g. `holding::a_removal_its_writer_made_blind_*`), so `deliver_copy` fires the removal with its header, signer and path to H's readers. If H held sync alone that leaks X's remover to them. The outbox reads the predicate at send time (`sealed_fire`), so a fire still owed when the grant arrives goes headerless; one sent before does not. That case also reads held writes whatever their validity, so a forged edge write giving H a role, held at the blind remover, makes it send nothing. The fix (a blind remover sends nothing; the "no role held" case goes) waits on the next item: without the fire a removed reader is never told.
- **A removed reader is not told by sync alone** (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Departed, §6): the departed reader cannot open what judging its removal needs. With the "no role held" case dropped, `a_removal_its_writer_made_blind_*` fails: Alice neither holds nor pends the removal. Found by instrumenting it:
  - The removal goes alone (its writer knew no departure, so no rotation in it). The blind node logs it under Bob's account's sync ID only (T's sync ID has no peer while partitioned); after the heal node 1 (and node 2) learn the departure, rotate T first (encrypting under the uncovered epoch is blocked), then log the removal under T's sync ID as a fresh encryption under the newest epoch: Alice fetches the entry and opens nothing. Node 2's own entry of it loses as spare to node 1's.
  - Its evidence alike: the remover's record (node 2's node thing, `MissingChain`) rides in the record blob, sealed under the newest read epoch; in general the copies of the remover's path edges made since the reader last synced do too.
  - Order: a departed member is served role entries up to `until` (the first-logged position of a rotation superseding its epoch); an entry the removal needs that a server logs after that rotation is never served there.
  - A prototype passed the scenario with the removal wrapped under an old epoch (a taking rule beside `giving_wrap_epoch` in `sync_ids/blobs.rs::epoch_under`) and the one record blob under it; the latter leaks every later record (`-hack` below).
  - Rule, modelled (roles-model `told`, whole family: spec holds, each control fails): per held commit c of X taking read from a holder M (an applied edge write leaving it below read; a box or maker departure alike), its **taking epoch** e(c) is the newest epoch held here among those what gave M read boxed it (the edge write giving it read that the departure leaves, or its start; a box departure's box; a maker's own epoch). Every node judging the departure logs c under e(c) (as a rotation's own wrap, not held back by a blocked epoch), with a record blob under e(c) carrying the records judging c needs (record blobs compete only within the epoch they open under: a taking epoch's class needs its commits' records, every other epoch the general set; the evidence copies alike, not modelled apart from records). An entry of c under e(c) never loses as spare to one under another epoch. A departed member's `until` is the first-logged position here of c's entry under e(c) (none logged: nothing opens), and it is served, past `until`, the entries under e(c) telling it (c's evidence copies, that record blob) and nothing else.
  - What the model rejected (each a control): e(c) the newest held epoch covering none of c's departures (`-uncovering`: a rotation boxing M nothing, made by a node that never held M's grant, is that epoch) or the newest held epoch boxed to M (`-boxed`: a later rotation's box M never took); `until` grown to the told entries' positions (`-range`: a pairing or re-seal giving the new epoch is wrapped under the old one M holds and lands between); the general record blob under e(c) (`-hack`: a record minted after the removal); a spare by blob ID (`-spare`).
  - Found beside it, in today's spec: `until` at the first rotation superseding the Hit's epoch (`-rotation-until`, hand world `told::tests::until_is_the_taking_entry`) serves a departed member a giving commit made knowing its removal, under an older epoch it holds, wherever a rotation made unaware of the removal (one boxing M) moves the Hit's epoch past it. The taking-entry `until` closes it.
  - Residual (not modelled: one removal per world): two removals sharing a taking epoch share its record blob class, so each departed party gets the records the other's evidence needs (a remover's record minted after its own removal). Per-commit classes need the record blob to name its commit.
  - Who writes the input: departures are the node's own judgement of applied edge writes (admins, a finalizer, or the holder itself); e(c) is an epoch M holds; the old epoch opens only role parts and records judging c needs.
