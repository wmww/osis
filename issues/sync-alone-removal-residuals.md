# Sync-alone removal: a blind remover's header, a step past the holder unsent
> Summary: gaps in telling a removed holder its removal: a remover that never held H's grant fires the removal with its header; a departed reader cannot open the removal or its evidence by sync alone (wrapped under epochs past its departure, or logged past its `until`); a removal whose sync rotation is blocked sends a sync-alone H only a step still bearing its key.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, engine, permissions

- **Blind remover**: `sync_alone_on` is false where no held write gives H a role (only the removal held, e.g. `holding::a_removal_its_writer_made_blind_*`), so `deliver_copy` fires the removal with its header, signer and path to H's readers. If H held sync alone that leaks X's remover to them. The outbox reads the predicate at send time (`sealed_fire`), so a fire still owed when the grant arrives goes headerless; one sent before does not. That case also reads held writes whatever their validity, so a forged edge write giving H a role, held at the blind remover, makes it send nothing. The fix (a blind remover sends nothing; the "no role held" case goes) waits on the next item: without the fire a removed reader is never told.
- **A removed reader is not told by sync alone** (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Departed, §6): the departed reader cannot open what judging its removal needs. With the "no role held" case dropped, `a_removal_its_writer_made_blind_*` fails: Alice neither holds nor pends the removal. Found by instrumenting it:
  - The removal goes alone (its writer knew no departure, so no rotation in it). The blind node logs it under Bob's account's sync ID only (T's sync ID has no peer while partitioned); after the heal node 1 (and node 2) learn the departure, rotate T first (encrypting under the uncovered epoch is blocked), then log the removal under T's sync ID as a fresh encryption under the newest epoch: Alice fetches the entry and opens nothing. Node 2's own entry of it loses as spare to node 1's.
  - Its evidence alike: the remover's record (node 2's node thing, `MissingChain`) rides in the record blob, sealed under the newest read epoch; in general the copies of the remover's path edges made since the reader last synced do too.
  - Order: a departed member is served role entries up to `until` (the first-logged position of a rotation superseding its epoch); an entry the removal needs that a server logs after that rotation is never served there.
  - Shown by a prototype (scenario passes): the removal wrapped under the newest held epoch covering none of the departures it makes (a "taking" rule beside `giving_wrap_epoch` in `sync_ids/blobs.rs::epoch_under`: the giving rule's mirror, a role-only commit taking read goes under an epoch its departed holders hold, as the rotation covering them is) and the record blob sealed under that old epoch. The second half was a hack: the one record blob under an old epoch leaks every later record of S to departed parties.
  - Proposed rule ("what judges a commit taking read goes as it does"): per held commit c of X taking read (an applied edge write leaving a holder of read below it), its taking epoch e(c) (greatest n held covering none of c's departures); c's blob, the copies of its evidence (`Engine::evidence_of`) and a **departure record blob** of its evidence's records go under e(c) (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what, §4 Encrypting under exception). Record blobs then compete per class (the epoch they open under: a taking epoch's class needs its departures' records, every other epoch the general set), so the general blob never displaces a departure one. A departed member's `until` grows to the first-logged positions of its departure's told entries (the existing re-Hit rule reopens a member whose `until` moved). Costs one record blob per removal while the removal is held. Model first: roles-model `told` has only a knowing remover; add a blind one whose chain the removed node lacks.
  - Who writes the input: departures are the node's own judgement of applied edge writes (admins, a finalizer, or the holder itself); the old epoch opens only role parts and records judging c needs.
- **Blocked sync rotation**: `inbox::deliver_step` sends H the first step recorded after its removal only. If the removal's sync rotation is blocked and a step is issued before it, that step still bears H's key and the step past it is never sent: H's readers keep naming H holder (the store side is told by Register). A fix sends steps while the parent step bears a sync key H was boxed, read from X's `secrets.0` boxes to H.
