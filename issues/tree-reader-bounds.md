# Tree reader bounds are implementation policy
> Summary: spec/wire.md §6 says nothing about minimum chunk sizes on read; osis-label bounds a chunk-tree reader's work by rejecting short non-final chunks and capping chunks visited, which the spec could state.
> Tags: sync, data-model

A reader of a hostile chunk tree could otherwise do unbounded work for no output: empty chunks and repeated links (legal, since identical chunks share a blob) let a 3-level tree cost about 262 000 fetches for 0 bytes. osis-label's `TreeReader` rejects as bad input any chunk other than the last that is empty or shorter than `CHUNK_MIN`, and visits at most `max_len / CHUNK_MIN + 1` chunks (`TooLarge` beyond), with the fan-out and level caps bounding the nodes per chunk. The spec states neither rule: every honest writer satisfies the first (the chunker never cuts below `CHUNK_MIN` except at the end), so adding "a chunk other than the last shorter than `CHUNK_MIN` is bad input" to spec/wire.md §6 would make the bound part of the format; until then it is policy, and another implementation may accept trees this one refuses.
