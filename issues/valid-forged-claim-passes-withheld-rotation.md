# A valid forged claim of s settles the next rotation without the listed commit
> Summary: Settled (spec/crypto.md §4) accepts "s established" by any valid settled claim of s, so a writer (later departed) that signed a forged claim F of s while valid lets a node withheld R_s (s's genuine rotation, carrying M's removal) establish the next epoch e and re-seal it to M. The keyset list closes only the invalid-F shape (pass-through-rewrite-injection); require the listed commits themselves.
> Tags: security, keys

Shape: owner A removes reader M, rotating read to s (R_s). Writer W (holder of s, may rotate read) signs F: s's keyset (copied, junk works too), s's history box and a box at s written again, listing nothing, so F is settled. A applies F (valid: W is a writer) and then removes W, rotating to e (keyset lists s: {R_s, F}). Node X gets every commit but R_s (withheld by W or M). At X: F is a valid settled claim, so s is established, so R_e is settled, e is established and current; M is still a reader (R_s unseen) without a box at e, and X re-seals e to M. W and M are both departed by e. Same with F not applied at A (list {R_s}): s is established by F all the same. Main has the same first clause (`is_established(s) ||`), so it is not a regression of supersede-list, but it is the class that branch set out to close: something other than the rotator's own list chooses the chain.

Repro (osis-engine `rotation::tests`): `forged_claim_withheld_rotation_spec_crypto_4` with peer 1 granted `Write` and peer 0 ingesting all of peer 1's packages (F included) before demoting it: e is established at peer 3, and `reseal_candidates` there lists M (peer 2) for read. An admin as W is "could anyway" (it could re-grant M while valid); a writer cannot grant.

Possible rule (not implemented): for each listed s, if the list is empty s is established; else s is keyed by a held `secrets.0` snapshot (admin-signed), or every listed commit is held and either one of them is a valid settled claim of s or all are invalid and settled. "One valid" rather than "every listed settled": a valid listed claim whose own list never settles (a writer's junk) must not stall the role. The "no valid claim of s held" condition then goes. Needs the settled differential's reference reading changed and the TLA assumption note.

Related, minor: requiring every listed invalid commit settled (branch supersede-list, against "any" before) lets a writer's junk claim C' of s, valid at the rotator and later invalid with R_s, stall R_e everywhere; the role heals by the next rotation from s's predecessor.
