# A link's first contact: what is left after the join
> Summary: a redeemer joins K from the link alone (K's sync ID carries the author chain as certificate copies and the records as a record blob; links::a_redeemer_joins_k_spec_permissions_7), but the redeemed thing is filed nowhere so nothing syncs it after the join, the convergence harness still exchanges contacts by hand (`history::introduce`: after a card's join the redeemer's account is never proved on the link's connection), and a link whose sharer never answers stays followed.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, tests

- **Filing after the join.** Once the join is held the link is unfollowed (spec/sync-set.md §2), and nothing files K's thing in the redeemer's synced group, so K is synced no more: osis `redeeming_a_link_joins_its_thing_spec_sync_inbox_6` (ignored) reads no content after `redeem`. Contact cards (`import_contact`) file nothing by design (spec/sync-set.md §4); whether `redeem` of a non-profile link files the thing (an accept, spec/sync-set.md §4) is the osis API's to decide.
- **Contact cards in the differential.** `history::introduce` grants profiles and files them by hand. Contact cards join (every account redeems every other's card, 3 nodes included), but a grant to a contact never reaches it after: the connection the redeemer dialed by the link proved L only, the redeemer's account was unknown to the sharer node then and is never proved on that connection once the join makes it known (spec/sync-opens.md §2: a proof is never sent unasked), so the grant's inbox blob is offered to no connection (`inbox::offers::may_take`: the recipient not proven, its inbox not open there) and nothing else of the account opens there. Every flows/keep/convergence test on contacts fails so. Reproducer (2 nodes) `links::a_grant_reaches_a_contact_by_card_spec_sync_inbox_5` (ignored). Needs an opens rule: a connection proves again (or is replaced) when the peer's known actors grow. Once it passes, replace `introduce` by cards: each node makes its card, every other redeems it and files the profile in its D.
- **The redeemer's other end** (spec/permissions.md §7): every route of K's sync ID failed to open it, one at least refusing L's static entry. Only the role-history end is built (`Engine::link_ended`), so a link whose sharer node never answers stays followed (one per redeem the user asked for, durable in `I/r/`) until K's history arrives.
- A link lists relays only (spec/wire.md §10), so a sharer with no relay is reached by a link only over a LAN.
- Record blobs pulled from a peer are taken (their records learned) but not kept: each reader logs its own record blob of the records its own store lacks (`read`'s `records`), so one record blob per node per sync ID.
