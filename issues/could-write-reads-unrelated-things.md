**Today** (engine `apply::tests_vouches::a_vouch_on_one_thing_keeps_nothing_on_another_its_voucher_holds_no_role_on_spec_perm_5_1`, `a_vouched_commits_parts_read_alike_across_a_restart_spec_perm_5_1`): X rotates keys on t and S in one commit c through team A; H removes X from A without c; W, admin on t alone, vouches on t. The entry counts only where W could write all of c, so c falls on both things: no vouch on t keeps a part on S (no escalation today), but W's receipt keeps nothing of c on t either: a liveness loss on main until the wiring (an honest commit over two things that the remover did not list falls on both unless a witness holding the right on every thing it writes received it in time). A node holding S reads W's role there; a node holding nothing of S reads none: where W holds a role on S, holders of t read c's part on t otherwise by whether they hold S, and S's admin can make a node release S.

**Stated paths** (notes/a1-spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches, `osis_perm::paths`, roles-model `paths` `Entry::OnThing`): an entry keeps c's parts on its vouch's thing alone and reads nothing of c's other things, so every holder of t reads alike and authority on t keeps nothing on S.
