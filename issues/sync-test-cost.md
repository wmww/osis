# The differential costs about twice its budget a history, all of it engine reads
> Summary: one differential history costs 7.7 G instructions clean, 8.3 G with faults (a world 2.9 G of it), against notes/sync-test-harness.md's 4 G; the rest of the cost is engine reads whose memo stamps move with every commit on a thing (Timelines per thing), a snapshot scan per body judged, and repeated signature checks: Fathom's lane.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, tests

Defaults fit the budget (convergence 20 seeds each, 359 G of 400; each simulation 6 seeds, 344 G of 350; the binary 750 G of 1,200), so this costs seeds, not time. Measured on a fault-free history of seed 0 (3 nodes, 40 steps, 724 deliveries, 881 settle rounds, 68 commits applied): 2,003 read judgements, 1,105 `sync_bodies` re-runs, 180 inbox judgements, 662 engine transactions, 73 signature examinations.

Causes, each in the engine (`core/engine`), with counts:
- **Timelines stamp every touched thing** (`engine/held.rs` `Timelines::moved`: `touched` and `changed()` alike): any commit on a thing moves whatever read that thing's timeline, though most readers want one aspect of it (its epochs, its roles). Readers in sync's facade: `sync_bodies` (through `thing_sync`, `owned_sync_ids`, `epochs`), `read_facts`, inbox opens (`epochs`, `account_kems`), `read_credentials`, `own_commits`. In the seed-0 history (measured with every self-moved judgement already fixed) the timeline key moved read judgements 8,469 times, `sync_bodies` 4,743, status own commits 1,048, opens credentials 185. Scaling: a reader's own write to its account's group re-judges every body of every thing it reads (keep.rs's `one_write_after` shape: 78 read judgements after 2 earlier writes to the thing, 150 after 20).
- **`body_facts_now` tries every snapshot ever held of each aspect** (`sync/bodies.rs`, `ix.snapshots`) against the body judged: one write synced to one reader joins 20 snapshot pairs after 3 earlier writes, 200 after 30 (`covered: join`; `stream: bytes` 1,640 and 16,800; `part read` 20 and 200).
- **Signatures checked about 2.6 times per commit per node** (issues/signature-checked-per-receiver.md; branch sig-once): 531 verifies for 68 commits applied across 3 nodes, about 2.5% of a history.
- An inbox blob no key here opens (another actor's inbox this node reads, as `introduce`'s profiles give) is tried again at each move of that actor's timeline: 25 open attempts a history, part of the first cause.
