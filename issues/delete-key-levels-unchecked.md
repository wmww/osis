# Delete-key check: what is left
> Summary: Delete keys are step keys and verdicts read the key check (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys; `Indexes::miskeyed`), so a part listing a level-1 or level-3 key no held step places applies nowhere. Left: a keyset published before its step makes an outside fire unjudgeable for good; a key verdict moving under a keyset leaves a freshly loaded store unlike the live one; the writer's fallback; link checks.
> Priority: critical
> Tags: security, sync, engine

Built: notes/delete-keys.md (a role secret's delete key is its step key; a writer lists the newest step its base's `sync.0` records, `Core::listed_step`; `Indexes::steps`, `Core::wrong_keys`, `Indexes::miskeyed`). Level 2 stays unchecked (the purge author's own key). Stores do not change: a delta with a wrong key applies at no node that judges it, so a store deleting it loses nothing.

## Left
1. **A keyset published before its step** (found by reading, no test): an outside fire lists the keys of the inbox's keysets its sender holds (spec/sync-inbox.md §2), and a key counts only where a step bearing it is recorded before the commit (spec/crypto.md §7). A rotation's keyset is written before the step registering it is issued (spec/sync-ids.md §6), so a fire sent in between is unjudgeable for good and `ingest_sealed_fire` refuses it: the offer is lost. Likewise any writer listing by the published key. To decide: "before" reads the keyset's stamp for a key a keyset publishes, or a sender lists the newest keyset whose step it holds.
2. **A key verdict moving under a keyset** (osis-engine `apply::tests_addenda::a_voided_removal_leaves_holders_as_they_were_spec_perm_5_1`, ignored): `check_rebuilt` finds a freshly loaded store's `secrets` index lacking an epoch's rotation info (`supersedes`, `rotations`) the live index holds. Guess: the `sync.0` write recording the rotation's step turns invalid with the voided removal, the rotation's keys turn wrong, and the live index keeps what a load never notes.
3. **A role held without delete keys** (ignored: osis-engine `retention::tests::writer_covers_once_its_delete_keys_come_in_any_order_spec_commits_5`, `resnapshot::owed_resnapshot_waits_for_delete_keys_spec_commits_5`, `osis` `sharing::role_before_delete_keys_reads_as_read_spec_crypto_7`): each withholds the commit giving a thing its secrets and expects a role with no keys to list; with the fallback below no setup reaches one (`ready_to_write_in`'s wait). Restate the tests on one, or delete the wait with them if none exists.
4. **The writer's fallback** (Interim: `CommitSecrets::key`, `published_keys`, the keyset's `delete_key` field): a node recording no step of the base lists the step key its newest held secret derives, else the base's published keyset key. The published key stays for outside fires; the held-secret fallback goes once no writer lacks its base's `sync.0`.
5. **Link checks** (spec/crypto.md §7: every link's delete keys against its delta's; a failing page link makes its delta bad, a chunk link reads as absent): not built.
6. Hand tests: a may-grant holder's grant listing its own key; a step arriving after the body listing it.
7. notes/disputes.md, Storage under a fork, reads a party's writes as valid on both sides of a fork: rewrite under F6 (a writer's commits list its side's step and apply nowhere on the other, notes/write-path-design.md §1).
8. The demoted writer's level-2 residue: issues/demoted-writer-deletes-at-stores.md.
