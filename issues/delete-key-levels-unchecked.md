# A commit's signer can delete its own blobs at stores whatever their purge-by
> Summary: A non-admin signer lists its own key at level 1 and deletes its fixed-`admin` parts (a may-grant holder's grant, a `secrets.0` box, `created.0`, `addenda.0`) at nodes keeping S unread, also after demotion (spec/sync-hostile.md §2). Delete keys are step keys and the key check is built (`sync_ids::steps`, `Indexes::miskeyed`), but no verdict reads it: a node holding a copy without the `sync.0` writes placing its keys cannot tell a wrong key from a base it lacks, and old certificates carry none. Turn it on with the switch to evidence (A2 brief 3 piece 2); link checks and the writer's fallback are left.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, sync, engine

## The hole
Levels follow purge-by (`Header::validate`, spec/wire.md §4). The hole is the key's identity: an `admin` part lists one level-1 key, and nothing a verdict reads checks that it is the thing's admin key, so the signer deletes the part at stores with only S's sync key. Linked blobs widen it: a link's delete keys are its writer's claim (spec/data-model.md §6), and pages of role-class deltas are linked blobs.

Stores cannot close it: a store cannot tell which thing a blob serves, and S's current admins already delete anything with the head's delete key.

## Direction
Delete keys are step keys (notes/redesign-plan.md A2): a write lists the level's key of the newest step its side holds for the base; the listed step, with its path back to the base's genesis step, rides with the write as evidence, so a reader of a thing made independent judges its derived-era content without reading the former base; readers check levels 1 and 3 against steps recorded before the commit, per thing; level 2 stays unchecked (the purge author's right, which the signer holds). Stores do not change: a delta with a wrong key is invalid wherever it is judged, so deleting it loses nothing. In a permanent admin dispute a writer's commits list its side's newest step and are invalid on the other side: intended (Tess, notes/write-path-design.md §1 F6).

## Built
- A role secret's delete key is its step key (`DeleteSecret::admin`, `DeleteSecret::write`; spec/crypto.md §7).
- A writer lists the keys of the newest step its base's `sync.0` records (`Core::listed_step`).
- The check (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys): `Indexes::steps` places each step of a `sync.0` state on a verified path from its genesis with the stamp its path is recorded from; `Core::wrong_keys` judges a header per thing; `Indexes::miskeyed` holds the verdict per (commit, thing), persisted with the parts row and judged again as steps, records or account epochs move (`Core::rekey`). Debug builds compare it with a from-scratch verdict at every re-judge (`validity::misjudged_one`), in every test.
- With `Engine::check_delete_keys` on (tests), a miskeyed thing's deltas are invalid and its edge writes read by nothing (osis-engine `validity::a_delta_listing_a_key_no_step_bears_is_invalid_spec_crypto_7`).

## Left
1. **Verdicts read the check** (`Core::check_keys`, Interim): only once the steps placing a listed key ride with every copy as its evidence (spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit, the delete-key clause; `evidence::` derives them). Turned on over old certificates, 178 osis-sync default tests fail: a reader of a copy (a link's redeemer, a holder's piece, an author's chain) holds role-class parts on a thing without that thing's genesis `sync.0` write, so every key reads as wrong. Judging only where some base up the chain has a genesis step held is no fix: a holder thing made under a base the node syncs, held as `roles.0` copies alone, reads as wrong there and right elsewhere (verdicts diverge).
2. **The writer's fallback** (`CommitSecrets::key`, `published_keys`, the keyset's `delete_key` field; Interim): a node recording no step of the base lists the step key its newest held secret of the role derives, else the one the base's `keys.0` keyset publishes. A link's redeemer writes its join on K as the link key, a reader with may-grant holding no admin or write secret of K, while it holds K's `keys.0` and `secrets.0` parts and no `sync.0` part of K (177 osis-sync default tests fail without the published key). A published key is the step key of its epoch, right only once a step bearing it is recorded before the write. All three go when every holder is handed the base's `sync.0` with its boxes (A2 brief 4, edge pieces): the keyset drops the field (spec/crypto.md §4 has none) and a writer recording no step cannot write.
3. **Link checks** (spec/crypto.md §7: every link's delete keys against its delta's; a page link failing makes its delta bad, a chunk link reads as absent): not built.
4. **An account's epochs**: the check takes the signing key of any held epoch of the account's chain (`Core::account_signing_keys`), the spec an epoch counting at the reader. A commit at an epoch that counts not is refused by the epoch rule; a key of such an epoch listed by another signer is not.
5. Hand tests under the check for a may-grant holder's grant listing its own key (its edge write read by nothing) and for a step arriving after the body listing it.
6. issues/demoted-writer-deletes-at-stores.md option 1 (a level-2 Delete needs the head's write key) closes the demoted writer's residue.
7. Designs that read a party's writes as valid on both sides of a fork, to rewrite under F6: notes/disputes.md, Storage under a fork.
