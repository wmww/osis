# Delete-key check: what is left
> Summary: Delete keys are step keys and verdicts read the key check (`sync_ids::steps`, `Indexes::miskeyed`): a part listing a level-1 or level-3 key no held step places applies nowhere, so a non-admin signer listing its own key deletes nothing a reader keeps. Left: a keyset published before its step leaves an outside fire unjudgeable for good; a key verdict moving under a keyset leaves a store loaded afresh unlike the live one; the writer's fallback; link checks; account epochs.
> Priority: critical
> Tags: security, sync, engine

## The hole
Levels follow purge-by (`Header::validate`, spec/wire.md §4). The hole is the key's identity: an `admin` part lists one level-1 key, and nothing a verdict reads checks that it is the thing's admin key, so the signer deletes the part at stores with only S's sync key. Linked blobs widen it: a link's delete keys are its writer's claim (spec/data-model.md §6), and pages of role-class deltas are linked blobs.

Stores cannot close it: a store cannot tell which thing a blob serves, and S's current admins already delete anything with the head's delete key.

## Direction
Delete keys are step keys (notes/redesign-plan.md A2): a write lists the level's key of the newest step its side holds for the base; the listed step, with its path back to the base's genesis step, rides with the write as evidence, so a reader of a thing made independent judges its derived-era content without reading the former base; readers check levels 1 and 3 against steps recorded before the commit, per thing; level 2 stays unchecked (the purge author's right, which the signer holds). Stores do not change: a delta with a wrong key applies at no node that judges it, so deleting it loses nothing. In a permanent admin dispute a writer's commits list its side's newest step and apply nowhere on the other side: intended (Tess, notes/write-path-design.md §1 F6).

## Built
- A role secret's delete key is its step key (`DeleteSecret::admin`, `DeleteSecret::write`; spec/crypto.md §7).
- A writer lists the keys of the newest step its base's `sync.0` records (`Core::listed_step`).
- The check (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys; notes/delete-keys.md): `Indexes::steps` places each step of a `sync.0` state on a verified path from its genesis with the stamp its path is recorded from; `Core::wrong_keys` judges a header per thing; `Indexes::miskeyed` holds the verdict per (commit, thing), read by every verdict: a miskeyed part applies nowhere and is unjudgeable until a step places its key (osis-engine `validity::a_delta_listing_a_key_no_step_bears_applies_nowhere_spec_crypto_7`).
- The steps reach whoever holds a thing's role parts: `sync.0` is a role part (spec/crypto.md §4 Roles secrets), and the evidence derivation carries the copies recording a listed key's steps on a thing outside the unit.

## Left
1. **A keyset published before its step** (not built; found by reading, no test): an outside fire lists the keys of the inbox's keysets its sender holds (spec/sync-inbox.md §2), and a key is right only where a step bearing it is recorded before the commit (spec/crypto.md §7). A rotation's keyset is written before the step registering it is issued (spec/sync-ids.md §6), so a fire sent in between lists a key whose step is stamped after it: unjudgeable for good, and `ingest_sealed_fire` refuses a fire not judged valid, so the offer is lost. The same window for any writer listing by the published key. To decide: whether "before" should read the keyset's stamp for a key a keyset publishes, or a sender lists the newest keyset whose step it holds.
2. **A key verdict moving under a keyset** (osis-engine `apply::tests_addenda::a_voided_removal_leaves_holders_as_they_were_spec_perm_5_1`, ignored): `check_rebuilt` finds the `secrets` index of a store loaded afresh without an epoch's rotation info (`supersedes`, `rotations`) that the live index holds. Not probed past the failing row. A guess: the `sync.0` write recording the step the rotation lists turns invalid with the voided removal, the rotation's keys turn wrong, and the live index keeps what the keyset noted while a load never notes it.
3. **A role held without delete keys** (two osis-engine tests and public `osis` `sharing::role_before_delete_keys_reads_as_read_spec_crypto_7` ignored: `retention::tests::writer_covers_once_its_delete_keys_come_in_any_order_spec_commits_5`, `resnapshot::owed_resnapshot_waits_for_delete_keys_spec_commits_5`): each withholds the commit giving a thing its secrets and hands its grant, expecting a role with no keys to list. A grant listing the thing's own step is unjudgeable there, so the node reads the thing as its former base's and lists that base's keys (probed: `holds_delete_keys` true at the setup). With the fallback below, no setup found reaches a role without keys (`ready_to_write_in`'s wait); restate the tests on one, or delete the wait with them if none exists.
4. **The writer's fallback** (`CommitSecrets::key`, `published_keys`, the keyset's `delete_key` field; Interim): a node recording no step of the base lists the step key its newest held secret of the role derives, else the one the base's `keys.0` keyset publishes. The published key stays for outside fires (spec/sync-inbox.md §2); the held-secret fallback goes once no writer lacks its base's `sync.0` (a link's redeemer holds K's since `sync.0` is a role part; not tried without it).
5. **Link checks** (spec/crypto.md §7: every link's delete keys against its delta's; a page link failing makes its delta bad, a chunk link reads as absent): not built.
7. Hand tests for a may-grant holder's grant listing its own key (its edge write read by nothing) and for a step arriving after the body listing it.
8. issues/demoted-writer-deletes-at-stores.md option 1 (a level-2 Delete needs the head's write key) closes the demoted writer's residue.
9. Designs that read a party's writes as valid on both sides of a fork, to rewrite under F6: notes/disputes.md, Storage under a fork.
