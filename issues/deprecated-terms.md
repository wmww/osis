# Deprecated terms and concepts
> Summary: The one registry of old words and concepts (Tess: each is gone or listed here, marked deprecated with what removing it takes): a table of those still in the repo (where, why, what removing takes), and the list of those gone, each with what stands in its place.
> Priority: low
> Tags: naming

Everything below is **deprecated**: never build on it, never add a use. Where a row points to another issue, that issue owns the work; this one only keeps the list whole. Not counted: `docs/` (refreshed on request), notes/complaints/ (history logs), libs/kit/patches/ (vendored), and words with another, current sense (the last paragraph). The current words: notes/terms.md.

## Gone
Nothing searches for these: a word here may come back with a new, legitimate sense (a word with no other sense goes in `tools/policy.py` `DEAD_WORDS` instead, and is refused). What stands in each one's place:
- **key actor** (refused by `tools/policy.py` `dead_words`): a thing minted with a key, independent from its minting (spec/permissions.md §3), an ordinary thing; a link key keeps seed-derived first secrets and its static entry.
- **account root** (refused): an account is an ordinary thing the account library makes (osis-account); a node's strongs sit on its node root.
- **client actor**, **main actor**, a client's **own actor** (refused): a client's thing (`Client::thing`) in a client session; the node itself in a main session. **Own actors** (refused): a node's own things (spec/sync-set.md §2). **Link actor** (refused): a link's thing.
- `UnknownActor` (refused), the Actor holder kind, and `actor` for a holder, a link's thing and a lost thing (HolderKind, Holder, LinkId, LostAccess): `UnknownKeys`, `HolderKind::Thing`, `Holder::thing`, `LinkId::thing`, `LostAccess::thing` (notes/terms.md, API words).
- **node card** and `reach.0` (refused), `node.0`: a node's entries in `dial.0` lists; its `peer://` URL is computed from listeners and relays (spec/sync-transport.md §3).
- **chain write** (refused): a write on the writer's own path (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Frontier).
- **signing chain**, account **epochs** and **sides** (refused): a node signs as its node thing; a removal is an ordinary role change (spec/permissions.md §8).
- `Engine::me`: `Engine::tid`, the node thing.
- **device** for a node: a node (one machine may run many nodes); its node thing, node root, the node's key.
- **offer** (a `grant@~0` fire carrying the holder's boxes, and the by-hand pairing offer of the same): the **grant signal** (values alone) and, by hand, a **locator** (spec/wire.md §10); the receiver gets in by its claim.
- **public link** (refused, the type and API call so named too): a **locator** (`Locator`, `Osis::locator`; spec/wire.md §10 format 2), a link that names a thing and where to find it and grants nothing, the public or anyone.
- **entry credential**, **entry box** (a step's box for a link key or the public), **claim set** and `claims_hash`, **static entry**, **public entry** (refused: `ClaimSet`, `StaticEntry`, `EntryBox`, `claims_hash`): a holder's **claim** in the commit boxing it (spec/sync-ids.md §3); the public's claim is its claim key with W in clear.
- **notice** (a role commit's copy sealed per connection; the stranded notice, a `stranded@~0` fire, is current), **tells**, **by role** / **ByRole**, **J** as an open proof and the **Departed** rule (judging **departed** peers), **read-secret**, **lapsed** and **sync-key credentials**, **Behind**, the **claim walk**, **sync copy**, **holders by copies**, **following by a locator alone**, a **step without rotation** listing readers, **step-key erasure** (the registrar erasing a step's private key: the step secret is the sync epoch's): one open rule (spec/sync-opens.md §4), claims and `Head` (spec/sync-ids.md §3, §6). Refused: "claim walk", "sync copy", "holders by copies", "read-secret credential", "sync-key credential", "ByRole", "Departed rule" (or clause; "departed" for a departure stays), "static entry", "entry box" (outside core/test/tla/rotation/Entry.tla, the checked rejected alternative).
- A thing's **several sync IDs** (a sync ID made by each promotion, "both stand, the greatest wins" over a fork's; refused: "greatest wins"): one sync ID per thing, fixed at minting by its genesis seed (spec/sync-ids.md §2); concurrent promotions are sibling first steps.
- **ticket** (the reference kind, the tickets aspect, key-change tickets), `ticket@~0`: grants belong to the thing granted and sync with it; a grant from outside reaches its holder as a grant signal (a `grant@~0` fire of values) that proves nothing. TLS session tickets are another sense.
- **removal notice**, `removal@~0`: a removal is evidence wherever a held write's path uses the removed hop; nothing delivers it elsewhere.
- **chain certificate**, the **author's chain**, "closed under authors", the search for any path giving a write its right: a commit states its path and is judged along it; its evidence is certificate copies per (commit, thing), read off one derivation (notes/evidence-derivation.md).
- **sole certificate**: a certificate copy is judged as any entry (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- **taken** grants, **takenness**, **accepting** or **taking up** a grant: a node's role is by the grants of what it syncs.
- **suspect** flags, lists, message and repair, **state digest**: nothing to repair; a dropped body counts as never received and is fetched from any holder.
- **group** as the unit of sync and sharing (**subgroup**, **synced group**, **retainer**, **release request**, **grant copy**, **tied grant**, **sync record**, **unsync**): a node holds its node closure (what its strong references reach), a sync ID carries a sync unit, a tag collects strong references, a thing is released when its last reference goes, sharing is a grant of a role.
- Kinds of node (**serving**, **blind**, **storage**, **keeper** node; **host** in host_prefix, `atHost`, MaxHost, `host^.0`, CipherHost, hosts_from), **admission** and **keep lists**: no node is a kind; "a node keeping S unread", "a peer", "a source"; a node keeps exactly the sync IDs of its node closure.
- **upload**: an entry is logged (put in a store) and held at a peer.
- **label** for a sync ID (`osis-label`, LabelRole, `label.0`): a sync ID.
- The **person** actor: an account. **Saved peers**: where to dial is a thing's `dial.0`, a grant's hint.
- **plain thing**, **keyed thing**, **thing with own keys**: dependent and independent thing. **first admin**: mint admin. **base actor**, **reader actor**: its mint admin, its independent. **minted under X**: minted with admin X. **owner key**: a thing's key.
- the retired-tag constant (the retired tid tag 1616): nothing; no format keeps a retired tag before stability.
- Eager delivery of removals, the remover's **errand** and **standing duty**: nothing logs a removal for its own sake; it reaches a far thing with that thing's next key rotation (a line stating "no eager delivery" passes).
- The models' **chained** sets (a write chained by a right) and the evidence closure variants built on them: evidence takes every edge write on a stated path's edges (spec/permissions.md §4); a reset names its entry's creation (spec/permissions.md §2).
- **key part** and its **edge pieces** (copies of every grant naming a holder under the holder's sync ID), the mint admin's **record-edge piece**, **public pieces**, **held for keys** (refused by `tools/policy.py` `STUB_DEAD_WORDS`, with **sealed copy** and `EdgePiece`): a unit carries what it references, whole or as a stub, and the stubs of the holders on its chains (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stubs); nothing is kept with a holder for having been granted something (notes/stubs.md).
- **Delivering an edge piece**, **sealed copy** (to a holder's own sync ID), **carried commits** of a grant fire, the writer-of-H branch: a grant need not reach its holder; a grant signal, when sent, carries values only (spec/sync-inbox.md §5); a holder that cannot open a rotation, grant or re-seal boxing it by its wrap takes it by its claim (spec/sync-ids.md §3).
- **Following by the public entry** (`follows_public`, `by_public`): X's stub carries the public's grant (spec/permissions.md §4 Stub).
- **Keys-open travel** (Role changes travel's keys-open half): the chain-holder stubs carry a hop's removal (spec/permissions.md §4 A hop's removal).
- The **keys** source class: the stub class (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source).
- **sync alone** as a holder kind with its own copies: the sync-only cut of a stub; "holding only sync" for the role.
- **entry** for what a boxing commit carries per holder (a claim key and W), with **entry block**, **entry page**, ENTRY_PAGE, EntryKeys, **read**/**sync**/**public's entry** (refused: "entry block", "entry page", EntryKeys): a **claim**, in a **claim block** and **claim pages** (`CLAIM_PAGE`), its W's payload `WPayload`; read, sync and public's claims (spec/sync-ids.md §3). "Entry" is a store's alone (spec/sync-ids.md §5). Wire labels moved with it: `"osis v1 claim block"`, `"osis v1 claim"`, `kdf("claim page")`.
- **claim** for the holder's Ed25519 key pair, **head claims**: its **claim key** (`osis_crypto::claim_key`, `kdf("claim key")`), **head claim keys**.
- **claim key** for the sync epoch's X25519 pair: its **step key** (the public half every step bearing its sync key carries); the private half is the **step secret** (`osis_crypto::step_secret`, `kdf("step key")`).
- **maker**, **minter** and **making** for what mints a thing, its mint admin or its minting (refused: "minter", "making commit", Minter): a thing is **minted** with a mint admin or a key (`CreationRecord::minted_with`, `MintedWith`) by its **minting**; the node that wrote it is the **minting node**; spec/sync-set.md §2 Minting a thing, spec/permissions.md §8 Minting. "Maker" keeps an app's developer and an epoch's maker (its rotation's signer, spec/crypto.md §4).
- `WallMs` (refused), the older name of `Time` below the API: `osis_base::Time`.
- **way in** (refused outside a prose use: "the same way in its copy"), for a holder's claim or for the grants and joins a holder holds a role by: its claim; its grants, joins, or ways.

## Writing rules for any pass (Tess)
- Not word replacement: read each use and rewrite what carries the wrong implication or is plain confusing, e.g. wherever "actor or not" is treated as a property a thing has outside any commit, a mint admin as special to an app, or a team as a kind of thing.
- **Never call an old concept by a new name.** Where the built thing is still the old concept, keep its word and make only the edits that are right looking forward. A section may be marked for deletion or rewriting once a named change lands; that is allowed, not required.

Not old terms (other senses, kept): **group** for a commitment group in a blob prefix, a group chat, a process group; **item** for a list item or a requirements item; **host** for a network or platform host and in **hostile**; **label** for a UI text label (kit, app, Masonry) and a run's or job's name in test tooling (`testenv`, `costs`, the roles-model's checks); **placed**, **placement** for positions in a crdt sequence or the paged store, a step placed in a step tree (`sync_ids::steps::Placed`), a key a step places; **device** for a machine (a stolen device, a phone, the GPU's) and a store's storage (store tests' `Device`); **box** in the encryption sense (a sealed or symmetric box, spec/crypto.md, spec/wire.md §7), for a machine ("this box", notes/build.md) and outpost's **box** (notes/outpost.md); **tombstone** for sync's delete tombstones and crdt element tombstones (never for a purged write); **chained** in osis-crdt; **vouch** for addenda and snapshots; **accept** for a connection or an answer; **admit** for osis-sync's request flow control (`Act::Admit`, `Sessions::admit`), the relay gate and memory commits; **keeper** for a connection-lived thing's keeper accounts (issues/connection-lived-data.md); **shared here** for a sync ID in a connection's shared set; **unsynced** for a write whose sync to disk failed (`Fault::Unsynced`) or a channel not synced; a node's or a forger's **own keys**, a thing's **secrets of its own** (what makes it independent); made or created **under** an epoch, a lock, a grant or an element view; **team** in demo data ("Team standup"); the app's **Person** kind, an address-book contact; `on_change`/`Change<T>` (a value changing, notes/api.md); **server** for a chat community (notes/realms.md) and in third-party names; **blind** as the header's 16 random bytes (spec/crypto.md §7); **open** for opening a sync ID, a blob, an epoch's boxes or a sealed fire (`open_fire`), the `@` signals being **external**; **batch** for a group of store writes, records or events; **ended**, **ending** for what has a span: a grant's or edge's `until`, a link, a stream, a pass, a connection, a subscription, a shared-set member (a thing is never ended but **destroyed**).
