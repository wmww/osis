# Deprecated terms and concepts
> Summary: The one registry of old words and concepts still in the repo (Tess: each is gone or listed here, marked deprecated with what removing it takes). Per row: the term, where it remains, why, what removing takes. Words are fixed by spec/requirements.md Terms and notes/api.md Rules; a row goes when its last use does.
> Priority: low
> Tags: naming

Everything below is **deprecated**: never build on it, never add a use. Where a row points to another issue, that issue owns the work; this one only keeps the list whole. Not counted: `docs/` (refreshed on request), history logs (notes/complaints/), and words with another, current sense (below the table).

Not old terms (other senses, kept): **placement** of sequence elements (osis-crdt, the paged store: positions in a list or text); **device** as storage or the machine an attacker holds (store `Device`, "an attacker holding the device"); the app's **Person** kind, an address-book contact; `on_change`/`Change<T>` (a value changing, notes/api.md); `EphemeralId` (an own write's local number, until `BatchId` folds, above); **server** for a chat community (notes/realms.md) and in third-party names; **blind** as the header's 16 random bytes (spec/crypto.md §7). **box** in the encryption sense (a sealed or symmetric box, "boxed to" an actor: spec/crypto.md, spec/wire.md §7) and for a machine ("this box", notes/build.md), and outpost's **box** (notes/outpost.md).
