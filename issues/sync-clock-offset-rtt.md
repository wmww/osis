# Clock offset from long round trips; own HLC ahead of the corrected clock blocks writes
> Summary: `estimate_offset` takes the median of round-trip samples whatever their round-trip time, so pings whose replies arrive a day later (a device suspended with pings in flight) set the corrected clock about 12 h ahead; once later samples bring it back, the node's own HLC is far ahead of its corrected clock and every local commit is refused (`Misuse("own commit not applicable")`, pending `Drift`) until real time catches up.
> Tags: sync, proto, node, crdt, tests

- Seen in the simulator: `osis_sim::replay("revocation_carol", 12)` (release sweep; ignored test `revocation_carol_seed_12`): a node reconnects, its three initial pings straddle a one-day `advance`, all three samples say +12 h, `SyncEngine` sets that offset, the node writes with `ts` 12 h ahead; later samples correct it; from then on its own commits examine as `Drift` (HLC 43 191 s ahead of the corrected clock) and its writes and the sync engine's own commits fail.
- osis-proto `hello::estimate_offset` (median of per-sample offsets, only `received >= sent` filtered); `SyncEngine::session_event` `ClockOffset` sets the median over connections at once.
- Fix: drop samples whose round trip exceeds a bound (a few seconds; the offset error is at most half the RTT) or take the minimum-RTT sample (NTP style); move the correction gradually or never backwards past the HLC; and judge a node's own new commits against its HLC rather than refusing them as `Drift` (spec/crdt.md §2.2's bound is for received commits). spec/sync.md §6 says "round-trip corrected, robust to an outlier"; an RTT bound could be stated there.
- Other symptoms of the same run shape in the 200-seed sweep: the sync engine's own commits fail (`sync: misuse: own commit not applicable` every pass, so must-hold bodies never go up: `revocation_carol` 74, 81), and other nodes hold the ahead node's heartbeats pending `Drift` at quiescence (`revocation_carol` 135).
