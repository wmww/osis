# No way to give up on a self-removal no host will take
> Summary: since self-removal-crash (round 13 item 206) a node that left a group keeps it until its own removal is placed at a host (`GroupSync::leaving`), then drops it; when no host of the group will ever take the removal (hosts gone, label replaced without it) the node keeps the left group indefinitely and the API offers no way out. Decide: a give-up call (drop now; other members may never learn of the removal, so they keep boxing the node) or accept and document the wait.
> Tags: api, node

From ostrich (round 13 item 206 review). The app sees `leaving` in `status` but cannot act on it. Options: `Osis::abandon(group)` (drops the group and its labels now, keeps the removal commit queued for delivery as a deposit to any host that appears later: honest about members possibly not learning); or a policy bound after which the node drops it alone (a picked number: notes/arbitrary-values.md); or accept the wait (the group is small: a left group holds only the removal and what it had). Fits Tess's whole-API review (issues/api-deferred.md).
