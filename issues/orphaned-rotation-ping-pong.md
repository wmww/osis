# Two rotators can rotate an orphaned epoch back and forth for good
> Summary: An epoch is orphaned at a node that lacks its secret when it boxes no holder that may rotate the role (spec/crypto.md §4). If a rotator's new epoch boxes no other rotator's path, each rotator's epoch is orphaned at the other, which rotates again: an endless exchange of role commits. The plain-first-admin case is fixed; no other path is known, and the rule itself does not rule one out.
> Priority: low
> Tags: keys, engine

- Seen with a plain first admin T of a doc getting secrets of its own: T was left out (no keyset), so the doc's write epoch reached only its maker; a writer of T's team and the admin then alternated rotations (`flows::plain_first_admin_gets_secrets_before_its_thing_spec_perm_3` covers the read loss; the loop showed in the net harness). Fixed by giving T secrets of its own first, where the node administers T (`rotation::own_secrets_rot`, up the record chain).
- Left: a plain-again thing (its only keyset part invalid) re-given secrets by an admin through a direct grant, not of its plain first admin T (`give_own_secrets_again`), still leaves T out: T's members lose the thing silently (nothing tells T's admin to give T secrets). No loop there: the granted admin path is boxed, so the epoch is not orphaned at T's writers.
- Other ways a rotator leaves every other rotator's path out, checked: a keyless path cannot rotate (no possession, `shown_roles`); a holder actor blocked or Seal-out at the rotator is rotated or merged by its own rotators first (`rotate_below`), so that is transient. Only a holder with no keyset at all (plain) is persistent.
- Guard in the rule, if another path turns up: a rotation due for orphaned alone is not made when its new epoch would box no holder that may rotate r (that epoch is orphaned at every other rotator lacking it). It trades the loop for a stall of that node's writes on the role, at no cost. Needs the crypto model (core/test/tla/rotation) to check it keeps every holder reading; not worth it while no path is known.
