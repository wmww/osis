> Summary: osis-perm's `paths` walk keeps no key grant until accounts are ordinary things (A4): the key hop stands at admin where the signer's record holds its key and gives nothing elsewhere, it takes no edge write on one, so its story differential skips the worlds with key moves and no differential writes a roster on a key-record thing.

> Tags: perm, tests

The walk follows roles-model `paths` rule for rule but for the key hop, which is read as an edge with A4 (notes/redesign-plan.md; notes/perm-paths.md, The key hop). Until then a commit signed by its signer's own key reads as in the model (admin where the signer's record names no mint admin, nothing elsewhere: `path::shape`, marked Interim; hand world `a_mark_off_a_descent_of_two_steps_applies_nowhere` (a row of `paths::hand::descent_leaves_and_until`), A's writes); no differential generator signs as a thing with a mint admin. Interim sites in osis-perm's tests against the model:
- **Keys**: `core/perm/tests/it/paths/mod.rs` `story_run` skips every story world with an edge write on a key hop or a commit signed by a key other than its signer's own (moves 54, 55), marked `// Interim (issues/perm-walk-trails-model.md)`. - **Rosters of key-record things**: the model takes edge writes on a thing whose record holds a key (`paths::tests` `a_key_outranks_no_admin`, `a_removed_key_makes_no_may_grant_grant`), the design past A4; spec/permissions.md §1 Interim and the walk apply none (perm `hand_content::an_edge_write_on_its_signer_is_bad_input`: refusing T's writes on itself refuses every edge write on T, since nothing else reaches T).
