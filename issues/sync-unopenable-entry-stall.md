# An entry that unwraps but does not open stalls the label
> Summary: An entry whose wrap opens under a held read secret but whose blob does not open as a commit (garbage plaintext, a prefix disagreeing with its header, or a commit of a newer format version) stays `Kind::Unknown` forever; `pass.rs` `unsettled` then puts `None` (every aspect) in the unsettled set, so no body is must-hold and the node uploads nothing more under that label. Any reader can do this with one deposit; a newer build's commit does it by accident, and is never parked either.
> Tags: sync, security, node, tests

- Seen in the simulator: `osis_sim::replay("malformed_blobs", 1)`, `osis_sim::replay("future_format", 1)` (tier 1 tests ignored with this issue). A reader's single deposit of a well-framed blob of 8 junk bytes, wrapped under the group's read secret, is enough: every node logs `blob ..: commit: cbor: trailing bytes` once, then `verdicts` shows `must-hold` frozen and `unsettled [None]`; later writes stay on their nodes (the quiescent check finds members reading differently, the writes at no server).
- `sync/inbound.rs` `open_one`: `open_commit` failing only logs a diagnostic; the entry stays `Unknown` and is retried. `sync/pass.rs` `unsettled`: an `Unknown` entry that unwraps under a held secret makes every aspect unsettled (meant for a snapshot not yet applied).
- Parking (spec/versioning.md §2): `open_commit` decodes the commit (`CommitBlob::decode`) before `Engine::ingest`, so a commit of format version `FORMAT_VERSION + 1` fails there (`UnknownVersion`) and never reaches `ingest`'s parking path; the node does not hold it parked (it does keep the blob).
- Fix: an entry that unwraps but fails to open as a commit for a malformed reason is marked bad (not `Unknown`), never retried, and left out of `unsettled`; whether writers delete it (must-not-hold, by delete key) is a separate call. A parked-class error (`ErrorClass::Parked`) hands the plaintext to `ingest` (parked, pending) and records the entry so it is neither retried nor unsettling (spec/versioning.md §2: relayed like a blind node does).
