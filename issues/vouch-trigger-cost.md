# Removals judged per component change, and `vouch_wake` per upstream party
> Summary: `flows/vouches.rs` judges again, at every change of its component, every held removal that does not apply (a lookup each) or has candidates (a pass over the demoted node's commits on each thing), forever; a removed party's never-applying removals add one judgement per honest write there each; `vouch_wake` holds one entry per removal per party upstream of the holders it names, so a removal of a team costs its whole membership.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, perm, security

Measured (debug, `costs` counters, `apply::tests_vouches` / `tests_addenda` worlds):
- **Non-applying removals**: X, removed, writes k junk removals of K on t (an empty frontier; never applying). A grant by W on t then judges k removals at W (k = 2: 2; k = 20: 20); a commit by K stamped before them (`note_applied`) judges k as well. Each judgement is lookups, no walk. Bounded by what X wrote, paid by every write in t's component at every node holding it, for as long as the removals are held (role history: for good). X naming a holder of its own and writing w backdated commits pays it k × w.
- **Removals with candidates**: a witness that wrote its addendum keeps the removal filed under its component while its candidates exist (always), so every role change there (anyone's grant) re-runs `addenda_owed`: one pass over the demoted node's commits on N (n = 5: 5 scanned; n = 50: 50) plus the addenda fixpoint on N (`validity::vouching_on`, uncached across calls). m such removals: m × their commits per role change. A removed party sets n by its own writes before R.
- **`vouch_wake`**: per judged removal, one entry per party in `Held::upstream` of the holders it names (memory, rebuilt at every open by judging every held removal). Removing an account names it and its devices (a few entries); removing a team names its members, their devices and its mint chain's holders: 100 removals of teams in a 1,000-member organisation are about 10^5 entries (about 10 MB) at every node holding them, forever. A removed party's junk removals naming a large team add a team's worth each.

Wanted: perm reporting the removals whose applied state or in-question set changed (so neither class is filed per component); the addenda check keyed on what it reads (the demoted node's commits received in the window are fixed once R is held; only the node's role on N and its own addenda counting change); `vouch_wake` keyed by the named holders, the wake asking whether an applied commit ordered no later than a filed removal (`ts` ≤ the newest filed: live traffic pays nothing) reaches a filed holder (`Held::reach` from its author and edge targets). Counting tests at k and 10k for each.
