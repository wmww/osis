# Pending commits keep expired values
> Summary: A commit (or a late part) held pending past its delta's expiry keeps the delta's values on disk until it applies, maybe never (spec/commits.md §5 wants no copy past T); dropping the part at T instead loses its non-value writes and breaks arrival-order independence. Needs an owner call.
> Tags: security, engine, retention, owner-call

Repro: `shred::repro_expired_while_pending_values_kept` (`#[ignore]`). Same for a late part that waits (`apply::late_parts` `Exam::Pending`) and for a pending commit a held purge request names (purge has the same shape until the commit applies).

Tried (branch pending-expiry, reverted): store pending/parked records without parts past T, an expiry timer per kept expiring part stripping it at T (shredded rewrite; an applied commit's late-part record left empty goes). It fixes the repro and a late-part variant, but fails `order::expired_residue_survives_unmerge_spec_crdt_4_6` and `order::valueless_list_order_independent_spec_crdt_4_7`: in the reversed order the commit arrives past T before its thing's `created.0`, so the dropped part's non-value writes and value-less stamps (spec/crdt.md §4.7: "its non-value writes stay"; the residue) never join, and an older value shows where the order as made reads absent.

Why it cannot be done locally: keeping the non-value writes needs the part verified (delta hash keyed by the thing's record nonce, signature by the author's record) before stripping, and while the commit waits for exactly those records (`UnknownCreated`, `MissingChain` of the author) the node cannot verify it; a stripped copy can never be verified later (the hash binds the full bytes). Where the records are known (other waits) a verified value-less copy could be kept as a residue, at a cost of a pending-residue form joined at apply.

Options: (A) security first: a delta held pending at T (or arriving past T while it waits) counts as never received; its non-value writes come only from snapshots; the order oracles treat it so. (B) convergence first: spec §5/§6 except copies held pending until they apply (bounded by the pending caps). (C) A plus the verified-residue path where verification is possible, narrowing the divergence to unverifiable waits.
