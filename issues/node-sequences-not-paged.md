# Texts and lists: what still loads whole or writes more than it needs
> Summary: Most sequence ops load only their pages (notes/engine-paged.md, Paged state), but an edit against a stale or repeated base, a list edit whose window the list does not lend, an op with struct or map keys, writing a whole collection, and a snapshot of it holding writes with an end load a paged sequence whole; a cold edit writes the stored placement's B-tree leaves (about twice the bytes of the edit's pages alone); collections inside list elements are not paged apart.
> Priority: low
> Tags: performance, engine, store, crdt

Counted: osis-engine `scale::text_edits_are_flat` (by hand), `scale::map_edit_is_flat`, `set_by_value_is_flat`; whole fallbacks show as `paged op whole:` cost spans.
- **Placement bytes per cold edit**: an insert or delete rewrites two placement-table leaves (the top with its group rows, and the block's), a move more; about 15 KB on top of the edit's own pages at 10^5 chars. Byte-packed blocks and chunks with compact group rows would halve the table but need a block-to-pack map across splits. Rejected: keying chunks near blocks (an insert's chunk is the newest dots', not near its block); moving the top and groups into the aspect unit (grows by about what it saves).
- **Whole loads**: an edit whose base no longer matches the read near its change or has another length (a session flushing against its old base after a remote change), or whose window repeats elsewhere in the base (exact without a whole load needs the session's base version); an op whose layered follow or placement fails other than by a miss (`Partial::whole_pass`); a list edit with large items whose window the list does not lend; ops whose map keys or set values have struct or map parts (matched as the writer's type projects them, which the index cannot see).
- **Moves**: a move's follow finds each moved subtree's end by depth (`Placement::past`); after many moves depths are uneven and it can read most blocks.
- **Snapshots once writes end**: a snapshot of a paged sequence (or a collection paged inside another's elements) holding writes with an end reads the stored state whole before joining what they superseded (`pages::joins_by_page`): `osis_crdt::Joined` cuts what it joins at a page's dot range, and a range record may straddle it. Fix: split the joined records at the page's bounds.
- **Not paged apart**: collections inside list elements or at a `Slots` position (needs the element record's anchors kept apart, osis-crdt work).
- O(aspect state) by nature (notes/engine-paged.md, Whole rewrites): a reset of a paged collection or above it, a validity rebuild, a page coming to hold a collection to page.
