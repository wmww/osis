# What a node pays for holding many things with their own sync ID
> Summary: Central since a closure is many sync IDs (notes/sync-unit-scope.md). Partly counted (notes/sync-unit-design.md §8): an idle reconnect's round trips, bytes and stranding judgements do not grow, nor a small commit's work, but a reconnect still re-judges every shared member's standing and certificate blobs (about 52 store rows each with a reader, 95 with a node keeping them unread); a first sync costs about 4 deliveries and 47 KB per unit. Left: the record holding its shared set, the first-sync batching, and counting making many at once, a removal's cascade and start-up.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, perf

Nothing bounds how many sync IDs one commit is carried under today; with a unit being one independent thing and its dependents a commit is under one per unit it writes.

Kept counts at n and 10n shared sync IDs: core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs (bytes of an idle reconnect; strandings and register plans judged at one; strandings at a commit; duties as time passes). No kept test counts a first sync.

## Counted (debug, at n and 10n; tables and method in notes/sync-unit-design.md §8)
- **Idle reconnect** (nothing out of date; core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs at 2 and 20 things; the ignored `idle::probe_counts` prints every counter): deliveries, bytes, `status: stranded` and, with a reader, `inbox: register judged` flat. Per shared sync ID with a reader: about 52 store rows read, 3 `opens standing`, 5 `read: certificate blobs`, 2 `sync set: entered`. With a node keeping them unread: about 95 store rows, 3 `opens standing`, 3 `read: certificate blobs`, 2 each of `inbox: register judged` and `status: lacking`, 7 `perm: roles walked`.
- **The first reconnect after first contact** proves only the account where the record names the cards' key actors too, so it re-tests every credential and sends whole filters (spec/sync-opens.md §3: other actors than the record names): O(shared sync IDs) once per contact. Goes with A4 (notes/redesign-plan.md: accounts as ordinary things): which actors a record names is the account machinery's.
- **One small commit**: deliveries, bytes and `status: stranded` flat; about 1 store row read per sync ID held (an engine `read` per thing); no duty judgement (`life:`) per thing.
- **A reader's restart while connected**: 373 B per sync ID at 6e38bcd03 (recount).
- **First sync of a closure** (a root with n independent members, the built model): per member 4.0 deliveries, 47 KB (23 KB of it sent by the new reader back to the maker, unexplained), 7.6 signs, 42 transactions, 16 step verifications and 16 stamp verifications. Kept unread under the root's one sync ID: 1.9 deliveries, 17 KB, 2 entries per member.
- **Held per sync ID** with one small value and two grants: a node keeping it unread 37 store rows (4.3 KB) and 6.2 blobs (35.9 KB); a reader 48 rows (5.2 KB), 8.2 blobs (43 KB) and 29.7 KB of engine rows. Six rows of about 500 B exist per sync ID whatever it holds. About half of a sync ID's entries name blobs also held under other sync IDs (certificate copies).

## To fix (each grows with the wrong thing: notes/principles.md, Fast by construction)
- **A reconnect re-judges every member.** A connection taking a record re-adds each member one by one (opens `take`: `member_moved`, `holding`, a `Moved::Joined` and `rejudge` per member; `release` a `Moved::Left` per member), and every consumer keys its per-member state by connection, so each is judged again: O(shared set) per reconnect, as spec/sync.md §2 allows ("the connections whose shared set it changes") and spec/sync-opens.md §6 asks ("a member is re-judged when a connection takes a record"). What is left, by consumer:
  - opens standing (core/sync/src/opens/holds.rs `standing`, an `Effects` keyed (connection, S)): `rejudge` wants it at each take. J is a function of the peer's named actors and S, so it can be keyed by (record, S) and followed while the record is held in memory, a take reading it as followed; spec/sync-opens.md §6 then says a member is judged when its standing, credentials or this node's sync set move, not when a connection takes the record.
  - certificate blobs (core/sync/src/read/mod.rs `cert_blobs`, `Ground::cert_blobs_missing`): `gone` reads every source's stream and `deletes` names each store source by `ConnId`, so a source leaving and joining moves every S's key. Keyed by record (`Source`) and read only where the own store's blobs leave something to delete or forget, a reconnect would move none.
  - inbox register plans and progress (core/sync/src/inbox/register.rs, keyed (connection, S)) at a store source, and lacking counts (core/sync/src/status/mod.rs `lacking_fx`, keyed (S, connection)): each starts from nothing at every connection. Both are facts of the record (where the store stands in S's path, what its stream showed: `known` is the record's already), so keyed by record they survive a reconnect.
  - `Moved` per member: streams' `holders`, status' `sources`, read's `Views` and inbox's offers and suspects are written per member at each take and release. The structure: the record holds its shared set, a connection takes it whole by one fact (record R is connected on C), and per-S readers that need the connection (status' `of`, heard out, read's `pullable`) read that fact through the record only where they depend on it; nothing per member is written at a take or release.
  - engine `things_entered` (core/engine/src/sync/set.rs) re-wants every followed link at each follow that is not quiet (`sync set: entered`, 2 per thing at a reconnect): a feed of links followed and unfollowed would want only those.
- The deliveries per unit at a first sync: opens found in one round should batch (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining).
