# What a node pays for holding many sync IDs
> Summary: Stage S1 of notes/redesign-plan.md. Counted in debug tests at n and 10n sync IDs: an idle reconnect's deliveries, bytes and named judgements (members judged, standing, strandings, register plans, lacking counts, certificate blobs, sync set entries) and a small commit's work are flat (core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs), but a reconnect still hands every shared member to streams, status, read and inbox (`Moved::Joined`/`Left` per member): about 24 store rows per member with a reader, 17 with a node keeping them unread, read by streams and read (named below). A first sync costs 4 deliveries and 47 KB per member, 23 KB of it sent back by the new reader, unexplained. Fix those, then count what is still uncounted.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, perf

Every independent thing has a sync ID of its own, and a node holds one per independent thing it reaches, so work per sync ID held at a reconnect or a commit grows with the wrong thing (notes/principles.md, Fast by construction; spec/requirements.md Cost). Kept counts at n and 10n shared sync IDs: core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs (bytes of an idle reconnect; strandings and register plans judged at one; strandings at a commit; duties as time passes); the ignored `idle::probe_counts` prints every counter. No kept test counts a first sync.

## Record-keyed state, as built
A kept record with a member stays in memory from its first take until it goes (evicted past `RECORD_BUDGET`, twin-dropped; one with none leaves memory when no connection holds it), its peer filter's entries dropped while no connection holds it and read again at its next take; opens gives `member_at` (stamped per (record, S), moved by no take or release) and `conn_of` ("record R is connected on C", stamped per record). Keyed by record and kept across its connections: J (`Holds::standing`, `Opens::followed`), register plans and progress, lacking counts. Each goes with S leaving the record or the sync set, or the record going (`flush` returns the records that went; the driver tells inbox and status, `record_gone`). What invalidates a record's member: J's reads moving (a removal or demotion, the account's key no longer counting: memo stamps), S's credentials or holding moving (`changed_at`), each judged in its step whether or not a connection holds the record (`Opens::judge`; a close with no connection is `Moved::Gone`, which drops the register keys and lacking count of (S, record)); what the record names moving at a take, or a take of a record read from its rows (a restart's: every member judged again); a step registered (the recorded path, a plan's read). Bound: the kept records with a member, all rows counted in `RECORD_BUDGET`; a hostile dialer makes a record per fresh key actor it proves (in memory only while connected), each evicting the least recently used past the budget (an honest record evicted costs its peer a first contact: a whole filter and every credential tested again), and gets a member only by proof that it may hold S.

## To fix
- **A reconnect still hands every member to every module** (24 and 17 store rows per member above). The take judges nothing that did not move (`opens: members judged` flat), but `Opens::take` still pushes a `Moved::Joined` per member and `release` a `Moved::Left` per member, and each consumer keeps per-(connection, S) state built from them. Store rows read per member at an idle reconnect, by caller (throwaway backtrace probe on `store rows read`, at the commit closing the take's re-judge):
  - read, 10: `Live::source` (read/live.rs) reads `Known::head` (a record row) for each source of S each time read's memos of S run; they run because `Views` (by (connection, S), stamped per (S, source)) moves at each `Left` and `Joined`. `SrcView` carries the connection and `full` per connection, so read's judgements of S depend on which connection holds the source: they must read the record's facts by source and the connection (`conn_of`) only where they fetch or ask.
  - streams, 13 (6 to 13 at a store): each `Joined` loads the source's `Place` (`AT_STREAM` and `JOIN_FROM` rows, 2 per member: the place is dropped from memory with its connection) and reads `Known::kept` (`KEPT`, 3 per member: compared with the class at each join, though the class is the record's and moves only at an open); each `Left` turns a member at the stream into stopped-at-the-cursor and the next `Joined` back, marking it `stale`, so the first applied message writes every member's place rows (5 per member). A member at the stream when its connection lets the record go is still at the stream's cursor: nothing per member need move at a release or a take.
  - opens, 0.7 to 0.9: the peer filter's entries are dropped while no connection holds the record and read again at its take (`records::filter_values`, O(filter rows), and a filter holds the shown credentials of every shared S). Keeping them costs up to 2^22 entries per record in memory (notes/sync-opens.md, Durability): a decision, not a defect, unless the filter is kept or tested without being read whole.
  - status (`sources` by connection), inbox (`offers.joined`, a suspect list sent at each member's join, register keyed by record already) read no rows per member but keep per-(connection, S) state built from the `Moved`.
  The structure: the take hands the record over by one fact ("record R is connected on C", `conn_of`, built); consumers key what they keep by record and read the connection only where they send or ask; `Moved` is then per member only for a member that opens, changes or closes. Streams' join and Pull state is protocol state (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining): its state table (below) comes first.
- **The first reconnect after first contact** proves only the account where the record names the cards' key actors too, so it re-tests every credential and sends whole filters (spec/sync-opens.md §3): O(shared sync IDs) once per contact. Goes with A4 (accounts as ordinary things).
- **A first sync**: opens found in one round should batch (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining): 4 deliveries per member now; and the 23 KB per member the new reader sends back.

## Rights ended while away
`opens::a_right_ended_while_away_serves_nothing_after_it_at_the_reconnect_spec_sync_opens_6` (world: node 2 away from both, node 1 hears of the change from node 0, then meets node 2), with the scripted tests that pin the judgement's step:
| How the right ends | What judges it with no connection | Served before judgement |
|---|---|---|
| removal held, at a reader | standing (J departed) and the rotation's credentials, in the step | nothing |
| removal, at a node keeping S unread | the registered step moves the credentials (`changed_at`) | nothing |
| a grant's `until` passing | J and the finalize's rotation | nothing |
| device revoked (node 2's account, paired to a new node, removes it) | the account's rotation rotates t; node 2's reconnect proves at a revoked epoch, so nothing opens whatever the record held (the world row passes with every away judgement removed) | nothing |
| a proof (or sync key) open ending at a rotation | the credentials move; `until` is the position then (`opens::an_until_is_the_position_of_the_change_while_away_spec_sync_opens_6`, scripted: a read-secret open, the log moving on before the take) | up to the change's position |
| fork side switch | a J move like any other (`opens::standing_is_followed_per_record_across_connections_spec_sync_opens_6`, scripted: J departed while away closes the member in its step) | nothing |
Not built as world rows: the proof open and the fork side switch (an account fork needs a removed node's competing rotation; the scripted rows pin the opens rule both reduce to). Mutations: the take never judging, with standing and credentials still judged in place, passes all rows; with both in-place judgements off as well, the first world row fails; with standing alone in place, the node-keeping-unread row fails; with credentials alone, every world row passes and the standing test fails (its change moves J only).

## Streams' state, as a reconnect touches it
Per connection, protocol state (stays the connection's): as server (`serve::Serve`) the follow cursor and `seq`, the first Pull seen, joins named and merging until an Applied, `unapplied`, `unpushed`, `joins_left`, `own`, the ephemeral listing per member, `ending`/`told_until` (a copy of the members' `until`); as requester (`pull::Pulling`) the Pull out with its joins, received cursor, `seq`, gaps, messages in hand and `in_hand`, fetches out and queued, `failed`, `settled`/`taken`, retry and rejoin waits, listings under way, Full/Refused to tell, `told_full`, `applied_owed`, `out_at`/`answered_at`; `caught`, `busy`/`busy_of`, `caught_at` (caught up per (connection, S), spec/sync-status.md §1: kept as is). Per source, the record's (durable rows, in memory only while a connection pulls): `Place` (cursor, at the stream, joining, stopped, closed, told, stale), `Known` (present, head, kept, refusals). Per S, `holders` (connections S is a member on: derivable from opens' members and `conn_of`). What moves: the record's (a member at the stream stays so across a release and a take; `kept` is written at an open, not compared at each join). What a hostile peer controls per message, every key's bound and flood test, and every clock read with its wake are still to be tabled before the change (spec/sync-streams.md §4).
