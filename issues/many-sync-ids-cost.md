# What a node pays for holding many sync IDs
> Summary: Stage S1 of notes/redesign-plan.md. Counted in debug tests at n and 10n sync IDs: an idle reconnect's deliveries, bytes, named judgements and streams' store rows, and a small commit's work, are flat (core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs); read still reads about 10 store rows per shared member at an idle reconnect (its acts and `full` name the connection: two decisions below), and status and inbox follow members per connection. A first sync costs 4 deliveries and 47 KB per member, 23 KB of it sent back by the new reader, unexplained. Fix those, then count what is still uncounted.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, perf

Every independent thing has a sync ID of its own, and a node holds one per independent thing it reaches, so work per sync ID held at a reconnect or a commit grows with the wrong thing (notes/principles.md, Fast by construction; spec/requirements.md Cost). Kept counts at n and 10n shared sync IDs: core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs (bytes of an idle reconnect; strandings and register plans judged at one; strandings at a commit; duties as time passes); the ignored `idle::probe_counts` prints every counter. No kept test counts a first sync.

## Record-keyed state, as built
A kept record with a member stays in memory from its first take until it goes (evicted past `RECORD_BUDGET`, twin-dropped; one with none leaves memory when no connection holds it), its peer filter's entries dropped while no connection holds it and read again at its next take; opens gives `member_at` (stamped per (record, S), moved by no take or release) and `conn_of` ("record R is connected on C", stamped per record). Keyed by record and kept across its connections: J (`Holds::standing`, `Opens::followed`), register plans and progress, lacking counts. Each goes with S leaving the record or the sync set, or the record going (`flush` returns the records that went; the driver tells inbox and status, `record_gone`). What invalidates a record's member: J's reads moving (a removal or demotion, the account's key no longer counting: memo stamps), S's credentials or holding moving (`changed_at`), each judged in its step whether or not a connection holds the record (`Opens::judge`; a close with no connection is `Moved::Gone`, which drops the register keys and lacking count of (S, record)); what the record names moving at a take, or a take of a record read from its rows (a restart's: every member judged again); a step registered (the recorded path, a plan's read). Bound: the kept records with a member, all rows counted in `RECORD_BUDGET`; a hostile dialer makes a record per fresh key actor it proves (in memory only while connected), each evicting the least recently used past the budget (an honest record evicted costs its peer a first contact: a whole filter and every credential tested again), and gets a member only by proof that it may hold S.

## To fix
S1 briefs 1 (the join per shared sync ID at an idle reconnect) and 2 (the store rows behind an idle reconnect) are not done: the judgements and streams' rows are flat, read's rows are not, and read, status and inbox wait on the decisions below.
- **A reconnect still hands every member to read, status and inbox** (14 and 7 store rows per member above). The take judges nothing that did not move (`opens: members judged` flat) and streams follows the record, but `Opens::take` still carries a `Moved::Joined` per member to the other three and `release` a `Moved::Left`, and each keeps per-(connection, S) state built from them. Store rows read per member at an idle reconnect, by caller (`store rows read <- module`, `osis_base::costs::scoped`; a throwaway backtrace probe for the call sites):
  - read, 10: `Live::source` (read/live.rs) reads `Known::head` (a record row) for each source of S each time read's memos of S run; they run because `Views` (by (connection, S), stamped per (S, source)) moves at each carried `Left` and `Joined`. Not a keying change alone, two decisions (stopped here):
    1. *Acts name the connection.* Each act a judgement adds (`Act::Deposit`, `Delete`, `DeleteFreed`, `Again`) carries `conn`, which is how "a restart or a reconnect carries each act out once more" works (notes/sync-read.md: a new `ConnId` is a new act). Keyed by source, the judgement no longer moves at a reconnect, so something else must say which acts a new connection is owed again. Proposal: acts by (S, commit, source); the act runs when `conn_of(source)` is some, and a take re-runs only the acts owed to that record (an index of pending acts per record: O(acts owed there), not O(members)); an act whose answer came (a Delete or deposit taken) is done for the record, not the connection, which changes what is resent after a reconnect (today: every act once more).
    2. *`full` is the connection's* (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full: told per connection, ended with it), and judge.rs reads `SrcView::full` in what counts as held at a store (`toward`, the covered-dropped and delete rules). Either full becomes a fact of the record (a spec change: told once per record, kept with what the source showed, ended by `full` false or S leaving the record), or the judgement stops reading it and only the act gates on it.
  - streams: none. A kept record is taken and released by one fact each (`Moved::Taken`, `Released`), its place kept in memory with the record (`Streams::record_left`), `Known::kept` written at an open; `idle::an_idle_reconnect_reads_no_place_per_member_spec_sync_streams_4` counts it. Opens still carries the members one by one to read, status and inbox (`Rejoin::Taken`, `Leaving::Connection` on a record; Interim), which streams ignores: they go once those three key by record.
  - opens, 0.7 to 0.9: the peer filter's entries are dropped while no connection holds the record and read again at its take (`records::filter_values`, O(filter rows), and a filter holds the shown credentials of every shared S). Keeping them costs up to 2^22 entries per record in memory (notes/sync-opens.md, Durability): a decision, not a defect, unless the filter is kept or tested without being read whole.
  - status, inbox: no rows per member, but each keeps per-(connection, S) state built from the carried `Moved`, and moving it is a decision too: status's `sources` feed held elsewhere with each source's reported position and `until` (per connection, spec/sync-opens.md §6) and, for one just left, the `LeftAt` taken at the release (`held_at`); caught up stays per (connection, S) (spec/sync-status.md §1). Keyed by record, held elsewhere must read the record's last reported position across connections (opens' `Positions` are per connection), which says what a record's report means once its connection is gone. Inbox sends a suspect list at each member's join (`suspects.joined`) and an offer per join (`offers.joined`): at a reconnect, whether the peer is owed them again is the same question as read's acts.
  The structure: the take hands the record over by one fact (built: `Moved::Taken`, `Released`); read, status and inbox key what they keep by record and read the connection only where they send or ask (the decisions above); then opens stops carrying members (`Rejoin::Taken`, `Leaving::Connection` on a record go) and `Moved` is per member only for a member that opens, changes or closes.
- **The first reconnect after first contact** proves only the account where the record names the cards' key actors too, so it re-tests every credential and sends whole filters (spec/sync-opens.md §3): O(shared sync IDs) once per contact. Goes with A4 (accounts as ordinary things).
- **A first sync**: opens found in one round should batch (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining): 4 deliveries per member now; and the 23 KB per member the new reader sends back.
