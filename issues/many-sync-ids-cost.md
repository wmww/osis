# What a node pays for holding many sync IDs
> Summary: Stage S1 of notes/redesign-plan.md. Counted in debug tests at n and 10n sync IDs: an idle reconnect's deliveries, bytes and named judgements (standing, strandings, register plans, lacking counts, certificate blobs, sync set entries) and a small commit's work are flat (core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs), but a reconnect still hands every shared member to streams, status, read and inbox (`Moved` per member) and re-judges each at the take: about 24 store rows per member with a reader, 17 with a node keeping them unread. A first sync costs 4 deliveries and 47 KB per member, 23 KB of it sent back by the new reader, unexplained. Fix those, then count what is still uncounted.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, perf

Every independent thing has a sync ID of its own, and a node holds one per independent thing it reaches, so work per sync ID held at a reconnect or a commit grows with the wrong thing (notes/principles.md, Fast by construction; spec/requirements.md Cost). Kept counts at n and 10n shared sync IDs: core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs (bytes of an idle reconnect; strandings and register plans judged at one; strandings at a commit; duties as time passes); the ignored `idle::probe_counts` prints every counter. No kept test counts a first sync.

## Record-keyed state, as built
A kept record with a member stays in memory from its first take until it goes (evicted past `RECORD_BUDGET`, twin-dropped; one with none leaves memory when no connection holds it), its peer filter's entries dropped while no connection holds it and read again at its next take; opens gives `member_at` (stamped per (record, S), moved by no take or release) and `conn_of` ("record R is connected on C", stamped per record). Keyed by record and kept across its connections: J (`Holds::standing`, `Opens::followed`), register plans and progress, lacking counts. Each goes with S leaving the record or the sync set, or the record going (`flush` returns the records that went; the driver tells inbox and status, `record_gone`). What invalidates a record's member: J's reads moving (a removal or demotion, the account's key no longer counting: memo stamps; while no connection holds the record the member is let go and judged at its next take), what the record names moving at a take (every member judged again), S's credentials or holding moving (`changed_at`), a step registered (the recorded path, a plan's read). Bound: the kept records with a member, all rows counted in `RECORD_BUDGET`; a hostile dialer makes a record per fresh key actor it proves (in memory only while connected), each evicting the least recently used past the budget (an honest record evicted costs its peer a first contact: a whole filter and every credential tested again), and gets a member only by proof that it may hold S.

## To fix
- **A reconnect still hands and judges every member** (24 and 17 store rows per member above). `Opens::take` pushes a `Moved::Joined` per member and re-judges each (`rejudge`: J kept, but credentials' beacons hashed again; marked Interim, core/sync/src/opens/mod.rs `take`), and `release` a `Moved::Left` per member; each consumer still keeps per-(connection, S) state built from them: streams' `holders` and joins, status' `sources` (by connection), read's `Views` (`by_conn`, stamps per (S, source) moved by each leave and join), inbox's offers and suspects (a suspect list sent at each member's join). The structure: the record holds its shared set; a connection takes it whole by one fact (`conn_of`, built), so consumers key what they keep by record (as register and lacking do) and read the connection only where they send or ask; `Moved` is then per member only for a member that opens, changes or closes, and the take re-judges only the members whose credentials (`changed_at` past the record's last judgement) or standing (let go while away) moved since. Streams' per-member join and Pull state is the largest part; it is protocol state (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining), so it needs its own state table.
- **A change ending a member of a record no connection holds is judged at the record's next take**, not in the step that applies it (spec/sync-opens.md §6). What holds: the take judges every member before anything of S goes on that connection, so nothing past a right that ended is served (`opens::a_right_ended_while_away_serves_nothing_after_it_at_the_reconnect_spec_sync_opens_6`: removal at a reader and at a node keeping S unread, a grant's `until`); but an ended member's `until` is the position as of the take, which differs from the step's only where it is not a superseding rotation's logged entry: a sync key or proof open at a reader (the log's end) and an open at a node keeping S unread (its head's event): there entries logged between the change and the take are served up to it. Piece 4 (judging a record's members in place, Interim in `Opens::take`) closes it; its take must still judge each member whose standing was let go or whose S changed while away (removing the take's re-judge fails the test above).
- **A record's per-member keys at inbox and status outlive S leaving the sync set while no connection holds the record** (register plans and progress, lacking counts: no `Moved::Left` reaches them; opens drops the member and its standing): they go only with the record (evicted, twin-dropped). Bounded by the sync IDs that were ever store members of a kept record, not by `RECORD_BUDGET`; drop them as S leaves (status' and inbox's sync set) when the per-member `Moved` goes.
- **The first reconnect after first contact** proves only the account where the record names the cards' key actors too, so it re-tests every credential and sends whole filters (spec/sync-opens.md §3): O(shared sync IDs) once per contact. Goes with A4 (accounts as ordinary things).
- **A first sync**: opens found in one round should batch (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining): 4 deliveries per member now; and the 23 KB per member the new reader sends back.
