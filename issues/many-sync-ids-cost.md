# What a node pays for holding many sync IDs
> Summary: Stage S1 of notes/redesign-plan.md. Briefs 1-2 are done: counted in debug tests at n and 10n sync IDs (core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs), an idle reconnect's deliveries, bytes, named judgements, members moved and store rows of streams, read, status and inbox are flat, and a small commit's work; opens carries no member at a take. Brief 3 in part: a first sync of a closure (core/sync/tests/it/first.rs) sends back no copy of what the maker holds and fetches each blob once (57 KB to 29 KB per member). A Fetch carries several sync IDs' bodies, so a first sync's deliveries stop growing per member (121 at 5n and 10n). Left: the echo, 22 step verifications and 2,900 store rows per member, opens' filter reload, about 15 fixed rows per input, the first reconnect after first contact.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, perf

Every independent thing has a sync ID of its own, and a node holds one per independent thing it reaches, so work per sync ID held at a reconnect or a commit grows with the wrong thing (notes/principles.md, Fast by construction; spec/requirements.md Cost). Kept counts at n and 10n shared sync IDs: core/sync/tests/it/idle.rs (bytes of an idle reconnect; strandings and register plans judged at one; strandings at a commit; duties as time passes); the ignored `idle::probe_counts` prints every counter. No kept test counts a first sync.

Held per sync ID, a thing with one small value granted to two accounts (flat, per unit, at 6e38bcd03): a node keeping it unread 37 store rows (4.3 KB), 12.2 entries naming 6.2 blobs of its own (35.9 KB), 8.6 engine rows (8.9 KB); a reader 48 store rows (5.2 KB), 14.2 entries, 8.2 blobs (43 KB), 40 engine rows (29.7 KB). About half a sync ID's entries name blobs held under other sync IDs too (certificate copies). Six rows of about 500 B exist per sync ID whatever it holds (`F/ H/ P/ Q/ U/ u/`, the path's 319 B the largest), plus 1.3 to 2 rows each of `T/ Y/ t/`.

What the new reader sent back, per member (21.3 KB at main before brief 3), cause by cause:
- **Its own copies of what the maker holds** (14.6 KB of FetchReply, 0.6 KB of Fetch, and the announcements of them): waste. Entering the closure, it judged each member's sync ID from the root's bodies before it had listed the member at any source, so it logged, under each member's sync ID, a fresh copy of every certificate there (the root's grants, its own and node 1's account chains), a fresh encryption of the member's keyset (9.3 KB) and its own record blob; the maker fetched each, found it lost to its own (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates: one of two record blobs wins; a copy of a commit held stands as it came) and dropped it. Spec: spec/sync-streams.md §5 (a node judges once order 0 is here), §3 Own store: an entry a source holds, pulled, stands as the log. Fixed: another's body or certificate is logged only past S's first sync here, a complete pass of S, or S's thing made here (`first::a_new_reader_logs_no_copy_of_what_its_source_holds_spec_sync_judgement_3`).
- **Fetch rounds**: one Fetch carries one fill class's bodies whatever sync IDs they are under (spec/wire.md §11.2 Fetch), so the reader sends 5 Fetches at 5n and 10n members (a Fetch per sync ID: 2.1 per member).
- **The echo** (2.8 KB of Stream, 1.1 KB of PullReply): needed by the spec as written. Both sides pull every shared member (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Stream: "both sides pull"), so the reader lists back to the maker each entry it took under each member's sync ID, which the maker holds; spec/sync-status.md §2 (held elsewhere) reads a peer's stream showing an own commit as held there. Candidate 3 of notes/thing-cost.md (no entry echoed to the peer it came from), waiting on S1 settling the entry flow (notes/redesign-plan.md, Slimmer things).

Maker to reader, the waste (10.3 KB per member at main): a certificate copy carried under every member's sync ID (the root's rotation 7 KB, the grant 1.1 KB, two more) was fetched once per sync ID announcing it: the held-bytes check ran only at the announcement, and a blob asked under one sync ID did not hold back its ask under another on the same connection. Fixed (`first::a_blob_is_fetched_once_whatever_sync_ids_carry_it_spec_sync_streams_4`).

By design, per member (each stage's baseline):
- The member's keyset (9.3 KB, four KEM keys) fetched twice: under the root's sync ID (its unit carries the member) and as another blob under the member's own (per-sync-ID copies, spec/sync-judgement.md §1). The multi-unit carriage stays; the keyset's size is A5's (cards, the KEM keys most things publish; notes/thing-cost.md).
- At the grant, the maker logs four certificate copies under each member's sync ID (the grant, its chain, node 2's account chain: spec/permissions.md §4 Role changes travel, Logging certificates) and stamps them: about 4 signs and 0.6 KB of entries per member before the reader connects: A2 (evidence per sync unit).
- A2 replaces the certificate copies by evidence along stated paths (held-model `evidence` `cost_with`, notes/evidence-model-findings.md Cost): copies under a sync ID are the chained role commits on the edges outside its unit that its bodies' paths use, flat in writes, one more per role change on such an edge, repeated under each sync ID carrying the thing; no rotations, no account chains. A unit written by one signer through [A] holds one copy (the signer's grant on A) plus that edge's changes.
- The reader writes one account ticket per member on its account root (issues/tickets-retired.md): a commit, a sign, transactions: goes at A2, which drops the account's tickets (notes/holder-access-study.md, direction 1).

## Record-keyed state, as built
A kept record with a member stays in memory from its first take until it goes (evicted past `RECORD_BUDGET`, twin-dropped; one with none leaves memory when no connection holds it), its peer filter's entries dropped while no connection holds it and read again at its next take; opens gives `member_at` (stamped per (record, S), moved by no take or release) and `conn_of` ("record R is connected on C", stamped per record). Keyed by record and kept across its connections: J (`Holds::standing`, `Opens::followed`), register plans and progress, status's members of S and lacking counts (the connection per record from `Taken`/`Released`), read's owed acts, inbox's suspect lists owed (notes/sync-status.md and notes/sync-inbox.md, State). A take or release is one fact (`Moved::Taken`, saying whether the record was just read from its rows, when the modules learn its members; `Released`); `Moved` is per member only for a member that opens, changes or closes. Each goes with S leaving the record or the sync set, or the record going (`flush` returns the records that went; the driver tells inbox and status, `record_gone`). What invalidates a record's member: J's reads moving (a removal or demotion, the account's key no longer counting: memo stamps), S's credentials or holding moving (`changed_at`), each judged in its step whether or not a connection holds the record (`Opens::judge`; a close with no connection is `Moved::Gone`, which drops the register keys and lacking count of (S, record)); what the record names moving at a take, or a take of a record read from its rows (a restart's: every member judged again); a step registered (the recorded path, a plan's read). Bound: the kept records with a member, all rows counted in `RECORD_BUDGET`; a hostile dialer makes a record per fresh key actor it proves (in memory only while connected), each evicting the least recently used past the budget (an honest record evicted costs its peer a first contact: a whole filter and every credential tested again), and gets a member only by proof that it may hold S.

- **22 step verifications per member** (`verify: sync step`): a member's sync ID has one or two steps; who verifies them again per message is not established.
