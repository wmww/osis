# A thing's witness copies: unbounded where no reader deletes, and a deleted witness copy never comes back
> Summary: Two gaps in the per-label bound on minting copies (spec/commits.md §5, A thing's mintings travel with its record; notes/expiry-creation-design.md). (1) A live thing's lower copies are deleted only by a node that may read it, so under a label none of whose placing nodes reads t, a reader minting t again k times with rising expiries leaves k copies. (2) A placer never places its own copy again once it is gone (`State::certs`), so a witness whose copy was deleted as lower and that is the witness again later (a request dropped the one above it) stays missing; once t is destroyed, a node knowing t by its record holds only the dropped minting and the requests and dates the destruction at the request, not at the witness's end.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, engine, node-sync

- (2) by a hostile writer: t's creation C expires at T. The writer mints t again, Y, expiring later (retention `author`); C's copies under labels carrying t's record are deleted. It purges Y: C is the witness again, live until T, but its placers placed it before. At T the record-only nodes there take the requests (Y's purge among them), hold Y dropped and no C, and read t destroyed at the purge: an edge write on t between the purge and T stands at t's readers, not at them.
- (2) by lag (the old Limit): a reader of t that has not received the purge of the old witness W deletes the new witness N's copy as lower than W; the destroyed t later reads destroyed at the purge there too.
- While t lives neither gap misjudges it: a copy is deleted only for a held one standing higher, and a node never takes a valued minting for a dead one (it lacks requests, never has extra), so the highest held copy expires no earlier than the true witness.
- Direction: once a node judges t destroyed, views agree (the requests and t's frozen history travel), so it may place t's witness again whatever `State::certs` says; check it cannot loop with `destroyed_certificate` (what `certificates` wants must be what it keeps). With that, any node may delete a lower live copy (the argument above makes it safe while live), closing (1). Test both with a label whose only placers cannot read t.
