# A pulled entry of a body outside S's required set is never judged
> Summary: read judges an entry under S only where `Engine::sync_bodies` lists S, so a pulled entry of a commit S carries no body of here stays for good and spreads; judging every held entry by `sync_bodies` alone is unsafe, because `sync_bodies` is neither settled when the commit is held nor the same at every node (certificates).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine

- Seen in `destruction::a_remint_expiring_earlier_keeps_the_lower_copy_spec_commits_5`: node 1 purges its only minting of t (the remint has not reached it), destroys t and logs the request q under D; node 2, where t never died, pulls it, lists no body of q under D, so `Read::verdict(D, q)` is `None` and the entry stays; node 0, joining later, pulls it from node 2.
- The obvious fix (every own-store entry of a held commit judged under S, `MustNot(OutsideRequired)` where S has no body of it: a feed of the store's entries, of `blob_parts` rows and of held commits, mapped to (S, commit)) drops q everywhere, but breaks two things (tried on `pulled-judge`, wip commit there):
  - **Not settled on arrival**: a held commit's `sync_bodies` gains S later as other commits arrive (roles, group membership, certificates); `closure_whole` does not cover it. Judged outside meanwhile, the pulled entry is dropped and tombstoned (and a writer of S deletes it at store sources). `convergence::histories_converge` seed 7 fails (node 0 reads v1, the others v8): ten (S, commit) pairs judged outside, then listed under S.
  - **Not the same at every node**: certificates are partly the logging account's own (`Piece::Own`: spec/permissions.md §4 "its account's chain certificates ... for each actor it has acted for"), so a reader of another account derives fewer of S's certificates. In the remint tests node 0 (account B, granted read on A's D) derives D's records without t, so its `sync_bodies` lists neither minting copy under D, while A's nodes do; node 0 kept them only because nothing judged them. Spec §2 counts "what is logged there" (any logger's certificates) in the required set, which no node derives today.
- What a fix needs (Tess / Fathom): (1) a per-(S, commit) "judgeable" signal from the engine: the commit's body under S will not move as missing inputs arrive (or the spec's Unjudged blobs clause read as "kept until the node can judge", with what counts); (2) a node-independent required set for certificate blobs (derive every logging account's chain, or judge another node's certificate blob by the deltas it holds, spec/sync-judgement.md §2). Without (2), a writer of one account deletes another account's certificate copies at store sources.
- `judge::body` already gives `MustNot(OutsideRequired)` with no meta and goes idle once nothing of it is left; `seen_added` and the commit feed miss a pulled blob of a commit already held (no new commit, often no new `blob_parts` row): the store's entry feed covers it.
