# Full-size sync differential: open failure classes
> Summary: `OSIS_FULL=1 differential::histories_hold_their_closure` over seeds 0..199 fails 7 at caefa1d35 (114, 131, 138, 146, 169, 180, 189). Two classes are understood: a holder boxed after a thing's keying never gets the history its boxes open (146, seed-0 reproducer below), and a connection whose sources never catch up while idle (114). A fix for the first (branch `keel-diff-200-wip`) regresses the run to 14 failures. Chain reads (138, 169), a keyless read (180) and 131 are unstudied.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, tests, tess-call

Seed numbers move whenever commit bytes change (worlds are seeded by ID): work from scripts, and turn each class into a hand test (notes/principles.md, Never pin a seed). Replay: `OSIS_FULL=1 OSIS_SYNC_SEED=<n> OSIS_SYNC_SEEDS=1`. Run the 200 seeds as four `tools/heavy` holds of 50 (about 4 minutes each on an idle box).

## A holder boxed after a keying lacks the keying's history (146)
- Seed-0 reproducer (fails at caefa1d35 on the must-hold check at quiet): on `contacts()`, Alice makes T under her root and X under T (both plain), grants Bob's account sync on X, sync on T, X sync on T, then Bob read on T; Bob accepts T and runs. Bob reads neither T nor X. Any one of the two extra grants alone passes.
- Cause: T's keying commit (keys.0, sync.0, secrets.0) carries the history box that gives T's derived epochs, and it wraps under a derived epoch (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what). Bob holds only T's newest epoch (from a ticket, not established), so he can never open the keying's entry under T's sync ID, nor the content sealed before the keying. Nothing else carries the keying to him: the holder piece for an account is boxes and `sync.0` only, and a ticket fire's chain keeps no `keys.0` but the author's. Without the keyset Bob counts T as plain, so his sync unit and bodies differ from Alice's (each node judges differently what S carries). With only the X grant, Bob happens to take the keying from a fire and passes.
- Spec gap: "the recipients take it by inbox" (crypto §5) and "its nodes read x's chain under x's own sync ID once the boxes' history opens it" (certs.rs `holder_piece`) assume a path for the history that does not exist for a holder boxed after the keying.
- Tried (branch `keel-diff-200-wip`, one commit): holder pieces and read-ticket fires carry the target's current `keys.0` commits projected to `keys.0` and `secrets.0`; grants' boxes and chained writes carry their role parts whole. Fixes the reproducer and seed 146, but the 200 seeds go from 7 to 14 failures: new 48 (a node kept unread keeps entries after its removal), 56 (index rebuilt from the store differs from the live one), 81 and 186 (settle loop past its bound), 22 (storm), and 102, 165, 181 hung (600 CPU-s, on a loaded box). Seed 48 shrinks to: `New(1), Give(1, 0, Holder::Node(0), Syncs), Give(1, 0, Holder::Node(2), Writes), Strong(2, None, 0), Run, Strong(0, None, 0), Run, Revoke(1, 0, Holder::Node(0))`. There the third account, now holding T's keysets, issues a step (a rotation), and the reader that registers steps at the removed node never re-judges its plan after the path grows (in one trace its `inbox::register` effect for (conn, S) was not judged again after the revocation, with a genesis Register out unanswered), so the removed node never stops. So the history fix exposes real bugs elsewhere rather than causing them, but it cannot land until they are fixed.
- Keel's rule ("a certificate copy of a role commit carries all of its role parts, never a subset") cannot hold as stated: an actor's current keys go as `keys.0` alone, and carrying the rest breaks `rotation::tests_carried::reader_removal_stays_below_spec_crypto_4` (a reader's removal from Xd would travel to X1's readers, against spec/requirements.md Permissions). The rule needed for this class is about history, not partial role commits: whoever is boxed an epoch must be able to get the history box of that epoch.
- Decision needed (tess-call): where a holder boxed after a keying (or any rotation it was not boxed by) gets the epoch's history box. Options: (a) the holder piece and read fires carry it (the branch); (b) a grant re-boxes the history itself (its `secrets.0` writes history entries to the new holder), so a grant is self-contained; (c) a giving commit is logged twice under S, once under the superseded epoch and once under the newest.
- 189 fails the same check with a different shape: the reader has no entry at all of a keying commit of a thing made under another thing while the holder logs it (unstudied; the script plays a strong removed and added again between the two things).

## A connection idle and never caught up (114)
- Script on world 114 (debugging aid): `New(2), Give(2, 0, Holder::Node(1), Reads), Strong(1, None, 0)`. Node 1 reads T as None. At quiet the session between nodes 1 and 2 is idle (no request out, none waiting, nothing unanswered), yet every sync ID on it shows that source not caught up at both ends, so node 1 never pulls T's entries. Not a closure bug: a streams or opens stall. No seed-0 reproducer yet.

## Reads the model rejects
- 138, 169: the node reads by its role commits through a chain path (a grant to a thing, then record edges) that held-model's closure does not count.
- 180: the node reads a value while holding no secret of the thing, through a keyless grant chain (`None with key access`).
- 131: a node reads a thing unlike the reference.
- None studied: first decide whether the model or the engine is right in each. Shrinks of these stop at the 150-candidate cap at 20 to 50 steps.

## Stale status sources after a power cycle
- Not reached by seeds 0..199. `Node::closed` returns early only when it holds no session for the connection; a session goes only in `closed` itself, which then closes it in streams and opens and hands its members on, and opens learns of a connection only through its session's messages. So that early return skips nothing, and the stale path is elsewhere; no reproducer, so no hand test.
