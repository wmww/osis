# What an account's nodes add to every unit
> Summary: Copies are cut per sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies, modelled; engine not built: the build job below): a writer's evidence copy drops its grant's `secrets.0` (6.1 KB a writing node a unit) and the mintings in evidence drop all but the parts judged. Left: A's public key record still carries every node's grant (its `keys.0` box records, about 900 B a node a unit), whose lever gives up a property beyond the requirement (tess-call); and boxes per holder (pieces, sync copies) are spec, the engine to build.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, permissions, keys, performance, tess-call

Counted: engine `scale::evidence_per_unit_spec_permissions_4` (an independent thing T minted with admin A, `Engine::certificates` at its minter, accounts of 3 and 21 nodes); figures in notes/thing-cost.md (Accounts at n and 10n nodes). The cut's rule and why: notes/certificates.md (The cut); model held-model `copy_cut`.

## The cut (spec landed, engine to build)
S's cut of a commit on a thing outside its unit: the parts S's readers read there, each whole, closed over the evidence of each (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies; spec/wire.md §4, spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§2, spec/sync-set.md §2 Stub follow it). Expected effect [E: from the counted parts, to measure]:
- A writing node's grant under T: its copy is the edge write (95 B) and, while the grant is at A's newest epoch, its `keys.0` records (515 B), one header: the 6115 B `secrets.0` goes. One writer: 34.1 KB to about 28 KB at n, 49.9 to about 44 KB at 10n; every node writing: 40.3 to about 28 KB at n, 168 to about 46 KB at 10n (the writers' increment is about 95 B each, flat).
- Mintings in evidence carry only what is judged: a node thing's minting as a key hop's evidence is its `signers.0` part (of 8.3 KB); A's minting, its `sync.0` part where a step places a listed key, and its `keys.0` while its keysets are A's newest. The pairing offer (25.2 KB: the grant's copy 7.1 KB whole, A's minting 9.7 KB, the pairer's node thing's minting 8.3 KB, two records) becomes the grant's edge piece (`roles.0` and the node's `secrets.0` piece, about 5 KB: the grant's history boxes stay in the rest piece) and those two cut mintings: roughly 7 KB.
- Found on the way, fixed by the rule: a copy cut to keys (`public_key_record_into`, `parts_of(.., ["keys.0"])`) holds a may-grant grant's records without its `roles.0`, so a reader outside A judges them as an overreach (`note_required`: `overreach`) and they count for nothing there. (Keys copies now go through `add_closure`: their evidence rides.)

**Build job** (engine, one branch): `Certs` per sync ID as cuts. `sync_certificates` names parts, not (commit, thing): evidence copies (`ev.required`, `add_closure`, destroyed things' frozen history) the edge-class parts, plus `sync.0` where a step places a listed key (`Reason`); key part edge pieces `roles.0` writing the edge, `secrets.0` where it boxes the holder, `sync.0` (sync alone: no evidence, as built); stubs and public pieces as edge pieces of the chain's holders; keys (`public_key_record_into`, `carried_demotions`, holder keys) `keys.0`; beside each `secrets.0` or `keys.0` part the commit's edge-class parts on the thing; `add_closure` over every named part. `add_copy` and its whole-role-class projection go. Logging: one blob per distinct cut of a (commit, thing); the required-set judgement takes a blob of (commit, thing) as an entry only when it holds exactly S's cut (a blob holding more or less is outside the required set, dropped by the existing timing). Pairing's `Offer` and grant fires carry the cut edge piece. Tests: the two found cases above, each failing on main; `scale::evidence_per_unit_*` and `pairing_a_node_*` counts in notes/thing-cost.md measured again (the [E] figures above); the engine against held-model `copy_cut`'s hand worlds where cheap.

## A's public key record grows with A's nodes (tess-call)
A's public key record (its record and current `keys.0` commits, spec/sync-inbox.md §1) goes under every thing A holds a role on. Each grant to a node records its boxes in A's `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), so the current `keys.0` commits hold every grant since A's last rotation: under the cut about 900 B a node a unit (header 287, records 515, the edge write 95 its records' verdict reads), whether or not the node writes there.

Who reads them: a writer of T that holds none of A's boxes and holds a node's newer keyset by another route finds A's epoch stale by the record and leaves A out (Depth0 `RsPf`). That is all: A's own holders read the keysets each box names in `secrets.0` itself; a writer of T holding no newer keyset of the node judges nothing by the record.

**Lever**: grants and re-seals record nothing (a rotation still records its own boxes in its commit, for Foreign); the public key record is A's newest rotation commits alone, so pairing adds nothing to any unit, and after a removal A's rotation carries one record per box (about 130 B, no header). Grant commits lose their `keys.0` part everywhere (515 B a grant). Spec: Box records narrowed to rotations; Stale's record clause and Carried's "commits holding the box records" go with it.

**What it gives up**: Depth0 `NoLeakDeep`, a property stronger than the requirement: a node removed below a holder actor reads no content written by a writer holding the knowing keyset of an actor between. TLC: `D0_rs_off_req` and `D0_pf_off_req` (D0_rs_off and D0_pf_off, every invariant but `NoLeakDeep`) pass, so `NoLeakKnowing` (spec/requirements.md Permissions as written: a writer holding a knowing keyset of each holder actor above the removal) holds without grant records; held-model `copy_cut` `Rule::NoRecords` shows the one observable that changes (which of T's epochs a reader may encrypt under). The window it opens: a party removed from a node reads T's new content through A's epoch boxing that node until A's admins rotate A, which is due at once where they hold the node's newer keyset (carried under A's sync IDs). That is the depth-0 trade notes/holder-actors.md states (a thing never looks inside its holder actors), applied one level up; today's protection holds only where T's writer happens to hold the newer keyset. Decision for Tess: take the lever (and drop `NoLeakDeep` from notes/test-properties.md and the D0_rs instances), or keep the records and their cost.

## Boxes travel per holder (spec landed, engine to build)
A rotation's `secrets.0` held every holder's boxes in one delta, so each unit carrying the rotation carried all of them, and a holder of sync alone saw every holder's box key (its tid) and the header's signer and path: against spec/requirements.md (Data a node cannot read: a sync holder "knows none of its role holders"), so a requirements violation, fixed in the spec, no requirement changed. The spec now (notes/certificates.md The cut; model held-model `box_cut`):
- **Pieces** (spec/wire.md §4): a `secrets.0` write is one delta per holder its records are keyed with, and one of the rest (history boxes, whole and keyless writes); the aspect entry's `hash` lists each piece's hash; each piece is a part, judged alone (spec/permissions.md §3, spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes). Established needs the rest piece (history box), not every box. A section's `Contents` pages as an element array so many pieces fit `MAX_BLOB`.
- **The cut**: an edge piece, stub or public piece carries its holder's piece only (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies).
- **Sync copies** (spec/wire.md §4, §9 form 2): a holder of sync alone gets `[commit ID, tid, its piece, sync.0 delta]`, no header; it counts once its box opens to a secret a step of the thing's sync ID bears (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates). spec/sync-ids.md §2 says what it learns.

Bytes per rotation [C, from spec/wire.md and the figures in notes/thing-cost.md: a sealed box about 1.2 KB a role, history about 350 B a role, a header about 290 B; a piece adds a 34 B hash to the header and about 32 B of delta framing], one role rotated:

| holders k | reader of the commit, today / pieces | unit carrying one holder's edge piece, today / pieces | holder of sync alone's copy, today / sync copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.8 / 1.9 KB | 1.8 / 1.6 KB | 1.8 / 1.25 KB |
| 10 | 12.6 / 13.3 KB | 12.6 / 1.9 KB | 12.6 / 1.25 KB |
| 100 | 121 / 127 KB | 121 / 5.0 KB | 121 / 1.25 KB |

Four roles (an account's removal, today about 6.7 KB a node left in each unit carrying it): a unit carrying one node's piece pays about 4.7 KB + 34 B a node, 136 KB to about 5.7 KB at 20 nodes. A rotation whose every holder's key part carries its edge piece: k times 1.2k KB today, k times (1.2 + 0.034k) with pieces: 12 MB to 0.46 MB at 100 holders. Readers pay about 66 B more a holder (5% at one role, 1.4% at four). The header stays O(k) for every carrier, as any k-thing commit's is.

**Build job** (engine, one branch, after or with the cut's): `secrets.0` writes as pieces (`osis_base` header `hash` as one or a list; a part per piece; the delta split by holder at write, joined at apply; dots shuffled across holders; `Contents` paging); validity per piece (`validity::rotation_required`, `note_required`); the cut names pieces (`sync_certificates`: an edge piece's `secrets.0` piece of its holder); sync copies (`SyncCopyPlaintext`, sealed form 2) for a holder of sync alone, counted by the step check; Established reading the rest piece; parts indexed by (ai, hash) wherever (commit, ai) identifies one (`ou/`, `Meta::missing_parts`, late parts). Tests: a grantee's box beside a junk box stands (failing on main: the delta is voided whole); a holder of sync alone's key part holds no header and no other holder's box key; a forged sync copy (no step bears it) is taken nowhere; `pairing_a_node_*` (the offer carries the node's piece, no history boxes) and a removal's unit cost counted at 2 and 20 nodes (the figures above, measured); the engine against held-model `box_cut`'s hand worlds where cheap.

Not designed: a node held for keys still fetches a holder's own rotation commits whole (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries): picking out its pieces needs a way to name a holder's piece to it alone.
