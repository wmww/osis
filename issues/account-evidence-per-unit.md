# What an account's nodes add to every unit
> Summary: Every unit a multi-node account holds a role in carries each of its nodes' grants (A's public key record: every box-recording `keys.0` commit at A's newest epoch, about 880 B a node), and each node writing there adds its grant's copy whole, 6.1 KB of its 7.1 KB the `secrets.0` boxes to that node, which no reader of the unit opens or needs to judge.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, permissions, keys, performance

Counted: engine `scale::evidence_per_unit_spec_permissions_4` (an independent thing T minted with admin A, `Engine::certificates` at its minter, accounts of 3 and 21 nodes); figures in notes/thing-cost.md (Accounts at n and 10n nodes).

- **A's public key record grows with A's nodes.** It is A's record and current `keys.0` commits (spec/sync-inbox.md §1), and goes under every thing A holds a role on (`shared_certificates`). Each grant to a node records its boxes in A's `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), so `Engine::current_keys` (the commits recording boxes at A's newest epoch) holds every grant since A's last rotation: with one node writing in T, 6 copies at 3 nodes, 24 at 21, about 880 B a node (each grant's header and its `keys.0` part). Third parties do read them: a box record naming a bad keyset makes that epoch of A stale (Stale), and a sealer to A must not seal to it. Lever: a public key record that does not grow with the holder's own holders, for example box records judged where A's admins re-seal and the keyset vouched for by its rotation alone; it touches the Stale and Foreign rules (core/test/tla/rotation/KemKeys.tla).
- **A writer's evidence carries its boxes.** The evidence of a write by node N through A is N's grant on A, a certificate copy: its commit's header and all its role-class parts on A (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies), so `secrets.0` (6115 B: A's boxes to N at four roles, sealed to N) and `keys.0` (515 B) ride beside the 95 B edge write the evidence needs. Every writing node adds about 6.2 KB to every unit it writes in (40.3 KB at 3 nodes, 168 KB at 21, all writing). The pairing offer pays the same: its 25.2 KB is three whole copies. Lever: an evidence copy holding a commit's edge-class parts (`roles.0`, `signers.0`) and the `sync.0` parts §4 asks for, the key part keeping the boxes; risk: one copy per (commit, thing) serves both today, so a unit needing both would hold two projections.
