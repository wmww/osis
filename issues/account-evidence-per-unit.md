# What an account's nodes add to every unit
> Summary: Copies are cut per sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies; engine built: notes/certificates.md The cut): T's unit at its minter 20.4 KB at n, 38.0 KB at 10n, the same with every node writing; a pairing offer 8.5 KB. Left: A's public key record still carries every node's grant (its `keys.0` box records with their `roles.0`, about 980 B a node a unit), whose lever gives up a property beyond the requirement (tess-call); boxes per holder (pieces, sync copies) are spec, the engine to build; a held copy holding more than S's cut is not removed.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, permissions, keys, performance, tess-call

Counted: engine `scale::evidence_per_unit_spec_permissions_4` (an independent thing T minted with admin A, `Engine::certificates` at its minter, accounts of 3 and 21 nodes), `pairing_a_node_spec_permissions_8`; figures in notes/thing-cost.md (Accounts at n and 10n nodes). The cut's rule and why: notes/certificates.md (The cut); model held-model `copy_cut`.

## The cut (built)
Measured against whole copies: one writer 34.1 to 20.4 KB at n, 49.9 to 38.0 KB at 10n; every node writing 40.3 to 20.4 KB at n, 168 to 38.0 KB at 10n (a writer's grant is evidence as its edge write, which A's keys copy of it already holds); the pairing offer 25.2 to 8.5 KB. Tests: `sync_ids::certs::tests::a_may_grant_grants_records_count_beside_its_grant_spec_permissions_4` (a keys copy holding a may-grant grant's records with its `roles.0`: failing with `keys.0` alone), `accounts::a_units_stub_is_carried_as_certificate_copies_spec_sync_set_2` (the stub carries no other holder's grant).

Left of the build job:
- **Copies holding more than S's cut**: such a held blob is no log of the body (`body_held_by` takes exactly the cut, `log_own` relogs only such a blob), but stays: `unnamed_copy` puts a copy outside the required set only when disjoint from the cut. Judging "other parts than S's cut" outside (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) unlogged a node's own fresh copy: S's body (`bodies_for`) names only parts held as bodies, so a copy opened with a part parked or waiting here reads as more than the cut (holding tests `a_keyless_grant_reads_nothing_through_it_*`, `removing_the_makers_only_path_parks_its_entries_*`, `the_last_reader_lowered_to_sync_keeps_the_store_for_a_later_reader_*`, `an_ended_way_found_keyless_drops_its_copy_*` failed so). The judgement wants the cut as named (parts named, held or not), compared against the blob's parts. What it costs: storage (the blob stays at every source of S) and exposure, not liveness (the body counts unlogged, so a node logs an exact copy beside it): a part once in S's cut (a holder's grant while that holder was on G's chain to a stub) stays readable to S's later readers, against "nothing is in it for having been in it" (spec/permissions.md §4). A writer of S gains nothing by logging a fatter copy: it could write what it reads into S's content.
- **Not cut**: `Fire::Grant`'s edge piece is cut to every holder the commit names on the target (the fire stores no holder); pairing's trust copies likewise.
- The engine against held-model `copy_cut`'s hand worlds: not done.

## A's public key record grows with A's nodes (tess-call)
A's public key record (its record and current `keys.0` commits, spec/sync-inbox.md §1) goes under every thing A holds a role on. Each grant to a node records its boxes in A's `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), so the current `keys.0` commits hold every grant since A's last rotation: under the cut about 980 B a node a unit (measured) (header 287, records 515, the edge write 95 its records' verdict reads), whether or not the node writes there.

Who reads them: a writer of T that holds none of A's boxes and holds a node's newer keyset by another route finds A's epoch stale by the record and leaves A out (Depth0 `RsPf`). That is all: A's own holders read the keysets each box names in `secrets.0` itself; a writer of T holding no newer keyset of the node judges nothing by the record.

**Lever**: grants and re-seals record nothing (a rotation still records its own boxes in its commit, for Foreign); the public key record is A's newest rotation commits alone, so pairing adds nothing to any unit, and after a removal A's rotation carries one record per box (about 130 B, no header). Grant commits lose their `keys.0` part everywhere (515 B a grant). Spec: Box records narrowed to rotations; Stale's record clause and Carried's "commits holding the box records" go with it.

**What it gives up**: Depth0 `NoLeakDeep`, a property stronger than the requirement: a node removed below a holder actor reads no content written by a writer holding the knowing keyset of an actor between. TLC: `D0_rs_off_req` and `D0_pf_off_req` (D0_rs_off and D0_pf_off, every invariant but `NoLeakDeep`) pass, so `NoLeakKnowing` (spec/requirements.md Permissions as written: a writer holding a knowing keyset of each holder actor above the removal) holds without grant records; held-model `copy_cut` `Rule::NoRecords` shows the one observable that changes (which of T's epochs a reader may encrypt under). The window it opens: a party removed from a node reads T's new content through A's epoch boxing that node until A's admins rotate A, which is due at once where they hold the node's newer keyset (carried under A's sync IDs). That is the depth-0 trade notes/holder-actors.md states (a thing never looks inside its holder actors), applied one level up; today's protection holds only where T's writer happens to hold the newer keyset. Decision for Tess: take the lever (and drop `NoLeakDeep` from notes/test-properties.md and the D0_rs instances), or keep the records and their cost.

## Boxes travel per holder (spec landed, engine to build)
A rotation's `secrets.0` held every holder's boxes in one delta, so each unit carrying the rotation carried all of them, and a holder of sync alone saw every holder's box key (its tid) and the header's signer and path: against spec/requirements.md (Data a node cannot read: a sync holder "knows none of its role holders"), so a requirements violation, fixed in the spec, no requirement changed. The spec now (notes/certificates.md The cut; model held-model `box_cut`):
- **Pieces** (spec/wire.md §4): a `secrets.0` write is one delta per holder its records are keyed with, and one of the rest (history boxes, whole and keyless writes); the aspect entry's `hash` lists each piece's hash; each piece is a part, judged alone (spec/permissions.md §3, spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes). Established needs the rest piece (history box), not every box. A section's `Contents` pages as an element array so many pieces fit `MAX_BLOB`.
- **The cut**: an edge piece, stub or public piece carries its holder's piece only (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies).
- **Sync copies** (spec/wire.md §4, §9 form 2): a holder of sync alone gets `[commit ID, tid, its piece, sync.0 delta]`, no header; it counts once its box opens to a secret a step of the thing's sync ID bears (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates). spec/sync-ids.md §2 says what it learns.

Bytes per rotation [C, from spec/wire.md and the figures in notes/thing-cost.md: a sealed box about 1.2 KB a role, history about 350 B a role, a header about 290 B; a piece adds a 34 B hash to the header and about 32 B of delta framing], one role rotated:

| holders k | reader of the commit, today / pieces | unit carrying one holder's edge piece, today / pieces | holder of sync alone's copy, today / sync copy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.8 / 1.9 KB | 1.8 / 1.6 KB | 1.8 / 1.25 KB |
| 10 | 12.6 / 13.3 KB | 12.6 / 1.9 KB | 12.6 / 1.25 KB |
| 100 | 121 / 127 KB | 121 / 5.0 KB | 121 / 1.25 KB |

Four roles (an account's removal, today about 6.7 KB a node left in each unit carrying it): a unit carrying one node's piece pays about 4.7 KB + 34 B a node, 136 KB to about 5.7 KB at 20 nodes. A rotation whose every holder's key part carries its edge piece: k times 1.2k KB today, k times (1.2 + 0.034k) with pieces: 12 MB to 0.46 MB at 100 holders. Readers pay about 66 B more a holder (5% at one role, 1.4% at four). The header stays O(k) for every carrier, as any k-thing commit's is.

**Build job** (engine, one branch; the cut is built, `Cut::EdgePiece` taking the whole `secrets.0` part where it should take the holder's piece, marked Interim): `secrets.0` writes as pieces (`osis_base` header `hash` as one or a list; a part per piece; the delta split by holder at write, joined at apply; dots shuffled across holders; `Contents` paging); validity per piece (`validity::rotation_required`, `note_required`); the cut names pieces (`sync_certificates`: an edge piece's `secrets.0` piece of its holder); sync copies (`SyncCopyPlaintext`, sealed form 2) for a holder of sync alone, counted by the step check; Established reading the rest piece; parts indexed by (ai, hash) wherever (commit, ai) identifies one (`ou/`, `Meta::missing_parts`, late parts). Tests: a grantee's box beside a junk box stands (failing on main: the delta is voided whole); a holder of sync alone's key part holds no header and no other holder's box key; a forged sync copy (no step bears it) is taken nowhere; `pairing_a_node_*` (the offer carries the node's piece, no history boxes) and a removal's unit cost counted at 2 and 20 nodes (the figures above, measured); the engine against held-model `box_cut`'s hand worlds where cheap.

Size: a part is identified by its aspect index everywhere in the engine and osis-sync (`Parts`, `PartSet`, `Meta::hold`/`valid_at`/`dropped`, `blob_parts`, `missing_parts`, the meta row, `ou/`); pieces make it (ai, hash), a refactor through both crates, which is why it was left from the cut's branch.

Not designed: a node held for keys still fetches a holder's own rotation commits whole (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries): picking out its pieces needs a way to name a holder's piece to it alone.
