# What an account's nodes add to every unit
> Summary: Copies are cut per sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies; engine built: notes/certificates.md The cut): T's unit at its minter 12.3 KB at n, 30.4 KB at 10n; a pairing offer 8.6 KB. `secrets.0` in pieces is built, snapshots too (an edge piece carries its holder's piece and the history boxes, 34 B a holder more); sync copies and `Contents` paging are spec, not built. Left: A's public key record still carries every node's grant (about 1 KB a node a unit), whose lever gives up a property beyond the requirement (tess-call); a held copy holding more than S's cut is not removed.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, permissions, keys, performance, tess-call

Counted: engine `scale::evidence_per_unit_spec_permissions_4` (an independent thing T minted with admin A, `Engine::certificates` at its minter, accounts of 3 and 21 nodes), `pairing_a_node_spec_permissions_8`; figures in notes/thing-cost.md (Accounts at n and 10n nodes). The cut's rule and why: notes/certificates.md (The cut); model held-model `copy_cut`.

## The cut (built)
Measured against whole copies: one writer 34.1 to 20.4 KB at n, 49.9 to 38.0 KB at 10n; every node writing 40.3 to 20.4 KB at n, 168 to 38.0 KB at 10n (a writer's grant is evidence as its edge write, which A's keys copy of it already holds); the pairing offer 25.2 to 8.5 KB. Tests: `sync_ids::certs::tests::a_may_grant_grants_records_count_beside_its_grant_spec_permissions_4` (a keys copy holding a may-grant grant's records with its `roles.0`: failing with `keys.0` alone), `accounts::a_units_stub_is_carried_as_certificate_copies_spec_sync_set_2` (the stub carries no other holder's grant).

Left of the build job:
- **Copies holding more than S's cut**: such a held blob is no log of the body (`body_held_by` takes exactly the cut, `log_own` relogs only such a blob), but stays: `unnamed_copy` puts a copy outside the required set only when disjoint from the cut. Judging "other parts than S's cut" outside (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) unlogged a node's own fresh copy: S's body (`bodies_for`) names only parts held as bodies, so a copy opened with a part parked or waiting here reads as more than the cut (holding tests `a_keyless_grant_reads_nothing_through_it_*`, `removing_the_makers_only_path_parks_its_entries_*`, `the_last_reader_lowered_to_sync_keeps_the_store_for_a_later_reader_*`, `an_ended_way_found_keyless_drops_its_copy_*` failed so). The judgement wants the cut as named (parts named, held or not), compared against the blob's parts. What it costs: storage (the blob stays at every source of S) and exposure, not liveness (the body counts unlogged, so a node logs an exact copy beside it): a part once in S's cut (a holder's grant while that holder was on G's chain to a stub) stays readable to S's later readers, against "nothing is in it for having been in it" (spec/permissions.md §4). A writer of S gains nothing by logging a fatter copy: it could write what it reads into S's content.
- **Sync copies not built** (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part: a sync-alone holder's piece carries no evidence, no edge write, signer or path): `edge_pieces_into` strips `roles.0` and evidence for a holder of sync alone (marked Interim), but `Engine::edge_piece` (a `grant@~0` fire, a sealed copy, an offer) carries the holder's whole piece with its evidence, so a sync-alone holder's readers learn X's roster and paths; and a stub's pieces (`stub_holders`) are cut by each holder's role on X, not by the unit's (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stub says sync copies where T holds sync alone; §2 Carriers and permissions §4 Copies condition on the holder's role: the spec to reconcile when sync copies are built).
- The engine against held-model `copy_cut`'s hand worlds: not done.

## A's public key record grows with A's nodes (tess-call)
A's public key record (its record and current `keys.0` commits, spec/sync-inbox.md §1) goes under every thing A holds a role on. Each grant to a node records its boxes in A's `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), so the current `keys.0` commits hold every grant since A's last rotation: under the cut about 980 B a node a unit (measured) (header 287, records 515, the edge write 95 its records' verdict reads), whether or not the node writes there.

Who reads them: a writer of T that holds none of A's boxes and holds a node's newer keyset by another route finds A's epoch stale by the record and leaves A out (Depth0 `RsPf`). That is all: A's own holders read the keysets each box names in `secrets.0` itself; a writer of T holding no newer keyset of the node judges nothing by the record.

**Lever**: grants and re-seals record nothing (a rotation still records its own boxes in its commit, for Foreign); the public key record is A's newest rotation commits alone, so pairing adds nothing to any unit, and after a removal A's rotation carries one record per box (about 130 B, no header). Grant commits lose their `keys.0` part everywhere (515 B a grant). Spec: Box records narrowed to rotations; Stale's record clause and Carried's "commits holding the box records" go with it.

**What it gives up**: Depth0 `NoLeakDeep`, a property stronger than the requirement: a node removed below a holder actor reads no content written by a writer holding the knowing keyset of an actor between. TLC: `D0_rs_off_req` and `D0_pf_off_req` (D0_rs_off and D0_pf_off, every invariant but `NoLeakDeep`) pass, so `NoLeakKnowing` (spec/requirements.md Permissions as written: a writer holding a knowing keyset of each holder actor above the removal) holds without grant records; held-model `copy_cut` `Rule::NoRecords` shows the one observable that changes (which of T's epochs a reader may encrypt under). The window it opens: a party removed from a node reads T's new content through A's epoch boxing that node until A's admins rotate A, which is due at once where they hold the node's newer keyset (carried under A's sync IDs). That is the depth-0 trade notes/holder-actors.md states (a thing never looks inside its holder actors), applied one level up; today's protection holds only where T's writer happens to hold the newer keyset. Decision for Tess: take the lever (and drop `NoLeakDeep` from notes/test-properties.md and the D0_rs instances), or keep the records and their cost.

## Boxes travel per holder (pieces and pieced snapshots built; sync copies and paging left)
A rotation's `secrets.0` held every holder's boxes in one delta, so each unit carrying the rotation carried all of them, and a holder of sync alone saw every holder's box key (its tid) and the header's signer and path: against spec/requirements.md (Data a node cannot read: a sync holder "knows none of its role holders"). The spec (notes/certificates.md The cut; model held-model `box_cut`):
- **Pieces** (spec/wire.md §4): a `secrets.0` write is one delta per holder its records are keyed with, and one of the rest (history boxes, whole and keyless writes); the aspect entry lists each piece's hash; each piece is a part (its own part index), judged alone. **Built**: the header holds an entry per piece; the writer splits a role commit's `secrets.0` with its records' counters shuffled; a receiver drops a piece holding two holders' records, or a holder's beside another, as not matching; validity, holding, late parts per piece; a rotation is made by the rest piece alone; the state and retention take a commit's pieces joined (notes/certificates.md Pieces in the engine).
- **The cut**: an edge piece, stub or public piece carries its holder's piece and, beside it where the holder reads X, the rest piece (history boxes, keyed with no holder): never another holder's. **Built** (`Engine::cut_parts`, `edge_pieces_into` for a holder of sync alone too, still with the header). The rest rides along because the holder opens the history boxes by the epochs its box gives and the commit's body under X's sync ID is wrapped under an epoch they supersede (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what): the design as first written (holder's piece only, pairing offers without history boxes) left every grantee with history unable to read what came before its grant (osis-sync's must-hold check failed across the suite). held-model `box_cut` models history boxes (`Rule::NoRest`: a grantee lacks the epochs before its grant; `Rule::RestToSync`: a holder of sync alone opening the sync role's history box learns departed holders, the names).
- Measured (engine `scale::removing_a_node_*`, notes/thing-cost.md): an account's removal at 2 and 20 nodes left is 16.3 and 135 KB; one node's edge piece of it 7.1 and 7.7 KB (34 B a node). The pairing offer 8.6 KB, flat.

Left of the build:
- **Sync copies** (spec/wire.md §4 `SyncCopyPlaintext`, §9 form 2; spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates): a holder of sync alone still gets a certificate copy (its piece and `sync.0`, with the header and X's record: the signer and path shown, the roster no more). What building it takes: a sync copy has no header, so it never applies as a commit, and spec/sync.md §3 (Kept unread) has the node hold of X only its sync secrets and the sync IDs it keeps, no record. The engine instead runs a kept-unread X on applied role commits and X's record: `Core::own_record`/`enter` (closure `Ctx::Unread`), `kept_sync_secrets` from `Core::epochs`, `owned_sync_ids`/`sync_recorders` from applied `sync.0`, `counts_independent`, `thing_sync_now`, `stubs_of`. So the build is a kept-unread store of its own per X (sync secrets by epoch from boxes opened with H's keys, each kept only where a verified step of X's sync ID bears its sync key; the steps from the copies' `sync.0` deltas, verified from genesis), every one of those consumers reading it for a kept-unread X with no record, the copy blob kind (encode, the piece-shape check, identity (commit, thing, holder)), osis-sync logging and counting it in H's key part (the lower blob ID of two counted), sealed form 2 for fires and offers, and `edge_pieces_into` dropping `out.records.insert(x)` and the header. Also an offer's trust records to a holder of sync alone still carry their edge writes (the roster): they go with this. Tests: a sync-alone holder learns no holder tid nor the rotator; a forged sync copy (no step bears it) is taken nowhere; garbage cannot take the real copy's slot; held-model `box_cut` `sync_alone` ported.
- **`secrets.0` snapshots in pieces**: built (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces, spec/crdt.md §8, spec/commits.md §5 Covered: a holder's piece states its own fragment's tables, the rest piece, always present, the snapshot's whole; covering is judged against the join of the pieces held, so a holder's piece and the rest cover that holder's and the rest's pieces of what the snapshot covers and reach every commit it absorbs). Tests: `tests_validity` `a_secrets_snapshot_travels_in_pieces_*` (pieces, b's edge piece of it, b covering as the admin, a whole snapshot refused); `commits::tests::a_dropped_piece_stands_for_its_body_*` (a dropped piece, not one never got, stands for the body).
- **`Contents` paging** (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces): a section's `[Part*]` is not paged, so a rotation's pieces of about 6,000 holders pass `MAX_BLOB` (a whole delta paged before).
- The engine against held-model `box_cut`'s hand worlds: `junk_beside` (`tests_validity` `a_grantees_box_stands_beside_a_junk_box_*`) and `granted_after_a_removal` (`a_grantee_reads_its_history_by_the_rest_*`) ported; `sync_alone` (a forged box at an epoch no step bears, the rotator unlearned) waits for sync copies.

Not designed: a node held for keys still fetches a holder's own rotation commits whole (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries): picking out its pieces needs a way to name a holder's piece to it alone.
