# What an account's nodes add to every unit
> Summary: Copies are cut per sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies, modelled; engine not built: the build job below): a writer's evidence copy drops its grant's `secrets.0` (6.1 KB a writing node a unit) and the mintings in evidence drop all but the parts judged. Left: A's public key record still carries every node's grant (its `keys.0` box records, about 900 B a node a unit), whose lever gives up a property beyond the requirement (tess-call); and a part is the finest cut, so a rotation's boxes travel whole.

## The cut (spec landed, engine to build)
S's cut of a commit on a thing outside its unit: the parts S's readers read there, each whole, closed over the evidence of each (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies; spec/wire.md §4, spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§2, spec/sync-set.md §2 Stub follow it). Expected effect [E: from the counted parts, to measure]:
- A writing node's grant under T: its copy is the edge write (95 B) and, while the grant is at A's newest epoch, its `keys.0` records (515 B), one header: the 6115 B `secrets.0` goes. One writer: 34.1 KB to about 28 KB at n, 49.9 to about 44 KB at 10n; every node writing: 40.3 to about 28 KB at n, 168 to about 46 KB at 10n (the writers' increment is about 95 B each, flat).
- Mintings in evidence carry only what is judged: a node thing's minting as a key hop's evidence is its `signers.0` part (of 8.3 KB); A's minting, its `sync.0` part where a step places a listed key, and its `keys.0` while its keysets are A's newest. The pairing offer (25.2 KB: the grant's copy 7.1 KB whole, A's minting 9.7 KB, the pairer's node thing's minting 8.3 KB, two records) becomes the grant's edge piece (`roles.0` and `secrets.0`, about 6.6 KB) and those two cut mintings: roughly 8-9 KB.
- Found on the way, both fixed by the rule: a copy cut to keys (`current_keys_into`, `parts_of(.., ["keys.0"])`) holds a may-grant grant's records without its `roles.0`, so a reader outside A judges them as an overreach (`note_required`: `grant_overreach`) and they count for nothing there; and keys copies bring no evidence (only key parts and stubs go through `add_closure`), so a `keys.0` commit by a node whose own grant no other copy needs is unjudgeable at a reader outside A.

**Build job** (engine, one branch): `Certs` per sync ID as cuts. `sync_certificates` names parts, not (commit, thing): evidence copies (`ev.required`, `add_closure`, destroyed things' frozen history) the edge-class parts, plus `sync.0` where a step places a listed key (`Reason`); key part edge pieces `roles.0` writing the edge, `secrets.0` where it boxes the holder, `sync.0` (sync alone: no evidence, as built); stubs and public pieces as edge pieces of the chain's holders; keys (`current_keys_into`, `carried_demotions`, holder keys) `keys.0`; beside each `secrets.0` or `keys.0` part the commit's edge-class parts on the thing; `add_closure` over every named part. `add_copy` and its whole-role-class projection go. Logging: one blob per distinct cut of a (commit, thing); the required-set judgement takes a blob of (commit, thing) as an entry only when it holds exactly S's cut (a blob holding more or less is outside the required set, dropped by the existing timing). Pairing's `Offer` and grant fires carry the cut edge piece. Tests: the two found cases above, each failing on main; `scale::evidence_per_unit_*` and `pairing_a_node_*` counts in notes/thing-cost.md measured again (the [E] figures above); the engine against held-model `copy_cut`'s hand worlds where cheap.

## A's public key record grows with A's nodes (tess-call)
A's public key record (its record and current `keys.0` commits, spec/sync-inbox.md §1) goes under every thing A holds a role on. Each grant to a node records its boxes in A's `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), so the current `keys.0` commits hold every grant since A's last rotation: under the cut about 900 B a node a unit (header 287, records 515, the edge write 95 its records' verdict reads), whether or not the node writes there.

Who reads them: a writer of T that holds none of A's boxes and holds a node's newer keyset by another route finds A's epoch stale by the record and leaves A out (Depth0 `RsPf`). That is all: A's own holders read the keysets each box names in `secrets.0` itself; a writer of T holding no newer keyset of the node judges nothing by the record.

**Lever**: grants and re-seals record nothing (a rotation still records its own boxes in its commit, for Foreign); the public key record is A's newest rotation commits alone, so pairing adds nothing to any unit, and after a removal A's rotation carries one record per box (about 130 B, no header). Grant commits lose their `keys.0` part everywhere (515 B a grant). Spec: Box records narrowed to rotations; Stale's record clause and Carried's "commits holding the box records" go with it.

**What it gives up**: Depth0 `NoLeakDeep`, a property stronger than the requirement: a node removed below a holder actor reads no content written by a writer holding the knowing keyset of an actor between. TLC: `D0_rs_off_req` and `D0_pf_off_req` (D0_rs_off and D0_pf_off, every invariant but `NoLeakDeep`) pass, so `NoLeakKnowing` (spec/requirements.md Permissions as written: a writer holding a knowing keyset of each holder actor above the removal) holds without grant records; held-model `copy_cut` `Rule::NoRecords` shows the one observable that changes (which of T's epochs a reader may encrypt under). The window it opens: a party removed from a node reads T's new content through A's epoch boxing that node until A's admins rotate A, which is due at once where they hold the node's newer keyset (carried under A's sync IDs). That is the depth-0 trade notes/holder-actors.md states (a thing never looks inside its holder actors), applied one level up; today's protection holds only where T's writer happens to hold the newer keyset. Decision for Tess: take the lever (and drop `NoLeakDeep` from notes/test-properties.md and the D0_rs instances), or keep the records and their cost.

## A part is the finest cut
A rotation's `secrets.0` part holds every holder's box, so every unit carrying it carries all of them: a stub or an edge piece of a thing with k holders pays about 1.5 KB a holder a rotation (an account's removal: 6.7 KB a node left, in each unit carrying the rotation, notes/thing-cost.md), and a holder of sync alone sees the box keys (the holders' tids) of each rotation boxing it, against spec/sync-ids.md §2 What a sync holder learns. A cut by box needs each box bound on its own: one aspect per boxed holder (`secrets.0` split by holder: about 70 B of header per holder for every reader of the commit, against 1.5 KB per holder per unit carrying it) or per-holder pages with their own hashes (against "a commit applies only with all its pages"). Not designed; worth it only once stubs of many-holder things are common.
