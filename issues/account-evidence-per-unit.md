# What an account's nodes add to every unit
> Summary: Copies are cut per sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies; engine built: notes/certificates.md The cut): T's unit at its minter 10.7 KB at n and 10n (one writer); a pairing offer 8.6 KB. `secrets.0` in pieces is built, snapshots too (an edge piece carries its holder's piece and the history boxes, 34 B a holder more); sync copies are built on every path (notes/certificates.md Sync alone); `Contents` paging is spec, not built. A's public key record carries no grant: pairing adds nothing per unit; a removal's rotation carries about 526 B a node of box records. Left: a held copy holding more than S's cut is not removed.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, permissions, keys, performance

Counted: engine `scale::evidence_per_unit_spec_permissions_4` (an independent thing T minted with admin A, `Engine::certificates` at its minter, accounts of 3 and 21 nodes), `pairing_a_node_spec_permissions_8`; figures in notes/thing-cost.md (Accounts at n and 10n nodes). The cut's rule and why: notes/certificates.md (The cut); model held-model `copy_cut`.

## The cut (built)
Measured against whole copies: one writer 34.1 to 20.4 KB at n, 49.9 to 38.0 KB at 10n; every node writing 40.3 to 20.4 KB at n, 168 to 38.0 KB at 10n (a writer's grant is evidence as its edge write, which A's keys copy of it already holds); the pairing offer 25.2 to 8.5 KB. Test: `accounts::a_units_stub_is_carried_as_sync_copies_spec_sync_set_2` (the stub carries sync copies alone, no other holder's grant).

Left of the build job:
- **Copies holding more than S's cut**: such a held blob is no log of the body (`body_held_by` takes exactly the cut, `log_own` relogs only such a blob), but stays: `unnamed_copy` puts a copy outside the required set only when disjoint from the cut. Judging "other parts than S's cut" outside (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) unlogged a node's own fresh copy: S's body (`bodies_for`) names only parts held as bodies, so a copy opened with a part parked or waiting here reads as more than the cut (holding tests `a_keyless_grant_reads_nothing_through_it_*`, `removing_the_makers_only_path_parks_its_entries_*`, `the_last_reader_lowered_to_sync_keeps_the_store_for_a_later_reader_*`, `an_ended_way_found_keyless_drops_its_copy_*` failed so). The judgement wants the cut as named (parts named, held or not), compared against the blob's parts. What it costs: storage (the blob stays at every source of S) and exposure, not liveness (the body counts unlogged, so a node logs an exact copy beside it): a part once in S's cut (a holder's grant while that holder was on G's chain to a stub) stays readable to S's later readers, against "nothing is in it for having been in it" (spec/permissions.md §4). A writer of S gains nothing by logging a fatter copy: it could write what it reads into S's content.
- The engine against held-model `copy_cut`'s hand worlds: not done.

## A's public key record per unit (built)
Grants and re-seals record nothing in `keys.0`; a rotation records its own boxes, for Foreign (spec/crypto.md §4 Box records). A's current `keys.0` commits are every commit with a write live in A's `keys.0` state (spec/permissions.md §4): its newest rotations and whichever commits last wrote the sync ID, deposit seed and step keys. Stale reads every box a held valid `secrets.0` body puts, not the state's (a rotation foreign at a node strips the state's: notes/holder-actors.md, Stale boxes). Depth0's deep-departure property (a writer holding none of A's boxes kept out by records), stronger than the requirement, is given up (Tess); `NoLeakKnowing` holds (`D0_rs_blind`, `D0_pf_blind`).

Measured (engine `scale::an_accounts_key_record_per_unit_*`, by hand `an_accounts_key_record_table`; A's copies under each unit T minted with admin A, at A's minter):

| | nodes of A | per unit: copies, bytes | distinct blobs over 1, 10, 100 units |
|---|---|---|---|
| after the pairings | 3 | 1, 8,480 B | 1 (8,480 B) at every count |
| after the pairings | 21 | 1, 8,480 B | 1 (8,480 B) |
| after a removal | 3 | 3, 16,554 B | 3 (16,554 B) |
| after a removal | 21 | 3, 26,024 B | 3 (26,024 B) |

Pairing adds nothing to any unit (the one copy is A's making commit, cut to `keys.0` and `sync.0`). After a removal a unit carries the making commit (8,480 B: sync ID, deposit seed, step), the removal's rotation cut to `keys.0` with its own `roles.0` (it demotes: Carried demotions; 7,646 B at 3 nodes, 17,116 B at 21) and the removed node's grant `roles.0` (428 B). Every unit carries the same cuts, so they dedup to one blob each whatever the count of units (spec/permissions.md §4: one blob per distinct cut). Wire: osis-sync `growth::a_holders_key_record_crosses_the_wire_once_*` (by hand).

Left, growing with A's nodes per unit: the rotation's box records, one per box (four roles a node), about 130 B each: 526 B a node. They serve Foreign alone, which judges a box at k naming a keyset held invalid or foreign: the simplest lever is one record per holder actor per rotation (every role's box to M names keysets of M chosen alike), a quarter of the bytes; dropping them needs Foreign judged from the rotation's own `secrets.0` piece, which keys-copy holders do not hold.

## Boxes travel per holder (pieces, pieced snapshots and sync copies built; paging left)
A rotation's `secrets.0` held every holder's boxes in one delta, so each unit carrying the rotation carried all of them, and a holder of sync alone saw every holder's box key (its tid) and the header's signer and path: against spec/requirements.md (Data a node cannot read: a sync holder "knows none of its role holders"). The spec (notes/certificates.md The cut; model held-model `box_cut`):
- **Pieces** (spec/wire.md §4): a `secrets.0` write is one delta per holder its records are keyed with, and one of the rest (history boxes, whole and keyless writes); the aspect entry lists each piece's hash; each piece is a part (its own part index), judged alone. **Built**: the header holds an entry per piece; the writer splits a role commit's `secrets.0` with its records' counters shuffled; a receiver drops a piece holding two holders' records, or a holder's beside another, as not matching; validity, holding, late parts per piece; a rotation is made by the rest piece alone; the state and retention take a commit's pieces joined (notes/certificates.md Pieces in the engine).
- **The cut**: an edge piece, stub or public piece carries its holder's piece and, beside it where the holder reads X, the rest piece (history boxes, keyed with no holder): never another holder's. **Built** (`Engine::cut_parts`, `edge_pieces_into` for a holder of sync alone too, still with the header). The rest rides along because the holder opens the history boxes by the epochs its box gives and the commit's body under X's sync ID is wrapped under an epoch they supersede (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what): the design as first written (holder's piece only, pairing offers without history boxes) left every grantee with history unable to read what came before its grant (osis-sync's must-hold check failed across the suite). held-model `box_cut` models history boxes (`Rule::NoRest`: a grantee lacks the epochs before its grant; `Rule::RestToSync`: a holder of sync alone opening the sync role's history box learns departed holders, the names).
- Measured (engine `scale::removing_a_node_*`, notes/thing-cost.md): an account's removal at 2 and 20 nodes left is 16.3 and 135 KB; one node's edge piece of it 7.1 and 7.7 KB (34 B a node). The pairing offer 8.6 KB, flat.

Left of the build:
- **Sync copies**: built on every path (notes/certificates.md Sync alone; Sync copies logged and bounded). Left:
  - Duplicate blobs of one sync copy at a store keeping S unread are not deleted there (record blobs are, `CertBlobsMissing::deletes`): a reader unlogs its own losing blob only.
  - held-model `box_cut`'s `sync_alone` world against the engine: covered by the delivery tests on a real rotation; a hand port is left (held-model has no sync role in `roles_model::Role`).
- **`secrets.0` snapshots in pieces**: built (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces, spec/crdt.md §8, spec/commits.md §5 Covered: a holder's piece states its own fragment's tables, the rest piece, always present, the snapshot's whole; covering is judged against the join of the pieces held, so a holder's piece and the rest cover that holder's and the rest's pieces of what the snapshot covers and reach every commit it absorbs). Tests: `tests_validity` `a_secrets_snapshot_travels_in_pieces_*` (pieces, b's edge piece of it, b covering as the admin, a whole snapshot refused); `commits::tests::a_dropped_piece_stands_for_its_body_*` (a dropped piece, not one never got, stands for the body).
- **`Contents` paging** (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces): a section's `[Part*]` is not paged, so a rotation's pieces of about 6,000 holders pass `MAX_BLOB` (a whole delta paged before).
- The engine against held-model `box_cut`'s hand worlds: `junk_beside` (`tests_validity` `a_grantees_box_stands_beside_a_junk_box_*`) and `granted_after_a_removal` (`a_grantee_reads_its_history_by_the_rest_*`) ported; `sync_alone`: Sync copies above.

Not designed: a node held for keys still fetches a holder's own rotation commits whole (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries): picking out its pieces needs a way to name a holder's piece to it alone.
