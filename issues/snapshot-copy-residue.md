# Copies of one write that no held delta settles
> Summary: where the receiver does not hold a write's own delta (never had it, or dropped it), a snapshot's copy of that write counts; two held copies that differ (one forged) tie in the join (first held wins: order-dependent), a reader's forged copy shows there, and a mover holding one imports it into the copy as its own write.
> Tags: security, node, crdt

What was fixed (issues/snapshot-forged-owned-dots.md, spec/permissions.md §5.2): where the delta owning a dot is held, a snapshot's copies under its dots count for nothing in both arrival orders (`strip_held_copies` on join and rebuild, `refill` when the delta comes after the snapshot), forged snapshots cover no body they disagree with (`covered`), and `moves::carriers` skips snapshots that disagree with held deltas. What remains:
- No held delta: copies of one dot from several snapshots (or a snapshot and the state) that differ tie in `join_write` (equal stamps), `join_entry` (keys) and seq `merge_elem` (creation, char): the first held stays. Deterministic options: a total order on same-stamp payloads in osis-crdt (range records included), or one designated carrier per dot (e.g. least snapshot ID) with `copies_outside` detecting the conflict and a rebuild resolving it. Either only buys determinism: nothing tells the honest copy from the forged one at such a node; only snapshot-author authority could (compaction is a writer's job, spec/sync.md §7), which `vouch for nothing` gave up for demoted compactors.
- Dropped delta: once the owner's delta drops, a held forged snapshot's copies count again (the dropped delta is no longer supplied); a later rebuild re-joins them (same-position ties keep the state's, joined first; other positions and extra dots return), and an in-place un-merge then differs from the debug rebuild check. A forged snapshot is usually covered by an honest one while the delta is held and dropped with it; not guaranteed.
- Moves: a mover that lacked the forged write's own delta when it moved imports the forged copy (its state showed it); `walks::open_equals_rebuild_random_histories_forged` sees it on copies (seed 294), so it checks only the originals for forged values.
- `retention::deaths` plays snapshot copies with `held_part` (dropped parts only, unreceived parts count as held); left as is.
