# A chain's last grant is hidden from a node syncing its target
> Summary: an account with read on H (an offer, H not synced), where H is X's admin, strongs X and holds sync on X: the model words say the account's unit holds X (it can read X through H), but no data node 2 holds or can reach names H's grant on X. Needs a model call: does a chain count before the node holds its grants?
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, tess-call

Script, at `holds_on(0, 3, 4, ..)`: `New(2), New(0), New(1), NewIn(1, 2), Give(1, 3, Holder::Node(2), Syncs), Strong(2, None, 3), Give(1, 2, Holder::Node(2), Reads)`. Thing 3 (X) is minted in thing 2 (H); node 2's account A gets sync on X, a strong from A to X, and read on H as an offer (no strong to H). First failure: "node 2's sync set holds thing 2 as None, the model Some(Keys)". Seed 10 of the full-size differential shrinks to it; 38, 78 and 151 shrink to the same question (scripts in issues/differential-full-size-classes.md), 38's with the holder holding read on X by a grant rather than as X's admin, and the account no role on X of its own.

What each side does:
- held-model (`closure::entry`) computes T's keys and a node's role over the world's grants: A holds read on X through H, so X is in A's sync unit, read whole, and H is held for keys up from X. The harness's `unoffered` filter only hides grants to the account that were never offered.
- The engine and spec judge by the grants the node holds (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own things, "as the node holds them"; Held for keys). Node 2 holds A's key part (A's pieces on X and, from the offer, on H) and X's sync copies. H's grant on X is in H's key part and X's roster: node 2 syncs neither readably, so A's keys do not open X there and X is kept unread.

Why the engine cannot follow the model's words: the only carriers of H's grant on X are X's roster (hidden from a node that cannot yet read X, spec/requirements.md Data a node cannot read) and H's key part. Node 2 knows of H only through the offer, and once it expires (`INBOX_LIFETIME`) it holds nothing of H at all, so no data path remains. While it lasts, finding which holder leads to X means fetching the key parts of every thing A reads, down every chain: the downward walk "keys found from the thing up" replaced, with fetches driven by grants alone (anyone granting A something makes A's readers fetch it) and cost in A's grants.

The question (spec/requirements.md, Concepts "sync unit: the part of its closure it can itself read" against Sync "a grant is the granted thing's own: its holder may ... not know of the grant"): does a role through a chain count at a node only once the node holds each grant on the chain (from a thing it reads, or a key part it holds: syncs the holder, or holds it for keys up from what it reads)?
- **Yes (recommended)**: matches the engine and spec today. Node 2 reads X once it syncs H (or anything that holds H's key part); until then X is kept unread. Work: requirements wording for the sync unit and line on grants (Tess); held-model `entry` computes `keys`/`unit`/roles per node over the grants it holds (the harness's `unoffered` approximation then goes); the differential's script becomes a hand test.
- **No**: the model's words stand and need a way to deliver H's grant on X to node 2 that respects storage attributable to synced things and Data a node cannot read; none found.
