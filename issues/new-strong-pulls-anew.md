# A new strong pulls its whole unit anew
> Summary: A strong newly reaching a thing outside the node closure pulls the unit's sync IDs anew from every source (`units::refetch`), re-listing the whole unit. A dependent thing another node makes no longer does (its minting brings its record), but an independent one still does at every reader (its content commit, carrying the strong, arrives in a later transaction than its rotation), and so does an attach (83 KB at 4 members, 141 KB at 40).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, performance

**Independent things** (probed: sync-harness world, node 0 makes independent things of its account A with `mint_independent`, A's second node node 1): node 1 takes the rotation minting the thing (its record) in one transaction and the content commit holding A's strong to it in the next, so the strong finds the thing outside the closure with its minting held, and the exemption of spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies ("unless that transaction brought its record in a commit of which the node held nothing when it began") does not apply: A's unit is pulled anew per make, PullReply 27 KB a make at 3 things, 34 KB at 30 (O(n²) over n makes).

The spec's rule is coarser than its reason: content on a thing counts as never received only once the pass that brought its minting (or the content) ends (spec/commits.md §4 Outside the node closure), so between the two transactions, inside one pass, nothing of the thing was forgotten. A rule by that reason ("no pull anew where the node held no commit minting the thing when the pass in flight began", or a durable per-thing mark set when something of it is forgotten as never received, cleared by the pull) needs a model decision: the pass form ties behaviour to pass boundaries, the mark is new state (notes/certificates.md rejects a durable mark per forgotten thing for its flood bound). Also check the other reason of the pull, a body judged outside the required set before the strong arrived, against whichever rule.

**Attach**: a pull limited to the attached thing's entries, if it meets the spec's reason for the pull anew (what was forgotten of the thing is fetched again); else state why the spec needs the whole unit.

`refetch_holders` (a new strong to an independent or unknown thing nothing carries: every own and read thing's sync IDs pulled anew) takes no exemption for a thing just minted: an edge piece naming it is dropped by the key-part rule (kept only so far), not by its minting being held, so a fresh minting says nothing about it.

Tests to add: pulls anew and bytes per independent make at n and 10n (none), and an attach at n and 10n members of the enclosing unit and of the attached thing, in default tests (as sync `growth::a_thing_another_node_makes_pulls_nothing_anew_*` for dependents).
