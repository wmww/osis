Built (2b): one blob per commit (spec/wire.md §4 sections, §5 commitment groups, §11.2 delete items per group; spec/sync.md §1, §3, §5; spec/crypto.md §5, §7; spec/requirements.md host visibility and the privacy line). Two refinements of the planned text, found at build: a section is per thing under the thing's read secret (own or derived by tid), not per reader actor under the actor's secret, because a plain group given secrets of its own at its first grant carries its earlier blobs' keys to new members only as its derived history, never the owner's secret; and a reader also derives a section's key from the label's group (the thing's record may be inside the section) and tries the group's own secret, under which per-label copies (certificate copies, cross-published `label.0`/`created.0`) are sealed as the wrap is. The section lists its thing's owner chain in the clear (a draft's record rides inside the section, so a reader could not otherwise derive its key; blob-key holders see every tid in the header already). The section wrap carries a random nonce (a rewrite seals the same commit's section again under the same read secret). A part whose thing's secret the writer lacks (a key actor's own record or card) stays out of the shared blob and travels as a certificate copy; a label whose actor reads a part only by role on a reader actor outside its closure (a thing placed from another actor's tree, whose rotations the label never carries) gets a per-label copy of the parts it may read, as certificate copies are. A card part sharing its commit with `created.0` or `keys.0` cannot go alone until step 4's strip (cert_judge's departed-node tests accept that).
