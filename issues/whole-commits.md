Building:
- **2b, one blob per commit** (notes/whole-commits.md §2.1, §10).
  - spec/wire.md §4: `CommitBlobPlaintext := [Header, signature, [Section*]]`, `Section := [[ai*], wrap, ciphertext]`: one section per reader actor among the commit's parts (spec/sync.md §3 entitlement), sorted by its first `ai`; `ciphertext` = AEAD of that actor's `[Part*]` under a fresh random section key; `wrap` = AEAD of the section key under `kdf("section", read secret of that actor)` at the newest epoch the writer holds, nonce from (commit ID, section index), AD = commit ID. A reader tries the read secrets of the actors of the tids the section's `ai` name. A section is opaque to a non-reader and copied verbatim into a fresh encryption.
  - spec/wire.md §5: the prefix lists commitments in groups, one group per distinct delete-key set among the parts' aspects, each group in header key order, groups in order of their first part; a `nobody` part's group is empty. Count fields widen accordingly (bounded by the header's keys).
  - spec/sync.md §3: Projection goes. Every label of every thing the commit touches carries the one blob (its label's wrap); hosts and multi-label nodes dedup by blob ID. No size split: a blob stays within `MAX_BLOB` by paging its deltas, largest first. Certificate copies and inbox blobs stay per-actor projections of role commits.
  - spec/sync.md §5: a Delete (timed or not) needs one valid delete signature per commitment group (an empty group blocks it); a Replace needs one from any group.
  - spec/sync.md (privacy): "Nothing stored or relayed links different groups of one owner beyond their overlap" gains "and the changes that span them"; spec/requirements.md: a host may see that one blob sits under labels sharing no thing.
  - Ephemeral commits: one blob each, held in memory, the whole commit within `MAX_BLOB`.
- **3, apply never waits for a part** (§2.2).
  - spec/commits.md §4: atomic apply (every "counts as held" clause), "a part waits and holds it", "the wait of a thing the node syncs holds the whole commit", "a thing's content applies whole or not at all" and the sync-set exception go. A node applies in one transaction every part it can open on a thing whose `created.0` it holds (and syncs, or holds by its own label); a part on a thing whose record it lacks waits alone and joins when the record arrives; the author's unknown record still holds the whole commit. A blob with more parts than applied joins the extra ones as late parts; one with fewer changes nothing. Sealed sections of things not synced stay unopened.
  - spec/commits.md §5: "A body is one blob of a commit (a projection ...)" becomes: a body is the commit's blob.
  - spec/requirements.md: the Deterministic exception ("while a part stays away ...") and the Atomic exception ("a node may show a commit without a part that is still to arrive ...") go; the atomic line reads: "A change is one unit: every label of every thing it touches carries all of it, each part readable by its thing's readers. A node applies all the parts it may read on things it holds at once; a part on a thing it holds later joins then." The roster line stays.
- **4, per-part strip** (§2.3).
  - spec/commits.md §5: a part whose body must go (keep-at-most bound, applied purge request, covering compaction, invalidity under `required.0^`) is stripped: dropped locally as today and, by a right holder, uploaded again as the commit's blob without that part (other sections verbatim), naming the old entry as replaced. One mechanism for keep at most, compaction and purge.
  - spec/sync.md §6: must-hold per blob, judged on the parts the node reads: must-hold if a readable part is must-hold and no other held blob of the commit under the label carries it; must-not-hold if a readable part must not be held; else may-hold. "Strict subset" becomes "fewer parts of the same commit wins while nothing the node reads is missing from it". A node never uploads or deletes on the strength of sections it cannot open.
  - spec/sync.md §5, §7: hosts delete unattended only at the commit's expiry or a timed delete carrying every group's signature; a rewrite stripping a dead part carries the live ones.
