# A withdrawn epoch's content stays with its box holders
> Summary: An epoch whose names every holder of its secret finds unmet (a named removal by an admin demoted concurrently) is withdrawn, but its secret goes in no history box (established nowhere it is held): a member its rotator left out, entitled all along, never reads what was encrypted under it, a durable read loss for an honest member. Its keys also block `secrets.0` compaction at its holders for good.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: keys, rotation, tess-call

- Shape: `rotation::tests::unmet_epoch_withdrawn_by_its_maker_spec_crypto_4`. R rotates read naming a2's removal of M (e_R boxes no M) and writes under e_R; at every node holding D (a2's demotion, ordered before) the removal applies nothing, M reads on, e_R is unestablished wherever its secret is held, and R withdraws it. M never gets e_R's secret: what R (or any node that established e_R before D reached it) wrote under e_R in that window M never reads. Users see it: a member of the group missing posts every other member reads. Nothing in spec/requirements.md states this as a Limit.
- Why no history box carries it: history boxes carry established secrets only (notes/crypto.md, R9: a node lacking a departure must not hand the epoch to the departed holder). "Refuted" is not final: a re-grant of a2 ordered between D and the removal, arriving later, makes the removal apply again, and then e_R is the epoch M was meant not to read. Carrying a refuted epoch's secret leaks in that case; refusing it loses reads in the common one. Needs Tess: a requirements Limit for the read loss, or a rule that tells a final refutation from a reversible one.
- Compaction: `may_compact_secrets` (spec/crypto.md §4: compact only while every epoch the state keys is established) is false at every holder of e_R's secret while e_R's boxes or history key stay in `secrets.0`, and no rotation removes them (`produce::removals` takes established epochs only). That actor's `secrets.0` then grows with every later rotation, uncompacted.
- Not checked by the rotation property: it compares only established epochs' secrets.
