# Sync differential fails at the gate's size on some seeds
> Summary: `differential::histories_hold_their_closure_plain` at small size fails on fresh offsets two ways: a node keeps or reads a thing through a role chain whose first grant it no longer takes up (two panics, one root: the unaccepted-grant class of issues/differential-full-size-classes.md, parked on a design question), and a caught-up reader lacks part of a must-hold minting body after a connection reset (one world in 50, studied, parked).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine, tests

**Seen**: seeds 959507911307, 318840271993 (gate offsets) fail on main. Replay: `OSIS_SYNC_START=<seed> OSIS_SYNC_SEEDS=1 cargo test -p osis-sync --test it -- --ignored --exact differential::histories_hold_their_closure_plain`; scripts replay in a scratch test (notes/sync-test-harness.md, Replaying a script).

## A role counted through a grant no longer taken up (two panics)
- Shrunk script (10 steps), fails on 50 of 50 worlds: `New(2), New(0), Run, Strong(1, None, 1), Give(2, 1, Holder::Node(1), Reads), Run, NewIn(0, 0), Unstrong(1, None, 1), Give(0, 2, Holder::Thing(1), Syncs), Strong(1, None, 2)`. Panic: `node 1 keeps 21 store entries under thing 2's sync ID, which it does not hold`. Seed 959507911307 (`node 1 reads thing 2: Some(Str("v4")), the model does not`) is the same shape: Bob drops his strong to T1 (on which he holds write), T1 is granted read on T2, Bob accepts T2 (on which he holds sync directly); `why_role` reads through Bob -Write-> T1 -Read-> T2.
- Seed-0 form: Alice grants Bob read on T1; Bob accepts, then drops his strong to T1; Alice mints T2 and grants T1 sync on T2; Bob writes a strong to T2. `why_role`: role Sync by the chain Bob -Read-> T1 -Sync-> T2, from the role commits T2's certificates bring.
- The model is right: a grant gives a role only while accepting takes it up (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Carried commits, Nothing before the strong; held-model `roles_from_reached`), so Bob's grant on T1 counts for nothing while no held strong reaches T1, and Bob has no role on T2. This is the unaccepted-grant class (issues/differential-full-size-classes.md, 138): an accepted-then-dropped grant is an unaccepted one.
- Two facets, both needed:
  1. `rotation::role_here` (`Core::keyed_role_now` over the account's edges) counts every chain, whatever the node takes up. With the rule (the account's own edges count only on things a held strong reaches), the 10-step script never enters T2. The full-size class's note says why a filter on `role_here` failed 10 engine and osis tests: the role is asked inside a transaction before its settle sets what is reached, LocalPeer worlds hold things with no strong, and the reached set itself depends on the role (entering a thing reads its strongs), so the rule is a fixpoint like the model's `closure`, not a filter.
  2. The account's own D ticket echoes a judgement back as a role: once Bob's node entered T2 kept unread (strong to T2 written before the strong to T1 is dropped: `..., Give(0, 2, Holder::Thing(1), Syncs), Strong(1, None, 2), Run, Unstrong(1, None, 1)` fails the same way), it wrote D's sync ticket for T2 (spec/sync-set.md §1 The account's tickets), and a sync word always counts (spec/sync-set.md §2 What a held ticket does: role commits "cannot decide" for a thing kept unread). So nothing the node learns afterwards lowers its role; D's ticket goes only when T2 is released, which the ticket itself prevents. So by `tickets::role_on` (the commits' role or a counting word) fixing (1) alone leaves this order failing.
- Parked: both facets are the design question the full-size class states (where in the role computation every caller agrees on what is taken up), plus a spec question for (2): whether a sync word of the account's own D ticket may count where the role commits the node holds show the chain it was judged by. Not a filter on one caller.

## A must-hold body a caught-up reader lacks (one world in 50)
- Shrunk script (6 steps): `New(1), Give(1, 0, Holder::Node(0), Reads), Run, Strong(0, None, 0), Lossy(Loss { drop: 0, dup: 25, delay: 0, delay_ms: 312 }), Run`; fails on seed 318840271993's world only (0 of 50 others). Panic: `must-hold bodies a caught-up reader lacks at quiet`: Bob mints T (dependent on his D), grants Alice read (T keyed, its own sync ID S); Alice's node accepts, her sibling node 2 lacks part of T's minting commit.
- What the trace shows (node 2): at the grant Bob's node logs the minting's blob verbatim under S (the blob it holds under D's sync ID). Node 2's fetch of S's head entries from Bob's node is lost with a duplicate's connection reset; it fetches the minting blob from Alice's node 0 first and applies the commit, its u:v.0 part (written while T's secrets derived from D's) unopened. The role commits arrive on Bob's next connection, after which every verdict moves to Must, and node 2 holds the blob and opens its wrap, but the commit's u:v.0 part stays missing at quiet: nothing hands node 2 a section of it under an epoch of T. On the other worlds node 2 reads T's first value, so some other path covers the part there (not yet traced).
- Open: whether a holder may pass the D-era minting blob on under S verbatim (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on: the wrap gates the header and the floors clause binds parts "on a thing whose read secrets are those of the wrap's sync ID's thing") or must log a fresh encryption of S's body, and which path gives the part on the passing worlds. Trace a passing world of the script next (`OSIS_SYNC_TRACE=all:<commit>,<blob>`).
