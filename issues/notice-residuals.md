# Notices: opened by every holder of the taking epoch; departed peers see deletes and heads
> Summary: a notice is sealed to X's read key at an old epoch, so any party that held that epoch and gets S's blobs (a reader lowered to sync, which stores all of S) opens later removals of holders granted while it read; a departed member is still sent S's Deleted and Head events.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, engine, sync, permissions

- **Every holder of the taking epoch opens a notice** (spec/permissions.md §4 Notices): the taking epoch is the newest epoch M's giving write boxed it, so every node that read X at that epoch holds its secret (epoch 0 for a holder from X's start: every reader X ever had with history). The serving filter (`Engine::tells`) guards only a departed member at a reader source. A reader lowered to sync keeps S as a store and gets every entry, so with the old secrets it kept (a hostile one keeps them) it opens the notice of each later removal of a holder granted while it read: the removal's header (signer, path, holder), its evidence and the remover's records, made after its own departure. spec/sync-hostile.md "a removed reader ... learns at most one rotation past what it read" fails for those. The old form (removal entries under current epochs) showed it nothing. roles-model `told` has one removal and no lowered-to-sync party, so it cannot show this. Directions: seal the notice to the removed holder's own read key (M's current epoch, so M's readers open it and no other former reader of X does), which changes the taking-epoch rule and the model; or accept as a limit (Tess's call).
- **Departed members get Deleted and Head events** (`streams::serve::Streams::event`): `shown` filters only `Added`; a departed member is sent every Deleted (blob IDs it never saw added) and S's head changes: the timing of S's deletes and rotations past its departure. The same holds for a peer held for keys (role entries alone), where the head is needed.
