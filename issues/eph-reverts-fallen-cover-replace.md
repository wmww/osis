# ephemeral_reverts_with_restarts_alike: a fallen own snapshot's covered list body never comes back
> Summary: `ephemeral::ephemeral_reverts_with_restarts_alike_large_spec_crdt_4_6` seed 8 (small size) fails on main: a thing the writer created, which the admin lists, is listed by the admin and not by the writer at the end. The writer's own `group.0` snapshot, made while cut off, falls with its demotion; the bodies it covered (the admin's snapshot and the add) are missing at the writer and never come back, since the admin's placement again of its snapshot was deleted by the writer before the demotion and is never judged again after it.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: tests, node

- Replay: `OSIS_EPH_START=8 OSIS_EPH_SEEDS=1 cargo test -p osis-node --test it -- --ignored --exact ephemeral::ephemeral_reverts_with_restarts_alike_large_spec_crdt_4_6` (about 2 s). Listed in tools/known_failures.txt.
- What the replay shows: writer b creates thing T in g (its add reaches admin a; a snapshot S1 of `g`'s `group.0` covers it, held valid at both); a host restart cuts b off; b writes, compacts `group.0` (S2, covering S1 and the add) and (as the trace reads) deletes S1's entry at the host. a judges that delete unfounded (it holds S2 invalid or not at all), places S1 again once; b deletes that too (`again_deleted`: stands). a then demotes b: S2 falls at b, which reopens S1 and the add (`Changed::undropped`), but holds no entry of them and the host holds none; a's `delete_stands` for S1 is never run again after the demotion, though its own head moved (the rotation), so S1 is never placed again.
- To find: why no pass at a judges S1 after its head moved (scope of the pass after a rotation, or the again mark), and whether a's view of the fall should clear the mark (`again_fell`: S2 arrived at a invalid, so nothing fell there).
