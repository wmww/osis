# A departed party can read the fresh deposit seed
> Summary: accepted residual. The fresh deposit seed an admin's read rotation writes sits in `keys.0`, which travels wrapped under epochs the departed party still holds in three places: the rotation commit itself (wrapped under the epoch it supersedes, spec/crypto.md §5), the keyset commits a rotation, grant or re-seal of an actor holding M carries (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried), and the actor's chain certificates under any label the departed party still reads. A party that sees one can keep depositing under the actor's labels (filling their deposit budgets) until the seed is refreshed again. No clean fix exists without moving the seed out of certificate-visible `keys.0`.
> Tags: security, sync, engine

Severity: low (deposits only: junk fills a label's deposit budget, grants and heartbeats wait, admins delete junk; anyone holding an actor's `keys.0` can already do this to its inbox, notes/sync.md).

Exact windows, for X departing actor M (rotation by an admin writing M's fresh seed):
- **Open connection**: X holds M's label open on a connection to a host when the remover's `reregister` uploads the rotation commit there (spec/sync.md §5: role commits before the step). It is pushed to X at once, or fetchable until the Register right behind it; the new head (another sync key) then ends X's open there. After the new head no connection of X can open the label (old sync key gets only the child step; X is in no claim set), so outside these windows the rotation commit is unreachable for X at an honest host.
- **A host without the new step** (unverified whether a writer's pass uploads it there): a writer of M's label uploading the rotation commit (a closure body, wrapped under the superseded epoch) to a host where no admin has registered the new step yet lets X open there under the old sync key and download it until an admin's pass registers the step there.
- **Carried** (spec/crypto.md §4): for X's departure from M where M holds a role on actor A, A's rotation, grant or re-seal sealing M at its new keyset places M's rotation commit (its `keys.0`, seed included) under A's labels, wrapped under A's epochs X may still read (A is not rotated for a departure inside M). Lasts while X can open that entry there.
- **Certificates elsewhere**: M's `keys.0` delta (seed included) goes whole into M's chain certificates under every label where a node acting as or through M writes; if X still reads such a label (X removed from team M, still a direct holder on a project H that M holds a role on), X reads the seed for as long as it reads that label.

Why no fix: a seed-only commit wrapped under the new epoch would become M's certificate `keys.0` (certificates carry only `latest_on(actor, "keys.0")`) and drop the keysets from later chain certificates; deriving the seed from the newest read secret plus a public nonce breaks inbox deposits (the inbox is by default the home label, and granters hold only `keys.0`); making certificates carry the newest writer of each `keys.0` field still leaves the certificate window. The refresh therefore only stops a departed party that sees none of the above.

Where: core/engine/src/rotation/mod.rs `refreshes_deposit` / `fresh_deposit_seed`; core/engine/src/rotation/mod.rs `carry`; core/node/src/sync/pass/register.rs `reregister`.

## Worse than junk deposits (chat design review)
A departed writer's deposit can be a valid commit, e.g. an edit of its own old content on a thing it still owns, placed in the shared space: promotion checks no authority (`pass/mod.rs` promote/`due_deposits`, by reading) and readers apply it. Proposed smallest fix: a deposited entry's content deltas on closure things are not applied from that entry and not promoted. Honest non-writers never deposit closure content (spec/sync.md §3, `may_deposit`: role commits only), so this matches requirements ("posting into a shared space needs write on its actor") and leaves only junk deposits, the severity above. Apply path not traced.
