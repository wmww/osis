# A departed party can read the fresh deposit seed
> Summary: accepted residual. The fresh deposit seed an admin's read rotation writes sits in `/keys`, which travels wrapped under epochs the departed party still holds in three places: the rotation commit itself (wrapped under the epoch it supersedes, spec/crypto.md §5), the §4 exception copies of a member actor's `/keys` (spec/permissions.md §4), and the actor's chain certificates under any label the departed party still reads. A party that sees one can keep depositing under the actor's labels (filling their deposit budgets) until the seed is refreshed again. No clean fix exists without moving the seed out of certificate-visible `/keys`.
> Tags: security, sync, engine

Severity: low (deposits only: junk fills a label's deposit budget, grants and heartbeats wait, admins delete junk; anyone holding an actor's `/keys` can already do this to its inbox, notes/sync.md).

Exact windows, for X departing actor M (rotation by an admin writing M's fresh seed):
- **Open connection** (closable, issues/opens-survive-new-step.md): X holds M's label open on a connection to a server when the remover's `reregister` uploads the rotation commit there. It is pushed to X at once (before the Register in the same batch) or fetchable on that connection, which stays open across the new step; X opens it under the superseded epoch. After the new head, a fresh connection of X cannot open the label (old sync key gets only the child step; X is in no claim set), so outside these windows the rotation commit is unreachable for X at an honest server.
- **A server without the new step** (unverified whether a writer's pass uploads it there): a writer of M's label uploading the rotation commit (a closure body, wrapped under the superseded epoch) to a server where no admin has registered the new step yet lets X open there under the old sync key and download it until an admin's pass registers the step there.
- **§4 exception** (spec/permissions.md §4): for X's departure from M where M is a member of actor A (or below one), A's non-rotating members place M's current `/keys` commit, projected to its `/keys` delta (seed included), under A's read epoch that does not cover the departure, which X reads. Lasts while that entry is under A's label and X can open it there (X keeps A's access until A is rotated).
- **Certificates elsewhere**: M's `/keys` delta (seed included) goes whole into M's chain certificates under every label where a node acting as or through M writes; if X still reads such a label (X removed from team M, still a direct member of a project H that M is a member of), X reads the seed for as long as it reads that label.

Why no fix: a seed-only commit wrapped under the new epoch would become M's certificate `/keys` (certificates carry only `latest_on(actor, "/keys")`) and drop the keysets from later chain certificates; deriving the seed from the newest read secret plus a public nonce breaks inbox deposits (the inbox is by default the home label, and granters hold only `/keys`); making certificates carry the newest writer of each `/keys` field still leaves the certificate window. The refresh therefore only stops a departed party that sees none of the above.

Where: core/engine/src/rotation/mod.rs `refreshes_deposit` / `fresh_deposit_seed`; core/engine/src/labeling/certs.rs `inside_member_revokes`; core/node/src/sync/pass.rs `reregister`.
