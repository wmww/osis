# A departed party can read the fresh deposit seed
> Summary: accepted residual. The fresh deposit seed an admin's read rotation writes sits in `keys.0`, which travels wrapped under epochs the departed party still holds in three places: the rotation commit itself (wrapped under the epoch it supersedes, spec/crypto.md §5), the keyset commits a rotation, grant or re-seal of an actor holding M carries (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried), and the actor's chain certificates under any sync ID the departed party still reads. A party that sees one can keep depositing under the actor's sync IDs (filling their deposit budgets) until the seed is refreshed again. No clean fix exists without moving the seed out of certificate-visible `keys.0`.
> Priority: low
> Tags: security, sync, engine

spec/crypto.md §3 asks for a fresh seed in each read rotation but does not claim it stops a departed party.

Repro (the rotation commit path): Dave's plain `t`, granted to group H and synced under its own sync ID; H's reader Bob reads `t`; Dave removes H (`set_role(t, H, None, ..)`); after sync Bob's `keys.0` of `t` has Dave's new `deposit_seed`, from `t`'s own sync ID, where the removal's body is (one blob: the boxes need the old wrap, `keys.0` does not). A holder who read the thing through another sync ID does not learn it. By the same design (a holder's new `keys.0` goes under its targets' sync IDs, spec/permissions.md §3, §4) a reader of target S learns holder T's current seed with no right on T; a revoke brings no keyset of a thing no actor path connects to the sync ID. (Shown before the sync rebuild; no osis-sync test yet.)

Severity: low (junk deposits only; a deposit of valid closure content by a non-writer is never promoted, spec/sync-inbox.md §4: junk fills a sync ID's deposit budget, grants wait, admins delete junk; anyone holding an actor's `keys.0` can already do this to its inbox, notes/inbox.md).

Windows, for X departing actor M (rotation by an admin writing M's fresh seed):
- **Before the new step**: the rotation commit (a closure body, wrapped under the superseded epoch) is logged before the Register of the new step (spec/sync-ids.md §6: role commits before the step). Until the step lands at a peer keeping M's sync ID unread, X can open there under the old sync key and fetch it; the new head (another sync key, X in no claim set) then ends X's open.
- **Carried** (spec/crypto.md §4): for X's departure from M where M holds a role on actor A, A's rotation, grant or re-seal sealing M at its new keyset logs M's rotation commit (its `keys.0`, seed included) under A's sync IDs, wrapped under A's epochs X may still read (A is not rotated for a departure inside M). Lasts while X can open that entry there.
- **Certificates elsewhere**: M's `keys.0` delta (seed included) goes whole into M's chain certificates under every sync ID where a node acting as or through M writes; if X still reads such a sync ID (X removed from team M, still a direct holder on a project H that M holds a role on), X reads the seed for as long as it reads that sync ID.

Why no fix: a seed-only commit wrapped under the new epoch would become M's certificate `keys.0` (certificates carry only `latest_on(actor, "keys.0")`) and drop the keysets from later chain certificates; deriving the seed from the newest read secret plus a public nonce breaks inbox deposits (the inbox is by default the home sync ID, and granters hold only `keys.0`); making certificates carry the newest writer of each `keys.0` field still leaves the certificate window. The refresh therefore only stops a departed party that sees none of the above. Not yet weighed: sealing the seed in the rotation's boxes rather than in `keys.0` (who deposits then needs a box).

Where: core/engine/src/rotation/mod.rs `refreshes_deposit` / `fresh_deposit_seed`; the Register order in core/sync/src/inbox/register.rs.
