# No own cover past a blob
> Summary: Ephemeral deltas are never paged (spec/crdt.md §8), so one aspect's own state is covered only while its delta fits a blob (`MAX_BLOB`, 8 MiB), and an actor's cover only while its header lists stay inline (`CHUNK_THRESHOLD`, over a thousand aspects). Past either no cover is built and the author's commits pile up (lossless, unbounded at the author; servers and holders stop at their caps); a single take past a blob is not sent. Residual of the 64 KiB version, which unpaged deltas fixed.
> Tags: engine, ephemeral

- Today: `compact_own_ephemeral` reports `EphemeralRefused` and weighs again once the held commits double (`cover_est`); `own_ephemeral_load` now reports the measured cover even when it cannot be built, so the `COVER_FACTOR` load checks fail there (correctly). A take whose delta does not fit is dropped with `EphemeralRefused`: those writes are in no commit and no later cover (covers join held commits and unsent writes, not the state).
- Why left: at default caps (16 MiB per label at servers and holders) a cover past 8 MiB is refused anyway (a cap must hold `COVER_FACTOR` times the covers). The header-list limit is the likelier one in practice (many ephemeral things per actor).
- Fix directions: split a cover into several commits by aspect (`drop_absorbed` already unions absorbers across commits; node-sync's `eph_cover`/`eph_held_at` assume one absorber and one blob per label and would need all of them); for one aspect past a blob, a per-aspect limit refused at write (needs a cheap bound on the own state's encoded size) or real paging (page blobs as lane and server ephemeral entries).
