# Rotators each lacking the other's fork stall for good
> Summary: With "rotate only holding every current secret" (spec/crypto.md §4, Boxes), two writers each holding only their own fork of a role, both blocked by a departure, can neither rotate nor re-seal each other (a re-seal is encrypting under the blocked epoch): neither writes again once every node holding both forks is gone. Honest histories reach it.
> Tags: keys, engine, liveness

**Repro**: `rotation::tests::forks_unboxed_with_departure_stall_spec_crypto_4` (ignored, fails: 6 of 24 seeds reach the shape, all 6 stall). B and D, writers of G granted concurrently, each rotate G's read without knowing the other holds it (each leaves the other out); admin A removes reader X from its own view (its removal epoch boxes both) and is never heard from again. B and D each hold their own fork and A's epoch; when each one's newest held epoch is its own fork (not covering X's departure), each is blocked. Rotating would supersede the other's fork, made by a writer (`rotation::may_supersede` false); re-sealing the other at its own fork fails `require_covered` in `reseal_one`. With A online, A holds every fork and merges. Before the rule each rotated with its newest secret and went on (losing the other's fork from its history box).

**Who sees what**: every write under G fails at B and D ("does not cover a known departure"). The way out by hand: B or D removes the other (then `lacked_made_by` lets it rotate).

**Candidate fix** (tried in review): `reseal_one` rotates where it can (`ensure_rotated`) and re-seals even while the role stays blocked, instead of `require_covered`. The probe passes; osis-engine tier 1, `withheld_departures_leak_nothing_large`, `forged_departs_leak_nothing_large` and `forked_reseals_settle_large` pass. Argument: a re-seal boxes current epochs' secrets (and their history boxes, which the rotation commit already carried) to a current holder only, at a keyset Seal allows, so it exposes a blocked epoch to nobody new. It needs a spec change: spec/crypto.md §4 Encrypting under counts "a box carrying its secret", so a re-seal at a blocked epoch is forbidden today; the exception would be "a re-seal's boxes to a current holder". Needs its own security review (holder actors at stale or keyless boxes, possession) and a TLA check (Depth0 has `HeldAll`; re-seal at blocked epochs is not modelled).
