# A forged half-valid rotation of a holder actor leaks to its removed forger
> Summary: A writer of holder actor M whose role on M runs through another holder actor can publish a rotation of M whose `keys.0` part is valid and whose `secrets.0` part fails Authority. Its keyset is a held valid keyset (an anchor, a Seal target) but its epoch is established nowhere, so no knowing rotation of M ever lists it: A's admins keep sealing M at it, and the forger opens A's new content after its removal, for good. Modelled fix (not built): Authority judged from proofs in the `keys.0` keyset itself (A's admins hold M's rotations projected to `keys.0`, so judging the `secrets.0` part fails there), reading keysets as of just before the commit's key (reading them as held now has no unique fixpoint around holder-actor cycles).
> Priority: critical
> Tags: security, keys, engine, tess-call

Replay: `OSIS_ROTATION_START=<seed> OSIS_ROTATION_SEEDS=1 cargo test -p osis-engine --lib -- --ignored --exact rotation::tests_property::rotation_holds_tier2_spec_crypto_4`. Seeds 0..1000 failing when filed: 75, 107, 130, 147, 319, 331, 450, 482, 531, 607, 631, 875 (stale once the generator or wire changes). Each holds such a half-valid rotation; 607 fails the "made by a rotator that saw it depart" check, the others the "encrypts under" check; all have holder actors and forgers.

## Shape (seed 482)
- Peer F is first admin of actor S; S holds a role on actor M; F reaches M only through S.
- F leaves M by a self-removal that also rotates M (`tests_departs::Forge::Rotating`). Its `keys.0` part on M is valid (judged by role at the commit's key); its `secrets.0` part fails Authority (F shows no possession of S). So M's keyset k_F is held valid everywhere, while k_F's epoch is established nowhere (`EpochInfo::rotations` takes valid `secrets.0` parts only).
- A box of A to M names k_F, so k_F is an anchor of M for A.
- M's honest admin removes F and rotates M knowingly. That keyset lists M's current epochs, never k_F's (not established), so it does not descend from k_F, and k_F is not stale (no held valid keyset lists it, its body is valid).
- A's admin then seals M at k_F (newest keyset descending from the anchor). It cannot judge k_F (it lacks its secret: the stated limit), so the departure from M blocks nothing. F holds k_F's secret and opens A's new epochs. The spec limit says this lasts "until A is sealed at a keyset of M descending from a rotation of M that knows"; here no such keyset can exist.

## The property and the options
Property: a keyset counts (held valid, anchor, Seal target) exactly when its rotation counts. Today "held valid keyset" is the `keys.0` body's validity, and "established" is the rotation commit's (its `secrets.0` part), so they can split.
- **A. Authority judges the whole rotation**: a rotation failing Authority makes its `keys.0` delta on M invalid too (spec/crypto.md §4 Authority, spec/permissions.md §5.2). Then k_F is held invalid, A's boxes naming it are stale, A rotates. Cost: a validity cycle. Authority reads the possession keys of held valid keysets of A's holder actors, and those keysets' validity would now depend on their own rotations' Authority, which reads keysets of their holder actors, and so on around a holder-actor cycle. The fixpoint must be shown unique and arrival-order independent, or Authority must read possession keys from keysets valid by role rules alone (a second notion of "valid keyset").
- **B. Keep validity, widen staleness and Seal**: a keyset whose every rotation body sits in a commit whose `secrets.0` part on M is invalid counts as held invalid for Seal, anchors and Stale (rule 2), while Possession keeps reading every role-valid keyset's keys. No cycle (Seal and Stale feed no validity), but two notions of a valid keyset.

## Model results (core/test/tla/rotation/README.md, `D0_half*`, `Authority.tla`)
- `D0_half` reproduces the leak under today's rules (fails `NoLeakMember`).
- **A judged on the `secrets.0` part does not hold** (`D0_half_a_ns` fails): A's admin holds M's rotations only as certificates projected to `keys.0` (spec/permissions.md §4, Carried), never their `secrets.0` part, so it cannot judge Authority, holds k_F valid and seals M at it as today. It passes only where every node syncs M (`D0_half_a`). B fails the same way: A's admin cannot see whether k_F's epoch is established.
- **A holds when Authority is judged from what travels with the keyset** (`D0_half_keys_ns` passes): the rotation's keyset in `keys.0` carries the possession proof (per holder actor the author's role runs through, a signature by the possession key of a keyset of it over the new keyset), so every node holding the keyset judges the rotation alike. Then the keyset, the epoch's establishment and the boxes all follow one verdict on the `keys.0` body. A box removal that is no rotation (a re-seal replacing a box) has no keyset and keeps its box signature.
- **The validity cycle is real** with Authority reading keysets as held now (`Authority.tla`, rule `"held"`): around a 2-cycle two fixpoints (arrival order picks one), around a 3-cycle none (re-judging loops forever: three rotations boxing nobody, each valid iff the next actor has no valid keyset). Both come from the antitone clauses (a holder counts only while it has a valid keyset; a keyset with no valid body is not judged). **Fix: read keysets as of just before the commit's key**, as Authority already reads the author's roles: a keyset counts for a rotation only by a body ordered before it, so validity is a recursion down the key order (rule `"order"`: one result, re-judging ends, in every 2- and 3-cycle configuration). Honest rotations lose nothing: a writer holds a keyset's secret only after its rotation commit, and the HLC orders that commit first. A keyset whose only bodies come at or after the rotation counts as not held there (not judged), within the existing made-up-keyset limit (issues/keys-depth0-removed-member-rotates.md).
- Not built: the fix moves possession into `keys.0` (wire, spec/crypto.md §4 Authority, spec/wire.md §7, osis-keys possession, the writer), a design change past the brief. Rejected: carrying rotations' `secrets.0` parts as certificates (a box per holder per role in every certificate).
