# The gate takes 30 minutes; Tess's limit is 5
> Summary: The whole gate (default tests, every selected property, sim at 15 seeds) is to take 5 minutes, capped there, by fixing perf, not by shrinking sizes; on ostrich an every-crate gate holds the lock 17.5 min (10,240 CPU-s) and takes about 22 min in all. Where the time goes, what was fixed, and each remaining perf problem with what fixing it would save.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: tests, performance

## The budget
5 minutes wall for the whole gate but its lock wait (notes/build.md Time limits, `gate`), on ostrich (16 threads): at most about 16 x 300 = 4800 CPU-s if every thread is busy, about 4000 at the box's usual 85%. The unlocked steps take 30-60 s warm (instant checks 3-14 s, clippy and the GUI lint a few s warm, builds 0-120 s after a rebase, deny 4 s; the unification check, when a manifest changed, 116 s / 1770 CPU-s with a GUI rebuild): so the hold gets about 4 minutes, 3500 CPU-s. Breadth that leaves the gate waits for release checks (notes/coordination.md), so sizes stay; the work per seed has to fall about 4x.

## Where it goes (ostrich, main 7f0db4ff5 with the harness fixes below; CPU-s, wall at load 12-30)
- Default tests: 4550-4730 CPU-s, 385-495 s (10,745 G instructions; 3479 tests, 35 binaries). CPU-bound: even alone on the box, at 16 threads it cannot go under about 290 s. Over the `test` limit alone (10 s CPU or 15 s wall): 32 tests (notes/time-limits.md); the biggest `order::held_set_decides` (337 CPU-s before the harness fixes below, 7% of the run), `shred::past_expiry_and_purge_alike_in_every_order_spec_commits_5` 66, `net_records::no_connection_resumes_...` 36, osis `port_clients::client_session_differential_against_grants` 31, `stream::stream_differential_paged_spec_sync_10` 26. The long tail (3400 tests at 0.2-5 CPU-s) is most of it: per-change engine cost (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md) and the debug checks (notes/testing.md: index check about 10%, rebuild check 2.5% of osis-node `it`).
- Properties, every crate selected, each timed alone before the harness fixes below: 35 tests, 6095 CPU-s, 13,988 G. `held_set_decides_exhaustive` 3307 (54%), `rotation_holds_large` 418, `held_set_decides_plain` 426, `incremental_equals_from_scratch_large` 375, `epochs_model_agrees_large` 267, `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` 181 (100 s wall: 2 seeds of 90 s each), `random_churn_converges_large` 141, `stream_check_matches_whole_large` 107 (single-threaded, 107 s wall), `relay_differential_large` 109, `crdt_equals_model_large` 93; the other 25 under 75 each.
- Sim at 15 seeds, each scenario alone: 52 scenarios, 3414 CPU-s, 6985 G. `member_churn` 982 (29%; one seed about 200 s wall), `team_reads_member_thing` 224, `grant_to_group` 103, `work_sets` 102, `deposit_flood` 87, `purge_tombstones` 81; the rest 11-75.

## Harness fixes made (same coverage)
- `held::compare` ran the held-model up to four times per node judged (`evaluate`, then `timeline` up to three times, each a full `forgotten` walk), and again for every arrival order of the same held set: now once per distinct input per history (`History::judged`). `order::held_set_decides` 910 -> 703 G; `held_set_decides_exhaustive` 7259 -> 5660 G at one offset (8 seeds, 6926 orders, the same 205,974 aspects over 35,508 nodes compared).
- `order::fresh` made a new account (key generation, signatures) for every order: forked from one made per thread; each held set's oracle makes its own, so every order is still compared against a node that never opened. `held_set_decides` 703 -> 660 G.
- `order::shown` printed every read with `Debug` and compared the strings: a fifth of `held_set_decides_exhaustive` was `fmt`. Reads are compared as values, printed only for a message.
- `stream_check_matches_whole_large_spec_8` ran 600 fixed seeds on one thread (107 s wall): 600 on the seed runner from `OSIS_CRDT_START`, so each gate takes a fresh range (a seed's types and page parameters are its residue mod 9: any 600 cover them alike), 17 s wall in the gate. `grant_and_removal_orders_large` ran its seeds in a loop: on the seed runner, 24 -> 8-14 s wall.
- `tombstone_alike_in_every_order_exhaustive` (57 s) and `forged_parts_beside_a_part_past_expiry_alike_in_every_order_exhaustive` (35 s) played their orders one after another on one thread: the same cases on a thread per core (`par_map`), 9 s for both.
- `LocalPeer`'s `Peer::receive_with` decoded every delivered blob twice besides the engine's own decode (an eagerly built tuple for the bad-input branch, and `note`'s decode for the id): the id comes from the outcome, the blob is decoded only for bad input or a held commit missing a part. `rotation_holds_large` 31.2 -> 24.1 G (4 seeds); every `LocalPeer` test gains alike.
- `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` reloaded every node's every aspect from the store after every step (`World::seen`, stored check), though a step changes one or two nodes: a node and aspect whose read point (`Engine::point`: a read repeats while it stands) and cached state are those of its last stored check is not reloaded. Seed 3: 530 -> 378 G.
- `epochs_model_agrees_large` checked every observer at every receipt time of any observer: the check is a function of (world, observer, held set), so a time that brought the observer nothing is skipped (300 worlds: 21,246 -> 13,883 checks); the model's verdict carries its side and parties, so each check judges once instead of four times. Default test 8.6 -> 4.2 G.
- `incremental` (`answers`, under `MOVES`) asked `valid_from` once per point though it takes no point: once per node and thing. Default test 21.8 -> 16.7 G.

## Remaining perf problems, with what each would save
1. `member_churn` (sim): 43% of it in `Engine::body_keeps` -> `retention::bounds::replaced_at`, called by node-sync `must_hold`/`keeps` on every label pass: per-pass recomputation of a per-change fact (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md, owned there). About 420 CPU-s of the sim's 3414, and its one 200 s seed sets the sim's wall.
2. `held_set_decides_exhaustive`: ingest about 62% (every order replays every commit into a fresh node: the property's own work), `Engine::check_index` 28% (2 seeds profiled), `osis_crdt::stream::stream` 15%. The index check samples per engine (first and sixteenth changing transaction), and every order is a forked engine. Nothing in the property's structure makes those samples redundant: the fork base holds only an account, so each fork's sampled states are its order's own; the one repeat, the open of the base, is inside each check and not separable. Orders sharing a prefix reach equal states, but recognising one needs a digest of the store per sampled transaction. The lever is the sampling policy itself, issues/tests-4x.md pattern 2 (a Tess call): about 500 CPU-s here.
3. `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` (seed 3, 378 G after the fix above): `World::seen`'s stored checks of the nodes a step changed, and `osis_commit::verify::stream_part` 25%. Of `stream_part`, the canonical re-check proper (re-encoding pages, `Pager::push`, `encode_entry`, `prune_stable`, `Out::finish`) is about 7% of the property; the rest is decoding and the joins the callers do. It runs on every re-read of a stored part (`apply::atomic::stream_stored`: the debug rebuild check 11%, apply's joins 6%, the copy index 3%, `settle_none` 2%), so a stored part is verified again at each read: a product inefficiency, but about 7% here and less elsewhere. Fix when worth it: a decode-only stream for stored parts (the wire order of streamed positions comes from the check's root re-encode today, so that order needs another source).
4. A property's wall is its slowest seed when it has few: `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` (2 seeds, 60-90 s each), `held_set_decides_exhaustive` (8 seeds whose orders share the threads; one seed about 1/8 of 2110 CPU-s). Cheaper seeds (items 2, 3) are the fix; more threads per seed only where a seed's orders are independent (exhaustive already does).
5. Profiled (one or two seeds, or the default sibling): `rotation_holds_large` is ingest and sync (`LocalPeer::sync_all` 75%; harness decode fixed above). `incremental_equals_from_scratch_large`: the property's own oracles (`answers` before and after every arrival under `MOVES`, `Timeline::evaluate` from scratch each step), whose `Timeline::role` at past points walks again each query (`roles_walk`). `epochs_model_agrees_large` after the fix: `random_world` 26%, perm itself 26%, the model's judgment the rest. `relay_differential_large`, `random_churn_converges_large`: node-sync passes (`judge_label`, `must_hold`, `body_keeps`: issues/derived-state-recomputed.md) and `check_index` 17% in relay (each `Net::restart` is a new engine, so its first samples fire again: item 2's policy). No harness waste left found.
6. Default tests: nothing short of the engine work (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md: its `Measured` puts node-sync passes at about 70% of a typical osis test) moves the 4500 CPU-s much; the 32 tests over the `test` limit hold about 900 CPU-s. Of the biggest: `shred::past_expiry_and_purge_alike_in_every_order` is 84% the forensic attack (`osis_store::attack`, every row ever written against every key found, twice per case for 195 cases): the property's definition, brute force by design; `net_records::no_connection_resumes_...`, `stream::stream_differential_paged`: node-sync passes, `check_index` 11-16% and the rebuild check in `drop_body`; osis `port_clients::client_session_differential_against_grants`: three seeds of 40 steps on a real node at default width (issues/tests-4x.md pattern 4).

## The gap
An every-crate gate with these fixes (load 17): unlocked steps 250 s (builds after a rebase 121 s, the unification check with a GUI rebuild 116 s; warm and without a manifest change about 15 s), then the hold 1048 s, 10,240 CPU-s: default tests 398 s / 4101, properties 352 s / 3600 (6095 CPU-s before the fixes, each alone), sim 297 s / about 2540. The 5-minute gate leaves the hold about 4 minutes and 3500 CPU-s: about 3x less work. Candidates: item 1 about 420 CPU-s of the sim and its 200 s seed; item 2 about 500; item 3's re-verification about 7% of that property; item 5's harness waste is fixed above (each of those properties 23-50% less), the rest is engine work. That is about 1000-1500 of the 6700 CPU-s to cut; the rest is the default tests' 4100 and the engine's per-change cost under every test (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md), which no harness fix reaches. Even with properties and sim at zero, the default tests alone are over the hold's budget (398 s wall, 4101 CPU-s).
