# The gate takes 30 minutes; Tess's limit is 5
> Summary: The whole gate (default tests, every selected property, sim at 15 seeds) is to take 5 minutes, capped there, by fixing perf, not by shrinking sizes; each stage's limit is its share of the cap. On ostrich a gate takes about 15 min (8000 CPU-s), its hold 11 min; every stage is about 2x its share, the builds after a rebase 7x. Where the time goes, what was fixed, and each remaining perf problem with what fixing it would save.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: tests, performance

## The budget
5 minutes wall for the whole gate but its lock wait (notes/build.md Time limits, `gate`), on ostrich (16 threads): at most about 16 x 300 = 4800 CPU-s if every thread is busy, about 4000 at the box's usual 85%. Each gate stage's limit is its share of that cap (notes/build.md Time limits, `share of`): the hold gets 105 u (241 s, 3795 CPU-s), the steps outside it 24 u (about 55 s), so an over-cap gate names the stages over their share. Breadth that leaves the gate waits for release checks (notes/coordination.md), so sizes stay; the work per seed has to fall about 3x.

## Where it goes (ostrich, main 7f0db4ff5 with the harness fixes below; CPU-s, wall at load 12-30)
- Default tests: 4550-4730 CPU-s, 385-495 s (10,745 G instructions; 3479 tests, 35 binaries). CPU-bound: even alone on the box, at 16 threads it cannot go under about 290 s. Over the `test` limit alone (10 s CPU or 15 s wall): 5 tests, each multi-threaded, none over 11 s wall (notes/testing.md, Default-test cost); the biggest `order::held_set_decides` (144 CPU-s, 11 s wall). The long tail (3400 tests at 0.2-5 CPU-s) is most of it: per-change engine cost (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md) and the debug checks (notes/testing.md: index check about 10%, rebuild check 2.5% of osis-node `it`).
- Properties, every crate selected, each timed alone before the harness fixes below: 35 tests, 6095 CPU-s, 13,988 G. `held_set_decides_exhaustive` 3307 (54%), `rotation_holds_large` 418, `held_set_decides_plain` 426, `incremental_equals_from_scratch_large` 375, `epochs_model_agrees_large` 267, `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` 181 (100 s wall: 2 seeds of 90 s each), `random_churn_converges_large` 141, `stream_check_matches_whole_large` 107 (single-threaded, 107 s wall), `relay_differential_large` 109, `crdt_equals_model_large` 93; the other 25 under 75 each.
- Sim at 15 seeds (`--from 7000`, under the lock at load 15-20): 1840 CPU-s, 3472 G, 123-128 s wall, CPU-bound and balanced (wall within about 7% of CPU over 16 threads; longest-first order would win about 3 s). Flat: `member_churn` 208 (11%; 7-18 s a seed), `team_reads_member_thing` 123, `purge_tombstones`, `work_sets`, `grant_to_group`, `deposit_flood` 64-66 each; the other 46 scenarios 7-57 each.

## Harness fixes made (same coverage)
- `held::compare` ran the held-model up to four times per node judged (`evaluate`, then `timeline` up to three times, each a full `forgotten` walk), and again for every arrival order of the same held set: now once per distinct input per history (`History::judged`). `order::held_set_decides` 910 -> 703 G; `held_set_decides_exhaustive` 7259 -> 5660 G at one offset (8 seeds, 6926 orders, the same 205,974 aspects over 35,508 nodes compared).
- `order::fresh` made a new account (key generation, signatures) for every order: forked from one made once per process; each held set's oracle makes its own, so every order is still compared against a node that never opened. `held_set_decides` 703 -> 660 G.
- `order::shown` printed every read with `Debug` and compared the strings: a fifth of `held_set_decides_exhaustive` was `fmt`. Reads are compared as values, printed only for a message.
- `stream_check_matches_whole_large_spec_8` ran 600 fixed seeds on one thread (107 s wall): 600 on the seed runner from `OSIS_CRDT_START`, so each gate takes a fresh range (a seed's types and page parameters are its residue mod 9: any 600 cover them alike), 17 s wall in the gate. `grant_and_removal_orders_large` ran its seeds in a loop: on the seed runner, 24 -> 8-14 s wall.
- `tombstone_alike_in_every_order_exhaustive` (57 s) and `forged_parts_beside_a_part_past_expiry_alike_in_every_order_exhaustive` (35 s) played their orders one after another on one thread: the same cases on a thread per core (`par_map`), 9 s for both.
- `LocalPeer`'s `Peer::receive_with` decoded every delivered blob twice besides the engine's own decode (an eagerly built tuple for the bad-input branch, and `note`'s decode for the id): the id comes from the outcome, the blob is decoded only for bad input or a held commit missing a part. `rotation_holds_large` 31.2 -> 24.1 G (4 seeds); every `LocalPeer` test gains alike.
- `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` reloaded every node's every aspect from the store after every step (`World::seen`, stored check), though a step changes one or two nodes: a node and aspect whose read point (`Engine::point`: a read repeats while it stands) and cached state are those of its last stored check is not reloaded. Seed 3: 530 -> 378 G.
- `epochs_model_agrees_large` checked every observer at every receipt time of any observer: the check is a function of (world, observer, held set), so a time that brought the observer nothing is skipped (300 worlds: 21,246 -> 13,883 checks); the model's verdict carries its side and parties, so each check judges once instead of four times. Default test 8.6 -> 4.2 G.
- `held_set_decides` ran a seed per thread, its orders in turn (plain, the default test), or a seed's orders on its share of the cores (exhaustive: 2 of 16 for 8 seeds), so the slowest seed set the wall: every seed's tasks (orders, late parts, clocks, a partial set then the rest, reopens) run on every core, each holding one of the process's permits (`testenv::pooled_map`; osis-perm's shard permits are the same `Permit`). Alone under the lock at load 18-25, base against branch: exhaustive 120 -> 90 s (offset 0) and 178 -> 106 s (offset 531) wall, plain 35 -> 34 s and 60 -> 30-37 s, the default test 33 -> 12 s; instructions within 4% (a late-parts order's split is its own rng draw now, so the sample differs).
- Loops over fixed seeds on one thread: `stream::stream_differential_no_write_dropped_spec_sync_10` (28.5 -> 7.5 s wall) and osis `port_clients::client_session_differential_against_grants` (28.5 -> 6.9 s) on the seed runner; `retention::purge_rights_both_arrival_orders_spec_perm_6` built the same synced writers per case (two `sync_all` of five peers, 75% of it): built once and copied, 14 -> 3 G.
- `incremental` (`answers`, under `MOVES`) asked `valid_from` once per point though it takes no point: once per node and thing. Default test 21.8 -> 16.7 G.
- `held_set_decides_exhaustive` tried every order again with late parts (`split: usize::MAX`), half its cost (late-part ingest 41%): its named three orders and a seeded quarter of the rest per held set with a multi-part commit (`Twins::Share`, `TWIN_SHARE`), none for a held set without one (its twin delivered the same bytes). Alone under the lock, base against branch: offset 0 2172 -> 1255 G, 88 -> 54 s wall; offset 531 2146 -> 1247 G, 83 -> 53 s. The default test keeps every twin of its fixed seeds (a share there saved 3%).

- The sim's Phase A convergence check read every up node's every aspect after every step: a node is read again only when its engine or `Engine::point` moved (one node a write or delivery, every node a tick), the same judgment. Phase A 70.1 -> 48.3 G (6 seeds). Node-sync's duplicate judgment (`judge`, the duplicate with the higher blob ID) computed `readable` for every lower copy before asking if any is genuine: it stops at the first lower readable-equal genuine copy, the same answer. `deposit_flood` 38.1 -> 31.9 G (4 seeds). Sim at 15 seeds, base against branch under the lock: 3472 -> 3388 G, 127.6 -> 120.3 s wall.

## Remaining perf problems, with what each would save
1. Sim: what a seed costs is node-sync and ingest (`SyncEngine::handle` about 70%: `judge_label` 29% with `body_sends`, `judging`, `drop_covered`, `must_hold`/`part_keeps` under it; `log_events_from` 18% with ingest, signature checks and pager commits), the debug index check about 5%: issues/derived-state-recomputed.md. A third is calm setup before the first faulty `run`: sharing it across a sweep's seeds is issues/sim-shared-setup.md (a Tess call, about 30% of the stage). `deposit_flood`'s honest nodes judge each duplicate against every other copy of its commit (`stamped_parts`, `deletable`): quadratic in the copies a flood leaves.
2. `held_set_decides_exhaustive` (8 seeds 1250 G, 53-54 s wall alone): what is left is the property's own work per order (`node_checks`, `Engine::check_index`, held-model, the quarter of orders with late parts): no prefix to share (a setup copy for late-parts orders saved nothing), no check repeated. Lever, a Tess call: the index check's sampling (issues/tests-4x.md pattern 2: each order is a new engine sampled at its first and sixteenth transaction; about 190 CPU-s before the twin cut, so about 120 now).
3. `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` (seed 3: 306 G): `World::seen`'s stored checks of the nodes a step changed, and the decoding and joins its readers do. A stored part is read decode-only (notes/engine-paged.md, Checked once); the full canonical check runs only where bytes enter. That cut seed 3 by 12% (347 -> 306 G), the default `paged_apply_matches_whole` 8% (58.8 -> 54.1 G), node `stream::stream_differential_paged` 5% (37.8 -> 35.9 G), osis `sources::known_stays_here_and_unknown_goes_to_the_node` 5% (1.80 -> 1.72 G). What is left is the property's own work.
4. A property's wall is its slowest seed when its seeds are single-threaded and few: `paged_apply_matches_whole_large` (2 seeds, 60-90 s each). `held_set_decides` spreads every seed's orders over every core (above), so its wall is about its CPU over the box's threads.
5. Profiled (one or two seeds, or the default sibling): `rotation_holds_large` is ingest and sync (`LocalPeer::sync_all` 75%; harness decode fixed above). `incremental_equals_from_scratch_large`: the property's own oracles (`answers` before and after every arrival under `MOVES`, `Timeline::evaluate` from scratch each step), whose `Timeline::role` at past points walks again each query (`roles_walk`). `epochs_model_agrees_large` after the fix: `random_world` 26%, perm itself 26%, the model's judgment the rest. `relay_differential_large`, `random_churn_converges_large`: node-sync passes (`judge_label`, `must_hold`, `part_keeps`: issues/derived-state-recomputed.md) and `check_index` 17% in relay (each `Net::restart` is a new engine, so its first samples fire again: item 2's policy). No harness waste left found.
6. Default tests: nothing short of the engine work (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md: its `Measured` puts node-sync passes at about 70% of a typical osis test) moves the 4500 CPU-s much; the 5 tests over the `test` limit hold about 190 CPU-s, each multi-threaded and under 11 s wall. `shred::past_expiry_and_purge_alike` is 84% the forensic attack (`osis_store::attack`, every row ever written against every key found, twice per case for 105 cases): the property's definition, brute force by design; `net_records::no_connection_resumes_...` (12 seeds), `stream::stream_differential_paged` (3 seeds on the seed runner, one of them most of it): node-sync passes, `check_index` 11-16% and the rebuild check in `drop_body`.

## The gap
An every-crate gate with these fixes (load 17): unlocked steps 250 s (builds after a rebase 121 s, the unification check with a GUI rebuild 116 s; warm and without a manifest change about 15 s), then the hold 1048 s, 10,240 CPU-s: default tests 398 s / 4101, properties 352 s / 3600 (6095 CPU-s before the fixes, each alone), sim 297 s / about 2540 (the sim alone at 15 seeds: 120-160 s, about 1850 CPU-s). The 5-minute gate leaves the hold about 4 minutes and 3500 CPU-s: about 3x less work. Candidates: item 1's shared sim setup about 550 CPU-s (a Tess call); item 2's index check about 120; item 5's harness waste is fixed above (each of those properties 23-50% less), the rest is engine work. That is about 1000-1500 of the 6700 CPU-s to cut; the rest is the default tests' 4100 and the engine's per-change cost under every test (issues/derived-state-recomputed.md), which no harness fix reaches. Even with properties and sim at zero, the default tests alone are over the hold's budget (398 s wall, 4101 CPU-s).
