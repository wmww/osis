> Priority: normal
> Tags: node, sync, proto, spec, security

Read with issues/net-one-peer.md (6c bullet, Sync model, Interim states), spec/sync.md §5 Opens and §10 Shared set. Words: an **unread holder** of L is a node that keeps L and cannot read it (it holds L sync-only); a **reader** reads L. Neither is a kind of node: one node is a reader of some sync IDs and an unread holder of others.

## The rule
**L is in a connection's shared set exactly while each side syncs L and holds, judged by itself on what it holds now, proof that the other may hold L.** Proof is one of:
- **J**: a proven actor holds a role on L, sync or above, through the rosters held here (J counts sync);
- **a secret credential** shown on this connection: a read secret, or the proof of a sync key (its match value derives from the sync secret, Decision 3); its Hit, or its tag answering one (keyed by the secret, bound to the side and connection);
- **an open signature**, bound to its signer's side and this connection, by a credential key of the newest step of L this side knows (a reader: the newest step `label.0` records, or the head a static entry's grant names; a node that cannot read L: its head): its sync key, a claim of its claim set, a key actor its static entries list. A Hit's, or the answer's. A signature by an older step's key proves only that the peer held L once (a removed holder keeps it: model `ON_old`), so an open at an older step (the walk) is toward the peer only by J.

A tag on a public value (a sync key, deposit key, claim or static-entry beacon) proves nothing, and neither does which side dialed. Every other 6c rule follows from this one: the listed-peer gate, the dial-direction rules and the tag-on-public-value opens are what it removes.

## Decisions
1. **Side byte, not "listeners never sign"** (closes issues/net-static-hit-to-dialer.md). The open signature's digest becomes BLAKE3(canonical `[side, label_id, challenge, static_key]`), `side` the signer's (0 dialer, 1 listener), as the connection proof's digest already is (`osis_crypto::proof_digest`). Why: one mechanism (the connection proof, Hit and tag are bound to side and connection already); spec/sync.md §10 "which side dialed changes nothing else" holds, while "listeners never sign" is a carve-out by dial direction that leaves a listener unable to prove anything: two unread holders then never open both ways on one connection (model `O_nolisten_kk`: `NoPair` never violated), an unread holder that dials a reader can never be served by it, and a link redeemer opens only where it dials. With the side byte the dial-direction rules already built can go (Removal audit).
2. **The replier signs its answer.** `Shared` items become `[code, Opt(tag), Opt(grant), Opt(sig)]`: an accepted Hit on a public credential (a sync key, a claim, a static entry) is answered with the replier's open signature by the sync key of the step the open is made at (the Hit's sync key; for a claim, its step's; for a static entry, the head its grant names), where the replier holds that sync secret. A proof Hit needs none: its tag is keyed by the sync secret. The tester opens L only on a valid tag, its credential still admissible, and its own proof of the replier (J, a secret class's tag, or that signature by its own newest step's sync key: a walk's Hit at an older step opens toward the peer only by J); otherwise it ends the Hit with `Lost` as built for a credential gone. One Hit and one answer open L both ways; no half-open state. This closes the issue's first part (a dialer keeping L's claim sets tags a reader's static Hit and is served L), the same hole for a proof's or a sync key's tag, and the leak issues/net-shared-set.md lists under Public match values ("As a replier it can tag a Hit on a public value").
3. **Store credentials go to every connection; every holder tests them whoever dialed; the proof derives from the sync secret.** An unread holder's filter shows its head's sync key (`SYNC`), deposit key and claim-set entries (as built); a reader's shows, beside its read credentials (still only to a known actor), the proof (`PROOF`) of the newest recorded step's sync key of each L it reads, now to every connection. The proof's match value is `kdf("sync proof", s, "")`, `s` the 32-byte seed of that sync key (`kdf("label sync", sync secret, "")`), not the public key: its Hit and its tag prove the sync secret as a read secret's do, so a proof Hit carries no open signature and its answer none. A reader tests its `SYNC` credential (the newest step of L it knows there, §5 Credential); an unread holder tests its head's `SYNC` and `PROOF` where it holds that sync secret. So each pair (reader with unread holder, two unread holders) has a credential on both sides that both test: a change on either side makes it pending there and the pair is found at that side's next test (the pending rule of issues/net-shared-set.md, which a one-sided credential breaks). Readers still never show `SYNC` and never test `PROOF`, so two readers never match a store credential and open only by read secret, static entry or J. Privacy: only a holder of that step's sync secret (one that may open L with the reader anyway) computes the proof's beacon, so a reader's proof entry tells nobody holding only L's public values (a stranger with `label.0`, a removed holder after the rotation) that it syncs L; spec/requirements.md (a node holding data it cannot read learns no identities; a connection reveals the groups it opens) holds with no new question. The public-key proof as built would have told anyone holding `label.0` (model `ON_proofpub`: and opening on its tag would be unsafe). Cost: a reader that lacks the sync secret of its newest recorded step (its box not yet arrived) shows no proof and is found by J only until it arrives. The unread holder's own `SYNC` entry stays public, the open question in issues/net-one-peer.md (A sync-only holder's filter goes to every connection); deriving it from the seed too would leave its deposit-key and claim-set entries telling the same to `label.0` holders, so that alone closes nothing.
4. **J has levels**: `Reader` (read or above), `SyncHolder` (sync alone), `Departed`, `Unknown` (`osis_engine::labeling::judge`). The open rule takes `Reader | SyncHolder`; ByRole stays reader-only (an unread holder holds no roster, so a ByRole never opens there); Deposit items go where J is either holder level (an unread holder of an inbox takes them by its Deposit rules).
5. **A source is a member opened by a store credential, or a peer J judges a `SyncHolder`.** `read_as_store` (a `LabelAt` on the connection and a member) becomes `shared_source(conn, l)`: the member's class is `SYNC`, `PROOF` or a claim, or J of the connection is `SyncHolder`; never `READ` or `JUDGED`. A static-entry open is a source only by J (a static entry is shown by readers and unread holders alike). One open path; its class is the credential's.
6. **Per-label walk state by entitlement.** A `LabelAt` (the walk, Behind items, registration, stale-head state) exists on a connection for L exactly while L is a member there by a store credential, or J judges the peer a `SyncHolder` of L. Found from the roster: an index from account to the things it holds sync-only on (built with the roster, `Core`), so a connection costs the labels its peer's account holds sync on, never every label read here. The stale-head trigger is 3h2's, at those connections only; `a_sync_only_dialer_behind_a_new_sync_key_gets_the_step` is this rule (the reader's walk Hits the dialer's old sync key, opens, registers the child). Unread holders walk nothing and register nothing: they test their head's credentials only, so two unread holders on different heads share L once either is moved by a reader that judges it entitled (as net-one-peer.md, Stale head, says). Limit, stated: where a reader dialed an unread holder that does not know the reader's account, the holder proves nothing back (spec/sync.md §10 Order), so J is unknown there; at the same sync key the holder's proof Hit opens L and the reader walks and registers as at any member, but a holder whose head is behind under another sync key is neither opened nor registered by that reader: it is moved where it dials, or by a reader it knows (its granter).
7. **What a reader keeps of a source: the pair's record, else the connection.** `PeerKey::Node`, `StreamAt` (`y/c`), `Conn::unrecorded` and the dialed-key rows go. Cost, stated: an unread holder knows only the accounts it reads anything of (its granter, contacts), and a record needs each side to know the other (spec/sync.md §10 Pair key), so a reader the unread holder does not know has no record with it and lists L there from the start at each connection (entries, not bodies: bodies are fetched only where the blob ID is missing). That is issues/net-reconnect.md's Limit (a peer with no known actor), which assumed the unread holder's account is always known both ways: rewrite it to say the Limit covers every reader the holder's account does not know. Count it in a test (below). Not decided here, and a requirements question if it matters: a record found by one-sided knowledge (the side that knows proves first) needs spec/requirements.md's "proofs only to a side that already knows them" read differently.

## Model: core/test/tla/net/NetOpen.tla
A reader and an unread holder (or two unread holders) beside Adv, a removed sync holder: it holds an older step's sync secret (one the reader still records, which its walk may Hit) and every public value of L, and no secret of the head.
