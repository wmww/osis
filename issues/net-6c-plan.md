# Step 6c build plan: the intersection
> Summary: The build plan for issues/net-one-peer.md step 6c, to start once net-6b merges: one rule (each side opens L only on its own proof that the peer may hold L: J counting sync, a read secret, or an open signature bound to its signer's side and this connection; a tag on a public value proves nothing), the wire changes (a side in the open signature's digest, closing issues/net-static-hit-to-dialer.md; the replier's open signature in `Shared`), seven commits that each keep main green, the interim states each ends, the removal audit with its grep, the tests by name, and the TLA model `NetOpen` (checked).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: node, sync, proto, spec, security

Read with issues/net-one-peer.md (6c bullet, Sync model, Interim states), spec/sync.md §5 Opens and §10 Shared set. Words: an **unread holder** of L is a node that keeps L and cannot read it (it holds L sync-only); a **reader** reads L. Neither is a kind of node: one node is a reader of some sync IDs and an unread holder of others.

## The rule
**L is in a connection's shared set exactly while each side syncs L and holds, judged by itself on what it holds now, proof that the other may hold L.** Proof is one of:
- **J**: a proven actor holds a role on L, sync or above, through the rosters held here (J counts sync);
- **a read secret** shown on this connection (its Hit, or its tag answering one: keyed by the secret, bound to the side and connection);
- **an open signature** by a credential key of the step the open is made at (a sync key, a claim, a key actor's signing key), bound to its signer's side and this connection: a Hit's, or the answer's.

A tag on a public value (a sync, proof, deposit, claim or static-entry beacon) proves nothing, and neither does which side dialed. Every other 6c rule follows from this one: the listed-peer gate, the dial-direction rules and the tag-on-public-value opens are what it removes.

## Decisions
1. **Side byte, not "listeners never sign"** (closes issues/net-static-hit-to-dialer.md). The open signature's digest becomes BLAKE3(canonical `[side, label_id, challenge, static_key]`), `side` the signer's (0 dialer, 1 listener), as the connection proof's digest already is (`osis_crypto::proof_digest`). Why: one mechanism (the connection proof, Hit and tag are bound to side and connection already); spec/sync.md §10 "which side dialed changes nothing else" holds, while "listeners never sign" is a carve-out by dial direction that leaves a listener unable to prove anything: two unread holders then never open both ways on one connection (model `O_nolisten_kk`: `NoPair` never violated), an unread holder that dials a reader can never be served by it, and a link redeemer opens only where it dials. With the side byte the dial-direction rules already built can go (Removal audit).
2. **The replier signs its answer.** `Shared` items become `[code, Opt(tag), Opt(grant), Opt(sig)]`: an accepted Hit on any credential but a read secret is answered with the replier's open signature by the sync key of the step the open is made at (the Hit's sync key or proof's; for a claim, its step's; for a static entry, the head its grant names), where the replier holds that sync secret. The tester opens L only on a valid tag, its credential still admissible, and its own proof of the replier (J, a read-secret class, or that signature); otherwise it ends the Hit with `Lost` as built for a credential gone. One Hit and one answer open L both ways; no half-open state. This closes the issue's first part (a dialer keeping L's claim sets tags a reader's static Hit and is served L), the same hole for a proof's or a sync key's tag, and the leak issues/net-shared-set.md lists under Public match values ("As a replier it can tag a Hit on a public value").
3. **Store credentials go to every connection; every holder tests them whoever dialed.** An unread holder's filter shows its head's sync key (`SYNC`), deposit key and claim-set entries (as built); a reader's shows, beside its read credentials (still only to a known actor), the proof (`PROOF`) of the newest recorded step's sync key of each L it reads, now to every connection. A reader tests its `SYNC` credential (the newest step of L it knows there, §5 Credential); an unread holder tests its head's `SYNC` and `PROOF` where it holds that sync secret. So each pair (reader with unread holder, two unread holders) has a credential on both sides that both test: a change on either side makes it pending there and the pair is found at that side's next test (the pending rule of issues/net-shared-set.md, which a one-sided credential breaks). Readers still never show `SYNC` and never test `PROOF`, so two readers never match a store credential and open only by read secret, static entry or J. Privacy: a reader's proof entry tells anyone holding L's `label.0` who meets it that it syncs L, as an unread holder's sync-key entry already does; it extends the open Tess question in issues/net-one-peer.md (A sync-only holder's filter goes to every connection) to readers. Reversible: showing proofs only to known actors costs the pending guarantee where the unread holder proves no account (then the reader must test every `SYNC` credential at each arrival, O(labels)).
4. **J has levels**: `Reader` (read or above), `SyncHolder` (sync alone), `Departed`, `Unknown` (`osis_engine::labeling::judge`). The open rule takes `Reader | SyncHolder`; ByRole stays reader-only (an unread holder holds no roster, so a ByRole never opens there); Deposit items go where J is either holder level (an unread holder of an inbox takes them by its Deposit rules).
5. **A source is a member opened by a store credential, or a peer J judges a `SyncHolder`.** `read_as_store` (a `LabelAt` on the connection and a member) becomes `shared_source(conn, l)`: the member's class is `SYNC`, `PROOF` or a claim, or J of the connection is `SyncHolder`; never `READ` or `JUDGED`. A static-entry open is a source only by J (a static entry is shown by readers and unread holders alike). One open path; its class is the credential's.
6. **Per-label walk state by entitlement.** A `LabelAt` (the walk, Behind items, registration, stale-head state) exists on a connection for L exactly while L is a member there by a store credential, or J judges the peer a `SyncHolder` of L. Found from the roster: an index from account to the things it holds sync-only on (built with the roster, `Core`), so a connection costs the labels its peer's account holds sync on, never every label read here. The stale-head trigger is 3h2's, at those connections only; `a_sync_only_dialer_behind_a_new_sync_key_gets_the_step` is this rule (the reader's walk Hits the dialer's old sync key, opens, registers the child). Unread holders walk nothing and register nothing: they test their head's credentials only, so two unread holders on different heads share L once either is moved by a reader that judges it entitled (as net-one-peer.md, Stale head, says).
7. **What a reader keeps of a source: the pair's record, else the connection.** `PeerKey::Node`, `StreamAt` (`y/c`), `Conn::unrecorded` and the dialed-key rows go. Cost, stated: an unread holder knows only the accounts it reads anything of (its granter, contacts), and a record needs each side to know the other (spec/sync.md §10 Pair key), so a reader the unread holder does not know has no record with it and lists L there from the start at each connection (entries, not bodies: bodies are fetched only where the blob ID is missing). That is issues/net-reconnect.md's Limit (a peer with no known actor), which assumed the unread holder's account is always known both ways: rewrite it to say the Limit covers every reader the holder's account does not know. Count it in a test (below). Not decided here, and a requirements question if it matters: a record found by one-sided knowledge (the side that knows proves first) needs spec/requirements.md's "proofs only to a side that already knows them" read differently.

## net-peer-key-superseded
Commit 6 ends its second cause at the root (no row is keyed by a dialed key; a peer is kept by its record or not at all), so a peer moving between proving no known actor and proving one leaves nothing behind. Its first cause (a new record while the old one lingers, both sides unable to link them) is untouched by 6c and needs its own fix, the issue's direction: a source's rows inside its record, counted by its budget and dropped with it. Commit 7 rewrites that issue to the first cause only.

## Model: core/test/tla/net/NetOpen.tla
A reader and an unread holder (or two unread holders) beside Adv, which holds every public value of L and no secret of its head, picks its Hello challenges from those it has seen, dials and is dialed by anyone, shows every public credential, answers any Hit with a tag and replays every signature it was sent on any connection. `Safe`: no honest node opens L toward Adv. `NoPair` (a witness, expected violated): two honest nodes open L both ways on one connection.
