# Members connected to each other stop syncing a thing they share
> Summary: Dogfood smoke pass, three accounts on loopback: a link joiner's first post reached the chat's maker but not another member for minutes (its `status` Synced); a told grant, accepted by holding, gave no role or content for minutes; both cleared only after both daemons restarted. Reproducible: after `unhold` and `hold` again, the grantee never gets the thing back (no record of it, both sides `NotConnected` while connected). Cause unknown.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync

Setup: three accounts on loopback QUIC, `--no-lan`, each daemon given `--dial` addresses; bob added to alice's chat by `chat add`, carol by `chat link`/`chat join`. Bob's and carol's logs showed many `closed: one connection per pair` as each dialed the other (bob also by carol's `peer://` address from her dial entry). A direct `sync --thing X` at carol, connected to bob, timed out. Later relays (carol posting while bob was down, then bob back dialing alice only) worked at once.

Suspects: the duplicate-connection rule (spec/sync-opens.md §1, the later of two stays) leaving a connection open with nothing open on it; a pass that ended while a needed evidence copy (carol's join under K) was missing and was not begun again. A reproducer needs two nodes dialing each other while a third relays, a link join, and a restart.

Seen again, and staying: carol, granted read on bob's X by a told grant (its signal valid on her inbox), held X, `unhold` then `hold` again through her daemon: X is released, and after minutes and a restart of bob's daemon her node knows no record of X (`inspect thing`: `known=false`), her role is none, and both nodes' `status` show X `NotConnected` while connected to each other. A direct `sync --thing X` at carol, dialing bob, times out. So the told grant's signal does not bring X back once X was released: check that a followed signal keeps its thing's stub (spec/sync-set.md §2 First entry) after the node held the thing and let it go, and that the stub opens X's sync ID at bob.
