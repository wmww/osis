# Platforms and input: build the design
> Summary: what remains of notes/app.md (Platforms and input): touch is a mouse to Masonry's widgets (a swipe on Cards reorders and records an edit, a drag in a field selects, nothing scrolls by touch, no long press, no selection menu, paste chips stand in for it); a focused field is never brought into view, so with a soft keyboard up it can be hidden.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: app, android

Steps (one branch each, in order; 3 needs 2; 4 is independent):
2. Touch gestures in kit (`input::Input`, which already counts taps from the platform's thresholds) and masonry widgets by pointer type: drag scrolls `Page` and `VirtualList` with fling, in the page's one-scroll order (notes/app.md, One scrollable per page: the list defers to its page while what is around it shows; a wheel step crossing the list's edge stops there and drops the rest, which a fling must carry across instead); long press picks up a row; in text, drag scrolls, long press selects a word with handles. Long press: a timer thread waking the shell's `Waker` after `Thresholds::long_press`.
3. Selection menu: kit's model (when, what, where) over `Platform::show_selection_menu` (the shape is there; both shells answer no), Android `ActionMode`, kit-drawn elsewhere, secondary click on desktop; the paste chips (`PasteTo`) go.
4. The focused field in view. Nothing asks for it: no widget calls `request_scroll_to`/`request_scroll_to_this` (the scroll-into-view path works: `masonry_core-pan.diff`, `Page`'s `RequestPanToChild`, test `layout::scroll_into_view_shows_it`). Wanted: when the space shrinks under a focused field (keyboard insets, resize) or its caret moves, the page brings the caret's area into view (a text widget asking with its caret rect, or `Ui::set_insets`/`resized` asking for the focused widget). Also: a field that fills (a note's body) takes what the page leaves it, so a short page squeezes it to a sliver (8 px when a keyboard leaves 60 px) and its text clips instead of the page scrolling; it should keep at least its text's height.
