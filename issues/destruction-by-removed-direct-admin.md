# A removed admin's backdated destruction of a thing it held directly stands
> Summary: a destruction's right reads no cut on an edge into the destroyed thing ordered after it (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction; draft notes/a1-spec/permissions.md §6 Destruction; roles-model `holds`; osis-perm `paths`), so a key holding admin on T by a direct edge, stolen and removed, destroys T for good by a request backdated before its removal, unlisted. Every other purge request falls with such a cut. Against spec/requirements.md Deletion ("A purge counts only if its author had the right to make it, judged as an edit's validity is").
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, perm, tess-call

World (roles-model `paths::purge::tests::a_destruction_stands_against_a_later_cut_on_its_own_thing_alone`, `on_thing`): H grants Z admin on T1 directly; H removes Z from T1 at 6 listing nothing; Z's destruction of T1 stamped 4. The held set is the same whether honest Z destroyed at 4 before H (not yet holding it) removed it, or a thief with Z's key made it after the removal and backdated it. The rule keeps it in both: the thing is gone at every node that holds the request, for good, and nodes that erased it fetch nothing back.

What a stolen admin key can destroy for good, today on main (spec/commits.md §5: "judged without the edge writes on the thing stamped after the request") and in the walk alike: every thing its key actor holds admin on by an edge into that thing (its path's last hop being its own edge on T), by backdating past its removal. Along a longer path (admin of A, A admin of T) the removal cuts the first hop, which the destruction reads: it falls as any request (`hand_purge::a_removed_signers_backdated_request_or_destruction_takes_nothing`; `single::every_order_and_forget_of_a_destruction_racing_a_cut_reads_as_from_scratch`). The purge worlds meet it in `PSetup::o_cut` (ORG admin on T1 directly, B removing ORG from T1 at 3, the thief destroying along [ORG] stamped 2 or 3, M's removal of X from ORG listing it): the oracle expects the destruction to stand by this rule and counts each such view as noted (`destroyed past a cut into its thing`, 1,836 views of 28,000 at the gate's size); whatever is decided here changes that expectation. notes/purge-model.md property 2 states the exception.

Why the rule is so: "nobody writes a thing after its destruction", so a removal on T stamped after the destruction is read as a write to a destroyed thing. Reading the removal instead (a destruction judged as any request, the after-destruction refusal applying only to parts after a destruction that stands so) makes an honest destruction raced by a removal not listing it fall and come back where copies remain: For Tess 2 (notes/purge-model.md), the choice already made for every other request. Question for Tess: should a destruction follow that choice (a removed key destroys nothing), or is destroying deliberately final (state it in the requirements as the one place gone means gone against a stolen key)?
