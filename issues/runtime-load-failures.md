> Summary: Two osis `runtime::` failures seen only with the module run 6-8 copies at once (load 30-60): the engine debug check "pending commit left out of reexamine: its boxes teach <actor> something" (`own_serving_node_...`, about 1-2%, main too), and a direct-link `redeem` stuck before the redeemer knows the target (about 0.5% of direct redeems).
> Tags: engine, osis, tests

Repro: copy the osis `it` binary, run `it-bin runtime::` in 6-8 parallel loops of 20 rounds.
- **Reexamine check** (`core/engine/src/apply/mod.rs`, the `pending_boxes` debug_assert in the reexamine check): fires on a tokio worker of a node in `runtime::own_serving_node_in_process_spec_data_model_9`, which then times out waiting. 2-3 per 160 runs, on main as on the branch that filed this. A pending commit whose boxes would teach an actor something was not re-examined, so it may stay pending when it could apply. Next: record the commit's kind and the actor, and which trigger should have named it.
- **Direct redeem stuck**: `redeem` of a direct write link (`redeem_direct_no_peer_...`, `status_says_connected_with_a_direct_peer`, `redeem_direct_writes_at_once_...`) times out at 30 s; a probe at 20 s showed the redeemer with no role on the target and no creation record of it (its first wait: K's edge on the target not yet held), the sharer fine. About 6 in 1,100 direct redeems at load 35-60. Side by side in one window (300 `runtime::` runs each, 6 loops of each binary at once): 6 stuck on the branch that added the delete-key wait (4 in `redeem_direct_writes_at_once_...`, 3 redeems per run, 2 in `redeem_direct_no_peer_...`), 0 on main; every stuck one at the first wait (redeemer: no role, no creation record of the target), before the join, so not the delete-key wait after it. Next: when stuck, dump what the redeemer holds of K's label and whether its session with the sharer is up.
