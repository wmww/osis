# A holder whose inbox lists no host is re-sealed only over a connection nothing makes
> Summary: a holder left out of a rotation (its rotator lacked its grant, or rotated concurrently) reads nothing new until an admin's re-seal reaches its inbox; with that inbox at no host it goes only as a Deposit item on a direct connection, which no rule dials, so with every node online at one shared host the holder can stay stranded for good.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: keys, node-sync

- Real shape: an account made with no saved peer (`Osis::create(.., &[], ..)`, cli `init` with none) holding a role on a hosted thing, which it syncs at that thing's hosts. Its grant came over a connection (a link); the re-seal needs another, from an admin able to re-seal it.
- What happens: the copy under the thing's labels is wrapped under the epoch the holder lacks (spec/permissions.md §3 Re-seal), so only the inbox copy opens. The holder is stranded and its notice reaches the admins' inboxes (hosted ones work), but the re-seal they write waits in their outboxes (`Dest::Nodes`). Nodes dial co-readers by `reach.0`, which only an account's own nodes read (spec/sync.md §10), so nodes of different accounts that never met do not connect.
- Bounded once connected: the pending Deposit item lands on the first connection between the holder and a re-sealing admin (osis-node `reseals::reseal_to_hostless_inbox_waits_for_a_connection_spec_sync_5`). With the inbox at a host it lands at once, and a lost or expired copy is replaced by another admin's re-seal (`reseals::reseal_reaches_grantee_left_out_spec_permissions_3`).
- Same question as a grant to such an account (issues/net-first-contact.md, Grants; issues/net-connect.md, one-way knowledge and meeting points), but worse: the holder loses access it had, not an invite it has not seen. Options: the holder dials the admins its stranded notice went to (it knows their accounts from the thing's roles; their addresses only by a contact dial list), the hosts it syncs the thing at carry deposits for its inbox, or treat "no host" as accepting this (documented, shown as stranded in status).
