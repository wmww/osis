# What a node holding many things still pays per write
> Summary: Making one plain thing under the account root still grows with what is held (debug instructions per make, averaged over 100 makes: 36/46/54 M at 100/400/800 held). Paged state decode is flat (about 5 page loads and 2.7 M per make), so a page cache across transactions would not help; the growth is small remainders spread over sync read settle, follow and cover judgments. A 2000-thing commit costs about 17 M instructions per thing (debug).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, performance

Measured with `tools/icount` (per make: the run with 100 more makes less the run without, divided by 100; 20 makes swing with where compactions of `holds.0` fall) and `tools/cpuprof` on osis `it` (debug), profile shares scaled by the instruction count. What one thing stores, downloads and signs, and the changes left for it: notes/thing-cost.md.

- **Paged state: flat.** The account root's `holds.0` gains a strong per make; it pages from about 75 strongs (notes/engine-paged.md), so a make writes one 3-6 KB page and store bytes per make are flat (`scale::a_set_grown_one_element_a_write_writes_what_it_gained`). Joins (`ensure_touch`) and pieces read against the state (`Tx::state_at`) load and evict the pages they reach: `state page load` 4.6/5.1/5.6 per make at 100/400/800 held, about 2.7 M instructions (`state::decode` and `ensure_touch`), growing 0.2-0.4 M from 100 to 400. Rejected: keeping a session's decoded pages across transactions (bounded): it saves at most that 2.7 M, none of the growth, at the price of a cache that must stay equal to the store under rollback.
- **Small remainders**, about 1-3 M each from 400 to 800 (sampled, noisy; every `costs` span is flat in held things but `stream: bytes` and `covered: snapshot part bytes`, which grow about 10% per doubling): sync `Read::settle`, `Engine::follow`, `read_facts_now`, `covered_join`/`covered_read`, `held_part_at` (from about 200 held the `holds.0` snapshot pages, so a cover judgment streams its leaves instead of reading its decoded copy), `Status::held_elsewhere`.
- **Per thing of a big commit** (`commits::changes_on_as_many_things_spec_requirements_decentralization`, 38 G at 2000 things): `write::framing_for` and `follow_certificates` (a `Piece::Chain` per thing, each naming the account's commits). A commit's delete keys are found once per delete base (`secrets::KeyCache`, `published_keys`).
