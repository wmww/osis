# What a node holding many things still pays per write
> Summary: Making one dependent thing minted with admin the account root still grows with what is held (debug instructions per make, averaged over 100 makes: 34/42/47 M at 100/400/800 held). Left, per make from 100 to 800 held (instruction-sampled): compaction weighing about 3 M, `Status::held_elsewhere` about 1.8 M, debug whole checks about 2.2 M, covered body drops and forgetting about 2.7 M. A 2000-thing commit costs about 17 M instructions per thing (debug).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, performance

Measured with `tools/icount` (per make: the run with 100 more makes less the run without, divided by 100) and `CPUPROF_INSTR=2000000 tools/cpuprof` on osis `it` (debug), with the measured makes on a thread of their own so a FILTER on it leaves the setup out. What one thing stores, downloads and signs: notes/thing-cost.md. Counted in `making::making_a_dependent_thing_costs_what_it_changed`: role lookups read back from the thing (`perm: roles walked`), a leaf moves its component's whole-timeline reads once (`timelines: components moved`), the chain reach of what reaches its mint admin gains it without a walk (`perm: chain reach walked`).

- **Compaction weighing** (`Engine::compactions`, `Load::of`, `snapshot_cost`): at 800 held the root's aspects are weighed about once per 10 makes without a snapshot following, and each weighing encodes the whole state (`snapshot_cost`: a page at a time, O(state) work): O(held) at a rate that falls slower than 1/held. Find why the estimate passes `FACTOR` times the last snapshot cost so soon after a weighing that declined.
- **Own commits as a whole set** (`Status::held_elsewhere`, engine `OwnCommits::rows`): a sync ID's own commits are handed over as one `Arc<BTreeSet>` per change (the engine's row cloned by `Arc::make_mut` while status holds the old one), and status re-diffs it against `by_commit` (every commit of every sync ID). Needs a change feed of own commits, so both sides move by what changed.
- **Debug whole checks** (`check_states`, `check_rebuilt`): O(state) per transaction by design; debug only.
- **Body drops and forgetting** (`drop_body` → `shred_covered`, `forget_commits`): each streams its body (`stream: bytes` grows about 10% per doubling) and rewrites the pages it reaches; not yet traced to what grows.
- **Paged state: flat.** The account root's `holds.0` pages (from about 3 KB); a make writes one 3-6 KB page and store bytes per make are flat (`scale::a_set_grown_one_element_a_write_writes_what_it_gained`). Rejected: keeping a session's decoded pages across transactions: it saves at most about 2.7 M, none of the growth, at the price of a cache that must stay equal to the store under rollback.
- **Per thing of a big commit** (`commits::changes_on_as_many_things_spec_requirements_decentralization`, 38 G at 2000 things): `write::framing_for` and `follow_certificates` (each thing's certificates name the account's commits). A commit's delete keys are found once per delete base (`secrets::KeyCache`, `published_keys`).
