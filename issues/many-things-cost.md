# What a node holding many things still pays per write
> Summary: Making one dependent thing still grows with what the node holds (instructions per make: about 35/60/86/107 M from 0-100/100-200/200-400/400-500 held): the certificates follow re-deriving the account root's certificates whole (about 40% at 500 held, uncounted by the making test), compaction weighing, the own-commits set handed to status whole, debug whole checks, and covered body drops. A 2000-thing commit costs about 17 M instructions per thing (debug).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, performance

Counted: osis `making::making_a_dependent_thing_costs_what_it_changed` (role lookups, timeline components moved, chain reach). What one thing stores and sends: notes/thing-cost.md. Measure with `tools/icount` and `tools/cpuprof` (per make: a run with 100 more makes less one without).
- **Certificates follow** (`Engine::follow_certificates`, `sync_certificates`): a make moves the account root's certificates, derived again whole: `close_records` runs `minting_evidence` (a closure per minting) for every record of the unit, and the follow's `Effects` re-records its O(unit) reads. About 40% of a make at 500 held. The follow runs under `costs::paused` and `osis_perm::uncounted`, so the making test's flat-count check cannot see it: count it there (`certified: certificates` derives one thing per make at n and 10n, but its work is O(n)). Lever: each record's contribution (`minting_evidence` with its record chain) a memo per record, merged per follow; the `ev` copy `Engine::certificates` makes for a thing not read must not share those answers.
- **Compaction weighing** (`Engine::compactions`, `snapshot_cost`): the node root's aspects are weighed about once per 10 makes at 800 held without a snapshot following, each weighing encoding the whole state: O(held) at a rate falling slower than 1/held. Find why the estimate passes `FACTOR` times the last snapshot cost so soon after a weighing that declined.
- **Own commits as a whole set** (`Status::held_elsewhere`, engine `OwnCommits`): a sync ID's own commits are handed over as one `Arc<BTreeSet>` per change, diffed against the last one taken and `unheld` copied whole: O(own commits) per own commit (about 10% of a commit at 1,000 own commits). Needs a change feed of own commits.
- **Debug whole checks** (`check_states`, `check_rebuilt`): O(state) per transaction by design.
- **Body drops and forgetting** (`shred_covered`, `forget_commits`): each streams its body and rewrites the pages it reaches; not yet traced to what grows.
- **Per thing of a big commit** (osis `commits::changes_on_as_many_things_spec_requirements_decentralization`): `write::framing_for` and `follow_certificates`.

Flat: paged state (a make writes one page; `scale::a_set_grown_one_element_a_write_writes_what_it_gained`). Rejected: keeping a session's decoded pages across transactions (saves none of the growth, needs a cache equal to the store under rollback).
