# What a node holding many things still pays per write
> Summary: Making one dependent thing still does some work in proportion to what the node holds: the certificates follow merges and diffs the account's whole certificate (set operations, no derivation), debug whole checks, and covered body drops and forgetting (not yet traced). Making 2 000 things, then writing and applying one commit on all of them, costs about 45 M instructions per thing (debug), most in the certificates follow (`follow_certificates`, `close_records`, `Evidence::closure_of`, and the memo comparisons of its per-thing answers).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, sync, performance

Counted: osis `making::making_a_dependent_thing_costs_what_it_changed` (role lookups, timeline components moved, chain reach, the certificates follow's record contributions and reads, status's own commits taken; the least of three makes, so a cost spread over many makes, such as compaction, is not in it). What one thing stores and sends: notes/thing-cost.md. Measure with `tools/icount` and `tools/cpuprof` (per make: a run with 100 more makes less one without).
- **Certificates follow, merge and diff** (`Engine::sync_certificates`, `close_records`, `Certified::put`): each record's contribution is a memo (`Core::record_certs`) and stubs come from `Closure::beyond`, so a make derives one record's; but the follow still builds the account's certificate whole (its `ev.edges` loop, one memo hit and merge per record) and `put` diffs it against the last: O(certificate) set operations per make. Lever: a certificate as a counted union of per-source contributions with change feeds from the derivation (`ev.edges`, `required`) and the unit, rows written from the union's differences; a source going recloses that read thing from its memos (cycles in record contributions).
- **Debug whole checks** (`check_states`, `check_rebuilt`): O(state) per transaction by design.
- **Body drops and forgetting** (`shred_covered`, `forget_commits`): each streams its body and rewrites the pages it reaches; not yet traced to what grows.
- **Per thing of a big commit** (osis `commits::changes_on_as_many_things_spec_requirements_decentralization`): `write::framing_for` and `follow_certificates`.

Flat: paged state (a make writes one page; `scale::a_set_grown_one_element_a_write_writes_what_it_gained`); compaction weighing per make, amortized (`compact: bodies weighed` 1.45 per make after 40 held, 1.47 after 400; a snapshot's encode is O(state) at a rate falling as 1/held, with a fixed ~3 KB of snapshot below which the rate falls slower). Every snapshot of the account's `holds.0` is preceded by one weighing that declines: the trigger compares with the snapshot cost found at the last weighing, the state grew since, so the estimate passes `FACTOR` times a stale cost one body early; the decline records the fresh cost, and the next body passes it. That doubles the weighings, a constant; a rule anticipating the state's growth would be a tuning, not a fix. Rejected: keeping a session's decoded pages across transactions (saves none of the growth, needs a cache equal to the store under rollback).
