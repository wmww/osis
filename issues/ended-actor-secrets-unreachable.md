# A newcomer cannot read through an ended actor's own secrets
> Summary: When actor t ends, its authority stands (its frozen edges travel with its dependents), but its secrets do not: a thing x whose secrets are boxed to t as a holder actor reaches a reader through t only by t's own read secret, and nobody boxes t's secrets once t ended (no write on t). A reader of x through t that joins after the ending never reads x. Repro: `ending::repro_newcomer_reads_no_dependent_keyed_by_an_ended_actor`.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, permissions

- Case: t (first admin g) has its own secrets (b holds an edge on it); x (first admin t) has secrets of its own (it has holders), boxed to its holders, t among them. t ends by expiry. d, granted read on g afterwards, reaches x through g, t (record edges); x's box for t opens only under t's read secret, which reaches readers by boxes in t's `secrets.0`. Nobody writes t after its ending, so d gets none and reads nothing of x. Nodes that held t's secrets at the ending still read x.
- Same hole for existing readers: a rotation of x after the ending boxes its new epoch to t's keyset (if it boxes t at all), which nobody new can open.
- Not a history problem: rosters agree at every node (`ending::an_ended_first_admins_edges_reach_a_node_that_held_no_dependent_spec_commits_5`); only secrets cannot travel, since a box is a write on its thing.
- Tess approved (a). Not built: it changes who a box goes to (`Core::key_holders`, `rotation::recipients`, re-seal triggers) and what receivers accept (a box to a holder of the ended actor, not of x), so it needs a roles-model run.
- Directions: (a) boxing to an ended holder actor boxes to its frozen holders instead (an ended actor holds no keys; what it held its holders hold, as its frozen roster says): x's admins re-seal x for readers through t without writing t; (b) an ending keeps the actor's `secrets.0` and lets its frozen admins write re-seal boxes there (a write on an ended thing: against "nobody writes it after its ending"); (c) a stated limit: things keyed through an ended actor are read only by those who held its secrets. (a) follows from B's principle; it changes `rotation/members` (who a box goes to) and needs a model run.
