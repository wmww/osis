# A party removed from a holder actor rotates the group where its removal is unknown
> Summary: Depth 0 (issues/keys-depth0.md) dropped the §4 exception that placed revokes of departures inside holder actors under the group's label. A writer W of G through X1 <- X2, removed from X2 by an admin who does not sync G, rotates G before it learns its removal; G's admin writes under W's epoch. Model-checked (Depth0.tla `D0_rm_*`): beyond the stated limit, for good when nobody syncing G holds the removal. Both placement fixes fail the limit; the possession rule (a rotation through a holder actor seals it only at a keyset whose secret it holds, shown by a signature) passes, and is built (below); its Box clause (every box judged alone, re-seals and grants included) was dropped; a forged keyset of X1 by W stays the stated limit. Test: osis-node `revokes::nested_departure_removed_writer_waits_spec_crypto_4`.
> Tags: security, keys, engine, node

## Shape
G <- X1 <- X2 <- {W (write), R (read)}; B, admin of X1 and X2, does not sync G. B removes W from X2 (rotating X2) and rotates X1 (its box to X2 is stale). W gets X1's new `keys.0` under G's label (a target's) before X2's commits, so it does not yet know its removal; G's box to X1 is stale, so W rotates G, sealing X1 at X1's new keyset. G's admin a1 never sees inside X1, holds no revoke of W, judges W's rotation valid and writes under it; W holds that secret. Today R places the revoke under G only once W's entry is there (spec/permissions.md §4 Placing revokes); the deleted exception placed it ahead of time.

## Model (core/test/tla/rotation/Depth0.tla, README table)
`Via` writers rotate, re-seal and write through holder actors until they know their removal; their rotation is invalid where it is known. Nodes learn only their labels (`NoSync`), a holder's `keys.0` under a target's label may come without the keysets it names (`Split`). Two properties: `NoLeakLimit` is the stated limit at any depth ("a device removed from M reads A's new content until M's knowing keyset reaches A's writers"): no leak to the removed device of content written by a writer holding, for each holder actor of A, a keyset the device cannot open that lists every keyset it can. `NoLeakSettled` is weaker: no leak once every node holds what it syncs and needs no rotation.

- **Today (`spec`)**: beyond the limit. a1 holds X1's knowing keyset and writes under W's epoch (`NoLeakLimit`). With R syncing G the leak ends once R's revoke reaches a1 (a third party's sync, after W's entry); without R it never ends: `NoLeakSettled` fails and `Excludes` fails (a1's newest epoch stays W's; W's rotation, sealed to X1's current keyset, is never stale, so nothing rotates W out).
- **Fix 1, readers place ahead (`ahead`)**: same as today. Still a race (a1 may use W's epoch before the revoke arrives, holding X1's knowing keyset), and with no other member of X2 syncing G nobody places it.
- **Fix 2, the remover places under every label X2 reaches (`remover`)**: ends the leak (`NoLeakSettled` passes) but fails `NoLeakLimit` while the placement is in flight; and B must find and write under labels it does not sync (the upward walk depth 0 deleted).
- **Possession (`possess`)**: passes every property, with and without R, including liveness (`D0_rm_possess0`; `D0_rm_possess` safety only). W, lacking X1's new keyset's secret, cannot rotate; an unprompted rotation before X1 moved seals X1's old keyset and is stale at a1 once a1 holds X1's new one (within the limit).

## Built (branch depth0): possession (spec/crypto.md §4, Holder actors)
- **Key and signature**: each keyset publishes `possession_key` (Ed25519 from `kdf("possession key", s)`, like `delete_key`); a symmetric box to a holder actor carries `possession`, a signature by that key over the box (its AD and wrap, domain `"osis v1 possession"`, spec/wire.md §7). Per box, not per commit: what is shown is "the author holds the secret of the keyset it boxes M at", bound to that box; a box cannot be copied into another epoch.
- **Authority**: a rotation of A (its commit writes A's keyset), a delta writing more than entries, or one removing a box to a holder actor the author holds that role on without putting it again signed, is invalid where the author holds the role it needs on A only through holder actors of A with a held keyset that it does not box signed (where it holds no such role at all, §5.2's re-grants and frontiers judge it as before). The `secrets.0` delta failing it is invalid (its epochs count for nothing). A re-seal overwriting G's stale box to X1 writes a removal of it: Authority (`removed_member_reseal_refused_spec_crypto_4`).
- **Writers**: a node rotates only where Authority holds, else waits, blocked by the stale box (`rotation::members::authority`, `ensure_rotated`). The role a rotation's `secrets.0` delta needs is at least write (a read rotation too); judging it by the highest role rotated let a writer through a keyless holder commit a read rotation its own validity refused (cycle harness seed 9540070).
- **Box (dropped)**: every box to M by an author holding that role on M (paths not through A) had to be signed, re-seals and grants included, and a writer lacking M's secret left M out. Two faults, both from the cycle harness at full size (`cycle_removals_leak_nothing_random_large`, 46 of 1000 seeds): (1) an invalid box still opens for whoever holds the keyset it names, but rules 2, 4 and 4b judge only valid boxes: an admin of A and B in a 3-cycle sealing B to C before it held the grant giving it a role on C wrote a box later judged invalid, so the key cycle it closed was never re-keyed or tainted and a party removed from A read A's new content (21 seeds; model `D0_box_rule` fails `NoLeakKnownAll`, `D0_box_ok` passes); (2) it counted the author's role on M along keyless grants, where it can never hold M's secret, so M was left out for good (25 seeds). Without Box, the cases it was taken for: a removed W's re-seal overwriting the stale box is a removal (Authority, above); a re-seal of a missing box hides no stale box (there was none), and an epoch W holds through X1 boxes X1 already (Authority makes W's own rotations box it signed), so it turns stale when X1 rotates; the model's `Reseal` under `possess` no longer restricts W, and `D0_rm_possess0` (liveness included) and `D0_rm_possess` pass.
- **Not judged by an unknown keyset**: a box naming a keyset the node holds no valid body of passes (as such a box is not stale); judged again once the body arrives (`apply::keys::keyset_changed`). Requiring the body instead made A's readers who cannot see M's keysets (a certificate carried in a commit they cannot open, an account's keys behind the account's label) refuse honest rotations for good. Within the stated limit: such a node holds no knowing keyset of M.
- **Residual (stated limit, spec/requirements.md Permissions)**: a malicious removed W that is a writer of X1 (it is, to write G through it) can still, where its removal is unknown, write a keyset of X1 itself (or name one nobody holds) and seal and sign at it: "a keyset of M the removed party forged where the removal has not arrived". Possession stops the plain case (sealing at X1's real new keyset) and the ignorant honest writer; it does not make forgeries of an actor one writes impossible. Review: naming a keyset nobody holds needs no write at all, and such a box is never stale (no keyset lists it), so the leak lasts until A rotates for another reason, not "until M's next rotation that knows" (spec/requirements.md Permissions limit): open question for Wren/Tess.
- Tests: osis-node `revokes::nested_departure_removed_writer_waits_spec_crypto_4` (the repro); osis-engine `rotation::tests_possession` (W waits; a forged rotation sealing X1 unsigned, one leaving X1 out, a bare box removal, a re-seal over the stale box: each refused at A and B, A rotates and W opens nothing; judged again when X1's keyset arrives); osis-keys `possession` unit tests.
