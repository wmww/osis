# Keys at depth 0: build plan
> Summary: Tess's Q2 = A (notes/keys-requirements-study.md 2.1): a group seals to a member actor's keyset and never looks inside it. Rules 1-4b (anchored sealing, listed staleness, fork merging, no key cycles with taint) and possession are built on branch depth0 (Built, Tests below).
> Tags: keys, engine, security, spec

## Rules (spec/crypto.md §4 and spec/permissions.md §3 after the change)
1. **Seal**: a rotation, grant or re-seal of A seals holder actor M, per role, at the newest (n, nonce) valid keyset of M the node holds that is or lists (transitively, through held `keys.0` commits of keysets valid at the node: a forged keyset lists everything) every valid keyset the boxes to M at A's current epochs name (its anchors). None: while no keyset, tainted or not, descends from every anchor (they fork), it waits for M's merge; else M is left out of that role and re-sealed once one exists.
2. **Stale**: a box to M is stale when its keyset is held invalid or a held valid keyset of M lists it. A node MUST NOT encrypt under an epoch of A with a stale box; it rotates first. An unknown keyset is not stale. Not "any newer keyset": that moves A onto an ignorant fork (study §4).
3. **Merge**: a node that may rotate M rotates while a current epoch misses a departure it knows, or while M has more than one current epoch and it is the fork's merger (it made a current epoch other than the newest, or no maker of one may rotate). Without it an ignorant fork that loses the (n, nonce) tie stays current and A, anchored on it, never moves; and two knowing forks anchoring A leave M unsealable for good (cycle harness seed 1838).
4. **Cycles**: no key cycles. Actor grants have an agreed order (total, consistent with causality). A node takes the grants it holds in that order and gives one key access only if the holder's key does not already yield the granting actor's over the keyed grants before it: the earlier grant wins, the later reads nothing. Keyless grants are not sealed to. A node that may rotate A rotates while a current epoch of A boxes an actor its held grants give no key access (the knowing re-key). The first admin's implicit grant (notes/owner-removal-plan.md step 2) counts as a grant at the thing's creation and so always wins (study §4).
   - **Hidden grant** (model-checked, study §4): a sealer missing the earlier grant takes the later one as keyed and seals into a key cycle; a node removed from X then reads X's new content through Y. Plain rule 4: until every admin of Y holds the grant and re-keys Y and X rotates onto it (a third party's sync; unbounded if a host withholds the grant).
   - **4b, taint (taken, Wren: an endless leak under a withholding host is worse than re-keying while it withholds)**: a keyset boxing an actor with no key access, in the node's view, is stale (rule 2) and never sealed to. Then the leak stops once the writer holds the grants and the cycle's commits ("until the next knowing re-key" with the writer as the knowing party). Cost: while a grant stays hidden, concurrent re-seals by the knowing and the ignorant side re-form the cycle between fresh keysets, one extra rotation per race, until the grant arrives. Recommendation: take 4b (same predicate as the knowing re-key; churn instead of leak under withholding), and word the requirement limit as in step 1.
5. Unchanged: A's own departures by frozen coverage at depth 0 (names of A's own removals, departs mark, establishment), forks, history boxes, claims, stranding, lockout recovery; the remover still places the removal and every affected actor's new `keys.0` under every label the removed party could read; a holder's `keys.0` goes under its targets' labels (the carrier for rule 2, and what lets a node judge a forged keyset invalid).

## Built (branch depth0)
Rules 1-4b as specified above, spec/crypto.md §4 (Holder actors), spec/permissions.md §3. Pure functions in `osis_keys::members` (`seal_keyset`, `stale`, `boxing_keyless`, `key_access`) and `covers::merge_due`; the engine gathers inputs in `rotation::members`. Departures are the actor's own (`rotation::departures`). Deviations and additions, each where the model is silent or the code needed a precise reading:
- **Merge** of any fork, by its merger (was: only a fork missing a departure, by anyone, which stays; model `D0_fork_old` fails `SealsAll`, seed 1838: two admins of M rotated knowing the removal, A's two epochs anchored M at both, no keyset descended from both, M out of A's write and admin for good). Every admin merging ping-pongs: two nodes holding the same fork merge alike and fork again on every sync round (osis `sharing::two_nodes_act_on_a_link_spec_permissions_7` hung). The merger is the maker of a current epoch other than the newest, or anyone when no such maker may rotate: nodes holding the same fork agree on the newest, so one merges (`osis_keys::fork_merger`, model `D0_fork_live` passes with races unbounded). An epoch whose secret the node lacks counts.
- **Seal waits while the anchors fork** (`osis_keys::Seal::Wait`): leaving M out dropped its anchors, and the next re-seal (no box to M at A's current epochs, so no anchors) sealed M at its newest keyset, perhaps an ignorant fork: content a writer wrote under the epoch knowing M's merge became readable to the removed party (`D0_fork_out` under the new merge, `D0_fork_old_safety` under the old rules too: a stale box made A rotate over forked anchors). A rotation, grant or re-seal of A boxing M waits until M merges; A's writers meanwhile encrypt as §4 allows. A removal goes without its rotation then (the role ends, the rotation stays due; `removal_goes_alone_while_seal_waits_spec_permissions_3`, model `D0_fork_rm`): waiting held the removal, so a writer of M withholding its merge kept the removed party's role. A tainted anchor leaves M out as before (its re-key descends from it): waiting there held A's removals while a grant stayed hidden from M's admins (16 of 1000 cycle seeds failed their removal).
- **Boxes judged**: rules read the `secrets.0` state's boxes only; an invalid delta's box carries a secret its author held and could hand on (`invalid_grant_box_judges_nothing_spec_crypto_4`, `reader_junk_boxes_force_no_rotation_spec_crypto_4`). A held-boxes rule counting them let a reader force a rotation per junk commit.
- **Taint and the knowing re-key** look at holders whose grant is keyless. A box to a departed actor holder is a departure (the covers rule), not a taint: counting it made a member's self-removal from M taint M's keyset and leave M out of A, the old inside-departure lockout again (the model has no departing actor holders).
- **Stale and keyless boxes block only roles the node may rotate** (spec/crypto.md §4 says so). A reader held back by a stale box placed no chain certificates, so the holder actor's new keyset never reached the label's rotators (the deleted §4 exception had carried it).
- **Carried**: a rotation, grant or re-seal places the rotation commits of the keysets its boxes name under the actor's own labels (the model's `Carry`). A host withholding a forged keyset's body from a writer that knows the removal keeps it unknown there, so not stale: within the stated limit ("where the removal or the forgery has not arrived"). The model's `Carry` is atomic (learning an epoch learns the keysets its boxes name); here a writer can hold A's box naming M's knowing keyset kk without kk's body: kk is then no anchor, and its next rotation of A seals M at the older keyset the removed party opens (within the stated limit: it holds no knowing keyset of M); a node holding kk finds that box stale and moves A back (`tests_members::lacking_carried_keyset_spec_crypto_4`). Treating an unheld anchor as leaving M out would close it at the cost of a lockout while a host withholds the body; not modelled.
- **Agreed order**: a grant's standing since its earliest write without a break (`Timeline::standing_since`); a first admin's grant before every write, ties by tids; owners before every grant (the implicit grant of step 2).
- **A new grant's key access** is judged with the owners of both ends among the grants (an actor owned by its target closes a cycle through the implicit grant before it holds anything): a thin client minted by account P and granted on P is keyless, boxed nothing (`osis::sync_basics::contact_outlives_the_accounts_role_changes_spec_permissions_7` caught the rotation that judging it later caused, cutting off the profile's bearer contacts).
- **Lost access**: a node that left a holder actor finds its own self-removal behind losing what that actor reads by walking the actor's holders (`lost::own_leaving`), no longer through departures.
- **Possession** (issues/keys-depth0-removed-member-rotates.md: a removed member writer's rotation valid where its removal is unknown): a rotation, or a removal of a box to a holder actor, counts its author's role through a holder actor only where it boxes it with a signature by the keyset's possession key (spec/crypto.md §4, Authority). No rule judges a box alone: the Box clause hid key cycles from rules 4 and 4b and left holders out for good (same issue, Box (dropped)). `osis_keys::possession`, judged in `apply::validity::possessed`.
