# Ephemeral holder costs left after the cost-bounds fixes
> Summary: one ephemeral blob is decoded (about 200 times its size for tiny map entries, up to a GB or more for an 8 MiB blob) before the node cap sees its decoded charge; a replayed take still scans the held parts of its aspects; the CRDT's in-memory size per map entry (about 2 KiB) is what makes the decode ratio. Low.
> Tags: security, engine, node, crdt, ephemeral

Fixed (notes/ephemeral.md Node cap): the node cap counts decoded memory (`Engine::ephemeral_charge`), removed members' commits are let go at once, absorbed lookups are indexed (`Remote::absorbers`), a replay judged contained compares a piece of the cover (`Fragment::contains`). Measurements: `scale::eph_*` (release, by hand).

Left:
- Decode before the charge: an entry is admitted while the label's count is under the cap, and its charge is known only once the engine decoded and held it, so a label is past its cap by one entry at most, but that entry can be an 8 MiB blob of tiny entries: about 1.7 GB decoded (218x measured, `scale::eph_decoded_memory`). The same holds transiently for any verified blob (pending ones are decoded at retry). Fix direction: a decode budget in `osis_crdt::decode_snapshot` (elements or `approx_bytes`), passed the room left under the cap; or a per-blob bound on decoded size relative to the cap.
- CRDT memory per element: a tiny map entry takes about 2 KiB decoded (`Eid` 120 B and its key index copy, `Entry` 56 B, a boxed `Fragment` 176 B per value, B-tree nodes), a small register part with its engine records about 2.3 KiB. That is what makes ephemeral (and durable, while loaded) states large; a leaner layout would shrink every cap's real meaning.
- `eph_containers` for a take (a commit listing nothing) still walks every held part on each of its aspects (cheap per part; `contains` only for later covers listing something there): a writer holding many covers on one aspect costs holders O(those covers) per replayed take. Bounded by the cap; an index of cover parts per (author, aspect) by `ts` would make it O(log).
