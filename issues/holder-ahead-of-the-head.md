# A holder acting under a label before its admin re-registers it leaves its certificates unplaced
> Summary: with the granter's chain in the inbox blob for a labeled thing, a writer applied the grant and synced the group's label before the admin's pass re-registered it at the group's new epoch; its node card never reached the label and a third node held its writes pending `MissingChain` for good (sims `snapshot_rights`, `purge_tombstones`, `ephemeral`, `coverer_revoked` at seed 1). Root cause not pinned; the chain now goes only for an unlabeled thing, where no such window exists.
> Tags: node-sync, tests

Repro: make `send_inbox` (`core/node/src/sync/local.rs`) attach `inbox_chain` for every blob (drop the `unlabeled` condition) and run `cargo test -p osis-sim --test it -- net::snapshot_rights library::purge_tombstones`: node 2 ends with the writer's content commits pending `MissingChain(<node b>)`; its trace shows node b's `keys.0` arriving under the group's label but never its `created.0`/`node.0` card. With the chain off both pass.

Setting: `shared_group` labels a plain group at its owner's derived epoch, then grants, which gives the group secrets of its own; the admin re-registers the label at its next pass. A grantee that can apply the grant at once (the chain in hand) syncs the label as a writer before that pass. Hypotheses: its deposits under the old head are signed by the new deposit key (code 9: "dropped when the client holds no other", spec/sync.md §5) and its card is marked placed (`State::certs`) though it never landed (`own_card_read` excludes `created.0`, so it is not placed again); or its uploads under the old head's write key are refused and not retried. Either way a node ahead of the host's head should wait or retry once the head moves. Worth a sim scenario: a grant applied before the admin's re-registration pass.
