# Node rules that follow the plain record chain, not standing record edges
> Summary: hosting admission (`SyncEngine::owns`), the inbox stamp/copy rule (`inbox_about`) and the re-send check (`acted`) follow the record chain as created, so a first admin whose record edge was removed still counts. Plain fix: walk standing record edges (`Core::inbox_chain`), as the inbox sender rule does.
> Tags: node, permissions

A first admin's record edge can be removed like any edge (spec/permissions.md §1); after that it has no standing on the thing. The inbox sender already stops there (`Core::inbox_chain`: up the chain only while `key_holders` of the thing hold its first admin; spec/sync.md §2 "while its record edge stands"). Three node rules still follow `record_chain` (records only, edges ignored):

- **Hosting admission** (core/node/src/sync/host.rs `SyncEngine::owns`, used by `Admit::Own`): a group counts as the node's own when its record chain reaches the node or its person. A person removed from a group it first-administered keeps admitting that group's labels on its host. Storage only: the group's admins picked the host in `label.0`, and the label's content stays sealed to current readers.
- **Inbox stamp and copy** (core/node/src/sync/pass/inbox.rs `inbox_about`, used by `inbox_stampable` and `inbox_certificates`): an inbox commit is about the inbox's actors when it writes an edge or box of an actor whose record chain reaches one of them. A removed first admin's node stamps such a blob (keeping it at the host, against the deposit budget) and copies its parts into its home label. Not a leak: the blob already reached that inbox (a stale sender's choice), and the copy carries only what it holds; but it spends the label's budget on things it no longer administers. spec/sync.md §3 (inbox copy: "an actor whose record chain reaches it") states the static rule too.
- **Re-send check** (core/node/src/sync/local.rs `acted`): the holder acted on a delivery when a newer commit by it writes a named thing or one whose record chain reaches it. Liveness only (a missed re-send or an extra one); a write below a named thing is evidence of receipt whatever edges stand since, so this one may rightly stay static. spec/sync.md §5 (Re-send) states it.

Plain fix: replace `record_chain(x).any(..)` with `inbox_chain(x).contains(..)` in `owns` and `inbox_about` (and decide `acted`), and say "through standing record edges" in spec/sync.md §3 (and §5 if `acted` changes). Test: remove a first admin's record edge, then check its host stops admitting the group's label and its inbox stops stamping a grant on the group.
