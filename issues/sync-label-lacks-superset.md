# "The label lacks" a body: a must-not-hold superset counts or not
> Summary: sync §6 says a body is held when a blob of its commit holds its uncovered deltas; read literally a must-not-hold superset (a purged blob awaiting deletion) counts, so the writer waits. node-sync applies that only when snapshots cover part of the body; for a whole body it looks for an identical entry and uploads it beside the superset. Pick one reading and make both paths follow it.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: none (TLA S17 is literal and passes; no node test puts a purged superset and its rewrite under one label). Construct: writer A applies a purge of one delta of commit C whose two-delta blob P is under label L; P waits for a carrier (live writes, no covering snapshot yet); A's body of C is now the one-delta rewrite Q.

- Spec (spec/sync.md §6): "uploads every must-hold body the label lacks (a blob of the commit holding every delta of the body that no snapshot under the label covers holds it)". Nothing excludes a must-not-hold holder; models/tla/sync/AMBIGUITIES.md S17 reads it literally (Q waits until P is deleted).
- node-sync, `body_sends` (crates/osis-node/src/sync/pass.rs:660): when the needed parts are fewer than the body's, `carried_by` (pass.rs:1186) accepts any genuine entry of C holding them, whatever its own fate: literal. When needed equals the body (nothing covered), only `entry_for` (pass.rs:663, identical parts) is asked, so A uploads Q while P is there. A and every node that applied the purge keep Q (its parts are their body: `judge`, `desired`, pass.rs:1642); a writer that has not applied the purge yet finds Q a strict subset of its body P (pass.rs:1655-1680; P's extra delta is not covered) and deletes it at once (StrictSubset is carried), and A uploads Q again until that writer applies the purge.
- Fix options: (a) literal everywhere: in the whole-body case also skip the upload when a genuine superset entry is present (`carried_by` with `b.parts`); spec gets "even one that is itself must-not-hold". (b) A must-not-hold blob holds nothing: both paths ignore entries whose fate is delete, and the strict-subset rule only counts supersets that are not must-not-hold (spec §6 "another held blob" gets the same qualifier), so Q stays and P's deletion is not needed for it. (b) avoids the wait; (a) is the smaller change.
