# An honest rotator whose genuine claim of s is unsettled lists a forged claim alone
> Summary: A rotation's keyset lists, per superseded epoch s, the valid settled rotation commits of s its rotator applied (spec/crypto.md §4). Where the rotator's R_s is not settled (its predecessor R_p withheld from it; or the rotator holds s by snapshot alone), a writer's forged valid claim F of s (listing nothing) is its only valid settled claim, so it lists s: {F}; a node withheld R_s then settles e and re-seals it to the reader R_s removed.
> Tags: security, keys

Reproduced (review of forged-valid-claim, osis-engine `rotation::tests`, 5 peers): owner A (0) rotates read (R_p), removes M (2) by R_s; writer W (1) syncs from A and signs F (`forge_claim`); admin B (4) ingests every package of 0 and 1 but R_p: s is current there through F, R_s unsettled; B demotes W, rotating to e (listing s: {F}); X (3) ingests everything but R_s: e established, `reseal_candidates` lists M. Main had the same hole through its first clause.

Listing every valid claim instead brings back the stall the branch avoided (a writer's valid junk claim whose list never settles stalls every rotation listing it). Directions (none tried): a rotator does not rotate from s while it holds an unsettled valid claim of s carrying a departure (a removal or demotion by another rides in its rotation commit; a writer's claims carry none), or lists such claims whatever their state. Snapshot-only rotators: the compaction snapshot carries, per epoch it keys, the valid settled claim IDs its writer held, recorded by a joiner as listed claims of s; a snapshot-only rotator holding R_s unsettled (what R_s lists compacted away) lists s with nothing, and "s established" by F then passes it.
