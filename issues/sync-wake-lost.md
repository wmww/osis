# A sync engine stops asking to be woken
> Summary: `SyncEngine::handle` emits `Action::Wake { at }` only when `next_wake()` differs from the last one it emitted; drivers (the tokio driver and the simulator) forget a wake once it fires. When a Tick leaves `next_wake()` unchanged (a due item that cannot be serviced yet, or one serviced without moving its time), no Wake follows, and the node is never ticked again until network or local activity: resyncs, keep-at-most drops and expiries, heartbeats and retries stall.
> Tags: sync, node, retention, tests

- Seen in the simulator: `osis_sim::replay("keep_at_most", 62)` (regression test `keep_at_most_seed_62`): an hour after the last write both nodes still hold all eight bodies kept at most 10 minutes after replaced; the node has open, synced, idle labels and no pending wake; one explicit `Engine::tick()` drops the seven replaced bodies at once. Seeds 42, 46, 51, 55 (the server still holds a replaced body: no tick, so no timed delete) are probably the same.
- Candidates for the unchanged time: `next_wake` includes outbox items whose `after` passed but whose server has no ready connection, and `retry` times already due; all stay in the past and equal across Ticks.
- Fix: clear the remembered wake on `Event::Tick` (so a Wake always follows a Tick), never report a time already past (use now + a small delay, or drop unserviceable items from the wake), and include the engine's own timers (retention timers, parked-for-drift commits) in `next_wake`.
- Also expiry: `osis_sim::replay("disappearing", 104)` (regression test `disappearing_seed_104`): an hour past its expiry node 0 still reads a disappearing note; it has no pending wake.
