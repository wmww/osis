# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: A few rough edges remain in how devices sync through servers: some waiting entries are rechecked more often than needed, and a label's own membership change rechecks everything it stores. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Pass cost left** (notes/sync.md Work sets): a pass judges in full, O(entries) of the label, when the label's own actor's roster changes or it gets or loses secrets of its own (a grant to the group, and the flows' re-seal after it), when the group's held epochs change, at the clock horizon of a roster (`until`, writes ahead of the clock) of a label a membership change reached. A membership change elsewhere re-judges the label's commits on things downstream of it (`Held::reach`) and the other nodes' cards. Re-judged at every pass while they wait: orphan candidates (certificate copies by authors who left), sends in flight or held, relay waits, deletes and timed deletes in flight, bodies whose coverage is not established, chunks of dropped deltas a live tree may still link, certificate-linked blobs. Re-judged on any write to their aspect: dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier, keep-at-most bodies before their timed delete (all live entries of a map aspect under keep-at-most; narrowing them needs the engine to say which bodies a write killed: the hook below). Re-judged on any membership-class write: deletes waiting for a delete key. Every pass also copies the group's closure several times (`prepare`, `body_ctx` for the inputs and the bodies, the `set_syncing` loop): O(things in the group). Local passes (labels with no server) judge everything. Simpler alternative to the engine-side triggers (Applied ids, `/created` things, `Joined`, drops, timers): one engine hook noting "commit c changed" whenever its record, dropped parts, validity or timers change, as the sync-state setters do; not done here (engine/ and apply/ were in other hands).
- **Local serving** (a label synced with no server, notes/networking.md Serving): no chain completion or promotion.
