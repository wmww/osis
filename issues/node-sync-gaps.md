# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: Rough edges left in node sync: role changes upstream of a label re-judge all of it (cost, inside budget so far), and two nodes deleting on stale views can briefly leave a label without kept live writes. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Pass cost left** (cost, not correctness; notes/node-sync-work-sets.md). A pass judges the whole label, O(entries), when a roster or owner link upstream of the label's actor changes (a grant to the group and the re-seal after it; a grant on the group's owner, e.g. an account adding a node, full-passes every label of that owner's groups), when the actor gets or loses secrets of its own, when the group's held epochs change, and at a roster's clock horizon (`until`, writes ahead of the clock). Measured (`scale::sync_roles_pass_cost`, release, 10^3 commits per label): one full pass per node, 0.12-0.18 s; linear, so about 1.5 s at the 10^4 of the budgets, inside "rare events under a few seconds" (notes/testing.md Performance budgets). The case that could miss it: an owner grant over many labels (k labels × N); measure that before narrowing (`OSIS_SCALE_LABEL`, more groups per owner). Narrowing is not just exactness: `Held::reach` of the label's actor holds about the whole closure, so it needs a split between rosters that change what the actor reads (`may_read(x, ·)`) and who reads it (cards, orphans, certificates, validity). Small gain left: a horizon from a closure reader's roster other than the actor's could feed `moved` instead of a full pass. Also re-judged while they wait, bounded by what waits (transient): at every pass, orphan candidates, sends in flight or held, relay waits, deletes in flight, bodies whose coverage is not established, chunks of dropped deltas a live tree may link, certificate-linked blobs; on any write to the aspect, dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier; on any role-class write, deletes waiting for a delete key.
- **Crossed deletes on stale views** (transient, self-healing; spec/sync.md §7, TLA S4/S12/S18): a carrier is judged on the deleter's view, and the TLA model's `Delete` checks it against the host atomically, so it misses deletes in flight. Two nodes can each delete the other's carrier: a purger deletes the purged body p (its rewrite q present) while a writer that has not applied the purge deletes q as a strict subset of p; or two writers that purged different deltas of one commit each delete the other's rewrite and p. The label then lacks the kept live writes until each node uploads its body again: `entry_for` skips entries gone at the host, so `body_sends` uploads a fresh encryption (the stale writer may re-upload p until it applies the purge, then deletes it). Fix if the window matters: model in-flight deletes, then a tiebreak like S12's.
- **Memory caches that grow per blob** (growth, small): `SyncEngine::genuine` (a verdict per commit blob ever judged, about 70 B) is never trimmed, deleted and forgotten blobs included; a long-running node overwriting often keeps one per overwrite until restart. Trim it where the blob's record goes (`forget_blob`, `forget_entry_blob`, `absent_at`), keeping `false` verdicts while an entry stays `Bad`.
