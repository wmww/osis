# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: A few rough edges remain in how devices sync through servers: a membership change still makes every label recheck everything it stores. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Pass cost left** (notes/sync.md Work sets): a pass judges in full, O(entries) of every label, on any membership or `/label` write or roster change on the node (also of actors unrelated to the label), at the clock horizon of a roster (`until`, writes ahead of the clock), and while an entry that opens under a held secret is not opened (every aspect unsettled: once when it arrives, once when it opens; commits come inline, so rarely). Re-judged at every pass while they wait: orphan candidates (certificate copies by authors who left), sends in flight or held, relay waits, deletes and timed deletes in flight, bodies whose coverage is not established, chunks of dropped deltas a live tree may still link, certificate-linked blobs. Re-judged on any write to their aspect: dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier, keep-at-most bodies before their timed delete (all live entries of a map aspect under keep-at-most). Every pass also copies the group's closure several times (`prepare`, `body_ctx` for the inputs and the bodies, the `set_syncing` loop): O(things in the group). Local passes (labels with no server) judge everything. Simpler alternative to the engine-side triggers (Applied ids, `/created` things, `Joined`, drops, timers): one engine hook noting "commit c changed" whenever its record, dropped parts, validity or timers change, as the sync-state setters do; not done here (engine/ and apply/ were in other hands).
- **Local serving** (a label synced with no server, notes/networking.md Serving): no chain completion or promotion.
