# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: Rough edges left in node sync: membership changes upstream of a label re-judge all of it (cost, inside budget so far), a node serving a label with no server does not judge orphans, and two nodes deleting on stale views can briefly leave a label without kept live writes. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Pass cost left** (cost, not correctness; notes/sync.md Work sets). A pass judges the whole label, O(entries), when a roster or owner link upstream of the label's actor changes (a grant to the group and the re-seal after it; an edge on the group's owner, e.g. a person adding a device, full-passes every label of that owner's groups), when the actor gets or loses secrets of its own, when the group's held epochs change, and at a roster's clock horizon (`until`, writes ahead of the clock). Measured (`scale::sync_membership_pass_cost`, release, 10^3 commits per label): one full pass per node, 0.12-0.18 s; linear, so about 1.5 s at the 10^4 of the budgets, inside "rare events under a few seconds" (notes/testing.md Performance budgets). The case that could miss it: an owner edge over many labels (k labels × N); measure that before narrowing (`OSIS_SCALE_LABEL`, more groups per owner). Narrowing is not just exactness: `Held::reach` of the label's actor holds about the whole closure, so it needs a split between rosters that change what the actor reads (`may_read(x, ·)`) and who reads it (cards, orphans, certificates, validity). Small gain left: a horizon from a closure reader's roster other than the actor's could feed `moved` instead of a full pass. Also re-judged while they wait, bounded by what waits (transient): at every pass, orphan candidates, sends in flight or held, relay waits, deletes in flight, bodies whose coverage is not established, chunks of dropped deltas a live tree may link, certificate-linked blobs; on any write to the aspect, dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier; on a marked aspect (`Changed::aspects`), keep-at-most bodies before their timed delete with no write dead (after one died, on any write); on any membership-class write, deletes waiting for a delete key. A keep-at-most map body whose entry was removed stays live while the tombstone holds its dot (spec/commits.md §5), so no timed delete until a reset prunes the tombstone; else it goes at its grace bound or the next compaction.
- **Local serving: no orphans** (rare liveness gap): on a label served with no server, another writer's stamped certificate copy whose chain is incomplete (its author rotated out mid-upload) stays, and a node syncing only from this one holds it pending. At a server admins delete such orphans (`orphan`); locally the simpler fix would be to complete the chain as `local_promote` does for deposits, for entries by authors that no longer read the label's actor. No test constructs it yet (needs a peer upload cut between the commit and its copies).
- **Crossed deletes on stale views** (transient, self-healing; spec/sync.md §7, TLA S4/S12/S18): a carrier is judged on the deleter's view, and the TLA model's `Delete` checks it against the server atomically, so it misses deletes in flight. Two nodes can each delete the other's carrier: a purger deletes the purged body p (its rewrite q present) while a writer that has not applied the purge deletes q as a strict subset of p; or two writers that purged different deltas of one commit each delete the other's rewrite and p. The label then lacks the kept live writes until each node places its body again: `entry_for` skips entries gone at the server, so `body_sends` places a fresh encryption (the stale writer may re-place p until it applies the purge, then deletes it). Fix if the window matters: model in-flight deletes, then a tiebreak like S12's.
