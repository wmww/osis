# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: A few rough edges remain in how devices sync through servers: a new member joining through a share link may reveal a little to a dishonest server while it catches up, shared inboxes are emptied only by expiry, a pass still looks at every stored item, and some departed devices' contact cards linger. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Hinted label walk** (a link joiner or recovery device reaching a label it knows only by ID): claims on a step the server answered with go out before the step can be verified (`Path` needs an opened label, spec/wire.md §11.2), now only with the keys of the actors the hint is for (notes/sync.md, Open), so a dishonest server handing out its own step can still recognise that grantee's pseudonym. Closing it needs `Path` to answer a claim-key holder (wire change). Also: a label whose genesis was registered at the owner's derived epochs stays unverifiable for such a joiner (the osis API's `sync()` and `contact_card` give the group its own secrets first).
- **Consumed inbox blobs** are not deleted early (spec/sync.md §3, a MAY): the receiving device's certificate copy carries the grant, its boxes and the named things' `/created` and `/label`, but a later member applies that `/label` only once the granter's chain arrives, and until then opens the thing's label by the inbox blob's hints. Deleting consumed blobs made every `link_redeem` sweep seed fail. A safe rule needs the copy to carry the granter's chain too, or an "every reader consumed it" signal.
- **Pass cost**: bodies are cached per commit now (`Core::bodies`), but a pass still judges every present entry, runs `covered` per (snapshot, body) and `dropped_chunks` over every commit: O(entries) per change at scale. `/groups` is kept incrementally (notes/node.md).
- **Certificates residue**: a departed node's card goes only after an applied removal of the node's own edge is held (`stale_card`): a node that departs through its person's removal or an expired `until` keeps its `/node` and `/reach` under the label. Judging those departures needs the view to hold the node's chain, as for the own-edge case.
