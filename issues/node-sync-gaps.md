# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: A few rough edges remain in how devices sync through servers: some waiting entries are rechecked more often than needed, and a label's own membership change rechecks everything it stores. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Pass cost left** (notes/sync.md Work sets): a pass judges in full, O(entries) of the label, when a roster or owner link upstream of the label's actor changes (a grant to the group and the flows' re-seal after it; an edge on the owner of the group, such as a person adding a device, full-passes every label of that owner's groups) or the actor gets or loses secrets of its own, when the group's held epochs change, at the clock horizon of a roster (`until`, writes ahead of the clock) of a label a membership change reached. A membership change elsewhere re-judges the label's commits on things downstream of it (`Held::reach`; one walk over the label's commits) and the other nodes' cards. Re-judged at every pass while they wait: orphan candidates (certificate copies by authors who left), sends in flight or held, relay waits, deletes and timed deletes in flight, bodies whose coverage is not established, chunks of dropped deltas a live tree may still link, certificate-linked blobs. Re-judged on any write to their aspect: dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier, keep-at-most bodies before their timed delete (all live entries of a map aspect under keep-at-most; narrowing them needs the engine to mark the bodies a write killed: `Core::changed` could, from the deaths it records). Re-judged on any membership-class write: deletes waiting for a delete key. The engine's change hook (`Core::changed`) marks every commit on an aspect whose validity flipped, that expired, was purged or took a late delta, as the `Changed` events did: marking only the commits whose parts changed (the waits on the aspect still woken) would make those O(change); not done, it narrows what is judged. Every pass still walks the group's closure once (one copy in `body_ctx`, the `set_syncing` check, the comparison with the last pass's closure in `prepare`): O(things in the group); a closure-change mark from `/groups` would remove it. Local passes (labels with no server) judge everything.
- **Local serving** (a label synced with no server, notes/networking.md Serving): no chain completion or promotion.
- **A full label keeps a purged body** (spec/sync.md §7, TLC S18): a purged body with a live delta waits for the purger's rewrite; while the label is at its cap (code 12) the rewrite is refused and both stay, so the purged ciphertext stays at the server until space frees.
- **Crossed deletes on stale views** (spec/sync.md §7, S4/S12/S18): a carrier is judged on the deleter's view, and the TLA model's `Delete` checks it against the server atomically, so it misses deletes in flight. Two nodes can each delete the other's carrier: a purger deletes the purged body p (its rewrite q present) while a writer that has not applied the purge deletes q as a strict subset of p; or two writers that purged different deltas of one commit each delete the other's rewrite (it holds a delta they dropped) and p. The label then lacks the kept live writes until the nodes place them again (their states keep them). Fix if it matters: model in-flight deletes, then a tiebreak like S12's.
