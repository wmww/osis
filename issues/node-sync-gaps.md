# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: Open items of osis-node's sync layer after node-sync landed: replaced labels synced by admins for good, no P2P member peers, inbox re-send and consumed-blob deletion, hinted labels reached through a link actor, revokes about seen authors, cost of a pass, in-memory outbox and stranded times, lazy-chunk policy, certificate projections, simulator phase B.
> Tags: sync, node

- **Replaced labels** (residue): an admin keeps syncing every replaced label of its groups for good (membership-class commits and certificates stay there; nothing decides it is done); members drop a replaced label once they read the replacement.
- **P2P member peers** (spec/sync.md §5 last bullets, §10 LAN): only server connections; no serving side on a node, no `/reach`-authenticated peers, no LAN discovery.
- **Inbox**: no re-send of an unconsumed inbox blob after `INBOX_LIFETIME` by the sender, no deletion of consumed inbox blobs by the inbox group's admins; `take_direct` packages are in memory only (the inbox outbox is durable). A deposit refused for a label not registered yet (code 13), a rate limit, a full budget or a stale head goes back to the outbox for `retry_ms`.
- **Hinted label of a group reached through a link actor** (the osis API's link tests): a redeemer reaches the target only through K's pending grant (`UnknownCreated` of the target), so it holds no `/label` of the target and opens the hinted label by guessed index with the target's read secrets from the pending boxes. The edge case: when the target rotated after that grant (finalizing a single-use link removes L from K, and L's departure cascades to the target), the redeemer's key is stale and the server answers with the next step, whose parent it cannot verify. Fixed: the walk goes on (claims on that step) and the server's whole path is verified once a key opens the label (`LConn::walk`); residue: claims are sent against a step not yet verified (a dishonest server could hand out a step key of its choosing to recognise the claimant), and a label whose genesis was registered at the owner's derived epochs stays unverifiable for such a redeemer (the osis API's `sync()` and `contact_card` give the group its own secrets first). Also: a label that failed to open is retried at the next tick when a membership commit arrives (new secrets), not after `resync_ms`.
- **Timed deletes** always wait for a carrier snapshot (live writes are assumed: the engine does not say whether a body still has live writes).
- **Untested review items**: a server-sent wrap that does not open to the known blob key is ignored and relayed stamps are checked against the head (code in `sync/inbound.rs`, no adversarial test yet: the in-process harness has no raw client); "outside" deletes wait for a settled view (no dedicated test).
- **Grantee certificate copy** (spec/sync.md §3) and **revokes / roster snapshots about authors seen under a label** (spec/permissions.md §4): the hook exists (`SyncEngine::place_certificate`), the triggers belong to membership-flows.
- **Certificates**: roster snapshots go along whole (`/members`, `/tenures` deltas), not projected to the chain's members; others' certificates are never judged (left as may-hold), so a departed node's card stays until an admin deletes it.
- **Cost**: every pass recomputes the label's bodies from every held commit touching the closure (projection, `covered` joins against each snapshot entry) and judges every present entry; fine for tests, O(commits) per change at scale. `/groups` is recomputed on every `/group` change (node-core, now also on late deltas), O(groups²) at open.
- **Lazy chunks**: `fetch_missing` fetches every unopened entry; a read that misses a chunk should fetch that tree only (needs the missing blob ID from osis-label's reader).
- **Volatile state**: stranded-heartbeat times and outstanding fetches are in memory (a restart may place one more stranded heartbeat).
- **Hints**: a label learned from an inbox blob is opened by guessing its index 0..4 (a reader cannot derive the label ID); the hint goes once `/label` of the thing names it.
