# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: A few rough edges remain in how devices sync through servers: a new member joining through a share link may reveal a little to a dishonest server while it catches up, shared inboxes are emptied only by expiry, a membership change still makes every label recheck everything it stores, and some departed devices' contact cards linger. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Hinted label walk** (a link joiner or recovery device reaching a label it knows only by ID): claims on a step the server answered with go out before the step can be verified (`Path` needs an opened label, spec/wire.md §11.2), now only with the keys of the actors the hint is for (notes/sync.md, Open), so a dishonest server handing out its own step can still recognise that grantee's pseudonym. Closing it needs `Path` to answer a claim-key holder (wire change). The actor set's pending part comes only from the commits of inbox blobs naming the thing, decoded unverified: a sender of such an inbox blob (anyone with the actor's public inbox and deposit keys) who knows the tid of another actor X this node holds can still widen the set to X (accepted). Also: a label whose genesis was registered at the owner's derived epochs stays unverifiable for such a joiner (the osis API's `sync()` and `contact_card` give the group its own secrets first).
- **Consumed inbox blobs** are not deleted early (spec/sync.md §3, a MAY): the receiving device's certificate copy carries the grant, its boxes and the named things' `/created` and `/label`, but a later member applies that `/label` only once the granter's chain arrives, and until then opens the thing's label by the inbox blob's hints. Deleting consumed blobs made every `link_redeem` sweep seed fail. A safe rule needs the copy to carry the granter's chain too, or an "every reader consumed it" signal.
- **Pass cost left** (notes/sync.md Work sets): a pass judges in full, O(entries) of every label, on any membership or `/label` write or roster change on the node (also of actors unrelated to the label), at the clock horizon of a roster (`until`, writes ahead of the clock), and while an entry that opens under a held secret is not opened (every aspect unsettled: once when it arrives, once when it opens; commits come inline, so rarely). Re-judged at every pass while they wait: orphan candidates (certificate copies by authors who left), sends in flight or held. Re-judged on any write to their aspect: dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier, keep-at-most bodies before their timed delete (all live entries of a map aspect under keep-at-most). Local passes (labels with no server) judge everything.
- **Certificates residue**: a departed node's card goes only after an applied removal of the node's own edge is held (`stale_card`): a node that departs through its person's removal or an expired `until` keeps its `/node` and `/reach` under the label. Judging those departures needs the view to hold the node's chain, as for the own-edge case.
- **Local serving** (a label synced with no server, notes/networking.md Serving): the blobs a certificate copy linked (`place_linked`) are not deleted locally once the copy goes; no chain completion or promotion.
