# Node sync: what is approximate or not there yet
> Summary: A few rough edges remain in how devices sync through servers: some waiting entries are rechecked more often than needed, and a label's own membership change rechecks everything it stores. Nothing here loses data.
> Tags: sync, node

Repro: known gaps, no failing test.

- **Pass cost left** (notes/sync.md Work sets): a pass judges in full, O(entries) of the label, when a roster or owner link upstream of the label's actor changes (a grant to the group and the flows' re-seal after it; an edge on the owner of the group, such as a person adding a device, full-passes every label of that owner's groups) or the actor gets or loses secrets of its own, when the group's held epochs change, at the clock horizon of a roster (`until`, writes ahead of the clock) of a label a membership change reached. A membership change elsewhere re-judges the label's commits on things downstream of it (`Held::reach`; one walk over the label's commits) and the other nodes' cards. Re-judged at every pass while they wait: orphan candidates (certificate copies by authors who left), sends in flight or held, relay waits, deletes and timed deletes in flight, bodies whose coverage is not established, chunks of dropped deltas a live tree may still link, certificate-linked blobs. Re-judged on any write to their aspect: dropped bodies with live writes waiting for a carrier. Re-judged on any validity flip, un-merge, expiry, purge or late delta of their aspect (`Changed::aspects`): keep-at-most bodies before their timed delete. Re-judged on any membership-class write: deletes waiting for a delete key. Every pass still computes `published_under` (`labeled_groups` scans every held aspect key: O(aspects held)) and clones the group's `/label`. Local passes (labels with no server) judge everything. Seen while testing: a keep-at-most map body holding an entry stays live after the key's removal or a reset of the map, so it never gets a timed delete and goes at its grace bound or the next compaction (like an erased text).
- **Local serving** (a label synced with no server, notes/networking.md Serving): no chain completion or promotion.
- **A full label keeps a purged body** (spec/sync.md §7, TLC S18): a purged body with a live delta waits for the purger's rewrite; while the label is at its cap (code 12) the rewrite is refused and both stay, so the purged ciphertext stays at the server until space frees.
- **Crossed deletes on stale views** (spec/sync.md §7, S4/S12/S18): a carrier is judged on the deleter's view, and the TLA model's `Delete` checks it against the server atomically, so it misses deletes in flight. Two nodes can each delete the other's carrier: a purger deletes the purged body p (its rewrite q present) while a writer that has not applied the purge deletes q as a strict subset of p; or two writers that purged different deltas of one commit each delete the other's rewrite (it holds a delta they dropped) and p. The label then lacks the kept live writes until the nodes place them again (their states keep them). Fix if it matters: model in-flight deletes, then a tiebreak like S12's.
