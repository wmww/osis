# A member keeps the head it opened at
> Summary: A node that opens a label while an admin registers a child step (the grant's rotation) keeps the old step as its head for good: the first Sync after open has no cursor (range reconciliation carries entries, not Head records) and later cursor syncs start past the Head record. It then drops writers' stamps (they do not verify under the old write key), so it relays nothing, and a writer member never stamps.
> Tags: sync, node, tests

- Seen in the simulator: `osis_sim::replay("head_moves_during_open", 1)` (tier 1 test `head_moves_during_open`, ignored): readers R (both servers) and C (s1 only) granted back to back; their open was answered with the genesis step, the admin's Register of the child was processed before their Subscribe, and their cursor-less Sync came back without the Head record. R's `describe()` shows head = genesis while the server's head is the child; R judges the writer's bodies must-hold at s1 but uploads nothing (`Added` stamps filtered in `sync/inbound.rs`: `verify_stamp` under the stale head's write key). Also seen as a writer grantee whose writes never leave the node (keys from the old head: no established write epoch there). A reconnect fixes it (the stale sync key walks to the head), which the other scenarios do after setup (`NetSim::reconnect_all`).
- Fix (node-sync, maybe proto): after an open, confirm the head before the first pass (a `Path` after the reconciliation, or have `SyncReply`/the reconciliation end report the current head and run `new_head` on it), or re-open when a stamp fails to verify under the known head.
