# The winner of a single-use link race can be stranded
> Summary: When the sharer applies the losing redemption first, it rotates K for L's departure boxing the loser (epoch n+1), then rotates again once the winner's earlier redemption arrives (epoch n+2, boxing the winner). The winner has no box at n+1, so it cannot open the rotation commit to n+2 (wrapped under n+1) nor K's or the target's label; nothing re-seals it automatically, and a manual `reshare` of K to the winner does not recover it either (the re-seal commit is held but n+2 never becomes established there).
> Tags: flows, keys, sync, tests

- Seen in the simulator: `link_race` seeds 6, 7, 16 of 1..16 (`osis_sim::replay("link_race", 6)`): at quiescence the winner's node knows K's epoch 3 (the loser's) without its secret, reads nothing written after the race; the sharer holds 4.
- Spec reading (spec/permissions.md §3 Re-seal, spec/sync.md §5, notes/sync.md Limits): a member that missed a rotation is locked out until re-sealed; the stranded heartbeat should trigger the re-seal. Gaps: node-sync never calls `Engine::reseal_due` nor `reshare` on a stranded heartbeat (issues/keys-established-epochs.md says it does); and a re-seal at n+2 with history boxes did not establish n+2 at the winner here (check how a re-sealed member establishes an epoch whose rotation commit it cannot open: the history box for n+1 should open it).
- Possible flows-side avoidance: none found within the spec (rotating before encrypting under K is required; the loser's redemption is valid at the sharer until the winner's arrives).
