# A writer's delete keys arriving is no purge-on-replace trigger
> Summary: `retention::settle_none` covers a replaced purge-on-replace body only at a node that may write (role and the delete keys every write lists, spec/crypto.md §7), judged when the body, its replacement, a validity flip or a role change arrives. A node whose role came first and whose delete keys of the thing come later (a first-holder grant's `keys.0` commit after its edge) becomes a writer holding both with no trigger, so whether it covers depends on arrival order, not only when: keys first, it covers at once; keys last, it keeps the body until another writer's cover or the next trigger on the aspect, for good if neither comes. Breaks spec/requirements.md Purge on replace ("gone at every writer holding it as soon as it holds the replacement") and replace.rs's "a function of the held set". Safe side for erasure (the body is kept longer, and the replacer always covers, since it could write, so the body goes wherever that cover arrives).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: retention, engine

## Direction
Mark `none_due` for the held purge-on-replace aspects of the things whose delete keys this node comes to hold: a rebuild of `keys.0` or `secrets.0` of a delete base, a promoted inbox hint, a base change by a first-holder grant. `Core::timelines_changed` marks those under a moved role through `ix.held.reach`; the same reach from the base would do, but costs O(the base's purge-on-replace aspects) at every rotation. Better to mark only on the false-to-true change of `secrets::holds_delete_keys_in` per base (rare: once per base per node), which needs that answer remembered per base.
