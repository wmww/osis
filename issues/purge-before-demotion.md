# A purge applied before its requester's demotion arrives stays
> Summary: spec/permissions.md §8 says a request drops a delta iff its author held the right at its time. A node holding the delta and request, then the demotion that took the right before the request's key, has dropped the body and keeps its writes value-less for good; a node that got the demotion first keeps the body. Arrival order decides. Same for every class, not new with the purge sets. A compaction snapshot's right has the same window.
> Tags: engine, permissions

Test: `retention::purge_rights_any_arrival_order_spec_perm_8` pins it (orders with the demotion last). `on_late` re-drops a held value-less part "even if the purge that made it so is no longer judged allowed", so a copy arriving again does not undo it. A waiting part held value-less for a request (`retention::strip_purged`) likewise drops value-less once applied (`stripped_joined`), whatever the request's judgment then.

Fix to weigh: re-judge purges of an aspect when the requester's timeline changes, take the delta out of the value-less list and take the body again from a holder (a dropped body counts as never received). The cost is a re-join path on every timeline change; the harm is one stale requester's drop at nodes that had not heard yet, and fresh holders still serve it. `order::arrival_order_purge_*` has a purge step but only the owner purges, so it does not see this; let others purge there once fixed.

Upload war while both are online: the node that got the demotion first holds the body as must-hold and uploads it; a node with the stale drop judges it must-not-hold and deletes it, at every wake, for good, and the uploader's entries pile up (seen in `tombstone::a_fallen_purge_shows_no_tombstone`, any retention, on main too). A permanent delta adds that node's header-only blob to the war.

Snapshots: a right holder that drops a body under a snapshot and then learns that its actor had lost the right at its key has dropped it too (and may have deleted it at hosts). Reads stay alike, since the snapshot stays valid and carries the live writes; only history differs (`deletion::stale_drop_reads_alike_spec_commits_5`, and with the snapshot falling the kept copies return). `snapshot_right` itself judges alike in every order (`retention::snapshot_right_any_arrival_order_spec_commits_5`). `uncover` does not ask for the right: a right-holding snapshot superseded by another writer's must keep its drops. So a part falling back needs another way to tell a stale drop.
