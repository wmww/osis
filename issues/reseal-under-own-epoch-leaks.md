# A fresh encryption of another's commit re-seals it under the sealer's epoch
> Summary: `Engine::seal` (a body placed with no encryption of it held, or logged again past a delete) seals every section it can open under the sealer's newest current read epoch (spec/crypto.md §5 Section: "the epoch is chosen as for a wrap"), not the epoch its writer used. A node holding an epoch's secret but lacking the removal that epoch's rotator knew (unestablished there) then re-seals content written knowing the removal under an epoch the removed holder reads: the content-level twin of the leak R9 closes for secrets.
> Priority: critical
> Tags: security, keys, sync

- Reproducer (engine, checked by hand): A first admin, a2 and R and V admins, M reader of g. a2 removes M (W, with its rotation). R, holding W, rotates read (e_R: names W, boxes V, not M) and writes `w` under e_R. V gets everything of R's but W: it holds e_R's secret (unestablished: W missing), opens R's write, and M is a reader there. `lp.sealed(V, [R's write])` (a fresh encryption, as node-sync's `place` makes one) then `deliver_sealed(M, ..)`: M opens it and reads `w`. R's own entry of the same write stays closed to M.
- Paths in node-sync that seal another's body fresh: `place` with no encryption of it held (a body that came by a direct connection), and a placement again past a delete it finds not standing (`again`: always sealed now; only the sections it cannot open are copied verbatim). A host may withhold W from V; a writer's delete, or an honest cover V lacks, makes V log again.
- spec/requirements.md: "Removing a holder never re-encrypts existing content" and "the holder stops reading new content once a writer has written after the removal": re-sealing under the sealer's epoch breaks both whenever the sealer lacks a departure the writer knew.
- Also in the withdrawn-epoch shape (issues/withdrawn-epoch-content-unread.md): a box holder of the refuted e_R sealing R's write fresh hands it to M, so the stated Limit holds only through entries as written; in the world where a re-grant voids the refutation it is that issue's leak.
- Direction (one rule, from R9): a section is re-sealed only under an epoch the node may pass on that the original covers, never under one the node chose for itself: copy verbatim every section of a held genuine blob (opened or not), and seal fresh only a part no held blob carries, under the epoch established here that it opened with (refusing when that epoch is not established here). Needs a model before building (Rotation.tla does not model re-encryption).
