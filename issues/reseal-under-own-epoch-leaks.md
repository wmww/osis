# Content another wrote, encrypted again under the sealer's epoch
> Summary: Engine side built (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, `labeling::opened`, Reseal.tla): a node encrypts another's part under an epoch it chooses (a section, a wrap, a copy, a snapshot carrying it) only while an epoch it opened a genuine section of that part under is established there; else the held section goes verbatim or nothing is made. Left: the new sync's calls (Sync side), headers first received header-only, the no-section exemption.
> Priority: critical
> Tags: security, keys, sync

The leak (reproduced: `rotation::tests_reseal`): a2 removes reader M (W); R, holding W, rotates read (e_R, boxing V and not M) and writes under it. V holds e_R's secret but not W (a peer withholds it, or V holds A's demotion of a2 that voids W: the withdrawn-epoch shape), so e_R is not established at V and V's newest epoch is one M holds. A fresh encryption of R's write by V (logging it with no encryption held, logging again past a delete), or V's compaction snapshot of the aspect, put R's content under V's epoch: M read it. Breaks spec/requirements.md Permissions ("removing a holder never re-encrypts existing content"; "stops reading new content once a writer has written after the removal").

## The rule (built)
- Content twin of R9: what a node read only under epochs not established at it, it passes on only as the ciphertext it came in. `Engine::open_commit` records per part the epoch each section opened under and the delta's hash (genuine: checked against the header where the thing's record is held, else compared with the held delta at use); `labeling::opened::passes_on` judges establishment at use (a demotion voiding W unestablishes e_R later).
- `Engine::seal`: every section of `extra` goes verbatim first; other parts are sealed under the node's epoch only where `passes_on`; else left out, and `None` when the body needs them.
- Wraps (`Engine::wrap_secret` with the body's parts): `None` while a part the blob carries does not pass on, or a content part of the commit it opened (and holds content of) has no genuine opening under an epoch established here (the header), so neither the header nor a per-sync-ID copy (whose wrap is its only gate) reaches a party the node does not know was removed.
- Snapshots (`retention::snapshot::may_snapshot`): a content aspect waits (`MayWrite::Later`) while a held body it would carry does not pass on.
- A part read in no section (an inbox payload, a direct connection, LocalPeer's plain delivery) passes on: it came under no epoch (Open paths). So do role-class parts (records, keysets, roles, boxes sealed each to its recipient) and parts held with no content (dropped, value-less leftovers) for sections and snapshots: only content parts are recorded, and a part's rows go when it is dropped or held value-less (kept, they follow the bodies' churn: the growth tests count them).
- Openings of a commit already held count too (it came first in no section, or with other parts): the re-open records what its rows lack, written by that blob's ingest. Openings are written in the ingest transaction that keeps the commit held or pending, so a restart before a pending commit applies loses none (`openings_of_a_pending_write_survive_a_restart_spec_crypto_5`).
- Model: core/test/tla/rotation/Reseal.tla (`run.sh reseal`): the build passes with liveness; sealing under the opened epoch itself passes too, established or not; the sealer's newest epoch, a forged opening counted, establishment judged at the opening, and openings lost by a restart counted as none each leak; content read in no section leaks where it reaches a node lacking the removal (`RS_exempt`), and refusing it is safe and live (`RS_closed`).

## Sync side (osis-sync and the facade)
- Logging again, a deposit, any fresh encryption of a body: call `Engine::seal(body, class, sections, rng)` with `sections` = every section of every held genuine blob of the commit, opened or not (`OpenedBlob::sections`, not `sealed`), never a copy's. The engine copies them verbatim under a fresh blob key, salt and blob ID and seals only parts no given section carries.
- `None` from `seal`: nothing may be made now (the body needs a part this node read only under an epoch not established here and no held section carries); retry when keys change (W arriving establishes e_R).
- Wraps: take the wrap secret from `Engine::wrap_secret(g, Some((commit, parts)))` with the parts the blob carries, every time (a reused blob too); `None` means the blob is not wrapped for that sync ID now.
- Every received commit blob goes through `Engine::open_commit` before its plaintext is ingested, in the same process: the openings ride in memory (`Core::openings`) until that commit's next ingest, which writes them where it keeps the commit (held or pending) and lets go of them whatever the outcome. A blob opened and never ingested keeps its openings in memory until restart: the facade either ingests every commit it opens or calls `Engine::openings_done`.
- Never ingest another's content in no section (the plaintext `ingest` of an inbox payload or a direct connection's commit): a part read in no section passes on as the node's own (below).

## Open paths
- **Header-only blobs first received so**: a header this node never read in a section (a lasting header from a peer keeping S unread, no body ever opened) has no openings, so its wrap goes under the node's newest epoch and hands the header to a removed party it does not know of. A header of a commit whose sections it opened is judged by those openings while it holds their content; a purged or dropped part's are gone, so a lasting header after a purge is open the same way. Closing the rest needs the epoch the commit's own entry was wrapped under: the new sync would tell the engine when it unwraps one (a proposed `Engine::note_unwrapped(commit, sync ID's thing, epoch)`, recorded as an opening of the header), and the header would then pass only where that epoch is established here.
- **Content read in no section passes on** (`labeling::opened`): fail-closed (no opening, no fresh encryption) is safe and live in the model (`RS_closed`), and no sync path carries another's content so; it is not built because LocalPeer's plain delivery, the engine and node tests' main harness, carries every commit in no section (13 engine tests compact aspects so). Close it once that harness delivers sections.

## Limits
- An aspect holding a write this node read only under an epoch not established here (withheld removal, or a withdrawn epoch: never established) is not compacted by this node while so. Not a new growth: other writers' valid snapshots still drop its bodies here (spec/sync-compaction.md §1), and its size is that of the writes, as with no compaction.
