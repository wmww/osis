# A membership cycle with no common admin strands forever
> Summary: a departure inside a membership cycle is covered only by rotating the cycle in one commit, which needs a node that may rotate every actor of it. Two people's shared tags placed in each other (X's admins and Y's admins disjoint) have none: after a removal from X, X's and Y's writers, and everything above them, never encrypt again. A simpler rule (leave an uncovered member actor out of a rotation, re-seal it after it rotates) would be live and would drop the joint-rotation machinery.
> Tags: security, engine, permissions

Setup: Alice admins tag X, Bob admins tag Y; Bob granted X read on Y, Alice granted Y read on X (notes/tags.md: "only what you may share" needs admin of the target, so this is the natural mutual-share shape). Alice removes D from X. The removal commit rotates X alone (she may not rotate Y), boxing Y at its old epoch, which D holds: X stays uncovered for Alice. Y's box for X names X's superseded epoch: Y uncovered for Bob. `rotate_cycle` needs one node that may rotate both: nobody. Every actor above X or Y is uncovered too (its box names a stale or uncovered epoch). The only way out is by hand (an admin of both, or removing one cycle edge). `cycle_removals_leak_nothing_random_large` counts such runs (2 of 200 stay uncovered).

Alternative (replaces joint rotation, Tarjan, `joint_set`, `cycle_partners`, `rotate_together`): a rotation does not box a member actor M for role r when the epoch of M it would name does not cover a departure the new epoch must cover (boxing to it is encrypting under it); M is left out and re-sealed once it rotates. In the cycle: X rotates leaving Y out (covered); Y's admin rotates Y, boxing X at X's new epoch (covered: it boxes neither D nor Y); X's admin re-seals Y at Y's new epoch. Each admin acts alone; re-seals supersede nothing, so no ping-pong. The same rule could replace intermediate stranding on acyclic chains (`rotation_helps`). Costs: M's members lose the actor until M rotates and an admin of the actor re-seals (re-seal is a SHOULD with triggers); the re-seal trigger must skip a member actor whose newest epoch does not cover (else it undoes the omission). Needs a spec change to spec/permissions.md §3 (Membership cycles, Who rotates) and spec/crypto.md §4.
