# Safe revocation needs one node holding everything the member could write
> Summary: a demotion's frontier is the revoker's own receipts (spec/permissions.md §5.1), and writes it had not received are lost until re-grant (spec/requirements.md Permissions), so the pre-sync (§5.3) must download, decrypt and verify every label of every thing the member could write before stamping. spec/requirements.md also says total data may exceed what any node tracks and removal cost is "never to content or history". Both cannot hold: in a tree larger than any one node, every removal loses some of the member's data, or the removal is made from a node sized to the tree. The design states the rule and the pre-sync, not this consequence.
> Tags: permissions, spec, owner-call

Scale: removing a device of a person who writes to 500 groups syncs 500 labels first; removing a member from a 100 GB organisation from a phone that holds 2 GB of it loses that member's unseen edits in the other 98 GB (`WaitForSync::Required` fails, `Try`/`Skip` lose). Organisations are told to demote from a node that tracks the tree (notes/api.md Demotion), which reintroduces a node that holds everything.

## Recommended fix (owner decision: adds a rule to the timeline)
Let a demotion say what it audited, so removal and audit can be separated:
1. The demotion carries, beside its frontier, the **scope**: the set of things (or labels) whose writes by X it audits. A write of X on a thing in scope is covered iff the frontier lists it, as today. A write on a thing out of scope is judged as after an `until` (spec/permissions.md §5.1: "the member's own timestamps decide, within the allowed clock drift") until the next explicit write on that edge whose scope includes the thing.
2. A later explicit write on the same edge (a removal of the already-absent edge is one, §2) with a wider scope and frontier audits the rest. So a phone removes X from A at once (access ends: the rotation is in the same commit), and a node holding the tree audits X's content later, label by label, each with the frontier of what it had synced.
3. The pre-sync becomes per scope: `demote` on a small node uses the scope of the labels it syncs; `WaitForSync::Required` means "sync the scope first", not the tree.
Exposure during the gap is exactly today's `until` exposure (a backdated forgery stands until the audit), so nothing new is trusted. Cost: perm's walk tracks per (X, thing) whether the last explicit write audited it (the `lowered_unaudited` state already exists per edge; it becomes per (edge, thing)), the frontier type gains a scope, the membership model and harnesses follow. A demotion with no scope field means "everything", so today's commits are the special case.
If the owner prefers not to add a rule: state the trade in spec/requirements.md Cost ("Removing a member costs ... plus syncing what it could write before stamping, or losing what the remover had not received").
