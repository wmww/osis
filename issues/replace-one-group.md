# Replace by one commitment group removes other things' parts
> Summary: A Replace needs a delete signature for one commitment group only (spec/sync.md §5), and the host cannot see what the replacing blob holds. So a label writer holding one thing's delete key (a writer of t, the author by level 2, ...) removes the whole one-blob commit at the host, parts on other things and of other retention classes included (an `admin`-only part by its author). Honest holders upload the body again (must-hold); with none online, a later reader never gets those writes. Dead writes within keep-at-least are not uploaded again at all (§6: nothing live left to carry).
> Tags: security, sync

Repro: osis-node `hostile_host::replace_by_one_group_keeps_other_things_parts_spec_sync_5` (ignored): w, a writer of t only, replaces c's commit across t and u with junk while c and the admin are away; d, arriving later, reads u's old value. Passes without the replace, and with c back online before d.

Before step 2b a projection held one retention class, so a Replace needed exactly the delete authority of what it removed. Needs a design call (not a local fix): e.g. a Replace needs every group as a Delete does, with a purger's rewrite going through a holder of the remaining groups' keys or uploaded beside the original and the original deleted once every group's holder agrees; or the requirement relaxed in spec/requirements.md (deletion authority at an untrusted host is per commit, restored by honest holders).

Related, same root (a node copies sections it cannot check): a writer uploads a blob of the commit whose B section keeps its genuine wrap over a corrupted ciphertext. B's readers mark that blob bad; a node that cannot read B finds it genuine (`genuine` checks opened parts only) and may copy the corrupted section verbatim into its own fresh encryption (`sealed_sections` takes any genuine blob's), which B's readers then mark bad whole. A purge rewrite built that way replaces the good blob. Recovery again rests on B's holders uploading.
