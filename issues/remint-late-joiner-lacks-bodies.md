> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine

- Repro: the remint test, then for each node every held commit whose `sync_bodies` lists D with no entry under D (`holds`). Seen with and without the read judgement of pulled entries (branch `pulled-judge`), so it predates it.

  - No node lists a commit under any sync ID with no blob of it anywhere, but for may-hold bodies: a probe at every quiet of every osis-sync test, and of the remint scenario at 24 world seeds (each node, held commit and sync ID in its `sync_bodies`, blobs of the commit opened or sealed at any node), finds only the remint's own header-only minting copy at node 2 before the purge (may-hold: a held header places it). The probe of the must-hold-and-no-entry form at quiet finds node 0's creation commit here and the flows case below, besides bodies a test's partitions hold off.
