# In the remint test, D bodies listed under D are held by no entry at two nodes
> Summary: in `destruction::a_remint_expiring_earlier_keeps_the_lower_copy_spec_commits_5`, at quiet, node 0 (another account's reader of D) holds no entry under D of its own account's creation commit, which its `sync_bodies` lists under D, and node 1 lists a `group.0` commit on D under D with no blob of it opened or sealed anywhere; two writers of one thing also lack each other's certificate copies though both list them.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: sync, engine

- Repro: the remint test, then for each node every held commit whose `sync_bodies` lists D with no entry under D (`holds`). Seen with and without the read judgement of pulled entries (branch `pulled-judge`), so it predates it.
  - Node 0: its account's creation commit (`created.0`, `keys.0`, `sync.0`, `roles.0`, `account.0`, `secrets.0` of its account and devices actor), a certificate under D; its blobs held, none under D, no tombstone. A non-writer of D logs its own certificates in its own store (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store) and deposits them at store sources; neither happened.
  - Node 1: a `group.0` commit on D that `sync_bodies` lists under D, with no blob opened or sealed (`commit_blobs` empty): never logged anywhere. Likely engine-side (a commit made with no sealed blob, or one `sync_bodies` lists wrongly).
- Also in `flows::granted_and_read(Role::Write)` (both accounts writers of t): at quiet node 1 holds no entry of node 0's certificate copies under t's sync ID (blob held and opened, commit listed under S as a certificate copy, read's verdict must-hold, no tombstone), and node 0 lacks two of node 1's. The test `flows::writers_of_two_accounts_keep_each_others_certificates_spec_sync_judgement_2` asserts only that neither drops or deletes them. Likely the Own store "stands" reading: an entry of another blob of the commit stands as the body's log, so the copy carrying the certificate part is never pulled or logged.
