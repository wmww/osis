# Grants without history: what is left
> Summary: Grants without history (osis `GrantOpts::reads`, CLI `grant --reads`; design notes/history-less-grant.md, spec/crypto.md §4 Floors, State at a cut, §5 Passing on) are built; left: from now through holder actors (Tess decided; design below), the group/thing default, record blobs at a cut for things under a group's sync ID (unchecked), chain certificates before the cut, the setting past the cap, owed cut snapshots written at pass end, an osis-level test across sync, and the sync side below.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, spec, permissions, keys, crypto, engine

## Left to build
- **Records before the cut** (liveness; sync side): a thing's record is no content, so Passing on lets it go under the cut's epoch; spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut says the snapshot's writer logs the records of the things its snapshots are on as a record blob (spec/wire.md §3) under that epoch. The rebuilt sync logs record blobs for what a sync ID's certificates name (read's `cert_blobs_missing`, `Engine::seal_records`): a from-now grantee on a thing's own sync ID reads its current state (osis-sync `siblings::a_from_now_grantee_after_a_removal_*`). Unchecked: things under a group's sync ID (a chat's messages), whose records a certificate may not name; no osis-level from-now test reads across sync.
- **Things the actor holds a role on** (decided, Tess: from now reaches transitively, where practical; next step, not built): a newcomer granted on G without history reads every thing G holds a role on (a realm's channels), transitively, as it reads G: the current state, no past. Today it reads their whole history: G's cut is a rotation of G, so each thing T holding a box to G finds that box stale (the cut's keyset supersedes the one it names, Holder actors, Stale) and rotates, sealing G at the cut's keyset k; that rotation's history box carries T's earlier secrets, and N, holding k's secret, opens them. Design (a contained rule set, but new rules across actors, so it needs the model first):
  1. **A cut keyset**: G's keyset at a cut records that it is one (spec/wire.md §7, one flag); a reader of T's certificates judges the flag only on a keyset whose rotation commit (carried to T's sync IDs, Carried) is a grant without history by an admin of G, its `roles.0` delta carried with it as Carried demotions carry theirs (else a history-less writer of G, which may write G's `keys.0`, clears the flag and reads T's past). This shows T's readers that G admitted someone without history (G's demotions already travel so).
  2. **A holder actor's floor on T** (notes/history-less-grant.md, A party's floor, extended): a keyset of G that is or descends from a cut keyset is boxed at an epoch of T only where that epoch's rotation boxes it there itself and writes a floor of at least its own n (a cut on T); never by a grant, re-seal or tip add at an older epoch. No edge write: any node that may rotate T's read makes it, so T's admins need not act and nothing designated waits.
  3. **The rotation** staleness already forces (above) is made a cut on T whenever it seals a holder actor at such a keyset: its history box carries nothing earlier, so it removes no box. G's earlier keysets keep their boxes at T's earlier epochs: G's members with history, their new nodes and later grants with history on G read T's past through them; N holds none of G's earlier secrets, so none of those boxes.
  4. **Transitive**: T's cut is a cut keyset of T, so each thing T holds a role on cuts likewise when its staleness rotation runs. State at a cut runs unchanged per actor (T's writers owe T's snapshots as T's seal floor rises).
  - Cost: no extra rotation (staleness rotates each T anyway), but each such rotation is a cut: T's writers write a snapshot per aspect of T (about 0.5 KB each plus state) per history-less join on G, for every reachable thing; every cut leaves a dropped epoch on T, so a later grant with history on T (or the tip add) writes one box per such cut: in a realm with frequent from-now joins this grows with the number of joins, per channel. That growth is the practicality question to settle in the model (e.g. a tip add of the newest dropped epoch carrying the earlier ones in its own history entry).
  - Before building: extend Cut.tla with one holder actor (keysets, staleness rotation, the flag forged by a history-less writer) and check NoPast for N on T, PriorKeep for G's members with history on T.
- **The encoding's residue** (decided, Tess: accepted as metadata): a holder granted without history gets the CRDT metadata a snapshot carries (spec/crdt.md §5), which no read shows: the author and stamp of every live write (who wrote each current value, when), each sequence element's creation anchor and earlier position write, every max/min contribution observed and every counter contribution with its author, a record's or element's concurrent writes hidden under a removal, and deleted text elements without their chars (where and when text was deleted). spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut states it. Rejected: a cut snapshot form folding max/min bags and dropping hidden writes (CRDT rule changes for metadata only; the earlier position write and the stamps cannot go anyway).
- **Expiring snapshots' cost** (open): a cut writes one expiring snapshot per distinct expiry of a live value (an aspect whose default expiry gives each write its own T: one commit per live value, about 0.5 KB each), each cut from the state's carried writes (`write_expiring`, O(state) once plus O(carried) per expiry). While an aspect holds a live expiring snapshot, `expiring_copies` decodes every held snapshot of it whole, lasting ones included, on each snapshot write and each undo of a part on it (an undo elsewhere reads along the delta's paths). Options: bucket expiries (ends values early at the holder, never late), or judge only the dots an undo touches.
- **The default**: a group or thing default (an aspect, set the way the retention setting is) for whether grants hand history (Tess: per grant, with a default). The API has the per-grant option only (`GrantOpts::reads`, whose default keeps what a holder reads and gives a new holder everything). Not started: it is a new aspect with its own writer rules, independent of the floor rules.
- **Chain certificates before the cut**: a newcomer verifies a role commit from before its cut only with its author's chain certificates; certificate copies are wrapped under read epochs (`Engine::wrap_secret`), so it opens none placed before its cut. Engine tests deliver commits off the actor raw, which hides this. Fix: certificate copies of chain commits under the roles secret too (check first that a chain copy carries nothing a newcomer must not read), or have the facade place what a newcomer lacks. The same holds for the `roles.0` copies a keyset's carried demotions bring (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions): a demotion on an actor below the target placed before the cut is a copy under a read epoch the newcomer does not hold, and a node holds no keyset without the demotions it carries, so the newcomer establishes nothing until they reach it under an epoch it holds.
- **The setting past `MAX_RETENTION_ENTRIES`**: the setting written again under a cut names each AK the one-way rule voids; where that would pass the cap, the writer refuses it and the grant fails. Not reached by any test.
- Limits stated in spec/crypto.md §4 Floors: the tip add needs the right to rotate read (a box put needs write, spec/permissions.md §3: admitting readers' box puts would let a reader box anyone at a current epoch and force rotations, and "dropped" cannot be judged from the delta alone); a writer's low-floor epoch after a cut never reaches the holder it bars (its content; its role commits do), the low-floor rotation boxing it at the next epoch (model `X_host2_all`).
- **Low floor behind unmet names**: the low-floor rotation waits while the node holds an unsuperseded epoch whose names it does not meet. That ends when the named commit arrives, or when the epoch is withdrawn or superseded; an epoch whose maker may still rotate and never withdraws (gone, or a hostile writer's planted epoch) holds it until another rotation lists that epoch (a removal of that writer does). The same wait as the withdraw it leans on (notes/crypto.md, Unsuperseded and withdraw).
- Known masking: the cut removing prior holders' boxes at the epochs it lists is caught only with the tip add off (the tip add boxes them again).
- **Two commits per history-less grant**: the grant (a role commit, wrapped under the epoch the cut supersedes, so the newcomer cannot open a setting inside it) and the setting written again under the cut, made in one local transaction (neither without the other at the writer). A newcomer holding the grant but not yet the setting reads no setting until the setting commit arrives: bounded by sync, as any commit.

## Sync side
- **A newcomer fetches the role commits from before its cut**: it opens their `roles.0` sections, and the wraps of the blobs carrying them, under the roles secrets its cut's history entry carries (`Engine::read_secrets` lists them after each epoch's secret). It must fetch and open those blobs, wherever in the sync ID's log they lie (their order is 0, spec/crypto.md §5: first), and must not take a body it cannot open as a reason to stop.
- **Bodies from before a cut placed again**: `Engine::wrap_secret` gives `None` for a blob carrying a part (or header) that may not go under the newest epoch (the floor clause), so a pre-cut body that must be placed again (a placement past a delete) is not placed; holders with history keep it as first placed. Wrapping it under the epoch it was opened under would place it for them (its holders opened its sections already): sync could ask for that epoch instead.
- **First-sync fetch order**: a holder with no old secrets would fetch and hold content bodies it cannot open for nothing; a history-less holder should skip fetching bodies below its floor that are not role commits (the same stop a node-local cap would be, issues/partial-history-fetch.md). Whether a blob is one it can open is known only by trying its wrap.
- **spec/sync-ids.md §2 Role keys in use** assumes every holder holds older secrets by history; a history-less holder opens from its cut's step on, and a walk to an older step must not strand it; it must still reach the older role commits (above).
- **Inbox of a rotation**: a rotation boxing a holder that holds no box at the epoch the commit is wrapped under goes to it by inbox (spec/permissions.md §3; `rotation::unreached`): a holder of a floor is never boxed at a fork beside its cut, under which the low-floor rotation is wrapped.
- **End to end**: no osis-level or chat-level test reads a from-now grant across sync. Write one through the osis API: a group with things made before the grant, a from-now reader that reads their current state and no earlier version.
