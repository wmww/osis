# Grants without history: what is left
> Summary: Grants without history (osis `GrantOpts::reads`, CLI `grant --reads`; design notes/history-less-grant.md, spec/crypto.md §4 Floors, State at a cut, §5 Passing on) are built and read across sync; left: from now through holders (Tess decided; design below, model first), certificate copies before the cut, the per-thing default, the expiring snapshots' cost, and the sync fetch side.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, spec, permissions, keys, crypto, engine, sync

## Left to build
- **Things G holds a role on** (decided, Tess: from now reaches transitively, where practical): a newcomer granted on G without history reads every thing G holds a role on (a realm's channels), transitively, as it reads G: the current state, no past. Today it reads their whole history: G's cut is a rotation of G, so each thing T holding a box to G finds that box stale (the cut's keyset supersedes the one it names, spec/crypto.md §4 Holder actors, Stale) and rotates, sealing G at the cut's keyset k; that rotation's history box carries T's earlier secrets, and N, holding k's secret, opens them. Design (a contained rule set, but new rules across holders, so it needs the model first):
  1. **A cut keyset**: G's keyset at a cut records that it is one (spec/wire.md §7, one flag); a reader of T's certificate copies judges the flag only on a keyset whose rotation commit (carried to T's sync IDs, Carried) is a grant without history by an admin of G, its `roles.0` delta carried with it as Carried demotions carry theirs (else a history-less writer of G, which may write G's `keys.0`, clears the flag and reads T's past).
  2. **A holder's floor on T** (notes/history-less-grant.md, A party's floor, extended): a keyset of G that is or descends from a cut keyset is boxed at an epoch of T only where that epoch's rotation boxes it there itself and writes a floor of at least its own n (a cut on T); never by a grant, re-seal or tip add at an older epoch. No edge write: any node that may rotate T's read makes it.
  3. **The rotation** staleness already forces is made a cut on T whenever it seals a holder at such a keyset: its history box carries nothing earlier. G's earlier keysets keep their boxes at T's earlier epochs, so G's members with history read T's past through them; N holds none of them.
  4. **Transitive**: T's cut is a cut keyset of T, so each thing T holds a role on cuts likewise when its staleness rotation runs.
  - Cost: no extra rotation, but each such rotation is a cut: T's writers write a snapshot per aspect of T per history-less join on G, for every reachable thing; every cut leaves a dropped epoch on T, so a later grant with history on T (or the tip add) writes one box per such cut, growing with the joins. That growth is the practicality question to settle in the model (e.g. a tip add of the newest dropped epoch carrying the earlier ones in its own history entry).
  - Before building: extend Cut.tla with one holder (keysets, staleness rotation, the flag forged by a history-less writer) and check NoPast for N on T, PriorKeep for G's members with history on T.
- **Certificate copies before the cut**: a newcomer judges a write from before its cut by the evidence S carries; a certificate copy of a role commit on a thing outside S's unit is wrapped under the read epoch it was logged at (`Engine::wrap_secret` wraps under the roles secret only a copy holding role parts of S's own actor), so the newcomer opens none logged before its cut. Hidden where the author's account is a contact (the sync harness introduces every account); not checked since the sync rewrite. Fix: certificate copies under the roles secret too (check first that a copy carries nothing a newcomer must not read), or log again what a newcomer lacks. The same holds for the `roles.0` copies a keyset's carried demotions bring (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions).
- **The default**: a thing default for whether grants hand history (Tess: per grant, with a default; an aspect set the way the history setting is). The API has the per-grant option only (`GrantOpts::reads`). A new aspect with its own writer rules, independent of the floor rules.
- **Expiring snapshots' cost**: a cut writes one expiring snapshot per distinct expiry of a live value (an aspect whose default expiry gives each write its own T: one commit per live value, about 0.5 KB each), each cut from the state's carried writes (`snapshot::write_expiring`). While an aspect holds a live expiring snapshot, `snapshot::expiring_copies` decodes every held snapshot of it whole on each snapshot write and each undo of a part on it. Options: bucket expiries (ends values early at the holder, never late), or judge only the dots an undo touches.

## Sync side
- **Role commits from before a cut**: a newcomer opens their `roles.0` sections, and the wraps of the blobs carrying them, under the roles secrets its cut's history entry carries (`Engine::read_secrets` lists them after each epoch's secret). It must fetch and open those blobs wherever in the log they lie (their order is 0, spec/crypto.md §5), and must not take a body it cannot open as a reason to stop. Untested beyond the author being a contact.
- **Bodies from before a cut logged again**: `Engine::wrap_secret` gives `None` for a blob carrying a part (or header) that may not go under the newest epoch (the floor clause), so a pre-cut body that must be logged again (past a delete) is not; holders with history keep it as first logged. Wrapping it under the epoch it was opened under would log it for them: sync could ask for that epoch instead.
- **First-sync fetch order**: a history-less holder fetches and holds pre-cut content bodies it cannot open, for nothing; it should skip bodies below its floor that are not role commits (the stop a node-local cap would be, issues/partial-history-fetch.md). Whether a blob is one it can open is known only by trying its wrap.
