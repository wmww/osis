# Option: grant a role without earlier history
> Summary: Every grant carries the history box, which opens every earlier epoch, so a new holder reads all retained content. Tess wants adding members without revealing history as a core option, not a chain of chats (notes/chat.md Left out, segments). Also useful for documents and folders, which cannot be segmented. Design below is model-checked (core/test/tla/rotation/Cut.tla): a cut rotation, a floor per keyset, and a carry rule on history entries that also judges the epoch's maker, checked against a hostile writer granted without history.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: security, spec, permissions, keys, crypto, engine
> Queued: build-ready after net step 8 (or beside the late net steps when agents are free); design checked in the model

History is a separate entry any holder of the old secret may add to a current epoch's history (spec/crypto.md §4, History add), and every rotation, grant and re-seal writes what its writer holds into the shared history box: without a rule change, any co-holder re-adds pre-grant secrets to the newcomer's epoch. A first sync (spec/sync.md §6) fetches what does not open last, after everything else; a holder with no old secrets would fetch and hold such bodies for nothing, so this option should stop that: the same stop a node-local cap would be (issues/partial-history-fetch.md).

`retention^.0` (spec/commits.md §5 The setting) must reach a history-less holder: its deltas are read like content, so a newcomer that cannot open an earlier epoch's setting does not know it: it shows and keeps history by no setting and fills no expiry or `final` from it. The grant must carry the setting in force or re-seal it.

This is not forward secrecy against a stolen device: that needs erasable per-device keys (notes/chat.md Rejected designs).

## Tess's decisions
- **Per grant, with a default**: a grant may say "without history"; a group or thing default is an aspect, set the way the history retention setting is (spec/commits.md §5 The setting).
- **Two choices only**: a newcomer sees history from when it was granted, or all of it. No admin-chosen start point.
- **Showing history later**: allowed if easy (it is a later grant with history to that holder).
- **Removed and re-added without history**: keeps what it could read before its removal, and must not get what was written between its removal and its re-add.
- **Metadata**: a newcomer may be able to tell that earlier entries exist (counts, sizes, times), but osis exposes none of it by default (no API surfaces entries the holder cannot open, nor counts of them).

## Design (checked in core/test/tla/rotation/Cut.tla)
The spec changes named below come with the build; none is made yet.

Principle: a box or history entry hands a secret only to a party entitled to it; a holder granted without history is entitled to what follows its grant's cut, and to what its account's nodes could already read (a new node of a member may sync anything its existing nodes display). Every rule below is that principle applied to one writer of boxes or entries, or to one holder of an epoch.

- **Cut**: a grant without history is, in the same commit, a read rotation (the cut) whose history entry carries no earlier secret (the names only, as every entry). It lists and supersedes like any rotation and boxes every current holder plus the grantee. A rotation removes only the boxes at epochs its history box carries, so the cut removes none of a current holder's: prior holders keep their boxes at the epochs it lists, which open everything before.
- **Floor**: each keyset carries a floor, one integer: a cut's own n; any other rotation's, the greatest floor among the epochs its keyset lists. A floor above its keyset's n makes the keyset invalid (judged from the keyset alone: no honest floor exceeds it). A node reads a keyset's floor as at least the floor of each epoch it supersedes that the node holds (an honest keyset already is; this lifts a forged one). So everything in a cut's causal past has a floor below the cut's n, whatever its writers claimed: floors never exceed n, and n grows along every supersedes chain.
- **A party's floor** on the actor: its newest grant's: a cut's n; a grant with history, its author's floor (a party hands no history it was not given: a history-less admin or inviter grants history to nobody, itself included). A node reaching the actor by several paths (its account granted without history, a holder actor granted with it) has the least floor over them: a path with history entitles it to everything. Through a holder actor, the floor is that actor's own on the actor (its members' grants on it are on its own epochs, which say nothing of the actor's). A node reads a party's floor from the party's grants it holds; validity of anything the party writes already needs them.
- **Carry rule** (the History add change): a history entry at epoch e carries the secret of f only when f's floor is at least e's floor and at least the floor of e's maker. It binds every writer of entries: the rotation's own history box, a grant's or re-seal's superset entry, and the History add. spec/crypto.md §4 History add becomes: a node holding e's secret, e established and not blocked at it, that holds an established superseded secret **that the carry rule lets in at e** and no live entry of the key lists, puts an entry carrying it. So no co-holder add, prior holder's later rotation, merge or re-seal brings a secret from below a cut into an epoch at or after it, nor to a maker that forged a low floor.
  - Why the maker: every other holder of e holds it by a box, and honest boxes follow the boxing rule; the maker holds e's secret by making it, so no box rule screens it. A history-less writer that lists only pre-cut epochs (an honest floor 0) or writes a low floor would otherwise hold an epoch into which co-holders add pre-cut secrets. A holder boxed at e against the boxing rule was boxed by a holder of e, who could hand it anything e opens; the rule does not chase it.
  - The maker's floor is read by its newest grant, never at the rotation's point: a member removed and re-added without history may stamp a rotation before its removal (a re-grant makes such writes valid again, spec/permissions.md §5.2), where its floor was 0. The carry rule judges only new entries at current epochs, so the newest grant is the one that counts.
- **Boxing rule**: a holder is boxed (rotation, grant, re-seal, tip add) only at epochs whose floor is at least its floor; a rotation's maker holds its epoch regardless, and a re-add boxes the member at what its removal listed (Re-added without history, below). Needed beside the carry rule: a node holding the grant may hold a fork made beside the cut by a rotator that did not know the grant (or may not have established the cut yet), and re-sealing the newcomer there hands it everything that fork's history box carries. A rotation by a node holding the grant always has such a floor; the rule bites on re-seals and grants. Its own nodes count no epoch below that floor as current (spec/crypto.md §4 Current gains the clause): never boxed such an epoch, a node holds one only as a commit it can read whose superseding chain it cannot (a re-added member holds its removal's rotation commit, wrapped under an epoch it kept), and it would show current with no secret (model, `X_readd` before the clause).
- **Dropped epochs**: those a held rotation lists but may not carry (by the carry rule at that rotation): what a cut lists, and a fork beside a cut that a later merge lists. Nothing re-seals them into later epochs; their holders keep their boxes. A grant with history boxes the grantee at each current epoch and at each dropped epoch it holds. The **tip add** (the History add's per-holder form): a holder of a dropped epoch boxes it to a current holder allowed it (boxing rule) that, from the live boxes and entries the node holds, cannot open it (a box removal counts once the rotation making it is established there). Showing history later is a grant with history, by a party whose own floor allows it.
- **Removed members**: a rotation takes a departed holder's boxes only at the epochs it carries, as anyone's: a removal's rotation carries what follows the last cut before it (everything, without cuts) and no rotation carries an epoch below its floor, so a removed member's boxes below a cut stand. A new node of it opens only what its earlier nodes could (what those standing boxes open), never an epoch made knowing its removal, since nothing made knowing the removal boxes it (`NoAbsence`, which bounds what any node of it obtains).
- **Compaction snapshots**: a `keys.0` snapshot's keyset carries the floor. An epoch a node holds established from a `secrets.0` snapshot alone has no known floor or maker: as e it takes no entry carrying anything (the carry rule cannot pass it) and boxes only holders of floor 0; as f it counts as floor 0 (carried only where no cut is above it). Both err toward carrying less; the epochs' holders keep their boxes, and the tip add reaches holders with history.
- **Re-placing role commits**: a history-less holder cannot open blobs wrapped under epochs below its floor, yet must hold every commit a rotation names (Established) and the roster. A rotation places again, under its own epoch and projected to their `roles.0` deltas, the role commits on the actor it holds that are wrapped only under epochs below its floor: at a cut, the actor's role history; at a merge or due rotation over a fork beside a cut, that fork's role commits. Projected, because a removal commit carries its rotation's keyset: held whole without the chain that supersedes it, it shows that pre-cut epoch as current at the newcomer (model, `X_names` before the projection). Chain certificates likewise (about 11.5 KB per member).
- **Re-added without history**: the re-add is a cut that also boxes the member at each epoch its removal's rotation listed (one box per such epoch, those the re-adder opens), which open what the member read before its removal: a new node of it reads what its earlier nodes could before the removal, the removal's causal past (`ReaddFresh`). An epoch beside the removal (made not knowing it) keeps m's box there only while no rotation has carried it; the re-add does not box it again. Rotations holding the re-add carry none of those epochs (they sit below its floor), and one not holding it holds no such box to remove, so the boxes stand. Its absence gives it nothing: epochs made knowing the removal sit below the re-add's floor unless they follow the re-add, and the removal's rotation lists only what came before it.
- **Also**: a holder skips fetching bodies it cannot open (issues/partial-history-fetch.md); the retention setting in force reaches it (the grant carries or re-seals it).
- **Cost**: one integer per keyset. Per cut: prior holders' boxes at the epochs it lists stay (one per holder per role), and the actor's role commits are placed again projected (grows with cuts times roster). A grant with history writes one box per dropped epoch (grows with cuts and cut-adjacent forks, not writes). A re-add without history writes one box per epoch its removal listed (one, unless the removal merged a fork).

### What a newcomer gets
- Never a secret of an epoch in its grant's causal past (made before the cut's rotator held the grant), except one a hostile member holds (which it could box to anyone anyway).
- Every epoch that follows its cut, from the live state alone (a new node of it included), a hostile writer's low-floor epoch included.
- Content written beside the grant, on a fork whose rotator did not know it, never reaches it, merge or not: that fork's history box opens the past.
- Content written beside the grant under another cut of at least the same n (two history-less grants racing) does reach it once merged: content beside the grant, never before it (`StrictNoPast` probe).
- A hostile member granted without history (writer, admin or inviter) gets nothing from before its cut, by its own rotations with any floor or listing, or through anyone it grants with history.

### Model (Cut.tla)
One actor, one key role. Admins a and b rotate, grant, remove and re-seal, a also rotating while it holds a fork; readers add history; commits arrive in any order a node can read them (wrapped under an epoch it holds, boxing or granting it, or placed again projected), and a commit counts only once its author's grant is held. The attacker: hostile parties (a history-less writer h, or a writer m with history) rotate listing any epochs they know of, with any floor the validity rule lets through, the rest of the rotation following the rules from that keyset; a hostile history-less admin grants an accomplice q with history. Know(x): every secret x ever opens from any box or entry ever written; Fresh(x): what a new node of x opens from the live boxes. Sizes and times: core/test/tla/rotation/README.md (History cuts). Properties: `NoPast` (property 1; for an honest newcomer up to what a hostile party holds; none at all for a hostile one and its accomplice), `PriorKeep` (settled: every honest holder with history, prior holders included, opens every epoch from the live state; property 2), `NoAbsence` (property 3: a removed member, any node of it, obtains no epoch made knowing its removal, the removal's own included, before any re-add), `NewReads`, `Unlocked`, `Alike` (settled: every honest holder holds every current epoch, established, all alike; property 4), `ReaddFresh` (settled: a re-added member's new node opens every epoch its removal's rotator held), over the concurrent shapes of property 5. Settled asks nothing of hostile parties.

### Gaps the build must cover (argued, not modelled)
- **Holder actors** (Seal, left out, stale): a party's floor is the least over its paths (A party's floor, above). The model has single-node parties.
- **Compaction snapshots** (above): the model has none.
- **Hostile n**: the model keeps a hostile rotation's n honest (the greatest listed n plus one). A larger n raises the n of every rotation after it, so a later cut still sits above its floor; beside a cut it acts as a concurrent cut (content beside the grant). A smaller n supersedes nothing at or above it.
- **Grant changes**: a party's newest grant may be a change of its grant (role, `until`, renewal) rather than a new one. The history choice rides on the grant value and a change keeps it unless its writer shows history (Showing history later); else promoting or renewing a history-less holder hands it history by the author's floor.
- **Hostile boxes**: the model's hostile rotation boxes by the boxing rule. Extra boxes hand only what their writer opens (counted in `NoPast`); missing boxes and removed ones (any writer may remove boxes) lock holders out, not specific to cuts (notes/crypto.md, Lockouts).
- **A history-less re-adder**: the re-add boxes m only at the removal's listed epochs the re-adder opens; one granted without history after the removal opens none of them, so m's new node opens only what stands below the last cut before the removal. The build must have a holder that opens them box them (a tip add, m allowed them by its re-add). The model's admins all have history.
- **A hostile member re-added without history**: the model's hostile m rotates only before its removal. An epoch made knowing the removal is carried only by a node holding the removal, where m's later writes count only with its re-add, which gives m its floor.
- Withdraw, void names, labels and steps (Decided 3).

### Decided (Tess)
1. **New devices see what old devices display:** a member's new device may sync anything its account's existing devices display. So a re-add without history boxes the member at the epochs its removal's rotation listed, and rotations take a removed member's boxes only where they carry, as anyone's: a removed member's later device opens what its earlier devices could (Re-added without history, Removed members, above).
2. **Two history-less adds at once:** each newcomer can read what was written around the other's add, never anything from before its own (accepted).
3. **Labels and steps** (build): spec/sync.md §2 Role keys in use assumes every holder holds older secrets by history; a history-less holder opens from its cut's step on, and a walk to an older step must not strand it. Not modelled.
