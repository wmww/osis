# Option: grant a role without earlier history
> Summary: Built: grants without history (a cut in the grant's commit, a floor per read epoch in its history entries and per party, the carry and boxing rules, the own-floor clause of Current, dropped epochs, the tip add, roles secrets (every holder opens every role commit's `roles.0`, so nothing is placed again), the low-floor rotation, re-add without history, the setting in force written again; osis `GrantOpts::reads` (`Reads::FromNow`/`All`, kept by default), CLI `grant --reads`; spec/crypto.md §4 Floors, Roles secrets). Left: content from before a cut encrypted again after it (a fresh encryption, a compaction snapshot) reaches the newcomer (critical, needs Tess); the group/thing default, the setting past the cap, and the sync side (rewrite Phase 1 input). Design checked in core/test/tla/rotation/Cut.tla.
> Priority: critical
> Tags: security, tess-call, spec, permissions, keys, crypto, engine

Tess wants adding members without revealing history as a core option (notes/chat.md Left out, segments); also for documents and folders, which cannot be segmented. The rules as built are spec/crypto.md §4 Floors; the engine side is `rotation::floors` and `osis_keys::RoleEpochs` (floors on the read view). Tests: osis-engine `rotation::tests_cut` (one per model shape, and the randomized invariant `nothing_before_a_cut_reaches_its_holder_spec_crypto_4`).

## Left to build
- **Content encrypted again** (security, needs Tess): content from before a cut reaches the newcomer once a holder with history encrypts it again under an epoch at or after the cut: a fresh encryption of an old commit (node-sync seals a body again: a placement past a delete, a body with no held encryption) and a compaction snapshot of an aspect written before the cut both go under the node's newest epoch. Reproduced by the ignored `tests_cut::content_encrypted_again_after_a_cut_stays_hidden_spec_crypto_4` (both shapes); the same path hands a member re-added without history what was written while it was away. The model covers secrets only (every commit in it is a role commit), so it cannot see this. The rule that closes it extends spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on from establishment to floors: a part goes under an epoch of the node's choosing only while the epoch it was written or opened under has a floor at least the chosen epoch's (own writes need the epoch they were sealed under recorded, as `opened.rs` records others'). Fresh encryptions then carry such parts as their held sections, verbatim. The open question is snapshots: under that rule no holder may compact an aspect written before a cut under any epoch after it, so such an aspect's history is never compacted again (a document edited for years after one history-less grant). The alternative, that a newcomer reads the current state but not earlier versions, contradicts "from when it was granted" for chat (a channel's state is its messages). Tess decides which. Wraps alone: an old blob placed under a new label is wrapped under the node's newest epoch, so the newcomer opens its header (tids, aspect keys, delta hashes: more than the counts and times Metadata allows) though not its sections; the same rule would gate those wraps (as `header_passes` does for establishment).
- **The default**: a group or thing default (an aspect, set the way the retention setting is) for whether grants hand history (Tess: per grant, with a default). The API has the per-grant option only (`GrantOpts::reads`, whose default keeps what a holder reads and gives a new holder everything). Not started: it is a new aspect with its own writer rules, independent of the floor rules.
- **Chain certificates before the cut**: a newcomer verifies a role commit from before its cut only with its author's chain certificates; node-sync places those as certificate copies wrapped under the read secret (`Engine::wrap_secret(g, None)`, sync/pass/certs.rs), so it opens none placed before its cut. Engine tests deliver commits off the actor raw, which hides this. Fix: certificate copies of chain commits under the roles secret too (check first that a chain copy carries nothing a newcomer must not read), or have the facade place what a newcomer lacks. Sync-side (frozen while the rewrite runs).
- **The setting past `MAX_RETENTION_ENTRIES`**: the setting written again under a cut names each AK the one-way rule voids; where that would pass the cap, the writer refuses it and the grant fails. Not reached by any test.
- Limits stated in spec/crypto.md §4 Floors: the tip add needs the right to rotate read (a box put needs write, spec/permissions.md §3: admitting readers' box puts would let a reader box anyone at a current epoch and force rotations, and "dropped" cannot be judged from the delta alone); a writer's low-floor epoch after a cut never reaches the holder it bars (its content; its role commits do), the low-floor rotation boxing it at the next epoch (model `X_host2_all`).
- **Low floor behind unmet names**: the low-floor rotation waits while the node holds an unsuperseded epoch whose names it does not meet. That ends when the named commit arrives, or when the epoch is withdrawn or superseded; an epoch whose maker may still rotate and never withdraws (gone, or a hostile writer's planted epoch) holds it until another rotation lists that epoch (a removal of that writer does). The same wait as the withdraw it leans on (notes/crypto.md, Unsuperseded and withdraw).
- Known masking: the cut removing prior holders' boxes at the epochs it lists is caught only with the tip add off (the tip add boxes them again).
- **Two commits per history-less grant**: the grant (a role commit, wrapped under the epoch the cut supersedes, so the newcomer cannot open a setting inside it) and the setting written again under the cut, made in one local transaction (neither without the other at the writer). A newcomer holding the grant but not yet the setting reads no setting until the setting commit arrives: bounded by sync, as any commit.

## Sync side (sync rewrite Phase 1 input)
- **Complete pass**: the low-floor rotation is a pass-end rotation (`Due::PassEnd` in `rotation::pass_end_rotations`, run by `Engine::reseal_due`), as re-seals, withdraws and History adds are. The engine needs nothing new: the new sync's `note_complete_pass(actor, began)` (notes/sync-boundary.md E5) per actor after a complete pass of that actor's sync IDs. Without complete passes a fork beside a cut is never merged for the newcomer.
- **A newcomer fetches the role commits from before its cut**: it opens their `roles.0` sections, and the wraps of the blobs carrying them, under the roles secrets its cut's history entry carries (`Engine::read_secrets` lists them after each epoch's secret). It must fetch and open those blobs, wherever in the sync ID's log they lie (their order is 0, spec/crypto.md §5: first), and must not take a body it cannot open as a reason to stop.
- **First-sync fetch order**: a holder with no old secrets would fetch and hold content bodies it cannot open for nothing; a history-less holder should skip fetching bodies below its floor that are not role commits (the same stop a node-local cap would be, issues/partial-history-fetch.md). Whether a blob is one it can open is known only by trying its wrap.
- **spec/sync-ids.md §2 Role keys in use** assumes every holder holds older secrets by history; a history-less holder opens from its cut's step on, and a walk to an older step must not strand it; it must still reach the older role commits (above).
- **Inbox of a rotation**: a rotation boxing a holder that holds no box at the epoch the commit is wrapped under goes to it by inbox (spec/permissions.md §3; `rotation::unreached`): a holder of a floor is never boxed at a fork beside its cut, under which the low-floor rotation is wrapped.
- None of the Phase 2 hooks (sync boxes, `add_history` at sync, the sync-ID layer) is extended: the cut rotates read (and write where a holder actor must be shown at write), the tip add boxes read only.

## Tess's decisions
- **Per grant, with a default**: a grant may say "without history"; a group or thing default is an aspect, set the way the history retention setting is (spec/commits.md §5 The setting).
- **Two choices only**: a newcomer sees history from when it was granted, or all of it. No admin-chosen start point.
- **Showing history later**: allowed if easy (it is a later grant with history to that holder).
- **Removed and re-added without history**: keeps what it could read before its removal, and must not get what was written between its removal and its re-add.
- **Metadata**: a newcomer may be able to tell that earlier entries exist (counts, sizes, times), but osis exposes none of it by default (no API surfaces entries the holder cannot open, nor counts of them).

## Design (checked in core/test/tla/rotation/Cut.tla)

Principle: a box or history entry hands a secret only to a party entitled to it; a holder granted without history is entitled to what follows its grant's cut, and to what its account's nodes could already read (a new node of a member may sync anything its existing nodes display). Every rule below is that principle applied to one writer of boxes or entries, or to one holder of an epoch.

- **Cut**: a grant without history is, in the same commit, a read rotation (the cut) whose history entry carries no earlier secret (the names only, as every entry). It lists and supersedes like any rotation and boxes every current holder plus the grantee. A rotation removes only the boxes at epochs its history box carries, so the cut removes none of a current holder's: prior holders keep their boxes at the epochs it lists, which open everything before.
- **Floor**: each epoch's history entries carry a floor, one integer (only a holder of the epoch's secret writes an entry, so no other party sets it; a keyset could be re-written by any writer, model `X_floor_any`): a cut's own n; any other rotation's, the greatest floor among its maker and the holders it boxes. A node reads the greatest its opened live entries write, at most n. So everything in a cut's causal past has a floor below the cut's n: floors never exceed n, and no honest box or entry hands a newcomer anything before its cut, whatever floors hostile holders write (`HonestPast`; without the cap a hostile high floor is carried past a later cut, `X_cap_nocap`).
- **A party's floor** on the actor: its newest grant's: a cut's n; a grant with history, its author's floor (a party hands no history it was not given: a history-less admin or inviter grants history to nobody, itself included). A node reaching the actor by several paths (its account granted without history, a holder actor granted with it) has the least floor over them: a path with history entitles it to everything. Through a holder actor, the floor is that actor's own on the actor (its members' grants on it are on its own epochs, which say nothing of the actor's). A node reads a party's floor from the party's grants it holds; validity of anything the party writes already needs them.
- **Carry rule** (the History add change): a history entry at epoch e carries the secret of f only when f's floor is at least e's floor and at least the floor of e's maker. It binds every writer of entries: the rotation's own history box, a grant's or re-seal's superset entry, and the History add. spec/crypto.md §4 History add becomes: a node holding e's secret, e established and not blocked at it, that holds an established superseded secret **that the carry rule lets in at e** and no live entry of the key lists, puts an entry carrying it. So no co-holder add, prior holder's later rotation, merge or re-seal brings a secret from below a cut into an epoch at or after it, nor to a maker that forged a low floor.
  - Why the maker: every other holder of e holds it by a box, and honest boxes follow the boxing rule; the maker holds e's secret by making it, so no box rule screens it. A history-less writer that lists only pre-cut epochs (an honest floor 0) or writes a low floor would otherwise hold an epoch into which co-holders add pre-cut secrets. A holder boxed at e against the boxing rule was boxed by a holder of e, who could hand it anything e opens; the rule does not chase it.
  - The maker's floor is read by its newest grant, never at the rotation's point: a member removed and re-added without history may stamp a rotation before its removal (a re-grant makes such writes valid again, spec/permissions.md §5.2), where its floor was 0. The carry rule judges only new entries at current epochs, so the newest grant is the one that counts.
- **Boxing rule**: a holder is boxed (rotation, grant, re-seal, tip add) only at epochs whose floor is at least its floor; a rotation's maker holds its epoch regardless, and a re-add boxes the member at what its removal listed (Re-added without history, below). Needed beside the carry rule: a node holding the grant may hold a fork made beside the cut by a rotator that did not know the grant (or may not have established the cut yet), and re-sealing the newcomer there hands it everything that fork's history box carries. A rotation by a node holding the grant always has such a floor; the rule bites on re-seals and grants. Its own nodes count no epoch below that floor as current (spec/crypto.md §4 Current gains the clause): never boxed such an epoch, a node holds one only as a commit it can read whose superseding chain it cannot (a re-added member holds its removal's rotation commit, wrapped under an epoch it kept), and it would show current with no secret (model, `X_readd` before the clause).
- **Dropped epochs**: those a held rotation lists but may not carry (by the carry rule at that rotation): what a cut lists, and a fork beside a cut that a later merge lists. Nothing re-seals them into later epochs; their holders keep their boxes. A grant with history boxes the grantee at each current epoch and at each dropped epoch it holds. The **tip add** (the History add's per-holder form): a holder of a dropped epoch boxes it to a current holder allowed it (boxing rule) that, from the live boxes and entries the node holds, cannot open it (a box removal counts once the rotation making it is established there). Showing history later is a grant with history, by a party whose own floor allows it.
- **Removed members**: a rotation takes a departed holder's boxes only at the epochs it carries, as anyone's: a removal's rotation carries what follows the last cut before it (everything, without cuts) and no rotation carries an epoch below its floor, so a removed member's boxes below a cut stand. A new node of it opens only what its earlier nodes could (what those standing boxes open), never an epoch made knowing its removal, since nothing made knowing the removal boxes it (`NoAbsence`, which bounds what any node of it obtains).
- **Compaction snapshots**: a `secrets.0` snapshot carries the history entries, so an epoch held by one keeps its floor. Its maker is unknown where no rotation commit of it is held: read as the greatest floor of A's holders (it errs toward carrying less; the epochs' holders keep their boxes, and the tip add reaches holders with history).
- **Roles secrets**: a history-less holder cannot open what lies below its floor, yet must hold every role commit on the actor to judge as holders do: the roster, the commits names name, and every edge write a later demotion's audit may revive (spec/permissions.md §5.1: an audit drops the demoted party's writes its frontier leaves out, so the write each dropped one overrode stands again; any edge write may matter, so no projection of the role history is enough). Each epoch has a roles secret, `kdf("roles", secret)`, which also names the epoch (the nonce rule runs through it); a commit's role parts (`roles.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0`: edges, public keys, sealed boxes and entries) go in a section of their own under it, and a blob carrying them is wrapped under it; every history entry carries the roles secret of every earlier epoch its writer holds, the carry rule aside (one from a pending commit's entry is for opening only, as a reading-only secret). So every holder opens every role commit's role parts and nothing else below its floor, and a role commit made anywhere (beside a cut, on a fork) reaches it once a rotation over its epoch carries that roles secret. Nothing is placed again: a cut costs one entry of roles secrets (one per earlier epoch), not the role history (`tests_cut::cut_cost_does_not_grow_with_role_history_spec_crypto_4`).
- **Low floor**: a current epoch whose floor is below a holder's (a fork beside a cut; a hostile low-floor epoch) bars that holder's re-seal there, so the read rotation is due at the end of a complete pass (`floors::low_floor_due`); its floor is the greatest of its holders', so it settles, and its entry carries the low epoch's roles secret. Without it a role commit on such a fork waits at the newcomer for good (no rotation need ever happen). Not while the node holds an unsuperseded epoch whose names it does not meet: it may lack a removal that epoch descends from and box the removed member (model `X_rmrace`). The rotation goes by inbox to a holder not boxed at the epoch it is wrapped under (the newcomer, never boxed at the fork).
- **Re-added without history**: the re-add is a cut that also boxes the member at each epoch its removal's rotation listed (one box per such epoch, those the re-adder opens), which open what the member read before its removal: a new node of it reads what its earlier nodes could before the removal, the removal's causal past (`ReaddFresh`). An epoch beside the removal (made not knowing it) keeps m's box there only while no rotation has carried it; the re-add does not box it again. Rotations holding the re-add carry none of those epochs (they sit below its floor), and one not holding it holds no such box to remove, so the boxes stand. Its absence gives it nothing: epochs made knowing the removal sit below the re-add's floor unless they follow the re-add, and the removal's rotation lists only what came before it.
- **Also**: a holder skips fetching bodies it cannot open (issues/partial-history-fetch.md); the retention setting in force reaches it (the grant carries or re-seals it).
- **Cost**: one integer per history entry, and a roles secret per earlier epoch in it. Per cut: prior holders' boxes at the epochs it lists stay (one per holder per role); nothing is placed again. A grant with history writes one box per dropped epoch (grows with cuts and cut-adjacent forks, not writes). A re-add without history writes one box per epoch its removal listed (one, unless the removal merged a fork).

### What a newcomer gets
- Never a secret of an epoch in its grant's causal past (made before the cut's rotator held the grant), except one a hostile member holds (which it could box to anyone anyway).
- Every epoch that follows its cut, from the live state alone (a new node of it included), but one whose own entry writes a floor below its own: a hostile writer's, or one made by a node not holding its grant (it may hold a removal made after the cut, not the cut). What is written there never reaches it; the low-floor rotation boxes it at the next epoch.
- Every role commit on the actor (Roles secrets), wherever it was made.
- Content written beside the grant, on a fork whose rotator did not know it, never reaches it, merge or not: that fork's history box opens the past.
- Content written beside the grant under another cut of at least the same n (two history-less grants racing) does reach it once merged: content beside the grant, never before it (`StrictNoPast` probe).
- A hostile member granted without history (writer, admin or inviter) gets nothing from before its cut, by its own rotations with any floor or listing, or through anyone it grants with history.

### Model (Cut.tla)
One actor, one key role. Admins a and b rotate, grant, remove and re-seal, and rotate where a current epoch's floor is below a holder's (the low floor); readers add history; floors are read from the history entries (the greatest an opened entry at the epoch writes, at most n; a rotation's the greatest of its maker's and its holders'); commits arrive in any order a node can read them (every commit here is a role commit: wrapped under an epoch whose roles secret it holds, boxing it, or granting it), and a commit counts only once its author's grant is held. The attacker: hostile parties (a history-less writer h, or a writer m with history) rotate listing any epochs they know of, with any floor, the rest of the rotation following the rules from that keyset, and put entries with any floor at epochs whose secret they hold; a hostile history-less admin grants an accomplice q with history. Know(x): every secret x ever opens from any box or entry ever written; Fresh(x): what a new node of x opens from the live boxes. Sizes and times: core/test/tla/rotation/README.md (History cuts). Properties: `NoPast` (property 1; for an honest newcomer up to what a hostile party holds; none at all for a hostile one and its accomplice), `HonestPast` (no honest box or entry hands a newcomer anything before its cut, whatever floors hostile parties write), `PriorKeep` (settled: every honest holder with history, prior holders included, opens every epoch from the live state; property 2), `NoAbsence` (property 3: a removed member, any node of it, obtains no epoch made knowing its removal, the removal's own included, before any re-add), `NewReads` (but an epoch whose own entry writes a floor below the cut's: a hostile writer's, or one made by a node not holding the grant though it lists what descends from the cut, e.g. holding a removal made after the cut but not the cut; its history may open the past, so it never reaches the newcomer: content written beside the grant), `Unlocked`, `Alike` (settled: every honest holder holds every current epoch, established, all alike, but epochs hostile parties made: a hostile rotation commit wrapped where a holder cannot open it stays unestablished there; property 4), `Roster` (settled: every honest holder holds every role commit by an honest author valid at it), `ReaddFresh` (settled: a re-added member's new node opens every epoch its removal's rotator held), over the concurrent shapes of property 5. Settled asks nothing of hostile parties.

### Gaps the build must cover (argued, not modelled)
- **Holder actors** (Seal, left out, stale): a party's floor is the least over its paths (A party's floor, above). The model has single-node parties.
- **Compaction snapshots** (above): the model has none.
- **Chain certificates**: the model's commits need no author chain (Left to build).
- **Hostile n**: the model keeps a hostile rotation's n honest (the greatest listed n plus one). A larger n raises the n of every rotation after it, so a later cut still sits above its floor; beside a cut it acts as a concurrent cut (content beside the grant). A smaller n supersedes nothing at or above it.
- **Grant changes**: a party's newest grant may be a change of its grant (role, `until`, renewal) rather than a new one. The history choice rides on the grant value and a change keeps it unless its writer shows history (Showing history later); else promoting or renewing a history-less holder hands it history by the author's floor.
- **Hostile boxes**: the model's hostile rotation boxes by the boxing rule. Extra boxes hand only what their writer opens (counted in `NoPast`); missing boxes and removed ones (any writer may remove boxes) lock holders out, not specific to cuts (notes/crypto.md, Lockouts).
- **A history-less re-adder**: the re-add boxes m only at the removal's listed epochs the re-adder opens; one granted without history after the removal opens none of them, so m's new node opens only what stands below the last cut before the removal. The build must have a holder that opens them box them (a tip add, m allowed them by its re-add). The model's admins all have history.
- **A hostile member re-added without history**: the model's hostile m rotates only before its removal. An epoch made knowing the removal is carried only by a node holding the removal, where m's later writes count only with its re-add, which gives m its floor.
- Withdraw, void names, labels and steps (Decided 3).

### Decided (Tess)
1. **New devices see what old devices display:** a member's new device may sync anything its account's existing devices display. So a re-add without history boxes the member at the epochs its removal's rotation listed, and rotations take a removed member's boxes only where they carry, as anyone's: a removed member's later device opens what its earlier devices could (Re-added without history, Removed members, above).
2. **Two history-less adds at once:** each newcomer can read what was written around the other's add, never anything from before its own (accepted).
3. **Labels and steps** (build): spec/sync-ids.md §2 role keys in use assumes every holder holds older secrets by history; a history-less holder opens from its cut's step on, and a walk to an older step must not strand it. Not modelled.
