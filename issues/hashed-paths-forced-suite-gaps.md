# Hashed paths: what the suites find with the form forced
> Summary: with every commit writing two things or more forced into the hashed path form (`Core::hashed_through` set by default), engine and sync suites fail in two classes: a part of one thing sealed beside another thing's parts is split off where its readers cannot open it, and a part not held on a thing whose path is unknown is judged invalid where the clear form judges it from the header. Honest writers state differing paths only once both close (notes/a1-spec/README.md step 2).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, commit, sync

The engine states its real per-thing paths only where all are equal; where they differ it states one for all (Interim, notes/a1-spec/README.md step 2), so only the test hooks write the hashed form. Run a suite in it by setting `hashed_through: Some(..)` in `Core`'s constructor (engine/mod.rs) on a wip commit.

- **Section membership.** `seal_commit` splits sections by (key, thing) where paths are hashed. In the clear form a part may sit under another thing's key: a dependent creation's parent `holds.0` part and the new thing's parts go under the new thing's read secret. Hashed, the parent's part gets a section of its own carrying the parent's `Salted`, and a reader of the new thing selects secrets by the section's thing (`sync_ids::blobs` `thing_of`), so it opens neither: `holding::a_reader_of_a_holder_holds_its_targets_boxes_to_it_spec_sync_judgement_2` (reader 1 missing parts 0 and 2 while it holds the created thing's `created.0`), `differential::histories_hold_their_closure`, `first::a_first_sync_of_n_sync_ids_takes_the_same_round_trips_spec_sync_streams_4` (121 deliveries at 5n, 109 at n). Spec/wire.md §4 says a section holds one thing's parts in both forms; the clear form's mixing is the thing to settle first.
- **Validity of a part not held.** A part whose section has not arrived is judged from the header where the path is in the clear (its actor known), and invalid where it is hashed and unknown (no actor). The held-model judges it as the clear form does: `order::held_set_decides`, `order::judged_under_the_walks_side_after_every_ingest_spec_permissions_5_1` (a `holds.0` part: model valid, node invalid), `order::a_creation_pruned_under_a_later_delete_is_replaced_spec_commits_5`, `order::a_removal_from_an_actor_audits_what_the_actors_grants_reach_spec_permissions_5_1`, `closure::a_thing_once_in_the_node_closure_holds_as_one_never_in_it_spec_commits_5`, `flows::lockout_bundled_with_own_rotation_recovered_spec_perm_3`. Either such a part is unjudgeable (pending, neither valid nor invalid, everywhere that reads validity) or the model learns the hashed form. An undo already treats it as not received (`undo::undone_parts`).
- Not defects: `rotation::tests_custody::an_outsider_places_one_milliseconds_rotations_by_id_before_depth_spec_permissions_5_4` searches commit IDs, `thing_cost::` measure bytes, `validity::the_engine_states_one_path_for_every_thing_spec_wire_4` asserts the clear form.
