# Hashed paths: what the suites find with the form forced
> Summary: with every commit writing two things or more forced into the hashed path form (`Core::hashed_through`), engine and sync suites fail in two classes, both one fact: a path is read from the header where no section of its thing is held. The split form (notes/path-form.md, DRAFT) replaces the hashed form and closes both; this issue goes once its steps 1 and 2 land and the suites pass with the split form forced.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: engine, commit, sync

The engine states its real per-thing paths only where all are equal; where they differ it states one for all (Interim, notes/a1-spec/README.md step 2), so only the test hooks write the hashed form. Tess settled that a write spanning things reached through different actors is one commit (notes/a1-spec/README.md Open 17), so the engine must write a multi-path form for real: notes/path-form.md designs it (each thing's path sealed in the header under its independent's roles secret, the key also in every encryption of its parts) and lists what replaces each piece of the hashed form, in two build steps.

- **Section membership.** `seal_commit` splits sections by (key, thing) where paths are hashed, so a dependent creation's parent `holds.0` part gets a section of its own a reader of the new thing does not open (`holding::a_reader_of_a_holder_holds_its_targets_boxes_to_it_spec_sync_judgement_2`, `differential::histories_hold_their_closure`, `first::a_first_sync_of_n_sync_ids_takes_the_same_round_trips_spec_sync_streams_4`). The split form puts only path keys in sections, one section per thing, so sections stay as in the clear form.
- **Validity of a part not held.** A part whose section has not arrived is judged from the header where the path is in the clear, and invalid where it is hashed and unknown; held-model judges it as the clear form does (`order::held_set_decides`, `order::judged_under_the_walks_side_after_every_ingest_spec_permissions_5_1`, `order::a_creation_pruned_under_a_later_delete_is_replaced_spec_commits_5`, `order::a_removal_from_an_actor_audits_what_the_actors_grants_reach_spec_permissions_5_1`, `closure::a_thing_once_in_the_node_closure_holds_as_one_never_in_it_spec_commits_5`, `flows::lockout_bundled_with_own_rotation_recovered_spec_perm_3`). In the split form the path is in the header and every holder of the thing holds its key (or reads it from the encryption it opened); these tests are the check, run with the split form forced on a wip commit.
- Not defects: `rotation::tests_custody::an_outsider_places_one_milliseconds_rotations_by_id_before_depth_spec_permissions_5_4` searches commit IDs, `thing_cost::` measure bytes, `validity::the_engine_states_one_path_for_every_thing_spec_wire_4` asserts the clear form.
