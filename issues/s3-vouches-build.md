## Questions a builder would otherwise guess
- **"Of its own"**: a node acts as its account; read "no vouch of its own counting" as authored by its account, so sibling nodes do not each write one once one counts. The node that wrote R is excluded, its siblings are not (the model has one node per account).
- **Which thing**: a vouch goes on each thing N where c has a role-class part; c's role-class parts on other things are checked too ("W could write c"), so one entry on any of them is as strong. The model has one thing.
- **Non-edge role-class parts** (a rotation of X, a `sync.0` write) are in the counting rule (the role their aspect needs) but not modelled: roles-model writes are edge writes only. perm reads them from `HeldSet::role_parts` (hand test `a_vouched_rotation_stands`).
- **Actor holders** (tess-call candidate): §5.1 says "c's author is X", X the holder R lowers. With an account or other actor as holder, its nodes' commits never count (perm follows the text: `a_vouch_counts_only_for_commits_of_the_removed_holder`). Probably wrong for accounts ("a node acts as its account"): decide whether c's author is X or reaches X (the nodes R demotes), then fix spec and the test.
- **"Lowering a grant"**: perm reads it as the model does, against the edge's value just before R (some component lower), not the edge as restored: an explicit write that audits only through a restored standing (after a self-removal or `until`) keys no counting vouch (`a_vouch_keyed_by_a_write_lowering_nothing_counts_for_nothing`). The spec should say which.
- **`demotion_scope`** appends R to the timeline (the second walk, its frontiers as listed) and judges no vouch again.
- **The candidate list** per (R, N) (X's commits received by R's `ts`, not in R's frontier) is fixed once R is applied: receipt times are recorded per commit (`flows/addenda.rs` reads `received`). Kept while R is held; growth is per removal per witness, as the vouches themselves.
- **The promised property**: spec/permissions.md §5.1, What vouches keep: the timeline equals the one with, beside the counting entries, every commit a witness other than R's writer and X received by R's `ts` and could write at the end of the first walk listed. Checked under `Rules::state`: 0 failures in `vouches-state` (1M) and `vouches-exh-state` with `witnessed` reduced to its end clause and the reference's extra joined with `judge`'s counted entries. The model's `kept` (equality with a reference that also lists what a witness could write as it applied R, and omits counted entries) fails in two random worlds under state writing; both fail only through that "as it applied R" clause, which the spec does not promise. The spec's form is roles-model's `kept_end` (checked under `Rules::state` in `check`) and perm's `vouch_kept_diff`.
- **Travel**: a vouch goes wherever its removal's required sets are (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) with its author's chain to its thing; the engine's certificate placement (`sync_ids/certs.rs`, `sync/certified.rs`) needs that rule, and the sync differential's oracle with it.
- **Cost of counting**: each entry asks "would W's write of c apply at a point" in the first walk, and survival scans later explicit lowerings of W: index explicit lowerings by holder; never re-judge all vouches on a write.
- **A vouch or frontier naming X's lowering of the remover voids the removal** (core/test/roles-model/README.md, Vouches, Limits: Listing). The answer is a writing rule, not a judging one: a remover and a witness name what they received in the order received, leaving out a role write with which their own walk would no longer apply the removal (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, The list). Not in the spec's Writing clause yet: it waits for roles-model `vouches` to hold it (notes/path-model-findings.md, To model next, 10). Build the engine's list building so that the test is one more filter over the candidates.
