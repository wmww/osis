## Left, in order
1. **The remover's leaving out** (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, The list): not in spec/permissions.md §5.1 Frontier; for stage A1, where the list becomes a leaf per thing. With today's writer rule it would change nothing reachable: the engine writes R only where its author holds the authority at R's key in its own walk, in which every received write it lists already stands, and a listed write is neither audited by R nor one of R's seeds; trusting R adds only R's seeds, unlisted writes, which leaving out cannot help (one corner: X's writes on a thing T reaches where X's class does not fall are seeds but not listed; if the remover's authority rests on one, R trusted applies with no list at all, and the per-entry rule would leave out everything). Build it in A1 with a test that reaches it.
2. **held-model and the order harness**: vouches and the gaps rule in held-model's role judgment, and an emphasis whose nodes write vouches and addenda (`order::under_test`, `local_peer_off`, `setup` turn both off), so `held_set_decides` compares them. Until then `held_set_decides` checks nothing of either.
3. **Travel as a body**: spec/permissions.md §4 sends a vouch wherever R goes, a body included; `sync_ids/certs.rs` adds vouches to certificate sets only. Where c matters to a sync ID S through a chain, R is on that chain (a lowering of the edge c's author's authority passes through) and travels as a certificate with its vouches. Left: R a body under S (on a thing of S's unit other than S's thing), c a body there too through a role part on a thing N2 of the unit, and the only vouch on a thing N1 outside it (c writes both; the witness holds N1, not N2): S's readers judge c's part on N2 unlisted while N1's readers keep it, until the vouch reaches them otherwise. Fix: with each role commit held as a body under S, its vouches as certificate copies.
4. **A late grant bringing a party into question**: `vouches::note_applied` makes a removal due again when a commit whose edge writes target a party filed under it (`Indexes::vouch_wake`), ordered no later than it, applies; tested only for a late-applying edit of the removed party (`an_edit_received_in_time_that_applies_late_is_vouched_for`). Wanted: a hand test with a holder thing X removed by R and a grant making Y a member of X, ordered before R, arriving after R at a witness that received Y's role write through X in time.
5. **Addenda against a rotation and admin-only content** (spec/permissions.md §5.5 reads "plain content (§5.2)"; §5.2 now lets addenda list admin-only content for a witness holding admin): the engine's epoch-rule addenda (`validity::plain_part`, `signing::answer_rotation`) still leave admin-only content out. Decide the reading and make §5.5 say it.
6. **Trigger cost**: issues/vouch-trigger-cost.md (non-applying removals and removals with candidates judged at every change of their component; `vouch_wake` per party upstream).
7. **roles-model's window**: `vouches.rs` (`derive_vouches`, `kept`) lists what a witness received by R's `t`, R possibly received earlier (`recv[r][wn] < rw.t`); the spec's window also ends at R's arrival. No comparison reads it (the differentials compare judging on given vouches; the order harness writes none), but `kept` checks a wider promise than the spec's: align it when held-model plays vouches (item 2).

Also open from the perm build:
- Counting is judged again at each update of a component holding a vouch keyed by a held removal: one pass over its history (the explicit lowerings) and its falls and demotions per update, no walk while the counting entries stay the same. Index the lowerings and falls by point with the walk when this shows in a count. `vouch::Held::same_parts` (whether a listed commit's role parts arrived) is the same shape: one lookup per commit listed by entries keyed by a held removal, per update of the component; a removed party's vouches keyed by a held removal add to both in proportion to what they list.
- **Duplication** (notes/growth.md, its vouches row): every witness writes its own vouch per (R, N). Writing only while no vouch at all (anyone's) counts for c would cut that to about one per (R, N), but another's vouch may stop counting (its voucher lowered) and each witness would then owe again on that change; not decided.

## Questions a builder would otherwise guess
- **"Of its own"**: a node acts as its account; read "no vouch of its own counting" as authored by its account, so sibling nodes do not each write one once one counts. The node that wrote R is excluded, its siblings are not (the model has one node per account).
- **Which thing**: a vouch counts only on a thing where c has a role-class part (spec §5.1), so it joins no component; c's role-class parts on other things are checked too ("W could write c"), so one entry on any of them is as strong. The model has one thing.
- **Non-edge role-class parts** (a rotation of X, a `sync.0` write) are in the counting rule (the role their aspect needs) but not modelled: roles-model writes are edge writes only. perm reads them from `HeldSet::role_parts` (hand test `a_vouched_rotation_stands`).
- **`demotion_scope`** appends R to the timeline (the second walk, its frontiers as listed) and judges no vouch again.
- **The candidate list** per (R, N) (X's commits in R's window, not in R's frontier) is fixed once R is held (a commit applying after makes R due again, `note_applied`): receipt times are recorded per commit (`flows/vouches.rs` reads `received`). Kept while R is held; growth is per removal per witness, as the vouches themselves.
- **The promised property**: spec/permissions.md §5.1, What vouches keep: the timeline equals the one with, beside the counting entries, every commit a witness other than R's writer and X received by R's `ts` and could write at the end of the first walk listed. Checked under `Rules::state`: 0 failures in `vouches-state` (1M) and `vouches-exh-state` with `witnessed` reduced to its end clause and the reference's extra joined with `judge`'s counted entries. The model's `kept` (equality with a reference that also lists what a witness could write as it applied R, and omits counted entries) fails in two random worlds under state writing; both fail only through that "as it applied R" clause, which the spec does not promise. The spec's form is roles-model's `kept_end` (checked under `Rules::state` in `check`) and perm's `vouch_kept_diff`.
- **Travel**: a vouch goes wherever its removal's required sets are (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) with its author's chain to its thing; the engine's certificate placement (`sync_ids/certs.rs`, `sync/certified.rs`) needs that rule, and the sync differential's oracle with it.
- **Cost of counting**: each entry asks "would W's write of c apply at a point" in the first walk, and survival scans later explicit lowerings of W: index explicit lowerings by holder; never re-judge all vouches on a write.
- **A vouch or frontier naming X's lowering of the remover voids the removal** (core/test/roles-model/README.md, Vouches, Limits: Listing). The answer is a writing rule, not a judging one: a remover and a witness name what they received in the order received, leaving out a role write with which their own walk would no longer apply the removal (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, The list). The witnesses' rule is spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches, Writing, and What vouches keep reads "and with which R still applies" (roles-model `vouches` world 48946 under `-state-stands`: the reference without it keeps what the witnesses rightly left out); the remover's is item 3.
