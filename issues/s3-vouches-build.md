## Left, in order
3. **The remover's leaving out** (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, The list): not in spec/permissions.md §5.1 Frontier; for stage A1, where the list becomes a leaf per thing. With today's writer rule it would change nothing reachable: the engine writes R only where its author holds the authority at R's key in its own walk, in which every received write it lists already stands, and a listed write is neither audited by R nor one of R's seeds; trusting R adds only R's seeds, unlisted writes, which leaving out cannot help (one corner: X's writes on a thing T reaches where X's class does not fall are seeds but not listed; if the remover's authority rests on one, R trusted applies with no list at all, and the per-entry rule would leave out everything). Build it in A1 with a test that reaches it.
4. **held-model**: vouches and the gaps rule in its role judgment. `order::held_set_decides*` compares role-write validity with held-model, but its nodes write neither addenda nor vouches (`order::under_test`, `local_peer_off`, `setup`), so it does not need them until an emphasis plays them; add both with that emphasis.
5. **A gap in the writing trigger**: a removal judged with no candidate is judged again only at an open, though its candidates may grow after while their receipts stay in R's window: a commit received before R that applies only after R was judged (pending on its author's chain meanwhile), or one that comes into question at R as a later-arriving commit ordered before R makes its author act through X (a grant naming a member of a holder X). Filing every removal under its component would re-judge each at every change of it (per write, per removal held). Wanted: perm reporting the removals whose in-question set changed, and the engine making due the removals of a component a commit received by their `ts` joins late.
6. **Travel as a body**: spec/permissions.md §4 sends a vouch wherever R goes, a body included; `sync_ids/certs.rs` adds vouches to certificate sets only. Where c matters to a sync ID S through a chain, R is on that chain (a lowering of the edge c's author's authority passes through) and travels as a certificate with its vouches. Left: R a body under S (on a thing of S's unit other than S's thing), c a body there too through a role part on a thing N2 of the unit, and the only vouch on a thing N1 outside it (c writes both; the witness holds N1, not N2): S's readers judge c's part on N2 unlisted while N1's readers keep it, until the vouch reaches them otherwise. Fix: with each role commit held as a body under S, its vouches as certificate copies.
7. **notes/addenda.md** Rules and growth rows to what is built after 1-2; issue closed.

## Questions a builder would otherwise guess
- **"Of its own"**: a node acts as its account; read "no vouch of its own counting" as authored by its account, so sibling nodes do not each write one once one counts. The node that wrote R is excluded, its siblings are not (the model has one node per account).
- **Which thing**: a vouch counts only on a thing where c has a role-class part (spec §5.1), so it joins no component; c's role-class parts on other things are checked too ("W could write c"), so one entry on any of them is as strong. The model has one thing.
- **Non-edge role-class parts** (a rotation of X, a `sync.0` write) are in the counting rule (the role their aspect needs) but not modelled: roles-model writes are edge writes only. perm reads them from `HeldSet::role_parts` (hand test `a_vouched_rotation_stands`).
- **`demotion_scope`** appends R to the timeline (the second walk, its frontiers as listed) and judges no vouch again.
- **The candidate list** per (R, N) (X's commits in R's window, not in R's frontier) is fixed once R is held (item 5 for commits applied after): receipt times are recorded per commit (`flows/vouches.rs` reads `received`). Kept while R is held; growth is per removal per witness, as the vouches themselves.
- **The promised property**: spec/permissions.md §5.1, What vouches keep: the timeline equals the one with, beside the counting entries, every commit a witness other than R's writer and X received by R's `ts` and could write at the end of the first walk listed. Checked under `Rules::state`: 0 failures in `vouches-state` (1M) and `vouches-exh-state` with `witnessed` reduced to its end clause and the reference's extra joined with `judge`'s counted entries. The model's `kept` (equality with a reference that also lists what a witness could write as it applied R, and omits counted entries) fails in two random worlds under state writing; both fail only through that "as it applied R" clause, which the spec does not promise. The spec's form is roles-model's `kept_end` (checked under `Rules::state` in `check`) and perm's `vouch_kept_diff`.
- **Travel**: a vouch goes wherever its removal's required sets are (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) with its author's chain to its thing; the engine's certificate placement (`sync_ids/certs.rs`, `sync/certified.rs`) needs that rule, and the sync differential's oracle with it.
- **Cost of counting**: each entry asks "would W's write of c apply at a point" in the first walk, and survival scans later explicit lowerings of W: index explicit lowerings by holder; never re-judge all vouches on a write.
- **A vouch or frontier naming X's lowering of the remover voids the removal** (core/test/roles-model/README.md, Vouches, Limits: Listing). The answer is a writing rule, not a judging one: a remover and a witness name what they received in the order received, leaving out a role write with which their own walk would no longer apply the removal (notes/account-membership-design.md §4, The list). The witnesses' rule is spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches, Writing, and What vouches keep reads "and with which R still applies" (roles-model `vouches` world 48946 under `-state-stands`: the reference without it keeps what the witnesses rightly left out); the remover's is item 3.
