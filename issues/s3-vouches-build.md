## Pieces, in order
Each ends with `cargo test -p <crate>` and clippy green for the crates it touches.
1. **Aspect**: `aspect vouches.0: map<blob, frontier>;` in schema/core.osis beside `addenda.0`; role-class (`core_types::is_role_class`), a role part sealed in the roles section (commit crate's section split), never compaction-snapshotted (the snapshot writer and the receiver's check), `put_new` entries only. Fixed retention follows from role-class.
2. **osis-perm, the two walks**: the first walk is today's walk; the counting rule per entry (R applies as an explicit lowering of X; c is X's, before R; W could write c at the vouch's position; survival against later explicit lowerings of W not listing the vouch); the second walk reads counting entries as frontier entries (audit, seeds, disputes). A component holding no counting entry walks once. Count, as a default test, walk steps per write at n and 10n vouches held in other components and in the same component with none counting: the work must follow the component's own vouches, never all vouches held (notes/principles.md, Check scaling).
3. **Differential**: in core/perm/tests/it/differential.rs a vouches run beside `edges_run`: `vouches::random_world(rng, Rules { state: true, ..FIXED })` and every k-th world of `vouches::exhaustive`, forged vouches included, each world mapped to one thing with a `vouches.0` commit per model vouch; compare applied bits, final authority and the trust sides (`trust_sets`) against `vouches::judge`. The controls `vouches: false` and an entry counted without the authority check must fail it.
4. **Addenda gaps in perm**: `Timeline::vouching` takes admin-only content (not role-class) vouched for by a writer holding admin at the addendum's key, against demotions below admin; flip `addenda_model_agrees` to `addenda::GAPS` worlds (`random_world_with(.., GAPS, true)`, the gaps family).
5. **held-model**: vouches and the gaps rule in its role judgment, so `held_set_decides` compares on them.
6. **Engine writing**, per (removal R, thing N), state-based for vouches and addenda alike (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Vouches, Writing; §5.2 Addenda, Writing): `flows/addenda.rs` goes from "answer each removal once" to "owed while the node's view shows a listable commit it could vouch for and none of its own counts"; vouches beside it in the same flow. Triggers: applying R, any applied role commit or trust-set change that can change the node's authority on N or a vouch's counting (re-check only the (R, N) whose inputs moved); the "cannot write yet" wait as today. Restart: the owed set is a function of the held set, receipts and trust set, so it is re-derived at open or kept as an index rebuilt from them. Sim or engine tests: a witness granted after R, a witness lowered by a node that never got its vouch writing again, a witness unaware of its own demotion (roles-model `a_witness_unaware_of_its_own_demotion_never_vouches_again`), each with its control.
7. **notes**: notes/addenda.md Rules and notes/growth.md rows to what is built; issue closed.

## Questions a builder would otherwise guess
- **"Of its own"**: a node acts as its account; read "no vouch of its own counting" as authored by its account, so sibling nodes do not each write one once one counts. The node that wrote R is excluded, its siblings are not (the model has one node per account).
- **Which thing**: a vouch goes on each thing N where c has a role-class part; c's role-class parts on other things are checked too ("W could write c"), so one entry on any of them is as strong. The model has one thing.
- **Non-edge role-class parts** (a rotation of X, a `sync.0` write) are in the counting rule (the role their aspect needs) but not modelled: roles-model writes are edge writes only. Hand tests in perm for a vouched rotation and its control before trusting it.
- **The candidate list** per (R, N) (X's commits received by R's `ts`, not in R's frontier) is fixed once R is applied: receipt times are recorded per commit (`flows/addenda.rs` reads `received`). Kept while R is held; growth is per removal per witness, as the vouches themselves.
- **The promised property**: spec/permissions.md §5.1, What vouches keep, promises keeping for what the witness could write at the end of the first walk. `vouches-state` fails in two of 1M random worlds against the model's stronger `kept` (which also asks "held the authority as it applied R"); one is traced to that clause, the other (one stays-made op, among expiring edges) is not. M2 brief 1 follow-up: rerun `vouches-state` with `witnessed` reduced to its end clause and trace the second world before piece 3 asserts on stays-made.
- **Travel**: a vouch goes wherever its removal's required sets are (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) with its author's chain to its thing; the engine's certificate placement (`sync_ids/certs.rs`, `sync/certified.rs`) needs that rule, and the sync differential's oracle with it.
- **Cost of counting**: each entry asks "would W's write of c apply at a point" in the first walk, and survival scans later explicit lowerings of W: index explicit lowerings by holder; never re-judge all vouches on a write.
