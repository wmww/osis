# Chat join, message and removal costs: the wire-format knobs left
> Summary: Engine waste and the implementation knobs are done: in a chat of 4 a join sends 45 KB, a message 2.2 KB, removing a link joiner 26 KB plus about 3 KB per remaining member (39 KB at 8, ≈ 0.6 MB at 200), and the other members only heartbeats. What remains is the wire format: every message pays its header three times (2.2 KB for a few bytes of text; fixable at the price of a second fetch per message), and every keyset carries one 1.2 KB post-quantum key per role and a removal seals two boxes per remaining member; merging those per actor breaks role separation as first proposed, two safe variants need an owner call.
> Tags: performance, sync, node, crypto, tests, owner-call

Repro: `cargo test --release -p chat -- --ignored costs_join_message_removal --nocapture` (tier 2, asserts the budgets below with margin; `OSIS_CHAT_N` sets the size). `OSIS_TRACE_UPLOADS=1` prints one line per entry sent (blob class, commit, aspects; `SyncEngine::trace`).

Chat of 16: removal 62 KB, others 7.8 KB.

Regression after the node-sync merge (join 48 KB, others during a removal 39 KB), fixed: (1) every member device of K read K's inbox and placed its own copy of K's grant on C with C's `/label` and `/created` (2.8 KB per join, then C's new `/label` after each rotation); an inbox copy is now skipped when entries already hold it, counted like chain certificates. (2) Each joiner's home label holds the copy of its grant by L (spec/sync.md §3), so L was an author seen there and each joiner placed L's revoke with the remover's whole chain (≈ 12 KB each) when a joiner was removed; revokes about an author now go only where an entry of that author is judged invalid or pending (spec/permissions.md §4: a revoke changes no verdict on writes its frontier covers). Kept as design: the grant's copy under the joiner's home with K's `/label` and `/created` (+3.8 KB per join, spec/sync.md §3), frontiers listing the remover's chain certificates by commit ID and snapshots' `Absorbed`/`Authors` (+0.6 KB per removal at 4). "Before" is the start of this round (after sync round 2 and flows round 2). What changed:
- Chain certificates per label, not per node (spec/permissions.md §4): a copy or closure blob of the same commit placed by any node counts; closure parts are never copied (a copy duplicating them was deleted as a strict subset); another node's fresh demotion or new `/keys` is left to its author for 30 s (`SETTLE_MS`), so members that learn of a rotation under another label first do not all copy it. Join 79 → 41 KB, others 68 → 1.1 KB, removal 85 → 70 KB. The regression after flows round 2 was this: joiners copied every other node's chain (the sharer's card, person and recovery key) under both labels, and every member re-placed K's and C's new `/keys` after each rotation.
- One rotation per link-joiner removal: `revoke_joiner` removes the joiner and L in one commit (spec/commits.md §2). 70 → 34 KB.
- The chat actor is no longer placed in the creator's home group (app choice, notes/api.md). 34 → 26 KB.

## Breakdown now (chat of 4, blob bytes; about 120 B of frame per entry on top)
- **Join 45 KB**: the joiner's chain certificates under K's and C's labels, 11.5 KB each (its node card 3.1 KB, its person's creation commit 8.4 KB: the person keyset 5.2 KB and the recovery key's 2.2 KB, mostly hybrid KEM keys of 1216 B per role); the grant on K 1.2 KB and its copy under C 0.5 KB; the persona's own secrets 5.4 KB, its grant on C 0.9 KB, its content commit 2.0 KB (3 projections); two inbox deposits 4.5 KB (to the joiner's own person, to C); the grant's copy under the joiner's home with K's `/label` and `/created` 3.8 KB.
- **Message 2.1 KB of blobs**: one commit, 3 projections (`/created`, text + sent time, the placement on the persona's `/group`), each repeating the 467 B header and the signature.
- **Removal 26 KB**: K's rotation with both removals 9.8 KB (2.7 KB per remaining member: a read and a write sealed box; `/keys` 2.6 KB; link and history boxes), its certificate copy under C 3.4 KB (spec/permissions.md §3), C's rotation 4.2 KB, the `/label` steps of K and C under their labels and cross-published under the home label 3.6 KB, inbox blob to the removed joiner 1.9 KB, registrations 0.9 KB, the persona leaving C's group 0.4 KB. Each further member adds ≈ 3 KB (boxes plus claim-set slots).
- **Others**: a heartbeat each (0.4 KB).

## Remaining knobs (wire format; not done, each needs a spec change)
- **Header once per commit**: projections carry the commit ID and their parts, the header and signature travel once. A message 2.5 → ≈ 1.4 KB, but a reader fetches two blobs per message instead of one (slower first display on a slow link). Or the app drops the placement and finds messages by owner (needs a query API): ≈ −0.7 KB.
- **One KEM key per keyset, one box per member for all roles: blocked by role separation** (owner call, touches spec/requirements.md). Today an actor publishes one 1216 B hybrid key per role and a member gets one 1.35 KB box per role. Collapsing either is unsafe as stated: a single key must be held by the actor's readers, so a team that is admin of a doc would hand its read-only members the doc's admin secret (delete tokens, label keys); binding role and epoch in the box AD stops replay, not this. One box sealed at the member's top role instead locks its lower-role members out. Analysis: notes/crypto.md (KEM key per role). Two variants keep separation:
  - **A. One ML-KEM key per actor, an X25519 key per role**: the ML-KEM half (held by all members, from the read secret) is shared, the X25519 half stays per role; one box per member with one ML-KEM ciphertext and a layer per role keyed by both halves. Keyset 3.6 → 1.3 KB, a read+write rotation's `/keys` 2.4 → 1.25 KB, a removal's per-member cost 2.7 → ≈ 1.35 KB (≈ 0.6 → 0.33 MB at 200 members), a join ≈ −7 KB. No behavior change. Cost: separation between an actor's roles becomes classical only: a lower-role member of A who keeps boxes sealed to A and later has a quantum computer opens A's write and admin boxes; it learns no content (it already reads A's), but gets those things' delete tokens, so it could delete their data at untrusted servers. Conflicts with "secrets that must stay secret for years resist a future quantum attacker" for delete power.
  - **B. Roles share a key only while their holders coincide**: A's rotator writes one keyset for several roles when every member of A holding the lower role holds the higher one (true of a person by convention, a persona, a link actor K); the key derives from the highest role's secret, so confidentiality never depends on it. Same savings in the chat. Costs: granting A a member below a shared range first rotates those roles apart, and that member reads things A belongs to only after each one's writer re-seals A (a new re-seal trigger, a delay the user sees); A's co-readers learn that A has no member in that role range (roster shape, not entries).
  - Recommendation: A if the owner accepts quantum-insider delete power as out of scope (signatures are already classical), else B, else keep per-role keys.
- **Claim sets**: padded to a power of two, 32 B per slot; 8 KB per step per label at 200. Minor.
- Not measured: downloads (each member fetches K's rotation once per label it syncs).
