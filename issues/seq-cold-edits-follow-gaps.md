# A cold op's runs redo their earlier work, so a few far-apart ops can pass the runs' budget
> Summary: engine pages: an op held in part runs again from scratch after each miss, so a commit missing at k places costs about k runs of its whole follow work; two ops whose run costs about half a pass (seed 13 step 147) or three far-apart ops pass the one-pass budget and load whole (`seq_cold_edits_keep_placement_plain`: "same delta as on the whole state", 1 of 160 seeds).
> Priority: normal
> Tags: tests, engine, crdt

`run_attached` (and the tests' `run_ops`) runs a commit's ops on the pages loaded, loads what the run missed and runs again, until a run misses nothing or the runs' work passes about one whole pass of the paged sequences (`pages::absorb`). A run stops at its first miss, so a commit missing at k places takes about k runs, and each run repeats the follow work of every op before its miss: the total grows about as k times one run's work, not as the work of what changed.

- Seed 13 (`OSIS_SEQ_COLD_START=13 OSIS_SEQ_COLD_SEEDS=1`, debugging aid only), step 147, an own two-op commit: runs of 46, 46, 1082, 1082, 2376 units against a budget of 4173; the engine loads whole, and the property compares the gave-up result.
- Constructed: `[Move 200 3 → 3900, Move 1500 2 → 2600, Insert 3300]` on a fresh 4000-char text: ten runs, the last two about 1000 and 1800 units, pass the budget. The same two-op shape passes (`seq_ops_far_apart_run_until_done_spec_crdt_6_1`, 11 runs).
- A single run's cost is itself high for a few ops: each placement of a run or move charges `order.len() + cap` (about 2√n) beside the rows it walks, and a move re-places the outside nodes hanging below its range too.

Directions, not yet weighed: keep what earlier runs found (load ahead the blocks, chunks and pages every op's indices need before the first run, as `attach_ops` does for an op's own index); or charge a run only for work past the previous run's miss. Raising the budget is no fix (any constant fails at some k).

Settled here, no longer failing: the op runs' count cap (bound by work, as the join's dry runs); a delete or move reading every block after its range (an op reads the rows of its range by block counts, `Placement::rows`; a range whose first node's dot-map chunk is not loaded is void, not read from position 0); cuts charged a block walk each where a row already starts (they charge the rows looked at); a received move closing a cycle, or any delta writing on a node of a broken cycle, lays the placement out anew (§6.2, by design: the property excuses exactly those steps, computed from the whole state's placement). The no-op-cut charge fix has no constructed reproducer: it needs a cold state whose page record is coarser than the stored rows (one range record over single positioned rows, seed 90011 step 29 `delete 472 4`: 34 cuts), which a short hand-built history did not produce.
