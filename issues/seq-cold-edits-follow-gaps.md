# Cold sequence edits still drop or rebuild work on some histories
> Summary: engine pages: `seq_cold_edits_keep_placement_plain` fails about 1 seed in 4 (seen at offset 90000: 10 of 40): op runs capped by count, a received move's anchor ending mid-row, cut and move work past the follow's budget, an own two-op commit missing every block.
> Priority: normal
> Tags: tests, engine, crdt

The property (engine `engine::pages`, `OSIS_SEQ_COLD_START`/`_SEEDS`) fails in four ways. Seeds below are debugging aids (replay with `OSIS_SEQ_COLD_SEEDS=1`), never tests: each fix needs a constructed hand test.

1. **Op runs capped by count** ("same delta as on the whole state", seeds 90005, 90008, 90014, 90015): an own two-op commit with a move (`insert 3114 3, move 1167 4 3603`; `move 1695 3 1363, move 2985 4 797`) still misses after `OP_RUNS` (8) runs: each run stops at its first miss. The engine then loads whole (`run_attached`, counted `paged op whole: runs`); the property's own loop compares the eighth run's void result. Same flaw as the join's prefetch had: a count of runs is no bound. Fix by the prefetch's rule (runs until one completes or their work passes about one whole pass), in `run_attached` and the tests' `for _ in 0..OP_RUNS` loops.
2. **Received move onto an anchor mid-row** ("placement kept", 90020 step 51 `move 110 5 1530, insert 3222 2`): no miss left, `place_moved` gives up because the destination anchor does not end its row (`ending` fails). Suspect: `Lin::moved` cuts only where the anchor is inside a *record* (`inside`), but a stored row can be a chain of several records (block flag 8), so an anchor ending its record mid-row gets no cut.
3. **Work past the follow's budget** ("placement kept", 90011 step 29 own `delete 472 4`: `cut_row` on a creation chain reshifts the rest of the chain, 4103 of 4037; 90004 step 63 received `move 575 2 1003, delete 3786 46`: each dry run of a move costs about 800, six runs pass the prefetch budget). Whether either is the documented whole-pass fallback (notes/engine-paged.md: "a move's follow finds the end of moved subtrees by depth, which after many moves can read most blocks") or a cost to cut is open.
4. **An own two-op commit misses every block** ("64 of 64 blocks loaded by one edit (no move)", 90012, 90013, 52045 step 143 `insert 1210 3, delete 3594 4`; "29 of 64", 90007): the fifth op run notes all stub blocks but one at once, so something in resolving the second op (`seq::Layered`?) walks every row.
