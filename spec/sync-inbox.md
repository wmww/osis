# Sync: inbox, open signals, deposits and links
> Summary: Area 5: an account's inbox and its three open signals, public key records, sealed fires (how anyone holding a thing's public key record fires an open signal), deposits and Deposit items, promotion and clearing, tickets on the inbox (ticket sync IDs, key changes), removal and stranded notices, contact cards, and links' first contact.

**Principle:** an inbox is a thing like any other; a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry, taken by any holder of the sync ID whose verification it passes (signature, budget), whoever offers it, and stamped only at a writer that judged it. A ticket grants nothing and makes nothing sync: only a strong in the node closure does.

## 1. Inbox
- Each account P has one **inbox** I: a thing with own keys whose first admin is P, created in the account's creation, read by P's nodes only (P holds it by its record edge, and no role above sync is granted on it; other accounts MAY hold sync on it, their nodes keeping it unread to store and forward), strongly referenced by P's account root D (spec/sync-set.md §1). P's `keys.0` names it (spec/crypto.md §3).
- I carries three open signals (spec/data-model.md §2, §9): `ticket@~0`, arriving tickets (§5), `removal@~0`, removal notices (§5), and `stranded@~0`, stranded notices (§5). How long their fires stay is I's own history setting (spec/commits.md §5 The setting: only an entry naming an open signal keeps its fires, the default never); the account's creation writes I's `retention^.0` with a `keep` entry for a duration of `INBOX_LIFETIME` (spec/wire.md §2) naming each of the three.
- The inbox of an actor with own keys that is not an account is the actor itself: it takes its open-signal fires (tickets, removal notices, stranded notices) on itself, as the same three signals, kept as its own history setting keeps them (none written by core: without one a fire is out of history at once, spec/commits.md §5); an account's go to its inbox. Key actors have no inbox: a node receives P's seeds only over a connection to a node of the account (pairing); a link key holds its sync ID from its link and claims the current step by its static entry (spec/sync-ids.md §3).
- **Public key record** of a thing T with own keys: its creation record and its current `keys.0` commits (spec/permissions.md §4). An account's **public record**: its creation record, its current `keys.0` commits and rotations (spec/permissions.md §5.5), and its inbox's public key record; it goes as certificates wherever the account's current `keys.0` commits go (spec/permissions.md §4), the profile's sync ID included (§5 Contact card).
- I's sync ID is synced, kept and served by the same rules as every sync ID: whoever keeps it (read or unread) carries the deposits for it.

## 2. Open signals
- An open (`@`) signal of thing T may be fired by anyone holding T's public key record (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Open fires). A node that may write T, or reads T, fires as it writes (a non-writer by deposit, spec/sync-judgement.md §1). Any other node fires by a **sealed fire**:
  - the fire is a commit by the sender's own account, acting for it, whose one delta is the fire, with its AK's default retention (`admin` for the inbox's), its delete keys those of T's `keys.0` keysets it holds (spec/crypto.md §7 Header list), its delta hash under T's dedup secret (spec/crypto.md §3: from T's creation record), and its prefix as any commit encryption's (spec/sync-ids.md §1);
  - its blob holds the commit and its author's chain certificates for its actor (spec/permissions.md §4: the account's record and its rotations to the epoch it signs at), sealed to the KEM key of T's newest read keyset the sender holds (bytes: spec/wire.md §9); its entry's wrap is filler;
  - it goes as a Deposit item under T's sync ID (§3), signed with the deposit key T's deposit seed derives (spec/sync-ids.md §2), within T's deposit budget.
- A sender that does not sync T, or cannot read T's current epoch, keeps the sealed fire outside any store while its **delivery** is pending: shown, never "sent", until a peer takes its Deposit item (answer 0, §3); then it ends. A fire to an account whose nodes the sender never meets stays pending.
  - A fire not yet taken by any peer is kept durably by its sender and survives a restart. If T's public keys change before a peer takes it, the sender seals it again (to the newest read keyset of T it then holds).
- Who fired is the commit's author, verified by its chain; a fire grants nothing. Blocking a sender is the app's.

## 3. Deposits
- **Deposit** (an Offer Deposit item, spec/wire.md §11.2: `[S, blob, wrap, sig]`, `sig` by S's head's deposit key): add one entry with the given wrap (filler for a sealed fire) and no stamp, flagged deposited.
- **Who takes it**: every node holding S, read or kept unread, by one rule: it takes a Deposit item whose signature verifies under its head's deposit key and whose blob is of the durable or ephemeral class, within S's deposit budget and its peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5), whatever the connection proved and whoever offers it. Answers: 0 taken (also when the blob is held or was dropped: nothing changes); refused for now (4 rate, 5 budget full, 12 cap); else 14 (a bad signature, an unknown sync ID, a relay-only blob), never the head.
  - An ephemeral deposit is held as an ephemeral entry, counting toward S's ephemeral cap and the deposit budget alike.
  - **Budget**: local policy, with a recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2), one per sync ID, shared by its durable and ephemeral deposits. An entry leaves the budget when stamped, deleted or expired, or with the node's restart for an ephemeral one.
  - A Deposit item for a sync ID the node will hold but does not yet (in its sync set, no head held yet: its grant or registration arriving) is refused for now (4); one for a sync ID it does not sync, 14.
- **Sending**: a deposit of a node's own write (sealed, wrapped and signed when made, judged valid first) is logged deposited in its own store, which readers pull, and goes as a Deposit item to each source of S that keeps S unread (it pulls stamped entries only) until that source answers it, also after the node lost read access to S's actor (spec/permissions.md §3). A reader's deposit of a body it holds (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) is offered again at each judgement finding that source lacks it.
  - A sealed fire goes as a Deposit item to each peer that may hold S: one where S is open, one where an account holding a role on S's thing as the sender judges it (for an inbox: its account) is a proven actor, or one whose latest filter shows S's deposit key (spec/sync-opens.md §5 Filter; a filter's false positive is answered 14); never to another peer.
  - One unanswered Deposit item per connection.
  - **Refused for now** is no answer: sent again after a back-off (local policy, bounded; one taken ends the back-off of the node's others at that peer and sync ID) until taken or moot.
  - **14**: signed again with the current deposit key and sent again where that changes the signature, else not sent on that connection again until the receiver's next filter arrives (what it holds changed).
  - **Moot**: a sealed fire once its delivery ends (§2).
- What a node must log under S and cannot stamp, it deposits: spec/sync-judgement.md §1.

## 4. Promotion and clearing
- **Promotion**: a writer that verifies a deposited commit S must hold stamps it in its own store (promotes it; a source keeping S unread pulls the stamp), only once its author's chain (spec/permissions.md §4) is under S, and only for parts whose author held write on S's actor at the commit's `ts`, or that are fires of an open signal (spec/permissions.md §5.2 Open fires), as the writer judges it.
  - It promotes only a deposit holding a delta S must hold that no other entry under S in its store holds stamped or as its own certificate copy: one stamped copy per delta, however many encryptions are deposited.
  - Or, by its bytes alone, a deposit that is no duplicate or strict subset of any one entry of the commit under S held stamped there as the writer judges blobs (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Duplicates: a section it cannot open or check is byte-identical to none of that entry's), while no entry of the commit held stamped there has each delta S must hold that it carries held by another such entry: one such promotion per commit while it stands. An unpromoted deposit stays on the deposit budget.
  - A delta S must hold here, valid at the writer: one its bodies carry (on a thing of its sync unit). No chain certificate is promoted: the author's chain of a commit promoted goes under S as the promoter's own certificate copies.
  - A promoter completes the chain it holds, logging the missing deltas as its own certificate copies with the promotion. The author's chain under S leaves out every `keys.0` but the author's own. A certificate copy is never deleted for an incomplete chain.
  - A record blob counts toward the chain only stamped or the promoter's own; a deposited record blob is never promoted (it waits for clearing).
  - A writer that holds neither the commit applied nor the chain leaves the deposit unpromoted. It never promotes an entry whose bytes it dropped or removed within its tombstone. A writer promotes its own deposits too.
  - **A sealed fire** is promoted in place: the writer opens it with the read secret of the keyset it is sealed to, verifies the commit's signature, its delta against the header's delta hash and the chain it carries, takes the chain's records and accounts' rotations as certificates with the commit, all or none (none of a fire that does not apply valid), and stamps the blob itself (same blob, filler wrap). One holding any delta but one fire of an open signal of S's thing is not promoted. Stamped, it is a blob of its commit under S like any other (spec/sync-judgement.md §2); S's readers open it as the writer did.
- **List blobs** a non-writer deposits; a writer promotes one once each thing list in it verifies against the root of a rotation it holds.
- **Clearing deposits**: a node holding S's delete key keeps at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` entries and `DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES` bytes, per source keeping S unread and in its own store, of the deposits there it can do nothing with: every deposited entry whose bytes it has received except one it opened as a commit it would promote. Each counts at its blob's size, whether or not the node still holds its bytes. Past either bound it deletes them, those with no prefix expiry first, then earliest to expire first.
- A cleared deposit is must-not-hold for the node that cleared it (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- Deposits are sized for clearing from bytes held under any sync ID.

## 5. Tickets and notices
- **Ticket** of thing T: its tid, sync ID and one secret of it, boxed to one actor (bytes, who issues one: spec/data-model.md §8 Tickets). It is a weak and works wherever the node holds it (spec/sync-set.md §4).
- **Delivery of a grant**: a node granting an actor a role on T fires a `ticket@~0` on the grantee's inbox (§1): `thing` a ticket for T boxed to the grantee, of the read secret for read and above, of the sync secret for sync. To an account it may not hold as a contact, `profile` too: a ticket of read for the granting account's profile, for which it first grants the grantee read on its profile.
  - A sealed fire of a ticket also carries `grant`: the role commit giving the ticket's epoch (a grant's, a key change's or a re-seal's) projected to its `roles.0`, `keys.0` and `secrets.0` parts on T, with its author's chain and their records, as a removal notice carries its commit. That commit is wrapped under an epoch it supersedes, which the holder may lack (spec/crypto.md §5 Who wraps under what): the holder takes it from the fire, each part only where judged valid, once the walk takes the ticket at a strong (Nothing before the strong) and while T has no own keys there.
  - Accepting writes a strong to T, and so D's ticket for T (spec/sync-set.md §1 The account's tickets). An account that wants to dial the granter writes a strong to the granter's profile too, whose ticket came with the fire.
- **Nothing before the strong**: until a strong in the node closure reaches T (spec/sync-set.md §2; §4 Accepting), nothing an arriving ticket for T names is fetched, dialed or applied: its secret opens nothing, its sync ID is not opened, its profile is not dialed for it.
- **Ticket sync IDs**: a ticket's sync ID is its issuer's word: to a node reading T until a `sync.0` it holds records it (spec/sync-ids.md §4 Owner); a node keeping T unread holds no `sync.0` of it, so to it always (spec/sync.md §3). Until then the node syncs the sync ID of every ticket for T it holds and can open, but one another thing owns here (a held `sync.0` records it, a destroyed thing keeps it, a followed link names it): a forged sync ID cuts no thing off its own.
  - **S's things**: a sync ID belongs to the sync set, not to one thing. S's things are every thing that owns it here (its owner by a held `sync.0`, else each thing whose held tickets name it); S is synced once for all of them, whatever their order, read while a read secret of one of them is held, else kept unread (spec/sync.md §3). What arrives under S is judged for each: an entry opens under a read secret of any of S's things and is S's body for each thing whose sync unit carries it; S opens toward a peer that may hold S for any of them, shows each one's credentials and dials each one's tickets' profiles. A rule acting on S's recorded path (signing, steps, stranding) acts on its owner by `sync.0` alone: a sync ID only tickets name records none. Nothing is tried in turn and nothing retried: a forged claim only adds a thing whose secrets open nothing there, until the genuine thing's own `sync.0` arrives and S is its alone.
  - A hinted open by a sync key holds only at a head of that key, whatever the peer answers.
- **Taking its secret**: a read secret from a ticket is held for reading only (spec/crypto.md §4 Reading-only secrets) until the node's roles on T give it (spec/permissions.md §3 No secret taken back); a node keeping T unread takes a sync secret by spec/sync.md §3 alone. The secret is held as its epoch's and establishes nothing.
- **An old ticket**: an old secret alone opens nothing. A reader that is behind catches up where a reading peer opens on its role (spec/sync-opens.md §5 ByRole).
  - **Key changes**: a node making a rotation of T fires, after it, a fresh ticket for T at the new epoch on the inbox of each holder of sync alone on T (the sync secret) and of each holder the rotation boxes that held no box at the read epoch it is wrapped under (a holder granted again after a gap: the read secret); a re-seal does so for the holder it boxes, where that holder held no box at a current read epoch.
  - Limit: a node starting from an old ticket (one no node of its account replaced in D, spec/sync-set.md §1: none took the fresh one within the inbox's history) reaches T only where it reaches a peer that reads T.
- **Removal notice**: a node writing an edge write that takes a holder of read on T below read (a removal or demotion, not a self-removal or finalize) fires a `removal@~0` on that holder's inbox: `commit` the edge write's commit (header and deltas) projected to its `roles.0` parts on T, `chain` its author's chain certificates for T, each projected to its chain deltas, `records` their creation records (as `CommitBlobPlaintext` and `creation` values, spec/wire.md §4, §3).
  - A node acts on one only while it reads T and holds T's roster: it verifies the commit (its signature, its deltas against the header's delta hashes), takes the chain's records, `roles.0` and `keys.0` deltas and accounts' rotations as certificates, and holds the commit's `roles.0` parts as a role commit of T, checked against the roster it holds like any (spec/permissions.md §5.1). Once that write applies in its timeline and takes the holder below read, the node has lost access (spec/permissions.md §3 Lost access): it releases T or keeps the retained copy the removal says (spec/permissions.md §3 Retain). Any other notice changes nothing.
- **Stranded notice**: a `stranded@~0` fire (spec/data-model.md §9) on the thing whose current epoch the sending node cannot read, fired by a stranded node with no writer of it connected (spec/permissions.md §4 Stranded), a sealed fire like any (§2); its commit expires `INBOX_LIFETIME` after it is made; a writer of the thing acts on it when its node takes it, whatever the thing's history setting, and judges the re-seal it names itself (spec/permissions.md §3 Re-seal).
- **Reaching a member**: whoever changes T's keys or removes a holder reaches that holder's inbox by the holder's public record (§1), which every fellow holder holds (spec/permissions.md §4).
- **Contact card**: a link (spec/permissions.md §7) with read role on an account's profile. The profile's sync ID carries its own aspects (the dial list among them, spec/sync-set.md §1) and the account's public record (§1): the records as a record blob, the `keys.0` commits and rotations as certificate copies. Importing a card redeems its link, which accepts the profile (spec/sync-set.md §4).

## 6. Links
- Link text (spec/wire.md §10): seed, target, K, K's sync ID, and as first-contact hints the sharer node's dialable key and relays and its configured addresses; no other addresses. `make_link` works on every node.
- **First contact**: the redeemer dials the link's peers (the sharer node, and the nodes it dials by configured address, such as one keeping K unread) and proves the link key; its credential of K is the link key's static entry, which every node holding K's roster or head claim set holds, so K opens with the sharer offline, at readers and at nodes keeping K unread alike. An accepted static Hit's answer carries the head's entry box, the path from genesis and a signature by the head's sync key, verified before opening (spec/sync-opens.md §5). A node holding K's finalize opens nothing by the link.
- A redeemer not joined within a time of its node's choosing (local policy) stops following the link: it forgets the link key and K's sync ID and dials the sharer no more; redeeming the link again starts afresh.
- The join's transaction writes strongs to K and the target in the redeemer's account root, and so a ticket for each in it (spec/sync-set.md §4 A redeem is an accept), so both stay held once the join is held and the link is unfollowed; the link's peers stay addresses to dial while the target is held (spec/permissions.md §7).
- A link's end is read only from its sharer's filter; a sharer node not syncing K reads as refused while the link may stand (K's finalize is the end that holds).
- **Finalize**: any sharer node holding the seed thing and admin on K finalizes once a complete pass of K's sync ID is done (spec/sync-status.md §1; spec/permissions.md §7). Two finalizers need no order: each finalize is an explicit removal of the link key, and removals of one grant compose.
