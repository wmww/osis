# Sync: inbox, external signals, deposits and links
> Summary: Area 5: external signals on any thing, a thing's card (its keys and inbox, published where others read them), public key records, sealed fires and sealed copies (how a party outside a thing reaches it), deposits and Deposit items, promotion and clearing, grants from outside, stranded notices, contact cards, and links' first contact.

**Principle:** an inbox is a thing like any other; a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry, taken by any holder of the sync ID whose verification it passes (signature, budget), whoever offers it, and stamped only at a writer that judged it. A message on an inbox grants nothing and makes nothing sync: only a strong in the node closure does, and a role is by the grants of the thing as synced (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own things).

## 1. Inbox and card
- Any thing may take the two external signals (spec/data-model.md §2, §9): `grant@~0`, grants from outside (§5), and `stranded@~0`, stranded notices (§5). Which of them it takes from parties holding no write on it, and how long their fires stay, is its own setting (spec/commits.md §5 The setting: only an entry naming an external signal enables it and keeps its fires, the default never); core writes none: until its admins enable a signal, a fire of it by a party holding no write on the thing is invalid (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires). A thing's **inbox** is where grants to it from outside go: at a node, the thing named by the card of it the node holds with the greatest `since` (ties by the inbox's tid), else itself.
- **Card** of a thing A: the value of A's card aspect `:<A>:card.0` (spec/data-model.md §9) on any thing C, written along a path through A (it counts only so, spec/permissions.md §5.2): A's inbox where it is another thing, and A's and the inbox's current `keys.0` commits with their evidence and records (spec/wire.md §9 Holder keys form). Who reads C reads A's card: on a thing the public reads, every party. A reader takes a card's commits as a receiver takes holder keys (spec/wire.md §9: only what is on A's or the inbox's authority chain, each kept only if judged valid). A party granting to A from outside seals to A's keysets and fires at the inbox by what the cards of A it holds carry (spec/permissions.md §2 Granting to a thing).
  - A is **externally grantable** while a card of A stands and its inbox enables `grant@~0`.
  - A card's `since` is the time its inbox was chosen: a card naming another inbox than A's inbox at its writer (a move) writes a `since` above that of every card of A its writer holds; any other write of it keeps the `since` of A's inbox at its writer. A delta putting a card whose `since` is later than `wall_ms` of its commit's `ts` is invalid; a writer whose move needs a later `since` stamps the commit at least there.
  - A node that may write C along a path through A, holding `keys.0` commits of A or its inbox, with their `keys.0` parts on them, newer than those a card of A on C carries, or a card of A with a greater `since` than that card's, writes that card again: A's inbox at the node, with its `since`, and A's and that inbox's current `keys.0` commits it holds. A card on a thing no node may write along a path through A stays as written.
- An account's inbox I (spec/permissions.md §8) is a thing of its own, minted with admin the account A, read by A's members and held by a strong from A's `holds.0`; making the account writes I's `retention^.0` with a `keep` entry for a duration of `INBOX_LIFETIME` (spec/wire.md §2) naming each signal, enabling both, and A's card naming I on A's profile. Other things MAY hold sync on I, their nodes keeping it unread to store and forward.
- **Public key record** of an independent thing T: its creation record and its current `keys.0` commits (spec/permissions.md §4), each with its evidence (spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit: the steps placing the keys it lists among them), as a card carries them. It goes as certificate copies wherever T's current `keys.0` commits go (spec/permissions.md §4), the sync ID of each thing T holds a role on included.
- An inbox's sync ID is synced, kept and served by the same rules as every sync ID: whoever keeps it (read or unread) carries the deposits for it.

## 2. External signals
- An external (`@`) signal of thing T that T's current setting enables may be fired by anyone holding T's public key record (from a card of a thing whose inbox T is, or as a fellow holder); one it does not, by T's writers alone (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires). A node that holds T and may write or read it fires as it writes (a non-writer by deposit, spec/sync-judgement.md §1), and only a fire T's current setting keeps and, by a non-writer, enables: one it does not is refused to its maker, naming the setting (spec/commits.md §5 Signals). Any other node fires by a **sealed fire** (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing; a sealed fire's sender is bound by no setting, its receivers judge it; one owed sealed stays sealed if its sender comes to read T and T's setting does not keep it):
  - the fire is a commit signed as the sender's node thing, stating a path to the thing it fires for (an own thing, or its account), whose one delta is the fire, with its AK's default purge-by (`admin` for the inbox's), its delete keys T's step keys in the `keys.0` it holds (spec/crypto.md §7 Header list), its delta hash under T's dedup secret (spec/crypto.md §3: from T's creation record), and its prefix as any commit encryption's (spec/sync-ids.md §1);
  - its blob holds the commit and its evidence (spec/permissions.md §4: its signer's record and its path's edge writes), sealed to T's read key in force in the `keys.0` it holds (spec/crypto.md §3: written by an admin with each step, so no writer of T chooses the key outsiders seal to); while none is in force (a read keyset of another epoch than the step keys' read key) or it carries no KEM key, its delivery stays pending (bytes: spec/wire.md §9); its entry's wrap is filler;
  - it goes as a Deposit item under T's sync ID (§3), signed with the deposit key T's deposit seed derives (spec/sync-ids.md §2), within T's deposit budget.
- A sender that does not sync T, or cannot read T's current epoch, keeps the sealed fire outside any store while its **delivery** is pending: shown, never "sent", until a peer takes its Deposit item (answer 0, §3); then it ends. A fire to a thing whose nodes the sender never meets stays pending. A fire on T pending once the sender holds nothing of T (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs) goes: no key of T is left to seal it to.
  - A fire not yet taken by any peer is kept durably by its sender and survives a restart. If T's read key in force changes before a peer takes it, the sender seals it again (to the one it then holds), offering what it sealed before no more; while none is in force it offers nothing.
- Who fired is the commit's signer, verified by its evidence; a fire grants nothing (a `grant@~0` fire carries an offer, §5). Blocking a sender is the app's.
- **Sealed copy**: an edge piece delivered from outside H (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece) goes as a sealed fire does, under H's sync ID, its payload a certificate copy on another thing with its evidence in place of a fire's commit (spec/wire.md §9), or, to a holder of sync alone, its sync copy alone (spec/wire.md §4). It is bound by no setting: a node reading H takes it as a sealed fire's commit is taken (§4), whole or not at all, and holds what it carries as any role commit (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs). It is never promoted: what it carries in H's key part (spec/permissions.md §4) H's writers log under H's sync ID as their own certificate copies. A notice (spec/permissions.md §4 Notices) carries a copy on the thing whose sync ID it is under, taken as a sealed copy is; it is logged as itself, never as an entry of its commit.
- A node keeping T unread cannot read T's setting: it takes a fire's Deposit item by signature and budget alone (§3), as any deposit. A fire T's writers judge invalid (a signal T does not enable) is never promoted (§4) and leaves the budget by clearing or at its expiry.

## 3. Deposits
- **Deposit** (an Offer Deposit item, spec/wire.md §11.2: `[S, blob, wrap, sig]`, `sig` by S's head's deposit key): add one entry with the given wrap (filler for a sealed fire) and no stamp, flagged deposited.
- **Who takes it**: every node holding S, read or kept unread, by one rule: it takes a Deposit item whose signature verifies under its head's deposit key and whose blob is of the durable or ephemeral class, within S's deposit budget and its peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5), whatever the connection proved and whoever offers it. Answers: 0 taken (also when the blob is held or was dropped: nothing changes); refused for now (4 rate, 5 budget full, 12 cap); else 14 (a bad signature, an unknown sync ID, a relay-only blob), never the head.
  - A node reading S opens a deposit it takes as any entry it takes (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Fill order), whether or not it writes S: a stamp arriving later for the blob it holds fetches nothing.
  - An ephemeral deposit is held as an ephemeral entry, counting toward S's ephemeral cap and the deposit budget alike.
  - **Budget**: local policy, with a recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2), one per sync ID, shared by its durable and ephemeral deposits. An entry leaves the budget when stamped, deleted or expired, or with the node's restart for an ephemeral one.
  - A Deposit item for a sync ID the node will hold but does not yet (in its sync set, no head held yet: its grant or registration arriving) is refused for now (4); one for a sync ID it does not sync, 14.
- **Sending**: a deposit of a node's own write (sealed, wrapped and signed when made, judged valid first) is logged deposited in its own store, which readers pull, and goes as a Deposit item to each source of S that keeps S unread (it pulls stamped entries only), never at a member being ended (spec/sync-opens.md §6), on each connection of that source once its digests agreed there (spec/sync-opens.md §3 Agreement), until that source takes it there (answers 0) or it is moot; also after the node lost read access to S's actor (spec/permissions.md §3). A reader's deposit of a body it holds (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) is offered again at each judgement finding that source lacks it.
  - A sealed fire goes as a Deposit item to each peer that may hold S: one where S is open, one that proved a thing holding a role on S's thing as the sender judges it (for an inbox: a holder of what names it), or one whose latest filter shows S's deposit key (spec/sync-opens.md §5 Filter; a filter's false positive is answered 14); never to another peer.
  - One unanswered Deposit item per connection.
  - **Refused for now** is no answer: sent again after a back-off (local policy, bounded; one taken ends the back-off of the node's others at that peer and sync ID) until taken or moot.
  - **14**: signed again with the current deposit key and sent again where that changes the signature, else not sent on that connection again until the receiver's next filter arrives (what it holds changed).
  - **Moot**: a sealed fire once its delivery ends (§2).
- What a node must log under S and cannot stamp, it deposits: spec/sync-judgement.md §1.

## 4. Promotion and clearing
- **Promotion**: a writer that verifies a deposited commit S must hold stamps it in its own store (promotes it; a source keeping S unread pulls the stamp), only once the commit's evidence for the parts S must hold (spec/permissions.md §4) is under S and a complete pass of S (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun after the deposit arrived and after what its verdict reads last moved has ended, and only for parts whose signer held write on S's actor at the commit's `ts`, or that are fires of an external signal valid as such (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires: enabled by the current setting), as the writer judges it.
  - It promotes only a deposit holding a delta S must hold that no other entry under S in its store holds stamped or as its own certificate copy: one stamped copy per delta, however many encryptions are deposited.
  - Or, by its bytes alone, a deposit that is no duplicate of any one entry of the commit under S held stamped there and loses to none as needed (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Per blob) as the writer judges blobs (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Duplicates: a section it cannot open or check is byte-identical to none of that entry's), while no entry of the commit held stamped there has each delta S must hold that it carries held by another such entry: one such promotion per commit while it stands. An unpromoted deposit stays on the deposit budget.
  - A delta S must hold here, valid at the writer: one its bodies carry (on a thing of its sync unit). No certificate copy is promoted, a sealed copy included (§2): the evidence of a commit promoted goes under S as the promoter's own copies.
  - A promoter completes the evidence it holds, logging what S lacks of it as its own certificate copies with the promotion. A certificate copy is never deleted for incomplete evidence.
  - A record blob counts toward the evidence only stamped or the promoter's own; a deposited record blob is never promoted (it waits for clearing).
  - A writer that holds neither the commit applied nor its evidence leaves the deposit unpromoted. It never promotes an entry whose bytes it dropped or removed within its tombstone. A writer promotes its own deposits too.
  - **A sealed fire** is promoted in place: the writer opens it with the read secret of the keyset it is sealed to, verifies the commit's signature, its delta against the header's delta hash and the evidence it carries, takes its records and its evidence with the commit and the blobs it carries (spec/wire.md §9), all or none (none of a fire that applies invalid or cannot be judged; one whose `ts` is ahead of the clock waits as any commit does, spec/commits.md §4, and is taken for what it carries, §5, once it applies valid), and stamps the blob itself (same blob, filler wrap). One holding any delta but one fire of an external signal of S's thing is not promoted. Stamped, it is a blob of its commit under S like any other (spec/sync-judgement.md §2); S's readers open it as the writer did.
- **Clearing deposits**: a node holding S's delete key keeps at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` entries and `DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES` bytes, per source keeping S unread and in its own store, of the deposits there it can do nothing with: every deposited entry whose bytes it has received except one it opened as a commit it would promote. Each counts at its blob's size, whether or not the node still holds its bytes. Past either bound it deletes them, those with no prefix expiry first, then earliest to expire first. A sealed copy to a holder of sync alone whose sync copy it logs under S as its own (§2) it deletes at once.
- A cleared deposit is must-not-hold for the node that cleared it (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- Deposits are sized for clearing from bytes held under any sync ID.

## 5. Grants from outside and notices
- **Grant from outside**: a node writing an edge write naming holder H, where it may not write H (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece; one leaving H sync alone goes as sealed copies, §2), fires `grant@~0` on the inbox a card of H it holds names (§1): H's edge piece on the target from that commit, as a sealed copy carries one (spec/wire.md §9): `grant` the role commit's certificate copy on its target, with its evidence, their records and the blobs their deltas link.
  - It is an **offer**: what H's nodes need to reference the target and start syncing it (the thing, its sync ID, H's boxes), each held only as the copy and its evidence judge it: anyone holding a card of H may fire, and a box no evidence judges gives no secret. It proves nothing and gives no role: the grant is the target's own data (spec/permissions.md §2), and once a node syncs the target its role is by the target's grants (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own things), whatever becomes of the fire. Until then the node judges by the copy the offer carries, as it holds it.
  - It is held while the current setting of H's inbox enables and keeps `grant@~0` (§1: `INBOX_LIFETIME` on an account's inbox), whoever fired it; what it carries is held with it (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs) and goes with it unless the node syncs the target. Disabling the signal ends the offers held and breaks no grant, role, key or sync step.
  - A removal from outside goes as a grant does, kept as long (a holder of sync alone's goes nowhere: the step past its keys tells it, spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece): H's nodes are opened the target's sync ID no more but ended (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Departed) and learn their removal by it, or from the target's role entries any reader of it serves them up to their departure (spec/sync-opens.md §6). A grant's fire offered again after its edge ended meets the removal, the later write, wherever a node still holds the edge; past the keep of both, neither is taken (spec/commits.md §5 Signals).
  - A grant by a node that may write H needs no fire: it is logged under H's sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece).
  - **By hand**: the granting node MAY instead hand the offer, with its trust records on the target (spec/permissions.md §5.1 Sides), to a node of H over a channel their users provide, and then owes no fire; the taking node takes each piece as a carried commit (below) and holds the target by a strong (spec/permissions.md §8 Pairing is one such). The offer names the granting node's peers as a link does (spec/wire.md §10 `peer`): addresses the taking node dials while it holds the target, as a joined link's (§6). An offer to a holder of sync alone is its sync copies alone (the grant's and those of the target's `sync.0` writes held, spec/wire.md §4): no header, evidence or trust record; the taker takes them as sync copies of H, which give it nothing until one counts (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates).
- **Carried commits**: a commit a `grant@~0` fire carries is taken as an arriving role commit of its target, with the evidence and records it carries, each part only where judged valid and pending on what it lacks (spec/commits.md §4), by every node holding the fire valid; it is in H's key part while held (spec/permissions.md §4).
- **Stranded notice**: a `stranded@~0` fire (spec/data-model.md §9) on the thing whose current epoch the sending node cannot read, fired by a stranded node with no writer of it connected (spec/permissions.md §4 Stranded), a sealed fire like any (§2); its commit expires `INBOX_LIFETIME` after it is made; a writer of the thing acts on it when its node takes it valid (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires), however long the thing's setting keeps it, and judges the re-seal it names itself (spec/permissions.md §3 Re-seal).
- **Reaching a holder**: whoever fires a grant from outside reaches the holder's inbox by a card of the holder (§1), or the holder itself by its public key record; whoever delivers a sealed copy reaches the holder's own sync ID by its public key record, which every fellow holder of the target holds (spec/permissions.md §4).
- **Contact card**: a public link (spec/wire.md §10) to a thing's **profile**: a thing the public reads, minted with admin the thing it presents (an account's, spec/permissions.md §8), holding that thing's card (§1) and its `dial.0` (spec/sync-set.md §1). Importing a card redeems its link, which writes a strong to the profile (spec/sync-set.md §4); every sync unit holding that strong carries the profile whole (spec/sync-set.md §2 T's keys open).

## 6. Links
- Link text (spec/wire.md §10): seed, target, K, K's sync ID, and as first-contact hints the sharer node's dialable key and relays and its configured addresses; no other addresses. `make_link` works on every node.
- **First contact**: the redeemer dials the link's peers (the sharer node, and the nodes it dials by configured address, such as one keeping K unread) and proves the link key; its credential of K is the link key's static entry, which every node holding K's roster or head claim set holds. An accepted static Hit's answer carries the head's entry box, the path from genesis and a signature by the head's sync key, verified before opening (spec/sync-opens.md §5). A node holding K's finalize opens nothing by the link.
- A redeemer not joined within a time of its node's choosing (local policy) stops following the link: it forgets the link key and K's sync ID and dials the sharer no more; redeeming the link again starts afresh.
- The join's transaction writes strongs to K and the target (spec/sync-set.md §4); the link's peers stay addresses to dial while the target is held (spec/permissions.md §7).
- A link's end is read only from its sharer's filter; a sharer node not syncing K reads as refused while the link may stand (K's finalize is the end that holds).
- **Finalize**: any sharer node holding the seed thing and admin on K finalizes once a complete pass of K's sync ID is done (spec/sync-status.md §1; spec/permissions.md §7).
