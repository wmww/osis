# Sync: inbox, deposits and invites
> Summary: Area 5: an actor's inbox, inbox blobs (what they carry, re-send, stamping in place, hints, taking them), deposits and Deposit items, promotion and clearing, grant copies and contact cards, and links' first contact.

**Principle:** an inbox is a sync ID like any other; a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry, taken by any holder of the sync ID whose verification it passes (signature, budget), whoever offers it. A deposited entry becomes a stamped one only at a writer that judged it.

## 1. Inbox
- An actor with role secrets names its home sync ID in `keys.0` as its **inbox**, with the deposit seed, in the commit that gives it the sync ID. A sender uses the inbox the actor's `keys.0` names; a sender holding none cannot reach it.
- An account's inbox is its D's home sync ID (spec/permissions.md §1): every node of the account reads it. Other key actors have none: a node receives its grant on D only over a connection to a node of the account (pairing); a link key holds its sync ID from its link and claims the current step by its static entry (spec/sync-ids.md §3).
- The inbox is synced, kept and served by the same rules as every sync ID: whoever keeps it (read or unread) carries the deposits for it. An inbox this node reads is logged here whatever its payloads name.

## 2. Inbox blobs
- **Inbox blob** (spec/crypto.md §5; layout and payload spec/wire.md §9): the parts about actor G of a role commit, boxed to G, with the sync IDs of the things it names that have sync IDs of their own.
  - **What it carries about G**, any of: a grant with G's boxes (a sync grant: G's box of the sync secret, the payload naming the thing's sync IDs, so G derives their sync keys); a change or removal of G's grant of read or above (G holding sync alone acts on its boxes alone, spec/sync-judgement.md §6, and is sent none); a re-seal of G; a rotation boxing G where G held no box at the read epoch before it.
  - To a recipient holding less than read on a thing, it carries no author's chain or `keys.0`. A lowering or removal of G's grant carries the commit's `roles.0` parts; any other, to a recipient holding less than read on every thing it names (sync alone, or removed since), those things' `secrets.0` parts and the sync IDs only.
  - The sending node's dialable key and URLs ride as a hint (spec/wire.md §9 position 4), for dialing only.
  - **Logging**: a writer of the inbox logs it stamped in its own store; any other sender logs it deposited in its own store and offers it as a Deposit item (§3). The inbox thing's admins may delete it earlier than its expiry.
  - **Re-send**: its prefix expiry is `INBOX_LIFETIME`, after which the sender re-sends, and sooner once the sync ID it went under is no longer the recipient's inbox (`keys.0`), until the sender has seen the recipient take it: a commit by the recipient writing a thing the blob names or a thing whose record chain (spec/permissions.md §1) reaches such a thing, stamped after the blob's commit and opened here under a read epoch current here of that thing (a commit sealed under an older epoch may predate the take and shows nothing), or an open of a sync ID the blob names at this node on a connection where the recipient is a proven actor (J judges it a holder, spec/sync-opens.md §4) by a credential the blob's grant gives: to a recipient reading the thing here, a read secret at a current epoch of it (an open by an older secret shows nothing of a grant again after a removal); to a holder of sync alone, a store credential. A sender that reads the inbox it logged the blob under stamped has taken it itself (it holds the commit): the delivery ends once logged, and the inbox's other readers and sources get the blob by syncing the inbox, and its grant later by the sender's grant copy (§5). One it logged deposited ends only as any other. A sender MAY bound the number of re-sends.
  - **Stamping in place**: a writer of the inbox's thing stamps a deposited inbox blob in place when either its commit is applied, valid in every part the blob carries, and about an actor whose inbox the sync ID is; or its user declined it (an invite request, below; spec/sync-set.md §4). It does not when another blob of that commit under the sync ID is stamped, or the blob's prefix expiry is more than `INBOX_LIFETIME + 2 × MAX_DRIFT` ahead of its wall clock. A blob so stamped leaves the deposit budget and stays readable until it expires. Every other inbox blob stays deposited until it expires or is cleared (§4).
  - **Following a payload sync ID**: the payload's sync IDs are the sender's word. A recipient follows (opens) a payload sync ID only while the blob is unexpired and its commit is applied, or held unjudged, and not judged invalid; for a thing it knows no sync ID of, whose read or sync secret the commit gave it (a box of it that opens); for a sync ID no `sync.0` it has applied records; at most one per blob. A sync box gives no read hint.
- **Sync ID hints**: the sync ID an inbox blob names is its sender's word until a `sync.0` the node holds records it.
  - Blobs naming one sync ID under different things are tried in turn: under the first thing not failed since the sync ID's last retry, each retried after `resync_ms`.
  - A hinted open by a sync key holds only at a head of that key, whatever the peer answers.
  - What a connection delivers under a sync ID is read under the thing the sync ID is opened under on that connection; elsewhere a sync ID belongs to the thing that owns it by a held `sync.0`.
  - A hint ends once the node syncs that sync ID under the hint's thing.
- **Taking** an inbox blob: a recipient node applies the commit of an opened inbox blob, and follows what it names, only once it takes it:
  - the commit is already held (applied, or an entry of it held; a sibling node that accepted it logs its copy under home); or
  - its sender is **connected**: the header's author is a key actor whose creation record the node holds and under whose key (an account's: at the epoch it names, with that epoch's chain held) the commit's signature verifies, and the author has an effective role now (spec/permissions.md §1, as this node judges it) on a thing in the node's sync set, on a thing up such a thing's record chain, or on an account whose contact card the node imported; or
  - the user accepted it (spec/sync-set.md §4).
  - A blob not taken is an **invite request**: its commit is not applied, its chain certificates are not ingested, and nothing it names is fetched, followed or dialed. It is judged again as the node's state changes.
- **Delivery** is pending, shown, until a node of the recipient takes it, never "sent"; a grant to an account whose nodes the sender never meets stays pending.

## 3. Deposits
- **Deposit** (an Offer Deposit item, spec/wire.md §11.2: `[S, blob, wrap, sig]`, `sig` by S's head's deposit key): add one entry with the given wrap (filler for an inbox blob) and no stamp, flagged deposited.
- **Who takes it**: every node holding S, read or kept unread, by one rule: it takes a Deposit item whose signature verifies under its head's deposit key and whose blob is of the durable or ephemeral class, within S's deposit budget and its peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5), whatever the connection proved and whoever offers it. Answers: 0 taken (also when the blob is held or was dropped: nothing changes); refused for now (4 rate, 5 budget full, 12 cap); else 14 (a bad signature, an unknown sync ID, a relay-only blob), never the head.
  - An ephemeral deposit is held as an ephemeral entry, counting toward S's ephemeral cap and the deposit budget alike.
  - **Budget**: local policy, with a recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2), one per sync ID, shared by its durable and ephemeral deposits. An entry leaves the budget when stamped, deleted or expired, or with the node's restart for an ephemeral one.
  - A Deposit item for a sync ID the node will hold but does not yet (in its sync set, no head held yet: its grant or registration arriving) is refused for now (4); one for a sync ID it does not sync, 14.
- **Sending**: a deposit of a node's own write (sealed, wrapped and signed when made, judged valid first) is logged deposited in its own store, which readers pull, and goes as a Deposit item to each source of S that keeps S unread (it pulls stamped entries only) until that source answers it, also after the node lost read access to S's actor (spec/permissions.md §3). A reader's deposit of a body it holds (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) is offered again at each judgement finding that source lacks it.
  - An inbox blob a node logged deposited goes as a Deposit item to each peer that may hold the inbox: one where S is open, one where the recipient is a proven actor, or one whose latest filter shows S's deposit key (spec/sync-opens.md §5 Filter; a filter's false positive is answered 14); never to another peer.
  - One unanswered Deposit item per connection.
  - **Refused for now** is no answer: sent again after a back-off (local policy, bounded; one taken ends the back-off of the node's others at that peer and sync ID) until taken or moot.
  - **14**: signed again with the current deposit key and sent again where that changes the signature, else not sent on that connection again until the receiver's next filter arrives (what it holds changed).
  - **Moot**: an inbox blob once its delivery is forgotten or sealed again.
- What a node must log under S and cannot stamp, it deposits: spec/sync-judgement.md §1.

## 4. Promotion and clearing
- **Promotion**: a writer that verifies a deposited commit S must hold stamps it in its own store (promotes it; a source keeping S unread pulls the stamp), only once its author's chain for the deposited deltas (spec/permissions.md §4) is under S, and only for parts whose author held write on S's actor at the commit's `ts` (as the writer judges it).
  - It promotes only a deposit holding a delta S must hold that no other entry under S in its store holds stamped or as its own certificate copy: one stamped copy per delta, however many encryptions are deposited.
  - A delta S must hold here, valid at the writer: one its bodies carry (on a thing of its closure its actor may read); an edge write or box naming as holder a thing of the closure its actor may read; a key actor's own `keys.0`, `node.0` or `reach.0` delta in the newest valid commit of its own on that aspect. No other chain certificate is promoted: it goes under S as the promoter's own certificate copy, in the author's chain of a commit promoted.
  - A promoter completes the chain it holds, logging the missing deltas as its own certificate copies with the promotion. The author's chain under S leaves out every `keys.0` but the author's own. A certificate copy is never deleted for an incomplete chain.
  - A record blob counts toward the chain only stamped or the promoter's own; a deposited record blob is never promoted (it waits for clearing).
  - A writer that holds neither the commit applied nor the chain leaves the deposit unpromoted. It never promotes an entry whose bytes it dropped or removed within its tombstone. A writer promotes its own deposits too.
- **List blobs** a non-writer deposits; a writer promotes one once each thing list in it verifies against the root of a rotation it holds.
- **Clearing deposits**: a node holding S's delete key keeps at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` entries and `DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES` bytes, per source keeping S unread and in its own store, of the deposits there it can do nothing with: every deposited entry whose bytes it has received except one it opened as a commit it would promote and an inbox blob it would stamp (§2); opened invite requests count, and go last. Each counts at its blob's size, whether or not the node still holds its bytes. Past either bound it deletes them, in this order:
  1. all but opened invite requests with a prefix expiry within that bound, those with no expiry or a later one before the rest, then earliest to expire first;
  2. then those requests, earliest to expire first.
- A cleared inbox blob is must-not-hold for the node that cleared it (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- Deposits are sized for clearing from bytes held under any sync ID.

## 5. Grant copies and contact cards
- **Grant copy**: a grantee node that takes a grant for an actor (from the inbox, from K's sync ID, by pairing, or as its sender, §2 Re-send) logs a certificate copy of the grant, with the `sync.0` commits of the sync IDs it reached and those things' records (a record blob, spec/wire.md §3), under that actor's home sync ID; not when an entry there already holds the grant's parts, counted as for chain certificates (spec/permissions.md §4).
  - From an inbox blob only a node with admin on the actor copies, and only, of a valid applied role commit: the carried parts on the things where a `roles.0` delta writes the grant of the actor or a `secrets.0` delta boxes it; with the `created.0` commit and every `sync.0` commit of those things that the payload names (no `sync.0` commit of a thing the actor reads no more); and the author's chain of each (spec/permissions.md §4), without any `keys.0` but its author's own; and nothing from a blob whose expiry is less than `INBOX_LIFETIME` / 2 away.
  - Nothing else an inbox blob carries is logged. A node runs this whenever it opens an inbox blob, its commit held or not.
- **Contact card**: a link (spec/permissions.md §7) with read role to an account's profile thing P, a group of itself with no items: its sync ID carries P's own aspects (the dial list among them, spec/sync-set.md §1), the account's record (a record blob) and, as certificate copies, its `keys.0` commits and rotations (spec/permissions.md §5.5).

## 6. Links
- Link text (spec/wire.md §10): seed, target, K, K's sync ID, and the sharer node's dialable key and relays as first-contact hints; no other addresses. `make_link` works on every node.
- **First contact**: the redeemer dials the sharer node by the link's key and proves the link key; its credential of K is the link key's static entry, which every node holding K's roster or head claim set holds, so K opens with the sharer offline, at readers and at nodes keeping K unread alike. An accepted static Hit's answer carries the head's entry box, the path from genesis and a signature by the head's sync key, verified before opening (spec/sync-opens.md §5). A node holding K's finalize opens nothing by the link.
- A link's end is read only from its sharer's filter; a sharer node not syncing K reads as refused while the link may stand (K's finalize is the end that holds).
- **Finalize**: any sharer node holding the seed thing and admin on K finalizes once a complete pass of K's sync ID is done (spec/sync-status.md §1; spec/permissions.md §7). Two finalizers need no order: each finalize is an explicit removal of the link key, and removals of one grant compose.
