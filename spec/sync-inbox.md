# Sync: inbox, external signals, deposits and links
> Summary: Area 5: an account's inbox and its two external signals, public key records, sealed fires and sealed copies (how a party outside a thing reaches it), deposits and Deposit items, promotion and clearing, grants from outside, stranded notices, contact cards, and links' first contact.

**Principle:** an inbox is a thing like any other; a deposit is the one way a non-writer adds an entry, taken by any holder of the sync ID whose verification it passes (signature, budget), whoever offers it, and stamped only at a writer that judged it. A message on an inbox grants nothing and makes nothing sync: only a strong in the node closure does, and a role is by the grants of the thing as synced (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own actors).

## 1. Inbox
- Each account P has one **inbox** I: an independent thing whose mint admin is P, created in the account's creation, read by P's nodes only (P holds it by its record edge, and no role above sync is granted on it; other accounts MAY hold sync on it, their nodes keeping it unread to store and forward), strongly referenced by P's account root D (spec/sync-set.md §1). P's `keys.0` names it (spec/crypto.md §3).
- I carries two external signals (spec/data-model.md §2, §9): `grant@~0`, grants from outside (§5), and `stranded@~0`, stranded notices (§5). Which of them I takes from parties holding no write on it, and how long their fires stay, is I's own setting (spec/commits.md §5 The setting: only an entry naming an external signal enables it and keeps its fires, the default never); the account's creation writes I's `retention^.0` with a `keep` entry for a duration of `INBOX_LIFETIME` (spec/wire.md §2) naming each, enabling both.
- The inbox of an independent thing that is not an account is the thing itself: it takes its external-signal fires (grants from outside, stranded notices) on itself, as the same two signals, each only once its own setting enables it and kept as that setting keeps them (core writes none: until its admins enable a signal, a fire of it by a party holding no write on the thing is invalid, spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires). An account's go to its inbox. Key actors have no inbox: a node receives P's seeds only over a connection to a node of the account (pairing); a link key holds its sync ID from its link and claims the current step by its static entry (spec/sync-ids.md §3). An account's inbox stands for the account's own sync ID for what reaches the account from outside (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part, Interim).
- **Public key record** of an independent thing T: its creation record and its current `keys.0` commits (spec/permissions.md §4). An account's **public record**: its creation record, its current `keys.0` commits and rotations (spec/permissions.md §5.5), and its inbox's public key record; it goes as certificate copies wherever the account's current `keys.0` commits go (spec/permissions.md §4), the profile's sync ID included (§5 Contact card).
- I's sync ID is synced, kept and served by the same rules as every sync ID: whoever keeps it (read or unread) carries the deposits for it.

## 2. External signals
- An external (`@`) signal of thing T that T's current setting enables may be fired by anyone holding T's public key record; one it does not, by T's writers alone (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires). A node that holds T and may write or read it fires as it writes (a non-writer by deposit, spec/sync-judgement.md §1), and only a fire T's current setting keeps and, by a non-writer, enables: one it does not is refused to its maker, naming the setting (spec/commits.md §5 Signals). Any other node fires by a **sealed fire** (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing; a sealed fire's sender is bound by no setting, its receivers judge it; one owed sealed stays sealed if its sender comes to read T and T's setting does not keep it):
  - the fire is a commit by the sender's own account, acting for it, whose one delta is the fire, with its AK's default purge-by (`admin` for the inbox's), its delete keys those of T's `keys.0` keysets it holds (spec/crypto.md §7 Header list), its delta hash under T's dedup secret (spec/crypto.md §3: from T's creation record), and its prefix as any commit encryption's (spec/sync-ids.md §1);
  - its blob holds the commit and its evidence (spec/permissions.md §4: its signer's record, and an account signer's rotations to the epoch it signs at), sealed to the KEM key of T's newest read keyset the sender holds (bytes: spec/wire.md §9); its entry's wrap is filler;
  - it goes as a Deposit item under T's sync ID (§3), signed with the deposit key T's deposit seed derives (spec/sync-ids.md §2), within T's deposit budget.
- A sender that does not sync T, or cannot read T's current epoch, keeps the sealed fire outside any store while its **delivery** is pending: shown, never "sent", until a peer takes its Deposit item (answer 0, §3); then it ends. A fire to an account whose nodes the sender never meets stays pending. A fire on T pending once the sender holds nothing of T (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs) goes: no keyset of T is left to seal it to.
  - A fire not yet taken by any peer is kept durably by its sender and survives a restart. If T's public keys change before a peer takes it, the sender seals it again (to the newest read keyset of T it then holds).
- Who fired is the commit's signer, verified by its evidence; a fire grants nothing (a `grant@~0` fire carries an offer, §5). Blocking a sender is the app's.
- **Sealed copy**: an edge piece delivered from outside H (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece) goes as a sealed fire does, under H's sync ID (an account's: its inbox's), its payload a certificate copy and its evidence in place of a fire's commit (spec/wire.md §9). It is bound by no setting: H's writers promote it iff it is in H's key part.
- A node keeping T unread cannot read T's setting: it takes a fire's Deposit item by signature and budget alone (§3), as any deposit. A fire T's writers judge invalid (a signal T does not enable) is never promoted (§4) and leaves the budget by clearing or at its expiry.

## 3. Deposits
- **Deposit** (an Offer Deposit item, spec/wire.md §11.2: `[S, blob, wrap, sig]`, `sig` by S's head's deposit key): add one entry with the given wrap (filler for a sealed fire) and no stamp, flagged deposited.
- **Who takes it**: every node holding S, read or kept unread, by one rule: it takes a Deposit item whose signature verifies under its head's deposit key and whose blob is of the durable or ephemeral class, within S's deposit budget and its peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5), whatever the connection proved and whoever offers it. Answers: 0 taken (also when the blob is held or was dropped: nothing changes); refused for now (4 rate, 5 budget full, 12 cap); else 14 (a bad signature, an unknown sync ID, a relay-only blob), never the head.
  - A node reading S opens a deposit it takes as any entry it takes (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Fill order), whether or not it writes S: a stamp arriving later for the blob it holds fetches nothing.
  - An ephemeral deposit is held as an ephemeral entry, counting toward S's ephemeral cap and the deposit budget alike.
  - **Budget**: local policy, with a recommended minimum (spec/wire.md §2), one per sync ID, shared by its durable and ephemeral deposits. An entry leaves the budget when stamped, deleted or expired, or with the node's restart for an ephemeral one.
  - A Deposit item for a sync ID the node will hold but does not yet (in its sync set, no head held yet: its grant or registration arriving) is refused for now (4); one for a sync ID it does not sync, 14.
- **Sending**: a deposit of a node's own write (sealed, wrapped and signed when made, judged valid first) is logged deposited in its own store, which readers pull, and goes as a Deposit item to each source of S that keeps S unread (it pulls stamped entries only), never at a member being ended (spec/sync-opens.md §6), on each connection of that source once its digests agreed there (spec/sync-opens.md §3 Agreement), until that source takes it there (answers 0) or it is moot; also after the node lost read access to S's actor (spec/permissions.md §3). A reader's deposit of a body it holds (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) is offered again at each judgement finding that source lacks it.
  - A sealed fire goes as a Deposit item to each peer that may hold S: one where S is open, one that proved an account holding a role on S's thing as the sender judges it (for an inbox: its account), or one whose latest filter shows S's deposit key (spec/sync-opens.md §5 Filter; a filter's false positive is answered 14); never to another peer.
  - One unanswered Deposit item per connection.
  - **Refused for now** is no answer: sent again after a back-off (local policy, bounded; one taken ends the back-off of the node's others at that peer and sync ID) until taken or moot.
  - **14**: signed again with the current deposit key and sent again where that changes the signature, else not sent on that connection again until the receiver's next filter arrives (what it holds changed).
  - **Moot**: a sealed fire once its delivery ends (§2).
- What a node must log under S and cannot stamp, it deposits: spec/sync-judgement.md §1.

## 4. Promotion and clearing
- **Promotion**: a writer that verifies a deposited commit S must hold stamps it in its own store (promotes it; a source keeping S unread pulls the stamp), only once the commit's evidence for the parts S must hold (spec/permissions.md §4) is under S and a complete pass of S (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun after the deposit arrived and after what its verdict reads last moved has ended, and only for parts whose signer held write on S's actor at the commit's `ts`, or that are fires of an external signal valid as such (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires: enabled by the current setting), as the writer judges it.
  - It promotes only a deposit holding a delta S must hold that no other entry under S in its store holds stamped or as its own certificate copy: one stamped copy per delta, however many encryptions are deposited.
  - Or, by its bytes alone, a deposit that is no duplicate of any one entry of the commit under S held stamped there and loses to none as needed (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Per blob) as the writer judges blobs (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Duplicates: a section it cannot open or check is byte-identical to none of that entry's), while no entry of the commit held stamped there has each delta S must hold that it carries held by another such entry: one such promotion per commit while it stands. An unpromoted deposit stays on the deposit budget.
  - A delta S must hold here, valid at the writer: one its bodies carry (on a thing of its sync unit). No certificate copy is promoted but a sealed copy (below): the evidence of a commit promoted goes under S as the promoter's own copies.
  - A promoter completes the evidence it holds, logging what S lacks of it as its own certificate copies with the promotion. A certificate copy is never deleted for incomplete evidence.
  - A record blob counts toward the evidence only stamped or the promoter's own; a deposited record blob is never promoted (it waits for clearing).
  - A writer that holds neither the commit applied nor its evidence leaves the deposit unpromoted. It never promotes an entry whose bytes it dropped or removed within its tombstone. A writer promotes its own deposits too.
  - **A sealed fire** is promoted in place: the writer opens it with the read secret of the keyset it is sealed to, verifies the commit's signature, its delta against the header's delta hash and the evidence it carries, takes its records and accounts' rotations as evidence with the commit and the blobs it carries (spec/wire.md §9), all or none (none of a fire that applies invalid or cannot be judged; one whose `ts` is ahead of the clock waits as any commit does, spec/commits.md §4, and is taken for what it carries, §5, once it applies valid), and stamps the blob itself (same blob, filler wrap). One holding any delta but one fire of an external signal of S's thing is not promoted. Stamped, it is a blob of its commit under S like any other (spec/sync-judgement.md §2); S's readers open it as the writer did.
  - **A sealed copy** is promoted in place likewise, iff its copy is in the key part of S's actor (spec/permissions.md §4), its evidence taken with it.
- **List blobs** a non-writer deposits; a writer promotes one once each thing list in it verifies against the root of a rotation it holds.
- **Clearing deposits**: a node holding S's delete key keeps at most `DEPOSITS_WAITING` entries and `DEPOSITS_WAITING_BYTES` bytes, per source keeping S unread and in its own store, of the deposits there it can do nothing with: every deposited entry whose bytes it has received except one it opened as a commit it would promote. Each counts at its blob's size, whether or not the node still holds its bytes. Past either bound it deletes them, those with no prefix expiry first, then earliest to expire first.
- A cleared deposit is must-not-hold for the node that cleared it (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- Deposits are sized for clearing from bytes held under any sync ID.

## 5. Grants from outside and notices
- **Grant from outside**: a node writing an edge write naming holder H, where it may not write H (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece), fires `grant@~0` on H's inbox (§1): `grant` the role commit's certificate copy on its target, with its evidence and their records. To an account it may not hold as a contact, it first grants that account read on its own account's profile, a grant from outside like any.
  - It is an **offer**: what H's nodes need to reference the target and start syncing it (the thing, its sync ID, H's boxes). It proves nothing and gives no role: the grant is the target's own data (spec/permissions.md §2), and once a node syncs the target its role is by the target's grants (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own actors), whatever becomes of the fire. Until then the node judges by the copy the offer carries, as it holds it.
  - It is held while H's current setting enables and keeps `grant@~0` (§1: `INBOX_LIFETIME` on an account's inbox), whoever fired it; what it carries is held with it (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs) and goes with it unless the node syncs the target. Disabling the signal ends the offers held and breaks no grant, role, key or sync step.
  - A removal from outside goes as a grant does, kept as long: H's nodes are opened the target's sync ID no more (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Departed) and learn their removal by it. A grant's fire offered again after its edge ended meets the removal, the later write, wherever a node still holds the edge; past the keep of both, neither is taken (spec/commits.md §5 Signals).
  - A grant by a node that may write H needs no fire: it is logged under H's sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4 Delivering an edge piece).
- **Carried commits**: a commit a `grant@~0` fire carries is taken as an arriving role commit of its target, with the evidence and records it carries, each part only where judged valid and pending on what it lacks (spec/commits.md §4), by every node holding the fire valid; it is in H's key part while held (spec/permissions.md §4).
- **Stranded notice**: a `stranded@~0` fire (spec/data-model.md §9) on the thing whose current epoch the sending node cannot read, fired by a stranded node with no writer of it connected (spec/permissions.md §4 Stranded), a sealed fire like any (§2); its commit expires `INBOX_LIFETIME` after it is made; a writer of the thing acts on it when its node takes it valid (spec/permissions.md §5.2 External fires), however long the thing's setting keeps it, and judges the re-seal it names itself (spec/permissions.md §3 Re-seal).
- **Reaching a holder**: whoever fires a grant from outside or delivers a sealed copy reaches the holder's inbox by the holder's public record (§1), which every fellow holder of the target holds (spec/permissions.md §4).
- **Contact card**: a link (spec/permissions.md §7) with read role on an account's profile. The profile's sync ID carries its own aspects (the dial list among them, spec/sync-set.md §1) and the account's public record (§1): the records as a record blob, the `keys.0` commits and rotations as certificate copies. Importing a card redeems its link, which writes a strong to the profile (spec/sync-set.md §4).

## 6. Links
- Link text (spec/wire.md §10): seed, target, K, K's sync ID, and as first-contact hints the sharer node's dialable key and relays and its configured addresses; no other addresses. `make_link` works on every node.
- **First contact**: the redeemer dials the link's peers (the sharer node, and the nodes it dials by configured address, such as one keeping K unread) and proves the link key; its credential of K is the link key's static entry, which every node holding K's roster or head claim set holds. An accepted static Hit's answer carries the head's entry box, the path from genesis and a signature by the head's sync key, verified before opening (spec/sync-opens.md §5). A node holding K's finalize opens nothing by the link.
- A redeemer not joined within a time of its node's choosing (local policy) stops following the link: it forgets the link key and K's sync ID and dials the sharer no more; redeeming the link again starts afresh.
- The join's transaction writes strongs to K and the target (spec/sync-set.md §4); the link's peers stay addresses to dial while the target is held (spec/permissions.md §7).
- A link's end is read only from its sharer's filter; a sharer node not syncing K reads as refused while the link may stand (K's finalize is the end that holds).
- **Finalize**: any sharer node holding the seed thing and admin on K finalizes once a complete pass of K's sync ID is done (spec/sync-status.md §1; spec/permissions.md §7).
