# Permissions
> Summary: The rules of holders, signing keys, stated paths, role grants and mint admins (a thing minted with a key, a node's own things), role secrets and rotation, evidence, validity (role timeline, content), purge rights, links, and accounts as an arrangement of ordinary things (making, pairing, removing a node).

## 1. Concepts
- **Terms**:
  - Rights are **roles** (sync < read < write < admin) given by **role grants** (**grants**; "edge" in rule text means a grant, a key's among them, or a record edge; a **hop** of a path is one of its edges, the key hop first, below). A thing with a role on a thing is a **holder** of that role on it.
  - **Sync** gives the sync secret (§3) and nothing to read: its holder derives the thing's sync key (spec/sync-ids.md §2), so it keeps and serves the thing's sync IDs unread (spec/sync.md §3), and opens no section, roster or content of it.
  - A **role commit** (spec/commits.md §2) carries writes of **role-class aspects** (spec/data-model.md §9).
  - The **role timeline** (§5.1) orders edge writes (§2).
  - A strong (spec/data-model.md §8) grants no right on its target, and removing one revokes none.
  - A value in a CRDT set is an **element**.
- **Signing key** (a thing's **key**): a public signing key holding a grant on a thing, by which it signs as that thing. It is a value: it has no tid, aspect or roster, and nothing is sealed to it.
  - **Key grant**: a grant whose holder is a key, stored in the thing's `signers.0` (spec/data-model.md §9), read by the thing's readers; the role timeline (§5.1) reads it as it reads any grant. Its value is a role (admin, write, read or sync), an optional `until` and the may-grant flag (§2). A thing's creation record names a mint admin or holds one key (spec/wire.md §3); the **record's key** holds admin on the thing from before every edge write, as the mint admin does by the record edge, until an edge write changes it.
  - **Signed as**: every commit names the thing S it is signed as and is signed by one key k (spec/wire.md §4 field 1). A commit acts only as S and the things its paths list (Path).
  - **Key hop**: the edge (k, S): k's grant on S, or the record's key's admin. It is an edge like any: granted, changed, lowered and removed by edge writes (§2), walked and judged as any (§5), giving what its value gives.
  - No kind of thing signs: a thing signed as holds roles, takes grants, has dependents and is listed on paths like any thing.
- **The public**: a holder that is no thing, standing for every party: its grant on a thing gives every party that role there. It is named by the **zero tid** (15 zero bytes, which no creation record hashes to) wherever a holder is named (`roles.0`, box keys). It has no record, aspects, roster, key, sync ID, inbox or card: nothing signs as it, no path lists it, nothing holds a role on it, and it is no holder actor (spec/crypto.md §4 Holder actors). Its boxes are of the open form (spec/crypto.md §4 Boxes) and its entries' claims are computed by anyone, their W in clear (spec/sync-ids.md §3): whoever names the target's sync ID opens it by the public's claim at the head while the public is boxed there, and, once the public is removed, by the sync proof of an epoch the public held, served up to its end (spec/sync-opens.md §4). Its open boxes are in every stub of the target (§4 Copies, a stub's cut). Every other rule reads its grant as any grant: edge writes on it, its departures and the rotations they force are ordinary.
- **Path**: for each thing T it writes, a commit states the things [A1, …, Ak] it writes T along, 0 ≤ k ≤ `MAX_PATH_THINGS`, none of them S and none twice (spec/wire.md §4 field 2: read off the commit's one `Path`, or T's entry of a `Split`).
  - Its **members** for T are [S, A1, …, Ak, T]; [S] where T is S and nothing is listed (S acting as itself); for a self-removal (§2) [S, A1, …, Ak], ending at the holder of the grant it lowers, and none for a key lowering its own grant. A part whose members name a thing twice, or whose marks are invalid (spec/wire.md §4 field 2), applies nowhere and is invalid.
  - Its **hops**: the commit's key hop (k, S), then one per pair of consecutive members p, q: a **descent** where p is on q's record chain (Record chain, below) and the path does not mark the hop direct (spec/wire.md §4 `marks`), the record edges from p down to q in order; else their **direct edge**, p's grant on q (q's record edge where p is q's mint admin). The path's **edges** are the edges of those hops in order, the key hop first; its **last edge** is the last of them (the key hop where S acts as itself).
  - A path **gives** role r at a point where every edge of it, the key hop among them, gives at least r there (its value in the role timeline, §5.1, each `until` judged at the part's `ts`). A path with no member (a key lowering its own grant) gives admin.
  - **Direct mark** (spec/wire.md §4 `marks`): a mark says the hop uses p's direct edge on q where p is on q's record chain two or more record edges above it; a mark on any other hop makes the part invalid, so each list of edges has one statement. A marked hop where p holds no direct edge on q gives nothing.
  - Nothing but the stated path judges a part (§5): another path that would also give the role does not.
  - A node's **own things** are the things its keys hold grants on: its **node thing** (spec/sync-set.md §1) and any other (a link key it redeems by, §7). It signs only as them. Every role and authority this spec gives "a node" is its own things' (their effective roles, below).
  - Organizations, bots, shared identities and accounts (§8) are ordinary things with rosters; a thing holding a role on one acts through it by listing it on its path.
  - A **link's thing** K (§7) is a thing its creation record marks as a link's.
- **Mint admin**: a creation record names the thing's **mint admin**, fixed by the tid (spec/wire.md §3), and the thing is **minted with admin** that one; or it holds a key and names none.
  - **Record edge**: the record gives the mint admin an admin edge on the thing from before every edge write (the walk's initial state, §5.1; not an edge write: it applies by the tid alone and puts nothing in question). It stores nothing; edge writes change or remove it like any edge, a removal resetting no entry of it (§2). The mint admin is history: once its edge is gone it has nothing on the thing but what grants give it.
  - The **record chain** of a thing: its mint admin, and so on up to a thing whose record names none, within `MAX_CHAIN_DEPTH` links. A delta written on a thing further down (judged from the creation records alone) is invalid, its `created.0` excepted; the rest of its commit is judged as usual.
- **Standing admin**: a holder of an admin edge on the thing itself with no `until`, the record edge and a key's grant included while they stand, whatever the holder. A timed admin edge is no standing admin; neither is admin through another path.
- **The last admin**: a node MUST NOT sign a role commit that, in its own walk with the commit applied, leaves a thing that had a standing admin with none, unless the commit carries the adminless mark (§2). This covers every way an edge write can do so, on a dependent or an independent thing: removing or lowering the last standing admin's edge (below admin, or giving it an `until`), an admin's self-removal (§2), and a removal whose audit (§5.1) drops the edge writes that made the last standing admins. A thing's destruction (spec/commits.md §5) and an `until` passing are no edge writes and are not covered.
  - It is the writer's check, not a validity rule: a reader judges a role commit alike with or without the mark. A commit that leaves a thing with no standing admin applies as any other: one signed by a node that skips the check, or two admins' self-removals made concurrently, each with the other standing in its author's view.
  - A dependent thing left so stays dependent: its content stays readable through derivation by the mint admin's holders.
- **With no admin left** (no admin edge on it, timed ones included), nobody changes a thing's grants or its admin-only aspects but by may-grant grants, self-removals and finalizes below admin (§2); holders keep reading and writing as granted, writers still rotate the roles at or below write (§3), and nobody may destroy it by request (§6): only its record's `expires` ends it.
  - A record needs no authority. A node names as mint admin only a thing its own things hold write on (themselves included). A thing may be created by a commit carrying its `created.0` alone (a role commit, spec/commits.md §2).
- **Role grant**: a record (holder, target, role), stored in the target's `roles.0`, on the target; what it reaches beyond follows from paths.
- **Effective role** of a thing X on a thing at time t: the maximum over paths from X to the thing of the minimum role along the path, evaluated with the timeline (§5.1) at t. A path climbs role grants, record edges included while they stand; a thing holds admin on itself. Key grants are no part of it: a key holds nothing a secret is sealed to. It decides keys (§3), sync units and holders; validity reads stated paths only (§5).
- **Admin** = write + change role grants + purge (§6) + write admin-only (`^`) aspects (spec/data-model.md §2; §5.2). An admin grant on a dependent thing grants changing its role grants and purge on that thing.
- Admins are equal and may remove each other, the mint admin included, and a key holding admin among them.
- An edge write whose holder is its target is not applied (§5.1).
- The role-class aspects of a thing (spec/data-model.md §9) are written only by its admins, whatever other role a node holds, except may-grant grants, self-removals and finalizes (§2), and what rotations and re-seals write into `secrets.0` and `keys.0` (§3).
- Ephemeral writes (spec/data-model.md §2) need the role the durable write would need.

## 2. Role grant writes
- **Edge writes**: granting, changing and removing a grant are writes to the target's `roles.0`, or for a key's grant its `signers.0`, each in its own role commit (spec/commits.md §2), along a path giving admin on the target (§1 Path), but a self-removal, a finalize and a may-grant grant (below). An edge write that is none of those three is **explicit**.
  - Every edge write resets the holder's **current entry**: the entry created by the edge write in force on that grant in its writer's walk (§5.1: the last applied write on it, when it put an entry), whatever the `roles.0` state holds of it.
  - A removal of a holder with no current entry (a record edge, §1, or a grant already removed) resets an entry of its own delta: a record keyed by the holder at a fresh counter of the delta, holding the reset alone (spec/crdt.md §5.6). Its creation is that delta, so it is never waited for. A removal of an absent grant is an edge write too.
  - A grant or change also writes a new entry holding the whole grant (spec/crdt.md §5.6 `put_new`).
  - A `roles.0` delta writing inside an entry it does not create is bad input (judged from the delta and its header: the entry's dot is outside the delta's own dots).
  - A reset names the write it ends: its entry's creation. A role commit with a removal (a write leaving the holder no grant for keys: none, or `until` 0) resetting an entry whose creation the reader does not hold applies none of its edge writes until that creation is held; a commit's edge writes apply together or not at all. A reset beside a put of a live grant for its key decides nothing (the put sets the grant) and is not waited for. One whose creation the reader never gets never applies there. A reset carrying another key than its entry's creation removes no grant (spec/crdt.md §5.6).
- A grant may carry `until: timestamp`: the grant counts as removed from `wall_ms(t) >= until`. Such a grant carries `retain`: whether its `until` passing says retain (§3, Retain), as its writer wants, else as the thing's default when written (`retain` of its current `retention^.0` setting, spec/commits.md §5 In force); a reader reads `retain` only beside an `until`.
- A grant **without history** carries `floor`: the n of the read rotation its commit makes (the cut, spec/crypto.md §4 Floors); its holder reads the current state (snapshots, spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut) and is handed no commit written before it. A change of the grant keeps its `floor` unless its writer shows history (a grant with history, at its author's floor); a grant without history is written by an admin of the target, to a holder holding no role on it.
- **An `until` passing** is a loss (§3) the node judges by its own clock, with no role commit behind it. A node whose read access only an `until` ended (walked with every grant's time ahead, it has a role):
  - First hears out each sync ID of the thing it reads (as §3, When it learns it). For each, one of these holds:
    - it has a source, each of its own commits with an entry under it is held elsewhere by a source's report (spec/sync-status.md §2), and a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun since it found the role gone ended; or
    - no peer is to be waited for on it (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for).
  - Meanwhile its writes from before the `until` go out (a peer whose head is past its sync key serves it nothing past that step's end, spec/sync-opens.md §4), and a renewal held by then keeps its role.
  - Then it drops.
  - It drops anyway `LOST_HEARD_WAIT_MS` after it first found the role gone.
  - A role commit received beside the ended grant that does not itself end the role changes nothing of this. A removal, demotion or a grant written with `until` 0 (below) ends it at once; a finalize (below) counts as the `until` it finalizes.
- **Finalize**: a removal of another holder's grant carrying no frontier that is not a self-removal (below). It applies (§5.1) iff the grant is past its `until` at the removal's `ts` (as the timeline orders it: a renewal ordered before it counts, and the finalize then ends a superseded write and applies nothing) and its path gives write on the target and a role there at least the grant's (a timed admin grant needs admin); else it applies nothing, whoever wrote it.
  - It is no explicit write: it carries no frontier, puts nothing in question, gets no verdict and leaves the hop as restored as it was (§5.1), so the removed holder's own timestamps still decide its writes (§5.1, §5.2).
  - It is the grant's departure for keys (spec/crypto.md §4) and carries its rotation (§3) like any removal; one finalize may remove several ended grants of one target (spec/commits.md §2).
  - It says retain or erase as its `until` would (§3, Retain: the grant's `retain`).
  - A node with write on thing T holding a grant on T it may finalize, past its `until` by its own clock, MUST write the finalize before its next commit encrypting under T other than by sealing a box to T (spec/crypto.md §4), and boxes that holder nothing meanwhile (a grant renewing it excepted).
- A removal says **retain or erase** (§3, Retain).
  - Its plain entry reset says erase.
  - Its entry reset with the grant written anew with `until` 0 (role read, no may-grant flag), gone at every clock, says retain, and is a removal all the same (§5.1), in one commit with its rotation. A client writes `until` 0 for nothing else.
  - It says what its remover wants, or else what the thing's default says: `retain` of its current `retention^.0` setting (spec/commits.md §5 In force), none erasing.
  - A self-removal says what its author wants, whatever the default.
  - A receiver reads what the removal says, never the default.
  - A may-grant grant gone when written does not apply (§5.1).
- A grant carries a **may-grant flag** (`may_grant`): whether its holder may add new holders up to the grant's role. A `roles.0` delta putting a value that reads as no grant is bad by itself (spec/commits.md §4), and so applies nothing, its entry resets included.
- **Path's may-grant**: a path gives may-grant on T at role r at a point iff every edge of it, the key hop among them, gives at least r there, and its last edge gives admin there (a record edge among them) or carries the flag. A path giving may-grant at admin gives admin.
- **May-grant grant**: an edge write whose path does not give admin on T applies as a may-grant grant iff it adds a grant for a holder (a thing, a key or the public) with no current grant on T (an expired grant counts as none), its value giving a role at its `ts`, and its path gives may-grant at that role. The new grant may carry the flag. It carries no frontier, puts nothing in question, gets no verdict and is never trusted (§5.1), and carries the grantee's boxes for the roles it gives (a key's grant: none). Over an expired grant still in the state at a higher role it is that grant's departure for keys, which carries its rotation (§3): a client writes it only once the grant is finalized.
  - Its `secrets.0` aspect is one delta holding only new keyed entries (puts, spec/crdt.md §5.6: each entry's dot is created by that delta): boxes keyed to its grantees at or below each one's granted role and history boxes at or below the highest granted role, with no reset of the value, a field, a map or any entry; it writes no `keys.0`. This is judged from the commit alone.
  - The exemption holds only where the commit puts a grant on that thing and every such put applies as a may-grant grant (§5.1; another write of the commit applying, such as a self-removal, gives none).
  - A `secrets.0` delta that breaks this, or a `keys.0` delta, gets no may-grant exemption and is judged like any such delta (the highest role it rotates, boxes or records, at least write, §3, §5.2).
  - The grant's write is judged on its own (§5.1).
  - Holders by may-grant cannot change or remove any other edge.
- **Self-removal**: an edge write on grant (X, T) whose path ends at X: X is its last listed thing, or the thing it is signed as where it lists none. A write on key k's grant on S in a commit signed by k as S that states no thing for S is a self-removal too, its path empty. It applies iff its path gives admin on X (a thing leaving by itself: by its key hop alone; a key: whatever its grant gives) and it **lowers**: removes the grant, or sets no component of role, may-grant flag and `until` above the grant's value and no `floor` below it (an unchanged rewrite and a removal of an absent grant lower); one that does not lower applies nothing. A lowering of another holder's grant whose path ends at the target is explicit.
- A self-removal needs no authority on the target, carries no rotation (§3) and no frontier, and puts nothing in question (§5.1); it rests on the grant it lowers as on its path.
- **Key grants** (§1) are written by edge writes like any grant, with these differences only:
  - the entry is in `signers.0`, keyed by the key; its value carries no `retain` and no `floor`, and a removal is the plain entry reset (the `until` 0 form is bad input): nothing is sealed to a key, so the write carries no boxes and no rotation (§3), and says neither retain nor erase;
  - a lowering of key k's grant on S carries a frontier like any (§5.1 Frontier): the commits k signed as S.
- **Adminless mark** (spec/wire.md §4 field 17, bit 1): a role commit's writer sets it on a commit it means to leave a thing with no standing admin (§1, The last admin); only that check reads it. A reader stores and forwards it with the header and judges nothing by it.
- **Logging a lowered grant**: a lowering has no logging rule of its own. It is an edge write on every path through its hop, so it is in the evidence of every held commit stating such a path (§4), under each sync ID whose bodies hold one.
- **Granting to a thing** (the writer's check, as The last admin, §1): a node writes a grant or change naming holder H, a thing, only where it may write H or holds H's current `keys.0` (the newest it holds: from a card of H, spec/sync-inbox.md §1 Card, or H's public key record, §4), and only where H's current keyset of each role the write boxes H at carries a KEM key (spec/crypto.md §3 Published KEM keys) or the node rotates that role of H in the same transaction, where it may (§3); else it refuses the write. A removal or lowering is never refused for this.
- **Granting to the public** (the writer's check): a node grants the public at most read, without the may-grant flag; it needs no card or KEM key.
- The roster apps read from `roles.0` is the role timeline's roster (§5.1), not the CRDT join of the stored deltas.

## 3. Secrets and rotation
- Each role secret of an independent thing is sealed to every holder of that role or higher, every epoch (spec/crypto.md §4).
- A **dependent thing** (no holder for keys but its mint admin, and no edge write on it) uses derived role secrets (spec/crypto.md §3). Before any grant on it, it becomes an **independent thing**: it gets random secrets of its own by a rotation from the derived epochs, boxed to its current holders for keys, and a `keys.0` (any other edge write on it lowers its mint admin's edge, its last standing admin's: §1, The last admin).
  - A thing **minted with a key** is independent from its minting: the commit minting it, signed by that key as it, is a rotation of all four roles from none (spec/crypto.md §3), writing its `keys.0` and its sync ID's genesis and first steps. Its secrets are its minter's, held by the key store that minted it and boxed to nobody.
  - Where the commit making a thing independent in the transaction creating it writes content on it, its record goes in that rotation commit, its first commit (spec/commits.md §2), and the content in a commit of its own after, unless that content expires: then the record goes alone with its expiry.
  - A thing that is to be granted a role on anything becomes independent first; so does a dependent holder for keys of a thing becoming independent (its mint admin), and a thing whose card is written, with its inbox (spec/sync-inbox.md §1), where the node administers it; a thing's inbox is named only in its own `keys.0`, so only once it is independent.
  - **Own secrets add only**: a thing becoming independent changes in no way who reaches it. Its mint admin is a current holder for keys like any other (admin, by the record edge, §1): the rotation boxes it as Holder actors says (spec/crypto.md §4), whatever the rotator syncs. So the mint admin still reads the thing, every sync ID that carried the thing keeps carrying it (that sync ID's thing's keys still open it, by the record edges, spec/sync-set.md §2), and every holder of the mint admin keeps reading or keeping it, a later one included. A rotator holding only a keyset of the mint admin older than its newest seals there, so a party removed from the mint admin since opens the thing's new epochs until a node holding the newer keyset, which finds that box stale, rotates (spec/crypto.md §4, Stale).
  - An admin node that sees a current holder of a dependent thing other than its mint admin, or a dependent thing holding a role with key access on an independent thing (left so by its only keyset part turning invalid, spec/crypto.md §3), SHOULD make it independent the same way, boxing every current holder but one whose grant it may finalize (§2), deciding at the end of a complete pass (below) of the sync IDs of its independent (spec/crypto.md §3), a role commit writing an edge to the thing counting as one of the thing. Concurrent ones are concurrent rotations of the thing's one sync ID (spec/sync-ids.md §2): their epochs fork (spec/crypto.md §4).
- **Required rotation**: a departure (spec/crypto.md §4: removal, demotion, finalize) of holder M from thing A requires rotating every role secret M could open through A:
  - on A, the roles M left;
  - on every independent thing that A's roles open (the targets of A's grants, record edges included, and so on transitively), the roles A holds there among those the roles M left open through A. Those rotations are required through A's keyset: A's rotation lists the keyset M could open, so each box to A at it is stale (spec/crypto.md §4, Holder actors), and so on up.
  - Content is not re-encrypted.
- The write by another node that removes or demotes M on A and the rotation of A's roles it requires are one role commit (spec/commits.md §2). A node may hold its parts at different times (spec/commits.md §4).
  - The removal's rotation is the remover's one rotation of those roles of A in that act: the rotations it makes first (below; a finalize's on A included, its departure then covered by the removal's rotation) rotate none of them.
  - The removal applies at once, whatever the remover holds (no secret of A is needed for the rotation, spec/crypto.md §4, Boxes). It carries the rotation when its author may rotate those roles; otherwise the write goes alone, a finalize included: its departure stays uncovered, so A's rotation is due and no node knowing it encrypts under A until one is made (spec/crypto.md §4, Encrypting under); a node knowing it that may rotate a thing whose epoch boxes A at A's uncovered keyset rotates that thing first, leaving A out (Seal), and A is re-sealed to it once A has rotated.
  - A removal or demotion taking a party below write or below admin carries itself in the keysets of A's rotation in the same commit, and every later rotation of A carries it (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions).
- A self-removal (§2) carries no rotation: the departure stays uncovered until the next node that may rotate does so. Things above the target leave it out meanwhile (spec/crypto.md §4, Seal), so a node that may rotate it SHOULD do so at the end of its next complete pass (below) of the target's sync IDs or of a thing it holds a role on, not only before its next write.
- Every other rotation is required of whoever may make it, when it next encrypts under the secret (spec/crypto.md §4). Nobody is required to sync anything to find it.
- **Who rotates**: a node with effective role write or above on a thing may rotate its roles at or below that role (a writer rotates sync, read and write, an admin all four). A role through a keyless grant or a destroyed thing (spec/crypto.md §4, Key access, Destroyed) is no right to rotate: wherever a rule reads "may rotate", the role counts over grants with key access held by no destroyed thing only. Validity counts every grant (§5.2).
  - A rotation seals the new secrets to every current holder of each rotated role, writes the history boxes and the rotated roles' keysets in `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §3, §4). The other roles keep their epochs.
  - A holder that cannot open a rotation, grant or re-seal boxing it by the commit's wrap takes it by its entry there (spec/sync-ids.md §3 Taking an entry).
  - Its validity is the author's role at its key (§5.2), for its `keys.0` and `secrets.0` deltas alike: each is judged against the role it needs (below), a `keys.0` part's verdict reading nothing else of the commit and a `secrets.0` part's only its edge-class parts on the thing (§2 May-grant grant), so a copy holding one is judged as the whole commit (§4 Copies); a rotation whose author lacks the role is invalid whole, its keysets included.
  - A `secrets.0` delta needs, per entry record it carries (each piece is a delta, judged alone: spec/wire.md §4 Pieces) (a put, a write into an entry, a removal), the role its key names, and at least write unless it puts history boxes only (a history add, spec/crypto.md §4). A keyless record, a key that is no box or history key, and a write or reset of the whole value or of a field need admin.
  - A `keys.0` delta needs write when it touches only the `sync`, `read` and `write` fields and box records (`sealed`, spec/crypto.md §4 Box records), each record (a put, a write into an entry, a removal) needing the role its key names as a box does, and admin when it touches any other field, writes or resets the whole value or the `sealed` field, or carries a keyless record, a key that is no box key, or a record of a role whose keyset field it does not write.
- A node that may rotate a required rotation MUST do so before it next encrypts under a secret it would replace; it MAY rotate eagerly.
- Sealing to a holder actor M's keyset is encrypting under M's epoch (spec/crypto.md §4): a node about to rotate, grant or re-seal A first makes the rotations of its holder actors it is required to make, so A's boxes name their new keysets.
- **Holder actors** (spec/crypto.md §4): a rotation of A (a removal's included), a grant on A or a re-seal boxes a holder actor M at the keysets Seal gives, per role (jointly while M's anchors fork), or leaves it out of that role and the roles above until one exists (then re-sealed, below); none waits for a rotation of M. A departure from M or below it is M's to rotate for: it reaches A's boxes only as M's new keyset, which makes them stale, and a demotion of a writer or admin there travels with that keyset (spec/crypto.md §4, Carried demotions).
  - A grant that would leave a holder actor with key access out of every role is refused.
- **Role cycles**: things each holding a role on the next, back to the first, form a cycle. No key cycle forms: the grant that would close one is keyless (spec/crypto.md §4, Key access), so its holder reads nothing of the target through it.
  - A node that lacks the earlier grant takes the later one as giving key access and boxes into a cycle. Its box is stale (tainted) at every node that holds both grants, which rotates; until the writer holds the grants and the commits of the cycle, a party removed from one thing on it may read the other's new content (spec/requirements.md, Permissions).
- **Re-seal**: a node that may rotate a role may write a current holder's boxes for that role's current epochs, to the holder's newest keyset it holds (a holder actor: the keyset Seal gives, spec/crypto.md §4) and with those epochs' history boxes, changing nothing else (spec/crypto.md §4).
  - It is logged under the thing's sync IDs like any role commit.
  - A node that may rotate r SHOULD re-seal a current holder of r that it sees without a box at a current epoch of r, or that a Stranded message or a stranded fire names (§4), before it next writes under the thing.
  - **Complete pass**: it decides at the end of a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1) of the thing's sync IDs. A Pull sent before a role commit of the thing applied ends no pass after it. Once it applies a role commit of the thing, or a `keys.0` commit of a holder its re-seal trigger then names (the keysets Seal boxes it at), from elsewhere, a node begins a pass of each of the thing's sync IDs (a Pull from its stream's cursor, spec/sync-streams.md §4, at each source whose last pass of it ended before the commit, once any Pull under way there ends).
  - It re-seals no holder without key access, no holder actor Seal leaves out (above) or a holder whose grant it may finalize (§2), and only at established epochs (spec/crypto.md §4).
  - It re-seals for a role only when holding the secret of such a current epoch the holder lacks a box at. It boxes the ones it holds and replaces no box the holder has; another holder boxes the rest, and where none that may rotate r holds one, the epoch is orphaned and rotated (spec/crypto.md §4). A re-seal a Stranded message or a stranded fire asks for writes the holder's boxes at every current epoch held, unless each is already the box Seal gives now; a report again naming the same holder with the same current `keys.0` commits asks nothing more.
  - A grant or re-seal boxes the holder at the current epochs held whether or not an epoch is blocked at the node (spec/crypto.md §4, Encrypting under: a box carrying a secret is encrypted under its recipient's key), after the rotations the node may make.
  - **Left out**: a holder H of r on a thing is **left out** of an epoch e of r when e's rotation was made by a node that did not hold H's current grant, and that grant was made by a node that did not hold e's rotation: neither boxes H at e. H reads nothing sealed under e until a node that may rotate r, holding e's secret and that grant, re-seals H (at the end of its next complete pass of the thing's sync IDs), or a rotation boxing H supersedes e (an orphaned e is rotated, spec/crypto.md §4). So once every node holds every commit, H stays left out of e only while no running node that may rotate r holds e's secret and no node that may rotate r writes under the thing (spec/requirements.md: a holder granted concurrently with a change of keys).
- **History add** (spec/crypto.md §4, History box): a node holding the secret of a current epoch of r, established and not blocked at it, that holds an earlier superseded established secret of r no live history entry of that epoch lists, writes an entry carrying what it holds, whatever its role on the thing, deciding at the end of a complete pass; logged under the thing's sync IDs like any role commit (a non-writer by deposit, spec/sync-inbox.md §3).
- **Sync ID role keys** (spec/sync-ids.md §2) derive from the newest epoch of sync, write and admin; a step's step key from the claim key of the sync epoch it bears (spec/sync-ids.md §3); only an admin issues a step (any reader registers it, spec/sync-ids.md §6). An admin issues the next step of a sync ID its thing owns once the step is due and the rotation commits it registers are logged under that sync ID here (stamped under the recorded head), whatever peers it reaches (spec/sync-ids.md §6); none while a role of the thing is blocked at it (spec/crypto.md §4: the step comes after the rotation that is made). A step is due when any holds:
  - it holds a sync, write or admin epoch newer than the one its recorded head registers (the step is a child of that head, spec/sync-ids.md §6);
  - its thing's `keys.0` has no read key in force and the admin vouches for its read keyset's epoch (spec/crypto.md §3): until a step, fires from outside wait (spec/sync-inbox.md §2);
  - its thing's deposit seed derives a deposit key other than the head's (a **deposit-only** step), once a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1) of the sync ID begun after the step came due has ended, while it holds no unapplied `keys.0` write of the thing signed by a current admin of it and misses no valid `keys.0` part of the thing that it knows of. The step stays due from the moment it first came due while the seed changes again.
- A holder that missed rotations is served them by the one open rule (spec/sync-opens.md §4) and reads them under the epochs they supersede (spec/crypto.md §5); one a rotation left out is re-sealed, the re-seal carrying its entry (a rotation compacted strands it until then).
- A node that removes a holder MUST, in the same sync, log the removal and the affected things' new `keys.0` under every sync ID it tracks that the removed party could read (as certificate copies where the bodies there do not carry them, §4), before anything it writes after them (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store: what it logs together goes in the order made).
- **Lost access**: a node that learns it lost read access to a thing MUST drop those role secrets and every local unit it can no longer read by another path (the thing's own, whether or not it counts as independent here, spec/sync-set.md §2), except what it retains (Retain, below); a commit of which it holds the header alone was read by no secret and stays. A node holding sync on the thing still keeps its sync secrets and its store of the thing's sync IDs: they become kept unread (spec/sync.md §3). Its role there is its own things' role by the thing's grants as it holds them, the public's among them (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own things); a node keeping the thing unread holds none of its grants and drops no sync secret for lost access: it keeps the sync secrets its entries gave it, judged by its steps (spec/sync.md §3, spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops): it drops exactly the secrets no such role covers, sync secrets below sync, every other below read.
  - **When it learns it**: a node syncing a sync ID of the thing keeps syncing it, and keeps the secrets, until a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1) of each such sync ID begun after it first judged the loss ends (a role commit arriving later does not move that bar; a loss that ends and comes again is judged afresh); then it judges again and drops only if the loss still stands (what the pass brought may give the role back). A node syncing none drops at once. Own self-removals and losses by an `until` alone wait as below and in §2.
  - Reading-only secrets of the thing (or of one a role on which gives one on it) whose source commit is unjudged or pending (spec/crypto.md §4) are no loss of access while the node holds no other secret of it.
  - A held pending role commit signed by its author that boxes the node a read epoch newer than every one it held keeps its boxes through the drop. The node reads nothing from them before it applies. Boxes only at epochs it held are dropped.
  - A held redemption (a commit removing its signer's own grant on a thing beside a grant there) granting the node, that stands void only because an applied redemption by the same signer removed that grant first, with no explicit write on that grant since (an unfinalized single-use link race, §7), is no loss yet: the node keeps what that grant would give and keeps syncing its sync IDs until such a write arrives.
  - A node that lost read access through a self-removal (§2) by an own thing or a thing on its own things' paths, whichever node made it, drops once, under each sync ID it reads that the commit has a body under, that commit is held elsewhere (spec/sync-status.md §2) or no peer is to be waited for on that sync ID (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for). With no such sync ID it drops at once.
  - One that lost it by an `until` passing alone (a finalize counting as its `until`) drops as §2 says.
  - **No secret taken back**: a node takes a role secret of a thing from an applied `secrets.0` box (wherever and whenever it opens, a holder actor's box among them) only when its roles on the thing now give that role's secrets (read or above: every role's; sync: the sync secret; through grants with key access only, spec/crypto.md §4 Holds) or an unfinalized link race (above) keeps what reaches the thing; a box held before its roles give them (a rotation applied before the grant it follows) gives them once they do. From a box its own entry reached (spec/sync-ids.md §3 Taking an entry: in a commit not yet applied, its evidence under the sync ID it opens) likewise, unless no ended edge on the thing of an own thing or a thing it reads is held (a grantee or a link's redeemer opens the sync ID before it holds its grant), or the box's epoch covers every departure from the thing of the holder the entry is for (an own thing, a thing it reads, the public) known here, as far as what the node holds tells (spec/crypto.md §4 Covers): the public's entry is alike for every party, so a removed party takes from it what any other does (redeeming a locator, the superseded epoch its rotation commit is wrapped under included); the thing is judged as above once role commits on it apply. A history entry is none of these: one opened under a secret the node held before that entry's ingest gives the earlier epochs and roles secrets it lists (spec/crypto.md §4 Roles secrets) whatever the node's roles (they read nothing that secret's holder was not given), so a node holding only the epochs its entry gave, a redeemer's among them, reads the role commits under earlier ones that give it its role or show it the public's grant; one opened only under a secret the same ingest gave the node and its roles do not keep (a box concurrent with its removal) gives nothing. Reading-only secrets (spec/crypto.md §4) are as above.
  - Of a copy (spec/sync-judgement.md §1) opened under a sync ID, a node takes no content part (spec/data-model.md §9: not role-class, `addenda.0`, `created.0` or `retention^.0`) on a thing whose independent (spec/crypto.md §3) the sync ID's thing may not read now, and keeps none of the copy's bytes; it fetches the copy again once it holds secrets of that independent thing.
- **Retain**: a node that lost read access to thing A retains a read-only copy of A's things when one of its ways to A retains.
  - A **way** is a holder H of A, in one of these forms (a record edge is a grant here):
    - a grant of H on A at read or above that ended saying retain (its role the last value's before a removal), the node holding read or above on H just before the write that last changed it;
    - a grant of H on A at read or above alive.
  - Either way only through a grant with key access (spec/crypto.md §4 Key access: a keyless grant reads nothing through it), as it last stood for keys: an ended grant no removal ended stands for keys (every `until` disregarded) and is judged so; one a removal ended, over the writes that applied before that removal.
  - An ended grant says what the last write on it on the timeline (§5.1, the writes that applied) says:
    - a removal, for itself (§2);
    - a value with another `until`, passed: its `retain` (§2).
  - It retains only while the node has H: it is H, holds read or above on H now, retains H itself, or holds H's secrets above sync not dropped in the same judging (a loss of H not yet carried out; the copy is judged again when it is) (least fixpoint).
  - Everything else erases: a removal saying so, a timed grant written to erase, a write that did not apply. A default set later changes no removal or ending before it.
  - **The copy** is what the node held when it dropped: state, bodies and their timers.
    - Its secrets and `secrets.0` go as above, and so does what it held of the things' sync IDs: it syncs none of them, logs and serves nothing of it, and cannot write it.
    - Its pending parts on the things' content go. A later part on it is not joined, so a purge request arriving later reaches the copy only in a commit the node takes for another part, and need not.
    - Evidence stays and updates as on any thing outside the node closure (§4). Validity still follows what the node holds (§5.2).
    - Expiry and retention (spec/commits.md §5) run as on any held body. No body of a copy is must-hold, or known to anyone (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
    - A thing the node acts as for a client (spec/sync-transport.md §6) reads the copy while it is a holder the copy hangs on, or holds a role now both on its mint admin and on such a holder, a holder that is itself a copy counting by its own holders. It writes nothing there.
  - The node records per copy the holders it retains through, durably.
    - A holder leaves that record when the timeline shows its way no longer retains (as above) or the node no longer has it. A copy with none is erased as lost.
    - A role on A with a secret of it again ends the copy: the node is a holder with old data, nothing more.
    - The node's user may erase a copy at any time, with what is kept only through it.

## 4. Evidence
**Evidence** is what a reader needs to judge a held write: it rides down in the sync unit holding the write. It is a function of what a node holds now.
- A write's authority is checked along its stated path (§1 Path): each edge a record edge (the tid commits to the mint admin) or set by edge writes, its key hop the signer's record's key or set by edge writes. No secrets are needed.
- **Evidence of a commit** for a thing T it writes, at a node: the least set holding, of what the node holds,
  - every edge write on the commit's key hop, on each edge of the path it states for T (a descent's record edges included) and on each edge its own edge writes on T set (the creations a removal's resets name among them, §2): grants, changes, lowerings (each whole, its frontier inside it), self-removals, may-grant grants and finalizes, whatever their validity or order, each with the other edge writes of its commit on the same thing;
  - for each delete key the commit lists at level 1 or 3 for T, and B the thing up T's record chain whose steps bear it (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys), every `sync.0` write on B recording a step on a path from the genesis step of that step's sync ID to a step bearing that key;
  - the creation records of the thing signed as, of every member of that path and of T, and those up their record chains (record blobs, spec/wire.md §3; never a copy of the minting commit);
  - closed: the evidence of each edge write and `sync.0` write in the set, for the thing it writes, along the path its own commit states.
  - An external fire's (§5.2 External fires): its signer's record.
  - Nothing else: no `keys.0` commit (judging needs no KEM key), no edge of a path the commit does not state.
- **Evidence of a sync ID S**, at a node: the evidence of each body under S (spec/sync-judgement.md §1) for each thing of S's sync unit it has a part on; a commit's part on a thing outside the unit brings none. It is a function of the commits the node holds and S's sync unit as the node judges it, computed again at every change of either (a thing leaving the unit turns what the remaining bodies still need of its role commits into copies). Nothing is in it for having been in it: an entry leaves with the last held commit needing it. Verdicts never decide it.
- **Role entries** of an independent thing H: its role-class parts under its own sync ID (its roster, `signers.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0`, `sync.0`), bodies there, fetched whole (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries). A grant is the target's own data and syncs with it (§2); a node's role on X is by X's grants as it holds them (spec/sync-set.md §2 Own things).
- **Stub** of an independent thing X in a sync unit U of thing T (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stubs): X's role entries cut to U's readers. Where T's keys open X: the certificate copy (Copies, a stub's cut) of each held commit with an edge-class, `secrets.0` or `sync.0` part on X, each with its evidence, and X's public key record (its current `keys.0` commits: X's sync ID and step keys). Where they do not (T holds only sync): the **sync-only cut**, X's sync ID and the holders on T's chains to X (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stubs: each independent holder, neither T nor X, on a chain of grants with key access from T to X at sync or above at each hop, by the role entries its logger holds; spec/wire.md §4 `SyncOnlyCut`), logged by a reader of U holding X's role entries, or holding an entry at X giving T a sync secret, naming none (spec/sync-ids.md §3 Taking an entry: an entry is a sync holder's cut); a node holding it opens X's sync ID by the claims of T and of each holder it names, and takes X's sync secrets from their sync entries there (spec/sync-ids.md §3). Of X's roster it tells U's readers only those holders. A reader of U judges each copy as any certificate copy; a node holding the stub keeps it current from X's own sync IDs (spec/sync-set.md §2 Holding a stub).
- **A hop's removal**: a grant on a chain by which U's keys open what U carries is a role entry of the hop's target, which U carries whole or as a stub (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stubs), so its lowering or removal reaches every node reading U as those role entries are kept current.
- **Current `keys.0` commits** of a thing T, at a node: every held commit whose `keys.0` body on T has a live write (spec/commits.md §5 Bodies and deaths), a compaction snapshot among them, so together they rebuild T's `keys.0` state whole: the current keysets with their rotations' box records, the sync ID, deposit seed, step keys and inbox.
- **Copies**: an entry of S's evidence on a thing of S's sync unit is a body (spec/sync-judgement.md §1); one on a thing outside it is a **certificate copy**: identified by (commit ID, thing), it holds its commit's header and **S's cut** of the commit on that thing, and nothing else of the commit (spec/wire.md §4). S's cut is the commit's parts on the thing that S's readers read there, each whole (its delta hash binds it, pages included), as what S carries names them:
  - S's evidence: its edge-class parts, and its `sync.0` part where it records a step placing a listed key;
  - a stub (Stub): its edge-class parts; its `secrets.0` pieces boxing T, a holder on T's chains to the thing, or the public, and its rest piece; its `sync.0` part;
  - keys (a public key record, below; Carried and Reported, spec/crypto.md §4; holder keys): its `keys.0` part;
  - beside a `secrets.0` or `keys.0` part, its edge-class parts on the thing: a `secrets.0` part's verdict reads them (§2 May-grant grant), and beside `keys.0` they carry the edge writes its rotation makes (a removal).
  - Each part of the cut brings the evidence of its commit for its thing (Evidence of a commit; those copies cut alike).
  - Nothing else: a piece of a holder S's readers do not open boxes of, a `keys.0` or `sync.0` part nothing under S reads, is in no cut. One copy serves every entry under S that needs it. A node logging copies of one (commit, thing) under several sync IDs logs one blob per distinct cut, wrapped under each sync ID whose cut it is (spec/sync-judgement.md §1).
- **Logging certificates**: any node holding an entry of S's required set (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) that syncs S logs it there as it logs bodies (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store: stamped by a writer of S, else deposited); none waits for the commit's signer. Besides:
  - the records go as one record blob of the logging node's carrying every record S carries (each record S's evidence names, but one a body under S delivers: of a thing of the sync unit holding no epochs of its own when minted, whose minting section S's readers open from a member of its record chain they hold epochs of, spec/sync-judgement.md §1, and that the evidence of no role entry of S's thing names, since a node holding that thing's stub takes role entries alone, spec/sync-set.md §2 Holding a stub), logged when no record blob in its own store carries exactly those ; a record blob logged by any node counts once opened, while it is held in the node's own store or at a source of S (its stream shows it, a message showing it in hand included); of two such blobs, one carrying every record S's required set holds that the other carries, and more, or the same with fewer others, or the same and as many under a lower blob ID, wins: the own store holds the one no counted blob beats, fetching it where shown when it lacks it, and the other leaves the own store with no tombstone once one beating it is held there, and is deleted at a source keeping S unread that shows both (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Certificate blobs);
  - a certificate copy or sync unit blob logged by any node counts once the node has opened it as the same commit and checked each of its deltas against the header's delta hash (spec/commits.md §4), while it is held in the node's own store and at every source of S; a sync-only cut counts likewise once opened, while S's unit carries its thing's stub where its unit holds only sync;
  - a blob of a rotation commit of S's thing (wrapped under the epoch it supersedes, spec/crypto.md §5) counts only if it opens under the newest read epoch;
  - for each destroyed thing (spec/commits.md §5) a live thing of S's sync unit needs (a destroyed thing on a path a body under S states, named mint admin or holder on a thing of the sync unit that is not destroyed or on such a destroyed thing), the records up its record chain and every held edge write on it with its evidence (as copies where S's sync unit does not hold the destroyed thing; its own bodies go with its destruction), and, for every thing whose record any of these carries, its witness as a header-only copy and, destroyed, the requests destroying it (spec/commits.md §5, A thing's mintings travel with its record), except what an entry under S already holds;
- The grantee's public key record (its creation record and current `keys.0` commits, spec/sync-inbox.md §1) goes under the target's sync ID as certificate copies (cut to keys, Copies) with their evidence, so every admin can seal to and list the grantee: keys, in no commit's evidence. The public has none.
- A role commit naming a holder is applied before the deletes it causes (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Role entries first).
- A demotion or `keys.0` write by another node is its signer's to log (§3); a node MAY wait up to `SETTLE_MS` after receiving one before copying it.
- A promoter completes the evidence it holds (spec/sync-inbox.md §4).
- **Stranded**: a node is stranded on a sync ID when it cannot read the sync ID's current epoch by any key it holds: it reached the head and after a complete pass there cannot open the epoch it registers, or every source refused every open it had to try (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Admissible: its sync proofs and its claims at the source's step). A stranded node MUST send a Stranded message (spec/wire.md §11.2) naming the sync ID and the holder to each connected peer that proved a thing whose proven role (spec/sync-opens.md §2) on the sync ID's thing is write or above, as the node judges it, and to each such peer when it connects. With none connected, it fires a stranded notice (`stranded@~0`, spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Stranded notice) on the sync ID's thing, which that thing's writers see later. It sends each, per peer and per fire, once per sync key of its head. Either names a current holder to re-seal (§3), which the receiver checks itself, at the current epochs whatever the node's head.
  - Each carries the **holder keys** of the holder the node reads the sync ID's thing through as it judges it (an own thing, or a holder of that thing its own things hold a role on): that holder's current `keys.0` commits, each cut to keys (Copies), with their evidence (spec/wire.md §9). A receiver that may re-seal the thing takes them, when they name a current holder of it, as evidence before it decides, and takes nothing of them off the holder's **authority chain** (no keyset but the holder's and the cards of those on the chain who wrote it, no record or edge write of anything else): the holder, then each one's mint admin and current holders, transitively, as the receiver judges it, judged again as what it takes extends it (a record learned extends it before any commit is judged), nor any such commit that it does not judge valid with every edge write it makes applied (spec/wire.md §9). A node gone from the holder learns the keyset it re-seals the holder with only so (spec/crypto.md §4, Reported). A message or fire whose keys do not fit goes without them.
- A commit whose evidence has not arrived is pending (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable).
- A node that does not sync a thing applies the evidence it holds of it; its timeline for that thing runs over those edge writes.
- A node keeps another thing's records, edge writes and `sync.0` writes only while evidence it holds needs them, and its `keys.0` commits only as keys (spec/commits.md §5 What a held thing needs).

## 5. Validity
Validity is a deterministic function of the commits a node holds and its trust set (§5.1, Trust set).

### 5.1 Layer 1: role timeline
The timeline covers the **edge-class parts**: edge writes (writes to `roles.0` and `signers.0`, §2). Every other role-class part (`secrets.0`, `keys.0`, `sync.0`) is judged like content (§5.2) by the role its aspect requires (§1, §3). The walk below is one joint computation over every thing's edge-class parts.
- An edge write is **on** the edge (holder, target), the holder a thing or a key: its **hop** h. A value **gives** at a `ts` its role, or none where it has an `until` at or before that `ts`. A value **lets grant** what it gives where that is admin or the value carries the may-grant flag, else nothing (§2 Path's may-grant).
- **Rests on**: an edge-class part rests on each edge of its stated path, its commit's key hop first (§1 Path); a self-removal rests also on the hop it lowers.
- **Lowering**: an edge write that is no self-removal and no finalize (§2) whose value gives below admin at its own `ts` (no grant, a role below admin, or an `until` at or before its `ts`). An **end**, an explicit write whose value gives admin at its `ts` with an `until` after it, is no lowering whatever its hop held before: it applies as a grant (step 2), carries no list and is no dispute candidate.
- **Edge as restored**, of a hop h at a point of a pass: what h gives and lets grant there, each raised to the highest that the values h has taken in the pass since the last explicit write on it give and let grant (that write's value and the may-grant grants' since), each such value read with its `until` disregarded; a self-removal and a finalize leave it as it was. A record edge and a record's key (§1) no explicit write in the pass has set are admin in both. A write **takes h down** where what its value gives at its `ts` is below what h as restored gives, or what it lets grant there is below what h as restored lets grant; it **would raise** h where either is above.
- A lowering's **list** is its frontier (below); it **names** a part whose commit's ID is in it. Nothing else speaks for an edge-class part a list does not name.

1. **Order**: order every held edge-class part, over every thing, by (`ts`, thing signed as, commit ID): a part's **key** is its position in this order. A commit's parts on no hop of a path it states come first, then those on such a hop: its key hop (a key lowering its own grant) or an edge of the path it states for any thing it writes, with that thing as the path's last member (a self-removal of the path's last thing, the record edge a descent ends with). Within each group they are ordered by thing (tid, bytewise), on one thing its edge writes by holder tid, then those on a key's grant by key, bytewise.
2. **Passes**: the walk is a run of passes. Each starts before every part, with each thing's record edge in force and each record's key at admin (§1) and no other hop set, and takes the parts in key order, skipping the drop set D. D and a **verdict** for each lowering judged (applied or void) carry from pass to pass; nothing else does. A commit's parts take effect one at a time, each judged with the earlier ones in effect.
   - A part with no verdict whose members name a thing twice, or whose marks are invalid (spec/wire.md §4 field 2), applies nowhere. Any other part with no verdict **applies** at its point iff, each hop read as it gives at the part's `ts`:
     - **by admin**: every edge of its path, the key hop among them, gives admin. So apply an edge write that is no self-removal and no finalize (it is then **explicit**); a self-removal, its path ending at the holder, where it lowers (§2).
     - **as a may-grant grant**: an edge write that does not apply by admin, where §2 May-grant grant holds.
     - **as a finalize**: where §2 Finalize holds.
   - A lowering with verdict **applied** applies as an explicit write, whatever its path gives there, where it does not raise h as restored in this pass; where it would raise h, it applies only where every edge of its path, the key hop among them, gives admin. It is judged no more and puts nothing in question. One with verdict **void** is skipped.
3. **Judging a lowering**: a lowering L of hop h with no verdict that applies by admin in a pass and takes h down is judged there:
   - L **puts in question** every held edge-class part of another commit, ordered before L, that rests on h, is not in D, is not void, and whose **need** at h the hop as restored met and L's value at L's `ts` does not. A part's need at h is what it applied by in this pass: admin for a part applied by admin; for a may-grant grant its role, and on its path's last edge its role as what the edge lets grant; for a finalize write. A part this pass has not applied needs the least it could apply by: an edge write adding a grant (no self-removal, no finalize, its value giving a role at its `ts`) a may-grant grant's need, a finalize write, any other admin.
   - L's **drops** are those of them its list does not name, and with each every other edge-class part on the same thing of the same commit that is not in D and not void: a commit's edge-class parts on a thing fall together, as a list keeps them together.
   - L is **void** iff its drops would take its authority: `keeps(L)` fails (step 4). Void, L does not apply and drops nothing.
   - Otherwise its verdict is **applied**: L applies; its drops join D at once, and where it has any the pass ends once L's commit has taken effect, the next pass replaying the parts before the earliest drop unchanged.
   - A lowering that applies without taking h down, or as a may-grant grant, or does not apply, gets no verdict there and puts nothing in question; a later pass reaching it applying by admin and taking h down judges it then. A lowering goes at most once from no verdict to one.
4. **Keeps**: for the lowering L being judged, `keeps(W)` holds for L, or for a part W the pass applied before L, iff every hop e that W rests on by its path keeps admin for W. Of the parts the pass applied on e ordered before W, latest first:
   - pass over each of L's drops and each drop made in this pass by an earlier part of L's commit;
   - a self-removal whose value gives admin at W's `ts` is passed over; one giving less decides that e does not keep admin;
   - a part that is no lowering and no self-removal (a grant, an end, a finalize) for which `keeps` fails in turn is passed over;
   - the first one left decides: e keeps admin iff its value gives admin at W's `ts`.

   With none left, e keeps admin iff it is a record edge or the record's key. Each step reads only parts ordered before the one it checks, in the pass as it stood at L; nothing is walked again.
5. **Settling**: a pass that adds nothing to D ends the walk; it is the **settled walk**. Nothing is ever undropped. The walk is a function of the held commits and the trust set, whatever order they came in; D and the verdicts hold only within one walk. The timeline is the settled walk: every rule that reads whether an edge-class part applies, the roles and the keys' grants at a point, reads it. The roster and the keys' grants are its final state.

- **Frontier**: every lowering R of hop h that its writer makes as an explicit write embeds, as commit IDs, never counters, the commits **resting on h** that its writer's node A had received as of R's `ts` T: every commit with a part resting on h (content, purge requests, addenda and compaction snapshots among them; for a key hop (k, S): every commit k signed as S), but the commits with edge-class parts that The list (below) leaves out. A received commit with a `Split` entry A cannot open (spec/wire.md §4) is listed. No self-removal, may-grant grant, finalize or end carries a frontier, and no other write does.
  - Nothing is exempt and nothing is read as absent: besides R itself, R's list alone decides which parts resting on h before R are kept, in R's judging (step 3) and in a trusted R's seeds (Trust set) alike, whatever R's own path rests on.
  - **Own cover**: every received commit resting on h that A still holds is listed: a commit is listed whether A still holds its body, dropped it or compacted it. A commit A no longer holds (forgotten, spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) cannot be listed: before R, for each aspect such commits write where a live write of such a commit stands in A's state only through a cover (its commit forgotten at A, or never received there), A writes a compaction snapshot of the aspect from its whole state marked as its **own cover** (spec/wire.md §4 field 17, bit 0), stamped before R, and R's frontier names it. A list naming a snapshot names every commit that snapshot carries, for the deltas of the snapshot's thing and aspect it carries copies of (§5.2 Copies). An aspect A may not snapshot (no role, delete keys not here) gets none. Only the node that wrote an own cover drops a held body as covered under it (spec/commits.md §5 Covered), whether or not R is held, so a removal that does not apply leaves every other holder as it was.
  - Where a frontier would pass the list bound a node resolves (`MAX_LIST_BYTES`), A leaves out, as few as fit: first the plain-content commits (§5.2, Addenda) it received last, which its own addenda list instead; then the purge-request commits it received last, each losing its right. A frontier over the bound without them is refused, so a removal whose commits with edge-class parts alone pass the bound cannot be written.
  - Ephemeral commits are listed as durable ones (spec/commits.md §2); one A restarted since receiving, or forgot (spec/sync-compaction.md §2), is not received.
  - **Received**: a commit counts as received once A decrypted its header and verified its signature (the header pins its deltas by hash), whether or not A holds, applies or stores its deltas; relaying is not receiving. A commit forgotten at A (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) is not received.
  - A frontier MUST NOT list a commit A had not received as of T.
  - A frontier above `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` is a chunked large value referenced by link (spec/commits.md §1). The frontier is part of R's commit: every copy of R carries it whole.
- **The list**: which received commits with edge-class parts resting on h a writer names. Let `end(F)` be read in A's own walk over what it had received as of T and R, with R trusted and its frontier F: nothing where R does not apply; else the earliest `ts`, at or after T, at which a hop R rests on by its path stops giving admin (an `until` passing, or a write stamped by then setting it below), or no end where none does; ordered: nothing, then any `ts` by its value, then no end.
  - Where `end` of everything received resting on h is no end, A names all of it.
  - Otherwise A starts from the received commits resting on h without edge-class parts, and takes those with edge-class parts one at a time in the order it received them, keeping commit c iff `end` with c added is not nothing and is no earlier than `end` without it.
  - No reader checks any of this: every node reads the list as written. A list naming what takes R's authority leaves R void at every node alike (step 3).
- A demotion by `until`, self-removal or finalize puts nothing in question. The next explicit write on that hop, by any node, is judged with its list against the hop as restored.
- **Trust set**: a node's walk takes one more input, its trust set: held lowerings carrying a frontier that it sides with. Nodes holding the same commits under the same trust set read alike. Which writes a node trusts is its own, kept across restarts, carried by nothing on the wire.
  - **Seeds** of a trusted write R on hop h: every held edge-class part of another commit ordered before R that rests on h, that R's list does not name, and whose least need at h (step 3, as for a part a pass has not applied) R's value at R's `ts` does not meet; and with each every other held edge-class part on the same thing of the same commit ordered before R.
  - D starts, before the first pass, at the seeds of every trusted write. A trusted write the settled walk does not apply is **void**: every void write loses its seeds at once, and the walk is redone from scratch without them, until none is void.
  - **Sides**: a **fight** is a held lowering R carrying a frontier and a seed of R. A lowering **cannot stand beside** R when R's seeds take it, its seeds take R, or it is ordered before R and lowers a hop R rests on.
    - **Its own**: a node records as trusted every lowering carrying a frontier that it makes, when it makes it. Its own are the commits it made, not those its key signed: one that reaches it from outside is another party's.
    - **Held first**: when the commits a node takes in between two readings bring it a commit of another party that is a seed of a held lowering R, no recorded lowering is R or cannot stand beside R, and the node's reading before them applied R (other than as a may-grant grant; whether or not R took its hop down there), the node records R as trusted. The seeds are read over what the node now holds; the reading is the one it already had.
    - **No order, no side**: where the node held the seed before R, or first held both after the same reading, it records nothing. Nothing else orders the two: not their `ts`, not their IDs, not their place among the commits taken in together.
    - **A side is an answer**: a recorded side stays until the node's user chooses again. **Choosing R** records R as trusted and drops the record of every lowering that cannot stand beside R.
    - **The counter-removal is told**: where a recorded lowering's seeds take a held lowering carrying a frontier of a hop whose holder is not the thing it is signed as, the node reports that one beside its disputes for as long as it holds both and choosing it would change its reading.
  - A **dispute** is a held lowering R of a hop whose holder is not the thing R is signed as (a key never is), no self-removal and not one the walk with R trusted applies as a may-grant grant, not in the trust set and not dropped by a trusted write (its seeds or its drops), whose trust would change the node's reading: which edge-class parts apply, which deltas are valid (§5.2), or the roles and the keys' grants in the final state, read with R added to the trust set. Where two or more lowerings meeting every clause but the last each drop, in the node's reading, a held lowering of a hop whose holder is not the thing it is signed as (**reach-back** lowerings), a reach-back R not applied as a may-grant grant with them all trusted is also a dispute where that reading differs from the one with every reach-back lowering but R added. Disputes are keyed by (R, holder) and reported; the walk acts on the trust set alone.
- A part a list keeps applies like any other: one that takes R's authority leaves R void (step 3), so its writer leaves it out (The list).

### 5.2 Layer 2: content
- **Position**: this layer judges a commit at the start of its `ts`: before every edge-class part at that `ts`, after every one at an earlier `ts`, whatever things they are signed as. Its **key** below is that position, and "ordered before/after" compares it with edge-class parts' keys (§5.1).
- **Content**: a delta on thing N whose commit states path P for N (§1 Path), needing role r (write; admin for an admin-only aspect, below), is judged along P's **hops**: P's edges, its commit's key hop first. Nothing but P judges it: another path that would also give r does not. A delta whose members name a thing twice or whose marks are invalid (spec/wire.md §4 field 2) is invalid. Any other is valid iff it **stands**, or P is re-granted for r (Re-grant, below).
  - A **cut** of P below r is a lowering the timeline applies as an explicit write on a hop e of P, whose value gives below r at its `ts` and that takes e from r or above as restored (§5.1).
  - The delta **stands** where, for each hop e of P, every cut of e below r ordered after the delta answers it, and either e gives at least r just before the delta's key (each `until` judged at the delta's `ts`), or the last part the timeline applies on e before that key is a cut answering the delta and e's value just before that cut gives at least r at the delta's `ts`.
  - A cut L **answers** a delta where L's list names its commit, whatever their order, or a counting addendum vouches for it against L (Addenda, below). An answer speaks for its own cut alone: a delta is valid as if the cuts answering it had not cut it, every other cut of its path still refusing it, and no list gives a role the path never gave.
- **Addenda**: a write of `addenda.0` on N (spec/data-model.md §9): an entry its delta creates (spec/crdt.md §5.6 `put_new`; a write into an entry it did not create says nothing), keyed by the commit ID of a removal R, holding a `frontier` listing commit IDs. An addendum A, its commit signed as W, **vouches for** a delta on N stated along path P **against** R when all of:
  - R is a cut of P below the role the delta needs (write; admin for an admin-only aspect), ordered after the delta's commit and before A, and A's entry lists the delta's commit;
  - every hop of the path A states for N gives write just before A's key, and admin where the delta needs admin;
  - the delta's commit is not signed as W;
  - the delta is content: its commit is a content commit (not a compaction snapshot) carrying no purge request, and its aspect is not role-class;
  - A is valid: it is judged as a plain delta on N along its own path by the rules above, itself vouched for by other addenda or not, against a removal. Validity with addenda is the least fixpoint over every commit at once: the addenda valid without any addendum count first, then those they vouch for, until nothing changes.
  - A's list counts while its body is held; without the body (dropped, purged) A is still a write at its key, listing nothing, so a later addendum may vouch for it and its body is fetched again once it is valid. An addendum written by a role commit is no addendum. Addenda are kept as role commits are (fixed retention, spec/commits.md §5) and never compacted.
  - Every cut's list answers that cut for every commit it names, and every addendum answers its removal for the commits ordered before that removal, however many removals and re-grants follow; a later cut of the path needs its own answer.
  - Nothing checks when W received what it lists, so a writer that lists commits it had not received by R's `ts` makes them count.
  - **Writing**: a node writes addenda unasked. For each cut R of a hop h applied in its view taking h below write or below admin, and each thing N in its node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2 Writing): whenever its own current view shows a content commit (no compaction snapshot) with a delta on N stated along a path with hop h, ordered before R, not made by itself, that it received in R's **window** (by R's `ts` and at an earlier millisecond than it received R, both as of its wall clock; Received, §5.1) and R's list does not name, that an addendum it wrote now would vouch for, and that no counting addendum of its own vouches for, it writes one addendum on N keyed by R listing every such commit it received in R's window that R's list does not name, whether or not it would vouch for it now. So it writes on applying R, when it first holds the role after (granted again, its side switched), and whenever what it holds or trusts changes so that none of its addenda vouches for such a commit. One addendum per thing, so no body of one thing travels in another thing's commit; R's writer writes them too, for what its frontier left out (§5.1). A node that cannot write on N yet (its delete keys not here) writes it once it can.
- **External fires**: a delta on an external signal (`@`, spec/data-model.md §2) of thing N valid by the rules above is valid (a writer's fire, whatever the setting). Any other is valid iff N's current setting (spec/commits.md §5 The setting, In force) **enables** the signal: an entry of its own in `rules` names the AK. It is so judged whatever its stated path gives, once its commit's signature verifies and its key hop gives a role just before its key (§5.1); it reads no edge (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable) and no cut touches it.
  - It is judged again whenever N's current setting changes (a setting held, dropped, turning valid or invalid), as any delta whose ground changes; one turning valid counts as never received where it was dropped as invalid (§6 Invalid deltas).
  - An invalid one is listed nowhere but as its writer's own (spec/commits.md §7 Listed parts), so it reaches no listener, and is never promoted (spec/sync-inbox.md §4).
- A delta of an **admin-only** aspect (AK marker `^`, spec/data-model.md §2) is judged by the rules above with admin in place of write: its path giving admin on N, cuts below admin, addenda whose path gives admin (Addenda). A writer that does not hold admin on N MUST NOT write one; every node judges it alike from the AK alone, also on an ephemeral commit's part and a compaction snapshot's delta.
- A `retention^.0` delta (spec/commits.md §5 The setting) is judged by those rules as an admin-only delta; no other delta's validity depends on it but a fire valid only as an external fire (External fires, above).
- A delta of A's card aspect `:<A>:card.0` (spec/data-model.md §9) writing anything but a reset is invalid unless A is the thing it is on or among the members its commit states for that thing (§1 Path); else it is judged by the rules above, a compaction snapshot's alike (below): one carrying a card states a path through A.
- **Compaction snapshot**: a snapshot S's delta of an aspect is judged as a delta of that aspect along S's stated path by the rules above, at S's key, needing the strictest role a write it could carry needs (admin for `sync.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0` and admin-only aspects, else write). `created.0` is always valid.
  - **Own cover**: S marked as an own cover (spec/wire.md §4 field 17, bit 0) is also valid when every hop of its path gave the role S needs just before S's key, and a lowering R the timeline applies as an explicit write (whatever it takes from its hop at the reading node), signed as the thing S is signed as, ordered at or after S, has a list naming S's commit. It then stands while R applies, whatever cuts its path later (a later lowering that names neither drops R with it, §5.1).
  - An invalid S gives nothing: its body joins nothing, covers nothing (spec/commits.md §5).
  - A valid S vouches for every write it carries: no carried write is judged along its own commit's path.
- **A write's own commit** is the one holding it on that aspect: for a session-form write, the commit whose range holds its dot; for a commit-form one (source `Commit(C)`), C.
- **Copies**: a valid snapshot speaks for current state alone. A list (a frontier or an addendum's) naming a snapshot names the commits it carries, for the deltas of the snapshot's thing and aspect it carries copies of; a role commit is never carried, so no list names one through a snapshot; a snapshot the node does not hold names nothing there. Its writes join like any write (spec/crdt.md §4.1), wherever held, also where the receiver holds the write's own delta: a copy differing from the delta's is never compared with it; the copies join by the copy order. A node attributes a write's signer and time only to a commit it holds and verified. So a valid snapshot by a writer not yet holding a removal carries the removed party's write stamped after the removal, wherever that snapshot is held, and every later snapshot of the aspect carries it as state.
- A delta's writes count while it is valid. An invalid delta's writes are un-merged (spec/crdt.md §4.6), and the held valid snapshots' copies of them stay, as the snapshots' writes with the payload they carry (a copy of a deleted text element's creation without its char never gives the char). Every read is a function of the valid writes held: an invalid delta influences nothing, a sequence's order included (an element it created does not exist for ordering, spec/crdt.md §6.1), so holding its body or not changes no read.
- A record carrying no `ts` (a tombstone) carries no write.
- **A snapshot that turns invalid, or whose body drops**, gives nothing and vouches no more: the state is the join of the bodies held (spec/crdt.md §4.6).
  - Each body of that aspect dropped as covered (compaction or purge on replace, spec/commits.md §5, spec/sync-compaction.md §1) whose covers (the held valid snapshots that covered it when it dropped) none carries any more counts as never received from then on (spec/commits.md §5 Never received) and joins as a late part (spec/commits.md §4) when it arrives again. A coverer carries it if it is held with its body and valid, or dropped as covered under covers one of which carries it; a forgotten one (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) as the covers it dropped under do.
  - **A cover is its writer's edit**: a cut of its path whose list does not name it makes it invalid (addenda vouch for no snapshot, Addenda above), and the live writes only it carried are lost wherever no node still holds their bodies; a re-grant brings them back only from a node holding them (§6 Invalid deltas).
- **Re-grant**: a path P is **re-granted** for r when every hop of P gives at least r in the timeline's final state, every grant with an `until` counted as removed, and every hop has a first point. Each delta stated along P needing r and ordered after P's **first point** is then valid again, including those in the gap and those a cut's list left out. Edge-class parts in the gap stay dropped.
  - P's first point is the latest, over P's hops, of each hop's first point: the key of the first held edge-class part setting that hop at r or above (its role read with its `until` disregarded) that is made **by right**. A record edge's and a record's key's first point is the start: a path whose hops are all so is re-granted from the start.
  - A held edge-class part is made by right iff its members name no thing twice, it is no self-removal and no finalize, and, each hop read just before its key in the settled walk and each `until` at its `ts`, either every edge of its path, the key hop among them, gives admin, or it is an edge write adding a grant (its value giving a role at its `ts`) and its path gives may-grant at its role (§2). Applied or dropped alike: a grant a later removal drops still counts, and so does one stamped before its maker's removal.
- One dot names one write: two commits of one session with overlapping ranges on one aspect are both invalid (spec/commits.md §4).
- A delta that becomes invalid is un-merged (spec/crdt.md §4.6). A signal's deltas join no state, so a validity change on one changes only which of them count (spec/commits.md §5 Signals).
- **Ephemeral parts**: a part of an ephemeral commit (spec/commits.md §2) is judged as the durable part of its kind would be, at the commit's key, along its stated path, needing the aspect's role (§1):
  - a session-form one as a content delta;
  - a snapshot one as a compaction snapshot delta.
  - A part on a core aspect (spec/data-model.md §9) is invalid.
  - Frontiers and addenda name ephemeral commits as durable ones. What a valid ephemeral snapshot carries it vouches for, as any snapshot does; one turned invalid gives nothing, and a body dropped as covered by it counts as never received, as for a durable snapshot. What becomes invalid is un-merged (spec/crdt.md §4.6).

### 5.3 Revoking (client behavior)
- A lowering (§5.1) is stamped at once with what its writer had received. Content through the lowered hop that it leaves out survives where another writer had received it by its `ts` (§5.2, Addenda); the edge-class parts its list leaves out, and the writes no writer had received, are lost: every writer drops them as invalid (§6 Invalid deltas). Writes that a valid snapshot by another writer carries stay wherever that snapshot is held (§5.2, Copies), until an admin's reset or revert supersedes them.
- A client that wants to keep more MAY sync the sync IDs of the things the lowered hop gave write on first, for as long as it chooses, and stamp regardless after.

## 6. Purge rights
- **Purge author**: a commit has one purge author, for every delta of it (spec/wire.md §4 field 2): a `Path`'s index (0 the thing signed as, j the listed thing Aj), a `Split`'s stated tid, else the thing signed as. It is a member of the path of every thing the commit writes (§1 Path): a split commit's path entry for a thing whose members leave it out holds nothing (spec/wire.md §4 field 2). Its level-2 delete key is the commit's one (spec/crypto.md §7).
- **A purge request** is an edit: its commit's `purge.0` part (spec/commits.md §5) on each thing it names, content-class, judged along the path its commit states for that thing.
- **The right**: a delta's purge-by names who may purge it (spec/commits.md §5). A request holds, over each delta it names, each member of that set it holds, judged at the request's key along its path for the delta's thing:
  - **admin** (in every set): the request holds admin;
  - **writer** (in `writer`): the request holds write;
  - **author** (in `author_or_admin` and `writer`): the request holds write, and its path for the thing holds the delta's purge author, as the thing it is signed as or a listed thing.
- **Holding a role**: a request holds role r exactly where a delta of its commit needing r, along its path, at its key would be valid (§5.2 Content): it stands against the cuts of its path below r, each answered by its own list alone, or its path is re-granted for r. A cut whose list does not name the request takes the right however early its `ts`; one that names it leaves the purge standing against that cut; a re-grant gives the right back to a request made in the gap, as to its writes. An addendum vouches for no purge request (§5.2).
- **A purge request** drops a named delta iff it holds a right over it. What it names (spec/wire.md §4 field 8): by author P, the deltas on the aspect stamped before it whose purge author is exactly P, never every delta whose path holds P; by whole aspect, every delta stamped before it; by commit, that one delta, stamped before it. Each named delta is judged alone, so the class of one never widens the others (a writer's request by author leaves that author's `admin` deltas). A request is no edge-class part: no lowering drops it; a cut takes its right as it takes an edit's validity.
- **Copies**: of a delta whose header the node does not hold, the class and purge author are those the held valid snapshots carrying it state (spec/commits.md §5 Purge, Without the header): it is dropped only where the right stands by what every one of them states.
- A node does not sign a purge request that would drop none of the held deltas it names.
- **Judgment** follows the role timeline the node holds, in any arrival order:
  - A named delta a request comes to drop is dropped then.
  - A delta whose writes are gone for a request when no held request drops it any more counts as never received (spec/commits.md §5): its writes are no longer gone, the node fetches it again where present and pulls it when a holder logs it again (spec/sync-judgement.md §3).
  - A snapshot held as its leftover for carrying a purged write (spec/crdt.md §4.7) stays held so with any such fall, without the write, which the write's own body gives back wherever it joins; a copy of the snapshot that arrives replaces it, stripped again of what is still gone. While it lacks a write no longer gone it counts as never received for fetching (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies), its leftover read meanwhile.
  - A body no node holds stays gone; the delta then reads as never received.
- **Destruction** (spec/commits.md §5): a request on `created.0` holds the `admin` right alone (the only purge-by a minting has) and names every delta of the thing, of every aspect, whatever its `ts`. Its right is judged as any request's: a cut of its path on any hop, the destroyed thing's own included, takes it unless the cut's list names it, however late the cut's `ts` (edge writes on a destroyed thing are held whatever their stamp, spec/commits.md §5).
- **Who may undo a purge so**: the writer of a cut of the request's path whose list omits the request. A list may name a message and omit its purge, and the message is back wherever a copy is left.
- A node deletes at peers that keep a sync ID unread on its judgment after syncing, with no waiting time (between readers each judges its own store).
- The same right decides whether a compaction snapshot may drop a body it covers (spec/commits.md §5); a body a right holder dropped under a snapshot whose right later falls stays dropped.
- At a peer that keeps a sync ID unread a delete key proves the right as of the delta and the sync ID's current role keys (spec/sync-ids.md §6), so a holder demoted since can still delete there what its old role allowed, until a writer holding the body logs it again (spec/sync-judgement.md §3; a reader's relay of the deleted blob meets its tombstone).
- **Invalid deltas**: a node holds no delta it judges invalid past the transaction that judged it (spec/commits.md §5 Invalid). It keeps only:
  - a delta of a role commit: the timeline reads every held edge-class part, of any validity (§5.1, spec/commits.md §4), and a sync ID holds the commit whole (a deposit is promoted whole, spec/sync-inbox.md §4);
  - a delta of its own commit: its user is told the write was refused and may write it again from it.
  - An ephemeral delta goes alike. A commit with no part held left and a part so dropped stays known only while an entry of it is (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting, spec/sync-compaction.md §2); no node keeps an entry of a body it judges invalid in its own store, its own included (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store); unstamped entries a source still shows count against the node's bounds on unvouched input (spec/commits.md §4 Pending storage).
  - A delta judged invalid drops as invalid even where a held snapshot covers it, never as covered.
  - A delta dropped so that turns valid again counts as never received and is fetched from any holder; with none it stays gone, at every node alike. Its writer keeps it and logs it again once it judges it valid (spec/sync-judgement.md §3), as does any node that held it valid. The drop is no right's: no node deletes it at peers for it (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- What the right promises is that honest nodes keep the body. Which nodes carry it follows the thing's sync IDs: a thing leaving a sync ID's sync unit leaves its stores whatever its purge-by (spec/sync-judgement.md §2), and holders keep their copies.

## 7. Links
- A **link** consists of K, an independent thing minted with admin S, a thing the sharing node is an admin of, of its choosing (a person's account, §8), with the `link` field in its creation record naming the target (spec/wire.md §3; the field is its creator's word: no node checks it and no authority depends on it; only a joiner's own leave reads it, below), granted the link's role on the target and holding it by a strong in its `holds.0`, and L, the **link key**: a thing minted with the key the link's seed derives, its creation record naming K (spec/wire.md §3), its secrets derived from the seed (spec/crypto.md §3), with a grant (role r, with the may-grant flag) on K for the link's role r, read or above.
  - That thing is K's only admin.
  - A client making the link writes the target's `retain` default then in force on K (§3) when it says retain.
- **Link text** encodes: L's seed, the target tid, K's tid, K's sync ID, and the link's peers: the sharer node's dialable key and relays, and the addresses the sharer node is configured to dial (spec/wire.md §10).
- **First contact**: the redeemer dials the link's peers, each of which serves K's sync ID as at any peer when it holds it: the sharer's node as a peer that reads it, a configured node (one keeping K unread) as one that keeps it (spec/wire.md §10).
  - A link carries only those peers.
  - The redeemer dials each with an ephemeral transport key, finds K through its shared set (the link key's claim at the head, spec/sync-opens.md §4) and opens K's sync ID there as at any peer. Once its join is held it keeps the link's peers, not the seed, as addresses to dial (spec/sync-opens.md §1 Where to dial) while an own thing holds a role on the target.
  - The link's peers are the sharer's explicit choice; a link without them reaches the redeemer only through peers it already shares K with. The sharer's node serves K's sync ID and the target's from its own store (spec/sync-ids.md §5).
- L holds boxes in K's `secrets.0` like any holder, and every commit boxing it carries its entry (spec/sync-ids.md §3), sealed to the KEM key its seed's read secret derives. The sharer makes L in the transaction granting it on K, and holds it as K's holder, by no strong (spec/sync-set.md §2 Making a thing).
- **Redeem**:
  1. Derive L's keys from the seed.
  2. Ask Head of K's sync ID, open it by L's claim at the head (spec/sync-opens.md §4), take K's secrets by L's entry (spec/sync-ids.md §3), pull K's sync ID and L's boxes.
  3. Wait until the held role history shows K's grant on the target (a client making a link delivers that grant under K's sync ID), and grant nothing without it.
  4. Then, signing as L, grant the **joiner** (the thing the redeemer joins for: an own thing or a thing its own things administer, such as its account) by may-grant (§2) to K with role r and seal K's secrets to it. L writes in commit form and deposits the commit under K's sync ID (spec/sync-inbox.md §3) unless r is write or above.
  - A redeemer keeps the seed until its grant is at a source of K's sync ID (a peer holds it, or the sharer's node applied it), proving L on every connection to the sharer's node until then, and SHOULD discard it then.
  - A thing holding K by a grant (a join by any node acting as it) writes no other: a redeem of the link then is done at once, and one under way stops.
  - A rotation of K by a holder's departure (a joiner's leave, a removal) changes nothing for the link: L stays a holder and the rotation carries its entry.
  - The link has ended, and the redeemer stops there, when either holds:
    - every route of K's sync ID (the link's node and the peers it shares K with, as known) has failed to open it, at least one by refusing L's claim;
    - a held role history shows L's grant, or K's on the target, removed or past `until`.
  - A redeemer not joined MAY stop after a time of its node's choosing (spec/sync-inbox.md §6).
  - A redeemer that stops, for any reason, forgets the seed and so loses read access to K and what K holds (§3).
- **Joiner's leave**: a holder of K by a grant other than L and K's mint admin leaves K (a self-removal, §2) once a held role history shows K's edge on the target that field names removed or past `until`; K's edge on any other thing ends nothing. A node acting as it MUST write it once it holds that history. A grant of K again brings back no joiner.
- `until` on L's grant expires the link. **Single use** (the link's `flags` bit 0, spec/wire.md §10) = the redemption also removes L's own grant in the same commit (a self-removal).
- **Finalized**: L's grant past `until` is finalized like any ended grant (§2), by any writer of K: that removal rotates K and puts nothing in question. Besides, an expired or self-removed L is finalized by a sharer node writing an explicit removal of L (§5.1), which puts L's may-grant grants in question: a node that holds the link's seed thing and admin on K MUST write it once L's edge expired or was self-removed (or finalized) with no explicit write on it since, after a complete pass of K's sync IDs (spec/sync-status.md §1) that ended, for an expiry, after `until` (it waits for that pass however long it takes).
  - A may-grant grant as L that the removal's frontier does not name is dropped (§5.1 step 3); one it names stands.
- The sharer's link seed thing is minted with admin K's mint admin S, in the transaction making the link, with strongs from S's `holds.0` to the seed thing and to K. The explicit removal of L from K (above, or a revoke) removes S's strong to the seed thing in the same transaction, so the seed thing goes (spec/sync-set.md §3). A removal from S does not end a link: the removed party reads through L until L is removed from K. An implementation MUST tell whoever removes a holder of S which of S's links still work, and let any node acting as S remove their L.
- **Bearer use**: act as L without writing anything. Read only.
- A joiner that may still hold the seed is revoked only together with L, both removals in one commit with one rotation of K (spec/commits.md §2); a new link is a new L.
- The sharer keeps the seed on the seed thing (above: aspect `link:seed.0`, a map {0: K's tid, 1: the seed, 2: the target's tid, 3: single use}) and writes L's `keys.0` and K's deposit seed.

## 8. Accounts
An account is an arrangement of ordinary things that a library above core makes; no rule of core reads it, and nothing below is a rule any node judges.
- An **account** is an independent thing A. A node acts for it as its node thing N (spec/sync-set.md §1) holding a grant on A: admin for a node that may pair and remove, else write. Its commits for A are signed as N and state paths through A (§1 Path: [A], [A, B] for a thing B A holds a role on). A node keeping accounts unlinked has a node thing and key per account.
- **Making**: the node mints A with N as mint admin and writes a strong to A in its node root's `holds.0`, naming A in its `settings^.0` (spec/sync-set.md §1), in one transaction with A's inbox (spec/sync-inbox.md §1) and profile (spec/sync-inbox.md §5 Contact card), things minted with admin A, held by strongs from A's `holds.0`, the inbox enabling both signals, A's minting naming the inbox in A's `keys.0`. It then writes A's card on the profile (spec/sync-inbox.md §1; A and the inbox become independent, §3) and grants the profile the public read. Things made for the account are minted with admin A, its dependents, held by strongs from A; so are its links' K (§7).
- **Pairing** a node: the new node shows its node thing's public key record to a node of A holding admin on A, over a channel the two nodes' users provide. That node writes the new node thing's grant on A (§2, boxing A's secrets to it) and hands the new node, over the same channel, A's locator (spec/wire.md §10: A, its sync ID, the nodes to dial). The new node writes a strong to A in its node root's `holds.0`, naming A in its `settings^.0`, and redeems the link for its node root (spec/sync-set.md §4): it enters A at any reader of A, its grant's notice (§4 Notices) giving it A's secrets. It takes sides in disputes on A by §5.1 Sides as their commits reach it.
- **Removing** a node: the explicit removal of its node thing's grant on A, with its list and A's rotation (§3; spec/crypto.md §4 Holder actors: what A holds roles on re-keys as A's keysets go stale), saying erase unless its user says otherwise; the remover also removes the node's `dial.0` entry (keyed by its node thing) on A and on A's profile and its entries on A in `caps^.0` (spec/sync-set.md §1). The removed node learns its removal from any reader of A it syncs with, which serves it A's role entries up to the removal's rotation (spec/sync-opens.md §6); holding no grant of write or admin on A, it acts for no account (spec/sync-set.md §1 Settings) and may make one.
