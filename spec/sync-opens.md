# Sync: connections and opens
> Summary: Area 3: whom a node dials and when, connection proofs and the policy on whom to dial, prove to and sync with, the pair's record and agreement, the shared set (the one open rule, credentials, filters, Offer) and how an open ends.

**Principle:** each side opens S toward a peer only on its own proof, judged on what it holds now, that the peer may hold S; who dialed proves nothing. Connecting never depends on what the other side holds: first a connection, then the shared set.

## 1. Connections
- A node connects to peers below this layer (spec/sync-transport.md); this section says only whom it dials and when.
- **Where to dial** (addresses are priorities, never a filter on what opens): its own nodes (`reach.0` under home), contacts' nodes (their profile's `dial^.0`, spec/sync-set.md §1), the granting node of each grant it holds (the inbox blob's hint, spec/sync-inbox.md §2), a link's peers (spec/sync-inbox.md §6), configured addresses, and every node the transport discovers (spec/sync-transport.md §4). Nothing else is learned from other accounts' data: a reader dials a node keeping S unread only at an address it knows so.
- **Whom to dial**: the nodes of accounts that hold sync or above on a sync ID it reads (a role on its thing, directly or through the actors holding it, by the rosters it holds), the granter of what it keeps unread (the newest grant's hint, kept while it keeps the thing), its own nodes while their sync sets intersect (another node's synced group, as it walks it, holds the thing), and discovered nodes; within `max_peers` (local policy), which counts connected nodes that share something, never candidates.
- **Keep or close**: a connection a node dialed stays while its shared set has a member, an Offer or Lost is unanswered, a deposit to it may land (one out, ready or refused for now), or the peer may still answer what this side sent; else the dialer closes it. The peer may still answer until a Ping the dialer sent with all of this already so comes back with no other frame before it. A listener closes none for sharing nothing (spec/sync-transport.md §5).
- **Redial**: a node not connected to a peer it would dial dials it again `REDIAL_MS` (local policy) after its last dial of that peer; one whose connection it closed sharing nothing (Keep or close) only after a change this node knows of that could make the peer share something (a new sync ID, credential, role or address), and no sooner than `REDIAL_MS` after its last dial. A peer known at several addresses is dialed at the one learned at the best priority, and after a failed dial at once at the next of its others, in turn; once each has failed in a turn the rule above applies. A new address of the peer, or acting for an actor its connection did not prove (below), dials it at once. Due dials go by the priority of where the peer was learned, then the longest undialed first. At 0 a failing peer is dialed at every event and a quiet one at every change; at infinity a peer is dialed again only at a new address. Opens are never retried: they are judged again at each change (§6).
- **Dial timeout**: a dial not up `DIAL_TIMEOUT_MS` (local policy) after it started, whatever steps the transport takes, closes as failed; the redial rule then applies. At 0 no dial comes up; at infinity one silent route or peer stalls that peer for good.
- **One connection per pair**: two connections of one pair, whoever dialed each: of one record, twinned (below), or on both of which the peer proved the same node (another node of this account, §2). When one side dialed both, the one it dialed later stays (a node dials each key at most once at a time, so the earlier is one it gave up: its close not yet arrived, or dialed by an earlier run of the node). Otherwise the one of better route rank (spec/sync-transport.md §1, both ends see the same) stays; among two of one rank the one with the smaller `BLAKE3(key = r, dialer challenge || listener challenge)` stays, `r` each connection's own pair key. The other closes, at both ends, whatever order they arrived in; a twinned one's record goes with it at both ends, and each member it held is re-marked (§5 Testing) for the record that stays. A node whose dialed connection closed so does not dial that peer while the other lasts. Limit: two connections on which either side proved no actor to the other keep no record and are not told apart: both stay. A node dials each key at most once at a time.
- **Twin**: two nodes that dial each other at once, neither Resume listing a record the other holds, make two records (§3). A dialer that makes a new record on reading the listener's answer sends `Twin` (spec/wire.md §11.2) on each other connection it holds whose record names an actor the new record names, its tag `BLAKE3(key = r, "twin" || listener static key || dialer challenge || listener challenge)`: `r` the new record's pair key, the static key and challenges those of the connection the Twin goes on. The receiver answers `Twinned` with `BLAKE3(key = r, "twinned" || listener static key || dialer challenge || listener challenge)`, same inputs, when `r` is the pair key of the record of another connection it holds, naming an actor the record of the connection the Twin came on names; else with none. The two connections are twinned at the receiver when it answers so, and at the sender when it reads that answer.
- A node never dials its own key. A connection proves once, at its start: a node that comes to act for an actor it did not prove on a connection where it proved its own (a link key held since), or for its account at an epoch other than the one it proved there (a rotation of its account taken, its seed held), closes it, and a dialer dials again.
- **Policy**: which nodes a node dials, proves its actors to and syncs with is its account's choice, overridable per node (local policy, spec/requirements.md Offline and network). A node may skip a dial candidate; prove no actor on a connection (its Prove or Proved then carries none, as dialer before anything is proven, as listener once the dialer's proofs are judged); or close a connection once the peer's proofs are judged. By default it dials every candidate above, proves to every peer and syncs with every peer.
- Proofs and shared sets are run only after the peer's Hello; a connection up but without the peer's Hello is no peer yet (and the node is not idle while it waits, spec/sync-status.md §3).

## 2. Connection proofs
- Each side proves the actors it acts for over the connection's challenges (bytes: spec/wire.md §11.2 Prove, Proved), as its policy allows (§1):
  - its account by a signature at an epoch, with the signed rotations of that account the verifier lacks; a prover missing newer rotations of its own account learns them by syncing its account like any data. A verifier takes those rotations as received commits, all of them judged together; as nothing carries them, it keeps only those that count once all are taken and, while no rotation it held before is in a rejection with a counting one (spec/permissions.md §5.4, Warning), the tips of the lines the Warning then names, each with the rotations it needs held; every other one (one that would pend among them) is kept nowhere. Both sides judge them by spec/permissions.md §5.4 alone, no timestamp read. A removed node keeps the seed of the epoch its removal's rotation closes: it proves the account only to a verifier that does not hold that rotation;
  - a key actor (a link key) by its signing key, matched to a static entry the verifier holds;
  - its own node key only to nodes of its own account: a dialer when the key it dials is, by the `reach.0` it holds, a node holding a role on its account's devices actor D; a listener when the dialer proved its account or such a node. A verifier takes a node proof as the peer's node only when it is such a node of its own account; a node of another account is the account it proves.
- An account's proof counts for an account this node holds nothing of at that account's first key (its creation record's); a key actor proved on a connection is the key actor of a static entry its Hit names whether this node held anything of it or not (§5).
- **Order**: the dialer proves first, after the listener's Hello; the listener answers with its own proofs. Each side proves once a connection, at its start.
- Nothing a connection proves comes from group data; a dial list is for dialing only, never proof.

## 3. Records and agreement
- **Pair key**: two nodes that each proved an actor to the other keep one record of each other, found by a 32-byte pair key `r` known only to the two. No node key finds a record.
  - After `Proved` a dialer the listener proved an actor to sends `Resume` (spec/wire.md §11.2): a fresh nonce and, for each of its records naming a proven actor of this session (most recent first), `BLAKE3(key = r, "resume" || listener static key || dialer challenge || listener challenge)`, challenges as sent in Hello.
  - A record is honoured only while a proven actor of this session is named by it: an account proved at an epoch open here, a node, or a key actor.
  - A record names what its last session's proofs counted for, the account with the key it proved with. What the peer is trusted as (J, §4; a report it makes), for a record kept or a connection up, is judged on what this node holds now: the account while that key is at an epoch open here, the node while it is a node of this node's account, each key actor. Who it may be, for waiting on it (spec/sync-set.md §3, spec/sync-status.md §1), is the node it names while that is a node of this node's account, else the account it names at the key it proved with until a rotation carrying a removal supersedes that key's epoch (spec/sync-set.md §3 Leaving), and each key actor: a node removed from this node's account is no one to wait for; a node of another account whose epoch closed by a rotation carrying no removal is still that account (back, it proves the new key and resumes its record).
  - The listener answers `BLAKE3(key = r, "resumed" || listener static key || dialer challenge || listener challenge)` of the first listed record it holds, else none. An absent or unexpected answer finds nothing.
  - On no match, both sides make a new record with `r = kdf("pair key", dialer nonce, listener nonce)`, written before the listener's answer goes (listener) or when the answer is read (dialer).
  - The listener sends its answer before any message of the connection's shared set.
  - A connection on which either side proved no actor to the other keeps no record; its pair key is `kdf("pair key", dialer challenge, listener challenge)`, kept nowhere. A dialer the listener proved no actor to sends NoRecord in place of Resume; a listener the dialer proved no actor to answers Resume Error 1, and the dialer then sends NoRecord. On NoRecord the listener runs the set under that pair key.
  - `r` and the nonces cross only inside the connection's own messages. Two connections to one peer use one record (§1).
- A **record** keeps: the shared set (each member with the credential class and beacon it was opened by, none for an open by J, its `until` while it is being ended, and where it is in the stream: at the stream's cursor, or joining from its own position), `acked_pos`, this side's last filter generation sent and acknowledged with its whole entries and additions since, the peer's latest filter, the stream's cursor, and what this node read of the peer as a source (presence of entries per member, the deletes it saw there and its logged-again-once state, spec/sync-judgement.md §3-§4, the source's step, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk, and the peer budget charges of what the peer brought, spec/sync-ids.md §5). While a connection holds the record it also keeps, for each member that closed on it, where that member stopped and what its source showed (§6 Where it stopped); these go when the connection ends. A connection that takes a record starts from all of these; a session proving other actors than the record names starts with every credential pending and a whole filter.
- What a node keeps of a peer lives in their record and nowhere else: no row is keyed by a node key, and a record dropped (evicted under `RECORD_BUDGET`, least recently used first, spec/wire.md §2) drops everything of that peer with it. A peer without a record is read from the start at each connection.
- **`changed_at`**: a per-sync-ID position of the node, advanced when S is new to it or a credential of S changes. A node that stops holding S drops S from every record in the step that stops it.
- **Agreement** (bytes: spec/wire.md §11.2 Digest, Members): on a connection with a record, each side's first message after `Resumed` is `Digest`, the xor over the record's shared set of `BLAKE3(key = r, sync_id)`, computed from the record before anything else on the connection. A side compares the peer's digest with the one it sent, once a connection.
  - On a difference, each side sends its set as `Members` pages on reading the peer's `Digest`, before acting on any later message. On the peer's last page it closes each member only it holds, keeping nothing of where it stopped (§6), and re-marks pending (§5 Testing) every sync ID in exactly one of the two lists.
  - No Offer is sent before the peer's `Digest` is read, and no Pull before the digests matched or the peer's last `Members` page is read.

## 4. Shared set
- The **shared set** of a connection: the sync IDs the two sides share on it, judged by each side on what it holds now. A side keeping no shared set on a connection yet (before a record or NoRecord, §3) answers its shared-set requests Error 14 (spec/wire.md §11.2).
- **The rule**: S is in a connection's shared set exactly while each side syncs S (spec/sync-set.md §2) and holds, judged by itself on what it holds now, proof that the other may hold S. Proof is one of:
  - **J**: an actor the peer proved on this connection holds sync or above on S's thing, by the rosters held here;
  - a **secret credential** the peer showed on this connection: a read secret of S's thing, or a sync proof (spec/sync-ids.md §2) of S's head sync key;
  - an **open signature** by a credential key of the newest step of S this side knows (its sync key, a claim of its claim set, or a key actor's static entry listed there), bound to the signer's side and this connection: under `"osis v1 sync open"`, digest per spec/wire.md §11.2 over (the signer's side, sync ID, the other side's Hello challenge, the listener's static key).
- **Departed**: a side MUST NOT open S toward a peer J judges departed (below) by any credential at an epoch that does not cover every departure it knows of (spec/crypto.md §4), whatever credential the peer shows; until this side holds such an epoch, nothing opens toward that peer.
- A tag on a public value proves nothing; which side dialed proves nothing; a step older than the newest the judge knows proves nothing.
- **J** of S for a connection, judged on the rosters held here: **reader** if a proven actor holds S's read role; **sync holder** if a proven actor holds sync on it and none read; **departed** if a proven actor once held a role on S's thing and none holds one now; else **unknown**. A node keeping S unread holds no roster of S: J is unknown there and it judges no departure (until the rotation a departed holder still holds the current sync secret there). A node key proves no account: only the account's own proof at an open epoch counts as the account. Opens take reader or sync holder; ByRole is reader-only.
- **Admissible** credentials of S at a node, computed per connection from what it holds, never on a peer's word, in two lists: those it **shows** (its filter, §5) and those it **tests** (its Hits). The **read secrets** below are its read secrets of S's thing at a current epoch (spec/crypto.md §4; with none current, the newest held; for a sync ID an inbox blob named, before its `sync.0` is held, the secret its box gave).
  - reading S: shows its read secrets, the static entries of the key actors S's roster lists, and the sync proof of the newest recorded step's sync key; tests its read secrets, the static entries of the key actors it acts for (its link or card), and, toward a source keeping S unread, the walk's credentials of that source's step (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk);
  - keeping S unread: its **step** is its head while a sync secret of the thing it holds derives the head's sync key; it shows that sync key, its sync proof, the head's deposit key and every key of the head's claim set (claims and static entries). Otherwise (no head, none registered yet, or a head past its keys, spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops) each sync secret it holds whose sync key no step of its path bears (with no head, every one) is a step, at none: it shows their sync keys and sync proofs, nothing else. It tests its steps' sync keys and their sync proofs. S is still held with none shown, so its arrival in the sync set and each head move go out as a filter generation;
  - none where J is departed, unless at an epoch covering every departure (Departed above); unknown blocks nothing.
- A **source** is a member of the shared set. Its class is the credential that opened it: a **store** source (opened by its head's sync key, a sync proof by a node keeping S unread, a claim or a static entry: the peer keeps S unread, so this node may Register, Delete and deposit there, spec/sync-ids.md §6), or a **reader** source (J reader, a read secret, ByRole).
- Per-sync-ID walk state (spec/sync-ids.md §6) exists on a connection exactly while S is a member by a store credential or J judges the peer a sync holder. A node keeping S unread walks and registers nothing. Limit: a reader that dialed a node keeping S unread, not knowing that node's account, opens it at the same sync key only; one behind under another key is moved where it dials, or by its granter.

## 5. Filters, testing and Offer
(bytes: spec/wire.md §11.2 Beacons, Offer, Lost, Shared, NoRecord)
- **Beacon**: `BLAKE3(key = kdf("beacon", match, S), r)`, `match` the credential's value (the read secret, the static entry, the raw sync or deposit public key, or the proof). A filter entry is the first `f` bits of a beacon, `f = ceil(log2(n)) + 8` for a whole filter of n entries.
- A **filter** of beacons of the shown credentials goes to each connection, whoever the peer proved (spec/sync-hostile.md §2). A side sends one generation at a time, the next after the last is answered (an Error answering it, or an Offer, is a refusal of it; a refused generation is followed, after `resync_ms`, by a whole one): the first whole, later ones additions (`base` the last generation the peer acknowledged, on this connection or an earlier one of the record) of the sync IDs whose credentials changed, sent also when they add no entry; a whole one replaces additions once they pass its entries. On a connection with a record the first generation is additions of the sync IDs whose `changed_at` is past `acked_pos`, sent even when there are none, unless the peer acknowledged no generation of the record or not the last one sent (then it is whole). The receiver keeps the peer's latest filter in the record; one that does not fit `RECORD_BUDGET` is tested and kept for the connection only (the peer's next additions on it name a base not held, answered 13, and a whole one follows). Nothing is ever removed from a stored filter.
- **Testing**: a side tests the beacons of its tested credentials of the sync IDs whose `changed_at` is past `acked_pos` against the peer's latest filter, at each filter arrival (before acting on later messages), at each change of what it holds, and after an unanswered Offer is answered, with at most one Offer unanswered. `acked_pos` rises, durably in the record, only with no Offer unanswered, to the lower of the position the peer's last acknowledged generation reached and the position tested. **Re-marking** S for a record (a `Lost` received for a member open here, a digest difference, the close of a member open here, a twinned record going with S its member, §1) lowers its `acked_pos` and tested position below `changed_at(S)` and makes a test due; no other record changes. A sync ID outside this node's sync set has no `changed_at`: re-marking it changes nothing. A Hit that opened nothing here (its box or grant failed, its credential no longer admissible) is tried again on that connection only once something re-marks S or moves `changed_at(S)`.
- **Offer**: Hits first, then ByRole for S still not open when the Offer goes (one Offer may carry a Hit and a ByRole on S); Deposit items (spec/sync-inbox.md §3) at any time after the peer's `Digest` is read. A refusal of a Hit, Static or ByRole item is one code (14), never a reason; Deposit and Behind items are answered with their own codes (spec/sync-inbox.md §3, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Behind).
- **Hit**: `BLAKE3` keyed by `kdf("beacon hit", match || S)` over the concatenation, as for Resume (§3), of a side byte (0 the dialer, 1 the listener), the listener's static key, the dialer's Hello challenge, the listener's Hello challenge and the beacon: it names no sync ID and is bound to its sender's side and this connection. Its **answer** (`Shared`) carries a tag keyed `kdf("beacon reply", match || S)` over the same concatenation with the replier's side byte.
- **Proof** a Hit carries of its credential (§4): for a read secret or a sync proof, the Hit itself (keyed by the secret); for a sync key or a claim, an open signature by it; for a static entry, a proven actor of this connection that is the entry's key actor, or, in a Static item (spec/wire.md §11.2), that key actor's signing key with its open signature. A deposit key's Hit carries none and opens nothing (answered 14).
- **Proof** an answer carries: for a read secret or a sync proof, its tag (keyed by the secret); for a sync key, a claim or a static entry, an open signature (`sig`) by the sync key of the replier's **step**: at a node keeping S unread (§4), the step whose sync key was hit, else its head; its newest recorded step at a reader; for a static entry, the head `grant` names. A side accepts such a Hit only while it holds the sync secret of that step.
- **Opening**: each side opens S on its own proof (§4). The receiver accepts a Hit that is one of its shown credentials its filter to this peer shows, carrying its proof, and opens S; the session learns of the open before the answer goes, so a request right after it is served. The tester opens S on the answer's valid tag, its credential still admissible, and its own proof of the replier (J, or the answer's proof). One Hit and its answer open S both ways.
  - For a static entry the answer also carries the head's entry box for that entry and the path to the head, from what the replier keeps; the tester takes the box's secrets, and opens, only when the path verifies from its genesis step, the box's sync secret derives the head's sync key (spec/sync-ids.md §3) and `sig` verifies under it; it opens nothing on an answer that fails.
  - **ByRole** `[S, [Hit*]]` for S a side holds that J judges the peer reads: the receiver opens S when it holds S and J is reader, or a listed Hit is its own of a read secret or a static entry, carrying its proof; an accepted ByRole opens S at its sender while J is still reader there.
  - Once all its Offer's answers are read, a tester ends with `Lost` (§6) each S the peer accepted (a Hit with a valid tag, or a ByRole) and it did not open, unless another Hit on S opened it. An acceptance without a valid tag names no S back: the peer showed no knowledge of S.

## 6. Ending opens
- A member is re-judged at each change of what this node holds that touches S (its credentials, head or sync set), and when a connection takes a record; nothing else re-judges it.
  - A member J judges reader or sync holder stays; one opened by a credential stays while that credential is admissible.
  - A member opened by a credential no longer admissible, whose peer J judges neither holder nor departed, is **ended**: it gets `until`, the highest position at which an entry in S of a rotation superseding that credential's epoch was first logged, else the node log's end, judged in the step that applies the change, before any PullReply or Stream of it, for every record holding the member whether connected or not. An ended member is served only up to `until` (spec/sync-streams.md §4) and closes with a `Lost` naming `until` once its reported position is at or past `until`.
  - At a node keeping S unread, a member stays while its credential is one of its steps' (§4), and is otherwise ended with `until` the position of its head's event when judged, before the node answers anything after the head moved. So an open by a sync key ends at the head's event.
  - Any other (J departed, S left the sync set) closes at once with a `Lost`. The event that ends sharing is still sent.
  - A member once ended stays ended until it closes, also when judged holder or admissible again (it opens again by testing).
- **Lost**: a sync ID the sender no longer shares here, with where it stopped for the receiver: the receiver's **reported position** for it, or the member's `until` where lower (none where it reported none); a Lost for S that left the sender's sync set names none. A reported position is one the receiver stored: on a connection, the first Pull's cursor and each join's position in the first Pull naming it, then the cursor of each Applied, each as of the message that position covered (a join's own position until the message merging it is covered; a member opened after the first Pull has none until a join names it; after a reply from the start, a member at its own position follows the stream once that reply is covered); reported positions last the connection.
  - A node receiving a Lost for a member open there closes it and answers with its own Lost naming where that member stopped for the sender; a Lost naming no member open at its receiver is not answered. A member is closed (back to testing) before its `Lost` goes. A `Lost` is never sent again; one lost with its connection is found by the next digest.
- **Where it stopped**: each side keeps, for a member closed on a connection, where it stopped (its rejoin there) and what its source showed of S (presence, head and refusals, spec/sync-judgement.md §3, spec/sync-streams.md §4), only while that connection lasts:
  - a side that closed the member at its `until` keeps where its own stream holds it, until the peer's Lost for it arrives;
  - the peer's Lost, received for a member open here or one whose stop this side keeps, sets where it stopped to the position it names, and with none keeps nothing;
  - a side that closed the member at once (J departed, S left its sync set) or for a digest difference keeps nothing, whatever Lost follows.
  - A pull of S from the start at that source meanwhile (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full and Refused, spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops) stands over where it stopped.
  - With nothing kept, what its source showed of S goes at once and its rejoin is from the start. When the connection ends (a restart included), every kept stop and what its source showed go. A node that stops holding S sends a Lost naming S and none on each connection keeping S's stop.
