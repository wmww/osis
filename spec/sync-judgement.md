# Sync: the judgement per sync ID
> Summary: Area 4: what goes under a sync ID, the required set and the must-hold / may-hold / must-not-hold judgement, how a reader acts on it in its own store and at peers keeping S unread, the bounded "logged again once" rule, destruction and emptying, and what a node keeping S unread does instead.

**Principle:** a reader's store of S holds what its own judgement says S needs, and it acts on a peer only over what that peer's stream showed; every delete and every logging again is bounded by a change of ground (a new head at that peer, or a held part falling here). A node keeping S unread judges nothing but signatures (§6).

Consequences, each a rule below: a node's own store follows its own judgement alone, so it needs no delete key to drop an entry from it (§3); between readers nothing but streams passes, so no Delete is sent between readers and no reader's view is decided by another's word (§3); a delete at a peer is sent only for a reason every holder sees after one exchange, and only once that peer took what this node logs for it (§3); a disagreement the two cannot settle (a cover one side lacks or hides) costs at most one more logging per change of ground (§4).

## 1. What goes under a sync ID
- **Entitlement**: a thing's **reader actor** is the thing if it has secrets of its own, else the nearest actor up its record chain that has them (a key actor is its own). **S's actor** is S's thing, the thing whose home sync ID S is; S's **closure** is that thing's closure (spec/sync-set.md §2). S's actor may read a thing iff the thing's secrets derive from its own or it holds read or above on the thing's reader actor. A commit's blob belongs under S iff S's actor may read a part of it on a thing of the closure (or published there, spec/sync-ids.md §4).
  - Only a writer of S stamps entries (spec/sync-ids.md §6).
  - A node that is not one deposits what it must log there (its account's chain certificates, rotations and thing lists, its invite grant or self-removal, its listings, its history adds (spec/permissions.md §3), and the revokes it must place by spec/permissions.md §4) and relays or deposits the must-hold bodies it holds (spec/sync-ids.md §2).
- **One blob per commit**: a commit is logged as one blob holding every part of it the logging node holds and can seal, each part in the section of its thing under the thing's read secret (spec/wire.md §4; a part of a thing whose read secret that node lacks, a key actor's own record or card, travels as a certificate copy). Every sync ID of every thing the commit touches carries the one blob under its sync ID's wrap; nodes syncing several of those sync IDs dedup it by blob ID. No size split: a commit stays within `MAX_BLOB` by paging its deltas (spec/crdt.md §8), largest first, or is refused at the writer.
  - A blob's **body** under a sync ID is the parts of it S's actor may read: a node judges what a sync ID needs, holds, lacks or had deleted by bodies (spec/sync-ids.md §2). A delta it holds that the actor may not read still counts for the blob itself: one the node dropped makes the blob must-not-hold for it (spec/sync-ids.md §2), so two blobs are copies of each other only with the same delta set (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Duplicates). A section the node cannot open it copies verbatim when it encrypts the commit again (a tombstoned entry, a purge rewrite).
  - A section opens with the thing's read secret at the epoch the writer used: a reader of the thing holds it (its own, through its history boxes; a plain thing's by derivation through the record chain the section lists from a member it holds epochs of), and a holder of a role on the thing's reader actor keeps up with its rotations through that actor's sync ID (spec/sync-ids.md §2).
  - **Per-sync-ID copies**: besides certificate copies (below), a sync ID whose readers could not open a part's section in the shared blob gets a fresh blob of the parts its actor may read, one section under its blob key (spec/wire.md §4), never shared with another sync ID; judged like any body of the commit. Exactly two cases: a role commit with such a part on a thing other than S's actor (its boxes are what give the readers that thing's secrets: a rotation, a grant, a cross-published `sync.0`, spec/sync-ids.md §4); a part on a thing whose read secret derives from neither S's actor nor an actor in its closure that is not destroyed (a cross-published `created.0`, §2; a thing added from another actor's tree, read by role on a reader actor whose rotations the sync ID does not carry; a dependent of a destroyed actor, whose boxes went with it, spec/commits.md §5 Destruction). Only a copy holds such a body there: a shared blob of the same parts neither holds nor carries it, and goes as the copy's duplicate (spec/sync-compaction.md §1). A section that opens under its blob key is a copy's (this one or a certificate copy): it is never copied into another encryption.
- **Retag**: when a thing joins a group, any writer of the sync ID that holds the thing's live blobs adds them under it; when it leaves, delete-key holders delete them under the old sync ID as they see the change (spec/sync-compaction.md §1).
- **Certificate blobs**: chain certificates, accounts' rotations and things' witnesses and destroying requests (header-only copies), list blobs, record blobs and revokes (spec/permissions.md §4, §5.5): copies of the parts of a role commit about one actor, thing lists, or raw creation records (spec/wire.md §3).
  - A chain's parts go as certificate copies under a sync ID exactly where its bodies do not carry them: on a thing outside its closure, or one its actor may not read.
  - A certificate copy is one section of its parts under its blob key (spec/wire.md §4; a header-only copy has none). A node logging the same parts of a commit under several sync IDs logs one blob, wrapped under each; parts that differ per sync ID are separate blobs. One a peer refuses under a sync ID as tombstoned (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Refused) goes there again as a fresh encryption. List and record blobs are a fresh encryption per sync ID.
  - The chunk and tree-node blobs of a linked frontier or header list go under it too, as the same blobs (a link names them) with a filler wrap.
  - A writer promoting a deposited commit completes its author's chain under the sync ID from what it holds (spec/sync-ids.md §6).
- **List blobs**: a blob carrying thing lists of one rotation (spec/permissions.md §5.5; bytes spec/wire.md §4, List tree), wrapped per sync ID like a commit, with no expiry and no commitments, fetched first (order 0). Under a sync ID it carries only thing lists of things in the sync ID's closure or touched by a commit logged there as a certificate. A node holds a list blob's thing lists once it holds its rotation; one opened beside the rotation (in one batch) is held as the rotation applies. A non-writer deposits it; a writer promotes it once each thing list in it verifies against the root of a rotation it holds.
- **Unjudged blobs**: a receiver keeps blobs a peer logged under a sync ID even if its own closure disagrees, while it cannot judge them: a commit it holds pending (not parked) or an entry of the sync ID it can open and has not applied, an entry awaiting a record included, within its bounds on unjudged input (spec/commits.md §4 Pending storage); then §6 decides.

## 2. Required set and judgement
S's **required set** is its closure blobs (§1) plus what is logged there beside them:
- the certificate blobs of spec/permissions.md §4, judged by the deltas a blob holds, whatever else its commit writes, a list blob by its thing lists, a record blob by its records (never must-not-hold but under an emptied sync ID, below: no delete key covers it); a header-only copy of a minting its thing's witness supersedes, by spec/commits.md §5 (A thing's mintings travel with its record); a destroyed thing's certificates (its frozen edges and requests' parts outside the closure, header-only copies of its mintings and requests anywhere) by the sync ID's things needing it (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction);
- the grantee's certificate copies and cross-published `sync.0` commits (§1; spec/sync-inbox.md §5);
- unexpired inbox blobs.

Per node, a body it holds is **must-hold**, **must-not-hold** or **may-hold** at a sync ID. A body meeting a must-not-hold condition is must-not-hold whatever else holds of it.
- A delta is **kept** while it is before its keep-at-least bound (in history by the settings the node holds, spec/commits.md §5 The setting). One out of history is neither must-hold nor fetched; a node that dropped it fetches it none when a later setting puts it back in history.
- **must-hold**: in the required set and, for a closure body, one of:
  - a delta of it kept, whatever snapshot covers it;
  - established uncovered: the node can join it, no held snapshot of the aspect under the sync ID covers it (spec/commits.md §5), and the sync ID holds no snapshot of the aspect that this node does not hold (forgotten, dropped) stamped later than the body (one stamped no later never saw it, spec/crdt.md §2.2); a signal's delta is never joined, so it is must-hold only while kept.
- **Header-only blob**: in the required set only as a certificate (above) or as the lasting header of a commit lasting on a thing of the closure whose actor the sync ID's reads (spec/commits.md §5), with no other body of the commit wanted there, and then is must-hold, whatever snapshot covers it. One of a commit with a permanent delta or a purge request that is not lasting here is may-hold, but on a thing destroyed by release alone (no header of it lasts): another node may judge it lasting.
- **must-not-hold**, any of:
  - purged;
  - expired (its blob's prefix expiry, spec/sync-ids.md §1, whatever of it the sync ID's actor reads);
  - outside the required set, judged only while no group of the closure holds a part of its `group.0` as a header only (spec/crdt.md §5.5): what that part lists may be in the closure;
  - a blob of commit C whose delta set is a strict subset of another held blob of C under that sync ID that is not itself must-not-hold and whose extra deltas include one only a blob carries: kept or holding a live write, and covered by no snapshot under the sync ID, established as for must-hold (not while an entry under the sync ID is being fetched or pending);
  - under D's home sync ID, another node's card commit (spec/permissions.md §4) once a later valid card commit of that node writing the same aspect is under the sync ID (an entry of it this node opened and checked against its header);
  - under D's home sync ID, another node's card commit, for `node.0` and `reach.0`, once that node is a node of the account no more after an applied edge write on D ended its grant (removed it, or its `until` passed).
  - any entry under a sync ID of a destroyed thing the node keeps nothing of, once its destruction is told (§5).
- **may-hold**: the rest: covered, out of history, parked, unfetched pages, a certificate blob whose author has no other blob under the sync ID.

## 3. Acting on the judgement
- **Driven by events**: an entry is judged when an event touches it: it is logged, pulled, fetched, deleted, expires or is refused somewhere; a commit, snapshot, purge or role change on its aspects is held or falls here; its thing's closure changes; a source's stream shows it, shows it gone or refuses it. A new source is judged entry by entry as its stream's events arrive. Judging is to a fixpoint: a judgement that changes the closure or a held part re-judges what that change touches, and nothing else.
- **Own store**: while a reader can pull it (it publishes `reach.0` or a connection shares S), a node logs in its own store what S must hold of its own (its must-hold bodies, its certificates), whatever peers are connected, stamped when it writes S. Of the entries in its store it drops what its judgement finds must-not-hold (a body with live writes only once a carrier is under S, spec/sync-compaction.md §1), or what expired, whatever delete keys it holds: delete keys only authorize deletes at other nodes.
- **What a source holds** (`known`): the entries its stream showed present and not removed since, and nothing else; a write's acknowledgement, a send or a deposit counts for nothing until the stream shows it. A source showing an entry held here marks presence only. Ephemeral entries have no log position (spec/sync-streams.md §3) and are lost at a restart without an event, so `known` holds one only as the stream on the connection up now listed it (every join lists them, spec/sync-streams.md §4).
- **Between readers** a node serves its shared set by the stream alone (spec/sync-streams.md §4): a reader that lacks an entry pulls it; no Delete, Register or Deposit item is sent between readers, and a deletion in a reader's stream says only that it no longer holds the entry.
- **At a source keeping S unread** (a store source, spec/sync-opens.md §4), over `known`:
  - **Writer**: such a source pulls what it lacks of this node's store, stamped for the source's head; a writer stamps for that head what it logs there.
  - **Reader that is not a writer**: for each must-hold body it holds that the source lacks, an entry of it relayed under its stamp when the source's head write key verifies it, else a fresh encryption deposited (a Deposit item, spec/sync-inbox.md §3) while it judges the body valid (any author's, others' content included: its author's write is checked at promotion, spec/sync-inbox.md §4).
  - **Deletes**: a holder of the needed delete key or level keys deletes each entry `known` lists there that its judgement finds must-not-hold, once `known` shows a carrier of each live delta (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Carriers; a purged delta needs none). At a source full for S (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full) a carrier it lacks counts as held: it refused it for the room the delete frees, and pulls S again once that room grows, so it takes the carrier with the delete (Agreement of grounds). Without a key nothing is sent and the entry stays there until a holder connects or it expires.
  - **Role entries first**: every delete and every logging again (§4) toward a source waits while that source lacks a role commit or request of this node's that it can take now (stamped for its head; not present and not deleted there, one it refused included), read from this node's own store and the source's `known`, never from what this node sent; a source on an older head waits for none, and a full source (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full) is skipped.
  - **Agreement of grounds**: a node deletes only for reasons every holder sees after one exchange: a cover, purge or expiry the source holds or takes with the delete. A delete at a source stands for it (gone there, not present, no useless wrap); a body expiring within `MAX_DRIFT` is not logged again after a delete.
- **Neither** logs nor relays a body a delete removed from S (an entry of it that is not a certificate copy, within `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`: a blob whose body it was, §1) while both of these hold:
  - each of its deltas only a blob carries (as for a strict subset, §2) is held there by another blob of the commit that is not must-not-hold;
  - no held purge request signed by ones that could hold the right (spec/commits.md §4) names a delta of it without dropping it (spec/permissions.md §6).
  - So once a delete took a delta covered by a snapshot under S, no one logs it again, even a node that judges it replaced later and still keeps it. Once no valid snapshot under S covers it, its holders log it again.
- **Dropped bodies**: a node that dropped a body keeps knowing its entries (not its key or wrap) and each one's prefix salt once it had the blob (a delete-key signature's, spec/crypto.md §7), never its bytes; the bytes of a blob its freed links judged freed (spec/sync-compaction.md §1), whoever brings them, are not stored. When the body counts as never received again (spec/permissions.md §5.2, §6) its own tombstones of the body's entries end, and it fetches them again where present (each entry under its own sync ID), and otherwise pulls the body when a holder logs it again. A commit forgotten with a released thing (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) is not fetched again: its entries are must-not-hold for that node, and it forgets each once no source holds it.
  - A body logged again by a node whose own entry was dropped is never offered or counted as a copy until its bytes are held again; an entry without bytes is no duplicate.
- **Tombstones**: a tombstone only keeps the identical blob out, against stale relays and bundles. A source reporting an entry a node removed within that node's tombstone (a stale push, a pull crossing the removal) is counted present there and judged as any other entry. The node does not fetch or store it for that (bytes it receives give only their salt). The node keeps what the entry was (its kind: commit and parts, chunk, linked blob; its prefix salt; and whether it is a certificate copy) with the tombstone, across restarts. Never log again under a blob ID tombstoned under that sync ID.
- **Header-only parts**: nothing is judged outside a closure while one of its groups holds part of its `group.0` only as a header, and only while the node knows an entry carrying that part under S or a delete of one within its tombstone; else the part counts as gone, as for Forgetting.
- **Restart and forgetting**: forgetting a commit or thing rebuilds the sync state of what it touched by the same reload a restart does, memory class kept; a blob holding a forgotten part loses key and bytes, and its entries under kept sync IDs stay shredded.

## 4. Logged again once
- Past a delete at a source that it does not find standing (§3 Neither), a node logs the body again once, as a fresh encryption of its own under that sync ID (a fresh blob ID per sync ID, never one logged under another sync ID).
- A delete of that encryption stands, within its tombstone (`TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`), while the source's head is the step it was logged at and no held part on the body's aspects has fallen at this node since, whatever carries the body (the deleter holds a cover this node lacks: one on its way, or one the deleter hides).
- Once the head moves or such a part falls (a role change can make the deleter's cover or killing write fall), the node logs it again once more. Nothing else lets it log the body again there.
- A part counting missing again lifts this node's own tombstones of that commit's entries (§3 Dropped bodies), so its own tombstone never blocks fetching it again.
- So per (source, body) a node logs at most one encryption per change of ground, and a loop between a deleter and a node logging again ends within one exchange of each change.

## 5. Destruction and emptying
A destroyed thing (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction) with secrets of its own may be read under its own sync IDs alone, so its destruction is told there and nowhere else.
- **Telling**: from its destruction a node holding the thing's secrets keeps syncing its own sync IDs as an ordinary sync ID (S stays in the sync set, spec/sync-set.md §3), logging the destroying requests there before anything there is deleted. The destruction is **told** at a node once an entry of each destroying request is held at a source of S, or at once for a destruction by expiry alone (every node judges it alone).
- **Emptying**: told, and keeping nothing there (the thing's required set is empty), everything under its own sync IDs is must-not-hold: a holder of the delete key of the newest step deletes every entry it knows there (by the key of each source's head); it forgets the sync IDs once its sources are heard out (spec/sync-status.md §1) with no entry and no tombstone known there; only then does it drop the thing's secrets (no entry, no delete within its tombstone, no blob, no freed link, no commit acting for it left). A node holding no such key forgets them once told.
- **Untold** (away for the whole window, or learned it past it): the node leaves the sync IDs, deleting nothing there; deleting there untold would loop with a node that takes the thing back without the deleted bodies.
- A released thing may come back from a holder that missed the request; a node that forgot it takes it back as never received; a record arriving after the forget is not judged destroyed again.
- Limit: a late write reaching a peer before its writer hears of the destruction may be dropped as the only copy (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction).

## 6. Kept unread
A node keeping S unread (spec/sync.md §3) holds, per thing, only a **sync record**: the thing's tid, its sync IDs, and its sync secrets by epoch, each taken from a box sealed to its own account's key (a grant's inbox blob, a rotation boxing it, an entry box). It holds no roster, chain, `keys.0` or commit of the thing, and runs no judgement of what S carries.
- **Keeps** S while its synced group's closure lists the thing and it holds a sync secret of it. A grant's sync secret is taken from the box alone; the granter's right to grant is not checked (the box's secret already gives what the grant would).
- **Takes** into its store, by spec/sync-ids.md §6: entries stamped under its head's write key that it pulls, deposits that verify under its head's deposit key within budget (spec/sync-inbox.md §3), steps by Register, and Deletes signed by the needed keys on a connection where S is open by a store credential; it removes entries at expiry and when its caps give way. Nothing else changes its store.
- **Serves** S to every source (spec/sync-opens.md §4) from its store, and pulls S from every source.
- **Stops** keeping S when its removal reaches it: an inbox blob (spec/sync-inbox.md §2) writing its grant on the thing below sync or removing it, verified as a box-less notice signed at the granting thing's current admin or write key as the notice's commit header shows; it then drops S at once (it has no commit of its own under S), shredding its store of S.
- **Stranded**: when its head moves to a step whose sync key no sync secret it holds derives, it opens S nowhere (its credential is gone, spec/sync-opens.md §6) and reports itself stranded (spec/sync-status.md §3), but keeps its store until a box of the new sync secret arrives (a re-seal or the rotation's box, spec/sync-inbox.md §2) or its removal reaches it. It never drops data on a head move alone: a holder whose box is late would otherwise lose a copy that may be the only one.
- No epoch rule, no reading of role commits: a node keeping S unread decides its own grant by the boxes sealed to it and the removal notices to it, nothing else.
- **Read and unread move**: a node raised to read keeps its store and opens what is there (S becomes read); a node lowered to sync keeps its store and drops its engine state of S (S becomes kept unread); neither shreds anything.
- Limits: it can be made to keep ciphertext of a removed grant until the removal notice reaches it (bounded by its caps and by its account unlisting the thing); a writer may stamp anything past the caps as a role entry (spec/sync-ids.md §6).
