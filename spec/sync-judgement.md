# Sync: the judgement per sync ID
> Summary: Area 4: what goes under a sync ID, the required set and the must-hold / may-hold / must-not-hold judgement, how a reader acts on it in its own store and at peers keeping S unread, the bounded "logged again once" rule, destruction, and what a node keeping S unread does instead.

**Principle** (area 4, this file and spec/sync-compaction.md): a reader's store of S holds what its own judgement says S needs, and a body leaves it only for a reason every holder sees after one exchange (expiry, purge, a right holder's covering snapshot, or S not needing it); a node acts at a peer only over what that peer's stream showed, deletes there only by a Delete signed for the right, and every delete and every logging again is bounded by a change of ground (a new head at that peer, or a held part falling here). A node keeping S unread judges nothing but signatures (§6).


## 1. What goes under a sync ID
- **Entitlement**: a thing's **independent** is the thing itself if it is independent, else the nearest thing up its record chain that is independent (spec/crypto.md §3). **S's actor** is S's thing, the thing whose sync ID S is (spec/sync-ids.md §4 Owner); S's **sync unit** is that thing's sync unit (spec/sync-set.md §2): the things of its closure, reached through such things only, that S's actor may read. S's actor may read a thing iff the thing's secrets derive from its own or it holds read or above on the thing's independent through grants with key access (spec/sync-set.md §2 T's keys open). A commit's blob belongs under S iff S's actor may read a part of it on a thing of the sync unit.
  - Only a writer of S stamps entries (spec/sync-ids.md §6).
  - A node that is not one deposits what it must log there (what of S's required set it holds that S lacks, spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates; its may-grant grant or self-removal, its external fires and its history adds (spec/permissions.md §3)) and relays or deposits the must-hold bodies it holds (§3).
- **One blob per commit**: a commit is logged as one blob holding every part of it the logging node holds and can seal, each part in the section of its thing under the thing's read secret (spec/wire.md §4; a part of a thing whose read secret that node lacks travels as a certificate copy). Every sync ID of every thing the commit touches carries the one blob under its sync ID's wrap. No size split: a commit stays within `MAX_BLOB` by paging its deltas (spec/crdt.md §8), largest first, or is refused at the writer.
  - A blob's **body** under a sync ID is S's body of its commit (Per-sync-ID copies, below). A part of it **sealed below the node's floor** never counts missing there (it never opens it, spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut, Roles secrets): a part other than a role part, on a thing whose independent is A, of a read rotation of A to an epoch of n at most the node's floor on A, while the node holds no read secret of A of lower n (the rotation's sections lie under an epoch it supersedes, spec/wire.md §4; so too where S's body of it is a copy, which S's readers make from what they open). It stays a part of the body, judged as at every reader. A node judges what a sync ID needs, holds, lacks or had deleted by bodies (§2). A delta it holds that S's actor may not read still counts for the blob itself (§2 Per blob). A section the node cannot open it copies verbatim when it encrypts the commit again (a tombstoned entry, a strip's rewrite), and only where every held blob of the commit of the body's kind carries it: a strip left it out at a node that opened it.
  - A section opens with the thing's read secret at the epoch the writer used: a reader of the thing holds it (its own, through its history boxes; a dependent thing's by derivation through the record chain the section lists from a member it holds epochs of), and a holder of a role on the thing's independent keeps up with its rotations through that independent's sync ID (spec/sync-ids.md §2).
  - **Sealed sections**: a node opens a section under a thing's read secret only where it holds the thing (in its node closure in any context, spec/sync-set.md §2) or the section is a roles section (spec/crypto.md §4 Roles secrets); a section of a thing it does not hold stays sealed whatever secrets it has, but for the creation record minted there of a thing whose record it lacks and that is not destroyed here, which it takes alone. A held blob's sealed section opens again as its thing enters the node closure, as a secret of the thing arrives, and when S is pulled anew (§3 Dropped bodies).
  - **Per-sync-ID copies**: S's body of a commit is the parts of it on things of S's sync unit that S's actor may read; it is the shared blob where S's readers can open every section, else one per-sync-ID copy: a fresh blob of those parts, one section under its blob key (spec/wire.md §4), never shared with another sync ID. What S's evidence and its actor's key part name of the commit (§2) on a thing outside the sync unit is no part of that body: it is a certificate copy, one per thing (Certificate blobs, below), so a commit has under S at most one body and one copy per thing outside the sync unit, each an entry judged alone as a body is. S's readers can open a part's section in the shared blob iff its thing's read secret derives from S's actor or from a thing of S's sync unit that is not destroyed (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction), it is no role part other than `created.0` on a thing other than S's actor, and S's actor may read it (a part only S's evidence or key part names is outside the sync unit). Only a copy holds such a body there: a shared blob of the same parts neither holds nor carries it, and goes as the copy's duplicate (spec/sync-compaction.md §1). A section that opens under its blob key is a copy's (this one or a certificate copy): it is never copied into another encryption.
- **Retag**: when a thing joins a sync unit, any writer of the sync ID that holds the thing's live blobs adds them under it; when it leaves, each holder drops its entries there by its own view and delete-key holders delete them under that sync ID once their own view agrees (§2), while a holder whose view still holds it logs it again (§3 Own store; spec/sync-compaction.md §1).
- **Certificate blobs**: certificate copies (spec/permissions.md §4 Copies: per (commit, thing), a commit's role-class parts on one thing outside S's sync unit), header-only copies (things' witnesses and destroying requests) and record blobs (raw creation records, spec/wire.md §3).
  - S's evidence, its actor's key part and its sync unit's stubs (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stub) go as certificate copies exactly where its bodies do not carry them: on a thing outside its sync unit.
  - A certificate copy is one section of its parts under its blob key (spec/wire.md §4; a header-only copy has none). A blob of one section under its blob key holding parts on one thing alone, that thing outside S's sync unit, is under S an entry of the commit's copy on that thing, in the required set while S's evidence, key part or a stub names parts on it; any other blob of the commit is an entry of its body. A node logging one copy under several sync IDs logs one blob, wrapped under each. One a peer refuses under a sync ID as tombstoned (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Refused) goes there again as a fresh encryption. List and record blobs are a fresh encryption per sync ID.
  - The chunk and tree-node blobs of a linked frontier or header list go under it too, as the same blobs (a link names them) with a filler wrap.
- **Unjudged blobs**: a receiver keeps blobs a peer logged under a sync ID even if its own view of S's sync unit disagrees, while it cannot judge them: a commit it holds pending (not parked) or an entry of the sync ID it can open and has not applied, an entry awaiting a record included, within its bounds on unjudged input (spec/commits.md §4 Pending storage); then §6 decides.

## 2. Required set and judgement
S's **required set** is its sync unit blobs (§1) plus what is logged there beside them, one rule for every blob whoever logged it, judged by each node from what it holds now (a function of the held commits and S's sync unit, never of what was once required):
- S's **evidence** (spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a sync ID): a certificate copy is in it when its identity's parts are in S's evidence, judged by the deltas the blob holds, the same at every node; a record blob by its records; a destroyed thing's certificates (its frozen edges and requests' parts outside the sync unit, header-only copies of its mintings and requests anywhere) by the sync ID's things needing it (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction). A copy holding other parts than one thing's role-class parts of its commit is no certificate copy.
- the **key part** of S's actor (spec/permissions.md §4 Key part): its role-class parts and its edge pieces.
- the **stubs** S's sync unit carries (spec/sync-set.md §2 Stub).
- role commits naming a holder whose keys S's actor's keys open (spec/permissions.md §4 Role changes travel), while held.
- Each is applied before the deletes it causes (§3 Role entries first).
Per node, a body it holds is **must-hold**, **must-not-hold** or **may-hold** at a sync ID. A body meeting a must-not-hold condition is must-not-hold whatever else holds of it.
- A delta is **kept** while it is before its keep-at-least bound (in history by the current setting, spec/commits.md §5 The setting). One out of history is neither must-hold nor fetched; a node that dropped it fetches it none when a later setting puts it back in history.
- **Loggable**: a node judges must-hold only what it may log under S now (§3 Own store): a body it may seal under S, or a held blob of the commit it may pass on verbatim under a wrap for S (both by spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on and Who wraps under what). Whatever else of the required set it is entitled to is may-hold: kept if it arrives, never fetched, relayed, deposited or owed.
- **must-hold**: in the required set, loggable and, for a sync unit body, one of:
  - a delta of it kept, whatever snapshot covers it;
  - established uncovered: the node can join it, no held snapshot of the aspect under the sync ID covers it (spec/commits.md §5), and the sync ID holds no snapshot of the aspect that this node does not hold (forgotten, dropped) stamped later than the body (one this node judged invalid does not count); a signal's delta is never joined, so it is must-hold only while kept.
- **Header-only blob**: in the required set only as evidence (above) or as the lasting header of a commit lasting on a thing of the sync unit that S's actor may read (spec/commits.md §5), with no other body of the commit wanted there, and then is must-hold, whatever snapshot covers it. One of a commit with a permanent delta or a purge request that is not lasting here is may-hold.
- **must-not-hold**, any of:
  - purged (every delta of it; a purged delta beside a live one strips, Per blob);
  - expired (its blob's prefix expiry, spec/sync-ids.md §1, whatever of it the sync ID's actor reads);
  - outside the required set, judged only while no thing of the sync unit holds a part as a header only whose entry under S, or a delete of one within its tombstone, the node knows (its body may hold a strong to it); a header-only part with neither counts as gone, as for Forgetting; and only at the end of a complete pass of S (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun after both the entry came into the node's store and what decides the commit's body under S here (role commits, the strongs that decide the sync unit, evidence touching it) last moved; until then it is unjudged. An entry this judgement drops leaves with no tombstone (§3 Tombstones);
  - a blob that strips, or loses as needed to another held blob of its commit under that sync ID (Per blob, below);
  - a deposit this node cleared (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Clearing deposits), unexpired or not.
- **Per blob**: each held blob of a commit under S is judged on the sections the node opened, never on one it cannot open.
  - A delta **strips** here when the node holds its body no more other than while invalid: held as its leftover, or dropped (purged, past its bounds, destroyed, or as covered, on an aspect whose current setting may put it under purge on replace, while a held valid snapshot of its aspect reaches it by its tables, spec/commits.md §5 Covered). A blob carrying one is must-not-hold, whatever the body's verdict. The node logs the rest of the body as the commit's blob without it (the **rewrite**: a held blob that is it verbatim, else a fresh encryption) and the old entry leaves its store; a holder of its delete keys deletes it at each source keeping S unread whose stream shows, for each delta of it the node still holds, another entry of the commit not must-not-hold carrying it, and for each delta it strips as covered an entry of a held valid snapshot reaching it, from `MAX_DRIFT` past that delta's keep-at-least bound. A delta dropped while invalid, as covered on an aspect whose current setting puts nothing under purge on replace, or as covered by its signer's own replaces alone, strips nothing: §3 Deletes and the carriers rule decide those (spec/sync-compaction.md §1).
  - Of two held blobs A and B of the commit of A's kind, neither stripping, A **loses** to B when B carries each delta of A the node holds and:
    - **as needed**: A is of the body's kind and B carries each delta of A, each section of A the node did not open (over what it checked, spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Duplicates) and a delta only a blob carries that A lacks: kept or holding a live write, and covered by no snapshot under the sync ID, established as for must-hold (not while an entry under the sync ID is being fetched or pending). A is must-not-hold, and deleted at a source whose stream shows B.
    - **as spare**: B may stand as the body's log (an entry holding the body, no deposit awaiting promotion), and A carries a section B lacks that the node did not open, or the same sections under a higher blob ID, or the same sections while A may not stand. A leaves the own store and is no blob logged verbatim; this is never a ground for a delete at a peer.
- **Judged invalid**: a body no part of which is valid here, each invalid (dropped as invalid, or held as this node's own commit, spec/permissions.md §6) or dropped as covered, one at least invalid, not a role commit's, carrying no purge request that holds the right over a delta (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting), is may-hold unless a must-not-hold condition holds: it is not logged, it leaves the own store with no tombstone (§3) and it is never deleted at a peer.
- **may-hold**: the rest: not loggable, covered, out of history, parked, unfetched pages, dropped here, judged invalid.

## 3. Acting on the judgement
- **Driven by events**: an entry is judged when an event touches it: it is logged, pulled, fetched, deleted, expires or is refused somewhere; a commit, snapshot, purge or role change on its aspects is held or falls here; its thing's sync unit changes; a source's stream shows it, shows it gone or refuses it. A new source is judged entry by entry as its stream's events arrive. Judging is to a fixpoint: a judgement that changes the sync unit or a held part re-judges what that change touches, and nothing else.
- **Own store**: while a reader can pull it (it publishes a `dial.0` entry, or a peer shares S: on a connection up, or at its last connection while its record is kept, spec/sync-opens.md §3), a node logs in its own store what S must hold of its own (its must-hold bodies, its certificates), whatever peers are connected, stamped when it writes S (another's body or certificate, record blobs included, only once S's first sync here ended, spec/sync-streams.md §5, or a complete pass of S begun after what decides it last moved did, spec/sync-status.md §1; a node that made S's thing has no first sync of it: an entry a source holds, pulled, stands as the log): a held blob of the commit not tombstoned under S verbatim, under a wrap for S, else a fresh encryption (both by spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on, which may make neither now). What it logs together goes in its commits' `ts` order (spec/crdt.md §2.2), so its own in the order it made them. Only an entry whose blob holds S's body of the commit (§1: of the body's kind, carrying each of its parts) is that log: a certificate copy of fewer parts, logged before the body grew, is not. An entry of the body under S it pulled stands as that log as it came (a blob holding a part that does not match its header's delta hash, or whose prefix disagrees with its header, is no encryption of the commit, spec/commits.md §4 Judged alone, and is not kept; one held that proves so once its header's lists resolve leaves the store with no tombstone). Where it holds the write key of the head its store serves, an entry, its own or pulled, stands only stamped under that key: when the head moves it stamps each own entry of a must-hold body in place (same blob and wrap), and with no own entry and none standing one pulled entry; an entry of a role commit a recorded step registers stands, and is stamped, under that step's parent (the earliest such step's whose write key it holds; spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps). A stamp vouches for the body under S, which it opened and checked; sections of the blob it cannot open go as they came, judged by their readers alone (spec/commits.md §4 Judged alone). An entry of a body judged invalid (§2) leaves its store with no tombstone, its own commit's included (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting); valid again, the body counts missing and is fetched where shown, or pulled when a holder logs it again (its signer, holding it, logs it once must-hold). A record blob in its store, its own or pulled, carrying none of the records S's certificates now hold, or losing to another there (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates), leaves its store with no tombstone. Of the entries in its store it drops what its judgement finds must-not-hold or **covered and dropped** (a may-hold body a held valid snapshot under S covers, its keep-at-least bound past, spec/sync-compaction.md §1; a body with live writes only once a carrier is under S; a must-hold body never is), or what expired, whatever delete keys it holds: delete keys only authorize deletes at other nodes. Such an entry leaves S's log here even where a local rule keeps its body (its own body under another writer's cover, spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Own bodies): the body stays local data only and no stream from here lists it.
- **What a source holds** (`known`): the entries its stream showed present and not removed since, and nothing else, kept while S is a member there (spec/sync-opens.md §6: one away stays; one whose peer is judged departed at a reader source closes at once, one whose peer is no one once no connection holds its record) or its stop is kept (§6 Where it stopped), and no longer; a write's acknowledgement, a send or a deposit counts for nothing until the stream shows it. A source showing an entry held here marks presence only. It records an entry only if, once the showing message's bodies are settled, the node holds it or a tombstone of it under S, or took its body while that message was in hand (opened to what no entry keeps: a page, records), or counts it (a record blob, spec/permissions.md §4), or fetches it lazily (spec/sync-streams.md §5); a deletion or refusal with code 6 only for such an entry or one it records there. Ephemeral entries have no log position (spec/sync-streams.md §3) and are lost at a restart without an event, so `known` holds one only as the stream on the connection up now listed it (every join lists them, spec/sync-streams.md §4).
- **Between readers** a node serves its shared set by the stream alone (spec/sync-streams.md §4): a reader that lacks an entry pulls it; no Delete, Register or Deposit item is sent between readers, and a deletion in a reader's stream says only that it no longer holds the entry.
- **At a source keeping S unread** (a store source, spec/sync-opens.md §4), over `known`:
  - **Writer**: such a source pulls what it lacks of this node's store, stamped for the source's head; a writer stamps for that head what it logs there.
  - **Reader that is not a writer**: for each must-hold body it holds that the source lacks, an entry of it relayed under its stamp when the source's head write key verifies it, else a fresh encryption deposited (a Deposit item, spec/sync-inbox.md §3) while it judges the body valid (any author's, others' content included; its signer's write is checked at promotion, spec/sync-inbox.md §4). Nothing but role commits is deposited at a source standing on an earlier step of its recorded path: it registers the path there first (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps), which waits on the source holding the role commits the step depends on.
  - **Deletes**: a holder of the needed delete key or level keys deletes each entry `known` lists there that its judgement finds must-not-hold or covered and dropped (above; its wait: spec/sync-compaction.md §1), once `known` shows a carrier of each live delta (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Carriers; a purged delta needs none). At a source full for S (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full) a carrier it lacks counts as held. Without a key nothing is sent and the entry stays there until a holder connects or it expires. What is owed at a source is judged from `known` alone, connected or not: a delete owed stays owed while `known` lists the entry there, across the source's absence and this node's restarts, and is sent once the source is connected, again on each later connection until the source takes it (answers 0). A body judged invalid (§2) or dropped as invalid is never deleted at a peer (spec/permissions.md §6; spec/sync-hostile.md §2).
    - A delete resting on a verdict (a body covered by a snapshot judged valid, a body judged must-not-hold by a validity it reads) is sent only once a complete pass of S (spec/sync-status.md §1) begun after that verdict's inputs last moved has ended (spec/commits.md §4 Provisional).
  - **Role entries first**: every delete toward a source waits while that source lacks a role commit or request of this node's, and every logging again (§4) while it lacks one S must hold here (its own or pulled), that it can take now (stamped for its head; not present and not deleted there, one it refused included), read from this node's own store and the source's `known`, never from what this node sent; a source on an older head waits for none, and a full source (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full) is skipped. A delete toward a source also waits while that source's stream shows a role commit this node does not hold: it fetches it first.
  - **Agreement of grounds**: a node deletes only for reasons every holder sees after one exchange: a cover, purge or expiry the source holds or takes with the delete. A delete at a source stands for it (gone there, not present, no useless wrap); a body expiring within `MAX_DRIFT` is not logged again after a delete.
- **Neither** logs nor relays a body a delete removed from S (an entry of it that is not a certificate copy, within `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`: a blob whose body it was, §1) while both of these hold:
  - each of its deltas only a blob carries (as for a loss as needed, §2 Per blob) is held there by another blob of the commit that is not must-not-hold;
  - no held purge request that could hold the right (spec/commits.md §4) names a delta of it without dropping it (spec/permissions.md §6).
  - Once a delete took a delta covered by a snapshot under S, no one logs it again, even a node that judges it replaced later and still keeps it. Once no valid snapshot under S covers it, its holders log it again.
- **Dropped bodies**: a node that dropped a body keeps knowing its entries (not its key or wrap) and each one's prefix salt once it had the blob (a delete-key signature's, spec/crypto.md §7), never its bytes; the bytes of a blob its freed links judged freed (spec/sync-compaction.md §1), whoever brings them, are not stored. When the body counts as never received again (spec/permissions.md §5.2, §6) its own tombstones of the body's entries end, it opens again each entry of it its store still holds, and it fetches the others again where present (each entry under its own sync ID), asks for it by ID (below), and otherwise pulls the body when a holder logs it again. What is forgotten as never received (a released thing's content, spec/commits.md §5 A released thing; a waiting part whose last carrier went, §4 Pending storage; a part of a thing not held) has no own tombstone from then on: forgetting it ends them. It is fetched again once a strong in the node closure reaches the thing again (spec/sync-set.md §2): a strong newly in a read sync unit to a thing that was outside the node closure, and not leaving it (spec/sync-set.md §3), when the storage transaction adding the strong began, unless that transaction brought its record in a commit of which the node held nothing when it began (content on a thing counts as never received only while the node holds a commit minting it, spec/commits.md §4 Outside the node closure, and that commit stays known while a part of it may still bring the thing in, §5 A released thing), and for which no secret it holds opens, ends the own tombstones of the commits minting that thing it holds, and the node pulls that unit's sync IDs anew from every source (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Joining: from the start) and opens again each entry of them its store holds that did not open to something held or left a section sealed.
  - A body logged again by a node whose own entry was dropped is never offered or counted as a copy until its bytes are held again; an entry without bytes is no duplicate.
  - **Asked by ID**: a body counting as never received under S (a snapshot held as its leftover lacking a write no longer gone included, spec/permissions.md §6) is asked for by its commit ID from each peer reading S, on each connection where S is open, once: when S opens there, or when the body comes to count so while it is open (a Want, spec/sync-streams.md §4). The peer answers from what it holds, whatever its own judgement of the body (no must-hold rule of its decides): an entry of its store holding S's body of the commit, else a fresh encryption of that body under S (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on), else none; a leftover is no body. The answer is taken as a fetched entry of S and judged as any. With no peer holding it nothing more is sent until S opens again on a connection: the body reads as at a node that never received it.
- **Tombstones**: a tombstone only keeps the identical blob out, against stale relays. Dropping an entry judged outside the required set writes none. A source reporting an entry a node removed within that node's tombstone (a stale push, a pull crossing the removal) is counted present there and judged as any other entry. The node does not fetch or store it for that (bytes it receives give only their salt). The node keeps what the entry was (its kind: commit and parts, chunk, linked blob; its prefix salt; and whether it is a certificate copy) with the tombstone, across restarts. Never log again under a blob ID tombstoned under that sync ID.
- **Restart and forgetting**: forgetting a commit or thing leaves the sync state of what it touched as a restart would derive it, memory class kept; a blob holding a forgotten part loses key and bytes, and its entries under kept sync IDs stay shredded. An own entry logs a commit held here: a commit forgotten whole by any rule but lost access (released, needed by nothing held, covered, invalid) takes the node's own entries of its blobs under every sync ID with it, with no tombstone, in the step that forgets it (a crash between finishes it at the next start); one forgotten with lost access keeps them, each opening again once the access returns.

## 4. Logged again once
- Past a delete at a source that it does not find standing (§3 Neither), a node logs the body again once, as a fresh encryption of its own under that sync ID (a fresh blob ID per sync ID, never one logged under another sync ID).
- A delete of that encryption stands, within its tombstone (`TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`), while the source's head is the step it was logged at and no held part on the body's aspects has fallen at this node since, whatever carries the body. A snapshot of the aspect judged invalid here counts as a fall, whether it was held before or arrived invalid.
- The stand's state (per source and body: the encryption logged again and its ground: the head it was logged at, and the falls of held parts on the body's aspects so far) and the deletes this node saw at that source live in that peer's record (spec/sync-opens.md §3), durably, so a restart is no change of ground: a logging again owed or waiting there (§3 Role entries first) is owed after it as before; toward a peer with no record they last the connection, so each new connection to it allows one more logging.
- Once the head moves or such a part falls, the node logs it again once more. Nothing else lets it log the body again there.
- A part counting missing again lifts this node's own tombstones of that commit's entries (§3 Dropped bodies), so its own tombstone never blocks fetching it again.
- Per (source, body) a node logs at most one encryption per change of ground, and a loop between a deleter and a node logging again ends within one exchange of each change.

## 5. Destruction
A destroyed thing's own sync IDs (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction) are judged as any held thing's: synced while the thing is in the node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2), each body the destruction dropped must-not-hold (§2, deleted at peers that keep it unread), each request destroying it held as any commit with a part on the thing (lasting, spec/commits.md §5 Purge), and stopped with the thing's release (spec/sync-set.md §3).
- A late write reaching a peer before its writer hears of the destruction may be dropped as the only copy (spec/commits.md §5 Destruction).

## 6. Kept unread
A node keeping S unread holds, per thing, only the sync IDs it keeps for it and the sync secrets it holds (spec/sync.md §3 Kept sync IDs), and runs no judgement of what S carries. "A sync secret it holds" below is a held sync secret of S's thing (spec/sync.md §3).
- **Keeps** S while its node closure (spec/sync-set.md §2) holds the thing and its head's sync key is one a sync secret it holds derives; once the node closure no longer holds it, it leaves S by spec/sync-set.md §3 (a later strong keeps S again). Before any step of S is registered with it, it has no head: it opens S by each sync secret it holds (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Admissible). No granter's right is checked: S is kept only by a strong in the node closure and the sync secret its role gives (spec/sync-set.md §2).
- **Takes** into its store, by spec/sync-ids.md §6, only while it holds a sync secret deriving its head's sync key: entries stamped under its head's write key that it pulls, deposits that verify under its head's deposit key within budget (spec/sync-inbox.md §3), and Deletes signed by the needed keys on a connection where S is open by a store credential; steps by Register at any head; it removes entries at expiry and when its caps give way. Nothing else changes its store.
- **Serves** S to every source (spec/sync-opens.md §4) from its store, and pulls S from every source.
- **Stops** when a Register moves its head to a step whose sync key no sync secret it holds derives: it drops every entry of S at once, with no deletion event, keeping its path, and takes nothing while its head stays such a step. It shows only the sync keys of the secrets it holds that no step of its path bears (spec/sync-opens.md §4). An edge piece boxing its head's sync secret (a late one, a re-seal's or a grant's again at that epoch) keeps S again, and it fetches S anew: from the start at every source it holds a position at. Nothing but a verified Register moves its head; it never waits for its own entries to be held elsewhere first.
- **Read and unread move**: a node raised to read keeps its store, opens what is there and pulls S anew from each source (S becomes read; an entry it refused keeping S unread, spec/sync-streams.md §4 Refused, is shown again; one stopped fetches S anew, as above); so does one raised from kept unread to held for keys, and one raised from held for keys to read (its sources showed it role entries alone); a node lowered to sync keeps its store, under its caps (spec/sync-ids.md §5), keeps for the thing the sync IDs of its own reading (spec/sync.md §3), and drops its state of what S carries (S becomes kept unread); neither shreds anything.
