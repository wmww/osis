# Sync: hostile peers
> Summary: Area 8: what a hostile peer can and cannot do to sync, the bounds on its input, and the stated limits.

**Principle:** a peer's word changes only what that peer holds; nothing it says is taken as identity, authorization or deletion, and every message costs bounded work.

## 1. Bounds
- Identity and authorization come only from proofs on the connection (spec/sync-opens.md §2) and from verified data; a stream's `Deleted`, a refusal and a head the peer names are its word only.
- Bad bytes are ignored; no peer input causes unbounded allocation, work, storage or diagnostics (spec/wire.md §12).
- A filter breaking spec/wire.md §11.2 Beacons is answered Error 2 and closes the connection. One test pass in flight per peer.
- Records, Resume, Beacons, Pull and Members floods are bounded by message size, `max_reply`, `RECORD_BUDGET` and the shared set; only a peer that proved a thing makes a record.
- An ephemeral commit is room-checked whole before any decode and waits only once its signature checks.
- Unstamped entries a peer brings count against its peer budget (spec/sync-ids.md §5); deposits against the deposit budget (spec/sync-inbox.md §3).
- Nothing reads a clock above the transport: a skewed peer affects only its own writes.
- A peer without a credential or J learns only the entry count of its own filter and random bits; beacons are unlinkable across peers; only ByRole, Lost, Behind and Deposit items carry a sync ID, each to a judged holder, except a Deposit reaching a peer through a false positive of its filter (spec/sync-inbox.md §3 Sending), which learns nothing beyond the deposit's existence.
- A node holding only S's public values opens nothing; a static item reflected through a second dial, or an older step's signature, opens nothing toward the peer.
- A removed sync holder gets nothing after the rotation's step; a removed reader or revoked link learns at most one rotation past what it read. A peer judged departed is served only the notices of its removals and the public's edge (spec/sync-opens.md §6).
- A forged head, box or grant, or a substituted box, teaches nothing.
- A grant signal is values held only while the holder's admins enable `grant@~0`: it gives no role and makes nothing sync beyond following its thing's sync ID by role, opened only by a peer judging the holder's node a holder; a forged one names a sync ID that gives nothing (what is taken under it is judged by the thing's record and role commits). An entering node names that sync ID in ByRole to every peer it meets until it enters. A grant is the target's own data: it changes nothing a node fetches of the holder.
- A thing's sync ID is taken only from its `sync.0` by the owner rule (spec/sync-ids.md §4): no peer's word names one.
- A node keeping S unread drops its copy only when a Register verified on its path moves its head (a head a peer names, a stream or a box moves nothing).
- A peer that drops what it pulls cannot keep a node from idle: lacking counts only what a source pulls.
- A peer lying in its own log (hiding a cover) costs at most one logging again per change of ground (spec/sync-judgement.md §4).
- A section a node cannot open or check, forged or left out of a copy, makes no blob a duplicate of another there, nor one lose to another as needed (spec/sync-compaction.md §1 Duplicates), stamped or not.

## 2. Stated limits
- A node removed from an account still proves its node thing and claims its path through the account; a verifier not yet holding the removal counts the claim (spec/sync-opens.md §2).
- A thing the public reads (spec/permissions.md §1) is read by every party that holds its sync ID or a unit carrying it, stores included; its claim set's public entry shows it to anyone holding the sync ID. Once the public is removed, a reader holding the removal shows its superseded read secrets to every peer (spec/sync-opens.md §4, lapsed secrets): anyone holding one (a party that read S's thing while the public did, or a former reader) learns that it holds S, and is served S up to that epoch's supersession, what that secret reads.
- A node keeping S unread shows that it keeps S to anyone holding S's public values (its filter goes to every connection).
- A depositor holding a thing's `keys.0` but not its roster learns from 0 against 14 whether a node holds the thing's sync ID (whether a thing that node proved is among its holders).
- A peer showing a thing's deposit key (anyone holding its public key record) is offered the sealed fires a connected node sends that thing (spec/sync-inbox.md §3): it learns that this connection's node fires there, when, and the blobs' padded sizes.
- A peer can force work linear in its filter per small message (each additions page re-sorts and tests; a Hit then Lost re-tests); there is no rate limit.
- A peer can refuse role entries or hold requests open: what waits on it there (deletes, logging again) waits, the stall rule closes the connection (spec/sync-streams.md §1), and other sources are unaffected.
- A writer may stamp any entry as a role entry past the caps (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Role entries). Anyone able to deposit can fill the deposit budget while no admin is online (spec/requirements.md Data a node cannot read). A lowered holder can delete at a node keeping S unread what its old role allowed until a writer logs it again (spec/permissions.md §6).
- No node checks the level-2 delete key a header lists (spec/crypto.md §7 Checked keys): it is the purge author's right, which the signer holds. A demoted writer holding S's sync key can delete, at a node keeping S unread, blobs of commits it signed whose purge-by includes their author; their holders log each back once (spec/sync-judgement.md §4), and what a reader holds is not lost.
- Under the default policy a node proves its own things and their claimed paths to every peer (spec/sync-opens.md §1 Policy), so any node it connects to, whoever dialed, learns its node thing and its accounts. Any peer learns about how many credentials a node shows (its filter's entry count). A peer proving fresh things makes a record at each connection, holding its filter (up to 2^22 entries and as many additions, about 10 MB coded): a few dozen such connections pass `RECORD_BUDGET`, the least recently used records go, and their peers are read from the start at their next connection.
- Memory is bounded per connection and per input, not node-wide (spec/sync-transport.md §5 Served connections): a party opening many connections, or connections that never open a session, grows a node's memory with their number.
- Departed (spec/sync-opens.md §4) is judged only of a proven thing: a departed party proving none opens S by a current read secret or sync proof it still holds, until the rotation covering its departure is held here.
- A node keeping S unread whose box of its head's sync secret arrives after that step's Register (a grant again) drops its copy and fetches S again (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops); what only it held is lost, what a reader holds is not.
- A removed admin holding the delete key of a step on a node's path, while that delete key is the node's head's, can register there a branch under a sync key the node holds no box for, by a child of the head or by an endorsement: the node drops its copy, and its head is on a branch no reader records, so it holds S again only once its node closure releases the thing and enters it again (stopping shreds its path). It needs a node still at a head whose delete key the former admin (removed or demoted) holds, and lasts until the removal's step reaches that node; once a reader registered a step past it there, Register refuses it (a branch is taken only off a step bearing the head's delete key or endorsed by it, spec/sync-ids.md §6 Register).
- A node keeping S unread on a step off the recorded path, whose own step rotated the admin secret, is moved on only once an admin holding that step's admin secret endorses a deeper recorded step (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Steps); while no admin holds it (a dispute where the winning side removed every admin of that epoch), it stays on its step, serving holders of its sync key, until its node closure lets the thing go.
- A writer stamps the sections of a blob it cannot open as they came (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Own store): a reader of S's thing can encrypt a genuine commit with junk in another thing's section and, where no writer holds an entry of that commit standing, have it stamped (the lowest blob ID of those held) and kept at stores of S within S's caps; one such blob per commit per sync ID, at most a blob's size. That thing's readers drop the junk section (spec/commits.md §4 Judged alone) and take the part from its own sync IDs.
- A depositor (anyone holding S's deposit key) can deposit a commit with junk in place of a section a writer of S cannot open: once stamped, that writer promotes one more deposit of the commit by its bytes alone (spec/sync-inbox.md §4 Promotion), and every later variant, the genuine deposit included, stays deposited, so readers of that section syncing through stores of S lack the body until a writer of S that opens the section is online and promotes the genuine deposit by its delta (a junk section opens nowhere, so no holder deletes it as bad). Two stamped blobs per commit at most, as far as a writer that cannot open the section judges; variants not promoted count against the deposit budget.
- Blobs of one commit differing only in sections a delete-key holder cannot open or check (a forged section, a section encrypted again) all stay under S, as far as that holder judges, until one that checks them does, each counted against S's caps.
- A body dropped as invalid is never deleted at a peer (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Deletes), so a writer's invalid entry can take a full store's room there until it expires or the caps give way.
- A holder that cannot open a thing's grant, re-seal or rotation boxing it by its wrap holds nothing of what it gives until it meets a reader of the thing serving it the notice (spec/permissions.md §4 Notices); a store alone never gives it. A store sees a notice as one more entry of its commit's size.
- A node whose only reachable sources keep a thing unread and hold no role entries of a holder on its path to it gets no key change of that holder there.
- Evidence takes every held edge write on a stated path's edges whatever its validity (spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit): a writer of a thing without admin can sign any number of edge writes on an edge into it, none applying, and every honest node holding them and a body stating a path through that edge carries them under that body's sync ID, so that unit's storage grows with what the non-admin writes; and a writer stating a path through things no grant joins makes the writes on those edges evidence under its sync ID, so its readers see a roster that gives no role on its things.
- A writer of a holder H holding only sync on X can state a path through H's edge on X in a body under H's sync ID: the writes on that edge are its evidence (spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit), so H's readers see their headers, signers and paths, which spec/sync-ids.md §2 What a sync holder learns otherwise keeps from them. An edge write on X that is not valid changes nothing else they receive.
- A node past its keys holding the sync secret of an epoch no step bears shows it, and a reader holding that secret opens S there; nothing moves (the node takes nothing).
