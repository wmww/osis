# Sync: sync IDs and the store
> Summary: Area 2: blobs, sync IDs and their role keys, steps, entries and the claim key, `sync.0`, the node's store per sync ID, and the operations a store takes (Register, Head, stamps, wraps, Delete, expiry, relay-only, caps).

**Principle:** a sync ID belongs to its thing: fixed by the thing's genesis seed when it is minted, its keys derived from the thing's current role secrets, its path of steps verifiable from genesis by any node without trusting a peer. A store takes only what verifies under its own head.

## 1. Blobs
- A **blob** is one of:
  - an encrypted commit (header plus unpurged deltas; spec/commits.md);
  - an encrypted chunk, tree node or page (spec/data-model.md §6, spec/crdt.md §8, a header list spec/commits.md §1);
  - a sealed fire (spec/sync-inbox.md §2).
- A node keeping S unread cannot tell them apart except as §5 states.
- **Layout**: **clear prefix** || ciphertext. The prefix holds a salt, an optional expiry `T`, the blob's **class** and the delete-key commitments, one per level (spec/crypto.md §7). Ciphertext: spec/crypto.md §5. Bytes, padding, **Blob ID** and the `MAX_BLOB` bound: spec/wire.md §5.
- An add event or fetch answer whose bytes do not hash to its blob ID is ignored, whoever sends it.
- **Class**: durable, ephemeral or relay-only, set as spec/wire.md §5 states; how each is held: §5, §6 Relay-only.
- **Several blobs of one commit**: one commit may travel as several blobs (purge rewrites, certificate copies, concurrent re-encryptions): same header and commit ID, each with its own key, salt and ID.
- **Prefix of a commit encryption**: every encryption of a commit computes its prefix from the header and the aspects its sections list (spec/wire.md §4):
  - expiry = the commit's `expires`;
  - one commitment group per distinct delete-key set among those aspects, blinded by the new salt (spec/crypto.md §7; spec/wire.md §5);
  - a header-only blob (spec/commits.md §5) has neither.
  - A node opening a blob of a commit refuses it unless its prefix expiry is the one this rule gives (from the header's `expires`, class and timestamp, and whether the blob carries sections), whatever else it can check of the prefix.
- **Chunk reuse**: a chunk reused by a later delta keeps its blob and prefix, unless it must be re-encrypted.
  - It is re-encrypted when one of these holds:
    - its class is not the new delta's commit's;
    - its expiry is earlier than the new delta's;
    - its commitments to the thing's delete keys (levels 1 and 3) are not exactly those of the keys the reusing delta lists;
    - it commits to a level-2 key other than the reusing delta's, and that delta lists no level-2 key or only a level-2 key;
    - its link's salt or delete keys are not what its prefix bears (spec/data-model.md §6): a reused link is copied verbatim.
  - Beside a level-1 or level-3 key a level-2 commitment (the purge author's) need not match.
  - The link alone decides it: its link blind and the thing give the commitments (spec/crypto.md §7), whichever commit introduced the chunk and whether the node ever held that commit.
- **Repair** of a chunk or tree node blob:
  - A node repairs the blob when all of these hold:
    - a held, unexpired delta of the node's own commit or compaction snapshot links the blob through its large values' trees, the blob not one that commit, a repair, encrypted afresh (a blob a repair reused counts as any own commit's);
    - some source of S it reached has deleted the blob (a purger that had not seen the reuse) and none shows it present;
    - the node may write the aspect and holds the blob.
  - It judges these by its view once it has applied every entry of S it can open (those it learned of with the deletion included) and holds no commit pending (not parked) on the aspect.
  - To repair, it writes each large position of the aspect's current value whose tree holds the blob again, whole and without a reset (`put_new`, spec/crdt.md §5.6), reusing every other chunk and re-encrypting the blobs it repairs (one commit for those of one own commit), with that delta's purge-by and expiry (a snapshot's: the aspect's default purge-by, no expiry).
  - A repair carries current state, as a snapshot does (spec/crypto.md §5 Passing on: the floors clause binds it not, the departures clause does): the live value at a position whose tree reaches the blob. A blob only replaced versions reach is history and is not repaired, even where its bytes match a live one's.
  - How often is local policy.

## 2. Sync IDs and role keys
- **One sync ID per thing**, fixed when it is minted: the sync ID of the genesis step of its genesis seed (spec/crypto.md §3). It is the thing's one sync ID, for its life: holders are removed, the sync ID is never replaced or retired. A commit that makes a thing independent (spec/permissions.md §3) MUST record in its `sync.0`, in that one commit, the genesis step and a child of it registering the commit's new epochs (§3). Every admin making it independent builds the same genesis step, so admins doing so at once give it one sync ID and sibling first steps: concurrent rotations of one sync ID (§6 Steps). A thing that becomes dependent again and then independent again gets the same sync ID. A node syncs a thing under that one sync ID alone (§4 Owner).
  - Every holder of a role on an independent thing syncs it through that sync ID, whatever else holds the thing.
  - A dependent thing has none: it syncs under the sync IDs of the sync units holding it (spec/sync-set.md §2, spec/sync-judgement.md §1). A thing minted with a key records its genesis step and first step in its minting commit (spec/permissions.md §3).
  - A thing's `sync.0` and `created.0` writes go where its other parts go (spec/sync-judgement.md §1): no sync ID carries them for another thing up its record chain.
  - A link's sync ID is its K's sync ID.
- **Sync ID** value: derived from the genesis step's delete public key (spec/wire.md §8).
- **Genesis step** of genesis seed g: every key from g (its role keys as below, g standing for each role secret and for the deposit seed) and its step key (spec/wire.md §8), signed by its own delete key: deterministic, so every holder of g builds the same bytes. It registers no epoch; only its first child does, and no entry is bound to it.
- **Role keys**: Ed25519 keys, the first three from the thing's own role secrets (spec/crypto.md §3):
  - **sync** `kdf("sync key", sync secret, "")`: every holder of sync or above derives it (spec/permissions.md §1);
  - **write** `kdf("sync write", write secret, "")`;
  - **delete** `kdf("sync delete", admin secret, "")`;
  - **deposit** `kdf("sync deposit", deposit seed, sync ID)`, from the deposit seed of the thing's actor (spec/crypto.md §3).
- **Sync proof** of a sync key: `kdf("sync proof", s, "")`, `s` the 32-byte seed of that sync key. Only a holder of the sync secret computes it; holding the public sync key gives nothing.
- **What sync gives**: the sync key, so its holder opens S at peers by the sync proof (spec/sync-opens.md §4), pulls it, holds it and serves it; what its store takes and removes, reading nothing: spec/sync-judgement.md §6.
- **Who grants sync**: the one grant rule (spec/permissions.md §2): an admin, or a holder by a path giving may-grant at sync or above.
- **Removing a sync holder** is a departure that rotates the sync role (spec/permissions.md §3): the head's sync key moves with the next step, ending every open by the old key (spec/sync-opens.md §6). Removing a reader rotates read and sync.
- **What a sync holder learns**: of the thing, its tid alone, as an opaque identifier (its grant names the thing by tid); never its creation record (mint admin, link target, record chain), the tids of what its sync unit holds (S's blobs are opaque, and the node closure stops at a thing kept unread, spec/sync-set.md §2), its roster, content or members: of the thing's role commits it opens only its own entries (§3 Entries: its sync secrets), and of a stub its unit carries the sync-only cut (spec/permissions.md §4 Stub: the sync ID and the holders on the unit's chains to that thing, never its other holders); its removal it learns by the step past its keys alone (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops). Besides: its sync ID, the path, each role commit's entry count padded to a power of two, entry counts, sizes and timing, and which things' nodes open S with it. Through holder actors: a grant to G on H boxes H's sync secret to G's sync keyset, and a sync holder of G never learns H's sync ID.

## 3. Steps and entries
- **Steps**: a tree. Layout, signature and **Step ID**: spec/wire.md §8.
  - A step holds: sync ID, `prev` (the step ID of its parent; none in the genesis step), the four public keys (sync, write, delete, deposit), its **step key**: the public half of the claim key (below) of the sync epoch whose sync key it bears (the genesis step's from its seed, §2), and a signature by the parent's delete key (the genesis step by its own).
  - Every node holding S has one **head** per S: a node reading S the newest step its `sync.0` records (§4): of the recorded steps no recorded step is the parent of whose path from genesis verifies, the deepest, equal depths by lowest step ID; a node keeping S unread the newest step registered with it (§6 Register).
  - An admin issues steps (§6 Steps).
- **Claim key** of sync epoch e of thing X: the X25519 key pair whose private key is `kdf("claim key", sync secret of e, roles secret of r)` (spec/wire.md §8), r the read epoch e's sync keyset names (`claim`, spec/crypto.md §3): every reader of X derives it; a node holding only sync secrets cannot, so no store links a holder's KEM key to X. Every step bearing e's sync key carries its public half as its step key, so steps of one sync epoch share it and a step without a rotation changes no claim.
- **Claim** of holder M at sync epoch e, per entry of M (Entry): the Ed25519 key pair from `kdf("sync claim", DH(claim key of e, M's X25519 key), sync_id || step_key)` (spec/wire.md §8), M's X25519 key the X25519 half of the KEM key that entry is sealed to. M derives it from its own private key and the step key of a step bearing e's sync key, trying each read and sync keyset epoch of M it holds; a node acting for M, reading it or holding its sync secrets derives M's claims alike, by the keysets it holds of M. The **public's claim** at e is the Ed25519 key pair from `kdf("public claim", sync_id, step_key)`: anyone holding a step of e computes it.
- **Entries** of holder M in a commit, at a sync epoch e of the entry's thing X: one per role the commit boxes M at, read (at any read epoch) and sync (at e), each M's claim and a **W** sealed (hybrid, spec/crypto.md §1) to the KEM key of the keyset of M that role's box is to (a sealed box's keyset, a joint box's last, a symmetric box's epoch, whose KEM key the writer derives; spec/crypto.md §4; of several read boxes, the newest epoch's): the **read entry**'s W the blob's key and the key of the section holding the commit's role parts on X (spec/wire.md §4), so M opens the commit's role parts on X whatever epochs it holds; the **sync entry**'s the sync secret of e (its opener never sees the roster). So a node holding M's sync secrets alone takes X's sync secret by M's sync entry, and only a node holding a read secret of M opens M's read entry. The public's entry: one, its claim, W in clear (the read entry's where it is boxed at read). Layout: spec/wire.md §5 Entry blocks.
- **Entry block**: every encryption of a commit holding role parts on an independent thing X as a body (a role commit, a role compaction snapshot; a copy is none) carries in its clear prefix, per current sync epoch e of X at its maker with the commit applied, one block bound to e: the entries of each holder the commit boxes on X (a `secrets.0` piece keyed with it holding a box at a read epoch, or at sync at e), the public's among them, an entry whose box's keyset neither carries a KEM key nor is held by the writer left out; padded with dummy entries to a power of two, at least one dummy (at least two entries, so a role commit boxing nobody carries one block of two dummies); in random order; signed by e's sync key. So a grant, a re-seal, a rotation (bound to its new sync epoch), a snapshot boxing holders, and the commit making a thing independent each carry their holders' entries: grant and entry are one write. A node making the encryption that holds no claim key of e (no read secret) carries a block of dummies alone.
- **Bound**: a block is bound to the sync epoch whose sync key its signature verifies under. A node indexes the claims of the blocks of the entries it holds under S by their sync key, and those bound to its head's sync key are the **head claims**: what it shows (spec/sync-opens.md §4).
- **Taking an entry**: a node holding a claim at a head finds its entry by that claim in the blocks of S's entries it pulls, opens W with its KEM secret, and takes: a read holder, the commit's role parts by the blob and section keys (its boxes there, judged as spec/permissions.md §3 No secret taken back says of an entry); a sync holder, the sync secret, kept only where it derives the block's sync key; the public's, as M's own: every node holding S that does not read it looks for the public's entry in the blocks bound to its head, since anyone computes the public's claim. A W that does not open, or opens to keys that open nothing, gives nothing.
- **Role keys in use**: a node acts at a peer keeping S unread with the role keys of the epochs that peer's head registers; it derives them from the matching epochs it holds. Every holder of a key in use holds the older secrets through the history boxes, except a holder granted without history, which opens only from its grant's step on: a walk (§6) never strands it for an older step.

## 4. `sync.0`
- **`sync.0`** on an independent thing: `map<sync ID, { steps: map<step ID, step> }>`. Role-class, admin-only.
  - Each step is held with its **endorsements** (§6 Register), by endorsing delete key, kept for good. A node ignores an endorsement that does not verify.
  - **Owner**: a thing X's sync ID is the one its genesis seed gives. A node holding the seed (an admin secret of X's independent chain, spec/crypto.md §3) takes that one alone. Any other node takes as X's sync ID the one X's current `keys.0` names (admin-written, spec/crypto.md §3), or, holding no `keys.0` of X, one its `sync.0` records, once some step on a verified path from that sync ID's genesis step (each step signed by its parent's delete key, the genesis by its own, whose delete key hashes to the sync ID) bears the sync key of a sync secret of X it holds; while it holds none, or reads X by derived secrets (spec/crypto.md §3), it takes the named one as a hint. A node keeping X unread takes, of the sync IDs it follows X by (spec/sync-set.md §2 Sync set: a stub's, a signal's, a locator's, or the one of its own reading), the one a step of its path bears a held sync secret's key on; while none does, each is a hint it follows. Any other `sync.0` entry names no sync ID of X: it is ignored wherever `sync.0` is read.
  - A step enters `sync.0` only by an admin's write; no node records a step on a peer's word.
  - One role commit may carry the `sync.0` deltas of many things.

## 5. The store per sync ID
A node keeps one store of ciphertext for all the sync IDs it holds, read and kept unread alike; plaintext and everything derived from a secret is the engine's, above the store. The store is the truth for ciphertext: after a crash, a node derives again what a stored row gives that it lacks, and drops whatever it derived from a row the store does not hold.

Per sync ID:
- **Path**: the steps from the genesis step to the head (§3): every step's role keys, step key, `prev` and signature. A node reading S holds the path its `sync.0` records; a node keeping S unread the path registered with it.
- **Entries**: blob ID; wrap or, for chunk/tree/page blobs and sealed fires, a random filler; stamp or none; deposited flag; optional pending deletion time; its blob's entry blocks' claims by sync key (§3 Bound). An unstamped entry's charge to the peer that brought it lives in that peer's record (Peer budget).
- **Tombstones** (blob ID) of deleted entries, kept `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`; an ephemeral entry's `MAX_DRIFT`, in memory.
- Its events go in the store's one node log (spec/sync-streams.md §3).

Across sync IDs:
- A blob's bytes are deleted when no sync ID references them; a blob fetched under one sync ID is taken under every other held sync ID whose entries name it.
- A commit blob the node opened or sealed may be held as what makes it again byte for byte (its length, blob key, entry blocks, and each section's key and wrap or the section as it came, with the commit as held) in place of its bytes, made whenever read; once the node can no longer make it while an entry names it, the bytes are kept again first.
- Ephemeral entries and their tombstones are held in memory only and lost at a restart. Relay-only blobs are never held.
- **Caps** (the node's `caps^.0` entry, spec/sync-set.md §1, spec/data-model.md §9: `policy` and per-thing `things`, applied to each sync ID the thing owns, local policy) govern only the sync IDs the node keeps unread and count only their entries; a sync ID moving between read and kept unread is counted again. A reader's own sync IDs take nothing from the room others may use.
- **Peer budget**: an entry that gives the node nothing held (it does not open, opens to a commit not judged or known only for a part dropped as invalid, or to records pending, spec/commits.md §4 Records; or a commit held here, adding none of its parts; or a sync-only cut, spec/wire.md §4, anyone's word, what it gives kept only while an entry of it is), counts its size against the peer that brought it unless stamped: a stamp vouches only under the node's head write key, otherwise the entry is charged (a reader checks a peer's stamp at arrival and again on each head move, charging a held entry whose stamp stopped verifying to the peer that brought it, or not keeping it when that peer is gone; a stamp it holds is not replaced by one that does not verify). Charged entries count within a bound per peer (local policy); past it such entries are not kept. The charges live in the bringing peer's record (spec/sync-opens.md §3). A peer with no record is charged per connection, its charges going when that connection closes; a dropped record takes its charges with it. A source going so hands each entry charged to it that still gives nothing held to a peer's record whose stream showed it present (spec/sync-streams.md) and whose bound it fits, charged there from then on; one no such record takes is dropped, to be fetched again where present. Likewise the bringer of an entry kept uncharged: another record that showed it, or none.

## 6. Operations at a store
Each operation names a sync ID S. Writes are signed with the role keys of the receiving store's head; a Delete needs S open on the connection (spec/sync-opens.md §4).

- **Steps** (who issues and registers):
  - Only an admin issues a step, once the rotation commits it registers are logged under S here (spec/permissions.md §3), whatever peers it reaches: when it holds an established sync, write or admin epoch newer than the one its recorded head registers, or its thing's `keys.0` has no read key in force over a read keyset it vouches for, or its deposit seed derives another deposit key (spec/permissions.md §3 next-step triggers), a child of its recorded head, recorded in `sync.0` in the step's commit, which writes its keys as the thing's step keys in `keys.0` (spec/crypto.md §3). No step is issued or registered under a head this node has not recorded.
  - A node reading S registers at each source that keeps S unread (a store source, spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source) the recorded steps after that source's head, in order: each only once that source holds (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 `known`) the role commits of S's actor the step's epochs depend on (at once at a source that takes nothing: one on a step past its keys, S open there by the sync proof of a sync key that step does not bear, until it stands at a step bearing that key; spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops) (the rotations of the epochs it registers and of those they supersede, but those an earlier recorded step waits on, so each is waited on by one step, which a source at its parent can take stamped; for a role whose epoch in the step no secret it holds tells, the rotations of that role's current epochs it knows, at the step that brought the role's newest key on the path alone; a step waits on nothing a source at its parent cannot take, so a source moved past a commit's step gets it as a deposit, spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Giving a body), which the registrar logs stamped under the source's head for it to pull. A remover logs the removal and the new `keys.0` before what it writes after them (spec/permissions.md §3).
  - At a source whose head is a step its `sync.0` records off the recorded path (a sibling branch), it registers in one Register the recorded steps after the deepest step the two paths share, through the first deeper than the source's head, each needing its role commits shown as above, when that shared step's delete key is the head's, else through the first recorded-path step deeper than the head that its `sync.0` records endorsed by the head's delete key, with that endorsement; nothing while the recorded path is no deeper than the head, at a head its `sync.0` does not record, or with neither. A branch that does not fit `MAX_MESSAGE` is not sent.
  - Any reader holding the recorded path moves a source on an older step, not only an admin; Register needs no secret and no open, and is relayable by any node.
  - A deposit-only step (a new deposit key, no other role newer) waits as spec/permissions.md §3 says: a complete pass of S begun after it came due, and no unapplied or missing `keys.0` write of S's actor known.
  - No rotation answers a fork: sibling steps stand, and a step due for another reason lists past the one its issuer recorded; a source on another is moved by a branch (above).
  - **Endorsements owed**: an admin of S's thing holding the admin secret of delete key d endorses its recorded head by d, in a `sync.0` write, when some step its `sync.0` records off the recorded path bears d, is shallower than the head, and the deepest recorded-path step on its path from genesis bears another delete key, unless a recorded-path step deeper than every such step of d is already endorsed by d. It needs no rotation commit logged first: the steps it lets a source take are registered as above.
- **Register** (one or more steps, each a child of the one before, each signed by its parent's delete key): taken only for a sync ID the node keeps unread (spec/sync.md §3) and in its sync set; a node reading S refuses every Register of S (code 14), as does a node not syncing S.
  - Into an empty path: accepted only if the first step is a genesis step whose delete key hashes to the sync ID and each later step verifies under the one before.
  - Onto a path: the leading steps the path holds at their places are passed over; the rest is a **branch** off the path's step its first step's `prev` names. Accepted when that step's delete key is the head's or the Register carries an **endorsement** of the branch's last step by the head's delete key (a signature over its step ID, spec/wire.md §8), each step verifies under its `prev`'s delete key, and the last is deeper than the head (a longer path from genesis); the path becomes the path to that step followed by the branch, its head the last. A child of the head is the one-step branch; a list ending at the head is answered OK and changes nothing. So sibling steps at equal depth stand, and no Register moves a head to a shallower step.
  - Otherwise refuse (a branch no deeper than the head, or off a step bearing another delete key than the head's without such an endorsement, a step that does not verify under the one before, a genesis step the path does not hold, an operation under keys of a step it does not hold) with code 9 and nothing more (other codes: spec/wire.md §11.2). A refused registration never carries the head (a registrar learns it from the stream where S is open, or by Head).
  - **Storage caps**: a step counts toward the sync ID's and the store's caps; a branch counts what it adds less what the path after its first step's parent held. Past them a Register into an empty path is refused (code 12); a branch is accepted at most once per sync ID per a policy interval (local policy, non-zero), else refused (code 12), and never refused for a cap otherwise.
  - **Refused for now** (codes 4, 12): the registrar keeps its open and registers again after a back-off (local policy, bounded), never at once.
  - A restart of S at a registrar (S dropped and synced again) forgets what it registered where.
- **Walk** (a reader opening S at a store source, spec/sync-opens.md §4, and moving it along the path). A reader opens S at a source by a credential of that source's **step**, the newest step it knows the source's path holds: the source's head event (spec/sync-streams.md §4), a Head answer (below), else its own recorded head; it knows a step there only once it verifies (a child of a step it holds, or a path from genesis). It keeps the source's step in their record (spec/sync-opens.md §3).
  - **Test**: when spec/sync-opens.md §5 Testing says, it tests its credentials (spec/sync-opens.md §4 Admissible), opening S by the one open rule (spec/sync-opens.md §4); an older step's proof also brings the source its moves (Moving it).
  - **Stranded** there (spec/permissions.md §4): it holds no credential the source shows.
  - **Moving it**: open, it registers the recorded steps after the source's step when that step is on the recorded path, the branch past it when it is a recorded step off it (Steps), and nothing when it is a step past its `sync.0`. While the source names no step it registers its genesis step there first, at once (a source holding no step takes it before any entry it pulls; one holding a step refuses it and its head event follows). A refused Register changes no view; the next head event does.
- **Head** (spec/wire.md §11.2 Head): any node holding S answers a Head naming S with its path (a reader its recorded path, a node keeping S unread the path registered with it), from the step after the one the request names (none: from genesis) through its head, at most ⌊`REPLY_BUDGET` / 292⌋ steps (the asker asks again from the last), whatever the connection proved and whatever is shared on it; a node not holding S answers 13. The asker takes an answer only as a verified path (a child of the step it named, or from genesis) and reads the head's step key and sync key from it; it is the answerer's word on its own head, nothing more.
- **Stamp**: a signature under S's write key, domain `"osis v1 stamp"` (spec/crypto.md §2), over (sync ID, blob ID, wrap). A node taking the entry checks it against the write key of its head; stamps already taken stay valid across new steps.
- **Role entries**: an entry deletes are judged by (spec/sync-judgement.md §2: a role commit, a commit carrying a request, or a blob such a commit links), and a record blob, is stamped, or signed as a deposit, under `"osis v1 role entry"` in place of the domain above; a node keeping S unread checks either domain alike, and takes a role entry outside S's and its store's caps (it counts toward neither): a stamped one with no bound of its own, a deposited one within the deposit budget. A reader that holds an entry and is shown it under another valid stamp keeps the lower (as bytes).
  - A node holding S's thing as a stub (spec/sync-set.md §2 Holding a stub) is shown only S's role entries (it declares its class opening S, spec/sync-opens.md §4 Source; spec/sync-streams.md §4), and fetches each whole (spec/wire.md §4 Pieces: a holder's piece travels without the other holders' only in a copy).
- **Taking a stamped entry**: a node keeping S unread takes the stamped entries it pulls (spec/sync-streams.md §4) by these rules, each with wrap and stamp.
  - Anyone holding a stamped entry may relay it. A stamp under a superseded write key is refused (code 9): such an entry goes as a deposit (spec/sync-inbox.md §3).
  - Refused: a bad stamp; a blob whose prefix expiry has passed; a blob ID with a tombstone; a blob above `MAX_BLOB` (code 3); an ephemeral blob past S's ephemeral cap (code 12).
  - A stamped entry of a deposited entry's blob ID replaces its wrap and stamp and clears its deposited flag.
  - A stamped entry's wrap and stamp never change while it lives: taking its blob ID again changes nothing and logs no event, as does a deposit of a held blob ID.
- **Relay-only** entry: the blob is not stored. It goes on to S's other holders by the stream (spec/sync-streams.md §4, a Stream with no cursor) and leaves no entry, log event, tombstone or expiry.
  - Refused:
    - without an expiry or with one more than `2 × MAX_DRIFT` ahead (code 1);
    - at or past its expiry (code 7);
    - above `MAX_RELAY_BLOB` (code 3);
    - at a node keeping S unread, without a stamp (Stamp, above) verifying under its head's write key (code 9);
    - past S's relay rate, or past the equal share of it of the source it came from (each connection sharing S, and the node's own sends, one source each) (code 4).
  - **Taken once**: every node holding S, reading it or keeping it unread, takes a relay-only blob ID once per sync ID until its expiry, and drops a repeat. It remembers a bounded number of them: past the bound the earliest-expiring is forgotten and its expiry becomes the sync ID's **floor**, at or below which relay-only blobs of that sync ID are dropped.
- **Wraps**:
  - **A node holding the blob's key**: it takes only a wrap opening to that key. An entry a source holds stamped, under any step's write key, under any other wrap counts as lacking at that source (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) for as long as the source holds it; a wrap that does not open at a source gets a fresh encryption there. A wrap opening under no read secret held counts so only until a read secret arrives under which it opens to the blob's key.
  - **A node holding no key**: it keeps its wrap unless one of these holds:
    - the new one opens under a held read secret and its own does not;
    - its own opened to a key the blob refused;
    - neither opens and the new one is stamped and its own is not.
  - At a node holding no key, an entry stamped at a source under a wrap that does not open while the node's own does counts as lacking there; when a new wrap that opens replaces another, entries at the sources that gave the other count as lacking there too.
- **Delete**: taken at a node keeping S unread only on a connection where S is open by a credential of its head other than the public's claim (spec/sync-opens.md §4), else refused whole (code 8); by the delete key, or of one entry by delete keys of levels: one delete signature per commitment group of the blob's prefix, each verifying under that group's commitment at its level (spec/crypto.md §7; one signature may cover several groups committing to the same key), plus a signature under the head's write key when any level is 3 (thing write secret), else its sync key; any other is refused. An empty group (a header list's) is never covered: such a blob goes by the delete key or at its expiry. No Delete is sent between readers (spec/sync-judgement.md §3).
  - Removal leaves a tombstone (§5).
  - **Certificate blobs** (record blobs, spec/permissions.md §4) commit to a writer delete key of S's thing (spec/crypto.md §7 Certificate blobs). A writer of S deletes one at a source keeping S unread holding it and another of its kind that wins over it (by `known`, spec/sync-judgement.md §3; spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates), judged by its own view of S's certificates once S's sources are heard out (spec/sync-status.md §1); nothing else is a ground. A Delete of one with no winner there (a hostile writer's) leaves that store without it until S's certificates of that kind next change.
- **Expiry**: at the prefix's `T` every node deletes the entry outright: no tombstone.
- **Caps at a node keeping S unread**: it caps storage per sync ID and the memory of ephemeral entries per sync ID (and in total), counting each entry's, log record's and tombstone's overhead besides its blob.
  - An ephemeral entry past its cap is refused (code 12).
  - At the total cap the sync ID using most gives way: its oldest entries (by their latest log event), deleted with no tombstone; role entries never.
  - It may rate-limit deletes, deposits, registrations and relay-only entries per sync ID (code 4, refused for now); pulled stamped entries are refused for room alone (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full).
