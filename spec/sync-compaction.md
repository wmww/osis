# Sync: compaction and ephemeral commits
> Summary: How nodes compact aspects and drop covered bodies (trigger, own bodies, carriers, freed links, sweep, repair, duplicates), and how ephemeral commits are sent, held in memory, compacted, capped and relayed.

**Principle:** area 4's (spec/sync-judgement.md): a body S still needs leaves it only by expiry, purge or a right holder's covering snapshot; this file says how such snapshots are made and what they drop.

## 1. Compaction and deletion
- **Compact**: a node that may write an aspect logs snapshot commits (spec/commits.md §2) for the aspects it compacts, then drops the bodies they cover and deletes, at peers that keep S unread, those it may purge.
  - **Trigger**, durable and ephemeral alike: a node that may write a content aspect (not role-class, not fixed retention, not a signal) writes a snapshot of it once the bodies it holds of it that a snapshot written now would cover and drop (not snapshots; past their keep-at-least bound; with an `expires`, only held value-less past it; not one a held valid snapshot already covers and the node drops under it, Own bodies and Other nodes below), two at least, cost more than twice a snapshot of it, each counted as what dropping it frees: its deltas' bytes with the pages they link (spec/crdt.md §8) and the chunk and tree-node blobs its deltas link that no live state of its thing links, plus a per-part and a per-entry charge; a held snapshot it would drop (durable; ephemeral, its own) adds the chunk and tree-node blobs it links that no live state links, but counts as no body. When it weighs is local policy; a peer's refusal triggers nothing.
    - **Cover once quiet**: such a node also writes one, whatever those bodies cost, once one of them is an expired write's leftover (spec/crdt.md §4.7) and the aspect has been quiet at the node for `MAX_DRIFT`: in that time no write of it was held there (a snapshot is none), none of its writes held there expired or turned valid or invalid, and the node ran throughout. It writes at most one per quiet time. A leftover is logged under no sync ID, so a node that missed its write may read differently until a writer holding the leftover has been quiet for `MAX_DRIFT`.
  - It drops a body once its keep-at-least bound (spec/commits.md §5 Keep bounds, a live body's included: Kept while live) is past on its clock. Any held valid writer's snapshot covering it drops it, the purge right or not (spec/commits.md §5 The setting); deleting it at peers that keep S unread needs the right and waits `MAX_DRIFT` more (spec/commits.md §5 Keep at least).
  - **Own bodies**: a node drops a delta it wrote none of whose writes is dead (spec/commits.md §5 Bodies and deaths) only under a cover it wrote itself (its own snapshot, its own replaces): another writer's snapshot may later count for nothing (a demotion not listing it), and the author's copy then brings the write back (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 must-hold).
  - **Other nodes**: every node that holds a snapshot does the same for its bodies, deleting at peers that keep S unread where it holds the right, judging a body again while it is before that time or not established covered or uncovered.
  - **Carriers**: a body is deleted at a sync ID only while other entries under it carry each of its deltas that holds a live write, is kept (spec/sync-judgement.md §2), or was dropped as covered and is not purged: a snapshot under the sync ID covering it (a held delta by the join of spec/commits.md §5 Covered, whatever carrier was recorded for it before), or another blob of the commit holding it that is not must-not-hold and not an identical body with a higher blob ID (of two duplicates the higher goes without waiting).
  - **No carrier**: a right holder that must delete a body with live writes and finds no carrier logs a covering snapshot first when it may write the aspect, unless it holds a blob of the commit that will carry them (a purge's rewrite).
  - **Freed links**: a node dropping a body (as covered, by expiry or purge, an ephemeral commit forgotten, §2; a delta held on as its leftover, its delta's) first puts each link its deltas hold (their values' tree roots and their pages; a leftover's values' roots) in a local freed set of the delta's thing. A body dropped as invalid frees nothing: it stays at peers with what it links (spec/permissions.md §6).
    - Each freed link is judged when its delete is sent, against the bodies (delta, snapshot or leftover) and live states of its thing the node holds then: one their trees reach leaves the set, kept. A judgment needs every node blob of those trees: one not held is fetched first, and the thing's links wait for it.
    - A link no held source reaches is freed: the links of a node or page blob join the set (it is fetched first when not held), the blob's bytes are dropped, and the node keeps of it only its blob ID, salt, delete keys and thing blind, no key.
    - A node holding the blob uses the link only when its prefix bears the link's delete keys under the link's thing blind (spec/crypto.md §7): else it is no piece of that thing by that link, leaves the set, and nothing is dropped or deleted through it.
    - A freed link is deleted at each peer that keeps S unread and holds an entry of it, under a sync ID of its thing: by the sync ID delete key when its held prefix bore the link, else by level, signed for the link's salt and thing blind with the delete keys it names. It leaves the set once the node knows no entry of it.
    - A node that lost read access to a thing (spec/permissions.md §3) discards the thing's freed set; of the blobs the forgotten parts reach through held node blobs, it drops those no remaining source of the thing reaches and whose prefix bears their link.
  - Deletes at a peer that keeps S unread wait as spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Role entries first says.
  - Two snapshots covering each other (equal `ts`, spec/commits.md §5) are covered-equal; a compactor drops the one with the higher commit ID.
  - **A snapshot turning invalid**, durable or ephemeral: a node whose held valid snapshot turns invalid, at once, forgets its own tombstones of the entries of the commits it dropped as covered (ephemeral: or superseded, §2) on that aspect, takes the entries it knows of them as not held (a durable one's part is missing again, spec/permissions.md §5.2), fetching them where present (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies), and each of its streams (spec/sync-streams.md §4) carrying a sync ID whose closure holds the thing asks for the peer's live ephemeral entries again (a Pull with `ephemeral`), so the copies peers still have reach it again. A durable commit forgotten under it (spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting, its tombstones gone) reads as never received and comes back only when a holder logs it again.
- No node snapshots a signal (spec/commits.md §5 Signals): its bodies leave by expiry, purge, as invalid, or out of history.
- **Sweep**: before a snapshot of a durable content aspect (not role-class, not `retention^.0`), a node writes, in as many ordinary content commits as `MAX_DELTA_BARE` (spec/crdt.md §3.3 rule 7) needs, each at its delta stamp, a reset of each map entry and list element (at any depth below live ones) that is not live (spec/crdt.md §5.6, §5.7), holds a write beside its creation and its reset, and whose creation is dead; and a delete of each text element whose creation is dead and that holds an undelete's char.
  - A write here: in a map any write under a key, else a value-carrying one or `W`.
  - A creation is dead when the record has a reset of its own, or when no creation of it is held while the node's state reflects the delta that wrote it: that delta applied here and valid, or reached by a held valid snapshot's `Frontiers` or `Absorbed` (spec/crdt.md §8).
  - A live map record holds what it reads as (spec/crdt.md §5.6 Live): its fragment joined with its dot's key-erased record's.
  - Any other record lacking its creation is left: the creation may arrive, or turn valid. A payload of an enum case that is not current is left (spec/crdt.md §5.4).
  - A reset so repeated stays if the first remover turns out invalid.
- **Repair**: a node that may write an aspect of a thing in a sync ID's closure, past its first sync there (spec/sync-streams.md §5), acts on each entry of a suspect list a connected peer sent it for the sync ID (spec/permissions.md §4) whose digest differs from the node's own digest of that aspect, if it holds state of the aspect.
  - It writes a snapshot of the aspect from its whole state (spec/commits.md §2) and logs it like any must-hold body, unless it already wrote one from that same state for that node.
  - It judges each entry once per suspect list.
  - It SHOULD report every entry it judged (a diagnostic), repaired or not.
- **Duplicates**: of two blobs of one commit with the same delta set under one sync ID (not only the same body, spec/sync-judgement.md §1), both copies or both not (spec/sync-judgement.md §1 Per-sync-ID copies), a delete-key holder deletes the one with the higher blob ID; where the sync ID's body of them is a per-sync-ID copy, a shared blob beside a copy, whatever its ID. Of two at a peer that keeps S unread where one counts as lacking (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Wraps), that one, and it counts as no copy of the other.

## 2. Ephemeral commits
- **Sending**: a node's own writes of ephemeral aspects (spec/data-model.md §2) are ephemeral commits (spec/commits.md §2) like its durable writes are durable commits.
  - **One commit**: a write's ephemeral writes are one commit (spec/commits.md §3; in session form, without `expires` under the node's session, with one under its second session, spec/crdt.md §2.1).
  - **Delete**: a delete, and an undo of a creation (spec/crdt.md §4.6), writes no ephemeral commit: its durable commit carries a purge request naming each ephemeral value aspect of the thing that may hold content (as the aspects its resets name, spec/crdt.md §4.6, by held headers), written as a `purge.0` write when no other part of the commit is on the thing (spec/commits.md §5 Purge). Every node applying it, the writing node first, holds the thing's ephemeral writes of commits stamped before it value-less (spec/permissions.md §6: the request's author needs write on the thing, every ephemeral delta's purgeable-by being `writer`); a later ephemeral write stands.
  - **Ephemeral signals**: a write's ephemeral signal writes go as one relay-only commit, at once.
  - Its large values are chunked and its deltas page as durable ones do (spec/data-model.md §6, spec/crdt.md §8); a commit that still does not fit a blob is refused at the writer (spec/commits.md §1, §2).
- **As durable bodies**: an ephemeral commit's bodies are judged, logged, relayed and deleted exactly as durable bodies are (spec/sync-judgement.md §1-§4, spec/sync-ids.md §6, §1): under the sync IDs of the closures its things are in, must-hold while established uncovered, stamped under the head's write key of the peer that pulls them (so a refusal under an older key, code 9, is answered by stamping again under the new head), relayed or deposited by readers, deleted at a sync ID once a snapshot there carries them, logged again as a fresh encryption where the old blob is tombstoned.
  - A thing no closure holds syncs no more (spec/sync-set.md §2): nothing of it goes anywhere, and its bodies under a sync ID it left are deleted there like a durable thing's, by a node that held the body in that closure (after a start a node's view of the closure is what has arrived so far: it deletes nothing for being outside it).
  - **Presence**: an ephemeral entry counts as held at a peer only as `known` lists it (spec/sync-judgement.md §3). A node knows an ephemeral entry it holds no body of only while `known` lists it at some source or its tombstone lasts; then it forgets it, with no tombstone.
  - **Tombstones**: an ephemeral entry's tombstone lasts `MAX_DRIFT`, at every node alike (spec/sync-ids.md §5), and an announcement of a blob tombstoned here within that time is not fetched (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Tombstones).
- **Holding**: what differs from durable bodies is storage: every node holds the ephemeral commits it makes or receives, their blobs per sync ID and every record about them (entries, presence, tombstones, carriers), in memory only, never in its store or a bundle, until it restarts or they go (below).
  - **Verification**: a received one is verified as any commit (spec/commits.md §4) before its blob is kept, logged or served anywhere.
    - One whose author's record, a part's thing's record or a chain it needs is not held is unjudgeable (spec/commits.md §4): nothing of it is kept; its entry counts against the node's cap below until it is fetched again.
    - Parts are judged (spec/permissions.md §5.2); an invalid part is dropped as a durable one (spec/permissions.md §6) and counts against no cap.
    - A session-form one applies atomically and an invalid part is un-merged (spec/crdt.md §4.6).
    - A part past its `expires` is held as its leftover, value-less, at every node (spec/crdt.md §4.7), one arriving past it included.
    - Purge requests (durable commits) drop its deltas as any (spec/commits.md §5 Purge, spec/permissions.md §6): held as their leftovers, a waiting part included, and missing again where the request falls.
    - An ephemeral commit more than `MAX_DRIFT` ahead of the wall clock waits as a durable one does.
  - **Forgetting**: a node forgets an ephemeral commit whole, header included, once none of its parts is held any more (each covered, superseded or dropped as invalid; an expired leftover is held until covered or superseded, a purged one until forgotten as a durable one, spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting) and none waits, keeping of its header only what deleting its entries at peers that keep S unread needs while they have any. One with a part dropped as invalid or held as a purged leftover is forgotten as spec/commits.md §5 Forgetting says for either class: its header stays until no entry of it is known and no delete of one is within its tombstone, so a copy is not taken while it stays invalid and, valid again, it is fetched again (spec/commits.md §5 Invalid).
    - A peer asking for an entry the node listed as held there (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) lacks it: one whose commit's body the node does not hold is absent there.
    - A copy of a forgotten commit arriving again applies and goes again by the same rules.
- **Compaction**: any writer of the aspect compacts, by the trigger of §1.
  - **Dropping covered bodies**: every node drops a held body a held valid ephemeral snapshot covers (spec/commits.md §5) once its keep-at-least bound passes (spec/commits.md §5 Keep bounds): at once where no setting keeps history for the aspect of any of its deltas.
    - A node drops a body it authored, live or not, only under one of its own snapshots (§1 Own bodies; no demotion frontier lists an ephemeral commit, spec/permissions.md §5.1), but a replaced body under purge on replace, which any writer's cover drops (spec/commits.md §5).
    - A snapshot stamped no later and covered by another goes the same way.
    - Of covered-equal snapshots the higher commit ID goes (§1).
  - **A snapshot turning invalid**: as §1; a node that may write the aspect and still holds a valid ephemeral part of it also writes a snapshot of the aspect from its state and logs it (a new commit, which a peer's delete tombstone on the old entries, spec/sync-ids.md §6, does not stop).
  - **Frontier**: a snapshot's frontier stops below a body of a session that is invalid or missing at the writer (spec/crdt.md §8), so that session's later bodies are not covered until it is valid and held there.
  - **New sessions**: a node one of whose session-form writes under its current sessions turns invalid begins new sessions (spec/crdt.md §2.1) for what it writes from then on.
  - **Deleting at a peer that keeps S unread**: a writer deletes the entries of a dropped commit at such a peer (spec/crypto.md §7, level 3) once an entry of a snapshot covering it is held there. One dropped for no coverer (expired, its things freed) goes at once.
    - At a peer full for S a covering snapshot it lacks counts as held there (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Deletes).
    - A forgotten commit's pages and trees go by its freed links (§1).
  - **Superseded parts**: a node also drops a held part of a plain ephemeral commit that has `expires` once the part carries no reset (spec/crdt.md §4.2; tombstones included) and is superseded by a valid held part of another plain ephemeral commit of its author on the same aspect, whatever else either commit holds.
    - The superseding commit and part qualify when all of these hold:
      - its `expires` is not earlier;
      - it carries no reset;
      - each of its writes has a later `ts` than every write of the superseded part and is not into a record it does not create;
      - its delta joined with the superseded part reads (spec/crdt.md §4.4) as it does alone.
    - This is judged when the superseding part is held valid (on arrival, at its build, or when it turns valid) and when the superseded part goes value-less at its expiry.
    - The dropped part's writes then vanish whole, as if never applied, at every node, its author included, so a later removal or undo of the superseding write does not show them again.
    - Once a commit with a part so dropped is forgotten, its author deletes its entries at a peer that keeps S unread once a superseder or coverer of its parts is held there, a superseder standing for the snapshot in Deleting at a peer that keeps S unread, and no other node deletes them.
    - One whose every part is superseded on arrival is not held.
    - An own write with `expires` superseded by a later own write before it was sent is never sent.
- **Caps**:
  - **At a node, for what it reads**: per sync ID, a node holds at most a configured number of bytes of ephemeral bodies from others, each counted at an estimate of the memory it takes held (decoded, its pages' content included, with the records beside it; local policy, at least its encoded size). Each page entry counts too, at least its encoded size.
    - Nothing of a commit is decoded before its header lists, the pages its deltas link and the root nodes of the large values they and their pages link are held (it waits for them meanwhile). Then it is applied only when a bound on that estimate made from its bytes, its pages' and the lengths its value roots claim for what of their trees is not held (local policy) fits the room left under the cap, checked before any decode and before any chunk of its values is fetched, each time it is applied, so also when it applies after waiting (for its blobs, its `ts`, an epoch); others are not applied there.
    - Once it applies, the node fetches the rest of its values' trees at once (they are held in memory at peers); each blob counts on arrival as any entry.
    - An entry refused so is fetched again once the sync ID's held bytes dropped below what they were at the refusal, one that can never fit (larger than the whole cap) not at all.
    - A commit held only as purged leftovers counts by its own estimate against the sync IDs its entries counted under, until it is forgotten or held otherwise, not through its entries (deleting them at peers leaves it counted).
  - **At a node that keeps S unread**: it carries S's ephemeral entries up to its cap (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Caps). An entry it refuses reaches no holder through it until room appears there (an entry leaving, expiry included), when it pulls S again (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full).
- **Relay-only commits**:
  - **Dropped unopened**: a node drops a relay-only blob unopened when one of these holds:
    - it is larger than `MAX_RELAY_BLOB`;
    - its prefix expiry is passed or more than `2 × MAX_DRIFT` ahead of its wall clock;
    - it is a repeat, or at or below the sync ID's floor (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Relay-only, Taken once).
  - **Delivery**: it verifies the rest like an ephemeral commit and tells its valid ephemeral signal parts to listeners once (spec/data-model.md §2). It holds and logs nothing.
  - **Replay**: it remembers delivered relay-only commit IDs until their `ts` is `MAX_DRIFT` old, at most `MAX_DELIVERED` (past that the earliest delivered is forgotten and its `ts` becomes its author's floor unless lower, at or below which that author's relay-only commits are dropped), and drops relay-only commits whose `ts` is more than `MAX_DRIFT` from its wall clock.
  - **Passing on**: a node that reads S passes a verified new one on, stamped as received, and a node that keeps S unread those it takes (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Relay-only), to the peers spec/sync-streams.md §4 (Relay-only entries) names.
