# Cryptography
> Summary: The algorithms and key material of suite 1: primitives, signatures, key derivation (signing keys, things minted with a key, dependent and independent things), per-role epochs and boxes, wraps, hashes, delete keys and algorithm agility.

## 1. Suite 1 primitives
- **Signature**: Ed25519 (RFC 8032) with strict verification: reject non-canonical encodings and small-order points (as ed25519-dalek `verify_strict`).
  - A valid Ed25519 public key is the canonical encoding of a curve point not of small order.
  - Batch and single verification MUST agree.
- **AEAD**: XChaCha20-Poly1305: 32-byte key, 24-byte nonce, 16-byte tag.
- **Hash, KDF, MAC**: BLAKE3. `kdf(purpose, secret, input) = keyed_hash(derive_key("osis v1 " + purpose, secret), input)`; BLAKE3 XOF output where more than 32 bytes are needed.
- **Sealed box**: hybrid KEM X25519 + ML-KEM-768 (FIPS 203).
  - Sender: X25519 ephemeral exchange (reject a non-contributory result) giving `ss_x`, `ct_x` (the ephemeral public key); ML-KEM encapsulation giving `ss_m`, `ct_m`.
  - `K = derive_key("osis v1 hybrid kem", ss_x || ss_m || ct_x || ct_m || pk_x || pk_m)`.
  - The payload is sealed under `AEAD_K` with nonce 0 and the box's AD. Box layout: spec/wire.md §7.
  - Public key `pk_x || pk_m` (spec/wire.md §3). It is valid iff `pk_x` is not of small order and `pk_m` passes the FIPS 203 encapsulation-key check. Nothing is sealed to an invalid one.
- **Randomness**: OS CSPRNG.
- Every hash osis computes itself is BLAKE3.

## 2. Signatures
- Signed input is `domain || digest`: an ASCII domain string, then the 32-byte BLAKE3 digest of the signed canonical bytes. Digests per object: spec/wire.md.
- Domains, one per signed object:
  - `"osis v1 commit"` (input digest: the commit ID, spec/wire.md §4)
  - `"osis v1 sync step"` (a step, spec/wire.md §8)
  - `"osis v1 sync endorse"` (an endorsement of a step, input digest: the step ID; spec/wire.md §8)
  - `"osis v1 sync open"` (an open signature, spec/sync-opens.md §4)
  - `"osis v1 stamp"` (stamps, spec/sync-ids.md §6)
  - `"osis v1 deposit"` (spec/sync-inbox.md §3)
  - `"osis v1 role entry"` (a stamp or deposit signature of a role entry, in place of the two above, spec/sync-ids.md §6)
  - `"osis v1 delete"`
  - `"osis v1 connection proof"` (a connection proof, spec/wire.md §11.2: over the prover's role, the verifier's challenge and the listener's key)
- Signers: signing keys (commits signed as the thing their grant is on, spec/permissions.md §1; connection proofs, spec/sync-opens.md §2), sync ID role keys (spec/sync-ids.md §2), blinded delete keys (§7).

## 3. Keys
- **Notation**: a `kdf` written with two arguments has empty input; a tid as input is its 15 bytes; an Ed25519 key "from" a kdf output is the key with that 32-byte seed (RFC 8032).
- **Role secrets**: 32 random bytes per role (sync, read, write, admin) per epoch (§4).
- **Dependent thing** (spec/permissions.md §3): its role secrets derive from those of its mint admin (spec/permissions.md §1), and so on up its record chain while that one is dependent too. The first thing up that chain, itself included, that is independent is its **independent**.
  - Its **derivation chain**: its record chain up to and including its independent. Only keys use it (derivation, delete keys, section chains, spec/wire.md §4); it carries no authority.
  - Role secret = `kdf("thing <role>", mint admin's <role> secret, tid)`, one per epoch of the mint admin's role (forks included).
  - It sits at the epoch with n = min(mint admin's n, 2^62 − 1) that the nonce rule (§4) gives the thing, role and derived secret.
  - **Order n** (§4) of a derived epoch: the mint admin epoch's order n while the thing is dependent, its n once it is independent.
- **Making a thing independent** (spec/permissions.md §3) is a rotation of all four roles from its derived epochs: the history boxes carry the derived secrets, the commit wraps under the derived read epoch, `keys.0` is written.
- A node counts a thing as **independent**, with secrets of its own, iff it holds a role commit whose `keys.0` part is valid (spec/permissions.md §5.2) and wrote a keyset in the body the node held (its body since dropped or missing counts). A thing whose only such part turns invalid is dependent again (derived secrets), the secrets it learned from that commit kept for reading only (§4).
- **Signing key** (spec/permissions.md §1): one 32-byte seed; the Ed25519 key from `kdf("sign", seed)`.
- **A thing minted with a key** (spec/permissions.md §3) has role secrets of its own from its minting commit, a rotation of all four roles from none (§4): random, or for a link key `kdf("role <role>", seed)` of the link's seed (spec/permissions.md §7). The minting commit wraps under its own first read epoch, none preceding it. Its creation record commits to the key. A link key's creation nonce is `kdf("creation nonce", seed, "")` (spec/wire.md §3).
- **Independent thing**: one KEM key pair per role per epoch, `kdf("kem", role secret)`. The read-role pair's X25519 half is the thing's claim key (spec/sync-ids.md §3).
- **`keys.0`**: a struct holding the current keyset only, and the box records (§4, Stale).
  - Per role a `keyset` (spec/data-model.md §9; `demotions`, `demotes`: §4, Carried demotions).
  - `sealed`: per box of the thing to a holder actor, keyed as the box, the keysets of the holder it names (§4, Stale), written with the box by whoever writes it.
  - For an independent thing, also its **sync ID** (spec/sync-ids.md §2) and **deposit seed** (32 random bytes): what an external fire on it goes under and is signed with (spec/sync-inbox.md §2).
  - A role's entry is written by the node that rotates that role (spec/permissions.md §3).
  - An admin writes the sync ID and deposit seed; it MAY refresh the deposit seed at any time and MUST write a fresh one in each rotation of the sync role it makes.
  - `inbox`: the thing's inbox where it is another thing (spec/sync-inbox.md §1), written by an admin.
- **Dedup secret** of a thing = `derive_key("osis v1 dedup", creation nonce)`.

## 4. `secrets.0` and epochs
Terms in this section depend on each other; each is defined once, under its bold name.

### Epochs
- **Epoch**: each role secret of a thing has an epoch (n, nonce), independent per role. Each epoch names its suite ID.
- **Roles secret** of role r's secret s: `kdf("roles", s)`.
- **Nonce rule**: the nonce of role r's secret s of thing A at n is `kdf("epoch", kdf("roles", s), A's tid || role byte || le64(n))` (32 bytes; role byte 0 read, 1 write, 2 admin, 3 sync).
  - A secret is the secret of the epoch its (thing, role, n) and the nonce rule name; a roles secret alike names its epoch.
  - A box, history box entry or entry box carrying a secret is read as naming that epoch.
- **Rotation** of a set of roles (spec/permissions.md §3): each role gets a fresh random secret under epoch n+1 with the nonce the rule gives; the other roles keep their secret and epoch. n is the greatest n of the role's current and unsuperseded epochs at the rotator (0 for a thing with no epoch of the role at all).
- **Lists**: a rotation's keyset of r (§3) lists each current and each unsuperseded epoch of r at the rotator other than a derived one (§3) (spec/wire.md §7).
- **Supersedes**: what a rotation of r to epoch e supersedes is read from its rotation commit alone, whatever else the node held when the commit arrived:
  - the epochs below e's n its keyset lists;
  - at a node holding e's secret, the epochs e's history box lists below e's n.
- **Fork**: concurrent rotations of one role create several epochs with one n. Nodes keep all of them.
- A **rotation commit of** an epoch is a role commit writing its keyset in `keys.0` (and its history box). The keyset carries the digest of the rotation's names (Names, below; spec/wire.md §7).

### Boxes
- **`secrets.0`** = `{ boxes: map<(holder tid, role, epoch), box>, history: map<(role, epoch), history box> }`.
- A rotation of role r writes r's boxes at the new epoch and removes the boxes keyed with role r at the epochs whose secret its history box carries (below). A box at an epoch it cannot open, or does not hold established, stands: it still opens for its holder, which then adds that secret to the history (History box, below). Boxes keyed with other roles are untouched.
- A **box** seals one role's secret of one epoch to a holder, AD = (thing tid, holder tid, role, epoch, form fields). Forms (enum):
  - sealed: `{ recipient keyset epoch, suite ID, sealed box }` to the holder's KEM key for that role (§3);
  - symmetric: `{ epoch of s, wrap under kdf("box", s) }`, s the holder's own secret of that role, used when the writer holds s (the mint admin a dependent thing derived from);
  - joint: `{ recipient keyset epochs (two or more, ascending), suite ID, sealed box }`, the sealed payload sealed to the KEM key for that role of each keyset in turn, the last innermost: only a holder of every one opens it (Holder actors, Seal).
- A holder gets one box per (role, epoch) written to it, keyed (holder, role, epoch): sealed to its KEM key for that role, symmetric, or joint.
- A box whose secret does not name its key's (role, epoch) is ignored.
- A thing's **boxes**, as every rule of this section judges them, are those of its `secrets.0` state; the boxes of a `secrets.0` delta a node judges invalid count for nothing. Such a box still opens for its holder until that role's next rotation, which nothing it does brings about unless it rides on an edge write that counts (Edge writes): it carries any secret its signer held, and a signer that no longer holds that role has a departure of its own (Departures and covering, below).
- `keys.0` vouches for no secret: a node holding an epoch's secret derives its keyset itself (§3, §7).
- **Rotating needs only the right to rotate** (spec/permissions.md §3), never a secret of the role: a rotation lists and supersedes every current epoch, held or not; its history box carries the established secrets the rotator holds, and a holder of a superseded secret it lacks adds that secret (History box, below). A current epoch whose secret a node lacks, ordered before a departure it knows of, cannot cover it (Encrypting under): it rotates. Only the commit's wrap needs a secret (§5): a node holding no established read secret of the thing at all is stranded (spec/permissions.md §4) until re-sealed.
- A **grant** writes the grantee's boxes for every granted role at each of that role's current epochs, and at read each dropped epoch it holds (Floors); one without history is a cut (Floors).
- A rotation writes new boxes for every current holder of each rotated role, with the keysets and history boxes, in one role commit (the **rotation commit**; its boxes page, spec/crdt.md §8).
- A **re-seal** (spec/permissions.md §3) writes a current holder's boxes for the current epochs of the roles it names: the trigger's at the epochs the holder has no box at, replacing none (a box replaced is a removal, Authority).
- A holder actor is boxed as Holder actors (below) says, by a grant, a re-seal and a rotation alike, each box recorded in `keys.0` (Holder actors, Stale).
- **History box** (role r, epoch e) = AEAD under `kdf("history", secret_r[e])` of:
  - every distinct earlier `secret_r` (n below e's) of an established superseded epoch the writer holds that the carry rule lets in at e (Floors), and every one the live entries of the key it opens list;
  - the rotation's names (below), the same in every entry of the key.
  - the epoch's floor (Floors).
  - the roles secret of every earlier epoch (n below e's) whose secret or roles secret the writer holds, as its writer reads them, the carry rule aside, but those whose secret it carries (Roles secrets).
  - It is written by the rotation of e; a grant or re-seal boxing e writes it again, from what its writer holds; where e is blocked at the writer (Encrypting under), only what the live entries list (a history entry is encrypted under e), roles secrets alike.
  - **History add**: a node holding e's secret, e established and not blocked at it (Encrypting under), that holds such a secret (the carry rule letting it in at e), or such a roles secret, no live entry of the key it opens lists (a roles secret: as either), puts an entry carrying what it holds beside the entries there (resetting none), when it holds r on the thing, whatever it may rotate (spec/permissions.md §3), deciding at the end of a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1). Nothing re-seals a superseded epoch.
  - A reader tries each live entry of a history key (spec/crdt.md §5.6), not only their merged read, with the secret of e it holds.

### Floors
- A grant **without history** (spec/permissions.md §2) is, in the grant's own role commit, a rotation of the target's read role (the **cut**) whose history box carries no earlier secret, so it removes no box; its floor is its own n, and the grant's `floor` is that n. It lists and supersedes as any rotation and boxes every current holder and the grantee by the boxing rule.
- **Floor** of a read epoch: each entry of its history box writes one (spec/wire.md §7). A cut's is its own n; any other rotation's is the greatest floor among its maker and the holders it boxes at the new epoch (each boxed, none handed what lies below its own); a grant, re-seal or History add writes the epoch's floor as its writer reads it. A node reads an epoch's floor as the greatest the live entries it opens at the epoch write, at most its n: only a holder of the epoch's secret writes one, never a keyset or another party. A fixed epoch's floor is 0, as is that of an epoch an independent thing derived from its mint admin's; a dependent thing's epoch derived (§3) from its mint admin's epoch e has e's floor. An epoch none of whose entries the node opens has no known floor: 0 for the boxing rule and Current, none for the carry rule.
- Only read has floors: at every other role every floor is 0 and the rules below pass everything.
- **A party's floor** on thing A: by its edge in force on A, the edge's `floor` (absent: 0), at least the floor of that edge write's signer on A (a party hands no history it was not given); through each holder actor of A it holds read on for keys, that holder actor's floor on A; the least of these, 0 when it holds A by none. Along a cycle of signers, the greatest floor written on it. An epoch's **maker's floor** is that of its rotation commit's signer; where the node holds no rotation commit of it, the greatest floor of A's holders.
- **Carry rule**: an entry of the history box of read epoch e carries the secret of f only when f's floor is at least e's and at least e's maker's (never where either is unknown). It binds every entry: the rotation's own history box (its maker the rotator, its floor the one it writes), a grant's or re-seal's entry beyond what the live entries list, and the History add.
- **Boxing rule**: a rotation, grant, re-seal or tip add boxes a holder at a read epoch only where the epoch's floor is at least the holder's floor.
- **Low floor**: at the end of a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1), a node that may rotate read, holds a current read epoch whose floor is below that of a holder its rotation would box, and holds no unsuperseded read epoch it does not count established (whose names it does not meet: it may lack a removal), rotates read (a fork beside a cut; a writer's low-floor epoch after one): the holder is boxed at the new epoch, whose floor is the greatest of theirs, and its history entry carries the low epoch's roles secret.
  - A writer's rotation whose history box writes a floor below a holder's leaves that holder unboxed there, and it counts the epoch as no current one (Established and current): what is written under it never reaches that holder (the next rotation, Low floor, may not carry it), its role commits do (Roles secrets). Any writer may leave a holder unboxed (notes/keys.md, Lockouts).
- **Dropped**: an established epoch whose secret the node holds, not current, that a held rotation lists and may not carry (the carry rule at that rotation's epoch). A grant with history boxes the grantee at each dropped epoch the node holds that the boxing rule allows. **Tip add**: at the end of a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1), a node that may rotate read and holds a dropped epoch boxes it to each current holder of read the boxing rule allows whose live boxes, with the history entries the node opens through them, do not reach it.
  - A tip add is a box put, which needs the right to rotate read (spec/permissions.md §3): a dropped epoch whose every holder only reads reaches a holder lacking it once a node that may rotate read and holds it acts.
- **Re-added without history**: a grant without history to a party whose newest edge write on A, applied, is a removal also boxes it, beside the boxing rule, at each established epoch it holds that the rotation of that removal listed (none when the removal went without its rotation); the tip add boxes the rest of those epochs, by a node holding them, to a holder so re-added that does not reach them.
- **Roles secrets**: a node holds the roles secret of each epoch whose secret it holds, and of each epoch a history entry it opens under a secret not reading-only carries one of. A roles secret opens only the **role parts** (deltas on `roles.0`, `keys.0`, `secrets.0` and `sync.0`) sealed under its epoch, and the wraps of the blobs carrying them (§5); a node lacking the epoch's secret encrypts under it only the role parts of a commit giving read epochs (§5 Who wraps under what). So every holder of a thing, a holder of a floor included, opens every role commit's role parts on it (the roster, the names its rotations name, the keysets and history boxes it establishes epochs by, the steps that place the delete keys its parts list, §7; boxes and entries stay sealed to their holders and epochs), and nothing else from below its floor.
- **State at a cut**: a holder granted without history reads the current state, never a commit written before its cut (§5 Passing on): each aspect's state reaches it as a compaction snapshot (spec/commits.md §2) under an epoch it reads. A node's **seal floor** of A is the floor of the epoch it encrypts A's content under (Who wraps under what, §5); a part's floor is the greatest floor of an epoch it was opened under (its node's own: written under), none known for another's part read in no section.
  - A node that may write a content aspect (not a signal, nor of fixed retention) of a thing whose read secrets are A's (own or derived) owes a snapshot of it from its whole state, a duty as the one a purge leaves (spec/commits.md §5 Purge), where it holds a body of it whose floor is below its seal floor of A, or not known, until a held valid snapshot of the aspect whose floor is at least that seal floor covers each such body (spec/commits.md §5 Covered). It judges the bodies it holds once its seal floor of A rises (the cut's signer in the grant's own step, any other at the end of a complete pass, spec/sync-status.md §1), and each body of known floor below it as it applies, writing what that owes at the end of its next complete pass (so a node fetching history writes none the cut's snapshots carry: they follow the bodies, Order, §5). No node is designated: the cut's signer carries what it held at the cut, any writer what reached it later; a write beside the cut reaches the holder once a writer holding it completes a pass, and never while every writer holding it stays away.
  - A snapshot carries state alone: a signal's fires, replaced bodies and deleted text are in none (spec/commits.md §5 Signals; spec/crdt.md §3.3 rule 5), so no earlier version and no message from before the cut reaches the holder.
  - A snapshot carries the state as the encoding holds it (spec/crdt.md §5), not only its read: the authors and stamps of its live writes, a sequence element's earlier position write, every max or min contribution, a removed record's or element's later concurrent writes, deleted elements without their content. No read shows these.
  - Expiring values: the snapshot from its whole state carries none of them (spec/crdt.md §8). Beside it the node writes, for each expiry T of an owed body holding values that is after the `ts` it stamps it with (spec/commits.md §6), an expiring snapshot ending at T carrying the values its bodies ending at T give, so the holder reads each value until T, and no blob carries it past T.
  - An edit of a fire below the holder's floor is listed nowhere there (spec/commits.md §5 Signals): no version of a past fire reaches it.
  - A thing's record is no content (Passing on binds none), but rides in its minting's blob: a minting whose floor (its `created.0` part's) is below the node's seal floor of A, or not known, is no entry holding the record under a sync ID of A (spec/permissions.md §4 Logging certificates), so the record goes there as a record blob (spec/wire.md §3) under an epoch the holder reads. The holder learns it for the strong reaching the thing (spec/commits.md §4 Records) and applies nothing on a thing whose record it lacks.
- **The setting**: the signer of a grant without history writes next, under the cut, A's current `retention^.0` setting (spec/commits.md §5 In force), its floors joined over the settings it holds (One way): the holder opens no earlier setting.

### Names
- **Edge writes** of holder M on A, at a node: the edge writes on M's edge into A (spec/permissions.md §2) the node holds, their commits resolved (Met), in key order (spec/permissions.md §5.1). Each **applies** there (the role timeline applies it) or applies nothing (invalid at its point, or dropped by an audit), and **gives** M the role its value gives for keys (Holders for keys, below; none for a removal). One **takes** there when it applies, or would apply at its point over the edge the newest write before it that applies and gives M more set, else over the start (a second finalize of a grant another removal ended first). One **stands** there when it applies, or would apply at its point over the start or the edge some write before it that applies set (only the edge it meets differs). One that applies nothing **counts** when its signer held write on A just before its point (as its authority is judged there, spec/permissions.md §5.2), whatever befell the signer since. M's **start** is its grant for keys before the first of them (the record edge, else none).
- **Names**: a rotation of thing A names, from its rotator's own role timeline (never copied from another history box), each holder's edge writes on A that:
  - apply there and **lower** the holder: give it less than its grant for keys just before (a removal, demotion, self-removal or finalize);
  - apply nothing there, count and give the holder a role, **void**.
- A name is the write's commit key (spec/permissions.md §5.1), its holder, and whether it is void.
- **Departs mark**: when the rotation commit itself lowers a holder of A it cannot name itself. The names then carry its departs mark: its `ts`, signer and header `blind` (spec/wire.md §4).
- Every history box of one rotation carries the same names, and the rotated roles' keysets carry their digest (spec/wire.md §7): an entry is **bound** to each held valid rotation commit of its epoch whose keyset carries the digest of its names; a held valid `keys.0` compaction snapshot carrying the keyset beside a `secrets.0` snapshot carrying the entry counts as one, by its signer.
- The rotator logs every named commit under each sync ID it logs the rotation commit under (spec/sync-judgement.md §3).
- **Met**: the names of a history box entry are met at a node when both hold:
  - each name's commit is applied at the node with its `roles.0` delta on A, if it has one, held (a copy of the commit lacking it does not count) and resolved (spec/permissions.md §2: every entry its removals reset known, so its edge writes take part in the walk; a finalize held without the write it ends removes nobody); an applied name's write only while it stands there (Edge writes), a void name's write only while it applies nothing there.
  - its departs mark, if any, is met: the node holds a role commit of that `ts`, signer and `blind` with its `roles.0` delta on A, resolved (as above), each of its edge writes on A standing there.
  - A holder of an epoch's secret may put an entry at its history key with any names, bound to nothing: it establishes the epoch where the genuine entry would not and covers nothing.

### Established and current
- **Established**: an epoch e of r is established at a node in any of three cases.
  - It is derived (§3) from an established epoch of the mint admin, or a first epoch of a thing minted with a key, its minting commit held valid.
  - All of these hold:
    - the node holds a valid rotation commit of e (its own included) with both its `keys.0` and `secrets.0` deltas on the thing, or a compaction snapshot of `secrets.0` that keys a box or history box with e;
    - e's n is at most 2^62, or the node holds another established epoch of r with n at least e's n − 1;
    - if the node holds e's secret (other than for reading only), some live entry of e's history box that opens under that secret has its names met.
  - At a node keeping a sync ID S of the thing unread (spec/sync.md §3), which reads none of its role commits: r is sync, the node holds e's secret, and a step of its stored path of S bears that secret's sync key; e is established under S alone.
- A `secrets.0` compaction snapshot leaves out every live box and history box record at an epoch that would not be established at its writer were the names condition dropped (spec/crdt.md §8): it keys only epochs its writer verified, and a body writing such a record is not covered by it (spec/commits.md §5) nor listed in its `Absorbed` (spec/crdt.md §8), and stays beside it.
- **Current**: a role's current epochs are its established epochs that no established rotation superseded, whose floor is at least the node's own floor on the thing (Floors).
- **Unsuperseded**: the epochs that would be current at the node were the names condition of Established dropped (as a node holding none of their secrets counts them). One unsuperseded and not current has names the node does not meet, and is current wherever its secret is not held.
- A derived epoch (§3) of an independent thing that some held commit encrypts under is current too unless a rotation lists it (its history box carries it): its writer, which wrote before it held the thing's own epochs, rotates (a current epoch it wrote under that no rotation lists makes its rotation due). Other derived epochs of the mint admin's later epochs are none of the thing's.
  - A node counts a commit as encrypting under such an epoch when it sealed the commit's section of the thing, or of a dependent thing whose derivation chain (§3) runs through it, under that epoch or one derived from it, while the thing was dependent at the node.
  - Such a write concurrent with its writer's removal from the thing is read only by holders of the mint admin's secrets, as the writer may no longer rotate (one stamped after the removal is invalid anyway).
- **Newest**: a role's newest epoch is the current one with the greatest order n (its n, a derived epoch's per §3), ties by lowest nonce; "newer" compares alike.
- **Uses**: a node uses an epoch (encrypts under it, boxes its secret, puts it in a history box) only when it is established.

### Departures and covering
- **Holders for keys**: the holders of A by the role timeline (spec/permissions.md §5.1) with every applied edge write in effect and `until` disregarded, a removal's value saying retain (`until` 0, spec/permissions.md §2) counting as no edge; the record edge counts while it stands. A destroyed thing (spec/commits.md §5) is none: its own holders for keys stand in its place, each at the lesser of its role there and the destroyed thing's on A, and so on through destroyed things among them, a holder reached more than one way at the greatest such role, A itself never (Holder actors, Destroyed). Keys read no clock: an edge past its `until` stays a holder until its finalize (spec/permissions.md §2). "Current holder" in this section and in spec/permissions.md §3 means a holder for keys.
- **Departure** from role r of thing A: holder M holds less than r for keys, and its edge writes hold it at less than r (by the newest that applies, else its start), while its start or one of them that applies or counts gave it r or more (an **edge departure**); or M holds a box (Boxes) at a current epoch of r; or M is the signer of the held rotation commit of a current epoch of r (its **maker**, which holds its secret whatever the boxes say); in each case M no longer holding r on A (Holds, below; for a maker by any path with key access, through holder actors included). It **concerns** the roles of A it left: an edge departure each role above M's for keys up to the most its start or edge writes gave it, a box or maker departure r alone. A departure from a holder actor of A, or below one, is none of A's (Holder actors, below), but for a maker.
- **Holds**: the signer of a commit holds r on A at a node when its effective role on A (spec/permissions.md §1) over the node's applied edge writes, every `until` disregarded and a removal's retain value counting as no edge (Holders for keys), and through holder actors only by actor grants with key access (Holder actors, Key access: a keyless grant reads nothing through it), is at least r.
- **Covers**: an epoch e of role r of A **covers** a departure of M that concerns r when it is an edge departure and some live entry of e's history box, its names met and bound (Names) to a held valid keyset of e whose body's signer holds r on A, **pins** M below r. With M's **tail** its edge writes after the newest one that applies and gives it r or more (all of them when none does), the entry pins M below r when both hold:
  - when some edge write of M applies and gives it r or more, or none does and its start gives it r or more: some tail write giving M less than r takes there (Edge writes) and is named by the entry, or is the write of the commit the entry's departs mark names (by `ts`, signer and `blind`), its signer holding r on A;
  - every tail write giving M r or more that counts is named void by the entry.
  - Boxes decide nothing.
  - A rotator holding the role may name a departure it holds while boxing the departed party.

### Encrypting under
- **Encrypting under** an epoch means:
  - wrapping under a wrap key derived from its secret (§5);
  - sealing or wrapping a box under its secret or a KEM key derived from it. A box carrying a secret is encrypted under its recipient's key (a holder actor's keyset, Holder actors), not under the epoch it carries;
  - signing a stamp, step or delete under a sync ID role key derived from it.
  - Signing with a delete key (§7), opening a sync ID and reading are not encrypting under.
- A node MUST NOT **encrypt under** an epoch e of role r of thing A that is **blocked** at it; one that may rotate r (spec/permissions.md §3) rotates first, another waits for a rotation. e is blocked at a node holding its secret when:
  - e does not cover a departure from A the node knows of, other than an own thing's (spec/sync-set.md §2);
  - e has a box to a holder actor M naming an epoch of M blocked at the node (the box's sealing was encrypting under it), whatever the node's role on A or M; a joint box only when every epoch it names is, or one is by a box (below);
  - e has a stale box (Holder actors, below), whatever the node's role on A;
  - the node may rotate r, and e has a box to a holder past its key access to A (a box of a role it has no key access at, Holder actors).
  - An epoch e of a holder actor (Holder actors) whose secret the node lacks is blocked at it when e has a stale box (Holder actors, below; whatever the node's role on that holder actor), or when the earliest held valid `keys.0` body writing e as a keyset (Held keyset) is ordered (spec/permissions.md §5.1) before the newest edge write an epoch covering a departure from that holder actor the node knows of, other than its own, must name (the earliest tail write giving its holder less than the role, when one is needed, and every tail write giving the role or more that counts; Covers): e's rotator held nothing ordered after it, so e cannot cover it.
  - e is blocked **by a box** where it is blocked by the clauses on stale and past-key-access boxes, or by the box clause through a box to M at an epoch of M so blocked; **by a departure** otherwise.
  - Logging a commit as a certificate, or the node's own role commit, under A's sync IDs (spec/sync-judgement.md §3) is held back by the box clause only through things whose r the node may rotate, and by a stale box only at an epoch of such a thing: a node that may not still logs the keysets and revokes that tell their rotators.
  - An epoch whose secret the node lacks is judged by order and its stale boxes alone: a keyset of holder actor M ordered after a departure from M but made without knowing it (concurrent with it), or whose earliest body the node holds is a later re-write of it so ordered (a compaction snapshot), is sealed to, and the party removed from M opens what the node so seals until M's knowing keyset reaches it (Holder actors).
  - Exception: the wrap of a rotation commit itself (§5).
  - Exception: a departure whose newest edge write is the node's own self-removal (spec/permissions.md §2; made as an own thing or a thing its own things administer, whichever node made it) of a holder other than an own thing holds back none of the node's encrypting, here or under spec/permissions.md §4, until that commit is held elsewhere (spec/sync-status.md §2). Both are judged from what the node stores.
- A node that may rotate r also rotates r before it next encrypts under A while any current epoch of r, not only the newest, does not cover a departure it knows of (a fork made without knowing a departure stays current otherwise, and may be the newest; one whose secret the node lacks is judged by order alone: it does not cover a departure when its earliest held valid `keys.0` body writing it as a keyset is ordered before the newest edge write an epoch covering it must name, as for a holder actor's epoch above; one ordered after is judged by the nodes reading it), or has a stale box (Holder actors) or a box to a holder past its key access to A (the **knowing re-key**), or is **orphaned** (the node lacks its secret and no box of it the node holds is to a holder that may rotate r: no re-sealer holds it; its holders add it to the next epoch's history), or is a derived epoch the node wrote under (Established and current).
  - A rotation due only for current epochs whose secret the node lacks (ordered before a departure, or orphaned) is made only when it boxes, at r, a holder that may rotate r.
- **Forks** stand: no rule rotates r for having more than one current epoch. Holders are re-sealed at every current epoch, and the next rotation of r lists every current epoch, superseding them all; a holder actor anchored at forks of its keysets is sealed jointly (Holder actors, Seal).
- At the end of a complete pass of A's sync IDs (spec/sync-status.md §1, as re-seals are), and for the holder actors below A with it, a node that may rotate r also rotates r while an unsuperseded epoch of r is not current at it and the node **withdraws** it: it made the epoch, or the epoch's maker is unknown or may not rotate r; and a node that may rotate r of a holder actor M of A rotates it while A leaves M out of r at a current keyset of M (Holder actors, Left out, Re-key). This waits for the pass's end alone: no epoch it supersedes holds back the node's encrypting.
  - A node making a rotation of a holder actor M that this section requires makes, in the same step, the rotations then due at it on each thing M holds a role on that it may rotate, and upward from those it rotates. A removal's or finalize's own rotation (spec/permissions.md §3) is not followed so; its signer answers above when it next rotates what is due.
- **Established only**: a node encrypts under established epochs only, except under the epochs a rotation commit makes in that commit itself. The secret of any other epoch (one whose rotation commit it has not applied, or is pending, unjudgeable or invalid, spec/commits.md §4) is kept for reading.
- **Reading-only secrets**: a box opens with the node's own key and needs no record: it proves itself by what it opens. A secret learned only from a pending, unjudgeable (spec/commits.md §4) or invalid commit (from its boxes, or from history boxes opened with such a secret), or from a box while the node's roles do not give it (spec/permissions.md §3 No secret taken back), is kept for reading and never encrypted under until an applied valid box or a history box opened with a secret not so learned carries it. Such a commit's epochs count for nothing else: a box it holds that the node cannot open records nothing; what its history boxes list supersedes nothing. Sourced from a commit not applied in full, it goes when that commit's last part applies, it is judged bad, or its last entry leaves.

### Holder actors
A **holder actor** of A is a holder for keys of A (its mint admin included while the record edge stands). How a node boxes it depends on its keysets, on actor grants, and on which of its epochs are blocked at the node (Encrypting under); a departure from it or below it is none of A's, but for the demotions of writers and admins its keysets carry (Carried demotions).
- **Held keyset**: a keyset of role r of thing M is held at a node when the node holds a `keys.0` body of M writing it as r's keyset (a role commit's delta or a compaction snapshot; a dropped one counts) and the edge writes of every commit it carries (Carried demotions); until then it counts as not arrived. It is valid when some such body is valid (spec/permissions.md §5.2), held invalid when each such body is invalid.
- **Descends**: a held valid keyset k descends from epoch j when k's supersedes list names j, or names a held valid keyset that descends from j. A held invalid keyset passes nothing on.
- **Key access** (no key cycles):
  - An **actor grant** on A is a holder actor H's standing on A: its grant; a mint admin's record edge is a standing since A's creation, ordered before every write.
  - Actor grants are ordered by the key (spec/permissions.md §5.1) of the write that started H's present standing on A: the earliest applied edge write since which H has held a role on A for keys without a break. One standing since A's creation (a record edge) is ordered before every write. Ties go by A's tid, then H's.
  - An actor edge whose removal the node holds applied is no standing: key access is judged over the actor grants held less those, so a removal can give a later grant key access (re-sealed, spec/permissions.md §3).
  - A node takes the actor grants it holds in that order and gives each **key access** unless A's key already yields H's over the grants given key access before it (the grant would close a cycle): X's key **yields** Y's when X holds such a grant on Y, or on a thing whose key yields Y's. Such a grant is **keyless**: the earlier grant wins, the later reads nothing.
  - A holder H of A for keys has **key access** to A when some path of grants from H to A, through destroyed things only (Destroyed), has key access at every grant, at the greatest role over such paths, the lesser along each (Holders for keys): a frozen holder reached only through a keyless grant has none.
  - A rotation, grant or re-seal of A boxes no holder without key access to A, and none above the role it has key access at (a grant's holder: with the grant itself held, so a raise boxes the raised roles). A keyless grant is a role all the same (spec/permissions.md §5).
- **Tainted**: a keyset k of role r of thing M is tainted at a node when M's boxes include one at epoch k to a holder past its key access to M (a box to a departed holder, or above a holder's role, is a departure from M, not a taint).
- **Anchors** of holder actor M for role r of A: the held valid keysets of M that the boxes to M of role r at A's current epochs name (a sealed box's recipient keyset epoch, a symmetric box's epoch of s, each keyset a joint box names).
- **Left out**: a rotation of role r of A records in its keyset of r (spec/wire.md §7), per holder actor M holding r on A for keys that it boxes nothing of r, the keysets of M of role r it leaves M out at, each with its **causes**: each current keyset of M of role r blocked at the rotator (Encrypting under; judged at r whether or not a lower role left M out), and those A's current keysets of r record for M, less each that another of them descends from; a keyset recorded more than once takes the union of its causes. A rotation boxing M at r records nothing of M.
  - The causes of a keyset k are the commits the rotator's judgement of k read that a later commit can void or undo: for a departure k does not cover (from M, or from a holder actor whose epoch the box walk from k reached), its newest edge write the covering epoch must name (Covers); for a stale box (of k, or of an epoch so reached), the rotation commit of the held valid keyset whose list names the box's keyset; for a taint or a box past key access, the edge write of the grant without key access; for a record (below), its causes. A keyset blocked only by clauses with no such cause (a departure no edge shows, a box naming a keyset held invalid) is not recorded.
  - A cause **stands** at a node, by what the commit is there: one writing edges while one of its edge writes is a departure's that its holder has there, not the node's own (Encrypting under), or a grant giving no key access there; any other while its `keys.0` part is valid (a rotation commit).
  - M is **left out at**, for r, each held valid keyset of M that a valid keyset of a current epoch of r of A records for M with a cause that stands at the node, or one the node does not hold (a record is never read void for want of a cause). Such a keyset is blocked at the node by a box (Encrypting under), so Seal leaves M out at it.
  - A writer of A may record any keyset of M with any cause it holds: M is left out at it wherever a cause stands, until M's rotation (below), or M leaves A and A rotates without it.
  - **Re-key**: at the end of a complete pass of A's sync IDs (Encrypting under), a node that may rotate r of M rotates it while M, holding r or more on A for keys with key access, is left out of r of A at a current keyset of M.
- **Seal**: a rotation, grant or re-seal of A boxes holder actor M with key access, for role r, at keysets of M chosen among its **candidates**: the held valid keysets of role r of M that are not tainted.
  - At the newest (greatest n, ties by lowest nonce) candidate that is or descends from each anchor: symmetric when the node holds that epoch's secret, else sealed to its KEM key.
  - With none (the anchors fork), **jointly**: per anchor no other anchor descends from, the newest candidate that is or descends from it, less each another of them descends from; one joint box (spec/wire.md §7) opens only for a holder of every keyset it names.
  - With no candidate for some anchor (none at all, without anchors), M is left out of r and the roles above, and re-sealed (spec/permissions.md §3) once one exists.
  - When every keyset Seal gives is blocked at the node (§4 Encrypting under), or one is blocked by a box, M is left out of r and the roles above, and re-sealed once it is not.
- **Reported**: a node that left M out learns M's next keyset as M's readers log it under A's sync IDs, or, where they cannot (left out, they hold no current epoch of A to wrap it under), from their stranded reports (spec/permissions.md §4), which carry M's current `keys.0` commits; so a node that no longer follows M (gone from it, or never in it) still re-seals M.
- **Stale**: a box of A to holder actor M naming epoch k of M (a joint box: any keyset it names) is stale at a node when k is held invalid, tainted, or named by the supersedes list of a held valid keyset of M. A keyset the node holds no body of and finds in no such list is not stale.
  - **Box records**: whoever writes a box of A to a holder actor M (a rotation, grant or re-seal) records it in A's `keys.0` in the same commit, keyed as the box, naming the keysets of M the box names (`sealed`, spec/wire.md §7); a rotation removing a box removes its record. A record of epoch e of A that a held valid `keys.0` body of A puts (a delta's put or a snapshot's entry), naming such a keyset, is a stale box of e as well, whatever later bodies write at its key.
  - A node judges by the records it holds; a grant's or re-seal's record whose commit has not reached it judges nothing there.
- **Carried**: a rotation, grant or re-seal of A logs with its commit, under A's sync IDs, the held rotation commits of the keysets its boxes to holder actors name, and of those its keysets record holder actors left out at, as certificate copies (spec/permissions.md §4), with the demotions those keysets carry (Carried demotions) and the commits holding the box records of those keysets' epochs (Stale), each projected to `keys.0`, and the causes its keysets record (Left out), each with its parts on the thing it was judged on.
  - A node may hold A's box naming M's keyset k, or A's keyset recording M left out at k, without k's body (the box's commit arrived, the carried one not yet). k is then no anchor and M is not left out at it, and its next rotation, grant or re-seal of A seals M at the older keyset it holds, which a party removed from M may open; a node holding k finds that box stale and rotates (as below: that node held no knowing keyset of M).
- A keyset of M written without knowledge of a removal from M (a concurrent rotation of M, or one the removed party forged, where the removal or the forgery has not arrived) is sealed to as any other: the removed party opens A's epochs so sealed, written since the removal, until a keyset of M made knowing the removal reaches A's writers (with it the demotion, Carried demotions, which makes the removed party's later keysets of M and rotations of A invalid there; a reader's removal, which travels nowhere, by staleness alone).
- A departure from a holder actor M' of M reaches the node's sealing of M, and its encrypting under an epoch of A boxing M, only by stale boxes (Encrypting under): until a keyset of M' superseding the one M's box names reaches the node, M is sealed at that keyset, and A's epoch boxing it encrypted under, as any other, and the party removed from M' opens A's epochs so sealed.
- A node that has not seen the earlier grant of a cycle gives the later one key access and may seal into the cycle; a party removed from a thing on it may open the other things' epochs so sealed until a writer holding every grant of the cycle and its keysets has rotated them.
- **Destroyed**: a destroyed thing t (spec/commits.md §5) holds no keys: its holders for keys, its edges as they stood at its destruction (nobody writes t after it), are A's holders for keys in its place (Holders for keys). So a rotation, grant or re-seal of A boxes nothing to t and boxes t's holders as any holder (holder actors among them by Key access and Seal, at their current keysets, and left out as Left out says), and A's boxes to t are boxes to a holder that no longer holds their role (Departure: nothing covers it): A rotates. A party removed from t before its destruction is no holder of t, so none of A's.
  - An edge on t past its `until` but not finalized at the destruction stays a holder for keys of what t holds a role on: nobody writes t to finalize it.
- **Carried demotions**: a keyset of M lists the **demotions it carries** (spec/wire.md §7): the lowerings applied as explicit writes (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 3) its rotator holds applied that took a party below write or below admin on M, that party not holding that role on M again in the rotator's timeline. The walk records a demotion on everything the lowered edge's target reaches, so these include demotions on things below M, at any depth. Other commits are listed by commit ID (`demotions`); the rotation's own commit by `demotes`. A reader's removal is none: it stays below M.
  - The list is bounded by M's history, not by its rotations: at most every explicit demotion of a writer or admin of M or below it that does not hold that role again (a party demoted, re-granted and demoted again carries both demotions; a re-grant drops them all).
  - A node holds the keyset only with the edge writes of every commit it carries (Held keyset): the listed commits, and its rotation commit where `demotes`: the commit its write names (spec/crdt.md §2.1; a compaction snapshot's copy by its stamps, so the keyset is held whether or not that commit's `keys.0` part is).
  - Whoever places a keyset under a sync ID (Carried, Reported, spec/permissions.md §4) places with it the `roles.0` deltas of the commits it carries, with their evidence.
  - So a node holding a keyset of M made knowing a demotion of a writer or admin of M or below it holds that demotion: the demoted party's later commits on M and on what M holds a role on are invalid there (spec/permissions.md §5.2), its keysets of M held invalid and its rotations of those things establishing nothing.

## 5. Blobs, wraps, sealed fires
- **Blob encryption**: a fresh random 32-byte key per encryption; nothing encrypts a blob under a caller-chosen key (a copy's section is sealed under the fresh key before the blob is). Framing, padding, AD and blob ID: spec/wire.md §5; spec/sync-ids.md §1.
- **Wrap** (per sync ID, per blob): carries the blob's suite byte, blob key and order under the wrap key `kdf("wrap", read secret)` of the sync ID's thing; a blob carrying a role part (§4, Roles secrets) of the sync ID's thing's independent (§3), under `kdf("wrap", roles secret)` of that epoch. Deterministic. Bytes: spec/wire.md §5.
- **Path entry** (per thing, in a split commit's header, spec/wire.md §4 `Split`): `commitment ‖ sealed`. `sealed` is the thing's path under its **path key** `kdf("path", roles secret, blind ‖ tid)`, the read roles secret (§4) of the thing's independent (§3; the thing itself where independent) at the epoch the writer seals the thing's sections under (a writer holding no current read epoch of the independent: the newest roles secret of it it holds, else a fresh random 32-byte key; a reader tries every roles secret it holds), `blind` the header's, nonce 24 zero bytes. `commitment` = `kdf("path commit", path key, "")`, 32 bytes. A key is an entry's path key only if the entry commits to it: a reader takes no other, whatever carried it (an encryption of the thing's parts, a copy, a header-only copy, a roles secret). An entry whose committed key does not open it is bad (spec/wire.md §4). Every encryption of the thing's parts carries the key (spec/wire.md §4 `Contents`).
- **Section** (per thing, inside a commit blob): its parts under a fresh random section key used once; the key under `kdf("section", read secret of the thing)` (own or derived, §3; the record chain it derives through is in the clear beside it) with a fresh random nonce, AD = the commit ID. A reader takes the section key from the first held secret whose wrap gives a key that opens the section (a wrap may open under two secrets to two keys). Its role parts (§4, Roles secrets), if any, go in a section of their own, the key under `kdf("section", roles secret)` of the same epoch; nothing else goes under a roles secret. Bytes: spec/wire.md §4. The epoch is chosen as for a wrap (below, the exception for a commit giving read epochs included, judged by all the thing's parts in the commit); a copy's one section (spec/sync-judgement.md §1) goes under `kdf("section", blob key)`, so the blob's wraps gate it.
- **Passing on**: a node encrypts a content part (not role-class, `created.0`, `retention^.0` or addenda), while it holds content of it (not dropped, its writes not gone), under an epoch it chooses (a section of a fresh encryption of the commit, spec/sync-judgement.md §1, §4; the wrap of a blob carrying the part, a copy's included; a snapshot of its own carrying the write, spec/sync-compaction.md §1) only when both hold:
  - **floors** (a part written below a holder's floor is never passed on under an epoch that holder reads): the chosen epoch's floor (§4 Floors) is 0, or at most the floor of an epoch of the part's thing under which it opened a section carrying the part (its own part: the epoch it wrote it under, its newest current read epoch of the thing then). A snapshot, or a repair (spec/sync-ids.md §1), carries state, not the part: this clause binds none (§4 State at a cut);
  - **departures**: the part is its own, or some read epoch of the part's thing under which it opened a section carrying the part's genuine delta (its hash the header's, else the bytes of the delta it holds) is established at it; another's part it opened under no epoch never passes. A copy's section (one under its blob key) counts as opened under the read epoch, of the sync ID's thing, that the entry's wrap opened under; a sealed fire's delta as opened under the epoch of the keyset it is sealed to. For a blob's wrap under an epoch of a sync ID's thing T, a read epoch of T of floor at least the chosen epoch's counts here as one of the part's thing.
  - A blob's wrap also gates its header (thing IDs, aspect keys, delta hashes): a node wraps a blob of a commit under an epoch it chooses only while each content part of the commit passes the floors clause there (a part on a thing whose read secrets are those of the wrap's sync ID's thing), and, for each content part of a commit another wrote that it holds content of, the departures clause; and, once it unwrapped an entry of a blob of a commit another wrote, while some read epoch it unwrapped one under is established at it.
  - A fresh encryption (a fresh blob key, salt and blob ID) carries verbatim, for the parts it may not so encrypt, a held section of the commit (from a held blob, opened or not) none of whose parts it holds stripped or dropped; a part neither sealed nor so carried is left out, and a fresh encryption whose body needs it is not made.
  - A blob carrying a part it may not so encrypt is not wrapped by the node; a snapshot carrying such a write is not written while it may not.
- **Order**: 0 for a commit with a purge request or a part on a role-class aspect, `created.0` or `retention^.0`, else the commit's `ts` (a header-only blob too). It is read only to order a node's first sync (spec/sync-streams.md §5), and a wrong one changes nothing else.
- A wrap names no epoch: a reader tries the read secrets it holds, newest first, each followed by its epoch's roles secret, and the roles secrets it holds alone.
- **Who wraps under what**:
  - A node logging an entry of its own commit wraps under the newest current read epoch whose secret it holds (§4); Passing on (above) otherwise.
  - A node holding no current secret logs nothing: it is stranded (spec/permissions.md §4).
  - Exception: a commit **giving** read epochs of a thing (its `secrets.0` boxes them, or its `keys.0` writes a read keyset: a rotation, a grant, a re-seal) is wrapped, under every sync ID whose read secret derives from that thing's, under an epoch they supersede, never one they are. The epoch is the one with the greatest n, ties by lowest nonce (n, not order n, §4), among those whose roles secret the wrapping node holds (whose secret, where the commit carries a part other than a role part), and only once every epoch it gives is established at that node (§4 Established and current). Epochs that are the thing's first (n 0) supersede nothing: such a commit takes the plain rule.
    - A node holding none of them logs no such commit; any holder of one that holds the commit logs it (Passing on), and the recipients take it by inbox (spec/permissions.md §3).
- A sync ID's role keys at a peer are those of the epochs the peer's head registers (spec/sync-ids.md §3).
- **Sealed fire** (spec/sync-inbox.md §2): a commit (header and deltas) with its evidence, sealed to the read-role KEM key of the fired thing's newest read keyset the sender holds, in place of a blob key and sections. Its prefix is the commit's (spec/sync-ids.md §1); it has no blob key and its entry's wrap is filler. Bytes: spec/wire.md §9.

## 6. Hashes and links
- Keyed content hashes, under the dedup secret:
  - delta hash `kdf("delta", dedup secret, delta bytes)`;
  - link content hash of a chunk, page or tree node: spec/wire.md §6 (a header list's under its list key, spec/wire.md §4).
- **Link**: (link content hash 32, blob ID 32, suite byte || blob key 33, prefix salt 16, delete keys): the delete keys are those of the blob's one commitment group (§7), by level ascending, as its writer claims them: a holder of the blob checks them against its prefix before using them (spec/sync-ids.md §1).

## 7. Delete keys
- **Levels** of a delta, by its purge-by (spec/commits.md §5), one per member of its set:

  | level | purge-by set members | key |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1, admin | `admin`, `author_or_admin`, `writer` | the delete key of a step of its thing's delete base's sync ID (spec/sync-ids.md §2) |
  | 2, author | `author_or_admin`, `writer` | the delete key of the write secret of the commit's one purge author (spec/permissions.md §6) at its delete base |
  | 3, writer | `writer` | the write key of a step of its thing's delete base's sync ID |

- **Delete base**: a dependent thing (§3) uses those of its independent, the last thing of its derivation chain (§3): its delete base.
- **Delete key** of a role secret s: the Ed25519 key (secret scalar a, public key A = a·B, RFC 8032) with seed `kdf("sync delete", s, "")` for an admin secret and `kdf("sync write", s, "")` for a write secret: a step's delete and write keys (spec/sync-ids.md §2) are its epochs' delete keys.
- **Header list**: a header lists, per level of each delta, for the level's base (the delete base of the delta's thing at levels 1 and 3, of its purge author at level 2):
  - at levels 1 and 3, the key of that level of the newest step of the base's sync ID that the writer's `sync.0` records (its head, spec/sync-ids.md §3); a writer recording none cannot write;
  - at level 2, the delete key of the newest current write epoch of the base it holds;
  - A linked header list's chunks carry one empty group (spec/wire.md §4, §5): no delete key covers them; they go by the delete key or at their expiry (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Delete).
- Every delta lists exactly the levels its purge-by calls for (spec/wire.md §4).
- **Checked keys**: a reader checks each level-1 and level-3 key a header lists for a delta on a thing T against the steps of the things on T's record chain, T first: it is right iff, for one of them, it is that level's key of a step on a path from the genesis step of a sync ID that thing's `sync.0` records, each step verifying under its parent's delete key (spec/sync-ids.md §3), every step of it recorded there by a valid write stamped before the commit's `ts`. A delta on T of a commit listing for T a key that is not right applies nowhere, its edge writes there included, and is unjudgeable (spec/commits.md §4 Unjudgeable) until a held step places the key: a reader cannot tell a wrong key from a step whose `sync.0` write it lacks. The commit's deltas on other things are judged by their own keys. The steps that place a key ride with the commit as its evidence (spec/permissions.md §4 Evidence of a commit). Level 2 is not checked.
  - **First steps**: a commit before which no step on T's record chain is recorded, carrying T's `created.0` and signed by T's record's key as T (the commit minting T with a key, spec/permissions.md §3), lists for T the keys of the genesis step its own `sync.0` delta records: its keys for T are not checked. Only the record's key signs it, and nothing ordered before T's first step can have lowered it, so a key so listed is one T's admin from the start chose; any other commit, a later one carrying `created.0` again among them, is checked as above. A step's own `sync.0` write counts for no other commit: whether it is valid turns on the walk, which this check comes before.
  - Any thing up the chain, not T's delete base alone: a write of T's derived era lists its former base's step, as does a writer that has not received the commit giving T secrets of its own, and no header says which its writer knew. Every sync ID a `sync.0` records counts, owned or not (spec/sync-ids.md §4 Owner turns on the secrets a node holds; a verdict does not).
  - Every link's delete keys are checked against its delta's (spec/data-model.md §6): levels 1 and 3 equal the delta's; level 2 present iff the delta lists it, equal to the delta's unless the delta lists level 1 or 3 too (a reused chunk, spec/sync-ids.md §1 Chunk reuse). A page link failing it makes its delta bad by itself (spec/commits.md §4); a chunk link failing it reads as absent (spec/data-model.md §6).
- **Commitment** in a blob prefix = level byte || A' = h·A, h = `kdf("delete blind", A, prefix salt || blind)` read as 64 XOF bytes, little-endian, mod ℓ (the order of B).
  - A is taken as its 32-byte encoding.
  - `blind` of a commit blob is the 16 random bytes of its header. A blob reached by a link (a chunk, tree node, page or link page, of a value or of a header list) has its own **link blind**, the first 16 bytes of `kdf("link blind", blob key)`, and its `blind` is its **thing blind**, the first 16 bytes of `kdf("thing blind", link blind, tid)`, tid that of the thing whose dedup secret keys its link (a header list's: the header's `signer`): whoever holds a link to the blob and knows its thing can compute its commitments, nobody else, and commitments computed for another thing are not the blob's.
  - A blob commits to the delete keys of the deltas it serves, in groups, one per distinct delete-key set (spec/wire.md §5).
- **Certificate blobs** (record blobs, spec/wire.md §3): one group of one level-3 commitment to the write key of the newest step of the sync ID's thing's sync ID the sealer's `sync.0` records; `blind` is the blob's link blind. A reader refuses one whose prefix has any other shape, an expiry or another class. A Delete of one at a store is by that level (or by the head's delete key).
- **Delete signature** for a blob: Ed25519 (RFC 8032 signing) over `"osis v1 delete" || digest` (§2, spec/wire.md §11.2) with secret scalar h·a mod ℓ, nonce prefix `kdf("delete nonce", seed, prefix salt || blind)` (seed: the delete key's RFC 8032 seed), and public key A'.
  - It verifies strictly under A' (§1).
  - A node keeping the sync ID unread checks it against the blob prefix's commitment at its level in a group, one per group for a Delete, and requires the sync ID signature the levels call for (spec/sync-ids.md §6).
- A holder tries every epoch it holds of the level's secret up the thing's record chain and signs with the one whose key the header lists, or, for a linked blob, the link names (spec/sync-compaction.md §1; level 2 up the record chain of the dropped body's purge author).

## 8. Agility
- Keys, KEM public keys and sealed boxes are enums (`signing_key`, `kem_key`, `sealed`) with one case today. Cases are never removed; a new primitive is a new case.
- AEAD and KDF choices ride on the suite byte of wraps and links and the suite ID of a secret epoch. Suite 1 is the only suite.
- An unknown case or suite parks (spec/versioning.md §2).
- Migrating a thing to a new suite is a new epoch under that suite; old content stays under its suite. A signing key migrates by being replaced (a new key granted, the old one removed).
