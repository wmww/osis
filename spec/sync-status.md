# Sync: status
> Summary: Area 7: sources and being caught up per sync ID, the complete pass, when a node's own commit is held elsewhere, and what `status`, `wait_synced` and history completeness say per sync ID.

**Principle:** status says only what is true now, per sync ID, of the sources connected now. Nothing is "synced" with nobody connected, and no word of a peer counts until its stream showed it or it reported applied what it was sent.

## 1. Sources and caught up
- The **sources** of S at a node: the peers with S open on a connection up now (spec/sync-opens.md §4). A peer without S open, granted or not, is no source.
- A source is **caught up** on S when, on the connection S is open on, both streams stand at their ends (spec/sync-streams.md §4): this node's last Pull there was answered with `more` false, no Pull is out, no gap is waiting, no reply is in hand, and S is at the stream (no join of S in pages); and the source's last Pull was answered with `more` false and it is followed. And S is not busy there: no Stream carrying an event of S is in hand awaiting bodies; every event of S this node logged after the follow cursor is pushed, and every ephemeral event of S in its memory part listed; every message carrying an event of S served to the source is reported applied (by an Applied, spec/sync-streams.md §4 Reports; a Pull reports nothing); no join of S by the source is left in pages; and S, if it joined the connection after the source's first Pull, was read for the source since (a reply merging the source's join of S, or a reply from the start). Another sync ID's traffic on the connection moves nothing of S's caught up. A source **lacks** an entry only for what it would pull (stamped for its head, or a deposit it was offered); a full or refusing source (spec/sync-streams.md §4 Full, Refused) is named, counts as lacking what it lacks, and is not waited on.
- S is **heard out** at a node when every source but those full or refusing is caught up, and at least one such source is. With no source S is **unconnected**, one state, never synced; with only full or refusing sources it is not heard out.
- Readers are counted by identity (the things proven, spec/sync-opens.md §2), never by connection.
- A **complete pass** of S, begun at some moment, ends once S is heard out by streams read to their end from Pulls sent after that moment, and each peer whose record (spec/sync-opens.md §3) holds S as a member, while it does, has had its stream of S read to its end at its first connection after that moment, or `PASS_WAIT_MS` (local policy, spec/wire.md §2) has passed since the pass began. The wait runs on the monotonic clock; a pass's start is also dated durably on the wall clock while the pass is under way, and a pass under way at a stop is begun again at the next start, having waited what the wall clock shows since that durable start, between 0 and `PASS_WAIT_MS` (a start ahead of the wall clock counts as now, and is dated so); its place is that start's. With no source and no peer to be waited for on S (spec/sync-set.md §3 To be waited for) it ends at once. Moments are an order, not a clock: the node counts role commits applied from elsewhere, passes begun and Pulls sent in the order they happen, so a pass begun before a role commit applied never counts as after it, nor one after as before, whatever the clock reads; a Pull is sent after a pass began when it went out after the pass began. At 0 a complete pass is S heard out (a decision may miss what only an away peer holds, healed by a later one); at infinity one peer away for good holds every decision waiting on S's passes.

## 2. Held elsewhere
- A commit a node made, or a self-removal by an own thing or a thing on its own things' paths (spec/permissions.md §2) that it holds, is **held elsewhere** when each sync ID it syncs of the things the commit touches holds the parts of it that sync ID's thing reads that it must hold (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) in an entry at a source, or covered by a snapshot there, by what the source holds (`known`, spec/sync-judgement.md §3): present there, not counting as lacking there (spec/sync-ids.md §6 Wraps), or reported by a source that reads S (never one keeping S unread, which applies past what it refuses or does not pull, nor one holding S's thing as a stub or whose member is ended for its departure (spec/sync-opens.md §6), each shown part of S alone: each counts by `known` alone) at a position at or past where this node's log first held the entry (for a member being ended, the entry first logged at or below its `until`). A peer counts for a self-removal only where a thing it proved holds a role on the thing left with that self-removal applied, by this node's reading.
- A node records each such report on a commit not yet recorded so, durably, kept while the entry is, whatever it judges the commit must hold now. Copies of it elsewhere (certificate copies, sealed fires) do not wait for it (spec/commits.md §4).
- **By a source's report**: each entry of the commit under that sync ID recorded so. Lost access (spec/permissions.md §3 Lost access) and a loss by an `until` (spec/permissions.md §2) count own commits held elsewhere only so.

## 3. Status and waits
- `status` per sync ID: unconnected, or its sources each with caught up, lacking (with counts), full, refusing, or stranded; while a complete pass of it waits, the recorded peers it waits for; and per node, idle when no request, fill or Hello is outstanding on any connection (a dialed session waiting for the peer's Hello is not idle).
- A node keeping S unread reports S like any sync ID (its sources, its head, full), and stranded while its head is past its keys (spec/sync-judgement.md §6 Stops).
- **Stranded** at a source: as spec/sync-ids.md §6 Walk defines; and on a sync ID it reads while S is heard out (§1) and it holds no current read epoch of S's thing that it knows of (spec/permissions.md §4: after a complete pass there). The node reports itself stranded there and sends its Stranded message (spec/permissions.md §4) again when its holder's current keys move.
- `wait_synced(S)` ends when S is heard out and each of this node's own commits under S is held elsewhere; a writer's wait ends on a source keeping S unread holding the writer's entries by `known` (§2). S **held by no other node** holds no wait: it is read, and no holder has a role on its thing but this node's own things and things only they hold roles on (the public, holding one, is every other node) (who has S open does not count, a sync ID just joined being open nowhere yet). A wait on a thing covers every sync ID carrying it, and runs to its timeout while one some other node may hold is unconnected, or while none is held; a wait on all covers every sync ID held. A wait on all also waits until each sealed fire this node sends is delivered (spec/sync-inbox.md §2: while its delivery is pending). It never ends while S is unconnected and some other node may hold it.
- **History complete** for a thing: a complete pass (§1) of every sync ID carrying it.
- `set_role` writes at once.
- A wait on a thing that never arrives, or was left and forgotten, runs to its timeout; status names the thing unknown meanwhile.
- `SETTLE_MS` and `PASS_WAIT_MS` (spec/wire.md §2) are the only waits on others' liveness, and only for decisions taken at the end of a complete pass or about role commits.
