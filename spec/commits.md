# Commits
> Summary: A commit is a signed header plus deltas per touched aspect: header fields, commit ID and signature, commit kinds (ephemeral commits included), dot forms, verification and atomic application, retention and purge, expiry, undo.

## 1. Header
Encoding: spec/wire.md §4. Fields:
- Format version (spec/versioning.md §1).
- Author node tid and the actor tid it acts for (checked: spec/permissions.md §5.2).
- Kind: content or role (§2); compaction-snapshot marker; import marker (content only, with the origins table, spec/permissions.md §7); roster-snapshot marker (role only).
- `ts` (spec/crdt.md §2.2) and the dot form, node, commit or session (§3).
- Aspect list: per touched `(tid, ak)`: counter range `[lo, hi)`, retention (§5), optional `expires` (§6) and the delta hashes of its deltas. A content commit has one delta per aspect; a role commit may have several (§2), all sharing the aspect's range.
- Chunk list: the chunk, tree-node and page blobs the deltas introduce, each with the indices of the delete keys of the delta that created it (spec/crypto.md §7).
- Optional purge request (§5), optional `undoes: commit ID` (§7).
- A 16-byte random blind and the delete keys with their levels (spec/crypto.md §7); per aspect, the indices of the keys its deltas call for.
- Demotions only: the frontier (spec/permissions.md §5), inline or as a link to a chunk tree when it exceeds `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` (spec/data-model.md §6).
- Optional ephemeral marker: an ephemeral commit or a relay-only one (§2).
- The aspect, chunk, purge, delete-key and origin lists are each inline, or a link to a chunk tree of the list's canonical bytes when it exceeds `CHUNK_THRESHOLD`; those blobs carry the commit's level-2 commitment (spec/wire.md §5) and belong to every projection of it (spec/sync.md §3).
- Signature (spec/crypto.md §2).

- **Commit ID** = BLAKE3 of the canonical header without the signature. The signature is over the commit ID (spec/crypto.md §2).
- **Delta** bytes: spec/crdt.md §3. **Delta hash** = `kdf("delta", dedup secret of tid, delta bytes)` (spec/crypto.md §6).
- A commit may touch things of any owners and reader sets.
- A commit has no size limit: a delta pages (spec/crdt.md §8; an ephemeral commit's deltas never page, each must fit a blob, below), the header's lists go by link, a projection splits by size (spec/sync.md §3), and only blobs are bounded (`MAX_BLOB`, spec/data-model.md §6). Paging and chunking keep every delta and page within a blob.

## 2. Kinds
- **Content**: deltas on aspects that are not role-class.
- **Role**: deltas only on role-class aspects (spec/data-model.md §9). A role commit changes at most one `roles.0` edge, except: a single-use link redemption (the grant plus L's self-removal, both on K, spec/permissions.md §9); an explicit removal of several holders' edges on one target, none of them the author's own edge or a self-removal (spec/permissions.md §2), with one frontier and one rotation (revoking a link joiner together with L, spec/permissions.md §9); a roster snapshot (spec/permissions.md §6). These shape rules bind the writer; a receiver does not check them and judges each edge write on its own (spec/permissions.md §5.1). A grant also carries the grantee's `secrets.0` boxes; a removal or demotion carries the rotation of the target's roles it requires (spec/permissions.md §3); other rotations, re-seals and `keys.0` and `label.0` writes change no edge; all may cover many things. Role commits MUST NOT carry `expires`; their retention is fixed (§5) and a purge request never names them.
- A role commit MAY carry several deltas for one `(tid, ak)` (a roster snapshot one per edge or ledger entry, plus one per aspect carrying the reset alone): they share the aspect's counter range, their commit-level writes share the stamp `(ts, source, lo)`, and their creations use distinct counters; the aspect's delta is their join.
- `created.0` is carried by the thing's first durable commit, of either kind; a compaction snapshot, import or ephemeral commit never carries it (it is kept forever, §5); a compaction snapshot or import carrying `required.0^` is bad input likewise. A durable commit's `created.0` part MUST be exactly one minting of the record: the delta of one write setting the thing's record (typed by the `creation` struct, spec/data-model.md §9), nothing else; otherwise that delta is bad input (§4). The first content commit with a durable delta on an ephemeral thing (spec/data-model.md §1) also carries `created.0` of each ephemeral thing on that thing's owner chain and the durable adds of its ephemeral placements (spec/data-model.md §8).
- **Compaction snapshot**: content or role kind with the compaction-snapshot marker; snapshot deltas (spec/crdt.md §8); no own writes, empty counter ranges (spec/sync.md §7). Written by a node that may write the aspect (admin for `keys.0` and `secrets.0`, spec/permissions.md §5.2); a receiver judges this as for any delta of its author, and a snapshot failing it gives nothing (spec/permissions.md §5.2). `roles.0` and `tenures.0` are never compaction-snapshotted: a roster snapshot resets and rewrites them under its own stamps (spec/permissions.md §6).
- **Ephemeral commit**: a commit-form content compaction snapshot, or a session-form content commit (its author's own writes under its session, §3), with the ephemeral marker, carrying writes of ephemeral (`~`) aspects (spec/data-model.md §2), `placed.0~` among them; **relay-only** with the relay marker (writes of signal (`*`) aspects). An ephemeral commit of any marker carries no `created.0` delta: beside each delta a part carries the creation records (spec/wire.md §3, §4) of the ephemeral things (at its author) the delta is on or its `placed.0~` writes place that have the delta's thing's reader actor (spec/sync.md §3; a record gives its thing's dedup secret, so none goes to another reader), and of their ephemeral owner chains; a record hashes to its tid, so it needs no signature, and a receiver keeps those of things it does not hold as it keeps the commit (in memory). A snapshot one's deltas are snapshot deltas carrying their author's writes under foreign stamps (sessions of its author, spec/crdt.md §2.1) and no other node's writes; its other writes (under another node's session, a node or a commit source) are not joined; a snapshot one's every delta's retention is `writer` with no bounds; no chunks, pages (each delta must fit a blob alone, spec/crdt.md §8), purge, undo or list by link; a relay-only commit carries no `expires`. Nodes and hosts hold ephemeral commits in memory only (spec/sync.md §4, §9). A receiver judges each part of a snapshot one as a compaction snapshot's (spec/permissions.md §5.2) and joins valid parts into the ephemeral state, never the durable one; atomic apply does not apply to snapshot ones (each blob's parts join on arrival). A session-form one is verified, judged and applied as a content commit (§4). An aspect's storage class is its AK's lifetime marker (spec/data-model.md §2), alike at every node: a part on an AK without `~` in an ephemeral commit that is not relay-only, without `*` in a relay-only one, or with `~` or `*` in a durable commit is bad: a writer MUST NOT write one (a durable write on an ephemeral thing is a durable commit persisting it, spec/data-model.md §1), and a receiver drops it alone, as a part not matching its header entry (§4).
- **Import**: content kind with the import marker; deltas of writes re-sourced from origin commits, all on one thing, no own writes, empty counter ranges (spec/permissions.md §7).
- A commit spanning several kinds is invalid.

## 3. Dot forms
- **Node form**: dots `(author node, counter)`, counters per (aspect, node), contiguous; after a node opens its store, only on things whose counters it has caught up (spec/crdt.md §2.1).
- **Commit form**: dots `(commit ID, index)`, unlinkable across commits. Any author may use it. A key actor whose key is used from more than one device (link key, recovery key) MUST use it, and every role commit (roster snapshots included) MUST use it.
- **Session form**: dots `(Session(author node, nonce), counter)` (spec/crdt.md §2.1), counters per (aspect, session), contiguous from 1; the header carries the 16-byte nonce. Only an ephemeral content commit (§2) uses it, and an ephemeral content commit MUST use it.

## 4. Verification and application
- A commit's signature MUST verify; receivers dedup by commit ID. Each **delta** is judged on its own, by its author's authority on that delta's thing (spec/permissions.md §5); an invalid delta is un-merged alone (spec/crdt.md §4.6), the commit's other deltas stand.
- A commit is **pending**, not applied and counted as not received, while: the author's `created.0` record is unknown; the `created.0` record of an import's thing is unknown; its `ts` is more than `MAX_DRIFT` ahead (spec/crdt.md §2.2); a header list or frontier given by link (spec/wire.md §4) is not resolved; atomic apply waits; a part waits and holds it (below); every part of it the node holds waits; or it holds no part of it and a thing of it waits or a delta of it is missing and not counted as held (synced or not; but a permanent delta (§5) of it or an item of its purge request on a thing the node syncs counts here as a held part, so its other things and deltas gate it as those of a commit with a held part do). A part past its expiry decides none of this: a commit that would wait without it waits, and the part goes (§5).
- A **thing waits** while its `created.0` record is unknown, a `created.0` record on its owner chain is unknown, or the author is not on that chain and no owner link, ownerless thing's first admin (its record, spec/permissions.md §1) or holder named by an applied part's edge write, roster or ledger tenure, whether the walk applies it or not, leads from the author to it. A held **part waits** while its thing waits, a page of it is not held (spec/crdt.md §8), it is a roster snapshot's `tenures.0` delta linking a frontier whose blobs are not held (spec/permissions.md §6) or it is parked (spec/versioning.md §2). The wait of a thing the node syncs, or of a part on one, holds the whole commit, whether a part on that thing is held or not, but for the wait of a part that atomic apply counts as held when missing (below), and that of a linked frontier: they hold nothing back. Any other part waits alone, as does every part of a role commit (not a snapshot) once the node holds any delta of it and every late part of an applied commit: the commit's other parts apply, its waiting parts count as not received and each joins when its wait ends. A **thing's content** is its parts on aspects neither role-class nor `created.0` (of a commit other than an ephemeral snapshot): once a part of it is held, the thing's content applies whole or not at all, synced or not: a waiting part or a delta missing, neither counted as held (below), holds the rest of it. A held `created.0` part never waits for the commit's other parts: it applies when its thing does not wait, and the commit still waits whole, or its parts alone, as if nothing of it had applied. A node **syncs** what it was asked to and what the `group.0` values it holds put in those groups' closures (spec/sync.md §3), judged as of each examination. Which parts apply is a function of the parts and other data held and of what the node was asked to sync, never of arrival order or of which parts came together, with one exception, which lasts while a part stays away: parts that applied before a thing of the commit entered what the node syncs stay applied, on things other than that one and, of a role-class snapshot, on that thing too.
- **Atomic apply**: a node applies a commit only once it holds every delta of it that belongs under a label it syncs (spec/sync.md §3: entitlement, not group membership, decides), with the pages of every paged delta (spec/crdt.md §8); dropped (spec/crdt.md §8), expired or purged (named by a held purge request whose author may purge it, §5) deltas count as held, as does a delta under a keep at most once the node has applied another commit with a delta on the aspect (the delta held or not), of any validity, stamped no earlier than its commit, and its bound by §5 for a body with no live write has passed on the node's corrected clock, the first death taken at the earliest such commit's `ts` (an absolute bound passing with no such commit counts for nothing: a body with a live write leaves only with a carrier, §5), as does a delta that breaks an entry governing it of a held `required.0^` delta on its thing ordered before its commit, whatever that delta's validity, signed by a possible admin of the thing (below; §5), as does a delta that a compaction snapshot of its aspect stamped after its commit lists as included, whatever the snapshot's validity, while the node holds its body or its kept frontiers and `Absorbed` (§5) (its frontier for the commit's author, or session, reaches a node-form (session-form) delta's last counter, or its `Absorbed` names the commit), and a delta when the node holds a role commit with a frontier, of any validity, by another actor, whose edge write on its thing or a thing up its owner chain removes the edge of its author or the header's actor or sets it below the role the delta needs, or a roster snapshot's ledger entry with a frontier of such a holder with that role or above that no entry or roster edge of that role or above in the snapshot starts at the end of, signed by a **possible admin** of that thing, and that frontier does not list its commit, whatever follows them (its author, cut from the label, may never place it; a thing's possible admins, by what the node holds of any validity and whatever followed: the least set holding the thing and, with each member, its owner or first admin and any holder named with admin on that member by an edge write, roster or ledger entry a member signed), and every delta of a role commit (not a snapshot) once the node holds any delta of it (it may hold only a projection: a certificate copy or an inbox blob, spec/sync.md §3): the commit applies without them and each joins if it arrives later; chunk blobs of large atomic values (spec/data-model.md §6) are not waited for. It then joins all those deltas in one storage transaction and fires listeners after. Pending commits are re-examined when a thing enters or leaves what the node syncs. A delta on a key of another storage class than its commit's (§2) counts as held. A delta of an already applied commit that arrives later (the node began syncing its label after applying, the delta counted as held, or its body counts as never received, §5) is joined on arrival, a live one past its keep at most included. That rule does not read whether the delta holds a live write, so it also applies a commit without a live delta still on its way. With no such commit the commit waits, also for a delta that left with no carrier when every commit on its aspect as late has left (expired, purged) or none was (its write's superseder is stamped before it).
- Role commits are applied before content.
- Two commits by one node (one session) whose node-form (session-form) counter ranges on one aspect overlap are both invalid.
- A role or roster-snapshot commit in node form is bad input (§3).
- **Judged alone**: a fault of the header or signature refuses the commit, in any copy. Each delta is judged by itself, from the signed header, its bytes and held data; its fault never refuses another delta:
  - Bytes that do not match the header's delta hash, or a part index naming no delta, are not that delta: dropped, and any copy matching the hash is taken as if they never came. A blob holding nothing else is bad input.
  - A part on an aspect of the wrong storage class (§2) is dropped; such a delta is never waited for.
  - A delta that matches and is bad by itself (it does not decode; a `created.0` delta that is not one minting of its record; a `roles.0` write inside an entry it did not create; a carried write stamped after its commit; a linked frontier that spec/permissions.md §6 calls bad input by itself) never applies. Only those bytes can match, so nothing marks it: it is judged again if it comes again. While the commit is pending it stays held and counts as held for atomic apply. A blob holding only such deltas, of a commit that is a role commit, is applied, or has none of them on a thing the node syncs, is bad input; otherwise the commit applies without them. Where such a delta's thing's content, or the commit, waits, the delta is judged when that wait ends.
- **Copies**: before a thing's `created.0` record is known the hash of a delta on it cannot be checked, and before its author's the signature. A pending commit keeps the first copy of each such delta and of the signature; a copy that proves not to match goes, and a node then judges again the copies of the commit it holds as blobs, so that no copy keeps out what the author signed; this brings back no delta counting as never received (§5). A copy it no longer holds comes back as a holder's new upload (spec/sync.md §6).
- A commit blob whose clear prefix disagrees with its header (expiry, the commitments to the delete keys its deltas list; spec/sync.md §1) is bad input.

## 5. Retention and purge
Every delta carries **retention** on the wire: who may purge it, how long its body is kept, and its expiry (§6). The writer fills it from the schema's defaults per AK (spec/data-model.md §2); a commit may set it per aspect; an untyped write (no schema for the AK) defaults to `author_or_admin` with no keep bounds and no expiry. Retention is immutable: to change it, purge and write again.
- **Purgeable by**: a set, not a scale: `nobody` (none), `admin` (the thing's admins), `author` (the delta's author), `author_or_admin` (both), `writer` (every writer, so both too). No exception widens it: a body whose set has no holder stays until a keep-at-most bound or expiry, if any. A member of the set may drop the delta's body: by purge request (spec/permissions.md §8), by compaction, and by deleting its blobs at hosts (spec/sync.md §5). Delete keys follow it (spec/crypto.md §7). A **right holder** is a node that holds the right now.
- **Keep at least** and **keep at most**: each optional; each an absolute time, a duration after the body is replaced, or both (the later bound counts for at least, the earlier for at most). A relative keep at most exists only under `writer`: whoever replaces a body must be able to delete it. Where the two bounds disagree, keep at most wins.
- A **body** is one blob of a commit (a projection, spec/sync.md §3, holds the deltas of one retention class). A write **dies** when a later write supersedes it or at its delta's expiry (spec/crdt.md §4.8), at the superseding commit's `ts` or at T. A body is **replaced** as its writes die; per body a node tracks the first and last death so far. A node dates a death at the `ts` of the superseding write its join met: the arriving superseder's when it kills a held write, the state's winner's when the write arrives already superseded; a superseder arriving later does not move it. After a validity flip or an un-merge on the aspect, or when a write of a text element arrives out of order or as a copy of one it holds (a flag no newer than a held undelete, a creation under a held undelete, a copy of a held creation, spec/crdt.md §5.7: the state does not show which held write killed what; a flag no newer than a held delete kills nothing new, and a creation first arriving under a held delete dies at that delete, the state's winner) or is a delete over a held undelete (the creation may have died already at a delete the undelete beat), it dates the aspect's deaths again from what it then holds (each at the first superseding write among its held bodies and value-less residues (spec/crdt.md §4.7) in `(ts, ID)` order, a valid snapshot's copy of a write whose own delta is held invalid counted at the snapshot, else at its state's). A snapshot body's writes are those it carries, each dying for it no earlier than the snapshot's `ts`: one its author held live but the node holds superseded by a write with a lower `ts` dies for it at the snapshot's `ts`. A date is never earlier than the death (spec/crdt.md §4.8); nodes holding the same commits may hold different dates.
- A body is **covered** by a valid snapshot the node holds (spec/permissions.md §5.2) when joining each of its deltas into the snapshot's fragment of that aspect leaves its canonical bytes unchanged (spec/crdt.md §3.3): every live write of the body is in the snapshot, and canonical form carries no dead content. A node-form (session-form) body is covered only if the snapshot's frontier for its author (session) also reaches the body's counters, a body judged by commit ID (commit form, import) only if the snapshot's `Absorbed` lists its commit, and a snapshot body only if the covering snapshot's frontiers reach its frontiers and its `Absorbed` lists every commit the body's does (spec/crdt.md §8). Every write the covering snapshot holds under a dot the body holds a write of (or, for a body that is not a snapshot, under one of its own dots: its counter range, its commit ID, an import's origins) MUST be the body's write at the same position with the same `ts` and value (a value-less copy matches); a valid snapshot body's copies all count (spec/permissions.md §5.2); an invalid one's copies of writes whose own delta the node received and judges valid are left out of the join. A body the node cannot join, a snapshot whose pages the node has not fetched, or a snapshot body it has not received (a late part, or one counting as never received again, spec/permissions.md §5.2) establishes nothing: the body is neither covered nor uncovered for it (spec/sync.md §6). A body holding no delta is covered, unless it is a delta with an expiry the node has not received (a lasting header is must-hold all the same, spec/sync.md §6). A snapshot written from a node's whole state of an aspect covers every body of it the node had applied, except those spec/crdt.md §8 never covers.
- **Keep at least**: before the bound (last death so far plus D, or T) a body leaves the labels of its things' groups only by purge request: compaction MUST NOT drop it, right holders MUST NOT delete it at hosts and a demotion's frontier does not leave it out as covered (spec/permissions.md §5.1). A body with no dead writes is past a relative bound.
- **Keep at most**: the bound is T, or first death plus D for D after replaced, plus `AT_MOST_GRACE` (spec/wire.md §2) while the body has a **live write**: any write of its deltas the node's state holds by dot, including element records the state keeps only as anchors (dead per spec/crdt.md §4.8, kept per spec/crdt.md §5.7), but not an element's or entry's tombstone (it holds the remover's reset, not the creation); with both, the earlier. Each node judges liveness on its own state, so bounds may differ between nodes until they hold the same commits; a right holder uploads the killing commit before any delete in the same pass. Deaths being dated per node, a body is dropped no earlier than its bound counted from its writes' deaths and, for D after replaced, no later than D (plus `AT_MOST_GRACE` while it has a live write) after its first death as the node dated it, the `ts` of a superseder the node held by then. A body dropped for its keep bounds (at its bound or as its writes died) or for carrying a purged value counts as never received from then on, as a missing delta counted as held does (§4): its live writes stay in the state, the node does not fetch it on its own, and a copy that arrives again joins as a late part and is judged again (dropped at once past its bound or purged; held when a write that killed it, or one of its own, has turned invalid or valid). Its holders upload it while it is must-hold (spec/sync.md §6), so nodes holding the same bodies read alike. A part that waits (§4) when its expiry passes goes from the node at T, and one arriving past T goes if it waits or its commit would wait without it; either counts as never received: its non-value writes join only through snapshots. Every local copy goes with it. A waiting part that a held purge request names whose author may purge it (judged once the node holds the request and the commit's header) is held value-less from then on, once the node can check it (its thing's record and pages held): the node keeps it with its value-carrying writes stripped (spec/crdt.md §4.7) and no other local copy of it; it waits and applies as that copy, then drops as an applied part the request names does, so nodes holding the same commits read alike. A snapshot or import part has no values of its own: it waits whole and drops once applied. For any dropped delta (here or below), a received blob holding it that may also hold the only true copy of a waiting part of the same commit (one whose slot a copy not yet checkable holds, §4 Copies) is kept until that copy is judged. Where such a drop may leave a node reading less than one that kept the data, it reports the aspect suspect (spec/permissions.md §4). Under keep at most the erased data may persist in a body with a live write up to its bound (or the next compaction, which usually comes first), and at hosts until a right holder next syncs after the drop. By the bound every node MUST drop the body; its live writes stay in the node's state (spec/crdt.md §4.6); right holders delete it at hosts when next online after the bound. A node that may write the aspect and holds no snapshot covering a body with live writes writes one before dropping the body (it is held and uploaded like any must-hold body). A body with live writes is deleted at a label only while another entry under it carries them (spec/sync.md §7). A body with no live write needs no carrier: under at most 0 after replaced the node applying the kill that leaves it none drops it in that transaction and deletes it at hosts in the same sync; under D after replaced, a right holder that sees it with no live write sends hosts a delete timed at the bound (spec/sync.md §5).
- Between the bounds a right holder may drop a covered body (compaction, spec/sync.md §7) when the covering snapshot's own commit holds the right over it, as a purge request would at that snapshot's key (spec/permissions.md §8): a snapshot without the right carries the body's live writes and covers it for must-hold (spec/sync.md §6) but drops nothing, at any node. `nobody` makes that window empty. Snapshot bodies follow the same rules. A compaction snapshot whose retention (on the wire) has a keep at least (an absolute bound, or a relative one above 0) and a keep at most (either component) is a **carrier only**: it is valid and its writes join, it carries the live writes of bodies dropped at their keep-at-most bounds (above, spec/sync.md §7), but no body is dropped as covered by it and it covers none for must-hold (spec/sync.md §6). Each node judges this from the snapshot's retention alone, so alike; a delta's retention is immutable, so a later schema or default change re-judges nothing. Compaction skips an aspect whose snapshot would be a carrier only; a node writes one only as the carrier of a body with live writes it must drop (keep at most, purge). Inside one body the cut stays: under such retention a body's keep at most counts from its first death, so a write of it dying later keeps less than its keep at least (keep at most wins).
- **Required retention**: `required.0^` (spec/data-model.md §9) maps AK prefixes to a purgeable-by, keep bounds and an optional expiry duration. A delta answers to it unless it is a compaction snapshot's or its aspect has fixed retention or is not durable. The setting **in force** at a key is what the newest valid `required.0^` delta on the thing ordered before that key says (spec/permissions.md §5.2); none: nothing is required. The entry governing an AK is the one with its longest prefix, the empty prefix matching every AK; no entry: nothing is required. A delta **breaks** an entry that governs it if it does not carry exactly the entry's purgeable-by and bounds and, with a duration, `expires` = `wall_ms(ts)` + that duration, without one no `expires`. A delta that breaks the setting in force at its key is invalid; its commit's other deltas are judged on their own. Each `required.0^` delta is read alone: its value is the whole setting, and a delta not reading as the aspect's type, with more than `MAX_REQUIRED_ENTRIES` entries or with a prefix longer than `MAX_AK_BYTES`, requires nothing. A writer fills the required retention and expiry and refuses others, writes `required.0^` whole (never by path, within those bounds, with no entry a write could not meet) and does not undo it. A change judges only later keys; it rewrites no delta's retention.
- **Purge request**: a header field naming earlier deltas (of commits with a smaller `ts`; a later one is not named) by commit, by a whole aspect history, or by author on an aspect (spec/wire.md §4). A later-arriving delta a held request names falls under it as one held does. Every node applying it drops, at any time, the named deltas its author may purge (spec/permissions.md §8): from then on it holds their value-carrying writes value-less (spec/crdt.md §4.7) and drops every body carrying their values, snapshots included, whatever the order the request, the named delta's commit and the snapshot arrive in: a node judges a named delta once it holds the request and that commit's header (which the right and the writes it names depend on). A writer that, after applying it, holds no covering snapshot of the aspect under a label it writes re-snapshots there (duplicates are harmless, spec/sync.md §7). A request SHOULD name dead writes; the writer resets live content in the same commit. A commit carrying a request has a delta (it is what places the commit under the thing's labels): where no reset is wanted, a write of `purge.0` (spec/data-model.md §9). It MUST NOT name role-class deltas. A commit carrying a purge request MUST NOT carry `expires`; its blobs are kept at least until `wall_ms(ts) + ROLE_KEEP`, whatever its deltas' keep bounds (at least and at most).
- Purging drops bodies, never headers: a node keeps the header of every durable commit it applied (an ephemeral commit goes whole, header included, once none of its deltas is held, spec/sync.md §9). A node dropping a body for a purge keeps the commit as a blob without the purged deltas: same header and commit ID, new blob, held and uploaded like any body. With none left it holds only the header; a **header-only blob** goes under a label only as a lasting header (below); its prefix has no expiry and no commitments.
- A delta is **permanent** when it is durable, its aspect has no fixed retention, its retention has keep at least `abs` forever and no keep at most, and it has no `expires`. Only a purge request removes its body. A commit is **lasting** on thing t when a permanent delta of it on t is dropped (other than for breaking a required retention, spec/permissions.md §8), or when its purge request has dropped a permanent delta on t at this node (spec/permissions.md §8: its author held the right, as judged when the node held both): a device coming later counts a purged delta as held only by a held request (§4). A lasting commit's header is never forgotten, and every label of a group with t in its closure, whose actor may read t, holds a blob of the commit for good (spec/sync.md §6): a body of it, else a header-only blob. A purge of any other delta leaves nothing at a label. The commit reads as a held commit without that delta's body: its author, actor, `ts` and retention stay, and whether to show it is the reader's choice.
- Dropping a body means destroying every local copy (undo buffers and pre-reset snapshots included), its delta nonce and every local index entry derived from it, so that later compromise of the node recovers nothing (notes/local-store.md describes the mechanism); what the part was judged (its validity, the role its writes needed, and for a `secrets.0` or `keys.0` delta the epochs it boxed or keyed, whose introducing commit spec/crypto.md §4 asks for, and the sealed names of its boxes to the holders its edge writes put, a grant's) stays. Exception: a compaction snapshot's part dropped other than as covered (for its keep bounds, expiry or a purge) keeps its frontiers and `Absorbed` (counters and IDs, no content; spec/crdt.md §8); a part dropped as covered keeps neither (its covers reach them).
- A dropped body cannot be undone (§7); under at most 0 others' resets beat undo.
- Fixed retention: role-class deltas are `admin`, keep at least `ROLE_KEEP` (90 days) after replaced (a roster snapshot replaces `roles.0` and `tenures.0` deltas, compaction snapshots or later writes the other role-class aspects, spec/permissions.md §6); `created.0` and `required.0^` are `nobody`, kept forever (`abs` forever); `purge.0` is `author_or_admin` with no keep bounds, its snapshots too; a certificate copy carries its commit's; snapshots of role-class aspects keep it (`created.0` and `required.0^` are never snapshotted, §2). Other compaction-snapshot deltas are `writer` (a receiver checks this; the bounds below are the writer's, from what it holds) with bounds from the **own deltas** of the writes the snapshot carries live (live as for keep at most): keep at least the longest relative bound among them, with no absolute one; keep at most the strictest per component, the shortest relative bound and the earliest absolute bound later than the snapshot's `wall_ms(ts)`, an absolute bound at or before it counting as 0 after replaced. A write's own deltas are its own commits' deltas on the aspect (spec/permissions.md §5.2; every applied import listing its origin); for writes whose own commit's header the node does not hold, every held snapshot body of the aspect carrying one of them stands in, and if some such write no held snapshot body carries, every compaction snapshot of the aspect the node applied. No schema enters the bounds; a snapshot names the schema of the last applied delta of the aspect in `(ts, ID)` order that names one, else none. Import deltas carry their origin delta's (spec/permissions.md §7).

## 6. Expiry
- `expires: T` (wall ms, `T > wall_ms(ts)`, else invalid), per aspect of the commit. The schema may give a default per AK as a duration from the write. The blob prefix carries the T of the deltas it holds (spec/sync.md §1, §3).
- At T every value-carrying write of the delta dies (spec/crdt.md §4.7): every node holds them value-less and drops the body, whatever the delta's keep-at-least bound. A delta never received stays never received: a copy arriving after T joins value-less, and what a held snapshot gave under its dots goes with the snapshot (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
- Hosts delete the blob at T unattended (spec/sync.md §5).

## 7. Undo
- An undo commit (a revert) sets `undoes` to the undone commit's ID; its deltas are chosen per spec/crdt.md §4.6 (undoing a creation deletes the thing, so they may touch aspects the undone commit did not). A commit whose body was dropped cannot be undone. Redo is an undo of the undo commit. Undoing ephemeral writes (spec/data-model.md §2) writes ephemeral writes chosen the same way, from the writing node's in-memory record of them; no commit names them.
