# Commits
> Summary: A commit is a signed header plus deltas per touched aspect: header fields, commit ID and signature, commit kinds (ephemeral commits included), dot forms, verification and atomic application, retention and purge, expiry, undo.

## 1. Header
Encoding: spec/wire.md §4. Fields:
- Format version (spec/versioning.md §1).
- Author node tid and the actor tid it acts for.
- Kind: content or membership (§2); compaction-snapshot marker; import marker (content only, with the origins table, spec/permissions.md §7); roster-snapshot marker (membership only).
- `ts` (spec/crdt.md §2.2) and the dot form, node or commit (§3).
- Schema table `[(schema ID, revision)]` (spec/versioning.md §3).
- Aspect list: per touched `(tid, ak)`: counter range `[lo, hi)`, retention (§5), optional `expires` (§6), schema index or none, and the delta hashes of its deltas. A content commit has one delta per aspect; a membership commit may have several (§2), all sharing the aspect's range.
- Chunk list: the chunk, tree-node and page blobs the deltas introduce, each with the indices of the delete keys of the delta that created it (spec/crypto.md §7).
- Optional purge request (§5), optional `undoes: commit ID` (§7).
- A 16-byte random blind and the delete keys with their levels (spec/crypto.md §7); per aspect, the indices of the keys its deltas call for.
- Demotions only: the frontier (spec/permissions.md §5), inline or as a link to a chunk tree when it exceeds `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` (spec/data-model.md §6).
- Optional ephemeral marker: an ephemeral commit, a relay-only one or a presence one (§2).
- The aspect, chunk, purge, delete-key and origin lists are each inline, or a link to a chunk tree of the list's canonical bytes when it exceeds `CHUNK_THRESHOLD`; those blobs carry the commit's level-2 commitment (spec/wire.md §5) and belong to every projection of it (spec/sync.md §3).
- Signature (spec/crypto.md §2).

- **Commit ID** = BLAKE3 of the canonical header without the signature. The signature is over the commit ID (spec/crypto.md §2).
- **Delta** bytes: spec/crdt.md §3. **Delta hash** = `kdf("delta", dedup secret of tid, delta bytes)` (spec/crypto.md §6).
- A commit may touch things of any owners and reader sets.
- A commit has no size limit: a delta pages (spec/crdt.md §8; an ephemeral commit's deltas never page, each must fit a blob, below), the header's lists go by link, a projection splits by size (spec/sync.md §3), and only blobs are bounded (`MAX_BLOB`, spec/data-model.md §6). Paging and chunking keep every delta and page within a blob.

## 2. Kinds
- **Content**: deltas on aspects that are not membership-class.
- **Membership**: deltas only on membership-class aspects (spec/data-model.md §9). A membership commit changes at most one `/members` edge, except: a single-use link redemption (the grant plus L's self-removal, both on K, spec/permissions.md §9); an explicit removal of several members' edges on one target, none of them the author's own edge or a self-removal (spec/permissions.md §2), with one frontier and one rotation (revoking a link joiner together with L, spec/permissions.md §9); a roster snapshot (spec/permissions.md §6). A grant also carries the grantee's `/secrets` boxes; a removal or demotion carries the rotation of the target's roles it requires (spec/permissions.md §3); other rotations, re-seals and `/keys` and `/label` writes change no edge; all may cover many things. Membership commits MUST NOT carry `expires`; their retention is fixed (§5) and a purge request never names them.
- A membership commit MAY carry several deltas for one `(tid, ak)` (a roster snapshot one per edge or ledger entry, plus one per aspect carrying the reset alone): they share the aspect's counter range, their commit-level writes share the stamp `(ts, source, lo)`, and their creations use distinct counters; the aspect's delta is their join.
- `/created` is carried by the thing's first commit, of either kind; a durable compaction snapshot or import never carries it (it is kept forever, §5). A durable commit's `/created` part MUST be exactly one minting of the record: the delta of one write setting the thing's record (typed by the `creation` struct, spec/data-model.md §9), nothing else; otherwise the commit is bad input. The first content commit with a durable delta on an ephemeral thing (spec/data-model.md §1) also carries `/created` of each ephemeral thing on that thing's owner chain and the durable adds of its ephemeral placements (spec/data-model.md §8).
- **Compaction snapshot**: content or membership kind with the compaction-snapshot marker; snapshot deltas (spec/crdt.md §8); no own writes, empty counter ranges (spec/sync.md §7). Written by a node that may write the aspect; a receiver judges this as for any delta of its author, and a snapshot failing it gives nothing (spec/permissions.md §5.2). `/members` and `/tenures` are never compaction-snapshotted: a roster snapshot resets and rewrites them under its own stamps (spec/permissions.md §6).
- **Ephemeral commit**: a commit-form content compaction snapshot with the ephemeral marker, carrying ephemeral state (spec/data-model.md §2: writes of ephemeral aspects, writes on ephemeral things, the ephemeral side of `/group`) and the `/created` of the ephemeral things it writes; **relay-only** with the relay marker (signal writes); **presence** with the presence marker (its author's own writes of presence aspects, spec/data-model.md §2, and the records they need). Its deltas are snapshot deltas carrying their author's writes under foreign stamps (the author's session sources, spec/crdt.md §2.1, each named in `Authors`) and no other node's writes but `/created` records (of the ephemeral things it writes or places and their ephemeral owner chains, spec/sync.md §9; a record hashes to its tid); its other writes (of sessions its `Authors` does not attribute to its author, under node sources) are not joined; every delta's retention is `writer` with no bounds, `/created` included (retention does not apply to ephemeral data); no chunks, pages (each delta must fit a blob alone, spec/crdt.md §8), purge, undo or list by link; a relay-only or presence commit carries no `expires`. Nodes and servers hold ephemeral commits in memory only (spec/sync.md §4, §9); a receiver judges each part as a compaction snapshot's (spec/permissions.md §5.2) and joins valid parts into the ephemeral state, never the durable one. Atomic apply does not apply to them (each blob's parts join on arrival).
- **Import**: content kind with the import marker; deltas of writes re-sourced from origin commits, all on one thing, no own writes, empty counter ranges (spec/permissions.md §7).
- A commit spanning several kinds is invalid.

## 3. Dot forms
- **Node form**: dots `(author node, counter)`, counters per (aspect, node), contiguous.
- **Commit form**: dots `(commit ID, index)`, unlinkable across commits. Any author may use it. A key actor whose key is used from more than one device (link key, recovery key) MUST use it, and every membership commit (roster snapshots included) MUST use it, so no later commit of its author can void it (§4).

## 4. Verification and application
- A commit's signature MUST verify; receivers dedup by commit ID. Each **delta** is judged on its own, by its author's authority on that delta's thing (spec/permissions.md §5); an invalid delta is un-merged alone (spec/crdt.md §4.6), the commit's other deltas stand.
- A commit is **pending**, not applied and counted as not received, while: its author's chain certificates have not arrived; the `/created` record of a thing whose delta it holds or that it syncs, or of an import's thing, is unknown; any part of it is parked (spec/versioning.md §2); its `ts` is more than `MAX_DRIFT` ahead (spec/crdt.md §2.2); a roster snapshot's held `/tenures` delta links a frontier whose blobs are not held (spec/permissions.md §6), unless a later epoch start of that thing is applied; or atomic apply waits.
- **Atomic apply**: a node applies a commit only once it holds every delta of it that belongs under a label it syncs (spec/sync.md §3: entitlement, not group membership, decides), with the pages of every paged delta (spec/crdt.md §8); dropped or expired deltas count as held, and chunk blobs of large atomic values (spec/data-model.md §6) are not waited for. It then joins all those deltas in one storage transaction and fires listeners after. Pending commits are re-examined when the node starts or stops syncing a thing. A delta of an already applied commit that arrives later (the node began syncing its label after applying) is joined on arrival.
- Membership commits are applied before content.
- Two commits by one node whose node-form counter ranges on one aspect overlap are both invalid.
- A membership or roster-snapshot commit in node form is bad input (§3).
- A delta whose bytes do not match the header's delta hash is bad input.
- A commit blob whose clear prefix disagrees with its header (expiry, the commitments to the delete keys its deltas list; spec/sync.md §1) is bad input.

## 5. Retention and purge
Every delta carries **retention** on the wire: who may purge it, how long its body is kept, and its expiry (§6). The writer fills it from the schema's defaults per AK (spec/data-model.md §2); a commit may set it per aspect; an untyped write (no schema for the AK) defaults to `author` with no keep bounds and no expiry. Retention is immutable: to change it, purge and write again.
- **Purgeable by**: `nobody` < `admin` < `author` < `writer`. The named role may drop the delta's body: by purge request (spec/permissions.md §8), by compaction, and by deleting its blobs at blind nodes (spec/sync.md §5). Delete keys follow it (spec/crypto.md §7). A **right holder** is a node that holds the right now.
- **Keep at least** and **keep at most**: each optional; each an absolute time, a duration after the body is replaced, or both (the later bound counts for at least, the earlier for at most). A relative keep at most exists only under `writer`: whoever replaces a body must be able to delete it. Where the two bounds disagree, keep at most wins.
- A **body** is one blob of a commit (a projection, spec/sync.md §3, holds the deltas of one retention class). A write **dies** when a later write supersedes it or at its delta's expiry (spec/crdt.md §4.8), at the superseding commit's `ts` or at T. A body is **replaced** as its writes die; per body a node tracks the first and last death so far. A node dates a death at the `ts` of the superseding write its join met: the arriving superseder's when it kills a held write, the state's winner's when the write arrives already superseded; a superseder arriving later does not move it. After a validity flip or an un-merge on the aspect it dates the aspect's deaths again from what it then holds (each at the first superseding write among its held bodies in `(ts, ID)` order, else at its state's). A snapshot body's writes are those it carries, each dying for it no earlier than the snapshot's `ts`: one its author held live but the node holds superseded by a write with a lower `ts` dies for it at the snapshot's `ts`. A date is never earlier than the death (spec/crdt.md §4.8); nodes holding the same commits may hold different dates.
- A body is **covered** by a valid snapshot the node holds (spec/permissions.md §5.2) when joining each of its deltas into the snapshot's fragment of that aspect leaves its canonical bytes unchanged (spec/crdt.md §3.3): every live write of the body is in the snapshot, and canonical form carries no dead content. A node-form body is covered only if the snapshot's frontier for its author also reaches the body's counters, a body judged by commit ID (commit form, import) only if the snapshot's `Absorbed` lists its commit, and a snapshot body only if the covering snapshot's frontiers reach its frontiers (spec/crdt.md §8), so every holder of the covering snapshot counts what the dropped body gave as received. Every write the covering snapshot holds under a dot the body holds a write of (or, for a body that is not a snapshot, under one of its own dots: its counter range, its commit ID, an import's origins) MUST be the body's write at the same position with the same `ts` and value (a value-less copy matches); a snapshot body's copies of writes whose own delta the node received and judges valid count for nothing (spec/permissions.md §5.2) and are left out of the join (its copies of invalid deltas' writes, which it vouches for, are not). A body the node cannot join, a snapshot whose pages the node has not fetched, or a snapshot body it has not received (a late part, or one counting as never received again, spec/permissions.md §5.2) establishes nothing: the body is neither covered nor uncovered for it (spec/sync.md §6). A body holding no delta is covered. A snapshot written from a node's whole state of an aspect covers every body of it the node had applied, except those spec/crdt.md §8 never covers.
- **Keep at least**: before the bound (last death so far plus D, or T) a body leaves the labels of its things' groups only by purge request: compaction MUST NOT drop it and right holders MUST NOT delete it at blind nodes. A body with no dead writes is past a relative bound.
- **Keep at most**: the bound is T, or first death plus D for D after replaced, plus `AT_MOST_GRACE` (spec/wire.md §2) while the body has a **live write**: any write of its deltas the node's state holds by dot, including element records the state keeps only as anchors (dead per spec/crdt.md §4.8, kept per spec/crdt.md §5.7), but not an element's or entry's tombstone (it holds the remover's reset, not the creation); with both, the earlier. Each node judges liveness on its own state, so bounds may differ between nodes until they hold the same commits; a right holder uploads the killing commit before any delete in the same pass, so the window is one read. Deaths being dated per node, a body is dropped no earlier than its bound counted from its writes' deaths and, for D after replaced, no later than D (plus `AT_MOST_GRACE` while it has a live write) after its first death as the node dated it, the `ts` of a superseder the node held by then. A drop is final: a body dropped (at its bound or as its writes died) stays dropped when a write that killed it, or one of its own, later turns invalid or valid, so nodes holding the same commits may differ in whether they hold such a body and in whether its writes are in the aspect's state. Under keep at most the erased data may persist in a body with a live write up to its bound (or the next compaction, which usually comes first), and at blind nodes until a right holder next syncs after the drop. By the bound every node MUST drop the body; its live writes stay in the node's state (spec/crdt.md §4.6); right holders delete it at blind nodes when next online after the bound. A node that may write the aspect and holds no snapshot covering a body with live writes writes one before dropping the body (it is held and uploaded like any must-hold body). A body with live writes is deleted at a label only while another entry under it carries them (spec/sync.md §7). A body with no live write needs no carrier: under at most 0 after replaced the node applying the kill that leaves it none drops it in that transaction and deletes it at blind nodes in the same sync; under D after replaced, a right holder that sees it with no live write sends blind nodes a delete timed at the bound (spec/sync.md §5).
- Between the bounds a right holder may drop a covered body (compaction, spec/sync.md §7); `nobody` makes that window empty. Snapshot bodies follow the same rules.
- **Purge request**: a header field naming earlier deltas or a whole aspect history. Every node applying it drops, at any time, the named deltas its author may purge (spec/permissions.md §8): from then on it holds their value-carrying writes value-less (spec/crdt.md §4.7) and drops every body carrying their values, snapshots included, whatever the order the request, the named delta's commit and the snapshot arrive in: a node judges a named delta once it holds the request and that commit's header (which the right and the writes it names depend on). A writer that, after applying it, holds no covering snapshot of the aspect under a label it writes re-snapshots there (duplicates are harmless, spec/sync.md §7). A request SHOULD name dead writes; the writer resets live content in the same commit. It MUST NOT name membership deltas. A commit carrying a purge request MUST NOT carry `expires`; its blobs are kept at least until `wall_ms(ts) + MEMBERSHIP_KEEP`, whatever its deltas' keep bounds (at least and at most).
- Purging drops bodies, never headers: a node keeps the header of every commit it applied. A node dropping a body for a purge keeps the commit as a blob without the purged deltas (header-only when none remain, which is covered, spec/sync.md §6): same header and commit ID, new blob, held and uploaded like any body.
- Dropping a body means destroying every local copy (undo buffers and pre-reset snapshots included), its delta nonce and every local index entry derived from it, so that later compromise of the node recovers nothing (notes/local-store.md describes the mechanism). Exception: a compaction snapshot's part dropped other than as covered (for its keep bounds, expiry or a purge) keeps its frontiers and `Absorbed` (counters and IDs, no content; spec/crdt.md §8); a part dropped as covered keeps neither (its covers reach them).
- A dropped body cannot be undone (§7); under at most 0 others' resets beat undo.
- Fixed retention: membership deltas are `admin`, keep at least `MEMBERSHIP_KEEP` (90 days) after replaced (a roster snapshot replaces `/members` and `/tenures` deltas, compaction snapshots or later writes the other membership-class aspects, spec/permissions.md §6); `/created` is `nobody`, kept forever (`abs` forever); a certificate copy carries its commit's; snapshots of membership-class aspects keep it (`/created` is never snapshotted, §2). Other compaction-snapshot deltas are `writer` with bounds from the **own deltas** of the writes the snapshot carries live (live as for keep at most): keep at least the longest relative bound among them, with no absolute one; keep at most the strictest per component, the shortest relative bound and the earliest absolute bound later than the snapshot's `wall_ms(ts)`, an absolute bound at or before it counting as 0 after replaced. A write's own deltas are its own commits' deltas on the aspect (spec/permissions.md §5.2; every applied import listing its origin); for writes whose own commit's header the node does not hold, every held snapshot body of the aspect carrying one of them stands in, and if some such write no held snapshot body carries, every compaction snapshot of the aspect the node applied. No schema enters the bounds; a snapshot names the schema of the last applied delta of the aspect in `(ts, ID)` order that names one, else none. Import deltas carry their origin delta's (spec/permissions.md §7).

## 6. Expiry
- `expires: T` (wall ms, `T > wall_ms(ts)`, else invalid), per aspect of the commit. The schema may give a default per AK as a duration from the write. The blob prefix carries the T of the deltas it holds (spec/sync.md §1, §3).
- At T every value-carrying write of the delta dies (spec/crdt.md §4.7): every node holds them value-less and drops the body, whatever the delta's keep-at-least bound.
- Blind nodes delete the blob at T unattended (spec/sync.md §5).

## 7. Undo
- An undo commit (a revert) sets `undoes` to the undone commit's ID; its deltas are chosen per spec/crdt.md §4.6 (undoing a creation deletes the thing, so they may touch aspects the undone commit did not). A commit whose body was dropped cannot be undone. Redo is an undo of the undo commit. Undoing ephemeral writes (spec/data-model.md §2) writes ephemeral writes chosen the same way, from the writing node's in-memory record of them; no commit names them.
