# Commits
> Summary: What a commit is and how a node verifies, applies, retains and purges it.

## 1. Header
Encoding: spec/wire.md §4. Fields:
- Format version (spec/versioning.md §1).
- Author tid (an account, or a key actor signing as itself), the epoch an account signs at, and the actor tid it acts for (checked: spec/permissions.md §5.2, §5.5); a rotation (spec/crypto.md §9).
- Kind: content or role (§2); compaction-snapshot marker.
- `ts` (spec/crdt.md §2.2) and the dot form, commit or session (§3).
- Optional `expires` (§6), the expiry of every delta of the commit.
- Aspect list: per touched `(tid, ak)`: counter range `[lo, hi)`, retention (§5), the delta hash of its one delta, and for a durable signal's delta optionally the fire it `replaces` (§5 Signals).
- Optional purge request (§5), optional `undoes: commit ID` (§7).
- A 16-byte random blind (the commit blob's, spec/crypto.md §7) and the delete keys with their levels; per aspect, the indices of the keys its deltas call for.
- Explicit removals and edge writes that audit only: the frontier (spec/permissions.md §5.1 step 4), inline or as a link to a chunk tree when it exceeds `CHUNK_THRESHOLD` (spec/data-model.md §6).
- Optional ephemeral marker: an ephemeral commit or a relay-only one (§2).
- The aspect, purge and delete-key lists are each inline, or a link to a chunk tree of the list's canonical bytes when it exceeds `CHUNK_THRESHOLD`; those blobs carry the commit's level-2 commitment (spec/wire.md §5) and go under every sync ID the commit's blob goes under (spec/sync-judgement.md §1).
- Signature (spec/crypto.md §2).

- **Commit ID** = BLAKE3 of the canonical header without the signature. The signature is over the commit ID (spec/crypto.md §2).
- **Delta** bytes: spec/crdt.md §3. **Delta hash** = `kdf("delta", dedup secret of tid, delta bytes)` (spec/crypto.md §6).
- A commit may touch any things, of any reader sets.
- A commit's size is bounded by its one blob (spec/sync-judgement.md §1):
  - a delta pages (spec/crdt.md §8), but a relay-only commit's deltas never page;
  - the header's lists go by link, each at most `MAX_LIST_BYTES`;
  - the whole commit, its paged deltas' roots included, fits `MAX_BLOB` (spec/data-model.md §6), or the writer refuses it. Paging and chunking keep every page within a blob.

## 2. Kinds
- **Content**: deltas on aspects that are not role-class.
- **Role**: deltas only on role-class aspects (spec/data-model.md §9).
  - A role commit changes at most one `roles.0` grant, except:
    - a single-use link redemption (the grant plus L's self-removal, both on K, spec/permissions.md §7);
    - a removal of several holders' grants on one target, each gone or saying retain (spec/permissions.md §2), none of them the author's own grant or a self-removal, with one rotation: an explicit one with one frontier (revoking a link joiner together with L, spec/permissions.md §7), or a finalize of several ended grants (spec/permissions.md §2).
  - A removal of another holder's grant with no frontier (a finalize, spec/permissions.md §2) removes only grants past their `until` at its `ts`; an explicit removal always carries a frontier.
  - These shape rules bind the writer; a receiver does not check them and judges each edge write on its own (spec/permissions.md §5.1).
  - A grant also carries the grantee's `secrets.0` boxes; a removal or demotion carries the rotation of the target's roles it requires where its author may rotate them (spec/permissions.md §3); other rotations, re-seals and `keys.0` and `sync.0` writes change no grant; all may cover many things.
  - Role commits MUST NOT carry `expires`; their retention is fixed (§5) and a purge request never names them.
- **`created.0`** is carried by the thing's first durable commit, of either kind; a commit whose only deltas are `created.0` and that does not expire is a role commit.
  - A compaction snapshot or ephemeral commit never carries it (§5). A compaction snapshot carrying `retention^.0` is bad input likewise.
  - A durable commit's `created.0` part MUST be exactly one minting of the record: the delta of one write setting the thing's record (typed by the `creation` struct, spec/data-model.md §9), nothing else; otherwise that delta is bad input (§4).
- **Compaction snapshot**: content or role kind with the compaction-snapshot marker; snapshot deltas (spec/crdt.md §8); no own writes, empty counter ranges (spec/sync-compaction.md §1).
  - Written by a node that may write the aspect (admin for `keys.0` and `secrets.0`, spec/permissions.md §5.2). A receiver judges this as for any delta of its author, and a snapshot failing it gives nothing (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
  - `roles.0` is never compaction-snapshotted: the role timeline walks every edge write (spec/permissions.md §5.1).
- **Ephemeral commit**: a commit with the ephemeral marker, carrying writes of ephemeral (`+`) aspects (spec/data-model.md §2), one of:
  - a content commit (its author's own writes, in its form, §3);
  - a content compaction snapshot of one `+` aspect (an ephemeral snapshot, below).

  With the relay marker instead it is **relay-only**: writes of ephemeral signal (`+~`) aspects, commit form, snapshot deltas with empty tables. For an ephemeral commit of either form and any marker:
  - **Records**: it carries no creation record; its things' records come in durable commits, and a part on a thing whose record is not held waits for it (§4).
  - **Shape**: every delta has retention `writer` (§5). It carries no purge request (its deltas are purged by durable requests as any delta, §5 Purge) and no list by link. Its large values are chunk trees and its deltas page as a durable commit's (spec/data-model.md §6, spec/crdt.md §8). A relay-only commit's deltas never page (each must fit a blob alone: one linking a page is bad input), and it carries no `expires` and no `undoes`.
  - **Holding**: every node holds ephemeral commits in memory only, read or kept unread (spec/sync-ids.md §5; spec/sync-compaction.md §2). Nothing of them joins durable state.
  - **Receiving**: a receiver verifies, judges and applies it as the durable commit of its kind (§4, spec/permissions.md §5.2):
    - a content commit as one, with pending, apply and un-merge;
    - a snapshot one as a compaction snapshot, covering held bodies (§5, spec/sync-compaction.md §2);
    - a relay-only one is delivered and never held (spec/sync-compaction.md §2).
- An **ephemeral snapshot** is a compaction snapshot like any (spec/crdt.md §8, §5).
  - Its delta is the writer's state of the aspect, every author's writes under their stamps, with the tables over the sources that occur in it, the values of deltas with an `expires` value-less (spec/crdt.md §8: none is covered), a `secrets.0` snapshot without the records at epochs its writer has not verified (spec/crypto.md §4).
- **Storage class**: an aspect's storage class is given by its AK's markers (spec/data-model.md §2), alike at every node. A part is bad when it is on:
  - an AK that is not an ephemeral aspect (`+` and `.`) in an ephemeral commit that is not relay-only;
  - an AK that is not an ephemeral signal (`+` and `~`) in a relay-only commit;
  - an AK with `+` in a durable commit;
  - a signal AK in a compaction snapshot (§5 Signals);
  - an AK other than a durable signal (`~` without `+`), or a commit with an ephemeral marker, with `replaces` (§5 Signals).

  For such a part: a writer MUST NOT write one, and a receiver drops it alone, as a part not matching its header entry (§4).
- A commit spanning several kinds is invalid.

## 3. Dot forms
- **Commit form**: dots `(commit ID, index)`, unlinkable across commits. Any content commit, durable or ephemeral, may use it.
  - A key actor other than an account whose key is used from more than one node (link key) MUST use it, and every role commit and every compaction snapshot MUST use it.
- **Session form**: dots `(Session(author, nonce), counter)` (spec/crdt.md §2.1), counters per (aspect, session), contiguous from 1; the header carries the 16-byte nonce.
  - Content commits (§2) with own writes otherwise use it, durable and ephemeral alike.
  - Session form on any other kind (§2: role, compaction snapshot) is bad input.

## 4. Verification and application
- A commit's signature MUST verify; receivers dedup by commit ID.
- Each **delta** is judged on its own, by its author's authority on that delta's thing (spec/permissions.md §5); an invalid delta is un-merged alone (spec/crdt.md §4.6), the commit's other deltas stand.
- A commit is **pending**, not applied and counted as not received, while any of these holds:
  - it is parked (spec/versioning.md §2);
  - its author is an account and the rotation minting the epoch it names is not held (spec/permissions.md §5.5);
  - its `ts` is more than `MAX_DRIFT` ahead (spec/crdt.md §2.2);
  - a header list or frontier given by link (spec/wire.md §4) is not resolved;
  - it is unjudgeable (Unjudgeable).

  Nothing else makes it pending: what its parts wait for never holds its header (Apply).
- **Pending storage**: a pending commit, and a held part that waits, is kept only while the node holds something carrying its commit: an entry of it under a sync ID, a sealed fire carrying it or its author's chain (spec/sync-judgement.md §1, spec/sync-inbox.md §2), or the newest stranded report on a sync ID naming a current holder whose keys carry it (spec/permissions.md §4). With none left it counts as never received until a carrier brings it again. Entries that give the node nothing held (unopened, parked, unjudgeable, their commit pending, or their commit known only for a part dropped as invalid, spec/permissions.md §6), unless their stamp verifies under S's head write key (a stamp vouches only under the head; otherwise the entry is charged, spec/sync-ids.md §5), count against its local bounds on unvouched input per sync ID and source; past them it keeps neither the entry nor what pends from it or is known of its commit, and reports that to the app.
- **Records**: a record a record blob or a sealed fire carries (spec/wire.md §3, §9) is learned only while something here awaits it (Unjudgeable: a commit pending, or a part of one waiting, for it) or it is on the record chain of one learned so. A sealed fire's others are not kept: its chain and commit are taken first and again once one is learned. A record blob's others pend under Pending storage, its entry their carrier; each is learned once something awaits it.
- **Unjudgeable**: a commit is unjudgeable while its author's or actor's `created.0` record is unknown, or while every held part of it is; a held part is unjudgeable while its thing's record or a record on its thing's chain (spec/permissions.md §1) is unknown, or the author is not on that chain and no path of record edges and grants leads from the author to it (a fire of an open signal needs none, spec/permissions.md §5.2 Open fires): a grant is an applied part's edge write putting a grant, whether the walk applies it or not; a removal leads nowhere.
  - An unjudgeable commit pends, kept under Pending storage with its unjudgeable parts and its signature's verdict, and is examined again when what it awaits arrives (a record, an edge write leading to it); it may then await the next. A pending commit's unjudgeable parts stay in its stored copy.
  - A copy of a part that hashes to its header entry, under its thing's record held or the record the part itself carries, replaces a stored first copy of that slot that does not.
  - A part of an applied commit that is unjudgeable waits alone (Apply): kept under Pending storage, counted as not received, and examined again when what it awaits arrives.
  - The node reads the `secrets.0` boxes of what it opened (spec/crypto.md Reading-only secrets).
  - A commit that awaits and whose expiry passes counts as never received, as for a node it never reached: it flags nothing (spec/permissions.md §4 Suspect), and the entry shows nothing here until a writer compacts.
  - A commit that awaits is as if it had not arrived: a deletion decided meanwhile (a covering snapshot's author dropped by it, a body removed) stands, and the deleted body counts as never received until it is fetched again.
  - **Examined again**: a pending commit is examined only when something it awaits arrives: a record it or a part of it awaits; the held role inputs moving (an edge write, a record, a rotation), for one awaiting a reach, its actor's secrets or an epoch; the account's chain coming to hold the epoch it names; a blob, for one waiting for pages or header lists; the clock reaching its `ts` less `MAX_DRIFT` or its expiry; a purge request or expiry changing what is value-less on a thing it writes; an open (an upgrade may read a parked one). No arrival examines the others.
  - **Limits**: an insider can exhaust the reading-only budget and delay honest grants until its entries leave. A hostile writer with a k-aspect blob can cause k examinations by publishing k records one at a time. A commit awaiting a reach, its actor's secrets or an epoch is examined again at each change of the held role inputs: k such cost k examinations per role commit applied. What a peer makes pend is bounded by the bounds on unvouched input per source (Pending storage), never by evicting another source's; what a writer stamped is charged to the peer that brought it once its write key is not the head's.

  A commit with no part held, or whose every held part waits, applies as its header alone, and counts as a commit on each aspect its header lists.
- A held **part waits** while any of these holds:
  - a page of it is not held (spec/crdt.md §8);
  - it is parked (spec/versioning.md §2).
- **Apply**: a node applies in one storage transaction every part it holds that does not wait, and fires listeners after. Nothing waits for a part, the header included: a waiting part waits alone, counts as not received and joins when its wait ends, and a delta the node does not hold (stripped from the blob, in a section it does not open, never come) is absent, the commit reading as one without that delta's body (§5). Chunk blobs of large atomic values (spec/data-model.md §6) are not waited for.
- **Order independence**: which parts apply is a function of the parts and other data held, never of arrival order or of which parts came together.
- A thing's **possible admins**, by the role commits the node has applied (a pending one, parked included, gives nothing), of any validity and whatever followed: the least set holding the thing and, with each member, its first admin and any holder named with admin on that member by an edge write a member signed.
- A thing's **possible writers**, by the same data: the least set holding the possible admins of the thing and of each member, and any holder named with write or above on the thing or on a member by one of that thing's or that member's possible admins.
- A request's signers **could hold the right** of a delta's purgeable-by when its author and its actor each could, by any role any held write ever gave, whatever that role's validity: for `admin`, a possible admin of the thing; for `writer`, a possible writer of it; for `author_or_admin`, a possible admin, or the delta's actor or a possible writer of that actor, the actor being a possible writer of the thing.
- A delta of an already applied commit that arrives later (a blob of the commit holding more parts than the node applied: the node began syncing its sync ID after applying, or its body counts as never received, §5) is joined on arrival; a blob holding fewer changes nothing.
- Role commits are applied before content.
- Two commits of one session whose counter ranges on one aspect overlap are both invalid.
- **Judged alone**: a fault of the header or signature refuses the commit, in any copy. Each delta is judged by itself, from the signed header, its bytes and held data; its fault never refuses another delta:
  - Bytes that do not match the header's delta hash, or a part index naming no aspect, are not that delta: dropped, and any copy matching the hash is taken as if they never came. A blob holding nothing else is bad input.
  - A part on an aspect of the wrong storage class (§2) is dropped.
  - A delta that matches and is bad by itself never applies. Only those bytes can match, so nothing marks it: it is judged again if it comes again. Bad by itself means:
    - it does not decode;
    - a `created.0` delta that is not one minting of its record;
    - a `roles.0` write inside an entry it did not create, or a value that is no well-formed grant (spec/permissions.md §2);
    - a value held unchunked that spec/data-model.md §6 chunks;
    - a carried write stamped after its commit.
  - A blob holding only such deltas, none of its parts waiting, is bad input.
  - Where such a delta, or its commit, waits, the delta is judged when that wait ends.
- **Copies**: a pending commit's deltas are checked against their hashes before it is stored, but while its header lists are not resolved.
  - A pending commit keeps the first copy of each delta it could not check and of a signature it could not verify.
  - A copy that proves not to match goes, and a node then judges again the copies of the commit it holds as blobs; this brings back no delta counting as never received (§5).
  - A copy it no longer holds comes back when a holder logs it again (spec/sync-judgement.md §3).
- A commit blob whose clear prefix disagrees with its header (expiry, the commitments to the delete keys its deltas list; spec/sync-ids.md §1) is bad input. A node holding a blob it opened before the header's lists resolved opens it again once they do and judges it then: until then it counts as carrying no part of the commit.

## 5. Retention and purge
Every delta carries **retention** on the wire: who may purge it (its purgeable-by); its commit carries its expiry (§6). What history keeps of a replaced body is its thing's setting (The setting). The writer fills purgeable-by from the schema's default per AK (spec/data-model.md §2), and a commit may set it per aspect; an untyped write (no schema for the AK) defaults to `admin`. Retention is immutable: to change it, purge and write again.

### Who and how long
- **Purgeable by**: a set: `admin` (the thing's admins), `author_or_admin` (the delta's author and admins), `writer` (every writer, so both too). Admins may always purge. `nobody` (none) is the fixed retention of `retention^.0` (Fixed retention) and may be given to `created.0` (Destruction); on any other delta it is bad (§2).
  - A member of the set may drop the delta's body: by purge request (spec/permissions.md §6) and by deleting its blobs at peers that keep them unread (spec/sync-ids.md §6). Delete keys follow it (spec/crypto.md §7).
  - A **right holder** is a node that holds the right now.
- **Keep bounds**: a body in history (The setting) has a **keep at least** until it leaves history; a role-class body has a keep at least of `ROLE_KEEP` after replaced (Fixed retention). Purge on replace has no bound: it is a duty to cover (Purge on replace).

### The setting
- `retention^.0` (spec/data-model.md §9), admin-only, on the thing it governs: in `rules`, AKs mapped to an entry, in `default` an optional entry, in `retain` the default for removals (spec/permissions.md §2), and in `history_holds_targets` whether a strong in a held body of the thing's history holds its target (spec/data-model.md §8 Held history; the newest held valid setting decides). An entry has a `history` (absent: no history promised; `keep` until a time T and/or for a duration D after replaced, at least one; `purge_on_replace`), an optional `expires` duration (§6) and `final` (a writer refuses to replace a fire there, Signals).
- The entry governing an AK is its own in `rules`, else `default`; an open (`@`) signal's (spec/data-model.md §2) is its own only, `default` never; neither: no history promised.
- It governs every aspect of the thing but those with fixed retention, memory aspects included. Each delta is judged as an admin-only delta at its key (spec/permissions.md §5.2); no other delta's validity depends on a setting.
- Each `retention^.0` delta is read alone: its value is the whole setting; a delta not reading as the aspect's type, with a key in `rules` that is not an AK (spec/data-model.md §2) or with more than `MAX_RETENTION_ENTRIES` entries in `rules`, is no setting. A writer writes it whole (never by path, within those bounds, with no entry a write could not follow) and does not undo it.
- A grant without history is followed by its author's write of the setting in force (spec/crypto.md §4 Floors), so its holder holds it without the earlier settings, and by snapshots of the state written before it (spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut).
- **In force**: the held valid settings of a thing ordered by key are its timeline; at wall time t the setting in force is the newest by key with `wall_ms(ts) <= t`, before the first none. A setting is in force from its own commit on.
- **One way**: where an earlier setting of the timeline governs an AK with purge on replace, a later `keep` entry for that AK reads as no history.
- **Replaced at** r: §5 Bodies and deaths; a signal's body at its own `ts`; a purged part, for listing, at its request's `ts`.
- A setting **keeps** a body at t while t < max(r + D, T) under a `keep` entry (a missing bound counts as 0); never under no entry or purge on replace. A body **leaves history** at the first t >= r at which the setting in force at t does not keep it, and is out from then on: a setting acts from its own stamp, and bodies of one aspect leave in the order they were replaced.
- **In history**: a held valid replaced body that has not left, by the node's clock. That and the parts live in the state is **kept history** (§7).
- **Kept while live**: a held valid delta none of whose writes is dead (not replaced, Bodies and deaths), not a snapshot's or a signal's, is kept at least until the end of the last span of its aspect's timeline in which a body replaced at that moment would be kept (a `keep` entry with a D: to the span's end; with only a T: to T or the span's end, the earlier); for good while the newest setting has a D, or T is `FOREVER`. A role-class live delta is kept for good, as are `created.0` and `retention^.0` (Fixed retention). Elsewhere (no history promised, purge on replace, no setting at all) a live delta has no bound and drops under a cover like a replaced one out of history. Kept so, it is kept history for listings and undo (§7).
- **Out of history**: a body out of history (a live one where it is not kept while live) may be dropped by every node under any held valid writer's cover (§5 Covered, Own replaces), whether or not that writer holds the purge right over it: nothing promises to keep it, and dropping it is no purge. A node takes no copy of a body out of history, fetches none and holds none as must-hold (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
- **Dropped stays dropped**: a body a node dropped as covered or out of history is not missing when a setting arriving later puts it in history.
- **Waiting for it**: a writer holding no valid setting of a thing while a held header lists a valid `retention^.0` part on it not received writes no content on the thing until it holds a valid setting, or until a complete pass (spec/sync-status.md §1) of every sync ID it reads carrying the thing, begun after it began waiting for that part, ended without the part's body (none read: at once): the setting is unknown, not none, until a pass says no holder it hears from has it. Then, for that part, it writes as the settings it holds say (none: the defaults); the part's body arriving later is a late setting like any other (below).
- Every judgment here is a function of the settings and bodies held and the clock: a late setting may move a held body out of or back into history; a setting that turns invalid was never in force.

### Bodies and deaths
- Principle: a body drops in one of four ways: as covered (a valid compaction snapshot covers it, or its author's own whole replaces do, once it is past its keep bounds, Covered, Own replaces, Keep bounds), by expiry (§6), by purge request, or as invalid (Invalid, below). A signal's body is in no state, so nothing covers it: out of history, a node may drop it (Signals). A node drops nothing else on its own; one without write on the aspect writes no snapshot, so it drops only expired and invalid content, what a writer's snapshot covers and what an author's own replaces cover (Own replaces).
- A **body** is the commit's blob (spec/sync-judgement.md §1): its held deltas, each in its thing's section; a rewrite holds fewer. Each delta drops on its own, by its own bounds (Keep bounds), the body rewritten without it; the body leaves once each of its deltas has.
- A write **dies** when a later write supersedes it or at its delta's expiry (spec/crdt.md §4.8). A **live write** of a body is any write of its deltas the node's state holds by dot, resets and value-less writes included, and every sequence element record of its deltas the state holds, also one holding only others' writes. An element's or entry's tombstone is not one of its creator's (it holds the remover's reset, not the creation). A held valid body is **replaced** when it holds a write dead in the node's state (spec/crdt.md §4.8: one the state does not hold, or one winning in the body that the state holds, a sequence element's creation by its element's record as a live write, but not as a winning write, as a text element's creation under a winning delete or pruned by a reset its element's record outlives for a later flag) and joining it into the state leaves the state's canonical bytes unchanged (spec/crdt.md §3.3). A delta with an expiry held with its values is judged against the state holding them value-less, as a snapshot carries them (spec/crdt.md §8): it is replaced only once none of its values lives.
- When a body was replaced (asked where its thing's timeline keeps the aspect's history) is a function of the bodies the node holds, judged when asked:
  - a write of the body that the node's state supersedes (it wins in the body and not in the body joined into the state, spec/crdt.md §4.8) died at the earliest `ts` among the held valid writes superseding it, read from any held body holding one (spec/crdt.md §4.8 When: a write's own `ts`, also for a valid snapshot's copy of a write whose delta is held invalid);
  - a delta's body is replaced at the latest death of its writes, a snapshot's at the latest death among the writes it carries that no held valid snapshot stamped later carries (holds as a winning write, the same position, kind and stamp, spec/crdt.md §4.8; that one keeps their history); none such dead: not replaced;
  - so never earlier than the death (spec/crdt.md §4.8); nodes holding the same bodies judge alike, and one holding more judges no later; a drop as covered moves no body into or out of history and no replaced-at of a body in history (no date depends on which body holds a write: the cover carries the dropped body's live writes with their stamps, and a write only it dated was replaced no later than it);
  - Limit: a write's `ts` is its writer's word (a vouched copy's too), so a writer stamping early shortens the history of what it replaces; that only reclaims storage early, it erases nothing.
  - an expiry dates none: the delta's body goes at T (§6) and a snapshot holds the write value-less;
  - a write of a delta invalid here does not die (spec/crdt.md §4.6).

### Covered
- A body is **covered** by a valid snapshot the node holds (spec/permissions.md §5.2) when joining each of its deltas into the snapshot's fragment of that aspect leaves its canonical bytes unchanged (spec/crdt.md §3.3): every live write of the body is in the snapshot, and canonical form carries no dead content. A held body is judged by this join whatever the snapshot's tables say.
  - A node MUST NOT count a body covered unless the snapshot holds each live write of the body with the body's payload: a copy held with another payload leaves the body uncovered whichever wins the copy order (spec/crdt.md §4.1). A value-less write of the body (spec/crdt.md §4.7) is judged by the join alone.
  - A snapshot body is covered only if it is stamped no later than the covering snapshot.
  - An expiring snapshot (spec/crdt.md §8) covers only an expiring snapshot body of the same expiry: its copies end at its expiry.
  - A held body is covered only if the snapshot, where it carries the commit's writes, states for it the class and actor its header gives, and a held snapshot body only if the covering snapshot states for each commit both carry the class and actor the body states (spec/crdt.md §8 `Facts`; a snapshot's actor is its fact's, else the commit's author as that snapshot names it): a cover never leaves a commit's writes judged by other facts where its header is not held.
  - A valid snapshot body's copies all count in the join (spec/permissions.md §5.2); an invalid one's copies of writes whose own delta the node received and judges valid are left out of it.
  - A body the node cannot join, a snapshot whose pages the node has not fetched, or a snapshot body it has not received (a late part, or one counting as never received again, spec/permissions.md §5.2) establishes nothing: the body is neither covered nor uncovered for it (spec/sync-judgement.md §2).
  - A body holding no delta is covered (a lasting header is must-hold all the same, spec/sync-judgement.md §2), unless it is session-form and the snapshot's frontier has an entry for its session short of its counters (no entry: the snapshot holds no write of the session, spec/crdt.md §8).
  - A delta the node dropped as covered is covered while its covers carry it (spec/permissions.md §5.2), whatever later snapshots' tables say; once none does, it counts as never received.
  - A valid delta of a held commit that the node never received (a strip left it out of the blobs it holds, spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Per blob) is covered by a held valid snapshot whose tables reach it (spec/crdt.md §8: its session listed up to its counters, or its commit absorbed), and past its keep bounds the node drops it as covered under that snapshot, as one received. Until such a snapshot is held it counts as never received.
  - A body not held is not covered by a snapshot holding any of its writes value-less, unless the node holds them value-less past the delta's expiry (§6): a copy stripped for a purge that fell, or written by a node holding a request this one does not, gives no value.
  - A delta with an expiry held with its values is judged by the join like any body: a snapshot carries its values value-less (spec/crdt.md §8), so covers it only once none of them lives. One not held is not covered until the node holds it value-less (past T, spec/crdt.md §4.7); from then on its leftover is judged by the join.
  - A snapshot written from a node's whole state of an aspect covers every body of it the node had applied, except a delta with an expiry holding a live value and a `secrets.0` delta writing a record the snapshot leaves out (spec/crypto.md §4).
- A cover drops a delta's body only when it covers it and the body is past its keep bounds (judged per delta; Keep bounds): a live delta (The setting, Kept while live) as a replaced one, durable or ephemeral alike.
- A snapshot marked as an own cover (spec/permissions.md §5.1, §5.5, Own cover) drops a held body only at the node that wrote it, its removal or rotation held or not: it stands on that removal or rotation, which may fall. A delta never received (below) drops under it at any node, as under any cover: nothing held is lost.
- Limit: a snapshot cannot give back what it replaced. A dropped delta's values live on as its cover's copies, which a purge request naming it holds value-less (Purge); but once dropped, a purge that later falls, or another later judgment that would read the delta alone, restores nothing beyond those copies.

### Own replaces
- A **whole replace** is a plain durable content commit on a content aspect (not signal, not ephemeral) whose delta resets the aspect at its root (a root reset or a root register write, spec/crdt.md §5.1); it may carry an expiry. An **own replace** of an earlier plain content commit C is a whole replace of the same author (an account's nodes are one author), `ts` later than C's, valid and held here, that passes the covered test of §5 Covered with its delta in place of the snapshot's fragment.
- C drops as covered by an own replace once two own replaces of C are held valid here: the writer's previous replace stays until the next lands, so a replace that later counts for nothing costs what it alone carried. Which commits cover C is a function of the commits held: the same at every node holding the same commits.
- A commit with several parts goes under own replaces only whole: every part it still holds must be covered by own replaces, and it goes once the latest of the parts' keep-at-least bounds passes.
- A body not held and not dropped (not fetched yet) is not established as covered; a dropped one joins nothing and is.
- Every node, reader or writer, drops C under own replaces once it is out of history (past its keep-at-least bound), as under a snapshot, and judges C covered for must-hold (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) by the same test.
- An own replace that turns invalid, or drops other than as covered, gives nothing: a commit dropped under own replaces stays dropped only while two of them (or a valid snapshot covering it) are still held valid; otherwise it is missing again, as under a snapshot (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
- A delta with an expiry is judged by the join, not by its own lifetime: a later own whole replace covers it whether its values have expired or not.

### Forgetting
- A node forgets a commit only where forgetting changes no read: every part of it covered, dropped as invalid (an invalid delta influences nothing, spec/permissions.md §5.2) or a fire out of history (listed nowhere, Signals).
- A node forgets a content commit or content compaction snapshot, header and ID included, once every part of it dropped as covered (under any held valid writer's compaction snapshot, or its author's own replaces), as invalid (Invalid) or as a fire out of history or past its expiry (Signals), or is not held on a thing whose record is not held (never received, or marked forgotten: A released thing), and no entry of a blob of it is known and no delete of one of its own blobs is within `TOMBSTONE_LIFETIME`.
- A node forgets a content commit of either class, header and ID included, once it holds of it only parts held value-less for a purge request (Purge; not past its `expires`), one at least, none permanent, none a `created.0` delta, each part not dropped as covered is covered (Covered, Own replaces: a valid snapshot past the purge carries what its value-less writes kill), nothing of it waits, and no entry of a blob of it is known and no delete of one is within its tombstone. Its value-less writes go from the state with it.
- A node forgets such a commit carrying a purge request alike, every part dropped as covered or as invalid and one at least as invalid, once its request holds the right over no delta it could name (no purgeable-by class over any actor the author could write gives it: an invalid request purges nothing, spec/permissions.md §6) and it is not lasting.
- Otherwise kept: role commits, a thing's first commit (its `created.0` part never drops), a lasting commit, a commit carrying a purge request but as above, a compaction snapshot not so covered.
- A part dropped as invalid that turns valid after its commit was forgotten comes back only as a copy arriving again, applied and judged anew (spec/permissions.md §6: its author logs it again once it judges it valid; with no holder logging it, it stays gone). No node's own store keeps an entry of a body it judges invalid (spec/sync-judgement.md §2 Judged invalid), so its own entry never holds the commit known.
- **A released thing** (spec/sync-set.md §3): what a held thing needs of it to verify its own authority (its record, and its role commits on that chain, spec/permissions.md §4) is kept while it is needed (What a held thing needs), as certificates under the sync IDs still synced; everything else of it goes. The node forgets it in the transaction that releases it: every other held commit whose every part is on it or marked forgotten goes, header and ID included, role commits included; one whose every other part is marked forgotten or not held on a thing whose record is not held goes too, as a commit unjudgeable for those records (§4 Unjudgeable): its entries are not deleted at peers, and the node does not fetch them again until one of those records arrives; any other commit keeps its header, and its parts on the thing count as never received (Never received) and are marked forgotten. A copy of a marked part arriving joins it as any part never received, and the mark goes; a copy the node already held is no arrival, before or after a restart, nor is a held entry of a commit that went. The node keeps the marks across restarts. A commit going this way takes with it every per-aspect row of a thing whose record is not held that no held part is on, its purge items' included. Past what is kept as certificates, the thing is then unknown there, as at a node that never held it: no per-aspect row and no `ts` for undo. Its entries are forgotten as the entries of a body no longer held (spec/sync-judgement.md §3 Dropped bodies).
- **What a held thing needs**: a thing outside the node closure is kept (its record, every held commit on it, the secrets the node holds of it) only while a held thing needs it, as certificates, and goes whole, as never received, once nothing does (below). A thing is needed by a thing in the node closure whose current state (durable aspects, held fires included; spec/data-model.md §8 Known references) or held history (spec/data-model.md §8 Held history) holds a strong to it (a weak or a ticket only points and needs nothing; a held ticket keeps its thing only as a strong to it with that ticket does, below), whose record names it first admin, on which a held edge write names it holder, named holder by a held edge write on it (not by its record: the holder's sync ID carries the grant, spec/sync-judgement.md §2 A grant naming a holder), or on which a held commit it authored or acted in has an aspect (its body held or not); by the things a pending commit (§4 Pending storage) writes on, where it authored or acted in that commit or the commit awaits its record; and by the things in the node closure on which a held commit with an aspect on it has a part missing (one the node fetches again), while it does. It is held for itself (needed by nothing) while: in the node closure or not yet released from it (spec/sync-set.md §3); reached by a strong in the node closure while a ticket for it that opens here is held (spec/sync-set.md §2 What a held ticket does); a key actor that is no account; the K of a link it follows (spec/permissions.md §7); named holder by a held key actor (a link being redeemed, a bearer); a retained copy (spec/permissions.md §3 Retain); or under an own self-removal still to be sealed (spec/permissions.md §3 Lost access). Needs are followed transitively, cycles included. **Dropped when the pass that brought it ends**: a thing outside the node closure whose record arrived, or that a pending commit came to await, is judged once the sync pass that brought it ends (a complete pass ending, spec/sync-status.md §1); one that lost a need once the passes in flight end, at once with none in flight; a released thing, and what it is named holder on, in the transaction that releases it; what nothing needs then goes. So what a node holds of things outside its closure at quiet is a function of what it holds, never of arrival order. A node keeps an account on purpose (a contact, a peer it will grant) by holding something of it, normally a strong to its profile from a held thing (the profile needs its first admin); core keeps no list of known accounts. An account needs its inbox (its `keys.0` names it) and its profile, not its account root D (nothing outside D is judged by D, spec/permissions.md §1): D is kept by the rules above alone, as the first admin of a held thing under it. Another account's chain (its record, its rotations, its inbox's record and `keys.0`) goes with the last held thing that holds a strong to it, it wrote, acted in or holds a role on: a stranger's with its last fire.
- A node keeps, per aspect but a signal of a thing whose record it holds, the newest `ts` of a commit it forgot with a part valid there, across restarts: an undo counts what that commit may have held (§7; a signal reads absent before and after any write).
- **A forgotten commit's name**: a node forgetting a commit with writes of its own keeps, per aspect where a held compaction snapshot carries a value-carrying write (spec/crdt.md §4.7, valued or value-less) stamped with its `ts` and own source, its ID with that stamp, its purgeable-by class there, actor and `expires`, all from its header, across restarts, until no held snapshot of the aspect carries such a write or it holds the header again. A write carrying no value (a reset, a set element's tombstone among them, a flag, a position write, a value removal) keeps none: no purge acts on it. A purge by commit ID resolves through it to the stamp (Purge), and a request naming the stamp is judged by those facts as by the header (Purge, Without the header). Only the header binds an ID to a stamp. Limit: a node that never held the header (a late joiner holding only a snapshot carrying the write) is as any node that never saw the commit: it keeps no name and refuses a purge by that ID; a request from a node keeping the name, naming the stamp, still reaches the copies there, and a purge by author or whole aspect reaches them from any node. Once no node keeps the name, the write is purged only by author or whole aspect.
- Forgetting reads as never having received it: no list names it (spec/permissions.md §5.1, §5.5), and the covers it dropped under stand for it; a removal of its author, or a rotation of its author's account, names its writer's own cover instead, which carries its live writes from the writer's state (spec/permissions.md §5.1, §5.5, Own cover). A forgotten commit arriving again applies and goes again by the same rules.
- Limit: a cover that later counts for nothing (a removal of its author by a demoter that had not received it, an addendum that falls, an own cover whose removal falls with its remover) cannot give a forgotten commit back; its author's copy of a live write it still holds (spec/sync-compaction.md §1) comes back while the author counts. Loss is bounded: a snapshot or own replace that reached a node before the removal's `ts` still counts for what it carries.
- Limit: forgetting may narrow what undo allows (§7: the forgotten commit's header no longer shows it replaced early enough or reached by a kept snapshot), never widens it and never brings back data. Only the undoing node judges this (spec/crdt.md §4.6: a receiver never derives an undo's writes again), so nodes never disagree on a commit by it.

### Keep bounds
- **Keep at least**: before a body leaves history (on the node's clock; The setting) it leaves a node only by purge request, expiry or as invalid: compaction MUST NOT drop it, own replaces do not. Right holders MUST NOT delete it at peers that keep it unread before `MAX_DRIFT` past that, on their clock (until every node's clock agrees it left). A live body is bound alike while kept while live (The setting). A node judges the bound alike whether it may write or not.
- **Dated only where history is kept**: each delta of a body is dated for these bounds by its own writes alone, and only where some held valid setting keeps history (at any moment) for its own aspect; it is kept by its own aspect's setting alone, whatever other aspects of the thing or deltas of the body keep, and the body leaves when each delta may. A delta leaves history no earlier than replaced: replaced at r later than the clock, its body leaves a node until the clock reaches r only by purge request, expiry or as invalid (not as covered, under purge on replace too). Elsewhere no history is at stake: the delta adds no bound, so a covered body none of whose deltas is kept drops at once. A fire is dated by its own `ts`. Listing follows history alone (§7).
- On a commit carrying a purge request every body is kept at least until the purge floor (below), judged by the node's clock.
- **Purge on replace**: a body is under purge on replace when the entry governing its AK in the setting in force at its replaced-at is `purge_on_replace` (never a signal's: there the entry reads as no history). A node that may write the aspect (holding the role and the delete keys a write lists, spec/crypto.md §7) and holds such a body, replaced, that no held valid snapshot holding the right over it covers MUST write a compaction snapshot of the aspect from its whole state in the transaction in which it comes to hold one (applying the replacing write or the body, a validity change, a setting arriving or changing validity, or what makes it one that may write: a role change, or the thing's minting or those delete keys arriving last), unless its snapshot would not hold the right over that body either and a held valid snapshot covers it (that one drops it, below); duplicates from several writers are harmless (spec/sync-compaction.md §1). No writer is invalid for not knowing the setting. Every node MUST drop a held purge-on-replace body that a held valid snapshot covers, as soon as it holds both (and, where it is dated, its clock reaches the body's replaced-at, Dated only where history is kept): out of history, any writer's cover drops it. So the body is gone at every writer holding it and the setting in the transaction it learns of the replacement (or once its clock reaches it), and everywhere else when such a writer's snapshot arrives; a node without write keeps it until then, for good if no writer holding the body, its replacement and the setting ever syncs. Its snapshots' copies of it are judged by the setting like any body. If the replacing write later turns invalid, the valid snapshots carrying it vouch for it (spec/permissions.md §5.2, Copies): the replacement stays and the replaced body does not come back.
- A waiting part (§4), parked included, keeps its body until it applies, whatever its retention: before that only a purge request or its expiry acts on it (below). Limit: one that never applies is kept until one of those does, or for good.
- **Invalid**: a node drops a held delta it judges invalid at the end of the transaction that judged it, whatever its keep bounds, but for the deltas spec/permissions.md §6 keeps. While it stays invalid and its commit is known (Forgetting) a copy arriving again is not taken; once valid it counts as never received from then on, and the node fetches it again (spec/sync-judgement.md §3).
- **Never received**: a body dropped other than as covered, invalid or out of history (a part missing when purged, a part on a released thing the node forgot, Forgetting) counts as never received from then on.
  - The node does not fetch it on its own.
  - A copy that arrives again joins as a late part and is judged again.
  - Its holders log it while it is must-hold (spec/sync-judgement.md §2), so nodes holding the same bodies read alike.
- **A waiting part past its expiry**: a part that waits (§4) when its expiry passes goes from the node at T, and one arriving past T goes if it waits. Either counts as never received: its non-value writes join only through snapshots. Every local copy goes with it. That node may read what came before the part until it holds a writer's next compaction while that counts (spec/sync-compaction.md §1; one dropped while it did not count repairs nothing until it comes again, Invalid).
- **A waiting part under a purge**: a waiting part that an applied purge request names whose author may purge it (judged once the node holds the request and the commit's header) is held value-less from then on.
  - The node keeps it as its leftover (spec/crdt.md §4.7) and no other local copy of it; it waits and applies as that, so nodes holding the same commits read alike.
  - With a page of it not held, or parked (spec/versioning.md §2), it cannot be stripped: it goes at once with every local copy, as a part missing when purged.
  - If the request no longer drops it when its commit applies, it is fetched again like any missing delta.
  - A snapshot part is never named. A waiting one carrying a copy of a value the node holds value-less is held as its leftover, those copies value-less (spec/crdt.md §4.7 Copies), and joins as that; one with a page not held, or parked, is stripped once it can be read, at the latest when it joins.
  - Limit: a commit whose header lists are not resolved is judged by no request until they are; its parts keep their values until then or until its last carrier leaves (§4 Pending storage).
- Where such a drop may leave a node reading less than one that kept the data, it reports the aspect suspect (spec/permissions.md §4).
- **Out of history** every node may drop a covered body past its keep bounds (Covered) (compaction, spec/sync-compaction.md §1) under any held valid writer's snapshot or its author's own replaces, the purge right or not (The setting). Snapshot bodies follow the same rules. Deleting it at peers that keep it unread stays a right holder's (spec/sync-compaction.md §1).

### Signals
- A delta on a signal without `+` (spec/data-model.md §2) is never joined into a state, and no compaction snapshot carries or covers it. It is **replaced** the moment it is written: its body is history from its own `ts`, under the entry governing its AK (The setting; purge on replace reads as no history there).
- A writer MUST NOT write a fire the setting it holds would not keep (no entry: no listing); one written anyway is out of history at once. A sealed fire's sender (spec/sync-inbox.md §2) holds no setting of the thing and is bound by none: the setting its holders hold decides.
- Keep bounds apply unchanged, D counting from its own `ts` (Keep bounds); T forever keeps it for good. Before it leaves history and any purge floor ends it leaves only by purge request, expiry or as invalid.
- Out of history it is past every bound: a node may drop it, whole or as its leftover. A node takes no copy of it then, fetches none (one dropped as invalid that turns valid, or held value-less for a purge that falls, stays dropped: never missing again), and holds none as must-hold (spec/sync-judgement.md §2). Whether a node still holds it changes no read: it is not listed (§7 Listed parts).
- Expiry (§6) and purge requests apply to it per delta, as to any delta.
- **`replaces`**: a delta names a fire it replaces (§1, spec/wire.md §4), with that fire's floor as its writer reads it (spec/crypto.md §4 Floors: the floor of an epoch of its thing it opened a section carrying it under, its own: the one it wrote it under; 0 where none is known). A node whose own floor on the thing's actor is above it lists the delta nowhere, honored or not (a version of a fire from before its grant without history), unless it holds the named commit's part opened (or written) under an epoch of floor at least its own. It is **honored**, judged from headers alone, when the named commit O is listed on the same `(t, ak)` and valid (a purge tombstone counts), carries no `replaces` on that aspect, has the same verified `actor`, and sorts before the delta by `(ts, ID)`; else the delta is a fire of its own at its own place.
- A listed original's part reads as its newest listed honored version by `(ts, ID)` whose body the node holds or that is purged, valid versions only, else as itself; its stamp, place and order stay the original's. An honored version is not listed of its own. A node's own invalid part is marked and reads as itself.
- A writer gives an edit O's `expires` and O's floor, and writes `replaces` only naming a fire it would honor if written now: its actor's own, listed and valid there, carrying no `replaces`, under no `final` entry of the setting it holds. `final` binds the writer alone: an edit written anyway is honored.
- Listeners hear a write only as a listed commit (§7 Listed parts): told, at the end of each storage transaction, of every commit whose listed part on it reads otherwise after it (applied, joined late, turned valid or invalid, purged, dropped). A commit that leaves the listing and shows again is told again. An honored version is told as its original's change, and itself only when it is listed of its own before or after (it arrived before its original, or stands alone once its original is not listed).
- A validity change rebuilds no state for it: it changes only which of its bodies are valid (spec/permissions.md §5.2), and so its history.

### Purge
- **Purge request**: a header field naming earlier deltas (of commits with a smaller `ts`; a later one is not named, but for a minting: an item on `created.0` names every minting of its thing, §5 Destruction) by commit (by its stamp: `ts` and own source), by a whole aspect history, or by author on an aspect (spec/wire.md §4). It names only deltas with writes of their own, never a compaction snapshot's. It MUST NOT name role-class deltas.
  - A later-arriving delta an applied request names falls under it as one held does.
  - Every node applying it drops, at any time, the named deltas its author may purge (spec/permissions.md §6): from then on it holds their value-carrying writes value-less (spec/crdt.md §4.7) and holds each named delta's own body and every body carrying their values as its leftover (spec/crdt.md §4.7 Copies: snapshots carrying a copy under the named delta's dots), whatever the order the request, the named delta's commit and the carrying body arrive in. A snapshot has no values of its own and is never named, so a purge never drops one and never shows one as purged; its copies of the named deltas' values go as any carrying body's. A node judges a named delta once it holds the request and that commit's header (which the right and the writes it names depend on).
  - **Without the header** (forgotten, never received): a commit whose writes a held valid snapshot carries is judged by the snapshots' `Facts` for it (spec/crdt.md §8) and its copies' stamps: named and right as for a delta stamped `ts` with that source, class and actor and no expiry (a snapshot carries no value of a delta with one); dropped only if it stands by the class and actor every held valid snapshot carrying it states (its actor by that snapshot alone; one naming no author for it states nothing). Its writes are the writes those snapshots carry stamped with its `ts` and source; they are held value-less as a named delta's (spec/crdt.md §4.7 Copies), until the request no longer stands by them or the node holds the header, which then decides. A node keeping the commit's name (Forgetting) judges it by the name's facts alone, as by its header. So a purge reaches every copy of a value at every node holding the request and a copy, whatever it holds of the commit.
  - A writer of a named aspect where the request dropped something here (its author may purge a named delta whose header the writer holds, or copies of one it does not hold, Without the header) and that, after applying it, holds no valid snapshot of the aspect left whole by it (not held as its leftover), whose floor is at least its seal floor (spec/crypto.md §4 State at a cut: 0 on an actor never cut), that covers every named delta it had applied (held, or dropped as covered) writes a compaction snapshot of the aspect from its whole state, under each sync ID it writes there (duplicates are harmless, spec/sync-compaction.md §1), once it may write there (the thing's minting and the delete keys a write lists, spec/crypto.md §7); the duty outlives a restart (on an ephemeral aspect it goes with the aspect's held data) and ends with that snapshot, or once a held valid snapshot left whole covers those deltas or the node may no longer write there. A refused or no-op request changes nothing and asks for no snapshot.
  - A request SHOULD name dead writes; the writer resets live content in the same commit.
  - A commit carrying a request has a delta on each thing it names (it is what logs the commit under the thing's sync IDs): where no reset is wanted, a write of `purge.0` (spec/data-model.md §9), which carries no `expires`.
  - A commit carrying a purge request and an `expires` reaches only deltas of commits whose `expires` is at most its own (a delta with none is beyond it); a writer refuses one that names anything else. Its blobs are kept at least until `wall_ms(ts) + ROLE_KEEP` or its `expires`, the earlier, whatever its deltas' keep bounds.
- **Purging drops bodies, never headers**: a node keeps the header of every durable commit it applied, until it is forgotten (Forgetting).
  - An ephemeral commit goes whole, header included, once none of its deltas is held and none waits: covered or superseded (spec/sync-compaction.md §2); an expired one is held as its leftover, a purged one as its leftover until covered and forgotten (Forgetting); one with a part dropped as invalid goes as Forgetting says. A copy arriving again applies again and goes again by the same rules.
  - A node dropping a body for a purge keeps the commit as a blob without the purged deltas: same header and commit ID, new blob, held and logged like any body. With none left it holds only the header.
  - A **header-only blob** goes under a sync ID only as a lasting header (below) or as a certificate copy of a thing's witness or of a request destroying it (Destruction); its prefix has no expiry and no commitments.
- A delta is **permanent** when it is durable, its aspect has no fixed retention, a body of its AK replaced now would never leave history (a forever entry in force), and its commit has no `expires`. Only a purge request removes its body.
- A commit is **lasting** on thing t when either:
  - a permanent delta of it on t is dropped other than as invalid (an invalid delta shows nothing);
  - its purge request has dropped a permanent delta on t at this node (spec/permissions.md §6: its author held the right, as the node then judged; a request that falls stays lasting there).

  A lasting commit's header is never forgotten, and every sync ID whose sync unit holds t (spec/sync-set.md §2), whose actor may read t, holds a blob of the commit for good (spec/sync-judgement.md §2): a body of it, else a header-only blob. A purge of any other delta leaves nothing at a sync ID. The commit reads as a held commit without that delta's body: its author, actor, `ts` and retention stay, and whether to show it is the reader's choice.
- **Dropping a body** means destroying every local copy, its delta nonce and every local index entry derived from it. Holding one as its leftover destroys every copy of its values the same way; the leftover is a local body (never logged, deleted at peers as a dropped one).
  - What the part was judged stays: its validity, the role its writes needed, and for a `secrets.0` or `keys.0` delta the epochs it boxed or keyed, whose introducing commit spec/crypto.md §4 asks for, and the sealed names of its boxes to the holders its edge writes put, a grant's.
  - A leftover holding no value of its own writes (expired, purged) is never dropped for keep bounds; past its expiry it drops as covered (§5 Covered); a purged one is not dropped as covered. Otherwise it goes only with its thing (spec/permissions.md §3), as invalid, or for a copy of its body that holds values again (a fallen purge). A body held as its leftover only for the copies it carries is a body like a whole one for keep bounds and covering.
  - Exception: a compaction snapshot's part dropped other than as covered still counts for suspect flags (spec/permissions.md §4) from its header alone; a part dropped as covered need not (its coverer is valid and stamped no earlier). One held as its leftover is held.
- A dropped body cannot be undone (§7); under purge on replace others' resets beat undo.

### Fixed retention
- **Fixed retention**:
  - role-class deltas and `addenda.0`: `admin`, no history promised; each node keeps a replaced one `ROLE_KEEP` after replaced (a keep at least, §5 Keep bounds: compaction snapshots or later writes replace them; `roles.0` is never compaction-snapshotted, §2, so its deltas stay);
  - `retention^.0`: `nobody`, never out of history, never undone; it is never snapshotted (§2), carries no `expires` and goes with a commit of any purgeable-by;
  - `purge.0`: `author_or_admin`, no history promised, its snapshots too;
  - a certificate copy carries its commit's;
  - snapshots of role-class aspects keep it.
- Other compaction-snapshot deltas are `writer` (a receiver checks this); their history is the thing's setting, like any body's.
- No schema enters the bounds; a snapshot names the schema of the last applied delta of the aspect in `(ts, ID)` order that names one, else none.

### Destruction
- `created.0` has ordinary retention (its schema default is `admin`) and may carry `nobody`; it never leaves history.
- A record is identity and `created.0` is existence: a thing is known by its creation record, from a `created.0` delta or a record blob (spec/wire.md §3), and parts on it are judged by it; when it is destroyed, authority that passed through it stands as its frozen history says.
- A **minting** is a `created.0` delta; it needs no authority (spec/permissions.md §1). A node writes to a thing other than a key actor only while it holds a minting of it: one known by a record alone cannot be told from one that was destroyed.
- A thing whose every held minting is held value-less (expired, or purged by a request holding the right, spec/permissions.md §6) is **destroyed**. A purge item on `created.0` names every minting of its thing, whatever its `ts`: a thing is destroyed or not, so the only such item is the whole aspect (one by commit or by author is bad input, spec/wire.md §4).
- A destroyed thing's **destruction** is the point by which every minting of it is dropped, each at its `expires` or at the earliest request dropping it. A request's right to destroy a thing is judged without the edge writes on the thing stamped after the request (at a greater `ts`). **Nobody writes a thing after its destruction**: a part on a destroyed thing whose commit is stamped after its destruction (`wall_ms(ts) >= T` for an expiry, `ts` greater than the request's for a purge), other than a minting, is a part on an unknown thing, whatever its aspect. The destroyed thing: in the same transaction the node drops its states and every part on it as a body (spec/sync-ids.md §6 deletes them at peers that keep them unread) but its value-less `created.0` deltas, its role-class parts (no content) and the requests' `purge.0` parts, those written after its destruction dropped too. What stays is held by the ordinary rules alone: while the thing is in the node closure or a held thing needs it (What a held thing needs: a **dependent**, another thing whose authority paths it is on, by a held creation record naming it first admin or a held edge write naming it holder, or, for a key actor, a commit it signed or acted in), and it goes as any released thing; a request destroying it is a commit carrying a purge request, kept and logged as any (Forgetting; Purge: lasting), so a minting arriving later falls under it as any named delta. Parts on a destroyed thing arriving later are parts on an unknown thing (§4), but for its `created.0` deltas and, written before its destruction, its role-class parts and a request's `purge.0` part.
- Limit: a node that does not hold the thing (it never did, or released it) judges a minting reaching it as a node that never held the thing.
- A destroyed actor holds no keys: for keys its frozen holders stand in its place (spec/crypto.md §4, Holder actors, Destroyed), so what it held a role on is boxed to them without a write on it.
- What a thing needing a destroyed thing needs of it travels with it: its sync IDs carry the record, the frozen `roles.0` history and the destruction (spec/permissions.md §4), logged by its holders, and its parts whose read secret derives from the destroyed thing's as per-sync-ID copies (spec/sync-judgement.md §1), and a node not holding the actor takes them as it takes any released thing's certificates (A released thing), so it judges a dependent reaching it later as the others do. These certificates (under a sync ID whose sync unit lacks the destroyed thing, its `roles.0` parts and a request's `purge.0` part on it; under any sync ID, a minting's or a request's header alone that is no lasting header there) are judged by the sync ID's things needing it, whatever the node kept: must-not-hold once the node's view is settled (spec/sync-judgement.md §2) and no live thing of the sync unit needs it there, else may-hold.
- **A thing's mintings travel with its record**: wherever a thing's record goes as a certificate (spec/permissions.md §4), so does its **witness** and, of a destroyed thing, each held request dropping a minting of it, all as header-only copies (a minting's `expires` dates an expiry and its body is never logged once value-less; a request's header names what it destroys), each with its author's chain certificates.
  - A held minting's **end** is the earlier of its expiry and the earliest held request dropping it; it has none with neither. Ends are ordered: an expiry at `T` before a request whose `wall_ms(ts)` is at least `T`, after one stamped earlier; requests by (`ts`, author, commit ID); none last. Its **standing** is (valued, end): every minting not held value-less after every one that is, then by end. The witness is the held minting of the highest standing, ties by the lowest commit ID.
  - The thing is destroyed iff its witness is value-less, and its destruction is the witness's end: a part is written after it when `wall_ms(ts) >= T` for an expiry, `ts` greater than the request's for a request. So a node holding the witness (and, destroyed, the requests) judges the thing live or destroyed, and when, as one holding every minting does (a header alone is no destruction: a minting not expired and named by no held request may yet arrive), and refuses what was written on it after its destruction. One knowing a live thing by its record holds its witness beside any minting again, so a minting again destroys it there no more than at the nodes holding its creation (before the witness's copy arrives, one already expired reads it destroyed there until it does). A node holding an earlier witness holds the request that dropped it once the thing is destroyed.
  - A node logs a thing's witness under a sync ID only where no entry there holds a minting of the same standing. A kept copy stands in for a dropped one only if it cannot decide the thing's fate any earlier than the dropped one could: a header-only copy of a minting under a sync ID whose sync unit lacks the thing goes (must-not-hold, spec/sync-judgement.md §2) where it stands lower and an entry there holds a minting M of the witness's standing that expires no earlier (none: latest) and whose purge rights are contained in its own (spec/permissions.md §6: its set holds the admins, the author with the same header actor, every writer, each only where M's does), by any node judging so (one lacking a request ranks a minting no lower than one holding it does, so what is left reads the thing live while it is). A copy of a destroyed thing's witness or request a node logged before and that went is logged again while the sync ID needs the thing (Destruction): its requests travel with it, so every node judges the same witness. So the copies of a thing's mintings under a sync ID are its witness and its ties, whatever was minted, and only a right holder (spec/permissions.md §6) adds a request.
- A destroyed thing reads as absent to every read and write, its held record and request included.
- Nothing is kept per destroyed thing beyond what the ordinary rules keep. A copy of the creation arriving destroys the thing again.
- A destruction that falls brings the thing back only where a copy of it is still held or fetchable.
- Limit: anyone holding a thing's record may mint it again, stamped at any `ts` (an honest writer mints once, in its first commit). A durable minting keeps an expiring thing existing past its expiry or brings it back after it, and one whose retention the destroying request has no right over (`nobody`, another's `author`) keeps it from being destroyed by purge or brings it back. It never destroys a thing whose witness is held (A thing's mintings travel with its record), and travels only while it is the witness. It carries no content: the thing's other parts expire and are purged as before, and what a destruction dropped is back only where a copy is still held; brought back, the thing takes writes as any live thing, by its roster.

## 6. Expiry
- `expires: T` (wall ms, `T > wall_ms(ts)`, else invalid), one per commit: every delta of it expires at T. A commit carrying a fixed-retention aspect (§5) carries none. A content compaction snapshot carrying one is an expiring snapshot (spec/crdt.md §8): its copies end at T.
- A writer whose `ts` has reached the T it was given (its HLC ahead of its clock) writes `wall_ms(ts) + 1`.
- A default per AK as a duration from the write: the entry governing it in the newest valid setting the writer holds (§5 The setting), else the schema's. A commit is valid with the expiry it carries, whatever a setting says or later says.
- The blob prefix carries the commit's T (spec/sync-ids.md §1; spec/sync-judgement.md §1).
- A write is one commit. A commit creating a thing takes the commit's expiry on its `created.0`, so one commit is a whole thing that expires. A writer refuses, before anything applies, a write whose deltas want different expiries or that asks for an expiry beside a fixed-retention aspect; and one asking for durable, ephemeral and relay-only writes together (§2, spec/sync-compaction.md §2).
- At T every value-carrying write of the delta dies (spec/crdt.md §4.7): every node holds them value-less, the body and every held snapshot carrying a copy of their values as its leftover, whatever the delta's keep-at-least bound.
- A delta never received stays never received: a copy arriving after T joins value-less (held as its leftover from then on), and what a held snapshot gave under its dots goes with the snapshot (spec/permissions.md §5.2).
- Every node holding the blob, read or unread, deletes it at T unattended (spec/sync-ids.md §6), and the chunks of its linked header lists (spec/wire.md §5). A node holding the header keeps its lists with it.

## 7. Undo
- **Listed parts.** What a node shows of the commits on an aspect (a commit listing, spec/requirements.md API) is the **listed** parts: those in kept history (below), and this node's own invalid parts (marked invalid). A purged part is listed while it would be listed unpurged: not past its expiry (nor, a signal's, its keep bound), and its writes, held value-less, are in the aspect's current state or a body replaced at its request's `ts` would be in history; it shows as purged. Nothing else is listed, held or not: a body out of history, expired, dropped, or another node's invalid part. A commit is listed at an aspect while it has a listed part there.
- An undo commit (a revert) sets `undoes` to the undone commit's ID; its deltas are chosen per spec/crdt.md §4.6. An undo reverts every part of the commit or there is none: a commit with a valid part not in history (§5 The setting) nor kept while live (§5 Kept while live) by its thing's settings at the undo's `ts`, judged so whether or not the node holds its body, or whose body is not held (purged, expired, not received yet), cannot be undone (a signal's part, the creation record, a `retention^.0` part (never undone, §5), a `purge.0` part (it places a request, and a purge is never undone, §5 Purge) and parts on the things it created excepted: the undo writes nothing on those things and needs no body of theirs). So where no setting keeps history for an aspect, no other write there is undone. Redo is an undo of the undo commit. A signal reads absent before and after any write, so an undo writes nothing back for it (a purge request removes its writes, §5). An ephemeral commit is undone by an ephemeral undo commit, from the same kept history (§5) held in memory.
- **Kept history only.** An undo writes back content only from **kept history**: the held valid parts of the aspect ordered before the undone commit that have a write of their own in the aspect's current state (another part of the same body does not count), or whose body is in history (§5 The setting). A part neither is left out, whether or not the node holds it.
- Where the undone part's inverse writes back content it replaced (spec/crdt.md §4.6, by its writes' kinds), every part it may have replaced must be kept: each held valid part of the aspect ordered before it (by header, body held or not) is in kept history now, or (held or dropped, not one not received yet) the aspect's bodies replaced at the undone part's `ts` are in history now (one check per aspect), or is superseded by a part in kept history ordered before the undone part: a delta resetting the aspect's root or writing it as a register stamped after it, or, if it has writes of its own and no `expires`, a snapshot whose `Frontiers` reach its counters or whose `Absorbed` lists it (spec/crdt.md §8; a commit they do not name is not reached). A commit the node has forgotten (§5 Forgetting, spec/sync-compaction.md §2) counts, by a part on the aspect valid when it went, as a part not kept unless such a reset stamped after it is in kept history. Else there is no undo. Limit: one part out of history on the aspect (written before a setting kept history there, or past its bound) blocks such undos of every later part there until a part in kept history supersedes it. An undo only taking away what the part created needs no such check, only the undone part kept (above); its redo likewise needs the undo's parts kept.
